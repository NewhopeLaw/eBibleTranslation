# 2 Kings 8

¹ Elisha spoke to the woman who had raised her son, saying, “Get up, go with your household, and live wherever you can live, because the LORD has called for a famine, and it is coming to the land for seven years.” ² The woman got up and did as the man of God had said. She and her household went and lived in the land of the Philistines for seven years. ³ After the seven years had passed, the woman returned from the land of the Philistines and cried out to the king for her house and for her field. ⁴ The king was speaking to Gehazi, the servant of the man of God, saying, “Tell me now all the great things Elisha has done.” ⁵ As he was recounting to the king the story of the man who had revived the dead, there was the woman who had raised her son, crying out to the king about her house and her field. Gehazi said to the king, “This is the woman and this is her son, the one Elisha revived.” ⁶ The king asked the woman and she told him. The king gave her a eunuch, saying, “Return everything that belongs to her and all the produce of the field from the day she left the land until now.” ⁷ Elisha went to Damascus. Ben-Hadad, the king of Aram, was ill. It was reported to him, “The man of God has come to this place.” ⁸ The king said to Hazael, “Take a present in your hand and go meet the man of God and ask the LORD from him, saying, ‘Will I recover from this illness?’” ⁹ So Hazael went to meet him, carrying a present in his hand, and everything good from Damascus, a load of forty camels. He came and stood before him and said, “Your son, Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, sent me to you, saying, ‘Will I recover from this illness?’” ¹⁰ Elisha said to him, “Go, tell him, ‘You will live.’ But the LORD has shown me that he will surely die.” ¹¹ He set his face toward him, and Hazael was shamed. The man of God wept. ¹² Hazael said, “Why is my lord weeping?” He answered, “Because I know what evil you will do to the children of Israel. You will set fire to their strongholds, you will kill their chosen ones with the sword, you will dash their children to the ground, and you will rip open their pregnant women.” ¹³ Hazael replied, “What is your servant, this dead dog, that he could do such a great thing?” Elisha said, “The LORD has shown me that you will be king over Aram.” ¹⁴ He left Elisha and went to his master. He said to him, “What did Elisha say to you?” He replied, “He said to me, ‘You will live.’” ¹⁵ The next day, he took the pot and bathed in the water, then spread it over his face, and he died. Hazael became king in his place. ¹⁶ In the fifth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, and during the reign of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat became king of Judah. ¹⁷ He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign. He ruled in Jerusalem for eight years. ¹⁸ He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, just as the house of Ahab had done, because his wife, the daughter of Ahab, had become his wife. He did evil in the sight of the LORD. ¹⁹ Yet the LORD did not desire to destroy Judah for the sake of David His servant, as He had promised to give him a lamp for all the days. ²⁰ In his days, Edom rebelled from under the hand of Judah, and they set a king over themselves. ²¹ Joram went up with all his chariots, and at night he rose and defeated Edom who surrounded him and the commanders of the chariots. The people fled to their tents. ²² Edom rebelled from under the hand of Judah until this day. At that time, Libnah also rebelled. ²³ The rest of the acts of Joram and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? ²⁴ Joram rested with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David. Ahaziah his son became king in his place. ²⁵ In the twelfth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, Ahaziah son of Jehoram became king of Judah. ²⁶ Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he began to reign. He ruled in Jerusalem for one year. His mother’s name was Atalyah, daughter of Omri, king of Israel. ²⁷ He walked in the way of the house of Ahab and did evil in the sight of the LORD, just as the house of Ahab had done. He was a son-in-law of the house of Ahab. ²⁸ He went with Joram son of Ahab to war against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead. The Arameans struck Joram. ²⁹ The king returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds the Arameans had inflicted on him at Ramah, during his war with Hazael king of Aram. Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to see Joram son of Ahab, who was ill.

Notes
- v. 7: "Damascus" (דַּמֶּשֶׂק) is a proper name and is translated as such.
- v. 26: "Atalyah" (עֲתַלְיָהוּ) is a transliteration of the Hebrew name.
- v. 28-29: "Ramoth-gilead" (רָמֹת גִּלְעָד) and "Jezreel" (יִזְרְעֶאל) are proper names and are translated accordingly.
- v. 29: "Joram son of Ahab" and "Ahaziah son of Jehoram" are proper names and are translated as such, following the glossary.
