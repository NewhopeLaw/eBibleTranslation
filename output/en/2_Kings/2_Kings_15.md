# 2 Kings 15

¹ In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah king of Judah became king. ² He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jecheliah from Jerusalem. ³ He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done. ⁴ Yet the high places were still not removed, and the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on the high places. ⁵ The LORD struck the king, and he became a leper until the day of his death. He lived in a separate house, and his son Jotham ruled over his household and judged the people of the land. ⁶ The rest of the events of Azariah and all that he did—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. ⁷ Azariah rested with his ancestors and was buried with them in the City of David. Then his son Jotham became king in his place. ⁸ In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah king of Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam became king of Israel in Samaria for six months. ⁹ He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as his ancestors had done. He did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel astray. ¹⁰ Then Shallum son of Jabesh conspired against him and struck him down at Samaria in the citadel, along with the king's advisors Argob and Arieh, and fifty men of the Gileadites. He killed him and became king in his place. ¹¹ The rest of the events of Zechariah are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ¹² The word of the LORD had been spoken to Jehu saying, "Your descendants will sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation," and so it happened. ¹³ Shallum son of Jabesh became king. In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Shallum reigned in Samaria for one month. ¹⁴ Menahem son of Gadi came up from Tirzah and went to Samaria. He struck down Shallum son of Jabesh at Samaria and killed him, then became king in his place. ¹⁵ The rest of the events of Shallum and his conspiracy—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ¹⁶ Then Menahem went out and attacked Tiphsah and all its people, for it had not opened to him. He struck down all its pregnant women. ¹⁷ In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Menahem son of Gadi became king of Israel in Samaria for ten years. ¹⁸ He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, and he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel astray, all his days. ¹⁹ The king of Assyria, Pul, came into the land, and Menahem paid him a thousand talents of silver to have his hand with him and to keep the kingdom firm in his grasp. ²⁰ Menahem exacted this silver from Israel, from all the mighty men of valor, charging fifty shekels of silver from each man of Israel. So the king of Assyria withdrew and did not remain in the land. ²¹ The rest of the events of Menahem and all that he did—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ²² Menahem rested with his ancestors. Then his son Pekahiah became king in his place. ²³ In the fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem became king of Israel in Samaria for two years. ²⁴ He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, and he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel astray. ²⁵ Then Pekah son of Remaliah conspired against him. He struck him down at Ijon in the citadel, along with Argob and Arieh, and with fifty men of the Gileadites. He killed him and became king in his place. ²⁶ The rest of the events of Pekahiah and all that he did—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ²⁷ In the fifty-second year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah became king of Israel in Samaria for twenty years. ²⁸ He did evil in the eyes of the LORD and did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel astray. ²⁹ In the days of Pekah king of Israel, Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria came and captured Gilead, along with Abel-beth-maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead, Galilee, and all the land of Naphtali. He carried them away to Assyria. ³⁰ Then Hoshea son of Elah conspired against Pekah son of Remaliah. He struck him down and killed him, becoming king in his place in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah. ³¹ The rest of the events of Pekah and all that he did—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ³² In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel, Jotham son of Uzziah became king of Judah. ³³ He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jerushah daughter of Zadok. ³⁴ He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Uzziah had done. ³⁵ Yet the high places were still not removed, and the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on the high places. He built the upper gate of the house of the LORD. ³⁶ The rest of the events of Jotham and all that he did—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. ³⁷ In those days, the LORD began to send Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah. ³⁸ Jotham rested with his ancestors and was buried with them in the City of David his father. Then his son Ahaz became king in his place.

Notes
- v. 5: "the house of the leper" has been translated as "a separate house," since "leper" is likely a title here, not a description of the king's condition.
- v. 16: "struck down all its pregnant women" is a direct translation of "all its womb" (Hebrew: כָּל־הֶהָרוֹתֶיהָ), which is a rare and graphic expression.
- v. 35: "upper gate of the house of the LORD" reflects the specific reference to "the upper gate of the house of the LORD" (Hebrew: שַׁעַר בֵּית־יְהוָה הָעֶלְיוֹן).
