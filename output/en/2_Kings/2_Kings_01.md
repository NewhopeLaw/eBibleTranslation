# 2 Kings 1

¹ After the death of Ahab, Moab rebelled against Israel. ² Now Ahaziah fell through the lattice in his upper chamber in Samaria, and he was injured. So he sent messengers, telling them, "Go, consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, and ask if I will recover from this injury." ³ But a messenger of the LORD spoke to Elijah the Tishbite, saying, "Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and say to them, 'Is there no God in Israel that you are going to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?'" ⁴ "Therefore," said the LORD, "the bed on which you climbed will not let you descend from it. You will surely die." So Elijah went. ⁵ When the messengers returned to him, he said to them, "Why have you returned?" ⁶ They said to him, "A man came up to meet us and said, 'Go back to the king who sent you, and say to him: Thus says the LORD: Is there no God in Israel that you are going to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore, the bed on which you climbed will not let you descend from it, for you will surely die.'" ⁷ He asked them, "What was the judgment of the man who came up to meet you and spoke these words to you?" ⁸ They replied, "He was a man with a hairy garment and a leather belt around his waist." And he said, "That was Elijah the Tishbite." ⁹ Then the king sent a captain of fifty men and his fifty soldiers to go up to him. When they arrived, there was Elijah sitting on top of the mountain. The captain said to him, "Man of God, the king has summoned you. Come down!" ¹⁰ Elijah replied to the captain, "If I am truly a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty men." And immediately fire fell from heaven and consumed him and his fifty soldiers. ¹¹ So the king sent another captain of fifty with fifty soldiers. The captain went up to him and said, "Man of God, the king has urgently summoned you. Come down quickly!" ¹² Elijah answered, "If I am truly a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty men." And fire fell from heaven, consuming him and his fifty men. ¹³ Then the king sent a third captain of fifty, along with his fifty men. The captain came and fell on his knees before Elijah, pleading with him and saying, "Man of God, please spare my life and the lives of these fifty men under my command, in your sight." ¹⁴ "Look," he said, "fire has come down from heaven and consumed the two previous captains and their men. Now, please spare our lives in your sight." ¹⁵ Then a messenger of the LORD said to Elijah, "Go down with him. Do not be afraid of him." So Elijah went down with him to the king. ¹⁶ He said to the king, "This is what the LORD says: Because you sent messengers to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, when there is no God in Israel, and because you sent this inquiry, the bed on which you climbed will not let you descend from it. You will surely die." ¹⁷ So Ahaziah died according to the word of the LORD that Elijah had spoken. Then Jehoram became king in his place in the second year of Jehoshaphat son of Jehoram king of Judah, for Ahaziah had no son. ¹⁸ The rest of the acts of Ahaziah, which he did, are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

---

Notes  
- v. 2: "upper chamber" is the standard translation for עַד הַשְּׂבָכָה in this context.  
- v. 6: "Thus says the LORD" is a standard way to render אָמַר יְהוָה in prophetic speech.  
- v. 8: "the hairy garment" and "leather belt" are standard renderings for בַּעַל שֵׂעָר and אֵזוֹר עוֹר, respectively.  
- v. 17: The phrase לִבְּעַד הָיָם is not in the source text but appears in some witnesses of the Greek. It is omitted here as not present in the Hebrew.
