# 2 Kings 16

¹ In the seventeenth year of Pekah son of Remaliah, Ahaz son of Jotham became king of Judah. ² Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD his God, as David his father had done. ³ He walked in the way of the kings of Israel and even caused his son to pass through fire, following the detestable practices of the nations whom the LORD had driven out before the Israelites. ⁴ He burned sacrifices and offered incense on the high places, on the hills, and under every green tree. ⁵ Then Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel went up to Jerusalem to make war on it, and they besieged Ahaz, but could not defeat him. ⁶ At that time Rezin king of Aram recovered Elath for Aram, and the Judeans were driven from Elath, and the Edomites came to Elath and have lived there to this day. ⁷ Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, saying, "I am your servant and your son. Please come up and rescue me from the hand of the king of Aram and from the hand of the king of Israel, who are attacking me." ⁸ Ahaz took the silver and gold that were found in the house of the LORD and in the treasuries of the king’s palace and sent it as a bribe to the king of Assyria. ⁹ The king of Assyria responded and went up to Damascus. He captured the city and executed Rezin. ¹⁰ Then King Ahaz went to meet Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria in Damascus. He saw the altar that was in Damascus and sent to Uriah the priest the design and pattern of the altar, with all its details. ¹¹ Uriah the priest built the altar according to all that King Ahaz had sent from Damascus, and he did so until the king returned from Damascus. ¹² When the king came back from Damascus, he saw the altar and approached it, offering sacrifices on it. ¹³ He offered his burnt offering and his grain offering, poured out his drink offering, and sprinkled the blood of his peace offerings on the altar. ¹⁴ He also moved the bronze altar that was before the LORD from its place, from the front of the house and from the house of the LORD, and placed it at the side of the great altar. ¹⁵ The king gave orders to Uriah the priest, saying, "On the great altar offer the morning burnt offering and the evening grain offering, the king’s burnt offering and his grain offering, and the burnt offering and grain offering of all the people of the land, with their drink offerings. And on it you shall pour out all the blood of the burnt offerings and of the sacrifices. The bronze altar will be for me to inspect." ¹⁶ Uriah the priest did everything the king had commanded. ¹⁷ The king cut down the paneling of the doors and removed the basin from them. He took down the great bronze basin that stood on the cows beneath it and placed it on a stone pavement. ¹⁸ He also removed the Sabbath canopy that had been built in the house and the outer entrance to the king, turning them in the house of the LORD away from the face of the king of Assyria. ¹⁹ The rest of the events of Ahaz and all that he did are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. ²⁰ Ahaz rested with his ancestors and was buried with them in the City of David. Then his son Hezekiah became king in his place.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "the design and pattern of the altar" — refers to a detailed model or description sent to the priest for replication.
- v. 14: "moved the bronze altar that was before the LORD" — this altar is likely the original one in the temple, replaced by a new altar modeled after the one in Damascus.
- v. 15: "the bronze altar will be for me to inspect" — implies the king wanted to retain the old altar for surveillance or control.
