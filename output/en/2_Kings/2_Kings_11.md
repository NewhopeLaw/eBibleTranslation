# 2 Kings 11

¹ Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead, and she set out to destroy all the royal offspring. ² She took Jehoshabeath, the daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah, and took Joash the son of Ahaziah and hid him from among the sons of the dead king, along with his nurse, in the inner chambers of the palace and kept him from Athaliah so that he was not killed. ³ He remained with her in the temple of the LORD for six years. Athaliah reigned over the land. ⁴ In the seventh year, Jehoida sent and took the commanders of hundreds, the Carites and the guards, and brought them to him in the temple of the LORD. He made a covenant with them and swore them in the temple of the LORD. Then he showed them the son of the king. ⁵ He gave them these instructions: "This is what you are to do: A third of you, who are on duty at the Sabbath, are to guard the palace of the king. ⁶ A third is to be at the gate of the outer court, and a third at the gate behind the guards. You are to guard the house as if it were a fortress. ⁷ And you two companies are to be with you—every man going out on the Sabbath shall be under your protection, guarding the temple of the LORD for the king. ⁸ You shall surround the king on all sides, each man with his weapon in his hand. Anyone who comes into the inner court is to be put to death. Be with the king when he goes out and when he comes in." ⁹ The commanders of hundreds did all that Jehoida the priest had instructed them. Each man took his men—those going on duty and those coming off duty—and they came to Jehoida the priest. ¹⁰ The priest gave the commanders of hundreds the royal insignia—trophies and shields of King David. ¹¹ The guards stood, each man with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the temple to the left side, near the altar and the temple, surrounding the king. ¹² They brought out the son of the king, put the crown on his head and the signet ring on him, and made him king. They anointed him, and they clapped their hands and shouted, "Long live the king!" ¹³ When Athaliah heard the noise of the people running toward the temple of the LORD, ¹⁴ she came to the people in the temple of the LORD. She looked and there was the king standing on the pillar by the law, and the captains with their trumpets blowing to the king, and the whole people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried out, "Treason! Treason!" ¹⁵ Jehoida the priest gave orders to the commanders of the hundreds, the officers of the army, saying, "Take her out of the ranks and let whoever follows her be put to death with the sword. For the priest said, Let her not be put to death in the temple of the LORD." ¹⁶ They laid hands on her and led her through the entrance of the horses to the king’s house, where she was put to death. ¹⁷ Jehoida made a covenant between the LORD, the king, and the people, that they would be the people of the LORD, between the king and the people. ¹⁸ All the people of the land went to the temple of Baal, and they broke down his altars and his images and killed Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars. The priest placed officers over the temple of the LORD. ¹⁹ He took the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, and the guards, and the whole people of the land, and brought the king down from the temple of the LORD through the gate of the guards to the king’s house. He seated him on the throne of the kings. ²⁰ The whole people of the land and the city were at peace. Athaliah they had put to death with the sword in the king’s house.

Notes
- v. 10: "trophies and shields" renders חֲנִית וְשֶׁלְטִים, which are likely royal insignia or weapons.
- v. 15: "let whoever follows her be put to death with the sword" reflects the Hebrew command to prevent her death within the temple precincts, preserving its sanctity.
- v. 18: "killed Mattan the priest of Baal" is a direct rendering of the Hebrew text, which names the priest and his fate.
- v. 19: "the gate of the guards" is a contextual rendering of שַׁעַר הָרָצִים, referring to a specific entrance used by the guards.
