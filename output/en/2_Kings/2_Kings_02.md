# 2 Kings 2

¹ When the LORD took Elijah up in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha left from Gilgal. ² Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, for the LORD has sent me to Bethel." Elisha said, "As the LORD lives, and as your life lives, I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel. ³ The prophets from Bethel came out to meet Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that the LORD is taking your master away from you today?" Elisha said, "I know, be quiet." ⁴ Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, for the LORD has sent me to Jericho." Elisha said, "As the LORD lives, and as your life lives, I will not leave you." So they went to Jericho. ⁵ The prophets from Jericho came out to meet Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that the LORD is taking your master away from you today?" Elisha said, "I know, be quiet." ⁶ Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, for the LORD has sent me to the Jordan." Elisha said, "As the LORD lives, and as your life lives, I will not leave you." So they both went on. ⁷ Fifty of the prophets went with them and stood from a distance, while the two of them stood by the Jordan. ⁸ Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the water. The water was split in two sides, and they crossed on dry ground. ⁹ As they were going, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I should do for you before I am taken away from you." Elisha said, "May a double measure of your spirit be on me." ¹⁰ Elijah said, "You have asked a hard thing. If you see me taken from you, it will be so. If not, it will not be." ¹¹ As they were walking on, speaking, suddenly there appeared a chariot of fire and horses of fire, which separated the two of them. Elijah was taken up in the whirlwind to the heavens. ¹² Elisha watched and cried out, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horses!" Yet he could not see him anymore. He took hold of his own garments and tore them into two pieces. ¹³ He picked up the cloak that had fallen from Elijah and went back to the Jordan. ¹⁴ He took the cloak of Elijah that had fallen from him, struck the water, and said, "Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?" He struck the water, and it split in two sides, and Elisha crossed over. ¹⁵ The prophets who were at Jericho saw him from a distance and said, "The spirit of Elijah has settled on Elisha." So they came to meet him and bowed to the ground before him. ¹⁶ They said to him, "Look, your servants are fifty strong men. Let us go and search for your master. Perhaps the spirit of the LORD has lifted him and cast him on one of the mountains or in one of the valleys." He said, "You must not send." ¹⁷ They persisted until he was shamed, and he said, "Go." They sent fifty men, who searched for three days but could not find him. ¹⁸ They returned to him, and he was sitting in Jericho. He said to them, "Did I not tell you not to go?" ¹⁹ The people of the city said to Elisha, "Now, the settlement of this city is good in your sight, but the water is bad, and the land is barren." ²⁰ He said, "Bring me a new jar and put salt in it." They brought it to him. ²¹ He went out to the source of the water and threw the salt into it, saying, "Thus says the LORD, I have made this water wholesome. It will no longer bring death or barrenness." ²² The water has remained wholesome to this day, as Elisha spoke. ²³ He went from there to Bethel. While he was going up the road, some children came out of the city and mocked him, saying, "Go up, baldhead! Go up, baldhead!" ²⁴ He turned back, looked at them, and cursed them in the name of the LORD. Then two female bears came out of the forest and mauled forty-two of the children. ²⁵ He left from there and went to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

---

Notes
- v. 24: "mauled" is used as a natural English rendering of the Hebrew root נָבַל, which can mean "to tear" or "to maul" depending on context.
