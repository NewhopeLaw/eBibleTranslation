# 2 Kings 3

¹ Jehoram son of Ahab became king of Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years. ² He did evil in the sight of the LORD, though not as his father and mother had done. He removed the stone of Baal that his father had made. ³ Yet in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nabat, who led Israel astray, he clung to them and did not turn away from them. ⁴ Mesha king of Moab was a shepherd, and he paid Israel a tribute of a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams, with their wool. ⁵ When Ahab died, the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel. ⁶ King Jehoram went out from Samaria the same day and surveyed all Israel. ⁷ Then he sent to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, "The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me to Moab for war?" He said, "I will go, as you and I, as your people and my people, as your horses and my horses." ⁸ He asked, "Which way is the road?" He said, "The road through the wilderness of Edom." ⁹ So the king of Israel went, and the king of Judah, and the king of Edom, and they encircled the road for seven days. But there was no water for the camp or for the animals following them. ¹⁰ The king of Israel said, "Alas! The LORD has called these three kings to give them into the hands of Moab." ¹¹ Jehoshaphat said, "Is there a prophet of the LORD here, so that we might inquire of the LORD from him?" One of the servants of the king of Israel answered, "Here is Elisha son of Shaphat, who poured water on the hands of Elijah." ¹² Jehoshaphat said, "That is the word of the LORD." So they went down to him — the king of Israel, and Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and the king of Edom. ¹³ Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What do I have to do with you? Go to the prophets of your father and the prophets of your mother." The king of Israel said to him, "No, for the LORD has called these three kings to give them into the hands of Moab." ¹⁴ Elisha said, "By the LORD of Hosts, before whom I stand, were it not for the face of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would not look to you or see you." ¹⁵ Then he said, "Bring me a musician." And as the musician played, the hand of the LORD rested on him. ¹⁶ Elisha said, "Thus says the LORD: Make this wadi into banks of earth." ¹⁷ "For thus says the LORD: You will see no wind and no rain, but this wadi will be filled with water, and you and your livestock and your animals will drink." ¹⁸ And it will be light in the eyes of the LORD, and he will give Moab into your hands. ¹⁹ "You will strike every fortified city and every choice tree and every spring of water, and you will fill every good field with stones." ²⁰ In the morning, as the offering was going up, behold, water came from the way of Edom, and the land was filled with water. ²¹ All Moab heard that the kings had gone up to fight against them, and they cried out — every man girding on his belt and lifting up his weapon — and they stood on the border. ²² When they got up in the morning, the sun was shining on the water, and Moab saw the water from afar, red as blood. ²³ They said, "This is blood — the kings have perished by the sword. Let us go and plunder Moab." ²⁴ So they came into the camp of Israel, and Israel arose and struck Moab, and they fled before them. Israel continued to pursue and destroy them. ²⁵ They destroyed the cities, and every good field they filled with stones, every man casting a stone upon it and filling it. Every spring they stopped up, and every good tree they cut down until the stones of the wall lay bare, and the men with slings surrounded and struck them. ²⁶ The king of Moab saw that the battle was too strong for him, so he took seven hundred men who could wield swords and tried to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not. ²⁷ He took his firstborn son, the one who would reign after him, and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. Then a great wrath came upon Israel, and they withdrew from him and returned to their land.

Notes
- v. 14: "By the LORD of Hosts..." — The phrase יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת (YHWH Tseva'ot) is translated as "the LORD of Hosts."
- v. 16: "banks of earth" — The Hebrew גֵּבִים (gevim) is translated as "banks" in the context of damming the wadi.
- v. 22: "red as blood" — The Hebrew אֲדֻמִּים כַּדָּֽם (adumim kadam) is rendered as "red as blood."
