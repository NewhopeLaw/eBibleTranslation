# 2 Kings 6

¹ The sons of the prophets said to Elisha, “Now the place where we are sitting before you is too narrow for us.” ² “Let us go to the Jordan, and take from there one beam each, and build a place for ourselves there to dwell.” He said, “Go.” ³ One of them said, “Please, let me go with your servants.” He said, “You may go.” ⁴ He went with them, and they went to the Jordan and cut down trees. ⁵ One of them, while felling a beam, let the iron tool slip into the water and cried out, “Ah, my master! Look, it’s lost!” He was searching for it. ⁶ The man of God asked, “Where did it fall?” When he showed him the spot, Elisha cut down a tree and threw it into the water. The iron floated up. ⁷ He said, “Lift it up!” He reached out and took it. ⁸ Now the king of Aram was waging war against Israel, and he consulted with his servants, saying, “To what place should I set an ambush?” ⁹ Elisha sent to the king of Israel, saying, “Be careful not to pass through this place, for there the Arameans are lying in ambush.” ¹⁰ The king of Israel sent to the place Elisha had told him about, and he guarded it, not once or twice. ¹¹ The heart of the king of Aram was stirred by this and he said to his servants, “Who among us is giving this information to the king of Israel?” ¹² One of his servants said, “It is Elisha the prophet of Israel who tells the king of Israel what you say in your private chamber.” ¹³ He said, “Then go and find out where he is. I will send and take him.” They were told, “He is in Dothan.” ¹⁴ He sent horses, chariots, and a large army there at night, and they surrounded the city. ¹⁵ Elisha’s servant got up early in the morning and went out. When he saw the army surrounding the city, with horses and chariots, he said, “Ah, my master! What will we do?” ¹⁶ Elisha said, “Do not fear, for those with us are more numerous than those with them.” ¹⁷ Elisha prayed and said, “O LORD, please open his eyes so he may see.” The LORD opened the eyes of the young man, and he looked and saw that the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire around Elisha. ¹⁸ Then the Arameans came down to him, and Elisha prayed to the LORD and said, “Strike this people blind.” He struck them blind, just as Elisha said. ¹⁹ Elisha said to them, “This is not the city, nor this the road. Follow me, and I will lead you to the man you are seeking.” He led them to Samaria. ²⁰ When they came to Samaria, Elisha said, “O LORD, please open their eyes so they may see.” The LORD opened their eyes, and they looked and saw that they were in the middle of Samaria. ²¹ The king of Israel said to Elisha when he saw them, “My father, should I strike them?” ²² He said, “Do not strike them, for the ones you have captured with your sword and bow you should strike. But instead, set food and water before them, and let them eat and drink, and then send them back to their master.” ²³ He prepared a great feast for them, and they ate and drank. Then he sent them back to their master. From then on, the Aramean raiding bands did not come into the land of Israel. ²⁴ After this, Ben-hadad the king of Aram gathered his entire army and went up, laying siege to Samaria. ²⁵ A great famine arose in Samaria, so severe that a donkey’s head was worth eighty shekels of silver, and a fourth of a kab of dove’s dung was worth five shekels. ²⁶ Now the king of Israel was walking along the wall, when a woman cried out to him, “Save me, my lord the king!” ²⁷ He said to her, “By the LORD, I cannot save you. From where will I save you? Is it from the granary or from the winepress?” ²⁸ The king said to her, “What is the matter with you?” She said, “This woman said to me, ‘Give me your son, and we will eat him today, and tomorrow we will eat my son.’” ²⁹ “So I cooked my son and ate him,” she said, “and I told her, ‘Tomorrow you give me your son, and we will eat him.’ But she hid her son.” ³⁰ When the king heard the words of the woman, he tore his clothes, and he was walking along the wall. The people saw that the sack was on his flesh from inside. ³¹ He said, “Let the LORD do so to me, and more, if the head of Elisha the son of Shaphat remains today on his body.” ³² Elisha was sitting in his house, with the elders sitting with him. An messenger was sent to him before the messenger arrived. Elisha said to the elders, “Have you seen how this son of a murderer has sent to take my head? Watch when the messenger comes—shut the door and bar him at the door. Do you not hear the sound of your master’s footsteps behind him?” ³³ He was still speaking with them, when suddenly the messenger came down to him. He said, “This calamity is from the LORD. What hope do I have yet from the LORD?”

Notes
- v. 28: "This woman said to me" — the Hebrew reads "the woman said to me," but the context of the woman's accusation against another woman suggests the definite article is appropriate here.
