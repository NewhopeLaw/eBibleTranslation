# 2 Kings 20

¹ In those days, Hezekiah was near death. Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet came to him and said, "This is what the LORD says: Give orders for your household, for you are going to die and not live." ² Hezekiah turned his face toward the wall and prayed to the LORD, saying: ³ "O LORD, remember how I have walked before you in truth and with a loyal heart, and how I have done what is pleasing in your sight. And Hezekiah wept bitterly." ⁴ Isaiah had not yet left the middle court when a message from the LORD came to him, saying: ⁵ "Return and tell Hezekiah, leader of my people: This is what the LORD, the God of David your father, says: I have heard your prayer and seen your tears. I will heal you. On the third day you will go up to the house of the LORD. ⁶ "I will add fifteen years to your life and rescue you from the hand of the king of Assyria. I will defend this city for my sake and for the sake of David my servant." ⁷ Isaiah said, "Bring a poultice of figs." They brought it and placed it on his boil, and he recovered. ⁸ Hezekiah asked Isaiah, "What is the sign that the LORD will heal me and that I will go up to the house of the LORD on the third day?" ⁹ Isaiah said, "This is the sign from the LORD that he will do what he has said: the shadow will advance ten steps. If it turns back ten steps." ¹⁰ Hezekiah said, "It is easy for the shadow to advance ten steps. No, let the shadow turn back ten steps on the stair by the sundial of Ahaz." ¹¹ Isaiah the prophet cried out to the LORD, and the shadow turned back on the stair by the sundial of Ahaz, retreating ten steps. ¹² At that time, Merodach-baladan son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a present to Hezekiah because he had heard that Hezekiah was ill. ¹³ Hezekiah welcomed them and showed them everything in his house: the silver, the gold, the spices, the fine oil, and his storehouses of arms and all that was found in his treasuries. There was no item in his palace or throughout his realm that Hezekiah did not show them. ¹⁴ Then Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah and asked, "What did these men say, and where did they come from to you?" Hezekiah said, "They came from a distant land, from Babylon." ¹⁵ Isaiah asked, "What did they see in your house?" Hezekiah replied, "They saw everything in my house. There was no item in my house or in my treasuries that I did not show them." ¹⁶ Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, "Hear the word of the LORD. ¹⁷ "Look, days are coming when everything in your house and all that your ancestors stored up until this day will be carried off to Babylon. Nothing will be left," says the LORD. ¹⁸ "And some of your sons who are born to you will be taken and become eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon." ¹⁹ Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "Good is the word of the LORD that you have spoken." And he said, "Is there not peace and truth in my days?" ²⁰ The rest of the acts of Hezekiah and all his might, and how he made the pool and the aqueduct to bring water into the city—these are written in the scroll of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. ²¹ Hezekiah rested with his ancestors, and his son Manasseh became king in his place.
