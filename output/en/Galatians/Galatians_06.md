# Galatians 6

¹ Brothers, if someone is caught in a transgression, you who are spiritual should restore that person in a spirit of gentleness. Watch yourself, so that you too are not tempted. ² Carry one another's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of the Messiah. ³ For if anyone thinks they are something when in fact they are nothing, they deceive themselves. ⁴ Each one should examine their own work, and then they will have reason to boast only in themselves and not in another. ⁵ For each one will bear their own load. ⁶ Let the one who is taught the word share all good things with the one who teaches. ⁷ Do not be deceived. God is not mocked, for whatever a person sows, that will they also reap. ⁸ For the one sowing into their flesh will from the flesh reap destruction, but the one sowing into the Spirit will from the Spirit reap eternal life. ⁹ Let us not then grow weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap if we do not give up. ¹⁰ So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to all, especially to those who belong to the household of faith. ¹¹ See how large letters I am writing to you with my own hand. ¹² Those who want to make a good showing in the flesh are the ones who are trying to force you to be circumcised, only so that they may not be persecuted for the cross of the Messiah. ¹³ For even those who are circumcised do not themselves keep the law, but they want you to be circumcised so that they may boast in your flesh. ¹⁴ But to me, let it never be that I should boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus the Messiah, by which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world. ¹⁵ For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but a new creation. ¹⁶ And all who walk by this rule, peace and mercy be upon them and upon the Israel of God. ¹⁷ From now on, let no one cause me trouble, for I bear on my body the marks of Jesus. ¹⁸ The grace of our Lord Jesus the Messiah be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.

---

Notes
- v. 15: "a new creation" is a translation of καινὴ κτίσις, which Paul uses to emphasize spiritual rebirth over physical distinctions.
- v. 16: "the Israel of God" reflects the Greek Ἰσραὴλ τοῦ θεοῦ, likely indicating God’s renewed covenant community beyond ethnic Israel.
- v. 17: "the marks of Jesus" renders τὰ στίγματα τοῦ Ἰησοῦ, possibly referring to Paul's physical sufferings or scars from persecution.
