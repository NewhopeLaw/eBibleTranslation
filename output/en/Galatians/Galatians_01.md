# Galatians 1

¹ Paul, an apostle—not from human beings, nor through human beings, but through Jesus the Messiah and God the Father, the one who raised him from the dead, ² and all the brothers with me, to the assemblies in Galatia: ³ Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus the Messiah, ⁴ who gave himself for our sins so that he might rescue us from the present evil age in accordance with the will of our God and Father. ⁵ To him be glory forever and ever. Amen. ⁶ I am astonished that you are turning away so quickly from the one who called you in the grace of Christ to a different gospel. ⁷ For there is no other gospel; only some are disturbing you and wanting to pervert the gospel of the Messiah. ⁸ But even if we or an angel from heaven should preach to you a gospel contrary to what we preached to you, let that one be accursed. ⁹ As we have said before, so now I say again: if anyone preaches to you a gospel contrary to what you received, let that one be accursed. ¹⁰ Am I now trying to please people or God? Or do I seek to please people? For if I still sought to please people, I would not be a servant of Christ. ¹¹ For I want you to know, brothers, the gospel that I proclaimed to you is not according to human beings. ¹² For I did not receive it from human beings, nor was I taught it, but through a revelation of Jesus the Messiah. ¹³ You have heard, of course, about my former way of life in Judaism, how I used to persecute the assembly of God and destroy it. ¹⁴ And I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my contemporaries among my own people, being even more zealous for my ancestral traditions. ¹⁵ But when it pleased the one who set me apart from my mother’s womb and called me through his grace ¹⁶ to reveal his Son in me so that I might preach him among the Gentiles, I did not go at once to human beings or authorities, ¹⁷ nor did I go up to Jerusalem to the ones who had been apostles before me, but I went to Arabia, and then I returned to Damascus. ¹⁸ Then, after three years, I went up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas, and I stayed with him fifteen days. ¹⁹ I saw no other apostles except James, the Lord’s brother. ²⁰ What I write to you, behold, I testify before God that I am not lying. ²¹ Then I came to the regions of Syria and Cilicia. ²² I was unknown in person to the assemblies of Judea in Christ, ²³ but they had heard only that the one who formerly persecuted us is now preaching the faith he once tried to destroy. ²⁴ And they glorified God in me.

---

Notes
- v. 4: The phrase “the present evil age” translates “αἰῶνος τοῦ ἐνεστῶτος πονηροῦ,” which is a common New Testament expression for the current corrupt epoch.
- v. 5: The term “αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων” is translated as “forever and ever,” a standard rendering for the Greek phrase.
- v. 11: “Not according to human beings” reflects the contrast Paul draws between divine revelation and human instruction.
