# Galatians 3

¹ Oh foolish Galatians, who has bewitched you, considering before your eyes Jesus Christ was crucified? ² I want to know from you only this: Did you receive the Spirit from works of the law, or from hearing with faith? ³ Are you so foolish? After beginning with the Spirit, are you now being made complete by flesh? ⁴ So many sufferings have you endured in vain—if indeed in vain. ⁵ Then the one who is giving the Spirit to you and working mighty works among you—does he do it from works of the law, or from hearing with faith? ⁶ Just as Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him for righteousness. ⁷ You therefore know that those who are from faith, these are sons of Abraham. ⁸ For the Scripture, foreseeing that God would declare the nations righteous from faith, had already preached the good news to Abraham, saying, "In you all the nations will be blessed." ⁹ So those who are from faith are blessed with the faithful Abraham. ¹⁰ For all who are from works of the law are under a curse, for it is written, "Cursed is everyone who does not abide by all things written in the book of the law, to do them." ¹¹ But it is clear that no one is justified by the law before God, for "The righteous will live by faith." ¹² But the law is not of faith, for "The one who does them will live by them." ¹³ Christ has redeemed us from the curse of the law having become a curse for us, for it is written, "Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree," ¹⁴ in order that the blessing of Abraham might come upon the nations in Christ Jesus, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith. ¹⁵ Brothers, I speak according to human terms: Once a human being has ratified a covenant, no one puts it aside or adds to it. ¹⁶ Now the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed. It does not say, "And to the seeds," as though referring to many, but as referring to one, "And to your seed," who is Christ. ¹⁷ I say this: The covenant already ratified by God, the law, which came four hundred and thirty years later, does not nullify it to invalidate the promise. ¹⁸ For if the inheritance were from the law, it would no longer be from the promise. But God gave it to Abraham by promise. ¹⁹ Why then the law? It was added because of transgressions, until the seed would come to whom the promise was made, and it was ordained through angels and by a mediator. ²⁰ But a mediator is not of one, but God is one. ²¹ Is the law then contrary to the promises of God? May it never be! For if a law had been given that could give life, then indeed righteousness would be from the law. ²² But the Scripture has shut everyone under sin, so that the promise by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe. ²³ Before faith came, we were being held in custody under the law, having been imprisoned until the coming faith was revealed. ²⁴ So the law became our guardian to lead us to Christ, so that we might be justified by faith. ²⁵ But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian. ²⁶ For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus. ²⁷ For as many as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ. ²⁸ There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is neither male nor female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus. ²⁹ And if you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "from works of the law" and "from hearing with faith" reflect the contrast between legalism and faith central to Paul's argument.
- v. 13: "having become a curse for us" is a paraphrase of the original's "having become a curse," but the context clarifies it is Christ who bears the curse.
- v. 28: "there is neither... nor..." structure is used to reflect the original Greek's anaphora and parallelism.
- v. 29: "according to the promise" reflects the Greek "κατὰ ἐπαγγελίαν," indicating the nature of the inheritance.
