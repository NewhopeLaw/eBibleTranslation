# Galatians 5

¹ Christ set us free for freedom. Stand firm then, and do not again submit to the yoke of slavery. ² Listen, I Paul say to you that if you are circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing. ³ I testify again to every person who is circumcised that he is obligated to do the whole law. ⁴ You who are justified through the law have become estranged from Christ, and you have fallen away from grace. ⁵ For we through the Spirit, by faith, eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness. ⁶ For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but faith working through love. ⁷ You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth? ⁸ That persuasion did not come from the one calling you. ⁹ A little leaven leavens the whole batch. ¹⁰ I am confident in the Lord that you will have no other mind. But the one disturbing you will bear his judgment, whoever he may be. ¹¹ But I, brothers, if I still preach circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the offense of the cross has been abolished. ¹² Let those who trouble you be cut off. ¹³ For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but serve one another through love. ¹⁴ For the whole law is fulfilled in one word: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself." ¹⁵ But if you bite and devour one another, watch that you are not consumed by one another. ¹⁶ I say, walk in the Spirit, and you will not fulfill the desires of the flesh. ¹⁷ For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh. These are opposed to one another, so that you may not do what you want. ¹⁸ But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under law. ¹⁹ Now the works of the flesh are plain, which are fornication, uncleanness, sensuality, ²⁰ idolatry, sorcery, enmity, strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, rivalries, divisions, factions, ²¹ envy, drunkenness, revelry, and the like, of which I say in advance, just as I said before, that those who do such things will not inherit the kingdom of God. ²² But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, ²³ gentleness, self-control. Against these there is no law. ²⁴ And those who belong to Christ have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires. ²⁵ If we live by the Spirit, let us also walk by the Spirit. ²⁶ Let us not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness" — the Greek "περιπατεῖν" (to walk) is metaphorically rendered as "eagerly wait for" to reflect the dynamic expectation in the context of righteousness.
- v. 16: "you will not fulfill" — the Greek "τὴν ἐπιθυμίαν" is translated as "the desires" to avoid ambiguity about the subject of the fulfillment.
- v. 24: "have crucified the flesh" — the phrase is rendered as a past tense action, consistent with the aorist tense in the Greek, to emphasize the decisive nature of the action.
