# Hebrews 8

¹ Now the subject of the things we have spoken about has this kind of high priest, who has taken His seat at the right hand of the throne of the majesty in the heavens, ² a minister of the holy place and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord set up, not a human being. ³ For every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices. Therefore, it is necessary for Him to have something to offer. ⁴ But if He were on earth, He would not be a priest, since those offering gifts according to the law are already present. ⁵ (Indeed, those serve under a pattern and a shadow of the heavenly things, just as Moses was commissioned to complete the tabernacle, saying, "See," he says, "you must make all things according to the model shown you on the mountain.") ⁶ But now He has obtained a more excellent ministry, inasmuch as He is also the mediator of a better covenant, which has been established on better promises. ⁷ For if that first covenant had been faultless, there would have been no need to look for a second place. ⁸ For the one who criticizes them says, "Days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will make with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah a new covenant, ⁹ not like the covenant that I made with their ancestors when I took hold of their hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, for they did not remain in my covenant, and I turned away from them," says the Lord. ¹⁰ "For this is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel after those days," says the Lord, "I will give My laws into their minds, and I will write them on their hearts, and I will be their God, and they will be My people. ¹¹ And each one will not teach his neighbor and each one his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,' for they will all know Me, from the least to the greatest of them. ¹² For I will show mercy toward their iniquities, and I will no longer remember their sins." ¹³ In saying "new," He has made the first obsolete. And what is growing old and aging is near to disappearance.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "mediator of a better covenant" — The Greek uses "μεσίτης" (mediator), a term with legal and theological weight. It is retained in full context.
- v. 10: "I will give My laws into their minds, and I will write them on their hearts" — The Greek uses "νόμους" and "ἐπὶ καρδίας αὐτῶν ἐπιγράψω αὐτούς," which is rendered to emphasize internalization over mere external compliance.
- v. 13: "He has made the first obsolete" — "πεπαλαίωκεν" is translated as "made ... obsolete," reflecting the idea of being rendered outdated or ineffective.
