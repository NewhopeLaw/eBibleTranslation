# Hebrews 2

¹ Therefore, we must pay even more careful attention to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away. ² For if the word spoken through angels proved unalterable, and every violation and disobedience received a just retribution, ³ how shall we escape if we neglect such a great salvation, which, having first been spoken by the Lord, was confirmed to us by those who heard him, ⁴ with God bearing witness by signs and wonders, by various powers and by the Holy Spirit, distributed according to his will? ⁵ For the world that is to come, about which we speak, was not subjected to angels. ⁶ But someone has testified somewhere, saying, "What is a human that you remember him, or the son of a human that you care for him? ⁷ You made him for a little while lower than the angels; you crowned him with glory and honor, ⁸ and set all things under his feet." For in setting all things under him, he left nothing outside his control. But now we do not yet see all things under him, ⁹ but we see Jesus, who for a little while was made lower than the angels, crowned with glory and honor because of his death, so that by God's grace he might taste death for everyone. ¹⁰ For it was fitting that he, for whom and through whom all things exist, should bring many sons to glory, and through suffering to perfect the leader of their salvation. ¹¹ For the one who sanctifies and those who are sanctified are all one, and for this reason he does not put them to shame by calling them brothers, ¹² saying, "I will proclaim your name to my brothers; in the assembly I will praise you." ¹³ And again, "I will put my trust in him," and again, "Here am I, and the children whom God has given me." ¹⁴ Since then the children share in flesh and blood, he himself likewise partook of the same things, so that through death he might destroy the one who holds the power of death, that is, the devil, ¹⁵ and deliver those who through fear of death were subject to slavery all their lives. ¹⁶ For surely he does not take hold of angels, but of the seed of Abraham he takes hold. ¹⁷ Therefore, he had to be made like his brothers in every respect, so that he might become a merciful and faithful high priest in things pertaining to God, in order to make atonement for the sins of the people. ¹⁸ For since he himself was tested by what he suffered, he is able to help those who are being tested.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "all things under his feet" is a paraphrase of the Hebrew from Psalm 8, which is being quoted here. The Greek translation and context are considered.
- v. 13: The quote "Here am I, and the children whom God has given me" is a paraphrase of Isaiah 8:18, adapted to fit the Christological context.
