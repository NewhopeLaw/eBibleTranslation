# Philemon 1

¹ Paul, a prisoner for Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon our beloved partner and fellow laborer, ² and to Apphia the sister, and to Archippus the fellow soldier, and to the assembly in your house: ³ Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ⁴ I always give thanks to my God whenever I remember you in my prayers, ⁵ hearing of your love and the faith you have in the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints, ⁶ so that the fellowship of your faith may become active in the knowledge of every good thing that is among us in Christ, ⁷ for I have had much joy and comfort from your love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, my brother. ⁸ Therefore, though I have great boldness in Christ to command you what is fitting, ⁹ rather, I urge you out of love, such as Paul, the aged, now also a prisoner for Christ Jesus— ¹⁰ I urge you on behalf of my child, whom I begot in my imprisonment, Onesimus, ¹¹ who was formerly useless to you, but now is useful both to you and to me, ¹² whom I have sent back to you, this is my very heart; ¹³ whom I would have kept with me so that he might minister to me on my behalf in my imprisonment for the gospel, ¹⁴ but without your consent, I did not want to do anything, so that your good deed would not be by compulsion but by willing choice. ¹⁵ For perhaps this is why he was separated for a while, so that you might have him forever, ¹⁶ no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, as a beloved brother, especially to me, but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord. ¹⁷ So if you regard me as a partner, receive him as you would me. ¹⁸ But if he has wronged you or owes you anything, put that to my account; ¹⁹ I Paul have written it with my own hand, I will repay it. I do not want to say to you that you also owe me your life. ²⁰ Yes, my brother, let me have joy from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ. ²¹ Confident of your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I ask. ²² At the same time, prepare a guest room for me, for I hope that through your prayers I will be given to you. ²³ Greet you Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, ²⁴ Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow laborers. ²⁵ The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "prisoner for Christ" reflects the context of Paul's imprisonment.
- v. 17: "partner" is used for κοινωνόν, capturing the relational and shared mission sense.
- v. 18: "put that to my account" interprets ἐλλόγα as "charge to my account" in a financial idiom.
- v. 19: "I will repay it" renders ἀποτίσω, which is a financial term for paying back.
- v. 22: "prepare a guest room" reflects the hospitality context, not a literal room but a place for lodging.
