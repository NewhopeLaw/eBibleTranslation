# 1 Samuel 3

¹ The young Samuel was serving the LORD before Eli, and the word of the LORD was rare in those days; no vision was common. ² Now Eli was lying in his place, and his eyes had begun to grow dim so he could not see. ³ The lamp of God had not yet gone out, and Samuel was lying down in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was. ⁴ The LORD called to Samuel, and he said, "Here I am." ⁵ He ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." But Eli said, "I did not call. Go back and lie down." So he went and lay down. ⁶ The LORD called again, and Samuel got up and went to Eli, and said, "Here I am, for you called me." But Eli said, "I did not call, my son. Go back and lie down." ⁷ Now Samuel had not yet come to know the LORD, and the word of the LORD had not yet been revealed to him. ⁸ The LORD called a third time, and Samuel got up and went to Eli. Eli realized then that the LORD was calling the boy. ⁹ So Eli told Samuel, "Go, lie down. And if you are called again, say, 'Speak, LORD, for your servant is listening.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place. ¹⁰ The LORD came and stood and called as before, "Samuel, Samuel." And Samuel answered, "Speak, for your servant is listening." ¹¹ The LORD said to Samuel, "I am about to do something in Israel that will make anyone who hears it shake their ears. ¹² On that day I will carry out against Eli all that I have spoken about his house, beginning and ending. ¹³ I told him that I would judge his house forever for the sin he knows, for his sons have been blaspheming, and he did not stop them. ¹⁴ Therefore, I have sworn to the house of Eli that no atonement shall ever be made for the house of Eli by sacrifice or offering forever." ¹⁵ Samuel lay until morning, and then opened the doors of the house of the LORD. He was afraid to tell Eli what the vision was. ¹⁶ Eli called Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son." And Samuel answered, "Here I am." ¹⁷ He said, "What word did the LORD speak to you? Do not hide anything from me. If you do, may God deal with you in this way and even more if you hide a word from me of all the words the LORD spoke to you." ¹⁸ So Samuel told him everything and did not hide a thing. Eli said, "It is the LORD. Let him do what seems good to him." ¹⁹ Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him, and none of his words fell to the ground. ²⁰ All Israel from Dan to Beer-sheba knew that Samuel was a trustworthy prophet for the LORD. ²¹ The LORD continued to reveal himself in Shiloh, for the word of the LORD was made known to Samuel in Shiloh, and Samuel became a prophet of the LORD for all Israel.

---

Notes
- v. 7: "had not yet come to know the LORD" — the translation reflects the Hebrew's use of yada (to know) in the covenantal sense.
- v. 9: "Speak, LORD, for your servant is listening" — the Greek (LXX) uses "speak, for your servant is listening," but the Hebrew adds "LORD" explicitly, which is retained here.
- v. 11: "shake their ears" — a literal rendering of tzillin, preserving the poetic weight of the phrase.
- v. 13: "blaspheming" — from the root qalal, meaning to treat lightly or despise, often with religious connotations.
- v. 17: "deal with you in this way and even more" — the Hebrew uses a common formula for a curse, "thus may it be done to you...".
- v. 20: "from Dan to Beer-sheba" — the phrase is idiomatic in Hebrew for "the whole land of Israel."
- v. 21: "the word of the LORD was made known to Samuel in Shiloh" — the Hebrew uses "appeared" (niglah), indicating a revelation.
