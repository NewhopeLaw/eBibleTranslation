# 1 Samuel 25

¹ Samuel died, and all Israel gathered and mourned for him. They buried him in his house in Ramah. Then David rose and went down to the wilderness of Paran. ² There was a man in Maon whose fields were in Carmel. The man was very wealthy, with three thousand sheep and a thousand goats. He was shearing his sheep in Carmel. ³ The man's name was Nabal, and his wife's name was Abigail. The woman was insightful and good-looking, but the man was harsh and evil in his dealings, and he was a Calebite. ⁴ David heard in the wilderness that Nabal was shearing his sheep. ⁵ David sent ten young men and said to the young men, "Go up to Carmel and go to Nabal and greet him in my name. ⁶ Say to him, 'Peace be to you and to your house and to all that you have. ⁷ I have heard that you are shearing your sheep. Your shepherds have been with us, and we did not miss them, nor did we ask them for anything, all the days they were with us in Carmel. ⁸ Ask your young men and they will tell you. My young men found favor in your eyes. We have come on a good day. Please give whatever your hand finds good to your servants and to your son David.'" ⁹ The young men of David arrived and spoke these words to Nabal in David's name, and all these words in the name of David. They rested. ¹⁰ Nabal answered the young men of David and said, "Who is David, and who is the son of Jesse? Today there are many servants who are running away from their lords. ¹¹ Should I take my bread and my water and my meat, which I have killed for my shearer, and give it to these men, whose origin I do not know?" ¹² The young men of David turned back from their way and returned and came and told David all these words. ¹³ David said to his men, "Each of you gird on his sword." Each of them girded on his sword, and David also girded on his sword. They went up after David. Four hundred men went up with David, and two hundred remained with the supplies. ¹⁴ One of the young men of Nabal told Abigail, wife of Nabal, saying, "Look, David sent messengers from the wilderness to bless our lord, and he turned from them. ¹⁵ The men are very good to us. They did not miss us and did not ask us for anything, all the days we were with them. ¹⁶ They were a wall around us, both night and day, all the days we were with them, tending the sheep. ¹⁷ Now you must know and see what you will do, for evil has been completed toward our lord and toward his house. He is the son of a worthless man, and there is no speaking to him." ¹⁸ Abigail hurried and took two hundred loaves of bread, two wine skins, five prepared sheep, five seahs of parched grain, a hundred clusters of raisins, and two hundred cakes of figs. She put it all on donkeys. ¹⁹ She said to her young men, "Go on ahead of me. I am coming after you." She did not tell her husband Nabal. ²⁰ As she was riding on the donkey and going down in the cover of the mountain, look, David and his men were coming down to meet her. She met them. ²¹ David said, "In vain have I kept all his things in the wilderness, and nothing was taken from all that he had, and he returned me evil for good. ²² So may God do to David and add to it, if I leave anything of all Nabal's until morning." ²³ Abigail saw David and hurried and got off her donkey and fell before David's face on the ground and bowed to the earth. ²⁴ She fell at his feet and said, "The fault is on me, my lord. Let your servant speak in your ears and hear the words of your servant. ²⁵ Do not let my lord set his heart on this worthless man, Nabal, for his name is as his is. Nabal is his name and folly is with him. But I, your servant, did not see the young men whom you sent. ²⁶ And now, my lord, live, and the living God, who has kept you from going into bloodshed and has preserved your hand for yourself. And now may your enemies be like Nabal, and those who seek evil for my lord. ²⁷ And now, take the blessing that your servant has brought to my lord, and give it to the young men who walk on the feet of my lord. ²⁸ Forgive the trespass of your servant, for the Lord will build for my lord a faithful house, for my lord fights the Lord's battles, and no evil will be found in you from now on. ²⁹ And if a man arises to pursue you and to seek your life, then the life of my lord will be bound in the bundle of the living with the Lord your God, and the life of your enemies will be pierced in the center of their wound. ³⁰ And it will be that the Lord will do for my lord all the good that he has spoken over you, and will appoint you as ruler over Israel. ³¹ And let this not be for you a reproach or a stumbling block to your heart, my lord, to shed innocent blood or to save your hand for yourself. And the Lord will do good to my lord, and will remember your servant." ³² David said to Abigail, "Blessed is the Lord, the God of Israel, who has sent you today to meet me. ³³ And blessed is your discretion, and blessed are you, who have kept me today from going into bloodshed and have preserved my hand for myself. ³⁴ But indeed, live the Lord, the God of Israel, who has kept me from doing evil to you, that had you not come quickly to meet me, surely there would have remained to Nabal until morning, one who would have pierced the wall." ³⁵ David took from her hand what she had brought to him and said to her, "Go up in peace to your house. See, I have heard your voice and have shown favor to your face." ³⁶ Abigail went to Nabal, and look, he was holding a feast in his house, like the feast of a king. Nabal's heart was good toward him, and he was drunk very much. She did not tell him a small or a great matter until the morning light. ³⁷ And it happened in the morning when the wine left Nabal, his wife told him these words. His heart died within him, and he became like a stone. ³⁸ And it happened after ten days that the Lord struck Nabal and he died. ³⁹ David heard that Nabal was dead and said, "Blessed is the Lord, who has caused the strife of my reproach to cease from the hand of Nabal, and has preserved the servant from evil. And the evil of Nabal has returned on his head. And David sent and spoke about Abigail to take her to him as a wife. ⁴⁰ And David's servants came to Abigail in Carmel and spoke to her, saying, "David has sent us to take you to him as a wife." ⁴¹ She rose and bowed to the ground with her face to the earth and said, "Here is your servant, a servant to wash the feet of the young men of my lord." ⁴² She hurried and rose, Abigail, and mounted the donkey, and five of her young women went with her, following the messengers of David, and she became to him as a wife. ⁴³ David took Ahinoam of Jezreel, and both of them became his wives. ⁴⁴ And Saul gave Michal, his daughter, the wife of David, to Palti the son of Laish, who was from Gallim.

Notes
- v. 1: "the wilderness of Paran" — "Paran" is a geographic term referring to a region, often associated with the Negev.
- v. 3: "Calebite" — referring to the Kenite group associated with Caleb, a term indicating a nomadic or related tribe.
- v. 11: "give it to these men, whose origin I do not know?" — the original Hebrew uses a phrase that can mean "whose origin I do not know," but in context, it reflects Nabal's dismissive and suspicious attitude.
- v. 28: "build for my lord a faithful house" — the term "house" often refers to a dynasty or royal lineage in ancient Hebrew.
- v. 44: "Palti the son of Laish, who was from Gallim" — "Laish" is a personal name, and "Gallim" likely refers to a place or tribe.
