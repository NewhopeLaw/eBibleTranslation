# 1 Samuel 5

¹ The Philistines captured the ark of God and brought it from Ebenezer to Ashdod. ² The Philistines took the ark of God and brought it into the temple of Dagon and placed it beside Dagon. ³ When the people of Ashdod awoke the next morning, they saw that Dagon had fallen face down on the ground before the ark of the LORD. They picked up Dagon and set him back in his place. ⁴ But the next morning they came to the temple of Dagon and saw that Dagon had fallen again, this time face down on the ground before the ark of the LORD. His head and both his hands had been cut off, lying at the threshold, while only his body remained. ⁵ That is why to this very day the priests of Dagon and all who enter the temple of Dagon do not step on the threshold of the temple of Dagon in Ashdod. ⁶ The hand of the LORD was heavy on the people of Ashdod and its territory, and He struck them with a great plague. ⁷ When the men of Ashdod saw what had happened, they said, “The ark of the God of Israel must not remain with us, for His hand is hard upon us and upon Dagon our god.” ⁸ They sent messengers and gathered all the rulers of the Philistines to them and said, “What should we do with the ark of the God of Israel?” They said, “Let us move it to Gath.” So they moved the ark of the God of Israel to Gath. ⁹ After it was moved there, the hand of the LORD struck the city with a great panic. He smote the people from the least to the greatest, and they were struck with boils on their seats. ¹⁰ They sent the ark of God away to Ekron. When the ark of God came to Ekron, the people of Ekron cried out, “They have brought the ark of the God of Israel to us, to kill us and our people!” ¹¹ So they sent messengers again, and gathered all the rulers of the Philistines, and said, “Send the ark of the God of Israel back to its place, so that it may no longer kill us and our people. The plague is so severe in the city, and the hand of the God is heavy there.” ¹² The people who had not died were themselves struck with boils, and the cry of the city went up to heaven.

Notes
- v. 4: "threshold" is used for מִפְתַּן (miphtan), which is interpreted here as the threshold or doorstep of the temple.
