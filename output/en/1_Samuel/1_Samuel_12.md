# 1 Samuel 12

¹ Samuel said to all Israel, "I have heard your voices in everything you said to me, and I have made a king over you." ² Now here is the king going before you, and I am old and gray, and my sons are with you. I have walked before you from my youth until this day. ³ Here I am. Give judgment against me before the LORD and before his anointed: From whose ox have I taken? From whose donkey have I taken? Whom have I oppressed? Whom have I wronged? From whose hand have I accepted a bribe and would cover it over? If so, I will return it to you." ⁴ They said, "You have not oppressed us, nor wronged us, nor taken anything from any person's hand." ⁵ Samuel said to them, "The LORD is witness against you and his anointed this day that you have not found anything in my hand." And they said, "Witness." ⁶ Samuel said to the people, "The LORD who made Moses and Aaron and brought your ancestors out of the land of Egypt." ⁷ Now stand and let me judge you before the LORD about all the righteousness that the LORD has done for you and your ancestors. ⁸ When Jacob came into Egypt, and your ancestors cried out to the LORD, the LORD sent Moses and Aaron and brought your ancestors out of Egypt and settled them in this place. ⁹ They forgot the LORD their God, and he delivered them into the hand of Sisera, the commander of Hazor, and into the hands of the Philistines and into the hand of the king of Moab, and they fought against them. ¹⁰ They cried out to the LORD and said, "We have sinned, for we have forsaken the LORD and worshiped the Baals and the Astartes. Now save us from the hands of our enemies and we will serve you." ¹¹ The LORD sent Jerubbaal and Bedan and Jephthah and Samuel, and he saved you from the hands of your enemies all around, and you lived safely. ¹² You saw that Nahash the king of the Ammonites came against you, and you said to me, "No, but a king must reign over us," and the LORD your God is your king. ¹³ Now here is the king you chose, and the LORD has given you a king. ¹⁴ If you fear the LORD and serve him, and listen to his voice, and do not rebel against the mouth of the LORD, then you and also the king who rules over you will be after the LORD your God. ¹⁵ But if you will not listen to the voice of the LORD and rebel against the mouth of the LORD, then the hand of the LORD will be against you and against your ancestors." ¹⁶ Now stand and see this great thing that the LORD is doing before your eyes. ¹⁷ Is it not the time of the wheat harvest today? I will call to the LORD, and he will send thunder and rain, and you will know and see that your evil is great in the eyes of the LORD for asking for a king for yourselves." ¹⁸ Samuel called to the LORD, and the LORD sent thunder and rain on that day, and all the people greatly feared the LORD and Samuel. ¹⁹ The people said to Samuel, "Pray to the LORD your God for us and we will not die, for we have added to all our sins the evil of asking for a king for ourselves." ²⁰ Samuel said to the people, "Do not fear. You have done all this evil, but do not turn away from the LORD. Serve the LORD with all your hearts. ²¹ Do not turn away after things that are worthless, which cannot profit or save, for they are worthless. ²² For the LORD will not forsake his people because of his great name, for the LORD has taken you for himself as a people. ²³ Also, I certainly will not sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray for you, but I will teach you the good and the straight way. ²⁴ But fear the LORD and serve him in truth with all your hearts, for you have seen what the LORD has done for you. ²⁵ But if you do evil, you and your king will be added together."

---

Notes
- v. 14: "do not rebel against the mouth of the LORD" — rendered in line with the Hebrew idiom for defying divine will.
- v. 21: "after things that are worthless" — the Hebrew uses the word "tohu" ("wildness, emptiness") to refer to false gods or empty pursuits.
- v. 22: "because of his great name" — reflects the Hebrew phrase "ba'asher shemo ha'gadol," emphasizing the LORD's reputation and covenantal faithfulness.
