# 1 Samuel 9

¹ There was a man from the tribe of Benjamin, and his name was Kish, son of Abiel, son of Zeror, son of Becorath, son of Aphiah, son of a man from the tribe of Ephraim. ² He had a son, and his name was Saul, a young man of good appearance and handsome. There was no one among the sons of Israel better looking than he, from his shoulders upward he was taller than any of the people. ³ The donkeys of Kish, Saul’s father, were lost. Kish said to his son Saul, “Take one of the young men with you and go look for the donkeys.” ⁴ They went through the hill country of Ephraim and went through the land of Shalish, but did not find them. They went through the land of Shaalim and found none. They went through the land of Benjamin and did not find them. ⁵ They came to the land of Zuph, and Saul said to the young man who was with him, “Come, let’s return, or my father will stop looking for the donkeys and worry about us.” ⁶ The young man answered him and said, “Look, there is a man of God in this city, and the man is honored. Everything he says comes to pass. Now, let’s go there. Perhaps he will tell us the way we should go, the way we walked.” ⁷ Saul said to the young man who was with him, “But what can we bring to the man of God? For the bread has run out from our sacks, and there is no present to bring to the man of God. What is there for us?” ⁸ The young man continued to answer Saul and said, “Look, I have a quarter of a shekel of silver. I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us the way we should go.” ⁹ In Israel, when someone went to consult God, they used to say, “Come, let’s go to the seer,” for the prophet was called a seer in those days. ¹⁰ Saul said to the young man, “Good is your word. Let’s go.” They went to the city where the man of God was. ¹¹ As they were going up the slope of the city, they saw some young women coming out to draw water. They asked them, “Is there a seer here?” ¹² The young women answered them and said, “There is. Look, he is right before you. Hurry up, for he has come to the city today. A sacrifice is being prepared for the people on the high place today.” ¹³ “When you enter the city, you will find him. Before he goes up to the high place to eat, you will find him. The people will not eat until he comes, for he will bless the sacrifice first, and then the invited guests will eat. So now go up, for you will find him today.” ¹⁴ They went up to the city. While they were in the middle of the city, Samuel came out to meet them to go up to the high place. ¹⁵ The LORD had revealed to Samuel the same day, before Saul came, saying: ¹⁶ “Tomorrow about this time I will send a man from the land of Benjamin to you. Anoint him as leader over my people Israel. He will rescue my people from the hand of the Philistines, for I have seen my people, and their cry has come to me.” ¹⁷ When Samuel saw Saul, the LORD told him, “This is the man I told you about. He will lead my people.” ¹⁸ Saul approached Samuel in the middle of the gate and said, “Please tell me where the seer’s house is.” ¹⁹ Samuel answered Saul and said, “I am the seer. Go up before me to the high place. Eat with me today, and tomorrow I will send you away, and everything in your heart I will tell you.” ²⁰ “As for the lost donkeys of yours today, do not let your heart be concerned about them. They have been found. And who is the beauty of Israel? Is it not for you and for your father’s house?” ²¹ Saul answered and said, “Am I not a Benjamite, the son of a small man from the smallest of the tribes of Israel? And my family is the least of all the families of the tribe of Benjamin. Why then do you speak to me in this way?” ²² Samuel took Saul and the young man with him and brought them to the guest chamber, and he set a place for them among the thirty who were invited. ²³ Samuel said to the cook, “Give me the portion I gave you, the one I told you to set aside for yourself.” ²⁴ The cook took the thigh and put it in front of Saul. Samuel said to Saul, “Here is the leftover portion. Set it before you and eat, for it is for you as a sign that the others are witnesses. Eat.” So Saul ate with Samuel that day. ²⁵ They went down from the high place to the city and spoke with Saul on the roof. ²⁶ They got up, and it was time for the morning to break. Samuel called to Saul from the roof, saying, “Get up, and I will send you away.” Saul got up, and they both went out, he and Samuel, outside. ²⁷ As they were going down to the outskirts of the city, Samuel said to Saul, “Tell the young man to go on ahead of us. You wait here today, and I will tell you the word of God.”

Notes
- v. 9: The phrase “In Israel, when someone went to consult God, they used to say…” is a contextual explanation to clarify the meaning of “seer” and “prophet” based on the narrative context.
