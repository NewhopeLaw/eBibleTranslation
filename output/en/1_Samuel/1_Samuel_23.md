# 1 Samuel 23

¹ They told David, "Look, the Philistines are waging war in Keilah, and they are taking the grain stores." ² David asked the LORD, "Should I go and attack these Philistines?" The LORD said to David, "Go and attack the Philistines. Rescue Keilah." ³ But David's men said to him, "Look, we are here in Judah, afraid, and how much more if we go to Keilah, to the Philistine strongholds?" ⁴ David asked the LORD again, and the LORD answered him, "Rise up, go down to Keilah, for I will give the Philistines into your hand." ⁵ So David and his men went down to Keilah. They fought the Philistines and captured their livestock, and gave them a great beating. David rescued the people of Keilah. ⁶ Now when Abiathar, son of Ahimelech, had fled to David to Keilah, he had the ephod with him. ⁷ Saul was told that David had come to Keilah. He said, "God has delivered him into my hand, for he has shut himself in a city with gates and a bolt." ⁸ Saul summoned the whole people to war to go down to Keilah to attack David and his men. ⁹ David learned that Saul was plotting harm against him, and he said to the priest Abiathar, "Bring the ephod here." ¹⁰ David said, "O LORD, the God of Israel, you have heard, you have heard, your servant. Saul is seeking to come to Keilah to destroy the city because of me. ¹¹ Will the people of Keilah surrender me into his hand? Will Saul go down, as your servant has heard? O LORD, the God of Israel, please tell your servant." And the LORD said, "He will go down." ¹² David said, "Will the people of Keilah surrender me and my men into the hand of Saul?" The LORD said, "They will surrender you." ¹³ So David and his men, about six hundred people, rose up and went out from Keilah. They traveled wherever they went. When Saul was told that David had escaped from Keilah, he gave up pursuing. ¹⁴ David stayed in the desert in the strongholds, and in the mountain of Ziph in the wilderness. Saul pursued him every day, but God did not give him into his hand. ¹⁵ David saw that Saul had come out to seek his life, and David was in the mountain of Ziph in the forest. ¹⁶ Jonathan, the son of Saul, rose up and went to David in the forest. He strengthened his hand in the LORD. ¹⁷ He said to him, "Do not fear. Saul my father will not find you. And you will be king over Israel, and I will be second to you. Saul my father knows this." ¹⁸ So the two of them made a covenant before the LORD. Then David remained in the forest, and Jonathan returned to his house. ¹⁹ The men of Ziph went up to Gibeah to Saul and said, "Look, David is hiding among us in the strongholds in the forest, in the hill of Hachilah that is on the east side of Jeshimon. ²⁰ Now you, king, go down at any time you wish, and we will hand him over into your hand." ²¹ Saul said to them, "Blessed are you by the LORD, for you have had mercy on me. ²² Go now, prepare more, and find out, and see where his foot is, and where he is. For he said to me, 'He is in the open country.' Let him be like a hidden one." ²³ "See and find out all the hiding places where he hides, and come to me to Nachon. I will go with you, and if he is in the land, I will search for him in all the thousands of Judah." ²⁴ The men of Ziph rose up and went before Saul. David and his men were in the desert of Maon in the Arabah, on the east side of Jeshimon. ²⁵ Saul and his men went to seek him. They told David, and he went down to the rock in the desert of Maon. Saul heard and pursued David to the desert of Maon. ²⁶ Saul went one side of the mountain, and David and his men the other side. David was hurrying to escape from Saul, while Saul and his men were closing in on David and his men to seize them. ²⁷ An angel came to Saul, saying, "Hurry and go! For the Philistines are falling upon the land." ²⁸ So Saul turned back from pursuing David and went to meet the Philistines. Therefore, that place was called The Rock of Divisions.

Notes
- v. 6: "Abiathar, son of Ahimelech" — The text uses the form "ben Ahimelech" (son of Ahimelech), which is retained as a proper name.
- v. 11: "as your servant has heard" — The Hebrew uses "kima she'ema" ("as your servant has heard"), indicating David's reference to his own knowledge.
- v. 27: "an angel came to Saul" — The Hebrew uses "mal'akh" which is typically "messenger," but in this context, likely refers to a divine messenger or angel.
- v. 28: "The Rock of Divisions" — The Hebrew name for the place (selah ha-machlokot) is translated as a descriptive phrase.
