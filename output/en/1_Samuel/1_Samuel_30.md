# 1 Samuel 30

¹ On the third day, when David and his men came to Ziklag, the Amalekites had attacked the Negev and Ziklag, burned it with fire. ² They captured the women and everything in it, from the smallest to the greatest, not killing a single person, and took them and went away on their way. ³ David and his men came to the city, and there they found it burned with fire, and their wives and children and daughters taken captive. ⁴ David and the people with him cried out and wept until they had no more strength to weep. ⁵ Two of David's wives had been captured: Ahinoam the Jezreelitess and Abigail, the wife of Nabal the Carmelite. ⁶ David was greatly distressed, for the people were talking about stoning him, for all the people were grieved over their sons and daughters. But David found strength in the LORD his God. ⁷ David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Bring me the ephod." Abiathar brought the ephod to David. ⁸ David inquired of the LORD, saying, "Should I pursue this band? Will I overtake them?" And he said to him, "Go on, for you will certainly overtake them and you will certainly deliver them." ⁹ So David went with six hundred men who were with him and came to the Valley of the Berach, and the rest stayed behind. ¹⁰ David went with four hundred men, and two hundred stayed behind, because they were too exhausted to cross the Valley of the Berach. ¹¹ They found an Egyptian in the field, and they brought him to David and gave him bread to eat and water to drink. ¹² They gave him a piece of a cake of figs and two clusters of raisins, and he ate and his spirit returned to him, for he had not eaten bread or drunk water for three days and three nights. ¹³ David asked him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?" And he said, "I am an Egyptian youth, a servant of an Amalekite. My master left me behind, because I became ill three days ago." ¹⁴ We marched down to the Negev of the Kerethites and to what belongs to Judah and to the Negev of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag with fire." ¹⁵ David said to him, "Will you bring me down to this band?" He said, "Swear to me by God that you will not kill me or hand me over to my master, and I will bring you down to this band." ¹⁶ He brought him down, and there they were, scattered across the face of the whole land, eating and drinking and rejoicing in all the great plunder they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah. ¹⁷ David struck them from the twilight until the evening of the next day, and not one of them escaped, except four hundred young men who rode on the camels. They fled. ¹⁸ David rescued all the plunder they had taken, and he rescued his two wives. ¹⁹ Nothing from the small to the great, from the children and the daughters, or from the plunder, was missing. He restored everything to them. ²⁰ David took all the sheep and oxen and drove them ahead of the rest of the cattle, and it was said, "This is the plunder of David." ²¹ David came to the two hundred men who had fallen behind at the Valley of the Berach and brought them back to the Valley of the Berach. They went out to meet David and to meet the people with him, and David came to the people and asked them how they were. ²² All the wicked men among the men who had gone with David answered and said, "Because we did not go with you, we will not be given anything from the plunder you rescued, except each one his wife and his children. Let them go and return." ²³ David said, "You must not do this, my brothers. Because the LORD has given to us and preserved us, He has also given the band that came against us into our hand. ²⁴ Who will listen to you in this matter? For whether the one who went down to battle or the one who stayed behind with the equipment is the same—both must divide it equally." ²⁵ From that day onward, this became a statute and a law for Israel to this very day. ²⁶ David came to Ziklag and sent portions of the plunder to the elders of Judah, his comrades, saying, "Here is a blessing from the plunder of your enemies." ²⁷ To those in Bethel, to those in Ramoth of the Negev, and to those in Jattir, ²⁸ to those in Aroer, to those in Siphmoth, and to those in Eshtemoh, ²⁹ to those in Racal, to those in Keilah, to those in Hormah, ³⁰ to those in Carmel, to those in the cities of Judah, and to those in the cities of the Kenites, ³¹ to those in Hebron, and to all the places where David and his men had traveled.

Notes
- v. 1: "Ziklag" is rendered as a proper name. The Hebrew text uses the form צִקְלַג, consistent with the name table.
- v. 13: "Egyptian youth" follows from נַעַר מִצְרִי, with "youth" used to capture the term נַעַר in context.
- v. 14: "Negev of the Kerethites" is a direct translation of נֶגֶב הַכְּרֵתִי, with "Kerethites" as a proper name.
- v. 25: "statute and law" reflects מִשְׁפָּט וְחֹק, with both terms rendered to emphasize legal and customary authority.
- v. 26–31: These verses list locations to which David sent portions of plunder. The names are translated using the glossary and name table, including variations such as "Ramoth of the Negev" for רָמֽוֹת־נֶגֶב.
