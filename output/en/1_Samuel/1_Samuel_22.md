# 1 Samuel 22

¹ David went from there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. His brothers and the entire household of his father heard of this and went down to him there. ² Every man with a burden, every man in debt, and every man who was dissatisfied gathered to him. He became their leader, and with him there were about four hundred men. ³ David went from there to the region of Moab and said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and mother come to you until I know what God will do for me." ⁴ He showed the king of Moab kindness, and they stayed with him until David remained in the region. ⁵ Gad the prophet said to David, "Do not remain in the region. Go and take for yourself land in Judah." So David went and came to the forest of Hareth. ⁶ When Saul heard that David had been discovered and that the men with him were there, Saul was sitting in Gibeah under the terebinth at Ramah, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing around him. ⁷ Saul said to the servants standing around him, "Listen now, you men of Benjamin. If the son of Jesse gives you fields and vineyards, if he makes you all commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, ⁸ for you have all conspired against me. There is no one who reveals my ear when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse. None of you has any concern for me, because my son has raised his hand against me to become my enemy this very day." ⁹ Doeg the Edomite, who was standing over the servants of Saul, answered, "I saw the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Ahimelech the son of Ahitub. ¹⁰ He inquired of the LORD for him and gave him provisions and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine." ¹¹ So the king sent to summon Ahimelech the son of Ahitub, the priest, and all his brothers, the priests who were in Nob. They came to the king. ¹² Saul said, "Listen now, son of Ahitub." He answered, "Here I am, my lord." ¹³ The king said to him, "Why did you conspire with the son of Jesse, giving him bread and a sword and inquiring of God for him to rise up against me to become my enemy this very day?" ¹⁴ Ahimelech answered the king and said, "Who among all the king's servants is like David, a faithful man and the king's son-in-law, who does what the king commands him and is honored in the king's house? ¹⁵ Have I inquired of God for him today? Far be it from me! Let the king not hold anything against your servant or against the entire house of my father, for I did not know anything, small or great." ¹⁶ The king said, "You will surely die, Ahimelech, you and your entire house." ¹⁷ The king said to the men standing around him, "Go and strike down the priests of the LORD, for their hand is with David, and they knew he was fleeing, yet they did not tell me. But the servants of the king were unwilling to lay their hands on the priests of the LORD." ¹⁸ The king said to Doeg, "You, go and strike down the priests." So Doeg the Edomite went and struck down the priests, killing eighty-five men who were wearing the ephod. ¹⁹ He struck down the city of Nob, the city of the priests, with the edge of the sword, from man to woman, from infant to nursing child, from ox to donkey to sheep. ²⁰ One son of Ahimelech the son of Ahitub, whose name was Abiathar, escaped and fled after David. ²¹ Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the LORD. ²² David said to Abiathar, "I knew on that day that Doeg the Edomite was there, for he would certainly tell Saul. I am the one responsible for the lives of your entire family." ²³ Stay with me. Do not be afraid, for whoever seeks my life will seek yours as well, for you are safe with me."

---

Notes
- v. 14: "the king's son-in-law" — Ahimelech refers to David as the king's son-in-law, a status David acquired through his marriage to Michal (1 Sam 18:20–27).
- v. 22: "I am the one responsible" — David recognizes that his actions have put Abiathar's family in danger.
- v. 23: "you are safe with me" — David assures Abiathar that he will protect him as he would himself.
