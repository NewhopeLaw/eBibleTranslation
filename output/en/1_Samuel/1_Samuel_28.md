# 1 Samuel 28

¹ In those days the Philistines gathered their forces for war, to fight against Israel. Achish said to David, "You must know that you will go with me into the camp. You and your men." ² David said to Achish, "Then you will know what your servant will do." Achish said to David, "I will set you over my head as a bodyguard for all the days." ³ Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him. They buried him in Ramah, in his city, and Saul had removed the mediums and the diviners from the land. ⁴ The Philistines gathered and came and encamped at Shunem. Saul gathered all Israel and encamped at Gilboa. ⁵ When Saul saw the Philistine camp, he was afraid and his heart trembled greatly. ⁶ He inquired of the LORD, but the LORD did not answer him, neither through dreams, nor through the Urim, nor through the prophets. ⁷ Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who is a medium, that I may go to her and inquire." His servants said to him, "Here is a woman who is a medium in Endor." ⁸ So Saul disguised himself and put on other clothes, and he went with two men. They arrived at the woman at night and said, "Conjure for me with the medium, and raise for me the one I tell you." ⁹ The woman said to him, "You know what Saul has done. He has exterminated the mediums and the diviners from the land. Why then are you trying to trap my life and kill me?" ¹⁰ Saul swore to her by the LORD, "As the LORD lives, you will not be blamed for this matter." ¹¹ The woman said to him, "Whom should I raise for you?" He said, "Samuel the prophet. Raise him for me." ¹² The woman saw Samuel, and she cried out with a loud voice. She said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? You are Saul!" ¹³ The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What did you see?" The woman said to Saul, "I have seen gods ascending from the earth." ¹⁴ He said to her, "What did you see?" She said, "An old man is ascending, and he is wearing a robe." Saul recognized that it was Samuel and he bowed to the ground, prostrating himself. ¹⁵ Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me to raise me?" Saul said, "I am in great distress. The Philistines are waging war against me, and God has turned away from me. He has not answered me anymore, neither through the hand of the prophets nor in dreams. I called you so that you might tell me what I should do." ¹⁶ Samuel said, "Why are you asking me? The LORD has turned away from you and has become your enemy." ¹⁷ The LORD has done to you what he promised through my hand. He will tear the kingdom away from your hand and give it to your neighbor, to David. ¹⁸ Because you did not obey the voice of the LORD and did not carry out his anger against Amalek, therefore the LORD has done this to you today. ¹⁹ The LORD will also give Israel and you into the hand of the Philistines. Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me, and the LORD will give the camp of Israel into the hand of the Philistines." ²⁰ Saul fell to the full length of his body on the ground and was very afraid of the words of Samuel. He had no strength in him because he had not eaten any bread all day and all the night. ²¹ The woman came to Saul and saw that he was greatly distressed. She said to him, "Listen, your servant has heard your voice, and I have risked my life to obey what you said to me." ²² Now then, listen to the voice of your servant. I will place bread before you and eat, so that you may have strength to go on the journey." ²³ But he refused and said, "I will not eat." His servants and the woman pressed him, and he listened to their voices. He got up from the ground and sat on the bed. ²⁴ The woman had a fattened calf in the house. She hurried and slaughtered it, and she took flour and kneaded it and baked unleavened bread. ²⁵ She brought it before Saul and before his servants. They ate, and they got up and went that night.

Notes
- v. 3: "mediums and the diviners" — The Hebrew terms for "mediums" (חֲבֹנֹת) and "diviners" (יִדְּעֹנִים) reflect practices Saul had outlawed.
- v. 11: "Samuel the prophet" — The Hebrew uses the definite article with Samuel's name, emphasizing the identity of the raised figure.
- v. 15: "carry out his anger against Amalek" — The reference to Amalek recalls 1 Samuel 15, where Saul failed to obey God's command to destroy them completely.
