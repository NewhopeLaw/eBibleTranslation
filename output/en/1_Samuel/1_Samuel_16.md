# 1 Samuel 16

¹ The LORD said to Samuel, "How long will you mourn for Saul, since I have rejected him from being king over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go to Jesse of Bethlehem, for I have seen among his sons one who will be king over them." ² Samuel said, "How can I go? If Saul hears of it, he will kill me." The LORD replied, "Take along a heifer and say, 'I have come to sacrifice to the LORD.'" ³ Then you will invite Jesse to the sacrifice. I will tell you what to do, and you will anoint the one I designate for you." ⁴ Samuel did as the LORD had told him. He arrived in Bethlehem, and the elders of the city came trembling to meet him, saying, "Is your coming in peace?" ⁵ He answered, "Peace to you. I have come to sacrifice to the LORD. Purify yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice." He consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice. ⁶ When they came, Samuel saw Eliab and said, "Surely the LORD's anointed stands before Him." ⁷ But the LORD said to Samuel, "Do not look at his appearance or his stature, because I have rejected him. For the LORD does not look at the things a person looks at. People look at the outward appearance, but the LORD looks at the heart." ⁸ Jesse called Abinadab and had him pass before Samuel. Samuel said, "The LORD has not chosen this one either." ⁹ Jesse had Shammah pass by. Samuel said, "The LORD has not chosen this one either." ¹⁰ Jesse had his seven sons pass before Samuel, but Samuel said to Jesse, "The LORD has not chosen any of these." ¹¹ Samuel asked Jesse, "Are these all the young men?" Jesse replied, "The youngest is still out tending the sheep." Samuel said, "Send for him. We will not sit down until he arrives." ¹² Jesse sent and brought him in. He was reddish, with beautiful eyes and a handsome appearance. Samuel said, "Rise and anoint him, for this is the one." ¹³ Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the presence of his brothers. From that day onward, the spirit of the LORD rushed powerfully upon David. Then Samuel went to Ramah. ¹⁴ The spirit of the LORD departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the LORD tormented him. ¹⁵ The servants of Saul said to him, "Behold, an evil spirit from God is tormenting you." ¹⁶ "Let our lord command his servants to seek a man who is skillful in playing the lyre. And whenever the evil spirit from God comes upon you, he will play it for you. It will bring you comfort and peace." ¹⁷ Saul said to his servants, "Find me a man who is skillful at playing. Bring him to me." ¹⁸ One of the servants answered, "I have seen a son of Jesse of Bethlehem, who knows how to play the lyre. He is a brave warrior, a man of wisdom, and handsome in appearance. And the LORD is with him." ¹⁹ So Saul sent messengers to Jesse, saying, "Send me your son David, who is with the sheep." ²⁰ Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a wineskin, and a kid goat, and sent them with his son David to Saul. ²¹ David came to Saul and stood before him. Saul loved him very much, and he became his armor-bearer. ²² Saul sent to Jesse, saying, "Let David stand before me, for he has found favor in my sight." ²³ Whenever the evil spirit from God came upon Saul, David would take up the lyre and play it with his hands. Then Saul would find relief, and it would bring him comfort.

---

Notes
- v. 14: "an evil spirit from the LORD" — the Hebrew uses שָׂרָף (sārāp), often translated as "evil spirit," which is understood here as a divine judgment or trial.
