# 1 Samuel 2

¹ Hannah prayed and said, My heart is exalted in the LORD. My strength is lifted up in my God. My mouth is open to my enemies, for I rejoice in your salvation. ² There is none holy like the LORD, for there is no one besides you, and there is no Rock like our God. ³ Do not speak with arrogance, do not let great things come from your mouth, for the LORD is the God of knowledge, and he prepares deeds. ⁴ The bow of the mighty is broken, but those who were weak are girded with strength. ⁵ The full are humbled, and those who were hungry have ceased, yes, the barren gave birth to seven, and the one with many children becomes weak. ⁶ The LORD kills and gives life. He brings down to Sheol and raises up. ⁷ The LORD brings low and exalts. ⁸ He raises up the one from the dust, and from the ashpit he lifts up the poor to sit with nobles, and the throne of honor he gives them, for the LORD is the strength of the earth, and he sets it firmly on them. ⁹ He watches over the feet of the godly, but the wicked are hidden in darkness, for no one is strong by power. ¹⁰ The LORD will strike down his enemies, he will fight against those in the heavens. The LORD will judge the ends of the earth, and he will give strength to his king, and he will raise up the horn of his anointed. ¹¹ And Elkanah went back to his home in Ramah, and the boy was serving the LORD in the presence of Eli the priest. ¹² The sons of Eli were sons of Belial, who did not know the LORD. ¹³ The judgment of the priests against the people was this: whenever anyone would offer a sacrifice, a priest’s servant would come while the meat was boiling and with a three-pronged fork in his hand. ¹⁴ He would strike the shoulder, the leg, or the inner parts, and whatever the fork caught up, the priest would take it. That is how they did to all Israel who came to Shiloh. ¹⁵ Before the fat was burned, a priest’s servant would come and say to the man who was offering the sacrifice, "Give the flesh for roasting to the priest, and he will not take from you cooked meat, only raw." ¹⁶ The man offering the sacrifice would say to him, "Let them burn the fat as they please, and take for yourself whatever you desire. But he would say, 'No, now you must give it; otherwise, I will take it by force.'" ¹⁷ The sin of the young men was very great before the LORD, because they despised the offering of the LORD. ¹⁸ And Samuel, the servant of the LORD, was a boy wrapped in a single robe. ¹⁹ His mother would make him a small tunic and bring it to him each year when she came up with her husband to offer the sacrifice of the day. ²⁰ Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife, and said, "May the LORD give you a child from this woman in place of the plea that she made to the LORD." Then they went back to their home. ²¹ For the LORD had remembered Hannah, and she conceived and gave birth to three sons and two daughters. And the boy Samuel grew up in the presence of the LORD. ²² Now Eli was very old, and he heard all that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they slept with the women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting. ²³ He said to them, "Why do you do these evil things that I hear spoken by all these people?" ²⁴ "My sons," he said, "it is not good that I hear such a report. You are not honoring the people of the LORD." ²⁵ "If a man sins against another man, God may mediate for him, but if a man sins against the LORD, who can intercede for him? No, they will not listen to the voice of their father, for the LORD is determined to destroy them." ²⁶ And the boy Samuel continued to grow and was good both in the presence of the LORD and in the presence of people. ²⁷ Then a man of God came to Eli and said to him, "Thus says the LORD, 'Have I revealed myself to the house of your father when they were in Egypt, serving in Pharaoh's house?" ²⁸ 'I chose the house of your father from all the tribes of Israel to be my priests, to go up to my altar to burn incense, to carry the ephod before me. And I gave the house of your father all the offerings of the children of Israel. ²⁹ "Why then do you scorn my sacrifice and offering that I have commanded, and you honor your sons more than me, by fattening yourself on the first portion of every offering of Israel, my people?" ³⁰ "Therefore, says the LORD, the God of Israel, I said, 'Your house and the house of your father would walk before me forever.' But now the LORD says, 'Far be it from me! I will not honor those who honor me, and those who despise me I will despise.' ³¹ "Behold, the days are coming when I will cut off your arm and the arm of the house of your father from among Israel, even though your old age will be spent in your house. ³² "Then you will see sorrow in all that is good to Israel. And there will not be an old man in your house forever. ³³ "I will not take a man from my altar to pity his eyes or grieve his soul. And every one of your house who remains will die by the sword. ³⁴ "This will be the sign to you that will come to your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, in one day: both of them will die. ³⁵ "And I will raise up for myself a faithful priest, who will do all that is in my heart and in my soul, and I will build him a house. He will walk before my anointed forever. ³⁶ "And all who are left in your house will come to bow to him for a silver coin and a loaf of bread, and they will say, 'Please assign me one of the priestly duties to eat the bread.'"

Notes
- v. 10: "the horn of his anointed" — "anointed" is a translation of מָשִׁיחַ (māšiach), rendered as "anointed" to reflect its function as a title, not a name.
- v. 21: "grew up in the presence of the LORD" — the Hebrew uses the same verb as in v. 18 (לִשְׁמוּאֵל מְשָׁרֵת אֶת־פְּנֵי יְהוָה), translated as "serving" there but as "grew up" here, to reflect the different context.
- v. 35: "the faithful priest" — "faithful" is used to capture the meaning of נֶאֱמָן (ne'eman), which is a key term in the covenantal language here.
- v. 35: "in my heart and in my soul" — the Hebrew uses לִבִּי (lēb) and נַפְשִׁי (nafshi), both referring to inner resolve and emotional commitment.
