# Genesis 8

¹ God remembered Noah and all the living creatures and every beast that were with him in the ark. God caused a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters subsided. ² The fountains of the deep were sealed, and the floodgates of the heavens were closed. The rain ceased from the sky. ³ The waters receded gradually from the earth, going back and forth, until the end of one hundred and fifty days. ⁴ The ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on the mountains of Ararat. ⁵ The waters continued to decrease until the tenth month. On the first day of the tenth month the tops of the mountains became visible. ⁶ It came to pass at the end of forty days that Noah opened the window of the ark he had made. ⁷ He sent out the raven, which went back and forth until the waters had dried up from the earth. ⁸ Then he sent out the dove from himself to see if the waters had receded from the surface of the land. ⁹ But the dove found no resting place for the sole of its foot, so it returned to him into the ark, for water was still on the surface of all the earth. Noah stretched out his hand, took the dove, and brought it back to him into the ark. ¹⁰ He waited another seven days, and again sent out the dove from the ark. ¹¹ The dove came back to him in the evening, and there was an olive leaf torn off in its beak. Then Noah knew that the waters had receded from the earth. ¹² He waited another seven days, and again sent out the dove. This time it did not return to him anymore. ¹³ In the six hundredth and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters had dried up from the earth. Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked, and saw that the surface of the land had dried up. ¹⁴ In the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the land had dried. ¹⁵ Then God spoke to Noah, saying: ¹⁶ “Come out of the ark—you, your wife, your sons, and your sons’ wives with you. ¹⁷ Every living creature that is with you, every flesh of bird, beast, and all creeping things that crawl on the earth, let them come out with you, and they shall multiply and fill the earth.” ¹⁸ So Noah came out, along with his sons and his wife, and his sons’ wives with him. ¹⁹ Every living creature, every creeping thing, and every bird—all that moved on the earth—came out from the ark in their families. ²⁰ Noah built an altar to the LORD, and took from all the clean cattle and from all the clean birds, and offered burnt offerings on the altar. ²¹ The LORD smelled the pleasing aroma, and in his heart he said, “I will never again curse the ground because of humanity, for the inclination of the human heart is evil from youth. I will never again strike down every living thing as I have done.” ²² While the earth remains, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will not cease.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Remembered" is a poetic anthropomorphism for acting with covenantal faithfulness.
- v. 21: "In his heart he said" reflects the narrative voice describing God's internal resolve; this is not a direct quote.
- v. 22: "While the earth remains" reflects the Hebrew phrase le-olam ad, often used to express a perpetual covenant.
