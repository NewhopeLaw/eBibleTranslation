# Genesis 17

¹ Abram was ninety-nine years old when the LORD appeared to him. He said, "I am El Shaddai. Walk in my presence and be blameless. ² I will establish a covenant between me and you. I will multiply you greatly, very greatly." ³ Abram fell on his face, and God spoke to him, saying: ⁴ "I am making this covenant with you. You will be the father of a countless number of nations. ⁵ No longer will your name be Abram. Your name will be Abraham, for I have made you the father of a countless number of nations. ⁶ I will make you exceedingly fruitful. I will make you into nations, and from you will come forth kings. ⁷ I will establish my covenant between me and you, and between your descendants after you, generation after generation, as an everlasting covenant, to be your God and the God of your descendants after you. ⁸ I will give to you and to your descendants after you the land of your sojournings, the whole land of Canaan, as a permanent possession. And I will be their God. ⁹ God said to Abraham, "You must keep my covenant, you and your descendants after you, generation after generation. ¹⁰ This is my covenant, which you must keep between me and you and your descendants after you: Every male among you must be circumcised. ¹¹ You must circumcise the flesh of your foreskins, and it will be a sign of the covenant between me and you. ¹² Every male among you, whether born in your household or bought with your money from a foreign people not of your descendants, must be circumcised at the age of eight days. ¹³ Whether born in your household or bought with your money, every male must be circumcised. This will be my covenant in your flesh, an everlasting covenant. ¹⁴ Any male who is not circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin shall be cut off from his people. He has broken my covenant." ¹⁵ God said to Abraham, "As for your wife Sarai, you must no longer call her name Sarai. Her name is Sarah. ¹⁶ I will bless her, and from her I will give you a son. I will bless her, and she will become mothers of nations. Kings of peoples will come from her." ¹⁷ Abraham fell on his face, and he laughed. He said to himself, "Can a son be born to one who is a hundred years old? And can Sarah, who is ninety years old, bear a child?" ¹⁸ Abraham said to God, "If only Ishmael might live before you!" ¹⁹ God said, "No, but Sarah your wife will bear you a son. His name will be Isaac. I will establish my covenant with him, for an everlasting covenant for his descendants after him." ²⁰ As for Ishmael, I have heard you. I will bless him, and I will multiply him greatly. He will father twelve chieftains, and I will make him into a great nation. ²¹ But my covenant I will establish with Isaac, the son whom Sarah will bear to you, in this year at this time, next year." ²² God finished speaking to him, and the presence of God went up from Abraham. ²³ Abraham took Ishmael his son and all those born in his household and all those bought with his money, every male among the men of his household, and circumcised the flesh of their foreskins on the very day God had spoken to him. ²⁴ Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin. ²⁵ Ishmael his son was thirteen years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his fores ²⁶ On the very day Abraham was circumcised, and Ishmael his son. ²⁷ Every man in his household, whether born in the household or bought with money from a foreigner, was circumcised with him.

Notes
- v. 14: The Hebrew term נֶפֶשׁ (nephesh) is translated as "life" in "the life shall be cut off," which avoids the term "soul" and aligns with the glossary instruction to use contextually appropriate terms.
- v. 15-16: The name change from Sarai to Sarah is presented as a divine directive, with the promise of Sarah becoming "mothers of nations" to reflect the Hebrew plural used in reference to her.
