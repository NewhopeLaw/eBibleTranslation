# Genesis 6

¹ When the human began to multiply on the surface of the earth and daughters were born to them, ¹⁰ Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ¹¹ The earth had become corrupt before God, and the earth was filled with violence. ¹² God saw the earth and behold, it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted its way on the earth. ¹³ God said to Noah, “The end of all flesh has come before me, for the earth is filled with violence because of them. I am about to destroy them from the earth.” ¹⁴ Make yourself an ark of gopher wood. You shall make the ark with rooms, and you shall cover it inside and out with pitch. ¹⁵ This is how you must make it: three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high. ¹⁶ You shall make a window for the ark and finish it to a cubit from above. You shall place the door of the ark on its side. You shall make lower, second, and third decks. ¹⁷ And I am bringing a flood of waters upon the earth to destroy all flesh in which there is the breath of life from beneath the heavens. Everything on the earth will perish. ¹⁸ But I will establish my covenant with you, and you will enter the ark—you, your sons, your wife, and your sons’ wives with you. ¹⁹ From every living thing, from every flesh, you shall bring two of each into the ark to keep them alive with you—male and female they shall be. ² the sons of the gods saw the daughters of the human, that they were beautiful, and they took for themselves wives from all whom they chose. ²⁰ From the birds, according to their kind, and from the beasts, according to their kind, and from all crawling things on the earth, according to their kind—two of each will come to you to keep them alive. ²¹ And you, take for yourself every kind of food that is eaten, and gather it to yourself. It will be food for you and for them. ²² Noah did everything as God had commanded him. He did it. ³ The LORD said, “My spirit will not remain in the human forever, since he is flesh, and his days will be 120 years.” ⁴ The Nephilim were in the earth in those days and after that, when the sons of the gods came to the daughters of the human and they bore children to them. These were the mighty ones, the men of renown. ⁵ The LORD saw that the wickedness of the human was great on the earth, and that every thought of their minds was only evil all the time. ⁶ The LORD regretted having made the human on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart. ⁷ The LORD said, “I will blot out the human I have made from the face of the earth, from human to beast to creeping thing and to birds of the sky, for I regret that I made them.” ⁸ But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD. ⁹ These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generations. Noah walked with God.

Notes
- v. 2: "sons of the gods" — a difficult phrase; some interpret this as divine beings or angelic figures, others as rulers or descendants of Seth. The Hebrew is "benei elohim," literally "sons of gods."
- v. 4: "Nephilim" — the meaning of the Hebrew is debated. Here rendered as a proper noun, referring to a race of giants or legendary figures.
- v. 11: "had become corrupt" — the verb "shachah" can mean "corrupt" or "ruined," often used in moral or cosmic contexts.
- v. 17: "I am bringing a flood of waters" — the Hebrew is "mabbul," often rendered as "flood," but can imply a comprehensive or judgmental deluge.
