# Genesis 28

¹ Isaac summoned Jacob and blessed him. He gave him instructions and said to him, “Do not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan. ² Get up, go to Paddan-aram to the house of Bethuel, your mother’s father, and take a wife from there, from the daughters of Laban, your mother’s brother. ³ May El Shaddai bless you and make you fruitful, and multiply you, so that you become a congregation of peoples. ⁴ May He give you the blessing of Abraham, to you and to your offspring with you, so that you may inherit the land where you are now living, the land God gave to Abraham.” ⁵ Isaac sent Jacob away, and Jacob went to Paddan-aram to Laban, the son of Bethuel the Aramean, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau. ⁶ Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him to Paddan-aram to take a wife from there, and in his blessing he gave him instructions that he must not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan. ⁷ Jacob listened to his father and his mother, and went to Paddan-aram. ⁸ Esau saw that the daughters of Canaan were distasteful to Isaac, his father. ⁹ So Esau went to Ishmael and took Mahalat, the daughter of Ishmael, son of Abraham, sister of Nebaioth, as a wife in addition to the wives he already had. ¹⁰ Jacob left Beer-sheba and went toward Haran. ¹¹ He came upon a certain place and stayed there for the night, because the sun had set. He took a stone from the place and set it under his head, and he lay down in that place. ¹² He had a dream. There was a ladder set on the ground, with its top reaching to the heavens, and the angels of God were ascending and descending on it. ¹³ The Lord stood beside him and said, “I am the Lord, the God of Abraham your father, and the God of Isaac. The land on which you are now lying I will give to you and to your offspring. ¹⁴ Your offspring will be as the dust of the earth, and you will spread out to the west and to the east, to the north and to the south. All the families of the earth will be blessed through you and your offspring. ¹⁵ I am with you and will keep you wherever you go. I will bring you back to this land, for I will not leave you until I have done what I promised you.” ¹⁶ Jacob awoke from his sleep and said, “Surely the Lord is in this place, and I did not know it. ¹⁷ How awesome is this place! This is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of the heavens.” ¹⁸ Jacob rose early in the morning and took the stone he had set under his head and set it up as a pillar. He poured oil on its top. ¹⁹ He named the place Bethel, but the name of the city was Luz at first. ²⁰ Jacob made a vow, saying, “If God is with me and keeps me on this journey I am taking, and gives me bread to eat and clothing to wear, ²¹ and I return safely to my father’s house, then the Lord will be my God. ²² And this stone, which I have set as a pillar, will be a house of God. And of all that You give me I will surely give a tenth to You.”

---

Notes
- v. 9: “Nebaioth” is a transliteration of נְבָיוֹת, the sister of Mahalat.
- v. 19: “Luz” is the original name of the city, later called Bethel. The text explicitly notes the change.
