# Genesis 22

¹ After these things, God tested Abraham. He called to him, “Abraham!” and he replied, “Here I am!” ² He said, “Take your son, your beloved Isaac, and go to the land of Moriah. Sacrifice him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains I will name for you.” ³ Abraham rose early the next day, saddled his donkey, and took with him two of his young men and his son Isaac. He gathered the wood for the burnt offering and started out to go to the place God had told him about. ⁴ On the third day Abraham lifted his eyes and saw the place from afar. ⁵ He said to his young men, “Stay here with the donkey, while I and the boy go on ahead. We will worship there and return to you.” ⁶ Abraham took the wood for the offering and placed it on Isaac, his son. He carried the fire and the knife in his own hand, and the two of them walked together. ⁷ Isaac spoke to Abraham his father and said, “Father?” He replied, “Here I am, my son.” Isaac said, “The fire and the wood are here, but where is the lamb for the offering?” ⁸ Abraham said, “God will see to the lamb for the offering, my son.” And the two of them walked on together. ⁹ They arrived at the place God had told him about. There Abraham built an altar, arranged the wood, and bound his son Isaac and placed him on the altar, on top of the wood. ¹⁰ Abraham reached out and took the knife to slaughter his son. ¹¹ An angel of the LORD called to him from heaven and said, “Abraham! Abraham!” He answered, “Here I am!” ¹² He said, “Do not lay your hand on the boy or do anything to him, for now I know that you fear God and have not withheld your beloved son from me.” ¹³ Abraham lifted his eyes and saw a ram caught in the thicket, its horns entangled. He went and took the ram and offered it as a burnt offering in place of his son. ¹⁴ Abraham named the place *The LORD Will See*, as it is said to this day, “On the mountain of the LORD it shall be seen.” ¹⁵ The angel of the LORD called out to Abraham a second time from heaven. ¹⁶ He said, “By myself I swear, declares the LORD, that because you have done this thing and have not withheld your beloved son from me, ¹⁷ I will surely bless you and greatly multiply your descendants like the stars in the sky and like the sand on the seashore. Your descendants will possess the gates of their enemies. ¹⁸ And through your descendants all the nations of the earth will be blessed, because you have obeyed my voice.” ¹⁹ Abraham returned to his young men, and they rose and went together to Beer Sheba. Abraham lived at Beer Sheba. ²⁰ After these events, it was reported to Abraham, “Look, Milcah too has borne children to your brother Nahor.” ²¹ His firstborn was Uz, his brother Buz, and Kemuel the father of Aram. ²² And Kesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel. ²³ And Bethuel became the father of Rebekah. These eight Milcah bore to Abraham’s brother Nahor. ²⁴ His concubine, whose name was Reumah, also bore children: Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.

---

Notes
- v. 14: The name *The LORD Will See* (יהוה יראה) is a play on the Hebrew for “the LORD sees” and “the LORD will see.” The rendering reflects both meanings.
- v. 15: The angel’s second call is marked to emphasize its solemnity and the weight of the oath that follows.
- v. 16: “By myself I swear, declares the LORD” reflects the Hebrew *בִּי נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי נְאֻם־יְהוָה* and captures the divine solemnity of the oath.
- v. 20–24: These verses list descendants of Nahor through Milcah and Reumah. The structure and renderings follow the Hebrew carefully, distinguishing between the two mothers and their offspring.
