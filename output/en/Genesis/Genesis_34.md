# Genesis 34

¹ Dinah, the daughter of Leah, whom she had borne to Jacob, went out to see the daughters of the land. ¹⁰ You may live with us, and the land will be open before you. Settle and trade in it. The land is broad for you.” ¹¹ Shechem also said to his father and to his brothers, “Let me find favor in your eyes, and whatever you say to me I will give. ¹² Ask me for a great price and a gift, and I will give as you say to me. Just grant me the girl as my wife.” ¹³ The sons of Jacob answered Shechem and his father Hamor with guile, because he had violated his sister Dinah. ¹⁴ They said to them, “We cannot do this, to give our sister to a man who is uncircumcised. That would be a disgrace to us. ¹⁵ Only this will we agree to: if you will be like us, that each of you be circumcised. ¹⁶ Then we will give our daughters to you, and we will take your daughters for ourselves. We will dwell with you and become one people. ¹⁷ But if you will not listen to us to be circumcised, then we will take our sister and go.” ¹⁸ Their words found favor in the eyes of Hamor and in the eyes of his son Shechem. ¹⁹ And the young man did not delay to do the matter, for he loved the daughter of Jacob. And he was honored in the eyes of all his family. ² And Shechem the son of Hamor the Hivite, the ruler of the land, saw her. He seized her and lay with her, and violated her. ²⁰ Hamor and his son Shechem came to the gate of their city and spoke to the men of their city, saying: ²¹ “These men are at peace with us. Let them dwell in the land and trade in it, for the land is broad for them. Take their daughters as wives and give them our daughters. ²² But on this condition will these men agree to dwell with us and become one people: that every male among us be circumcised, as they are circumcised.” ²³ Their livestock and their property and all their cattle are ours. Only let us agree with them and they will dwell with us. ²⁴ And the men of the city listened to Hamor and to his son Shechem. Every male in the city who went out of the gate was circumcised. ²⁵ On the third day, when they were still in pain, two of Jacob’s sons, Simon and Levi, brothers of Dinah, each took his sword and came against the city unawares, and struck down every male. ²⁶ They put Hamor and his son Shechem to the sword, and took Dinah from the house of Shechem, and went away. ²⁷ The sons of Jacob came upon the slain and plundered the city, because they had violated their sister. ²⁸ They took their flocks and their herds and their donkeys and all that was in the city and all that was in the field. ²⁹ They took all their wealth and all their little ones and their wives and plundered everything in their houses. ³ His soul clung to Dinah, the daughter of Jacob, and he loved the girl. He spoke tenderly to her. ³⁰ Then Jacob said to Simon and Levi, “You have brought trouble upon me, to make me stink among the inhabitants of the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. And I am few in number, they will gather against me and strike me, and I and my house will be destroyed.” ³¹ And they said, “Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?” ⁴ So Shechem said to his father Hamor, “Acquire this young woman for me as my wife.” ⁵ Jacob heard that his daughter had been violated. His sons were still in the field with his livestock, so Jacob remained silent until they returned. ⁶ Hamor, the father of Shechem, went out to Jacob to speak with him. ⁷ The sons of Jacob came from the field when they heard about the matter. They were grieved, and they were furious, because what had been done was abhorrent in Israel to lie with the daughter of Jacob. Notes
- v. 31: The phrase is typically translated as "Should he treat our sister as a prostitute?" but the exact Hebrew "הכזוונה" is a poetic construction. "Cuno" is a rare term meaning "to treat as a prostitute" or "defile." The translation captures the sense of the Hebrew without adding interpretive commentary.
