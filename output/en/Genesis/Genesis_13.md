# Genesis 13

¹ Abram and his wife, along with all that he had, went up from Egypt to the Negev. ² Abram was very rich in livestock, in silver, and in gold. ³ He journeyed on from the Negev to Bethel, to the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between Bethel and Ai. ⁴ He went to the site of the altar he had made there before and called on the name of the LORD. ⁵ Lot, who was going with Abram, had flocks, herds, and tents. ⁶ But the land could not bear the two of them together, for their possessions were too numerous, and they could not live together. ⁷ A quarrel arose between the herdsmen of Abram and the herdsmen of Lot, and at that time the Canaanite and the Perizzite were living in the land. ⁸ Abram said to Lot, "Let there be no quarrel between me and you, and between my herdsmen and your herdsmen, for we are brothers. ⁹ The whole land is before you. Separate yourself from me. If you go to the left, I will go to the right; if you go to the right, I will go to the left." ¹⁰ Lot lifted up his eyes and saw the whole plain of the Jordan, for all of it was well watered, before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah, like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt—this was the plain of Zoar. ¹¹ So Lot chose for himself the whole plain of the Jordan, and Lot journeyed east. Thus they separated from one another, each going his own way. ¹² Abram settled in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled among the cities of the plain and pitched his tent as far as Sodom. ¹³ Now the men of Sodom were wicked and great sinners against the LORD. ¹⁴ The LORD said to Abram, after Lot had separated from him, "Lift up your eyes and look from the place where you are—northward, southward, eastward, and westward. ¹⁵ For all the land that you see I will give to you and to your descendants forever. ¹⁶ I will make your descendants as the dust of the earth. If anyone can count the dust of the earth, then your descendants can be counted." ¹⁷ "Rise up and walk through the length and breadth of the land, for I will give it to you." ¹⁸ Abram pitched his tent and went to live in the oaks of Mamre, which are in Hebron, and there he built an altar to the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "this was the plain of Zoar" — "Zoar" is a rare name; some traditions read "Beloved" or "the Well." "Zoar" is retained as a known name in the context of the Jordan plain.
- v. 18: "oaks of Mamre" — "Mamre" is a proper name, retained in the translation. The "oaks" refer to the terebinth trees, common in such contexts.
