# Genesis 23

¹ Sarah lived a hundred years and twenty years and seven years. These were the years of Sarah’s life. ² Sarah died in Kiriath Arba, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan. Abraham came to mourn for Sarah and to weep over her. ³ Abraham stood up from the face of his dead and spoke to the Hittites, saying, ⁴ “I am a stranger and a resident among you. Give me a burial place among you, so that I may bury my dead before me.” ⁵ The Hittites answered Abraham, saying to him, ⁶ “Listen, my lord. You are a noble of God among us. Choose the best of our burial places and bury your dead. No one among us will withhold it from you. You may bury your dead.” ⁷ Abraham stood up and bowed to the people of the land, the Hittites. ⁸ He said to them, “If it pleases you to bury my dead before me, listen to me and go to Ephron the son of Zohar.” ⁹ Let him give me the cave of Machpelah, which is his, in the edge of his field. He shall give it to me for the full price, and I will take it among you as a burial place.” ¹⁰ Ephron was sitting among the Hittites. Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham in the presence of the Hittites and all the men at the gate of his city, saying, ¹¹ “No, my lord. Listen to me. I give you the field and the cave in it. I give it to you in the presence of the sons of my people. Bury your dead.” ¹² Abraham bowed to the people of the land. ¹³ He said to Ephron in the presence of the people of the land, “But if you please, hear me. I will give you the silver for the field. Take it from me, and I will bury my dead there.” ¹⁴ Ephron answered Abraham, saying, ¹⁵ “My lord, listen to me. Four hundred shekels of silver between you and me. What is that? Bury your dead.” ¹⁶ Abraham listened to Ephron, and Abraham weighed out to Ephron the silver that he had spoken in the presence of the sons of Heth—four hundred shekels of silver, current among the merchants. ¹⁷ The field of Ephron, which was in Machpelah, in front of Mamre, the field and the cave in it, and all the trees in the field, wherever its boundary was around, ¹⁸ became Abraham’s possession in the presence of the sons of Heth, all the men who came to the gate of his city. ¹⁹ After this, Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the cave of the field of Machpelah, which is before Mamre, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan. ²⁰ The field and the cave in it were acquired by Abraham for a burial place from the Hittites.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: The Hebrew uses "years of Sarah’s life" twice to emphasize the full span of her life.  
- v. 4: The term "resident" (תּוֹשָׁב) may imply a sense of long-term settlement rather than temporary sojourn.  
- v. 6: The phrase “noble of God” (נְשִׂיא אֱלֹהִים) is a poetic title for someone of high spiritual or moral authority.  
- v. 9: "Current among the merchants" (עֹבֵר לַסֹּחֵר) refers to the standard weight or valuation in trade.
