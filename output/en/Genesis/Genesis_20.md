# Genesis 20

¹ Abraham left from there and journeyed to the Negev region, and he settled between Kadesh and Shur, and he sojourned in Gerar. ² Abraham said to Sarah, his wife, "Say you are my sister," and Abimelech, the king of Gerar, sent for Sarah and took her. ³ That night, God came to Abimelech in a dream and said to him, "You will surely die for the woman you have taken, for she is a married woman." ⁴ Abimelech did not approach her and said, "Lord, will you even kill a righteous person?" ⁵ "Didn’t he himself say to me, 'She is my sister'?" he said. "And she herself said, 'He truly is my brother.' I did this in purity of heart and clean hands." ⁶ The God of heaven replied in the dream, "I know that you did this with a pure heart, and that is why I have not let you touch her, and so I have protected you from sinning against me." ⁷ Now return the man’s wife, for he is a prophet. He will pray for you and you will live. But if you do not return her, you must know that you and all that is yours will die." ⁸ Abimelech got up early in the morning and called all his servants and repeated all these things to them. The men were very afraid. ⁹ Abimelech summoned Abraham and said, "What have you done to us? What wrong have I done to you that you have brought such a great sin upon me and my kingdom? You have done things that should not be done with me." ¹⁰ Abimelech asked Abraham, "Why did you do this thing?" ¹¹ Abraham answered, "I thought, 'Surely there is no fear of God in this place, and they might kill me because of my wife.'" ¹² "Also," he added, "she is the daughter of my father—though not of my mother—and she became my wife." ¹³ "And when God led me away from my father’s house, I said to her, 'This is the kindness you must do for me in every place we go: say that he is my brother.'" ¹⁴ Abimelech gave Abraham sheep, cattle, male and female slaves, and he returned Sarah, Abraham’s wife, to him. ¹⁵ Abimelech said to Abraham, "Here is my land before you. Live wherever you please." ¹⁶ He said to Sarah, "I am giving you a thousand pieces of silver to your brother. It is for you to make a place for yourself wherever you go with him." ¹⁷ Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female slaves, and they had children. ¹⁸ For the Lord had closed up every womb in the house of Abimelech because of Sarah, the wife of Abraham.

Notes
- v. 11: The phrase "רַק אֵין־יִרְאַת אֱלֹהִים" is translated as "I thought there was no fear of God," reflecting the uncertainty of the human condition in that place.
