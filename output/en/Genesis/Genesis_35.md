# Genesis 35

¹ God said to Jacob, "Arise, go up to Bethel and live there. Build an altar to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau." ² Jacob said to his household and to all who were with him, "Remove the foreign gods from among you, purify yourselves, and change your clothes." ³ "We will prepare and go up to Bethel. There I will build an altar to the God who answered me in my time of trouble and has been with me on the road I have walked." ⁴ They gave Jacob all the foreign gods they had in their possession, along with the earrings in their ears. Jacob buried them under the terebinth tree at Shechem. ⁵ They set out, and the God of the cities around them became hostile, but they did not pursue the sons of Jacob. ⁶ Jacob came to Luz in the land of Canaan, which is Bethel, and there with all the people who were with him. ⁷ He built an altar there and named the place El Bethel, for there the gods revealed themselves to him as he fled from his brother. ⁸ Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died, and was buried under the terebinth at Bethel. Jacob named the place Alon Bachut. ⁹ God appeared to Jacob again as he came from Paddan Aram, and blessed him. ¹⁰ God said to him, "Your name is Jacob. You will not be called Jacob anymore, but Israel will be your name." And he named him Israel. ¹¹ God said to him, "I am God Almighty. Increase and multiply. A nation and a company of nations will come from you, and kings will issue from your loins. ¹² And the land that I gave to Abraham and Isaac, I will give to you and to your offspring after you." ¹³ And God ascended from the place where he had spoken with him. ¹⁴ Jacob set up a pillar at the place where he had spoken with him. He poured a libation over it and anointed it with oil. ¹⁵ Jacob named the place where God had spoken with him there El Bethel. ¹⁶ They set out from Bethel, and while they were still a distance from the land of Ephrath, Rachel went into labor and struggled in childbirth. ¹⁷ As she struggled in childbirth, the midwife said to her, "Do not fear, for this too is a son for you." ¹⁸ When her life was in danger, she named him Ben-Oni, but his father named him Benjamin. ¹⁹ Rachel died and was buried along the road to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem). ²⁰ Jacob set up a pillar on her grave, and it remains the Pillar of Rachel's Burial to this day. ²¹ Israel moved on and pitched his tent beyond Migdol Edar. ²² While Israel was living in that land, Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine. Israel heard about it, and the sons of Jacob were twelve in number. ²³ Leah's sons, the firstborn of Jacob: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. ²⁴ Rachel's sons: Joseph and Benjamin. ²⁵ Bilhah, Rachel's servant, bore Dan and Naphtali. ²⁶ Zilpah, Leah's servant, bore Gad and Asher. These were the sons of Jacob born to him in Paddan Aram. ²⁷ Jacob came to Isaac his father at Mamre, in the city of Arba, which is Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac had dwelled. ²⁸ Isaac lived 180 years. ²⁹ He breathed his last and died, an old man and full of years. He was gathered to his people. His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "You will not be called Jacob anymore, but Israel will be your name" — the name change is a key moment in Jacob's transformation into Israel; the rendering follows the Hebrew without adding theological interpretation.
- v. 18: "Ben-Oni" is translated as "son of sorrow" to reflect the emotional context of the naming.
- v. 19: "Ephrath" is rendered as "Ephrath" rather than "Ephrathah" to avoid redundancy with the parenthetical explanation of Bethlehem.
- v. 20: "Pillar of Rachel's Burial" is a direct rendering of מַצֶּבֶת קְבֻֽרַת־רָחֵל, with no added interpretation.
