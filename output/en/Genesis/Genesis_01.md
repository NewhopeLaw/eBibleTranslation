# Genesis 1

¹ When God set about creating the heavens and the earth, ² the earth was wild and waste, and darkness covered the face of the deep. A wind from God was moving over the surface of the waters. ³ God said, Let there be light, and there was light. ⁴ God saw the light; it was good. God separated the light from the darkness. ⁵ God called the light Day, and the darkness Night. There was evening, and there was morning, the first day. ⁶ God said, Let there be a vault between the waters, to separate water from water. ⁷ God made the vault and separated the waters beneath the vault from the waters above the vault. It happened as God had said. ⁸ God called the vault Heaven. There was evening, and there was morning, the second day. ⁹ God said, Let the waters beneath the heavens gather to one place, and let dry ground appear. It happened as God had said. ¹⁰ God called the dry ground Earth, and the gathered waters He called Seas. God saw it was good. ¹¹ God said, Let the Earth bring forth vegetation, plants yielding seed, trees bearing fruit with seed in it, according to their kinds, on the Earth. It happened as God had said. ¹² The Earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed according to their kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in it according to their kinds. God saw it was good. ¹³ There was evening, and there was morning, the third day. ¹⁴ God said, Let there be lights in the vault of Heaven to separate day from night, and to mark seasons, days, and years. ¹⁵ Let the lights be in the vault of Heaven to give light on Earth. It happened as God had said. ¹⁶ God made the two great lights, the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night, and the stars. ¹⁷ God placed them in the vault of Heaven to give light on Earth. ¹⁸ To rule over day and night and to separate light from darkness. God saw it was good. ¹⁹ There was evening, and there was morning, the fourth day. ²⁰ God said, Let the waters swarm with creatures that have life, and let birds fly above the Earth across the vault of Heaven. ²¹ God made the great sea creatures and every living creature that moves, which the waters swarmed with, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. God saw it was good. ²² God blessed them, saying, Be fruitful, multiply, fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds multiply on Earth. ²³ There was evening, and there was morning, the fifth day. ²⁴ God said, Let the Earth bring forth living creatures according to their kinds: cattle and creeping things and beasts of the Earth, each according to its kind. It happened as God had said. ²⁵ God made the beasts of the Earth according to their kinds, the cattle according to their kinds, and every creeping thing of the ground according to its kind. God saw it was good. ²⁶ God said, Let us make humankind in Our image, according to Our likeness, and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the cattle, and over all the Earth, and over every creeping thing that creeps on the Earth. ²⁷ God created humankind in His image. In the image of God He created them; male and female He created them. ²⁸ God blessed them and said to them, Be fruitful, multiply, fill the Earth, and subdue it. Have dominion over the fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, and every living thing that moves on the Earth. ²⁹ God said, Look, I have given you every plant bearing seed, which is on the surface of all the Earth, and every tree with fruit bearing seed; it will be for your food. ³⁰ And to every beast of the Earth, to every bird of the sky, and to every creature that moves on the Earth, in which is the breath of life, I have given every green plant for food. It happened as God had said. ³¹ God saw everything He had made, and indeed it was very good. There was evening, and there was morning, the sixth day.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "When God set about creating" follows the dependent-clause reading of Genesis 1:1.
- v. 27: "the human" used for ha-adam until the narrative clearly names Adam.
- v. 31: "the sixth day" aligns with the Hebrew ordinal system.
