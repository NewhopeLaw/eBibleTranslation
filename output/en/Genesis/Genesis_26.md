# Genesis 26

¹ A famine occurred in the land, in addition to the earlier famine that had occurred in the days of Abraham. Isaac went to King Abimelech of the Philistines in Gerar. ² The Lord appeared to Isaac and said, “Do not go down to Egypt. Dwell in the land I will tell you about. ³ Sojourn in this land, and I will be with you and will bless you. To you and your descendants I will give all these lands, and I will confirm the oath I swore to your father Abraham. ⁴ I will multiply your descendants like the stars of the heavens and will give them all these lands. And in your descendants, all the nations of the earth will be blessed, just as I promised Abraham your father. ⁵ Because Abraham obeyed my voice and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my teachings.” ⁶ Isaac dwelled in Gerar. ⁷ The men of the place inquired about his wife, and he said, “She is my sister,” fearing, “Lest the men of the place kill me for Rebekah, because she is attractive in appearance.” ⁸ When Isaac had been there a long time, Abimelech king of the Philistines looked out the window and saw Isaac caressing Rebekah, his wife. ⁹ Abimelech called Isaac and said, “Surely she is your wife! Why did you say, ‘She is my sister’?” Isaac said to him, “Because I thought, ‘I may die on her account.’” ¹⁰ Abimelech said, “What is this you have done to us? One of the people might have slept with your wife and brought guilt upon us.” ¹¹ Abimelech commanded all his people, saying, “Whoever harms this man or his wife will be put to death.” ¹² Isaac sowed in that land and reaped a hundredfold in that year. The Lord blessed him. ¹³ The man grew wealthy, advancing until he became very powerful. ¹⁴ He had flocks, herds, and a great household, and the Philistines envied him. ¹⁵ All the wells that his father’s servants had dug in the days of Abraham his father the Philistines had stopped up, filling them with earth. ¹⁶ Abimelech said to Isaac, “Leave us. You have grown much mightier than us.” ¹⁷ So Isaac moved away from there and camped in the Wadi Gerar, where he dwelled. ¹⁸ Isaac returned and dug the wells of water that his father’s servants had dug, which the Philistines had stopped up after Abraham died. He gave them the same names his father had given them. ¹⁹ Isaac’s servants dug in the wadi and found a well of living water. ²⁰ The shepherds of Gerar quarreled with the shepherds of Isaac, saying, “The water is ours.” So he named the well Esek, for they contended with him. ²¹ They dug another well and quarreled over that one too. He named it Sitnah. ²² He moved away from there and dug another well, which they did not contend over. He named it Rehoboth, saying, “Now the Lord has given us room, and we will multiply in the land.” ²³ He went up from there to Beer-sheba. ²⁴ The Lord appeared to him that night and said, “I am the God of Abraham your father. Do not be afraid, for I am with you. I will bless you and multiply your descendants, because of Abraham my servant.” ²⁵ Isaac built an altar there and called on the name of the Lord, and there he pitched his tent. His servants dug a well there. ²⁶ Abimelech came to him from Gerar, and with him was Ahuzzath, his friend, and Phicol, commander of his army. ²⁷ Isaac said to them, “Why have you come to me, when you have hated me and driven me away from yourselves?” ²⁸ They said, “We have seen clearly that the Lord is with you. Let there now be an oath between us, between us and you, and let us make a covenant with you. ²⁹ If we mistreat you, as you have not mistreated us, and as we have only done good to you, then may the Lord bless you.” ³⁰ So they made a feast for him, and they ate and drank. ³¹ Early in the morning they took an oath with each other. Then Isaac sent them away, and they left him in peace. ³² On that day Isaac’s servants came and told him about the well they had dug and said, “We have found water!” ³³ He named it Beer-sheba, on which account the city is called Beer-sheba to this day. ³⁴ Esau was forty years old when he took two wives: Judith, the daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, the daughter of Elon the Hittite. ³⁵ They became a source of grief to Isaac and Rebekah.
