# Genesis 46

¹ Israel set out with all that was his and came to Beer-sheba, where he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. ² And God spoke to Israel in visions of the night and said, "Jacob, Jacob." And he said, "Here I am." ³ Then He said, "I am God, the God of your father. Do not fear going down to Egypt, for I will make you there into a great nation. ⁴ I myself will go down with you to Egypt, and I myself will bring you up again. And Joseph will place his hand on your eyes." ⁵ So Jacob rose from Beer-sheba. The sons of Israel carried their father Jacob, their little ones, and their wives, in the wagons that Pharaoh had sent to carry him. ⁶ They took their livestock and all the goods they had acquired in the land of Canaan and went to Egypt, Jacob and all his offspring with him. ⁷ His sons and his sons' sons, his daughters and his sons' daughters, and all his descendants, he brought with him to Egypt. ⁸ These are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt, Jacob and his sons. Reuben, the firstborn of Jacob: ⁹ the sons of Reuben: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. ¹⁰ The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Saul the son of the Canaanite. ¹¹ The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. ¹² The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah. Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul. ¹³ The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puvah, Iob, and Shimron. ¹⁴ The sons of Zebulun: Sered, Elon, and Jahleel. ¹⁵ These are the sons of Leah, whom she bore to Jacob in Padan-aram, together with Dinah his daughter. All the souls, his sons and his daughters: thirty-three. ¹⁶ The sons of Gad: Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, and Arodi, and Areli. ¹⁷ The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah. The sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel. ¹⁸ These are the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Leah. She bore these to Jacob. Sixteen souls in all. ¹⁹ The sons of Rachel, Jacob's wife: Joseph and Benjamin. ²⁰ To Joseph in the land of Egypt, who was born to him by Asenath, the daughter of Poti-Phera, the priest of On, were born Manasseh and Ephraim. ²¹ The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Hupham, and Ard. ²² These are the sons of Rachel who were born to Jacob. Fourteen souls in all. ²³ The son of Dan: Hushim. ²⁴ The sons of Naphtali: Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem. ²⁵ These are the sons of Bilhah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Rachel. She bore these to Jacob. Seven souls in all. ²⁶ All the persons who came from Jacob to Egypt, descendants from his body, not counting Jacob's wives. All the souls of the offspring of Jacob who came to Egypt: sixty-six. ²⁷ The sons of Joseph who were born to him in Egypt were two persons. All the souls of the house of Jacob who came to Egypt: seventy. ²⁸ He sent Judah ahead to Joseph to guide him to the land of Goshen. And they came into the land of Goshen. ²⁹ Then Joseph harnessed his chariot and went up to meet Israel, his father, to Goshen. And he saw him, and he fell upon his neck and wept again on his neck. ³⁰ Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die, after I have seen your face, for you are still alive." ³¹ Joseph said to his brothers and to the household of his father, "I will go up and tell Pharaoh, and I will say to him, 'My brothers and the household of my father, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me.' ³² The men are shepherds, for they have been keepers of livestock, and their flocks and herds and all they own have come with them." ³³ And he said, "When Pharaoh calls you and says, 'What is your occupation?' ³⁴ you shall say, 'Your servants have been keepers of livestock since our youth, even until now, just as our fathers were.' So that you may live in the land of Goshen, for the Egyptians despise all shepherds."

---

Notes
- v. 4: "Joseph will place his hand on your eyes" — a poetic and symbolic expression of Joseph's role in caring for Jacob.
- v. 34: "the Egyptians despise all shepherds" — reflects cultural context rather than a moral judgment; rendered clearly in modern terms.
