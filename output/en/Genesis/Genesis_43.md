# Genesis 43

¹ The famine was severe in the land. ² When they had finished eating the grain they had brought from Egypt, their father said to them, "Go again. Get me a bit more food." ³ Judah said to him, "The man gave us a warning. He said, 'You won't see my face again unless your younger brother is with you.' ⁴ If you are willing to send the boy with us, we'll go down and get food for you. ⁵ But if you don't send him, we won't go. The man made it clear to us: 'You won't see my face again unless your younger brother is with you.'" ⁶ Jacob said to them, "Why have you treated me so badly by telling the man there is another son of mine?" ⁷ They replied, "The man asked us, 'Does your father still live? Do you have another brother?' We told him the truth. We didn't know he would say, 'Bring your younger brother down.'" ⁸ Judah said to his father Israel, "Let the boy go with me, and we will go. We will go and come back, that you and we and our children may live. ⁹ I myself will be responsible for him. If I don't bring him back to you and leave him in your care, I will bear the blame before you forever." ¹⁰ If we hadn't delayed, we would have already gone twice. ¹¹ Jacob said to them, "Then do this: Take some of the best in the land with you in your bags and bring a gift to the man — a little balm, a little honey, spices, myrrh, nuts, and almonds. ¹² Take twice the money with you. Put the money returned to you in your bags — perhaps it was a mistake." ¹³ Take your brother too, and go back to the man. ¹⁴ May the God who has been with me from the beginning show you mercy before the man and let him release to you your other brother and Benjamin. As for me, if I must grieve, I will grieve." ¹⁵ So the men took the gift and put double the money in their bags, and Benjamin with them. They went down to Egypt and stood before Joseph. ¹⁶ When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the man in charge of his household, "Bring these men into the house. Slaughter an animal and prepare food. These men will eat with me at noon." ¹⁷ The steward did as Joseph had said. He brought the men into Joseph's house. ¹⁸ The men were afraid when they were brought into Joseph's house. They said, "It is because the money was returned to us in our sacks the first time that we're being brought here to be made to stumble and to be seized with our donkeys." ¹⁹ So they went to the man in charge of Joseph's house and spoke to him at the entrance of the house. ²⁰ They said, "My lord, we came down the first time to buy food. ²¹ When we reached the lodging place, we opened our sacks and each man found his money at the top of his sack — our money, in full weight. So we brought it back in our hands to give to us. ²² We brought down additional money with us to buy food. We didn't know who put our money in our sacks." ²³ The man said, "Don't be afraid. The God of your father has given you the money in your sacks. Your money has come to me. Now I will return your brother to you." ²⁴ Then the man brought the men into Joseph's house, gave them water, and they washed their feet. He gave fodder for their donkeys. ²⁵ The gift was prepared before Joseph came at noon, for they had heard they would eat there. ²⁶ Joseph came into the house, and they brought the gift to him inside. They bowed to him to the ground. ²⁷ He asked them about their well-being and said, "Is your aged father well, the one you spoke of? Is he still alive?" ²⁸ They replied, "Your servant our father is well. He is still alive," and they bowed low to him. ²⁹ His eyes looked up and saw Benjamin, his brother, the son of his mother. Joseph said, "This is your younger brother, the one you spoke of to me. God be gracious to you, my son!" ³⁰ Joseph was overcome with emotion for his brother and wanted to weep. He went into the inner room and wept there. ³¹ He washed his face and came out. He controlled himself and said, "Set the food." ³² They set food separately for him, separately for them, and separately for the Egyptians who ate with him, for the Egyptians would not eat with Hebrews — it was distasteful to them. ³³ The men sat before him in order of their birthright, from the firstborn to the youngest. The men were amazed at each other. ³⁴ Joseph took from the table the portions set before him and gave them to the men. But the portion for Benjamin was five times larger than for any of the others. They drank and were merry with him.

---

Notes
- v. 31: "controlled himself" — the Hebrew phrase יִתְאַפַּק likely means to compose oneself or steady one's emotions after weeping.
- v. 32: "it was distasteful to them" — the phrase הִיא תֹּעֵבָה is used to explain the Egyptian dietary taboo.
- v. 34: "five times larger" — the phrase חָמֵשׁ יָדוֹת is interpreted as "five times" the size of the portions given to others.
