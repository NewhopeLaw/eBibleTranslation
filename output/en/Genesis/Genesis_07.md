# Genesis 7

¹ The LORD said to Noah, “You and your entire household come into the ark, for I have seen you as righteous before me in this generation. ² From every clean beast you are to take for yourself seven pairs, male and female, and from the beast that is not clean you are to take two, male and female. ³ Likewise, from the birds of the sky—seven pairs, male and female, to keep alive a living seed on the surface of the whole earth. ⁴ For in seven more days I will rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will blot out every living thing I have made from the face of the ground.” ⁵ Noah did as the LORD had commanded him. ⁶ Noah was six hundred years old when the floodwaters came upon the earth. ⁷ Noah and his sons and his wife and his sons’ wives with him went into the ark because of the waters of the flood. ⁸ From the clean beast and the unclean beast, and from the birds and all that crawls on the ground, ⁹ two by two they came to Noah into the ark, male and female, as God had commanded Noah. ¹⁰ And it came to pass in the seventh day that the floodwaters were upon the earth. ¹¹ In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month—on that day all the fountains of the great deep burst forth, and the floodgates of the heavens were opened. ¹² The rain was upon the earth for forty days and forty nights. ¹³ On that very day Noah and Shem and Ham and Japheth, Noah’s sons, and Noah’s wife and the three wives of his sons with them entered the ark, ¹⁴ they and every living creature by its kind, and all the beasts by their kind, and all the crawling things that crawl on the earth by their kind, and every bird by its kind, every winged bird. ¹⁵ They entered the ark with Noah, two by two of every kind of flesh in which there was breath of life. ¹⁶ Those entering were male and female of every kind of flesh, as God had commanded him. And the LORD shut him in. ¹⁷ The flood was upon the earth for forty days, and the waters increased and lifted up the ark and it rose above the earth. ¹⁸ The waters increased greatly, very greatly upon the earth, and the ark floated on the surface of the waters. ¹⁹ The waters increased so greatly that all the high mountains under the entire sky were covered. ²⁰ The waters rose fifteen cubits over the mountains and covered them. ²¹ Every living thing that moved on the surface of the earth—birds, beasts, creeping things, and all the creatures that swarm on the earth, and every human being— ²² everything in whose nose was the breath of life, of all that was on dry land, perished. ²³ He wiped out every living thing that was on the face of the earth—human and beast and creeping thing and birds of the sky—and they were blotted out from the earth. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark. ²⁴ The waters prevailed on the earth for one hundred and fifty days.

---

Notes
- v. 20: "fifteen cubits" is a literal translation of "חמשׁ עשרה אמה", which is 15 cubits. This is a specific measurement, though some interpretations may equate it with "fifteen feet" or similar, depending on the cubit length assumed.
- v. 21: "everything in whose nose was the breath of life" translates "כֹּל אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁמַת־רוּחַ חַיִּים בְּאַפָּיו" literally. The phrase "in whose nose" reflects the Hebrew idiom, not a literal anatomical location.
- v. 24: "one hundred and fifty days" is a direct translation of "חמשׁים וּמְאַת יוֹם". Some manuscripts or traditions may render the number differently, but the text here is clear.
