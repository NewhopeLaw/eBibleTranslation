# Genesis 24

¹ Abraham was old, advanced in years. The LORD had blessed Abraham in all things. ² He said to his servant, the elder of his household, who was in charge of all he had, "Place your hand under my thigh, ³ and I will make you swear by the LORD, the God of heaven and the God of earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I live. ⁴ But you must go to my land and to my family, and take a wife for my son Isaac." ⁵ The servant said to him, "What if the woman is not willing to come back with me to this land? Should I bring your son back to the land from which you came?" ⁶ Abraham said to him, "Beware that you do not return my son there. ⁷ The LORD, the God of heaven, who brought me from my father's house and from the land of my birth and who spoke to me and swore to me, saying, 'To your offspring I will give this land,' He will send His angel before you, and you will take a wife for my son from there. ⁸ If the woman is not willing to follow you, you will be free from this oath of mine. Only do not return my son there." ⁹ The servant placed his hand under Abraham's thigh and swore to him concerning that matter. ¹⁰ Then the servant took ten of his master's camels, and all the good things of his master were in his hand. He rose and went to Aram-Naharaim, to the city of Nahor. ¹¹ He blessed the camels outside the city at the well of water at the time of evening, at the time of the women coming to draw water. ¹² He said, "O LORD, God of my master Abraham, please cause me to find favor in your sight and do kindness with my master Abraham. ¹³ I stand by the spring of water, and the daughters of the men of the city are coming out to draw water. ¹⁴ May it be that the young woman to whom I say, 'Lower your jar, that I may drink,' and she says, 'Drink, and I will also water your camels,' she will be the one you have appointed for your servant Isaac. Then I will know that you have shown kindness to my master." ¹⁵ It had not yet finished speaking when here was Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother, coming out with her jar on her shoulder. ¹⁶ The young woman was very good-looking, a virgin, and no man had known her. She went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came up. ¹⁷ The servant ran to meet her and said, "Please let me have a little water to drink from your jar." ¹⁸ She said, "Drink, my lord," and quickly lowered her jar to her hand and gave him a drink. ¹⁹ After she had given him a drink, she said, "I will also water your camels until they have finished drinking." ²⁰ She quickly emptied her jar into the trough and ran again to the well to draw water, and she watered all the camels. ²¹ The man was watching her in silence to know whether the LORD had made his journey successful or not. ²² When the camels had finished drinking, the man took out a gold ring weighing a half-shekel, and two gold bracelets for her hands, weighing ten shekels. ²³ He said, "Whose daughter are you? Please tell me, is there a place for us to stay?" ²⁴ She said to him, "I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore." ²⁵ She added, "We also have plenty of straw and feed, and also a place to spend the night." ²⁶ The man bowed down before the LORD. ²⁷ He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who has not abandoned His kindness and faithfulness toward me. The LORD led me on the way to the house of my master's brothers." ²⁸ The young woman ran and told her household about these things. ²⁹ Now Rebekah had a brother named Laban, and Laban ran out to the man at the spring. ³⁰ When Laban saw the ring and the bracelets on his sister's hands, and heard his sister Rebekah speak, saying, "This is how the man spoke to me," he went to the man and there he was, standing by the camels at the spring. ³¹ He said, "Come in, blessed be the LORD. Why do you stand outside? I have made the house ready, and a place for the camels." ³² The man went into the house, and he untied the camels, and gave straw and feed to the camels, and water to wash his feet and the feet of the men who were with him. ³³ He set food before him. He said, "I will not eat until I have spoken my words." He said, "Speak." ³⁴ He said, "I am the servant of Abraham." ³⁵ "The LORD has greatly blessed my master, and he has become great. He has given him sheep and cattle, silver and gold, male and female servants, and camels and donkeys. ³⁶ Sarah, my master's wife, bore a son to my master in his old age, and he gave him everything he has." ³⁷ "My master made me swear, saying, 'You must not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose land I live. ³⁸ But you must go to my father's house and to my family, and take a wife for my son.'" ³⁹ "I said to my master, 'What if the woman is not willing to come with me?' ⁴⁰ He said to me, 'The LORD, before whom I walk, will send His angel with you and make your journey successful, and you will take a wife for my son from my father's family and from my father's house.' ⁴¹ Then you will be free from your oath if you go to my family and they do not give her to you. But if they give her to you, then you will be free from your oath." ⁴² "I came today to the spring and said, 'O LORD, God of my master Abraham, please let me find success in my journey, the one on which I walk. ⁴³ I stand by the spring of water, and the young woman who comes out to draw water, I will say to her, 'Please give me a little water to drink from your jar.' ⁴⁴ She will say to me, 'Drink, and I will also water your camels.' That will be the woman the LORD has appointed for the son of my master.'" ⁴⁵ "I had not yet finished speaking in my heart when behold, Rebekah came, with her jar on her shoulder. She went down to the spring and drew water, and I said to her, 'Please give me to drink.'" ⁴⁶ "She quickly lowered her jar from her shoulder and said, 'Drink, and I will also water your camel.' I drank, and the camel also drank. ⁴⁷ I asked her, 'Whose daughter are you?' She said, 'I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore.' I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her hands. ⁴⁸ I bowed down before the LORD and blessed the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who had led me on a true path to take the daughter of my master's brother for his son." ⁴⁹ "Now then, if you will show kindness and faithfulness to my master, tell me, and if not, tell me, and I will turn to the right or to the left." ⁵⁰ Laban and Bethuel answered, "This matter has come from the LORD; we cannot speak to you either good or bad. ⁵¹ Here is Rebekah before you. Take her and go, and let her be a wife to your master's son, just as the LORD has spoken." ⁵² When the servant heard their words, he bowed to the ground before the LORD. ⁵³ Then the servant brought out articles of silver, articles of gold, and garments, and gave them to Rebekah. He also gave gifts to her brother and to her mother. ⁵⁴ They ate and drank, he and the men who were with him, and they spent the night there. He rose in the morning, and said, "Let me now go to my master." ⁵⁵ Her brother and her mother said, "Let the young woman stay with us a few days, ten or so, and then she may go." ⁵⁶ But he said to them, "Do not delay me, for the LORD has made my journey successful. Let me go so that I may go to my master." ⁵⁷ They said, "We will call the young woman and ask her what she thinks." ⁵⁸ They called Rebekah and said to her, "Will you go with this man?" She said, "I will go." ⁵⁹ So they sent their sister Rebekah, along with her nurse and Abraham's servant and his men. ⁶⁰ They blessed Rebekah and said to her, "Our sister, may you become thousands of myriads, and may your offspring possess the gates of those who hate them." ⁶¹ Rebekah and her young women rose and mounted the camels, and they followed the man. The servant took Rebekah and went away. ⁶² Isaac had come from Beer-lahai-roi and was living in the Negev. ⁶³ Isaac went out to meditate in the field in the evening, and he lifted his eyes and saw, and behold, camels were coming. ⁶⁴ Rebekah lifted her eyes and saw Isaac. She dismounted from the camel. ⁶⁵ She said to the servant, "Who is that man walking in the field toward us?" The servant said, "That is my master." She took the veil and covered herself. ⁶⁶ The servant told Isaac all the things he had done. ⁶⁷ Isaac brought Rebekah into the tent of Sarah his mother. He took Rebekah and she became his wife, and he loved her. Isaac was comforted after his mother's death.

Notes
- v. 1: "advanced in years" reflects the Hebrew's "בָּא בַּיָּמִים" which literally means "came in the days."
- v. 5: "the land from which you came" refers to the land of Ur, not explicitly named, as the text does not mention it.
- v. 14: "showed kindness to my master" reflects the Hebrew's "עָשִׂיתָ חֶסֶד עִם־אֲדֹנִֽי," where "חֶסֶד" is translated as "kindness" or "steadfast love."
- v. 21: "the LORD had made his journey successful" reflects the Hebrew's "הֹכִיחַ יְהוָה לְבֶן־אֲדֹנִֽי," where "הֹכִיחַ" is translated as "made successful."
- v. 48: "led me on a true path" reflects the Hebrew's "הִנְחַנִּי בְּדֶרֶךְ אֱמֶת," where "הִנְחַנִּי" is translated as "led me."
- v. 67: "was comforted after his mother's death" assumes the narrative context that Sarah, Isaac's mother, had died before this point.
