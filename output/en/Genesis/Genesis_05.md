# Genesis 5

¹ This is the account of Adam’s descendants. When God created Adam, he made him in God’s likeness. ² He created him male and female, blessed them, and called their name Adam on the day they were created. ³ Adam lived 130 years and fathered a son in his own likeness, in his own image. He named him Seth. ⁴ After fathering Seth, Adam lived 800 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ⁵ All the days that Adam lived totaled 930 years, and he died. ⁶ Seth lived 105 years and fathered Enosh. ⁷ After fathering Enosh, Seth lived 807 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ⁸ All the days that Seth lived totaled 912 years, and he died. ⁹ Enosh lived 90 years and fathered Cainan. ¹⁰ After fathering Cainan, Enosh lived 815 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ¹¹ All the days that Enosh lived totaled 905 years, and he died. ¹² Cainan lived 70 years and fathered Mahalalel. ¹³ After fathering Mahalalel, Cainan lived 840 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ¹⁴ All the days that Cainan lived totaled 910 years, and he died. ¹⁵ Mahalalel lived 65 years and fathered Jared. ¹⁶ After fathering Jared, Mahalalel lived 830 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ¹⁷ All the days that Mahalalel lived totaled 895 years, and he died. ¹⁸ Jared lived 162 years and fathered Enoch. ¹⁹ After fathering Enoch, Jared lived 800 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ²⁰ All the days that Jared lived totaled 962 years, and he died. ²¹ Enoch lived 65 years and fathered Methuselah. ²² After fathering Methuselah, Enoch walked with God for 300 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ²³ All the days that Enoch lived totaled 365 years. ²⁴ Enoch walked with God, and he was not, for God had taken him. ²⁵ Methuselah lived 187 years and fathered Lamech. ²⁶ After fathering Lamech, Methuselah lived 782 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ²⁷ All the days that Methuselah lived totaled 969 years, and he died. ²⁸ Lamech lived 182 years and fathered a son. ²⁹ He named him Noah, saying, “This one will bring us relief from our labor and from the toil of our hands from the ground that the LORD has cursed.” ³⁰ After fathering Noah, Lamech lived 595 years and fathered other sons and daughters. ³¹ All the days that Lamech lived totaled 777 years, and he died. ³² Noah was 500 years old when he fathered Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the account of Adam’s descendants" — The Hebrew is "sefer toledot," literally "book of generations," but here it functions as an introduction to a genealogical or narrative section.
- v. 24: "he was not" — The Hebrew is "ve’einenu," literally "and he was not," referring to Enoch’s mysterious disappearance. The phrase "for God had taken him" is an interpretive gloss.
- v. 29: "relief from our labor" — The Hebrew term "machamenu" is translated here as "relief," but could also carry a more emotional tone, such as "comfort" or "consolation."
