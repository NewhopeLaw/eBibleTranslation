# Genesis 25

¹ Abraham took another wife, and her name was Keturah. ² She bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. ³ Jokshan fathered Sheba and Dedan. Dedan's descendants were Ashurim, Letushim, and Leumim. ⁴ The descendants of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Avidah, and Eldaah. All of these were descendants of Keturah. ⁵ Abraham gave all he had to Isaac. ⁶ To the sons of his concubines, Abraham gave gifts and sent them away from Isaac, his son, to the east, to the land of the east, while he was still alive. ⁷ These are the years of the life of Abraham: he lived 137 years. ⁸ Abraham died at a good old age, having lived a full and prosperous life, and he was gathered to his people. ⁹ Isaac and Ishmael his sons buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite, the one across from Mamre. ¹⁰ The field Abraham had bought from the sons of Hittite—there Abraham and his wife Sarah were buried. ¹¹ After Abraham's death, God blessed Isaac his son, and Isaac lived near Beer-lahai-roi. ¹² These are the generations of Ishmael, son of Abraham, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's servant, bore to Abraham. ¹³ These are the names of the sons of Ishmael, in order of their birth: Nebaioth, Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, ¹⁴ Mishma, Dumah, Massa, ¹⁵ Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. ¹⁶ These were the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names by their settlements and strongholds—twelve rulers over their nations. ¹⁷ These are the years of the life of Ishmael: he lived 137 years, and he died, and was gathered to his people. ¹⁸ He lived from Havilah to Shur, which is opposite Egypt, toward Asshur. He fell among all his brothers. ¹⁹ These are the generations of Isaac, son of Abraham. Abraham fathered Isaac. ²⁰ When Isaac was forty years old, he took Rebekah daughter of Bethuel the Aramean from Padan-aram, the sister of Laban the Aramean, to be his wife. ²¹ Isaac prayed to the LORD on behalf of his wife, for she was barren. The LORD answered his prayer, and Rebekah his wife conceived. ²² The children struggled together within her, and she said, "If it is so, why am I like this?" So she went to inquire of the LORD. ²³ The LORD said to her, "Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples from your body will be separated. One people will be stronger than the other, and the older will serve the younger." ²⁴ When her days to give birth were fulfilled, there was a twin in her womb. ²⁵ The first one came out reddish all over, like a hairy blanket. They named him Esau. ²⁶ Afterward, his brother came out, and his hand was grasping Esau's heel. They named him Jacob. Isaac was sixty years old when they were born. ²⁷ The boys grew up. Esau became a skillful hunter, a man of the field, while Jacob was a quiet man, staying in tents. ²⁸ Isaac loved Esau because he ate game, but Rebekah loved Jacob. ²⁹ One day, Jacob was cooking a stew, and Esau came in from the field, exhausted. ³⁰ He said to Jacob, "Pour some of that red stuff over here, for I am exhausted." So he was named Edom. ³¹ Jacob said, "Sell me your birthright for today." ³² Esau said, "I am about to die. What good is a birthright to me?" ³³ Jacob said, "Swear to me now," and he swore to him. Jacob sold Esau his birthright. ³⁴ Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. He ate, drank, and then got up and left. Esau despised his birthright.

---

Notes
- v. 30: The name "Edom" (אֱדוֹם) is derived from the reddish stew Esau requested, and is explained here as a direct renaming.
- v. 34: "Despised his birthright" renders בָּז, which conveys contempt or disregard.
