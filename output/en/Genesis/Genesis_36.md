# Genesis 36

¹ These are the descendants of Esau, also known as Edom. ² Esau took wives from the daughters of Canaan: Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite; Aholibamah, daughter of Anah, daughter of Tsibyon the Horite; ³ and Basmath, daughter of Ishmael, sister of Nebayoth. ⁴ Adah bore Esau Eliphaz, and Basmath bore Reuel. ⁵ Aholibamah bore Jeush, Jaalam, and Korach. These were the sons of Esau born to him in the land of Canaan. ⁶ Esau took his wives, his children, his sons’ children, and all the members of his household, along with his livestock, his cattle, and all the possessions he had acquired in the land of Canaan. He went to a land away from his brother Jacob. ⁷ For their possessions were too many for them to live together, and the land of their dwellings could not bear them because of their livestock. ⁸ So Esau lived in the hill country of Seir. Esau is Edom. ⁹ These are the descendants of Esau, the father of Edom, in the hill country of Seir. ¹⁰ The names of Esau's sons were: Eliphaz, son of Adah, wife of Esau; and Reuel, son of Basmath, wife of Esau. ¹¹ The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Tzephah, Gatam, and Kenaz. ¹² Timna was a concubine to Eliphaz, son of Esau, and she bore to Eliphaz Amalek. These were the sons of Adah, wife of Esau. ¹³ The sons of Reuel were Nahath, Zerah, Shamma, and Mizzah. These were the sons of Basmath, wife of Esau. ¹⁴ The sons of Aholibamah, daughter of Anah, daughter of Tsibyon, wife of Esau, were Jeush, Jaalam, and Korach. ¹⁵ These were the chieftains of the sons of Esau. The sons of Eliphaz, the firstborn of Esau: chief Teman, chief Omar, chief Tzephah, chief Gatam, ¹⁶ chief Korach, chief Gatam, chief Amalek. These were the chieftains of Eliphaz in the land of Edom. These were the sons of Adah. ¹⁷ The sons of Reuel, son of Esau: chief Nahath, chief Zerah, chief Shamma, chief Mizzah. These were the chieftains of Reuel in the land of Edom. These were the sons of Basmath, wife of Esau. ¹⁸ The sons of Aholibamah, wife of Esau: chief Jeush, chief Jaalam, chief Korach. These were the chieftains of Aholibamah, daughter of Anah, wife of Esau. ¹⁹ These were the sons of Esau and their chieftains. He was Edom. ²⁰ These were the sons of Seir the Horite, who lived in the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, and Anah. ²¹ Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These were the chieftains of the Horites, sons of Seir in the land of Edom. ²² The sons of Lotan were Horim and Hemam, and his sister was Timna. ²³ The sons of Shobal were Alvan, Manachath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. ²⁴ The sons of Zibeon were Ayah and Anah. This Anah is the one who found the water in the wilderness while tending his father Zibeon’s donkeys. ²⁵ The sons of Anah were Dishon and Aholibamah, daughter of Anah. ²⁶ The sons of Dishan were Hemdan, Eshban, Yitrann, and Keran. ²⁷ The sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. ²⁸ The sons of Dishan were Uz and Aran. ²⁹ These were the chieftains of the Horites: chief Lotan, chief Shobal, chief Zibeon, chief Anah, ³⁰ chief Dishon, chief Ezer, chief Dishan. These were the chieftains of the Horites in the land of Seir, according to their clans. ³¹ These were the kings who ruled in the land of Edom before any king ruled over the sons of Israel. ³² Bela son of Beor ruled in Edom, and the name of his city was Dinhabah. ³³ Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah of Bashrah ruled in his place. ³⁴ Jobab died, and Husham of the land of the Temanites ruled in his place. ³⁵ Husham died, and Hadad son of Bedad, who had defeated Midian in the fields of Moab, ruled in his place. The name of his city was Avith. ³⁶ Hadad died, and Samlah of Masrekah ruled in his place. ³⁷ Samlah died, and Saul of the river country ruled in his place. ³⁸ Saul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor ruled in his place. ³⁹ Baal-hanan son of Achbor died, and Hadar ruled in his place. The name of his city was Pau, and the name of his wife was Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezah. ⁴⁰ These are the names of the chieftains of Esau, according to their clans, according to their places, by their names: chief Timna, chief Alva, chief Yetet, ⁴¹ chief Aholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon, ⁴² chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar, ⁴³ chief Magdiel, chief Iram. These were the chieftains of Edom, in their settlements, in the land of their inheritance. He was Esau, the father of Edom.

Notes  
- v. 12: "Timna was a concubine to Eliphaz" — The Hebrew is ambiguous whether Timna is a woman or a place. The reading assumes she is a woman based on context.  
- v. 24: "This Anah is the one who found the water..." — The reference to Anah finding water is a parenthetical explanation of a well-known figure in the context of Edom.  
- v. 39: "Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezah" — The Hebrew structure is complex, and the rendering assumes a genealogical chain.  
- v. 43: "the land of their inheritance" — The Hebrew uses "achuzah," which can mean "possession" or "inheritance," and is rendered here in context.
