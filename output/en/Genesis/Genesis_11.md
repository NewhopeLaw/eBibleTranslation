# Genesis 11

¹ Now the whole earth had one language and the same words. ² As they traveled eastward, they found a plain in the land of Shinar and settled there. ³ They said to one another, "Come, let us make bricks and burn them thoroughly." The brick became their stone and the clay their mortar. ⁴ They said, "Come, let us build a city and a tower with its top in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves, lest we be scattered over the face of the whole earth." ⁵ The LORD came down to see the city and the tower that the people of the earth had built. ⁶ And the LORD said, "Indeed, they are one people and they all have one language, and this is only the beginning of what they will do. Now nothing they propose to do will be impossible for them. ⁷ Come, let us go down and confuse their language, so that they cannot understand one another's speech." ⁸ So the LORD scattered them from there over the face of the whole earth, and they ceased to build the city. ⁹ Therefore the name of it was called Babel, because there the LORD confused the language of the whole earth, and from there the LORD scattered them over the face of the whole earth. ¹⁰ These are the generations of Shem. Shem was a hundred years old when he fathered Arpachshad two years after the flood. ¹¹ Shem lived after fathering Arpachshad five hundred years and had other sons and daughters. ¹² Arpachshad lived thirty-five years and fathered Shelah. ¹³ Arpachshad lived after fathering Shelah four hundred and three years and had other sons and daughters. ¹⁴ Shelah lived thirty years and fathered Eber. ¹⁵ Shelah lived after fathering Eber four hundred and three years and had other sons and daughters. ¹⁶ Eber lived thirty-four years and fathered Peleg. ¹⁷ Eber lived after fathering Peleg four hundred and thirty years and had other sons and daughters. ¹⁸ Peleg lived thirty years and fathered Reu. ¹⁹ Peleg lived after fathering Reu two hundred and nine years and had other sons and daughters. ²⁰ Reu lived thirty-two years and fathered Serug. ²¹ Reu lived after fathering Serug two hundred and seven years and had other sons and daughters. ²² Serug lived thirty years and fathered Nahor. ²³ Serug lived after fathering Nahor two hundred years and had other sons and daughters. ²⁴ Nahor lived twenty-nine years and fathered Terah. ²⁵ Nahor lived after fathering Terah nineteen years and one hundred years and had other sons and daughters. ²⁶ Terah lived seventy years and fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran. ²⁷ These are the generations of Terah. Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran. Haran fathered Lot. ²⁸ Haran died in the presence of his father Terah in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldeans. ²⁹ Abram and Nahor took wives. The name of Abram’s wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahor’s wife was Milcah, daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah and also the father of Iscah. ³⁰ Sarai was barren; she had no child. ³¹ Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran, his grandson, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of Abram his son, and they went out together from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan. They arrived at Haran and settled there. ³² The years of Terah were two hundred and five years, and Terah died in Haran.

Notes
- v. 9: "Babel" is a transliteration of the Hebrew word בָּבֶל, which plays on the verb בָּלַל (to confuse/mingle), as noted in the text. The name is explained as a reference to the confusion of language.
- v. 31: "Ur of the Chaldeans" is a direct translation of אוּר כַּשְׂדִּים, with "Chaldeans" used to correspond to the historical term.
