# 2 Timothy 4

¹ I solemnly declare before God and the Messiah Jesus, who is coming to judge the living and the dead, and to make his coming and his kingdom known: ² Preach the message; be ready at all times, whether convenient or not. Rebuke, warn, and encourage with all patience and instruction. ³ For there will come a time when people will no longer tolerate sound teaching, but according to their own desires they will gather teachers for themselves, being weighed down by their ears, ⁴ and they will turn away from the truth and be drawn away to myths. ⁵ But you, be alert in all things; endure hardship; do the work of an evangelist; fulfill your ministry. ⁶ For I am already being poured out, and the time of my dissolution is near. ⁷ I have fought the good fight, I have completed the race, I have kept the faith. ⁸ There is laid up for me now the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will award me on that day—not only to me but also to all who have loved his appearing. ⁹ Make every effort to come to me soon. ¹⁰ For Demas has abandoned me, having loved this present age, and he has gone to Thessalonica. Crescens has gone to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia. ¹¹ Luke alone is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me in ministry. ¹² Tychicus I have sent to Ephesus. ¹³ Bring the papyrus rolls I left with Carpus at Troas when you come. ¹⁴ Alexander the coppersmith did me much harm; may the Lord repay him for his deeds. ¹⁵ You too must guard yourself against him, for he has resisted our words fiercely. ¹⁶ In my first defense, no one stood with me, but everyone abandoned me—though I do not count them worthy of blame. ¹⁷ But the Lord stood with me and gave me strength, so that through me the message was fully proclaimed, and all the nations heard it. And I was rescued from the mouth of the lion. ¹⁸ The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and will bring me safely into his heavenly kingdom. To him be glory forever and ever. Amen. ¹⁹ Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus. ²⁰ Erastus remained in Corinth, and I left Trophimus sick in Miletus. ²¹ Make every effort to come before winter. Greet you Eubulus and Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers. ²² The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you all.

---

Notes
- v. 14: "Alexander the coppersmith" — the term "coppersmith" is a literal translation of χαλκεὺς (chalkeus), a term possibly used metaphorically or as an occupational label. No clear textual or contextual reason to deviate from the direct rendering.
- v. 18: "heavenly kingdom" — the term βασιλεία τοῦ θεοῦ (the kingdom of God) is translated as "heavenly kingdom" to reflect both the divine origin and the transcendent nature of the realm.
