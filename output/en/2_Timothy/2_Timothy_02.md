# 2 Timothy 2

¹ You then, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus, ² and what you heard from me through many witnesses, entrust to faithful people, who will also be able to teach others. ³ Share in suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. ⁴ No one serving in the army is entangled in the affairs of daily life, so as to please the one who enlisted him. ⁵ If anyone competes, they are not crowned unless they compete according to the rules. ⁶ A laboring farmer should be the first to receive the harvest. ⁷ Reflect on what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in all things. ⁸ Remember Jesus Christ, who was raised from the dead, a descendant of David, as in my gospel, ⁹ for which I suffer, even to the point of chains, as if I were a criminal. But the word of God is not bound. ¹⁰ For this reason I endure all things for the elect, so that they too may obtain salvation in Christ Jesus with eternal glory. ¹¹ The saying is trustworthy: If we died with him, we will also live with him; ¹² if we endure, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he will also deny us; ¹³ if we are unfaithful, he remains faithful, for he cannot deny himself. ¹⁴ Remind them of these things, testifying before the Lord, not to argue about words, which is of no use, and only leads to the ruin of those who hear. ¹⁵ Strive to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, correctly handling the word of truth. ¹⁶ Avoid irreverent and empty chatter, for it leads to further ungodliness. ¹⁷ And their speech will spread like gangrene. Among these are Hymenaeus and Philetus, ¹⁸ who have deviated from the truth, saying that the resurrection has already taken place, and they are overturning the faith of some. ¹⁹ But the solid foundation of God stands firm, having the inscription: The Lord knows those who are his, and everyone who names the name of the Lord must turn away from wickedness. ²⁰ Now in a large house there are not only gold and silver vessels, but also wooden and earthen ones, some for honor and some for dishonor. ²¹ If anyone cleanses themselves from these, they will become a vessel for honor, sanctified, useful to the Master, prepared for every good work. ²² Flee youthful passions, but pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, along with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart. ²³ Avoid foolish and uneducated controversies, knowing that they produce quarrels. ²⁴ A servant of the Lord must not quarrel, but must be gentle to all, able to teach, patient, ²⁵ instructing opponents with gentleness, so that God may perhaps grant them repentance and a knowledge of the truth, ²⁶ and they may escape from the snare of the devil, having been captured by him to do his will.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "descendant of David" reflects the genealogical claim in the gospel and aligns with Paul's emphasis on Christ's messianic lineage.
- v. 19: "The Lord knows those who are his" is a direct translation of the Greek, reflecting a scriptural principle of divine recognition.
- v. 20: "gold and silver vessels" is chosen over "vessels of gold and silver" for clarity and natural English phrasing.
- v. 26: "snare of the devil" uses "snare" for ἁλιέν, which conveys entrapment, and "captured by him to do his will" reflects the idea of being ensnared for the devil’s purpose.
