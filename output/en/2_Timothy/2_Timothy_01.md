# 2 Timothy 1

¹ Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, according to the promise of life in Christ Jesus, ² to Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord. ³ I thank the God whom I have served from my ancestors with a clear conscience, for I have not ceased to remember you in my prayers, night and day, ⁴ longing to see you, remembering your tears, so that I might be filled with joy. ⁵ I recall your unfeigned faith that was first nurtured in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that it is also in you. ⁶ For this reason I remind you to rekindle the gift of God that you have within you through the laying on of my hands, ⁷ because God has not given us a spirit of cowardice, but of power, love, and self-discipline. ⁸ So then, do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord or of me, his prisoner, but share in suffering for the gospel by the power of God, ⁹ who saved us and called us to a holy calling, not according to our works but according to his own purpose and grace, granted to us in Christ Jesus before the ages began, ¹⁰ now revealed through the appearing of our Savior Jesus Christ, who destroyed death and illuminated life and immortality through the gospel, ¹¹ for which I was appointed a herald, apostle, and teacher. ¹² For this reason I suffer these things, but I am not ashamed, because I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that he is able to guard what I have entrusted to him until that day. ¹³ Hold fast to the sound teachings you heard from me in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. ¹⁴ Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us. ¹⁵ You know that all in Asia turned away from me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes. ¹⁶ May the Lord show mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me, and was not ashamed of my chains. ¹⁷ Indeed, when he came to Rome he eagerly searched for me and found me— ¹⁸ may the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day—and how much he served in Ephesus, you yourself know better.

---

Notes
- v. 13: “sound teachings” renders *ὑποτύπωσιν ἔχε ὑγιαινόντων λόγων*, where *ὑποτύπωσιν* is translated as "hold fast to the pattern" but here rendered more naturally as "hold fast to the sound teachings."
- v. 14: “good deposit” renders *τὴν καλὴν παραθήκην*, using “deposit” for *παραθήκην* in the sense of a trust or charge, as commonly used in Pauline epistles.
