# Acts 22

¹ Brothers and fathers, listen to the defense I now make to you. ² When they heard that he was speaking to them in the Hebrew language, they gave him greater attention. He continued: ³ I am a Jew, born in Tarsus in Cilicia, but brought up in this city, instructed under the feet of Gamaliel according to the precise teaching of the ancestral law, zealous for God as all of you are today. ⁴ I persecuted this way all the way to death, seizing men and women and delivering them to prison. ⁵ The high priest himself bears witness to me, and the entire council of elders. From them I also received letters addressed to the brothers in Damascus, and I went there to bring even those who were there to Jerusalem to be punished. ⁶ It happened to me as I was traveling and approaching Damascus about noon that a sudden light from heaven flashed around me. ⁷ I fell to the ground and heard a voice saying to me, "Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me?" ⁸ I replied, "Who are you, Lord?" He said to me, "I am Jesus the Nazarene, whom you are persecuting." ⁹ The men who were with me saw the light but did not hear the voice that was speaking to me. ¹⁰ I said, "What should I do, Lord?" The Lord said to me, "Get up and go to Damascus, and there you will be told everything that is appointed for you to do." ¹¹ Since I could not see because of the brilliance of that light, I was led by hand by those who were with me to Damascus. ¹² A man named Ananias, who was devout according to the law and well spoken of by all the Jews living there, ¹³ came to me and laid his hands on me, saying, "Brother Saul, you have seen." At that very hour I looked up at him. ¹⁴ He said, "The God of our ancestors has chosen you beforehand to know his will and to see the Righteous One and to hear the voice from his mouth, ¹⁵ because you will be a witness for him to all people about what you have seen and heard. ¹⁶ And now what are you waiting for? Get up and be baptized and wash away your sins, calling on his name." ¹⁷ When I returned to Jerusalem and was praying in the temple, I was caught up in a trance ¹⁸ and saw him saying to me, "Hurry and get out of Jerusalem quickly, because they will not accept your testimony about me." ¹⁹ I said, "Lord, they themselves know that I used to be imprisoning and beating the ones who believed in you in the synagogues, ²⁰ and when Stephen the martyr of your testimony was killed, I was standing by, agreeing with it and guarding the coats of those who were killing him." ²¹ Then he said to me, "Go, because I am sending you far off to the nations." ²² They listened to these words until he said this, and then they raised their voices and shouted, "Remove this man from the earth, for it is not fitting for him to live!" ²³ As they were shouting and tearing their garments and throwing dust into the air, ²⁴ the commander ordered him to be brought into the barracks, saying he should be flogged so they could find out why they were shouting against him like that. ²⁵ As they were about to lash him with the rods, Paul said to the centurion standing by, "If this man is a Roman and uncondemned, are you going to flog him?" ²⁶ The centurion heard this and went to the commander, reporting, "What are you going to do? For this man is a Roman." ²⁷ The commander approached and said to him, "Are you a Roman?" He replied, "Yes." ²⁸ The commander said, "I bought my Roman citizenship for a great price." Paul said, "But I was born a Roman." ²⁹ Immediately the ones about to flog him stopped, and the commander was afraid, having realized that he was a Roman and that he had bound him. ³⁰ The next day, wanting to find out for sure what the Jews were accusing him of, he released him and ordered the high priests and the whole council to gather, and he brought Paul before them.
