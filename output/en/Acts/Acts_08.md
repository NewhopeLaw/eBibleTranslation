# Acts 8

¹ But Saul was in full agreement with his death. A great persecution arose that day against the assembly in Jerusalem; all of them scattered into the regions of Judea and Samaria, except the apostles. ² Devout men carried Stephen's body and made a great mourning over him. ³ Saul was ransacking the assembly house by house, dragging men and women into prison. ⁴ Those who had scattered went out proclaiming the word. ⁵ Philip went down to the city of Samaria and proclaimed to them the Messiah. ⁶ The crowds were united in listening to what Philip said and in seeing the signs he did; ⁷ for many who had unclean spirits cried out with a loud voice and came out; many who were paralyzed and lame were healed; ⁸ and there was great joy in that city. ⁹ A man named Simon had previously been prominent in the city, casting spells and astoundingly deceiving the Samaritan nation, claiming to be someone great, ¹⁰ to whom all paid attention from the smallest to the greatest, saying, "This man is the Great Power of God." ¹¹ They paid attention to him because for a long time his enchantments had amazed them. ¹² When they believed in Philip, who was proclaiming the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women. ¹³ Simon himself also believed, and when he was baptized, he stayed close to Philip, seeing great signs and miracles performed, and was astonished. ¹⁴ The apostles in Jerusalem, hearing that Samaria had accepted the word of God, sent Peter and John to them, ¹⁵ who came down and prayed for them so that they might receive the Holy Spirit; ¹⁶ for it had not yet fallen upon any of them, but they had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. ¹⁷ Then they laid their hands upon them, and they received the Holy Spirit. ¹⁸ But Simon, seeing that the Holy Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles' hands, brought them money ¹⁹ and said, "Give me this power too, that whoever I lay hands on may receive the Holy Spirit." ²⁰ Peter said to him, "May your silver perish with you, because you think you can buy the gift of God with money. ²¹ You have no share or lot in this matter, for your heart is not upright before God. ²² Therefore repent of this wrongdoing of yours, and pray to the Lord if perhaps the thought of your heart may be forgiven you; ²³ for I see that you are in bitter anger and bound in iniquity." ²⁴ Simon replied, "Pray to the Lord for me, that nothing of what you have said may come upon me." ²⁵ After they had testified and spoken the word of the Lord, they turned back to Jerusalem, proclaiming the gospel in many Samaritan villages. ²⁶ An angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, "Get up and go toward the south along the road descending from Jerusalem to Gaza; this is a desert place." ²⁷ He got up and went, and behold, a certain eunuch of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who was in charge of all her treasury, who had come to Jerusalem to worship, ²⁸ was returning and sitting in his chariot, reading the prophet Isaiah. ²⁹ The Spirit said to Philip, "Go up and join this chariot." ³⁰ Running up, Philip heard him reading Isaiah the prophet and said, "Do you understand what you are reading?" ³¹ He replied, "How can I, unless someone guides me?" And he invited Philip to come up and sit with him. ³² The passage of the scripture he was reading was this: "Like a lamb led to the slaughter, and like a sheep before its shearer is silent, so he did not open his mouth. ³³ In his humiliation, his judgment was taken away; who will speak of his generation? For his life was taken from the earth." ³⁴ The eunuch, responding to Philip, said, "I ask you, about whom does the prophet say this? About himself or about someone else?" ³⁵ Opening his mouth, Philip began from that scripture and proclaimed Jesus to him. ³⁶ As they went along the road, they came upon some water, and the eunuch said, "Look, water! What is keeping me from being baptized?" ³⁸ Then they stopped the chariot, and both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him. ³⁹ When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught up Philip, and the eunuch saw him no more, for he went on his way rejoicing. ⁴⁰ Philip was found in Azotus, and going through, he proclaimed the gospel in all the cities until he came to Caesarea.

Notes
- v. 37: The NRSV, NIV, and others include a verse 37 with the eunuch's declaration of faith. This verse is absent from the original Greek text of Acts 8, as found in most early and reliable manuscripts. It is likely an interpolation.
