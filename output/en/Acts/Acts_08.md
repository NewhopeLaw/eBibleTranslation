# Acts 8

¹ Saul was in agreement with his killing. On that day a great persecution began against the assembly in Jerusalem; all the members scattered into the regions of Judea and Samaria, except for the apostles. ² Devout men carried Stephen out and made a great burial for him. ³ Saul was troubling the assembly house by house, dragging both men and women down to prison. ⁴ The ones scattered went about proclaiming the message. ⁵ Philip went down to the city of Samaria and proclaimed the Messiah there. ⁶ The crowds listened with one accord to what Philip was saying and watched the signs he performed. ⁷ Many who had unclean spirits cried out in loud voices as they came out, and many who were paralyzed and lame were healed. ⁸ A great joy filled that city. ⁹ A man named Simon had previously been prominent in the city, doing magic and amazing the Samaritan people, claiming to be someone great. ¹⁰ Everyone paid attention to him, from the smallest to the greatest, saying, "This man is the Great Power of God called the Great." ¹¹ They were drawn to him because for a long time his magic had stunned them. ¹² When they believed what Philip was proclaiming, regarding the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus the Messiah, they were baptized, both men and women. ¹³ Simon also believed, and having been baptized, he clung to Philip, astonished at the great signs and wonders that were being done. ¹⁴ When those in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them. ¹⁵ These men went down and prayed for them so that they might receive the Holy Spirit. ¹⁶ For the Holy Spirit had not yet come upon any of them, only they had been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. ¹⁷ Then the apostles laid hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit. ¹⁸ But when Simon saw that the Holy Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles' hands, he brought them money ¹⁹ and said, "Give me this power too, so that anyone to whom I lay hands may receive the Holy Spirit." ²⁰ Peter said to him, "May your silver perish with you, because you thought you could buy the gift of God with money. ²¹ You have no share or part in this word, for your heart is not upright before God. ²² Therefore, repent of this wickedness of yours and pray to the Lord, in case perhaps the thought in your heart may be forgiven you. ²³ For I see that you are in bitter anger and bound in a knot of iniquity." ²⁴ Simon replied, "Pray for me to the Lord so that nothing of what you have said may come upon me." ²⁵ So, after testifying and speaking the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, proclaiming the gospel in many Samaritan villages. ²⁶ An angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, "Get up and go south along the road that descends from Jerusalem to Gaza. This is a wilderness." ²⁷ Rising up, he went, and behold, an Ethiopian eunuch, a court official of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who was in charge of her entire treasury, who had come to Jerusalem to worship, ²⁸ was returning and seated in his chariot, reading the prophet Isaiah. ²⁹ The Spirit said to Philip, "Go up and join that chariot." ³⁰ Running up, Philip heard him reading Isaiah the prophet and said, "Do you understand what you are reading?" ³¹ He replied, "How can I unless someone guides me?" And he invited Philip to come up and sit with him. ³² The section of the scripture he was reading was this: "Like a lamb led to the slaughter, and like a sheep before its shearers is silent, so he opened not his mouth. ³³ In his humiliation his judgment was taken away; who will declare his generation? For his life is taken from the earth." ³⁴ The eunuch answered Philip, "I ask you, about whom does the prophet say this—about himself or about someone else?" ³⁵ Philip opened his mouth and began from that scripture to proclaim Jesus to him. ³⁶ As they were going along the road, they came to some water, and the eunuch said, "Look, water. What hinders me from being baptized?" ³⁷ Philip said, "If you believe with all your heart, you may." And he answered, "I believe that Jesus is the Son of God." ³⁸ Then he ordered the chariot to stop, and both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him. ³⁹ When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught up Philip, and the eunuch saw him no more, for he went on his way rejoicing. ⁴⁰ Philip was found in Azotus, and he traveled through all the cities, proclaiming the good news until he came to Caesarea.

- v. 37: The verse number was missing in the initial translation; the source text includes verse 37, which explains the eunuch's belief and Philip's response.
