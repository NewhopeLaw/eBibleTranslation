# Acts 3

¹ Peter and John were going up to the temple at the hour of prayer, the ninth hour, ² when a man who had been born lame was carried to the temple, to be placed each day at the gate called the Beautiful, to ask for alms from those entering the temple, ³ who saw Peter and John about to go into the temple, and asked for alms. ⁴ Peter fixed his gaze on him, as did John, and said, Look at us. ⁵ But he fixed his gaze on them, expecting to receive something from them. ⁶ Peter said, Silver and gold I do not have, but what I do have I give to you: in the name of Jesus the Messiah of Nazareth, walk. ⁷ Then seizing him by the right hand, he raised him up. Immediately his feet and ankles were made strong, ⁸ and leaping up, he stood and began to walk, and entered with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God. ⁹ And all the people saw him walking and praising God, ¹⁰ and they recognized him as the one who used to sit asking for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple, and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him. ¹¹ Now Peter, holding the man by the hand, was standing with them on the portico called Solomon’s Porch, and the whole crowd gathered to them, astounded. ¹² Seeing this, Peter addressed the people, You Israelites, why are you surprised at this, or why do you stare at us as though we made him walk by our own power or piety? ¹³ The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of our ancestors, has glorified his Son Jesus, whom you handed over and denied before Pilate, when he had decided to release him, ¹⁴ but you rejected the Holy and Righteous One and asked that a murderer be given to you, ¹⁵ and you killed the Author of life, whom God raised from the dead. We are witnesses of that. ¹⁶ And through faith in his name, the one you see and know, the name has made this man strong, and the faith through him has given him this full recovery in the sight of all of you. ¹⁷ Now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, just as your rulers did, ¹⁸ but what God foretold through the mouths of all the prophets—that his Messiah would suffer these things—he has fulfilled in this way. ¹⁹ So repent, and turn back, so that your sins may be wiped out, ²⁰ so that times of renewal may come from the presence of the Lord, and he may send the Messiah you have been appointed, Jesus, ²¹ whom heaven must receive until the time of the restoration of all things, which God spoke long ago through the mouths of his holy prophets. ²² Moses said, The Lord your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers. You must listen to everything he tells you. ²³ And every soul who does not listen to that prophet will be destroyed from the people. ²⁴ And all the prophets, from Samuel and those who followed, as many as spoke, also announced these days. ²⁵ You are the children of the prophets and of the covenant that God made with your ancestors, saying to Abraham, In your offspring all the families of the earth will be blessed. ²⁶ The God who first raised up his Son, sent him to you to bless you, turning each of you from your evil deeds.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "in the name of Jesus the Messiah of Nazareth" — the phrase "the name" likely refers to Jesus himself, but here it is rendered idiomatically as "in the name of."
- v. 25: "in your offspring" — the translation reflects the context of Abrahamic blessing, not a direct reference to a singular descendant.
