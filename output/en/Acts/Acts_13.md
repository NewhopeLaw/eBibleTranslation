# Acts 13

¹ Now in Antioch there were prophets and teachers in the church: Barnabas, and Simeon called Niger, and Lucius the Cyrenian, and Manaen a childhood companion of Herod the tetrarch, and Saul. ² While they were serving the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, "Set apart for me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them." ³ Then, after fasting and praying and laying hands on them, they sent them off. ⁴ So, sent by the Holy Spirit, they went down to Seleucia, and from there they sailed to Cyprus. ⁵ And when they arrived in Salamis, they proclaimed the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews. John was with them as their assistant. ⁶ Going through the whole island as far as Paphos, they found a certain magician, a Jewish false prophet named Barjesus, ⁷ who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, a man of intelligence. This man called Barnabas and Saul to himself, desiring to hear the word of God. ⁸ But Elymas the magician—this is how his name is interpreted—stood in opposition to them, trying to turn the proconsul away from the faith. ⁹ Saul, who is also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, looked intently at him ¹⁰ and said, "You full of all deceit and trickery, son of the devil, enemy of all righteousness, will you not stop twisting the straight ways of the Lord? ¹¹ And now look, the hand of the Lord is on you, and you will be blind, not seeing the sun for a time." Immediately a dark mist fell on him, and he went around seeking someone to lead him by the hand. ¹² Then the proconsul saw what had happened and believed, amazed at the teaching of the Lord. ¹³ Now, leaving Paphos, the ones with Paul came to Perga in Pamphylia. But John turned back from them and returned to Jerusalem. ¹⁴ They themselves, having passed through Perga, arrived in Antioch in Pisidia. And entering the synagogue on the Sabbath day, they sat down. ¹⁵ After the reading of the law and the prophets, the synagogue rulers sent word to them, saying, "Men, brothers, if you have any message of encouragement for the people, speak." ¹⁶ Then Paul stood up and motioned with his hand and said, "Men of Israel and those who fear God, listen. ¹⁷ The God of this people Israel chose our ancestors and exalted the people during their sojourn in the land of Egypt, and with a mighty arm He brought them out of it, ¹⁸ and for about forty years He led them in the desert, ¹⁹ and He overthrew seven nations in the land of Canaan and gave their land to them as an inheritance ²⁰ for about 450 years. And after these things, He gave judges until the time of the prophet Samuel. ²¹ From there they asked for a king, and God gave them Saul, the son of Kish, a man of the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years. ²² And after removing him, He raised up to them David as their king, to whom He also testified, saying, 'I have found David, the son of Jesse, a man after My heart, who will do all My will.' ²³ From this man, according to the promise, God brought to Israel the Savior Jesus, ²⁴ after John had previously proclaimed a baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel. ²⁵ But when John had completed his course, he said, 'What do you suppose I am? I am not He. But look, He is coming after me, whose sandals I am not worthy to untie.' ²⁶ "Men, brothers, sons of the family of Abraham and those among you who fear God, to us the message of salvation has been sent. ²⁷ For the people living in Jerusalem and their rulers, having ignored this and not recognized the voice of the prophets that are read each Sabbath, fulfilled them. ²⁸ And although they found no cause for death, they asked Pilate to have Him executed. ²⁹ And when they had fulfilled all the things written about Him, they took Him down from the cross and laid Him in a tomb. ³⁰ But God raised Him from the dead; ³¹ and He appeared on many days to those who had come up with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are now His witnesses to the people. ³² And we declare to you the good news of the promise made to the fathers. ³³ God has fulfilled this promise to us by raising up Jesus, as it is written in the second Psalm: 'You are My Son, today I have begotten You.' ³⁴ That He raised Him from the dead, never again to return to decay, He said this: 'I will give you the holy and sure blessings of David.' ³⁵ For in another place He says, 'You will not let Your Holy One see decay; ³⁶ for David, having served the purpose of God in his own generation, fell asleep and was laid with his fathers and saw decay, ³⁷ but the one whom God raised saw no decay. ³⁸ "Therefore let it be known to you, brothers, that through Him forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you, ³⁹ and from everything from which you could not be justified by the law of Moses, in Him everyone who believes is justified. ⁴⁰ "Therefore be careful that the promise spoken by the prophets doesn't come upon you: ⁴¹ 'Look, you scoffers, be astonished and perish, for I am doing a work in your days, a work which you will not believe, even if someone tells it to you.' ⁴² As they were going out, they urged them to meet with them the next Sabbath to hear these things. ⁴³ After the synagogue was dismissed, many of the Jews and the God-fearing proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who, speaking to them, urged them to continue in the grace of God. ⁴⁴ The next Sabbath almost the whole city gathered to hear the word of the Lord. ⁴⁵ Seeing the crowds, the Jews were filled with jealousy and contradicted the things spoken by Paul, blasphemy. ⁴⁶ Standing bold, Paul and Barnabas said, "It was necessary for us to speak the word of God to you first. Since you reject it and do not judge yourselves worthy of eternal life, look, we turn to the Gentiles; ⁴⁷ for so the Lord has commanded us: 'I have set you as a light to the Gentiles, that you may bring salvation to the end of the earth.'" ⁴⁸ Hearing this, the Gentiles were glad and glorified the word of the Lord, and as many as were appointed to eternal life believed. ⁴⁹ And the word of the Lord spread through the whole region. ⁵⁰ But the Jews incited the prominent women who were God-fearing and the leading men of the city and stirred up persecution against Paul and Barnabas, and they drove them out of their territory. ⁵¹ But they shook off the dust of their feet toward them and went to Iconium. ⁵² The disciples were filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.

Notes
- v. 47: "I have set you as a light to the Gentiles" — the Greek is a quotation of Isaiah 49:6, but the translation here follows the Septuagint (LXX) wording.
