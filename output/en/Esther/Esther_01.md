# Esther 1

¹ In the days of Ahasuerus—this Ahasuerus ruled over 127 provinces, from India to Cush— ² when King Ahasuerus was seated on his royal throne in Susa the fortress, ³ in the third year of his reign, he held a feast for his officials and servants, for the might of Persia and Media, for the nobles of the provinces before him, ⁴ to display the wealth and glory of his kingdom, the honor and splendor of his greatness, for many days—180 days in all. ⁵ At the end of these days, the king held a feast for all the people found in Susa the fortress, from the greatest to the least, for seven days in the garden of the palace of the king. ⁶ The palace was adorned with tapestries of fine linen and bright-colored wool, fastened with cords of byssus and purple, on pillars of silver and bases of ivory, couches of gold and silver laid on porphyry and lapis lazuli and onyx, and drapes of fine linen and embroidered cloth. ⁷ The cups were of gold and silver, and the wine was abundant and sweet, as the king himself had provided. ⁸ Yet the banquet did not enforce a requirement by law; the king had decreed that each person could do as they pleased. ⁹ Queen Vashti also held a banquet for the women in the palace of King Ahasuerus. ¹⁰ On the seventh day, when the king was merry with wine, he said to his seven chief eunuchs—Mehuman, Bizta, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carcas, who served in the presence of King Ahasuerus— ¹¹ “Bring Queen Vashti to me, wearing the royal crown, so that I may display her beauty to the people and the nobles, for she is beautiful.” ¹² But Queen Vashti refused to come at the command of the king, as given by the eunuchs. Then the king became very angry, and his wrath burned within him. ¹³ The king said to the wise men who knew the times, “According to the law, what is to be done with Queen Vashti, for she has not obeyed the command of King Ahasuerus given by the eunuchs?” ¹⁴ The one closest to the king was Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan, the seven nobles of Persia and Media, who saw the king face to face and held the first place in the kingdom. ¹⁵ According to the law, what should be done to Queen Vashti, who has not obeyed the command of King Ahasuerus given by the eunuchs? ¹⁶ Memucan said before the king and the nobles, “Not only has Queen Vashti committed an offense against the king alone, but also against all the nobles and all the people who are in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus. ¹⁷ For the queen’s words will become known to all the women, and they will despise their husbands in their eyes, when the king says to bring Queen Vashti before him and she does not come. ¹⁸ On this day the palace women of Persia and Media who have heard the queen’s words will say the same to all the king’s nobles, and it will provoke great contempt. ¹⁹ If it pleases the king, let a royal decree go out from him, and let it be written in the laws of Persia and Media, so that it cannot be revoked, that Vashti is never to come before King Ahasuerus again, and let the king give her royal position to another who is better than she. ²⁰ And when the king’s decree is heard in all his kingdom, for it is vast, all the women will honor their husbands, from the greatest to the least. ²¹ The proposal pleased the king and the nobles, and the king did as Memucan suggested. ²² The king sent letters to every province, to province and province, and to people and people, in their own language, that every man should rule in his home and speak in the language of his people.

Notes  
- v. 1: "from India to Cush" — the Hebrew says "from India and to Cush" (the land of the Nubians in Sudan), suggesting a vast realm.  
- v. 22: The final decree is ambiguous in tone: does it promote local autonomy or enforce linguistic division? The text reads neutrally.
