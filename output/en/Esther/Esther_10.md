# Esther 10

¹ King Ahasuerus imposed a tax on the land and on the isles of the sea. ² And all the acts of his might and power, and the account of the greatness of Mordecai, whom the king had honored — are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Media and Persia? ³ For Mordecai the Jew was second to the king Ahasuerus, and great among the Jews, and beloved by the many of his brothers, seeking good for his people and speaking peace for all his descendants.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Media and Persia" is a literal rendering of the Hebrew, which includes "sefer divrei hayamim" (the book of the words of the days) — a standard formula for official historical records.
