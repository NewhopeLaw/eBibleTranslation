# Habakkuk 3

¹ A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, on shigionoth. ² O LORD, I have heard the report of you, and my heart trembled. At the sound of you, I was afraid. O LORD, revive your work in the midst of the years. In the midst of the years, make it known. In wrath remember mercy. ³ God comes from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah. His splendor covers the heavens, and his praise fills the earth. ⁴ His brightness is like the dawn. Horns come forth from his hands, and there, his power is concealed. ⁵ Before him goes plague, and fire breaks out at his feet. ⁶ He stands and measures the earth. He looks and startles the nations. The eternal mountains are shattered, and the perpetual hills bow down. His ways are eternal. ⁷ I saw the tents of Cushan in distress, and the canopy of the land of Midian trembling. ⁸ Was your wrath against the rivers, O LORD? Or your anger against the rivers? Or your fury against the sea, that you ride on your steeds, on your chariots of salvation? ⁹ Your bow is made ready. You stretched out your arrows. Selah. The earth is split open with rivers. ¹⁰ The mountains see you and tremble. A flood of water passes by. The deep gives its voice, lifting up its hands. ¹¹ The sun and the moon stand still in their courses. At the light of your arrows they run, at the gleam of your flashing spear. ¹² In anger you march the earth, in wrath you trample the nations. ¹³ You have gone forth to save your people, to save your anointed. You have crushed the head of the house of the wicked. You have stripped the foundations of cities down to the neck. Selah. ¹⁴ You pierced their rulers with your arrows. Their heads were scattered. They rushed like those who devour the poor in secret. ¹⁵ You crossed the sea on your steeds. The rivers, with their many waters. ¹⁶ I heard, and my inward parts trembled. My lips quivered at the sound. My bones grew cold. I tremble in my place, in the day of trouble, when the invader comes into the people. ¹⁷ For fig trees do not bloom, and there is no fruit on the vines. The olive yields no oil, and the fields produce no food. The flock is cut off from the fold, and there are no cattle in the stalls. ¹⁸ And yet I will rejoice in the LORD. I will take joy in the God of my salvation. ¹⁹ The LORD is my strength. He makes my feet like the feet of a deer. He will lead me on the heights, to sing praises to him, on my stringed instruments.

Notes
- v. 2: "In the midst of the years" reflects the Hebrew מִקֶּרֶב שָׁנִים, which is rendered as a poetic reference to time.
- v. 3: "Selah" is retained as a musical or interpretive marker.
- v. 9: "Selah" is again retained as a musical or interpretive marker.
- v. 10: "Selah" is retained. The line structure is adjusted to maintain poetic clarity.
- v. 13: "Selah" is retained as a musical or interpretive marker.
- v. 19: "On my stringed instruments" reflects the Hebrew נְגִינוֹתָי, referring to the instruments used in worship.
