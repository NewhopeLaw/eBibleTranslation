# Habakkuk 1

¹ The vision that the prophet Habakkuk saw: ² How long, LORD, will I cry for help and you not hear? I call to you, “Violence!” and you do not rescue. ³ Why do you show me injustice, and make me watch oppression? Why do I see strife and quarrels advancing? ⁴ Therefore the law is paralyzed, and judgment never goes forward, for the wicked surround the righteous — therefore judgment comes out perverted. ⁵ Look among the nations and see — wonder and be astonished, for I am doing a work in your days that you will not believe, even if someone tells it to you. ⁶ For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans, that fierce and impetuous nation, who march through the breadth of the earth to seize homes not their own. ⁷ Terror and dread go with them; their judgment comes from themselves, and their honor rises from their own strength. ⁸ Their horses are swifter than leopards, and fiercer than evening wolves. Their horsemen spread out, and they fly like an eagle that pounces to eat. ⁹ All of them come for violence — their faces are turned forward, and they gather captives like sand. ¹⁰ He scoffs at kings and mocks rulers. He laughs at every fortress, and heaps up dust and captures it. ¹¹ Then the wind passes through and they go by, and their might is their god. ¹² Are you not from eternity, O LORD my God, my Holy One? You will not die. You have established judgment, and laid the foundation as a rock for argument. ¹³ Your eyes are too pure to look on evil, and you cannot bear to see oppression. Why then do you look with silence as the wicked swallow up the righteous? ¹⁴ You make humans like the fish of the sea, like a creeping thing that has no ruler. ¹⁵ They lift everything in a hook, drag it in a net, and gather it in their dragnet. ¹⁶ Therefore they rejoice and are glad in their hearts — therefore they offer sacrifice to their net and pour out drink offerings to their dragnet, for in them their portion is rich and their food is choice. ¹⁷ Should they, then, empty their net and never cease to slaughter nations without mercy?

---

Notes  
- v. 10: "heaps up dust" renders the Hebrew "יִצְבֹּר עָפָר" — the verb "to heap up" and the noun "dust" are literal.  
- v. 11: "their might is their god" translates "זֹאת כֹּחוֹ לֵאלֹהֽוֹ" — the phrase is metaphorical, referring to their power as their deity.  
- v. 13: "your eyes are too pure to look on evil" renders "טְהוֹר עֵינַיִם מֵרְאוֹת רָע" — the construction is idiomatic, meaning "pure of eye" in the sense of moral clarity.  
- v. 15: "in a hook" and "in a net" reflect the Hebrew "בְּחַכָּה" and "בְּמִכְמַרְתּוֹ", respectively — both are literal terms for fishing gear.  
- v. 17: "empty their net" translates "הַעַל כֵּן יָרִיק חֶרְמוֹ" — the verb "to empty" and noun "net" are standard.
