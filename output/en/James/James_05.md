# James 5

¹ Come now, you who are rich, weep and wail over the troubles coming to you! ² Your wealth has rotted, and your clothes have become moth-eaten; ³ your gold and silver have corroded, and their rust will be a witness against you and will consume your flesh. You have stored up fire in the last days. ⁴ Look, the wages of the laborers who mowed your fields, which were withheld by you, are crying out, and the cries of those who reaped have reached the ears of the Lord of Hosts! ⁵ You have lived in luxury on the earth and in indulgence, fattening your hearts for the day of slaughter. ⁶ You have condemned and murdered the righteous. Does he not oppose you? ⁷ Therefore, be patient, brothers, until the coming of the Lord. Look, the farmer waits for the precious fruit of the earth, being patient over it until it receives the early and the late rain. ⁸ Be patient also, and strengthen your hearts, for the coming of the Lord is near. ⁹ Do not complain, brothers, against one another, so that you may not be judged. Look, the judge stands at the door! ¹⁰ Take the example, brothers, of the suffering and patience of the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord. ¹¹ Look, we commend those who endure. You have heard of the endurance of Job, and you have seen the end of the Lord, that the Lord is full of compassion and mercy. ¹² First of all, brothers, do not swear, not by heaven, nor by earth, nor by any other oath. Let your "Yes" be yes, and your "No" be no, so that you may not come under judgment. ¹³ Is anyone suffering among you? Let them pray. Is anyone cheerful? Let them sing praises. ¹⁴ Is anyone sick among you? Let them call for the elders of the church, and let them pray over them, anointing them with oil in the name of the Lord; ¹⁵ and the prayer of faith will save the one who is sick, and the Lord will raise them up. And if they have sinned, it will be forgiven them. ¹⁶ Therefore, confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous person is powerful when it is active. ¹⁷ Elijah was a man like us, and he prayed earnestly that it should not rain, and it did not rain on the earth for three years and six months; ¹⁸ and again he prayed, and the heavens gave rain, and the earth produced its fruit. ¹⁹ My brothers, if anyone among you has wandered from the truth and someone brings them back, ²⁰ know that whoever brings back a sinner from the error of their way will save their soul from death and cover a multitude of sins.

---

Notes
- v. 12: The use of "First of all" reflects the contextual emphasis on the command to avoid swearing oaths, which is a direct response to the previous exhortation on judgment and truthfulness.
