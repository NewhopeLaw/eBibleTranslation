# 1 Peter 4

¹ So since Christ suffered in the flesh, arm yourselves also with the same mindset, because whoever has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin, ² so that you may live the remaining time in the flesh no longer for human desires but for the will of God. ³ For the time that has passed is sufficient to do the will of the Gentiles, having gone on in sexual immorality, desires, drunken revelry, banquets, and in unclean idolatries, ⁴ in which they are surprised, because you do not join them in the same flood of debauchery, and they blaspheme you, ⁵ who will have to give an account to the one ready to judge the living and the dead. ⁶ For this reason, even to the dead the gospel was proclaimed, so that though judged in human terms, they may live in the Spirit according to God. ⁷ But the end of all things is at hand. Therefore, be self-controlled and alert for prayer. ⁸ Above all, keep love for one another fervent, because love covers a multitude of sins. ⁹ Be hospitable to one another without grumbling. ¹⁰ Each of you, as you received a gift, serve one another as good stewards of the varied grace of God: ¹¹ if anyone speaks, let it be as with the words of God; if anyone serves, let it be with the strength that God supplies, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom belong the glory and the power forever and ever. Amen. ¹² Beloved, do not be surprised by the fiery ordeal that is taking place among you as though something strange were happening to you, ¹³ but rejoice insofar as you share in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may also rejoice and be glad when his glory is revealed. ¹⁴ If you are insulted because of the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of God and the glory of God rest upon you. ¹⁵ Do not let any of you suffer as a murderer or a thief or as an evildoer or as a meddler, ¹⁶ but if as a Christian, let it not be a shame to you, but glorify God in this name. ¹⁷ For it is time for judgment to begin with the household of God. And if it begins with us, what will be the outcome for those who do not obey the gospel of God? ¹⁸ And if the righteous is scarcely saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner? ¹⁹ Therefore, let those who suffer according to the will of God commit their souls to a faithful Creator while doing good.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "in which they are surprised" — the Greek is ambiguous between "they are surprised" or "you are surprised." Here, the context suggests the Gentiles are the ones surprised by the believer's resistance to their lifestyle.
- v. 13: "share in the sufferings of Christ" — "κοινωνεῖτε τοῖς τοῦ Χριστοῦ παθήμασιν" is a participial phrase; translated as a verb clause for clarity.
- v. 17: "the household of God" — the Greek uses "οἶκος τοῦ θεοῦ," which is a metaphor for the Christian community, not the temple.
