# 1 Peter 5

¹ Elders among you, therefore, I appeal as a fellow elder and as a witness of the sufferings of the Messiah, and as one who will share in the glory that is going to be revealed, ² shepherd the flock of God in your midst, keeping watch over them not under compulsion, but willingly, as God wills; nor as seeking to gain from them, but eagerly, ³ nor as lording it over those assigned to you, but being an example to the flock. ⁴ And when the chief shepherd appears, you will receive the imperishable crown of glory. ⁵ Likewise, younger ones, submit yourselves to the elders. All of you clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, for God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble. ⁶ Therefore, humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, that He may exalt you in due time, ⁷ casting all your anxieties on Him, because He cares for you. ⁸ Be alert, be self-controlled. Your adversary the devil is prowling like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour. ⁹ Resist him, standing firm in the faith, knowing that the same sufferings are being fulfilled in your brothers who are in the world. ¹⁰ The God of all grace, who called you to His eternal glory in Christ, after you have suffered a little while, will Himself perfect, strengthen, establish, and support you. ¹¹ To Him be the power forever and ever. Amen. ¹² Through Silvanus, I wrote to you, whom I consider faithful, with just a few words, exhorting and testifying that this grace of God is true; stand in it. ¹³ Greetings to you from the chosen ones in Babylon, and from my son Mark. ¹⁴ Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "the chosen ones in Babylon" — the reference to "Babylon" is likely symbolic or metaphorical, though some interpret it as literal. The term "chosen ones" reflects συνεκλεκτὴ (syneklektē), meaning "fellow-chosen."
