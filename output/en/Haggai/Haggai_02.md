# Haggai 2

¹ On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the word of the LORD came by the hand of the prophet Haggai, saying: ² Say to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the rest of the people: ³ Who among you saw this house in its former glory? How do you see it now? Doesn’t it seem to you like nothing at all? ⁴ Now therefore, take heart, Zerubbabel, says the LORD. Take heart, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest. Take heart, all the people of the land, says the LORD. And do the work, for I am with you, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁵ The covenant I made with you when you came out of Egypt remains in effect, and my spirit is standing among you. Do not be afraid. ⁶ For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: Yet once more, I will shake the heavens and the earth and the sea and the dry land. ⁷ I will shake all the nations, and the treasures of all the nations will come, and I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁸ The silver is mine, and the gold is mine, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁹ The glory of this later house will be greater than the former one, says the LORD of Hosts. And in this place I will give peace, says the LORD of Hosts. ¹⁰ On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came again to Haggai the prophet, saying: ¹¹ Ask the priests about the law, saying: ¹² Suppose a man carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and he touches with the same fold some bread or a gruel offering or wine or oil or any kind of food, does it become holy? The priests answered, No. ¹³ Then Haggai said, If someone who is unclean touches any of these, does it become unclean? The priests answered, Yes, it becomes unclean. ¹⁴ Then Haggai answered and said, So this people, and this nation before me, says the LORD, and so all their works, and whatever they bring there is unclean. ¹⁵ Now therefore, take it to heart from this day forward, from the day the foundation stone of the LORD’s house was laid. ¹⁶ Consider what happened when you came into a storehouse of twenty measures of grain and it became ten, when you brought into the winepress fifty measures and it became twenty. I struck you with blight and mildew and hail, for all the labor of your hands, and you would not return to me, says the LORD. ¹⁷ Take it to heart from this day forward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day the foundation of the LORD’s house was laid. ¹⁸ From now on, will the seed remain in the barn, and until the vine and the fig tree and the pomegranate and the olive tree have not borne fruit? From this day I will bless. ¹⁹ And the word of the LORD came a second time to Haggai on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying: ²⁰ Say to Zerubbabel governor of Judah, I will shake the heavens and the earth. ²¹ I will overthrow the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the strength of the nations. I will overthrow chariots and their riders, and the horses and their riders will fall, each by the sword of his brother. ²² On that day, says the LORD of Hosts, I will take you Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant, and I will make you like a signet ring, for I have chosen you, says the LORD of Hosts.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The date is given in the Hebrew calendar as the seventh month, twenty-first day.
- v. 6: "Yet once more" is a reference to a future event in God's timeline, not to a previous shaking.
- v. 23: The metaphor of a signet ring implies Zerubbabel's authority and God's personal seal of approval.
