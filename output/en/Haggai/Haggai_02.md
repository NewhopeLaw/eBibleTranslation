# Haggai 2

¹ On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the word of the LORD came through the prophet Haggai, saying: ² Say to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying: ³ Who among you saw this house in its former glory? And how do you see it now? Does it not look to you as nothing? ⁴ But now be strong, Zerubbabel, declares the LORD; and be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest; and be strong, all you people of the land, declares the LORD; and do it, for I am with you, declares the LORD of Hosts. ⁵ I made a covenant with you when you came out of Egypt. My Spirit remains among you. Do not be afraid. ⁶ For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: Yet once more I will shake the heavens and the earth, and the sea and the dry land. ⁷ I will shake all the nations, and the treasures of all the nations will come in, and I will fill this house with glory, declares the LORD of Hosts. ⁸ The silver is mine, and the gold is mine, declares the LORD of Hosts. ⁹ The glory of this house in the latter days will be greater than in the former days, declares the LORD of Hosts. And in this place I will give peace, declares the LORD of Hosts. ¹⁰ On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Haggai, saying: ¹¹ Ask the priests a question about the law, saying: ¹² Suppose a man carries consecrated meat in the fold of his garment, and the fold of his garment touches bread or a gruel offering, or wine, or oil, or any food. Will it become holy? The priests answered, No. ¹³ Then Haggai said, If a person who is unclean touches any of these, will it become unclean? The priests answered, Yes, it will become unclean. ¹⁴ So the LORD replied through Haggai, This people, and this nation before me, and all their work, and whatever they bring here, is unclean. ¹⁵ Now take to heart from this day onward, from the moment the foundation stone was placed in the temple of the LORD. ¹⁶ Before this, when you brought in twenty bushels of grain, it became ten. When you drew fifty baskets of water to the cistern, it became twenty. ¹⁷ I struck you with blight, mildew, and hail, and all your labors came to nothing. Yet you did not return to me, declares the LORD. ¹⁸ Take to heart from this day onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day the foundation of the temple of the LORD was laid. ¹⁹ Consider whether the seed in the granary has yet to sprout, and whether the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree have yet to bear fruit. From this day I will bless you. ²⁰ Then the word of the LORD came to the prophet Haggai a second time, on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying: ²¹ Say to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, I will shake the heavens and the earth. ²² I will overturn the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the strength of the nations. I will overturn chariots and their riders, and the horses and their riders will fall, each by the sword of his fellow. ²³ On that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant, and I will make you like a seal, for I have chosen you, declares the LORD of Hosts.

---

Notes
- v. 8: The phrase "The silver is mine, and the gold is mine" is a clear statement of divine sovereignty over all things, including material wealth. This is not a metaphor but a declarative truth from the LORD.
