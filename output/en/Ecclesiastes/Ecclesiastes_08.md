# Ecclesiastes 8

¹ Who is like the wise? And who knows the explanation of a matter? The wisdom of the human can illuminate his face, and boldness of face can dominate. ² I obeyed the voice of the king, and I swore an oath to God. ³ Do not fear his presence, do not stand in a matter of evil, for whatever he desires he can do. ⁴ Where there is a word from the king, there is authority, and who can say to him, “What will you do?” ⁵ One who keeps the commandment will know no evil thing, and the heart of the wise will know the time and the judgment. ⁶ For to every desire there is a time and a judgment, because the evil of man is great upon him. ⁷ For no one knows what will be, and who can tell him what is to come? ⁸ No man is master of the wind to hold back the wind, and no dominion over the day of death, and no escape in war, and the wicked cannot ransom himself from evil. ⁹ All this I have seen and set my heart upon all the work that has been done under the sun, in the time when a man rules over man to his harm. ¹⁰ Therefore I have seen the wicked buried, and they came into the place of honor, and they are forgotten in the city where they did so. Also this is vanity. ¹¹ Because no decree is made for the work of evil to be done quickly, therefore the hearts of the sons of men are filled with them to do evil. ¹² One who sins does evil many times and is lengthened. I know that what is good will come to those who fear God, who fear before Him. ¹³ And good will not be to the wicked, and he will not lengthen days like a shadow, who does not fear before God. ¹⁴ There is an emptiness that is done on the earth, that there are righteous people who come to the same end as the wicked, and there are wicked people who come to the same end as the righteous. I said, even this is vanity. ¹⁵ And I have praised the joy, that there is no good for man under the sun except to eat and to drink and to rejoice, and let him enjoy in his labor the days of his life, which God has given him under the sun. ¹⁶ As I set my heart to know wisdom and to see the matter that is done on the earth, even in day and night, sleep does not come to his eyes. ¹⁷ And I have seen all the work of God, that a man cannot find the work that has been done under the sun, by the one who toils to seek and not find. Even if the wise says to know, he cannot find.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "wisdom of the human can illuminate his face" renders חָכְמַת אָדָם תָּאִיר פָּנָיו as a metaphor for wisdom bringing clarity or dignity.
- v. 16: "sleep does not come to his eyes" interprets the Hebrew idiom שֵׁנָה בְּעֵינָיו metaphorically as restlessness or lack of peace.
