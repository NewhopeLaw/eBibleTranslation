# Ecclesiastes 11

¹ Cast your bread upon the surface of the waters, for in the abundance of days you will find it again. ² Share a portion with seven, yes, with eight, for you do not know what evil may come upon the land. ³ If the clouds are full of rain, they will pour out on the earth; and if a tree falls to the south or to the north, the place where the tree falls is where it will remain. ⁴ One who watches the wind will not sow, and one who looks at the clouds will not reap. ⁵ Just as you do not know how the wind comes, or how a baby is formed in the womb of a woman, so you do not know the work of God who does all things. ⁶ In the morning sow your seed, and in the evening do not withhold your hand, for you do not know which will succeed, this or that, or whether both are good. ⁷ And light is sweet, and it is good for the eyes to see the sun. ⁸ If a person lives many years, let him enjoy them all, and remember the days of darkness, for they will be many—everything that comes is vanity. ⁹ Rejoice, young man, in your youth, and let your heart delight in the days of your youth. Walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes, but know that for all these things God will bring you into judgment. ¹⁰ Remove anger from your heart and turn away evil from your flesh, for youth and the dawn are vanity.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "Cast your bread upon the surface of the waters" is a metaphor, possibly related to trust in God or preparation for the future.  
- v. 5: "How a baby is formed in the womb of a woman" is a metaphor for the inscrutable works of God.  
- v. 9: "Walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes" follows the Hebrew text literally, though some interpretations suggest a cautionary tone.
