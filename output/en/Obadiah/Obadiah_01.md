# Obadiah 1

¹ A vision of Obadiah. This is what the Lord GOD says to Edom: We have heard from the LORD, and a messenger has been sent among the nations. Rise up and take your vengeance against her for war. ² Look, I have made you small among the nations; you are greatly disgraced. ³ Your heart was arrogant, and it lifted you up, as one dwelling in the clefts of the rock, high in your dwelling, saying in your heart, Who will bring me down to the earth? ⁴ Though you soar like an eagle, though you place your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down, says the LORD. ⁵ If thieves came to you, if night raiders, how were you plundered? Surely they took enough! If those who watch you closely came to you, they left no hiding place. ⁶ How has Esau been searched out, and his treasures uncovered! ⁷ You were driven to the boundary; all your allies have betrayed you. Those who were at peace with you have set a trap beneath you; there is no wisdom in them. ⁸ On that day, says the LORD, I will destroy the wise from Edom and understanding from Mount Esau. ⁹ And your warriors will be terrified, from Teman, so that from Mount Esau no one will remain. ¹⁰ Because of the violence of your brother Jacob, you will be covered with shame, and you will be cut off forever. ¹¹ On the day you stood indifferent, on the day strangers captured his might, and foreigners entered his gates, and on Jerusalem they cast lots. You were just like one of them. ¹² But do not look on the day of your brother, on the day of the stranger, and do not rejoice over the sons of Judah in the day of their destruction, and do not enlarge your mouth in the day of trouble. ¹³ Do not enter the gate of my people on the day of their destruction, do not look on their calamity on the day of their destruction, and do not take advantage of their power on the day of their destruction. ¹⁴ And do not stand at the crossroads to cut off their fugitives, and do not close in on their survivors in the day of trouble. ¹⁵ For the day of the LORD is near for all the nations. As you have done, so it will be done to you. Your recompense will return to you. ¹⁶ For as you drank on my holy mountain, all the nations will drink continually, they will drink and swallow, and they will be as if they had never been. ¹⁷ But on Mount Zion there will be an escape, and it will be holy. And the house of Jacob will possess their inheritance. ¹⁸ And the house of Jacob will be a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau will be stubble, and they will set fire to them and devour them. No survivor will remain to the house of Esau, for the LORD has spoken. ¹⁹ And the Negev will possess the mountain of Esau, and the lowland the Philistines. The fields of Ephraim and the fields of Samaria will be possessed, and Benjamin will possess Gilead. ²⁰ And the beginning of the exile will be for the sons of Israel, like the Canaanites to Sarepta, and the exile of Jerusalem, which is in Sepharad, will possess the cities of the Negev. ²¹ And saviors will ascend from Mount Zion to judge the mountain of Esau, and the kingdom will be the LORD’s.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the human" is not used for ha-adam in this chapter, as the narrative clearly refers to Edom and not to Adam as a proper name.
- v. 8: "the wise" reflects the Hebrew "chakamim," which means wise ones or sages.
- v. 18: "stubble" reflects the Hebrew "kash," which is a dry, easily burned plant material, used metaphorically here.
- v. 20: "Sarepta" and "Sepharad" are transliterated proper names, following the convention provided in the glossary.
- v. 21: "the LORD's" reflects the divine name YHWH in the context of the prophecy, as it follows directly from the LORD's declaration.
