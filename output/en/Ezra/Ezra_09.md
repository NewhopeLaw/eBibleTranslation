# Ezra 9

¹ When these things were completed, the leaders came to me, saying: "The people of Israel, the priests, and the Levites have not separated themselves from the peoples of the lands with their abominations, toward the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Jebusite, the Ammonite, the Moabite, the Egyptian, and the Amorite. ² They have taken wives from their daughters and from their sons, and the holy seed has mingled with the peoples of the lands. And the hand of the rulers and the leaders was in this sin from the beginning. ³ When I heard this report, I tore my garment and my robe, and I pulled hair from my head and beard, and I sat in mourning. ⁴ All who were trembling at the words of the God of Israel over the sin of the exile gathered to me. And I sat in mourning until the evening offering. ⁵ At the evening offering, I rose from my fasting. And while I tore my garment and my robe, I fell on my knees and spread out my hands to my God, the Lord. ⁶ I said, "O my God, I am ashamed and abashed to lift my face to you, for our iniquities are multiplied beyond our heads, and our guilt is great up to the heavens. ⁷ From the days of our ancestors, we have been in great guilt until this day, and because of our sins we have been given over to our kings, our priests, and into the hand of the kings of the lands, by the sword, by captivity, by plunder, and by disgrace of face, as it is this day. ⁸ And now, for a moment, grace has been found with our God to leave us a remnant and to give us a sign in our holy place, to light our eyes and give us life a little in our servitude. ⁹ For we are servants, and in our servitude our God has not abandoned us, but he has shown us steadfast love before the kings of Persia, giving us life to raise up the house of our God, to establish its ruins, and to give us a wall in Judah and in Jerusalem. ¹⁰ What can we say, O our God, after this? For we have abandoned your commandments. ¹¹ Which you commanded through your servants the prophets: 'The land you are going in to possess is a land defiled, a defilement of the peoples of the lands, by their abominations with which they have filled it from mouth to mouth in their uncleanness.' ¹² Therefore, do not give your daughters to their sons, and do not take their daughters for your sons, and do not seek their peace or their good forever, so that you may be strong and eat the good of the land and leave it to your children forever. ¹³ And after all the evil that has come upon us because of our actions and our great guilt, you, O our God, have not dealt with us according to our iniquities, but have given us this deliverance. ¹⁴ Should we then turn back to break your commandments and to become entangled with these peoples of abomination? Will you be angry with us until you destroy us, so that there is no remnant or deliverance left? ¹⁵ O Lord, God of Israel, you are righteous, for we have been left a remnant as it is this day. Here we are before you in our guilt, for there is none who can stand before you because of this.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "for a moment" reflects the meaning of "כִּמְעַט־רֶגַע" as a brief respite or grace.
- v. 15: "you are righteous" is a translation of "צַדִּיק", which here is best rendered as "righteous" in the sense of being just or fair in judgment.
- v. 15: "here we are before you in our guilt" captures the sense of "כִּי אֵין לַעֲמוֹד לְפָנֶיךָ עַל־זֹֽאת", which is a confession of unworthiness to stand in God's presence due to their sin.
