# Ezra 3

¹ The seventh month arrived, and the Israelites were in their towns; the people gathered as one man to Jerusalem. ² Joshua the son of Jozadak, together with his fellow priests, and Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, with his brothers, began to build the altar of the God of Israel in order to offer burnt offerings as it is written in the teaching of Moses the man of God. ³ They completed the altar on its foundation, for fear was upon them from the peoples of the lands. They offered burnt offerings to the LORD, burnt offerings at morning and at evening. ⁴ They observed the feast of booths as it is written, burnt offerings day by day in number, as the regulation required for each day. ⁵ And after this, continuing burnt offerings, for the new moons and for all the holy feasts of the LORD, and for all who willingly gave a freewill offering to the LORD. ⁶ From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the LORD, but the house of the LORD had not yet been built. ⁷ They gave silver to the stonecutters and to the carpenters, and food and drink and oil to the Sidonians and the Tyrians, to bring cedar trees from Lebanon to the sea of Joppa, as Cyrus the king of Persia had decreed. ⁸ In the second year after their arrival at the house of God in Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Joshua the son of Jozadak, together with the rest of their fellow priests and Levites and all those who had come from the exile to Jerusalem, set the Levites, from twenty years old and upward, in charge of the work of the LORD. ⁹ Joshua, together with his sons and brothers—Kadmiel and his sons, the men of Judah—stood as one to supervise the work in the house of God, the sons of Hinnom, their sons and their brothers the Levites. ¹⁰ The builders began to lay the foundation of the house of the LORD. The priests, clothed in their vestments, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with their cymbals, praised the LORD as David the king of Israel had arranged. ¹¹ They responded with praise and with thanksgiving to the LORD, saying, “Good is the LORD, for His steadfast love endures forever over Israel.” And all the people raised a great shout of praise to the LORD over the foundation of the house of the LORD. ¹² Many of the priests and Levites and the heads of the ancestral families who had seen the first house in its foundation wept with a great and loud voice, while many others raised a shout of joy with great gladness. ¹³ The people could not distinguish the sound of the joyful shouting from the sound of the weeping, for the people cried out with a great voice, and the sound reached as far as the distant places.

---

Notes
- v. 11: The phrase “good is the LORD” is a common refrain in Hebrew praise, often rendered as “good is the Lord.” The rendering here follows the Hebrew text directly.
- v. 12: The reference to “the first house” clearly alludes to Solomon’s Temple, though it is not named explicitly in the Hebrew.
