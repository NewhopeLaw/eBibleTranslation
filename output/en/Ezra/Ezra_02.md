# Ezra 2

¹ These are the people from the province who returned from the exile, the captivity that Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had taken to Babylon. They returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to their own city. ² These came with Zerubbabel: Jeshua, Nehemiah, Seraiah, Reelaiah, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispar, Bigvai, Rehum, Baanah, and the rest of the people of Israel. ³ The sons of Parosh were 2,172. ⁴ The sons of Shephatiah were 372. ⁵ The sons of Ara were 775. ⁶ The sons of Pahath-Moab, that is, of the sons of Jeshua and Joab, were 2,812. ⁷ The sons of Elam were 1,254. ⁸ The sons of Zattu were 945. ⁹ The sons of Zaccai were 760. ¹⁰ The sons of Bani were 642. ¹¹ The sons of Bebai were 623. ¹² The sons of Azgad were 2,222. ¹³ The sons of Adonikam were 666. ¹⁴ The sons of Bigvai were 2,056. ¹⁵ The sons of Adin were 454. ¹⁶ The sons of Ater, of Hezekiah, were 98. ¹⁷ The sons of Bezai were 323. ¹⁸ The sons of Jorah were 112. ¹⁹ The sons of Hashum were 223. ²⁰ The sons of Gibbar were 95. ²¹ The sons of Bethel and Ai were 123. ²² The men of Netophah were 56. ²³ The men of Anathoth were 128. ²⁴ The sons of Azmaveth were 42. ²⁵ The sons of Kirjath Jearim, Kephirah, and Beeroth were 743. ²⁶ The sons of Ramah and Geba were 621. ²⁷ The men of Micmas were 122. ²⁸ The men of Bethel and Ai were 223. ²⁹ The sons of Nebai were 52. ³⁰ The sons of Magbish were 156. ³¹ The sons of Elam were 1,254. ³² The sons of Harim were 320. ³³ The sons of Lod, Hadid, and Ono were 725. ³⁴ The sons of Jeroh were 345. ³⁵ The sons of Senaah were 3,330. ³⁶ The priests, the sons of Jedaiah of the house of Jeshua, were 973. ³⁷ The sons of Immer were 1,052. ³⁸ The sons of Pashhur were 1,047. ³⁹ The sons of Harim were 1,017. ⁴⁰ The Levites, the sons of Jeshua and Kadmiel, of the house of Hodia, were 74. ⁴¹ The singers, the sons of Asaph, were 128. ⁴² The gatekeepers, the sons of Shallum, the sons of Terah, the sons of Telmon, the sons of Akkub, the sons of Hatita, the sons of Shobai, altogether were 139. ⁴³ The Nethinim, the sons of Ziha, the sons of Hasupha, the sons of Tatman. ⁴⁴ The sons of Keros, the sons of Siaha, the sons of Padon. ⁴⁵ The sons of Lebanah, the sons of Hagabah, the sons of Akub. ⁴⁶ The sons of Hagar, the sons of Shalmai, the sons of Hanan. ⁴⁷ The sons of Giddel, the sons of Gahar, the sons of Reaiah. ⁴⁸ The sons of Rezin, the sons of Neekoda, the sons of Gazzam. ⁴⁹ The sons of Uzza, the sons of Paseah, the sons of Besai. ⁵⁰ The sons of Asnah, the sons of Meunim, the sons of Nephisim. ⁵¹ The sons of Bakbuk, the sons of Hacupha, the sons of Harhur. ⁵² The sons of Bazluth, the sons of Mehidad, the sons of Harsa. ⁵³ The sons of Barkos, the sons of Sisera, the sons of Tamah. ⁵⁴ The sons of Nethiah, the sons of Hatipha. ⁵⁵ The sons of the servants of Solomon, the sons of Sotai, the sons of the scribes, the sons of Parida. ⁵⁶ The sons of Jaalah, the sons of Darcon, the sons of Gidel. ⁵⁷ The sons of Saphatiah, the sons of Hattil, the sons of Pochereth of the house of the archers, the sons of Ami. ⁵⁸ All the Nethinim and the sons of the servants of Solomon were 312. ⁵⁹ And these came up from Tel Melah, Tel Harshah, Mer, Adon, and Imad. They could not identify their ancestral house or their genealogy, whether they were of Israel. ⁶⁰ The sons of Delaiah, the sons of Tobiah, the sons of Nekoda were 652. ⁶¹ From among the priests, the sons of Habiah, the sons of the Kohathite, the sons of Barzillai, who had married a daughter of Barzillai the Gileadite and took his name. ⁶² These searched for their genealogy in the records, but they were not found, and so they were excluded from the priesthood. ⁶³ The Tirshatha said to them that they should not eat of the holy things until a priest of Urim and Thummim stood. ⁶⁴ The whole assembly was together: 42,360. ⁶⁵ In addition to their servants and their women, these were 7,337, and also 200 singers and singers. ⁶⁶ Their horses were 736, their mules were 245. ⁶⁷ Their camels were 435, their donkeys were 6,720. ⁶⁸ And from the leading men of the fathers, upon their coming to the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, they freely gave to the house of God to establish it in its place. ⁶⁹ According to their ability, they gave to the treasury of the work: gold, 6,000 darics, and silver, 5,000 manehs, and 100 priestly garments. ⁷⁰ The priests, the Levites, and some of the people and the singers and the gatekeepers and the Nethinim settled in their towns throughout Israel.
