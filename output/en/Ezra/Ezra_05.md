# Ezra 5

¹ Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo prophesied to the people of Judah in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, in the name of the God of Israel, to them. ² Then Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Joshua the son of Jozadak began to build the house of God that was in Jerusalem, and the prophets of God were helping them. ³ In that time, there came to them Thathanai the governor beyond the river and Shethar-boznai and their associates, and they said to them, “Who has given you permission to build this house and to complete this project?” ⁴ Then they answered them, “Who are the names of the men who are building this city?” ⁵ The eyes of God were on the captivity of Judah, and He did not abandon them until a decree was lifted under Darius the king. Then a tax official was sent over this matter. ⁶ A letter was sent from Thathanai the governor beyond the river and Shethar-boznai and their associates to the people who lived beyond the river, the Apharsachthans, to Darius the king. ⁷ They sent a message to him, and this is what was written in it: To Darius the king, peace be with you. ⁸ Let it be known to the king that we have gone to the land of Judah to the great house of God, and it is being rebuilt with chosen stones, and wood is being set in its walls. The work is progressing well and is being accomplished in their hands. ⁹ Then we asked those elders, and we said to them, “Who has given you permission to build this house and to complete the project?” ¹⁰ And we asked them to give us the names of the men, so that we might write down the names of the men to you. ¹¹ Then they answered us, saying, “We are servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are rebuilding the house that had been built before many years, and a great king of Israel built it and completed it for them. ¹² But when our fathers angered the God of heaven, He gave them into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, who destroyed this house and carried the people away to Babylon. ¹³ But in the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus gave a decree for this house of God to be rebuilt. ¹⁴ And the vessels of God, the silver and the gold, which Nebuchadnezzar took from the house in Jerusalem and brought to the house in Babylon, King Cyrus took from the house in Babylon and gave them to Sheshbazzar the guardian of the treasure. ¹⁵ And he said to him, “Take all the vessels, go, and set them in the house in Jerusalem, in their place.” ¹⁶ Then Sheshbazzar went and laid the foundations of the house of God that was in Jerusalem, and from that time until now they have been building it, and it has not yet been completed. ¹⁷ Now, if the king is good, let him look in the house of the treasury in Babylon, and he will see that it was King Cyrus who decreed that this house of God in Jerusalem should be rebuilt, and when the king learns of this, he will send us.

---

Notes
- v. 14: The reference to Sheshbazzar as the guardian of the treasure is a historical detail from the text, not a title.
- v. 16: "From that time until now they have been building it, and it has not yet been completed" refers to the ongoing work at the time of writing, prior to the completion under Darius.
