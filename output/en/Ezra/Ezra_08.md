# Ezra 8

¹ These were the heads of their ancestral houses and the men who came up with me from the kingdom of Artaxerxes the king, from Babylon. ² From the sons of Phinehas: Gershom. From the sons of Ithamar: Daniel. From the sons of David: Hattush. ³ From the sons of Shecaniah: From the sons of Parosh: Zechariah and with him 150 men. ⁴ From the sons of Pachat-Moab: Eliyohu Enai the son of Zerahiah, and with him 200 men. ⁵ From the sons of Shecaniah the son of Jaheziel, and with him 300 men. ⁶ From the sons of Adin: Gedal the son of Jonathan, and with him 50 men. ⁷ From the sons of Elam: Yesaya the son of Atalyah, and with him 70 men. ⁸ From the sons of Shefathiah: Zevadyah the son of Michael, and with him 80 men. ⁹ From the sons of Yoav: Obadiah the son of Yechiyah, and with him 218 men. ¹⁰ From the sons of Shelomith: Ben Yosepfyah, and with him 160 men. ¹¹ From the sons of Bebai: Zechariah the son of Bebai, and with him 28 men. ¹² From the sons of Azgad: Yohanan the son of the small one, and with him 110 men. ¹³ From the sons of Adonikam: Later ones, and these were their names: Elifel, Yeiel, and Shemaya, and with them 60 men. ¹⁴ From the sons of Bigvai: Utai and Zakur, and with him 70 men. ¹⁵ I gathered them by the river that flows to Ahava, and we stayed there for three days. I examined the people, the priests, and from the sons of Levi I found none there. ¹⁶ Then I sent Eleazar, Ariel, Shemaya, and Elnathan, Yerib, and Elnathan, Nathan, and Zechariah, and Meshulam, leaders, and Yoyarib and Elnathan, who were understanding. ¹⁷ I instructed them on Adi the head in Casphyah, and I put words in their mouths to speak to his brothers the Nethinim in Casphyah, to bring us servants for the house of our God. ¹⁸ And they brought us, because the hand of our God was good to us, a man of understanding from the sons of Machli, the son of Levi, the son of Israel—Sheravyah and his sons and brothers, 18 in all. ¹⁹ And Chashavyah and with him Yeshayah from the sons of Merari, his brothers and sons, 20 in all. ²⁰ And from the Nethinim, whom David and the officials had given for the service of the Levites, 220 Nethinim, all of them were inscribed by name. ²¹ I called a fast there by the river Ahava, to humble ourselves before our God, to seek from Him a straight way for us, for our children, and for all our possessions. ²² For I was ashamed to ask the king for a force and horsemen to help us on the road, because we had said to the king, "The hand of our God is on all who seek Him for good, and His might and His anger are on all who abandon Him." ²³ And we fasted and sought from our God about this, and He listened to us. ²⁴ I set apart from the leaders of the priests two for Sheravyah, Chashavyah, and with them 10 from their brothers. ²⁵ I weighed for them the silver, the gold, and the vessels, the offering for the house of our God, which the king and his advisors and officials and all Israel who were found had lifted up. ²⁶ I weighed on their hands 650 talents of silver, and 100 talents of silver vessels, and 100 talents of gold. ²⁷ And 20 gold rings for adornment, a thousand, and copper vessels of fine shining, two kinds of goodly ones like gold. ²⁸ And I said to them, "You are holy to the Lord, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and the gold are a freewill offering to the Lord, the God of your fathers." ²⁹ Be watchful and guard until you weigh them in front of the leaders of the priests and the Levites and the leaders of the fathers of Israel in Jerusalem, in the chambers of the house of the Lord. ³⁰ And the priests and the Levites received the weight of the silver and the gold and the vessels to bring to Jerusalem to the house of our God. ³¹ And we set out from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem, and the hand of our God was on us, and He delivered us from the hand of the enemy and the one who lay in wait on the road. ³² And we arrived in Jerusalem and stayed there for three days. ³³ And on the fourth day we weighed the silver and the gold and the vessels in the house of our God, in the hand of Meremot the son of Uriah the priest, and with him Eleazar the son of Pinchas, and with them Yozbad the son of Yeshu and Noadiah the son of Binui the Levite. ³⁴ In number, in weight for all, and all the weight was written at that time. ³⁵ The ones who came from the captivity brought burnt offerings to the God of Israel, 12 bulls for all Israel, 96 rams, 77 lambs, 12 male goats for sin offerings, all as burnt offerings to the Lord. ³⁶ And they gave the king's tax to the king's governors and officials beyond the river, and they honored the people and the house of God.

Notes
- v. 13: "Later ones" is a rendering of "adonikam achronim," where "achronim" is interpreted as "later" rather than "last," reflecting the context of listing additional names.
- v. 18: "Sheravyah" is the transliteration of the Hebrew name שְׁרֵבְיָה.
- v. 27: The reference to "copper vessels of fine shining" is a contextual rendering of the Hebrew, which speaks to their quality and appearance, not literal shininess.
- v. 35: The numbers of animals for offerings are rendered as presented in the source text, with no theological interpretation.
