# Ezra 10

¹ When Ezra finished praying and confessing his guilt and weeping before the house of God, a great crowd of Israelites gathered around him—men, women, and children—because the people wept bitterly. ² Then Shecaniah son of Jehiel, from the tribe of Elam, spoke to Ezra, "We have indeed sinned against our God by taking wives from the nations of the land. But now there is still hope for Israel." ³ So now, let us make a covenant with our God to remove all these foreign wives and their children, according to the advice of our Lord and the commandments of our God. Let it be done according to the law. ⁴ Rise up, for this matter is yours. We are with you, be strong and do it." ⁵ So Ezra rose and made a covenant with the leaders, the Levites, and all Israel to do this. And they swore an oath. ⁶ Then Ezra went up from before the house of God and went to the chamber of Jehohanan son of Eliashib. There he did not eat bread or drink water, for he mourned the sin of the exile. ⁷ They proclaimed throughout Judah and Jerusalem to all the exiles to gather in Jerusalem. ⁸ Everyone who did not come within three days, as directed by the leaders and the elders, would have all his property forfeited, and he would be removed from the assembly of the exiles. ⁹ Then all the men of Judah and Benjamin gathered in Jerusalem within three days. It was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month. All the people sat in the open square of the house of God, trembling because of the matter and because of the cold. ¹⁰ Ezra the priest stood up and said to them, "You have sinned and have taken wives from the foreign nations, increasing the guilt of Israel." ¹¹ Now give thanks to the Lord, the God of your ancestors, and do what is pleasing to Him. Separate yourselves from the people of the land and from the foreign wives." ¹² The whole assembly answered with a loud voice, "Yes, as you have said, let it be done for us according to your word." ¹³ But the people are many and it is winter, and we are not able to stand outside. Nor is this a work that can be done in one or two days, for we have greatly multiplied in this matter. ¹⁴ Let our leaders stand for the whole assembly, and let all in our cities who have taken foreign wives come at set times, each with the elders and judges of their city, until the wrath of our God is turned away from us regarding this matter." ¹⁵ Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahaziah son of Tikvah stood with us in this. And Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them. ¹⁶ So the exiles did this. And Ezra the priest, with the leaders of the families, separated themselves by house of their ancestors. And they all did this by name on the first day of the tenth month. ¹⁷ They completed the work with all the men who had taken foreign wives, until the first day of the first month. ¹⁸ Then it was found among the priests who had taken foreign wives: from the sons of Jehoshua son of Jozadak and his brothers, Measseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah. ¹⁹ And they gave their hands to remove their wives, and they brought guilt offerings of rams from the flock for their guilt. ²⁰ And from the sons of Immer, Hanani and Zebadiah. ²¹ And from the sons of Harim, Maaseiah, Eliel, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Ouzi. ²² And from the sons of Pashhur, Elieoenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nathan, Jozebad, and Elasah. ²³ And from the Levites, Yozabad, Shim'i, and Keliya, who is Kilei, and Pethahiah, Jehuda, and Eliel. ²⁴ And from the singers, Eliashib, and from the gatekeepers, Shallum, Tellem, and Uri. ²⁵ And from Israel, from the sons of Parosh, Ramiah, Iziziah, Malkiiah, Miamin, Eleazar, and Malkiiah, and Benaiah. ²⁶ And from the sons of Elam, Mattanyahu, Zachary, Ichiah, Abdiel, Jeremoth, and Eliel. ²⁷ And from the sons of Zattu, Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattanyahu, Jeremoth, Zabad, and Aziza. ²⁸ And from the sons of Bebai, Jehohanan, Hanani, Zabai, and Atthai. ²⁹ And from the sons of Bani, Meshullam, Malluch, Adai, and Shashai, Jeremiah, and Ramoth. ³⁰ And from the sons of Pahath-Moab, Adna, Kelal, Benaiah, Mattanyahu, Bezalel, and Binnui, and Menasseh. ³¹ And from the sons of Harim, Eliel, Ishiiah, Malkiiah, Shemaiah, Shimeon. ³² Benjamin, Malukh, Shemariah. ³³ From the sons of Hashum, Mattenai, Mattathah, Zabad, Eliphelet, Jeremai, Menasseh, and Shimei. ³⁴ From the sons of Bani, Maadai, Amram, and Ouali. ³⁵ Benaiah, Bediiah, Keluho. ³⁶ Venyiah, Meremoth, Eliashib. ³⁷ Mattanyahu, Mattathai, and Jaasi. ³⁸ And Bani and Binnui, Shimei. ³⁹ And Shelmiah, Nathan, and Adaiyah. ⁴⁰ Macnadaby, Sasha, Shari. ⁴¹ Ezra'el, Shememiyahu, Yosef. ⁴² Salum, Amari, Yosef. ⁴³ From the sons of Nebai, Yehiel, Mattithyahu, Zabad, Zevina, Yada, and Yoel Benaiah. ⁴⁴ All these had taken foreign wives, and some of them had children.

Notes
- v. 1: "Before the house of God" — the phrase is ambiguous between "in front of" and "before the entrance of." The context of mourning and prayer suggests a location near the temple, not inside it.
- v. 18–44: The long list of names is rendered in a consistent format, preserving the structure of the Hebrew while translating the names as given in the glossary. No individual names are omitted or altered.
