# Ezra 7

¹ After these things, in the kingdom of Artaxerxes, the king of Persia, Ezra son of Seraiah son of Azaryah son of Hilkiah: ² son of Shallum son of Zadok son of Ahitub: ³ son of Amariah son of Azaryah son of Meraioth: ⁴ son of Zerahiah son of Uzzi son of Bukki: ⁵ son of Abishua son of Pinhas son of Eleazar son of Aaron the chief priest. ⁶ This was Ezra, who had come up from Babylon, a scribe skilled in the law of Moses that the Lord God of Israel had given. The king had given him all he asked for, for the hand of his God was on him. ⁷ So some of the people of Israel, the priests, Levites, and singers, gatekeepers, and Nethinim, went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king. ⁸ He arrived in Jerusalem in the fifth month, which was the seventh year of the king. ⁹ For on the first day of the first month he had set out from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month he arrived in Jerusalem, for the hand of his God had been good to him. ¹⁰ For Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of the Lord and to do it and to teach it in Israel, instruction and judgment. ¹¹ This is the decree of the royal letter that Artaxerxes the king gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, who was skilled in the words of the commandments of the Lord and his laws to Israel. ¹² [Aramaic] Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra, the priest and scribe, who is learned in the law of the God of heaven—peace. ¹³ [Aramaic] I command that any who are willing in my kingdom, from the people of Israel and their priests and Levites, may go with you to Jerusalem, as you wish. ¹⁴ [Aramaic] From before the king and the seven counselors, I have sent messengers to inspect Judah and Jerusalem according to the law of your God that is in your hand. ¹⁵ [Aramaic] And any silver and gold that the king and his counselors are willing to give to the God of Israel, who dwells in Jerusalem, let it be brought to him. ¹⁶ [Aramaic] And any silver and gold that you find in all the province of Babylon among the willing offerings of the people and priests, who are willing to give to the house of their God in Jerusalem, let them bring it. ¹⁷ [Aramaic] Whatever you find, whether bulls, rams, or lambs, and their burnt offerings and their grain offerings and their drink offerings, let them be brought to the altar of the house of your God in Jerusalem. ¹⁸ [Aramaic] And whatever good thing you and your brothers find in the remaining silver and gold, you may use to please your God. ¹⁹ [Aramaic] And whatever things are given to you for the service of the house of your God, which fall to you, let them be given over to the house of your God in Jerusalem. ²⁰ [Aramaic] And any remaining need of the house of your God, whatever you need to give, let it be given from the king's treasury. ²¹ [Aramaic] And from me, Artaxerxes the king has issued a decree to all the treasurers who are beyond the river: whatever Ezra the priest and scribe of the law of the God of heaven asks you, let it be done quickly for him, without delay. ²² [Aramaic] Up to one hundred talents of silver, up to one hundred kor of wheat, up to one hundred baths of wine, up to one hundred baths of oil, and salt as much as is needed. ²³ [Aramaic] Whatever is required by the will of the God of heaven, let it be done. Take care not to anger the kingdom of the king or his sons. ²⁴ [Aramaic] And to you, you are to know that no tax is to be imposed on the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and Nethinim, or on those who serve the house of this God. You must not oppress them. ²⁵ [Aramaic] And you, Ezra, by the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint judges and officials who will govern all the people beyond the river, everyone who knows the law of your God, and those who do not—teach them. ²⁶ [Aramaic] And whoever does not do the law of your God and the law of the king, let judgment be carried out against them, whether to death, or to exile, or to punishment, or to banishment. ²⁷ Blessed be the Lord, the God of our ancestors, who has put this in the heart of the king to honor the house of the Lord in Jerusalem. ²⁸ And grace was shown to me by the king and his counselors and the mighty men of the king. And I strengthened myself by the hand of my God on me, and I gathered leading men from Israel to go up with me.

---

Notes  
- v. 22: The Aramaic text specifies "talents" for silver and "kor" and "baths" for grain and liquids. These are retained as standard ancient measures.  
- v. 25: "Appoint judges and officials" is a direct translation of the Aramaic imperative structure.  
- v. 26: "To death, to exile, to punishment, or to banishment" reflects the Aramaic legal terminology, which is plural and cumulative.  
- v. 27: "To honor the house" is a natural rendering of the Aramaic phrase "to cause the house of the Lord to be praised."
