# Psalms 54

¹ For the Chief Musician. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David. ² When the Ziphites came and said to Saul, “Is David not hiding among us?” ³ God, in your name save me, and by your might judge me. ⁴ God, hear my prayer, give ear to the words of my mouth. ⁵ For strangers have risen against me, and violent men have sought my life. They do not set God before them. Selah. ⁶ Indeed, God is my helper. The Lord is the one who sustains my life. ⁷ Return evil to my adversaries. In your truth, destroy them. ⁸ I will willingly sacrifice to you. I will praise your name, O Lord, for it is good. ⁹ For you have rescued me from every trouble. And my eyes have seen my enemies.

---

Notes
- v. 2: The Hebrew text indicates that the Ziphites informed Saul about David's hiding place. The translation follows the Hebrew directly.
- v. 5: The word "Selah" is retained as in the original, though its precise meaning remains uncertain.
- v. 8: "I will willingly sacrifice" reflects the Hebrew נְדָבָה, which means "willingly" or "freely."
