# Psalms 134

¹ A song of ascents. Behold, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD, who stand in the house of the LORD in the night. ² Lift up your hands to the holy place and bless the LORD. ³ May the LORD bless you from Zion, the one who made the heavens and the earth.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "הִנֵּה" ("Behold") is retained for its emphatic function. The term "בֵּית־יְהוָה" is rendered "the house of the LORD" to reflect its liturgical setting.
- v. 3: "מִצִּיּוֹן" is translated as "from Zion" to maintain its geographic and symbolic reference.
