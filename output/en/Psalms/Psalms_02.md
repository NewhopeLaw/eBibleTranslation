# Psalms 2

¹ Why do the nations rage and the peoples conspire in vain? ² The kings of the earth have taken their stand, and the rulers have gathered together against the LORD and against His anointed. ³ Let us tear their bonds apart and cast their ropes from us. ⁴ The One who dwells in the heavens will laugh; the Lord will mock them. ⁵ Then He will speak to them in His anger, and in His fury He will alarm them. ⁶ But I have set My king on Mount Zion, My holy hill. ⁷ I will declare the decree of the LORD: He said to Me, “You are My son; today I have begotten You.” ⁸ Ask of Me, and I will give You the nations as Your inheritance, and the ends of the earth as Your possession. ⁹ You will shepherd them with a rod of iron, as clay vessels You will shatter them. ¹⁰ Now then, you kings, be wise; take warning, all you rulers of the earth. ¹¹ Serve the LORD with fear, and rejoice with trembling. ¹² Kiss the Son, lest He be angry and you perish from the way, when His anger flares suddenly. How blessed are all who take refuge in Him!

---

Notes
- v. 2: "His anointed" is used for *mashiach*, following the glossary.
- v. 7: "I will declare the decree" is a rendering of *asapher*, which can mean "declare" or "proclaim."
- v. 9: "Rod of iron" and "as clay vessels" preserve the poetic imagery in the Hebrew without adding interpretive clarification.
