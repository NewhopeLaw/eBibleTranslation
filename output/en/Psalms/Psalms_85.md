# Psalms 85

¹ For the music director, a psalm for the sons of Korah. ² You have been pleased, O LORD, with your land; you have restored the captivity of Jacob. ³ You have forgiven the iniquity of your people; you have covered all their sins. Selah. ⁴ You have restrained your anger; you have turned back from the fury of your wrath. ⁵ Turn to us again, O God of our salvation, and put an end to your anger toward us. ⁶ Will you be angry with us forever? Will you prolong your wrath from generation to generation? ⁷ You, O LORD, will turn and revive us; your people will rejoice in you. ⁸ Show us, O LORD, your steadfast love, and grant us your salvation. ⁹ I will hear what God the LORD will speak, for the LORD will speak peace to his people, to those who are his faithful ones and do not return to folly. ¹⁰ Indeed, his salvation is near for those who fear him, so that glory might dwell in our land. ¹¹ Steadfast love and faithfulness meet together; righteousness and peace kiss one another. ¹² Faithfulness sprouts from the earth, and righteousness looks down from heaven. ¹³ The LORD will indeed give what is good, and our land will give its increase. ¹⁴ Righteousness will go before him and will prepare the way for his steps.

---

Notes
- v. 9: The phrase "those who are his faithful ones and do not return to folly" reflects the reading of "chasidei" ("faithful ones") and the sense of "lo yashavu lekislah" ("they will not return to folly"). The Septuagint adds "those who turn to him with their heart," but the Hebrew does not include this.
