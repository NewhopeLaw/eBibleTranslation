# Psalms 112

¹ Praise the LORD. Happy is the human who fears the LORD, who greatly delights in his commandments. ² His descendants will be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright will be blessed. ³ Wealth and abundance dwell in his house, and his righteousness stands forever. ⁴ Light rises in the darkness for the upright. Gracious, compassionate, and just is the one who gives it. ⁵ Generous is the human who gives and lends, whose affairs are ordered justly. ⁶ For he will not be moved forever; the name of the righteous will endure as a lasting memory. ⁷ He does not fear ill news; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD. ⁸ His heart is secure and does not fear, until he sees his adversaries crushed. ⁹ He has scattered, he has given to the poor; his righteousness stands forever. His horn is exalted in glory. ¹⁰ The wicked see it and grow angry, they gnash their teeth and vanish. The desires of the wicked will perish.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "Light rises in the darkness for the upright" follows the poetic metaphor in the Hebrew and reflects the structure and meaning of the verse.
- v. 6: "The name of the righteous will endure" is a reading of the Hebrew לְזֵכֶר עוֹלָם יִהְיֶה צַדִּֽיק, where the subject of the verb is the name or memory of the righteous.
- v. 9: "His horn is exalted in glory" reflects the figurative language of רוּם קַרְנוֹ בְּכָבֽוֹד, where "horn" symbolizes strength or honor.
