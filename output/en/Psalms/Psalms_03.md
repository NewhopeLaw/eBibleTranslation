# Psalms 3

¹ A psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom. ² O LORD, how many are my adversaries, how many have risen against me! ³ Many say of my soul, “There is no salvation for him in God.” Selah. ⁴ But you, O LORD, are a shield around me, my glory, and the one who lifts up my head. ⁵ I cried to the LORD with my voice, and he answered me from his holy mountain. Selah. ⁶ I lay down and slept, I woke again, for the LORD sustained me. ⁷ I will not fear the multitude of people who surround me on every side. ⁸ Arise, O LORD! Deliver me, my God! For you have struck down all my adversaries, you have shattered the teeth of the wicked. ⁹ Salvation belongs to the LORD, and your blessing is upon your people. Selah.

---

Notes  
- v. 3: The phrase "selah" is preserved as "Selah" as in the source text. It is commonly thought to indicate a musical or liturgical pause, but its exact function is uncertain.  
- v. 5: "From his holy mountain" reflects the Hebrew "מֵהַר קָדְשׁוֹ" and is consistent with the imagery in the chapter.  
- v. 9: "Salvation belongs to the LORD" is a direct translation of "לַיהוָה הַיְשׁוּעָה" and captures the declarative tone of the verse.
