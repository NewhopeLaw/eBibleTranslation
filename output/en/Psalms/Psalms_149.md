# Psalms 149

¹ Praise the LORD. Sing to the LORD a new song; praise His praise in the assembly of the faithful. ² Let Israel rejoice in its Maker; let the sons of Zion be glad in their King. ³ Let them praise His name in dance; with tambourine and lyre, let them sing to Him. ⁴ For the LORD delights in His people; He exalts the humble in salvation. ⁵ Let the faithful rejoice in glory; let them sing for joy on their beds. ⁶ Let the praises of God be in their throats; let two-edged swords be in their hands. ⁷ To carry out judgment on the nations, to execute reproofs among the peoples. ⁸ To bind their kings with chains, their nobles with fetters of iron. ⁹ To carry out on them a judgment written; this is the glory for all His faithful ones. Praise the LORD!

---

Notes
- v. 1: The term הַלְלוּ יָהּ is a poetic contraction of הַלְלוּ יָהּ (Praise the LORD). The phrase שִׁיר חָדָשׁ (a new song) is standard in praise psalms.
- v. 6: "two-edged swords" (פִּיפִיּוֹת) is a poetic term for weapons of war, rendered clearly based on contextual and semantic understanding.
