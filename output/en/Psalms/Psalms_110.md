# Psalms 110

¹ A psalm of David. A declaration from the LORD to the Adonai: Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies a footstool beneath your feet. ² The LORD will send forth his rod of power from Zion; there you will rule among your enemies. ³ With you is the beginning of strength in the day of your might, in the radiance of the holy ones; from the womb of the dawn I have begotten you. ⁴ The LORD has sworn and will not change his mind: You are a priest forever, according to the order of Melchizedek. ⁵ The Lord is at your right hand; he will shatter kings in the day of his wrath. ⁶ He will execute judgment among the nations, filling them with corpses. He will shatter the heads upon the wide earth. ⁷ He will drink from the stream along the way; therefore he will lift up his head.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Adonai" is a title meaning "Lord" or "Master," used here to distinguish the one being addressed by the LORD.
- v. 3: "from the womb of the dawn I have begotten you" reflects the poetic imagery of being born or raised up at the time of the dawn, symbolizing a divine initiative.
- v. 4: "Melchizedek" is a proper name and appears in Hebrew as מלכי צדק. The form "Melchizedek" follows the English rendering of the name from the Septuagint and common tradition.
