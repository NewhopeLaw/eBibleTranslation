# Psalms 128

¹ A song of ascents. Happy are all who fear the LORD, those who walk in his ways. ² You shall eat the fruit of your labor; you shall be happy and fare well. ³ Your wife will be like a flourishing vine in the courtyards of your home; your children will be like olive shoots around your table. ⁴ Indeed, this is how the man who fears the LORD will be blessed. ⁵ May the LORD bless you from Zion; may you see the good things of Jerusalem all the days of your life. ⁶ And may you see children of your children — peace upon Israel.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "the fruit of your labor" is a contextual rendering of *kaprekh yadekha*, which literally means "the fruit of your hands."
