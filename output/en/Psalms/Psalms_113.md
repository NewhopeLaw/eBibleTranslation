# Psalms 113

¹ Praise the LORD. Praise, servants of the LORD, praise the name of the LORD. ² May the name of the LORD be blessed from now and forevermore. ³ From the rising of the sun to its going down, the name of the LORD is praised. ⁴ High above all nations is the LORD, above the heavens is his glory. ⁵ Who is like the LORD our God, the one who lifts up the one who sits? ⁶ The one who brings down to look upon the heavens and the earth. ⁷ The one who raises up from the dust, from the ashpit lifts up the poor, ⁸ to seat beside nobles, beside nobles of his people. ⁹ The one who seats the barren in the house, a mother rejoicing— praise the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 5: The phrase "the one who lifts up the one who sits" is a poetic inversion typical in Hebrew poetry, where the subject and object are reversed for rhetorical effect. The intended meaning is that the LORD is the one who exalts those who are seated in humility or low estate.
- v. 6: "The one who brings down to look upon the heavens and the earth" reflects the poetic structure and imagery in the Hebrew, where God's sovereignty is shown in his omniscience and oversight of all creation.
- v. 9: "A mother rejoicing— praise the LORD" captures the poetic line structure in Hebrew, where the second line echoes the opening call to praise.
