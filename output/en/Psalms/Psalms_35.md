# Psalms 35

¹ Of David. Contend, O LORD, with those contending against me; fight against those warring with me. ² Take up a shield and buckler and rise up to my aid. ³ Draw the sword and bar the way against those pursuing me. Say to my soul, “I am your salvation.” ⁴ Let those seeking my life be put to shame and caught in their guilt; let those plotting evil against me turn back and be disgraced. ⁵ Let them be like chaff before the wind, and may the angel of the LORD drive them away. ⁶ Let their path be darkness and stumbling, and may the angel of the LORD pursue them. ⁷ For they have secretly plotted my ruin, and in vain they have dug a trap for my life. ⁸ Let deception come to them, a snare they did not know; let the trap they set ensnare them. In their own snare may they fall. ⁹ And my soul will rejoice in the LORD; my heart will exult in his salvation. ¹⁰ All my bones will say: “O LORD, who is like you, saving the poor from the strong and the poor and needy from the one who robs them?” ¹¹ False witnesses rise up, who do not know me, and they question me. ¹² They repay me evil for good, and they strip my soul bare. ¹³ But as for me, when they grieved me, I wore sackcloth; I afflicted my soul with fasting, and my prayer returned to my bosom. ¹⁴ I walked as one mourning a brother, as one who mourns like a mother, I grieved. ¹⁵ And when I was weak, they rejoiced, and they gathered, and they attacked me—wicked men who did not know me, they tore and did not heal. ¹⁶ They mocked me with their taunts, they gnashed their teeth at me. ¹⁷ O LORD, how long will you look on? Rescue my soul from their destruction, my only one from the lions. ¹⁸ I will praise you in a great assembly, I will laud you among a mighty people. ¹⁹ Let not my foes rejoice over me in vain, let not my haters wink their eyes. ²⁰ For they speak to me words of peace, but they devise plots of deceit. ²¹ They opened wide their mouths against me, saying, “Aha, aha! We have seen it with our eyes.” ²² You, O LORD, have seen; do not be silent. O Lord, do not be far from me. ²³ Wake up, and rouse yourself for my cause. O my God, my Lord, for my right. ²⁴ Judge me, O LORD, according to your justice, O LORD my God, and let them not rejoice over me. ²⁵ Let them not say in their hearts, “Aha, our hearts desire it!” Let them not say, “We have devoured him!” ²⁶ Let them be put to shame together, those who rejoice at my misfortune. Let those who boast against me be clothed in disgrace and dishonor. ²⁷ Let those who delight in my righteousness rejoice and be glad, and let them always say, “Great is the LORD, who delights in the peace of his servant.” ²⁸ And my tongue will meditate on your justice; all the day your praise.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "my prayer returned to my bosom" — the Hebrew phrase תָּשֽׁוּב לְחֵיקִי is metaphorical, indicating that the psalmist's prayer was turned inward, perhaps in sorrow or frustration.
- v. 17: "my only one from the lions" — the Hebrew uses “ יחִידָתִי” which is typically feminine, but here used metaphorically for the psalmist's life or soul. Rendered as "my only one" to preserve the metaphor.
- v. 21: "Aha, aha! We have seen it with our eyes." — the doubling of "Aha!" (הֶאָח) in the Hebrew is rendered to reflect its forceful repetition.
- v. 27: "who delights in the peace of his servant" — the Hebrew is ambiguous between "delights in" and "delights to see." The former is chosen for clarity and stylistic consistency.
