# Psalms 1

¹ Happy is the human who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked, and does not stand in the path of sinners, and does not sit in the company of mockers. ² Instead, the joy of that human is in the teaching of the LORD, and in the teaching of the LORD they meditate day and night. ³ That human will be like a tree planted by streams of water, whose fruit they yield in its season, and whose leaves do not wither. In all that they do, they will succeed. ⁴ Not so the wicked—they are like chaff, which the wind blows away. ⁵ Therefore, the wicked will not rise in judgment, nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous. ⁶ For the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked will be destroyed.

---

Notes  
- v. 2: "teaching" is used for תּוֹרָה (torah), following the glossary.  
- v. 4: The metaphor of "chaff" is retained as a direct rendering of מֹץ (motz), though the Septuagint uses χνοῦς (chous), "dust," which is not adopted here.
