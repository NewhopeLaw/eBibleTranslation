# Psalms 15

¹ A psalm of David. O LORD, who may dwell in your tent? Who may rest in your holy mountain? ² One who walks blamelessly, and acts justly, and speaks truth in his heart. ³ Who does not slander with his tongue, who does no harm to his neighbor, and does not disgrace anyone near him. ⁴ In whose presence the wicked are despised, and who honors those who fear the LORD. One who swears to do evil and does not waver. ⁵ One who does not lend his silver at interest, and does not take a bribe against the innocent. One who does these things shall not be moved forever.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "O LORD" is a direct address to YHWH.
- v. 4: "One who swears to do evil and does not waver" refers to someone who makes a malicious oath and does not back down.
- v. 5: "Does not take a bribe against the innocent" reflects the Hebrew "שֹׁחַד עַל־נָקִי לֹא לָקָח," where "nakiy" means innocent.
