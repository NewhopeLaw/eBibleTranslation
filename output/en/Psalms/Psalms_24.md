# Psalms 24

¹ A psalm of David. The LORD owns the earth and all it contains, the inhabited world and its dwellers. ² For he founded it upon the seas and established it upon the rivers. ³ Who may ascend the mountain of the LORD, and who may stand in his holy place? ⁴ One whose hands are clean and whose heart is pure, who has not taken a false oath, whose neighbor they have not wronged. ⁵ That one shall receive a blessing from the LORD and righteousness from the God of their salvation. ⁶ This is the generation of those who seek him, who seek the face of the God of Jacob. Selah. ⁷ Lift up your heads, you gates, and be raised up, you ancient doors, that the King of glory may come in. ⁸ Who is this King of glory? The LORD, strong and mighty, the LORD mighty in battle. ⁹ Lift up your heads, you gates, and raise yourselves, you ancient doors, that the King of glory may come in. ¹⁰ Who is this King of glory? The LORD of Hosts, he is the King of glory. Selah.

---

Notes
- v. 4: The phrase "nashat lo shav nefeshi" is taken to mean "has not taken a false oath" or "has not wronged their neighbor," rather than a more abstract "has not lifted up their soul to falsehood," which is a common but less precise interpretation. The rendering here follows the more concrete reading.
- v. 6: "Doreshav" is rendered as "those who seek" rather than "generation that seeks." The plural form is better captured by "those who seek" to avoid implying a singular generation.
