# Psalms 48

¹ A song. A psalm for the sons of Korah. ² Great is the LORD and highly praised in the city of our God, in his holy mountain. ³ Beautiful in its splendor, the joy of all the earth, is Mount Zion, the northern slopes, the city of the great king. ⁴ God is known in its citadels as a fortress. ⁵ For behold, the kings have gathered, they passed by together. ⁶ They saw it and were astonished, they trembled, they were dismayed. ⁷ Trembling seized them there, pain like a woman in labor. ⁸ With a mighty wind, you will shatter the ships of Tarshish. ⁹ As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God, which God will establish forever. Selah. ¹⁰ We have received, O God, your steadfast love in the midst of your temple. ¹¹ According to your name, O God, so is your praise to the ends of the earth; your righteousness is full in your right hand. ¹² Let Mount Zion rejoice, let the daughters of Judah be glad for your judgments. ¹³ Walk around Zion, encircle her, count her towers, ¹⁴ take careful note of her ramparts, that you may tell it to the next generation: ¹⁵ for this is our God forever and ever; he will be our guide to eternity.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "With a mighty wind, you will shatter the ships of Tarshish" — the Hebrew reads "בְּרוּחַ קָדִים תְּשַׁבֵּר אֳנִיּוֹת תַּרְשִֽׁישׁ" (literally "with a wind of the east you will break ships of Tarshish"). The reference to "ships of Tarshish" is a poetic metaphor for distant or powerful adversaries. The translation follows the literal meaning without assuming additional context.
