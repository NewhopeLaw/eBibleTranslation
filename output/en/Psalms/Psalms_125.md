# Psalms 125

¹ A song of ascents. Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion — they will not be shaken. The one dwelling in Jerusalem will remain forever. ² The mountains stand around Jerusalem, and the LORD stands around his people — now and forevermore. ³ For the staff of the wicked will not rest on the portion of the righteous, so that the righteous may not stretch out their hands into wrongdoing. ⁴ The LORD is good to the good and upright in heart. ⁵ But the crooked the LORD will bring to ruin, along with those who do wrong. Peace be upon Israel.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion" — the comparison is rendered as a relative clause for clarity and natural English flow.
- v. 3: "The staff of the wicked will not rest on the portion of the righteous" — "portion" here refers to the inheritance or share, and "stretch out their hands into wrongdoing" renders the poetic parallelism of the Hebrew.
