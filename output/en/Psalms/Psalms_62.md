# Psalms 62

¹ For the Chief Musician. On Jeduthun. A psalm of David. ² My soul, wait only for God, for from him is my salvation. ³ He alone is my rock and my salvation, my stronghold. I shall not be moved greatly. ⁴ How long will you plot against a person? You all kill, like a leaning wall, a broken fence. ⁵ They plan to remove my honor, they run with their mouths pretending to bless, but in their hearts they curse—Selah. ⁶ My soul, wait for God, for from him is my hope. ⁷ He alone is my rock and my salvation, my stronghold. I shall not be moved. ⁸ My salvation and my glory is in God, my stronghold, the God who helps me. ⁹ Trust in him at all times, people; pour out your hearts before him, for God is our refuge—Selah. ¹⁰ Surely the human children are vanity, the children of men are a lie, they weigh themselves in the balance to rise, but they are altogether vanity. ¹¹ Do not trust in violence, nor covet gain. Do not set your hearts on wealth, for if it flows away, it is nothing. ¹² God has spoken once, twice have I heard this: strength belongs to God. ¹³ And to you, Lord, is steadfast love, for you repay each one according to his deed.

---

Notes
- v. 9: "people" is the plural of "human" (adam), following the glossary.
- v. 10: "vanity" and "lie" are used for the Hebrew terms hevel and sheker, which are central to the psalm’s theme of human transience and falsehood.
- v. 13: "steadfast love" is the assigned gloss for hesed.
