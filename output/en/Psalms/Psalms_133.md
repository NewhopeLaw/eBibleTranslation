# Psalms 133

¹ A song of ascents. Indeed, how good and how pleasant it is when brothers live together in unity! ² Like oil poured on the head, flowing down on the beard— Aaron’s beard, which flows down upon the edge of his robes. ³ Like the dew of Hermon that falls on the mountains of Zion— for there the LORD has commanded the blessing, life without end.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "the beard— Aaron’s beard" is a poetic repetition and emphasis in Hebrew, which is rendered in a way that preserves the imagery and reference without being redundant.
- v. 3: "Hermon" is a poetic reference to Mount Hermon, used here to evoke the imagery of dew descending. The phrase "falls on the mountains of Zion" is metaphorical and symbolic of divine blessing.
