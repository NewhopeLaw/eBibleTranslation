# Psalms 148

¹ Praise the LORD. Praise the LORD from the heavens; praise Him in the highest heights. ² Praise Him, all His messengers; praise Him, all His armies. ³ Praise Him, sun and moon; praise Him, all the stars that shine. ⁴ Praise Him, skies of the heavens, and the waters that are above the heavens. ⁵ Let them praise the name of the LORD, for He spoke and they were made. ⁶ He set them in place forever; He gave a decree that will not pass away. ⁷ Praise the LORD from the earth, you sea monsters and all the deeps. ⁸ Fire and hail, snow and frost, storm winds that do His word. ⁹ Mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars. ¹⁰ Beasts and all cattle, creeping things and birds that fly. ¹¹ Kings of the earth and all peoples, rulers and all judges of the land. ¹² Young men and also virgins, old men with the young. ¹³ Let them praise the name of the LORD, for His name alone is exalted; His glory is over the earth and the heavens. ¹⁴ He has raised a horn for His people, praise for all His faithful ones— for the children of Israel, the people who are near to Him. Praise the LORD!

---

Notes
- v. 3: "all the stars that shine" — the Hebrew "כּוֹכְבֵי אֽוֹר" is literally "stars of light," but the meaning is clear and natural in English.
- v. 8: "storm winds that do His word" — the Hebrew "רוּחַ סְעָרָה עֹשָׂה דְבָרֽוֹ" is rendered to reflect the poetic parallelism and the meaning that even the storm winds obey His command.
- v. 14: "raised a horn" — the Hebrew "וַיָּרֶם קֶרֶן" is a metaphor for exalting or empowering. "Raised a horn" is a standard idiom for this in poetic texts.
