# Psalms 63

¹ A psalm of David. When he was in the wilderness of Judah. ² God, my God, I seek you. My soul thirsts for you, my flesh longs for you, in a dry and weary land where there is no water. ³ So I have seen you in the sanctuary, to see your strength and your glory. ⁴ For your steadfast love is greater than life, my lips shall praise you. ⁵ So I will bless you all my life, in your name I will lift up my hands. ⁶ My soul is filled as with fat and marrow, and my mouth shall proclaim your praise with joyful lips. ⁷ If I remember you on my bed, I meditate on you at dawn. ⁸ For you have been my help, and in the shadow of your wings I will rejoice. ⁹ My soul clings to you, your right hand upholds me. ¹⁰ But they seek my life in vain; they will descend to the depths of the earth. ¹¹ They will be given over to the hand of the sword, their portion among the wicked. ¹² But the king will rejoice in God, all who swear by him will glory, for the mouths of those who speak falsely will be stopped.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "dry and weary land where there is no water" reflects the imagery of "earth of drought and weary" in the Hebrew, emphasizing the desolation.
- v. 6: "as with fat and marrow" reflects "like oil and marrow" in the Hebrew, capturing the richness and sustenance implied.
- v. 12: "portion among the wicked" is a translation of "the portion of jackals," which metaphorically refers to the cursed or condemned.
