# Psalms 119

¹ Happy are those whose ways are blameless, who walk according to the Law of the LORD. ² Happy are those who keep his testimonies, who seek him with all their hearts. ³ They do no wrong; they walk in his ways. ⁴ You have commanded your precepts to be kept diligently. ⁵ My heart rejoices in your statutes; I will not forget your word. ⁶ I will not be put to shame, for I have watched all your commandments. ⁷ I will thank you with an upright heart, as I learn your righteous laws. ⁸ I will keep your statutes; do not abandon me completely. ⁹ How can a young man keep his way pure? By keeping your word. ¹⁰ With all my heart I have sought you; do not let me stray from your commandments. ¹¹ I have hidden your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you. ¹² You are blessed, O LORD, teach me your statutes. ¹³ I have spoken of all your decrees with my mouth. ¹⁴ I have rejoiced in your law as much as in all riches. ¹⁵ I will meditate on your commandments and reflect on your ways. ¹⁶ I will delight in your statutes; I will not forget your word. ¹⁷ Be gracious to your servant, that I may live and keep your word. ¹⁸ Open my eyes, that I may behold the wonders of your law. ¹⁹ I am a foreigner on earth; do not hide your commandments from me. ²⁰ My soul longs for your statutes at all times. ²¹ You have rebuked the arrogant; those who stray from your commandments are accursed. ²² Remove from me disgrace and contempt, for I have kept your testimonies. ²³ Even princes sit together against me, but your servant meditates on your statutes. ²⁴ Your testimonies are my delight; they are my counselors. ²⁵ My soul clings to the dust; give me life according to your word. ²⁶ I have told of my ways, and you have answered me; teach me your statutes. ²⁷ Make me understand the way of your commandments, and I will meditate on your wondrous works. ²⁸ My soul melts away from sorrow; strengthen me according to your word. ²⁹ Remove the way of falsehood from me, and grant me your law in grace. ³⁰ I have chosen the way of faithfulness; I have set your judgments before me. ³¹ I have clung to your testimonies, O LORD; do not let me be put to shame. ³² I will run in the way of your commandments, for you have set my heart free. ³³ Teach me your way, O LORD, that I may keep your statutes diligently. ³⁴ Give me understanding, and I will keep your law; indeed, I will observe it with all my heart. ³⁵ Guide me in the path of your commandments, for I delight in it. ³⁶ Incline my heart to your testimonies, not to unjust gain. ³⁷ Turn my eyes from looking at vanity; give me life in your ways. ³⁸ Confirm your word to your servant, which you have promised to those who fear you. ³⁹ Take away the disgrace I dread, for your laws are good. ⁴⁰ How I long for your commandments! In your righteousness give me life. ⁴¹ Let your steadfast love come to me, O LORD, your salvation according to your promise. ⁴² Then I will answer those who taunt me, for I trust in your word. ⁴³ Do not take the word of truth from my mouth, for I have put my hope in your judgments. ⁴⁴ I will keep your law continually, for ever and ever. ⁴⁵ I will walk in a wide place, for I have sought your precepts. ⁴⁶ I will speak of your testimonies before kings and will not be put to shame. ⁴⁷ I will delight in your commandments, which I have loved. ⁴⁸ I will lift up my hands to your commandments, which I have loved, and meditate on your statutes. ⁴⁹ Remember your word to your servant, for which you have given me hope. ⁵⁰ This is my comfort in my trouble, that your word gives me life. ⁵¹ The arrogant have utterly derided me, yet I have not turned from your law. ⁵² When I remember your judgments from of old, I take comfort, O LORD. ⁵³ Zeal for your statutes has made me tremble; they are my delight. ⁵⁴ Your decrees are my songs in the house of my sojourning. ⁵⁵ I remember your name in the night, O LORD, and keep your law. ⁵⁶ This is my reward, for I have kept your precepts. ⁵⁷ I am your servant; give me understanding, that I may know your statutes. ⁵⁸ My soul is longing for your salvation; be gracious to me according to your word. ⁵⁹ I considered my ways and turned my feet to your statutes. ⁶⁰ I hastened and did not delay to keep your commandments. ⁶¹ The wicked have snared me, but I have not forgotten your law. ⁶² At midnight I rise to give thanks to you, for your just dealings. ⁶³ I am a companion of all who fear you, of those who keep your precepts. ⁶⁴ Your steadfast love, O LORD, fills the earth; teach me your statutes. ⁶⁵ You have done well to me, O LORD, according to your word. ⁶⁶ Teach me good judgment and knowledge, for I believe in your commandments. ⁶⁷ Before I was humbled, I went astray, but now I keep your word. ⁶⁸ You are good and do good; teach me your statutes. ⁶⁹ Falsehood clings to the wicked, but I keep your commandments with all my heart. ⁷⁰ Their hearts are as calloused as curdled milk, but I delight in your law. ⁷¹ It is good for me that I was afflicted, so that I might learn your statutes. ⁷² The law from your mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver pieces. ⁷³ Your hands have made me and shaped me; give me understanding, and I will learn your commandments. ⁷⁴ Those who fear you will see me and rejoice, for I have put my hope in your word. ⁷⁵ I know, O LORD, that your judgments are right and that you have afflicted me in faithfulness. ⁷⁶ Let your steadfast love comfort me according to your promise to your servant. ⁷⁷ Let your mercy come to me, that I may live, for your law is my delight. ⁷⁸ Let the arrogant be put to shame, for they have wronged me with lies. But I will meditate on your precepts. ⁷⁹ Let those who fear you turn to me, and those who know your testimonies. ⁸⁰ Let my heart be steadfast in your statutes, that I may not be put to shame. ⁸¹ My soul faints for your salvation; I put my hope in your word. ⁸² My eyes fail, longing for your promise. When will you comfort me? ⁸³ For I have become like a wineskin in the summer, yet I have not forgotten your statutes. ⁸⁴ How many are the days of your servant? When will you execute judgment on those who persecute me? ⁸⁵ The wicked have dug pitfalls for me, but I do not stray from your law. ⁸⁶ All your commandments are trustworthy; the false ones have hunted me, but help me! ⁸⁷ Though I am small and despised, I do not forget your commandments. ⁸⁸ Give me life in your steadfast love, and I will keep your testimonies. ⁸⁹ O LORD, your word is forever settled in the heavens. ⁹⁰ Your faithfulness endures to all generations; you have established the earth, and it stands firm. ⁹¹ By your judgments the present order stands, for all things are your servants. ⁹² Unless your law had been my delight, I would have perished in my affliction. ⁹³ I will never forget your precepts, for by them you have given me life. ⁹⁴ I am yours; save me, for I have sought your precepts. ⁹⁵ The wicked are waiting to destroy me, but I will consider your testimonies. ⁹⁶ I have seen the limit of all perfection; your commandment is exceedingly broad. ⁹⁷ How I love your law! It is my meditation all day long. ⁹⁸ Your commandments make me wiser than my enemies, for they are ever with me. ⁹⁹ I have more understanding than all my teachers, for your testimonies are my meditation. ¹⁰⁰ I understand more than the elders, for I keep your precepts. ¹⁰¹ I have kept my feet from every evil way, that I might keep your word. ¹⁰² I have not turned aside from your judgments, for you have taught me. ¹⁰³ How sweet are your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth! ¹⁰⁴ From your precepts I gain understanding; therefore I hate every false way. ¹⁰⁵ Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. ¹⁰⁶ I have sworn an oath and confirmed it, to keep your righteous laws. ¹⁰⁷ I am deeply afflicted; give me life, according to your word. ¹⁰⁸ Accept, O LORD, the willing offerings of my mouth, and teach me your judgments. ¹⁰⁹ My soul is always in my hand, yet I do not forget your law. ¹¹⁰ The wicked have laid a snare for me, but I do not stray from your commandments. ¹¹¹ Your testimonies I have taken as a heritage forever, for they are the joy of my heart. ¹¹² I have inclined my heart to perform your statutes forever, to the very end. ¹¹³ I hate double-mindedness, but I love your law. ¹¹⁴ You are my refuge and my shield; your word is my hope. ¹¹⁵ Turn away from me, you wicked, and I will keep your commandments. ¹¹⁶ Sustain me according to your promise, and I will live; do not let me be put to shame. ¹¹⁷ Strengthen me, and I will be delivered; I will observe your statutes continually. ¹¹⁸ You have rejected all the wicked of the earth like dross; therefore I love your testimonies. ¹¹⁹ You have made me jealous with what is not a god; but I delight in your law. ¹²⁰ My flesh trembles from fear of you, and I fear your judgments. ¹²¹ I have done what is right and just; do not leave me to be oppressed. ¹²² Be my surety, O LORD, for the good; let no wicked person oppress me. ¹²³ My eyes fail from looking for your salvation, for the promise of your righteousness. ¹²⁴ Deal with your servant according to your steadfast love, and teach me your statutes. ¹²⁵ I am your servant; give me understanding, that I may know your testimonies. ¹²⁶ It is time for the LORD to act, for they have broken your law. ¹²⁷ Therefore I love your commandments more than gold, yes, more than fine gold. ¹²⁸ Therefore I consider all your commandments to be right; I hate every false way. ¹²⁹ Your testimonies are wonderful; therefore my soul keeps them. ¹³⁰ The unfolding of your words gives light; it imparts understanding to the simple. ¹³¹ I open my mouth and pant, for I long for your commandments. ¹³² Turn to me and be gracious to me, as is your custom toward those who love your name. ¹³³ Steady my steps according to your word; let no iniquity have dominion over me. ¹³⁴ Redeem me from human oppression, that I may keep your precepts. ¹³⁵ Make your face shine upon your servant, and teach me your statutes. ¹³⁶ My eyes run down with tears, because people do not keep your law. ¹³⁷ Righteous are you, O LORD, and your judgments are right. ¹³⁸ You have commanded your precepts in righteousness and great faithfulness. ¹³⁹ My zeal has worn me out, because my enemies forget your words. ¹⁴⁰ Your word is exceedingly pure; therefore your servant loves it. ¹⁴¹ I am small and despised, yet I do not forget your commandments. ¹⁴² Your righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and your law is truth. ¹⁴³ Trouble and anguish have found me out, but your commandments are my delight. ¹⁴⁴ Your testimonies are righteous forever; give me understanding, and I will live. ¹⁴⁵ I call with my whole heart; answer me, O LORD. I will keep your statutes. ¹⁴⁶ I call to you; save me, that I may keep your testimonies. ¹⁴⁷ I rise before dawn and cry for help; I hope in your words. ¹⁴⁸ My eyes are awake before the watches of the night, that I may meditate on your word. ¹⁴⁹ Hear my voice according to your steadfast love; give me life, according to your justice. ¹⁵⁰ Those who seek mischief come near; they are far from your law. ¹⁵¹ You are near, O LORD, and all your commandments are true. ¹⁵² From of old I have known, from your testimonies, that you have established them forever. ¹⁵³ Look on my affliction and deliver me, for I have not forgotten your law. ¹⁵⁴ Defend my cause and redeem me; give me life according to your promise. ¹⁵⁵ Salvation is far from the wicked, for they do not seek your statutes. ¹⁵⁶ Your steadfast love is great, O LORD; give me life according to your justice. ¹⁵⁷ Many are my persecutors and my adversaries, but I do not turn from your testimonies. ¹⁵⁸ I saw the treacherous and loathed them, for they do not keep your word. ¹⁵⁹ Consider how I love your precepts! Give me life in your steadfast love. ¹⁶⁰ The sum of your word is truth, and every one of your righteous judgments endures forever. ¹⁶¹ Rulers have persecuted me without cause, but my heart fears your word. ¹⁶² I rejoice at your word like one who finds great spoil. ¹⁶³ I hate falsehood, but I love your law. ¹⁶⁴ Seven times a day I praise you for your righteous judgments. ¹⁶⁵ Great peace have those who love your law; nothing can make them stumble. ¹⁶⁶ I hope for your salvation, O LORD, and I do your commandments. ¹⁶⁷ My soul keeps your testimonies; I love them exceedingly. ¹⁶⁸ I keep your commandments and your testimonies, for all my ways are before you. ¹⁶⁹ Let my cry come before you, O LORD; give me understanding according to your word. ¹⁷⁰ Let my plea come before you; deliver me according to your promise. ¹⁷¹ Let my lips utter praise, for you teach me your statutes. ¹⁷² Let my tongue speak your word, for all your commandments are right. ¹⁷³ Let your hand be with me, for I have chosen your precepts. ¹⁷⁴ I long for your salvation, O LORD, and your law is my delight. ¹⁷⁵ Let my soul live, and it will praise you, and let your judgments help me. ¹⁷⁶ I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek your servant, for I do not forget your commandments.

Notes
- v. 9: The phrase "How can a young man keep his way pure?" is rendered directly and clearly, with "pure" representing the concept of moral and spiritual integrity.
- v. 105: "Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path" is a well-known metaphor, translated to retain its clarity and poetic imagery.
- v. 176: "I have gone astray like a lost sheep" reflects the metaphor from the Hebrew text, keeping it natural and accessible in English.
