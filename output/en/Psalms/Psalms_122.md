# Psalms 122

¹ A song of ascents. I rejoiced when they said to me, “Let us go to the house of the LORD.” ² Our feet are now standing in your gates, Jerusalem. ³ Jerusalem, you are built as a city whose unity is one. ⁴ For there the tribes go up, the tribes of the LORD, as a testimony to Israel, to give thanks to the name of the LORD. ⁵ For there are thrones for judgment, thrones of the house of David. ⁶ Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: “May peace come to those who love you.” ⁷ May peace be within your walls, and prosperity within your palaces. ⁸ For the sake of my brothers and companions, I will now speak of peace for you. ⁹ For the sake of the house of the LORD our God, I will seek your good.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "A song of ascents" is a title commonly associated with a pilgrimage song, hence the translation of "ma'lot" as "ascents."
- v. 3: The Hebrew "k'ir she'chubberet lah yachdav" is translated to "built as a city whose unity is one" to reflect the metaphor of unity in Jerusalem.
- v. 4: "Shivtei-Yah" is interpreted as "the tribes of the LORD" to reflect the divine association with the tribes of Israel.
- v. 5: The reference to "councils of judgment" is rendered as "thrones for judgment" to convey the judicial role within the city.
