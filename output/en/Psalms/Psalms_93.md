# Psalms 93

¹ The LORD has reigned, adorned in majesty. The LORD is adorned with strength, and has girded himself. Indeed, the earth is made firm, it will not totter. ² Your throne is prepared from of old; you are from eternity. ³ The rivers have lifted up, O LORD, the rivers have lifted up their voices. The rivers have raised their waves. ⁴ Mightier than the roaring of many waters, mightier than the crashing of the sea, the LORD is majestic in the high places. ⁵ Your testimonies are fully trusted; your house is very beautiful, O LORD, for length of days.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "reigned" is a dynamic rendering of בָּשַׂר (basar) with the context of YHWH's sovereign rule. The Septuagint uses ἐβασίλευσεν ("has reigned"), which is consistent with the intended sense.
- v. 4: "Mightier than the roaring of many waters" reflects the Hebrew comparison to "noises of many waters" (מִקֹּלוֹת מַיִם רַבִּים), where the LXX uses "roaring of many waters" (φωνῶν ὑδάτων πολλῶν). Both readings are valid, but the Hebrew's imagery is more general.
- v. 5: "very beautiful" is a natural rendering of נַאֲוָה, often used of something pleasing in form or appearance. The LXX uses πρέπει ("becomes," "is appropriate"), but the Hebrew's aesthetic sense is best captured by "beautiful."
