# Psalms 130

¹ A song of ascents. From the depths I call to you, O LORD. ² O LORD, listen to my voice. Let your ears be open to the voice of my pleas. ³ If you, O LORD, keep account of iniquities, who can stand? ⁴ For with you is forgiveness, that you may be revered. ⁵ I wait for the LORD; my soul waits, and I hope for his word. ⁶ My soul waits for the LORD more than watchmen wait for the morning, more than the watchmen wait for the morning. ⁷ Let Israel hope in the LORD, for with the LORD is steadfast love, and with him is rich redemption. ⁸ And he will redeem Israel from all his iniquities.

---

Notes
- v. 3: The line "If you, O LORD, keep account of iniquities" is a rhetorical question that emphasizes the gravity of sin and the need for divine mercy.
- v. 6: "More than watchmen wait for the morning" is a poetic intensification to highlight the longing for divine deliverance.
- v. 7: "Steadfast love" is used for חֶסֶד (chesed), a term that consistently refers to God's loyal, covenantal love.
