# Psalms 89

¹ A meditation by Ethan the Ezrahite. ² I will sing forever of the steadfast love of the LORD, declare your faithfulness to every generation with my mouth. ³ For I said, "Forever will steadfast love be built, your faithfulness will be established in the heavens." ⁴ I made a covenant with my chosen one, I swore to David my servant: ⁵ "Forever I will make his offspring, and I will build his throne for generation after generation." Selah. ⁶ And the heavens will declare your wonders, O LORD, and your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones. ⁷ For who in the clouds can be compared to the LORD? Who among the sons of the gods is like the LORD? ⁸ God, exalted in the council of the holy ones, great and awesome to all who surround him. ⁹ O LORD, God of Hosts, who is like you? Faithful, O LORD, and your steadfast love surrounds your people. ¹⁰ You rule over the might of the sea; with the tossing of its waves you still them. ¹¹ You struck Rahab like a slain one, with your mighty arm you scattered your enemies. ¹² Yours are the heavens, and yours the earth; you have founded the world and everything in it. ¹³ The north and the sea you created, Tabor and Hermon exult in your name. ¹⁴ Strength is with your arm, let your hand be exalted, let your right hand be raised. ¹⁵ Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne; steadfast love and truth go before your face. ¹⁶ Happy is the people who knows the shout of joy, O LORD, in the light of your face they walk. ¹⁷ In your name they will rejoice all day long, and in your righteousness they will be exalted. ¹⁸ For you are the glory of their might, and in your favor you raise the horn of your people. ¹⁹ For the LORD is their refuge, and the holy one of Israel, their king. ²⁰ Then you spoke in vision to your holy ones, and said: "I have placed a helper on the strong; I have raised a chosen one from the people." ²¹ I found David, my servant, with holy oil I anointed him. ²² My hand will be with him, and my arm will make him strong. ²³ No enemy will prevail over him, and no son of iniquity will harm him. ²⁴ And I will strike down his adversaries before him, and I will crush those who hate him. ²⁵ And my faithfulness and my steadfast love will be with him, and in my name his horn will be raised. ²⁶ I will set his hand on the sea, and his right hand on the rivers. ²⁷ He will call to me, "You are my Father, my God, and the rock of my salvation." ²⁸ And I will make him the firstborn, exalted above the kings of the earth. ²⁹ Forever I will keep my steadfast love for him, and my covenant with him will be faithful. ³⁰ And I will establish his offspring forever, and his throne as the days of the heavens. ³¹ If his children abandon my instruction, and do not walk in my judgments, ³² if they profane my statutes, and do not keep my commandments, ³³ I will punish their transgressions with a rod, and their iniquities with a scourge. ³⁴ But my steadfast love I will not remove from him, nor deal falsely in my faithfulness. ³⁵ I will not break my covenant, nor will I alter the utterance of my lips. ³⁶ Once I have sworn by my holiness, I will not be false to David. ³⁷ His offspring will endure forever, and his throne as the sun before me, ³⁸ and like the moon established forever—faithful is the witness in the heavens. Selah. ³⁹ But you have cast off and rejected, you have withdrawn from your anointed. ⁴⁰ You have broken the covenant of your servant, you have defiled his holy oil on the earth. ⁴¹ You have torn down every wall of his, you have laid his strongholds in ruins. ⁴² All who pass along the road have plundered him, he has become a reproach to his neighbors. ⁴³ You have raised the right hand of his adversaries, you have made all his enemies rejoice. ⁴⁴ You have turned back the shield of his might, you did not uphold him in battle. ⁴⁵ You have taken away from him the scepter of his strength; you have brought his throne to the ground. ⁴⁶ You have cut short the days of his reign, you have covered him with shame. Selah. ⁴⁷ How long, O LORD? Will you hide forever? Will your anger burn like fire? ⁴⁸ Remember, I ask you, how fleeting life is; why have you made all the children of humanity as nothing? ⁴⁹ Who is the man who will live and not see death, who can escape from the hand of Sheol? Selah. ⁵⁰ Where are your former steadfast loves, O Lord? You swore to David in your faithfulness. ⁵¹ Remember, O Lord, the reproach of your servants, the insults I have borne in the peoples' midst. ⁵² The insults of your enemies, O LORD, the insults of the foes of your anointed. ⁵³ Praise be to the LORD forever! Amen and amen.

---

Notes
- v. 4: The translation of כָּרַתִּֽי ("I made") as "I made a covenant" is based on the context of a covenant between God and David.
- v. 42: The Hebrew uses שַׁסֻּהוּ ("he has been plundered") in a poetic way to reflect the devastation of David's dynasty.
- v. 44: The meaning of "turned back the shield of his might" is inferred from the context of David's military strength being abandoned.
- v. 47: The question "How long, O LORD?" reflects the lament structure common in the Psalms.
- v. 48: The translation of זְכָר־אֲנִי ("Remember, I ask you") is based on the context of the psalmist's plea for God to remember his promises.
