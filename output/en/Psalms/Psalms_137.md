# Psalms 137

¹ By the rivers of Babylon we sat down and wept when we remembered Zion. ² On the willows there we hung our lyres. ³ For there our captors asked us for songs, and our tormentors demanded a joyous melody: "Sing for us a song of Zion!" ⁴ How can we sing the LORD's song in a foreign land? ⁵ If I forget you, Jerusalem, may my right hand forget its skill. ⁶ If I do not remember you, may my tongue cling to the roof of my mouth—if I do not place Jerusalem above all my joys. ⁷ Remember, LORD, the children of Edom, the day Jerusalem fell, the cry: "Tear it down! Tear it down! Even to its foundations!" ⁸ O daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, blessed is the one who repays you for what you have done to us— ⁹ blessed is the one who seizes your infants and dashes them against the rock.

---

Notes  
- v. 9: The final line is deliberately shocking and graphic, reflecting the intensity of the psalmist's grief and desire for retribution. The translation follows the Hebrew phrasing, which is direct and unambiguous.
