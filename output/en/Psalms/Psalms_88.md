# Psalms 88

¹ A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah. For the music director. On the sheminith, for a reprimand. A composition by Heman the Ezrahite. ² O LORD, God of my salvation, I have cried out by day, and in the night before you. ³ Let my prayer come into your presence. Incline your ear to my cry. ⁴ For my soul is filled with evils, and my life has come close to Sheol. ⁵ I am counted among those who descend to the pit, I have become like a man without aid. ⁶ I am free among the dead, like those who lie wounded in the grave, whom you remember no more, and they are cut off from your hand. ⁷ You have placed me in the lowest pit, in the dark, in the shadow of death. ⁸ You have laid your wrath upon me, and all your fierce acts have overwhelmed me. Selah. ⁹ You have distanced my familiar friends from me; you have made me a stench to them. I am confined and cannot go out. ¹⁰ My eyes have failed from affliction. I have cried to you, O LORD, all day long. I have stretched out my hands to you. ¹¹ Will you do wonders for the dead? Will the lifeless rise and give thanks to you? Selah. ¹² Will your steadfast love be declared in the grave, your faithfulness in the place of destruction? ¹³ Will your wonders be known in the darkness, your righteousness in the forsaken land? ¹⁴ And I, O LORD, have cried out to you. In the morning my prayer will come before you. ¹⁵ Why, O LORD, do you abandon my soul? Why do you hide your face from me? ¹⁶ I am poor and in distress from my youth, but when you raised me up, you led me into dismay and were crushed. ¹⁷ Your wrath has passed over me, and your terrors have shaken me. ¹⁸ They have surrounded me like water all day long. Together they have enclosed me. ¹⁹ You have distanced from me friend and neighbor, my familiar friends into darkness.

---

Notes
- v. 8: “Selah” is retained as a musical or liturgical marker, though its exact meaning is uncertain.
- v. 16: “were crushed” is an interpretive rendering of a damaged or ambiguous phrase, following the Septuagint but not the Hebrew text directly.
