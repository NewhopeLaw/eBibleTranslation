# Psalms 101

¹ A psalm of David. I will sing to you, O LORD, of steadfast love and judgment. I will make music to you. ² I will consider the path of the blameless—when will you come to me? I will walk in my house with a wholehearted disposition. ³ I will not set before my eyes anything that is worthless; I hate those who do wrong. Let them not cling to me. ⁴ A crooked heart will turn away from me. I will not know evil. ⁵ The one who slanders in secret, I will put to death. The proud of eye and wide in heart, I cannot eat with. ⁶ My eyes are on those who are faithful in the land, to dwell with them; the one who walks in the blameless way—he will serve me. ⁷ The one who makes himself arrogant in my house, who speaks lies, shall not remain before my eyes. ⁸ At dawn I will destroy all the wicked of the land, to root out from the city of the LORD all who do evil.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "Let them not cling to me" reflects the idea of the Hebrew "lo yidbakkuni" which is a poetic way of saying the speaker will not allow them to stay close.
- v. 5: "I cannot eat with" renders the Hebrew "lo uchal" which implies refusal to accept or tolerate.
- v. 6: "he will serve me" reflects the Hebrew "hu yesharteni" with "yeshar" meaning to serve or support.
- v. 8: "root out" renders "lekhitkot" which is a reflexive form meaning to be cut off or eradicated.
