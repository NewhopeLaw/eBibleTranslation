# Psalms 53

¹ For the Chief Musician. On the stringed instrument. A maskil of David. ² A fool said in his heart, There is no God. They are corrupt and have become abominable in their iniquities; there is none who does good. ³ God looked down from heaven upon the children of humanity, to see if there is a discerning one, one who seeks after God. ⁴ All of them have turned aside together. They are altogether unprofitable; there is none who does good, not even one. ⁵ Have those who do iniquity no knowledge? Those who eat up my people, as though they ate bread — they do not call upon God. ⁶ There they were in great fear, though there was no cause for fear. For God has scattered the bones of the one who delighted in them. They were put to shame, for God has rejected them. ⁷ O that someone from Zion would bring salvation to Israel! When God turns back the captivity of his people, Jacob will rejoice and Israel will be glad.

---

Notes  
- v. 2: The phrase "בְּלִבּוֹ" (in his heart) is rendered as "said in his heart" to preserve the Hebrew idiom.  
- v. 6: "פָּחֲדוּ־פַחַד" is a rare and complex expression. "There they were in great fear" attempts to capture the sense of a fear that was present without cause.  
- v. 7: The phrase "מִצִּיּוֹן" is translated as "from Zion" rather than "from the stronghold of Zion" to maintain clarity and consistency with the rest of the verse.
