# Psalms 87

¹ A song of the Korahites. A psalm. Its foundation is in the holy mountains. ² The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. ³ Glorious things are spoken of you, city of God. Selah. ⁴ I will remember Rahab and Babylon, those who know me: indeed, Philistia and Tyre, and Cush too—this one was born there. ⁵ And to Zion it will be said, "This man and that man were born in her, and the Most High himself founded her." ⁶ The LORD will record in writing the peoples: "This one was born there." Selah. ⁷ And the singers, like those in joyful throngs, all my springs are in you.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "Rahab" is used here as a poetic name for Egypt, though the Hebrew term רַהַב (Rahab) is not explicitly identified as Egypt in every context. The Septuagint renders it as Ῥαὰβ (Rahab), which may be a different cultural reference, but the Hebrew is clear in its symbolic use.
- v. 5: "This man and that man were born in her" reflects the poetic parallelism of the Hebrew. The Septuagint adds "Mother Zion," but this is not in the Hebrew and may be a theological expansion.
- v. 6: "The LORD will record in writing the peoples" follows the Hebrew’s poetic construction, which the Septuagint paraphrases slightly.
