# Psalms 124

¹ A song of ascents, by David. If it had not been for the LORD, let Israel now say— ² If it had not been for the LORD, when people rose up against us, ³ then the living would have devoured us, when their anger burned against us. ⁴ Then the waters would have swept over us, a torrent would have passed over our life. ⁵ Then the torrent would have passed over our life, like the waters of Sheol. ⁶ Praise the LORD! For he did not give us over as prey to their teeth. ⁷ Our life escaped like a bird from the snare of the fowler. The snare was broken, and we escaped. ⁸ Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "the waters of Sheol" reflects a common biblical metaphor for overwhelming, deadly forces. The term "Zidonim" (Zidonites) is not used in the Hebrew but appears in the Septuagint as "the waters of Sheol." I have followed the Hebrew reading "the torrent" (הַמַּיִם הַזֵּֽידוֹנִֽים), which can be interpreted as "the waters of Sheol" as a poetic metaphor.
