# Psalms 38

¹ A psalm of David. To remember. ² O LORD, do not rebuke me in your anger, nor discipline me in your wrath. ³ For your arrows have pierced me, and your hand has pressed down on me. ⁴ There is no healing in my flesh from the presence of your anger, no peace in my bones from my sin. ⁵ For my iniquities have gone over my head, a heavy burden they have become to me. ⁶ My companions have grown faint, my close friends have turned away from my iniquity. ⁷ I am worn out and greatly bowed down; all day I walk around in anguish. ⁸ For my soul is filled with emptiness, and there is no healing in my flesh. ⁹ I am worn out and brought very low; I cry out because of the groaning of my heart. ¹⁰ O LORD, all my longing is before you, and my groaning is not hidden from you. ¹¹ My heart is troubled, my strength has abandoned me, and the light of my eyes, too, is not with me. ¹² My friends and my companions from the near have drawn near and stood, and my neighbors from afar have stood. ¹³ And those who seek my life have plotted, those who seek my harm have spoken falsehoods, all day they have meditated. ¹⁴ But I am like a mute who does not hear, like one whose mouth does not open. ¹⁵ And I have become like a man who does not listen and has no reproof in his mouth. ¹⁶ For to you, O LORD, I have hoped. You will answer, O Lord my God. ¹⁷ For I said, "Lest my enemies rejoice over me, when my foot stumbles, they have grown bold over me." ¹⁸ For I am like a scourge prepared, and my pain is before me always. ¹⁹ For my iniquity I will declare, I will be anxious over my sin. ²⁰ And my enemies are alive and have grown strong over me, and my haters in falsehood have multiplied. ²¹ Those who repay evil for good have conspired against me, because they pursued righteousness. ²² Do not abandon me, O LORD, my God. Do not distance yourself from me. ²³ Haste to my help, O Lord, my salvation.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "To remember" is a translation of "לְהַזְכִּֽיר" (le-hazkir), which is a poetic term for remembrance or meditation, often used in psalms as a title.
- v. 4: "No peace in my bones" is a poetic expression for inner turmoil, common in Hebrew poetry.
- v. 14: "like a mute who does not hear" reflects the Hebrew metaphor of silence and helplessness.
- v. 15: "like a man who does not listen" continues the metaphor of muteness and passivity.
- v. 18: "I am like a scourge prepared" is a metaphor for being ready to be punished or struck down.
- v. 20: "in falsehood" clarifies the nature of the hatred expressed in the psalm.
