# Psalms 28

¹ To You, O LORD, I call, my God. Do not be silent to me, lest You be silent to me and I become like those who descend into the pit. ² Hear the voice of my plea when I call to You, when I raise my hands to the sanctuary of Your presence. ³ Do not drag me away with the wicked, nor count me among evildoers. Those who speak peace to their neighbors, yet harbor evil in their hearts— ⁴ give them according to their works, and according to the wickedness of their deeds. According to the work of their hands, give them; repay them for what they have done. ⁵ For they do not understand the works of the LORD, nor the deeds of His hands. He will tear them down, and they will not be rebuilt. ⁶ Praise be to the LORD, for He has heard the voice of my plea. ⁷ The LORD is my strength and my shield. In Him my heart trusted and I was helped. My heart was lifted up, and I will sing praises to Him. ⁸ The LORD is the strength of His people, the fortress of His salvation, His anointed. ⁹ Save Your people and bless Your inheritance; tend them and lift them up forever.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "Those who speak peace to their neighbors, yet harbor evil in their hearts" — the phrase "רָעָה בִּלְבָבָם" is rendered as "harbor evil in their hearts" to maintain the contrast with outwardly peaceful speech.
- v. 8: "His anointed" — "מְשִׁיחוֹ" is rendered as a possessive form ("His anointed"), not as a title. The context does not suggest a specific reference to a messianic figure.
