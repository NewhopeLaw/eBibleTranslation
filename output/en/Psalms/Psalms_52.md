# Psalms 52

¹ For the Chief Musician. A maskil of David. ² When Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul, saying, David has come to the house of Abimelech. ³ What profit is there in your boasting, O mighty one? The steadfast love of God remains all day long. ⁴ Your tongue plots destruction, like a sharpened razor that works deceit. ⁵ You love evil more than good, falsehood more than speaking righteousness. Selah. ⁶ You love all words of destruction, a deceitful tongue. ⁷ Therefore, God will cast you down forever. He will uproot you and remove you from your tent, and your root from the land of the living. Selah. ⁸ Then the righteous will see and fear, and laugh upon him. ⁹ Behold, the man who does not set God as his stronghold, but trusts in the abundance of his riches and strengthens himself in his own destruction. ¹⁰ But I am like an olive tree in God’s house. I have put my trust in the steadfast love of God forever, and for evermore. ¹¹ I will praise you forever, for you have done it. I will wait for your name, because it is good, in the presence of your faithful ones.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "The steadfast love of God remains all day long" — the Hebrew לְכָל־הַיּֽוֹם ("for all the day") is rendered to match the poetic contrast with the transient deceit of the wicked.
- v. 5: "Selah" — retained in the translation as a poetic or musical marker, as is customary.
- v. 9: "Behold, the man who does not set God as his stronghold" — the Hebrew לֹא־יָשִׂים אֱלֹהִים מָֽעוּזּוֹ is translated to read naturally in English while preserving the metaphor of divine refuge.
- v. 10: "I have put my trust in the steadfast love of God forever, and for evermore" — the doubling of the phrase (עוֹלָם וָעֶֽד) is rendered to emphasize the enduring nature of the psalmist's hope.
