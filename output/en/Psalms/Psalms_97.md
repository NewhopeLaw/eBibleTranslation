# Psalms 97

¹ The LORD is king. Let the earth rejoice, let many isles be glad. ² Cloud and gloom surround him, righteousness and judgment are the foundation of his throne. ³ Fire precedes him, and it burns around his enemies. ⁴ His lightning lights up the world; the earth sees and trembles. ⁵ The mountains melt like wax before the LORD, before the Lord of the whole earth. ⁶ Declare the heavens his righteousness, and let all the peoples see his glory. ⁷ Let all who make idols be put to shame, all who boast in false gods. Let every god bow to him. ⁸ Zion heard and rejoiced, and the daughters of Judah were glad because of your judgments, O LORD. ⁹ For you are the LORD most high over all the earth. You are exalted far above all gods. ¹⁰ Let lovers of the LORD hate what is evil. The LORD protects the lives of his loyal ones; he rescues them from the hand of the wicked. ¹¹ Light is sown for the righteous, and for those upright in heart there is joy. ¹² Rejoice, you righteous, in the LORD, and give thanks to his holy name.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "it burns around his enemies" — the Hebrew phrase תְּלַהֵט סָבִיב ("it burns around") is interpreted as fire burning around his enemies.
- v. 7: "let every god bow to him" — the Hebrew uses "all gods" (כָּל־אֱלֹהִים), which is retained here as a literal reference to deities.
- v. 8: "daughters of Judah" — the plural "daughters" is retained as in the Hebrew (בְּנוֹת יְהוּדָה), though this may be metaphorical for the people.
