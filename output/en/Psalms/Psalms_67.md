# Psalms 67

¹ For the choirmaster. To the tune of "Mizmor." A song. A psalm. ² May God be gracious to us and bless us. May he shine his face upon us. Selah. ³ May the nations learn your way, and all the earth know your salvation. ⁴ Let the nations give praise to you, O God; let all the nations give praise to you. ⁵ Let the nations rejoice and sing for joy, for you judge the nations with fairness, and guide all the peoples on the earth. Selah. ⁶ Let the nations give praise to you, O God; let all the nations give praise to you. ⁷ The land has given its produce. May God, our God, bless us. ⁸ May God bless us, and may all the ends of the earth fear him.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "To the tune of 'Mizmor'" — "Mizmor" is the Hebrew word for "psalm"; used here as the title of a musical piece.  
- v. 5: "for you judge the nations with fairness" — "mishor" is literally "straightness" or "levelness," here rendered as "fairness."
