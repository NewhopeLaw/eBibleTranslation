# Psalms 76

¹ To the Chief Musician, with stringed instruments; a psalm of Asaph, a song. ² God is known in Judah; his name is great in Israel. ³ His dwelling was in peace, and his tent was in Zion. ⁴ There he broke the might of the bow, shield and sword and war. There he shattered the horns. Selah. ⁵ You shine, exalted above the eternal mountains. ⁶ The hearts of the mighty were struck with fear; their sleep was broken. None among the mighty found their hands. ⁷ From your rebuke, God of Jacob, both horse and rider were thrown into confusion. ⁸ You are awesome; who can stand before your anger? ⁹ From heaven you announced judgment; the earth feared and was still. ¹⁰ When God arose to judgment, to save all the humble in the land. Selah. ¹¹ For the intent of man will honor you; the leftover intent will be a feast for you. ¹² Make vows and repay to the LORD your God; let all around him bring gifts. ¹³ He who silences the breath of rulers is terrible to the kings of the earth.

---

Notes
- v. 11: "the leftover intent will be a feast for you" reflects the poetic balance of "the intent of man will honor you" and the Hebrew's play on the root of נֶדֶר (vow/intent) and its remainder (שְׁאֵרִית). The reference to "feast" (חֵמֹת) is a poetic rendering of the term, which can also mean "offering" or "gift."
