# Psalms 114

¹ When Israel went forth from Egypt, from the house of Jacob among a people of strangers, ² Judah became his sanctuary, Israel his dominion. ³ The sea saw and fled, the Jordan turned back. ⁴ The mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs of the flock. ⁵ What ails you, O sea, that you flee? O Jordan, why do you turn back? ⁶ O mountains, why do you skip like rams? ⁷ Before the face of the Lord the earth quaked, before the face of the God of Jacob. ⁸ You who turned the rock into a pool of water, the flint into a spring of water.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "Judah became his sanctuary" reflects the singular use of "קָדְשׁוֹ" as "his sanctuary" rather than a people or object.
- v. 7: "Before the face of the Lord" preserves the Hebrew structure and poetic intensity.
