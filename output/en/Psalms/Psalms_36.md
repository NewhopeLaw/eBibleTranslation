# Psalms 36

¹ For the music director. Of the servant of the LORD, of David. ² The wicked declare: "Sin is in my heart. There is no fear of God before my eyes." ³ For he has plotted deceit in his sight to find his guilt to hate. ⁴ The words of his mouth are lawlessness and deceit; he has not learned to do good. ⁵ He plans evil on his bed, stands ready on every evil path, and does not despise wickedness. ⁶ The LORD, your steadfast love is in the heavens; your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ⁷ Your righteousness is like the mountains of God; your judgments are like the deep, vast. The LORD will save both man and beast. ⁸ How precious is your steadfast love, O God! All people take refuge in the shadow of your wings. ⁹ They are satisfied with the abundance of your house, and you water them with the stream of your delights. ¹⁰ For with you is the source of life; in your light, we see light. ¹¹ Extend your steadfast love to those who know you, and your righteousness to the upright in heart. ¹² Let not the foot of the arrogant come near to me, and let not the hand of the wicked move me. ¹³ There have fallen all the workers of iniquity, driven out and unable to rise.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "The wicked declare" — the Hebrew construction פֹּהֵ֥שׁ is used in a direct quotation, hence the shift to quotation marks.
- v. 3: "to find his guilt to hate" — the phrase לִמְצֹֽא עֲוֺנוֹ לִשְׂנֹֽא is rendered to reflect the sequence of finding and then hating.
- v. 13: "driven out and unable to rise" — the Hebrew uses a poetic parallelism with "fell" and "driven out" to reinforce the idea of complete downfall.
