# Psalms 55

¹ For the Chief Musician. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David. ² Listen, God, to my prayer, and do not be deaf to my plea. ³ Pay attention to me, and answer me. I am troubled in my speech and moaning. ⁴ Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked, for they have turned their minds against me and in anger they have persecuted me. ⁵ My heart is pained within me, and the terrors of death have fallen upon me. ⁶ Fear and trembling have come upon me, and the dread has covered me. ⁷ And I said, "Oh, that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and rest." ⁸ Lo, I would flee far off, I would wander alone in the wilderness. Selah. ⁹ I would find refuge from the windstorm, from the tempest. ¹⁰ Lord, let their tongues be put to shame, for I have seen violence and strife in the city. ¹¹ Day and night they encircle it, upon its walls, and there is iniquity and toil within it. ¹² And there is deceit, and it does not cease from its streets; treachery and strife are in its midst. ¹³ For had it been an enemy who insulted me, I could endure it; had it been my adversary who magnified himself over me, I could hide from him. ¹⁴ But you, O man of equal spirit, my guide and companion, ¹⁵ who once shared sweet counsel with me, who walked together in the house of God in harmony. ¹⁶ Let death come upon them, let the living descend to Sheol, for there is evil among their dwellings, in their midst. ¹⁷ I will call upon God, and the Lord will save me. ¹⁸ Evening and morning and at noon I will speak and declare, and He will hear my voice. ¹⁹ He will redeem my soul in peace from those who approach me, for there were many with me. ²⁰ Let God hear and bring them down, the One who has been from of old. Selah. For they have no change, and they do not fear God. ²¹ They have stretched out their hands in peace, breaking their covenant. ²² Their mouths were full of flattery; their hearts were deceitful. Their words were smoother than oil, yet they are arrows. ²³ Cast your burden upon the Lord, and He will sustain you; He will not let the righteous be shaken forever. ²⁴ But you, O God, will bring them down into the pit of destruction. Men of blood and deceit shall not have their days divided in half. But I will put my trust in You, O Lord.

---

Notes
- v. 15: "sweet counsel" reflects the meaning of "pleasant covenant" (מַמְתִיק סוֹד), but "covenant" is not the best fit in this context. "Counsel" is more natural.
- v. 22: "arrows" refers to the Hebrew word פְּתִחֽוֹת (pethachoth), meaning "arrows" or "darts," which is retained.
- v. 23: The phrase "Cast your burden upon the Lord" renders the original "הַשְׁלֵךְ עַל־יְהוָה" in a natural, contemporary idiom.
