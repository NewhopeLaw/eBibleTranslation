# Psalms 73

¹ A Psalm by Asaph: Truly, God is good to Israel, to those whose hearts are upright. ² But as for me, my feet nearly slipped, my steps had nearly gone astray. ³ For I envied the arrogant, seeing the peace of the wicked. ⁴ For there is no anguish in their death, and their body is sound and at ease. ⁵ They are not in trouble like others, nor are they punished like the rest of humanity. ⁶ Therefore, their pride surrounds them, and they wrap themselves in iniquity and wrong. ⁷ Their eyes glisten with fatness, their hearts overflow with thoughts. ⁸ They scoff and speak evil; they threaten from on high. ⁹ Their mouths are set like the heavens, and their tongues roam the earth. ¹⁰ Therefore, their people return to confusion, and their days are filled with abundance for them. ¹¹ And they say, How can God know? Is there knowledge in the Most High? ¹² Behold, these are the wicked, yet they live in peace, having gained power. ¹³ I have indeed declared my innocence in vain, washed my hands in innocence. ¹⁴ I have been afflicted all day long, and your correction has come at dawn. ¹⁵ If I had said, I will speak thus, I would have betrayed your children. ¹⁶ I thought to understand this, but it was a burden to me. ¹⁷ Until I entered the sanctuary of God, I came to understand their end. ¹⁸ Indeed, you have set them on slippery ground; you have cast them down to ruin. ¹⁹ How suddenly were they destroyed! They vanished as a dream when one awakens. ²⁰ Like a dream when one awakens, O Lord, when you look upon them, their image vanishes. ²¹ For my heart was stirred with envy, my inner being was shaken. ²² I have become brutish and did not understand; I was like a beast with you. ²³ Yet I am always with you, you have taken my right hand. ²⁴ You will guide me with your counsel and afterward receive me with glory. ²⁵ What do I have in heaven besides you? And apart from you, I desire nothing on earth. ²⁶ My flesh and heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever. ²⁷ For surely, those who are far from you will perish; you have destroyed all who are unfaithful to you. ²⁸ But as for me, it is good to be near God. I have made the Lord my refuge; I will tell of all your works in the gates of the daughter of Zion.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "Behold, these are the wicked..." — the Hebrew uses the plural form רְשָׁעִים ("wicked") and שַׁלְוֵי עוֹלָם ("at peace forever"), which are rendered directly.
- v. 17: "Until I entered the sanctuary of God" — the phrase מִקְדְּשֵׁי־אֵל is plural, but the singular is used here for poetic clarity.
- v. 25: "What do I have in heaven besides you?" — the Hebrew uses מִי־לִי בַשָּׁמָיִם ("what is with me in heaven?"), which is translated idiomatically to retain natural English flow.
