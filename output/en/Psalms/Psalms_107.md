# Psalms 107

¹ Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love is eternal. ² Let those who are rescued by the LORD say, who he has redeemed from the hand of the enemy. ³ From the lands he gathered them, from east and west, from north and sea. ⁴ They wandered in the desert, in a dry place. They did not find a path to a city, a place to dwell. ⁵ Hungry and thirsty, their life within them gave out. ⁶ And they cried out to the LORD in their distress, and he rescued them from their afflictions. ⁷ And he guided them along a straight path to walk to a city, a place to dwell. ⁸ Let them give thanks to the LORD for his steadfast love, and his wondrous deeds to the children of humanity. ⁹ For he filled the soul that was empty, and the soul that was hungry he filled with good. ¹⁰ Dwellers in darkness and shadow of death, prisoners in affliction and in iron. ¹¹ For they had scorned the words of God, and the counsel of the Most High they had rejected. ¹² And their heart was humbled in toil, they stumbled and had no strength. ¹³ And they cried out to the LORD in their distress, and he rescued them from their afflictions. ¹⁴ He brought them out from darkness and shadow of death, and he broke their chains. ¹⁵ Let them give thanks to the LORD for his steadfast love, and his wondrous deeds to the children of humanity. ¹⁶ For he broke the bars of bronze, and the iron bolts he shattered. ¹⁷ Fools turned aside from their way, and because of their iniquities they were afflicted. ¹⁸ Every food their soul despised, and they came near to the gates of death. ¹⁹ And they cried out to the LORD in their distress, and he rescued them from their afflictions. ²⁰ He sent his word and healed them, and he rescued them from their destruction. ²¹ Let them give thanks to the LORD for his steadfast love, and his wondrous deeds to the children of humanity. ²² And let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his works with song. ²³ Those who go down to the sea in ships, doing labor in the great waters. ²⁴ They saw the works of the LORD and his wondrous deeds in the deep. ²⁵ He spoke, and he raised a stormy wind, and the waves of it were lifted up. ²⁶ They rose to the heavens, they fell to the depths. Their life was in danger, their soul was tossed. ²⁷ They were tossed about, they swayed like a drunkard, and all their wisdom was wasted. ²⁸ And they cried out to the LORD in their distress, and he rescued them from their afflictions. ²⁹ He made the storm cease, and the waves of the sea were hushed. ³⁰ And they rejoiced because they were quieted. He brought them to the harbor of their desire. ³¹ Let them give thanks to the LORD for his steadfast love, and his wondrous deeds to the children of humanity. ³² And let them exalt him in the assembly of people, and in the gathering of elders let them praise him. ³³ He made rivers into a desert, and the outlets of water into a dry land. ³⁴ A fruitful land into a salt waste, because of the wickedness of those who dwelled there. ³⁵ He made a desert into a pool of water, and a dry land into springs of water. ³⁶ And he placed there hungry ones, and they built a city to dwell in. ³⁷ And they sowed fields, and planted vineyards, and made fruit for their harvest. ³⁸ And he blessed them, and they multiplied greatly, and their livestock did not diminish. ³⁹ And they were diminished and afflicted, through the oppression of wickedness and sorrow. ⁴⁰ He cast a curse on the nobles, and led them astray in a barren way, without a path. ⁴¹ And he rescued the poor from affliction, and made their families as flocks. ⁴² The upright will see and rejoice, and all iniquity will shut its mouth. ⁴³ Who is the wise and will keep these things, and understand the steadfast love of the LORD?

---

Notes
- v. 40: "He cast a curse on the nobles" reflects the poetic inversion of justice, where the mighty suffer due to their wrongdoing.
- v. 43: "Who is the wise and will keep these things" — the rhetorical question invites reflection on the lessons of divine steadfast love and human need for gratitude and humility.
