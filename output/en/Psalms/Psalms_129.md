# Psalms 129

¹ A song of ascents. Many have oppressed me from my youth, says Israel. ² Many have oppressed me from my youth, yet they did not prevail against me. ³ On my back they laid burdens, they stretched out their iniquity. ⁴ The LORD is righteous; he has cut off the necks of the wicked. ⁵ Let all who hate Zion be put to shame and driven back. ⁶ Let them be like the roof-dryer's stubble, before the sun scorches it. ⁷ Let the one who reaps not fill his hands, nor the one who gathers sheaves fill his cloak. ⁸ And let those who pass by say, "The blessing of the LORD be upon you, we bless you in the name of the LORD."

---

Notes
- v. 3: "laid burdens" is a contextual rendering of חָרְשׁוּ (charashu), literally "they dug" or "they pierced." The metaphor of burdens laid on the back is a poetic way to express oppression.
- v. 4: "cut off the necks" is a poetic and vivid way of expressing judgment or defeat. The Hebrew קִצֵּץ (kitsetz) literally means "to cut off" and may refer to the severing of the head or neck.
