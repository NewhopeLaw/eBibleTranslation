# Psalms 18

¹ To the choirmaster. A song of David, the servant of the LORD, which he spoke to the LORD on the day the LORD rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. ² And he said: "I love you, O LORD, my strength. ³ The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my savior, my God, my fortress where I take refuge, my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold. ⁴ I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised, and I will be saved from my enemies. ⁵ The cords of death entangled me, and the torrents of destruction overwhelmed me. ⁶ The cords of Sheol surrounded me; the snares of death confronted me. ⁷ In my distress I called upon the LORD, and to my God I cried for help. From his holy dwelling he heard my voice, and my cry to him reached his ears. ⁸ Then the earth quaked and trembled; the foundations of the mountains shook and were disturbed, because he was angry. ⁹ Smoke rose from his nostrils; fire from his mouth devoured; coals blazed out of him. ¹⁰ He bowed the heavens and came down; thick darkness was under his feet. ¹¹ He rode on a cherub and flew; he soared on the wings of the wind. ¹² He made darkness his canopy around him, his canopy of dark waters, thick clouds of the skies. ¹³ From the radiance before him, his clouds passed by, hailstones and coals of fire. ¹⁴ The LORD thundered in the heavens, and the Most High uttered his voice, hailstones and coals of fire. ¹⁵ He sent out his arrows and scattered them, with lightning he struck them down. ¹⁶ The channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were uncovered, by the rebuke of the LORD, by the blast of the breath of his nostrils. ¹⁷ He sent from above, he took me; he drew me out of many waters. ¹⁸ He rescued me from my mighty enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me. ¹⁹ They confronted me on the day of my distress, but the LORD was my support. ²⁰ He brought me out into a broad place; he rescued me, because he delighted in me. ²¹ The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; he repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands. ²² For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and I have not turned aside from my God. ²³ For all his statutes were before me, and I did not put his rules away from me. ²⁴ And I was blameless before him and kept myself from iniquity. ²⁵ And the LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his sight. ²⁶ With the righteous you show yourself righteous, and with the blameless you show yourself blameless. ²⁷ With the pure you show yourself pure, and with the crooked you show yourself shrewd. ²⁸ For you save the humble, but your eyes are on the haughty to bring them low. ²⁹ For you are my lamp, O LORD; and the LORD will light my darkness. ³⁰ For by you I run against a troop; by my God I leap over a wall. ³¹ The God who is perfect in his ways, whose words are pure, who is a shield for all who take refuge in him. ³² For who is God except the LORD? And who is a rock except our God? ³³ The God who girds me with strength, and makes my way perfect. ³⁴ He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me secure on the heights. ³⁵ He trains my hands for battle, and my arms can bend a bow of bronze. ³⁶ You have given me the shield of your salvation, and your right hand has held me up, and your gentleness has made me great. ³⁷ You have enlarged my steps beneath me, and my feet did not slip. ³⁸ I will pursue my enemies and overtake them; I will not turn back until they are destroyed. ³⁹ I will strike them down; I will crush them, and they will not rise again. They will fall beneath my feet. ⁴⁰ You have girded me with strength for battle; you made those who rise against me sink under me. ⁴¹ You have made my enemies turn their backs to me, and those who hate me to perish. ⁴² They cried, but there was none to save; to the LORD, but he did not answer them. ⁴³ I will beat them as fine as dust before the wind; I will scatter them like dirt in the streets. ⁴⁴ You will rescue me from the strife of the people; you will make me the head of the nations. A people I do not know will serve me. ⁴⁵ As soon as they hear of me, they will obey me; the foreigners will cower before me. ⁴⁶ The foreigners will fade away and come to an end, for they have not learned my might. ⁴⁷ The LORD lives, and blessed be my rock, and exalted be the God of my salvation. ⁴⁸ The God who gives me vengeance and subdues peoples under me. ⁴⁹ He rescued me from my enemies. You exalted me above those who rose against me; you saved me from the violent man. ⁵⁰ Therefore I will praise you, O LORD, among the nations, and to your name I will sing. ⁵¹ He who gives great salvation to his king and shows steadfast love to his anointed, to David and his offspring forever.

Notes
- v. 31: "perfect in his ways" — the Hebrew is "תָּמִים דַּרְכּוֹ", literally "perfect are his ways". The translation preserves the poetic structure and meaning.
- v. 36: "your gentleness has made me great" — the Hebrew is "עַנְוַתְךָ תַרְבֵּנִי", which can be translated as "your humility" or "your gentleness". The term "עַנְוָה" (anavah) can mean meekness, humility, or gentleness, and here is rendered as "gentleness" to preserve its positive connotation.
- v. 43: "I will beat them as fine as dust before the wind" — the Hebrew is "לְעָפָר עַל־פְּנֵי־רוּחַ", which uses poetic language to convey complete destruction.
