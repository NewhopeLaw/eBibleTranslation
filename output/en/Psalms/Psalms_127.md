# Psalms 127

¹ A song of ascents. If the LORD does not build a house, in vain have its builders labored on it. If the LORD does not guard a city, in vain does the watchman keep vigil. ² In vain is it for you to rise early, to stay awake until late, for those who eat the bread of toil — the LORD gives sleep to those he loves. ³ Behold, the children are a heritage from the LORD, the reward of the womb. ⁴ Like arrows in the hand of a warrior, so are the children of one’s youth. ⁵ Happy is the man whose quiver is full of them — they will not be put to shame when they speak with their enemies at the gate.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: The conditional clause "If the LORD does not..." is rendered as a dependent clause to reflect the Hebrew structure and meaning.  
- v. 2: "The bread of toil" is a literal translation of the Hebrew, and the reference to "those he loves" is consistent with the use of *ahav* in the passage.  
- v. 4: "Children of one’s youth" is a natural interpretation of *benot ha-ne'urim*, which combines the idea of youth and offspring.  
- v. 5: "Quiver is full of them" reflects the metaphor of children as arrows, suggesting strength and readiness.
