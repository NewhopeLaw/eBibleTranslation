# Psalms 20

¹ For the music director. A psalm of David. ² May the LORD answer you in the day of distress; may the name of the God of Jacob protect you. ³ May he send help from his holy dwelling and support you from Zion. ⁴ May he remember all your offerings and accept your burnt offerings. Selah. ⁵ May he grant you what you desire and fulfill all your plans. ⁶ We will rejoice in your salvation, and in the name of our God we will exult. May the LORD fulfill all your requests. ⁷ Now I know that the LORD has saved his anointed one; he answers him from his holy sky, with mighty strength from his right hand. ⁸ Some are on horses and some on chariots, but we will call on the name of the LORD our God. ⁹ They collapse and fall, but we rise up and are steadfast. ¹⁰ LORD, save the king; answer us when we call.

---

Notes  
- v. 4: "Selah" is preserved as in the text.  
- v. 7: "his holy sky" reflects "שְׁמֵי קָדְשׁוֹ" as "heaven of his holiness" or "holy sky."  
- v. 8: "on horses and on chariots" is a standard way of describing military might; the contrast with calling on the LORD is intentional.  
- v. 10: "the king" is not explicitly named, so the term is used directly as in the Hebrew.
