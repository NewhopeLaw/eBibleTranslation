# Psalms 8

¹ For the chief musician, on the lyre. A psalm by David. ² LORD our Master, how majestic is your name over all the earth, for your glory is raised above the heavens. ³ From the mouths of infants and nursing babies you have established strength, for the sake of your adversaries, to silence the enemy and the avenger. ⁴ When I look at the heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars, which you have set in place, ⁵ what is the human that you remember him, or the son of man that you take notice of him? ⁶ You have made him only a little lower than the divine, and with glory and majesty you have crowned him. ⁷ You have set him over the works of your hands; you have placed all things under his feet, ⁸ sheep and oxen, all of them, and also the beasts of the field, ⁹ the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea, whatever passes along the paths of the seas. ¹⁰ LORD our Master, how majestic is your name over all the earth.

---

Notes
- v. 6: The Hebrew term "אֱלֹהִים" is translated here as "the divine" to avoid confusion with the name of the God of Israel, while preserving the poetic contrast in the original text.
- v. 7: "תַּמְשִׁילֵהוּ" is translated as "You have set him" to reflect the poetic voice rather than a literal "rule over" or "govern," which might mislead the reader.
