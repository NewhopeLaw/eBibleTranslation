# Psalms 131

¹ A song of ascents by David. O LORD, my heart has not grown lofty, nor have my eyes looked up haughtily, nor have I walked in great or wondrous things beyond myself. ² If I had not calmed my soul, like a weaned child on its mother—like a weaned child, my soul lies on me. ³ Let Israel hope in the LORD from now and forever.

---

Notes
- v. 2: The metaphor of the weaned child is rendered as "like a weaned child on its mother" to maintain clarity and poetic resonance. The phrase "lies on me" reflects the Hebrew's reflexive construction and the sense of inner stillness.
