# Psalms 29

¹ A psalm of David. O you sons of the mighty, give to the LORD, give to the LORD glory and strength. ² Give to the LORD the glory of His name; worship the LORD in the splendor of holiness. ³ The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory has thundered— the LORD over the mighty waters. ⁴ The voice of the LORD is in power, the voice of the LORD in majesty. ⁵ The voice of the LORD shatters cedars; the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon, ⁶ and makes them leap like a calf of Lebanon, like a young bull among the forest. ⁷ The voice of the LORD splits the flames of fire. ⁸ The voice of the LORD convulses the wilderness; the LORD convulses the wilderness of Kadesh. ⁹ The voice of the LORD causes the deer to calve; the LORD strips the forests bare, and in His temple all say, "Glory!" ¹⁰ The LORD sits enthroned over the flood; the LORD sits as king forever. ¹¹ The LORD gives strength to His people; the LORD blesses His people with peace.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "sons of the mighty" (בְּנֵי אֵלִים) — a poetic designation for divine or exalted beings, often translated as "sons of God" but here rendered more naturally in context.
- v. 8: "wilderness of Kadesh" (מִדְבַּר קָדֵשׁ) — Kadesh is a known geographic location in the wilderness; it is treated as a proper name here.
