# Psalms 150

¹ Praise the LORD! Praise God in His holy place. Praise Him in the vault of His might. ² Praise Him for His mighty acts. Praise Him for His abundant greatness. ³ Praise Him with the blast of a horn. Praise Him with the lyre and the harp. ⁴ Praise Him with the tambourine and the dance. Praise Him with stringed instruments and flutes. ⁵ Praise Him with resounding cymbals. Praise Him with thunderous cymbals. ⁶ Let everything that breathes praise the LORD. Praise the LORD!

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the vault of His might" reflects the meaning of "בִּרְקִיעַ עֻזּֽוֹ" as "in the vault of His might" — a poetic reference to the heavens as God’s domain.
- v. 5: "resounding" and "thunderous" are used to distinguish the two forms of cymbals mentioned in the Hebrew text.
