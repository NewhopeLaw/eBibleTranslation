# Psalms 126

¹ A song of ascents. When the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion, we were like those who dream. ² Then our mouths were filled with laughter, and our tongues with songs of joy. Then it was said among the nations, "The LORD has done great things for them." ³ The LORD has done great things for us; we were glad. ⁴ Restore our fortunes, O LORD, like the streams in the Negev. ⁵ Those who sow in tears will reap with songs of joy. ⁶ Those who go forth weeping, carrying seed for sowing, will come back with songs of joy, carrying their sheaves.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "restored the fortunes of Zion" translates שׁוּב שִׁיבַת צִיּוֹן (shuv shivat Tzion), a poetic expression for the return from exile.
- v. 4: "streams in the Negev" translates כַּאֲפִיקִים בַּנֶּֽגֶב (ka'afiqim ba'negav), referring to sudden torrents of rain in the arid Negev region, symbolizing a miraculous renewal.
