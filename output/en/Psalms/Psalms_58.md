# Psalms 58

¹ For the Chief Musician. Do not destroy. A maskil of David. ² Do you speak of justice, people of upright judgment? Do you judge honestly, children of men? ³ Yet in their hearts they plot evil. On earth their hands weave violence. ⁴ The wicked are estranged from the womb. They stray from the womb, uttering lies. ⁵ Their rage is like the serpent. Like a deaf adder that stops its ear, ⁶ which does not listen to the voice of those who speak a poison, a potion, mixed by a wise one. ⁷ Let God crush their teeth in their mouth. Let the LORD shatter the grinding teeth of the lions. ⁸ Let them be like water that flows away. Let their arrows be scattered as they fall. ⁹ Let them be like a snared bird, snatched in a net. Let fire fall upon them, and they see no sun. ¹⁰ Before they realize your thorns, they are like green briers, like burning thorns consumed in a moment. ¹¹ The righteous will rejoice when he sees vengeance. The upright will wash their hands in the blood of the wicked. ¹² And people will say, "Indeed, there is fruit for the righteous. Indeed, there is a God who judges in the earth."

---

Notes
- v. 11: "The righteous will rejoice when he sees vengeance" — the Hebrew uses נָקָם (nakam), often translated as "vengeance" or "revenge," which is retained here for clarity and fidelity.
- v. 12: "Indeed, there is fruit for the righteous" — the translation of פְּרִי (peri) as "fruit" is literal, reflecting its metaphorical use in the psalm.
