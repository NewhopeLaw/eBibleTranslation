# Psalms 115

¹ Not to us, O LORD, not to us, but to your name give glory because of your steadfast love and because of your faithfulness. ² Why should the nations say, "Where is their God now?" ³ Our God is in the heavens; he does all that he wills. ⁴ Their images are silver and gold, the work of human hands. ⁵ They have mouths, but do not speak; they have eyes, but do not see. ⁶ They have ears, but do not hear; they have noses, but do not smell. ⁷ They have hands, but do not touch; they have feet, but do not walk; they do not make a sound in their throat. ⁸ Those who make them are like them, and so are all who trust in them. ⁹ O Israel, trust in the LORD; he is their help and their shield. ¹⁰ O House of Aaron, trust in the LORD; he is your help and your shield. ¹¹ You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD; he is your help and your shield. ¹² The LORD will remember us and will bless us; he will bless the House of Israel and bless the House of Aaron. ¹³ He will bless those who fear the LORD, the small ones along with the great. ¹⁴ May the LORD add to you, to you and to your children. ¹⁵ You are blessed by the LORD, who made the heavens and the earth. ¹⁶ The heavens belong to the LORD, but the earth he has given to the children of men. ¹⁷ The dead do not praise the LORD, nor do any who go down into silence. ¹⁸ But we will bless the LORD from this time onward and forevermore. Hallelujah!

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Not to us" is a liturgical refrain acknowledging divine action, not human merit.
- v. 3: The contrast between the divine and the idols is emphasized by the poetic juxtaposition.
- v. 8: "Those who make them are like them" underscores the futility of idolatry.
- v. 17: "The dead do not praise the LORD" echoes a common motif in wisdom and liturgical texts.
