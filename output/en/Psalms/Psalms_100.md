# Psalms 100

¹ A psalm for giving thanks. Shout to the LORD, all the earth. ² Serve the LORD with joy. Come before his presence with singing. ³ Know this: the LORD is the God who made us, and we are his people, the flock of his pasture. ⁴ Enter his gates with thanks, his courts with praise. Give thanks to him, bless his name. ⁵ For the LORD is good forever. His steadfast love endures from generation to generation.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "A psalm for giving thanks" reflects the technical meaning of "mizmor letodah."
- v. 5: "His steadfast love" and "his faithfulness" are standard renderings of chesed and emunah in poetic context.
