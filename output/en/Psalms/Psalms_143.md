# Psalms 143

¹ A psalm of David. O LORD, hear my prayer; give ear to my plea in your faithfulness, answer me in your righteousness. ² And do not bring judgment upon your servant, for no living soul is righteous before you. ³ For my enemy has crushed my life to the ground. He has made me sit in darkness like the dead of old. ⁴ My spirit is wrapped around me; my heart is troubled within me. ⁵ I have remembered days of old, I have meditated on all your works, on the works of your hands I have pondered. ⁶ I have stretched out my hands to you; my soul, like a weary land, waits for you. Selah. ⁷ O LORD, answer me quickly, my spirit fails. Do not hide your face from me, or I will be like those descending into the pit. ⁸ Let your steadfast love be heard by me in the morning, for in you I have trusted. Show me the path I must walk, for to you I have lifted up my soul. ⁹ Deliver me, O LORD, from my enemies. To you I have taken refuge. ¹⁰ Teach me to do your will, for you are my God. Your good spirit will guide me on even ground. ¹¹ For the sake of your name, O LORD, preserve my life in your righteousness. Bring my soul out of distress. ¹² And by your steadfast love you will destroy my enemies, and you will make all who afflict my life perish, for I am your servant.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "Selah" is retained as an untranslated musical or liturgical marker, as is traditional in psalmic texts.
- v. 10: "good spirit" is chosen over "holy spirit" to align with the context of divine guidance and the use of "ruach" in the Hebrew.
