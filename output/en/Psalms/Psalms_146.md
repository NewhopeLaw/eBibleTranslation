# Psalms 146

¹ Praise the LORD, O my soul. ² I will praise the LORD as long as I live; I will sing my praises to my God while I still draw breath. ³ Do not put your trust in princes, in a son of man, in whom there is no salvation. ⁴ His spirit departs, he returns to his earth; on that day all his plans come to nothing. ⁵ Happy is the one whose help is the LORD, whose hope is in the LORD his God. ⁶ He who made the heavens and the earth, the sea and all that is in them—faithfulness is His covenant forever. ⁷ He who brings justice to the oppressed, who gives bread to the hungry— the LORD releases the imprisoned. ⁸ The LORD opens the eyes of the blind, the LORD lifts up those who are bowed down; the LORD loves the righteous. ⁹ The LORD watches over the strangers; He upholds the orphan and the widow, but He causes the path of the wicked to be destroyed. ¹⁰ The LORD shall reign forever; your God, O Zion, from generation to generation—praise the LORD.

---

Notes  
- v. 10: "Zion" is used in the original as a poetic address to the people of God, and not as a literal city.
