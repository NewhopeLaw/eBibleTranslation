# Psalms 146

¹ Praise the LORD, O my soul. I will praise the LORD as long as I live; I will sing praises to my God as long as I am. ² Do not place trust in princes, in a son of humanity, for there is no salvation in him. ³ His breath departs, he returns to his earth; on that very day, all his plans perish. ⁴ Happy is the one whose help is the LORD, whose hope is in the LORD, his God. ⁵ The one who made heaven and earth, the sea and all that is in them, the one who keeps truth forever, ⁶ the one who does justice for the oppressed, who gives bread to the hungry. The LORD frees the captive, ⁷ the LORD opens the eyes of the blind, the LORD lifts up those who are bowed down. The LORD loves the righteous, ⁸ the LORD protects the foreigners, he takes up the orphan and the widow, but he makes the way of the wicked vanish. ⁹ The LORD will reign forever; your God, O Zion, will reign from generation to generation. Praise the LORD!

---

Notes
- v. 10: The Hebrew text includes a poetic repetition of praise at the end, which is rendered here in natural English with the name "LORD" for YHWH. The line is structured to mirror the opening call to praise.
