# Psalms 142

¹ A contemplation of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer. ² My voice cries out to the LORD; my voice begs the LORD. ³ I pour out my plea before him; I tell him my distress. ⁴ When my spirit is overwhelmed within me, you alone know my path. In this way I walk; they have hidden a trap for me. ⁵ I looked to my right and saw, but no one knew me; there is no one who seeks my life. ⁶ I cried to you, O LORD, and said: "You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living." ⁷ Hear my plea, for I am greatly humbled; deliver me from those who pursue me, for they are too strong for me. ⁸ Bring my soul out from prison, that I may praise your name. Let the righteous surround me, for you will repay me.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "A contemplation" (maskil) is a poetic or didactic psalm. The phrase "when he was in the cave" is a marginal note in the Hebrew and is not part of the actual title structure. It is retained here for clarity.
