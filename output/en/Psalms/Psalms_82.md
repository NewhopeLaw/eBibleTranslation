# Psalms 82

¹ A psalm for Asaph. God stands in the assembly of El, in the midst of the gods, he delivers judgment. ² How long will you judge unjustly? And how long will you show favor to the wicked? Selah. ³ Judge the orphan and the widow; defend the rights of the poor and the oppressed. ⁴ Rescue the poor and the needy; deliver them from the hands of the wicked. ⁵ They do not know, they do not understand; they walk in darkness. All the foundations of the earth are shaken. ⁶ I said, “You are gods, and sons of the Most High, all of you.” ⁷ But you will die like mortals, and fall like any other ruler. ⁸ Rise up, God, and judge the earth, for you are the one who will inherit all the nations.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "in the assembly of El" captures "בַּעֲדַת־אֵל" (in the assembly of El), which is a poetic way of referring to the divine assembly, perhaps as a council of gods or angelic beings. "El" is retained as a title for God here.
- v. 2: The phrase "how long will you judge unjustly" captures the rhetorical question in the Hebrew, emphasizing the ongoing failure of the divine council.
- v. 6: The rendering of "sons of the Most High" is based on the Hebrew "בְּנֵי עֶלְיוֹן", which is taken as "sons of the Most High" rather than "sons of the Most High God", given the context and tone.
- v. 7: "Mortals" is used for "כְּאָדָם" to emphasize the contrast with the divine status mentioned in verse 6.
