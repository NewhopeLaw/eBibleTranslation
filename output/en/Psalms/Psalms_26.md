# Psalms 26

¹ Judge me, O LORD, for I have walked in my innocence, and in the LORD I have trusted; I will not waver. ² Examine me, O LORD, and test me, refine my kidneys and my heart. ³ For your steadfast love is before my eyes, and I have walked in your truth. ⁴ I have not sat with the assembly of emptiness, nor have I entered the company of the secret schemers. ⁵ I have hated the gathering of evildoers, and I will not sit among the wicked. ⁶ I will wash my hands in purity and encircle your altar, O LORD. ⁷ To hear the voice of praise and to declare all your wonders. ⁸ O LORD, I love the beauty of your house and the place where your glory dwells. ⁹ Do not gather my soul together with the wicked, nor my life with men of blood. ¹⁰ Those in whose hands there is mischief, whose right hand is filled with bribes. ¹¹ But I have walked in my innocence; redeem me and be gracious to me. ¹² My foot stands in the straight path; in the assembly I will bless the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "wash my hands in purity" reflects the Hebrew נִקָּיוֹן as a state of cleanness, not a physical act of washing.
- v. 8: "beauty of your house" captures the Hebrew מְעוֹן, which can mean "dwelling" or "beauty of place."
- v. 10: "men of blood" is a conventional rendering for those involved in violent or unjust acts, as implied by the context.
