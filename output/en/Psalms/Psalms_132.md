# Psalms 132

¹ A song of ascents. Remember, LORD, all the vows of David. ² He swore an oath to the LORD, a vow to the Mighty One of Jacob. ³ If I had entered my tent, if I had gone up to my canopy, ⁴ if I had given sleep to my eyes, rest to my eyelids, until ⁵ I found a place for the LORD, a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob. ⁶ We heard it in Ephrathah, we found it in the fields of the forest. ⁷ Let us go to His dwelling; let us prostrate ourselves before the place where His feet stood. ⁸ Rise, LORD, to Your resting place—you and the ark of Your strength. ⁹ Let Your priests be clothed with righteousness, and let Your faithful ones shout for joy. ¹⁰ For the sake of David, Your servant, do not turn away the face of Your anointed. ¹¹ The LORD swore to David a truth He will not revoke: "From the fruit of your womb I will set upon your throne." ¹² If your children keep My covenant and the testimonies I teach them, their children also shall sit on your throne forever. ¹³ For the LORD has chosen Zion; He has loved it as His dwelling. ¹⁴ This is My resting place forever; here I will dwell, for I have desired it. ¹⁵ I will bless its harvest; I will satisfy its poor with bread. ¹⁶ I will clothe its priests with salvation, and its faithful ones shall sing for joy. ¹⁷ There I will make a horn grow for David; I have prepared a lamp for My anointed. ¹⁸ I will clothe His enemies with shame, but on Him My holiness shall shine.

---

Notes
- v. 11: "From the fruit of your womb" reflects the Hebrew "מִפְּרִי בִטְנְךָ" (mi-ph'ri betnekha), which is a poetic way of referring to David’s descendants.
- v. 17: "I have prepared a lamp for My anointed" reflects the metaphor of light for divine presence or guidance.
- v. 18: "My holiness shall shine" is a rendering of "נִזְרֽוֹ" (nitzro), which can mean "crown" or "radiance," here interpreted as a shining or shining glory.
