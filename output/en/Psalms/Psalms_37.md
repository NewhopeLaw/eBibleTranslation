# Psalms 37

¹ Of David. Do not be envious of the wicked, do not covet those who do wrong. ² For they will soon wither like grass, and like springtime greenery they will vanish. ³ Trust in the LORD and do good; dwell in the land and enjoy his faithfulness. ⁴ Rejoice in the LORD, and he will grant your heart's desire. ⁵ Commit your ways to the LORD and put your trust in him; he will bring it to pass. ⁶ He will reveal your righteousness like the light, and your justice like the noonday. ⁷ Be still before the LORD and wait for him; do not be envious of those who thrive in their ways, or of those who plot evil. ⁸ Refrain from anger and abandon wrath; do not be envious, for it leads only to harm. ⁹ For evildoers will be cut off, but the LORD’s people will inherit the land. ¹⁰ In a little while the wicked will be no more; though you look for their place, it will not be found. ¹¹ But the humble will inherit the land and delight in abundant peace. ¹² The wicked plot against the righteous, and gnash their teeth at him. ¹³ The LORD will laugh at them, for he sees the day of their coming. ¹⁴ The wicked have drawn swords and bent their bows to bring down the poor and the afflicted, to kill those who walk uprightly. ¹⁵ Their swords will pierce their own hearts, and their bows will be broken. ¹⁶ Better is a little that the righteous have than the abundance of many wicked. ¹⁷ For the arms of the wicked will be broken, but the LORD supports the righteous. ¹⁸ The LORD knows the days of the blameless, and their inheritance will last forever. ¹⁹ They will not be put to shame in the time of evil, and in the days of famine they will be satisfied. ²⁰ But the wicked will perish, and the enemies of the LORD will be like the splendor of the fields, they will vanish—like smoke they will vanish. ²¹ The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the righteous give generously. ²² For the LORD's blessed ones will inherit the land, but the cursed will be cut off. ²³ The LORD directs the steps of the man who is blameless, and his path is pleasing to him. ²⁴ Though he may fall, he will not be cast down, for the LORD is the one who upholds his hand. ²⁵ I was young, and now I am old, yet I have never seen the righteous forsaken, nor their children begging for bread. ²⁶ All day long they give generously, and their children will be a blessing. ²⁷ Turn away from evil and do good; dwell forever. ²⁸ For the LORD loves justice and will not abandon his faithful ones; they are kept safe forever, but the descendants of the wicked will be cut off. ²⁹ The righteous will inherit the land and dwell in it forever. ³⁰ The mouth of the righteous will speak wisdom, and his tongue will declare justice. ³¹ The law of his God is in his heart; his steps will not slip. ³² The wicked watch the righteous and seek to put him to death. ³³ The LORD will not abandon him to their power, nor will he let him be condemned when he is judged. ³⁴ Wait for the LORD and keep to his way; he will lift you up to inherit the land when the wicked are cut off. ³⁵ I saw a wicked, arrogant man, flourishing like a native cedar. ³⁶ He passed away, and there he was, no more; I looked for him, but he could not be found. ³⁷ Watch the blameless and see the upright, for the destiny of the peaceful is enduring. ³⁸ But the lawbreakers will perish together; the end of the wicked is cut off. ³⁹ The deliverance of the righteous comes from the LORD; in times of trouble he is their refuge. ⁴⁰ The LORD will help them and rescue them; he will deliver them from the wicked and save them, because they have trusted in him.

---

Notes  
- v. 2: "grass" and "springtime greenery" render חָצִיר and יֶרֶק דֶּשֶׁא.  
- v. 3: "dwell in the land" renders שְׁכָן־אֶרֶץ.  
- v. 6: "noonday" renders כַּֽצָּהֳרָֽיִם.  
- v. 7: "wait for him" renders דּוֹם, a unique use in the Psalms.  
- v. 11: "abundant peace" renders רֹב שָׁלֽוֹם.  
- v. 20: "the splendor of the fields" renders כִּיקַר כָּרִים, a rare phrase.  
- v. 23: "the man who is blameless" renders תָּם, often translated as "upright" or "blameless."  
- v. 29: "the righteous will inherit the land" is a repeated refrain in the psalm, rendered consistently.
