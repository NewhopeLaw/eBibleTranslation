# Psalms 47

¹ For the leader. For the sons of Korah. A song. ² All the nations, clap your hands. Shout to God with a voice of joy. ³ For the LORD is the Most High, awesome. A great king over all the earth. ⁴ He has subjected peoples to us, and nations beneath our feet. ⁵ He has chosen our inheritance for us, the beauty of Jacob, whom he loves. Selah. ⁶ God has gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of a horn. ⁷ Sing to God, sing. Sing to our king, sing. ⁸ For God is king over all the earth. Sing wisely. ⁹ God has reigned over the nations. God is seated on his holy throne. ¹⁰ The leaders of the peoples have gathered, the people of God Abraham, for the might of the earth is truly mighty.

---

Notes
- v. 2: “All the nations, clap your hands” is a direct rendering of כָּל־הָעַמִּים תִּקְעוּ־כָף, which uses תִּקְעוּ from the root קָפָה (to strike).
- v. 5: “the beauty of Jacob” is a poetic rendering of אֶת גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב, where גְּאוֹן can mean "beauty," "glory," or "pride." The context suggests "beauty" as a poetic term for inheritance.
- v. 10: “for the might of the earth is truly mighty” reflects the Hebrew’s use of מְאֹד נַעֲלָה, which is a superlative expression.
