# Psalms 118

¹ Give praise to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love is eternal. ² Let Israel say, for his steadfast love is eternal. ³ Let the house of Aaron say, for his steadfast love is eternal. ⁴ Let those who fear the LORD say, for his steadfast love is eternal. ⁵ From my distress I called to the LORD— the LORD answered me in the wide open. ⁶ The LORD is my help. I will not fear what any human may do to me. ⁷ The LORD is my help. I will look down upon my enemies. ⁸ It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in any human. ⁹ It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in mighty nobles. ¹⁰ All the nations surrounded me, but in the name of the LORD I drove them back. ¹¹ They surrounded me, they surrounded me, but in the name of the LORD I drove them back. ¹² They swarmed like bees, they burned like a fire of thorns, but in the name of the LORD I drove them back. ¹³ I was pushed hard to fall, but the LORD upheld me. ¹⁴ The LORD is my strength and my praise, and he has become my salvation. ¹⁵ The voice of joy and salvation is in the tents of the righteous. The right hand of the LORD does mighty deeds. ¹⁶ The right hand of the LORD is raised, the right hand of the LORD does mighty deeds. ¹⁷ I will not die, but I will live, and I will declare the works of the LORD. ¹⁸ The LORD disciplined me, but did not give me over to death. ¹⁹ Open to me the gates of righteousness; I will enter them, to give thanks to the LORD. ²⁰ This is the gate of the LORD— the righteous shall enter through it. ²¹ I will give thanks to you, for you answered me and became my salvation. ²² The stone that the builders rejected has become the chief cornerstone. ²³ This came from the LORD— it is wondrous in our eyes. ²⁴ This is the day the LORD has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it. ²⁵ O LORD, I beg you, save. O LORD, I beg you, grant success. ²⁶ Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the LORD. We bless you from the house of the LORD. ²⁷ God is the LORD, and he has shone upon us. Arrange the feast with branches reaching to the horns of the altar. ²⁸ You are my God, and I will give thanks to you; you are my God, and I will praise you. I will give thanks, for you answered me and became my salvation. ²⁹ Give praise to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love is eternal.

---

Notes  
- v. 29: The final verse mirrors the first and serves as a refrain, emphasizing the eternal steadfast love of the LORD.
