# Psalms 41

¹ To the choirmaster. A psalm of David. ² Happy is the one who shows concern for the poor and the needy; in a day of trouble, the LORD will deliver them. ³ The LORD will protect them and preserve them in the land; He will not give them over to the hand of their enemy. ⁴ The LORD will support them on the bed of affliction; You have turned all their couch away as they lie ill. ⁵ I said, "O LORD, be gracious to me; heal my soul, for I have sinned against You." ⁶ My enemies speak evil about me: "When will he die and his name be forgotten?" ⁷ When someone comes to see me, he speaks idly, his heart gathers up wrongdoing for himself. He goes out and spreads it abroad. ⁸ All my enemies conspire together against me. They plot evil against me. ⁹ They have laid a treacherous charge against me: "The one who lies down will not rise again." ¹⁰ Even the man who was my friend, in whom I trusted, the one who ate my bread, has turned his heel against me. ¹¹ But You, O LORD, be gracious to me and raise me up, and I will repay them. ¹² By this I know that You delight in me: that my enemy does not triumph over me. ¹³ And in my innocence You uphold me, and You place me before You forever. ¹⁴ Praise be to the LORD, the God of Israel, from eternity to eternity. Amen and Amen.

---

Notes  
- v. 10: "the one who ate my bread" reflects the Hebrew idiom for a close companion, not a literal meal.  
- v. 14: "Amen and Amen" is included as in the Hebrew, which uses the doubled form for emphasis.
