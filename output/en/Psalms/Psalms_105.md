# Psalms 105

¹ Give thanks to the LORD. Call upon his name. Make known among the nations his acts. ² Sing to him, make music to him. Tell of all his wondrous deeds. ³ Glorify his holy name. Let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. ⁴ Seek the LORD and his strength. Seek his presence continually. ⁵ Remember the wonders he has done, the miracles and the judgments of his mouth. ⁶ Descendants of Abraham his servant, sons of Jacob his chosen ones. ⁷ He is the LORD our God. His judgments are in all the earth. ⁸ He remembers his covenant forever, the word he commanded for a thousand generations. ⁹ The covenant he made with Abraham, and the oath he swore to Isaac. ¹⁰ He established it as a decree for Jacob, as an eternal covenant for Israel. ¹¹ Saying, "I will give you the land of Canaan, the portion of your inheritance." ¹² When they were few in number, few and strangers in the land. ¹³ They moved from one nation to another, from one kingdom to another people. ¹⁴ He did not allow anyone to mistreat them; he rebuked kings on their behalf. ¹⁵ Do not harm my anointed ones; do not touch my prophets. ¹⁶ He called a famine upon the land; the whole bread supply was broken. ¹⁷ He sent a man before them, Joseph who was sold as a slave. ¹⁸ They afflicted his feet with shackles, his soul went through fire, his life into exile. ¹⁹ Until the time of his word came, the word of the LORD refined him. ²⁰ The king sent and released him, the ruler of nations and freed him. ²¹ He set him over his house, master of all his possessions. ²² To instruct his officials as he pleased, and to teach his elders wisdom. ²³ Then Israel came into Egypt, and Jacob lived as a foreigner in the land of Ham. ²⁴ He greatly increased his people, made them stronger than their enemies. ²⁵ He turned their hearts to hate his people, to act deceitfully toward his servants. ²⁶ He sent Moses his servant and Aaron, whom he had chosen. ²⁷ He gave them signs and miracles in the land of Ham. ²⁸ He sent darkness, and it grew dark, but they did not rebel against his word. ²⁹ He turned their waters into blood, and the fish died. ³⁰ Swarms of frogs rose up in the land, in the chambers of their kings. ³¹ He spoke, and came flies and gnats into all their borders. ³² He gave their rains as hail, fire flashing in their land. ³³ He struck their vines and their fig trees, shattered the trees of their borders. ³⁴ He spoke, and came locusts and grasshoppers, without number. ³⁵ They ate up all the grass in their land, all the fruit of their soil. ³⁶ He struck all their firstborn, the first of all their livestock. ³⁷ He brought them out with silver and gold, with no weak among his tribes. ³⁸ Egypt was glad when they went, for fear had fallen upon them. ³⁹ He spread a cloud for a canopy, and fire to light the night. ⁴⁰ He asked, and brought quail, and filled them with bread from heaven. ⁴¹ He split a rock, and water flowed out, rivers ran in the desert. ⁴² For he remembered his holy word, his promise to Abraham his servant. ⁴³ And he brought his people out with joy, his chosen ones with gladness. ⁴⁴ He gave them the lands of nations, and the toil of peoples they inherited. ⁴⁵ So that they might keep his statutes, and observe his teachings. Praise the LORD!

---

Notes
- v. 17: "Joseph who was sold as a slave" — the Hebrew uses "נִמְכַּר יוֹסֵֽף" which is literally "Joseph was sold."
- v. 18: "his soul went through fire" — "בַּרְזֶל בָּאָה נַפְשֽׁוֹ" is metaphorically rendered to reflect the ordeal.
- v. 19: "the word of the LORD refined him" — the metaphor of fire and refinement is implied by the context.
- v. 22: "to teach his elders wisdom" — "יְחַכֵּֽם" is best rendered as "to teach wisdom."
- v. 44: "and the toil of peoples they inherited" — the Hebrew "עֲמַל לְאֻמִּים" is interpreted as the labor or toil of other nations.
- v. 45: "Praise the LORD" — the Hebrew "הַֽלְלוּ־יָֽהּ" is a poetic form of "Praise the LORD."
