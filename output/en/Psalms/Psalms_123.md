# Psalms 123

¹ A song of ascents. To you I lift up my eyes, the one dwelling in the heavens. ² Look, as the eyes of servants are toward the hand of their master, as the eyes of a servant toward the hand of her mistress, so our eyes are toward the LORD our God, until he shows us mercy. ³ Show us mercy, LORD, show us mercy. For we have had more than enough of contempt. ⁴ Our soul has had more than enough of the scorn from the smug and the contempt from the arrogant.

---

Notes  
- v. 2: The metaphor of eyes toward a master's hand is rendered to preserve the imagery of dependence and expectation.  
- v. 4: "The smug" (עִתְנַנִּים) reflects the connotation of smugness or self-satisfaction; "arrogant" (גְּאֵיוֹנִים) is used for those who are haughty or proud.
