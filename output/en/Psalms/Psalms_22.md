# Psalms 22

¹ For the music director. On the deer of the morning. A psalm of David. ² My God, my God, why have you abandoned me? Far from my salvation are the words of my groaning. ³ My God, I call out by day and you do not answer, and by night and it is not silence for me. ⁴ But you dwell in the holy ones, the praise of Israel. ⁵ Our ancestors put their trust in you, they trusted and you rescued them. ⁶ They cried to you and were saved, they trusted in you and were not put to shame. ⁷ But I am like a worm and not a man, a disgrace of people and despised by the crowd. ⁸ All who see me mock me, they speak with their tongues, they shake their heads. ⁹ Let the Lord rescue him, let him deliver him, because he delights in him. ¹⁰ For you are the one who brought me out from the womb, the one who made me trust, on my mother’s breasts. ¹¹ From the womb I was entrusted to you, from my mother’s womb my God you are. Do not be far from me, for trouble is near and there is no helper. ¹² My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue is stuck to my throat. And you have brought me to the dust of death. ¹³ Many bulls have surrounded me, strong bulls have enclosed me. ¹⁴ They opened their mouths against me, like a lion tearing and roaring. ¹⁵ Like water I was poured out, and all my bones are scattered. My heart is like melting wax in the middle of my entrails. ¹⁶ My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue is stuck to my throat. And you have brought me to the dust of death. ¹⁷ Many dogs have surrounded me, a band of evildoers has enclosed me. They pierced my hands and my feet. ¹⁸ They counted all my bones; they looked on and stared at me. ¹⁹ They divided my garments among themselves, and on my clothing they cast lots. ²⁰ But you, O Lord, do not be far from me. My help, hurry to my rescue. ²¹ Rescue my soul from the sword, my life from the power of the dog. ²² Save me from the mouth of the lion, and from the horns of the wild oxen you have answered me. ²³ I will proclaim your name to my brothers, in the midst of the assembly I will praise you. ²⁴ You who fear the Lord, praise him. All you offspring of Jacob, honor him, and revere him, all you offspring of Israel. ²⁵ For he did not despise nor abhor the affliction of the poor, and he did not hide his face from him, and when he cried out to him, he heard. ²⁶ From you comes my praise in a great assembly, I will pay my vows before those who fear him. ²⁷ The humble will eat and be satisfied. Those who seek the Lord will praise him. Their hearts will live forever. ²⁸ All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the Lord. All the families of the nations will bow before him. ²⁹ For the kingdom belongs to the Lord, and he rules over the nations. ³⁰ All the rich of the earth will eat and bow before him. All who go down to the dust will kneel before him. And my life will live for him. ³¹ A future generation will serve him. The coming generation will proclaim his righteousness to a people yet to be born—that he has done it. ³² They will come and declare his righteousness to a born people, for he has done it.

---

Notes  
- v. 16: The Hebrew reads literally “my strength is dried up like a potsherm,” but the Septuagint and some traditions read this as a reference to pierced hands and feet. The Hebrew as given in the source text does not include the reference to pierced hands and feet, so it has been rendered as it stands.  
- v. 32: The Hebrew text has “for he has done it.” The Septuagint reads differently, but the source text is followed.
