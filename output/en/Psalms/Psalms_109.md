# Psalms 109

¹ For the choir director. A psalm of David. O God, do not be silent about my praise. ² For the mouth of the wicked and the mouth of deceit have opened against me; they spoke against me with a lying tongue. ³ And words of hatred surrounded me, and they waged war against me without cause. ⁴ Because of my love, they entangled me, but I offered prayer. ⁵ And they placed evil upon me in place of good, and hatred in place of my love. ⁶ Send a wicked one against him, and let a deceiver stand at his right hand. ⁷ When he is judged, let the wicked be found guilty, and may his prayer become a sin. ⁸ May his days be few, and may another take his office. ⁹ May his children be orphans, and his wife a widow. ¹⁰ May his children wander as beggars and be driven from their ruins. ¹¹ May the creditor seize everything he has, and may strangers plunder his labor. ¹² May there be no one to show him kindness, nor to be a father to his orphans. ¹³ May his descendants be destroyed, and in one generation may his name be blotted out. ¹⁴ May the sin of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and may the iniquity of his mother not be erased. ¹⁵ May they be before the LORD always, and may his name be cut off from the earth. ¹⁶ Because he did not remember to show kindness, and he pursued the poor and the needy and the broken-hearted to destroy them. ¹⁷ And he loved curses, and they will come to him; and he did not want a blessing, and it will remain far from him. ¹⁸ And he clothed himself with curses as a garment, and they will come into him like water within his inner parts and like oil in his bones. ¹⁹ May this be to him as a garment he puts on, and as a belt with which he constantly girds himself. ²⁰ This is the work of those who slander me, by the LORD, and those who speak evil against my soul. ²¹ And you, O LORD, Lord, deal with me for the sake of your name, for your steadfast love is good, deliver me. ²² For I am poor and needy, and my heart is broken within me. ²³ I was like a shadow when it is cast down; I was shaken like a locust. ²⁴ My knees gave way from fasting, and my flesh was wasted away from oil. ²⁵ And I have become a disgrace to them; they saw me, they wagged their heads. ²⁶ Help me, O LORD my God, save me according to your great steadfast love. ²⁷ And let them know that your hand is this, and you, O LORD, have done it. ²⁸ Let them curse, but you bless; let them rise up and be put to shame, but your servant will rejoice. ²⁹ Let my adversaries be clothed with disgrace, and let them be covered with shame as with a mantle. ³⁰ I will greatly praise the LORD with my mouth, and in the midst of many I will extol him. ³¹ For he stands at the right hand of the poor, to save from those who condemn his life.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "For the choir director" is a translation of "לַמְנַצֵּחַ" (to the chief musician), a common heading in psalms.
- v. 21: "And you, O LORD, Lord" reflects the doubling of the divine name in "יְהוִה אֲדֹנָי", translated here as "the LORD Lord".
- v. 27: "you bless" and "your servant" follow the contextual shift from the psalmist's curses to a plea for divine blessing.
- v. 31: "to save from those who condemn his life" captures the meaning of "לְהוֹשִׁיעַ מִשֹּׁפְטֵי נַפְשֽׁוֹ", translating "shoftay" as "those who condemn" rather than "judges" to maintain the context of persecution.
