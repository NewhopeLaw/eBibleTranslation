# Psalms 68

¹ For the choirmaster. A psalm of David. A song. ² Let God rise up; let his enemies be scattered, and those who hate him flee before him. ³ Let them be driven away like smoke; let them vanish like wax melting before fire. Let the wicked perish before God. ⁴ But let the righteous rejoice. Let them exalt before God and be glad in joy. ⁵ Sing to God, sing praises to his name; make a highway for the one riding in the wilderness. His name is the LORD. Rejoice before him! ⁶ Father of orphans, judge of widows, God in his holy dwelling place. ⁷ God places solitary ones in a home. He leads out prisoners, those bound in chains. But the rebellious live in desolate places. ⁸ God went forth before his people; he marched through the wilderness. Selah. ⁹ The earth trembled, the heavens poured down before God—Sinai before God, the God of Israel. ¹⁰ You, O God, sent down abundant rain upon your inheritance. You refreshed the land when you sustained it. ¹¹ Your people live there; you provide for the poor through your goodness. ¹² The LORD gives the command, a great company of messengers carries it. ¹³ The kings of armies flee, they flee, and the women of the house divide the spoil. ¹⁴ Though they lie among the folds of the mountain, like wings of a dove covered with silver, its feathers with gold-like glitter. ¹⁵ When God stretched out his mighty arms, kings in Bashan fell like snow on Tabor. ¹⁶ Mountain of God, mountain of Bashan, a mountain with many peaks, a mountain of Bashan. ¹⁷ Why do you look on, you mountains with many peaks? The mountain God desires to dwell in, and the LORD will dwell there forever. ¹⁸ The chariots of God are thousands and thousands; the Lord of hosts is among them, the one on Sinai in the holy place. ¹⁹ You ascended on high, you led captive captives, you received gifts from people even from the rebellious, so that God may dwell there. ²⁰ May the LORD be blessed day by day. May the God of our salvation be praised. Selah. ²¹ Our God is the God of salvation, and to the Lord belong the paths of death. ²² But God will crush the heads of his enemies, the hairy crown of those who walk in guilt. ²³ The LORD said, “I will bring them back from Bashan, I will bring them back from the depths of the sea.” ²⁴ So that your feet may strike blood, the tongue of your dogs from your enemies. ²⁵ Your processions, O God, are seen. The processions of my God, my King, in the holy place. ²⁶ The leaders go ahead, the singers follow, in the midst of the young women playing tambourines. ²⁷ In the assemblies, bless God, the Lord, from the fountains of Israel. ²⁸ There, Benjamin, the youngest, leads them, and the princes of Judah, the princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali. ²⁹ Command your strength, O God. O God, do what you have done for us. ³⁰ From your temple in Jerusalem, kings will bring you tribute. ³¹ Rebuke the beasts of the reed, the herd of the bulls with their many calves, so that the people may be tested with silver. Scatter the nations who long for war. ³² May envoys come from Egypt. May Ethiopia stretch out her hands to God. ³³ Kings of the earth, sing to God, sing praises to the LORD. Selah. ³⁴ Sing to God who rides in the heavens, the ancient heavens. His voice is a mighty voice. ³⁵ Give strength to God. His glory is over Israel, his might in the clouds. ³⁶ Awesome is God in his holy places. The God of Israel gives strength and power to his people. Praise be to God!

---

Notes
- v. 13: "The kings of armies flee" reflects a poetic plural of majesty.
- v. 14: "like wings of a dove covered with silver" is a metaphorical image, not literal description.
- v. 21: "the paths of death" is a poetic expression for the dominion over death.
- v. 31: "rebuke the beasts of the reed" is a poetic metaphor, the meaning of which is preserved in context.
