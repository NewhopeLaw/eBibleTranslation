# Psalms 31

¹ For the choir director. A psalm of David. ² In you, O LORD, I have placed my trust. I will not be put to shame for all time. In your righteousness, rescue me. ³ Incline your ear to me quickly, and deliver me. Be to me a strong rock, a fortress of safety to save me. ⁴ For you are my rock and my fortress. Because of your name, lead me and guide me. ⁵ Rescue me from this trap they have set for me, for you are my protector. ⁶ Into your hands I commit my spirit. You, O LORD, the faithful God, have redeemed me. ⁷ I hate those who cling to worthless things and empty promises. But I have placed my trust in the LORD. ⁸ I will rejoice and be glad in your steadfast love, for you saw my distress and rescued me from my troubles. ⁹ You did not hand me over to the power of the enemy. You have set my feet in a broad place. ¹⁰ Have mercy on me, O LORD, for I am in distress. My eyes are troubled by anger; my soul and my body are weary. ¹¹ For my life has passed in sorrow, and my years in sighing. My strength has failed in affliction, and my bones have grown feeble. ¹² I have become a disgrace to all my enemies and a horror to my neighbors. My known companions have fled from me in fear. ¹³ I have been forgotten as if dead. I have become like a broken vessel. ¹⁴ For I have heard the talk of many who surround me. When they conspired together against me to take my life, they plotted. ¹⁵ But I have trusted in you, O LORD. I have said, "You are my God." ¹⁶ My times are in your hands. Deliver me from the hands of my enemies and from those who pursue me. ¹⁷ Show your face upon your servant. Save me in your steadfast love. ¹⁸ O LORD, let me not be put to shame, for I have called on you. Let the wicked be put to shame, and be silent in Sheol. ¹⁹ Let the lips of the deceitful be silent, the words of the wicked that accuse the righteous in arrogance and deceit be done away. ²⁰ How great is your goodness, which you have stored up for those who fear you, done for those who trust in you in the sight of the children of men. ²¹ You will hide them in the shelter of your presence from the troubles of men; you will keep them in a tent from the strife of tongues. ²² Praise be to the LORD, for he has shown me his steadfast love in a city of siege. ²³ And I said in my distress, "I have been rejected from before your eyes." But you, O LORD, heard the voice of my plea when I cried out to you. ²⁴ Let all your faithful ones love you, O LORD. The LORD is faithful and rewards those who act with excess pride. ²⁵ Be strong, and let your hearts be steadfast, all you who wait for the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "For the choir director" reflects the traditional reading of "lamnatzeach" as a musical instruction.
- v. 20: "done for those who trust in you" captures the sense of poetic action (pe'al) rather than passive construction.
- v. 24: "rewards those who act with excess pride" reflects the poetic justice theme in "ve-mesaleim al-yeter oseh gav'awa" with a slight interpretive nuance for clarity.
