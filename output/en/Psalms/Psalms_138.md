# Psalms 138

¹ To you, O LORD, I give thanks with my whole heart; in the presence of the gods I sing your praise. ² I bow toward your holy temple and praise your name for your steadfast love and for your faithfulness, for you have exalted your name and your word above all things. ³ On the day I called, you answered me; you strengthened me with strength in my soul. ⁴ Let all the kings of the earth give thanks to you, O LORD, for they have heard the words of your mouth. ⁵ And let them sing in the ways of the LORD, for great is the glory of the LORD. ⁶ For the LORD is exalted, and he looks after the lowly, but the lofty he knows from afar. ⁷ Though I walk in the midst of trouble, you give me life; when the fury of my enemies rises, you stretch out your hand and deliver me by your right hand. ⁸ The LORD will complete it for me; may the steadfast love of the LORD endure forever. May your works not be undone, O LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 4: The plural "kings" is used in the Hebrew, which is reflected in the English. 
- v. 6: The phrase "the lofty he knows from afar" reflects the Hebrew's contrast between the lowly and the lofty, with the LORD being aware of both.
- v. 7: The meaning of "you give me life" is inferred from the context of being delivered from trouble.
