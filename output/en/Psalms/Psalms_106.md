# Psalms 106

¹ Praise the LORD. Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good. His steadfast love is forever. ² Who can recite the mighty acts of the LORD, declare all his praises? ³ Happy are those who keep justice, who do righteousness in every time. ⁴ Remember me, LORD, in the favor of your people. Visit me with your salvation. ⁵ That I may see the goodness of your chosen ones, rejoice in the joy of your people, and glory with your heritage. ⁶ We have sinned with our ancestors, we have transgressed, we have done evil. ⁷ Our ancestors in Egypt did not understand your wondrous works. They did not remember the abundance of your kindness, but rebelled by the sea, the Red Sea. ⁸ And you rescued them for the sake of your name, to make known your might. ⁹ And you rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up, and you led them through the deep as through a desert. ¹⁰ And you rescued them from the hand of the enemy, redeemed them from the hand of their hater. ¹¹ And the waters swallowed their enemies, not one of them was left. ¹² And they believed your words, sang your praises. ¹³ But they quickly forgot your works; they did not wait for your counsel. ¹⁴ And they longed in the desert for their craving and tested God in the dry place. ¹⁵ And you gave them what they asked for, sent plenty into their hearts. ¹⁶ And they provoked Moses in the camp, and Aaron, the holy one of the LORD. ¹⁷ The earth opened and swallowed Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram. ¹⁸ And fire burned in their company, flame devoured the wicked. ¹⁹ They made a calf in Horeb and bowed to the idol. ²⁰ And they changed your glory into the image of a grass-eating ox. ²¹ They forgot the God who rescued them, who had done great things in Egypt. ²² Wonders in the land of Ham, awesome things on the Red Sea. ²³ And you said to destroy them. Had not Moses, your chosen one, stood in the breach before you, to turn your anger away from destroying them. ²⁴ And they despised a desirable land, did not believe your word. ²⁵ And they grumbled in their tents, did not listen to the voice of the LORD. ²⁶ And you raised your hand against them, to cast them down in the desert. ²⁷ And to cast down their descendants among the nations and scatter them in the lands. ²⁸ And they joined themselves to Baal Peor, ate sacrifices to the dead. ²⁹ And they incited the LORD with their deeds, and a plague broke out among them. ³⁰ And Phinehas stood up and interceded, and the plague was stilled. ³¹ And it was counted to him as righteousness for generation after generation, forever. ³² And they provoked at the waters of Meribah, and Moses was made angry because of them. ³³ Because they rebelled against his spirit, he spoke rashly with his lips. ³⁴ They did not destroy the nations that the LORD had commanded them. ³⁵ And they mingled among the nations and learned their deeds. ³⁶ And they served their idols, and they became a snare to them. ³⁷ And they sacrificed their sons and daughters to demons. ³⁸ And they poured out innocent blood, the blood of their sons and daughters they had sacrificed to the idols of Canaan. And the land was defiled with their blood. ³⁹ And they defiled themselves in their deeds, played the harlot in their practices. ⁴⁰ And the anger of the LORD burned against his people. He abhorred his heritage. ⁴¹ And he gave them into the hand of the nations, and those who hated them ruled over them. ⁴² And their enemies oppressed them, and they were brought low under their hands. ⁴³ Many times he rescued them, yet they rebelled in their counsel and were brought low in their sins. ⁴⁴ And he saw their affliction, heard their cry. ⁴⁵ And he remembered his covenant, and relented according to the abundance of his steadfast love. ⁴⁶ And he gave them into the hands of compassion before all who had taken them captive. ⁴⁷ Save us, LORD our God, and gather us from the nations, that we may give thanks to your holy name and glory in your praise. ⁴⁸ Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from eternity and to eternity. And let all the people say, “Amen! Praise the LORD!”

---

Notes
- v. 11: "Not one of them was left" — the Hebrew states that not one of their enemies was left, but the translation reflects the context in which the waters are said to have "swallowed" the enemies.  
- v. 23: "To turn your anger away from destroying them" — the Hebrew uses "to turn back" (שׁוּב) in reference to Moses' intercession, which is rendered here as "to turn your anger away from destroying them."  
- v. 47: "Let all the people say, 'Amen!'" — the Hebrew is ambiguous whether it's the people who affirm the statement or the statement itself ends with an "Amen." The latter is assumed here.
