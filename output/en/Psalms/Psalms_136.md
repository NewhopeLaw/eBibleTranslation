# Psalms 136

¹ Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good. For his steadfast love endures forever. ² Give thanks to the God of gods. For his steadfast love endures forever. ³ Give thanks to the Lord of lords. For his steadfast love endures forever. ⁴ To the one who performs great wonders alone. For his steadfast love endures forever. ⁵ To the one who made the heavens with understanding. For his steadfast love endures forever. ⁶ To the one who set the earth on the waters. For his steadfast love endures forever. ⁷ To the one who made the great lights. For his steadfast love endures forever. ⁸ The sun to rule the day. For his steadfast love endures forever. ⁹ The moon and stars to rule the night. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹⁰ To the one who struck Egypt in their firstborn. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹¹ And brought Israel out from among them. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹² With a strong hand and an outstretched arm. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹³ To the one who split the Red Sea into sections. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹⁴ And led Israel through its midst. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹⁵ And cast Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹⁶ To the one who led his people through the wilderness. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹⁷ To the one who struck great kings. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹⁸ And killed mighty kings. For his steadfast love endures forever. ¹⁹ Sihon, king of the Amorites. For his steadfast love endures forever. ²⁰ Og, king of Bashan. For his steadfast love endures forever. ²¹ And gave their land as an inheritance. For his steadfast love endures forever. ²² An inheritance to Israel, his servant. For his steadfast love endures forever. ²³ When we were brought low, he remembered us. For his steadfast love endures forever. ²⁴ And delivered us from our enemies. For his steadfast love endures forever. ²⁵ Who provides bread for all flesh. For his steadfast love endures forever. ²⁶ Give thanks to the God of the heavens. For his steadfast love endures forever.

---

Notes
- v. 22: "his servant" refers to Israel, reflecting the covenantal relationship between God and Israel.
- v. 26: "God of the heavens" is a direct translation of the Hebrew phrase, emphasizing God's sovereignty over creation.
