# Psalms 99

¹ The LORD reigns; let the nations tremble. He sits enthroned above the cherubim; the earth quakes. ² The LORD is in Zion; great and exalted is he above all the peoples. ³ Let them declare his name as great, for it is awesome and holy. ⁴ Strength is the king’s; he loves justice. You have established uprightness, you have made justice and righteousness in Jacob. ⁵ Exalt the LORD our God; bow down before his holy feet. He is holy. ⁶ Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among those who call on his name— they called to the LORD, and he answered them. ⁷ He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud; they kept his testimonies, and the statutes he gave to them. ⁸ O LORD our God, you answered them. You were a God who bore their burdens and took vengeance for all their schemes. ⁹ Exalt the LORD our God, and bow down at his holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "holy feet" is a literal translation of "רַגְלָיו קָדוֹשׁ", which has theological implications in some traditions, but the wording is clear.
- v. 6: "among his priests" and "among those who call on his name" are interpretive to clarify the relationship between the figures and the LORD.
- v. 8: "took vengeance for all their schemes" reflects the meaning of "נֹקֵם עַל־עֲלִילוֹתָם," where "עֲלִילוֹת" means "schemes" or "deeds."
