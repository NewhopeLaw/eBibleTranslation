# Psalms 13

¹ To the Choirmaster. A psalm of David. ² How long, LORD, will you forget me forever? How long will you hide your face from me? ³ How long must I wrestle with my thoughts, have sorrow in my heart each day? How long will my enemy triumph over me? ⁴ Look on me, LORD my God. Cause my eyes to shine, or I will fall into death. ⁵ Let not my enemy say, "I have triumphed over him," and let not my adversaries rejoice when I stumble. ⁶ But I trust in your steadfast love. My heart will rejoice in your salvation. I will sing to the LORD, for he has been good to me.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "How long" is a standard Hebrew idiom for lament, here repeated for emphasis. The phrase "will you forget me forever?" reflects the nuance of נֶצַח, which can mean "forever" or "always" depending on context.
- v. 4: The line "Cause my eyes to shine" translates the rare expression הָאִירָה, which literally means "make my eyes light up" — a poetic metaphor for restoration or relief.
- v. 6: "He has been good to me" translates גָמַל עָלָי ("he did a kindness to me") — a common idiom of gratitude.
