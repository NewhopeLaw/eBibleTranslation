# Psalms 95

¹ Come, let us sing joyfully to the LORD, let us shout aloud to the Rock of our salvation. ² Let us come before his face with thanksgiving, with psalms let us shout to him. ³ For the LORD is a great God, and a great King over all the gods. ⁴ In whose hands are the depths of the earth, and the peaks of the mountains belong to him. ⁵ The sea is his, and he made it; the dry land, his hands formed. ⁶ Come, let us bow down and kneel, let us bless the LORD, the one who made us. ⁷ For he is our God, and we are the people of his pasture, the flock of his hand. Today, if you will hear his voice: ⁸ Do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion, as in the day of testing in the wilderness. ⁹ When your ancestors tested me, and put me to the test, though they saw my works. ¹⁰ For forty years I rejected that generation and said, "A people of wandering hearts they are, and they do not know my ways." ¹¹ As I swore in my wrath, "If they enter my rest."

---

Notes  
- v. 7: The phrase "the flock of his hand" reflects the Hebrew phrase "צֹאן יָדוֹ" and uses "flock" rather than "sheep" for a more natural English idiom.  
- v. 10: "Rejected" renders the Hebrew "אָקוּט" (I rejected), which is the Qal form of "קָטָה," meaning to reject or repel. The Septuagint reads "προσώχθισα" ("I kept watch"), but the Hebrew is clear and the Septuagint likely reflects an interpretive reading.
