# Psalms 71

¹ In you, LORD, I have taken refuge. Let me never be put to shame. ² In your righteousness, rescue me and deliver me. Incline your ear to me and save me. ³ Be to me a rock of refuge, a fortress where I can always run for safety. You have commanded to save me, for you are my rock and my stronghold. ⁴ My God, rescue me from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the deceitful and cruel. ⁵ For you are my hope, LORD my God, my trust from my youth. ⁶ I have relied on you since birth, from the womb you have been my refuge. In you my praise has been ever constant. ⁷ I have been like a wonder to many, but you are my mighty Helper. ⁸ My mouth will be filled with your praise. I will sing your glory all the day, your majesty. ⁹ Do not cast me away in my old age. When my strength is gone, do not abandon me. ¹⁰ For my enemies have spoken against me, those who watch my life have conspired together, ¹¹ saying, "God has abandoned him. Pursue and seize him, for there is no one to deliver." ¹² God, do not be far from me. O my God, come to my rescue, hurry! ¹³ Let my adversaries be put to shame and brought to nothing. Let those who seek my harm be clothed with disgrace and humiliation. ¹⁴ But I will hope always, and add to all your praise. ¹⁵ My mouth will declare your righteousness, your salvation all the day, for I do not know how to count them. ¹⁶ I will come into the strength of the LORD, LORD, I will remember your righteousness alone. ¹⁷ God, you have taught me from my youth, and to this day I will declare your wonders. ¹⁸ Even to old age and gray hair, God, do not abandon me. Until I declare your arm to every generation to come, your strength. ¹⁹ And your righteousness, God, reaches to the highest heavens. You have done great things, God. Who is like you? ²⁰ You have shown me many troubles and evils, but you will turn around and bring me to life. From the depths of the earth you will again bring me up. ²¹ You will abound in your goodness and turn again to comfort me. From the depths you will again bring me up. ²² And I will praise you with the lyre, I will sing your truth, O God, with the harp, the Holy One of Israel. ²³ My lips will rejoice as I sing to you, and my soul, which you have redeemed. ²⁴ My tongue will meditate on your righteousness all the day, when those who seek my harm are put to shame and caught in their schemes.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "LORD" is the translation of YHWH. The Hebrew reads "beka YHWH chasiti," which is rendered as "In you, LORD, I have taken refuge."
- v. 6: "from the womb you have been my refuge" reflects the Hebrew phrase "mikev etzba" translated as "from the womb."
- v. 14: "add to all your praise" is a direct rendering of "vehotzifti al kol tehillatecha."
- v. 18: "your strength" reflects the Hebrew "gevuratecha," which is rendered consistently as "strength" or "might."
- v. 24: "caught in their schemes" is a paraphrase of the Hebrew "chaphru," meaning "dug" or "sought in secret," used here in the sense of plotting.
