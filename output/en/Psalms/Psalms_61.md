# Psalms 61

¹ For the Chief Musician. In instrumental music. Of David. ² Hear my prayer, O God. Attend to my plea. ³ From the ends of the earth I cry to you. My heart is overwhelmed. In a high rock you raise me up, you lead me. ⁴ For you have become my refuge, a tower of strength from the face of an enemy. ⁵ I will dwell in your tent forever. I will hide in the shelter of your wings. Selah. ⁶ For you, O God, have heard my prayers. You have given an inheritance to those who fear your name. ⁷ Days upon days of a king you will add to his years, even to the age of generation and generation. ⁸ The one who sits in the presence of God will dwell there forever. Mercy and truth will guard him. ⁹ So I will sing your name forever, to pay my vows, day after day.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "Selah" is retained as is, following the Hebrew manuscript.
