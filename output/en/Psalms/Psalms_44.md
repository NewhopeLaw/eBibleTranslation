# Psalms 44

¹ For the leader. For the sons of Korah. A maskil. ² God, in our ears we have heard, our ancestors have told us what you did in their days, in ancient times. ³ You, your hand, nations you dispossessed; you planted them, you crushed peoples and expelled them. ⁴ For it was not by their sword that they claimed the land, nor did their arm save them, but your right hand and your arm, and the light of your face, for you delighted in them. ⁵ You are the one, our King, O God; command the victories of Jacob. ⁶ In you, we will push through our enemies, in your name, we will humble those who rise against us. ⁷ For I do not rely on my bow, and my sword will not save me. ⁸ For you rescued us from our distress and put our enemies to shame. ⁹ To God we will sing praise all day long, and your name we will acclaim forever. Selah. ¹⁰ Yet now you have abandoned us, you have brought us into disgrace, and you did not go out with our armies. ¹¹ You have turned us back from our foes, and our enemies have devoured us. ¹² You gave us like sheep for food, and you scattered us among the nations. ¹³ You sold your people at no cost, and you did not profit from their price. ¹⁴ You made us a disgrace to our neighbors, a mockery and a taunt to those around us. ¹⁵ You made us an example among the nations, a head-shaking joke among peoples. ¹⁶ All day long my disgrace is before me, and the shame of my face has covered me. ¹⁷ From the voice of the one who mocks and insults, from the face of the enemy and the pursuer. ¹⁸ All this has come upon us, yet we have not forgotten you, nor have we broken your covenant. ¹⁹ Our hearts have not turned back, nor have our steps veered from your path. ²⁰ For you have crushed us in a place of suffering, and you have covered us with the shadow of death. ²¹ If we have forgotten the name of our God, and stretched out our hands to a foreign god, ²² Will God not examine these things? For he knows the hidden things of the heart. ²³ Indeed, for your sake we are being killed all day long, we are counted as sheep for the slaughter. ²⁴ Awaken, O Lord, why do you slumber, Lord? Arise, do not forsake us forever. ²⁵ Why do you turn your face away? Why do you forget our distress and our oppression? ²⁶ For our soul has sunk into the dust, our belly clings to the ground. ²⁷ Rise up, our Helper, and rescue us for the sake of your steadfast love.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "For the leader" is a reference to a musical or liturgical direction; "a maskil" is a type of psalm, often with a reflective or instructive tone.
- v. 10: "now you have abandoned us" reflects the shift in tone from past faithfulness to present distress.
- v. 20: "the shadow of death" is a poetic metaphor for despair or danger.
- v. 22: "Will God not examine these things?" is a rhetorical question affirming God's awareness and justice.
