# Psalms 80

¹ For the music director. A testimony for Asaph. A psalm. ² Shepherd of Israel, come near; the one who leads Joseph like sheep, the one who sits enthroned among the cherubim, appear! ³ In the presence of Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, stir up your might, and come to our rescue. ⁴ O God, restore us and reveal your face, and we will be saved. ⁵ O Lord, God of hosts, how long will you be angry with the prayer of your people? ⁶ You have fed them bread of tears, and given them tears to drink in abundance. ⁷ You have made us an object of ridicule to our neighbors, and our enemies mock us. ⁸ O God of hosts, restore us and reveal your face, and we will be saved. ⁹ You brought a vine from Egypt, you drove out nations and planted it. ¹⁰ You cleared the ground for it, and you caused its roots to take hold, and it filled the land. ¹¹ Its shadow covered the mountains, and its branches were like cedar trees. ¹² Its branches stretched toward the sea, and its roots reached to the river. ¹³ Why have you broken down its walls, so that all who pass by the road strip it? ¹⁴ A wild boar from the forest has ravaged it, and a beast of the field has devoured it. ¹⁵ O God of hosts, come back now; look from heaven, see, and attend to this vine. ¹⁶ And the vine you planted with your right hand, and the son you strengthened for yourself. ¹⁷ Burned in fire, scorched, and from your rebuke it perishes. ¹⁸ Let your hand be on the man you strengthened, on the son of humanity you fortified for yourself. ¹⁹ Then we will not wander from you; revive us, and we will call on your name. ²⁰ O Lord, God of hosts, restore us and reveal your face, and we will be saved.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "bread of tears" and "tears to drink in abundance" reflect the Hebrew's metaphorical and poetic language, capturing the sorrow and hardship described.
