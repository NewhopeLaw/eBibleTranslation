# Psalms 70

¹ For the choirmaster. A psalm of David. To be remembered. ² God, come to my aid. LORD, hasten to help me. ³ Let those who seek my life be put to shame and be humiliated. Let those who long for my downfall be turned back and be disgraced. ⁴ Let those who say to me, “Aha! Aha!” be turned back and be ashamed. ⁵ Let all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you. Let them always say, “The LORD is great!” May lovers of your salvation say continually, “The LORD is great!” ⁶ But I am poor and in need. LORD, be my helper. You are my aid and my deliverer. LORD, do not delay.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "To be remembered" (לְהַזְכִּֽיר) is an interpretive rendering of the word typically translated as "for remembrance" or "as a memorial." Here, it reflects the idea that this psalm should be recalled or preserved.
- v. 4: The phrase "Aha! Aha!" (הֶאָח הֶאָח) is a common expression of triumph in Hebrew literature, often used by enemies of the righteous. It is retained in direct form to preserve the tone.
