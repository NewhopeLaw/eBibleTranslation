# Psalms 75

¹ To the Chief Musician, do not destroy; a psalm of Asaph, a song. ² Let us give thanks to you, God; let us give thanks, and your name is near; declare your wonders. ³ For I will take an appointed time; I will judge uprightly. ⁴ The earth and all its inhabitants are shaken; I myself have firmly set its pillars. Selah. ⁵ I said to the lawless, "Do not act lawlessly," and to the wicked, "Do not raise your horn." ⁶ Do not raise your horns to the heights; do not speak against God with wrongdoing. ⁷ For not from the east, nor from the west, nor from the desert mountains, ⁸ For God is the judge. He will bring low this one and raise up that one. ⁹ For a cup is in the hand of the LORD, a wine of wrath, full of dregs. He will pour it out for this one and that one. Yet their portion will not be emptied, and all the wicked of the earth will drink it. ¹⁰ But I will declare forever; I will sing to the God of Jacob. ¹¹ All the horns of the wicked I will break, and the horns of the righteous will be exalted.

---

Notes
- v. 9: The phrase "wine of wrath, full of dregs" captures the meaning of "יין חָמַר" and "מֶסֶךְ" as a strong, punitive drink. The reference to "their portion will not be emptied" follows the Hebrew "לא שמריהו ימצאו" as a poetic negation of their escape.
- v. 11: "Horns" is used metaphorically in the Hebrew for power or pride; the contrast between "qarnê rish'a'im" (horns of the wicked) and "qeren tsaddiq" (horn of the righteous) is preserved.
