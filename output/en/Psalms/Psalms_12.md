# Psalms 12

¹ To the Choirmaster. On the eighth. A psalm of David. ² Save us, LORD, for the godly are no more—truth has vanished from the human race. ³ Each speaks falsely to his neighbor; with deceitful hearts and hearts they speak. ⁴ May the LORD destroy every deceitful tongue, the great-speaking tongue. ⁵ Those who said, "Let us magnify our tongue, let our lips be our own— who is our master?" ⁶ From the oppression of the poor and from the groaning of the needy now I will rise, says the LORD. I will place them in salvation; I will make it clear to them. ⁷ The words of the LORD are words of purity, silver refined in the furnace, tried on the earth, purified seven times. ⁸ You, LORD, will guard them; you will preserve them from this generation forever. ⁹ The wicked walk around on every side. They have exalted themselves beyond the thoughts of the human race.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "On the eighth" renders *al-hashminit*, a musical term referring to the eighth tone or mode, likely a reference to musical scale or tuning.  
- v. 5: "Let us magnify our tongue" reflects the rhetorical claim of self-determination in speech, with "our lips be our own."  
- v. 9: "beyond the thoughts of the human race" renders *kuru molot levaney adam*, with "thoughts" translating *molot*, which can also mean "deeds" or "intentions," but here "thoughts" fits well with the metaphor of arrogance.
