# Psalms 16

¹ A psalm of David. Keep me safe, O God, for I have taken refuge in you. ² I have said to the LORD, "You are my LORD, my goodness is not with anyone but you." ³ The holy ones who are on the earth—these are my delight, and the ones in whom all my desire rests. ⁴ Their sorrows have multiplied, and their haste is in vain. I will not join in their company of bloodshed, nor will I carry their names upon my lips. ⁵ The LORD is the portion of my inheritance and my cup; you yourself, O LORD, uphold my lot. ⁶ The lines have fallen to me in pleasant places. Yes, my inheritance is a beautiful land. ⁷ I will bless the LORD who has advised me. Even in the night my kidneys teach me. ⁸ I have set the LORD always before me. Because he is at my right hand, I will not be moved. ⁹ Therefore my heart is glad, and my glory rejoices. My flesh will dwell securely. ¹⁰ For you will not abandon my life to Sheol, nor will you allow your righteous one to see corruption. ¹¹ You will show me the way of life, a fullness of joy in your presence—abundance of pleasures at your right hand forever.

---

Notes
- v. 2: The phrase "my goodness is not with anyone but you" captures the poetic contrast of אַתָּה טוֹבָתִי בַּל־עָלֶיךָ. The word "goodness" is used to render טוֹבָתִי, which is literally "my good" but best understood as "my good fortune" or "my goodness" in a personal and relational sense.
- v. 5: The term "portion of my inheritance" reflects מְנָת־חֶלְקִי, which is a poetic way of speaking about one's share or allotment. The translation "you yourself, O LORD, uphold my lot" for תּוֹמִיךְ גּוֹרָלִי aims to preserve the poetic parallelism and the idea of divine support.
- v. 10: The reference to Sheol and not seeing corruption is taken directly from the Hebrew, with שָׁאוֹל for Sheol and שָׁחַת for corruption, preserving the poetic and theological contrast.
