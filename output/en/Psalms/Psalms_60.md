# Psalms 60

¹ For the Chief Musician. On Shushan Eduth. A maskil of David, to teach. ² When he stirred up Aram-Naharaim and Aram-Tzovah, and Yoav returned and struck Edom in the Valley of Salt, twelve thousand. ³ God, you have forsaken us. You have scattered us. You have raged and shown us no mercy. ⁴ You have shaken the earth, you have broken it. You have shattered its foundations, for it has been weakened. ⁵ You have shown your people hardships. You have given us a wine of confusion. ⁶ You have given a sign to your fears, to escape from the bowstring. Selah. ⁷ That your beloved may be rescued, save with your right hand and give ear to me. ⁸ God has spoken in his holy one. I will rejoice and divide Shechem, and I will measure the valley of the tents. ⁹ Mine is Gilead, and mine is Manasseh, and Ephraim is the strength of my head. Judah is my ruler. ¹⁰ Moab is the basin of my washing. On Edom I will cast my shoe. Over me the Philistines have shouted. ¹¹ Who will lead me into the city of siege? Who will guide me to Edom? ¹² Is it not you, O God, who have cast us off? And will you not go out, O God, with our armies? ¹³ Give us help from distress, and vain is the salvation of man. ¹⁴ In God we will do might, and he will trample our adversaries.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "Shechem" is a proper name, and the phrase "divide Shechem" reflects the historical reference to the city's division.
- v. 10: "Moab is the basin of my washing" is a metaphor interpreted from the Hebrew, reflecting a poetic expression of conquest or humiliation.
- v. 12: "O God, who have cast us off" reflects the meaning of the verb zanach, which can mean "forsake" or "abandon," used in a context of lament.
