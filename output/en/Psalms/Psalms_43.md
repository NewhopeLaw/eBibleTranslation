# Psalms 43

¹ Judge me, God, and plead my cause against an ungodly nation, from a deceitful and wicked man. Deliver me from their hands. ² For you are my God, my stronghold—why have you abandoned me? Why must I walk in gloom, oppressed by my enemy? ³ Send out your light and your truth; they will lead me and bring me to your holy mountain and to your dwelling places. ⁴ I will go to the altar of God, to the God who brings joy to my youth. I will praise you with the lyre, O God, my God. ⁵ Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why do you groan within me? Hope in God, for I will still praise Him, the salvation of my face and my God.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "Why must I walk in gloom" reflects the Hebrew "לָֽמָּה־קֹדֵר אֶתְהַלֵּךְ," where "קֹדֵר" is interpreted as a rare or poetic term for gloom or darkness.
- v. 3: "Send out your light and your truth" uses the imperative form typical in psalms of supplication. "They will lead me and bring me..." reflects the poetic parallelism of the Hebrew.
