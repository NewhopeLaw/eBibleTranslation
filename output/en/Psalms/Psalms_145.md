# Psalms 145

¹ A song of praise for David. I will exalt you, my God, the king. I will bless your name forever and ever. ² Every day I will bless you, and I will praise your name forever and ever. ³ Great is the LORD and highly praised; his greatness is beyond measure. ⁴ Generation after generation will speak of your deeds and declare your power. ⁵ They will speak of the splendor of your glory and recount your wondrous works. ⁶ They will declare your fearful wonders and speak of your greatness. ⁷ They will proclaim the abundance of your goodness and sing with joy about your righteousness. ⁸ The LORD is gracious and merciful, slow to anger and rich in steadfast love. ⁹ The LORD is good to all, and his mercy is over all his works. ¹⁰ All his works will praise him, and his faithful ones will bless him. ¹¹ They will declare the glory of your kingdom and speak of your power. ¹² To make known to the children of humankind your power and the splendor of your kingdom. ¹³ Your kingdom is a kingdom for all ages, and your dominion endures from generation to generation. ¹⁴ The LORD upholds all who are falling and raises up all who are bowed down. ¹⁵ The eyes of all look to you, and you give them their food in its season. ¹⁶ You open your hand and satisfy every living thing with favor. ¹⁷ The LORD is righteous in all his ways and holy in all his works. ¹⁸ The LORD is near to all who call upon him, to all who call upon him in truth. ¹⁹ He will fulfill the desire of those who fear him and hear their cry and save them. ²⁰ The LORD protects all who love him, but he will destroy every wicked thing. ²¹ My mouth will speak the praise of the LORD, and all flesh will bless his holy name forever and ever.

---

Notes
- v. 12: The phrase "לְהוֹדִיעַ לִבְנֵי הָאָדָם" is rendered as "To make known to the children of humankind" to preserve the poetic intent while using natural English.
