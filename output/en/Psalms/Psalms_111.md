# Psalms 111

¹ Praise the LORD. I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart, in the company of the upright and in the assembly. ² Great are the works of the LORD, studied by all who delight in them. ³ Glorious and majestic is his work, and his righteousness stands forever. ⁴ He has made a memorial of his wondrous deeds, gracious and compassionate is the LORD. ⁵ He provides food for those who fear him, and he remembers his covenant forever. ⁶ He has declared the strength of his works to his people, to give them the inheritance of the nations. ⁷ The work of his hands is truth and justice, all his commandments are trustworthy. ⁸ They stand forever and ever, established in truth and uprightness. ⁹ He has sent redemption to his people. He has decreed his covenant forever; holy and awesome is his name. ¹⁰ The beginning of wisdom is the fear of the LORD. Good understanding is for all who do it; praise of him stands forever.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Praise the LORD" is a heading or call to worship, but since the psalm is in the voice of an individual, it is rendered as a call embedded in the first line rather than as a separate heading.
- v. 10: "The beginning of wisdom" follows the Hebrew "re’shit chokhmah," which is a classic Hebraic expression. "Praise of him stands forever" reflects "tehillato ‘omedet la’ad," where "tehillah" is "praise" or "glory."
