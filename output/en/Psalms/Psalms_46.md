# Psalms 46

¹ For the leader. For the sons of Korah. On lilies. A song. ² God is our refuge and strength, a helper in troubles that find us very severely. ³ Therefore we will not fear when the earth is shaken and mountains tremble in the heart of the seas. ⁴ Let the waters roar, let them be troubled; let the mountains tremble in his might. Selah. ⁵ The river’s torrents gladden the city of God, the holy place of the Most High. ⁶ God is in her midst; she will not waver. God will help her at dawn. ⁷ Nations are in turmoil, kingdoms fall. He speaks, and the earth melts. ⁸ The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah. ⁹ Come, see the works of the LORD, the wonders he has placed on the earth. ¹⁰ He breaks the weapons of war to the ends of the earth, shatters the bow, breaks the spear, burns shields in the fire. ¹¹ Be still and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted on the earth. ¹² The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "Let the waters roar, let them be troubled" reflects the Hebrew's imperative form, which is poetic and rhetorical.
- v. 8: "The LORD of Hosts" and "the God of Jacob" reflect the compound title pattern from the glossary.
- v. 11: "Be still" renders the Hebrew "הַרְפּוּ", a form of the verb meaning "to be silent" or "to rest." The phrase is a call to reflection and acknowledgment of God’s sovereignty.
