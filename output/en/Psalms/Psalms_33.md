# Psalms 33

¹ Rejoice in the LORD, you upright ones. It is good for those who are righteous to praise. ² Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre, with a ten-stringed harp, sing to him. ³ Sing to him a new song, play skillfully with a loud sound. ⁴ For the word of the LORD is upright, and all his works are in faithfulness. ⁵ He loves righteousness and judgment. The steadfast love of the LORD fills the earth. ⁶ By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and by the breath of his mouth all their host. ⁷ He gathers the waters of the sea as a heap, and puts the depths into storehouses. ⁸ Let all the earth fear the LORD, and let all the inhabitants of the world tremble before him. ⁹ For he spoke, and it came to be; he commanded, and it stood firm. ¹⁰ The LORD frustrates the counsel of the nations; he nullifies the plans of the peoples. ¹¹ The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the thoughts of his heart through all generations. ¹² Happy is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people he has chosen as his heritage. ¹³ From heaven the LORD looks, he sees all the children of humanity. ¹⁴ From the place of his dwelling he looks out over all the inhabitants of the earth. ¹⁵ The one who fashions their hearts together, the one who understands all their works. ¹⁶ A king is not saved by a great army, a warrior is not delivered by great strength. ¹⁷ A horse is a false hope for rescue, in the abundance of its strength it cannot be saved. ¹⁸ Behold, the eyes of the LORD are on those who fear him, on those who wait for his steadfast love. ¹⁹ To deliver their lives from death, and to keep them alive in famine. ²⁰ Our soul has waited for the LORD, he is our help and our shield. ²¹ For in him our heart rejoices, because we trust in his holy name. ²² Let your steadfast love, LORD, be upon us, just as we have hoped in you.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "steadfast love" reflects חסד, a central theological term.
- v. 16-17: The contrast between human strength and divine faithfulness is central to the psalm’s message; the rendering follows the literal Hebrew but preserves the thematic contrast.
