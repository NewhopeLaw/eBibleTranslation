# 1 Kings 3

¹ Solomon made a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt and took Pharaoh's daughter and brought her to the city of David until he built a palace for himself, the house of the Lord, and the wall of Jerusalem. ² But the people were still offering sacrifices on the high places, because the house for the name of the Lord had not yet been built. ³ Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the statutes of David his father, but he also offered sacrifices and made offerings on the high places. ⁴ The king went up to Gibeon to offer sacrifices there, for it was the great high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar. ⁵ At Gibeon the Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream that night. And God said, "Ask what I should give you." ⁶ Solomon said, "You have shown great kindness to your servant David my father, walking in truth and righteousness and with an upright heart before you, and you have kept for him this great kindness, giving him a son who sits on his throne to this day. ⁷ And now, Lord my God, you have made your servant king in place of David my father, but I am just a little child, and I do not know how to go out or come in. ⁸ And your servant is among your people you have chosen, a people too numerous to count or number because of their abundance. ⁹ So give your servant an understanding heart to judge your people, to discern between good and evil, for who is able to judge this great people of yours?" ¹⁰ And the matter was good in the eyes of the Lord, for Solomon had asked for this thing. ¹¹ And God said to him, "Because you asked for this thing, and did not ask for long life for yourself, nor for wealth, nor for the life of your enemies, but asked for understanding to hear judgment, ¹² behold, I have done as you asked. I have given you a wise and discerning heart, one like no other before you, and no one after you will be like you. ¹³ And what you did not ask for, I have also given you—wealth and honor such as no one among the kings has had in all their days. ¹⁴ And if you walk in my ways, keeping my statutes and commandments as your father David walked, I will lengthen your days." ¹⁵ And Solomon awoke, and behold, it was a dream. He went to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and he offered burnt offerings and made offerings of well-being, and he held a feast for all his servants. ¹⁶ Then two women, who were prostitutes, came to the king and stood before him. ¹⁷ One of them said, "My lord, I and this woman live in the same house. I gave birth while she was in the house. ¹⁸ And on the third day after my birth, this woman also gave birth. We were together, and there was no one else with us in the house except the two of us. ¹⁹ This woman's child died during the night, because she lay on it. ²⁰ She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from my side while she was sleeping and placed him in her lap, and she placed her dead son in my lap. ²¹ I got up in the morning to feed my son, and behold, he was dead. And I looked at him in the morning, and behold, he was not the child I had given birth to." ²² The other woman said, "No, my son is the living one and your son is the dead one." And the other said, "No, your son is the dead one and my son is the living one." And they argued before the king. ²³ The king said, "This one says, 'My son is the living one and your son is the dead one,' and the other says, 'No, your son is the dead one and my son is the living one.'" ²⁴ The king said, "Bring me a sword." They brought the sword before the king. ²⁵ The king said, "Cut the living child in two and give half to one and half to the other." ²⁶ The woman whose child was alive, whose heart was moved for her son, said to the king, "No, my lord, give the child to her. Do not kill him. But she said, 'Neither to her nor to you will it be given. Do not cut it.'" ²⁷ The king answered and said, "Give the living child to her and do not kill it. She is the mother of it." ²⁸ And all Israel heard the judgment that the king had given, and they stood in awe of the king, for they saw that God's wisdom was in him to do justice.

Notes
- v. 2: "high places" – This refers to unauthorized altars or sites for worship, distinct from the central sanctuary in Jerusalem.
- v. 4: "Gibeon" – A town near Jerusalem, known for its high place of worship.
- v. 26: "Neither to her nor to you will it be given" – The Septuagint adds "Do not cut it" at the end, which is not in the Hebrew text. The Hebrew text as given ends with the woman's refusal to allow the child to be divided.
- v. 27: "she is the mother of it" – The phrase in Hebrew is "she is the mother," which is a clear but succinct way of affirming her claim.
- v. 28: "God's wisdom was in him to do justice" – The Hebrew text uses a phrase that can be read both as "wisdom of God" and "wisdom from God," which is captured here as "God's wisdom."
