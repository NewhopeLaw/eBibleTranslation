# 1 Kings 14

¹ At that time, Abijah, the son of Jeroboam, became ill. ² Jeroboam said to his wife, "Get up, please, and disguise yourself. No one should recognize you as the wife of Jeroboam. Go to Shiloh. There is Ahijah the prophet—he has spoken to me about becoming king over this people. ³ Take ten loaves of bread, some fig cakes, and honey in your hand, and go to him. He will tell you what will happen to the boy." ⁴ So the wife of Jeroboam did as he had told her. She got up and went to Shiloh and entered Ahijah's house. But Ahijah could not see, for his eyes had grown dim in his old age. ⁵ The LORD had told Ahijah, "Here comes the wife of Jeroboam seeking a word from you about her son, for he is ill. This and that you will say to her. And when she comes, she will be disguised." ⁶ It happened when Ahijah heard the sound of her feet as she came in at the door, he said, "Come in, wife of Jeroboam. Why are you disguised? I have been sent to you with a difficult message." ⁷ Go, tell Jeroboam this: Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, because I raised you up from among the people and made you a ruler over My people Israel, ⁸ and I tore the kingdom from the house of David and gave it to you. You were not like My servant David, who kept My commandments and walked after Me with all his heart, doing only what is right in My sight. ⁹ You have done evil in doing all that was before you. You went and made for yourself other gods and molten images to provoke Me, and you turned your back on Me. ¹⁰ Therefore, I am bringing evil on the house of Jeroboam. I will cut off from Jeroboam every male in the walls of a strong city and in a deserted city in Israel, and I will burn away the house of Jeroboam as one burns a dung heap until it is all consumed. ¹¹ The one who dies in the city, the dogs will eat, and the one who dies in the field, the birds of the sky will eat, for the LORD has spoken." ¹² "Now go home. When your feet enter the city, the boy will die. ¹³ All Israel will mourn for him and bury him, for he alone among the house of Jeroboam will find good in the eyes of the LORD, the God of Israel in the house of Jeroboam. ¹⁴ And the LORD will raise up for Himself a king over Israel, who will cut off the house of Jeroboam this very day and even now. ¹⁵ The LORD will strike Israel as the reed is shaken in the water. And He will cast Israel away from the good land that He gave to their fathers, and scatter them beyond the Euphrates, because they made for themselves graven images, provoking the LORD. ¹⁶ And He will give Israel into the hands because of the sins of Jeroboam, who sinned and who led Israel into sin." ¹⁷ The wife of Jeroboam got up and went, and returned to Tirzah. When she arrived at the threshold of the house, the boy was dead. ¹⁸ They buried him, and all Israel mourned for him, just as the LORD had spoken by His servant Ahijah the prophet. ¹⁹ The rest of the acts of Jeroboam, his wars, and how he reigned, are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ²⁰ The number of years that Jeroboam reigned was twenty-two years. And he lay down with his ancestors, and Nadab his son reigned in his place. ²¹ Rehoboam the son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city the LORD had chosen from all the tribes of Israel to place His name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. ²² Judah did evil in the eyes of the LORD, and He was stirred to jealousy by all that their ancestors had done in their sins. ²³ They also built for themselves high places and set up pillars and Asherahs on every high hill and under every green tree. ²⁴ There was also prostitution in the land. They did all the detestable things of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the children of Israel. ²⁵ In the fifth year of Rehoboam's reign, Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem. ²⁶ He took the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king's house. He took all of them, and he took all the shields of gold that Solomon had made. ²⁷ King Rehoboam made bronze shields in place of them, and he assigned them to the hands of the captains of the runners who kept the gate of the king's house. ²⁸ Whenever the king went into the house of the LORD, the runners would take them up and return them to the chamber of the runners. ²⁹ The rest of the deeds of Rehoboam and all that he did are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? ³⁰ There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days. ³¹ Rehoboam lay down with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. Abijah his son reigned in his place.

- v. 25: "Shishak" is a transliteration of the Hebrew, representing the Egyptian pharaoh. No adjustment made to align with Septuagint or other sources.
- v. 26: "shields of gold" refers to royal armor or ceremonial items, as per the Hebrew context. No theological or interpretive license taken.
