# 1 Kings 20

¹ Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, gathered all his forces and thirty-two kings with him, along with horses and chariots. He marched and encircled Samaria, then fought against it. ² He sent messengers to Ahab, king of Israel, in the city. ³ He said to him, "This is what Ben-Hadad says: Your silver and gold are mine. Your wives and children are mine." ⁴ Ahab, king of Israel, answered him, "Everything you ask for, my lord the king, is yours. I am yours, and all that I have is yours." ⁵ The messengers returned and said, "This is what Ben-Hadad says: I have sent messengers to you to say, 'Your silver and gold, your wives and children, I want them.'" ⁶ "But in truth, tomorrow I will send my servants to your house and to the houses of your officials. Everything your eyes desire, they will set their hands upon and take." ⁷ Then the king of Israel called together all the elders of the land and said, "Please see and understand. This man is seeking to harm me. He sent to me to take my wives, my children, my silver and gold, and I did not refuse him." ⁸ The elders and all the people said to him, "Do not listen to him or be swayed." ⁹ Ahab said to the messengers of Ben-Hadad, "Tell my lord the king, anything that you sent to your servant at first, I will do. But this one thing I cannot do." The messengers returned with that message. ¹⁰ Then Ben-Hadad sent to him again and said, "This is what I will do. May the gods do so to me and more if the dust of Samaria is enough for the sandals of all the people who are with me." ¹¹ Ahab replied, "Tell him, 'Let not the one who girds on armor boast like the one who puts off armor.'" ¹² When he heard this, while he and the kings were feasting in the tents, he said to his servants, "Set up a siege." So they set up a siege around the city. ¹³ Then behold, a prophet approached Ahab, king of Israel, and said, "This is what the LORD says: Have you seen all this great army? Today I will place it in your hands, so that you may know I am the LORD." ¹⁴ Ahab asked, "Who is it, then?" The prophet said, "This is what the LORD says: With the young men of the rulers of the provinces." Ahab asked, "Who will begin the battle?" The prophet said, "You will." ¹⁵ So Ahab summoned the young men of the rulers of the provinces, and they were 232. Then he summoned all the people of Israel—7,000 men. ¹⁶ They went out at noon, while Ben-Hadad was feasting in the tents, drunk, and 32 kings were helping him as he rested. ¹⁷ First, the young men of the rulers of the provinces went out. Ben-Hadad sent and reported to him, "Men have gone out from Samaria." ¹⁸ He said, "If they have come in peace, let them live. If they have come to fight, let them live." ¹⁹ These had gone out from the city—the young men of the rulers of the provinces and the forces behind them. ²⁰ They fought, and Israel struck Aram, and they fled. Israel pursued them, and the king of Aram escaped on a horse, with his chariots and horsemen. ²¹ The king of Israel went out and struck the horses and chariots. He defeated Aram with a great victory. ²² Then the prophet came to the king of Israel and said to him, "Go, strengthen yourself, and see what you will do, for in the next year the king of Aram will come up against you." ²³ The servants of the king of Aram had said to him, "Their gods are the gods of the hills. That is why they were stronger than us. But if we fight them in the valley, perhaps we will be stronger than them." ²⁴ Do this: Remove the kings from their places and put men of lower rank in their place. ²⁵ You must prepare for yourself an army like the army that has fallen from you, and horses like your horses, and chariots like your chariots. Then you will fight them in the valley. If you are not stronger than them, you will hear it." He listened to their voice and did so. ²⁶ In the next year, Ben-Hadad reorganized Aram and went up to fight against Israel. ²⁷ The Israelites also reorganized themselves and were weary. They went out to meet them. The Israelites camped before them like two flocks of goats, while Aram filled the land. ²⁸ Then a man of God came and said to the king of Israel, "This is what the LORD says: Because they said, 'The gods of the hills are stronger than the gods of the valleys,' I am about to give this great army into your hands, so that you may know I am the LORD." ²⁹ They camped opposite each other for seven days. On the seventh day, the battle began, and Israel struck Aram, killing 100,000 foot soldiers in one day. ³⁰ The survivors fled to Aphek and the city wall fell on 27,000 of them. The rest of the people were left, and Ben-Hadad fled and entered the city, room by room. ³¹ His servants said to him, "Now we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel are kind. Please let us put sacks around our waists and ropes on our heads and go out to the king of Israel. Perhaps he will spare your life." ³² So they put sacks around their waists and ropes on their heads and went out to the king of Israel. They said, "Your servant Ben-Hadad says, 'Please let my life be spared.'" Ahab said, "Is he still alive? He is my brother." ³³ The men suspected this and hurried, then said among themselves, "He is your brother, Ben-Hadad." So he said, "Go, bring him." So they went out to him, and Ben-Hadad was brought up on the chariot. ³⁴ He said to him, "The cities that my father took from your father I will return, and you will build outlying towns for yourself in Damascus, just as my father built them in Samaria. I will make a covenant and send you away." So he made a covenant with him and sent him away. ³⁵ One of the sons of the prophets said to his companion, "The LORD told me to strike this man." But the man refused to strike him. ³⁶ The prophet said to him, "Because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD, you are leaving and the lion will strike you." So he left from there, and the lion found him and struck him down. ³⁷ Then another man found him and said, "Strike me, please." The man struck him and wounded him. ³⁸ The prophet went on and stood before the king on the road, disguising himself with a patch over his eyes. ³⁹ When the king was passing by, he cried out to the king and said, "Your servant went out into the battle, and behold, a man ran and brought me a man, saying, 'Guard this man. If he is missing, you will pay with your life, or you must weigh out a talent of silver.'" ⁴⁰ "Your servant was doing just as he said, and he is not there." The king of Israel said to him, "That is your judgment. You have cut yourself off." ⁴¹ He hurried and removed the patch from his eyes, and the king of Israel recognized him. For he was one of the prophets. ⁴² He said to him, "This is what the LORD says: Because you let the man go free, you will pay with your life and your people will pay with their lives." ⁴³ So the king of Israel went to his house, broken and weary, and came to Samaria.
