# 1 Kings 18

¹ After many days, the word of the LORD came to Elijah in the third year, saying, "Go and show yourself to Ahab, and I will send rain on the surface of the land." ² So Elijah went to show himself to Ahab when the famine was severe in Samaria. ³ Ahab summoned Obadiah, who was in charge of the palace. Obadiah was a very devout man who greatly feared the LORD. ⁴ When Jezebel killed the prophets of the LORD, Obadiah took a hundred prophets and hid them in caves, fifty men in each cave, and gave them bread and water. ⁵ Ahab said to Obadiah, "Go through the land to all the springs of water and to all the brooks. Perhaps we may find some grass to keep the horses and donkeys alive, and not lose any of the livestock." ⁶ They divided the land to search it. Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went another way by himself. ⁷ While Obadiah was on the road alone, Elijah met him, and he recognized him and bowed to the ground and said, "Are you really Elijah, my lord?" ⁸ Elijah said to him, "I am. Go and tell your master, 'Here is Elijah.'" ⁹ Obadiah said, "Have I sinned, my lord Elijah, that you are giving your servant into the hand of Ahab to kill me?" ¹⁰ "As the LORD your God lives," said Elijah, "if there is a nation or kingdom where I have not sent my master to look for you, and they said, 'There is none,' and he bound the nation and the kingdom by an oath that they would not find you." ¹¹ "Now you are telling me to go and say to my master, 'Here is Elijah.'" ¹² "And when I leave you," said Elijah, "the spirit of the LORD will carry you to a place I do not know. Then I will go and tell Ahab, and he will kill me. And your servant has feared the LORD since his youth." ¹³ "Was it not told to my master what I did when I killed Jezebel's prophets of the LORD and I hid a hundred of them in caves, fifty men in each cave, and I gave them bread and water?" ¹⁴ "Now you are telling me to go and say to my master, 'Here is Elijah,' and he will kill me." ¹⁵ Then Elijah said, "As the LORD of Hosts lives, before whom I stand, today I will show myself to him." ¹⁶ So Obadiah went to meet Ahab and told him, and Ahab went to meet Elijah. ¹⁷ When Ahab saw Elijah, he said to him, "Are you the one who brings trouble to Israel?" ¹⁸ Elijah replied, "I have not brought trouble to Israel, but you and your father's house, by abandoning the commandments of the LORD and following the Baals." ¹⁹ "Now send and gather all Israel to me on Mount Carmel, and the four hundred prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of the Asherah who eat at Jezebel's table." ²⁰ So Ahab sent to all the Israelites and gathered the prophets to Mount Carmel. ²¹ Elijah went to meet all the people and said, "How long will you waver between two opinions? If the LORD is God, follow Him. If Baal is God, follow him." But the people did not respond to him. ²² Then Elijah said to the people, "I am the only prophet of the LORD left, and there are four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal." ²³ "Let them give us two bulls, and let them choose one for themselves, and they may prepare it and put it on the wood but not light the fire. I will prepare the other bull and put it on the wood, and I will not light the fire." ²⁴ "Then call upon the name of your gods, and I will call upon the name of the LORD. The god who answers by fire is the true God." All the people said, "Good!" ²⁵ Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, "Choose for yourselves one bull and prepare it first, for you are the many. Call upon the name of your god, but do not light the fire." ²⁶ So they took the bull that was given to them, and they prepared it, and they called upon the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, "O Baal, hear us!" But there was no voice and no answer. They danced around the altar they had built. ²⁷ At noon, Elijah mocked them and said, "Call louder, for he is a god. Perhaps he is meditating or in a deep sleep, or perhaps he is away and will return." ²⁸ So they cried louder, and they cut themselves with swords and lances until the blood flowed over them. ²⁹ They prophesied until the time of the evening offering, but there was no voice, no answer, and no one listening. ³⁰ Elijah said to all the people, "Come to me." All the people came to him, and he repaired the altar of the LORD that had been torn down. ³¹ Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, as the LORD had told him, saying, "Israel will be your name." ³² He built with the stones an altar in the name of the LORD, and he dug a trench around it large enough to hold two seahs of grain. ³³ He arranged the wood and cut up the bull and placed it on the wood. ³⁴ He said, "Fill four water jars and pour it on the offering and on the wood." He said, "Do it again," and they did so. "And again," he said, and they did. ³⁵ The water flowed around the altar and even filled the trench with water. ³⁶ At the time of the evening offering, Elijah the prophet went near and said, "O LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known today that You are God in Israel, and I am Your servant, and by Your word I have done all these things." ³⁷ "Answer me, O LORD, answer me, so that this people may know that You, O LORD, are God, and that You have turned their hearts back." ³⁸ Then fire from the LORD fell and consumed the offering, the wood, the stones, the dust, and the water in the trench. ³⁹ All the people saw it and fell on their faces, saying, "The LORD is God! The LORD is God!" ⁴⁰ Then Elijah said to them, "Seize the prophets of Baal; let none of them escape." So they seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the Wadi Kishon, and there he slaughtered them. ⁴¹ Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up, eat and drink, for the sound of the rushing of the rain is coming." ⁴² Ahab went up to eat and drink, while Elijah went to the top of Mount Carmel. He bowed himself to the ground and put his face between his knees. ⁴³ He said to his servant, "Go up now, look toward the sea." He went up and looked, and said, "I see nothing." Elijah said, "Go again seven times." ⁴⁴ On the seventh time, he said, "Look! A cloud as small as a man's hand is rising from the sea." Elijah said, "Go and tell Ahab to harness his chariot and go down, or the rain will overtake you." ⁴⁵ So it came to pass, as Elijah was speaking, that the sky darkened with clouds and wind, and there was a great rainstorm. Ahab rode off to Jezreel. ⁴⁶ The hand of the LORD was on Elijah, and he tightened his belt and ran ahead of Ahab, arriving before him at Jezreel.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "gave them bread and water" — this is a literal rendering of the Hebrew, reflecting the verb "kilkalam" from "lechem" and "mayim." No forced glossary departure.
- v. 39: "The LORD is God! The LORD is God!" — the phrase is repeated in the Hebrew for emphasis, hence the repetition in English.
- v. 40: "Wadi Kishon" — the Hebrew "Nachal Kishon" is translated according to the fixed convention for proper names.
