# 1 Kings 2

¹ The days of David were near death, and he called to Solomon his son, saying: ² "I am going the way of all the earth. Be strong, and be a man. ³ And you must keep the charge of the Lord your God, walking in His ways, keeping His statutes, His commandments, His judgments, and His testimonies, as it is written in the Torah of Moses, so that you may know what you must do and what you must turn aside to. ⁴ So that the Lord may fulfill His word, which He spoke to me, saying, 'If your children keep their way to walk before Me in truth, with all their hearts and with all their souls, then I will not remove from you a man from the throne of Israel.' ⁵ And you know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel, to Abner son of Ner and to Amasa son of Jether, and he killed them, and he put the blood of war in peace, and he put the blood of war in his belt that was on his waist and in his shoe that was on his foot. ⁶ And you must do with your wisdom and must not let his gray head go down to the grave in peace. ⁷ And to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, you must do kindness, and they will be at your table for eating, because they came near to me when I fled from the face of Absalom your brother. ⁸ And behold, with you is Shimei son of Gera son of the right hand of the young men, and he cursed me with a cursed curse on the day I went out to the camp, and he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, 'If I do not kill you with the sword.' ⁹ And now you must not clear him, for you are a wise man, and you will know what you must do to him, and you must bring down his gray head in blood to Sheol." ¹⁰ And David lay down with his fathers and was buried in the city of David. ¹¹ And the days that David reigned over Israel were forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. ¹² And Solomon sat on the throne of David his father, and his kingdom was firmly established. ¹³ And Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon, and he bowed to her, and she said, "Peace to you," and he said, "Peace." ¹⁴ And he said, "I have a word for you," and she said to him, "Speak." ¹⁵ And he said, "You know that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel set their faces on me to make me king, and the kingdom has turned away and has become to my brother, for it came to him from the Lord." ¹⁶ And now one request I am asking from you, do not turn your face from me," and she said to him, "Speak." ¹⁷ And he said, "Say to King Solomon, 'Do not turn your face from me, and give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife.'" ¹⁸ And Bathsheba said, "Good, I will speak to the king on your behalf." ¹⁹ And Bathsheba came to King Solomon to speak to him about Adonijah, and the king rose to meet her and kissed her, and he sat on his throne, and they placed a throne for the mother of the king, and she sat on his right. ²⁰ And she said, "One small request I am asking from you, do not turn your face from me," and the king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for I will not turn away your face." ²¹ And she said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother as a wife." ²² And King Solomon answered and said to his mother, "And why are you asking Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask for him the kingdom, for he is my brother, greater than I, and to him and to Abiathar the priest and to Joab son of Zeruiah, who is with him." ²³ And the king Solomon swore by the Lord, saying, "So do to me the Lord, and so add to it, that Adonijah spoke this word from his soul." ²⁴ And now the Lord lives, who prepared me and placed me on the throne of David my father, and He who did for me a house, as the Lord spoke, that today Adonijah must die." ²⁵ And the king Solomon sent in the hand of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he struck him, and Adonijah died that day. ²⁶ And to Abiathar the priest the king said, "Go to your town, Anathoth, to your fields, for you are a man of death today, and I will not kill you today, because you carried the ark of the Lord before my father, and because you mourned in all that my father mourned." ²⁷ And Solomon cast out Abiathar from being a priest to the Lord, to fill the word of the Lord, which He spoke over the house of Eli in Shiloh. ²⁸ And the news reached Joab, that Joab had leaned toward Adonijah, and he did not lean toward Absalom. And Joab fled to the tent of the Lord and held on to the horns of the altar. ²⁹ And it was told to King Solomon, saying, "Joab has fled to the tent of the Lord and is holding on to the horns of the altar." And Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, strike him." ³⁰ And Benaiah came to the tent of the Lord and said to him, "Thus says the king, 'Come out.'" And Joab said, "No, for here I will die." And Benaiah returned to the king and said, "Thus spoke Joab, and thus answered me." ³¹ And the king said to him, "Go and do to him as he said, strike him and bury him, and remove the guilt of the innocent blood that Joab shed from me and from the house of my father." ³² And the Lord will return his blood on his head, because he struck two men, righteous and good, and killed them with the sword, and my father David did not know the blood of Abner son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and the blood of Amasa son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah. ³³ And their blood will return on his head and on the head of his seed forever, and to David and to his seed and to his house and to his throne there will be peace forever from the Lord." ³⁴ And Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up and struck him and killed him, and he was buried in his house in the wilderness. ³⁵ And the king placed Benaiah son of Jehoiada over him as commander of the army, and Solomon placed Zadok the priest in the place of Abiathar. ³⁶ And the king sent and called Shimei, and said to him, "Build for yourself a house in Jerusalem and live there, and do not go out anywhere." ³⁷ And it will be, in the day of your going out and crossing the stream Kidron, you will know that you will die, and your blood will be on your head." ³⁸ And Shimei said to the king, "The word is good, as my lord the king has spoken, so will your servant do." And Shimei lived in Jerusalem many days. ³⁹ And it happened after three years, and two servants of Shimei fled to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath, and they told Shimei, saying, "Behold, your servants are in Gath." ⁴⁰ And Shimei rose, put a saddle on his donkey, and went to Gath to Achish to seek his servants. And Shimei went and brought back his servants from Gath. ⁴¹ And it was told to Solomon, saying, "Shimei has gone from Jerusalem to Gath and has returned with his servants." ⁴² And the king sent and called Shimei, and said to him, "Did I not swear to you by the Lord and testify to you, saying, 'In the day you go out from Jerusalem and go to the right or to the left, you will know that you will die,' and you said to me, 'The word is good, I have heard.'" ⁴³ And why have you not kept the oath of the Lord and the commandment I gave you?" ⁴⁴ And the king said to Shimei, "You know all the evil in your heart that you did to David my father. And the Lord will return your evil on your head. ⁴⁵ And King Solomon is blessed, and the throne of David will be established before the Lord forever." ⁴⁶ And the king commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out and struck him, and he died. And the kingdom was firmly established in the hand of Solomon.

Notes  
- v. 3: "mishmeret" is translated as "charge" to reflect its meaning as a duty or responsibility.  
- v. 10: The Hebrew uses "kavod" for "buried," which is more naturally rendered as "was buried" than "was honored."  
- v. 15: "hashalom" is translated as "Peace" to reflect the customary greeting.  
- v. 23: "lo yasiv et panav" is translated as "will not turn your face from me" to reflect the idiomatic expression.  
- v. 37: "nachal Kidron" is translated as "stream Kidron" to reflect the geographical reference.  
- v. 38: "tov hadavar" is translated as "The word is good" to reflect the approval and agreement.  
- v. 45: "baruch ve'kise David" is translated as "King Solomon is blessed" to reflect the context of the kingdom's stability.
