# 1 Kings 16

¹ The word of the LORD came to Jehu son of Hanani to Baasha, saying: ² "Because I exalted you from the dust and made you ruler over My people Israel, and you walked in the way of Jeroboam and caused My people Israel to sin by provoking Me to wrath with their sins, ³ behold, I will raise up calamity over Baasha and over his house. I will cut off the house of Baasha, just like I cut off the house of Jeroboam son of Nabat. ⁴ The one who dies in the city, the dogs will eat, and the one who dies in the field, the birds of the heavens will eat." ⁵ The rest of the deeds of Baasha and all that he did and his valor—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ⁶ Then Baasha lay down with his ancestors and was buried in Tirzah, and his son Elah became king in his place. ⁷ Also, by the hand of Jehu the prophet, the word of the LORD came to Baasha and to his house, for all the evil that he did in the eyes of the LORD, by provoking Him to wrath with the works of his hands, to be like the house of Jeroboam, and because he struck him. ⁸ In the twenty-sixth year of Asa king of Judah, Elah son of Baasha reigned over Israel in Tirzah for two years. ⁹ Then his servant Zimri, the commander of half the chariots, struck him down while he was in Tirzah, drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza, the palace overseer in Tirzah. ¹⁰ So Zimri came and struck him down and killed him in the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, and became king in his place. ¹¹ When he began to reign, while he was seated on the throne, he struck down the entire house of Baasha, leaving no one alive, neither a survivor on the wall nor a relative nor a friend. ¹² So Zimri destroyed the whole house of Baasha, as the LORD had spoken by His mouth to Baasha through the prophet Jehu. ¹³ This was for all the sins of Baasha and the sins of Elah his son, which they had committed and by which they led Israel to sin, provoking the anger of the LORD, the God of Israel, with their vanities. ¹⁴ The rest of the deeds of Elah and all that he did—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ¹⁵ In the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, Zimri reigned for seven days in Tirzah. The people who were with him were encamped at Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines. ¹⁶ When the people who were with him heard, saying, "Zimri has conspired and has also killed the king," they made Omri the commander of the army king over Israel that very day in the camp. ¹⁷ So Omri went up, along with all Israel with him, from Gibbethon and laid siege to Tirzah. ¹⁸ When Zimri saw that the city had been captured, he went into the fortress of the king's palace and set the palace on fire over himself and died. ¹⁹ Because of his sins, he had done evil in the eyes of the LORD by walking in the way of Jeroboam and in the sins he had committed, leading Israel to sin. ²⁰ The rest of the deeds of Zimri and the conspiracy he made—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ²¹ Then the people of Israel were divided into two halves. Half the people followed Tibni son of Ginath to make him king, and half followed Omri. ²² But Omri's faction prevailed over Tibni son of Ginath's. Tibni died, and Omri became king. ²³ In the thirty-first year of Asa king of Judah, Omri reigned over Israel for twelve years in Tirzah. He reigned six years in Tirzah. ²⁴ He bought the hill of Samer from Shemer for two talents of silver and built it, and he named the city after the name of Shemer, the master of the hill, Samer-on. ²⁵ Omri did evil in the eyes of the LORD more than all who were before him. ²⁶ He walked in the way of Jeroboam son of Nabat and in the sins he had committed, leading Israel to sin by provoking the LORD, the God of Israel, with their vanities. ²⁷ The rest of the deeds of Omri and all that he did and his valor—surely they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. ²⁸ Then Omri lay down with his ancestors and was buried in Samer-on, and his son Ahab became king in his place. ²⁹ Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in the thirty-eighth year of Asa king of Judah. Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samer-on for twenty-two years. ³⁰ Ahab son of Omri did evil in the eyes of the LORD more than all who were before him. ³¹ He was not content to walk in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nabat, but he also took a wife, Izebel daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians, and went and served Baal and worshipped him. ³² He built an altar to Baal in the house of the Baal, which he built in Samer-on. ³³ Ahab also made an Asherah pole, and Ahab did more to provoke the anger of the LORD, the God of Israel, than all the kings of Israel who were before him. ³⁴ In his days, Hiel the Bethelite built Jericho. He laid its foundation in Abiram his firstborn, and he set up its gates in Segub his youngest son, according to the word of the LORD spoken by Joshua son of Nun.

Notes
- v. 1: "the word of the LORD came to Jehu son of Hanani to Baasha" — the Hebrew structure is elliptical; the verb is directed both to Jehu and to Baasha, indicating that the message through Jehu is addressed to Baasha.
- v. 3: "I will cut off the house of Baasha, just like I cut off the house of Jeroboam" — the Hebrew uses the same verb and structure as in 1 Kings 14:10–11, indicating a direct comparison.
- v. 9: "drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza" — "drunk" is a translation of the Hebrew "shikhot," meaning "drunkenness," used here to describe the state of Elah at the time of his death.
- v. 16: "made Omri the commander of the army king over Israel" — the Hebrew implies that Omri was anointed king by the people in the camp, not a formal coronation.
- v. 30: "He was not content to walk in the sins of Jeroboam" — the Hebrew suggests a progression beyond the sins of Jeroboam.
- v. 34: "Hiel the Bethelite built Jericho" — the Hebrew specifies that the foundation and the gates were laid by his sons, as per the curse in Joshua 6:26.
