# 1 Kings 1

¹ King David was old and had grown up in years, and they wrapped him in garments, but he was not warm. ² His servants said to him, “Let my lord the king seek a young maiden for himself, and let her stand before the king and serve him. Let her warm his body, and let her lie in his arms.” ³ They searched for a beautiful maiden throughout all the territory of Israel, and they found Abishag the Shunammite, and they brought her to the king. ⁴ The maiden was very beautiful, and she served the king and attended to him. But the king did not know her. ⁵ Adonijah, the son of Haggith, was asserting himself, saying, “I will be king.” He prepared chariots and horsemen for himself, and fifty men to run before him. ⁶ His father had never stopped him from any of his actions, not even saying, “Why have you done this?” For he was very handsome in appearance, and he was born after Absalom. ⁷ His words were with Joab, the son of Zeruiah, and with Abiathar the priest, and they supported Adonijah. ⁸ But Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Nathan the prophet, and Shimei, and Rei, and the mighty men of David, were not with Adonijah. ⁹ Adonijah offered sacrifices, oxen and fattened calves and sheep in abundance, on the stone at Gihon, which is beside En Rogel. He invited all his brothers, the sons of the king, and all the men of Judah who were the king’s servants. ¹⁰ But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, or Benaiah, or the mighty men, or his brother Solomon. ¹¹ Nathan said to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon, “Have you heard that Adonijah the son of Haggith has become king, while our lord David does not know it?” ¹² “Now please listen to me,” Nathan said to her, “and let me give you advice to save your life and the life of your son Solomon.” ¹³ “Go in to the king, my lord the king,” Nathan said to her, “and tell him, ‘Did you not swear to your servant, saying, “Solomon your son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne”? Why then has Adonijah become king?’ ¹⁴ While you are still speaking there with the king, I will come in after you and fulfill your words.” ¹⁵ Bathsheba went in to the king, who was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was serving the king. ¹⁶ Bathsheba bowed and prostrated herself before the king, and the king said, “What do you want?” ¹⁷ She said to him, “My lord, you swore to your servant by the Lord your God, saying, ‘Solomon your son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.’ ¹⁸ But now, my lord the king, you do not know it, and Adonijah has become king. ¹⁹ He has slaughtered oxen and fattened calves and sheep in abundance, and he has invited all the sons of the king, and Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the army, but he did not invite your servant Solomon. ²⁰ And you, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are on you, to tell them who will sit on the throne of the king, your lord, after him. ²¹ And when my lord the king lies with your ancestors, I and my son Solomon will be guilty.” ²² While she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet came in. ²³ They told the king, “Here is Nathan the prophet.” He came before the king and bowed to the king on his face to the ground. ²⁴ Nathan said, “My lord the king, you said, ‘Adonijah shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.’ ²⁵ But today he has gone down and slaughtered oxen and fattened calves and sheep in abundance, and he has invited all the sons of the king, and the commanders of the army, and Abiathar the priest. They are eating and drinking before him, and they say, ‘Long live King Adonijah.’ ²⁶ But me, your servant, and Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Solomon your servant, he has not invited. ²⁷ If this matter has come from my lord the king, and you have not made it known to your servant, who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?” ²⁸ King David answered and said, “Call Bathsheba to me.” She came in before the king and stood before the king. ²⁹ The king swore and said, “As the Lord lives, who has delivered my life from every distress, ³⁰ as I swore to you by the Lord, the God of Israel, saying, ‘Solomon your son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne,’ so I will do today.” ³¹ Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground and prostrated herself before the king, and said, “Long live my lord the king, David, forever.” ³² King David said, “Call to me Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada.” They came before the king. ³³ The king said to them, “Take with you the servants of your lord and bring my son Solomon on the mule that belongs to me, and take him down to Gihon. ³⁴ And let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him there as king over Israel, and let you blow the trumpet and say, ‘Long live King Solomon!’ ³⁵ Then you shall go up after him, and he will come and sit on my throne, and he shall reign in my place, and I have commanded that he shall be prince over Israel and over Judah.” ³⁶ Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king and said, “Amen! May the Lord, the God of my lord the king, confirm it. ³⁷ As the Lord has been with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and may He make his throne greater than the throne of my lord the king David.” ³⁸ Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada and the Kerethites and the Pelethites went down and placed Solomon on the mule of King David, and they led him to Gihon. ³⁹ Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent and anointed Solomon. Then they blew the trumpet, and all the people said, “Long live King Solomon!” ⁴⁰ All the people followed him, and the people were playing on flutes and rejoicing with great joy, and the earth split with their sound. ⁴¹ Adonijah and all the guests with him heard it, and they had just finished eating. Joab heard the sound of the trumpet and said, “What is the reason for the noise in the city, the uproar?” ⁴² While he was still speaking, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came in, and said to Adonijah, “Come in, for you are a man of valor, and good news is with you.” ⁴³ Jonathan answered and said to Adonijah, “But our lord the king, David, has made Solomon king.” ⁴⁴ The king has sent with him Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet, and they have placed him on the mule of the king, ⁴⁵ and Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him king at Gihon, and they have gone up from there rejoicing, and the city is in tumult. That is the sound you have heard.” ⁴⁶ Solomon had already taken his place on the throne of the kingdom. ⁴⁷ The servants of the king also came in to bless King David their lord, saying, “May God make the name of Solomon better than your name, and may He make his throne greater than your throne.” The king bowed on the bed. ⁴⁸ And he said, “Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who has given me today a ruler to sit on my throne, and my eyes see it.” ⁴⁹ Then all the guests who had come to Adonijah got up and went each to his own house. ⁵⁰ Adonijah was afraid of Solomon and arose and went, and he took hold of the horns of the altar. ⁵¹ It was reported to Solomon, saying, “Here, Adonijah is afraid of the king, Solomon, and he has taken hold of the horns of the altar, saying, ‘Let the king, Solomon, today swear to me that he will not kill his servant with the sword.’” ⁵² Solomon said, “If he were a man of valor, not a single hair of his head would fall to the ground, but if wickedness is found in him, he will die.” ⁵³ The king sent and brought him down from the altar, and he came and bowed to King Solomon. Solomon said to him, “Go to your house.”

Notes
- v. 53: The translation of "κατήνεγκεν αὐτὸν ἀπάνωθεν τοῦ θυσιαστηρίου" in the Septuagint is used to guide the translation of the Hebrew, which is ambiguous in how Adonijah was removed from the altar.
