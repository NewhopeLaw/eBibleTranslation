# 1 Kings 6

¹ In the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites left the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign over Israel, in the second month, which is Ziv, the LORD’s house was built. ² The house that King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits long and twenty cubits wide and thirty cubits high. ³ The porch in front of the main hall was twenty cubits long, in line with the width of the house, ten cubits wide. ⁴ He made windows with lattice work in the house. ⁵ He built a wall for the house all around, with side rooms surrounding the main hall and the inner chamber, and he carved decorative beams all around. ⁶ The lower story was five cubits wide, the middle story six cubits wide, and the third story seven cubits wide, because he gave the house a projecting structure all around to prevent the walls from supporting the load. ⁷ The house was built with fully dressed stones, cut precisely and hewn, so that no hammer, chisel, or any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built. ⁸ The entrance to the middle story was at the side of the house on the right, with spiral staircases going up to the middle story and from there to the third story. ⁹ So Solomon built the house and completed it. ¹⁰ He built the side rooms around the whole house, each five cubits high, and he fastened the house with cedar beams. ¹¹ The word of the LORD came to Solomon, saying: ¹² This house you are building—if you walk in my statutes and do my judgments, and keep all my commandments by walking in them, then I will establish my word with you, which I spoke to your father David. ¹³ I will dwell among the children of Israel and will not forsake my people Israel. ¹⁴ So Solomon built the house and completed it. ¹⁵ He built the inner walls of the house with cedar, from the floor to the beams and the walls, inside and out; he covered the floor of the house with panels of cypress wood. ¹⁶ He built twenty cubits of the house with cedar panels from the floor to the beams, and he built from the house into the inner chamber, the Holy of Holies. ¹⁷ The house itself was forty cubits long, in front. ¹⁸ He lined the inside of the house with cedarwood, with carved designs and openwork, all cedar—no stone was visible. ¹⁹ He prepared the inner chamber in the middle of the house to place there the ark of the covenant of the LORD. ²⁰ In front of the inner chamber, twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide and twenty cubits high, he overlaid it with pure gold and made an altar of cedarwood. ²¹ Solomon overlaid the inside of the house with pure gold, and he made gold chains in front of the inner chamber and overlaid it with gold. ²² He overlaid the whole house with gold, until the whole house and all the altar in the inner chamber were covered with gold. ²³ He made two cherubim of carved wood, ten cubits high. ²⁴ The wingspan of one cherub was five cubits, and the wingspan of the other cherub was also five cubits, making ten cubits from one wingtip to the other. ²⁵ The height of both cherubim was the same—ten cubits—measured in one standard. ²⁶ The height of the first cherub was ten cubits, and the height of the second cherub was ten cubits. ²⁷ He placed the cherubim in the innermost part of the house, and they spread out their wings, so that one wing touched one wall and the other wing touched the opposite wall, with their wings touching each other in the middle of the house. ²⁸ He overlaid the cherubim with gold. ²⁹ He carved all the walls of the house all around—on the inside and outside—with carved cherubim, palm trees, and openwork designs. ³⁰ He overlaid the floor of the house with gold, inside and out. ³¹ For the entrance to the inner chamber, he made doors of carved olive wood, with five panels. ³² He made two doors of olive wood, carved with cherubim, palm trees, and openwork designs, and overlaid them with gold; he applied gold to the cherubim and to the palm trees. ³³ He made doors for the porch in the same way, with olive wood, with four panels. ³⁴ He made two doors of cypress wood, each with two panels; the first door had two panels, and the second door also had two panels. ³⁵ He carved cherubim, palm trees, and openwork designs and overlaid them with gold, applied evenly over the carved surfaces. ³⁶ He built the inner courtyard with three rows of hewn stone and a row of cedar beams. ³⁷ In the fourth year, the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid in the month of Ziv. ³⁸ In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, which is the eighth month, the house was completed in all its details and according to all its specifications. Solomon had taken seven years to build it.

Notes
- v. 38: The Septuagint adds additional details not found in the Hebrew text, but the Hebrew is clear and complete in its account of the construction timeline.
