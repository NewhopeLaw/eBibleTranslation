# 1 Kings 8

¹ Solomon then gathered the elders of Israel, the heads of the tribes and the leaders of the fathers' houses of the Israelites, to King Solomon in Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the Lord from the city of David, which is Zion. ² All the men of Israel gathered to the king in the month of Ethanim, at the festival, which is the seventh month. ³ The elders of Israel came, and the Levitical priests carried the ark. ⁴ They brought up the ark of the Lord, the tent of meeting, and all the holy vessels that were in the tent. The Levitical priests and the Levites brought them up. ⁵ King Solomon and the entire assembly of Israel, who had gathered with him, stood before the ark, sacrificing sheep and oxen in such great numbers that they could not be counted or told. ⁶ The Levitical priests brought the ark of the covenant of the Lord to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the temple, into the Most Holy Place, beneath the wings of the cherubim. ⁷ For the cherubim spread their wings over the place of the ark, covering the ark and its poles from above. ⁸ The poles were long enough that their ends were visible in the holy place, in front of the inner sanctuary, but not seen from outside. And they have remained there to this day. ⁹ Nothing was in the ark except the two stone tablets that Moses placed there at Horeb, when the Lord made a covenant with the Israelites after they came out of the land of Egypt. ¹⁰ When the Levitical priests had come out of the Most Holy Place, the cloud filled the house of the Lord. ¹¹ The Levitical priests could not stand to minister because the cloud had filled the house of the Lord, for the glory of the Lord filled the house. ¹² Then Solomon said, "The Lord has said that He would dwell in thick darkness. ¹³ I have now built for You a house of dwelling, a permanent place where You may rest forever." ¹⁴ The king turned around and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, and all the people of Israel stood. ¹⁵ He said, "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who has spoken with His mouth to David my father and fulfilled it with His own hand, saying: ¹⁶ Since the day I brought My people Israel out of Egypt, I did not choose a city from any of the tribes of Israel to build a house for My name to be there. But I chose David to rule over My people Israel. ¹⁷ It was David my father's desire to build a house for the name of the Lord, the God of Israel. ¹⁸ And the Lord said to David my father, 'Because it was in your heart to build a house for My name, you have done well. Yet you shall not build the house, but your son who will come from your body—he will build the house for My name.'" ¹⁹ The Lord has kept His promise that He spoke, and I have risen in place of David my father and have sat on the throne of Israel, as the Lord promised. I have built the house for the name of the Lord, the God of Israel. ²⁰ I have set a place there for the ark, where the covenant the Lord made with our ancestors when He brought them out of the land of Egypt is kept. ²¹ Solomon stood before the altar of the Lord, in front of the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands to heaven. ²² He said, "Lord, God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven above or on earth below, who keeps covenant and steadfast love for Your servants who walk before You with all their hearts. ²³ You have kept what You promised to Your servant David my father, and You have spoken with Your mouth and fulfilled it as this day. ²⁴ And now, Lord, God of Israel, keep for Your servant David my father what You promised him, saying, 'No man shall ever be cut off from you sitting on the throne of Israel, if only your children are careful to walk in My ways, as you have walked before Me.'" ²⁵ And now, God of Israel, please confirm Your word to Your servant David my father. ²⁶ For can God really dwell on the earth? Indeed, the heavens and the highest heavens cannot contain You. How much less this house that I have built! ²⁷ Yet turn Your attention to the prayer of Your servant and to his plea, Lord my God, to listen to the song and the prayer that Your servant is praying before You this day. ²⁸ Let Your eyes be open toward this house night and day, toward the place about which You said, 'My name will be there,' to listen to the prayer that Your servant is praying toward this place. ²⁹ And listen to the plea of Your servant and of Your people Israel, when they pray toward this place. From Your dwelling in heaven, hear and forgive. ³⁰ If someone sins against another and is made to swear an oath to him, and comes and swears before Your altar in this house, ³¹ then You, from heaven, hear and act, and judge Your servants, condemning the guilty by bringing his conduct on his own head, and vindicating the righteous by giving him his due. ³² When Your people Israel are struck before an enemy because they have sinned against You and return to You, confessing Your name, and pray and make supplication before this house, ³³ then You, from heaven, hear and forgive the sin of Your people Israel, and bring them back to the land You gave to their ancestors. ³⁴ When the heavens are shut up and there is no rain because they have sinned against You, and they pray toward this place, confessing Your name and turning from their sin because You have afflicted them, ³⁵ then You, from heaven, hear and forgive the sin of Your servants and Your people Israel, for You show them the good way to walk in, and send rain on Your land which You have given as an inheritance to Your people. ³⁶ If there is famine in the land, plague, blight, mildew, locusts, or army caterpillars; if their enemy lays siege to their cities; or if there is any disease, any plague, ³⁷ any prayer or plea from any man, from all Your people Israel, who each knows their own trouble and spreads out their hands toward this house, ³⁸ then You, from heaven, Your dwelling place, hear and forgive, and act, giving to each man according to all his ways, according to the knowledge of his heart—for You alone know the hearts of all the sons of men. ³⁹ So that they may fear You all the days they live on the land You gave to our ancestors. ⁴⁰ And also for the foreigner who is not of Your people Israel, who comes from a distant land for Your name's sake, ⁴¹ because they hear of Your great name, Your mighty hand and outstretched arm, and come and pray toward this house, ⁴² then You, from heaven, Your dwelling place, act on all that the foreigner calls to You for, that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name and fear You, like Your people Israel, and know that this house I have built is called by Your name. ⁴³ When Your people go out to war against their enemies, along the road You send them, and they pray to the Lord toward the city You have chosen and the house I have built for Your name, ⁴⁴ then You, from heaven, hear their prayer and their plea and act in judgment for them. ⁴⁵ For indeed, no man is without sin, and You turn them over to the enemy, and they are taken captive to a distant or near land. ⁴⁶ But if they repent in the land where they have been carried away, and pray with all their heart and soul toward their captors' land, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done evil, we have acted wickedly,' ⁴⁷ and they return to You with all their heart and all their soul in the land of their captors, and pray toward the land You gave to their ancestors, the city You have chosen, and the house I have built for Your name, ⁴⁸ then You, from heaven, Your dwelling place, hear their prayer and their plea and act in judgment for them. ⁴⁹ And You forgive Your people who have sinned against You and all their transgressions they have committed against You, and You give them compassion before those who have taken them captive, so they may give them compassion. ⁵⁰ For they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out of Egypt from the iron furnace. ⁵¹ Let Your eyes be open to the plea of Your servant and to the plea of Your people Israel, to listen to them in every time they call to You. ⁵² For You have set them apart for Yourself, from all the peoples of the earth, just as You spoke by the hand of Your servant Moses, when You brought our ancestors out of Egypt, Lord Yahweh. ⁵³ And it happened when Solomon had finished praying all this prayer and plea to the Lord, he rose from before the altar of the Lord, having knelt on his knees with his hands spread out to heaven. ⁵⁴ Then he stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel with a loud voice, saying, ⁵⁵ "Blessed be the Lord who has given rest to His people Israel, just as He promised, not one word of His good promise failed. ⁵⁶ May the Lord our God be with us, as He was with our ancestors. May He not abandon us or forsake us. ⁵⁷ Turn our hearts to Him to walk in all His ways and to keep His commandments, statutes, and judgments, which He commanded our ancestors. ⁵⁸ And let these words of mine, which I have pleaded before the Lord, be near the Lord our God day and night, to act in the judgment of His servant and the judgment of His people Israel, every day. ⁵⁹ So that all the peoples of the earth may know that the Lord is God, and there is none other. ⁶⁰ And may your hearts be whole with the Lord our God, to walk in His statutes and keep His commandments, as this day. ⁶¹ And the king and all Israel with him offered sacrifices before the Lord. ⁶² Solomon offered the peace offerings, the sacrifices he had made to the Lord—twenty-two thousand oxen and a hundred twenty thousand sheep. And the king and all the Israelites dedicated the house of the Lord. ⁶³ On that day the king dedicated the inner court that was in front of the house of the Lord, for there he had made the burnt offering, the grain offering, and the fat of the peace offerings. For the bronze altar before the Lord was too small to hold the burnt offering, the grain offering, and the fat of the peace offerings. ⁶⁴ So Solomon celebrated the feast at that time, and all Israel with him—a great assembly, from Lebo Hamath to the Brook of Egypt—before the Lord our God for seven days. Then for seven days more, fourteen days in all. ⁶⁵ On the eighth day he sent the people away, and they blessed the king and went to their homes, joyful and glad of heart for all the goodness the Lord had done for David His servant and for Israel His people. ⁶⁶ So Solomon completed the temple, and the king and all Israel with him were in it. They offered sacrifices before the Lord from the eighth day onward until the fifteenth day of the month, and then they went to their homes, joyful and glad of heart for all the goodness the Lord had done for David His servant and for Israel His people.

Notes
- v. 26: "can God really dwell on the earth?" reflects the rhetorical tone of the original.
- v. 30: "made to swear an oath to him" is a contextual rendering of נָשָׁא־בוֹ אָלָה.
- v. 44: "they are taken captive to a distant or near land" reflects the ambiguity of the Hebrew regarding the location of captivity.
- v. 46: "if they repent in the land where they have been carried away" is a nuanced rendering of the conditional structure in Hebrew.
