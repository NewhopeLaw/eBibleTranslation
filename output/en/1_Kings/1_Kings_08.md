# 1 Kings 8

¹ Then Solomon gathered the elders of Israel, and all the heads of the tribes, the leaders of the ancestral houses of the people of Israel, to King Solomon in Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the city of David, that is, Zion. ² And all the men of Israel gathered to King Solomon in the month of Ethanim, at the time of the festival, that is, the seventh month. ³ Then the elders of Israel came, and the priests carried the ark. ⁴ They brought up the ark of the LORD and the tent of meeting and all the holy vessels that were in the tent. The priests and the Levites brought them up. ⁵ King Solomon and the whole assembly of Israel, gathered with him, were before the ark, offering sacrifices of sheep and oxen so numerous they could not be counted or numbered for their abundance. ⁶ The priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the temple, into the Most Holy Place, under the wings of the cherubim. ⁷ For the cherubim spread out their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered the ark and its poles from above. ⁸ The poles were so long that their ends could be seen from the holy place, in front of the inner sanctuary, but not from outside. They remain there to this day. ⁹ Nothing was in the ark except the two stone tablets that Moses had placed there at Horeb, the tablets upon which the LORD had made a covenant with the people of Israel when they came out of the land of Egypt. ¹⁰ When the priests came out from the Most Holy Place, the cloud filled the house of the LORD. ¹¹ The priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD had filled the house of the LORD. ¹² Then Solomon said, "The LORD has said that he would dwell in thick darkness. ¹³ I have built you a glorious house, a place for you to dwell forever." ¹⁴ The king turned around and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, and all the assembly of Israel stood before him. ¹⁵ He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who has spoken with his mouth to my father David and fulfilled it with his hand, saying: ¹⁶ Since the day I brought my people Israel up from Egypt, I did not choose a city from all the tribes of Israel to build a house for my name, but I chose David to be over my people Israel. ¹⁷ It was in my father David's heart to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. ¹⁸ And the LORD said to my father David, 'Because you had this good desire to build a house for my name, you did well, for your heart was right. ¹⁹ Yet you shall not build the house. It is your son who will be born to you. He will build the house for my name.' ²⁰ And the LORD has kept his promise, which he made. I have risen as David's successor, and I sit on the throne of Israel, just as the LORD promised. I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. ²¹ I have placed there a place for the ark, which contains the covenant the LORD made with our ancestors when he brought them out of the land of Egypt." ²² Then Solomon stood before the altar of the LORD in front of the entire assembly of Israel and spread out his hands to heaven. ²³ He said, "O LORD, the God of Israel, there is no god like you in heaven above or on earth below, who keeps covenant and steadfast love for your servants who walk before you with all their hearts. ²⁴ You have kept your word to your servant David, your father, just as you spoke with your mouth and fulfilled with your hand, even as it is this day. ²⁵ And now, O LORD, the God of Israel, keep for your servant David, my father, what you promised him, saying, 'You will not lack a man before me sitting on the throne of Israel, as long as your children guard their ways to walk before me as you have walked before me.' ²⁶ And now, O God of Israel, let your word, which you spoke to your servant David, my father, be confirmed." ²⁷ "Is it indeed true that God dwells on earth? Look, even the heavens and the highest heavens cannot contain you, how much less this house I have built!" ²⁸ "Turn your attention to the prayer of your servant and his plea, O LORD my God, to listen to the song and the prayer that your servant is offering before you this day. ²⁹ Let your eyes be open to this house day and night, to the place of which you said, 'My name shall be there,' to listen to the prayer your servant offers toward this place. ³⁰ And hear the plea of your servant and your people Israel, whenever they pray toward this place. And you, from the place of your dwelling in heaven, will hear and forgive." ³¹ "If someone sins against another and is made to swear an oath to him, and comes before your altar in this house. ³² Then you will hear from heaven and act, judging your servants, punishing the guilty by making their ways fall upon their own head, and vindicating the righteous by giving him his righteousness." ³³ "When your people Israel are struck down before their enemies because they have sinned against you, and they turn to you and confess your name and pray before this house. ³⁴ Then you will hear from heaven and forgive the sin of your people Israel and bring them back to the land you gave to their ancestors." ³⁵ "When the sky is closed, and there is no rain, because they have sinned against you, and they pray toward this place and confess your name and turn from their sins, because you answered them. ³⁶ Then you will hear from heaven and forgive the sin of your servants and your people Israel, for you show the good path in which they should walk, and send rain on your land that you gave to your people for their inheritance." ³⁷ "When famine comes to the land, plague, blight, mildew, or when an enemy attacks them at their gates, whatever plague or disease there is. ³⁸ All prayer, all plea from any person, from all your people Israel, each one who knows his trouble and spreads out his hands toward this house. ³⁹ Then you will hear from heaven, your dwelling place, and forgive, and act, and give to every person according to all his ways, for you know his heart, for you alone know the heart of all the children of humankind." ⁴⁰ "So that they may fear you all the days they live on the land that you gave to our ancestors." ⁴¹ "And also for the foreigner who is not of your people Israel, who comes from a distant land because of your name. ⁴² Because they will hear your great name and your strong hand and outstretched arm, and come and pray toward this house. ⁴³ Then you will hear from heaven, your dwelling place, and act in every way the foreigner calls to you, so that all the peoples of the earth may know your name, to fear you as your people Israel do, and know that your name is called on this house I have built." ⁴⁴ "When your people go out to war against their enemies along the road that you send them, and they pray to the LORD toward the city you have chosen and the house I have built for your name. ⁴⁵ Then you will hear from heaven their prayer and their plea and will act justly for them." ⁴⁶ "For they will inevitably sin against you, for there is no one who does not sin, and you will be angry with them and deliver them into the hand of their enemies, and they will be taken away to a distant or nearby land. ⁴⁷ Yet when they come to their senses in the land where they were taken captive, and turn back and pray to you in the land of their captors, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted wickedly.' ⁴⁸ And they return to you with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captors, and pray to you toward the land you gave to their ancestors, the city you chose, and the house I have built for your name. ⁴⁹ Then you will hear from heaven, your dwelling place, their prayer and their plea and will act justly for them. ⁵⁰ And you will forgive your people who have sinned against you and all their transgressions which they have committed against you, and you will give them compassion in the sight of their captors, and they will show compassion on them." ⁵¹ "For they are your people and your inheritance, whom you brought out of the land of Egypt from the midst of the iron furnace. ⁵² Let your eyes be open to the plea of your servant and the plea of your people Israel, to listen to them whenever they call to you. ⁵³ For you have set them apart for yourself as your inheritance from all the peoples of the earth, just as you spoke through your servant Moses when you brought our ancestors out of Egypt, O Lord." ⁵⁴ Now it happened that when Solomon had finished offering all his prayer and plea to the LORD, he rose up from before the altar of the LORD, having been on his knees with his hands spread out to heaven. ⁵⁵ Then he stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel with a loud voice, saying: ⁵⁶ "Blessed be the LORD who has given rest to his people Israel, not one word of all his good promises has failed, all of which he spoke by the hand of his servant Moses." ⁵⁷ "Let the LORD our God be with us as he was with our ancestors, not abandoning us, not forsaking us. ⁵⁸ To turn our hearts toward him, to walk in all his ways, to keep his commandments, his statutes, and his ordinances, which he commanded our ancestors. ⁵⁹ And let my words, which I have pleaded before the LORD, be near to the LORD our God day and night, to act justly for his servant and his people Israel, day by day." ⁶⁰ "So that all the peoples of the earth may know that the LORD is God, and there is none else." ⁶¹ "And may your heart be whole with the LORD our God, to walk in his statutes and to keep his commandments, just as this day." ⁶² Then the king and all Israel with him offered sacrifices before the LORD. ⁶³ Solomon offered the peace offerings, the sacrifices of well-being to the LORD, twenty-two thousand oxen and one hundred twenty thousand sheep, and the king and all the people of Israel dedicated the house of the LORD. ⁶⁴ On that day the king dedicated the inner court before the house of the LORD, for there the burnt offerings, the grain offerings, and the fat of the peace offerings were made, because the bronze altar was too small to hold the burnt offerings, the grain offerings, and the fat of the peace offerings. ⁶⁵ At that time Solomon held the festival, and all Israel with him, a great assembly from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt, before the LORD our God, for seven days, and then another seven days, fourteen days. ⁶⁶ On the eighth day he sent the people away, and they blessed the king, and they went to their homes, happy and glad in heart because of all the good that the LORD had done for David his servant and for Israel his people.

Notes  
- v. 2: "Ethanim" is a Hebrew month; the translation follows the conventional rendering.  
- v. 3: "The priests carried the ark" refers to the Levitical priests, not necessarily Aaron’s lineage.  
- v. 27: The rhetorical question is rendered to retain the wonder and humility in Solomon’s prayer.  
- v. 54: "Kneeling on his knees" and "hands spread out to heaven" reflect the posture of prayer.  
- v. 64: "Mizbe'ach ha-nechoshet" is translated as "bronze altar" for clarity.
