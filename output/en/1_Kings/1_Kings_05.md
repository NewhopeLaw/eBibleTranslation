# 1 Kings 5

¹ Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the Euphrates River to the land of the Philistines and to the border of Egypt. The people of those lands brought tribute and service to Solomon all the days of his life. ² Now, the daily ration for Solomon was thirty kors of flour and sixty kors of fine wheat. ³ Ten oxen and twenty head of cattle, and a hundred sheep, not including the deer, gazelles, fallow deer, and fattened fowl. ⁴ For he had dominion over all the kings beyond the Euphrates, from Tiphsah to Gaza, and all the kings beyond the Euphrates brought peace to him, and there was peace on every side. ⁵ Judah and Israel lived in safety, each under his own vine and under his own fig tree, from Dan to Beer-sheba, all the days of Solomon. ⁶ Solomon had forty thousand stalls for chariots and twelve thousand horsemen. ⁷ The officials provided for the king and for all who came near the king's table, each month in order, without any lack. ⁸ The fodder and straw for the horses and the chariots came to the place where each man was stationed according to his assignment. ⁹ God gave Solomon wisdom and great discernment, and breadth of mind like the sand on the seashore. ¹⁰ Solomon's wisdom surpassed the wisdom of all the children of the east and the wisdom of Egypt. ¹¹ He was wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, Calcol, and Darda the sons of Mahol. And his fame spread to all the surrounding nations. ¹² Solomon spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs were five thousand in number. ¹³ He spoke about the trees, from the cedar of Lebanon to the hyssop that grows on the wall. He spoke about the animals and the birds, the creeping things and the sea creatures. ¹⁴ And people from all the nations came to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all the kings of the lands who had heard of his wisdom. ¹⁵ Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent his servants to Solomon, for he had heard that they had anointed him king in place of his father. For Hiram loved David all his days. ¹⁶ Solomon sent to Hiram, saying: ¹⁷ Do you know my father David, who could not build a house for the name of the LORD his God because of the wars that surrounded him until the LORD put them under his feet? ¹⁸ Now the LORD my God has given me peace on every side, no adversary and no misfortune. ¹⁹ And behold, I am determined to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, just as the LORD spoke to David my father, saying, "Your son, whom I will place on your throne, he will build the house for My name." ²⁰ So now, command and cause cedars to be cut for me from Lebanon, and my servants will be with your servants. I will pay your servants their wages according to all you say, for you know that we have no one who knows how to cut trees like the Sidonians. ²¹ And when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, he rejoiced greatly and said, "Blessed be the LORD today, who has given to David a wise son over this great people." ²² So Hiram sent to Solomon, saying, "I have heard the message you sent to me. I will do all your desire in cedar and in cypress trees. ²³ My servants will descend from Lebanon to the sea. I will set them in rafts at the sea until the place you send to me. And I will float them there, and you will take them. And you will do what pleases me to give food to my house." ²⁴ And thus Hiram gave to Solomon cedar and cypress trees, all that he desired. ²⁵ And Solomon gave to Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat as food for his house and twenty kors of pressed oil. Thus Solomon gave to Hiram annually. ²⁶ And the LORD gave Solomon wisdom, just as He had spoken to him. And there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and they made a covenant between themselves. ²⁷ Then the king levied a tax from all Israel. The tax was thirty thousand men. ²⁸ And he sent them to Lebanon. Ten thousand went each month, taking turns. One month they were in Lebanon, and two months they were at home. And Adoniram was over the tax. ²⁹ And Solomon had seventy thousand who carried loads on their backs and eighty thousand who were stonecutters in the mountains. ³⁰ In addition to the supervisors of Solomon's officials, who were over the work—three thousand three hundred who ruled the people, who were doing the work. ³¹ And the king ordered, and they quarried large stones, costly stones, to lay the foundation of the house, hewn stones. ³² And the builders of Solomon, and the builders of Hiram, and the Giblites, prepared the wood and the stones to build the house.

Notes
- v. 20: "all that he desired" — the text uses כָּל־חֶפְצֽוֹ, literally "all his desire," which is rendered naturally.
- v. 23: "I will float them there" — "niftas" (נִפַּצְתִּים) is from the root פצ"ת, meaning to float or set adrift, which is captured in the translation.
- v. 31: "hewn stones" — "אֲבָנֵי גָזִֽית" is translated as "hewn stones," reflecting the precise architectural term.
