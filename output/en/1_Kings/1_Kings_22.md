# 1 Kings 22

¹ Three years passed without war between Aram and Israel. ² In the third year, King Jehoshaphat of Judah descended to meet the king of Israel. ³ The king of Israel said to his servants, "Do you know that Ramoth Gilead belongs to us, yet we are idle in taking it from the hand of the king of Aram?" ⁴ He added to Jehoshaphat, "Will you accompany me to war at Ramoth Gilead?" Jehoshaphat replied, "As I am, so are you; as my people, so are your people; as my horses, so are your horses." ⁵ Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Please consult the LORD today." ⁶ The king of Israel gathered together four hundred prophets and said to them, "Shall I go to war at Ramoth Gilead or shall I hold back?" They all answered, "Go up, for the LORD will give the city into the hand of the king." ⁷ Jehoshaphat said, "Is there no further prophet of the LORD to consult?" ⁸ The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man by whom we might consult the LORD, but I hate him, for he never prophesies anything good to me, only evil—Micah son of Yimla." Jehoshaphat replied, "Do not let the king say that." ⁹ The king of Israel summoned a eunuch and said, "Hurry and bring Micah son of Yimla." ¹⁰ The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, were sitting, each on his throne, dressed in their robes, in the gate of Samaria, while all the prophets were prophesying before them. ¹¹ Zedekiah son of Kenaana made for himself two iron horns and said, "Thus says the LORD: With these you will gore Aram until they are destroyed." ¹² And all the prophets prophesied the same way, saying, "Go up to Ramoth Gilead and it will be successful. The LORD will give the city into the hand of the king." ¹³ The messenger who had gone to summon Micah said to him, "Look now, the words of the prophets are all favorable to the king. Please let your words also be like one of theirs and speak favorably." ¹⁴ Micah answered, "As the LORD lives, whatever the LORD says to me, that I will speak." ¹⁵ When he arrived before the king, the king said to him, "Micah, shall I go to war at Ramoth Gilead or shall I hold back?" Micah answered, "Go up, and it will be successful. The LORD will give the city into the hand of the king." ¹⁶ The king said to him, "How many times must I swear to you that you will speak to me nothing but the truth in the name of the LORD?" ¹⁷ Micah replied, "I saw all Israel scattered on the hills like sheep without a shepherd. The LORD said, 'There is no Lord for these; each may return to his house in peace.'" ¹⁸ The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Did I not tell you that he would not prophesy anything good to me, but only evil?" ¹⁹ Micah answered, "Therefore listen to the word of the LORD. I saw the LORD seated on His throne, and the whole host of heaven standing before Him, on His right and on His left. ²⁰ The LORD said, 'Who will entice Ahab, king of Israel, to go up and fall at Ramoth Gilead?' One said like this, and another said like this. ²¹ Then a spirit went out and stood before the LORD, and said, 'I will entice him.' The LORD asked, 'By what means?' The spirit answered, 'I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' The LORD said, 'You will entice and you will succeed. Go out and do so.' ²² And now behold, the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these prophets of yours, and the LORD has spoken evil about you." ²³ Then Zedekiah son of Kenaana struck Micah on the cheek and said, "Which way did the spirit of the LORD go from me to speak to you?" ²⁴ Micah replied, "You will see it on that day when you go into a private chamber to hide." ²⁵ The king of Israel said, "Take Micah and return him to Ammon the ruler of the city and to Joash the son of the king. ²⁶ Say to them, 'Thus says the king: Put this one in the prison and feed him only bitter bread and only bitter water until I return in peace.'" ²⁷ Micah said, "If you return in peace, the LORD has not spoken through me. But hear, you peoples, all of you!" ²⁸ The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth Gilead. ²⁹ The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into battle, but you wear your robes." The king of Israel disguised himself and went into battle. ³⁰ Now the king of Aram had commanded his chariot commanders, thirty-two in number, saying, "Do not fight with the small or the great, but only with the king of Israel alone." ³¹ When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they thought, "This is the king of Israel," and they turned to attack him. Jehoshaphat cried out. ³² When the chariot commanders saw that it was not the king of Israel, they withdrew from him. ³³ But a man shot at random and struck the king of Israel between the armor and the tunic. He told his charioteer, "Turn your hand and take me out of the battle, for I am wounded." ³⁴ The battle raged that day, and the king stood in his chariot facing Aram from morning until evening. Blood from his wound flowed into the lap of his chariot. In the evening, he died, and the blood from the battle flowed into the lap of his chariot. ³⁵ A herald went through the camp at the going down of the sun, saying, "Each man to his city, each man to his land!" ³⁶ The king died and they brought him to Samaria, and they buried the king in Samaria. ³⁷ The chariot was washed in the cistern of Samaria, and the dogs licked up his blood, and the harlots bathed in it, as the word of the LORD had spoken. ³⁸ The rest of the deeds of Ahab, all that he did, the house of ivory that he built, and all the cities he built—indeed, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. ³⁹ Ahab slept with his fathers, and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place. ⁴⁰ Jehoshaphat son of Asa reigned over Judah in the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel. ⁴¹ Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Azubah daughter of Shilhi. ⁴² He walked in all the ways of his father Asa; he did not deviate from them, doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD. ⁴³ Yet the high places were not removed, and the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places. ⁴⁴ Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel. ⁴⁵ The rest of the deeds of Jehoshaphat and his might, and what he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? ⁴⁶ The rest of the holy things that were left in the days of his father Asa were placed in the cities of Beer. ⁴⁷ There was no king set over the Edomites at that time. ⁴⁸ Jehoshaphat made ships of Tarshish to go to Ophir for gold, but they did not go, for the ships were wrecked at Ezion Geber. ⁴⁹ Then Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, "Let my servants go with your servants in the ships." But Jehoshaphat refused. ⁵⁰ Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of his father David. Jehoram his son reigned in his place. ⁵¹ Ahaziah son of Ahab reigned over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned two years over Israel. ⁵² He did evil in the sight of the LORD, walking in the way of his father and in the way of his mother and in the way of Jeroboam son of Nabat, who had led Israel astray. ⁵³ He served the Baals and worshipped them, provoking the anger of the LORD God of Israel in all the things his father had done. ⁵⁴ He even went so far as to send messengers to the Moabites to conscript them into Israel, after the death of Ahab.

Notes
- v. 43: "high places" (בָּמֽוֹת) is used in a context of idolatrous worship, hence the retention of the term in English.
