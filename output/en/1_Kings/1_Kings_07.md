# 1 Kings 7

¹ Solomon built his house in thirteen years, and he completed all his house. ² He built his house of cedars from Lebanon, a hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide and thirty cubits high, on four rows of pillars, cedar beams and carved cedar on the pillars. ³ He overlaid the beams with cedar above the side rooms that were on the pillars, forty and five in the row, fifteen on the column. ⁴ And he built three projecting structures and a projecting room opposite a projecting room three times. ⁵ And all the openings and the doorposts were square openings, projecting and retracted, three times. ⁶ He built the porch of the pillars fifty cubits long and thirty cubits wide and a porch in front of them, and pillars and a projecting part in front of them. ⁷ And the porch of the throne, where he would judge, the porch of judgment he built and overlaid with cedar from the floor to the ceiling. ⁸ And his house, where he would dwell, the inner courtyard was like the structure of this, and he built a house for the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Solomon had taken, like this porch. ⁹ All of these were costly stones, precise in measurements, hewn stones from the house and from outside, from the foundation to the coping and from outside to the great courtyard. ¹⁰ And laid with costly stones, large stones, ten-cubit stones and eight-cubit stones. ¹¹ And on top, costly stones, precise in measurements and cedar. ¹² And the great courtyard around it, three rows of hewn stones and a row of cedar beams, and for the inner courtyard of the house of the LORD and for the porch of the house. ¹³ And the king sent Solomon and took Huram from Tyre. ¹⁴ He was the son of a widow woman, from the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a Tyrian, a worker in bronze, and he was filled with wisdom and understanding and knowledge to make any work in bronze. And he came to the king Solomon, and he did all his work. ¹⁵ And he cast the two pillars of bronze, eighteen cubits the height of the pillar, and a line twelve cubits around the second pillar. ¹⁶ And two capitals to place on the heads of the pillars, cast bronze, five cubits the height of the capital and five cubits the height of the second capital. ¹⁷ Chains, a work of chain, threads, a work of threads for the capitals that were on the heads of the pillars, seven for the one capital and seven for the second capital. ¹⁸ And he made the pillars and two rows around the chainwork of the one to cover the capitals that were on the heads of the lily-shaped capitals, and so he did for the second capital. ¹⁹ And capitals that were on the heads of the pillars, a lily-shaped work, in the porch four cubits. ²⁰ And capitals on the two pillars also from above, opposite the belly that is beyond the chainwork and the lily-shaped capitals, two rows around the second capital. ²¹ And he set up the pillars for the porch of the temple and set up the right-hand pillar and called its name Yachin, and set up the left-hand pillar and called its name Boaz. ²² And on the heads of the pillars, a lily-shaped work, and the work of the pillars was completed. ²³ And he made the sea of cast bronze ten cubits from rim to rim, round about, and five cubits its height, and a line thirty cubits around it. ²⁴ And pomegranates below its rim around it, ten cubits surrounding the sea, two rows of pomegranates cast with it in one casting. ²⁵ Standing on twelve oxen, three facing north, three facing west, three facing south, and three facing east, and the sea on them above, and all their backs were enclosed. ²⁶ And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its rim was like the rim of a cup, a lily-shaped work, two thousand pomegranates around it. ²⁷ And he made the stands, ten of bronze, four cubits the length of the stand, and four cubits its width, and three cubits its height. ²⁸ And this was the work of the stand, latches for them and latches between the crossbars. ²⁹ And on the latches that were between the crossbars, lions and oxen and cherubim, and on the crossbars, also from above and from below, lion heads and ox heads, and the work of the pattern. ³⁰ And four bronze wheels to the stand, and bronze axles and four brackets, their axles cast from beneath the wheel, the axles cast from one piece of bronze. ³¹ And its mouth was from the house to the capital and one cubit, and its mouth was round, the work of it a cubit and half a cubit, and also on its mouth, handles and square latches, not round. ³² And four wheels under the latches, and the handles of the wheels were attached to the stand, and the height of the wheel was a cubit and half a cubit. ³³ And the work of the wheels was like the work of the chariot's wheel, their handles and their backs and their hubs, all cast. ³⁴ And four brackets to the four corners of the stand, from the stand its brackets. ³⁵ And on the head of the stand, half a cubit in height, round about, and on the head of the stand, its handles and its latches from it. ³⁶ He opened on the panels its handles and on its latches, cherubim, lions, and palm trees, like the pattern of each man and surrounding handles. ³⁷ Like this, he made the ten stands, one casting, one measure, a single pin for all of them. ³⁸ And he made ten basins of bronze, forty seahs the basin. One basin, four cubits the basin, one basin on one stand for the ten stands. ³⁹ And he placed the stands, five on the right side of the house and five on the left side of the house, and the sea he placed on the right side of the house facing south. ⁴⁰ And Huram made the basins and the shovels and the bowls, and Huram completed the work he did for the king Solomon in the house of the LORD. ⁴¹ Two pillars and two capitals that were on the heads of the pillars and two chainworks to cover the two capitals that were on the heads of the pillars. ⁴² And the four hundred pomegranates for the two chainworks, two rows of pomegranates for the one chainwork to cover the two capitals that were on the face of the pillars. ⁴³ And the ten stands and the ten basins on the stands. ⁴⁴ And the one sea and the twelve oxen under the sea. ⁴⁵ And the ladles and the shovels and the bowls and all the vessels that Huram made for the king Solomon in the house of the LORD were bronze, hammered. ⁴⁶ In the Jordan, the king cast them in the clay land between Sukkoth and Tzarten. ⁴⁷ And Solomon placed all the vessels in abundance, exceedingly abundant, so the weight of the bronze was not found out. ⁴⁸ And Solomon made all the vessels in the house of the LORD, the golden altar and the golden table for the bread of the Presence. ⁴⁹ And the lamps, five on the right and five on the left, before the inner sanctuary, golden, closed, and the flower and the lamps and the lampstands, golden. ⁵⁰ And the siphons and the ladles and the shovels and the bowls and the basins, golden, closed, and the handles for the doors of the inner house, the holy of holies, and the doors of the house for the temple, golden. ⁵¹ And the entire work that the king Solomon did in the house of the LORD was completed, and Solomon brought the holy things of David his father, the silver and the gold and the vessels, and placed them in the treasuries of the house of the LORD.

Notes
- v. 14: "Tyrian" is a reference to Tyre, not necessarily a person from Tyre, but this rendering is faithful to the Hebrew.
- v. 18: "lily-shaped capitals" refers to the design of the capitals on the pillars, which are described as resembling lilies.
- v. 21: "Yachin" and "Boaz" are the names of the two pillars in front of the temple.
- v. 23: "sea" refers to a large bronze basin used in the temple.
- v. 25: "oxen" are sculpted figures under the bronze basin, symbolizing strength and stability.
- v. 30: "handles" refer to the parts of the wheels connecting them to the stands.
- v. 44: "the one sea and the twelve oxen" refers to the large bronze basin and the sculpted oxen beneath it.
- v. 47: "hammered" refers to the method of crafting the bronze vessels.
- v. 51: "treasuries" refers to storage areas for the sacred items.
