# 1 Kings 9

¹ When Solomon had finished building the house of the LORD and the king's house, and all the works that he had desired to do, ² the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, as he had appeared to him at Gibeon. ³ And the LORD said to him, I have heard your prayer and your request that you have made before me. I have consecrated the house that you built, to put my name there forever, and my eyes and my heart will be there for all the days. ⁴ And you, if you walk before me as your father David walked, with a whole heart and with uprightness, to do all that I have commanded you, to keep my statutes and my judgments, ⁵ then I will establish the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, as I promised your father David, saying, No man shall take it away from you forever. ⁶ But if you turn away from me, you and your children, and do not keep my commandments and my statutes that I have set before you, and go and serve other gods, and worship them, ⁷ then I will cut off Israel from the land that I have given them, and the house that I have consecrated for my name I will cast away from before me, and Israel will become a proverb and a byword among all the peoples. ⁸ And this house will become high, so that everyone passing by will be amazed and say, Why did the LORD do so to this land and to this house? ⁹ And they will say, Because they forsook the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and held fast to other gods, and worshipped them and served them, therefore the LORD has brought all this evil upon them. ¹⁰ Now it came to pass that the time Solomon spent building the two houses, the house of the LORD and the king's house, was twenty years. ¹¹ Hiram the king of Tyre had supplied Solomon with cedar and fir trees and gold for whatever he desired. Then the king gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee. ¹² And Hiram went out from Tyre to see the cities that Solomon had given him, and they did not please him. ¹³ And he said, What are these cities you have given me, my brother? They are called the land of Cabul to this day. ¹⁴ And Hiram sent to the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold. ¹⁵ This is the matter of the levy that the king imposed on the people to build the house of the LORD and his own house and the Millo and the wall of Jerusalem and Hazor and Megiddo and Gezer. ¹⁶ Pharaoh the king of Egypt had come up and captured Gezer, and burned it with fire, and killed the Canaanite who lived in the city. And he gave it as a dowry to his daughter, Solomon's wife. ¹⁷ Solomon rebuilt Gezer and Beth Horon the Lower. ¹⁸ And Baalah, which is called Tadmor in the desert, and the store cities, and the cities for chariots and for horsemen. ¹⁹ And all the cities of the storehouses and the cities of the chariots and the cities of the horsemen, and everything that Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem and in Lebanon and in all the land under his dominion. ²⁰ All the people left of the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not of the people of Israel, ²¹ their descendants, who remained after them in the land, whom the people of Israel could not exterminate, Solomon made them pay tribute to this day. ²² But from among the people of Israel, Solomon did not make any servant, for they were men of war, his officials and his scribes and his officers and the commanders of his chariots and his horses. ²³ These are the officers of the overseers who were over the work for Solomon, five hundred and fifty men who ruled the people in their labor. ²⁴ But the daughter of Pharaoh went up from the city of David to the house that he had built for her, and he built the Millo. ²⁵ And Solomon offered up three times a year burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar that he built for the LORD, and he offered up the incense and completed the house. ²⁶ The king also made a fleet of ships at Ezion-Geber, which is by Eloth, on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom. ²⁷ And Hiram sent in the ships his servants, men skilled in the sea, with the servants of Solomon. ²⁸ And they came to Ophir and brought from there four hundred and twenty talents of gold, and they brought it to King Solomon.

---

Notes  
- v. 21: "whom the people of Israel could not exterminate" is a translation of the phrase "lo yachalchem" which is a rare construction; the context suggests that the Israelites did not manage to fully destroy these peoples.  
- v. 28: "four hundred and twenty talents of gold" is a direct translation of "arba me'ot v'esre kikar zahav," using the standard rendering for "kikar."
