# 1 Kings 12

¹ Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all Israel had come to make him king. ² When Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had been in Egypt and had fled from King Solomon, heard this, he was still in Egypt. ³ So they sent and called him, and Jeroboam and all the assembly of Israel came to Rehoboam. ⁴ They spoke to Rehoboam, saying, "Your father made our yoke heavy, but now lighten the harsh service your father imposed on us, the heavy burden he placed on us, and we will serve you." ⁵ Rehoboam said to them, "Come back in three days, and I will give you a response." So the people went away. ⁶ The king consulted with the elders who had served King Solomon while he was still alive, asking, "How should I respond to these people who are asking me to ease the burden?" ⁷ The elders answered him, "If you are kind and good to these people today, and if you speak kindly to them, they will be your servants forever." ⁸ But Rehoboam ignored the advice of the elders and instead consulted with the younger men who had grown up with him and were standing before him. ⁹ He asked them, "What do you advise me to say to these people who have asked me to lighten the burden my father placed on them?" ¹⁰ The young men who had grown up with him said to him, "Say to them, 'My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist. My father imposed a heavy burden on you, but I will add to it. My father used a whip to discipline you, but I will use scorpions!'" ¹¹ So Rehoboam answered the people according to the advice of the young men, saying, "My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add to it. My father used a whip to discipline you, but I will use scorpions!" ¹² The people went to Rehoboam on the third day, just as he had told them. ¹³ Rehoboam gave them a harsh reply, ignoring the advice of the elders. ¹⁴ He answered them according to the advice of the young men, saying, "My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add to it. My father used a whip to discipline you, but I will use scorpions!" ¹⁵ Since the king did not listen to the people, the matter had come about by the will of the LORD, to fulfill the word the LORD had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat. ¹⁶ When all Israel saw that the king would not listen to them, they responded to the king, saying, "What share do we have in David? What inheritance in the son of Jesse? Go to your tents, Israel! Now look to your own house, David!" So Israel went to their tents. ¹⁷ But the Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah remained under Rehoboam’s rule. ¹⁸ King Rehoboam sent Adoram, who was in charge of the corvée, but the people of Israel stoned him to death. King Rehoboam quickly mounted his chariot to flee to Jerusalem. ¹⁹ Israel has been in rebellion against the house of David to this day. ²⁰ When all Israel heard that Rehoboam had returned, they sent and called him to the assembly, and they made him king over all Israel. No one followed the house of David except the tribe of Judah alone. ²¹ Rehoboam went to Jerusalem, and he gathered all the house of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin—180,000 warriors—to go to war against the house of Israel and to restore the kingdom to Rehoboam, the son of Solomon. ²² Then the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying: ²³ "Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel and Benjamin, and to the rest of the people: ²⁴ 'Thus says the LORD: You must not go up to fight against your brothers, the people of Israel. Return to your homes, for this matter is from me.' The people listened to the word of the LORD and returned home as the LORD had commanded them." ²⁵ Jeroboam built up Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim and lived there. He went out and built Penuel. ²⁶ Jeroboam said in his heart, "Now the kingdom will return to David’s house." ²⁷ If this people goes up to offer sacrifices at the temple of the LORD in Jerusalem, their hearts will return to their master, Rehoboam king of Judah, and they will kill me and return to Rehoboam king of Judah. ²⁸ So the king made two golden calves, and he said to the people, "It is too much for you to go up to Jerusalem. Here are your gods, Israel, who brought you up from the land of Egypt." ²⁹ He placed one in Bethel and the other in Dan. ³⁰ This became a sin, and the people went to worship before one of them even as far as Dan. ³¹ He built high places and appointed priests from among the common people, who were not Levites. ³² Jeroboam made a festival in the eighth month on the fifteenth day, like the festival in Judah, and he offered sacrifices on the altar he had built in Bethel. He set up the priests of the high places he had made. ³³ He offered sacrifices on the altar he had built in Bethel on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, following the festival he had devised in his heart. He made a festival for the people of Israel and offered sacrifices on the altar to burn offerings.

Notes
- v. 1: "Rehoboam went to Shechem" — The name "Rehoboam" is used consistently as per the glossary. "Shechem" is the city name.
- v. 19: "Israel has been in rebellion" — This reflects the Hebrew phrase "יפשע ישראל" which is best rendered as "Israel has been in rebellion."
- v. 23: "Thus says the LORD" — The phrase "כֹּה אָמַר יְהוָה" is rendered in a way that maintains the prophetic tone.
