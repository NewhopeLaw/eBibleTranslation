# Daniel 5

¹ Belshazzar the king gave a great feast to his nobles, a thousand of them, and drank in the presence of a thousand. ² Belshazzar gave orders to bring the gold and silver vessels that his father Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple in Jerusalem, and the king and his nobles, his concubines, and his wives drank from them. ³ Then they brought the gold and silver vessels taken from the temple of the God of Jerusalem, and the king and his nobles, his concubines, and his wives drank from them. ⁴ While they drank wine, they praised the gods of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone. ⁵ At that moment, a hand emerged from a human form and wrote the inscription on the plaster of the wall of the royal palace, and the king watched the hand as it wrote. ⁶ The king’s appearance changed, his thoughts disturbed him, his loins loosened, and his knees knocked together. ⁷ The king called out in a loud voice to bring in the magicians, Chaldeans, and diviners, and said to the wise men of Babylon, "Whoever reads this writing and explains its meaning shall be clothed in purple, a golden chain shall be placed around his neck, and he shall rule as third in the kingdom." ⁸ But all the wise men of the king entered, yet none could read the writing or explain its meaning to the king. ⁹ Belshazzar the king was greatly disturbed, his face changed, and his nobles were troubled. ¹⁰ The queen, hearing the king and his nobles in the house of the banquet, entered and said, "Live forever, O king! Let not your thoughts trouble you, and let your face not change. ¹¹ There is a man in your kingdom in whom the spirit of the holy gods dwells. In the days of your father, wisdom and insight were found in him. Your father, King Nebuchadnezzar, appointed him to be chief of the magicians, Chaldeans, and diviners, ¹² for in him is a spirit of extraordinary understanding, wisdom, and insight. He interprets dreams, reveals mysteries, and solves riddles. His name is Daniel, whom the king called Belteshazzar. Now summon Daniel, and he will reveal the meaning of the writing." ¹³ Then Daniel was brought before the king, and the king said to Daniel, "You are Daniel, one of the exiles from Judah, whom my father the king brought from Judah. ¹⁴ I have heard that you have the spirit of the gods, and wisdom and extraordinary understanding in you. ¹⁵ Now the wise men and magicians have been brought in before me to read the writing and explain its meaning, but they cannot. You, however, can. If you can read this writing and explain its meaning, you will be clothed in purple, a golden chain will be placed around your neck, and you will rule as third in the kingdom." ¹⁶ Daniel answered before the king, "Let your gifts remain with you, and give the rewards of your house to another. But I will read the writing and explain its meaning to the king. ¹⁷ O king, the Most High God gave Nebuchadnezzar your father the kingdom, greatness, glory, and honor. ¹⁸ And from the greatness He gave him, all peoples, nations, and languages trembled and feared before him. He executed whom he wished, struck down whom he wished, elevated whom he wished, and brought low whom he wished. ¹⁹ But when his heart was lifted up and his spirit hardened so that he acted with arrogance, he was deposed from his royal throne, and his glory was stripped from him. ²⁰ He was driven away from people, and his heart was given to the beasts. He dwelled with the oxen, and his dwelling was among the grazing animals. He ate grass like an ox, and his body was nourished by the dew of heaven, until he knew that the Most High rules over the kingdom of people, and to whom He desires He gives it. ²¹ But you, Belshazzar, have not humbled your heart, even though you know all these things. ²² You have exalted yourself against the Lord of heaven. You brought the vessels of His house before you, and you and your nobles, your concubines, and your wives drank from them. You praised the gods of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone, which see not, hear not, and know not. But you did not glorify the God who holds your breath in His hand and all your ways. ²³ Therefore, from before Him a hand was sent, and this inscription was written. ²⁴ And this is the inscription that was written: Mene, Tekel, Parsin. ²⁵ And this is the meaning of the words: Mene, God has numbered your days and brought your kingdom to an end. ²⁶ Tekel, you have been weighed in the scales and found wanting. ²⁷ Parsin, your kingdom is divided, and given to the Medes and Persians. ²⁸ At this, Belshazzar gave the command, and they clothed Daniel in purple, placed the golden chain around his neck, and announced that he was to be third ruler in the kingdom. ²⁹ Then that very night Belshazzar the king of the Chaldeans was put to death, ³⁰ and the kingdom was given to the Medes and Persians.

---

Notes
- v. 23: The phrase "Parsin" is rendered as "your kingdom is divided and given to the Medes and Persians" to clarify the meaning of the final word in the inscription, which is likely related to "Persia."
- v. 30: The term "Chaldeans" is used here as a reference to the Babylonian people, consistent with the historical context.
