# Daniel 3

¹ King Nebuchadnezzar made an image of gold, its height sixty cubits and its breadth six cubits. He set it up in the plain of Dura in the province of Babylon. ² King Nebuchadnezzar sent for the governors, the prefects, the treasurers, the officers, the judges, the magistrates, and all the officials of the provinces to come to the dedication of the image he had set up. ³ The governors, the prefects, the treasurers, the officers, the judges, the magistrates, and all the officials of the provinces gathered to come to the dedication of the image that King Nebuchadnezzar had set up. They stood before the image. ⁴ A herald cried out with a loud voice, "To you it is commanded, peoples, nations, and languages, ⁵ that in the hour you hear the sound of the horn, of the flute, of the lyre, the harp, the drum, and every kind of musical instrument, you must fall down and worship the golden image that King Nebuchadnezzar has set up. ⁶ Whoever does not fall down and worship it will immediately be thrown into the furnace of blazing fire." ⁷ Therefore, when the peoples heard the sound of the horn, the flute, the lyre, the harp, the drum, and every kind of musical instrument, all the peoples, nations, and languages fell down and worshiped the golden image that King Nebuchadnezzar had set up. ⁸ Then certain Chaldeans came forward and accused the Jews. ⁹ They spoke to King Nebuchadnezzar, "O king, live forever! ¹⁰ You have decreed that every person who hears the sound of the horn, the flute, the lyre, the harp, the drum, and every kind of musical instrument must fall down and worship the golden image. ¹¹ Whoever does not fall down and worship will immediately be thrown into the furnace of blazing fire. ¹² There are some Jews, O king, whom you have placed over the affairs of the province of Babylon—Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. These men have not obeyed your decree, O king, and do not worship your gods or the golden image that you have set up." ¹³ Then King Nebuchadnezzar in anger and fury commanded to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. They were brought before the king. ¹⁴ King Nebuchadnezzar spoke to them, "Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, that you do not serve the king’s gods or worship the golden image that the king has set up? ¹⁵ Now if you are ready when you hear the sound of the horn, the flute, the lyre, the harp, the drum, and every kind of musical instrument, to fall down and worship the golden image that I have made, good. But if you do not worship it, you will be cast immediately into the furnace of blazing fire. And which god is there who can rescue you from my hands?" ¹⁶ Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego answered the king, "O king, we have no need to respond to you in this matter. ¹⁷ For there is a God in heaven who can rescue us from the furnace of blazing fire and will deliver us from your hand, O king. ¹⁸ But even if he does not, you must know, O king, that we will not serve your gods or worship the golden image that you have set up." ¹⁹ Then King Nebuchadnezzar was filled with anger and his face was distorted against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He commanded to heat the furnace seven times more than usual, and ordered the strongest of the soldiers to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego and cast them into the furnace of blazing fire. ²⁰ Then those men were bound in their tunics, cloaks, and turbans, and cast into the middle of the furnace of blazing fire. ²¹ The king's command was so urgent that the furnace was so hot that the fire killed those men who had thrown them in. ²² Then those three, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell into the middle of the furnace, bound. ²³ King Nebuchadnezzar was astonished and rose quickly to speak to his advisors. "Didn't we cast three men, bound, into the fire?" he asked. ²⁴ He answered, "Yes, O king." ²⁵ Then Nebuchadnezzar said, "Look! I see four men unbound, walking in the middle of the fire, and they are unharmed. The fourth looks like a son of the gods." ²⁶ Then King Nebuchadnezzar went near to the mouth of the furnace and said, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out!" Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego came out from the fire. ²⁷ Then the satraps, the prefects, and the governors saw these men, whose bindings had not touched them, whose hair had not singed, and whose clothes had not changed. There was no smell of fire on them. ²⁸ King Nebuchadnezzar said, "Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who sent his angel and rescued his servants who trusted in him. They disobeyed the king's command and were willing to die rather than serve any god except their own. ²⁹ I command that every people, nation, and language that speaks anything against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego be cut off, their houses destroyed, for there is no other god who can rescue like this." ³⁰ Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in the province of Babylon. ³¹ King Nebuchadnezzar said to all peoples, nations, and languages, "May peace be with you!" ³² The things that I have done with you, O God of heaven, have been done with your favor. ³³ May the king and his officials and his governors and all his people be prosperous forever!

Notes
- v. 25: The fourth figure in the fire is best understood as an angel or divine presence, though some interpretations suggest it may be a vision or symbolic representation. The text does not specify the identity directly.
