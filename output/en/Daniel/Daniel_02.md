# Daniel 2

¹ In the second year of King Nebuchadnezzar’s reign, the king had a dream. His spirit was troubled, and his sleep was taken from him. ² The king summoned the magicians, enchanters, sorcerers, and Chaldeans to appear before him and reveal to the king his dreams. ³ They came and stood before the king. The king said to them, "I had a dream, and my spirit is troubled to understand the dream." ⁴ [Aramaic] The Chaldeans replied to the king in Aramaic, "O king, live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will give the interpretation." ⁵ [Aramaic] The king said to the Chaldeans, "The order from me has been made clear. If you do not reveal to me the dream and its interpretation, you will be torn apart, and your houses will be destroyed. ⁶ [Aramaic] But if you reveal to me the dream and its interpretation, you will receive from me gifts, rewards, and great honor. So reveal to me the dream and its interpretation." ⁷ [Aramaic] They replied again and said, "The king should tell the dream to his servants, and we will give the interpretation of the dream." ⁸ [Aramaic] The king answered and said, "I know for certain that you are trying to gain time, because you see that the decree has been taken from me. ⁹ [Aramaic] If you do not tell me the dream, I know that you are speaking a false and deceptive word to me, until the time has passed. So tell me the dream, and I will know that you will give me its interpretation." ¹⁰ [Aramaic] The Chaldeans replied before the king and said, "There is no man on earth who can reveal the king’s word, for no great king or ruler has ever asked such a thing of an enchanter, a magician, or a Chaldean. ¹¹ [Aramaic] The word the king is asking is heavy, and there is none who can reveal it before the king except the gods, whose dwelling is not among all flesh." ¹² [Aramaic] Then the king, in rage and fury, said, "Put to death all the wise men of Babylon." ¹³ [Aramaic] The decree went out, and the wise men were being killed. They sought to put Daniel and his companions to death. ¹⁴ [Aramaic] Then Daniel spoke with purpose and understanding to Arioch, the chief official in charge of the king, who had gone out to put to death the wise men of Babylon. ¹⁵ [Aramaic] He said, "Why has the decree from the king been so hasty? Arioch explained the matter to Daniel. ¹⁶ [Aramaic] Then Daniel went to the king and asked him to give him time, so that he might give the interpretation to the king." ¹⁷ [Aramaic] Then Daniel went into his house, and to Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, his companions, he revealed the matter. ¹⁸ [Aramaic] They sought mercy from the God of heaven over this mystery, so that Daniel and his companions would not be destroyed with the rest of the wise men of Babylon. ¹⁹ [Aramaic] Then to Daniel, in a vision of the night, the mystery was revealed. Daniel blessed the God of heaven. ²⁰ [Aramaic] He said, "Blessed be the name of God from age to age, for wisdom and strength are his. ²¹ [Aramaic] He changes times and seasons; he removes kings and sets up kings. He gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those who have understanding. ²² [Aramaic] He reveals deep and hidden things, and knows what is in the darkness, and light dwells with him. ²³ [Aramaic] To you, O God of my ancestors, I give thanks and praise, because you have given me wisdom and strength, and have revealed to me what we asked of you, and have made known to me the king’s dream." ²⁴ [Aramaic] Then Daniel went to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to execute the wise men of Babylon, and said to him, "Do not execute the wise men of Babylon. Bring me before the king, and I will give the interpretation to the king." ²⁵ [Aramaic] Then Arioch, in haste, brought Daniel before the king and said to him, "I have found a man among the captives of Judah who will give the interpretation to the king." ²⁶ [Aramaic] The king said to Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, "Are you able to tell me the dream that I saw and its interpretation?" ²⁷ [Aramaic] Daniel replied before the king and said, "The mystery the king is asking is not for the wise, the enchanters, the magicians, or the astrologers to tell the king. ²⁸ [Aramaic] But there is a God in heaven who reveals mysteries, and he has revealed to King Nebuchadnezzar what will happen in the last days. The dream you had and the visions of your head on your bed are these. ²⁹ [Aramaic] O king, the thoughts on your bed have risen: what must happen after this, and the one revealing mysteries has made known to you what must happen. ³⁰ [Aramaic] But it is not because of the wisdom that is in me more than any living being that this mystery has been revealed to me, but so that the king may know the interpretation, and the thoughts of your heart may be understood. ³¹ [Aramaic] O king, you watched, and behold, there was an image, a great image, standing before you, and the appearance of it was fearsome. ³² [Aramaic] The head of the image was of fine gold, its chest and arms of silver, its belly and thighs of bronze, ³³ [Aramaic] its legs of iron, its feet partly of iron and partly of clay. ³⁴ [Aramaic] You watched until a stone was cut out, not by human hands, and it struck the image on its feet of iron and clay and broke them in pieces. ³⁵ [Aramaic] Then the iron, the clay, the bronze, the silver, and the gold were crushed together, and became like the chaff of the summer threshing floors. The wind carried them away so that not a trace of them was found. But the stone that struck the image became a great mountain and filled the whole earth. ³⁶ [Aramaic] This is the dream. Now we will tell the king its interpretation. ³⁷ [Aramaic] O king, you are the king of kings. The God of heaven has given you a kingdom, power, and strength, and glory. ³⁸ [Aramaic] Everywhere that the children of men dwell, the beasts of the field and the birds of the sky he has given into your hand, and has made you ruler over them all. You are the head of gold. ³⁹ [Aramaic] After you, another kingdom will rise, lesser than yours, and then a third kingdom of bronze, which will rule over all the earth. ⁴⁰ [Aramaic] Then there will be a fourth kingdom, strong as iron, for iron crushes and shatters everything. And just as iron crushes, so it will crush and shatter. ⁴¹ [Aramaic] And because you saw the feet and toes partly of baked clay and partly of iron, the kingdom will be divided, but part of the iron will be in it, just as you saw the iron mixed with the clay. ⁴² [Arama1c] And the toes of the feet, partly of iron and partly of clay, show that the kingdom will partly be strong and partly broken. ⁴³ [Aramaic] Because you saw the iron mixed with the clay, they will intermarry among themselves, but one will not hold to the other, just as iron does not mix with clay. ⁴⁴ [Aramaic] And in the days of those kings, the God of heaven will raise up a kingdom that will never be destroyed, and the kingdom will not be left to another people. It will crush and end all these kingdoms, and it will stand forever. ⁴⁵ [Aramaic] Just as you saw that a stone was cut out, not by human hands, and it crushed the iron, the bronze, the clay, the silver, and the gold, the great God has revealed to the king what will happen after this. The dream is true, and its interpretation is trustworthy." ⁴⁶ [Aramaic] Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell on his face and worshiped Daniel, and he presented an offering and fragrant incense to him. ⁴⁷ [Aramaic] The king answered Daniel and said, "Surely your God is the God of gods and Lord of kings, and the revealer of mysteries, for you have been able to reveal this mystery." ⁴⁸ [Aramaic] Then the king elevated Daniel, and gave him great gifts and many rewards, and set him over the entire province of Babylon and made him chief of the magicians of Babylon. ⁴⁹ [Aramaic] Daniel asked the king, and he set Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego over the affairs of the province of Babylon, while Daniel remained in the king’s court.
