# Daniel 6

¹ Darius the Mede received the kingdom in the third year of Cyrus’s reign. ² Darius made decrees to the governors of the kingdom and appointed over it 120 satraps so that they might be responsible for all the affairs of the kingdom. ³ He set over them three administrators, one of whom was Daniel. These administrators were appointed to oversee the satraps so that the king might not be troubled. ⁴ Daniel excelled over all the other administrators and satraps because a spirit of extraordinary wisdom resided in him. The king planned to place him in charge of the entire kingdom. ⁵ The administrators and satraps sought to find grounds for accusation against Daniel concerning the king’s law, but they could not find any fault in him, for he was trustworthy. ⁶ Then those men said, “We will not find grounds for accusation against Daniel unless it is in regard to his God’s law.” ⁷ So the administrators and satraps came to the king and said to him, “King Darius, live forever! Let a decree be issued and a law be enacted that for thirty days, whoever makes a request or brings an appeal to any god or man, besides you, O king, will be cast into the lions’ den.” ⁸ Therefore, O king, issue the decree and sign the writing so that it cannot be altered, for the law of the Medes and Persians cannot be revoked. ⁹ Then King Darius issued the decree and the writing was signed. ¹⁰ When Daniel learned that the decree had been issued, he went to his house. He had a window open in his upper room toward Jerusalem and, three times a day, he knelt down and prayed, giving thanks before his God, just as he had done before. ¹¹ Then those men came and found Daniel praying and making his request to his God. ¹² So they went and brought the matter before the king, saying, “O king, haven’t you issued a decree that for thirty days, whoever makes a request to any god or man besides you, O king, will be cast into the lions’ den?” The king said, “The matter stands firm, and the law of the Medes and Persians cannot be changed.” ¹³ Then those men answered and said before the king, “Daniel, one of the exiles from Judah, pays no attention to your decree or to the writing you have signed. He still prays three times a day.” ¹⁴ When the king heard this, he was deeply distressed. He was determined to rescue Daniel and to deliver him from the lions. ¹⁵ So those men said to the king, “O king, you must know that the law of the Medes and Persians is that no decree or law once issued by the king can be revoked.” ¹⁶ Then the king gave orders, and Daniel was brought and thrown into the lions’ den. The king said to Daniel, “Your God, whom you serve constantly, will rescue you.” ¹⁷ A stone was brought and placed over the mouth of the den, and the king sealed it with his ring and the rings of his lords, so that nothing about Daniel could be changed. ¹⁸ Then the king went to his palace and spent the night fasting; he did not eat anything, and he could not sleep. His sleep had fled from him. ¹⁹ At dawn, the king got up in haste and went to the lions’ den. ²⁰ When he approached the lions’ den, he cried out with a loud voice, “Daniel, servant of the living God, has your God, whom you serve constantly, been able to rescue you from the lions?” ²¹ Then Daniel spoke to the king, “O king, live forever! ²² My God sent His angel and shut the mouths of the lions, and they did not harm me, for I was found blameless before Him. And before you, O king, I have done no wrong.” ²³ Then the king was very glad and gave orders to bring Daniel out of the den. So Daniel was brought out of the den, and no injury was found on him because he had trusted in his God. ²⁴ Then the king gave orders, and those men who had accused Daniel were brought and thrown into the lions’ den, along with their children and their wives. Before they reached the bottom of the den, the lions overpowered them and crushed all their bones. ²⁵ Then King Darius wrote to all the peoples, nations, and languages who live throughout the entire land: “Peace be multiplied to you! ²⁶ From my presence this decree is issued, that in all the dominion of my kingdom people should tremble and fear before the God of Daniel, for He is the living God, who endures forever. His kingdom will not be destroyed, and His dominion will last to the end. ²⁷ He rescues and delivers, and He performs signs and wonders in heaven and on earth. He who rescued Daniel from the power of the lions.” ²⁸ Daniel prospered during the reign of Darius and during the reign of Cyrus the Persian. ²⁹ And Darius the Mede received the kingdom in the third year of Cyrus’s reign.
