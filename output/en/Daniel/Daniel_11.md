# Daniel 11

¹ In the first year of Cyrus, king of Persia, I was set up for strength and might. ² And now I will tell you the truth: behold, three more kings will stand up in Persia, and the fourth will be exceedingly rich beyond all, and after he is rich, he will stir up all the kingdoms of the Greeks. ³ And a mighty king will rise, and he will rule with great dominion, and he will do as he pleases. ⁴ But when he rises, his kingdom will be broken and divided into the four winds of heaven, not to his posterity, nor according to the dominion he held, for his kingdom will be abolished and given to others besides these. ⁵ And the king of the South will be strengthened, and one of his officials will be strengthened over him and will rule with great dominion. ⁶ And after his years, they will be joined, and the daughter of the king of the South will come to the king of the North to make an agreement with him, but she will not hold the strength of his arm, and his offspring will not be established; she will be given over, along with those who brought her and the child and the one who raised her, in those times. ⁷ And one will arise from the root of his kingdom, a shoot from the stem, and he will come to the host and enter into the stronghold of the king of the North, and he will do to them and will strengthen himself. ⁸ And indeed, he will carry off their gods, along with their incense burners, every desired vessel of silver and gold, in captivity to Egypt; and he will stand up over the king of the North. ⁹ And he will enter into the kingdom of the king of the South, and he will turn back to his own land. ¹⁰ And his sons will gather a great host in the middle of many, and he will come, flooding and passing through, and will remain and provoke until the strength is taken from him. ¹¹ And the king of the South will be enraged, and he will go out and fight with the king of the North, and he will raise a great host, and the host will be given into his hand. ¹² And he will take the host, and his heart will be exalted, and he will cast down tens of thousands, but he will not stand. ¹³ And the king of the North will return and raise a great host more than the former, and in the end of the days, after years, he will come with great strength and with great riches. ¹⁴ And in those times many will rise up against the king of the South, and the sons of the survivors of your people will rise up to set up a vision, and they will be weak. ¹⁵ And the king of the North will come and pour out a dike and take fortified cities, but the arms of the king of the South will stand, and the elect of his people will not be able to stand. ¹⁶ And the one coming to him will do as he pleases, and no one will stand before him; he will stand in the land of the Sabians, and it will be completed in his hand. ¹⁷ And he will set his face to come in the power of all his kingdom, and he will do all things right in his company; and he will give a daughter of the women to him to destroy her, and she will not remain, and it will not be for him. ¹⁸ And he will turn his face to the islands and seize many, and he will put out the rulers of their disgrace; but his disgrace will return to him. ¹⁹ And he will turn his face to the strength of his own land, and he will grow weak and fall, and he will not be found. ²⁰ And one will rise from his root, a plant of the kingdom, to take the kingdom in rebellion, acting in the glory of the kingdom; and in those days it will be broken, not by faces nor by war. ²¹ And one will stand up on his root, and he will be despised, and honor of the kingdom will not be given to him; and he will come in peace, and he will strengthen himself in divisions. ²² And the arms of the one who floods will be flooded before him and broken, and the ruler of the covenant. ²³ And from the alliance with him, he will make deceit and rise up, and he will overpower a few nations. ²⁴ In peace and in the abundance of a land, he will come and do what his fathers and the fathers of his fathers did not do; he will desecrate and plunder and seize their riches, and he will plan against Egypt and until the time. ²⁵ And his strength and his heart will be against the king of the South with a great host, and the king of the South will prepare for war with a great and very strong host, but they will not stand, because plans will be made against him. ²⁶ And those who eat the fine flour will break him, and his might will flood, and many will fall slain. ²⁷ And the two kings, their hearts will be toward evil, and they will speak falsehood at one table, and it will not succeed, for there is still an end to the time. ²⁸ And he will return to his land with great riches, and his heart will be toward a holy covenant, and he will do, and he will return to his land. ²⁹ At the time, he will return and go to the South, and it will not be as before, nor as after. ³⁰ And men will come to him from Kittim, and he will be humbled, and he will return and be angry against the holy covenant, and he will do, and he will return, and he will show hostility to those who abandon the holy covenant. ³¹ And from him, descendants will arise and defile the holy place of the stronghold, and they will remove the continual offering and set up the abomination of desolation. ³² And those who act lawlessly against the covenant will be stirred up in divisions; but the people who know their God will endure and do. ³³ And the wise of the people will understand many things, and they will fall by sword and flame, in captivity and in plunder in days. ³⁴ And when they fall, they will be helped by a small number, and many will be added to them in divisions. ³⁵ And from the wise ones, some will fall to be refined, tested, and purified until the end of the time, for there is still a time. ³⁶ And he will do as he pleases; the king will be exalted and magnified over every god, and he will speak great things, and he will prosper until wrath is completed, for it is made to be completed. ³⁷ And he will not care for the gods of his fathers, nor for the desire of women, nor for any god; for he will be exalted over all. ³⁸ And he will honor a god of the stronghold in his place, and a god that his fathers did not know, he will honor with gold and silver, with precious stone and with desired things. ³⁹ And he will do to the strongholds of those who flee with a foreign god, and he will multiply honor, and he will subdue many under him, and he will divide the land with gifts. ⁴⁰ And in the time of the end, he will make an agreement with the king of the South, and the king of the North will assemble against him with chariots and horsemen and with many ships, and they will enter the land and break and pass through. ⁴¹ And he will enter the land of the Sabians, and many will be weakened; but these will escape from his hand, Edom and Moab and the beginning of the sons of Ammon. ⁴² And he will stretch out his hand against the land, and Egypt will not be a place of rescue. ⁴³ And he will rule in the treasures of gold and silver, and in all the desired things of Egypt, Libyans and Cushites, in their strongholds. ⁴⁴ And reports and alarms will trouble him from the east and from the north, and he will go in great wrath to destroy many. ⁴⁵ And he will pitch his tents in the islands of the sea near the holy mountain of Sabians, and he will come to its end, and there will be no one to help him.

Notes
- v. 17: "to destroy her" — the construction implies the daughter is given to be destroyed, not used in the traditional sense.
- v. 21: "in divisions" — a reference to the destabilized or divided state of the kingdom, not to the people.
- v. 31: "abomination of desolation" — a standard term for an idol or profane object set up in the temple.
- v. 45: "the islands of the sea" — a possible reference to the region near the Mediterranean coast, not literal islands.
