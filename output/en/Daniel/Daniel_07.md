# Daniel 7

¹ In the first year of Belshazzar, king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream and visions of his head as he lay in bed. He wrote down the dream, and here is the summary of what he saw. ² Daniel said, "I had a vision in the night, and I saw the four winds of heaven rushing upon the great sea. ³ From the sea came up four great beasts, each different from the others. ⁴ The first was like a lion and had the wings of an eagle. I watched until its wings were plucked off, and it was lifted from the earth and made to stand on two feet like a man. And a human heart was given to it. ⁵ Then I saw another beast, the second, like a bear. It was raised up on one side and had three ribs in its mouth between its teeth. And this one was told, 'Arise, devour much flesh.' ⁶ After this, I looked again and saw another beast, like a leopard, with four wings of a bird under it. It had four heads, and to it was given dominion. ⁷ After this, I looked again and saw another beast, the fourth, terrifying and dreadful and very strong. It had iron teeth and it devoured and crushed, and it trampled the rest with its feet. It was different from all the other beasts, and it had ten horns. ⁸ I was considering the horns when another little horn came up, different from the others. Three of the previous horns were uprooted before it, and this horn had eyes like human eyes and a mouth speaking great things. ⁹ I kept watching until thrones were set in place and the Ancient of Days took his seat. His clothing was white as snow, and the hair of his head was like pure wool. His throne was a blazing fire, and its wheels were burning flames. ¹⁰ A stream of fire flowed out from before him. A thousand thousands attended him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him. A judgment was seated, and books were opened. ¹¹ I kept watching because of the great words the horn was speaking, until the beast was slain and its body was thrown into the fire. ¹² The remaining beasts had their dominion taken away, but they were allowed to live for a time, times, and half a time. ¹³ I kept watching in the night vision and saw one like a son of man coming with the clouds of heaven. He approached the Ancient of Days and was brought near to him. ¹⁴ To him was given dominion and glory and a kingdom, so that all peoples, nations, and languages would serve him. His dominion is an eternal dominion that will not pass away, and his kingdom will not be destroyed. ¹⁵ My spirit was troubled, Daniel, and the visions of my head terrified me. ¹⁶ I approached one of those standing there and asked him the truth about all this. He told me the interpretation and revealed the meaning to me. ¹⁷ These four great beasts are four kings who will rise from the earth. ¹⁸ But the holy ones of the Most High will receive the kingdom and possess it forever, even to the end of days. ¹⁹ I was curious to know about the fourth beast, so different from the others, dreadful beyond all, with iron teeth and copper claws that devour and crush, trampling the rest with its feet. ²⁰ I also wanted to know about its ten horns, the one that came up and displaced three of the horns, the one with eyes and a mouth speaking great things, and whose vision was greater than the others. ²¹ I watched, and that horn made war against the holy ones and prevailed over them. ²² Until the Ancient of Days came and judgment was given to the holy ones of the Most High, and the time arrived for the holy ones to take possession of the kingdom. ²³ The fourth beast is a fourth kingdom that will rise on the earth. It will be different from all the other kingdoms, devouring the whole land, trampling it, and crushing it. ²⁴ Its ten horns are ten kings who will rise, and after them another will rise, different from the former ones. He will humble three kings. ²⁵ He will speak against the Most High, oppress the holy ones of the Most High, and plan to change times and laws. He will be given into his hand until a time, times, and half a time. ²⁶ Judgment will be rendered, and his dominion will be taken away to be destroyed and annihilated, even to the end. ²⁷ The kingdom, the dominion, and the greatness of all the kingdoms under the whole heaven will be given to the holy ones of the Most High. Their kingdom is an eternal kingdom, and all rulers will serve and obey them. ²⁸ To this point, Daniel, my thoughts greatly disturbed me and my appearance changed, and the word in my heart was preserved.

Notes
- v. 1: "first year of Belshazzar" — the text uses "year 1" as a time reference; "first year" is used here for clarity.
- v. 8: "another little horn came up" — the text describes the rise of a new horn with specific characteristics, which is rendered clearly.
- v. 13: "one like a son of man coming with the clouds of heaven" — the imagery is translated directly from the source, avoiding theological interpretation.
- v. 25: "a time, times, and half a time" — this is a standard apocalyptic time expression, interpreted as three-and-a-half time units.
- v. 28: "my thoughts greatly disturbed me" — the emotional impact on Daniel is captured as described in the source text.
