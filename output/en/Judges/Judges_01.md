# Judges 1

¹ After Joshua died, the Israelites asked the LORD, "Who will lead us to fight the Canaanites first?" ² The LORD said, "Judah will go up. I have given the land into his hand." ³ Judah said to his brother Simeon, "Come up with me to my territory, and we will fight the Canaanites. I will also go with you to your territory." So Simeon went with him. ⁴ Judah went up, and the LORD gave the Canaanites and the Perizzites into his hand. He defeated them at Bezek, killing ten thousand men. ⁵ They found Adoni-Bezek in Bezek and fought with him. They defeated the Canaanites and the Perizzites. ⁶ Adoni-Bezek fled, but they pursued him and captured him. They cut off the tips of his fingers and toes. ⁷ Adoni-Bezek said, "Seventy kings with the tips of their fingers and toes cut off used to gather scraps from under my table. As I have done, so God has repaid me." So they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there. ⁸ The people of Judah fought with Jerusalem, captured it, and struck it down with the sword. They burned the city with fire. ⁹ Afterward, the people of Judah went down to fight the Canaanites living in the hill country, the Negev, and the lowland. ¹⁰ Judah went to the Canaanites living in Hebron, whose name earlier was Kirjath Arba. He defeated Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai. ¹¹ From there he went to the people living in Debir, whose name earlier was Kirjath Sepher. ¹² Caleb said, "Whoever defeats Kirjath Sepher and captures it, I will give my daughter Acsah to him as a wife." ¹³ Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, captured it. So Caleb gave his daughter Acsah to him as a wife. ¹⁴ When she came to him, she urged him to ask her father for a field. As they were leaving, the donkey she was riding on stumbled, and Caleb said to her, "What is the matter?" ¹⁵ She said to him, "Give me a blessing, for you have given me land in the Negev. Give me springs as well." So Caleb gave her the upper and lower springs. ¹⁶ The sons of Kenaz, Moses's brother-in-law, came up from the city of palm trees with the people of Judah to the desert of Judah in the Negev and settled among the people. ¹⁷ Judah went with his brother Simeon and defeated the Canaanites living in Zephath. They destroyed the city and renamed it Horam. ¹⁸ Judah captured Azah, its territory, Ashkelon, its territory, Ekron, its territory, ¹⁹ and the LORD was with Judah, so he took possession of the hill country, though he could not dispossess the people living in the lowlands because they had iron chariots. ²⁰ They gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had promised. From there he dispossessed the three sons of Anak. ²¹ The Jebusites living in Jerusalem were not dispossessed by the people of Benjamin. The Jebusites continued to live in Jerusalem among the people of Benjamin to this day. ²² The people of Joseph also went up, to Bethel, and the LORD was with them. ²³ The people of Joseph captured Bethel, whose name earlier was Luz. ²⁴ The guards saw a man going out from the city and said to him, "Show us the entrance to the city, and we will be kind to you." ²⁵ He showed them the entrance to the city, and they struck it down with the sword, killing the man and his whole family. ²⁶ The man went to the land of the Hittites, built a city, and named it Luz. That name remains to this day. ²⁷ The people of Manasseh did not dispossess the people of Beth Shan and its towns, Taanach and its towns, Dor and its towns, and the people of Ibleam and its towns, and the people of Megiddo and its towns, and the people of Napheth and its towns. The Canaanites continued to live in that land. ²⁸ When Israel became strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, but they did not dispossess them. ²⁹ The people of Ephraim did not dispossess the Canaanites living in Gezer, so the Canaanites continued to live in Gezer among them and became their forced laborers. ³⁰ The people of Zebulun did not dispossess the people of Kitron and Nahlol, so the Canaanites continued to live among them and became their forced laborers. ³¹ The people of Asher did not dispossess the people of Acre, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob. ³² The people of Asher continued to live among the Canaanites who were still living in the land, because they did not dispossess them. ³³ The people of Naphtali did not dispossess the people of Beth Shemesh and Beth Anath, so the Canaanites continued to live among them in Beth Shemesh and Beth Anath, and they became their forced laborers. ³⁴ The Amorites forced the people of Dan to live in the hill country, because they did not let them come down to the lowlands. ³⁵ The Amorites began to live in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim. The house of Joseph grew strong, and they became their forced laborers. ³⁶ The boundary of the Amorites stretched from Maaleh Akrabbim from the rock upward.

Notes
- v. 14: The text implies Acsah asked for springs, but the exact wording of her request is interpretive. The translation follows the sense of her "urging" her husband to ask her father, as the Hebrew is ambiguous about whether she asked directly or through him.
- v. 15: "Upper and lower springs" reflects Caleb giving her two distinct sources of water, likely referring to different elevations or locations.
- v. 27: The Greek version adds "Skithon" for Beth Shan, but the Hebrew text does not include it, so it is omitted.
- v. 36: "Rock upward" refers to the boundary extending upward from the rock at Maaleh Akrabbim.
