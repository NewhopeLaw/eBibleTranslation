# Judges 10

¹ After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man from the tribe of Issachar, rose up to deliver Israel. He lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim. ¹⁰ They cried out to the LORD and said, “We have sinned against you. We have abandoned our God and worshiped the Baals.” ¹¹ The LORD said to the people of Israel, “Did I not deliver you from Egypt and from the Amorites, and from the Ammonites and the Philistines? ¹² The Sidonians, the Amalekites, and the Maonites oppressed you, and you cried out to me, and I rescued you from their hands. ¹³ But now you have abandoned me and worshiped other gods. Therefore, I will no longer deliver you.” ¹⁴ Go and cry out to the gods you have chosen for yourselves. Let them deliver you in your time of distress. ¹⁵ Then the people of Israel said to the LORD, “We have sinned. Please do to us whatever seems good to you, but only deliver us today.” ¹⁶ They removed the foreign gods from among them and began to serve the LORD. His heart softened in the labor of Israel. ¹⁷ The Ammonites gathered and encamped in Gilead, and the people of Israel gathered and encamped in Mizpah. ¹⁸ The leaders of Gilead said to one another, “Who will be the man who will begin to fight against the sons of Ammon? He shall be the head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.” ² He judged Israel for twenty-three years, and then he died and was buried in Shamir. ³ After him, Jair the Gileadite rose up and judged Israel for twenty-two years. ⁴ He had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkeys, and he owned thirty cities. These cities are still called the Thirty of Jair to this day in the land of Gilead. ⁵ Jair died and was buried in Kamon. ⁶ The people of Israel again did evil in the sight of the LORD. They served the Baals and the Ashteroth, the gods of Aram, the gods of Sidon, the gods of Moab, the gods of the Ammonites, and the gods of the Philistines. They abandoned the LORD and did not serve him. ⁷ The anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and he handed them over to the power of the Philistines and the Ammonites. ⁸ That year they oppressed and crushed all the people of Israel who lived across the Jordan in the land of the Amorites in Gilead for eighteen years. ⁹ The Ammonites crossed the Jordan to fight against Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim. The people of Israel were greatly distressed.

Notes  
- v. 4: "thirty sons who rode on thirty donkeys" — the Hebrew is ambiguous whether the sons rode on thirty donkeys collectively or each had a donkey. The translation assumes each son had one.  
- v. 11-13: The LORD lists the nations from which Israel was delivered, but the Hebrew word order and phrasing are slightly disjointed. The translation aims for clarity and natural English flow.  
- v. 18: "the head over all the inhabitants of Gilead" — the Hebrew uses "rosh," which can mean "head" or "leader." The translation renders it as "head" to reflect the symbolic leadership role.
