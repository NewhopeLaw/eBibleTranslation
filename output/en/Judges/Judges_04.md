# Judges 4

¹ The Israelites continued to do evil in the eyes of the LORD, and Ehud died. ² The LORD handed them over to Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor. The commander of his army was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth of the Nations. ³ The Israelites cried out to the LORD, for he had nine hundred iron-chariots, and he oppressed the Israelites harshly for twenty years. ⁴ Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lapidot, was judging Israel at that time. ⁵ She judged Israel from under the palm tree of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the Israelites came to her for judgment. ⁶ She sent and called Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali, and said to him, "Has not the LORD, the God of Israel, commanded you to go and take ten thousand men from the people of Naphtali and from the people of Zebulun, ⁷ and lead them up to Mount Tabor, and take with you ten thousand men? I will lead Sisera, the commander of Jabin's army, and all his chariots and his people, to you at the Kishon River, and I will give him into your hands." ⁸ Barak said to her, "If you will go with me, I will go. But if you will not go with me, I will not go." ⁹ She said, "Certainly I will go with you, but the honor of the campaign will not be yours, for the LORD will sell Sisera into the hands of a woman." So Deborah rose and went with Barak to Kedesh. ¹⁰ Barak called out the people of Zebulun and the people of Naphtali to Kedesh, and ten thousand men went up with him, and Deborah went with him. ¹¹ Hever the Kenite had separated from the other Kenites, the descendants of Hobab, the brother-in-law of Moses, and had set up his tent as far as the acacia tree near Kedesh. ¹² The news was told to Sisera that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor. ¹³ Sisera called out all his nine hundred iron-chariots and all the people with him from Harosheth of the Nations to the Kishon River. ¹⁴ Deborah said to Barak, "Get up, for this is the day in which the LORD has given Sisera into your hands. Has not the LORD gone out ahead of you?" So Barak went down from Mount Tabor, and ten thousand men followed him. ¹⁵ The LORD threw Sisera, all his chariots, and all his army into confusion before Barak. Sisera dismounted from his chariot and fled on foot. ¹⁶ Barak pursued the chariots and the army to Harosheth of the Nations, and all the army of Sisera fell by the sword; not one was left. ¹⁷ Sisera fled on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Hever the Kenite, for there was peace between Jabin the king of Hazor and the tent of Hever the Kenite. ¹⁸ Jael came out to meet Sisera and said to him, "Come in, my lord. Don't be afraid." So he went into her tent, and she covered him with a cloak. ¹⁹ He said to her, "Please give me a little water, for I am thirsty." She opened a skin of milk and gave him a drink, and she covered him. ²⁰ He said to her, "Stand at the door of the tent. If a man comes and asks you, 'Is there a man here?' you must say, 'No.'" ²¹ Jael, the wife of Hever, took a tent peg and a hammer in her hand. She went quietly to him and drove the peg into his temple, and it went through into the ground, while he was asleep and exhausted. He died. ²² When Barak came to pursue Sisera, Jael went out to meet him and said to him, "Come, I will show you the man you are looking for." He went to her, and there was Sisera lying dead, with the tent peg in his temple. ²³ So God subdued Jabin the king of Canaan before the Israelites that day. ²⁴ The hand of the Israelites grew stronger and stronger against Jabin the king of Canaan until they had killed Jabin the king of Canaan.

Notes
- v. 2: "Jabin" and "Sisera" are proper names. "Hazor" and "Harosheth of the Nations" are place names. The Hebrew "חָצוֹר" is "Hazor," and "חֲרֹשֶׁת הַגּוֹיִם" is "Harosheth of the Nations."
- v. 11: "Hever the Kenite" is a proper name. "Kedesh" and "acacia tree" are place names. The Hebrew "קֶֽדֶשׁ" is "Kedesh," and "אֵלוֹן בְּצַעֲנַנִּים" is "acacia tree."
- v. 21: "Tent peg" is used to refer to the tool Jael used to kill Sisera. The Hebrew "יְתַד הָאֹהֶל" is "tent peg."
