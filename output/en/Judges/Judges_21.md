# Judges 21

¹ The men of Israel swore a vow at Mizpah, saying, "None among us will give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife." ² The people came to Bethel and sat there until evening before the LORD and lifted their voices and wept bitterly. ³ They said, "Why, O LORD God of Israel, has this happened in Israel, to leave one tribe unaccounted for among Israel?" ⁴ The next day they arose early and built an altar there and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. ⁵ The Israelites said, "Who among the tribes of Israel did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah? Whoever did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah should be put to death." ⁶ The Israelites took pity on Benjamin, their brother, and said, "This day one tribe has been struck down from Israel. ⁷ What shall we do for the remaining men to get wives? We have sworn by the LORD not to give our daughters to Benjamin as wives." ⁸ They said, "Which among the tribes of Israel did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah? And behold, no one from the men of Jabesh in Gilead has come to the camp from the assembly." ⁹ The people searched and found that no one was from the men who dwelled in Jabesh in Gilead. ¹⁰ The assembly sent twelve thousand men of valor from Israel and commanded them, "Go and strike the men who dwell in Jabesh in Gilead with the edge of the sword, along with the women and children." ¹¹ And this is the command you must give: every man and every woman who knows the intimacy of a man must be put to death, but you must take the virgins for yourselves." ¹² They found four hundred young women who had not known a man in the intimacy of a man, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, in the land of Canaan. ¹³ The entire assembly sent messengers to Benjamin, who had been at the rock of Rimmon, and called out to them in peace. ¹⁴ Benjamin returned to the Israelites at that time, and the Israelites gave them the women who had been spared from the daughters of Jabesh in Gilead. And so it was done for them. ¹⁵ The people took pity on Benjamin because the LORD had opened a breach among the tribes of Israel. ¹⁶ The elders of the assembly said, "What can we do for the remaining men to get wives, since a wife has been taken from Benjamin?" ¹⁷ They said, "The inheritance of the survivors belongs to Benjamin, so that no tribe may be wiped out from Israel. ¹⁸ But we cannot give them wives from our daughters, for we have sworn by the Israelites, saying, 'Cursed is the one who gives a wife to Benjamin.'" ¹⁹ They said, "Look, the festival of the LORD is in Shiloh, which is from the north of Bethel to the east of the sun, on the road that goes up from Bethel to Shechem and from the south to Lebonah. ²⁰ So command the men of Benjamin, saying, 'Go and lie in ambush in the vineyards, ²¹ and watch. If the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, then you must come out from the vineyards and each of you must take a wife for himself from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin. ²² And when their fathers or their brothers come to argue with us, we will say to them, 'Do us a kindness because we did not take any man's wife in the battle, for you did not give them to us. So you will not be guilty.'" ²³ The men of Benjamin did so. They took wives from the dancers they had captured, and they went and returned to their inheritance, built the cities and settled in them. ²⁴ At that time the Israelites went away, each to his tribe and to his family, and they went out from there, each to his inheritance. ²⁵ In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did what seemed right in his own eyes.

Notes
- v. 1: "swore a vow" — the Hebrew is "nisha'ah" (נִשְׁבַּע) indicating a binding oath or vow.
- v. 11: "virgins" — the Hebrew uses "betulah" (בְּתוּלָה), which means "maiden/virgin."
- v. 19: "the festival of the LORD is in Shiloh" — the Hebrew refers to the pilgrimage feast, possibly the Feast of Tabernacles or a local Shiloh-specific event.
- v. 25: "In those days there was no king in Israel" — this is an editorial comment, not a narrative statement.
