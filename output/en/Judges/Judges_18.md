# Judges 18

¹ In those days there was no king in Israel. In those days the tribe of Dan was seeking a place to claim as their inheritance, for it had not yet fallen to them by lot among the tribes of Israel. ² The people of Dan sent from among their clans five men from the farthest parts, men of strength from Zorah and from Eshtaol, to scout the land and inspect it. They said to them, "Go and inspect the land." They went to the hill country of Ephraim to the house of Micah and stayed there. ³ While they were with the house of Micah, they recognized the voice of the Levite youth and turned aside there. They said to him, "Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? And what is yours here?" ⁴ He said to them, "This and that Micah did for me, and he hired me, and I have become a priest to him." ⁵ They said to him, "Inquire of God so we may know whether our journey, which we are taking, will succeed." ⁶ The priest said to them, "Go in peace before the LORD, your journey that you are going on." ⁷ The five men went and came to Laish. They saw the people living there in safety, like Sidonians, quiet and secure, and there was no one in the land who ruled or oppressed. They were far from Sidon, and they had no dealings with anyone. ⁸ They went to their brothers in Zorah and Eshtaol and said to their brothers, "What are you doing?" ⁹ Their brothers said, "Arise and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold, it is very good. And you are hesitating? Do not delay to go in and take possession of the land." ¹⁰ When you go, you will come to a people at ease, and the land is wide, for God has given it into your hands. It is a place where there is no lack of anything on the earth." ¹¹ So they left from there, from the clans of Dan, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, six hundred men armed with weapons for battle. ¹² They went up and camped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. For this reason the name of the place was called Camp of Dan until that day. It is behind Kiriath-jearim. ¹³ They passed on from there and went to the hill country of Ephraim until they came to the house of Micah. ¹⁴ The five men who had gone to scout the land answered the people of Laish and said to their brothers, "Do you know that there are an ephod, teraphim, a carved image, and a metal idol in these houses? So now decide what you will do." ¹⁵ They turned aside there and came to the house of the Levite youth, the house of Micah, and asked him for peace. ¹⁶ Six hundred armed men with weapons of battle stood at the gate of the city, from the people of Dan. ¹⁷ The five men who had gone to scout the land went in, took the carved image, the ephod, the teraphim, and the metal idol, and the priest stood at the gate. The six hundred men armed with weapons of battle were with them. ¹⁸ Those who had gone into Micah's house took the carved image, the ephod, the teraphim, and the metal idol. The priest said to them, "What are you doing?" ¹⁹ They said to him, "Be quiet, put your hand on your mouth and come with us. Be a father and a priest to us. Is it better for you to be a priest for one man's house or to be a priest for a tribe and family in Israel?" ²⁰ The priest's heart was glad, and he took the ephod, the teraphim, and the carved image and went among the people. ²¹ They turned around and went away, and they put the children, the livestock, and their goods before them. ²² They had already distanced themselves from the house of Micah when the men who were in the houses, those with Micah's house, cried out and caught up with the people of Dan. ²³ They called out to the people of Dan and turned their faces back, and they said to Micah, "What is this matter of yours that you are crying out?" ²⁴ Micah said, "You have taken my gods that I made, and you have also taken the priest. Go, and what is left for me? And what are you saying to me, 'What is the matter with you?'" ²⁵ The people of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, lest fierce men come upon you and you and your household be destroyed." ²⁶ The people of Dan went on their way. Micah saw that they were stronger than him, so he turned back and returned to his house. ²⁷ The people of Dan took what Micah had made and the priest who was with him, and they went up to Laish to a people at ease and secure, and they struck them with the edge of the sword and burned the city with fire. ²⁸ There was no one to rescue them, for it was far from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone. It was in the valley of Lebo-hamath. They built the city and lived there. ²⁹ They named the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel. But the name of the city was Laish at first. ³⁰ The people of Dan set up the carved image for themselves. Jonathan, son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, he and his sons became priests for the tribe of Dan until the day of the exile of the land. ³¹ They placed the carved image that Micah had made, all the days the house of God was in Shiloh.

Notes
- v. 29: "Olam" is a rare name for the city; given the context, it is likely a proper noun and not a transliteration of the word "olam" meaning "everlasting," hence retained as "Oulam."
- v. 30: "Gershom" and "Manasseh" are standard Hebrew names and rendered accordingly.
- v. 31: The reference to "the house of God in Shiloh" is a contextual clarification of the time period when the carved image was used.
