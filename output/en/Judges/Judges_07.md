# Judges 7

¹ Jerubbaal, that is Gideon, and all the people with him, camped at En-hazor. Midian's camp was north of them, at the hill of Moreh in the valley. ² The LORD said to Gideon: "The people with you are too many for me to give Midian into their hands, lest Israel boast over me, saying, 'My own hand has saved me.'" ³ Now then, proclaim in the hearing of the people, saying: 'Whoever is afraid and timid, let him return home from the hill of Galilee.' So twenty-two thousand of the people turned back, and twelve thousand remained. ⁴ The LORD said to Gideon: "Still the people are too many. Bring them down to the water, and I will test them there for you. And it will be that whoever licks the water like a dog, I will have him go with you. Whoever kneels to drink, leave him." ⁵ So Gideon brought the people down to the water. The LORD said to Gideon: "Everyone who licks the water with his tongue like a dog, place him separately. Everyone who kneels to drink, leave him." ⁶ The number of those who lapped the water into their hands to their mouths was three hundred men, and all the rest of the people knelt to drink the water. ⁷ The LORD said to Gideon: "With these three hundred men who lapped, I will save you and give Midian into your hand. The rest of the people will each return to his place." ⁸ So they took the provisions in their hands and the trumpets, and all the Israelites were sent back to their tents. But the three hundred held fast, while Midian's camp was below them in the valley. ⁹ That night the LORD said to Gideon: "Rise, go down into the camp, for I am handing it over to you. ¹⁰ If you are afraid to go down, go with your servant Purah into the camp. ¹¹ Listen to what they say, and afterward your hands will grow strong to go down into the camp." So he went down with his servant Purah to the edge of the camp. ¹² Midian, Amalek, and all the people of the east were lying in the valley like locusts in number. Their camels could not be counted, like the sand on the seashore for abundance. ¹³ Gideon came and there was a man recounting a dream to his companion, saying, "I had a dream. I saw a cake of barley bread tumbling into Midian's camp. It came all the way to the tent and knocked it down, turning it upside down so the tent collapsed." ¹⁴ His companion answered him, "This can mean nothing else but that the sword of Gideon, the son of Joash, a man of Israel, has been given into his hand. God will deliver Midian and all the camp into his hands." ¹⁵ When Gideon heard the account of the dream and its interpretation, he bowed to the ground. He returned to the camp of Israel and said, "Arise, for the LORD has given Midian's camp into your hands." ¹⁶ He divided the three hundred men into three companies, and gave trumpets into the hands of all, empty jars, and torches inside the jars. ¹⁷ He said to them, "Look at me and do likewise. So it will be when I do, you will do. I am going to the edge of the camp. And it will be that as I do, so you will do. ¹⁸ I will blow the trumpet, and I and all with me will blow it. Then you also blow the trumpets all around the entire camp, and say, 'For the LORD and for Gideon!'" ¹⁹ Gideon and the hundred men with him went to the edge of the camp at the beginning of the watch in the middle of the night. They had just raised the sentries, and they blew the trumpets and broke the jars in their hands. ²⁰ The three companies blew the trumpets, broke the jars, and held the torches in their left hands and the trumpets in their right hands to blow. They cried out, "Sword for the LORD and for Gideon!" ²¹ Each man stood in place around the camp. The whole camp ran, shouting and fleeing. ²² The three hundred blew the trumpets, and the LORD set a sword in each man's hand against his companion and against all the camp. The whole camp fled, all the way to Beth-shittah and Zererath, as far as the edge of Abel-meholah, all the way to Tabbath. ²³ Men of Israel from Naphtali, from Asher, and from all Manasseh pursued them. ²⁴ Gideon sent messengers throughout the hill of Ephraim, saying, "Come down to meet Midian and seize the water for them as far as Beth-barah and the Jordan." So all the men of Ephraim seized the water for them as far as Beth-barah and the Jordan. ²⁵ They captured the two leaders of Midian, Or and Zeeb. They struck Or down at the rock of Or, and Zeeb they struck down at the winepress of Zeeb. Then they pursued Midian and brought the heads of Or and Zeeb across the Jordan.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "all the rest of the people" — The Hebrew uses יְתֵר (yeter) and כָּל (kol) to emphasize the complete separation between the three hundred and the remaining people.
- v. 25: "the heads of Or and Zeeb" — The heads are brought as trophies to Gideon, likely for identification and to confirm their deaths.
