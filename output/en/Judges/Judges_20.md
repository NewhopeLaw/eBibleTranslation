# Judges 20

¹ All the Israelites went out and gathered together as one man from Dan and Bethel, and the land of Gilead to the LORD at Mizpah. ² All the tribes of Israel stood before the LORD in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand foot soldiers, each armed with a sword. ³ The men of Benjamin heard that the Israelites had gone up to Mizpah. The Israelites said to the men of Benjamin, "Tell us, how this evil thing has happened to us!" ⁴ The Levite, the man whose concubine had been murdered, answered and said, "I came to Gibeah that belongs to Benjamin, I and my concubine, to spend the night. ⁵ The men of Gibeah rose up against me and surrounded my house during the night, they sought to kill me and abused my concubine, and she died." ⁶ I took hold of my concubine and cut her into pieces and sent her to every part of the land of the inheritance of the Israelites, for they have committed a crime and a disgrace in Israel. ⁷ Now you, all you sons of Israel, give yourselves a word and a plan here." ⁸ All the people stood as one man, saying, "We will not go anyone to his tent, nor will any man return to his house." ⁹ And now, this is the thing that we will do to Gibeah. We will go up to it by lot. ¹⁰ We will take ten men from a hundred, a hundred from a thousand, and a thousand from ten thousand to bring the punishment on the people to go to Gibeah of Benjamin, to do to it all the disgrace that it has done in Israel." ¹¹ All the men of Israel gathered together in the city as one man. ¹² The tribes of Israel sent men to every tribe of Benjamin, saying, "What evil thing has happened among you?" ¹³ And now, give us the men, the sons of Belial who are in Gibeah, and we will kill them and purge the evil from Israel." But the sons of Benjamin did not listen to their brothers the sons of Israel. ¹⁴ The sons of Benjamin gathered from the cities to go out to battle against the sons of Israel. ¹⁵ The sons of Benjamin mustered that day twenty-six thousand men from the cities, each armed with a sword, in addition to the men who lived in Gibeah, who mustered seven hundred chosen men. ¹⁶ From all the people, there were seven hundred chosen men who were left-handed, each of whom could sling a stone at a hair and not miss. ¹⁷ The men of Israel mustered apart from Benjamin four hundred thousand men armed with swords, all of them men of war. ¹⁸ They rose and went up to Bethel and asked of God. The sons of Israel said, "Who shall go up first to battle against the sons of Benjamin?" And the LORD said, "Judah shall go up first." ¹⁹ The sons of Israel rose at dawn and encamped around Gibeah. ²⁰ The men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and the sons of Israel arranged themselves to fight against Gibeah. ²¹ The sons of Benjamin went out from Gibeah and destroyed Israel that day twenty-two thousand men on the ground. ²² The people of Israel strengthened themselves, and added to their battle in the place where they had arranged themselves on the first day. ²³ The sons of Israel went up and wept before the LORD until evening, and asked of the LORD, saying, "Should we add to go out to battle against the sons of Benjamin, our brothers?" And the LORD said, "Go up to him." ²⁴ The sons of Israel came near to the sons of Benjamin on the second day. ²⁵ The sons of Benjamin went out to meet them from Gibeah on the second day, and they destroyed the sons of Israel again eighteen thousand men on the ground, all of them armed with swords. ²⁶ All the sons of Israel and the people went up to Bethel, and wept, and sat there before the LORD, and fasted that day until evening, and brought burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD. ²⁷ The sons of Israel asked of the LORD, and there was the ark of the covenant of the LORD in those days. ²⁸ And Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, was standing before it in those days, saying, "Should we add to go out to battle against the sons of Benjamin, our brothers, or should we hold back?" And the LORD said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give them into your hand." ²⁹ Israel set ambushes around Gibeah. ³⁰ The sons of Israel went up against the sons of Benjamin on the third day, and against Gibeah as before. ³¹ The sons of Benjamin went out to meet the people and were driven out of the city, and they began to strike down some of the people, one strike after another, on the roads, one going up to Bethel and one going to Gibeah in the field, about thirty men in Israel. ³² The sons of Benjamin said, "They are falling before us as at the beginning." And the sons of Israel said, "Let us flee and scatter them from the city to the roads," and they did so. ³³ Every man stood from his place, and they arranged themselves at Baal Tamar. And the ambush of Israel moved from its place from Marah Geba. ³⁴ They came from the front of Gibeah ten thousand chosen men from all Israel, and the battle was fierce, and they did not know that the evil was upon them. ³⁵ And the LORD struck Benjamin before Israel, and the sons of Israel destroyed Benjamin that day twenty-five thousand and one hundred men, all of them armed with swords. ³⁶ The sons of Benjamin saw that they were struck down, and the sons of Israel gave Benjamin a place, for they trusted in the ambush that they had set around Gibeah. ³⁷ But the ambush rushed and spread out to Gibeah, and the ambush was moved, and they struck the entire city with the edge of the sword. ³⁸ And the sign to the men of Israel was with the ambush of the battle, to bring them up a column of smoke from the city. ³⁹ The men of Israel saw that the ambush had captured Gibeah, and they stood in the battle. Benjamin began to strike down some of the men of Israel, about thirty men, because they said, "Surely they are falling before us, as in the first battle." ⁴⁰ And the column of smoke began to rise more than from the city, as a column of smoke. Benjamin looked behind him, and behold, the column of the city had gone up to the heavens. ⁴¹ The men of Israel turned back. And the men of Benjamin hurried, because they saw that the evil had come upon them. ⁴² And they turned their faces before the men of Israel toward the road of the desert and fled, and the battle pursued them, and those who came from the cities destroyed them in the middle of them. ⁴³ They struck the Benjaminites and pursued them from Nuah to the front of Gibeah toward the east of the sun. ⁴⁴ And eighteen thousand men of Benjamin fell, all of them men of valor. ⁴⁵ The rest turned and fled into the desert toward the rock of Rimmon, and the sons of Israel killed five thousand of them, and the sons of Israel went down after them to Gedem, and struck two thousand of them. ⁴⁶ And all the men who fell from Benjamin were twenty-five thousand men armed with swords that day, all of them men of valor. ⁴⁷ The rest turned and fled into the desert toward the rock of Rimmon, six hundred men, and they sat in the rock of Rimmon for four months. ⁴⁸ The men of Israel returned to the sons of Benjamin and struck them with the edge of the sword from every city, from man to beast, to everything that was found, and they burned with fire every city that was found.

Notes
- v. 34: "ten thousand chosen men from all Israel" – the Hebrew uses "עֶשְׂרִים אֲלָפִים" (twenty thousand), but the context of the ambush suggests a smaller number, possibly a scribal error. The Septuagint and the narrative flow suggest "ten thousand" is intended.
- v. 48: "they burned with fire every city that was found" – the Hebrew uses "שִׁלְּחוּ בָאֵשׁ" which is a rare construction, but "burned with fire" is the most faithful rendering.
- v. 45: "the sons of Israel went down after them to Gedem" – the location "Gedem" is obscure, but the name is preserved as given.
