# Judges 12

¹ A man from Ephraim raised a shout and crossed to Zephath, saying to Jephthah, "Why did you cross to fight the sons of Ammon without calling us to go with you? Your house is now burned with fire because of you." ² Jephthah said to them, "I and my people had a bitter quarrel with the sons of Ammon. I called to you, and you did not rescue me from their hand. ³ I saw that you would not rescue, so I committed my life to my own hand and crossed to the sons of Ammon, and the LORD gave them into my hand. Why have you come up against me today to fight against me?" ⁴ Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead and fought against Ephraim, and the men of Gilead struck Ephraim, because they said, "You are fugitives of Ephraim in the middle of Gilead, in the middle of Manasseh." ⁵ Then Gilead captured the fords of the Jordan for Ephraim, and it was that when the fugitives of Ephraim said, "Let me cross," the men of Gilead would ask them, "Are you an Ephraimite?" And they would say, "No." ⁶ Then they would say to them, "Say now, 'Shibboleth.'" And they would say, "Sibboleth," and could not get the words right, and they seized them and killed them at the fords of the Jordan. At that time, forty-two thousand from Ephraim fell. ⁷ Jephthah judged Israel six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and was buried in the cities of Gilead. ⁸ After him, Ibzan from Bethlehem judged Israel. ⁹ He had thirty sons and thirty daughters, and he sent the daughters out to marry beyond the territory. He brought thirty daughters in from outside for his sons. He judged Israel seven years. ¹⁰ Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem. ¹¹ After him, Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel ten years. ¹² Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun. ¹³ After him, Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel. ¹⁴ He had forty sons and thirty grandsons who rode on seventy donkeys. He judged Israel eight years. ¹⁵ Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite died and was buried in Pirathon, in the land of Ephraim, on the hill of the Amalekites.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "Sibboleth" is a phonetic rendering of the Hebrew שִׁבֹּלֶת (shibboleth). The Ephraimites' inability to pronounce it correctly is central to the narrative.
- v. 6: "Sibboleth" is a transliteration of the Hebrew שִׂבֹּלֶת (sibboleth), representing the mispronunciation intended in the story.
