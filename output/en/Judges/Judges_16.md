# Judges 16

¹ Samson went to Gaza and saw there a prostitute. He went in to her. ² The Gazites were told, "Samson has come here." They encircled him and waited through the night at the city gate, all night long, saying, "Let the morning light come, and we will kill him." ³ Samson lay down until mid-night, and then rose at the halfway point of the night. He seized the doors of the city gate, along with their two doorposts, and carried them up with the bar, placing them on his shoulders, and he climbed up to the top of the hill facing Hebron and left them there. ⁴ After this, he loved a woman in Sorek Valley, and her name was Delilah. ⁵ The leaders of the Philistines went up to her and said, "Seduce him and see where his strength lies, how great it is, and how we might bind him so as to subdue him. And we will give you each man a thousand and a hundred silver shekels." ⁶ Delilah said to Samson, "Tell me, please, where your strength lies, where you are bound so that you might be subdued." ⁷ Samson said to her, "If they bind me with seven fresh, unbroken ropes, I will weaken, and I will be like any other man." ⁸ The leaders of the Philistines brought her seven fresh, unbroken ropes, and she bound him with them. ⁹ The ambush was waiting for her in the room, and she said to him, "The Philistines are coming to you, Samson!" And he tore the ropes from his arms like a thread of flax when it is burned in the flame; no one knew his strength. ¹⁰ Delilah said to Samson, "You have tricked me, and you have spoken falsely to me. Now tell me where you will be bound." ¹¹ Samson said to her, "If they bind me with new ropes that have not been used, I will weaken, and I will be like any other man." ¹² Delilah took new ropes and bound him with them. She said to him, "The Philistines are coming to you, Samson!" The ambush was waiting in the room, and he tore them from his arms like a thread. ¹³ Delilah said to Samson, "You have tricked me again and spoken falsely to me. Tell me now where you will be bound." Samson said to her, "If you weave the seven strands of my hair into a loom and fasten it with a pin in the wall, then I will weaken, and I will be like any other man." ¹⁴ It happened that as Samson lay sleeping, Delilah took the seven strands of his hair, wove them into the loom, and fastened them with a pin in the wall. Then she said, "The Philistines are coming to you, Samson!" He woke from his sleep, pulled out the pin from the wall, took down the loom and the pin. ¹⁵ Delilah said to him, "How can you say you love me, and yet your heart is not with me? You have done this to me three times, but you still did not tell me where your strength is so great." ¹⁶ And it came to pass that as she pressed him with her words day after day and tormented him, he grew weary to the point of death. ¹⁷ Then he told her all his heart, saying to her, "No razor has come up on my head, for I am a Nazirite to God from my mother's womb. If my head is shaved, my strength will leave me, and I will weaken, and I will be like every other man." ¹⁸ Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart, and she sent word to the leaders of the Philistines, saying, "Come up now, for he has told me all his heart." The leaders of the Philistines came to her and brought the silver in their hands. ¹⁹ She made him lie down on her knees and called a man to shave off the seven strands of his head. Then she began to subdue him, and his strength left him. ²⁰ Delilah said, "The Philistines are coming to you, Samson!" He woke from his sleep and said, "I shall go out as before, and as before I shall be struck down!" But he did not know that the Lord had departed from him. ²¹ The Philistines seized him, gouged out his eyes, and brought him down to Gaza. They bound him with bronze shackles, and he became a miller in the prison house. ²² His hair began to grow again, as it had been when he was shaved. ²³ The leaders of the Philistines gathered for a great feast to their god Dagon, and to rejoice. They said, "Our god has given our enemy, Samson, into our hands." ²⁴ The people saw him, and they praised their god, saying, "Our god has given into our hands our enemy, the one who ruined our land and who multiplied our casualties." ²⁵ When their hearts were merry, they said, "Call Samson, and he will amuse us." They called Samson from the prison house, and he amused them. They put him between the pillars. ²⁶ Samson said to the young man who held his hand, "Let me go, so that I may lean against the pillars on which the house stands, and I will rest myself on them." ²⁷ The house was full of men and women, and there the leaders of the Philistines were. On the roof were about three thousand men and women watching Samson amuse them. ²⁸ Samson called to the Lord and said, "My Lord, YHWH, remember me, please, and strengthen me, just this once, O God, so that I may take vengeance on the Philistines for my two eyes." ²⁹ Samson grasped the two central pillars of the house, on which the house stood, and leaned one with his right hand and one with his left. ³⁰ He said, "Let my life die with the Philistines!" And with great strength he pushed the house down on the leaders and the entire people in it. The number of those he killed in his death was greater than the number he had killed in his life. ³¹ His brothers and the entire household of his father went down, took him, and brought him up. They buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol, in the tomb of his father Manoah. He judged Israel twenty years.

Notes
- v. 20: "the Lord had departed from him" – this reflects the Hebrew's "YHWH had departed from him," indicating a loss of divine presence and strength.
