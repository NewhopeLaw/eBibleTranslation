# Romans 10

¹ Brothers, my inner longing and my prayer to God for them is toward salvation. ² For I testify about them that they have zeal for God, but not with knowledge, ³ because they are ignorant of the righteousness of God, and are trying to establish their own righteousness, not submitting to the righteousness of God. ⁴ For the goal of the law is Christ, for righteousness to all who believe. ⁵ For Moses writes that the righteousness from the law is this: The one who does the law will live by it. ⁶ But the righteousness from faith says this: Do not say in your heart, "Who will ascend into heaven?" to bring down Christ; ⁷ or "Who will descend into the abyss?" to bring up Christ from the dead. ⁸ Rather, what does it say? "The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart"—this is the word of faith that we proclaim. ⁹ Because if you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved; ¹⁰ for with the heart one believes into righteousness, and with the mouth one confesses into salvation. ¹¹ For the Scripture says, "Everyone who believes in him will not be put to shame." ¹² For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, for the same Lord is Lord of all, rich to all who call upon him. ¹³ For "everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved." ¹⁴ But how will they call on him in whom they have not believed? And how will they believe in him of whom they have not heard? And how will they hear without a preacher? ¹⁵ And how will they preach unless they are sent? As it is written, "How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!" ¹⁶ But not all have obeyed the gospel. For Isaiah says, "Lord, who has believed our message?" ¹⁷ So faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ. ¹⁸ But I ask, "Did they not hear?" Yes, indeed, "Their voice has gone out to all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world." ¹⁹ But I ask, "Did Israel not understand?" First Moses says, "I will provoke you to jealousy with a nation not a nation, with a people without understanding I will anger you." ²⁰ And Isaiah dares to say, "I was found by those who did not seek me, I became visible to those who did not ask for me." ²¹ But to Israel he says, "All day long I stretched out my hands to a rebellious, disputing people."

---

Notes
- v. 4: "the goal of the law is Christ" — the Greek word *telos* is translated as "goal" to reflect its meaning of "end" or "purpose" in a theological context.
- v. 9: "the Lord Jesus" — "Lord" here reflects *kyrios*, which is rendered consistently with the divine title.
- v. 13: "everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved" — this is a direct translation of the Septuagint rendering of 2 Samuel 22:47 and Psalm 22:31.
- v. 18: "Their voice has gone out to all the earth..." — this is a paraphrase of Psalm 19:4b, adapted to fit the context of the gospel proclamation.
