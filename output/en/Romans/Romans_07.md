# Romans 7

¹ Do you not know, brothers and sisters, that I am speaking in terms of the law, that the law has dominion over a person for as long as they live? ² For a married woman is bound by law to her husband as long as he lives. But if her husband dies, she is released from the law of the husband. ³ Therefore, if the husband is alive, she would be called an adulteress if she becomes another man's wife. But if the husband dies, she is free from the law, and it is not adultery to become another man's wife. ⁴ So then, my brothers and sisters, you also died to the law through the body of Christ, so that you might belong to another, to the one who was raised from the dead, in order that we might bear fruit to God. ⁵ For when we were in the flesh, the passions of sin, which were stirred up by the law, worked in our members to bear fruit for death. ⁶ But now we have been released from the law, having died to the one in which we were held, so that we might serve in newness of spirit, and not in the oldness of the written code. ⁷ What then shall we say? Is the law sin? No! But I did not know sin unless through the law, for I would not have known desire unless the law had said, "You shall not covet." ⁸ But sin, having taken an occasion through the commandment, produced in me every kind of desire. For apart from the law, sin is dead. ⁹ I was once alive without the law. But when the commandment came, sin awakened, ¹⁰ and I died. I found the commandment which was supposed to lead to life, but it brought death. ¹¹ For sin, having taken an occasion through the commandment, deceived me and through it killed me. ¹² Therefore, the law is holy, and the commandment is holy, just, and good. ¹³ Then is that which is good death to me? No! But it is sin, so that it might become evident as sin, working death in me through what is good, in order that the excess of the sin might become evident through the commandment. ¹⁴ For I know that the law is spiritual, but I am fleshly, sold under sin. ¹⁵ For what I am doing, I do not understand. For I do not do what I want, but what I hate, that I do. ¹⁶ If then I do what I do not want, I agree with the law that it is good. ¹⁷ But now it is no longer I who do it, but the sin dwelling in me. ¹⁸ For I know that good does not dwell in me, that is, in my flesh. For the will is present with me, but the good deed is not. ¹⁹ For what I want to do is good, but what I do is evil. ²⁰ If then I do what I do not want, it is no longer I who do it, but the sin dwelling in me. ²¹ I find then a law at work in me, the one willing to do the good, but the evil is present with me. ²² For I delight in the law of God in my inner self. ²³ But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind and taking me captive to the law of sin that is in my members. ²⁴ Wretched man that I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death? ²⁵ Thanks be to God through Jesus Christ our Lord! So then, on the one hand, with the mind I myself serve the law of God, but on the other hand, with the flesh, the law of sin.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "brothers and sisters" is a modern inclusive rendering of ἀδελφοί.
- v. 7: "I did not know sin unless through the law" reflects the contrast Paul draws between ignorance of sin apart from the law and its revelation through commandments.
- v. 13: "working death in me through what is good" captures the paradoxical role of the law as revealing sin while not providing deliverance from it.
- v. 18: "the will is present with me, but the good deed is not" reflects the Greek's contrast between θέλειν (will) and ποιῆσαι (to do).
- v. 23: "the law of sin that is in my members" is a direct translation of νόμῳ τῆς ἁμαρτίας τῷ ὄντι ἐν τοῖς μέλεσίν μου.
