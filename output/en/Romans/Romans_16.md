# Romans 16

¹ I recommend to you Phoebe, our sister, who is also a servant of the assembly in Cenchreae, ² that you receive her in the Lord with all the worthiness of the saints, and help her in whatever she may need from you, for she has been a benefactor to many, and even to me personally. ³ Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus, ⁴ who risked their own lives for me, to whom not only I, but all the assemblies of the nations are grateful, ⁵ and the assembly that meets at their house. Greet my beloved Epenetus, who is the firstfruits of Asia for Christ. ⁶ Greet Mary, who has labored much on your behalf. ⁷ Greet Andronicus and Junia, my relatives and fellow prisoners, who are prominent among the apostles, and who were in Christ before me. ⁸ Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord. ⁹ Greet Urban, our fellow worker in Christ, and my beloved Stachys. ¹⁰ Greet Apelles, who is approved in Christ. Greet those from Aristobulus. ¹¹ Greet my relative Herodion. Greet those from Narcissus who are in the Lord. ¹² Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who have labored in the Lord. Greet Persis, the beloved, who has labored much in the Lord. ¹³ Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother, who is also mine. ¹⁴ Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them. ¹⁵ Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them. ¹⁶ Greet one another with a holy kiss. The assemblies of Christ greet you all. ¹⁷ I urge you, brothers, to watch out for those who cause divisions and scandals contrary to the teaching you learned, and to turn away from them; ¹⁸ for such people do not serve our Lord Christ, but their own belly, and they deceive the hearts of the innocent with flattery and sweet words. ¹⁹ For your obedience has come to all. I rejoice over this, but I want you to be wise in what is good, and innocent in what is evil. ²⁰ The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. ²¹ Greet you Timothy, my fellow worker, and Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my relatives. ²² I greet you, Tertius, the one who wrote this letter in the Lord. ²³ Greet you Gaius, my host, and the whole assembly. Greet you Erastus, the city treasurer, and Quartus, the brother. ²⁴ The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "fellow workers in Christ Jesus" renders "συνεργούς μου ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ".
- v. 7: "prominent among the apostles" reflects the meaning of "ἐπίσημοι ἐν τοῖς ἀποστόλοις", which literally means "well known among the apostles".
- v. 13: "Rufus, chosen in the Lord" renders "Ῥοῦφον τὸν ἐκλεκτὸν ἐν κυρίῳ", where "ἐκλεκτὸν" is translated as "chosen" rather than "elect" for clarity.
- v. 18: "their own belly" is a metaphor for self-interest, translating "τῇ ἑαυτῶν κοιλίᾳ", which is not to be taken literally.
