# Romans 5

¹ Since we have been justified through faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ, ² through whom we also have gained access by faith into this grace in which we now stand, and we boast in hope of the glory of God. ³ Not only that, but we also boast in our sufferings, knowing that suffering produces endurance, ⁴ endurance produces character, and character produces hope. ⁵ Now hope does not disappoint, because the love of God has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit given to us. ⁶ For while we were still weak, Christ died for the ungodly at the appointed time. ⁷ Indeed, scarcely would anyone die for a righteous person; for someone good perhaps even one might dare to die. ⁸ But God demonstrates his own love for us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us. ⁹ Therefore, since we have now been justified by his blood, we will be saved through him from the wrath. ¹⁰ For if, when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of his Son, how much more, having been reconciled, will we be saved through the life of his Son. ¹¹ Not only that, but we also boast in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received the reconciliation. ¹² Therefore, just as through one man sin entered into the world, and through sin death, and so death spread to all people, because all sinned— ¹³ for until the law, sin was in the world, but sin is not counted where there is no law, ¹⁴ and yet death reigned from Adam to Moses, even over those who did not sin in the likeness of Adam's transgression, who is the pattern of the coming one. ¹⁵ But it is not as if the gift is like the trespass. For if through the trespass of the one the many died, much more did the grace of God and the gift in grace, given by the one man Jesus Christ, overflow to the many. ¹⁶ And the gift is not like the trespass. For if the judgment from one was for condemnation, the gift from many trespasses is for justification. ¹⁷ For if through the trespass of the one death reigned through the one, much more will those who receive the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one, Jesus Christ. ¹⁸ Therefore, just as through one trespass the judgment came upon all people to condemnation, so also through one act of righteousness the judgment of life came upon all people to justification. ¹⁹ For as through the disobedience of the one man many were made sinners, so also through the obedience of the one many will be made righteous. ²⁰ Now the law came in so that the trespass might increase. But where sin increased, grace increased all the more, ²¹ so that just as sin reigned in death, so also grace might reign through righteousness to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "the many" in the phrase "the many died" is a general reference and not a specific numerical claim.
- v. 14: "pattern of the coming one" reflects the interpretive link to Christ as a type or foreshadowing in Adam.
- v. 19: "many will be made righteous" uses "many" to mirror the structure of the previous phrase, though some interpretations read "all."
