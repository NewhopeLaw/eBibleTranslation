# Romans 12

¹ Therefore, I urge you, brothers and sisters, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God, your reasonable act of worship. ² Do not be conformed to this present age, but be transformed by the renewing of your minds, so that you may discern what is the will of God—what is good, pleasing, and perfect. ³ For by the grace given to me I say to each one among you not to think more highly of himself than he ought to think, but to think with sober judgment, each according to the measure of faith that God has assigned to him. ⁴ For just as we have many members in one body and all the members do not have the same function, ⁵ so we, though many, are one body in Christ, and individually members one of another. ⁶ We have gifts that differ according to the grace given to us: if prophecy, then in proportion to the faith; ⁷ if service, in service; if teaching, in teaching; ⁸ if exhortation, in exhortation; the one giving, let him give simply; the one presiding, with diligence; the one showing mercy, with cheerfulness. ⁹ Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good, ¹⁰ loving one another with mutual affection, giving preference to one another in honor, ¹¹ not slothful in zeal, but fervent in spirit, serving the Lord, ¹² rejoicing in hope, enduring in affliction, persevering in prayer, ¹³ contributing to the needs of the saints, and pursuing hospitality. ¹⁴ Bless those who persecute you; bless and do not curse. ¹⁵ Rejoice with those who rejoice, weep with those who weep. ¹⁶ Be of the same mind toward one another; do not be haughty, but associate with the lowly. Do not be wise in your own estimation. ¹⁷ Do not repay anyone evil for evil; instead be mindful of doing what is good toward all. ¹⁸ If possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with all. ¹⁹ Beloved, do not avenge yourselves, but leave room for wrath, for it is written, "Vengeance is mine, I will repay, says the Lord." ²⁰ But if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him a drink; for in doing this, you will heap burning coals on his head." ²¹ Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "reasonable act of worship" reflects λατρείαν, which is rendered as "worship" in context.
- v. 2: "present age" reflects αἰῶνι τούτῳ, which is a reference to the current era or system of values.
- v. 6: "in proportion to the faith" reflects κατὰ τὴν ἀναλογίαν τῆς πίστεως; "proportion" is chosen to convey the relational measure.
- v. 20: "heap burning coals on his head" is a direct rendering of the Greek and reflects the idiom from Proverbs 25:22.
