# Amos 2

¹ This is what the LORD says about Moab: For three transgressions of Moab, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they burned the bones of the king of Edom into dust. ² I will send fire into Moab, and it will devour the foundations of the city walls. Moab will die in distress, with a cry and with the sound of a trumpet. ³ I will destroy their judge from among them, and kill all their officials with him, says the LORD. ⁴ This is what the LORD says about Judah: For three transgressions of Judah, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they despised the teaching of the LORD and did not keep his statutes, and were led astray by the worthless things their ancestors followed after. ⁵ I will send fire into Judah, and it will devour the foundations of Jerusalem. ⁶ This is what the LORD says about Israel: For three transgressions of Israel, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they sold the righteous for silver, and the poor for a pair of sandals. ⁷ Those who trample the dust of the earth on the heads of the poor and push aside the path of the humble, and a man and his father go to the same girl, in order to profane my holy name. ⁸ And they draped garments in the form of shrouds over every altar, and drank wine from the bowls of guilt in the house of their God. ⁹ Yet I brought down the Amorite before them, whose height was like the height of a cedar, and who was strong as an oak. I destroyed its fruit above, and its roots below. ¹⁰ And I brought you up from the land of Egypt and led you through the wilderness forty years, to inherit the land of the Amorite. ¹¹ And I raised up prophets from among your children, and nazirites from your young. Is there no such thing among the people of Israel? says the LORD. ¹² But you gave the nazirites wine to drink, and commanded the prophets, saying, "Do not prophesy." ¹³ Therefore, behold, I will roll under you as a cart full of sheaves rolls. ¹⁴ And escape will perish from the swift, and the strong will not retain his might, and the warrior will not save his life. ¹⁵ The archer will not stand, and the swift of foot will not escape, nor the rider on the horse save his life. ¹⁶ The brave will find no courage in the mighty, but the naked will flee that day, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 7: "the same girl" refers to the Hebrew "הַנַּעֲרָה" (ha-na'arah), which is rendered as "girl" to maintain clarity and context.
- v. 8: "in the form of shrouds" is a contextual interpretation of "חֲבֻלִים" (khavulim), which literally means "tied" or "bound," but the metaphor here seems to be of garments tied as a burial shroud.
- v. 13: "as a cart full of sheaves rolls" reflects the Hebrew metaphor for unstoppable destruction, maintaining the image without archaic phrasing.
