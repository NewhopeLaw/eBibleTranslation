# Amos 7

¹ Thus the Lord showed me, and behold, locusts coming up in the early morning, and behold, they had eaten the latter growth of the summer, and behold, they were devouring the king’s field. ² And it came to pass, if they finished eating the grass of the land, I said, "Lord YHWH, please forgive. Who will be able to stand up? Jacob is small." ³ The Lord relented concerning this. "It will not be," said the Lord. ⁴ Thus the Lord showed me, and behold, the Lord calling for judgment in fire, and it devoured a great deep and consumed the portion. ⁵ And I said, "Lord YHWH, cease. Who will be able to stand up? Jacob is small." ⁶ The Lord relented concerning this. "It will not happen," said the Lord YHWH. ⁷ Thus the Lord showed me, and behold, the Lord standing on an adamant wall, and in His hand an adamant. ⁸ And He said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "An adamant." And the Lord said to me, "I am placing an adamant in the midst of My people Israel. I will not pass over them any longer." ⁹ And the altars of laughter will be destroyed, and the sacred places of Israel will be laid waste, and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword. ¹⁰ Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to King Jeroboam of Israel, saying, "Amos is conspiring against you in the midst of the house of Israel. The land will not be able to bear all his words. ¹¹ For this is what Amos says: ‘The sword will strike Jeroboam, and Israel will be taken into exile from its land.’" ¹² And Amaziah said to Amos, "Prophet, go, flee to the land of Judah, and there eat bread and there prophesy. ¹³ But in Bethel you may no longer prophesy, for it is a king’s sanctuary and a house of a kingdom." ¹⁴ Then Amos responded to Amaziah, "I was no prophet, and I am not the son of a prophet. I was a shepherd and a dresser of sycamores. ¹⁵ And the Lord took me from after the flock and said to me, ‘Go, prophesy to My people Israel.’” ¹⁶ And now hear the word of the Lord. You are saying, "Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not speak against the house of Jacob." ¹⁷ Therefore thus says the Lord: Your wife will play the prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword. And your land will be divided by the line, and you will die on unclean ground. And Israel will be taken into exile from its land.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "latter growth of the summer" translates גִּזֵּי הַמֶּלֶךְ as the produce of the royal field, which is a late summer harvest.  
- v. 4: "the portion" likely refers to the land's portion or divine allotment, rendered as "the portion" to preserve the ambiguity of the Hebrew.  
- v. 7: "adamant wall" and "adamant" are used for חוֹמַת אֲנָךְ and אֲנָךְ. "Adamant" is a literal translation of the Hebrew, preserving the symbolic hardness of the wall.  
- v. 14: "dresser of sycamores" for שִׁקְמִים, a term for someone who prunes or tends sycamore trees.  
- v. 17: "unclean ground" translates אֲדָמָה טְמֵאָה, which could imply a place of ritual uncleanness or disgraceful death.
