# Amos 4

¹ Listen to this word, rich women of Bashan who pasture in Samaria, who crush the poor and trample the needy, who say to your masters, "Bring and let us drink!" ² The Lord swears by His covenant, "Surely days are coming upon you when you will be taken in hooks and your survivors in fishing lines." ³ You will be driven out, woman against woman, and cast out to the Har-Moran, says the Lord. ⁴ Go to Bethel and sin, to Gilgal and multiply sin, and bring your morning sacrifices to the altar, your tithes every three days. ⁵ And offer up leavened bread as a thank offering, and proclaim freewill offerings. Proclaim, "For this we love you, O children of Israel," says the Lord of hosts. ⁶ Yet I gave you cleanness of teeth in all your cities and lack of bread in every place, but you did not return to me, says the Lord. ⁷ And I withheld rain from you in the months of harvest and poured rain upon one city and upon another city I did not pour. One portion rained, and one portion on which I did not pour became dry. ⁸ Two or three cities wandered to one city to drink water and did not satisfy themselves, yet you did not return to me, says the Lord. ⁹ I struck you with blight and mildew; your gardens and vineyards and fig trees and olive trees were devoured by the worm, yet you did not return to me, says the Lord. ¹⁰ I sent plague among you like in the way of Egypt; I killed your young men with the sword and your horses in captivity, and I brought fire upon your camps and upon your hearts, yet you did not return to me, says the Lord. ¹¹ I overturned you as the gods overturned Sodom and Gomorrah, and you became like a brand plucked from a furnace, yet you did not return to me, says the Lord. ¹² Therefore I will do this to you, O Israel: because I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, O Israel. ¹³ For behold, the One who forms the mountains and creates the wind, and declares to man what His thoughts are, who makes dawn out of darkness and treads on the high places of the earth—the Lord of hosts is His name.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "rich women of Bashan" is a poetic translation of "parotot hashanah" (literally "cows of Bashan"), used to denote wealth and excess.
- v. 12: The phrase "prepare to meet your God" is a standard prophetic summons, translated directly from the Hebrew.
- v. 13: The poetic description of God's creative and sovereign power is rendered to reflect the original imagery and structure without archaic language.
