# Amos 5

¹ Listen to this word from the LORD, which I take up as a lament for the house of Israel. ² She has fallen and will not rise again. The virgin daughter of Israel has been cast down on her land, and no one will raise her up. ³ For thus says the Lord YHWH: The city that went out with a thousand will leave behind a hundred, and the city that went out with a hundred will leave behind ten to the house of Israel. ⁴ For thus says the LORD to the house of Israel: Seek me and live. ⁵ Do not seek Bethel, and do not enter Gilgal, and do not cross over to Beer-sheba, for Gilgal will surely be carried away, and Bethel will become a ruin. ⁶ Seek the LORD and live, lest He burn like a fire the house of Joseph, and it consume, and there be no one to quench the fire for the house of Israel. ⁷ Those who turn justice into bitterness and cast down righteousness to the earth have abandoned it. ⁸ The One who makes the sun and the moon, and turns the day into night, and brings darkness over the sea and pours it out upon the face of the earth—His name is the LORD. ⁹ The One who breaks the strong into pieces and the fortress into ruin will come. ¹⁰ They hate the one who reprimands in the gate, and they despise the one who speaks truth. ¹¹ Therefore, because you trample the poor and take a bribe from them, you will build houses but not dwell in them, plant vineyards but not drink their wine. ¹² For I know your many transgressions and your great sins—how you crush the righteous, take a bribe, and pervert justice in the gate. ¹³ Therefore, the wise man in that time will keep silent, for it is an evil time. ¹⁴ Seek good, not evil, that you may live. Let the LORD of hosts be with you, as you have said. ¹⁵ Hate evil, love good, and establish justice in the gate. Perhaps the LORD, the God of hosts, will have mercy on the remnant of Joseph. ¹⁶ Therefore, thus says the LORD, the God of hosts: In all the broad places there will be mourning, and in all the streets they will say, "Alas! Alas!" and the farmer will be called to mourning, and lamentation to those who know the end. ¹⁷ And in all the vineyards there will be mourning, for I will pass through you, says the LORD. ¹⁸ Woe to those who long for the day of the LORD! For what is this day of the LORD to you? It is darkness and not light. ¹⁹ Like one who flees from a lion and meets a bear, or enters a house and leans a hand on the wall and is bitten by a snake. ²⁰ Is not the day of the LORD darkness and not light, a time of gloom and not brightness for you? ²¹ I have hated, I have despised your festivals. I will not delight in your solemn assemblies. ²² Though you bring burnt offerings and your grain offerings, I will not accept them. And the sacrifice of your fatlings I will not look upon. ²³ Remove from me the noise of your songs. I will not hear the melody of your harps. ²⁴ But let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like a mighty stream. ²⁵ Did you offer to me sacrifices and grain offerings in the wilderness forty years, O house of Israel? ²⁶ And you carried the shrine of your king and the figure of your gods, the star of your gods which you made for yourselves. ²⁷ And I will drive you beyond Damascus, says the LORD, the God of hosts, His name.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "The One who makes the sun and the moon..." — the Hebrew uses poetic anthropomorphism to depict God's sovereignty over creation.
- v. 11: "trample the poor and take a bribe from them" — the Hebrew uses a poetic parallelism to describe corrupt behavior toward the vulnerable.
- v. 26: "the star of your gods" — the Hebrew likely refers to a celestial representation of the gods Baal or Molech, which the Israelites adopted in the wilderness.
