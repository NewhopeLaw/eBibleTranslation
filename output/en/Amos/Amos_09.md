# Amos 9

¹ I saw the Lord standing upon the altar, and He said, "Strike the altar and the thresholds will tremble, and I will dash their heads on every one. The survivors among them I will kill with the sword—no one will escape, no one will survive." ² If they hide in Sheol, My hand will reach them there. If they climb into heaven, I will bring them down. ³ If they hide on the peak of Carmel, I will search them out and take them. If they hide from My sight at the bottom of the sea, I will command the serpent and it will bite them. ⁴ If they go into exile before their enemies, I will command the sword to pursue them and kill them. I will fix My eyes on them for evil, not for good. ⁵ The Lord of Hosts, who touches the earth and causes it to tremble, will bring mourning to all who dwell in it. It will rise like a river and fall like the river of Egypt. ⁶ He who builds His vaults in the heavens and establishes His covenant on the earth, who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out on the surface of the land—His name is the Lord of Hosts. ⁷ Are you not like the children of Cush to Me, O people of Israel? says the Lord. Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt and the Philistines from Caphtor and Aram from Kir? ⁸ Behold, the eyes of the Lord God are upon the guilty kingdom, and I will destroy it from the face of the earth. But I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, says the Lord. ⁹ For I will command and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations as a sieve is shaken, yet not a single grain will fall to the ground. ¹⁰ All the wicked of My people will die by the sword, those who say, "Disaster will not come near us, and evil will not touch us." ¹¹ On that day I will raise up the fallen booth of David, and I will repair its breaches. I will restore its ruins and rebuild it as in the days of old. ¹² So that the remainder of Edom and all the nations upon whom My name is called may inherit it, says the Lord who does this. ¹³ Behold, days are coming, says the Lord, when the plowman will overtake the reaper and the treader of grapes the sower of seed. The mountains will drip sweet wine and all the hills will run with it. ¹⁴ I will restore the captivity of My people Israel, and they will rebuild the ruined cities and dwell in them. They will plant vineyards and drink their wine, and they will plant gardens and eat their fruit. ¹⁵ I will plant them on their land, and they will never again be plucked up from the land I have given them, says the Lord your God.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "thresholds will tremble" is a poetic rendering of "סִּפִּים" (doors/thresholds) for consistency with the violent imagery.
- v. 3: "the serpent" (נָחָשׁ) is a direct reference, not an allegory or specific figure.
- v. 6: "covenant" (אֲגֻדָּתוֹ) is a poetic rendering of the Hebrew word for "covenant" in the context of God's relationship with the earth.
- v. 11: "booth of David" (סֻכַּת דָּוִיד) is rendered directly without interpretive expansion.
- v. 15: "plucked up" (נָתַשׁ) is used rather than "cast out" to reflect the agricultural metaphor.
