# Amos 1

¹ These are the words of Amos, who was a shepherd from Tekoa. He saw a vision concerning Israel during the days of Uzziah, king of Judah, and during the days of Jeroboam, son of Joash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake. ² The LORD spoke from Zion, crying out, and from Jerusalem, giving his voice. The pastures of the shepherds mourned, and the crown of Carmel withered. ³ This is what the LORD says about Damascus: For three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they threshed the women of Gilead with iron implements. ⁴ I will send fire into the house of Hazael, and it will devour the fortresses of Ben-Hadad. ⁵ I will break the gate of Damascus and destroy those living in the valley of Aven, and I will cut off the holders of the scepter from Beth Edin. The people of Aram will be taken into exile, says the LORD. ⁶ This is what the LORD says about Gaza: For three transgressions of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they deported whole groups to Edom for sale. ⁷ I will send fire into the walls of Gaza, and it will devour its fortifications. ⁸ I will destroy those living in Ashdod and cut off the line of rulers from Ashkelon. I will extend my hand against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines will perish, says the Lord GOD. ⁹ This is what the LORD says about Tyre: For three transgressions of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they deported whole groups to Edom and forgot the covenant of brothers. ¹⁰ I will send fire into the walls of Tyre, and it will devour its fortifications. ¹¹ This is what the LORD says about Edom: For three transgressions of Edom, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they pursued their brother with the sword, showing no mercy, and they held a grudge forever, keeping the violence hidden. ¹² I will send fire into Teman, and it will devour the walls of Bozrah. ¹³ This is what the LORD says about the sons of Ammon: For three transgressions of the sons of Ammon, and for four, I will not turn back. Because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead to enlarge their territory. ¹⁴ I will set fire to the walls of Rabbah and burn its fortifications in a day of war, in a time of storm. ¹⁵ Their king will go into exile, along with their officials, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Tekoa" is translated as "Tekoa" following the proper name table.
- v. 1: "two years before the earthquake" follows the Hebrew phrase "שְׁנָתַיִם לִפְנֵי הָרָֽעַשׁ" which is dependent on context and historical interpretation.
- v. 1: "the earthquake" is referenced but not named in the text, so the translation remains general.
- v. 2: "Zion" and "Jerusalem" are standard names; "pastures of the shepherds" follows the context of mourning for fertile land.
- v. 3: "threshed the women" is an interpretive rendering of the violent act described in Hebrew.
- v. 13: "ripped open the pregnant women" is a direct translation of the Hebrew verb שָׁבַר ("broke") applied to the women of Gilead, reflecting the violent context.
