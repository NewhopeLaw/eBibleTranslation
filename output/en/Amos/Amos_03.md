# Amos 3

¹ Listen to this word the LORD has spoken to you, house of Israel, to all the family I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying: ² You alone I have known of all the families of the earth, so I will hold you accountable for all your sins. ³ Do two walk together unless they have agreed? ⁴ Does a lion roar in the forest when it has no prey? Does a young lion growl in its den unless it has caught something? ⁵ Does a bird fall to the earth without a snare? Does a trap spring from the ground without catching something? ⁶ If a trumpet is blown in a city, will not the people be alarmed? If there is evil in a city, has the LORD not done it? ⁷ For the LORD God does not act without revealing his plan to his servants the prophets. ⁸ A lion has roared—who would not fear? The LORD God has spoken—who would not prophesy? ⁹ Proclaim in the strongholds of Ashdod and in the strongholds of the land of Egypt, and say: "Gather yourselves on the mountains of Samaria and see the great commotions within her, the oppressions in her midst." ¹⁰ They do not know how to do right, says the LORD, the ones who store up violence and plunder in their strongholds. ¹¹ Therefore this is what the LORD God says: I will surround the land and bring down your strength from you, and your strongholds will be captured. ¹² This is what the LORD says: Just as a shepherd rescues two legs from a lion’s mouth, or a piece of an ear, so will the Israelites who dwell in Samaria be rescued from the corner of a bed and from the cushion in Damascus. ¹³ Hear this and bear witness in the house of Jacob, says the LORD God of Hosts. ¹⁴ For when I bring judgment on the sins of Israel, I will bring it upon the altars of Bethel. I will cut off the horns of the altar and bring it to the ground. ¹⁵ I will crush the house of the winter home over the house of the summer home, and the houses of ivory will be destroyed, and many houses will be laid waste, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "house of Israel" renders "בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל" as a whole-household/family reference rather than a political entity.
- v. 10: "store up violence and plunder" renders "אוֹצְרִים חָמָס וָשֹׁד" with "store up" as an active verb rather than a passive "have stored."
- v. 12: "from the corner of a bed and from the cushion in Damascus" renders poetic imagery from "בִּפְאַת מִטָּה וּבִדְמֶשֶׁק עָֽרֶשׂ" as metaphorical references to places of comfort, not literal furniture.
- v. 15: "houses of ivory" renders "בָּתֵּי הַשֵּׁן" based on the standard interpretation of "שֵׁן" as ivory, though some suggest it could mean "summer houses."
