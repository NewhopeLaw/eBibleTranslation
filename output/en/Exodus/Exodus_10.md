# Exodus 10

¹ The LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh, for I have hardened his heart and the hearts of his servants so that I may display these signs among them. ² And so that your children and the children of your children may tell of the things I have done in Egypt and my signs which I have placed among them, so that you may know that I am the LORD." ³ Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and said to him, "This is what the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: How long will you refuse to let my people go to serve me? ⁴ If you refuse to let my people go, then tomorrow I will bring a great swarm of locusts into your territory. ⁵ They will cover the face of the land, and no one will be able to see the land. They will eat the remaining stubble that the hail left behind, and they will eat every tree growing for you in the fields. ⁶ They will fill your houses, the houses of your servants, and the houses of all the Egyptians, more than any generation of your ancestors and the generations of your ancestors before you, since the day they were on the land until now." Then Moses turned and left Pharaoh. ⁷ Pharaoh's servants said to him, "How long will this be a trap for us? Let the men go and serve the LORD their God. Do you not yet know that Egypt is lost?" ⁸ Moses and Aaron were brought back to Pharaoh, and Pharaoh said to them, "Go, serve the LORD your God. Who exactly are the ones going?" ⁹ Moses said, "We will go, young and old alike, with our sons and daughters, with our flocks and herds, for we have a festival to the LORD." ¹⁰ He said to them, "Let it be as you say. May the LORD be with you, just as I let you go with your children. See, evil lies ahead for you. ¹¹ Not so. Only let the men go and serve the LORD, for that is what you are asking." He drove them out from Pharaoh's presence. ¹² The LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt, and bring up the locusts to cover the land of Egypt, and they will eat every plant of the land and everything the hail left behind. ¹³ Moses stretched out his staff over the land of Egypt, and the LORD sent an east wind blowing all that day and through the night. By morning it had blown the locusts. ¹⁴ The locusts flew into the entire land of Egypt and settled on every border of Egypt. It was a heavy plague, heavier than any that had come before, and there will never be another like it. ¹⁵ The locusts covered the face of the land, and the land was darkened. They devoured every plant of the land and every fruit of the trees that the hail left behind. Nothing green was left on tree or on any plant in the field throughout all the land of Egypt. ¹⁶ Pharaoh hurried to call for Moses and Aaron and said, "I have sinned this time against the LORD your God and against you. ¹⁷ Now, please, forgive my sin this one time, and entreat the LORD your God to remove this death from me." ¹⁸ Moses left Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD. ¹⁹ The LORD sent a strong wind from the sea, and it carried the locusts away and cast them into the Red Sea. Not one locust was left in all the borders of Egypt. ²⁰ But the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not let the Israelites go. ²¹ The LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky, and let darkness fall over the land of Egypt, so that it may be a darkness of gloom." ²² Moses stretched out his hand toward the sky, and thick darkness fell on all the land of Egypt for three days. ²³ No one could see another, and no one could rise from their place for three days, but all the Israelites had light in the places where they lived. ²⁴ Pharaoh called for Moses and said, "Go, serve the LORD. Take your livestock with you, but let your children remain with you." ²⁵ Moses said, "You must also give us sacrifices and burnt offerings to offer to the LORD our God. ²⁶ Our livestock must go with us; not a single animal will be left behind, for from them we must take what is needed to serve the LORD our God. We do not yet know what we must use to serve the LORD until we arrive there." ²⁷ The LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he refused to let them go. ²⁸ Pharaoh said to him, "Go from me, and be careful not to see my face again, for on the day you see my face, you will die." ²⁹ Moses said to him, "You have spoken rightly. I will not see your face again."

Notes
- v. 27: "He refused to let them go" — the Hebrew is ambiguous between "he would not let them go" and "he did not let them go." The context of Pharaoh's hardened heart suggests an ongoing refusal rather than a past action.
