# Exodus 40

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² On the first day of the first month you shall set up the Tent of the Meeting. ³ And you shall place in it the Ark of the Covenant, and you shall cover the Ark with the veil. ⁴ You shall bring in the Table, and set out its offerings. You shall bring in the Lampstand, and light its lamps. ⁵ You shall place the golden altar of incense in front of the Ark of the Covenant, and you shall set the veil of the entrance at the Tent. ⁶ You shall place the altar of burnt offering in front of the entrance to the Tent of the Meeting. ⁷ You shall place the basin between the Tent of the Meeting and the altar, and you shall put water there. ⁸ You shall set up the courtyard all around, and you shall set the veil of the entrance to the courtyard. ⁹ You shall take the anointing oil and anoint the Tent and all that is in it, and consecrate it and all its vessels, and it shall be holy. ¹⁰ You shall anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels, and consecrate the altar, and it shall be most holy. ¹¹ You shall anoint the basin and its stand, and consecrate them. ¹² You shall bring near Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of the Meeting, and you shall wash them with water. ¹³ You shall clothe Aaron with the holy garments, and anoint him and consecrate him, and he shall serve me. ¹⁴ You shall bring his sons near, and clothe them with tunics. ¹⁵ You shall anoint them as you anointed their father, and they shall serve me. And this anointing shall be to them an anointing of priesthood for all generations. ¹⁶ Moses did everything just as the LORD commanded him. ¹⁷ In the first month of the second year, on the first day of the month, the Tent was set up. ¹⁸ Moses set up the Tent, placed its frames, and set its crossbars, and set up its posts. ¹⁹ He spread the covering over the Tent, and placed the covering of the Tent over it, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²⁰ He placed the Testimony in the Ark and put the staves on the Ark, and placed the atonement cover over the Ark. ²¹ He brought the Ark into the Tent and set the veil of the curtain, and it covered the Ark of the Covenant, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²² He placed the Table in the Tent of the Meeting, on the north side of the Tent, outside the veil. ²³ He set out the Bread of the Presence in front of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²⁴ He placed the Lampstand in the Tent of the Meeting, on the south side of the Tent. ²⁵ He lit the lamps before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²⁶ He placed the golden altar of incense in the Tent of the Meeting, in front of the veil. ²⁷ He burned the incense on it, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²⁸ He set the veil of the entrance to the Tent. ²⁹ He placed the altar of burnt offering at the entrance of the Tent of the Meeting, and he offered on it the burnt offering and the grain offering, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³⁰ He placed the basin between the Tent of the Meeting and the altar, and there he put water for washing. ³¹ Moses and Aaron and his sons washed their hands and feet from there. ³² When they entered the Tent of the Meeting or approached the altar, they washed, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³³ He set up the courtyard all around the Tent and the altar, and he set the veil of the entrance to the courtyard. Moses completed the work. ³⁴ The cloud covered the Tent of the Meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the Tent. ³⁵ Moses could not enter the Tent of the Meeting, for the cloud had settled on it, and the glory of the LORD filled the Tent. ³⁶ When the cloud was lifted from above the Tent, the Israelites journeyed on in all their travels. ³⁷ And if the cloud did not rise, they did not journey until the day it was lifted. ³⁸ For it was the cloud of the LORD over the Tent by day, and fire in it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, in all their travels.

---

Notes
- v. 17: "the first day of the first month" — Hebrew "בְּיוֹם־הַחֹדֶשׁ הָרִאשׁוֹן בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ" — indicates the first day of the first month, i.e., the first full moon, likely referring to the month of Nisan.
