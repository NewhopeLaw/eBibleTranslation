# Exodus 39

¹ From the blue, purple, and crimson thread, they made the priestly garments for service in the holy place. They made the garments for Aaron, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ² They made the ephod of gold, blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, woven together. ³ They hammered out gold threads and wove them into the blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, with a pattern of interwoven chains. ⁴ They made its shoulder straps fastened together at both ends, so it could be joined securely. ⁵ The ephod’s embroidered chestpiece, worn over it, was the same work—gold, blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ⁶ They set into settings of gold, each one a separate setting, twelve engraved stones, set with names, the names of the sons of Israel. ⁷ They placed them on the ephod’s shoulder straps, as memorial stones for the sons of Israel, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ⁸ They made the breastpiece of judgment, a woven work, like the ephod—gold, blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen. ⁹ It was square and doubled, two layers, the length twice the width. ¹⁰ They filled it with four rows of stones: the first row was ruby, topaz, and beryl. ¹¹ The second row was turquoise, sapphire, and jacinth. ¹² The third row was chrysolite, onyx, and jasper. ¹³ The fourth row was lapis lazuli, carnelian, and garnet, each set in gold mountings. ¹⁴ The twelve stones were inscribed with the names of the sons of Israel, each with its own engraving, like a seal. Twelve names, one for each tribe. ¹⁵ They made chains of pure gold, braided chains for the breastpiece. ¹⁶ They made two gold rings and two gold clasps, and they placed the two clasps on the two ends of the breastpiece. ¹⁷ They fastened the two gold chains to the two clasps on the two ends of the breastpiece. ¹⁸ Then they fastened the other ends of the chains to the two rings on the lower part of the ephod’s shoulder straps, in front. ¹⁹ They made two gold rings and placed them at the two ends of the breastpiece, on the edge that faced in toward the ephod. ²⁰ They made two more gold rings and placed them on the lower part of the ephod’s shoulder straps, in front, as a counterbalance to the clasps above, at the seam of the ephod. ²¹ They fastened the breastpiece to the ephod with a blue thread, so that it would rest on the ephod’s shoulder straps and not come loose, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²² They made the ephod’s robe of woven material, all of blue. ²³ Its opening at the top was like a collar, a woven edging, so it would not tear. ²⁴ They made pomegranates of blue, purple, and crimson thread on the hem of the robe, all around. ²⁵ They made golden bells and placed them among the pomegranates on the robe’s hem, all around—bell and pomegranate, bell and pomegranate. ²⁶ The bells and pomegranates were for service, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²⁷ They made the linen undergarments for Aaron and his sons. ²⁸ They made the turban of fine linen, the sacerdotal sash of fine linen, and the sash for the trousers of fine linen, woven together. ²⁹ They made the sash for the ephod of fine linen and blue, purple, and crimson thread, a woven work, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³⁰ They made the plate of holiness, a pure gold diadem, and engraved on it an inscription like a seal: “Holy to the LORD.” ³¹ They tied a blue cord to it so it could be fastened to the turban, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³² All the work of the Tent of Meeting was completed. The Israelites had made everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³³ They brought the Tent to Moses: the Tent itself, all its furnishings—its frames, its crossbars, its posts, and its bases. ³⁴ They brought the covering of dyed rams’ skins, the covering of sea-ram skins, and the veil of the entrance. ³⁵ They brought the Ark of the Covenant, its poles, and the atonement cover. ³⁶ They brought the table, all its furnishings, and the bread of the Presence. ³⁷ They brought the pure lampstand, its lamps, the lamps for lighting, and all its furnishings, and the oil for the lamp. ³⁸ They brought the golden altar, the anointing oil, the incense, and the curtain for the Tent entrance. ³⁹ They brought the bronze altar, its bronze grating with its poles, all its furnishings, the basin, and its stand. ⁴⁰ They brought the hangings of the courtyard, its posts and bases, the curtain for the gate of the courtyard, its cords and pegs, and all the furnishings of the Tent for the Tent of Meeting. ⁴¹ They brought the garments for service in the holy place, the garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments for his sons for their priesthood. ⁴² The Israelites had made everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses. ⁴³ Moses saw all the work, and behold, they had done it just as the LORD had commanded. Moses blessed them.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "doubled" refers to the doubled thickness of the breastpiece, as its dimensions are described as "square and doubled."
- v. 24-26: The alternating pattern of pomegranates and bells is described in poetic rhythm to preserve the Hebrew structure.
- v. 30: "plate of holiness" reflects the context of the ephod and its role as a sacred diadem.
- v. 31: "diadem" is used to avoid confusion with "headpiece" and to reflect the sacred, elevated nature of the object.
