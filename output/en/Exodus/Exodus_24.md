# Exodus 24

¹ And to Moses the LORD said, "Go up to me, you and Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel, and you must bow down from afar to the LORD." ² "But Moses alone will come near to the LORD, and the others must not come near, and the people must not go up with him." ³ Moses went down and told the people all the words of the LORD and all the judgments, and the whole people responded with one voice, saying, "All the words that the LORD has spoken, we will do and we will obey." ⁴ Moses wrote all the words of the LORD. In the morning he built an altar at the foot of the mountain, and twelve pillars for the twelve tribes of Israel. ⁵ He sent the young men of the children of Israel, and they brought up burnt offerings and sacrificed peace offerings to the LORD of bulls. ⁶ Moses took half the blood and put it in basins, and half the blood he sprinkled on the altar. ⁷ He took the book of the covenant and read it in the ears of the people, and they said, "All that the LORD has spoken, we will do and we will obey." ⁸ Moses took the blood and sprinkled it on the people, and said, "Behold, the blood of the covenant which the LORD has made with you, concerning all these words." ⁹ Moses, Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up. ¹⁰ They saw the God of Israel, and beneath His feet it was like the work of sapphire stone, and like the very sky for its clarity. ¹¹ Yet He did not lay a hand on the chosen men of the children of Israel, and they saw the God and ate and drank. ¹² And the LORD said to Moses, "Go up to me on the mountain and remain there, and I will give to you the stone tablets, the law and the commandments which I have written to teach them." ¹³ Moses rose and Joshua his servant, and Moses went up to the mountain of the God. ¹⁴ To the elders he said, "Stay here for us until we return to you. And behold, Aaron and Hur are with you. Whoever has an issue must come to them." ¹⁵ Moses went up to the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain. ¹⁶ The glory of the LORD settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days. On the seventh day the LORD called to Moses from within the cloud. ¹⁷ The appearance of the glory of the LORD was like a consuming fire at the head of the mountain in the eyes of the children of Israel. ¹⁸ Moses went into the cloud and went up to the mountain. Moses was in the mountain forty days and forty nights.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The Septuagint adds "Joshua" as Moses' servant in the list of those ascending with him, which is not in the Hebrew. The Hebrew names are followed here.
- v. 12: The Greek version includes a clause about Joshua and the instruction to the elders, which is not in the Hebrew source. The Hebrew is followed without addition.
- v. 16-17: The Septuagint provides additional narrative detail (like the appearance of the glory as a consuming fire), but the Hebrew is clear and concise, so only the Hebrew is followed.
