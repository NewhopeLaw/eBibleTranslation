# Exodus 25

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² "Speak to the people of Israel, and let them take for me a contribution from every person whose heart is willing to give. You shall take my contribution. ³ And this is the contribution you shall take from them: gold and silver and bronze; ⁴ blue and purple and crimson thread, and fine linen, and goats' hair; ⁵ rams' skins dyed red, and badger skins, and acacia wood; ⁶ oil for the lamp, spices for the anointing oil, and for the incense; ⁷ onyx stones and other gems for the ephod and for the breastpiece. ⁸ "And they shall make for me a sanctuary, and I will dwell among them. ⁹ "According to all that I am showing you, the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its furnishings, you shall make it. ¹⁰ "You shall make an ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one and a half cubits high. ¹¹ You shall overlay it with pure gold, inside and outside, and make a gold molding around it. ¹² You shall cast four rings of gold for it and place them on its four corners, two rings on one side and two rings on the other side. ¹³ You shall make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold. ¹⁴ You shall insert the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, by which to carry the ark. ¹⁵ The poles shall remain in the rings of the ark; they shall not be removed from it. ¹⁶ You shall put into the ark the covenant that I will give you. ¹⁷ "You shall make a mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. ¹⁸ "You shall make two cherubim of hammered gold; you shall make them from the two ends of the mercy seat. ¹⁹ You shall make one cherub on one end, and one cherub on the other end. From the mercy seat you shall make the cherubim on both its ends. ²⁰ The cherubim shall spread out their wings above, overshadowing with their wings over the mercy seat, and their faces shall be toward each other. The faces of the cherubim shall be toward the mercy seat. ²¹ "You shall place the mercy seat on top of the ark, and into the ark you shall put the covenant that I will give you. ²² "And I will meet with you there, and from above the mercy seat, between the two cherubim that are on the ark of the covenant, I will speak to you about all that I command you for the people of Israel. ²³ "You shall make a table of acacia wood, two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high. ²⁴ You shall overlay it with pure gold and make a gold molding around it. ²⁵ You shall make a frame around it, a handbreadth in width, and a gold molding for the frame. ²⁶ You shall make four rings of gold for it and place the rings on the four corners of its base. ²⁷ The rings shall be opposite the frame, serving as sockets for the poles to carry the table. ²⁸ You shall make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, and the table shall be carried with them. ²⁹ You shall make its dishes and its bowls and its ladles and its pitchers, all of pure gold. ³⁰ You shall place on the table the bread of the Presence before me always. ³¹ "You shall make a lampstand of pure gold. You shall make it in the form of a hammered piece: its base, its shaft, its cups, its buds, and its flowers shall all be from it. ³² Six branches shall come out from its sides—three branches on one side and three branches on the other. ³³ On each of the three branches there shall be three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a flower; and on the three branches on the other side, each shall have three cups with a bud and a flower. ³⁴ In the same way, on the lampstand itself there shall be four cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a flower. ³⁵ A bud shall be under the two branches from the one side, and a bud under the two branches from the other side, and a bud under the two branches from the other side—just as it is for the six branches that come out of the lampstand. ³⁶ Their buds and their branches shall all be from it; the whole lampstand shall be one piece of hammered pure gold. ³⁷ You shall make its lamps, seven in number. They shall light the lamps to give light toward the front of the lampstand. ³⁸ You shall make its wick-trimmers and its basins of pure gold. ³⁹ You shall make all these utensils of a talent of pure gold. ⁴⁰ Look carefully and make them according to the pattern that I am showing you on the mountain."

Notes
- v. 2: "Take for me a contribution" is a rendering of תְּרוּמָה, "contribution" or "offering," rather than "gift," to emphasize its voluntary nature.
- v. 3-7: The list of materials is translated to preserve the poetic and rhythmic structure of the Hebrew, with items grouped by type and material.
- v. 31-35: The lampstand design is complex and hierarchical. The rendering follows the Hebrew structure, specifying the number of branches, cups, buds, and flowers in each segment.
- v. 36: "One piece of hammered pure gold" reflects the Hebrew מִקְשָׁה אַחַת, "a single piece," as per the text's emphasis on the unity of the lampstand.
