# Exodus 36

¹ Bezalel and Aholiab, and every skilled craftsman, whom the LORD had given wisdom and understanding in their hearts to know how to do all the work of the sacred service, did everything the LORD had commanded. ² Moses called Bezalel and Aholiab, and every skilled craftsman the LORD had given wisdom in their heart, and all whose hearts were stirred to come forward to the work and do it. ³ They took from before Moses all the contributions the Israelites had brought for the sacred service work, and they continued bringing offerings in the morning. ⁴ All the skilled artisans who were doing the sacred service work each brought their own part of the work they were doing. ⁵ They told Moses, "The people are bringing more than enough for the work the LORD has commanded to be done." ⁶ Moses gave the order and proclaimed in the camp, "No man or woman must do any more work for the offering of the sacred. The people were restrained from bringing." ⁷ The work was sufficient for all the work to be done and there was even more left over. ⁸ Every skilled artisan working on the tabernacle made ten curtains of fine linen, blue, purple, and crimson, embroidered with cherubim. ⁹ The length of each curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each was four cubits. All the curtains had the same measurement. ¹⁰ He joined five curtains together, and five curtains together. ¹¹ He made tassels of blue thread along the edge of each joined curtain and along the edge of the second joined curtain, in the same way. ¹² He made fifty tassels on each joined curtain, and fifty tassels on the joined curtain of the second set, the tassels corresponding one to one. ¹³ He made fifty golden clasps and joined the curtains together with the clasps so the tabernacle became one. ¹⁴ He made twelve curtains of goats' hair for the tent over the tabernacle. ¹⁵ The length of each curtain was thirty cubits, and the width of each was four cubits. All twelve curtains had the same measurement. ¹⁶ He joined five curtains together and six curtains together. ¹⁷ He made fifty tassels along the edge of the joined curtain on the outer side, and fifty tassels along the edge of the joined curtain on the second joined side. ¹⁸ He made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent so it became one. ¹⁹ He made a covering of dyed ram skins and a second covering of sea cow hides on top. ²⁰ He made the frames for the tabernacle, acacia wood uprights. ²¹ The length of each frame was ten cubits, and the width of each frame was one and a half cubits. ²² Each frame had two crossbars, joined one to the other. He did this for all the frames of the tabernacle. ²³ He made twenty frames for the tabernacle, for the southern side. ²⁴ He made forty silver bases, two bases under each frame for its two crossbars, and two bases under each frame for its two crossbars. ²⁵ For the second side of the tabernacle, the northern side, he made twenty frames. ²⁶ And forty bases of silver, two bases under each frame, and two bases under each frame. ²⁷ For the front of the tabernacle toward the east he made six frames. ²⁸ And two frames he made for the corners of the tabernacle at the front. ²⁹ They were joined together at the bottom and were joined together fully at the top to the one ring. He did this for both of them for the two corners. ³⁰ They were eight frames, and their bases were sixteen silver bases, two bases for each frame. ³¹ He made five crossbars of acacia wood for the frames of the one side of the tabernacle. ³² And five crossbars for the frames of the second side of the tabernacle and five crossbars for the frames of the tabernacle at the front. ³³ He made the central crossbar to run through the frames from end to end. ³⁴ He overlaid the frames with gold, and made their rings of gold, and overlaid the crossbars with gold. ³⁵ He made the curtain, blue, purple, and crimson, and fine linen, embroidered with cherubim. ³⁶ He made four acacia wood posts for it, overlaid them with gold, and cast for them four silver bases. ³⁷ He made a screen for the entrance of the tent, blue, purple, and crimson, and fine linen, woven work. ³⁸ Its posts were five, their crossbars and their tops and their hooks were overlaid with gold, and their bases were five bronze.

Notes
- v. 5: "The people are bringing more than enough" — the Hebrew uses "marbim" (increase) to describe the surplus, which is rendered idiomatically as "more than enough."
- v. 8: "Embroidered with cherubim" — the Hebrew "ma'aseh hashev" refers to intricate needlework or embroidery.
- v. 9: "Cubits" is the standard measure here; no need to clarify unless the context demands it.
- v. 15: "Goats' hair" is the standard translation for "ezim," referring to the hair of goats.
- v. 20: "Acacia wood uprights" — the Hebrew "etz shittim om'dim" describes the acacia wood posts used for the tabernacle structure.
- v. 23-25: The tabernacle has three sets of frames: southern (23), northern (25), and eastern (27), all corresponding to the spatial layout described in Exodus.
