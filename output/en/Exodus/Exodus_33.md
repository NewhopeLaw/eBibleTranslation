# Exodus 33

¹ The LORD said to Moses, "Go up from here you and the people you brought up from the land of Egypt, to the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'To your descendants I will give it.'" ² I will send an angel before you, and I will drive out the Canaanite, the Amorite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite. ³ The land is flowing with milk and honey, but I will not go up among you, for you are a stiff-necked people, lest I consume you on the journey." ⁴ The people heard this bad report and mourned, and no one put on his ornaments. ⁵ The LORD said to Moses, "Say to the people of Israel, 'You are a stiff-necked people. I will go up in your midst for a moment and consume you, but now take off your ornaments from you and I will see what I will do to you.'" ⁶ The people of Israel stripped themselves of their ornaments from Mount Horeb. ⁷ Moses took the tent and pitched it outside the camp, far from the camp, and called it the Tent of Meeting. And it came to pass that whoever sought the LORD would go out to the Tent of Meeting which was outside the camp. ⁸ Whenever Moses went out to the Tent, all the people stood at their tent entrances, looking after Moses until he entered the Tent. ⁹ When Moses entered the Tent, the pillar of cloud would descend and stand at the entrance of the Tent, and the LORD would speak with Moses. ¹⁰ All the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the Tent, and they rose up and bowed down, each at the entrance of his tent. ¹¹ The LORD spoke to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend. And when Moses returned to the camp, his young assistant, Joshua son of Nun, did not leave the Tent. ¹² Moses said to the LORD, "See, you say to me, 'Lead up this people,' yet you have not shown me whom you will send with me. And you said, 'I have known you by name and have found favor in your sight.'" ¹³ Now if I have found favor in your sight, show me your ways, that I may know you, so that I may find favor in your sight. And consider that this is your people." ¹⁴ The LORD said, "My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest." ¹⁵ Moses said to him, "If your presence does not go, do not bring us up from here." ¹⁶ How then will it be known that I and your people have found favor in your sight, except by going with us so that we may be distinguished, I and your people, from all the peoples on the face of the earth?" ¹⁷ The LORD said to Moses, "I will do this thing also that you have spoken, for you have found favor in my sight and I have known you by name." ¹⁸ Moses said, "Show me your glory." ¹⁹ The LORD said, "I will cause all my goodness to pass before you, and I will proclaim my name 'the LORD' before you. I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy." ²⁰ He said, "You cannot see my face, for no human may see me and live." ²¹ The LORD said, "There is a place by me where you may stand on the rock. ²² When my glory passes by, I will place you in a cleft of the rock and cover you with my hand until I have passed by. ²³ Then I will remove my hand and you will see my back, but my face will not be seen."

---

Notes
- v. 5: "I will go up in your midst for a moment" — the Hebrew "reg‘a‘ echad" is translated as "for a moment" to convey the fleeting nature of the action.
- v. 18: "Show me your glory" — "kavod" is rendered as "glory," a standard translation for this word in theological contexts.
- v. 20: "You cannot see my face... and live" — the Hebrew "ve-chai" is translated as "and live" to maintain the dramatic contrast between seeing God and surviving it.
