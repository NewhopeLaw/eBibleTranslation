# Exodus 3

¹ Moses was tending the flock of Jethro, his father-in-law, a priest of Midian. He led the flock into the wilderness and came to Mount Horeb, the mountain of God. ² An angel of the LORD appeared to him in a flame of fire from the midst of a bush. Moses looked, and there the bush was on fire, yet it was not consumed. ³ Moses said, “I will go over and see this great sight. Why is the bush not burning up?” ⁴ When the LORD saw that he had turned aside to look, He called to him from the bush: “Moses, Moses!” He answered, “Here I am.” ⁵ He said, “Do not come any closer. Remove your sandals from your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy ground.” ⁶ He continued, “I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.” Moses hid his face, for he was afraid to look directly at God. ⁷ The LORD continued, “I have seen the suffering of My people in Egypt. I have heard their groaning and I know their affliction. ⁸ I have come down to deliver them from the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them up from that land into a good and spacious land, a land flowing with milk and honey, the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites. ⁹ Now, behold, the cry of the Israelites has reached Me, and I have seen how the Egyptians oppress them. ¹⁰ Come, I will send you to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, that you may lead My people, the children of Israel, out of Egypt.” ¹¹ Moses said to God, “Who am I, that I should go to Pharaoh and lead the Israelites out of Egypt?” ¹² God answered, “I will be with you. And this shall be the sign to you that I have sent you: when you have brought the people out of Egypt, you shall worship God on this mountain.” ¹³ Moses said to God, “When I come to the Israelites and say, ‘The God of your fathers has sent me to you,’ they will ask me, ‘What is His name?’ What should I say to them?” ¹⁴ God said to Moses, “I am what I am.” And He added, “Say this to the Israelites: ‘I am’ has sent me to you.” ¹⁵ God continued, “Say this to the Israelites: ‘The LORD, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.’ This is My name forever, and this is My title for all generations.” ¹⁶ Go and gather the elders of Israel together and say to them, “The LORD, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, has appeared to me, saying, ‘I have visited you and seen what is being done to you in Egypt. ¹⁷ I have brought you up from the affliction of the Egyptians into the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, into a land flowing with milk and honey.’” ¹⁸ They will listen to your voice. You and the elders of Israel will go to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and say to him, “The God of the Hebrews has called us. Let us go, then, on a three-day journey into the wilderness to sacrifice to the LORD our God.” ¹⁹ I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go unless forced by a strong hand. ²⁰ I will stretch out My hand and strike Egypt with all My wonders that I will do among them. Then I will let you go. ²¹ I will give the people favor in the eyes of the Egyptians, and when you leave, you will not go away empty-handed. ²² Every woman will ask from her neighbor and the woman living in her house for silver and gold ornaments and clothing. You will put these on your sons and daughters, and you will plunder the Egyptians.”

---

Notes
- v. 14: The divine name is not explicitly spelled YHWH in this verse, but the context clearly refers to it. Rendered as “I am what I am” and then as “I am.”
- v. 15: The divine name is explicitly stated as YHWH (the LORD) in the context of addressing the Israelites. Rendered accordingly.
