# Exodus 9

¹ The LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh and say to him, 'This is what the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: Let my people go to serve me.' ² If you refuse to let them go, and continue to hold on to them, ³ behold, the hand of the LORD will strike your livestock in the field, your horses, donkeys, camels, cattle, and sheep, with a very severe plague. ⁴ The LORD will distinguish between the livestock of Israel and the livestock of Egypt, and not one of the Israelites' animals will die." ⁵ The LORD set a time and said, "Tomorrow the LORD will do this in the land." ⁶ The next day the LORD did as he said. All the livestock of Egypt died, but not one of the Israelites' animals died. ⁷ Pharaoh sent to look, and behold, not one of the Israelites' animals had died. Pharaoh's heart became hardened, and he did not let the people go. ⁸ The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Take full handfuls of furnace ashes and let Moses throw it into the air in front of Pharaoh." ⁹ It will become fine dust over the whole land of Egypt, and it will become a rash that bursts open, creeping on both people and livestock, throughout the entire land of Egypt." ¹⁰ They took furnace ashes and stood in front of Pharaoh. Moses threw it into the air, and it became a rash that bursts open, creeping on both people and livestock. ¹¹ The Egyptian magicians could not stand before Moses because of the rash, for the rash was on the magicians and on all the Egyptians. ¹² The LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had told Moses. ¹³ The LORD said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh, and say to him, 'This is what the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: Let my people go to serve me.' ¹⁴ For this time I will send all my plagues into your heart and into your servants and your people, so that you may know that there is none like me in all the earth. ¹⁵ For now I have stretched out my hand to strike you and your people with the plague, and you will be wiped out from the land. ¹⁶ But I have kept you in for the sake of showing you my power and so that my name may be declared in all the earth. ¹⁷ Are you still holding on to my people, keeping them from going?" ¹⁸ The LORD said, "Tomorrow I will send a very heavy hailstorm, such as has not occurred in Egypt from the time it was founded to the present. ¹⁹ Now, quickly, send your livestock and everything you have in the field. Let every person and every animal that is found in the field go, and do not bring them into the house, or they will be struck by the hail and die." ²⁰ Those among Pharaoh's servants who feared the word of the LORD brought their servants and livestock into the houses. ²¹ But those who did not pay attention to the word of the LORD left their servants and livestock in the fields. ²² The LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky, and let hail fall on the whole land of Egypt, on people and livestock and on every plant growing in the fields in the land of Egypt." ²³ Moses stretched out his staff toward the sky, and the LORD sent thunder and hail, and fire ran along the ground. The LORD rained hail on the land of Egypt. ²⁴ There was hail, and fire flashing in the middle of the hail, a very heavy storm, such as had never been seen in all the land of Egypt since it became a nation. ²⁵ The hail struck the entire land of Egypt, from people to animals and every plant in the fields and every tree in the fields, breaking them all. ²⁶ Only in the land of Goshen, where the Israelites were, was there no hail. ²⁷ Pharaoh sent and called for Moses and Aaron and said to them, "I have sinned this time. The LORD is just, but I and my people are wicked. ²⁸ Entreat the LORD on my behalf and stop the thunder and hail, and I will release you. You will not need to remain here." ²⁹ Moses said to him, "As I leave the city, I will stretch out my hands to the LORD. The thunder will cease and the hail will no longer fall, so that you may know that the land belongs to the LORD. ³⁰ But you and your servants I know have yet to fear the LORD, the God." ³¹ The flax and barley were struck, for the barley had already sprouted and the flax was in bud. ³² The wheat and spelt were not struck, for they were too late. ³³ Moses went out of the city and stretched out his hands to the LORD. The thunder and hail ceased, and the rain did not fall on the land anymore. ³⁴ Pharaoh saw that the rain, the hail, and the thunder had ceased. He sinned again, hardening his heart and the hearts of his servants. ³⁵ Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not let the Israelites go, just as the LORD had spoken to Moses.

---

Notes
- v. 9: "fine dust" renders עָפָר, translated as "dust" in context, not "ash" or "soot" as in some versions.
- v. 11: "rash that bursts open" renders שְׁחִין פֹּרֵחַ, following the Septuagint's ἕλκη φλυκτίδες ἀναζέουσαι, indicating a severe, eruptive skin condition.
- v. 30: "the God" is a stylistic choice to render אֱלֹהִים without a specific article, as the text does not identify the deity further in this context.
