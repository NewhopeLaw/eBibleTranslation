# Exodus 19

¹ In the third month, the Israelites left Egypt, on this day they arrived at the wilderness of Sinai. ² They traveled from Refidim and arrived at the wilderness of Sinai, and they encamped in the desert. Israel encamped before the mountain. ³ Moses went up to God, and the LORD called to him from the mountain, saying, "This is what you must say to the house of Jacob, and you must tell the children of Israel: ⁴ You have seen what I did to the Egyptians and how I bore you up on eagle’s wings, and brought you to myself. ⁵ Now, if you will indeed listen to my voice and keep my covenant, you will be my treasured possession out of all the peoples, for the whole earth is mine. ⁶ You will be a kingdom of priests and a holy nation. These are the words you must speak to the children of Israel." ⁷ Moses came and called the elders of the people, and he set before them all these words that the LORD had commanded him. ⁸ All the people answered together and said, "Everything the LORD has spoken we will do." Moses brought the words of the people back to the LORD. ⁹ The LORD said to Moses, "I am coming to you in a thick cloud, so that the people may hear me speaking with you and may believe you forever." Moses told the people the words of the LORD. ¹⁰ The LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow. Let them wash their clothes, ¹¹ and be ready on the third day, for on the third day the LORD will come down on Mount Sinai before the eyes of all the people. ¹² You must set boundaries around the people, saying, 'Take care not to go up the mountain or touch its edge. Everyone who touches the mountain will surely die.' ¹³ No hand may touch it. Any human or animal that touches it will be stoned or shot with an arrow. They will not live until the horn and the ram’s horn are sounded. Then those who are allowed may go up on the mountain." ¹⁴ Moses went down from the mountain to the people and consecrated them. They washed their clothes. ¹⁵ He said to the people, "Be ready for three days. Do not go near a woman." ¹⁶ On the third day, just as morning broke, there were sounds, lightning, and a heavy cloud on Mount Sinai, and the sound of a very loud trumpet. All the people in the camp trembled with fear. ¹⁷ Moses led the people out of the camp to meet God, and they stood at the foot of the mountain. ¹⁸ Mount Sinai was completely covered in smoke, because the LORD had descended on it in fire. The smoke rose like the smoke of a furnace, and the whole mountain trembled violently. ¹⁹ The sound of the trumpet grew stronger and stronger. Moses spoke, and God answered him in a voice. ²⁰ The LORD came down on Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain, and called to Moses to the top of the mountain. Moses went up. ²¹ The LORD said to Moses, "Go down and warn the people, so they do not break through to gaze at the LORD, and many of them fall. ²² Also, the priests who come near to the LORD must consecrate themselves, lest the LORD break out against them." ²³ Moses said to the LORD, "The people cannot go up to Mount Sinai, for you yourself have warned us, saying, 'Set boundaries around the mountain and consecrate it.'" ²⁴ The LORD said to him, "Go down, and you and Aaron may go up. But the priests and the people must not break through to go up to the LORD, lest the LORD break out against them." ²⁵ Moses went down to the people and spoke to them.

---

Notes
- v. 13: The Hebrew is ambiguous about whether the "horn" and "ram’s horn" refer to ceremonial signals (as translated) or are part of the divine manifestation. The Septuagint clarifies the former reading, which is followed here.
- v. 20: The Hebrew says "the top of the mountain" twice, with the second instance likely referring to the mountain's height in general. The Septuagint confirms that both refer to the summit.
