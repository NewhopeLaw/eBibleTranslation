# Exodus 26

¹ You shall make the tabernacle with ten curtains of twisted fine linen, hyacinth, purple, and crimson, embroidered with cherubim. ² Each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide; all the curtains shall be of equal measure. ³ Five curtains shall be joined to one another, and five curtains shall be joined to one another. ⁴ You shall make loops of hyacinth thread along the edge of one joined curtain, and in the same way you shall make loops along the edge of the other joined curtain. ⁵ Fifty loops you shall make on one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make on the edge of the other joined curtain, so that the loops may be opposite one another. ⁶ You shall make fifty golden hooks, and with the hooks you shall join the curtains, one to another, so that the tabernacle becomes one. ⁷ You shall make eleven curtains of goats’ hair for a covering over the tabernacle. ⁸ Each curtain shall be thirty cubits long and four cubits wide; all the eleven shall be of equal measure. ⁹ You shall join five curtains together into one piece, and six curtains together into one piece. The sixth curtain you shall fold over the front of the tabernacle. ¹⁰ You shall make fifty loops along the edge of one of the joined curtains, and fifty loops along the edge of the other joined curtain. ¹¹ You shall make fifty bronze hooks, and with the hooks you shall join the loops together, and the covering shall be one. ¹² The remaining strip of the covering shall be half a curtain in length; this strip shall hang down over the back of the tabernacle. ¹³ One cubit of the overhanging strip shall extend to one side and one cubit to the other, to cover the sides of the tabernacle on both sides. ¹⁴ You shall make a covering for the tent of ram skins dyed red, and a second covering of badger skins on top. ¹⁵ You shall make the frames of the tabernacle out of acacia wood. ¹⁶ Each frame shall be ten cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. ¹⁷ Each frame shall have two tenons, joined one to another; you shall make all the frames of the tabernacle in this way. ¹⁸ You shall make twenty frames for the tabernacle, on the southern side. ¹⁹ You shall make forty silver bases under the twenty frames: two bases under each frame for its two tenons, and two bases under each frame for its two tenons. ²⁰ For the second side of the tabernacle, the northern side, you shall make twenty frames. ²¹ You shall make forty bases for them of silver: two bases under each frame for its two tenons, and two bases under each frame for its two tenons. ²² You shall make six frames for the rear of the tabernacle on the west side. ²³ You shall make two frames for the corners of the tabernacle on the rear side. ²⁴ These shall be joined at the bottom and shall be perfectly aligned at the top into a single ring. You shall make them in this way for both the corners at the rear. ²⁵ These six frames shall be eight in number, and their bases of silver shall be sixteen: two bases under each frame for its two tenons, and two bases under each frame for its two tenons. ²⁶ You shall make five bars of acacia wood for the frames on one side of the tabernacle. ²⁷ You shall make five bars for the frames on the other side of the tabernacle and five bars for the frames on the rear side of the tabernacle that faces the west. ²⁸ The central bar shall pass through the middle of the frames, from one side to the other. ²⁹ You shall overlay the frames with gold, and you shall make rings of gold to fit the bars, and you shall overlay the bars with gold. ³⁰ You shall set up the tabernacle according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain. ³¹ You shall make a curtain of hyacinth, purple, crimson, and fine linen, embroidered with cherubim. ³² You shall place it on four pillars of acacia wood overlaid with gold, with golden hooks fastened to four silver bases. ³³ You shall place the curtain under the hooks and bring into the space behind the curtain the ark of the covenant. This curtain shall separate for you the holy place from the most holy place. ³⁴ You shall place the mercy seat on the ark of the covenant in the most holy place. ³⁵ You shall place the table outside the curtain, and the lampstand opposite the table on the southern side of the tabernacle; and you shall place the table on the northern side of the tabernacle. ³⁶ You shall make a screen for the entrance of the tent of hyacinth, purple, crimson, and fine linen, woven with embroidery. ³⁷ You shall make five pillars of acacia wood for the screen, and you shall overlay them with gold, with golden hooks, and you shall cast for them five bases of bronze.

---

Notes
- v. 21: The phrase "the northern side" is inferred from the context, as the Hebrew for "north" (צָפוֹן) is used metaphorically here to describe the second side of the structure.
- v. 33: "the space behind the curtain" is used to clarify that the ark is to be brought into the area separated by the curtain.
- v. 35: The orientation of the lampstand and table is inferred from the Hebrew terms for "southern" (תֵּימָנָה) and "northern" (צָפֽוֹן) as used in architectural descriptions.
