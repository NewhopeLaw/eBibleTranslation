# Exodus 28

¹ Bring near to yourself Aaron your brother and his sons with him from among the people of Israel to serve me. Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar are the sons of Aaron. ² You shall make sacred garments for Aaron your brother, for glory and for beauty. ³ You shall speak to all the wise-hearted, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, that they may make Aaron's garments to consecrate him to serve me. ⁴ These are the garments they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a tunic with checkered pattern, a turban, and a sash. They shall make sacred garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons to serve me. ⁵ They shall take gold, blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen. ⁶ They shall make the ephod of gold, blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, a woven work of skilled craftsmanship. ⁷ It shall have two shoulder pieces joined together at its two ends, fastened together. ⁸ The woven work on it, which is over it, shall be of the same kind made from it—gold, blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen. ⁹ You shall take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel. ¹⁰ Six names on one stone, and the six remaining names on the second stone, according to their birth order. ¹¹ A work of a skilled engraver, like a seal impression, you shall engrave the two stones with the names of the sons of Israel, set in gold filigree. ¹² You shall place the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, as stones of remembrance for the sons of Israel. Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel on his two shoulders before the LORD as a memorial. ¹³ You shall make gold filigree settings. ¹⁴ Two braided chains of pure gold, twisted in a looped pattern, you shall make. You shall place the twisted chains of the looped pattern on the gold filigree settings. ¹⁵ You shall make a breastpiece of judgment, a woven work like the ephod. You shall make it of gold, blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen. ¹⁶ It shall be square, doubled over. Its length and its width shall be equal. ¹⁷ You shall set in it a setting of four rows of stones. The first row shall be a carnelian, a topaz, and an emerald. ¹⁸ The second row shall be a beryl, an onyx, and a jacinth. ¹⁹ The third row shall be a ligure, an agate, and an amethyst. ²⁰ The fourth row shall be a chrysolite, a beryl, and an amethyst. They shall be set with gold filigree in their settings. ²¹ The stones shall be engraved with the names of the sons of Israel, twelve in all, each with its own name, like seal impressions. They shall be for the twelve tribes. ²² You shall make on the breastpiece looped chains of pure gold, twisted in a braided pattern. ²³ You shall make two gold rings and place them on the two ends of the breastpiece. ²⁴ You shall place the two twisted gold chains on the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece. ²⁵ The two free ends of the chains you shall fasten to the two gold filigree settings and attach them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod, in front. ²⁶ You shall make two gold rings and place them on the two ends of the breastpiece, on its edge facing the ephod. ²⁷ You shall make two gold rings and place them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, beneath, in front, as a counterweight to the woven part of the ephod. ²⁸ The breastpiece shall be fastened to the rings of the ephod with a thread of blue, so that the breastpiece remains attached to the woven part of the ephod and does not come loose from the ephod. ²⁹ Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel on the breastpiece of judgment on his heart, whenever he enters the holy place, as a memorial before the LORD continually. ³⁰ You shall place the urim and the thummim in the breastpiece of judgment, and they shall be on Aaron’s heart when he comes before the LORD. Aaron shall bear the judgment of the sons of Israel on his heart before the LORD continually. ³¹ You shall make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue. ³² It shall have an opening at the top, with a woven hem all around. A woven edging like a collar shall be at the opening, so it will not tear. ³³ You shall make pomegranates of blue, purple, and crimson all around the hem of the robe, with golden bells between them all around. ³⁴ A golden bell and a pomegranate, a golden bell and a pomegranate, shall be on the hem of the robe all around. ³⁵ It shall be on Aaron to serve, and the sound of it shall be heard when he enters the holy place before the LORD and when he leaves, so that he may not die. ³⁶ You shall make a plate of pure gold and engrave it like a seal impression: “Holy to the LORD.” ³⁷ You shall fasten it with a blue thread, and it shall be on the turban, in front of the turban. ³⁸ It shall be on Aaron’s forehead, and Aaron shall bear the iniquity of the holy things that the sons of Israel consecrate to all their gifts. It shall be on his forehead continually as a sign of acceptance before the LORD. ³⁹ You shall weave the tunic of fine linen and make the turban of fine linen. You shall make the sash in a skilled manner. ⁴⁰ For the sons of Aaron you shall make tunics and you shall make for them sashes and caps, for glory and for beauty. ⁴¹ You shall clothe Aaron your brother and his sons with them. You shall anoint them, fill their hands, and consecrate them, so that they may serve me. ⁴² You shall make for them linen undergarments to cover the flesh from the hips to the thighs. ⁴³ They shall be on Aaron and on his sons when they enter the Tent of Meeting or when they come near the altar to serve in the holy place, so that they may not bear guilt and die. It is a perpetual statute for him and for his descendants after him.

Notes
- v. 31: "entirely of blue" reflects the Hebrew כְּלִיל תְּכֵֽלֶת, which uses the verb "to be complete" in a material sense, indicating the robe is entirely blue.
- v. 33: "golden bells" and "pomegranates" follow the Septuagint's clearer structure when compared to the Hebrew, which is ambiguous in its phrasing. The Greek supports the interpretation of alternating golden bells and pomegranates.
- v. 36: "Holy to the LORD" is the standard translation of קֹדֶשׁ לַֽיהוָֽה, following the Septuagint's ἁγίασμα κυρίου.
