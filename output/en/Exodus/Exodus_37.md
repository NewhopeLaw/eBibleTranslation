# Exodus 37

¹ Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one and a half cubits high. ² He overlaid it with pure gold, inside and out, and made a gold molding all around it. ³ He cast four gold rings for it on its four corners, two rings on its one side and two rings on its second side. ⁴ He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold. ⁵ He inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark to carry the ark. ⁶ He made a lid of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. ⁷ He made two cherubim of hammered gold, making them from the two ends of the lid. ⁸ One cherub from the one end and one cherub from the second end, making the cherubim from the two ends of the lid. ⁹ The cherubim had their wings spread upward, overshadowing the lid with their wings, and their faces turned to each other, facing the lid the cherubim were facing. ¹⁰ Bezalel made the table of acacia wood, two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high. ¹¹ He overlaid it with pure gold and made a gold molding all around it. ¹² He made a rim an handbreadth around it and made a gold molding for its rim all around. ¹³ He cast four gold rings and placed the rings on the four corners at the base of its four legs. ¹⁴ The rings were opposite the rim as holders for the poles to carry the table. ¹⁵ He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold to carry the table. ¹⁶ He made the vessels on the table, its bowls, its ladles, its jars, and its incense ladles, all of pure gold. ¹⁷ He made the lampstand of pure gold, hammered from one piece: its base, its shaft, its cups, its calyxes, and its flowers were all from it. ¹⁸ Six branches came from its sides, three branches from its one side and three branches from its second side. ¹⁹ Three cups, each with a calyx and a flower, on each one branch; three cups, each with a calyx and a flower, on each one branch. The same for the six branches coming from the lampstand. ²⁰ On the lampstand itself were four cups, each with a calyx and a flower. ²¹ A calyx under the two branches from it, a calyx under the two branches from it, a calyx under the two branches from it for the six branches coming from it. ²² Their calyxes and their branches were all of it, one piece of hammered pure gold. ²³ He made its seven lamps and their snuffers and their trays, all of pure gold. ²⁴ He made it with a talent of pure gold, and all its vessels. ²⁵ He made the incense altar of acacia wood, one cubit long, one cubit wide, square, and two cubits high. Its horns were of it. ²⁶ He overlaid it with pure gold, its top, its surrounding walls, and its horns, and made a gold molding all around it. ²⁷ He made two gold rings for it beneath its molding, on its two sides, as holders for the poles to carry it. ²⁸ He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold. ²⁹ He made the anointing oil, sacred oil, and the incense, pure beaten work.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Bezalel" is the proper name for the artisan mentioned in Exodus 31:2 and 35:30–35.
- v. 25: "incense altar" refers to the smaller altar used specifically for burning incense, distinct from the bronze altar of burnt offering.
- v. 29: The anointing oil and incense are to be treated as sacred substances, their composition specified in Exodus 30.
