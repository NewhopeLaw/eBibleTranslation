# Exodus 38

¹ He made the altar of burnt offering of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide, square, and three cubits high. ² He made its horns on its four corners, its horns were of it, and he overlaid it with bronze. ³ He made all the vessels of the altar, its pots, its shovels, its basins, its forks, and its firepans; all its vessels he made of bronze. ⁴ He made for the altar a grating, a lattice work of bronze, beneath its ledge from below to its halfway point. ⁵ He cast four rings for the bronze grating, at its four corners, as holders for the poles. ⁶ He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with bronze. ⁷ He inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar to carry it, a framework of planks he made it. ⁸ He made the basin of bronze and its stand of bronze, according to the design of the mirrors of the women who served at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ⁹ He made the courtyard, for the southern side on the south, the hangings of the courtyard were six measures of fine linen, a hundred cubits. ¹⁰ Its posts were twenty and its bases twenty, posts of bronze and their hooks of silver. ¹¹ For the northern side, a hundred cubits; its posts were twenty and its bases twenty, posts of bronze and their hooks of silver. ¹² For the western side, the hangings were fifty cubits; its posts were ten and its bases ten, hooks of the posts and their caps of silver. ¹³ For the eastern side, facing the sunrise, fifty cubits. ¹⁴ The hangings were fifteen cubits on each side of the gate; its posts were three and its bases three. ¹⁵ For the second side of the gate, on each side, the hangings were fifteen cubits; its posts were three and its bases three. ¹⁶ All the hangings of the courtyard around were six measures of fine linen. ¹⁷ Its bases for the posts were bronze, hooks of the posts and their caps of silver, and the tops of the bases were overlaid with silver; all the posts of the courtyard were so overlaid. ¹⁸ The curtain for the gate of the courtyard was a woven work of blue, purple, and crimson, and fine linen, twenty cubits long and five cubits wide, corresponding to the hangings of the courtyard. ¹⁹ Its posts were four and its bases four, posts of bronze and their hooks of silver, and the tops of the posts were overlaid and their caps of silver. ²⁰ All the pegs of the Tent and of the courtyard around were bronze. ²¹ These are the records of the Tent of Meeting, the Tent of the Testimony, which were counted by Moses under the direction of the Levites, by the hand of Itamar son of Aaron the priest. ²² Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made all that the LORD had commanded Moses. ²³ With him was Aholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, a craftsman, an engraver, and a weaver in blue, in purple, in crimson, and in fine linen. ²⁴ All the gold used in the work of the sanctuary, in all the holy work, the gold of the contribution was twenty-nine talents and seven hundred thirty shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary. ²⁵ The silver of the census of the congregation was a hundred talents and seventeen hundred seventy and five shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary. ²⁶ A half-shekel of the sanctuary per person was given, for everyone who passed into the census, from twenty years and upward, for six hundred thousand five hundred fifty. ²⁷ The hundred talents of silver were used to cast the bases of the holy things and the bases of the curtain, a hundred bases, one talent per base. ²⁸ And the one thousand seven hundred seventy and five were made into hooks for the posts and to overlay their tops and to fasten them. ²⁹ The bronze of the contribution was seventy talents and two thousand four hundred shekels. ³⁰ He made from it the bases of the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and the bronze altar and its grating that was for it, and all the vessels of the altar. ³¹ And the bases of the courtyard around, and the bases of the gate of the courtyard, and all the pegs of the Tent and all the pegs of the courtyard around.
