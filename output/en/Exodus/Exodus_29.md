# Exodus 29

¹ This is the ritual you shall perform for them, to consecrate them to serve me: Take one young bull, two unblemished rams. ² Take unleavened bread, cakes of unleavened bread mixed with oil, and wafers of unleavened bread anointed with oil, made from fine flour. ³ You shall place them on one basket and bring them to the basket, along with the bull and the two rams. ⁴ You shall bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting and wash them with water. ⁵ Take the garments, and clothe Aaron your brother with the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod itself, and the breastpiece. Fasten the ephod on him with its clasps. ⁶ Place the turban on his head and set the plate of the holy crown on the turban. ⁷ Take the anointing oil, pour it on his head, and anoint him. ⁸ Bring his sons and clothe them with their tunics. ⁹ Gird them with sashes and put the headdress on them, so they may serve me. This shall be a permanent law for them. Then you shall consecrate Aaron and his sons by filling their hands. ¹⁰ Bring the bull before the Tent of Meeting. Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the bull. ¹¹ You shall slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ¹² Take some of the bull's blood, and with your finger, apply it to the horns of the altar. Then pour out the remainder of the blood at the base of the altar. ¹³ Take all the fat that covers the inwards, the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them. Place them on the altar as a burnt offering. ¹⁴ But the bull’s flesh, its hide, and its entrails, you shall burn outside the camp as a sin offering. ¹⁵ Take one of the rams. Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of this ram. ¹⁶ Slaughter the ram and take its blood and sprinkle it around the altar. ¹⁷ Cut the ram into pieces, wash its inwards and legs, and place the pieces and the head on the altar as a burnt offering. ¹⁸ The whole ram shall be burned as a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD. ¹⁹ Take the second ram. Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram. ²⁰ Slaughter the ram and take some of its blood and put it on the right earlobe of Aaron, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. Then do the same for each of Aaron’s sons: their right earlobes, their right thumbs, and their right big toes. Then pour out the remainder of the blood around the altar. ²¹ Take some of the blood from the altar and some of the anointing oil, and apply it to Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments. Thus, Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments shall be sanctified. ²² Take the ram’s fat, the fat that covers the inwards, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat on them, and the right leg. This is a ram of fullness. ²³ Take one unleavened cake from the basket, one unleavened wafer mixed with oil, and one wafer from the unleavened bread that is before the LORD. ²⁴ Place all of these on the hands of Aaron and his sons, and wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. ²⁵ Take them from their hands and burn them on the altar, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD. It is a fire offering to the LORD. ²⁶ Take the breast of the ram of fullness, which belongs to Aaron. Wave it before the LORD as a wave offering, and it shall be yours as a portion. ²⁷ Consecrate the wave breast and the lifted leg of the ram of fullness, which belong to Aaron and his sons. This is a fixed portion for Aaron and his sons from the people of Israel, for it is a gift lifted as an offering from the people of Israel. It is an offering from their peace offerings to the LORD. ²⁸ The garments of holiness belonging to Aaron shall be passed to his sons after him, for them to be anointed and for them to have their hands filled, to serve me. ²⁹ For seven days the priest who serves in place of Aaron from among his sons, the one who enters the Tent of Meeting to serve in the holy place, shall wear them. ³⁰ Take the ram of fullness and cook its flesh in a holy place. ³¹ Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram and the unleavened bread that is in the basket at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ³² They shall eat what atones for them, to consecrate them, to fill their hands. No outsider may eat it, for it is holy. ³³ If any of the flesh of the fullness offering or of the bread remains until the next morning, you must burn the remainder in the fire. It must not be eaten, for it is holy. ³⁴ You shall do this for Aaron and his sons as I have directed you. For seven days you shall fill their hands. ³⁵ Offer a bull of sin offering each day for atonement. Purify the altar when you anoint it, and anoint it to consecrate it. ³⁶ For seven days you shall purify the altar and consecrate it, and the altar shall be most holy. Anyone who touches it shall be consecrated. ³⁷ This is what you shall do on the altar: two year-old male lambs without blemish each day, a continual offering. ³⁸ One lamb you shall offer in the morning and the other in the evening. ³⁹ A tenth ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, a quarter of a hin of wine for the one lamb. ⁴⁰ The second lamb you shall offer in the evening, like the offering of the morning and like its drink offering. It is a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD. ⁴¹ A continual burnt offering for generations to come at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you and speak to you. ⁴² I will meet with the people of Israel there and be sanctified in My glory. ⁴³ I will consecrate the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and I will consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve me. ⁴⁴ I will dwell among the people of Israel and be their God. ⁴⁵ They will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt to dwell among them. I am the LORD their God. ⁴⁶ They will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt to dwell among them. I am the LORD their God.

---

Notes
- v. 21: "filling their hands" refers to the formal commissioning ritual for priests.
- v. 27: "portion" here refers to Aaron's share of the offerings, a gift from the people.
- v. 36: "Purify the altar" refers to the repeated sprinkling of blood to consecrate it.
