# Exodus 29

¹ This is the matter you must do for them to consecrate them to serve as priests for me: take one young bull and two unblemished rams. ² Make unleavened loaves, unleavened wafers mixed with oil, and unleavened thin cakes spread with oil, all of fine wheat flour. ³ Place them in one basket and bring them to the basket, along with the bull and the two rams. ⁴ Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and wash them with water. ⁵ Take the garments and dress Aaron with the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod itself, and the breastpiece, and fasten the breastpiece to the ephod with its craftsmanship. ⁶ Place the turban on his head and put the crown of consecration on the turban. ⁷ Take the anointing oil and pour it on his head, anointing him. ⁸ Bring his sons and dress them in tunics. ⁹ Gird them with sashes, and place the priestly sashes on them, so that it may be a perpetual priesthood for them. You must also consecrate Aaron and his sons' hands. ¹⁰ Bring the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the bull. ¹¹ Then you will slaughter the bull in the sight of the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ¹² Take some of the bull’s blood and put it with your finger on the horns of the altar, and pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar. ¹³ Take all the fat that covers the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and burn them as a food offering on the altar. ¹⁴ Burn the bull’s flesh, together with its hide and dung, outside the camp as a sin offering. ¹⁵ Take one of the rams, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram. ¹⁶ Then you will slaughter the ram and take its blood and sprinkle it around the altar. ¹⁷ Cut the ram into pieces, wash its inner parts and legs, and place them with its pieces and head on the altar. ¹⁸ Burn the whole ram as a burnt offering on the altar, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, a food offering for the LORD. ¹⁹ Take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram. ²⁰ Then you will slaughter the ram and take some of its blood and put it on the right earlobe of Aaron and his sons, on the thumb of their right hands, and on the big toe of their right feet. Then sprinkle the rest of the blood around the altar. ²¹ Take some of the blood from the altar and some of the anointing oil and smear it on Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments, consecrating them and their clothes. ²² Take the fat of the ram—the fat that covers the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh—since this is the ram of ordination. ²³ Take one unleavened cake, one unleavened wafer with oil, and one thin cake from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD. ²⁴ Put all these on the hands of Aaron and his sons and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. ²⁵ Then take them from their hands and burn them as a burnt offering on the altar, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ²⁶ Take the breast of the ram of ordination that belongs to Aaron and wave it as a wave offering before the LORD. It will be your portion. ²⁷ Consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the heave offering from the ram of ordination, from Aaron and from his sons. ²⁸ These will be for Aaron and his descendants forever from the Israelites, for it is a heave offering. And it will be a heave offering from the Israelites from their peace offerings, a heave offering for the LORD. ²⁹ The garments of the holy vestment that belong to Aaron will be passed to his sons after him to anoint them and to ordain them. ³⁰ The priest who succeeds him among his sons, whenever he enters the Tent of Meeting to serve in the holy place, must wear them for seven days. ³¹ You must take the ram of ordination and boil its flesh in a holy place. ³² Aaron and his sons will eat the flesh of the ram and the unleavened bread from the basket at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ³³ They will eat what was used to atone for them for ordaining their hands. A foreigner must not eat it, for it is holy. ³⁴ If any of the flesh of the ordination offering and the bread remains until morning, you must burn the leftovers in the fire. It must not be eaten, for it is holy. ³⁵ Do this for Aaron and his sons exactly as I have commanded you for seven days to ordain their hands. ³⁶ Make a sin offering for the day of atonement and purify the altar when you anoint it. Anoint it to consecrate it. ³⁷ For seven days you must purify the altar and consecrate it. And the altar will be most holy, and whoever touches the altar must be consecrated. ³⁸ This is what you must do on the altar: bring two unblemished year-old lambs every day as a continual offering. ³⁹ Slaughter the one lamb in the morning and the other lamb in the evening. ⁴⁰ Accompany each lamb with a tenth of fine flour mixed with oil, a quarter of a hin, and a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering. ⁴¹ Slaughter the second lamb in the evening, as you did the morning lamb, and make its drink offering as a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD. ⁴² This will be a continual burnt offering for your generations at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting in the sight of the LORD, where I will meet with you to speak to you. ⁴³ I will meet there with the Israelites, and I will be consecrated in their midst by my glory. ⁴⁴ I will consecrate the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and I will consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve as priests for me. ⁴⁵ I will dwell among the Israelites and will be their God. ⁴⁶ And they will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt to dwell among them. I am the LORD their God.

Notes
- v. 5: "the tunic, the robe of the ephod" – The Hebrew specifies the tunic as "kuttonet" and the robe as "me'il ha'epod," which is rendered here as "the robe of the ephod" to clarify its relation to the ephod.
- v. 20: "the right earlobe of Aaron and his sons" – The Hebrew text uses "tenukhe yemen" for "right earlobe," with "tenukhe" meaning "the earlobe."
- v. 43: "by my glory" – The Hebrew "bekavod" is rendered as "by my glory" to reflect the divine presence and majesty.
