# Exodus 1

¹ These are the names of the children of Israel who came to Egypt with Jacob, each one with his household: ² Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah. ³ Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin. ⁴ Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. ⁵ In total, the number of souls in Jacob’s lineage was seventy, including Joseph who was already in Egypt. ⁶ Then Joseph died, along with all his brothers and that entire generation. ⁷ The children of Israel multiplied and thrived, increasing greatly in number until the land was filled with them. ⁸ A new king arose over Egypt who did not know Joseph. ⁹ He said to his people, “Look, the people of Israel are too numerous and powerful for us. ¹⁰ Come, let us be wise with them, in case they multiply further and in war they join our enemies and rise up against us, taking over the land.” ¹¹ So he set taskmasters over them to afflict them with hard labor. He built store-cities for Pharaoh: Pithom and Raamses. ¹² Yet the more they were oppressed, the more they multiplied and spread, to the dismay of the Egyptians. ¹³ The Egyptians then made the Israelites work brutally. ¹⁴ They embittered their lives with harsh labor, with mortar and bricks, and every kind of work in the fields, all the labor imposed on them with cruelty. ¹⁵ Then Pharaoh spoke to the Hebrew midwives, whose names were Shiphrah and Puah. ¹⁶ He said, “When you assist the Hebrew women in childbirth, and you see them on the birthstool, if it is a boy, kill him; but if it is a girl, let her live.” ¹⁷ The midwives, however, feared God and did not do as the king of Egypt had commanded. They let the children live. ¹⁸ So Pharaoh called for the midwives and said to them, “Why have you done this thing, letting the children live?” ¹⁹ The midwives replied to Pharaoh, “The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women, for they give birth before the midwife arrives.” ²⁰ And God was kind to the midwives, and the people multiplied and grew very strong. ²¹ Because the midwives feared God, He gave them houses. ²² Then Pharaoh gave orders to all his people, saying, “Every newborn Hebrew boy you must cast into the river, but every girl you may let live.”

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "the children of Israel" is a standard rendering for בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, which can also be "the Israelites."  
- v. 15: Midwives are commonly referred to as such in modern English, rather than "those who assisted in birth" or "those who delivered."  
- v. 22: "the river" refers to the Nile; the Hebrew does not name the river, but the context makes it clear.
