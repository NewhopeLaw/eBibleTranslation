# Exodus 23

¹ Do not accept false testimony. Do not act as a malicious witness by joining with the wicked. ² Do not follow the crowd in doing evil, nor stand in a dispute to tip the scales with the majority. ³ Do not ignore the poor in their dispute. ⁴ If you encounter the ox or donkey of your enemy wandering, you must return it to him. ⁵ If you see the donkey of your enemy lying down under its load and are tempted to leave it, you must help carry it with you. ⁶ Do not pervert the judgment of your poor in their case. ⁷ Stay far from falsehood; do not kill the innocent and righteous, for I will not acquit the wicked. ⁸ Do not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds those who see and twists the words of the righteous. ⁹ Do not oppress a foreigner. You have known the life of the foreigner, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt. ¹⁰ For six years you may sow your land and gather its yield. ¹¹ But in the seventh year, you must let it rest and leave it untended, so that the poor among your people may eat it, and what remains will be for the wild animals. Do the same for your vineyard and your olive grove. ¹² For six days you may do your work, but on the seventh day you must rest, so that your ox and donkey may rest, and your hired worker and the foreigner may refresh themselves. ¹³ In all that I have commanded you, be careful to observe. Do not mention the names of other gods. Let no other name be heard on your lips. ¹⁴ Three times a year you must celebrate festivals to me. ¹⁵ Keep the Festival of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you must eat unleavened bread, as I have commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of Abib, for it was in this month that you came out of Egypt. You must not appear before me empty-handed. ¹⁶ And you must keep the Festival of the Harvest, of the firstfruits of your work that you have sown in the field, and the Festival of Ingathering at the end of the year when you gather your labor from the field. ¹⁷ Three times in the year all your males must appear before the Lord your God. ¹⁸ You must not sacrifice to me with leavened bread, and the fat of my festival must not remain overnight until morning. ¹⁹ The first of the firstfruits of your land you must bring to the house of the Lord your God. You must not boil a young goat in its mother’s milk. ²⁰ See, I am sending an angel before you to guard you on the journey and to bring you to the place I have prepared. ²¹ Be careful before him, and listen to his voice. Do not rebel against him, for he will not bear your sin. My name is in him. ²² But if you listen to his voice and do all that I say, I will be an enemy to your enemies and a foe to those who oppose you. ²³ For my angel will go before you, and I will bring you into the land of the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, and I will destroy them. ²⁴ You must not bow down to their gods or worship them. You must not do as they do, but you must destroy their altars and break their sacred pillars. ²⁵ You must worship the Lord your God, and I will bless your bread and your water, and I will remove sickness from among you. ²⁶ There will be no barrenness or sterility in your land. I will complete the number of your days. ²⁷ I will send my terror before you and throw every people before you. I will give all your enemies into your hands. ²⁸ I will send hornets before you, and I will drive out the Hivites, the Canaanites, and the Hittites from your way. ²⁹ I will not drive them out in a single year, lest the land become desolate and wild animals multiply against you. ³⁰ Little by little I will drive them out from before you, until you have taken possession of the land. ³¹ I will set your boundary from the Red Sea to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the Euphrates River. I will deliver into your hands the people who live in the land, and I will drive them out from before you. ³² You must not make a covenant with them or with their gods. ³³ They must not live in your land, lest they lead you to sin against me. For if you serve their gods, they will be a snare to you.

Notes
- v. 7: "I will not acquit the wicked" — the Hebrew "לֹא־אַצְדִּיק רָשָֽׁע" is straightforward, though some translations render it as "I will not justify the wicked." The meaning here is consistent with the context of divine justice.
- v. 17: "all your males" — the Hebrew uses זָכָר (male) in the plural, likely referring to every male Israelite.
- v. 22: "I will be an enemy to your enemies" — the Septuagint adds a longer theological statement about being a "royal priesthood and a holy nation," but the Hebrew does not include those words.
- v. 31: "Euphrates River" — the Hebrew reads "the great river, the Euphrates," which is translated accordingly.
