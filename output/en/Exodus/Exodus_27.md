# Exodus 27

¹ You shall make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide; the altar shall be square, and three cubits its height. ² You shall make its horns on its four corners; its horns shall come from it, and you shall overlay it with bronze. ³ You shall make a rim for the altar, and its basin and its jars and its shovels and its basins—all the vessels of the altar—you shall make of bronze. ⁴ You shall make a grate for it, a bronze grating, and you shall make four bronze rings for it on its four corners. ⁵ You shall place this grating under the altar’s base, underneath; the grating shall reach halfway up the altar. ⁶ You shall make for the altar poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with bronze. ⁷ You shall insert the poles into the rings, and the poles shall be on the two sides of the altar when it is borne. ⁸ Hollow planks you shall make it, as I showed you on the mountain. In this way they shall make it. ⁹ You shall make the courtyard of the tabernacle; on the southern side, the curtain of the courtyard shall be a hundred cubits in length on one side. ¹⁰ Its pillars shall be twenty, and their bases twenty of bronze; their hooks and their bands shall be of silver. ¹¹ Likewise, on the northern side, the curtain shall be a hundred cubits in length; its pillars shall be twenty, and their bases twenty of bronze; their hooks and their bands shall be of silver. ¹² The width of the courtyard on the western side shall be fifty cubits; its pillars shall be ten, and their bases ten. ¹³ The width of the courtyard on the eastern side, toward the sunrise, shall be fifty cubits. ¹⁴ The height of the curtain on one side shall be fifteen cubits; its pillars three, and their bases three. ¹⁵ And the height of the curtain on the second side shall be fifteen cubits; its pillars three, and their bases three. ¹⁶ For the gate of the courtyard you shall make a screen, twenty cubits high, of hyacinth, purple, crimson, and fine linen, embroidered; its pillars shall be four, and their bases four. ¹⁷ All the pillars of the courtyard around shall be overlaid with silver; their capitals shall be of silver, and their bases of bronze. ¹⁸ The length of the courtyard shall be a hundred by a hundred, and its width fifty by fifty; its height five cubits, of fine linen, and its bases of bronze. ¹⁹ All the furnishings, all the tools, and all the pegs of the courtyard shall be of bronze. ²⁰ You shall command the people of Israel to bring you pure, pressed oil of olives for the lamp, to keep a lamp burning constantly. ²¹ Aaron and his sons shall arrange it before the tabernacle, beyond the curtain that is over the covenant, from evening to morning before the LORD, a perpetual statute throughout their generations from the people of Israel.

Notes
- v. 8: "Hollow planks you shall make it" — the Hebrew uses נְבוּב לֻחֹת (nevuḇ luhōt), literally "hollow planks," which is a rare expression. The Septuagint uses κοιλον σανιδωτὸν (koilon sanidōton), "hollow with planks," which supports the rendering here.
