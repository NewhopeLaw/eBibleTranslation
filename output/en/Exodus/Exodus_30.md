# Exodus 30

¹ Make a censer for incense, of acacia wood. ² A cubit long and a cubit wide, it shall be square. Its height shall be two cubits, and from it its horns shall rise. ³ Overlay it with pure gold, its top, its sides all around, and its horns. Make a gold molding all around it. ⁴ Make two gold rings for it, under its molding, on its two sides. They shall be for poles to carry it. ⁵ Make the poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold. ⁶ Set it in front of the veil that is before the ark of the testimony, in front of the mercy seat where I have made Myself known to you. ⁷ Aaron shall burn incense on it in the morning, when he arranges the lamps. He shall burn incense on it. ⁸ When Aaron lights the lamps at twilight, he shall burn incense on it, a continual offering before the LORD throughout your generations. ⁹ You must not burn any strange incense on it, or an offering or a drink offering. It is holy to the LORD. ¹⁰ Aaron shall make atonement for its horns once a year, with the blood of the atonement offering. Once a year he shall make atonement for it. It is most holy to the LORD. ¹¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹² When you take a census of the people of Israel according to their numbers, each person must give a ransom for their life to the LORD, so that no plague may come upon them when they are counted. ¹³ This is what they must give: everyone who passes through the counting must give half a shekel according to the shekel of the sanctuary, twenty gerahs being the half-shekel. It is a contribution to the LORD. ¹⁴ Everyone who passes through the counting, from twenty years old and upward, must give a contribution to the LORD. ¹⁵ The wealthy must not give more, and the poor must not give less than half a shekel, when they give the contribution to the LORD, to atone for their lives. ¹⁶ You must take the silver contribution from the people of Israel and use it for the work of the tent of meeting. It shall be a memorial for the people of Israel before the LORD, to atone for their lives. ¹⁷ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁸ Make a bronze basin with its bronze stand for washing. Set it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it. ¹⁹ Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet from it. ²⁰ When they enter the tent of meeting, they must wash with water, or else they will die. When they approach the altar to serve, they must wash, to offer incense to the LORD. ²¹ They must wash their hands and their feet, or else they will die. It shall be a lasting ordinance for them, for Aaron and for his descendants throughout their generations. ²² The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²³ You must take fragrant spices: ten measures of fragrant myrrh, five measures of sweet cinnamon, five measures of fragrant cane, ²⁴ and thirty measures of cassia, according to the sanctuary shekel, and a hin of olive oil. ²⁵ Make these into a sacred anointing oil, a mixture made by the perfumer. It shall be sacred anointing oil. ²⁶ You shall anoint with it the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony, ²⁷ the table and all its vessels, the lampstand and all its vessels, the altar of incense, ²⁸ the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels, the basin and its stand. ²⁹ You shall consecrate them, and they shall be most holy. Whatever person touches them shall be consecrated. ³⁰ You shall anoint Aaron and his sons, and consecrate them, to serve Me. ³¹ You shall speak to the people of Israel, saying: This anointing oil is sacred to Me for your generations. ³² It must not be poured on a human body, and its formula must not be copied by you. It is sacred. It is sacred to you. ³³ Whoever mixes a copy of it, or whoever puts any of it on a foreigner, shall be cut off from his people. ³⁴ The LORD said to Moses: Take for yourself sweet spices: onycha, and galbanum, and sweet frankincense, and pure frankincense, each in equal parts. ³⁵ Make them into incense, a mixture made by the perfumer, salted, pure and holy. ³⁶ You shall beat some of it into fine powder, and put some of it before the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I have made Myself known to you. It is most holy to you. ³⁷ The incense you make must not be made by you for yourselves. It is sacred to the LORD. ³⁸ Whoever makes a copy of it to smell its fragrance shall be cut off from his people.

---

Notes
- v. 23: The measure "five measures of sweet cinnamon" (half of ten myrrh) is rendered as "half of this" to maintain the proportional comparison in context.
- v. 34: The spices listed in v. 34 differ slightly from v. 23. The Septuagint adds "stacte," but the Hebrew source uses "sweet spices: onycha, galbanum, sweet frankincense, and pure frankincense." The translation follows the Hebrew without aligning with the Greek.
