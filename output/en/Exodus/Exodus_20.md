# Exodus 20

¹ God spoke all these words, saying: ² "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. ³ "You must not have other gods besides me. ⁴ "You must not make for yourself a carved image or any likeness of what is in the heavens above or on the earth below or in the waters under the earth. ⁵ "You must not bow down to them or serve them, for I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the parents upon the children to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me, ⁶ but showing steadfast love to thousands of those who love me and keep my commandments. ⁷ "You must not take the name of the LORD your God in vain, for the LORD will not leave you unpunished who takes his name in vain. ⁸ "Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. ⁹ "Six days you shall labor and do all your work, ¹⁰ but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God. You must not do any work—you, your son, your daughter, your male and female servant, your livestock, and the foreigner living among you, ¹¹ for in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and he rested on the seventh day. Therefore, the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy. ¹² "Honor your father and your mother, so that you may live long in the land that the LORD your God is giving you. ¹³ "You must not murder. ¹⁴ "You must not commit adultery. ¹⁵ "You must not steal. ¹⁶ "You must not give false testimony against your neighbor. ¹⁷ "You must not covet your neighbor’s house. You must not covet your neighbor’s wife, nor his male servant, nor his female servant, nor his ox, nor his donkey, nor anything that is your neighbor’s." ¹⁸ All the people saw the thunder, the lightning flashes, the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking. The people were terrified and stood at a distance. ¹⁹ They said to Moses, "You speak to us, and we will listen. Let not God speak to us, lest we die." ²⁰ Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid, for God has come to test you, so that the fear of him may be with you, and you may not sin." ²¹ The people stood at a distance, while Moses approached the thick cloud where God was. ²² The LORD said to Moses, "Say this to the people of Israel: 'You have seen that I have spoken to you from the heavens. ²³ "You must not make for yourselves gods of silver or gods of gold. You must not make for yourselves anything to worship besides me. ²⁴ "You must make for me an altar of earth and sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your sheep and your oxen, in every place where I cause my name to be remembered. I will come to you and bless you. ²⁵ "But if you make an altar of stone, you must not build it with hewn stones, for by raising your tool upon it you would profane it. ²⁶ "You must not go up by steps onto my altar, lest you expose your nakedness on it."

Notes
- v. 13–17: The order of the commandments in the source text differs from the Septuagint and traditional numbering. The Hebrew places "You must not murder," "You must not commit adultery," "You must not steal," "You must not give false testimony," and "You must not covet" in sequence 13–17. The Septuagint reverses the order of "murder" and "adultery" but this is a known textual variation. The Hebrew reading is followed here.
- v. 24: The phrase "in every place where I cause my name to be remembered" is a direct translation of "בְּכָל־הַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר אַזְכִּיר אֶת־שְׁמִי" (bekhol hamakom asher azkir et shemi). The Septuagint adds a clause about the altar being made of earth or stone, which is reflected here in the translation of the following verse.
