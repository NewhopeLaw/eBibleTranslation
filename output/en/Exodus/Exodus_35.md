# Exodus 35

¹ Moses assembled the entire congregation of the Israelites and said to them, "These are the things the LORD has commanded to be done." ² Six days work shall be done, but on the seventh day you shall have a Sabbath of complete rest, a holy sabbath to the LORD. Whoever does any work on that day shall be put to death. ³ You must not kindle a fire in any of your dwellings on the sabbath day. ⁴ Moses spoke to the entire congregation of the Israelites, saying, "This is the thing the LORD has commanded, saying: ⁵ Take from among you an offering to the LORD. Whoever is generous in heart, let them bring it: the offering of the LORD—gold, silver, and bronze. ⁶ Blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and fine linen, and goat hair, ⁷ and tanned ram skins dyed red, and sea cow hides, and acacia wood, ⁸ and oil for the lamp, and the anointing oil, and the incense for the altar, ⁹ and onyx stones and other gemstones for the ephod and the breastpiece. ¹⁰ Everyone who is skillful among you must come and do all the work the LORD has commanded." ¹¹ "You must make the tabernacle, its tent, and its covering, its clasps and its beams, its crossbars and its posts, and its bases. ¹² You must make the ark and its poles, the mercy seat and the curtain of the veil. ¹³ You must make the table and its poles, and all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence. ¹⁴ You must make the lampstand and its utensils and its lamps, and the oil for the lamp. ¹⁵ You must make the altar of incense and its poles, the anointing oil and the incense, and the curtain for the entrance, at the entrance of the tabernacle. ¹⁶ You must make the altar of burnt offering and its bronze grate, its poles, and all its utensils, the basin and its stand. ¹⁷ You must make the curtain of the courtyard, its posts and its bases, and the curtain for the gate of the courtyard. ¹⁸ You must make the pegs for the tabernacle and the pegs for the courtyard, and their cords. ¹⁹ You must make the garments of the sacred service, the garments of the priesthood for Aaron the priest, and the garments for his sons to serve in the sacred place." ²⁰ The entire congregation of the Israelites withdrew from before Moses. ²¹ Every man whose heart was stirred and every woman whose spirit was willing brought an offering to the LORD for the work of the tent of meeting and for all the service of it and for the sacred garments. ²² The men came from the women, bringing rings, earrings, signets, pendants, and all kinds of gold ornaments, and every man who offered a freewill offering of gold to the LORD. ²³ Whoever had found blue, purple, or crimson yarn, or fine linen, or goat hair, or tanned ram skins dyed red, or sea cow hides, brought them. ²⁴ Whoever had offered silver or bronze brought it as an offering to the LORD, and whoever had found acacia wood for any work of the project brought it. ²⁵ Every woman who was skilled in weaving brought their own thread, blue, purple, or crimson, and fine linen. ²⁶ Every woman whose spirit was willing and who was skilled wove the goat hair. ²⁷ The contributors brought onyx stones and other gems for the ephod and the breastpiece, ²⁸ and the spices and the oil for the lamp, the anointing oil, and the incense. ²⁹ Every man and woman whose spirit was willing brought whatever was needed for the work that the LORD had commanded to be done through Moses. The Israelites gave their offerings freely to the LORD. ³⁰ Moses said to the Israelites, "Look, the LORD has called by name Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah. ³¹ He has filled him with the spirit of God, with wisdom, with insight, and with knowledge in every kind of craft. ³² And to devise artistic designs, to work in gold, silver, and bronze, ³³ in cutting stones for setting, and in carving wood, in every kind of workman's craft. ³⁴ And he has given him the ability to teach, both he and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan. ³⁵ He has filled them with wisdom of heart to do every kind of work, the work of the artisan, the designer, and the weaver in blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and those who work in all kinds of artistic designs."

---

Notes
- v. 5: "Take from among you an offering to the LORD" renders נְדִיב לִבּוֹ as "generous in heart" to reflect the inner motivation behind the giving. 
- v. 9: "onyx stones and other gemstones" for אַבְנֵי־שֹׁהַם וְאַבְנֵי מִלֻּאִים to avoid assuming specific gem names. 
- v. 28: "the spices and the oil for the lamp" for וְהַבֹּשֶׂם וְאֶת־הַשָּׁמֶן לְמָאוֹר to clarify the function of the oil.
