# Proverbs 19

¹ A wicked person who walks with integrity is better than a fool who speaks with his lips. ² A person without knowledge desires nothing, and one who stumbles with his feet commits sin. ³ The folly of a person leads him astray in his path, and his heart resents the LORD. ⁴ Wealth brings many friends, but the poor are separated even from their neighbor. ⁵ A false witness will not go unpunished, and the one who lies in testimony will not escape. ⁶ Many seek the favor of a noble, and all people are to a generous giver. ⁷ All the brothers of the wicked hate him, and his neighbor is far from him; he calls them with words, but they are not there. ⁸ A man who buys wisdom loves his own soul; one who guards understanding will find good. ⁹ A false witness will not go unpunished, and the one who lies in testimony is ruined. ¹⁰ It is not fitting for a fool to take pleasure, nor for a slave to rule over people. ¹¹ A person's wisdom makes his anger slow, and his glory is to overlook an offense. ¹² A king's rebuke is like the growl of a lion, but his favor is like dew on the grass. ¹³ A foolish son is a disgrace to his father; a quarrelsome wife is like constant dripping. ¹⁴ A house and wealth are inherited from ancestors, but a wise wife is from the LORD. ¹⁵ Lazy hands cause poverty, and idle fingers bring hunger. ¹⁶ One who keeps the commandment preserves his life, but the one who ignores his ways will die. ¹⁷ One who lends to the LORD will be repaid, and he will repay the person fully. ¹⁸ Discipline your son, for there is hope; do not set your heart on causing him to die. ¹⁹ A hot-tempered man must bear the punishment; if you spare him, you will have to do so again. ²⁰ Listen to advice and accept instruction, so that you will be wise in the end. ²¹ Many are the plans in a person's heart, but the counsel of the LORD is what stands. ²² The desire of a person is his steadfast love, and the good of the upright is better than a deceitful person. ²³ The fear of the LORD is for life, and one who rests in it will not be found guilty of wrongdoing. ²⁴ A lazy man buries his hand in the dish, and even his mouth refuses to bring it back. ²⁵ A scoffer will be struck down, and a fool will be humbled; the one who corrects a wise person will gain understanding. ²⁶ One who robs his father and drives out his mother is a son who brings shame and disgrace. ²⁷ My son, do not stop listening to instruction, so that you may avoid the words of knowledge. ²⁸ A witness who lies gives a false judgment, and the mouth of the wicked devours iniquity. ²⁹ Punishments are prepared for scoffers, and beatings are for the foolish.

---

Notes
- v. 11: "wisdom makes his anger slow" reflects the metaphorical use of "sekel" as a measure of mental discernment.
- v. 23: "rests in it" is a natural rendering of "yalin" in the context of living securely.
- v. 25: "gain understanding" (from "yavin da'at") is a natural rendering of the Hebrew's emphasis on a wise person's comprehension.
- v. 28: "mouth of the wicked devours iniquity" captures the figurative sense of "yb'la awen," where "devour" reflects the Hebrew verb's intensity.
