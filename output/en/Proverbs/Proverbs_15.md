# Proverbs 15

¹ A gentle answer turns away anger, but a harsh word stirs up wrath. ² The tongue of the wise brings understanding, but the mouth of fools speaks folly. ³ In every place the eyes of the LORD are watching both the evil and the good. ⁴ A healing tongue is a tree of life, but folly in it breaks the spirit. ⁵ A fool despises his father's discipline, but whoever keeps instruction is prudent. ⁶ The house of the righteous is full of strength, and the offspring of the wicked are uprooted. ⁷ The lips of the wise speak wisdom, but the heart of fools does not. ⁸ The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is His delight. ⁹ The way of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but the one who seeks righteousness He loves. ¹⁰ Discipline is bitter to the one who leaves the path, and whoever hates correction will die. ¹¹ Sheol and destruction are in the sight of the LORD, how much more the hearts of human beings! ¹² The scoffer will not love being rebuked, nor will he go among the wise. ¹³ A heart at peace brings a joyful countenance, but a heart full of sorrow makes the spirit droop. ¹⁴ A discerning heart seeks knowledge, but the mouth of fools speaks folly. ¹⁵ All the days of the afflicted are bad, but the one with a good heart has a continual feast. ¹⁶ Better a little with the fear of the LORD than great wealth with unrest. ¹⁷ Better a meal of vegetables with love than a feast of meat with hatred. ¹⁸ A hot-tempered man stirs up strife, but the one slow to anger calms a quarrel. ¹⁹ The way of the lazy is like a thornbush, but the path of the upright is smooth. ²⁰ A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish son is a mother's disgrace. ²¹ Folly is joy to the one without a heart, but a man of understanding walks uprightly. ²² Plans are overturned without consultation, but with many counselors they will be established. ²³ Joy is in a person's answer, and how good is a word spoken in its time! ²⁴ The way of life is upward for the discerning, so that he may turn from Sheol below. ²⁵ The LORD will overturn the house of the arrogant, but He will set a boundary for the widow. ²⁶ An abomination to the LORD are evil plans, but pure are the words of the upright. ²⁷ A man who corrupts his house takes bribes, but the one who hates gifts will live. ²⁸ The heart of the righteous considers how to answer, but the mouth of the wicked pours out evil. ²⁹ The LORD is far from the wicked, but He hears the prayers of the righteous. ³⁰ The light in the eyes brings joy to the heart, and good news strengthens the bones. ³¹ An ear that listens to reproof brings life, which is set among the wise. ³² The one who ignores discipline harms himself, but the one who accepts correction gains a heart. ³³ The fear of the LORD is instruction in wisdom, and before honor comes humility.

---

Notes  
- v. 6: "offspring of the wicked are uprooted" — the Hebrew "נֶעְכָּֽרֶת" is interpreted as "uprooted" to convey the idea that their lineage or works will not endure.  
- v. 25: "He will set a boundary for the widow" — the Hebrew "יַצֵּב גְּבוּל אַלְמָנָֽה" is translated to reflect God's protection and care for the vulnerable, especially widows.  
- v. 27: "A man who corrupts his house takes bribes" — the Hebrew "בּוֹצֵעַ בָּצַע" is rendered as "takes bribes" to clarify the moral implication of corruption through receiving unjust gifts.
