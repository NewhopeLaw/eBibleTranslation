# Proverbs 24

¹ Do not envy evil men, nor desire to be among them. ² For their hearts scheme deceit, and their lips speak trouble. ³ A house is built with wisdom, and a home is made firm with understanding. ⁴ With knowledge, storehouses are filled with all kinds of precious and pleasant wealth. ⁵ A wise man is stronger than a warrior, and a man of understanding is mightier than a strong man. ⁶ For you make war with counsel, and victory comes through many counselors. ⁷ Wisdom and understanding are too high for a fool; they do not open their mouth in the gate. ⁸ The one who plans to do evil will be called a schemer. ⁹ A fool's plot is sin, and disgrace is for a scoffer. ¹⁰ If you falter in time of trouble, how little strength you have! ¹¹ Rescue those being taken to death, and keep others from being slain, if you can. ¹² For if you say, “Surely we do not know this,” does He not who searches hearts not know it? The Keeper of your soul knows it, and He will repay each man according to his deeds. ¹³ My son, eat honey, for it is good, and the sweet taste is on your tongue. ¹⁴ So you will know wisdom for your soul, and if you find it, your hope will not be cut off. ¹⁵ Do not lie in wait for the wicked, O righteous one, and do not intrude upon their resting place. ¹⁶ For a righteous man may fall seven times and rise again, but the wicked will stumble into calamity. ¹⁷ Do not rejoice when your enemy falls, nor let your heart be glad when he stumbles. ¹⁸ Lest the Lord see it and be displeased, and He turns His anger away from him. ¹⁹ Do not take pleasure in evildoers, nor covet the wicked. ²⁰ For there is no future for the evil; the lamp of the wicked will be extinguished. ²¹ Fear the Lord, my son, and the king, and do not associate with those who are different. ²² For they will suddenly rise, and the punishment of both will be unknown. ²³ Also, these are for the wise: to show favor in judgment is not good. ²⁴ One who says to a wicked man, “You are righteous,” will be cursed by people, and nations will curse him. ²⁵ But to those who rebuke him, it will be well, and upon them will come the blessing of the good. ²⁶ The lips of the one who gives a truthful answer kiss the mouth. ²⁷ Prepare your work outside, and make it ready in the field; after that, build your house. ²⁸ Do not become a false witness against your friend, nor deceive with your lips. ²⁹ Do not say, “As he did to me, so I will do to him; I will repay each man according to his deed.” ³⁰ I passed by the field of a lazy man and the vineyard of a man with a lazy heart. ³¹ And look, it was completely overgrown with thorns; its surface was covered with weeds, and its stone wall was broken down. ³² Then I saw it and reflected; I looked at it and took a lesson. ³³ A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to lie down — ³⁴ and your poverty will come like a traveler, and your need will be like one who is on guard.

---

Notes
- v. 12: The translation follows the reading that the speaker is addressing a person who claims ignorance about someone, and it is countered by the omniscience of the One who examines hearts.
- v. 23: The phrase "to show favor in judgment is not good" is a rendering of the line that warns against partiality in decisions.
- v. 27: The instruction to "Prepare your work outside" is taken to mean planning or preparing for future work.
- v. 29: The line is rendered to match the context of not responding to evil with evil.
