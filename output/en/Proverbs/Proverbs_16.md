# Proverbs 16

¹ A man may plan his heart, but the answer of his tongue is from the LORD. ² Every way of a man is right in his eyes, but the LORD weighs the spirits. ³ Commit your works to the LORD, and your plans will be established. ⁴ The LORD has made all things for His purpose, even the wicked for a day of evil. ⁵ The arrogance of the proud is an abomination to the LORD; no hand that acts unjustly will go unpunished. ⁶ In steadfast love and truth, iniquity is atoned for; and in the fear of the LORD, one turns from evil. ⁷ In the favor of the LORD a man’s ways are pleasing; even his enemies will make peace with him. ⁸ Better a little with righteousness than much wealth with injustice. ⁹ The heart of a man may plan his way, but the LORD establishes his steps. ¹⁰ A command is on the lips of the king; in judgment, he does not alter his mouth. ¹¹ A balance and weights of justice belong to the LORD; all the works of His hands are measured. ¹² The doing of evil is an abomination to kings, for on righteousness a throne is established. ¹³ The desire of kings is the lips of justice; and one who speaks uprightly is loved. ¹⁴ The anger of a king is like the messenger of death, but a wise man will atone for it. ¹⁵ The light of a king’s face gives life, and his favor is like a cloud bringing rain. ¹⁶ Acquire wisdom—how much better it is than silver! Acquire understanding—how much better it is than gold! ¹⁷ The path of the upright turns away from evil; the one who guards his soul keeps his way. ¹⁸ Before a fall is pride, and before a stumble is a proud heart. ¹⁹ Better is a humble spirit among the lowly than the dividing of spoils among the proud. ²⁰ A discerning man will find good, and one who puts trust in the LORD will be blessed. ²¹ One who is wise of heart is called understanding; and the sweetness of his lips increases learning. ²² A source of life is wisdom to the one who possesses it; but the instruction of fools is folly. ²³ A wise heart will guide its mouth, and on its lips it will add learning. ²⁴ Words of the wise are honeyed; how sweet they are to the soul, and how healing to the bones! ²⁵ There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the ways of death. ²⁶ The soul of the worker labors for himself, for his mouth is driven by his hunger. ²⁷ A man of mischief plots evil, and on his tongue a burning flame lies ready. ²⁸ A man of deceit sends a messenger, and a divisive tongue kindles a flame. ²⁹ A violent man entices his friend and leads him down a path that is not good. ³⁰ One whose eyes are fixed to plot deception, whose lips mark out evil, he is a furnace of wickedness. ³¹ The crown of old age is a life lived in righteousness. ³² Better is a patient man than a warrior, and one who rules his spirit than one who captures a city. ³³ The casting of the lot is in the lap, but the whole judgment is from the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 33: "The casting of the lot is in the lap" — the image of the lot (goral) being cast into the lap (kopher) is a metaphor for human decision-making, while the final outcome ("the whole judgment") is reserved to the LORD.
