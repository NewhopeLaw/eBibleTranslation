# Proverbs 4

¹ Listen, children, to a father's discipline and give attention to know understanding. ² For I have given you a good instruction; do not abandon my teaching. ³ I was a son to my father, tender, and a beloved only child to my mother. ⁴ He taught me and said to me: Let your heart hold fast to my words; keep my commandments and live. ⁵ Acquire wisdom, acquire understanding; do not forget, and do not turn away from the words of my mouth. ⁶ Do not abandon her, and she will guard you; love her, and she will keep you. ⁷ The beginning of wisdom is to acquire wisdom; with all your acquiring, acquire understanding. ⁸ Cherish her, and she will exalt you; honor her, and she will give you honor. ⁹ She will place a wreath of grace on your head; she will give you a crown of splendor as a shield. ¹⁰ Listen, my son, and take my words; let many years of life be added to you. ¹¹ I have taught you the way of wisdom; I have guided you in the paths of uprightness. ¹² When you walk, your steps will not falter; when you run, you will not stumble. ¹³ Hold fast to discipline; do not let it slip from you. Guard it, for it is your life. ¹⁴ Do not enter the path of the wicked, and do not walk in the way of the evil. ¹⁵ Avoid it; do not pass by it. Turn away from it and go on. ¹⁶ For they will not sleep unless they do harm; their sleep is taken away unless they cause someone to stumble. ¹⁷ For they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence. ¹⁸ But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn, shining brighter and brighter until the day is fully come. ¹⁹ The way of the wicked is like darkness; they do not know what causes them to stumble. ²⁰ My son, give attention to my words; incline your ear to my sayings. ²¹ Let them not escape from your eyes; keep them within your heart. ²² For they are life to those who find them, and healing for all flesh. ²³ Above all else, guard your heart, for from it come the sources of life. ²⁴ Put away crooked speech from yourself; keep deceitful words far from you. ²⁵ Let your eyes look directly forward; let your gaze be fixed before you. ²⁶ Make level the paths for your feet, and all your ways will be established. ²⁷ Do not turn to the right or to the left; remove your foot from evil.

---

Notes
- v. 17: "the bread of wickedness" and "the wine of violence" reflect metaphorical language, aligning with the context of moral corruption.
- v. 23: "Above all else" translates the Hebrew "מִכָּל" (mikol) in context, emphasizing the primacy of guarding the heart.
- v. 27: The final verse uses "remove your foot from evil" to mirror the parallel imperative and convey the idea of avoiding wrongdoing.
