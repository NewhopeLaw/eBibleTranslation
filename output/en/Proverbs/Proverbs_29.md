# Proverbs 29

¹ A man who refuses correction has a stubborn neck; suddenly he breaks, and there is no healing. ² When the righteous are numerous, the people rejoice; but when the wicked rule, the people groan. ³ A man who loves wisdom brings joy to his father, but one who keeps mistresses squanders his wealth. ⁴ A king upholds the land through justice, but a man of bribes ruins it. ⁵ A man who sows flattery over his companion spreads a net under his feet. ⁶ In the sin of a wicked man there is a trap, but the righteous will triumph and rejoice. ⁷ The righteous understand the judgment of the poor, but the wicked do not grasp knowledge. ⁸ Rebels destroy a city, but wise men turn away wrath. ⁹ A wise man judges an ignorant man, but the ignorant man rages and laughs, and finds no peace. ¹⁰ Men of bloodshed hate the innocent, but the upright seek his life. ¹¹ A fool vents all his spirit, but a wise man holds it back in measure. ¹² A ruler who listens to a false word makes all his servants lawless. ¹³ A creditor and a debtor meet together — the LORD sees both of them. ¹⁴ A king who judges the poor with truth will establish his throne forever. ¹⁵ A rod and reproof give wisdom, but a youth left uncorrected disgraces his mother. ¹⁶ When lawless men multiply, so do crimes; but when the righteous are overtaken, they see it. ¹⁷ Discipline your son, and he will bring you rest, and give delight to your soul. ¹⁸ Without vision, the people perish; but he who keeps the teaching is blessed. ¹⁹ Words will not correct a stubborn servant — even if he understands, he will not listen. ²⁰ Have you seen a man quick with words? There is more hope for a fool than for him. ²¹ One who indulges his slave from youth will in the end find him rebellious. ²² A man of anger stirs up conflict, and a man of wrath digs up sin. ²³ The pride of a man brings his downfall, but one of humble spirit will be upheld by glory. ²⁴ One who shares with a thief hates his own soul — if they are made to swear and will not reveal it. ²⁵ Fear of man lays a snare, but the one who trusts in the LORD is safe. ²⁶ Many seek the favor of rulers, but from the LORD comes the judgment of a man. ²⁷ The abomination of the righteous is a lawless man, and the abomination of the wicked is the path that is upright.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "makes all his servants lawless" — this reflects the idea that a corrupt ruler's influence spreads to his subordinates.
- v. 24: "if they are made to swear and will not reveal it" — this follows the Hebrew's structure, which implies a refusal to testify.
- v. 25: "Fear of man lays a snare" — "Fear of man" refers to being overly concerned with human opinion or approval, which leads to being ensnared.
