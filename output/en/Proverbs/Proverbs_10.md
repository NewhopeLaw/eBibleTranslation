# Proverbs 10

¹ The proverbs of Solomon: A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish son is the mother’s disgrace. ² Wrongdoing will not save the wicked, but righteousness rescues from death. ³ The Lord will not let the soul of the righteous go hungry, but the desires of the wicked will be thwarted. ⁴ A hardworking hand is a head of gold, but lazy hands bring poverty. ⁵ A wise son stores up in the summer, but a disgraceful son sleeps during harvest. ⁶ Blessings crown the head of the righteous, but the mouth of the wicked hides violence. ⁷ The memory of the righteous is a blessing, but the name of the wicked will rot. ⁸ A wise-hearted person accepts commandments, but a fool’s lips lead to ruin. ⁹ One who walks with integrity walks with confidence, but the one who twists his ways will be known. ¹⁰ One who winks with the eye sows discord, but a fool’s lips lead to ruin. ¹¹ A fountain of life is the mouth of the righteous, but the mouth of the wicked hides violence. ¹² Hatred incites quarrels, but love covers all sins. ¹³ Wisdom is found in the lips of the discerning, but a rod is for the back of the heartless. ¹⁴ The wise guard knowledge, but the mouth of a fool is near ruin. ¹⁵ The wealth of the rich is their city of strength, but the downfall of the wicked is their ruin. ¹⁶ The fruit of the righteous is for life, but the fruit of the wicked is for sin. ¹⁷ The path of life is for the one who keeps instruction, but the one who turns from correction strays. ¹⁸ One who hides hatred with lying lips, and one who spreads gossip is a fool. ¹⁹ In many words, there is no lack of wrongdoing, but one who holds back his lips is discerning. ²⁰ A refined silver tongue is the tongue of the righteous, but the heart of the wicked is worthless. ²¹ The lips of the righteous feed many, but fools, heartless, will die. ²² The blessing of the Lord enriches, and no sorrow will come with it. ²³ Doing evil is a joke to a fool, but wisdom is the delight of the discerning. ²⁴ The fear that the wicked have will come to them, but the desire of the righteous will be granted. ²⁵ Like a storm that passes, the wicked will vanish, but the righteous will stand firm forever. ²⁶ Vinegar is bitter to the teeth and smoke is harsh to the eyes, so is the lazy to those who employ them. ²⁷ The fear of the Lord adds days, but the years of the wicked will be cut short. ²⁸ The hope of the righteous is joy, but the expectation of the wicked will perish. ²⁹ The path of the Lord is a stronghold for the innocent, but ruin for those who do evil. ³⁰ The righteous will not be shaken forever, but the wicked will not dwell in the land. ³¹ The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom, but the tongue of the wicked is destroyed. ³² The lips of the righteous know what pleases, but the mouth of the wicked is twisted.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish son is the mother’s disgrace." — The Hebrew uses "יְשַׂמַּח־אָב" and "תוּגַת אִמּוֹ" to contrast the effect of the wise and foolish sons on their parents.
- v. 15: "The wealth of the rich is their city of strength" — The Hebrew metaphor "קִרְיַת עֻזּוֹ" is translated as "city of strength."
- v. 18: "One who hides hatred with lying lips" — The Hebrew uses "מְכַסֶּה שִׂנְאָה שִׂפְתֵי־שָׁקֶר" for the person who hides malice.
- v. 22: "The blessing of the Lord enriches, and no sorrow will come with it." — The Hebrew uses "תַּעֲשִׁיר" and "לֹֽא־יוֹסִיף עֶצֶב עִמָּֽהּ," indicating the blessing brings prosperity without grief.
