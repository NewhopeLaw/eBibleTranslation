# Proverbs 8

¹ Is wisdom not calling out? Does understanding not raise its voice? ² At the highest points, above the crossroads, it stands firm; ³ by the gates, at the city's doorways, it calls to the people. ⁴ To you, people, I call out; my voice is directed to the human race. ⁵ Pay attention, you simple-minded ones, to learning; you fools, set your hearts to understanding. ⁶ Listen, for I speak what is noble, and from the opening of my mouth, what is upright. ⁷ For my mouth speaks truth, and the words of my lips detest wickedness. ⁸ All the words of my mouth are just; there is nothing crooked or twisted in them. ⁹ They are all clear to the one who understands, and straight to those who find knowledge. ¹⁰ Take instruction, and not silver; gain knowledge, and not gold refined. ¹¹ For wisdom is better than jewels, and all that you may desire cannot be compared to her. ¹² I, wisdom, dwell with prudence, and knowledge I find delight in. ¹³ The fear of the LORD is to hate evil: pride, arrogance, and the evil way, and the twisted mouth I hate. ¹⁴ Counsel and sound judgment are mine; I have insight, I have strength. ¹⁵ By me, kings reign, and rulers decree justice. ¹⁶ By me, rulers rule; by me, the mighty govern the people justly. ¹⁷ I love those who love me, and those who seek me find me. ¹⁸ Riches and honor are with me—enduring wealth and righteousness. ¹⁹ My fruit is better than gold, better than fine gold; my yield is better than silver refined. ²⁰ I walk in the way of righteousness, in the center of the paths of justice, ²¹ to give an inheritance to those who love me and to fill their treasuries. ²² The LORD created me at the beginning of his works, before his deeds of old. ²³ From eternity I was established, from the beginning, before the earth was formed, ²⁴ when there were no depths, and no springs of water had yet been made, ²⁵ before the mountains were settled in place, before the hills I was brought forth, ²⁶ before he made the earth or the fields, or the dust of the world, ²⁷ when he prepared the heavens, I was there, when he drew a circle on the face of the deep. ²⁸ When he established the clouds above, and when he secured the fountains of the deep, ²⁹ when he set a limit for the sea, so that the waters should not transgress his command, and when he laid the foundations of the earth, ³⁰ I was by him, a trusted one, and daily I was his delight, rejoicing before him always, ³¹ rejoicing in the inhabited world, and delighting in the human race. ³² Now then, you children, listen to me; keep my ways well. ³³ Pay attention to instruction and gain understanding; do not turn away. ³⁴ Happy is the one who listens to me, watching at my doors daily, keeping watch at the posts of my entrances. ³⁵ For whoever finds me finds life and obtains favor from the LORD. ³⁶ But whoever acts wickedly harms their own soul; all who hate me love death.

---

Notes
- v. 22: The phrase "YHWH created me" (יְֽהוָה קָנָנִי) is rendered as "The LORD created me" to reflect the context of wisdom's origin as a divine creation.
- v. 23: The phrase "from eternity I was established" (מֵעוֹלָם נִסַּכְתִּי) uses "established" to convey the sense of being set in place from the beginning of time.
- v. 35: "Obtains favor" (יָפֶק רָצוֹן) is a rendering of the Hebrew idiom that suggests receiving favor or approval from the LORD.
- v. 36: "Acts wickedly" (חֹטְאִי חֹמֵס) is chosen to reflect the sense of willful transgression.
