# Proverbs 11

¹ The Lord detests dishonest scales, but He delights in a true weight. ² When arrogance comes in, disgrace follows, but with the humble comes wisdom. ³ The upright person’s truthfulness protects them, but the deceiver’s self-interest ensnares them. ⁴ Wealth will not help on the day of wrath, but righteousness rescues from death. ⁵ The righteousness of the innocent straightens their path, but in their wickedness, the wicked stumble. ⁶ The righteousness of the upright saves them, but the deceitfulness of the wicked traps them. ⁷ When a wicked man dies, hope is lost, and the pride of sinners vanishes. ⁸ The righteous are delivered from trouble, but the wicked fall into it. ⁹ With a deceitful mouth, one destroys a companion, but the understanding of the righteous saves. ¹⁰ By the goodness of the righteous, a city is exalted, and by the downfall of the wicked, there is rejoicing. ¹¹ By the blessing of the upright, the city is raised, but by the mouth of the wicked, it is torn down. ¹² One who belittles a companion lacks sense, but a person of understanding keeps quiet. ¹³ A gossiping person reveals secrets, but a trustworthy spirit conceals a matter. ¹⁴ Without guidance, a people will fall, but in many counselors, there is safety. ¹⁵ Woe to the one who guarantees for a stranger, and to the one who puts up security for a foe. ¹⁶ A woman of worth supports honor, but the violent cling to wealth. ¹⁷ One who shows kindness to themselves is a person of loyalty, but one who withholds favor is cruel. ¹⁸ A wicked person does deeds of falsehood, but one who sows righteousness reaps truth. ¹⁹ Just as righteousness leads to life, so the one who pursues evil pursues death. ²⁰ The Lord detests crooked hearts, but He delights in those who are blameless in their ways. ²¹ The wicked will not escape punishment, hand in hand, but the seed of the righteous will be saved. ²² Like a nose ring in a pig’s snout, so is beauty in a woman without sense. ²³ The desire of the righteous is always good, but the hope of the wicked is wrongdoing. ²⁴ There are those who scatter and yet gain more, and those who withhold too much end in need. ²⁵ The soul that blesses is satisfied, and one who waters will also be watered. ²⁶ One who withholds grain will be cursed, but the blessing rests on the head of the one who shares. ²⁷ One who seeks good pursues favor, but one who seeks evil will encounter it. ²⁸ One who trusts in wealth will fall, but the righteous will flourish like a green shoot. ²⁹ One who neglects their household inherits the wind, but a foolish servant becomes a wise man’s heir. ³⁰ The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, and the one who takes lives is a wise man. ³¹ Even the righteous who live on earth will be put to death, let alone the wicked and the sinner.

---

Notes
- v. 29: "The one who takes lives is a wise man" reflects a challenging interpretation of the Hebrew, where the meaning is opaque. The phrase likely contrasts the "wise man" with the one who neglects his family, but the precise connection is uncertain.
- v. 31: The reading "Even the righteous who live on earth will be put to death, let alone the wicked and the sinner" is a literal translation of the difficult Hebrew, which may be a proverbial expression of the fate of the wicked.
