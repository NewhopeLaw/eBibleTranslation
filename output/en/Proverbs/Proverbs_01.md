# Proverbs 1

¹ Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel. ² To learn wisdom and discipline, to understand words of insight. ³ To receive discipline of discernment, righteousness, judgment, and equity. ⁴ To give understanding to the simple, knowledge and discretion to the young. ⁵ Let the wise listen and grow in learning, and the discerning acquire understanding. ⁶ To understand a proverb and a figure of speech, the words of the wise and their riddles. ⁷ The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and discipline. ⁸ Listen, my son, to your father's discipline; do not neglect your mother's instruction. ⁹ For they are a garland of grace for your head and a chain for your neck. ¹⁰ My son, if the wicked entice you, do not consent. ¹¹ If they say, "Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us ambush an innocent man for no reason, ¹² we will swallow them alive like the grave, and whole, like those who descend to the pit; ¹³ we will find all kinds of precious wealth, we will fill our houses with plunder; ¹⁴ cast your lot among us; let us all have one purse." ¹⁵ My son, do not walk in the way with them, keep your foot from their path. ¹⁶ For their feet run to evil, and they hurry to shed blood. ¹⁷ For in vain they spread a net where their wings are spread. ¹⁸ And they are ready to make themselves guilty, to lie in wait for their own souls. ¹⁹ So are the ways of all who do violence; the life of the transgressor is taken. ²⁰ Wisdom calls aloud in the streets, she raises her voice in the broad places; ²¹ at the head of the noisy streets she cries out; at the entrance of the city gates she declares her words: ²² "How long, you simple ones, will you love being simple? And you scoffers, how long will you scoff? And you fools, how long will you hate knowledge? ²³ Turn back to my reproof; behold, I will pour out my spirit to you, I will make my words known to you. ²⁴ Because I called and you refused, I stretched out my hand and no one paid attention; ²⁵ you ignored all my counsel and would not accept my reproof. ²⁶ I too will laugh at your disaster; I will mock when fear comes upon you. ²⁷ When fear comes upon you like a storm, and your distress arrives like a whirlwind, when your troubles come and your calamity descends, ²⁸ then they will call to me, but I will not answer; they will seek me diligently but will not find me. ²⁹ For they hated knowledge and did not choose the fear of the LORD. ³⁰ They did not want my counsel; they despised all my reproof. ³¹ Therefore they will eat the fruit of their own way, and be filled with the consequences of their own schemes. ³² For the return of the simple will destroy them, and the ease of the fools will destroy them. ³³ But whoever listens to me will dwell securely and be at ease, without fear of evil.

---

Notes
- v. 14: "one purse" is a literal translation of "כִּיס אֶחָד" which metaphorically means "shared resources or common wealth."
- v. 32: "the return of the simple" is a translation of "מְשׁוּבַת פְּתָיִם," where "return" is used in the sense of their eventual downfall or reckoning.
- v. 32: "the ease of the fools" is a translation of "שַׁלְוַת כְּסִילִים," where "ease" conveys a false sense of security leading to destruction.
