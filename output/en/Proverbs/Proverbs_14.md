# Proverbs 14

¹ A wise woman builds her house, but a foolish woman with her hands tears it down. ² One who walks uprightly fears the LORD, but the one whose ways are crooked is despised. ³ The mouth of a fool is a rod of pride, but the lips of the wise preserve them. ⁴ Without oxen, the barn is empty, but with many calves, the strength of the bull is evident. ⁵ A faithful witness does not lie, but a false witness is a fool. ⁶ A scoffer seeks wisdom and finds none, but knowledge is easy for the discerning. ⁷ Turn away from a fool, and you will not find knowledge of wisdom in his lips. ⁸ The wisdom of the prudent understands their path, but the foolishness of fools is deception. ⁹ Fools mock at guilt, but among the upright there is favor. ¹⁰ The heart of the one who knows will feel the bitterness of their soul, and a stranger does not share in their joy. ¹¹ The house of the wicked will be destroyed, but the tent of the upright will flourish. ¹² There is a path that seems right to a person, but its end is the way of death. ¹³ Even in laughter the heart aches, and the end of joy is sorrow. ¹⁴ From their ways, the one with a greedy heart will be satisfied, and from their own schemes, a good person will live. ¹⁵ A fool believes every word, but the discerning one watches their step. ¹⁶ A wise person fears and turns from evil, but a fool is obstinate and full of confidence. ¹⁷ One quick-tempered acts foolishly, and a man of schemes is hated. ¹⁸ Fools inherit foolishness, but the discerning ones acquire knowledge. ¹⁹ The wicked will slide in their downfall before the good, and the wicked will dwell at the gate of the righteous. ²⁰ A friend of the poor is hated, but many are the friends of the rich. ²¹ One who despises their neighbor sins, but the one who honors the humble is blessed. ²² Do not the plotters of evil walk astray? But those who plant good with kindness and truth will find it. ²³ In every labor there is a surplus, but in idle talk, only want. ²⁴ The crown of the wise is their wisdom, but the folly of fools is folly. ²⁵ A true witness delivers lives, but a false witness is a deceiver. ²⁶ In the fear of the LORD is strength, and it will be a refuge for his children. ²⁷ The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, turning one away from the snares of death. ²⁸ When the people are many, the glory of a king is great, but when the people are few, the ruler is ruined. ²⁹ One who is slow to anger has great understanding, but one who is quick-tempered stirs up folly. ³⁰ A calm heart is the life of the flesh, but a broken spirit dries up the bones. ³¹ One who oppresses the poor insults their Maker, but one who honors the poor shows kindness to the needy. ³² In his own ruin, the wicked is driven out, but the righteous finds refuge in death. ³³ In the heart of the wise, wisdom rests, but in the heart of fools, it is hidden. ³⁴ Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a disgrace to the peoples. ³⁵ The desire of a king is for a discerning servant, and his folly will bring disgrace.

---

Notes
- v. 12: The reading "the way of death" reflects the Hebrew phrase דַּרְכֵי־מָוֶת, which is rendered as a contrast to the path that seems right.
- v. 19: The phrase "the wicked will dwell at the gate of the righteous" reflects the Hebrew שַׁעֲרֵי צַדִּֽיק, literally "gates of the righteous," used metaphorically to mean their presence or authority.
- v. 34: The translation "a disgrace to the peoples" for חַטָּֽאת aligns with its use here as a noun meaning "sin," used in the context of the moral failure of nations.
- v. 22: The phrase "those who plant good with kindness and truth will find it" is a paraphrase of the Hebrew, which is more metaphorical and poetic in style.
