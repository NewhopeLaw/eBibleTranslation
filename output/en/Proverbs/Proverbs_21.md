# Proverbs 21

¹ The heart of a king is like streams of water, in the hand of the LORD. In every direction he desires, he turns it. ² Every way a person seems just in their own eyes, but the LORD weighs the hearts. ³ Doing righteousness and justice is more pleasing to the LORD than a sacrifice. ⁴ A proud look and a haughty heart are a lamp for the wicked — their sin is glaring. ⁵ Plans are like a net to the one who is ready, and every wise man is ready for abundance. ⁶ One who makes false promises with their tongue pursues emptiness — seekers of death are those who chase it. ⁷ The spoil of the wicked brings ruin, for they refuse to do justice. ⁸ The crooked paths are twisted, but the upright man’s deeds are straight. ⁹ It is better to dwell on the roof of a house than to share a house with a quarrelsome woman and her strife. ¹⁰ A wicked person’s soul desires evil — in their eyes, no one is innocent. ¹¹ The punishment of a scoffer teaches a fool, and the discerning man gains knowledge. ¹² The discerning man knows the heart of the wicked — and turns the wicked to evil. ¹³ One who stops their ears to the cry of the poor — they too will cry out, but no one will answer. ¹⁴ A gift given in secret calms anger, and a bribe in the bosom brings strong wrath. ¹⁵ Joy to the righteous in doing justice, and a curse to the workers of iniquity. ¹⁶ A man who wanders from the path of understanding will rest in the assembly of the dead. ¹⁷ A man who is poor loves mirth — he who loves wine and oil will not be rich. ¹⁸ A ransom for the righteous is the wicked, and a substitute for the upright is the deceiver. ¹⁹ It is better to live in the wilderness than with a quarrelsome and angry woman. ²⁰ A treasure is precious, and oil in the dwelling of the wise — but fools consume it. ²¹ One who pursues righteousness and steadfast love will find life — righteousness and honor. ²² A wise man climbs the walls of strong cities and brings down the fortress in which the wicked had trusted. ²³ One who guards their mouth and their tongue guards their life from troubles. ²⁴ A scoffer who is arrogant — his name is Zed, and he acts through deceit. ²⁵ The desires of the lazy will kill them, for their hands refuse to work. ²⁶ The wicked crave all day long for evil, but the righteous gives and does not withhold. ²⁷ The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination — for they bring it with deceit. ²⁸ A false witness will be destroyed, but the man who listens faithfully will speak. ²⁹ A wicked man dares with his face, but the upright man understands his way. ³⁰ There is no wisdom, no understanding, no counsel against the LORD. ³¹ A horse is prepared for the day of battle — but salvation is from the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "nir reshaim chata’at" — the phrase is ambiguous. The rendering assumes "a lamp for the wicked — their sin is glaring," where "nir" is a lamp and "reshaim" the wicked.
- v. 24: "zad yahir leitz shemo" — "Zed" is a name possibly derived from "zad" (deceit) or an otherwise unattested term; the name remains speculative but is rendered as "Zed" to preserve the Hebrew nuance.
- v. 27: "zavach resha'im to'evah ki be-zimah yevi'enu" — "the sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination — for they bring it with deceit." "Be-zimah" is a rare term, meaning "with deceit" or "in a conspiracy."
