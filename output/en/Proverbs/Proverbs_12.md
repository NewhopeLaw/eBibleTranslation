# Proverbs 12

¹ One who loves discipline loves knowledge, but one who hates reproof is a fool. ² A person who finds favor is better than one who seeks to please the Lord, but a schemer is condemned. ³ No one is established through wickedness, but the roots of the righteous remain firm. ⁴ A woman of valor is a crown to her husband, but a woman who brings shame is like rot in her own flesh. ⁵ The plans of the righteous produce justice, but the schemes of the wicked are deceit. ⁶ The words of the wicked are traps of blood, but the mouth of the upright delivers them. ⁷ When the wicked are turned away, they vanish, but the house of the righteous stands. ⁸ A person will be praised according to their wisdom, but a heart devoid of understanding will be despised. ⁹ Better is a poor man who has his own servant than a fool who boasts of wealth and lacks food. ¹⁰ The righteous know the soul of their animals, but the mercy of the wicked is cruel. ¹¹ One who tills their land will be satisfied with food, but the soul of the lazy is a wasteland. ¹² The wicked desire what is unjust, but the root of the righteous brings forth fruit. ¹³ With the sin of their words, the wicked are ensnared, but the righteous escape from trouble. ¹⁴ From the fruit of a person’s mouth, their life is filled with good, and the reward of their hands will return to them. ¹⁵ A fool’s way seems right in their own eyes, but the one who listens to counsel is wise. ¹⁶ A fool reveals their anger on the same day, but a wise person conceals disgrace. ¹⁷ One who declares loyalty speaks what is just, but a witness of lies is a deceiver. ¹⁸ There are words like the pricks of a sword, but the tongue of the wise brings healing. ¹⁹ A truthful tongue endures forever, but a lying tongue is only momentary. ²⁰ Deceit is in the heart of those who plot evil, but those who plan peace are filled with joy. ²¹ No evil will befall the righteous, but the wicked are filled with evil. ²² The Lord detests lying lips, but He delights in those who act faithfully. ²³ A discerning person guards knowledge, but the heart of fools reveals folly. ²⁴ The hand of the diligent rules, but the lazy will be made a slave. ²⁵ A troubled heart weighs down a person, but a good word brings joy. ²⁶ The righteous are more precious than a friend, but the way of the wicked leads to ruin. ²⁷ A lazy hand does not achieve its aim, but the wealth of a diligent person is precious. ²⁸ In the path of righteousness is life, but the way of the wicked leads to death.

---

Notes  
- v. 4: "a crown to her husband" and "rot in her own flesh" reflect the metaphorical structure in the Hebrew.  
- v. 11: "a wasteland" (Hebrew רֵיקִים) is a poetic way to render "emptiness" or "futility" in the context of laziness.  
- v. 26: The Greek version adds "will pursue evil" in the second clause, but the Hebrew only says "their way leads to ruin," which is reflected in the translation.  
- v. 28: The final couplet echoes the structure of Proverbs 11:19 and is rendered to preserve that symmetry in meaning and tone.
