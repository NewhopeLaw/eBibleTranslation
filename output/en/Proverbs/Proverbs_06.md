# Proverbs 6

¹ My son, if you have become surety for your neighbor, you have given your hand to a stranger. ² You have ensnared yourself by the words of your mouth, you have been caught by the words of your mouth. ³ Do this, my son, and be rescued: because you have come into the grasp of your friend. Go, be bold and rouse your friend who trusted you. ⁴ Do not let sleep fall on your eyes, nor slumber on your eyelids. ⁵ Rescue yourself, like a gazelle from the hand, like a bird from the hand of the fowler. ⁶ Go to the ant, you lazy one, observe her ways and become wise. ⁷ She has no master, no overseer or ruler. ⁸ She prepares her food in the summer, she gathers her provisions in the harvest. ⁹ How long will you lie down, you lazy one? When will you rise from your sleep? ¹⁰ A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to lie down. ¹¹ Then poverty will come to you like a wanderer, and need like one armed. ¹² A man of no sense walks along with iniquity, his mouth speaks perverse things. ¹³ His glance he winks, with his foot he signals; with his fingers he gestures. ¹⁴ Deceit is in his heart, he plots evil constantly, spreading strife. ¹⁵ Therefore sudden destruction will come upon him, and a break without remedy. ¹⁶ These six things the Lord hates, and seven are an abomination to his soul. ¹⁷ Haughty eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood, ¹⁸ a heart that plots evil, feet that run to do evil, ¹⁹ a false witness who speaks lies, and one who stirs up strife among brothers. ²⁰ My son, keep your father’s command, and do not forsake your mother’s teaching. ²¹ Let them remain bound on your heart always; let them be tied around your neck. ²² When you walk, it will guide you, when you sleep it will guard you, and when you wake it will speak with you. ²³ For a lamp is the command, and the teaching is light, and the reproofs of discipline are the way of life. ²⁴ To keep you from an evil woman, from the smooth tongue of a foreigner. ²⁵ Do not let your heart be drawn to her beauty, do not let her eyes ensnare you. ²⁶ For the price of a prostitute is as much as a single loaf, but a woman who takes a man’s life will hunt him. ²⁷ Can a man carry fire in his bosom and his clothes not be burned? ²⁸ Or can one walk on hot coals and his feet not be scorched? ²⁹ So is the one who goes to a married woman; he will not remain unpunished. No one who touches her will go free. ³⁰ It is not considered a disgrace for a thief to steal, if he steals to satisfy his hunger. ³¹ But if he is caught, he will pay sevenfold; he will give all the wealth of his house. ³² The one who commits adultery is a foolish heart; he who destroys himself will do so. ³³ He will find wounds and disgrace, and his shame will not be wiped out. ³⁴ For a man’s jealousy is a burning fire, and he will not show mercy on the day of reckoning. ³⁵ He will not take a bribe at all, nor will he be appeased, though many gifts are offered.

---

Notes
- v. 16: "These six things the Lord hates, and seven are an abomination to his soul" — the structure implies a list of seven, but the text says six "things" and seven "abominations," which is a minor textual ambiguity. The translation follows the text as written.
