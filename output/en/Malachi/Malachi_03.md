# Malachi 3

¹ Behold, I am sending my messenger to prepare the way before me, and suddenly the Lord you are seeking will come to his temple, the messenger of the covenant you desire. Behold, he is coming, says the LORD of Hosts. ² But who can endure the day of his coming? Who can stand when he appears? For he is like a refiner's fire and a launderer's soap. ³ He will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver, purifying the sons of Levi and refining them like gold and silver, so they can offer a righteous offering to the LORD. ⁴ Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to the LORD, as in the days of old and in previous years. ⁵ I will come near to you for judgment, and I will be a swift witness against those who use sorcery, those who commit adultery, those who swear falsely, those who oppress laborers, those who mistreat widows, orphans, and foreigners, and those who do not fear me, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁶ For I am the LORD, I have not changed. You, the children of Jacob, have not perished. ⁷ From the days of your ancestors, you have turned from my statutes and have not kept them. Return to me, and I will return to you, says the LORD of Hosts. But you ask, In what way should we return? ⁸ Do people rob God? Yet you are robbing me. And you ask, In what way are we robbing you? In tithes and offerings. ⁹ You are cursed with a curse, for you are robbing me, the whole people of you. ¹⁰ Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, so there will be food in my house. Test me now in this, says the LORD of Hosts. If you do not open the windows of heaven and pour out a blessing so great it cannot be measured, ¹¹ I will rebuke the devourer for you, and it will not destroy the fruit of your land. The vine in the field will not wither, says the LORD of Hosts. ¹² All nations will call you blessed, for you will be a land that is desired, says the LORD of Hosts. ¹³ Your words have been harsh against me, says the LORD. And you ask, What have we said against you? ¹⁴ You say, It is useless to serve God. What gain is there in keeping his requirements, or in walking with reverence before the LORD of Hosts? ¹⁵ But now we consider the arrogant blessed. Indeed, those who commit evil build themselves up, and they defy God and escape unscathed. ¹⁶ Then those who fear the LORD spoke to one another, and the LORD listened and heard. He recorded a book of remembrance for those who fear the LORD and for those who honor his name. ¹⁷ They will belong to me, says the LORD of Hosts, on the day I act. I will spare them, as a father spares a son who serves him. ¹⁸ Then you will see a distinction between the righteous and the wicked, between those who serve God and those who do not. ¹⁹ For behold, the day is coming, burning like a furnace, when all the arrogant and every evildoer will be like stubble. The coming day will burn them up, says the LORD of Hosts, leaving them neither root nor branch. ²⁰ But for those who fear my name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings. And you will go out, leaping like calves from the stall. ²¹ You will trample the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet on the day I act, says the LORD of Hosts. ²² Remember the instruction of my servant Moses, the laws and decrees I gave him at Horeb for all Israel. ²³ Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the great and awesome day of the LORD comes. ²⁴ He will turn the hearts of the fathers toward their children and the hearts of the children toward their fathers, otherwise I will come and strike the land with a curse.

---

Notes
- v. 7: "from the days of your ancestors" — the Hebrew uses "לְמִימֵי אֲבֹתֵיכֶם" which is a poetic construction meaning "from the days of your ancestors."
- v. 24: "turn the hearts" — the Hebrew uses a poetic parallelism that is best rendered as a reciprocal turning.
