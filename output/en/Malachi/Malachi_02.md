# Malachi 2

¹ And now, this command is for you, the priests. ² If you do not listen, and if you do not place it in your heart to give glory to my name, says the LORD of Hosts, I will send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings, and I will curse them, because you have not placed it in your heart. ³ I will rebuke your seed, and I will scatter dung upon your faces, dung from your feasts, and you will be carried away with it. ⁴ And you will know that I have sent this command to you, to make a covenant with Levi, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁵ My covenant was with him for life and peace, and I gave him fear, and he feared me, and from my name he held back reverence. ⁶ The teaching of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found in his lips; in peace and in uprightness he walked with me, and many he turned back from iniquity. ⁷ For the lips of a priest should keep knowledge, and the law should be sought from his mouth, for he is an angel of the LORD of Hosts. ⁸ But you have turned aside from the way, and you have caused many to stumble in the law, you have destroyed the covenant of Levi, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁹ And I will make you despised and abased before all the people, according to the extent that you do not keep my ways and take faces in the law. ¹⁰ Is not one father to us all? Is not one God who created us? Why then have we betrayed one another, to profane the covenant of our ancestors? ¹¹ Judah has betrayed, and an abomination has been done in Israel and in Jerusalem, for Judah has profaned the holy things of the LORD, the ones he loved and took as a wife, a foreign woman. ¹² The LORD will cut off the man who does this, from the tents of Jacob, and from those who present offerings to the LORD of Hosts. ¹³ And this you do again: you cover the altar of the LORD with tears, with weeping and groaning, from the weariness of your hands. Will I look upon the offering and accept it from your hands? ¹⁴ And you say, "Why?" Because the LORD has testified between you and your wife of your youth, whom you have betrayed, and she is your companion and your wife of the covenant. ¹⁵ And not one did he make, but one who was left with his spirit. And what is the one seeking? The offspring of God. And you, keep your spirit, and do not betray your wife of your youth. ¹⁶ For the one who sends her away, says the LORD, the God of Israel, and covers his garment with violence, says the LORD of Hosts. Keep your spirit, and do not betray. ¹⁷ You have wearied the LORD with your words, and you say, "In what way have we wearied him?" By saying, "Everyone who does evil is good in the eyes of the LORD, and in them he delights." Where is the God of justice?

---

Notes
- v. 11: The phrase "a foreign woman" in reference to "a daughter of a foreign god" is an interpretive rendering based on the context of Judah's covenant violation.
- v. 15: The reading of "not one did he make, but one who was left with his spirit" interprets the Hebrew as referring to God's creation of one human and the unity of marriage.
