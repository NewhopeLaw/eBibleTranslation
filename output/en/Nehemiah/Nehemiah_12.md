Notes
- v. 25: "Mattanyah, Bakbukiyyah, Ovadyah, Meshulam, Talmon, and Akuv were gatekeepers, keeping the watches at the gates" — the Hebrew lists them as gatekeepers with their roles, and the structure is clearly a list of names followed by their shared role.
- v. 35–36: The genealogy of Zakaryah and his brothers' involvement in the songs is presented as a continuation, not as separate clauses, so the translation is structured accordingly.
- v. 47: "a daily portion each day" — the Hebrew uses a double expression of daily to emphasize the regularity, hence the repetition in English.
