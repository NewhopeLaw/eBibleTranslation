# Nehemiah 3

¹ Eliashib the high priest, along with his fellow priests, rebuilt the Sheep Gate. They dedicated it, and they set up its doors, and they dedicated it up to the Tower of the Hundred, and up to the Tower of Hananel. ² Next to them, the men of Jericho built. Next to them, Zaccur son of Imri built. ³ The Fish Gate was built by the descendants of Hassenuah. They covered it, and they set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars. ⁴ Next to them, Meremoth son of Uzzi the son of Harha built, next to them Meshullam son of Berechiah the son of Meshezabel built, next to them Zadok son of Baana built. ⁵ Next to them, the Tekoites built, and their leaders did not press their necks into the service of their master. ⁶ The Old Gate was rebuilt by Joiada son of Pasheah and Meshullam son of Besodiah. They covered it, and they set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars. ⁷ Next to them, Melatiah the Gibeonite and Jadon the Meronothite, along with the men of Gibeon and Mizpah, rebuilt for the seat of the governor across the river. ⁸ Next to them, Uzziel son of Harhania the goldsmith built, and next to them Hananiah son of the goldsmith rebuilt. They extended to Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall. ⁹ Next to them, Rephaiah son of Hur, the ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, rebuilt. ¹⁰ Next to him, Jediah son of Harumaph rebuilt, in front of his house, and next to him, Hattush son of Hashabneiah rebuilt. ¹¹ A second section was rebuilt by Malchijah son of Harim and Hashub son of Mattan of Moab, up to the Tower of the Furnaces. ¹² Next to him, Shallum son of Halohesh, the ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, rebuilt, he and his daughters. ¹³ The Valley Gate was rebuilt by Hanun and the men of Zanoah. They rebuilt it and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars, and they built a thousand cubits of the wall to the Dung Gate. ¹⁴ The Dung Gate was rebuilt by Malchijah son of Rekab, the ruler of the district of Beth Haccerem. He and his sons rebuilt it. They covered it, and they set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars. ¹⁵ The Fountain Gate was rebuilt by Shalun son of Kallash, the ruler of the district of Mizpah. He rebuilt it and its roof, and he set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars, along with the wall of the Fish Pool, as far as the steps descending from the city of David. ¹⁶ After him, Nehemiah son of Azbuk, the ruler of half the district of Beth Zur, rebuilt to the place opposite the tombs of David, to the artificial pool, and to the House of the Heroes. ¹⁷ After him, the Levites, Rehum son of Bani, rebuilt. Next to him, Bashaiah son of Mattaniah, the ruler of half the district of Keilah, rebuilt. ¹⁸ After him, their kinsmen, Binnui son of Hinnom, the ruler of half the district of Keilah, rebuilt. ¹⁹ Then next to him, Ezer son of Jeshua, the ruler of the district of Mizpah, rebuilt from the wall opposite the projecting tower, a second section. ²⁰ After him, Baruch son of Zabbai rebuilt from the projecting tower to the entrance of the house of Eliashib the high priest. ²¹ After him, Meremoth son of Uriah son of Koz rebuilt from the entrance of Eliashib's house to the end of Eliashib's house. ²² After him, the priests, the men of the district, rebuilt. ²³ After them, Benjamin and Hashub rebuilt in front of their house. After them, Azariah son of Maaseiah son of Ananiah rebuilt near his own house. ²⁴ After him, Binuiah son of Henadad rebuilt from the house of Azariah to the turning point, to the corner. ²⁵ Palal son of Uzzai began at the turning point and the projecting tower that comes out from the king's upper house at the court of the guard. After him, Pedaiah son of Parosh rebuilt. ²⁶ The Nethinim lived on the mound, as far as the entrance of the Water Gate toward the east, and the projecting tower. ²⁷ After him, the Tekoites rebuilt a second section from opposite the great projecting tower to the wall of the Ophel. ²⁸ Above the Horse Gate, the priests, each one in front of his own house, rebuilt. ²⁹ After him, Zadok son of Immer rebuilt in front of his own house. After him, Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, the gatekeeper of the East, rebuilt. ³⁰ After him, Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun the sixth, rebuilt a second section. After him, Meshullam son of Berechiah rebuilt opposite his private room. ³¹ After him, Malkijah son of Harapim rebuilt to the house of the Nethinim and the chamber of the guards, opposite the Gate of the Watch, as far as the projecting tower. ³² Between the projecting tower and the Sheep Gate, the metalworkers and the merchants rebuilt. ³³ When Sanballat heard that we were rebuilding the wall, he was very angry. He was furious and mocked the Jews. ³⁴ He said in front of his brothers and the army of Samaria, "What are these feeble Jews doing? Do they think they will succeed? Will they sacrifice? Will they finish in a day? Will they bring back life to these stones from the heaps of dust? These are burned bricks!" ³⁵ Tobiah the Ammonite was with him, and he said, "What they are building—if a fox comes up, it will tear down their stone wall." ³⁶ "Hear, our God, how we are mocked, and return their disgrace on their own heads and give them over to be mocked in the land of captivity. ³⁷ And do not cover their sins or their iniquities before you, for they have provoked you in the presence of the builders." ³⁸ So the wall was built, and the entire wall was joined together to half its height, and the people had a heart to do it.

Notes
- v. 1: "dedicated it" (קִדְּשׁוּהוּ) reflects the act of setting the gate apart for sacred or official use.
- v. 10: "Hattush" (חַטּוּשׁ) is a transliteration of the Hebrew name.
- v. 15: "the wall of the Fish Pool" refers to the area surrounding the pool, likely related to the city's water supply.
- v. 34: "feeble Jews" (עַמְּלוּלִים) is a Hebrew term meaning "laborers" or "feeble ones" and is used here in a derogatory sense.
- v. 35: "fox" (שׁוּעָל) is used metaphorically to imply the wall is easily destroyed.
