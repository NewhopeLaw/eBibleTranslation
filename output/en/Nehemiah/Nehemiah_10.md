# Nehemiah 10

¹ And in all this we made a covenant and wrote, and on the seal were our leaders, our Levites, our priests: ² and on the seals Nehemiah the Tirshatha, son of Hachaliah, and Zadok: ³ Seraiah, Azaryah, Jeremiah: ⁴ Pashhur, Amarayah, Malkiyah: ⁵ Hattush, Shubanyahu, Malukh: ⁶ Harem, Meremot, Obadyah: ⁷ Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch: ⁸ Meshullam, Abiyah, Maiman: ⁹ Meazeyah, Bilgai, Shemaiah—these are the priests. ¹⁰ And the Levites, Jesuah the son of Azanyah, Binnui of the sons of Henadad, Kadmiel: ¹¹ and their brothers, Shebanyahu, Hodiyah, Kaliyth, Palaiyahu, Hanan: ¹² Michai, Rehob, Hashavbyah: ¹³ Zachur, Sherevbyah, Shebanyahu: ¹⁴ Hodiyah, Bani, Bininu: ¹⁵ the leaders of the people, Parosh, Pachath-Moab, Elam, Zatthu, Bani: ¹⁶ Bunni, Azgad, Bebai: ¹⁷ Adoniya, Bigwai, Adin: ¹⁸ Atter, Hizkiyah, Azur: ¹⁹ Hodiyah, Hashum, Betzay: ²⁰ Charif, Anathoth, Nibai: ²¹ Magpia, Meshullam, Hezir: ²² Meshizabai, Tzadok, Yadua: ²³ Phaltiyah, Hanan, Anayah: ²⁴ Hoshai, Hananyahu, Hashov: ²⁵ Halosh, Pelchaya, Shovek: ²⁶ Ruhum, Hashavnah, Meaaseiyah: ²⁷ and Ahiyah, Hanan, Anan: ²⁸ Malukh, Harem, Baanah: ²⁹ and the rest of the people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the Nethinim, and all those who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the teaching of the God, their wives, their sons and their daughters, all who knew and understood: ³⁰ strengthening themselves over their brothers, swearing an oath and entering into a curse and an oath to walk in the teaching of the God that was given in the hand of Moses, the servant of God, to keep and do all the commandments of our Lord and His judgments and His statutes: ³¹ and that we did not give our daughters to the peoples of the land, and we did not take their daughters for our sons. ³² And the peoples of the land who brought their goods and all their wares on the day of the Sabbath to sell, we did not buy from them on the Sabbath or on the holy day. We also let the sabbatical year rest, and we did not demand from any hand. ³³ And we made decrees upon ourselves to give a third of the shekel each year for the service of the house of our God: ³⁴ for the bread of the Presence, and for the continual offering, and for the continual burnt offering of the Sabbaths, the new moons, the appointed feasts, and the holy things, and for the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and for all the work of the house of our God. ³⁵ And we cast lots for the wood offering, the priests, the Levites, and the people, to bring to the house of our God, to the house of our ancestors, at set times each year to burn on the altar of our Lord God, as it is written in the Torah: ³⁶ and to bring the firstfruits of our land and the firstfruits of every tree each year to the house of the Lord: ³⁷ and the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as it is written in the Torah, and the firstlings of our cattle and of our sheep to bring to the house of our God to the priests who serve in the house of our God: ³⁸ and the first of our grain offerings and our tithes, and the fruit of every tree, new wine and oil, to the priests, to the rooms of the house of our God, and the tithe of our land to the Levites, and the Levites tithed in all the cities of our service. ³⁹ And the priest, the son of Aaron, was with the Levites in the Levitical tithe, and the Levites brought up the tithe of the tithe to the house of our God, to the storehouses of the house of God. ⁴⁰ For the sons of Israel and the Levites brought into the storehouses the tithes of grain, wine, and oil, and there the vessels of the sanctuary and the priests who served, and the gatekeepers and the singers; and we did not leave the house of our God.

Notes
- v. 1: "covenant" is a rendering of "אֲמָנָה" (amanah), meaning "covenant" or "agreement."
- v. 29: "knowing and understanding" is a translation of "יוֹדֵעַ מֵבִֽין" (yodea mevin), emphasizing both knowledge and comprehension.
- v. 31: "peoples of the land" is a general term for the surrounding non-Israelite nations.
- v. 32: "third of the shekel" refers to the standard weight unit used in Israelite religious offerings.
- v. 34: "work of the house of our God" is a general term for all temple-related duties and expenses.
- v. 35: "wood offering" refers to the Levitical duty of providing wood for the temple altar.
- v. 38: "rooms of the house of our God" refers to storage areas within the temple complex.
- v. 40: "vessels of the sanctuary" includes all sacred items used in temple worship.
