# Nehemiah 6

¹ When Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and our other enemies heard that I had built the wall, and that no crack remained in it—not even the gates had I put in place at that time— ² they sent to me, saying, “Come, let us meet together at the villages in the open valley.” And they were plotting to do me harm. ³ So I sent messengers to them, saying, “I am doing a great work, and I cannot come down. Why should the work be interrupted while I finish it, and then I would come to you?” ⁴ They sent to me four times in this way, and I answered them in this way each time. ⁵ Then Sanballat sent to me a fifth time, with an open letter in his servant’s hand. ⁶ Written in it was: “It has been heard among the nations, and Gesham says, ‘You and the Jews are planning rebellion. That is why you are building the wall, and you are becoming their king.’” ⁷ “Also, you have set up prophets to speak over you in Jerusalem, saying, ‘You are a king over Judah.’ And now the king will hear these things. Now come, let us consult together.” ⁸ I sent to him in reply, “These are not the things that have happened, as you claim. You are lying from your own heart.” ⁹ For all of them are trying to intimidate us, saying, “Their hands will fall away from the work, and it will not be done.” But now I have strengthened my hands. ¹⁰ I went into the house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah the son of Mehetabel, who was a priest and was locked in. He said, “Let us meet in the house of God, in the inner court, and we will close the doors of the house, because they are coming to kill you at night.” ¹¹ I said, “What man is there who would go into the temple and live? I will not go in.” ¹² I realized, and saw that it was not from God, for the prophecy was a lie. Tobiah and Sanballat had paid him. ¹³ For they had hired him to frighten me and make me do this, so I could sin and they would have a bad name for me to disgrace me. ¹⁴ Remember, O my God, Tobiah and Sanballat for these things they did, and also Noahad the prophet, and the other prophets who were trying to intimidate me. ¹⁵ The wall was completed on the twenty-fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty-two days. ¹⁶ When our enemies heard about it, and all the surrounding nations saw, they fell greatly in their eyes, and they recognized that this work had been done by our God. ¹⁷ In those days many letters were sent among the people of Judah to Tobiah, and Tobiah’s letters came to them. ¹⁸ For many in Judah were bound by oath to him, since he was a relative of Shecaniah the son of Arah, and Johanan his son had taken the daughter of Meshullam the son of Berechiah as a wife. ¹⁹ And Tobiah’s friends were saying in my presence, and my words were being carried to him. Tobiah sent letters to intimidate me.

---

Notes  
- v. 10: "was locked in" — the Hebrew uses a rare verb (עָצוּר), which literally means "locked in." The context suggests Shemaiah was in a secured or secluded place.  
- v. 15: "the twenty-fifth day of the month Elul" — the Hebrew calendar term is rendered directly; "fifty-two days" refers to the total time taken to build the wall.  
- v. 14: "Noahad the prophet" — the name is from the Greek, as the Hebrew is unclear; the Septuagint is followed here.
