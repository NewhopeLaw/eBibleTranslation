# Nehemiah 7

¹ When the wall was completed, I set up the doors, and the gatekeepers, the singers, and the Levites were assigned their duties. ² I gave instructions to Hanani my brother and to Hananiah the commander of the fortress in Jerusalem, because he was a man of truth and one who feared God more than many. ³ I told them, “The gates of Jerusalem must not be opened until the heat of the sun has passed. While they are still on guard, let the doors be shut and fastened. Assign watchmen to the residents of Jerusalem, each man at his post and each man opposite his house.” ⁴ The city was wide and large, but the people were few within it, and there were no houses built. ⁵ My God put it into my heart to gather the nobles and the officials and the people to consult, and I found the book of the register of those who had come up at first. I found it written in it: ⁶ “These are the people from the land who came up from the captivity of the exile, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had taken away. They returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to his city. ⁷ Those who came with Zerubbabel were Jeshua, Nehemiah, Azaryah, Rammah, Nahamani, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispereth, Bezeth, and Nahum, the sons of So they were excluded from the priesthood. ⁶⁵ And the Tirshatha said to them that they must not eat of the most holy things until a priest arises to consult with Urim and Thummim. ⁶⁶ The whole assembly was one: four thousand, two hundred, and sixty. ⁶⁷ In addition to their servants and their maids, these were seven thousand three hundred thirty-seven, and their singers and their singing women: two hundred forty-five. ⁶⁸ The camels: four hundred thirty-five. The donkeys: six thousand seven hundred twenty. ⁶⁹ Some of the heads of the fathers gave to the work. The Tirshatha gave to the treasury gold bowls, one thousand, silver minas fifty, priestly garments three hundred and fifty. ⁷⁰ And from the heads of the fathers gave to the treasury of the work gold bowls two hundred and silver minas two thousand two hundred. ⁷¹ And the rest of the people gave to the treasury of the work gold bowls two thousand and silver minas two thousand, and priestly garments sixty-seven. ⁷² The priests and the Levites and the gatekeepers and the singers and the people and the Nethinim and all Israel settled in their cities. The month of the seventh month arrived, and the people of Israel were in their cities.

Notes  
- v. 2: "Hananiah the commander of the fortress" — "commander of the fortress" is a literal translation of *shar habirah*; other translations may use "captain of the guard" or similar.  
- v. 64: "they were excluded from the priesthood" — the Hebrew uses *va-yiggalu* (וַיְגֹֽאֲלוּ), which can mean "were removed" or "were excluded."  
- v. 65: "Urim and Thummim" — a sacred lot or means of divine consultation, preserved in the priestly breastplate.  
- v. 69-71: "gold bowls" — the Hebrew term is *zarad* (זָרַד), often translated as "golden bowls" or "golden vessels." The reference to "minas" is to ancient weight units.  
- v. 72: "the month of the seventh month" — refers to the Hebrew month of Tishri, which is the seventh month in the religious calendar and marks the beginning of the Jewish New Year.
