# Nehemiah 11

¹ The leaders of the people settled in Jerusalem, while the rest of the people cast lots, one in ten to live in Jerusalem, the holy city, and nine parts in the towns. ² The people blessed all the men who volunteered to live in Jerusalem. ³ These are the leaders of the land who settled in Jerusalem and in the towns of Judah, each man in his inheritance, among his cities: Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the temple servants, and the descendants of Solomon's servants. ⁴ In Jerusalem, from the sons of Judah and the sons of Benjamin, from the sons of Judah: Ataiyahu, son of Uziyahu, son of Zekaryah, son of Amarayah, son of Sefatayah, son of Mahalalel, from the sons of Perez. ⁵ And Mattanyah, son of Baruch, son of Kallakhzeh, son of Hozeh, son of Adayah, son of Yoyariv, son of Tzakaryah, son of Hasloni. ⁶ All the sons of Perez living in Jerusalem were four hundred and sixty-eight men of valor. ⁷ From the sons of Benjamin: Salau, son of Meshulam, son of Yo'ad, son of Pedayah, son of Kolaiah, son of Mattanyah, son of Ityael, son of Yeshaya. ⁸ And after him: Gabay, Salay, nine hundred and twenty-eight. ⁹ Yohel, son of Zekri, was their overseer, and Yehuda, son of Hasnuah, second in command over the city. ¹⁰ From the priests: Yeda'yah, son of Yoyariv, Yakin. ¹¹ Shrayah, son of Hilkiyah, son of Meshulam, son of Tzadok, son of Merayot, son of Akhituv, stood before the house of God. ¹² And their brothers, who worked in the temple, were eight hundred and twenty-two, and Adayah, son of Yeroham, son of Pelalayah, son of Amatsi, son of Zekaryah, son of Paschur, son of Malkiyah. ¹³ Their brothers, the heads of the families, were two hundred and forty-two, and Emashsay, son of Esdrayel, son of Akhazy, son of Meshilmoth, son of Imir. ¹⁴ And their brothers, the men of valor, were one hundred and twenty-eight. Their overseer was Zavdi'el, son of Hagadolim. ¹⁵ From the Levites: Shmayah, son of Hashav, son of Azrikam, son of Hashavvayah, son of Buny. ¹⁶ And Shavtay and Yozvad, over the outer work of the house of God, heads of the Levites. ¹⁷ Mattanyah, son of Mikah, son of Zavdi, son of Asaf, was the head of the beginning, Yehudeh for the prayer, and Bukkuqyah, second among his brothers, and Avdah, son of Shammay, son of Galal, son of Yedutun. ¹⁸ All the Levites in the holy city were two hundred and eighty-four. ¹⁹ The gatekeepers: Akuv, Talmon, and their brothers, who guarded the gates, were one hundred and seventy-two. ²⁰ The rest of the Israelites, the priests and Levites, lived in all the towns of Judah, each man in his inheritance. ²¹ The temple servants lived in Ofel, Tziha, and Gishpa, under the oversight of the temple servants. ²² The Levite overseer in Jerusalem was Uziy, son of Bany, son of Hashavvayah, son of Mattanyah, son of Mikay, from the sons of Asaf, who led the singers in the work of the house of God. ²³ For the king had given them a command and they had a trust with the singers, one day at a time. ²⁴ Pathaiyahu, son of Meshizab'el, from the sons of Zera, son of Judah, served at the king's side, handling every matter for the people. ²⁵ In the towns and fields, from the sons of Judah, they settled in Kiryat Arba and its towns, and in Dibon and its towns, and in Biktza'el and its villages. ²⁶ And in Yeshua, and in Moladah, and in Beth Palest. ²⁷ And in the valley of Su'al, and in Beer Sheva and its towns. ²⁸ And in Tsiklag, and in Mekonah and its towns. ²⁹ And in Ein Rimmon, and in Tzera'ah, and in Yarmut. ³⁰ Zanah, Adulam and its villages, Lachish and its fields, Ezeqah and its towns. They encamped from Beer Sheva to the Valley of Hinnom. ³¹ The sons of Benjamin lived in Geba, Mikmash, Aiya, and Beth El and its towns. ³² Anathoth, Nob, Ananyah. ³³ Khazor, Ramah, Gitteyim. ³⁴ Khadid, Tsvo'im, Nevlat. ³⁵ Lod, Ono, the Valley of the Craftsmen. ³⁶ And from the Levites, portions were allocated to Judah for Benjamin.

Notes
- v. 3: "the descendants of Solomon's servants" — the Hebrew "benei avdei Shlomo" is literally "sons of the servants of Solomon," likely referring to temple or royal servants.
- v. 11: "stood before the house of God" — the Hebrew "neged beit ha'elohim" is rendered in context as "stood before the house of God," indicating a role in service.
- v. 22: "from the sons of Asaf" — the Levitical group known for temple music, here indicating their role in leading the singers.
- v. 23: "one day at a time" — the Hebrew "yom be-yomo" is a fixed expression meaning "each day in turn."
- v. 30: "the Valley of Hinnom" — the Hebrew "Gey Hinom" is the standard term for the Valley of Hinnom, a well-known location in Jerusalem.
