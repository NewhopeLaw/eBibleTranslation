# Nehemiah 4

¹ When Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabians, the Ammonites, and the Ashdodites heard that the wall of Jerusalem was being rebuilt and the breaches were being closed, they were very angry. ² They all conspired together to come and fight against Jerusalem, to make it a target for themselves. ³ We prayed to our God and stationed guards around us, day and night, because of them. ⁴ Judah said, "The strength of the burden is heavy, and the dust is numerous. We cannot build the wall." ⁵ Our enemies said, "Let them not know, let them not see, until we come among them and kill them and stop the work." ⁶ When the Jews who lived near us came ten times from all the places, they said to us, "You are turning back against us from every place." ⁷ I stationed men beneath the wall at the breaches, and I set the people in families with their swords, their spears, and their bows. ⁸ I saw and stood up and said to the nobles and to the rulers and to the rest of the people, "Do not fear them. Remember the great and awesome God, and fight for your brothers, your children, your daughters, your wives, and your homes." ⁹ When our enemies heard that it had become known to us, the Lord broke their counsel, and we all returned to the wall, each to his work. ¹⁰ From that day on, half of my servants worked on the task, while the other half held the spears, the shields, the bows, and the armor, with the commanders behind every house of Judah. ¹¹ The builders were working on the wall, and the ones carrying burdens were loading with one hand on the task and holding the shield with the other. ¹² The builders, each one had his sword tied to his side while building, and the one who was beating the trumpet was near me. ¹³ I said to the nobles and to the rulers and to the rest of the people, "The task is great and wide, and we are separated on the wall, far from each other." ¹⁴ "Wherever you hear the sound of the trumpet, there you shall gather to us. Our God will fight for us." ¹⁵ We worked on the task, half of us holding the spears from the rising of the dawn until the stars appeared. ¹⁶ At that time I said to the people, "Let each man and his servant spend the night within Jerusalem, so that they may be a guard for us by night and a task by day." ¹⁷ There was no man or his servant who put off his clothes at night. Each of us kept his hand on his weapon, even when drawing water.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The Hebrew lists the adversaries as "the Arabians and the Ammonites and the Ashdodites" — these are rendered as such to reflect their geographic or ethnic identities.
- v. 11: "the ones carrying burdens" refers to those hauling building materials, not laborers in general.
- v. 14: "Wherever you hear the sound of the trumpet" is a direct translation of the Hebrew, preserving the instruction to regroup upon hearing the trumpet.
- v. 17: "each of us kept his hand on his weapon" is a contextual rendering of the Hebrew "no one took off his clothes" — the phrase is used metaphorically to indicate constant readiness for battle.
