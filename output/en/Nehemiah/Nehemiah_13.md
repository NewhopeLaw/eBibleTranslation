# Nehemiah 13

¹ On that day the Book of Moses was read in the ears of the people, and it was found written in it that an Ammonite or a Moabite should not enter into the assembly of God forever. ² For they did not meet the Israelites with bread or water, and they had hired Balaam to curse them, but our God turned the curse into a blessing. ³ When they heard the law, they separated all the mixed ones from Israel. ⁴ Before this, Eliashib the priest was assigned to the chamber of the house of our God, near Tobiah. ⁵ He made for himself a large storeroom, and there they used to give the grain offering, the frankincense, the vessels, the tithes of the grain, the wine, and the oil, the Levitical command, the singers, the gatekeepers, and the priests' offerings. ⁶ Yet I was not in Jerusalem at that time, for in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes the king of Babylon I went to the king, and after some time, I was granted an audience with the king. ⁷ I came to Jerusalem and discovered the evil that Eliashib had done for Tobiah, making him a storeroom in the courtyard of the house of God. ⁸ It deeply distressed me, and I threw all the household goods of Tobiah out of the storeroom. ⁹ I said, and they cleansed the storerooms, and I returned there the household goods of the house of God, the grain offering, and the frankincense. ¹⁰ I realized that the portions for the Levites had not been given, and the Levites and singers, who performed the service, had fled to their fields. ¹¹ I contended with the officials and said, “Why has the house of God been abandoned?” I gathered them and set them in their posts. ¹² All Judah brought tithes of grain, wine, and oil into the storehouses. ¹³ I assigned Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites to oversee these storehouses. And under them was Hanan son of Zachur son of Mattaniah, for they were considered trustworthy, and they were responsible for distributing to their brothers. ¹⁴ Remember me, O my God, for this, and do not wipe out the steadfast love that I have shown to the house of my God and to its services. ¹⁵ In those days I saw in Judah people treading winepresses on the Sabbath, and bringing in sheaves, loading them on donkeys, and also wine, grapes, and figs, and every kind of burden, and bringing them into Jerusalem on the Sabbath. I testified against them on the day they sold goods. ¹⁶ The Tyrians had settled there, bringing fish and every kind of merchandise and selling them on the Sabbath to the people of Judah and in Jerusalem. ¹⁷ I contended with the people of Judah and said to them, “What evil deed is this that you are doing, profaning the day of the Sabbath?” ¹⁸ Did not your ancestors do the same, and did not our God bring all this evil upon us and upon this city? And you are adding more wrath to Israel by profaning the Sabbath. ¹⁹ When the gates of Jerusalem were about to be closed before the Sabbath, I said, “Let the gates be shut, and do not open them until after the Sabbath.” I stationed my young men at the gates to prevent burdens from coming in on the Sabbath. ²⁰ The traders and sellers of every kind of merchandise stayed outside Jerusalem once or twice. ²¹ I testified against them and said to them, “Why are you lying in wait before the wall? If you do this again, I will lay hands on you.” From that time they did not come in on the Sabbath. ²² I said to the Levites who were being purified and coming in to guard the gates, to sanctify the Sabbath. Remember me, O my God, for this, and have mercy on me according to your great steadfast love. ²³ In those days I saw the Jews taking in Ashdodite, Ammonite, and Moabite women. ²⁴ Their children spoke half the language of Ashdod, and they did not learn to speak the language of Judah, but spoke the language of a people and a nation. ²⁵ I contended with them and cursed them, and I beat some of them and tore their hair, and I swore to them in God, “If you give your daughters to their sons, or if you take their daughters for your sons or yourselves, ²⁶ did not Solomon, the king of Israel, sin in these things? Yet he was not the most numerous of kings, nor was there a king like him beloved of God, and God made him king over all Israel. Even him the foreign women led astray. ²⁷ And you—do you dare to do this great evil, to act defiantly toward our God by taking foreign women?” ²⁸ From the sons of Joiada, son of Eliashib the high priest, was Tobiah’s son-in-law, Sanballat the Horonite. I drove them from me. ²⁹ Remember them, O my God, over the redemption of the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and the Levites. ³⁰ I cleansed them from every foreigner and set watches for the priests and Levites, each in their service. ³¹ For the offering of the wood in appointed times and for the firstfruits, remember me, O my God, for good.

Notes
- v. 14: “steadfast love” is used for חסד, consistent with the glossary.
- v. 19: “did not open them” refers to the gates. The Hebrew is clear, so no ambiguity is present.
- v. 26: “Solomon... sin in these things” uses "in these things" to reflect the context of taking foreign women, which is clear in the text.
- v. 28: “Tobiah’s son-in-law, Sanballat the Horonite” is a direct rendering of the Hebrew, with no ambiguity in the relationship described.
