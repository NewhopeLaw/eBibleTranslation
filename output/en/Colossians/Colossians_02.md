# Colossians 2

¹ I want you to know how much of a struggle I have on your behalf and on behalf of those in Laodicea and of all who have not seen my face in the flesh, ² so that their hearts may be comforted, knit together in love, and overflowing with the riches of full knowledge, to the understanding of the mystery of God, Christ, ³ in whom are all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge hidden. ⁴ I say this so that no one may deceive you with persuasive arguments. ⁵ For even though I am absent in the flesh, I am with you in spirit and I rejoice and see your order and the firmness of your faith in Christ. ⁶ So, just as you received Christ Jesus the Lord, walk in him, ⁷ rooted and built up in him and established in your faith, just as you were taught, overflowing with gratitude. ⁸ See to it that no one deceives you by philosophical arguments and empty deceptions, according to human traditions, according to the elements of the world, and not according to Christ; ⁹ for in him the fullness of the godhead dwells bodily, ¹⁰ and you are complete in him, who is the head of every rule and authority, ¹¹ in whom you were also circumcised with a circumcision not made with hands, by putting off the body of the flesh, by the circumcision of Christ, ¹² having been buried with him in baptism, in which you were also raised with him through faith in the energy of God who raised him from the dead, ¹³ and when you were dead in your trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, he made you alive together with him, forgiving us all our trespasses, ¹⁴ having erased the certificate of debt that stood against us, with its legal demands; this he set aside, nailing it to the cross; ¹⁵ having stripped the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame, triumphing over them in him. ¹⁶ So then, let no one judge you in food or in drink or in respect of a festival or of a new moon or of the sabbaths, ¹⁷ which are a shadow of what is to come, but the substance is of Christ. ¹⁸ Let no one disqualify you, desiring to be humble and to worship the angels, gazing at things that have been seen, puffed up in his mind by his fleshly nature, ¹⁹ and not holding fast to the head, from whom the whole body, through joints and ligaments, is nourished and knit together, growing in the increase of God. ²⁰ If you died with Christ from the elements of the world, why are you still living as if you were in the world? ²¹ “Let no one touch, let no one taste, let no one handle,” ²² referring to all things destined for destruction through human commands and teachings, ²³ which have the appearance of wisdom in self-imposed worship and humility and severity to the body, but are not of value in restraining the desires of the flesh.

---

Notes
- v. 23: The phrase "πλησμονὴς τῆς σαρκός" is translated as "restraining the desires of the flesh," preserving the contrast between external rituals and internal transformation.
