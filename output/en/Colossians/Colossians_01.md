# Colossians 1

¹ Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy the brother, ² to the saints and faithful brothers in Christ who are in Colossae: Grace to you and peace from God our Father. ³ We always thank God the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, praying for you constantly, ⁴ since we have heard of your faith in Christ Jesus and your love for all the saints, ⁵ because of the hope laid up for you in the heavens, which you have already heard in the message of the gospel of truth. ⁶ The gospel is bearing fruit among you and growing, just as it has done in all the world, since the day you heard and understood the grace of God in truth; ⁷ as you have learned from Epaphras, our beloved fellow servant, who is a faithful minister of Christ on our behalf, ⁸ who also made known to us your love in the Spirit. ⁹ For this reason, since the day we heard, we have not stopped praying for you and asking that you may be filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding, ¹⁰ walking in a manner worthy of the Lord, to please him in every good work, bearing fruit and growing in the knowledge of God, ¹¹ strengthened with all power, according to the might of his glory, for every endurance and patience with joy, ¹² giving thanks to the Father who has made you worthy to share in the inheritance of the saints in the light, ¹³ who has rescued us from the authority of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of the Son of his love, ¹⁴ in whom we have redemption, the forgiveness of our sins. ¹⁵ He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation, ¹⁶ for in him all things were created in the heavens and on earth, the visible and the invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities—all things have been created through him and for him. ¹⁷ And he is before all things, and in him all things hold together, ¹⁸ and he is the head of the body, the church; he is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, so that he might become preeminent in all things, ¹⁹ for in him the fullness of God was pleased to dwell, ²⁰ and through him to reconcile to himself all things, whether on earth or in the heavens, by the blood of his cross. ²¹ And you who once were alienated and enemies in your minds because of your evil deeds— ²² yet now he has reconciled you in the body of his flesh through death, so that he might present you holy and blameless and beyond reproach before him, ²³ if indeed you remain in the faith, established and steadfast, not shifting from the hope of the gospel that you heard, which was proclaimed in every creation under heaven, of which I Paul became a servant. ²⁴ Now I rejoice in what I suffer for you, and I fill up in my flesh what is lacking in the afflictions of Christ on behalf of his body, which is the church. ²⁵ Of this church I became a servant according to the stewardship from God given to me for your benefit, to make the word of God fully known, ²⁶ the mystery hidden for ages and generations, now made manifest to the saints, ²⁷ whom God was pleased to reveal what is the wealth of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory. ²⁸ Whom we proclaim, admonishing every person and teaching every person in all wisdom, so that we may present every person mature in Christ, ²⁹ for which I also labor and struggle according to his energy that works powerfully in me.

---

Notes
- v. 20: The phrase "διʼ αὐτοῦ εἴτε τὰ ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς εἴτε τὰ ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς" is rendered as "whether on earth or in the heavens" to maintain the balanced structure and clarify the universal scope of reconciliation.
- v. 25: "οἰκονομίαν" is translated as "stewardship," which captures the sense of responsibility and management of a divinely assigned task.
- v. 27: "τί τὸ πλοῦτος τῆς δόξης τοῦ μυστηρίου τούτου" is translated as "what is the wealth of the glory of this mystery" to preserve the rhetorical and theological emphasis on the revelation.
