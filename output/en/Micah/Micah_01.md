# Micah 1

¹ The word of the LORD that came to Micah the Morasthite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, concerning what he saw about Samaria and Jerusalem. ² Listen, all peoples, pay attention, earth and all that fills it. The Lord GOD will bear witness against you from His holy dwelling. ³ For look, the LORD is coming from His place and will descend, walking upon the high places of the earth. ⁴ The mountains will tremble beneath Him, and the valleys will split open like wax before fire, like water rushing down a slope. ⁵ Because of Jacob’s sin, all this, and because of the sins of the house of Israel. What is Jacob’s sin? Is it not Samaria? And what is the iniquity of the house of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem? ⁶ I will make Samaria a heap in the field, a place for planting vineyards. I will break its stones and expose its foundations. ⁷ All her carved images will be smashed, and all her wages will be burned with fire, and all her idols I will make desolate. For from wages of a harlot she was gathered, and with wages of a harlot she will return. ⁸ Therefore I will lament and wail, I will go barefoot and naked. I will make a lament like jackals and a mourning like daughters of doves. ⁹ For her wound is incurable, for it has come to Judah, it has reached the gate of my people, to Jerusalem. ¹⁰ In Gath, do not rejoice. In Beth-le-aphrah, do not celebrate. Roll yourself in dust, for they will pass you by. ¹¹ The one living peacefully in her cities will not come out. The one living in Shepharam will mourn for her, the one from Beth-horon will be taken from her. ¹² Who can give good news to the one living in prosperity, when disaster has come from the Lord to the gate of Jerusalem? ¹³ The noise of a chariot, of a waggon. The one living in Laish, the first of sin, this is the daughter of Zion. In you were found the sins of Israel. ¹⁴ Therefore you will give tribute for the inheritance of Gath, and the houses of Aijalon will be a lie to the kings of Israel. ¹⁵ Yet still the heir of your father will come to you, the one living in Maroth, until Adullam will come the glory of Israel. ¹⁶ Shave your heads and cut off your hair for the children of your delight. Expand your baldness like the vulture, for they have been taken from you.

---

Notes
- v. 7: "wages of a harlot" reflects the Hebrew "מִשְׁנֶה זוֹנָה" (mishneh zonah) which literally means "whore's wages" but is rendered here to retain the metaphor.
- v. 11: "Shepharam" (שְׁפְרָם) is a rare place name; "Beth-horon" (בֵּית הֹרֹן) is from the Hebrew. Both are retained as transliterated proper names.
- v. 13: "Laish" (לַיְשׁ) is a town mentioned in the Hebrew, and "the first of sin" reflects the Hebrew "רֵאשִׁית חַטָּאת" (re'shit chatta'at).
- v. 14: "Aijalon" (אֵיכָלֹן) is a town name; "tribute for the inheritance of Gath" reflects the Hebrew "שִׁלּוּחִים עַל מוֹרֶשֶׁת גַּת" (shiluchim al morehset Gat).
- v. 15: "Maroth" (מָרוֹת) and "Adullam" (עֲדֻלָּם) are retained as transliterated proper names.
- v. 16: "children of your delight" is a poetic rendering of "בְּנֵי תַּעֲנוּגָיִךְ" (beney ta'anugayich), which refers to beloved children or offspring.
