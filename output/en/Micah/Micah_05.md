# Micah 5

¹ And you, O Bethlehem of Ephrathah, too small to be among the clans of Judah, from you there will emerge for me one who is to rule over Israel, and his origins are from the ancient days. ² Therefore, he will give them until the time when she who is in labor has brought forth, and the rest of his brothers will return to the people of Israel. ³ And he will rise and shepherd in the might of the LORD, in the glory of the name of the LORD his God. And they will dwell securely, for now he will be great to the ends of the earth. ⁴ And it will be peace: when Assyria comes into our land and treads upon our citadels, we will raise against him seven shepherds and eight rulers of men. ⁵ They will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in its gates, and they will deliver from Assyria when it enters our land and treads upon our borders. ⁶ And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, like dew from the LORD, like plants on the grass that do not wait for a person nor hope for the sons of men. ⁷ And the remnant of Jacob will be in the nations, among many peoples, like a lion among the beasts of the forest, like a young lion among the flocks of sheep, which, when it passes through, tramples and devours, and no one rescues. ⁸ Lift your hand against your adversaries, and all your enemies will be cut off. ⁹ And it will be in the day, says the LORD, that I will cut off your horses from among you and destroy your chariots. ¹⁰ And I will cut off the cities of your land and tear down all your strongholds. ¹¹ And I will cut off sorcerers from your hand, and those who practice divination will no longer be among you. ¹² And I will cut off your idols and your pillars from among you, and you will no longer bow down to the work of your hands. ¹³ And I will root out your song from among you and destroy your cities. ¹⁴ And I will act with wrath and with fury, taking vengeance on the nations that did not listen.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "when Assyria comes into our land" — the Hebrew uses a rare phrase "כִּֽי־יָבוֹא בְאַרְצֵנוּ" which is rendered as a conditional "when."
- v. 8: "Lift your hand against your adversaries" — the Hebrew is imperative, but the subject is omitted; the context implies the people or their leader.
- v. 13: "I will root out your song" — the Hebrew uses "נָתַשְׁתִּי" from the root נָתַשׁ, meaning to throw out or root out, applied to "שִׁירֶיךָ" (your songs).
