# Micah 6

¹ Now hear what the LORD says: Rise up, plead your case with the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice. ² Hear, O mountains, the LORD’s case, and you enduring foundations of the earth, for the LORD has a case with his people, and with Israel he will contend. ³ O people, what have I done to you, or how have I wearied you? Answer me. ⁴ For I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and rescued you from the house of slavery. I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam before you. ⁵ O people, remember now what Balak king of Moab plotted, and what Balaam son of Beor answered him, from Shittim to Gilgal, so that you may know the righteousness of the LORD. ⁶ With what shall I come before the LORD, and bow before God Most High? Shall I come with burnt offerings, with calves a year old? ⁷ Will the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams, with ten thousands of streams of oil? Shall I give him my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul? ⁸ It has been told you, O human: what is good and what the LORD requires of you— nothing but to do justice, and to love steadfast love, and to walk humbly with your God. ⁹ The LORD’s voice calls to the city— and the one who fears the name will see it— “Listen, O staff, and who has appointed it?” ¹⁰ Still, does a house of wickedness store up dishonesty, and a bag filled with deceit? ¹¹ Shall I weigh out with dishonest scales and use a bag with false weights? ¹² Those whose wealth is full of violence, and whose inhabitants speak lies, whose tongue is deceit on their lips. ¹³ And I too will begin to strike you, to destroy you for your sins. ¹⁴ You will eat, but not be satisfied; and hunger will be in your midst. You will be anxious and not be rescued. And the one who escapes will be handed over to the sword. ¹⁵ You will sow, but not reap. You will press olive oil, but not anoint yourselves with it. You will tread grapes, but not drink wine. ¹⁶ For you have kept the statutes of Zambri, and all the works of the house of Ahab, and you have walked in their counsels, so that I will give you up to destruction, and those who dwell in it to mockery, and the disgrace of my people you will bear.

---

Notes
- v. 5: The reference to Balak and Balaam is to Numbers 22–24, where Balaam’s oracles affirm Israel’s destiny despite Balak’s attempts to curse them. The phrase "from Shittim to Gilgal" alludes to key locations in Israel’s wilderness journey (Num 25, Josh 5).
- v. 8: "To walk humbly with your God" is a nuanced rendering of "to walk in humility with your God" (לֶכֶת עִם־אֱלֹהֶיךָ בַּצְּנוּעַ). The term "humbly" captures the meaning of בַּצְּנוּעַ.
- v. 9: The Septuagint (and other versions) reads "the one who fears the name will be saved," but the Hebrew text appears to say "the one who fears the name will see it." The reference is unclear, possibly to a future restoration or judgment.
- v. 16: "Zambri" and "house of Ahab" are names of historical Israelite figures. Zambri is otherwise unattested in the Hebrew Bible, and the reference may be to a local or symbolic figure.
