# Micah 7

¹ Alas for me, for I have become like those who gather summer stubble, like a green shoot in a vineyard, with no grapes to eat—the first fruits of my soul have withered. ² The righteous have perished from the land, and there is no one upright among people. All of them conspire with blood; each one traps his brother, and each man lies in wait. ³ The powerful have their hands out to do good, the ruler demands justice, and the judge speaks falsehood. The desires of their hearts are deceit, and they devour it. ⁴ Their best is like a rotting gourd, their upright walk is crooked. The day of your punishment is coming. Now your ruin is at hand. ⁵ Do not trust in a friend, do not put your hope in a commander. Guard the opening of your mouth from the one who lies with you in bed. ⁶ For a son dishonors his father, a daughter rebels against her mother, a bride against her mother-in-law. A man’s enemies are the members of his own household. ⁷ But as for me, I will look to the LORD. I will wait for the God of my salvation. My God will hear me. ⁸ Let not my enemy rejoice over me because I have fallen. I will rise up. If I sit in darkness, the LORD will be a light for me. ⁹ I will endure the LORD’s anger, because I have sinned against him, until he contends with me and executes my judgment. He will bring me out into the light, and I will see his righteousness. ¹⁰ And your enemy will see it and be put to shame, the one who said, “The LORD be your God!” My eyes will see her, and now she will be trampled like mud in the streets. ¹¹ A day to rebuild your walls—on that day your decrees will be removed. ¹² On that day your cities will be divided, one for Assyria and another for the city of Milo, and one for Milo and another to the river and the sea, from sea to sea, from mountain to mountain. ¹³ And the land will become desolate because of its inhabitants, for the fruit of their actions. ¹⁴ Shepherd your people with your staff, the flock of your inheritance, who dwell alone in the forest of Carmel. Let them graze in Bashan and Gilead as in days of old. ¹⁵ As in the days of your coming out from the land of Egypt, I will show them wonders. ¹⁶ Nations will see and be shamed, for all their might. They will lay hands over their mouths; their ears will be deafened. ¹⁷ They will lick the dust like a serpent, crawl on the ground like a snake. In their strongholds they will be terrified. Because of the LORD our God they will be afraid and will fear you. ¹⁸ Who is a God like you, who pardons iniquity and passes over the transgression of the remnant of his inheritance? He does not retain his anger forever, because you love steadfast love. ¹⁹ He will again have compassion on us; he will tread down our iniquities and cast all our sins into the depths of the sea. ²⁰ You will give truth to Jacob, steadfast love to Abraham, as you swore to our ancestors in days of old.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "I have become like those who gather summer stubble" — the metaphor is retained as a direct comparison.
- v. 4: "like a rotting gourd" — "gourd" (kediq) is a literal rendering; the decay imagery is clear.
- v. 11: "remove your decrees" — "remove" refers to the removal of oppressive laws or regulations.
- v. 12: "divided, one for Assyria and another for the city of Milo..." — the term Milo is a reference to a fortified city; the translation follows the poetic structure of the Hebrew.
