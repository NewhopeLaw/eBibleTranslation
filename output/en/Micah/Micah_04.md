# Micah 4

¹ In the last days, the mountain of the LORD will be established at the head of the mountains and will rise above the hills. Peoples will flow to it. ² And many nations will go and say, "Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob, that he may teach us his ways and that we may walk in his paths." For the law will go out from Zion, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem. ³ And he will judge among the nations and will arbitrate for many peoples. They will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will not lift sword against nation, and they will no longer learn war. ⁴ Each person will sit under their own vine and under their own fig tree, and no one will make them afraid, for the mouth of the LORD of Hosts has spoken. ⁵ For all the peoples will walk, each in the name of their god, but we will walk in the name of the LORD our God forever and ever. ⁶ In that day, says the LORD, I will gather the broken and those who are scattered, and those I have afflicted. ⁷ And I will make the broken a remnant and the scattered a strong nation. The LORD will reign over them on Mount Zion from now on and forever. ⁸ And you, O tower of the flock, hill of the daughter of Zion, to you shall come the former dominion, the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem. ⁹ Why do you cry out now, daughter of Jerusalem? Is there no king among you, or has your counselor perished? For pangs like those of a woman in childbirth have seized you. ¹⁰ Writhe and labor, daughter of Zion, like a woman in childbirth, for now you will leave the city and dwell in the field. You will go as far as Babylon, there to be rescued, there to be redeemed by the LORD from the hand of your enemies. ¹¹ And now, many nations have gathered against you, saying, "Let us disgrace her and let our eyes look upon Zion." ¹² But they do not know the purpose of the LORD, nor do they understand his plan, for he has gathered them like the sheaves of a threshing floor. ¹³ Arise, and thresh, daughter of Zion, for I will make your horn iron and your hooves bronze. You will dash many peoples, and I will dedicate their wealth to the LORD, and their might to the Lord of the whole earth. ¹⁴ Now you are in labor, daughter of an army, for a siege is set against us. With the rod they strike the ruler of Israel on the cheek.

---

Notes  
- v. 8: "tower of the flock" is a poetic rendering of מגדל עדר (migdal edar), reflecting its function as a protective structure for flocks.  
- v. 13: "horn" is a metaphor for strength and power (keren). "Hooves" (parsot) is a poetic translation of פְּרָסִים, which can also mean "horns" or "rams" — the context suggests military imagery.  
- v. 14: "daughter of an army" is a poetic interpretation of בַּת־גְּדוּד, which literally means "daughter of the band/company." The reference to a siege and the ruler struck on the cheek reflects judgment and suffering.
