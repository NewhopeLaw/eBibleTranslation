# 2 Corinthians 5

¹ We know that if the earthly house of our tent is destroyed, we have a building from God, a house not made by hands, eternal in the heavens. ² For this very reason we groan, longing to put on the heavenly dwelling, ³ so that when we have been clothed, we will not be found naked. ⁴ For those who are in the tent groan, being burdened, because we do not wish to be unclothed but to be clothed, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life. ⁵ The one who has equipped us for this very task is God, who has given us the deposit of the Spirit. ⁶ Therefore we are always bold, and we know that while we are at home in the body we are away from the Lord— ⁷ for we walk by faith, not by sight. ⁸ We are bold, yes, and we would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord. ⁹ So we make it our aim, whether at home or away from the body, to be pleasing to him. ¹⁰ For we all must appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may receive what is due for what he has done in the body, whether good or evil. ¹¹ Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade people, but we are revealed to God; and I hope we are revealed in your consciences also. ¹² We are not again commending ourselves to you, but giving you an opportunity to boast on our behalf, so that you may have an argument with those who boast in appearance and not in heart. ¹³ For if we are beside ourselves, it is for God; if we are in our right mind, it is for you. ¹⁴ For the love of Christ compels us, since we have concluded this: One died for all, therefore all have died; ¹⁵ and he died for all so that those who live might no longer live for themselves, but for the one who died for them and was raised. ¹⁶ So from now on we regard no one from a human standpoint; even though we once knew Christ from a human standpoint, we no longer know him in that way. ¹⁷ So if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come. ¹⁸ And all things are from God, who reconciled us to himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation: ¹⁹ that God was in Christ reconciling the world to himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and entrusting to us the message of reconciliation. ²⁰ Therefore we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were entreating through us. We implore on behalf of Christ: Be reconciled to God. ²¹ Him who knew no sin he made to be sin on our behalf, so that we might become the righteousness of God in him.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "earthly house of our tent" is a metaphor for the body, rendered to reflect the contrast with the heavenly dwelling.
- v. 14: "One died for all" is intentionally indefinite to reflect the original's general reference to Christ without using "Jesus" or "Christ" in this context.
- v. 16: "regard no one from a human standpoint" reflects the Greek's contrast between κατὰ σάρκα ("according to the flesh") and spiritual understanding.
- v. 19: "entrusting to us the message of reconciliation" reflects the Greek’s δόντος ἡμῖν τὸν λόγον τῆς καταλλαγῆς.
