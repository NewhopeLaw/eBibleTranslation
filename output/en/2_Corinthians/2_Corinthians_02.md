# 2 Corinthians 2

¹ For this reason I judged it necessary for myself not to come again to you in sorrow. ² For if I grieve you, who is the one who brings me joy if not the one who is grieved because of me? ³ And I wrote the same thing, so that when I come I may not be grieved by those I need to rejoice over, trusting in all of you that my joy is all of yours. ⁴ For I have written to you out of great affliction and anguish of heart, with many tears, not to grieve you but so that you may know the greater love I have for you. ⁵ Now if someone has grieved, they have not grieved me, but in part, so as not to burden all of you. ⁶ This punishment from the majority is enough, ⁷ so that rather you should forgive and comfort him, or he may be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow. ⁸ Therefore I urge you to show him love. ⁹ For this is why I also wrote, so that you may know the test of you, whether you are obedient in all things. ¹⁰ And whatever you forgive, I also forgive. For indeed if I have forgiven anything, I have forgiven it in the presence of Christ for your sake, ¹¹ so that we may not be outwitted by Satan, for we do not remain ignorant of his intentions. ¹² For when I came to Troas for the gospel of Christ and the door was opened to me by the Lord, ¹³ I had no comfort in my spirit, because I could not find my brother Titus there. So I said goodbye to them and went to Macedonia. ¹⁴ Now to the God of all grace, who always leads us in triumph in Christ and who spreads abroad the fragrance of the knowledge of Him everywhere, ¹⁵ for we are the fragrance of Christ to God, both to those being saved and to those perishing— ¹⁶ to the one group a fragrance of death leading to death, to the other a fragrance of life leading to life. And who is sufficient for these things? ¹⁷ For we are not like so many who trade in the word of God, but as from sincerity, and as from God, in Christ we speak before God.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "in the presence of Christ" — The phrase ἐν προσώπῳ Χριστοῦ is rendered as "in the presence of Christ," following the sense of representing Christ's authority.
