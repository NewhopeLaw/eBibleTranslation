# 2 Corinthians 12

¹ I must boast. It is not helpful, but I will proceed to visions and revelations of the Lord. ² I know a man in Christ fourteen years ago—whether in the body I do not know, or out of the body I do not know, but God knows—such a one was taken up to the third heaven. ³ I know such a man—whether in the body or apart from the body I do not know, but God knows— ⁴ that he was caught up into paradise and heard inexpressible words, which it is not lawful for a person to speak. ⁵ I will boast about such a person, but not about myself, except in my weaknesses. ⁶ For if I wish to boast, I am not a fool, because I am telling the truth. But I will refrain, so that no one may think more of me than what he sees in me or hears from me, ⁷ because of the excess of the revelations. Therefore, in order that I might not be exalted beyond measure, a thorn in the flesh was given to me, an angel of Satan to strike me, to keep me from being exalted. ⁸ Because of this, I prayed to the Lord three times to take it away from me. ⁹ He said to me, “My grace is sufficient for you, for power is perfected in weakness.” Therefore, I most gladly will boast in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may dwell over me. ¹⁰ So I rejoice in weaknesses, in insults, in needs, in persecutions, in distresses, for the sake of Christ. For when I am weak, then I am strong. ¹¹ I have become a fool. You have forced me. For I was compelled to speak in your presence. I have not been lacking in anything among the super-apostles, though I am nothing. ¹² Signs of an apostle were performed among you in every kind of endurance, with signs and wonders and mighty works. ¹³ What is it that you were treated more harshly than the other churches, except that I myself did not burden you? Forgive me this wrong. ¹⁴ Behold, for the third time I am ready to come to you, and I will not burden you. For I do not seek your possessions, but you yourselves; for children are not required to store up for their parents, but parents for their children. ¹⁵ But I am willing most gladly to be spent and consumed for your souls. If I love you more, am I loved less? ¹⁶ Let it be. I did not burden you. But being crafty I took you by deception. ¹⁷ Did anyone of those whom I sent to you ever exploit you? ¹⁸ I urged Titus and I sent along the brother. Did Titus exploit you? Did we not walk in the same Spirit? Did we not follow the same footprints? ¹⁹ Have you supposed that we are defending ourselves to you? We speak in the presence of God in Christ. But all things, beloved, are for your building up. ²⁰ For I fear that when I come I might not find you as I hope, and that I will be found by you not as you hope. Perhaps there will be quarrels, jealousy, anger, factions, slanders, whispers, swollen heads, disorders. ²¹ Perhaps when I come again my God will humble me among you, and I will mourn many of those who have sinned previously and not repented in their uncleanness, fornication, and lewdness which they have done.

Notes
- v. 2: "taken up to the third heaven" — The phrase "third heaven" is a traditional rendering of the Greek τρίτου οὐρανοῦ. Some interpreters understand this as a reference to paradise, which is equated with the third heaven in some Jewish cosmologies.
- v. 5: "I will boast about such a person, but not about myself" — The Greek uses ὑπὲρ τοῦ τοιούτου to emphasize that the boasting is not about the speaker.
- v. 9: "power is perfected in weakness" — The Greek uses τελεῖται for "perfected," which can also mean "completed" or "brought to completion." The context suggests a theological point about divine power being complete or effective in human weakness.
- v. 11: "I have not been lacking in anything among the super-apostles" — The Greek uses ὑπερλίαν ἀποστόλων, which is a rare term that may refer to the "over-apostles" or "super-apostles" mentioned in the text.
