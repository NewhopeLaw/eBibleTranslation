# 2 Corinthians 13

¹ This is the third time I am coming to you. Every matter must be established by the testimony of two or three witnesses. ² I have said this before and now I say it again, as if present the second time and absent in body now, to those who have sinned and to all the rest: if I come again, I will not spare. ³ For you are testing the one who speaks through me, Christ. He is not weak in you, but he is powerful in you. ⁴ For indeed, he was crucified in weakness, but he lives by the power of God. And indeed, we are weak in him, but we will live with him by the power of God for you. ⁵ Test yourselves to see if you are in the faith; examine yourselves. Or perhaps you do not recognize this, that Jesus Christ is among you? Unless you are rejected. ⁶ But I hope you will recognize that we are not rejected. ⁷ We pray to God that you do no evil, not so that we may be approved, but so that you may do what is good, even though we are rejected. ⁸ For we cannot do anything against the truth, but only for the sake of the truth. ⁹ For we rejoice when we are weak, but you are strong. And this is also our prayer, for your completion. ¹⁰ Therefore, I write these things while absent, so that when I am present I will not use the authority the Lord has given me abruptly, for building up and not for tearing down. ¹¹ Finally, brothers, rejoice, be built up, be encouraged, have the same mind, be at peace, and the God of love and peace will be with you. ¹² Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the saints greet you. ¹³ The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with all of you.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "he was crucified in weakness" — the translation reflects the metaphorical contrast between the crucifixion (a symbol of weakness) and the life that follows through divine power.
- v. 5: "you are testing the one who speaks through me, Christ" — the verb "test" is used to reflect the Greek πειράζετε, which here is in the context of evaluating spiritual authenticity.
- v. 7: "we are rejected" — the translation of ἀδόκιμοι in this verse is consistent with the glossary and the context of being unapproved or rejected.
