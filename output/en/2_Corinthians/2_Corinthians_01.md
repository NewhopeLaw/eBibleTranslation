# 2 Corinthians 1

¹ Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy the brother, to the church of God that is in Corinth, together with all the saints who are throughout all Achaia: ² Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ³ Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort, ⁴ who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those who are in affliction, with the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God. ⁵ For as the sufferings of Christ overflow to us, so also by Christ does our comfort overflow. ⁶ Whether we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; whether we are comforted, it is for your comfort that works in patient endurance of the same sufferings that we also suffer. ⁷ And our hope for you is firm, knowing that just as you are partakers of the sufferings, so you also are partakers of the comfort. ⁸ For we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of the affliction we experienced in Asia: we were overwhelmed beyond our strength, so that we despaired even of life. ⁹ Indeed, we ourselves had the sentence of death within ourselves, so that we would not trust in ourselves, but in God who raises the dead. ¹⁰ He rescued us from so great a death and will rescue us, in whom we have put our hope that he will also rescue us, ¹¹ with your assistance in our behalf by prayer, so that many may give thanks for us on our behalf on many peoples' behalf. ¹² For our boast is this: the testimony of our conscience, that we conducted ourselves in the world in holiness and sincerity before God, not in fleshly wisdom but in the grace of God, more especially toward you. ¹³ For we are not writing to you anything other than what you read or know, and I hope that you will know fully, ¹⁴ just as you have partially known us, that we are your boast just as you are our boast in the day of our Lord Jesus. ¹⁵ And with this confidence, I had planned to come to you before, so that you might have a second benefit, ¹⁶ and through you to pass through to Macedonia, and again from Macedonia to come to you, and be sent from you to Judea. ¹⁷ Therefore, have I used lightness in this planning? Or do I reason according to the flesh, so that with me the Yes is Yes and the No is No? ¹⁸ But God is faithful, for our word to you is not Yes and No. ¹⁹ For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you through us—through me and Silvanus and Timothy—is not Yes and No, but in him the Yes is yes. ²⁰ For all the promises of God find their Yes in him. Therefore through him also the Amen is spoken to God for glory through us. ²¹ Now the one who establishes us with you in Christ and who anointed us is God, ²² who also sealed us and gave the down payment of the Spirit in our hearts. ²³ I call God as witness upon my life that I did not come to Corinth out of reluctance. ²⁴ Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are coworkers for your joy, for you are standing in your faith.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "apostle of Christ Jesus" is used in a titular sense, aligning with the common understanding of "apostle" in early Christian literature.
- v. 17: "lightness" refers to a lack of seriousness or recklessness, a rare use of the term in a theological context.
- v. 20: "Amen" is used in reference to God's affirmation through Christ, not as a liturgical term.
- v. 22: "down payment" is the standard translation for "arrabon," referring to a pledge or guarantee.
- v. 24: "lord it over" is a metaphor for exerting undue authority or control, consistent with the context of Paul's defense of his approach to leadership.
