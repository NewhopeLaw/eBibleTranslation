# 1 Corinthians 14

¹ Pursue love, and earnestly desire the spiritual gifts, but especially that you may prophesy. ² For one who speaks in a tongue does not speak to people but to God, for nobody understands him, since he is speaking mysteries in the Spirit. ³ But one who prophesies speaks to people for their building up, encouragement, and comfort. ⁴ One who speaks in a tongue builds up himself, but one who prophesies builds up the church. ⁵ I wish all of you would speak in tongues, but I prefer that you would prophesy. For the one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in tongues, unless the one who speaks in tongues interprets, so that the church may be built up. ⁶ Now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, what good will I be to you? Unless I bring you a word of revelation, or knowledge, or prophecy, or teaching? ⁷ Just as an inanimate object that makes a sound, whether pipe or harp, if it does not give a distinction in the tones, how will the one playing the pipe or the harp be recognized? ⁸ For if the trumpet gives an indistinct sound, who will prepare for battle? ⁹ So also you, if you do not give a clear sound in the tongue, how will the meaning be recognized? You will be speaking into the air. ¹⁰ So many voices, if there are many kinds of sounds in the world, and none is without sound; ¹¹ if I do not know the power of the sound, I will be a foreigner to the one speaking, and the one speaking will be a foreigner to me. ¹² So also you, since you are eager for spiritual gifts, seek to excel for the building up of the church. ¹³ Therefore, the one who speaks in a tongue should pray to interpret. ¹⁴ For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful. ¹⁵ What then is this? I will pray with the Spirit, and I will also pray with the mind; I will sing with the Spirit, and I will also sing with the mind; ¹⁶ for if you give thanks in the Spirit, how can the one who fills the place of the uninformed say “Amen” to your thanksgiving, since he does not know what you are saying? ¹⁷ For you may be giving thanks well, but the other is not being built up. ¹⁸ I thank God that I speak in tongues more than all of you. ¹⁹ But in the church I would rather speak five words with my mind, so that I may teach others, than ten thousand words in a tongue. ²⁰ Brothers, do not become children in mind, but in malice be infants, yet in mind be mature. ²¹ It is written in the law, I will speak to this people in tongues and in the lips of strangers, and even so they will not listen to me, says the Lord. ²² So tongues are for a sign, not for those who believe, but for the unbelievers; but prophecy is not for the unbelievers, but for those who believe. ²³ So if the whole church comes together at one place, and all of them speak in tongues, and some who are uninformed or unbelievers come in, will they not say that you are out of your minds? ²⁴ But if all prophesy, and some who are unbelievers or uninformed come in, they are convicted by all, they are judged by all, ²⁵ the hidden things of their heart are revealed, and so they will fall on their face and worship God, declaring that God is truly among you. ²⁶ What then, brothers? When you come together, each one has a hymn, has a teaching, has a revelation, has a tongue, has an interpretation. Let all things be for building up. ²⁷ If anyone speaks in a tongue, let it be by two or at most three, and one at a time, and let someone interpret. ²⁸ But if there is no interpreter, let him keep silent in the church and speak to himself and to God. ²⁹ Let the prophets speak, two or three, and let the others discern. ³⁰ If something is revealed to someone seated among them, let the one who is speaking first keep silent. ³¹ For you can all prophesy one by one, so that all may learn and all may be encouraged. ³² (And the spirits of the prophets are subject to the prophets, ³³ for God is not a God of disorder but of peace, just as in all the churches of the saints.) ³⁴ Women should remain silent in the churches, for they are not allowed to speak, but they should be in submission, as the law also says. ³⁵ If they wish to learn anything, let them ask their husbands at home, for it is disgraceful for a woman to speak in church. ³⁶ Or did the word of God originate with you, or has it come to you alone? ³⁷ If anyone thinks he is a prophet or spiritual, let him recognize what I am writing to you is the command of the Lord. ³⁸ But if anyone does not recognize this, let him not recognize himself. ³⁹ Therefore, brothers, desire to prophesy, and do not forbid speaking in tongues. ⁴⁰ But let everything be done in an orderly and decent manner.

---

Notes
- v. 14: The translation "my mind is unfruitful" reflects the contrast between the active spirit and the passive mind in speaking in tongues.
- v. 23: "uninformed" (ἰδιῶται) is used to distinguish those who are not spiritually discerning.
- v. 34-35: The instruction about women in the church is rendered directly from the Greek, with "uninformed" (ἰδιῶται) and "disgraceful" (αἰσχρὸν) reflecting the text's tone and context.
- v. 37: "the command of the Lord" is chosen to emphasize the authority of the instruction.
