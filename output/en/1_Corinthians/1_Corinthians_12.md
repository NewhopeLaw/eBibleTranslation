# 1 Corinthians 12

¹ Now concerning spiritual things, brothers, I do not want you to be unaware. ² You know that when you were pagans, you were led as captives to silent idols. ³ Therefore, I make known to you that no one speaking in the Spirit says, "Cursed be Jesus," and no one can say, "The Lord Jesus," except in the Holy Spirit. ⁴ Now there are different kinds of spiritual gifts, but the same Spirit; ⁵ and there are different kinds of services, but the same Lord; ⁶ and there are different kinds of activities, but the same God who energizes all things in all people. ⁷ To each is given the manifestation of the Spirit for the common good. ⁸ For to one is given through the Spirit a word of wisdom, and to another a word of knowledge according to the same Spirit, ⁹ to another faith by the same Spirit, and to another gifts of healing by the one Spirit, ¹⁰ to another workings of power, to another prophecy, to another discernment of spirits, to another various kinds of tongues, to another the interpretation of tongues. ¹¹ All these are done by the one and the same Spirit, distributing to each individually as the Spirit wills. ¹² For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so also is Christ. ¹³ For by one Spirit we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free, and we were all given to drink one Spirit. ¹⁴ For in fact the body is not one member but many. ¹⁵ If the foot were to say, "Because I am not a hand, I am not part of the body," it is not for that reason not part of the body. ¹⁶ And if the ear were to say, "Because I am not an eye, I am not part of the body," it is not for that reason not part of the body. ¹⁷ If the whole body were an eye, where would the hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the sense of smell be? ¹⁸ Now God has placed the members, each one of them, in the body as He willed. ¹⁹ And if the whole were one member, where would the body be? ²⁰ Now there are many members, but one body. ²¹ The eye cannot say to the hand, "I have no need of you," nor again the head to the feet, "I have no need of you." ²² But in fact, the parts of the body that seem to be weaker are indispensable, ²³ and those parts that we consider less honorable, we give them greater honor, and our less attractive parts are given greater dignity, ²⁴ while our more honorable parts have no need of this. But God has so composed the body, giving more honor to the less honorable part, ²⁵ so that there may be no division in the body, but that the members may have the same care for one another. ²⁶ If one member suffers, all the members suffer with it; if one member is honored, all the members rejoice with it. ²⁷ You are the body of Christ and individually members of it. ²⁸ And God has appointed in the church first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then powers, then gifts of healing, of helping, of administration, and of speaking in different tongues. ²⁹ Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work miracles? ³⁰ Do all have gifts of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret? ³¹ But pursue the greater gifts. And yet I will show you a still more excellent way.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "spiritual things" renders "πνευματικῶν" as a general category, not "spiritual gifts" specifically.
- v. 3: "Cursed be Jesus" is a literal translation of "Ἀνάθεμα Ἰησοῦς," which is a strong expression of rejection.
- v. 28: "helping" is used for "ἀντιλήμψεις," based on the context of mutual care and support.
- v. 31: "still more excellent way" is a standard rendering of "καθʼ ὑπερβολὴν ὁδὸν," reflecting Paul's reference to love in the next chapter.
