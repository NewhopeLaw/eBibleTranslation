# 1 Corinthians 16

¹ Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I directed the churches of Galatia, so you should do also. ² On the first day of every week, each of you should set aside a portion from your earnings, as things prosper, so that when I arrive there will be no need for collections. ³ When I come, I will send those whom you have approved by letter to carry your contribution to Jerusalem. ⁴ If it seems appropriate for me to go also, I will come with them. ⁵ I will come to you when I have passed through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia, ⁶ and perhaps I will stay with you for a while or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I go. ⁷ For I do not want to see you now by the way, because I hope to stay with you for a while, if the Lord permits. ⁸ I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost, ⁹ for a large and effective door has opened to me, and there are many adversaries. ¹⁰ If Timothy comes, see that he is with you without fear, for he is working the Lord's work just as I am. ¹¹ Therefore, let no one despise him. Send him on his way in peace, so that he may return to me—indeed, I am waiting for him with the brothers. ¹² Now concerning Apollos, our brother, I have urged him many times to visit you with the brothers, but it was not his will to come now. He will come when opportunity permits. ¹³ Be alert, stand firm in the faith, be courageous, be strong. ¹⁴ Let all your actions be done in love. ¹⁵ I urge you, brothers, to note our household of Stephanas, that they were the first in Achaia to dedicate themselves to the service of the saints. ¹⁶ Therefore, you too should submit to those who labor and work among you. ¹⁷ I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus, because they filled what was lacking from you. ¹⁸ For they refreshed both my spirit and yours. Therefore, recognize such people. ¹⁹ The churches of Asia send greetings to you. Aquila and Prisca, with the church in their house, send many greetings to you in the Lord. ²⁰ All the brothers send greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss. ²¹ The greeting is in my own hand—Paul. ²² If anyone does not love the Lord, let that person be accursed. Maranatha. ²³ The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. ²⁴ My love is with all of you in Christ Jesus.

---

Notes
- v. 22: "Maranatha" is transliterated as "Maranatha," since its meaning is disputed and could be a shortened form of either "Our Lord has come" or "The Lord comes." This is a rare word and its rendering is left as is.
