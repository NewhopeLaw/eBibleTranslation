# 1 Corinthians 6

¹ Does any one of you dare to have a dispute settled with another in the presence of unjust people, and not in the presence of the holy ones? ² Or do you not know that the holy ones will judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you not unworthy of the smallest judgment? ³ Do you not know that we judge angels, let alone worldly matters? ⁴ If then you have worldly judgments to settle, do you appoint those who are looked down on in the assembly to be your judges? ⁵ I say this to your shame. Is there really no one among you wise enough to make a decision between his brothers? ⁶ But a brother goes to court with a brother, and this among unbelievers. ⁷ So already you are being defeated because you take legal action against one another. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be cheated? ⁸ But you yourselves wrong and cheat, and this among brothers. ⁹ Or do you not know that the wicked will not inherit God's kingdom? Do not be deceived. Neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, ¹⁰ nor effeminate men, nor homosexuals, nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor slanderers, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God. ¹¹ And some of you were like this, but you were cleansed, you were sanctified, you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus and in the Spirit of our God. ¹² All things are allowed for me, but not all are beneficial. All things are allowed for me, but I will not be dominated by any. ¹³ Food is for the stomach, and the stomach for food; yet God will do away with both. The body, however, is not for immorality but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body. ¹⁴ And God both raised the Lord and will raise us by His power. ¹⁵ Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Then shall I not make the members of Christ members of a prostitute? Never! ¹⁶ Or do you not know that the one who is joined to a prostitute becomes one body with her? For it is said, "The two will become one flesh." ¹⁷ But the one who is joined to the Lord becomes one spirit with Him. ¹⁸ Flee from immorality. Every other sin that a person commits is outside the body, but the one who is immoral sins against his own body. ¹⁹ Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, ²⁰ for you were bought at a price. So glorify God in your body.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "the smallest judgment" reflects the literal meaning of "κριτηρίων ἐλαχίστων" (smallest judgments), which refers to minor legal matters.
- v. 7: "are being defeated" reflects the Greek "ἥττημα ὑμῖν ἐστιν" (you are defeated), indicating a moral or spiritual weakness.
- v. 12: "I will not be dominated by any" preserves the force of "οὐκ ἐγὼ ἐξουσιασθήσομαι ὑπό τινος" (I will not be enslaved by anyone), which is a strong statement of self-mastery.
- v. 19: "temple" is used for "ναὸς," which refers to a sacred dwelling place, here of the Holy Spirit.
