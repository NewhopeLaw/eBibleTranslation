# Zephaniah 2

¹ Gather and huddle, the nation that is not refined. ² Before the blossom passes, before the decree comes, before the day of the LORD's wrath comes upon you, before the day of the LORD's anger arrives. ³ Seek the LORD, all you humble of the earth, who have done what is right; seek righteousness and seek humility, perhaps you will be hidden in the day of the LORD's wrath. ⁴ For Gaza will be abandoned, and Ashkelon will become a wasteland; Ashdod at midday will be driven out, and Ekron will be uprooted. ⁵ Woe to those who dwell in the coastal district, the people of Crete. The word of the LORD is against you, O Canaan, land of the Philistines, and I will destroy you so that no one lives there. ⁶ The coastal district will become a pasture for shepherds and a fold for flocks. ⁷ The district will become for the remnant of the house of Judah. On them they will graze, in the houses of Ashkelon they will lie at evening, for the LORD their God will visit them and restore their captivity. ⁸ I have heard the taunts of Moab and the boasts of the sons of Ammon, with which they have insulted my people and magnified themselves beyond their borders. ⁹ Therefore, I live, says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, that Moab will become like Sodom, and the sons of Ammon like Gomorrah—a wasteland covered with salt, a land where nothing is sown, a land forever abandoned. The remnant of my people will plunder them, and the remainder of the nations will possess them. ¹⁰ This will be their reward for their pride, because they insulted and magnified themselves against the LORD of Hosts. ¹¹ The LORD will appear against them, and he will destroy all the gods of the earth. Every island of the nations will bow to him, each in their place. ¹² Also you, Cushites, you will be wounded by my sword. ¹³ And he will stretch out his hand toward the north and destroy Assyria, and he will make Nineveh a wasteland, a dry land like the desert. ¹⁴ And herds will lie in her midst, all the beasts of the land. Also, jackals and hedgehogs will lie in her cisterns. The voice of a howler will be in her gates; birds will roost in her capitals, for her cedar beams will be stripped. ¹⁵ This is the city that was carefree, the one that lived in security, the one who said in her heart, "I am and there is none besides me." How she has become a wasteland, a resting place for beasts! Every passerby, who walks over her, will hiss and wave his hand.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the nation that is not refined" — the Hebrew הַגּוֹי לֹא נִכְסָֽף is a metaphor for those untempered or untested.
- v. 9: "the remainder of the nations will possess them" — the Hebrew וְיֶתֶר גּוֹיִי יִנְחָלֽוּם is translated to reflect the context of possession and inheritance.
- v. 15: "I am and there is none besides me" — the Hebrew אֲנִי וְאַפְסִי is a self-referential assertion of uniqueness and security.
