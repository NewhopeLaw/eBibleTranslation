# Zephaniah 3

¹ Woe to the defiant, the rebellious city, the one who rests in security, who says in her heart, "I am, and there is none besides me." How she has become a wasteland, a resting place for beasts! Every passerby who walks over her will hiss and wave his hand. ² O rebellious and unrepentant city, the dove! She did not listen to a voice, she did not accept instruction. She did not trust in the LORD, she did not draw near to her God. ³ Her rulers in her midst are roaring lions, her judges are evening wolves, who leave nothing for the morning. ⁴ Her prophets are wind-borne, treacherous men. Her priests have defiled the holy, they have transgressed the law. ⁵ The LORD is just in her midst, he will not do wrong. In the morning he will show his judgment in the light. He will not hide, nor will he let iniquity be unknown in judgment, nor will he allow it to prevail. ⁶ I have cut off nations, their corners have been removed. I have destroyed their cities, so that no traveler passes through them, and their towns are without people, no one lives there. ⁷ I said, "Surely you will fear me, take instruction, and your dwelling will not be cut off." But they refused to listen, they acted corruptly in all their deeds. ⁸ Therefore wait for me, says the LORD, until the day I rise for judgment, for my judgment is to gather nations, to gather kingdoms to pour out my wrath on them, all the fury of my anger, for with fire of my jealousy will the whole earth be consumed. ⁹ For then I will turn to the peoples a clear tongue, that all may call on the name of the LORD to serve him, with one yoke. ¹⁰ From the ends of the rivers of Cush, my scattered ones, the daughter of Puthai, will bring my offering. ¹¹ In that day you will no longer be shamed for all your deeds you have done against me, for then I will cut off from you those who exalt your pride, and you will no longer add to being exalted on my holy mountain. ¹² I will leave in your midst a people humble and lowly, who will take refuge in the name of the LORD. ¹³ The remnant of Israel will not do wrong, nor will they speak falsely, nor will deceitful tongues be found in their mouths. For they will graze and lie down, with no one to terrify them. ¹⁴ Rejoice, daughter of Zion, shout, daughter of Israel. Be glad and rejoice with all your heart, daughter of Jerusalem. ¹⁵ The LORD has taken away your judgments, he has removed your enemies. The King of Israel is in your midst; you will not see evil again. ¹⁶ In that day it will be said to Jerusalem, "Do not fear, O Zion, do not let your hands fall." ¹⁷ The LORD your God is in your midst, a mighty one who will save. He will rejoice over you with gladness, he will be quiet in his love, he will exult over you with singing. ¹⁸ I will gather those who mourn over you, whom you have sent away. You have borne their disgrace. ¹⁹ Behold, I will do it for you in that time, says the LORD. I will save the oppressed and the outcast, I will gather them and make them a praise and a name in all the earth. ²⁰ In that time I will bring you back, in that time I will gather you. For I will give you a name and a praise among all the peoples of the earth, when I turn your captivity before your eyes, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The title "the dove" (הַיּוֹנָֽה) is a metaphor for Jerusalem.
- v. 10: "rivers of Cush" (נַֽהֲרֵי־כוּשׁ) is a poetic reference to distant lands.
- v. 12: "daughter of Puthai" (בַּת־פוּצַי) is a poetic reference to Cushite lands.
