# 1 Thessalonians 5

¹ Now concerning times and moments, brothers, you have no need to be written to, ² for you yourselves know exactly that the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night. ³ When they say, "Peace and safety," then sudden destruction will come upon them, as labor pains upon a pregnant woman, and they will not escape. ⁴ But you, brothers, are not in darkness, so that the day may catch you like a thief, ⁵ for you are all children of the light and children of the day. We are not of the night or of darkness. ⁶ So then, let us not sleep as the rest do, but let us be alert and sober. ⁷ For those who sleep, night is for sleeping, and those who get drunk, night is for drunkenness; ⁸ but we are of the day, so let us be sober, having put on the breastplate of faith and love, and the helmet of the hope of salvation. ⁹ For God has not destined us for wrath, but for obtaining salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ, ¹⁰ who died for us so that whether we are awake or asleep, we might live together with him. ¹¹ Therefore, encourage one another and build one another up, just as you are doing. ¹² We ask you, brothers, to acknowledge those who labor among you and preside over you in the Lord and instruct you, ¹³ and to respect them very highly in love for their work. Be at peace among yourselves. ¹⁴ We also urge you, brothers, to admonish the disorderly, encourage the faint-hearted, help the weak, be patient toward all. ¹⁵ See that no one repays evil for evil to anyone, but always pursue what is good, both for one another and for all. ¹⁶ Always rejoice, ¹⁷ pray without ceasing, ¹⁸ in all things give thanks, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you. ¹⁹ Do not quench the Spirit, ²⁰ do not despise prophecies, ²¹ but test everything, hold fast what is good, ²² abstain from every kind of evil. ²³ Now may the God of peace himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole spirit, soul, and body be kept blameless at the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ. ²⁴ Faithful is the one who calls you, who will also do it. ²⁵ Brothers, pray for us. ²⁶ Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss. ²⁷ I charge you by the Lord to have this letter read to all the brothers. ²⁸ The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.

---

Notes
- v. 3: “labor pains upon a pregnant woman” is a direct translation of the metaphor, avoiding any euphemistic or archaic phrasing.
- v. 14: “admonish the disorderly” retains the active, corrective tone of the Greek.
- v. 23: “kept blameless at the appearing” reflects the future, eschatological context of παρουσία.
- v. 27: “I charge you by the Lord” reflects the solemnity of ἐνορκίζω, not softened to "I urge" or similar.
