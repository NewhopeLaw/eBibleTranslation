# 1 Thessalonians 4

¹ Therefore, brothers, we urge you and appeal to you in the Lord Jesus, that just as you received from us the way you ought to walk and please God, and just as you are walking, you should abound even more. ² For you know what commands we gave you through the Lord Jesus. ³ For this is the will of God, your sanctification, that you abstain from sexual immorality, ⁴ that each one of you knows how to own a vessel in sanctification and honor, ⁵ not in passion of lust, just as the nations who do not know God, ⁶ that none of you should overstep or take advantage of his brother in this matter, for the Lord is the avenger in all these things, just as we also told you and testified before. ⁷ For God did not call us for impurity, but for sanctification. ⁸ Therefore, the one who disregards this does not disregard a human being, but the God who gives his Holy Spirit to you. ⁹ Now concerning love for brothers, there is no need for us to write to you, for you yourselves have been taught by God to love one another; ¹⁰ and indeed you are doing this toward all the brothers who are throughout all of Macedonia. Still, we urge you, brothers, to abound even more, ¹¹ and to be eager to be quiet and to do your own work and to labor with your hands, just as we commanded you, ¹² so that you may walk decently toward those outside and have no need of anyone. ¹³ But we do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, about those who have fallen asleep, so that you may not grieve as the rest who have no hope. ¹⁴ For if we believe that Jesus died and rose, so also God will bring with him those who have fallen asleep through Jesus. ¹⁵ For this we tell you by the word of the Lord, that we who are alive, who are left until the coming of the Lord, will by no means precede those who have fallen asleep; ¹⁶ for the Lord himself, in command, in the voice of an archangel and in the trumpet of God, will descend from heaven, and the dead in Christ will rise first, ¹⁷ then we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And so we will always be with the Lord. ¹⁸ Therefore, encourage one another with these words.

---

Notes
- v. 15: "the word of the Lord" is used to indicate an authoritative revelation or teaching, not a direct quote from Jesus. The nature of this "word" is not specified in the text.
- v. 16: "the trumpet of God" may be metaphorical or refer to a literal trumpet call, but the text does not clarify. The translation remains neutral.
