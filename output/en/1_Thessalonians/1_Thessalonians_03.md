# 1 Thessalonians 3

¹ Therefore, we no longer endured being left alone in Athens, ² and we sent Timothy, our brother and coworker in God’s gospel of Christ, to you, in order to strengthen you and comfort you regarding your faith, ³ so that no one would be shaken by these trials. For you yourselves know that this is the reason we are here: ⁴ for when we were with you, we foretold to you that we would be persecuted, just as it happened, and as you know. ⁵ Therefore, I no longer endured it and sent him to find out about your faith, in case the one who tempts has tempted you and our labor is in vain. ⁶ But now, when Timothy came to us from you and brought us good news about your faith and love, and also that you always have good memories of us, longing to see us just as we long to see you, ⁷ we were comforted, brothers, in every need and trial of ours because of your faith, ⁸ for now we live because you stand firm in the Lord. ⁹ For what thanksgiving can we offer to God in return for all the joy we have in you before our God, ¹⁰ night and day praying most earnestly to see your face and to complete the deficiencies of your faith? ¹¹ May God the Father of us and the Lord Jesus direct our way to you; ¹² and may the Lord enrich you and cause you to overflow with love for one another and for all, just as we also do for you, ¹³ in order to strengthen your hearts without blemish in holiness before God your Father when the Lord Jesus comes with all his holy ones.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "overflow with love" reflects the force of πλεονάσαι and περισσεύσαι, both of which imply abundance and exceeding measure.
- v. 13: "strengthen your hearts" is a translation of στηρίξαι ὑμῶν τὰς καρδίας, a common idiom for firm resolve or steadfastness.
