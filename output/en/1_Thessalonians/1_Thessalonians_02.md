# 1 Thessalonians 2

¹ For you yourselves know, brothers, our entrance among you was not in vain, ² but after enduring and being insulted, as you know, in Philippi, we spoke boldly in our God to you the gospel of God in much struggle. ³ For our encouragement does not come from deceit, nor from uncleanness, nor from guile, ⁴ but as we have been tested by God to be entrusted with the gospel, so we speak, not to please people but God, who examines our hearts. ⁵ For we have never flattered in words, as you know, nor have we pretended greed, God is the witness, ⁶ nor have we sought glory from human beings, from you or from others, ⁷ although we have the right to be burdensome, as apostles of Christ. But we were gentle among you, as a nurse tenderly cherishes her own children. ⁸ Thus, being affectionately fond of you, we are eager to give to you not only the gospel of God, but also our very lives, because you have become dear to us. ⁹ Remember, brothers, our labor and toil: working night and day so as not to be a burden to any of you, we proclaimed to you the gospel of God. ¹⁰ You are witnesses, and God is too, how holy, just, and blameless we were among you who believe, ¹¹ just as you know how we treated each one of you as a father treats his own children, ¹² encouraging, exhorting, and testifying, that you might walk worthily of God, who has called you into His own kingdom and glory. ¹³ And for this reason we give thanks to God without ceasing, because receiving the message of hearing from us, you accepted not the word of men but the word of God, which also works in you who believe. ¹⁴ For you, brothers, have become imitators of the assemblies of God in Judea in Christ Jesus, for you have suffered the same things from your own countrymen as they have from the Jews, ¹⁵ who also killed the Lord Jesus and the prophets, and drove out us, and are not pleasing to God, and are adversaries to all people, ¹⁶ hindering us from speaking to the Gentiles so that they might be saved, in order to fill up their sins always. But their wrath has come to the end. ¹⁷ But we, brothers, being orphans from you for a short while in person, not in heart, have been all the more eager to see your face with great longing. ¹⁸ For we wanted to come to you, I, Paul, once and again, and Satan blocked us. ¹⁹ For what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting before our Lord Jesus at His coming, if not you? ²⁰ For you are our glory and our joy.

---

Notes
- v. 16: "fill up their sins always" reflects the force of "πληρῶσαι αὐτῶν τὰς ἁμαρτίας πάντοτε," which is idiomatic in this context.
