# Ezekiel 37

¹ The hand of the LORD was on me, and He led me out in the wind of the LORD and placed me in the middle of the valley, which was full of dry bones. ² He led me around among them in a circle, and behold, there were many indeed on the face of the valley, and they were very dry. ³ He said to me, "Son of man, can these bones live?" I said, "O Lord, you know." ⁴ Then He said to me, "Prophesy to these bones and say to them, 'Dry bones, hear the word of the LORD.'" ⁵ "This is what the Lord, the LORD, says to these bones: I will bring breath into you and make you live. ⁶ I will place sinews on you, and raise flesh over you, and cover you with skin, and I will put breath in you and make you live. Then you will know that I am the LORD." ⁷ I prophesied as I was commanded. And it came to pass, when I prophesied, that there was a sound, like a noise, and the bones came together, each to its bone. ⁸ I looked, and behold, sinews were on them, and flesh had grown, and skin covered them, but there was no breath in them. ⁹ Then He said to me, "Prophesy, O son of man, to the breath. Prophesy and say to the breath, 'This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Come from the four winds, O breath, and breathe into these slain, and make them live.'" ¹⁰ I prophesied as He had directed me, and the breath came into them, and they lived, and stood up on their feet, a very great force. ¹¹ Then He said to me, "Son of man, these bones are all the house of Israel. They say, 'Our bones are dried up, and our hope is lost. We are cut off.'" ¹² Therefore prophesy and say to them, "This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Behold, I will open your graves and bring you up from your graves, my people. I will bring you back to the land of Israel. ¹³ Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I open your graves and bring you up from your graves, my people. ¹⁴ I will put My breath in you, and you will live, and I will place you on your land. Then you will know that I am the LORD. I have spoken, and I will do it, says the Lord." ¹⁵ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹⁶ "And you, son of man, take for yourself a stick and write on it, 'For Judah and for the children of Israel his companions.' Take another stick and write on it, 'For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim and all the house of Israel his companions.' ¹⁷ Bring them close to each other for yourself into one stick, and they will become one in your hand." ¹⁸ "And when your people, the children of your people, say to you, 'Will you not tell us what these are to you?' ¹⁹ say to them, 'This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Behold, I will take the stick of Joseph that is in the hand of Ephraim and the tribes of Israel his companions, and I will place them on it—the stick of Judah—and make them one stick, and they will become one in My hand.'" ²⁰ "The sticks on which you write will be in your hand before their eyes. ²¹ And say to them, 'This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Behold, I will take the children of Israel from among the nations where they have gone, and I will gather them from all around and bring them into their land. ²² I will make them one nation in the land, in the mountains of Israel. One king will be their king for them, and they will no longer be two nations, nor will they ever again be divided into two kingdoms. ²³ They will no longer defile themselves with their idols, nor with their abominations, nor with any of their transgressions. I will save them from all their dwelling places where they sinned, and I will purify them. They will be My people, and I will be their God. ²⁴ My servant David will be king over them, and one shepherd will be for them all. They will walk in My judgments and keep My statutes and do them. ²⁵ They will dwell on the land that I gave to My servant Jacob, the land where their fathers lived. They and their children and their children's children will dwell there forever, and David My servant will be their prince forever. ²⁶ I will make a covenant of peace with them. It will be an eternal covenant with them. I will multiply them and will set My sanctuary among them forever. ²⁷ My dwelling place will be with them, and I will be their God, and they will be My people. ²⁸ Then the nations will know that I am the LORD who sanctifies Israel, when My sanctuary is among them forever."

---

Notes
- v. 14: "I have spoken, and I will do it" is a direct rendering of the phrase "דִּבַּרְתִּי וְעָשִׂיתִי" which is a strong declaration of divine resolve.
- v. 19: "the stick of Joseph that is in the hand of Ephraim" is a direct rendering of "עֵץ יוֹסֵף אֲשֶׁר בְּיַד־אֶפְרַיִם" to preserve the metaphor and the reference to Ephraim's leadership in the Josephite tribal grouping.
- v. 26: "a covenant of peace" is a direct translation of "בְּרִית שָׁלוֹם" to reflect the covenant's purpose and nature.
- v. 28: "when My sanctuary is among them forever" is a direct translation of "בִּהְיוֹת מִקְדָּשִׁי בְּתוֹכָם לְעוֹלָֽם" to emphasize the permanence and presence of the sanctuary among the people.
