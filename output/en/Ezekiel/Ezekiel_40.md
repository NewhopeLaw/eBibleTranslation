# Ezekiel 40

¹ In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, in the first month, on the tenth day of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city was struck down, on that very day the hand of the LORD came upon me, and he brought me there. ² He showed me visions of God, leading me into the land of Israel and placing me on a very high mountain, with something like a city built on the south side. ³ He brought me there, and there was a man whose appearance was like that of bronze, with a measuring line of linen in his hand and a measuring rod. He was standing at the gate. ⁴ The man spoke to me, "Son of man, look with your eyes and listen with your ears, and set your heart to all that I am showing you, for you have been brought here to show these things to the house of Israel." ⁵ I saw a wall outside the house, all around it. The man held the measuring rod, which was six cubits long, with a span. He measured the width of the structure, one rod wide and one rod high. ⁶ He went into the gate facing the east and climbed its steps, measuring the threshold of the gate, one rod wide and one rod wide for the threshold. ⁷ The doorway was one rod in length and one rod in width, and between the doorposts was five cubits. The threshold of the gate, from the inner courtyard to the house, was one rod wide. ⁸ He measured the inner courtyard of the gate, one rod wide. ⁹ He measured the inner courtyard of the gate, eight cubits in length and two cubits in width, and the inner courtyard of the gate, from the house. ¹⁰ The doorposts of the gate, facing the east, were three on one side and three on the other, with the same measure for the three, and the same for the inner doorposts on either side. ¹¹ He measured the width of the gate opening, ten cubits, and the length of the gate, thirteen cubits. ¹² There was a border in front of the doorposts, one cubit on one side and one cubit on the other, and the doorpost was six cubits on one side and six cubits on the other. ¹³ He measured the gate from the top of the doorpost to the top of the gate, twenty-five cubits wide, with one opening facing another. ¹⁴ He made the inner doorposts sixty cubits in width, and the inner courtyard of the gate, all around. ¹⁵ On the front of the gate, the porch of the inner courtyard was fifty cubits wide. ¹⁶ There were closed windows on the doorposts and on the inner courtyard of the gate, all around, and similarly on the inner doorposts and windows all around, and palm trees on either side of the doorposts. ¹⁷ He led me into the outer courtyard, and there were colonnades and a pavement for the courtyard, thirty colonnades on the pavement. ¹⁸ The pavement was adjacent to the sides of the gates, matching the length of the gates, the lower pavement. ¹⁹ He measured the width of the outer courtyard in front of the gate, from the threshold of the inner courtyard outward, a hundred cubits to the east and the north. ²⁰ The gate facing the north of the outer courtyard, he measured its length and width. ²¹ Its doorposts were three on one side and three on the other, with doorposts and inner doorposts, and it matched the measure of the gate facing the east, fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. ²² Its windows and inner doorposts and palm trees matched the measure of the gate facing the east, and it had seven steps ascending to it, with inner doorposts before them. ²³ The gate of the inner courtyard opposite the gate to the north was like the gate facing the east. He measured the courtyard from gate to gate, a hundred cubits. ²⁴ He led me toward the south, and there was a gate facing the south. He measured its doorposts and inner doorposts according to these measurements. ²⁵ Its windows and inner doorposts were all around, like these windows, fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. ²⁶ It had seven steps ascending to it, with inner doorposts before them, and palm trees on either side of the doorposts. ²⁷ The gate of the inner courtyard facing the south was measured from gate to gate, a hundred cubits. ²⁸ He led me into the inner courtyard through the southern gate and measured the southern gate according to these measurements. ²⁹ Its doorposts and inner doorposts and inner doorposts matched these measurements, and its windows and inner doorposts all around were fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. ³⁰ The inner doorposts were all around, fifty cubits long and five cubits wide. ³¹ The inner doorposts were connected to the outer courtyard, with palm trees on the doorposts and eight steps leading to it. ³² He led me into the gate facing the east and measured it according to these measurements. ³³ Its doorposts and inner doorposts and inner doorposts matched these measurements, and its windows and inner doorposts all around were fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. ³⁴ The inner doorposts were connected to the outer courtyard, with palm trees on the doorposts on either side and eight steps leading to it. ³⁵ He led me into the gate facing the north and measured it according to these measurements. ³⁶ Its doorposts and inner doorposts and inner doorposts and windows all around matched these measurements, fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. ³⁷ The inner doorposts were connected to the outer courtyard, with palm trees on the doorposts on either side and eight steps leading to it. ³⁸ There were colonnades and doorways on the doorposts of the second gate, where the burnt offering would be placed. ³⁹ In the inner courtyard of the gate were two tables on one side and two tables on the other, for slaughtering the burnt offerings, the sin offerings, and the guilt offerings. ⁴⁰ At the side of the inner courtyard facing the north, near the gate, were two tables, and on the other side of the inner courtyard were two tables. ⁴¹ Four tables on one side and four tables on the other side, at the corners of the gate, eight tables in total, on which the sacrifices would be placed. ⁴² Four tables for the burnt offerings, made of polished stone, one and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one cubit high. On them were the vessels in which the burnt offerings and sacrifices would be placed. ⁴³ The thresholds were one span wide, prepared inside the house all around and with the meat of the sacrifice on the tables. ⁴⁴ Outside the inner gate were rooms in the inner courtyard, facing the south side of the gate to the north, and one on the north side of the gate, facing the south. ⁴⁵ He said to me, "The room facing the south is for the priests who guard the charge of the house." ⁴⁶ "The room facing the north is for the priests who guard the charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, the Levites who come close to the LORD to serve him." ⁴⁷ He measured the courtyard, a hundred cubits long and a hundred cubits wide, square, with the altar in front of the house. ⁴⁸ He brought me into the porch of the house and measured the porch's doorposts, five cubits on one side and five cubits on the other. The width of the gate was three cubits on one side and three cubits on the other. ⁴⁹ The length of the porch was twenty cubits and the width twelve cubits, and there were steps leading to it, with pillars on either side of the doorposts.

Notes
- v. 49: The mention of "pillars on either side of the doorposts" is inferred from the Hebrew structure, which does not use the word for "pillars" directly but implies their presence through context.
