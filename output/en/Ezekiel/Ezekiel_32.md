# Ezekiel 32

¹ In the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the first of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, take up a lamentation over Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and say to him: You compared yourself to a lion among the nations, and to a sea monster in the seas, but you trampled your rivers and stirred the water with your feet, and your streams overflowed. ³ Thus says the Lord: I will cast a net over you in a great assembly of peoples, and I will lift you up in my hook. ⁴ I will stretch you on the ground, fill the plains with your carcass, and set all the birds of the sky upon you, and fill the land with beasts of every kind. ⁵ I will scatter your flesh on the mountains, and fill the valleys with your blood. ⁶ I will water the land with your flowing blood from the mountains, and the ravines will be filled with your overflow. ⁷ When you are destroyed, I will cover the sky and darken its stars; I will veile the sun with a cloud, and the moon will not shine. ⁸ All the luminaries of the sky I will darken over you, and I will bring darkness over your land," says the Lord GOD. ⁹ "I will provoke the hearts of many peoples when I lead your captives to the nations, to lands they do not know. ¹⁰ And I will make many nations mourn over you, and their kings will be stunned, when I brandish my sword against their faces, waiting for the day of your fall. ¹¹ For thus says the Lord: The sword of the king of Babylon shall come to you. ¹² With the swords of the mighty, I will cast down your might. Plunderers from every nation will fall upon you, and they will destroy the pride of Egypt, and all her strength will be broken. ¹³ I will destroy all her livestock from the many waters, and no human foot will disturb them again, and the hoof of beasts will not trample them. ¹⁴ Then her waters will be still, and her rivers will flow like oil," says the Lord. ¹⁵ "When I make Egypt a waste and the land desolate because of the fullness of its inhabitants, when I scatter all who live in it, they will know that I am the LORD. ¹⁶ A lamentation will be made, and the daughters of the nations will lament for her. The daughters of the nations will lament for Egypt and for all her strength," says the Lord GOD. ¹⁷ In the twelfth year, in the first month, on the fifteenth of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹⁸ "Son of man, wail for the might of Egypt, and send down her daughters to the depths of the earth, to those who have gone down into the pit. ¹⁹ To whom have you looked with envy, O Egypt, fallen among the slain? ²⁰ They will fall with you in the middle of those who are struck down by the sword, and all their might will lie with you. ²¹ And they will speak to you from the depths of the grave: 'To whom are you superior? You have gone down to lie with the uncircumcised, among those struck down by the sword.' ²² There, Assyria and all her assembly will be. All of them are the slain, fallen by the sword; their graves are in the depths of the pit, and their assembly surrounds their grave. All of them are the slain, struck down by the sword, ²³ who brought their terror into the land of the living. ²⁴ There, Elam and all her might will be. All of them are the slain, struck down by the sword, who went down uncircumcised into the depths of the earth, who brought their terror into the land of the living. They took their punishment with those who descend into the pit, ²⁵ in the middle of the slain. ²⁶ There, Meshech and Tubal and all their might will be. All of them are the slain, uncircumcised, struck down by the sword, who brought their terror into the land of the living. ²⁷ And they shall rest with the ancient giants who fell long ago, who descended into Sheol with their weapons of war, and placed their swords under their heads. Their iniquities will be upon their bones, for they caused terror in the land of the living. ²⁸ And you too will rest with the uncircumcised, among those struck down by the sword. ²⁹ There, the rulers of Assyria who gave their might to the sword will be. They will rest with the slain, among those who descend into the pit. ³⁰ There, the rulers of the north, the commanders of Assyria, who descended struck down, with their terror and might, will rest uncircumcised, among those struck down by the sword. They carried their punishment with those who descend into the pit. ³¹ These will Pharaoh see, and he will be comforted over all his might," says the Lord GOD. ³² "For I have caused his terror to fall in the land of the living, and he will rest among the uncircumcised, with those struck down by the sword, Pharaoh and all his might," says the Lord GOD.

Notes
- v. 2: "trampled your rivers and stirred the water with your feet" — the Hebrew uses poetic imagery of Pharaoh's dominance over the waters, which is rendered in a natural way without archaic phrasing.
- v. 21: "To whom are you superior?" — the Hebrew uses a wordplay on the root *gāḇāh* ("exalted") and *gōḇāh* ("superior"), which is preserved in the question's phrasing.
- v. 26: "Meshech and Tubal" — these are proper names from the ancient Near East, and are rendered as such.
- v. 29: "the rulers of Assyria" — the Hebrew uses the word *nāsîm* ("rulers") to refer to Assyrian leaders, which is clearly translated.
- v. 32: "the land of the living" — the Hebrew *erets hachayyim* is a fixed expression referring to the world of the living, and is rendered as such to preserve the contrast with Sheol.
