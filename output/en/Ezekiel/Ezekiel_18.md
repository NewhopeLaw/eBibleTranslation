# Ezekiel 18

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² What is this proverb you have among yourselves in the land of Israel: "The fathers ate sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge"? ³ I live, says the Lord GOD, if this proverb is still current among the people of Israel. ⁴ For all lives are mine. Just as the life of the father, so is the life of the son. The soul that sins is the one that will die. ⁵ If a man is righteous and does what is just and right, ⁶ he does not feast on the mountains, nor does his eye look to the things of the house of Israel, nor does he defile his neighbor's wife, nor does he draw near to a woman who is in her uncleanness. ⁷ He does not oppress anyone, returns the pledge of the one who is in debt, and does not steal. He gives his bread to the hungry and covers the naked with a garment. ⁸ He does not give money on interest, nor does he take a profit. He keeps his hand from wrongdoing and executes a just judgment between a person and his neighbor. ⁹ He walks in my statutes and keeps my judgments to do them. He is righteous, and he will live, says the Lord GOD. ¹⁰ If he has a son who is violent, a shedder of blood, and does any of these things, ¹¹ and though he did not walk in the way of his father, who was righteous, but instead feasted on the mountains and defiled his neighbor's wife, ¹² he oppressed the poor and the needy, stole, did not return the pledge, and looked to idols, committing abominations, ¹³ he gave money on interest and took profit. He will not live. He has done all these abominations; he will die, and his blood will be on his own head. ¹⁴ Suppose he has another son who sees all the sins his father committed, is afraid, and does none of these things. ¹⁵ He does not feast on the mountains, nor does his eye look to the things of the house of Israel, nor does he defile his neighbor's wife. ¹⁶ He does not oppress anyone, does not take a pledge, does not steal. He gives his bread to the hungry and covers the naked with a garment. ¹⁷ He keeps his hand from wrongdoing, takes no interest or profit, does what is just, and walks in my statutes. He will not die for his father's sin. He will live. ¹⁸ But if his father was a man who oppressed and robbed, did evil in the midst of his people, and died for his sin, ¹⁹ you may say, "Why did the son not suffer the fate of the father, since the son did what is just and right and kept all my statutes?" He will live. ²⁰ The soul that sins will die. A son will not bear the iniquity of the father, nor will a father bear the iniquity of the son. The righteousness of the righteous will be on him, and the wickedness of the wicked will be on him. ²¹ If a wicked person turns from all his sins that he has committed and keeps all my statutes and does what is just and right, he will live and will not die. ²² All his sins he committed will no longer be remembered. In the righteousness he has done, he will live. ²³ Do I take pleasure in the death of the wicked? says the Lord GOD. Rather, would they not turn from their way and live? ²⁴ If a righteous person turns from his righteousness and does all the abominations that the wicked person does, would all his righteousness be remembered? He will die for the sin he committed and for the abomination he did. He will die. ²⁵ You may say, "The way of the LORD is not fair." Listen, house of Israel. Is my way not fair? Are not your ways unfair? ²⁶ If a righteous person turns from his righteousness and does wrong, he will die for the wrong he did. He will die. ²⁷ If a wicked person turns from his wickedness and does what is just and right, he will preserve his life. ²⁸ He turns from all the sins he committed, and he will live. He will not die. ²⁹ You may say, "The way of the LORD is not fair." Listen, house of Israel. Is my way not fair? Are not your ways unfair? ³⁰ Therefore, I will judge each of you according to your ways, house of Israel, says the Lord GOD. Turn and return from all your sins, so that iniquity will not be a stumbling block for you. ³¹ Cast away all your transgressions, which you have committed against me, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. Why should you die, house of Israel? ³² For I take no pleasure in the death of the one who dies, says the Lord GOD. Turn, and live.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "the soul that sins is the one that will die" reflects the Hebrew emphasis on individual responsibility.
- v. 10: The term "violent" (פָּרִיץ) is used to convey the rebellious and destructive nature of the son.
- v. 13: "blood will be on his own head" is a fixed idiom meaning personal accountability.
- v. 30: "stumbling block" (מִכְשׁוֹל) is the standard rendering for this context.
- v. 31: "make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit" captures the idea of inner transformation, common in prophetic literature.
- No other real choices or uncertainties in the chapter.
