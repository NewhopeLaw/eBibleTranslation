# Ezekiel 42

¹ He led me into the outer courtyard along the northern path and brought me into the chamber facing the section and the building toward the north. ² The length of the chamber was a hundred cubits, and its width fifty cubits. ³ Opposite the twenty cubits of the inner courtyard and opposite the pavement of the outer courtyard, they were built in three tiers. ⁴ The walkway in front of the chambers was ten cubits wide, facing the inner courtyard, and its length was a hundred cubits. Their doors faced north. ⁵ The upper chambers were shorter, for they lacked the projections of the lower and middle chambers in their construction. ⁶ For they were triple in number, yet had no columns like the columns of the outer courtyards; thus they extended beyond the lower and middle chambers from the ground. ⁷ The wall facing outside, adjacent to the outer courtyard, was fifty cubits long. ⁸ For the length of the chambers facing the outer courtyard was fifty cubits, and the total facing the temple was a hundred cubits. ⁹ The doors of these chambers led inward from the east side, as one entered from the outer courtyard. ¹⁰ The width of the outer wall followed the pattern of the partition and the building, with chambers facing it. ¹¹ The walkway before them was like the appearance of the chambers facing north, matching their length, width, exits, and design, as well as their doors. ¹² The doors of the chambers facing south were at the head of the walkway, opening toward the inner courtyard wall, as one approached. ¹³ He said to me, "The chambers facing north and south, which are opposite the partition, are the holy chambers where the priests who are near the LORD will eat the holy of holies. There they will place the holy of holies, the offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering, for the place is holy." ¹⁴ The priests must not go out from the holy place into the outer courtyard, so that they remain holy in their service. They must not wear their garments of service when leaving, for they are holy. Instead, they must put on other garments before coming near the people. ¹⁵ He completed measuring the inner house and led me out through the gate that faces east, then measured the entire perimeter of the complex. ¹⁶ He measured the east side with the measuring rod, five hundred cubits in circumference. ¹⁷ He measured the north side, five hundred cubits in the measuring rod. ¹⁸ He measured the south side, five hundred cubits in the measuring rod. ¹⁹ He measured the west side, five hundred cubits in the measuring rod. ²⁰ He measured the four sides as a whole, surrounding it with a wall, five hundred cubits in length and five hundred cubits in width, to distinguish between the holy and the common.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "the holy of holies" is a translation of "קָדְשֵׁי הַקֳּדָשִׁים," a term used to describe the most sacred items, not the innermost sanctuary itself.
- v. 14: "the priests who are near the LORD" reflects the meaning of "כֹּהֲנִים אֲשֶׁר־קְרוֹבִים לַיהוָה," referring to the priests who have a closer relationship with God.
- v. 16–19: The measurements for each side are given as "five hundred cubits in the measuring rod," where "in the measuring rod" is added to clarify the unit of measurement.
