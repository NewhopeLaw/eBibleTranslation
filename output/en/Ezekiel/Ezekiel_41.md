# Ezekiel 41

¹ He brought me into the sanctuary and measured the porch. It was six cubits wide on one side and six cubits wide on the other, the width of the entrance to the house. ² The width of the doorway was ten cubits, and the sideposts of the doorway were five cubits on one side and five cubits on the other. He measured the length of the porch to be forty cubits and its width twenty cubits. ³ He led me into the inner courtyard and measured the sidepost of the doorway at two cubits, the doorway at six cubits, and the sideposts of the doorway at seven cubits on one side and seven cubits on the other. ⁴ He measured the length to be twenty cubits and the width twenty cubits, facing the house. He said to me, "This is the Holy of Holies." ⁵ He measured the wall of the house at six cubits. The width of the side panels was four cubits, all around the house, all around. ⁶ The side panels were placed side by side, thirty times three. There were openings in the wall of the house for the side panels, all around, so that the panels could be held in place but not attached to the house wall itself. ⁷ The upper part of the side panels grew wider toward the top, as the house itself expanded upward. The house was wider on the upper level than on the lower, and the lower level rose to meet the middle. ⁸ The height of the house, including the side panels, was equal to the length of the measuring rod—six cubits. ⁹ The outer width of the wall of the side panel was five cubits, and the inner space between the side panels of the house was also measured. ¹⁰ The space between the alcoves was twenty cubits all around the house, all around. ¹¹ The doors of the alcoves were placed one to the north and one to the south. The width of the opening in each was five cubits, all around. ¹² The projection facing the inner courtyard was like the projection toward the sea. Its width was seventy cubits, and the width of its wall was five cubits, all around. Its length was ninety cubits. ¹³ He measured the house in length at a hundred cubits, and the projection and its walls also measured a hundred cubits in length. ¹⁴ The width of the front of the house and the projection was a hundred cubits. ¹⁵ He measured the length of the projection facing the back of the house, and the projection on both sides measured a hundred cubits. The inner house and the wing of the courtyard were included in this measurement. ¹⁶ The doors, the windows with latticework, and the side panels were all around the three levels, with carved wood reaching up from the floor to the windows, and the windows were covered. ¹⁷ From above the doorway to the inner house and outside, along the entire wall, both inner and outer, there were measurements. ¹⁸ They were adorned with cherubim and palm trees, with a palm tree between each cherub. Each cherub had two faces. ¹⁹ One face of a human was toward one palm tree, and one face of a lion was toward the other palm tree, all around the entire house. ²⁰ From the floor up to above the doorway, the cherubim and palm trees were carved into the walls of the temple. ²¹ The temple was square, and the appearance of the Holy of Holies was the same as the appearance of the sanctuary. ²² The altar was made of wood, three cubits high and two cubits long, with two cubits in width. It had horns, and its base and walls were also made of wood. He said to me, "This is the table that is before the LORD." ²³ The temple had two doors, and the Holy of Holies had two doors. ²⁴ Each of the two doors had two inner doors, two doors for one and two doors for the other. ²⁵ Carvings were made on them, like the cherubim and palm trees on the doors of the temple, as they were carved on the walls of the holy place, and wooden carvings were on the side of the porch, from the outside. ²⁶ There were latticed windows, and the carvings extended from one side to the other of the porch, as well as the side panels of the house and the beams.

Notes  
- v. 5: "four cubits" for "רֹחַב הַצֵּלָע אַרְבַּע אַמּוֹת" is consistent with the glossary and context.  
- v. 8: "equal to the length of the measuring rod" is a translation of "מְלוֹ הַקָּנֶה שֵׁשׁ אַמּוֹת", where "קָנֶה" is a measuring rod, which is six cubits long.  
- v. 10: "the space between the alcoves" is a rendering of "בֵּין הַלְּשָׁכוֹת", where "לְשָׁכוֹת" refers to alcoves or side panels.  
- v. 22: "table" is used for "שֻׁלְחָן", which here refers to a table rather than a literal table in English, as the context implies a sacred furnishing.
