# Ezekiel 21

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, face toward the south and prophesy against the forest of the Negev. ³ Say to the forest of the Negev, 'Listen to the word of the LORD. This is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to kindle a fire in you, and it will consume every green tree and every dry tree. It will not be quenched. The flame will burn, the fire will blaze, and all faces will be scorched from the Negev to the north.' ⁴ All flesh will see that I am the LORD. I have lit it, and it will not be quenched." ⁵ Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD, are they saying about me, 'He is just a talebearer'?" ⁶ And the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ⁷ "Son of man, face toward Jerusalem and prophesy against the holy places. ⁸ Say to the land of Israel, 'This is what the LORD says: Here I come, and I will bring my sword out of its sheath, and I will cut off from you the righteous and the wicked. ⁹ Because I will cut off from you the righteous and the wicked, my sword will come out of its sheath against all flesh, from the Negev to the north. ¹⁰ All flesh will know that I am the LORD. I have drawn my sword out of its sheath, and it will not return again." ¹¹ "And you, son of man, groan with the breaking of your loins and with bitterness, groan before their eyes. ¹² And when they ask you, 'Why are you groaning?' you will say, 'Because it has come, and every heart will grow faint, all hands will be powerless, every spirit will be crushed, and every knee will become like water. It has come, says the Lord GOD.'" ¹³ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹⁴ "Son of man, prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: A sword, a sword, sharpened and made bright, ¹⁵ sharpened to slay, made bright to flash, to be held by the hand, to be a sword of judgment. It is a sword of judgment for the house of Israel. ¹⁶ It is given to be held by the hand, to be shaken in the grasp. It is sharpened, a sword sharpened for slaughter. It is a sword of judgment, a sword of judgment for the house of Israel. ¹⁷ Cry out and wail, son of man, for it is the sword of my people, it is upon all the rulers of Israel. Their people have fallen by the sword, so strike your thigh for my people. ¹⁸ For if the sword is to be sharpened, and will it not be used? says the Lord GOD.'" ¹⁹ "And you, son of man, prophesy and strike your hand against your hand, so that the sword may be doubled. The sword, the third sword, is for those who are to be killed. It is the sword of judgment, a great sword that is sharpened to pierce. ²⁰ It is to confuse the mind, to multiply the stumbling blocks at every gate. I have set its edge to slay, its brightness to shine, to be held by the hand to be sharpened for slaughter. ²¹ Turn to the right, set your face toward the right, where you see the place your face is prepared for." ²² "And I myself will strike my hand against my hand, and pour out my wrath. I am the LORD, I have spoken." ²³ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ²⁴ "And you, son of man, set for yourself two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to come. From one land they will go out, both of them. A man will set a signpost at the head of the road of a city, he will set it. ²⁵ One road will lead to the great city of the sons of Ammon, and the other to Jerusalem in the siege. ²⁶ For the king of Babylon has stood at the head of the two roads, to cast lots, to ask with arrows, to consult the liver. ²⁷ To his right hand is the casting of the lot for Jerusalem, to set a siege wall, to open the mouth in a shout, to raise a voice with a trumpet, to set a siege wall on the gates, to cast up a mound, to build a siege ramp. ²⁸ But it will be to them a false casting of the lot in their eyes; they will take oaths with false oaths, and it will be to bring about their capture. ²⁹ Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have remembered your iniquities in the time of your exposure, in the manifestation of your sins, in the sight of all your deeds, because you have remembered in the palm of your hand, you will be captured. ³⁰ And you, wicked one, ruler of Israel, who has come on the day of the end in the time of iniquity, ³¹ this is what the Lord GOD says: Remove the diadem, take off the crown. It is not for this, it is not for this. Exalt the low, and bring down the high. ³² Wickedness, wickedness, wickedness. I will make it so, and it will not be until the one to whom it belongs comes, and I will give it to him." ³³ "And you, son of man, prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says to the sons of Ammon and to their reproach. Say, "Sword, sword, drawn for slaughter, made bright for confusion, to be held by the hand. ³⁴ In vain you have had visions; in falsehood you have cast lots, to give you over to the necks of the slain wicked ones, who have come in the time of the end." ³⁵ Return to your sheath in the place where you were made, in the land of your buyers. I will judge you there. ³⁶ And I will pour out my wrath on you with fire, my fury will consume you, and I will give you into the hands of violent men who destroy. ³⁷ You will be fuel for the fire, your blood will be in the heart of the land. You will not be remembered, for I am the LORD. I have spoken."

Notes  
- v. 5: "He is just a talebearer" — the Hebrew "מְמַשֵּׁל מְשָׁלִים" (literally "he is a talebearer, a talebearer") reflects a rhetorical question about whether the prophet is merely spreading stories.  
- v. 11: "groan with the breaking of your loins" — the phrase "שִׁבְרוֹן מָתְנַיִם" (literally "breaking of the loins") is a metaphor for deep emotional distress.  
- v. 12: "every knee will become like water" — the Hebrew "תֵּלַכְנָה מַיִם" is a poetic expression for weakness or collapse.  
- v. 14-16: "sharpened and made bright" — the metaphor of the sword is central to the chapter, and the terms used for its sharpening and brightness are repeated for emphasis.  
- v. 27: "to set a siege wall" — the Hebrew "לִפְתֹּחַ פֶּה" is translated contextually as "to open the mouth" (i.e., to begin a shout or call to arms).  
- v. 31: "Remove the diadem, take off the crown" — this is a reference to the removal of leadership or honor, using the imagery of royal insignia.
