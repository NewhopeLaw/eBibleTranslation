# Ezekiel 1

¹ In the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, I was among the exiles on the bank of the river Chebar, when the heavens opened and I saw visions of God. ² On the fifth of the month—this was the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin— ³ the word of the LORD came to Ezekiel, son of Buzi the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans, by the river Chebar. And the hand of the LORD was upon him. ⁴ I looked, and behold, a storm wind was coming from the north, a great cloud, and fire flashing within it, and a brightness around it; and within the fire, something like the gleam of lightning. ⁵ Within it were forms of four living creatures, and this was their appearance: they looked like human beings. ⁶ Each had four faces, and each had four wings. ⁷ Their legs were straight, and their feet were like the paw of a calf, gleaming like burnished bronze. ⁸ Their hands were human hands, reaching out beneath their wings on all four sides; and their faces and their wings were on the four of them. ⁹ Their wings were joined to one another; they did not turn when they moved, but each went straight ahead. ¹⁰ Their faces were this way: the face of a human on the right side of the four, the face of a lion on the right side of the four, the face of an ox on the left side of the four, and the face of an eagle on the left side of the four. ¹¹ Their faces and their wings were spread out above them—each had two wings joined to its neighbor and two covering their bodies. ¹² Each moved straight ahead wherever the spirit would go, without turning when they moved. ¹³ The appearance of the living creatures was like burning coals of fire, like torches, and a brightness flashing between them, and fire was their flame, and from the fire came lightning. ¹⁴ And the living creatures moved like a flash of lightning. ¹⁵ I looked at the living creatures, and behold, there was a wheel on the earth next to the four of them, one wheel for each of the four. ¹⁶ The appearance of the wheels and their operation was like the gleam of a beryl, and the four of them had the same appearance, and the operation of the wheels was like a wheel within a wheel. ¹⁷ They moved on their four sides, without turning when they moved. ¹⁸ Their frames were high and awesome, and their frames were full of eyes, all around the four of them. ¹⁹ And when the living creatures moved, the wheels moved with them; and when the living creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose. ²⁰ Wherever the spirit would go to move, there the wheels would move, and the wheels rose with them, for the spirit of life was in the wheels. ²¹ When they moved, they moved; when they stood, they stood; and when they rose from the ground, they rose with them, for the spirit of life was in the wheels. ²² Over the heads of the living creatures was something like a vault, shining like crystal, stretched out above them. ²³ Beneath the vault their wings were spread out—each to its neighbor, two covering their bodies and two covering their bodies. ²⁴ I heard the sound of their wings, like the sound of many waters, like the voice of the Almighty. When they moved, it was like the sound of a chariot rushing forward, and when they stood still, their wings would settle. ²⁵ And there was a voice from above the vault, which was above their heads, when they stood still. ²⁶ Above the vault, which was above their heads, there was something like the appearance of a sapphire stone, like the shape of a throne, and on the throne was a figure like a human being above it. ²⁷ I saw something like the gleam of bronze, like the appearance of fire, all around him from the waist upward and downward, and around him was a brightness. ²⁸ Like the appearance of a rainbow in the clouds on a rainy day—so was the appearance of the brightness surrounding him. It was the appearance of the glory of the LORD, and I saw it and fell on my face, and I heard a voice speaking.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "When God set about creating" has been omitted per Genesis 1:1 instruction.  
- v. 10: The faces are assigned directionally but not necessarily to individual creatures, so the translation avoids assigning them to specific beings.  
- v. 16: "like a wheel within a wheel" reflects the Hebrew's emphasis on the nested, interlocking motion.  
- v. 28: The comparison to a rainbow is rendered as an explanatory simile, not an object.
