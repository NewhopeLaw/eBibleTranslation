# Ezekiel 36

¹ You, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel and say to them, O mountains of Israel, listen to the word of the LORD. ² This is what the Lord GOD says: Because an enemy spoke against you, saying, "Aha! We will take it as an eternal waste for ourselves," ³ therefore prophesy and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Because you were despised and hated by those around you, becoming a possession for the nations remaining, and you have stirred up speech with your tongue and became a reproach to the peoples, ⁴ therefore, O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the LORD. This is what the Lord GOD says to the mountains, the hills, the ravines, the wadis, the wastelands, the ruins, and the deserted cities, which have become a possession and a disgrace to the remaining nations around, ⁵ therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Surely, with fire from my jealousy I have spoken against the remaining nations, and against Edom entirely, for they took my land for themselves with full-hearted joy, with reckless hearts to cast it out as a waste. ⁶ Therefore prophesy about the land of Israel and say to the mountains, the hills, the ravines, and the wadis: This is what the Lord GOD says: I have come in my jealousy and in my wrath to speak, because you have borne the reproach of nations. ⁷ Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I raise my hand that the nations around you will themselves bear their reproach. ⁸ But you, O mountains of Israel, your vineyards and your fruit will be eaten by my people, for they are coming back. ⁹ For I am coming to you, and I will look upon you, and you will be tilled and sown. ¹⁰ I will multiply people upon you, the whole house of Israel, to the end. The cities will be inhabited, and the ruined places will be rebuilt. ¹¹ I will multiply people and animals upon you, and I will cause you to dwell as in your past and as in the days before you. Then you will know that I am the LORD. ¹² I will cause people to come upon you, my people Israel, and they will possess you. You will become a possession for them, and you will no longer be barren to them. ¹³ This is what the Lord GOD says: Because they said to you, "You devour people and you have made the nations your prey," ¹⁴ therefore you will no longer devour people, and you will no longer make the nations your prey, says the Lord GOD. ¹⁵ And no longer will the reproach of the nations be heard against you, and no longer will you bear the disgrace of the peoples, and the nations will no longer make you barren, says the Lord GOD. ¹⁶ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹⁷ Son of man, the house of Israel dwelled in their land and defiled it with their ways and with their deeds. They made it unclean as the uncleanness of a woman in her menstrual period. ¹⁸ I poured out my wrath upon them for the blood they shed on the land and for the idols with which they defiled it. ¹⁹ I scattered them among the nations, and they were scattered in the lands. I judged them according to their ways and their deeds. ²⁰ And they went into the nations where they went, and they profaned my holy name, for they said, "These are the people of the LORD, and they have gone out from his land." ²¹ And I took pity on my holy name, which the house of Israel profaned among the nations where they went. ²² Therefore say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: I do not do this for your sake, O house of Israel, but for the sake of my holy name, which you profaned among the nations where you went. ²³ And I will consecrate my great name, which was profaned among the nations, which you profaned in their midst. And the nations will know that I am the LORD, says the Lord GOD, when I consecrate myself before them in your sight. ²⁴ And I will take you out from the nations and gather you from all the lands and bring you into your land. ²⁵ I will sprinkle clean water upon you and cleanse you from all your uncleanness and from all your idols. ²⁶ I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you. I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. ²⁷ And I will put my spirit within you and cause you to walk in my statutes and to keep my decrees and do them. ²⁸ You will dwell in the land I gave to your ancestors, and you will be my people, and I will be your God. ²⁹ I will save you from all your uncleanness. I will summon grain and multiply it, and I will not let famine come upon you. ³⁰ I will multiply the fruit of the tree and the yield of the field, so that you will no longer bear the disgrace of famine among the nations. ³¹ You will remember your ways, the evil of your deeds, and the wrongs you did, and you will loathe yourselves in your own sight for your iniquities and for your abominations. ³² Not for your sake do I do this, says the Lord GOD, but let it be known to you and be ashamed and confounded for your ways, O house of Israel. ³³ This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day I cleanse you from all your iniquities, I will cause the cities to be inhabited and the ruined places will be rebuilt. ³⁴ The desolate land will be tilled, no longer a desolation in the sight of all who pass by. ³⁵ And they will say, "This once desolate land has become like the garden of Eden, and the ruined and desolate cities and those torn down now have strong walls." ³⁶ And the nations that remain around you will know that I am the LORD, for I build the ruined places and replant the desolate land. I am the LORD. I have spoken, and I will do it. ³⁷ This is what the Lord GOD says: I will also do this for the house of Israel. I will multiply them like sheep. ³⁸ Like sacred sheep, like the sheep of Jerusalem in their festivals, so will the ruined cities be full of the people of humans, and they will know that I am the LORD.

Notes
- v. 3: The Hebrew phrase "שַׁמּוֹת וְשָׁאֹף" is ambiguous but here rendered as "were despised and hated" based on context.
- v. 23: The phrase "וְיָדְעוּ הַגּוֹיִם כִּֽי־אֲנִי יְהוָֽה" is interpreted as "and the nations will know that I am the LORD" to maintain the theological emphasis.
- v. 26: "לֵב בָּשָֽׂר" is translated as "a heart of flesh" to retain the metaphor while preserving clarity.
- v. 33: "טַהֲרִי" is translated as "cleanse" to align with the context of purification.
- v. 37-38: The metaphor of "sheep" is retained as it is central to the imagery of restoration and blessing.
