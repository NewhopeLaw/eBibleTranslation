# Ezekiel 16

¹ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, declare the abominations of Jerusalem. ³ Say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says to Jerusalem: Your origin and birth were from the land of Canaan, your father an Amorite and your mother a Hittite. ⁴ On the day of your birth, your navel was not cut, you were not washed in water, you were not salted with salt, nor were you wrapped in swaddling clothes. ⁵ No one took pity on you to do any of these for you, but you were cast out into the field, your life despised on the day you were born. ⁶ And I passed by you and saw you lying in your blood. I said to you, 'Live by your blood,' and I said to you, 'Live by your blood.' ⁷ You grew like a plant in the field. You multiplied and grew strong, and you came to the cities of the land. Your breasts were full, your hair had grown, but you remained naked and shamed. ⁸ And I passed by you again and saw you at a time to be loved. I spread out my wings over you and covered your shame. I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you," says the Lord GOD, "and you became mine. ⁹ "I washed you in water, cleansed the blood from you, and anointed you with oil. ¹⁰ I clothed you in fine linen, shod you with leather, and wrapped you in silk and fine cloth. ¹¹ I adorned you with jewelry, placed bracelets on your arms and a ring on your nose. ¹² I put a nose ring on your face, earrings in your ears, and a crown of glory on your head. ¹³ You were adorned with gold and silver, your garments were of fine linen, silk, and embroidered cloth. You ate fine flour, oil, and honey. You became very beautiful and prospered like a queen. ¹⁴ And your name spread among the nations because of your beauty, for I had placed glory on you," says the Lord GOD. ¹⁵ "You trusted in your beauty and committed adultery on your name. You poured out your whoring lust on every passerby, and you became more and more promiscuous. ¹⁶ You took your fine garments and made for yourself idolatrous altars. You committed adultery with them, though they brought you no joy or fulfillment. ¹⁷ You took my jewelry of gold and silver, which I had given you, and made for yourself images. You committed adultery with them. ¹⁸ You took your embroidered garments and covered them, and you used my oil and incense in front of them. ¹⁹ My food, which I had given you—fine flour, oil, and honey—you offered in front of them as a pleasing aroma," says the Lord GOD. ²⁰ "You took your sons and daughters, whom I had given you, and sacrificed them as food. You used them as offerings in your unfaithfulness. ²¹ You even slaughtered your own children and gave them to them to eat. ²² All these acts of abomination, you did not even remember the days of your youth, when you were naked and shamed, lying in your blood. ²³ "After all your evil deeds," says the Lord GOD, ²⁴ "you built for yourself a high place and made for yourself a monument in every street. ²⁵ You built your monument at the head of every road, defiled your beauty, and spread your feet to every passerby. You multiplied your whoring. ²⁶ You committed adultery with the sons of Egypt, your neighbors, strong in flesh, and multiplied your whoring to provoke me. ²⁷ "Behold, I will stretch out my hand against you and take away your decrees. I will give you into the hands of those who hate you—daughters of the Philistines who despise you and act treacherously. ²⁸ You committed adultery with the daughters of Assyria, yet you were never satisfied. You committed adultery and were never satisfied. ²⁹ You multiplied your whoring with the land of Canaan, even with Chaldea, yet you were never satisfied. ³⁰ "How corrupt is your heart, you daughter of Jerusalem," says the Lord GOD, "in doing all these deeds of a prostitute, becoming so powerful in your whoring. ³¹ You built a monument at the head of every road, and your foundation was in every street. You became a prostitute collecting payments. ³² A woman committing adultery is like you, taking payment from her husband. ³³ To all the prostitutes you give payment, and you paid your lovers with all your whoring to come to you from all around. ³⁴ And in this you became more corrupt than the other women in your whoring. Others did not prostitute themselves to you, but you paid them and received nothing in return. Thus you became corrupt. ³⁵ "Therefore, prostitute, hear the word of the LORD. ³⁶ This is what the Lord GOD says: Because you poured out your bronze, and your shame was exposed in your whoring with your lovers and in all the abominations of your heart, and in the blood of your children whom you gave to them. ³⁷ Therefore, behold, I will gather all your lovers with whom you mingled and all whom you loved, along with all whom you hated. I will gather them around you and expose your shame to them, and they will see all your shame. ³⁸ And I will judge you as adulteresses and murderers are judged. I will pour out my wrath and jealousy. ³⁹ I will give you into their hands, and they will destroy your monuments and tear down your foundations. They will strip you of your clothes and take your jewelry. They will leave you naked and shamed. ⁴⁰ They will gather a crowd against you and stone you with stones and cut you down with their swords. ⁴¹ They will burn your houses with fire and make judgments against you in the sight of many women. I will turn you away from whoring, and you will no longer give payments. ⁴² I will place my anger on you and remove my jealousy from you. I will rest, and I will no longer be angry. ⁴³ Because you did not remember the days of your youth, but grieved me in all these things, and now I will place your ways on your head," says the Lord GOD, "and I have done this for all your abominations. ⁴⁴ Everyone who speaks against you in a parable will say, 'As the mother, so the daughter.' ⁴⁵ You are the daughter of your mother, the one who abandoned her husband and children. You are the sister of your sisters who abandoned their husbands and children; your mother is a Hittite, your father an Amorite. ⁴⁶ Your older sister is Samaria, she and her daughters who live to your left, and your younger sister, the one who lives to your right, is Sodom and her daughters. ⁴⁷ Yet you did not walk in their ways or commit their abominations. You have gone beyond them in all your deeds. ⁴⁸ "I am alive," says the Lord, "if Sodom, your sister, and her daughters had done what you and your daughters have done. ⁴⁹ The sin of Sodom, your sister, was pride. She and her daughters were full of bread and at ease, but they did not help the poor and the needy. ⁵⁰ They were arrogant and did abominations before me, and I removed them as I saw. ⁵¹ Samaria did not commit half your sins. You multiplied your abominations beyond them. Yet they were justified in their sins because of yours. ⁵² You also will bear the punishment you dealt to your sisters in their sins, for which you acted treacherously. They will be justified because of you, and you will be shamed and bear your own disgrace. ⁵³ I will restore their captives, the captives of Sodom and her daughters, and the captives of Samaria and her daughters, along with your own captives, in their midst, ⁵⁴ so that you may bear your disgrace and be shamed for all you have done to provoke me. ⁵⁵ And your sister Sodom and her daughters will be restored to their former state, and Samaria and her daughters will be restored to their former state, and you and your daughters will be restored to your former state. ⁵⁶ Sodom, your sister, will not be a byword in your mouth in the days of your pride, ⁵⁷ before your disgrace was revealed, just as the shame of the daughters of Aram and all your surrounding neighbors, the daughters of the Philistines who surrounded you, ⁵⁸ you will bear your own disgrace and your own abominations," says the LORD. ⁵⁹ For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will do to you as you have done, as you have treated with contempt, breaking my covenant. ⁶⁰ I will remember my covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish with you an everlasting covenant. ⁶¹ You will remember your ways, and be shamed in your dealings with your older and younger sisters. I will give them to you for daughters, not by covenant. ⁶² I will establish my covenant with you, and you will know that I am the LORD. ⁶³ So that you may remember and be ashamed, and no longer have an open mouth because of your disgrace when I forgive you for all you have done," says the Lord GOD.

Notes
- v. 5: "cast out into the field" — the Hebrew uses a poetic idiom for being abandoned.
- v. 16: "idolatrous altars" — the text uses a rare term for altars that are idolatrous.
- v. 20: "sacrificed them as food" — the Hebrew implies using children as sacrificial offerings, which is a strong idiom for moral corruption.
- v. 52: "bear the punishment you dealt to your sisters" — the Hebrew uses a metaphor for reciprocal justice.
