# Ezekiel 14

¹ Some elders from Israel came to me and sat before me. ² Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ³ "Son of man, these men have placed their schemes in their hearts, and the punishment of their guilt they have set before their faces. Should I answer them? ⁴ Therefore, speak to them and say to them: Thus says the Lord GOD: Every human from the house of Israel who places his schemes in his heart and the punishment of his guilt before his face, and goes to the prophet, I, the LORD, will respond to him according to what fills his heart, ⁵ in order to ensnare the house of Israel in their hearts, because they have turned away from me through their schemes. ⁶ Therefore, say to the house of Israel: Thus says the Lord GOD: Repent and turn back from your schemes and from all your abominations. Turn your faces. ⁷ Because every human from the house of Israel and every foreigner who sojourns in Israel who turns away from me and places his schemes in his heart and the punishment of his guilt before his face, and goes to the prophet to inquire of me, I, the LORD, will respond to him in what he has within himself. ⁸ I will set my face against that man and make him an object of ruin and a proverb, and I will cut him off from the midst of my people. Then you will know that I am the LORD. ⁹ If the prophet is deceived and speaks a word, I, the LORD, have deceived that prophet, and I will stretch out my hand against him and destroy him from the midst of my people Israel. ¹⁰ The guilt of the one who inquires will rest on him, just as the guilt of the prophet. ¹¹ So that the house of Israel no longer strays from me and no longer defiles itself with all its sins, and they may become my people, and I will be their God," declares the Lord GOD. ¹² Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹³ "Son of man, if a land sins against me by grievous wrongdoing, and I stretch out my hand against it and break its staff of bread and send famine upon it, and cut off from it people and beasts, ¹⁴ yet if these three men are in it—Noah, Daniel, and Job—they will be saved by their righteousness," says the Lord GOD. ¹⁵ "Even if I were to send a plague of fierce animals to devastate the land and punish it, so that it becomes a wasteland and no one passes through it because of the wild beasts, ¹⁶ these three men will be in it, alive," says the Lord GOD. "Even if they had sons or daughters, they would be saved only by themselves, and the land will become a wasteland. ¹⁷ Or if I were to send the sword to that land and say, 'The sword shall pass through the land,' and I cut off from it people and beasts, ¹⁸ these three men will be in it, alive," says the Lord GOD. "They will not save their sons or daughters; they alone will be saved, and the land will become a wasteland. ¹⁹ Or if I were to send a plague to that land and pour out my wrath on it in blood to cut off from it people and beasts, ²⁰ then Noah, Daniel, and Job will be in it, alive," says the Lord GOD. "Even if their sons or daughters remain, they themselves will be saved by their righteousness and preserve their lives. ²¹ For thus says the Lord GOD: Even if I were to send all four judgments—sword, famine, wild beasts, and plague—on Jerusalem to cut off from it people and beasts, ²² and if survivors remain in it, people who escape, sons and daughters, they will come out to you. You will see their ways and their deeds, and you will be consoled for the evil I have brought on Jerusalem for everything I have brought upon it. ²³ You will be comforted when you see their ways and their deeds, and you will know that I did not act in vain in all that I did in it," says the Lord GOD.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "filling his heart" reflects the sense of יָשִׂים נֹכַח פָּנָיו as "place before his face," indicating a mindset or internal conviction.
- v. 9: The phrase "I, the LORD have deceived that prophet" does not imply divine deception in a moral sense but rather that the Lord allows the prophet to be deceived as part of a just judgment.
- v. 22-23: The "survivors" (פְּלֵטָה) are understood as those who escape destruction, not as a general remnant but as individuals who remain after the judgments.
