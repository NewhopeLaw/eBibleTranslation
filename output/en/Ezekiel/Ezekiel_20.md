# Ezekiel 20

¹ In the seventh year, on the fifth day of the tenth month, the elders of Israel came to seek the LORD and sat before me. ² Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ³ "Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Have you come to seek Me? I am alive, says the Lord GOD. Will I indeed answer you? I, the Lord GOD.' ⁴ "Will I judge them? Will I judge you, son of man? Declare to them the abominations of their ancestors. ⁵ "Say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day I chose Israel and raised my hand in an oath to the descendants of Jacob, I made myself known to them in the land of Egypt and raised my hand before them, saying, "I am the LORD your God." ⁶ "On that day I raised my hand before them to bring them out of the land of Egypt into a land I had prepared for them, a land flowing with milk and honey, the best of all lands. ⁷ "I said to them, 'Each of you must cast away the abominations in your eyes and not defile yourselves with the idols of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.'" ⁸ "But they rebelled against Me and refused to listen to Me. They did not cast away the abominations in their eyes and did not abandon the idols of Egypt. Then I said I would pour out My wrath on them and finish My anger against them in the land of Egypt. ⁹ "Yet I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the nations among whom they were, for I had made Myself known to them in their sight to bring them out of the land of Egypt." ¹⁰ "I brought them out of the land of Egypt and led them into the wilderness. ¹¹ "I gave them My decrees and My laws, which they must observe so that they may live by them. ¹² "I also gave them My Sabbaths as a sign between Me and them, so that they might know that I am the LORD, the One who sanctifies them." ¹³ "But the house of Israel rebelled against Me in the wilderness. They did not walk in My decrees and rejected My laws, which would give life if they obeyed them. They profaned My Sabbaths very much. Then I said I would pour out My wrath on them in the wilderness to destroy them. ¹⁴ "Yet I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the nations who had seen them brought out of the land of Egypt." ¹⁵ "Moreover, I raised My hand against them in the wilderness, swearing not to bring them into the land I had given them, a land flowing with milk and honey, the best of all lands. ¹⁶ "Because they rejected My laws and did not follow My decrees, and profaned My Sabbaths, their hearts went after their idols. ¹⁷ "My eye was too tender to destroy them, so I did not annihilate them in the wilderness." ¹⁸ "I said to their children in the wilderness, 'Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers, do not keep their laws, and do not defile yourselves with their idols.'" ¹⁹ "I am the LORD your God. Walk in My decrees and keep My laws, and do them. ²⁰ "Consecrate My Sabbaths and let them be a sign between Me and you, so that you may know that I am the LORD your God." ²¹ "But their children also rebelled against Me. They did not walk in My decrees and did not keep My laws, which would give life if they obeyed them. They profaned My Sabbaths. Then I said I would pour out My wrath on them in the wilderness to finish My anger against them." ²² "Yet I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the nations who had seen them brought out in their sight." ²³ "I also raised My hand against them in the wilderness, swearing to scatter them among the nations and disperse them across the lands, ²⁴ "because they had not obeyed My laws, rejected My decrees, profaned My Sabbaths, and their eyes followed the desires of their ancestors." ²⁵ "So I gave them decrees that were not good and laws that would not keep them alive. ²⁶ "I defiled them with their offerings, by making every mother's womb pass through Me, so that I might destroy them, to show that I am the LORD." ²⁷ "Therefore, speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Have you indeed grown weary of Me, the house of Israel, in your rebellion? ²⁸ "I brought you into the land I had sworn to give you. You saw every high hill and every leafy tree, and there you offered sacrifices and made offerings, and there you gave incense and poured out drink offerings. ²⁹ "I asked them, 'What is this high place you are going to?' So they named it a high place, which it still is to this day. ³⁰ "Therefore, say to the house of Israel: 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Have you indeed defiled yourselves like your ancestors by going after their abominations and prostituting yourselves after them? ³¹ "And you defile yourselves with your offerings, with your sons whom you burn in the fire, in all your idols, even to this day. And I will hold you accountable, house of Israel. I, the Lord GOD, will ask—will I indeed ask you? I am alive, says the Lord GOD. ³² "Will you indeed say, "We will be like the nations, like the families of the lands, serving wood and stone"? ³³ "As surely as I live, says the Lord GOD, with a strong hand, with an outstretched arm, and with poured-out wrath I will rule over you. ³⁴ "I will bring you out from the nations and gather you from the lands where you are scattered, with a strong hand, with an outstretched arm, and with poured-out wrath. ³⁵ "I will bring you into the wilderness of the nations and judge you face to face. ³⁶ "As I judged your ancestors in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will judge you, says the Lord GOD. ³⁷ "I will pass you under the rod and bring you into the covenant of judgment. ³⁸ "I will remove from you those who rebel and those who have sinned against Me, taking them out from the land where they live and from the land of Israel. They will not come into the land of Israel, and you will know that I am the LORD.'" ³⁹ "And you, house of Israel, this is what the Lord GOD says: Will you indeed go and serve your abominations? If you do not listen to Me and still profane My holy name with your offerings and idols, ⁴⁰ "then I will gather you from the nations and bring you from the lands where you have been scattered, and I will be sanctified among you in the sight of the nations. ⁴¹ "I will accept you with a sweet aroma when I bring you out from the nations and gather you from the lands where you were scattered, and I will be sanctified in you before the eyes of the nations. ⁴² "You will know that I am the LORD, when I bring you into the land of Israel, into the land I swore to give to your ancestors. ⁴³ "There you will remember your ways and all your deeds by which you defiled yourselves, and you will loathe yourselves in all your evil that you have done. ⁴⁴ "And you will know that I am the LORD, when I act against you for the sake of My name, not according to your evil ways or your corrupt deeds, says the Lord GOD."

---

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "in the seventh year" is dependent on the context of the exile, which is the starting point for Ezekiel's prophetic ministry.
- v. 28: The term "high place" (bamah) is used in the context of idolatrous worship, and its naming reflects the people's continued attachment to their ancestral customs.
- v. 31: "With your sons whom you burn in the fire" refers to the practice of offering children in fire as sacrifices, a clear violation of God's laws.
- v. 40: "I will be sanctified among you" emphasizes the restoration of a holy people and the visible demonstration of God's holiness to the surrounding nations.
- v. 44: "Not according to your evil ways" underscores the divine initiative in redemption, not based on human merit.
