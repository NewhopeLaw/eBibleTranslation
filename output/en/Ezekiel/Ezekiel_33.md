# Ezekiel 33

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, speak to your people and say to them: When I bring a sword upon the land, the people of the land will take one man from among them and appoint him as a watchman. ³ When he sees the sword coming upon the land, he will sound the trumpet and warn the people. ⁴ The one who hears the sound of the trumpet and does not take warning, the sword will take his life; his blood will be on his own head. ⁵ The one who hears the sound of the trumpet and takes warning, his life will be spared. ⁶ If the watchman sees the sword coming and does not sound the trumpet, and the people are not warned, the sword will take from among them. The watchman will be held accountable for their blood. ⁷ "You, son of man, I have made a watchman for the house of Israel. When you hear a word from my mouth, you must warn them from me. ⁸ If I tell the wicked, 'You will die,' and you do not warn the wicked to turn from their ways, the wicked will die in their iniquity, and I will hold you accountable for their blood. ⁹ But if you warn the wicked to turn from their ways and they do not turn, the wicked will die in their iniquity, but you will save your own life. ¹⁰ "You, son of man, say to the house of Israel: 'You say, "Our sins and our iniquities are on us, and we have perished because of them. How can we live?"' ¹¹ Say to them: 'As the living God, I do not desire the death of the wicked. Turn back from your ways, house of Israel, why should you die?' ¹² "You, son of man, say to your people: The righteousness of the righteous will not save him on the day of his transgression, nor will the wickedness of the wicked cause them to stumble on the day they turn from their wickedness. The righteous cannot live by their righteousness on the day they sin. ¹³ When I tell the righteous, 'You will live,' and he trusts in his righteousness and commits iniquity, none of his righteous deeds will be remembered. By the iniquity he has committed, he will die. ¹⁴ And when I tell the wicked, 'You will die,' and he turns from his iniquity and does justice and righteousness, ¹⁵ if he gives back a pledge and returns what he has stolen, and walks in my statutes without doing iniquity, he will live and not die. ¹⁶ None of his past sins will be remembered. By the justice and righteousness he has done, he will live. ¹⁷ But your people will say, 'The way of the Lord is not just.' Yet it is their way that is not just. ¹⁸ When the righteous turns away from his righteousness and does iniquity, he will die by them. ¹⁹ When the wicked turns away from his wickedness and does justice and righteousness, he will live by them. ²⁰ You will say, 'The way of the Lord is not just.' But it is every person who is judged according to their ways, house of Israel. ²¹ It came to pass in the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the fifth of the month, during our exile, a fugitive came to me from Jerusalem, saying, 'The city has been struck.' ²² The hand of the LORD was on me in the evening before the fugitive came. He opened my mouth until the morning. When the fugitive came to me, I spoke, and my mouth was no longer closed. ²³ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ²⁴ "Son of man, those who dwell in the ruins in the land of Israel say, 'Abraham was one, and he inherited the land, but we are many. The land has been given to us as our possession.' ²⁵ Therefore, say to them: 'As the living God, I swear, you will eat blood and lift your eyes to your idols. You will shed blood, and the land will be your possession. ²⁶ You have stood by the sword, you have committed abominations, and each of you has defiled your neighbor's wife. The land will be your possession. ²⁷ Say to them: 'As the living God, I swear, those in the ruins will fall by the sword, those on the field will be given to the wild animals to eat, and those in the strongholds and caves will die by plague. ²⁸ I will make the land desolate and waste, and the pride of its strength will perish. The mountains of Israel will be desolate, with no one passing through. ²⁹ Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I make the land desolate and waste because of all their abominations they have done. ³⁰ "You, son of man, your people who speak against you at the walls and in the doorways of the houses, each one speaking to his brother, saying, 'Come now, let us hear what word comes from the LORD.' ³¹ They will come to you as a people and sit before you. My people will listen to your words, but they will not do them, for their mouths are filled with falsehood. Their hearts are set on their own desires. ³² You will be to them like a song of falsehood, sweet in sound and well played. They will listen to your words, but they will not do them. ³³ When it comes, they will say, 'It has come!' Then they will know that a prophet was among them.

Notes
- v. 21: "the twelfth year" — the twelfth year of the reign of Jehoiachin's exile.
- v. 23: "The word of the LORD came to me" — this transition indicates a new oracle following the news of Jerusalem's fall.
- v. 24: "those who dwell in the ruins" — refers to Israelites living in the devastated land after the destruction.
- v. 25: "you will eat blood" — metaphor for violence and idolatry among the people.
- v. 26: "each of you has defiled your neighbor's wife" — alludes to incest and adultery, common in ancient Near Eastern contexts.
- v. 27: "those in the ruins will fall by the sword" — the divine judgment on the people in various locations: ruins, open fields, and fortifications.
- v. 31: "their mouths are filled with falsehood" — contrasts the false appearance of seeking divine word with their actual rejection of it.
