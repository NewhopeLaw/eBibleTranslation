# Ezekiel 4

¹ You, son of man, take a clay tablet for yourself, and place it before you, and draw on it a city, Jerusalem. ² Place a siege around it, build a wall against it, surround it with a rampart, and set up camps around it, with siege towers all around. ³ You, take an iron pan for yourself, and place it as an iron wall between you and the city. Face it, and it shall be under siege. You shall press it, and it shall be a sign for the house of Israel. ⁴ You, lie on your left side, and place the guilt of the house of Israel on it. For the number of days you lie on it, you will bear their guilt. ⁵ I have given you their two guilts for the number of days: three hundred and ninety days. You will bear the guilt of the house of Israel. ⁶ And when you have finished these, lie on your right side, and bear the guilt of the house of Judah forty days. A day for a year, a day for a year I have given to you. ⁷ And face the siege of Jerusalem, with your arm uncovered, and prophesy to it. ⁸ I will place restraints on you, and you shall not turn from side to side until your days of siege are complete. ⁹ You, take wheat and barley, millet and lentils, spelt and fitches, and put them into one vessel. Make them into your bread for the number of days that you lie on your side, three hundred and ninety days, you will eat it. ¹⁰ Your food you will eat by weight, twenty shekels a day. From time to time you will eat it. ¹¹ And water you will drink by measure, a sixth of a hin, from time to time you will drink. ¹² And you will eat a cake made of barley, and it will be baked in human dung before their eyes. ¹³ And the Lord said, “Thus will the sons of Israel eat their bread unclean in the nations where I have driven them.” ¹⁴ I said, “Ah, Lord YHWH, behold, my soul has not been defiled, and I have not eaten what is unclean or dead from my youth to this day. No unclean meat has entered my mouth.” ¹⁵ Then He said to me, “Behold, I give to you cow dung in place of human dung, and you will make your bread from it.” ¹⁶ And He said to me, “Son of man, behold, I will break the staff of bread in Jerusalem. They will eat bread by weight and in anxiety, and water by measure and in weariness. ¹⁷ So that they will lack bread and water, and each man will look at his brother and be famished, and they will melt away in their guilt.”

---

Notes
- v. 14: "Ah" is a standard expression of emotional response in Hebrew, here indicating surprise or distress.
- v. 16: "staff of bread" is a metaphor for the normal supply or provision of food.
- v. 17: "melt away" is a poetic expression, likely meaning to weaken or perish.
