# Ezekiel 27

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, take up a lament over Tyre,

³ and say to Tyre, the one who dwells at the gate of the sea, the merchant of the peoples to many islands:  
Thus says the Lord GOD to Tyre:  
‘You said, "I am beautiful, I am perfect in beauty."  

⁴ Your borders are in the heart of the sea, and your builders made you perfect in beauty. ⁵ Cedar from Lebanon was made into your beams; from Bashan, cypress and juniper were taken to build your decks. ⁶ From the islands of the Kittim, ivory was used for your seats, and they gave you royal gifts from the daughters of Aram. ⁷ Fine linen from Egypt was your sail to make you a banner; blue and purple from the islands of Elishah was your covering.

⁸ The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers.  
The wise men of Tyre were in you; they were your pilots.  

⁹ Elders of Gebal and their wise men were in you.  
All the ships of the sea and their sailors were in you to trade with you.  

¹⁰ The Persians and Lydians and Libyans were in your might; men of war, shields and helmets were hung in you.  
They gave you splendor.  

¹¹ The sons of Arvad and your warriors were on your walls, and guards on your towers.  
Their bows were hung on your walls around.  
They completed your beauty.  

¹² Tarshish was your merchant in abundance of all kinds of wealth, with silver, iron, tin, and lead, which they gave to your market.

¹³ Greece and Tubal and Meshech—these brought you persons alive for trade;  
they gave you the weapons of bronze for your war.  

¹⁴ From the house of Togarmah, horses, dromedaries, and mules were given to your market. ¹⁵ The sons of Dedan, merchants of many islands, brought back ivory and ebony, and they gave your hire. ¹⁶ Aram was your merchant in abundance of your work; with balm, onyx, and aromatic spices, and ram’s horn and cinnabar, they gave to your market.

¹⁷ Judah and the land of Israel were your merchants.  
They brought you wheat and millet, honey and oil, and balm.  

¹⁸ Damascus was your merchant in the abundance of your work, with wine from Helbon and wool from Maaleh and ram skins. ¹⁹ And Dan and Javan gave you polished iron to make your war weapons—your shields and spears. ²⁰ Dedan was your merchant in garments for riding. ²¹ Arabia and all the chieftains of Kedar were your merchants, bringing camels, rams, and goats, by which you were traded. ²² The traders of Sheba and Raam brought you the head of every fragrance, and all precious stones and gold they gave to your market. ²³ Haran and Canneh and Eden, the traders of Sheba and Ashur and Chilmad—these were your merchants. ²⁴ These were your traders with richly woven textiles, in blue and fine linen, and in embroidered linens fastened in cords and sealed in boxes, and they were brought to your market. ²⁵ The merchant ships of Tarshish filled you with your cargo, and you were made very heavy in the heart of the sea. ²⁶ The seafarers brought you across great waters; the east wind shattered you in the heart of the sea. ²⁷ Your wealth and your wares, your market, your sailors and your pilots, your traders and your warriors who were in you, and all the multitude in your center, will fall into the heart of the sea on the day of your fall. ²⁸ At the sound of your cry, the merchants will tremble. ²⁹ And all who hold oars will descend from their ships, and all the traders of the sea will stand on the land. ³⁰ And they will raise their voices over you and cry out in grief, and they will throw dust on their heads, and they will roll in the dust. ³¹ And they will shave themselves for you, and gird on sackcloth, and weep bitterly for you with bitter mourning. ³² And your children will raise a lament over you, saying, "Who is like Tyre, like a city in the sea, destroyed in the middle of the sea?" ³³ When your wares were poured out from the sea, you enriched many peoples with your wealth, and with your wares you enriched the kings of the earth.

³⁴ Now you are shattered in the sea, in the depths of the waters, your wares and all your multitude in your center.  
All your sailors have fallen.  

³⁵ All the inhabitants of the islands have been stunned over you, and the kings of the islands have their faces in disarray, and they have shed tears. ³⁶ Merchants among the peoples have cried out over you; you have perished and will no longer exist forever."

---

Notes  
- v. 13: "persons alive" — Hebrew "נֶפֶשׁ אָדָם" is literally "life of man," but in mercantile contexts it often refers to people as commodities.  
- v. 25: "the east wind" — "רוּחַ הַקָּדִים" is literally "the wind of the east," but "east wind" was a metaphor for a destructive storm in ancient Near Eastern thought.  
- v. 33: "you enriched" — the verb is plural, but "you" is singular. The plural could be a poetic plural or a reference to Tyre's collective wealth.  
- v. 36: "will no longer exist forever" — "עַד־עוֹלָם" is "until forever," but the phrase is used in Hebrew to mean "forever," so the meaning is clear.
