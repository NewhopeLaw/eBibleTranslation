# Ezekiel 34

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy and say to the shepherds, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the shepherds of Israel who have fed themselves! Should not the flock be fed by the shepherds? ³ You have eaten the fat, worn the wool, and slaughtered the strong, yet you have not fed the flock. ⁴ You have not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, bound up the broken, brought back the strayed, or sought the lost. Instead, you have ruled them with harshness and cruelty. ⁵ Because of this, the flock has been scattered and has become prey to every wild beast in the field. They have been scattered, because there was no shepherd. ⁶ My sheep have wandered over every mountain and every high hill. They have been scattered over the whole face of the earth, with no one to seek or to search for them. ⁷ Therefore, you shepherds, listen to the word of the LORD. ⁸ "I, the living one, declares the Lord GOD, that because the flock has become prey and has been scattered, with no one to seek or to search for them, the shepherds have fed themselves instead of the flock. You have ruled them with harshness and cruelty. ⁹ Therefore, you shepherds, listen to the word of the LORD. ¹⁰ "This is what the Lord GOD says: I am coming to the shepherds. I will seek out my flock from their hand and cause them to cease from pasturing the flock, so that the shepherds will no longer feed themselves. I will rescue my flock from their mouths, and they will no longer be prey for them. ¹¹ "For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will surely seek out my flock and take care of them. ¹² Just as a shepherd takes care of his flock on the day when he gathers them together from the fields, so I will gather my flock from every place where they were scattered on a cloudy and dark day. ¹³ I will bring them out from the nations and gather them from the countries, and I will bring them into their land. I will pasture them on the mountains of Israel, in the valleys and in all the inhabited places of the land. ¹⁴ I will give them good pasture, and on the high mountains of Israel they will lie down in good grazing land. There they will rest in good pasture, and there they will feed on rich pasture on the mountains of Israel. ¹⁵ I will feed my flock and I will let them rest, declares the Lord GOD. ¹⁶ I will seek out the lost and bring back the strayed, bind up the broken, and strengthen the sick. But the strong and the strong I will destroy; I will feed them with judgment. ¹⁷ "And I will judge between one sheep and another, between rams and goats. ¹⁸ Is it not enough for you to have fed on the good pasture, but you must trample the rest of the pasture with your feet? Is it not enough for you to have drunk the clear waters, but you must muddy the rest with your feet? ¹⁹ Must my sheep that you have trampled with your feet drink the water that you have muddied with your feet? ²⁰ Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says to them: I will surely judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep. ²¹ Because you have shoved with side and shoulder and butted all the weak ones with your horns until you scattered them abroad, ²² I will rescue my flock and they will no longer be prey. I will judge between sheep and sheep. ²³ I will raise up one shepherd over them, my servant David, and he will pasture them. He will be their shepherd. ²⁴ And I, the LORD, will be their God, and my servant David will be their prince among them. I, the LORD, have spoken. ²⁵ "I will make a covenant of peace with them and remove the wild beasts from the land, so that they may live securely in the wilderness and rest in the woods. ²⁶ I will give rain to them and the land around my mountain. It will become a blessing, and the rain will come in its season. It will be a rain of blessing. ²⁷ The trees of the field will bear fruit, and the land will yield its increase. They will live securely on their land and know that I am the LORD, when I break the yoke that is on them and rescue them from the hands of those who have enslaved them. ²⁸ They will no longer be prey for the nations, and the beast of the earth will no longer devour them. They will live securely, with no one to terrify them. ²⁹ I will raise up for them a plant of renown, and they will no longer be food for the famine in the land, nor bear the reproach of the nations. ³⁰ They will know that I am the LORD their God, and they will be my people, the house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD. ³¹ You are my sheep, my flock that I pasture, and I am your God, declares the Lord GOD.

---

Notes
- v. 18: The contrast between the good pasture and the muddied water is a metaphor for the shepherds' exploitation of the flock, not literal water and pasture. The metaphor is retained in the translation.
