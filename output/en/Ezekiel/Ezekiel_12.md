# Ezekiel 12

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² Son of man, you are living among the rebellious, people with eyes to see and yet they do not see, ears to hear and yet they do not hear. They are a rebellious house. ³ And you, son of man, make for yourself exile-like equipment, and carry it out in their sight during the day, and move from your place to another place in their sight, so that they may see that they are a rebellious house. ⁴ Carry your equipment out like the gear of someone in exile, in their sight during the day, and you yourself go out at evening in their sight, as someone leaving exile. ⁵ In their sight, dig through a wall and go out through it. ⁶ In their sight, carry it on your shoulder and go out covered, your face hidden, and you must not see the land—because I have made you a sign for the house of Israel. ⁷ I did as I was commanded. I carried my gear out as the gear of someone in exile during the day, and in the evening I dug through the wall with my hand and went out, carrying it on my shoulder in their sight. ⁸ The word of the LORD came to me in the morning, saying: ⁹ Son of man, have they not said to you in the house of Israel, "What are you doing?" ¹⁰ Say to them, "This is what the Lord GOD says: The prince who is in Jerusalem and all the house of Israel who are among them will bear this load. ¹¹ Say to them, I am their sign. Just as I have done, so will it be done to them. They will go into exile and captivity. ¹² The prince in their midst will carry it on his shoulder, hidden behind a wall, and go out through it, covering his face so that he will not see the land with his eyes. ¹³ I will cast my net over him and catch him in my snare, and I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, and he will not see it, and there he will die. ¹⁴ I will scatter all his allies around him and all who support him in every direction, and I will draw my sword after them. ¹⁵ And they will know that I am the LORD, when I disperse them among the nations and scatter them across the lands. ¹⁶ I will leave behind some of them as a remnant from the sword, from famine, and from plague, so that they may recount all their abominations among the nations where they have gone, and they will know that I am the LORD. ¹⁷ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹⁸ Son of man, your bread will be eaten with trembling, and your water will be drunk in anxiety and distress. ¹⁹ Say to the people of the land, "This is what the Lord GOD says to those living in Jerusalem, in the land of Israel: They will eat their bread in distress, and their water will be drunk in anguish, because their land will be emptied of its fullness, by the violence of all who live in it. ²⁰ The inhabited cities will be destroyed, and the land will become desolate, and they will know that I am the LORD." ²¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ²² Son of man, what is this proverb you have among the land of Israel, saying, "The days are long, and every vision perishes"? ²³ Therefore say to them, "This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to this proverb, and it will no longer be spoken in Israel. Rather, I will say to them, 'The days are near, and the fulfillment of every vision.'" ²⁴ For there will no longer be any false vision or deceptive omen in the house of Israel. ²⁵ For I am the LORD. I will speak the word I speak, and it will be done. I will not delay any longer, because in your days, which are a rebellious house, I will speak and do it, declares the Lord GOD. ²⁶ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ²⁷ Son of man, the house of Israel is saying, "The vision he sees is far off, and the time is distant. He prophesies." ²⁸ Therefore say to them, "This is what the Lord GOD says: I will not delay my words any longer. I will speak, and I will do it, declares the Lord GOD."

---

Notes
- v. 2: "a rebellious house" — the Hebrew "בֵּית מְרִי" is rendered consistently as "rebellious house" throughout the chapter, referring to the people of Israel as a collective entity.
- v. 7: "I carried my gear out as the gear of someone in exile" — the Hebrew uses "כִּכְלֵי גוֹלָה" literally "as the gear of exile," which is translated to reflect the action of carrying belongings as if in exile.
- v. 12: "hidden behind a wall" — the phrase "בַּקִּיר" is translated to "behind a wall" to capture the idea of concealment.
- v. 22: "proverb" — the Hebrew "מָֽה־הַמָּשָׁל" is here interpreted as a proverb, a common saying, rather than a parable.
- v. 25: "I will not delay my words any longer" — the Hebrew "לֹא תִמָּשֵׁךְ עוֹד" is translated to reflect the idea of not prolonging or delaying.
