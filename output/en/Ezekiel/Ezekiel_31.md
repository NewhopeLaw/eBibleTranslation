# Ezekiel 31

¹ In the eleventh year, in the third month, on the first of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, say to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and to his hordes: To whom did you compare yourself in your greatness? ³ Look, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon, noble in its boughs, and lofty in its height, with its top among the clouds. ⁴ The waters nourished it, the deep raised it up, its rivers surrounded its roots, and its branches sprouted out to all the trees of the field. ⁵ Therefore, its height surpassed all the trees of the field, and its boughs were multiplied and its branches extended because of the abundance of water. ⁶ Its boughs provided shelter for all the birds of the sky, and under its branches, all the beasts of the field bore their young. In its shade, all the nations of the earth dwelled. ⁷ It was beautiful in its height, because of the multitude of its boughs. For its roots ran deep into many waters. ⁸ The cedars in the garden of God did not rival it, nor did the oaks resemble its boughs, nor the fir trees match its branches. No tree in the garden of God was like it in beauty. ⁹ I made it beautiful in the abundance of its boughs, and all the trees of Eden, which were in the garden of God, envied it. ¹⁰ Therefore, thus says the Lord GOD: Because you exalted yourself in height and gave your head among the clouds, and your heart was lifted up in your pride, ¹¹ I will hand you over to a foreign king, and he will deal with you according to his wickedness. I will cast you out. ¹² Foreigners, fierce in their nations, will cut you down and cast you to the mountains. On every high hill and in every valley, your branches will fall. Your boughs will be broken in all the pathways of the earth. From your shade, all the peoples of the earth will be driven away and cast you down. ¹³ On your downfall, all the birds of the sky will dwell, and all the beasts of the field will come to your boughs. ¹⁴ That no tree with roots in water may exalt itself above them, and no tree give its head among the clouds, and none of the drinking trees of water may stand up to them. For they are all given to death, to the lowest depths among the children of men, to those going down into the pit." ¹⁵ "Thus says the Lord GOD: On the day it went down to Sheol, I mourned. I covered it over with the deep and restrained its rivers. I held back the abundant waters and cast darkness upon Lebanon and all the trees of the field. ¹⁶ At the sound of its fall, the nations were shaken. When I brought it down to Sheol with those going down into the pit, all the trees of Eden, the best of Lebanon and all the drinking trees of water, were comforted in the land. ¹⁷ They too went down with it to Sheol, with those slain by the sword. Its offspring, those who dwelled in its shade, perished among the nations. ¹⁸ To whom did you compare yourself in glory and in greatness, O trees of Eden? You are brought down to the lowest depths, lying among the slain by the sword. He is Pharaoh and all his hordes," says the Lord GOD.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "the garden of God" is a poetic rendering of the Hebrew "garden of God" (גַּן־אֱלֹהִים), reflecting a sacred or idyllic setting rather than a literal garden.
- v. 15: "Sheol" (שְׁאוֹל) is used in its traditional sense to denote the abode of the dead.
- v. 18: "lowest depths" (אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית) is a poetic expression for the deepest part of the underworld or grave.
