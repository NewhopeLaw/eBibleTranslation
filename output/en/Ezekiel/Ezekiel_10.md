# Ezekiel 10

¹ I saw, and behold, above the vault over the heads of the cherubim was something like a sapphire stone, the appearance of a throne over them. ² He said to the man dressed in linen, "Go in between the wheels, beneath the cherubim, and fill your hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim, and scatter them over the city." And he went in, right before my eyes. ³ The cherubim stood at the right side of the house as the man entered, and the cloud filled the inner courtyard. ⁴ The glory of the LORD departed from the cherubim to the threshold of the house, and the house was filled with the cloud, and the courtyard was filled with the radiance of the glory of the LORD. ⁵ The sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard all the way to the outer courtyard, like the voice of God Almighty speaking. ⁶ When he commanded the man dressed in linen, saying, "Take fire from between the wheels, from between the cherubim," he went in and stood by the wheel. ⁷ The cherub stretched out his hand from between the cherubim toward the fire that was among the cherubim, took it, and gave it to the man dressed in linen, who took it and went out. ⁸ I saw that the cherubim had the form of a human hand beneath their wings. ⁹ I saw, and behold, four wheels were stationed beside the cherubim—one wheel beside each cherub. The appearance of the wheels was like the appearance of a beryl stone. ¹⁰ Their appearance was all the same, as if one wheel were inside another. ¹¹ When they moved, they moved in the direction of their four sides without turning. For wherever the head faced, they went in that direction, and they did not turn when they moved. ¹² Their bodies, their backs, their hands, their wings, and the wheels were full of eyes, all around the four of them. ¹³ The name of the wheels was "Galgal," as I heard. ¹⁴ Four faces were on each one: the face of a cherub, the face of a human, the face of a lion, and the face of an eagle. ¹⁵ The cherubim were the living creatures I saw by the Chebar River. ¹⁶ When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved with them. When they lifted their wings to rise from the ground, the wheels did not turn back. ¹⁷ When they stood, they stood; when they rose, they rose with them, for the spirit of life was in them. ¹⁸ The glory of the LORD departed from the threshold of the house and stood upon the cherubim. ¹⁹ The cherubim lifted their wings, rose from the ground in front of my eyes. As they went out, the wheels went with them. They stood at the gate of the eastern entrance of the house of the LORD, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them. ²⁰ These are the living creatures I saw beneath the God of Israel by the Chebar River, and I recognized them to be cherubim. ²¹ Each had four faces and four wings, and beneath their wings was the form of human hands. ²² Their faces were the same as the faces I saw beneath the glory of the God of Israel by the Chebar River, and each moved straight ahead, according to their appearance.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "something like a sapphire stone" — the Hebrew is metaphorical.  
- v. 2: "fill your hands with coals of fire" — the imagery of fire is symbolic, not literal.  
- v. 10: "as if one wheel were inside another" — the structure is a nested or concentric wheel.  
- v. 13: "Galgal" — the Hebrew term for "wheel," used as a proper name here.  
- v. 14: "face of a cherub" — the cherub's own face is included in its four faces.
