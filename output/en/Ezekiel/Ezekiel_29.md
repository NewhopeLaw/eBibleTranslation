# Ezekiel 29

¹ In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, set your face toward Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt. ³ Say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: I am coming against you, Pharaoh, the great crocodile lying in the middle of your rivers, who says, "My rivers are mine, and I made them." ⁴ I will cast nets for your feet and bring your river fish to stick to your wings, and I will lift you up from the middle of your rivers. ⁵ I will cast you down to the ground, along with all your river fish, and they will fall on the face of the field. They will not be gathered or collected, and I will give them as food for the beasts of the earth and the birds of the sky. ⁶ Then all the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am the LORD, because you have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel. ⁷ When they grasped you in their hand, you broke, and when they leaned on you, you split, and their loins were torn. ⁸ Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: I am bringing a sword against you and cutting off humans and cattle from you. ⁹ And the land of Egypt will become a desolation and a waste, and they will know that I am the LORD. For they said, "My rivers are mine, and I made them." ¹⁰ Therefore, I am coming against you and your rivers, and I will make the land of Egypt a waste and a desolation from Migdol to the border of Cush. ¹¹ No human or beast will pass through it for forty years. It will not be inhabited for forty years. ¹² I will make the land of Egypt a desolation in the midst of lands that are desolate, and its cities in the midst of ruined cities will be a desolation for forty years. I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them into lands. ¹³ For this is what the Lord GOD says: After forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples among whom they were scattered. ¹⁴ I will return the captivity of Egypt and bring them back to the land of Pathros, to the land from which they were exiled. There they will be a lowly kingdom. ¹⁵ Among the nations it will be lowly, and it will not again lift itself up above the nations. I will diminish them so that they will not be numerous among the nations. ¹⁶ And the house of Israel will no longer be a hope or a reminder of sin when they turn after them, and they will know that I am the Lord GOD." ¹⁷ In the twenty-seventh year, on the first of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹⁸ "Son of man, Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, exerted his might in a great labor against Tyre—every head shaven, every shoulder rubbed raw, yet he received no wages for himself or for his army from Tyre for the labor he performed. ¹⁹ Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: I am giving Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, the land of Egypt. He will carry off its wealth, take its plunder, and it will be a wage for his army. ²⁰ For the labor he performed on my behalf, I am giving him the land of Egypt. This is the declaration of the Lord GOD. ²¹ On that day I will raise up a horn for the house of Israel, and I will open your mouth in their midst, and they will know that I am the LORD."

---

Notes
- v. 3: "crocodile" is used to render "תַּנִּים" (taninim), often translated as "dragon" or "serpent" in other traditions. Here, based on context and the metaphor of Egypt's rivers, "crocodile" is more appropriate.
- v. 17: "twenty-seventh year" refers to the regnal year of the exiled Judean community. The dating is preserved as in the Hebrew text.
- v. 18: "every head shaven, every shoulder rubbed raw" is a vivid description of hard labor. The Hebrew uses poetic parallelism, and the rendering aims to preserve that imagery in natural English.
