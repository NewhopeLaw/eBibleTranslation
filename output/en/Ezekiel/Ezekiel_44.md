# Ezekiel 44

¹ He turned me back by the way of the outer gate facing east, and it was shut. ² The Lord said to me, "This gate will remain shut. It will not be opened, and no one will enter through it, for the Lord, the God of Israel, has entered through it and will remain shut. ³ The prince, he alone will sit there to eat bread. He will enter through the porch of the gate and go out by the same way. ⁴ He brought me by the way of the northern gate and showed me the house. I looked, and behold, the glory of the Lord filled the house of the Lord, and I fell on my face. ⁵ The Lord said to me, "Son of man, set your heart, see with your eyes, and hear with your ears everything I am telling you regarding all the statutes of the house of the Lord and all its laws. Set your heart to understand the entrance of the house and all its exits from the sanctuary. ⁶ And say to the rebellious house, to the house of Israel, thus says the Lord GOD: Enough of all your abominations, house of Israel. ⁷ In bringing in foreign sons with uncircumcised hearts and uncircumcised flesh to be in My sanctuary, you have profaned My house by offering to Me your bread, fat and blood. You have broken My covenant with all your abominations. ⁸ And you did not guard My holy things, but you made them your chargekeepers in your sanctuary. ⁹ Therefore, thus says the Lord GOD: No foreigner with an uncircumcised heart and uncircumcised flesh may ever enter My sanctuary, not even any foreigner who is among the children of Israel. ¹⁰ Only the Levites who withdrew from Me when Israel went astray after their idols, and who carried their guilt— ¹¹ they will serve in My sanctuary as gatekeepers at the gates of the house and serve the house. They will slaughter the burnt offerings and the sacrifices for the people and stand before them to serve them. ¹² Because they served them before their idols and became a snare of guilt to the house of Israel, therefore I have raised My hand against them, says the Lord GOD, and they will bear their guilt. ¹³ They will not come near Me to serve Me, nor will they come near My holy things, the holy of holies. They will bear their disgrace and the abominations they committed. ¹⁴ But I will make them keepers of the charge of the house for all its work and for everything that is done in it. ¹⁵ The Levitical priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept My charge of My sanctuary when Israel went astray from Me, they will come near Me to serve Me, and they will stand before Me to offer to Me fat and blood, says the Lord GOD. ¹⁶ They will enter My sanctuary and come near My table to serve Me. They will guard My charge. ¹⁷ When they enter the inner court gates, they will wear linen robes, and no wool will come upon them when they serve in the inner court and in the house. ¹⁸ Linen turban will be on their heads and linen sashes on their waists. They will not wear anything that tightens around the body. ¹⁹ When they go out to the outer court, to the people, they will remove the robes in which they served and leave them in the holy chambers, and they will put on other garments, and they will not sanctify the people with their garments. ²⁰ Their heads they will not shave, and their beards they will not let grow. They will cover their heads. ²¹ No priest will drink wine when he enters the inner court. ²² A widow or a divorced woman they will not take as wives, but only virgins from the descendants of Israel, or a widow who is a widow of a priest. ²³ They will teach My people the difference between the holy and the common, and between the unclean and the clean. ²⁴ They will stand in judgment on disputes and judge by My judgments. They will keep My laws and My statutes in all My appointed feasts and will sanctify My Sabbaths. ²⁵ They will not go near a dead body to become unclean, except for a father, mother, son, daughter, brother, or sister who is a sister who has not married. Then they may become unclean. ²⁶ After he is cleansed, seven days will be counted for him. ²⁷ And on the day he enters the inner court to serve in the sanctuary, he will offer his sin offering, says the Lord GOD. ²⁸ And I will be their inheritance. They will not receive a possession in Israel, for I am their possession. ²⁹ The offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering they will eat. And every dedication in Israel will belong to them. ³⁰ The first of all the firstfruits of everything, and every offering of everything from all your offerings will be for the priests. And the first of your produce you will give to the priest, to place a blessing on your house. ³¹ No priest will eat the carcass or the torn flesh of bird or beast.

---

Notes
- v. 7: "you have profaned My house by offering to Me your bread, fat and blood" — "bread" here is a poetic term for the offerings made to God, not literal bread.
- v. 13: "the holy of holies" — the phrase "holy of holies" is used to emphasize the highest level of holiness, distinct from other holy things.
- v. 28: "I will be their inheritance" — the Levites are to have no territorial inheritance in Israel but are to be served by the people, with God Himself as their inheritance.
