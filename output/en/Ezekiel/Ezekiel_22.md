# Ezekiel 22

¹ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "And you, son of man, are you to judge the city of bloodshed? Then make known to her all her abominations. ³ Say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: You are a city that has spilled blood in your midst—your time has come, and I will bring confusion upon you to make you unclean. ⁴ In your blood that you have spilled, you have sinned, and in your abominations that you have done, you have become unclean. Your days are near their end, and your years have come to their appointed time. Therefore, I have made you a disgrace among the nations, a mockery to all lands. ⁵ Those near you and those far from you will mock you—unclean in name, great in abomination. ⁶ The leaders of Israel, each one for his own household, have become participants in your bloodshed. ⁷ Father and mother have been treated with contempt among you. The foreigner has been oppressed in your midst, the orphan and the widow have been robbed by you. ⁸ You have despised My holy things and profaned My Sabbaths. ⁹ There are men among you who are like informers, shedding blood. On the mountains, they have committed crimes in your midst. ¹⁰ You have exposed the nakedness of your father, and in your midst, you have practiced uncleanness with a woman in a state of ritual impurity. ¹¹ One man has committed an abomination with his neighbor's wife, another has defiled his daughter-in-law in wickedness, and another has humiliated his sister, the daughter of his father, in your midst. ¹² You have accepted bribes to shed blood, you have taken interest and usury, and you have exploited your neighbor through oppression. You have forgotten Me, says the Lord GOD." ¹³ Then I struck my hand against my thigh for the iniquities they had done and for the blood they had spilled in their midst. ¹⁴ "Will your heart hold out, or will your hands remain strong in the days when I act against you? I am the LORD; I have spoken, and I will do it. ¹⁵ I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you among the lands, and your uncleanness will be removed from you. ¹⁶ And you will inherit as a possession in the sight of the nations, and you will know that I am the LORD." ¹⁷ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹⁸ "Son of man, behold, the house of Israel has become like a mixture to Me—all of them bronze, iron, tin, and lead, mixed together in the middle of silver. ¹⁹ Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have become a single mixture, I will gather you into the middle of Jerusalem. ²⁰ Just as silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead are gathered into the middle of a furnace, to be blown into with fire to melt them, so I will gather you in My anger and in My wrath, and I will blow into you and melt you. ²¹ And I will blow into you with the fire of My wrath, and you will be melted in the middle of her. ²² Just as silver is melted in the middle of a furnace, so you will be melted in the middle of her. Then you will know that I am the LORD, I have poured out My wrath on you." ²³ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ²⁴ "Son of man, say to her: 'You are a land that was not washed, nor was there rain upon you in the day of wrath. ²⁵ Her leaders in her midst are like roaring lions tearing prey, killing people with violence and taking their possessions. Her widows have multiplied in her midst. ²⁶ Her priests have despised My law and profaned My holy things. They did not distinguish between the holy and the common, and between the unclean and the clean. They have hidden their eyes from My Sabbaths, and I have been profaned among them. ²⁷ Her rulers in her midst are like wolves tearing prey, to shed blood for greed, to destroy lives for profit. ²⁸ Her prophets have smeared salve on them, seeing falsehood, giving false oracles, saying, "This is what the Lord GOD says," when the LORD has not spoken. ²⁹ The people of the land have practiced oppression and taken what was not theirs. They have exploited the poor and the needy, and treated the foreigner with no justice. ³⁰ I sought among them for a man who would stand as a wall, someone to stand in the breach before Me on behalf of the land, so that I would not destroy it. I found no one. ³¹ Therefore, I will pour out My wrath on them in fire—My fury will consume them, and I will bring their ways to an end on their heads, says the Lord GOD."

---

Notes  
- v. 11: "committed an abomination with his neighbor's wife" — a literal reading of "to'evah" as "abomination" is used here to maintain the strong moral tone, as it often does in Hebrew legal and prophetic texts.  
- v. 30: "someone to stand in the breach" — the term "breach" (p'sach) is a metaphor for a gap in a wall, representing an opportunity to prevent disaster.  
- v. 31: "I will bring their ways to an end on their heads" — the phrase "on their heads" (al rosham) is a common idiom for judgment or consequence falling upon someone.
