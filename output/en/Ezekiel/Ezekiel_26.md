# Ezekiel 26

¹ In the twelfth year, on the first of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, because Tyre said, 'Ah, Jerusalem has been shattered! The gates of the peoples have been broken. I have turned her into a ruin, and her treasures are now mine!' ³ therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I am turning my face against you, Tyre, and I will raise many nations against you, as the sea raises its waves. ⁴ They will destroy your walls and tear down your towers. I will scrape your dust from you and make you a ruin to be a place of desolation. ⁵ You will lie in ruins in the middle of the sea. For I have spoken, says the Lord GOD. You will become a possession for the nations. ⁶ Your daughters in the field will be slain by the sword, and they will know that I am the LORD. ⁷ For thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I will bring Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, from the north, a king of kings, with horses and chariots and with an army and a great people. ⁸ He will kill your daughters in the field with the sword, and he will set a siege wall against you and build a ramp around you and raise a siege against you. ⁹ He will break down your walls and tear down your towers with his weapons. ¹⁰ The dust from the hooves of his horses will cover you, and the noise of his horses and the rumble of his chariots will make your walls tremble. When he enters your gates, it will be like troops entering a city that has been broken open. ¹¹ With the weapons of his horses, he will trample all your avenues. Your people will be slain with the sword, and your mighty strength will fall to the ground. ¹² He will plunder your wealth and seize your goods. He will tear down your walls and destroy your beautiful houses. He will throw your stones, your timber, and your dust into the sea. ¹³ I will put an end to the sound of your songs; your music will be heard no more. ¹⁴ I will make you a ruin, a place for the trampling of feet. You will be a waste, never to be rebuilt again. For I the LORD have spoken, says the Lord GOD. ¹⁵ Thus says the Lord GOD to Tyre: Will it not be because of the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan and the slain are slain in you, that the islands will tremble? ¹⁶ All the rulers of the sea will descend from their thrones, and they will remove their robes and strip off their embroidered garments. They will clothe themselves with trembling, sit on the ground, and be afraid of your terror. They will mourn for you. ¹⁷ They will raise a lament for you and say to you: 'How you have perished, you city of the sea, praised on the waters, the city whose dominion reached the sea and whose inhabitants gave their treasures to all who dwelled in her!' ¹⁸ Then the islands will fear on the day of your fall, and all the islands that are in the sea will be alarmed at your departure. ¹⁹ For thus says the Lord GOD: When I make you a ruin, like the ruins that are not inhabited, and I raise the deep over you and the great waters will cover you, ²⁰ I will bring you down to those who descend into the pit, to the people of old, and I will settle you in the depths of the earth, an eternal ruin, with those who descend into the pit, so that you will not be inhabited, and you will not exist on the land of the living. ²¹ I will destroy you, and you will no longer exist forever, says the Lord GOD."

---

Notes  
- v. 2: "I have turned her into a ruin, and her treasures are now mine!" — the Hebrew implies a boastful claim by Tyre that it has destroyed Jerusalem and taken its wealth.  
- v. 20: "the people of old" — the Hebrew is ambiguous, possibly referring to ancient peoples or the dead.  
- v. 21: "you will no longer exist forever" — the Hebrew uses a phrase that can mean "no more" or "lost forever," but it is not a direct reference to annihilation in the metaphysical sense.
