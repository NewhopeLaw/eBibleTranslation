# Ezekiel 47

¹ He brought me back to the entrance of the house, and there I saw water flowing out from beneath the threshold of the house toward the east, for the face of the house looked toward the east. The water flowed from the right side of the house, from the south, down toward the altar. ² He led me through the gate facing north, and walked me around outside to the gate facing east, and there I saw water flowing from the right side of the gate. ³ The man held a measuring rod in his hand. He measured a thousand cubits and led me through the water, which was ankle-deep. ⁴ He measured a thousand more and led me through the water, which was now waist-deep. He measured another thousand and led me through, the water now up to my waist. ⁵ He measured a thousand more, and I could not step through, for the water had risen and become a river that could not be crossed. ⁶ He said to me, "Have you seen this, son of man?" Then he brought me back to the bank of the river. ⁷ When I turned around, I saw a great number of trees growing along both sides of the river. ⁸ He said to me, "These waters flow toward the eastern region and go down into the Arabah. They will enter the Dead Sea and will make its waters fresh. ⁹ Every living creature that swarms, wherever the river flows, will live. And there will be a great abundance of fish, for these waters will bring life and healing to everything they touch. ¹⁰ Fishermen will stand along the river from En-gedi to En-e-glaim. The fish will be of many kinds, like the fish of the Great Sea, very numerous. ¹¹ But the brackish pools and marshes will not be made fresh. They will remain salty. ¹² Along the banks of the river, on either side, will grow all kinds of edible trees. Their leaves will not wither, and their fruit will not fail. They will bear fruit every month, for the waters of the sanctuary flow from them. Their fruit will be for food, and their leaves for healing." ¹³ Thus says the Lord GOD: "These are the boundaries by which you will divide the land to be inherited by the twelve tribes of Israel. Draw it up as a map. ¹⁴ You will inherit it, each of you, as your brothers. I have sworn with my hand to give this land to your ancestors, and this land will fall to you as your inheritance. ¹⁵ This is the boundary on the north side: from the Great Sea you will draw the boundary line toward Hethlon, to the town of Zedadah. ¹⁶ Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which lie between the boundary of Damascus and the boundary of Hamath, and the city of Hazar-enan, which lies on the boundary of Hauran. ¹⁷ The boundary will run from the sea to En-gannim, on the northern side, to the boundary of Hamath. ¹⁸ The eastern boundary will run between the territory of Hauran and the territory of Damascus, and between Gilead and the land of Israel, following the course of the Jordan from the Sea of the East to the Great Sea. ¹⁹ The southern boundary will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, to Kadesh, and on to the Great Sea. This will be the southern boundary. ²⁰ The western boundary will run from the Great Sea to the town of Hamath, to the entrance of Hamath. This will be the western boundary. ²¹ You will divide this land among yourselves by tribe for the people of Israel. ²² And whether it falls to you or to the foreigners living among you, who have had children among you, they will be to you like native-born Israelites. They too will inherit land among the tribes of Israel. ²³ In whatever tribe the foreigner resides, you will give him his inheritance," says the Lord GOD.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "En-gedi" and "En-e-glaim" are transliterations from Hebrew names; the Septuagint reads "Ingadêin" and "Enagaleim," but the Hebrew names are retained here.
- v. 14: "I have sworn with my hand..." reflects the Hebrew "נָשָׂאתִי אֶת־יָדִי," which is a formal and binding declaration.
- v. 22: "foreigners living among you" is a translation of "הַגֵּרִים הַגָּרִים בְּתוֹכְכֶם," emphasizing their settled presence.
