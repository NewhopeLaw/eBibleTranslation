# Ezekiel 48

¹ These are the names of the tribes, from the northern side to the eastern side, along the border of Hethlon. From there to the town of Zedadah, to the border of Hamath, to the town of En-gannim, on the northern side, to the border of Hamath, and the eastern side faces the sea. This is Dan's portion. ² On the border of Dan, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Asher's portion. ³ On the border of Asher, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Naphtali's portion. ⁴ On the border of Naphtali, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Manasseh's portion. ⁵ On the border of Manasseh, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Ephraim's portion. ⁶ On the border of Ephraim, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Reuben's portion. ⁷ On the border of Reuben, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Judah's portion. ⁸ On the border of Judah, from the eastern side to the western side, will be the offering. This offering, set apart for the LORD, will be fifty thousand cubits in length and fifty thousand cubits in width, corresponding to the size of one tribe's portion from east to west. The sanctuary will be in the middle of it. ⁹ The offering set apart for the LORD will be fifty thousand cubits in length and ten thousand cubits in width. ¹⁰ This offering for the priests will be fifty thousand cubits in length to the north, ten thousand cubits in width to the west, ten thousand cubits in width to the east, and fifty thousand cubits in length to the south, with the sanctuary of the LORD in the middle. ¹¹ This will be for the priests, the sons of Zadok, who guarded my charge and did not go astray in the way of the Israelites, unlike the Levites. ¹² A portion from the offering of the land will be given to them, set apart as most holy, from the boundaries of the Levitical lands. ¹³ The Levites' portion will be adjacent to the priests' boundaries, fifty thousand cubits in length and ten thousand cubits in width. The entire length will be fifty thousand cubits and the width ten thousand cubits. ¹⁴ Nothing in it may be sold, exchanged, or transferred. The first fruits of the land must not be taken from it, for it is holy to the LORD. ¹⁵ The remaining five thousand cubits in width, on the fifty-five thousand cubits side, will serve as a buffer for the city, for its dwelling and its open spaces, with the city in its center. ¹⁶ These are its dimensions: on the north side, five hundred and four thousand cubits; on the south side, five hundred and four thousand cubits; on the east side, five hundred and four thousand cubits; and on the west side, five hundred and four thousand cubits. ¹⁷ The buffer for the city will be five hundred and fifty cubits on the north side, five hundred and fifty cubits on the south side, five hundred and fifty cubits on the east side, and five hundred and fifty cubits on the west side. ¹⁸ The remaining length, opposite the offering of the holy portion, will be ten thousand cubits on the east side and ten thousand cubits on the west side. These will be the holy offering, and its produce will be for the food of those who serve the city. ¹⁹ Those who serve the city will be from all the tribes of Israel. ²⁰ The entire offering will be fifty thousand by fifty thousand, a square. You will set apart the holy offering from the city's possession. ²¹ The remaining land, on both sides of the holy offering and the city's possession, up to the eastern and western boundaries, will be the ruler's portion. On the western boundary, up to fifty-five thousand cubits, it will include the ruler's portions. The holy offering and the sanctuary of the house will be in the middle of it. ²² From the Levitical land and the city's possession, in the area belonging to the ruler, between the boundary of Judah and the boundary of Benjamin, the ruler's portion will be. ²³ The remaining tribes, from the eastern side to the western side, is Benjamin's portion. ²⁴ On the border of Benjamin, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Simeon's portion. ²⁵ On the border of Simeon, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Issachar's portion. ²⁶ On the border of Issachar, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Zebulun's portion. ²⁷ On the border of Zebulun, from the eastern side to the western side, is the tribe of Gad's portion. ²⁸ On the border of Gad, to the southern side, facing the Negev, the boundary will be from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, to Kadesh, on the Great Sea. ²⁹ This is the land that you will divide for the tribes of Israel as their inheritance. These are their divisions, says the Lord GOD. ³⁰ These are the exits of the city, on the northern side, five hundred and four thousand cubits in measure. ³¹ The gates of the city will be named after the tribes of Israel, three gates on the north side: one gate for Reuben, one gate for Judah, and one gate for Levi. ³² On the eastern side, five hundred and four thousand cubits, and three gates: one gate for Joseph, one gate for Benjamin, and one gate for Dan. ³³ On the southern side, five hundred and four thousand cubits in measure, and three gates: one gate for Simeon, one gate for Issachar, and one gate for Zebulun. ³⁴ On the western side, five hundred and four thousand cubits in measure, and three gates: one gate for Gad, one gate for Asher, and one gate for Naphtali. ³⁵ The entire perimeter will be eighteen thousand cubits. The name of the city will be "The LORD's Day," for it will be established there.

Notes
- v. 8: "This offering, set apart for the LORD, will be fifty thousand cubits in length and fifty thousand cubits in width" — the Hebrew is complex, but the structure indicates the offering's dimensions and its correspondence to one tribe's portion.
- v. 28: "Facing the Negev" — the Hebrew uses "תֵּימָנָה" which is a poetic or ancient term for the Negev (southern region).
- v. 35: "The name of the city will be 'The LORD's Day'" — the Hebrew phrase "מִיּוֹם יְהוָה" is translated as "The LORD's Day" to convey the meaning of a day belonging to the LORD, though the exact phrase is rare in biblical Hebrew.
