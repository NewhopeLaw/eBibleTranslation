# Ezekiel 23

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother. ³ They committed harlotry in Egypt in their youth; there they developed their breasts, there they were deflowered. ⁴ Their names were Aholah the elder and Aholibah her sister. They became mine and bore sons and daughters. Their names were Samaria for Aholah and Jerusalem for Aholibah. ⁵ Aholah committed harlotry while under my care, and she set her heart on her lovers, on the Assyrians nearby. ⁶ They were dressed in blue, captains and officials, young men of beauty, all of them horsemen who rode on horses. ⁷ She gave her harlotry to them, the chosen sons of Assyria, all of them. And in every one she loved, in all her lusts, she became defiled. ⁸ And she did not abandon her harlotry from Egypt; for there, they had lain with her in her youth, and they had deflowered her and poured out their harlotry upon her. ⁹ Therefore, I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, into the hands of the sons of Assyria, upon whom she had set her heart. ¹⁰ They uncovered her shame, took her sons and daughters, and killed her with the sword. And she became a byword among the women, and judgments were executed upon her daughters. ¹¹ And her sister Aholibah saw this, and she became envious in her passion over her sister and increased her harlotry in comparison to her sister. ¹² She set her heart on the sons of Assyria, captains and officials, those close to her, dressed in fine garments, horsemen who rode on horses—handsome young men, all of them. ¹³ And I saw that they were defiled, one and the same path for the two of them. ¹⁴ She added to her harlotry and saw men depicted on the wall, images of Chaldeans, carved in relief. ¹⁵ They had sashes around their waists, dyed in the fashion of the time, their heads shaved, their appearance like that of the sons of Chaldea, the land of their birth. ¹⁶ She set her heart on them at the sight of her eyes and sent messengers to them to Chaldea. ¹⁷ And the sons of Babylon came to her, to the bed of harlots, and defiled her with her harlotry. And she became defiled in them, and her spirit turned from them. ¹⁸ And she exposed her harlotry, and her shame; and my spirit turned from her, just as my spirit had turned from her sister. ¹⁹ And you increased your harlotry to remember the days of your youth, when you had committed harlotry in Egypt. ²⁰ And you set your heart on the Chaldeans, whose bodies were like those of donkeys, whose private parts were like those of horses. ²¹ And you recalled the iniquity of your youth, the things you did in Egypt in your dwelling, where your breasts were in your youth. ²² Therefore, Aholibah, this is what the Lord GOD says: "Surely I will raise up your lovers against you, from whom your spirit turned away, and I will bring them against you from all around. ²³ The sons of Babylon and all the Chaldeans, Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the sons of Assyria with them, young men, chosen men, captains and officials, all of them numerous and renowned, horsemen riding on horses. ²⁴ And they will come against you from the north, with chariots and wheels, and with a host of people, with shields and spears, and they will set a siege against you all around. ²⁵ And I will give judgment before them, and they will judge you in their judgments. And I will give my jealousy into you, and they will deal with you in wrath and anger. They will pluck out your nose and your ears, and the remainder of you will fall by the sword. They will take your sons and your daughters, and the remainder of you will be devoured by fire. ²⁶ And they will strip you of your robes, and take the ornaments of your splendor. ²⁷ And I will remove your iniquity from you and your harlotry from the land of Egypt, and your eyes will no longer look toward them, and Egypt will no longer be remembered by you again. ²⁸ For thus says the Lord GOD: "Surely I will give you into the hands of those you hate, from whom your spirit turned away. ²⁹ And they will deal with you in hatred, and take all your labors and your toil, and you will be naked and shamed, and your harlotry and your iniquity will be exposed. And your harlotry ³⁰ did these things to you, in your going after the nations, and you were defiled in their thoughts. ³¹ You walked in the way of your sister, and I will give her cup into your hand. ³² Thus says the Lord: "You will drink the cup of your sister, deep and wide, and full to the point of intoxication. ³³ And you will be filled with sorrow, and the cup of destruction, the cup of your sister. ³⁴ And you will drink it, and I will remove your festivals and your new moons. For I have spoken," says the Lord. ³⁵ "Therefore, this is what the Lord says: "Because you have forgotten me and cast me behind your back, you too must bear your iniquity and your harlotry." ³⁶ And the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "Son of man, will you judge Aholah and Aholibah, and declare to them their abominations? ³⁷ For they committed adultery, and blood was in their hands; they committed adultery through their thoughts, and they led away the children I had given them to be devoured by idols. ³⁸ Even more, they did this to me: they defiled my sanctuary on the same day, and they profaned my Sabbaths. ³⁹ And in the day they slaughtered their children for their idols, they entered my sanctuary on that day to profane it. And indeed, they did these things in my house. ⁴⁰ And even though they sent messengers to distant men, to whom they had sent a messenger, and when they came, they washed themselves and made their eyes bright and took up cosmetics. ⁴¹ And they sat on a costly bed, with a table set before them. My incense and my oil delighted them. ⁴² And a voice of music was made in them, and toward men coming in a multitude from the wilderness, they gave rings on their hands and a crown of splendor on their heads. ⁴³ And I said, "Would they not commit adultery? And indeed, she committed harlotry, and she herself became a harlot. ⁴⁴ And they came to her, just as one comes to a harlot, so they came to Aholah and to Aholibah, the ones who committed iniquity. ⁴⁵ And righteous men will judge them, judgment of adulteresses and judgment of those who shed blood, because they are adulteresses and blood is in their hands. ⁴⁶ For thus says the Lord GOD: "Raise a host against them and give them over to disgrace and mockery. ⁴⁷ And stone them with the stones of the host and strike them down with their swords. Their sons and their daughters you will kill, and their houses you will burn with fire. ⁴⁸ And I will remove iniquity from the land, and all the women will be corrected, and they will no longer do as Aholah and Aholibah did. ⁴⁹ And I will lay your iniquity upon you, and you will bear the guilt of your thoughts, and you will know that I am the LORD."

Notes  
- v. 14: "images of Chaldeans" — "Chaldeans" translates כַּשְׂדִּים (Casdim), referring to people or deities of Chaldea.  
- v. 17: "bed of harlots" — "harlots" is a rendering of מִשְׁכַּב דֹּדִים (mishkav dodim), literally "bed of lovers," but in context, "bed of harlots."  
- v. 20: "whose bodies were like those of donkeys" — "donkeys" corresponds to שָׂרִיד (saris), translated as "donkey" in context; "private parts" is from אַיִן (ayin), often used for genitalia.  
- v. 30: "in your going after the nations" — "going after" translates עַל אֲשֶׁר נִטְמֵאת בְּגִלּוּלֵיהֶם (al asher nitmate b'gilloteihem), meaning "in which you were defiled by their idols."  
- v. 35: "cast me behind your back" — "cast me behind your back" corresponds to שְׂאִי זִמָּתֵךְ וְאֶת־תַּזְנוּתָֽיִךְ (sa'i zimmat'kha ve'et taznutayikh), literally "bear your iniquity and your harlotry," but "cast me behind your back" is a paraphrase of the sentiment of turning away.
