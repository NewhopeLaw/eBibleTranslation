# Ezekiel 46

¹ Thus says the Lord GOD: The gate of the inner courtyard facing east will remain closed for six working days. It will be opened on the Sabbath and on the new moon. ² When the prince comes, he will enter through the inner gate, stand at the threshold of the gate, and the priests will offer his burnt offering and his peace offering. He will bow at the threshold of the gate and then go out. The gate will not be closed until evening. ³ The people of the land will bow at the threshold of this gate on the Sabbaths and on the new moons, in the presence of the LORD. ⁴ On the Sabbath the prince will offer to the LORD six unblemished male lambs and one unblemished ram. ⁵ The grain offering will be one ephah for the ram and a grain offering of his own gift for the lambs, and oil, a hin for each ephah. ⁶ On the new moon, the prince will offer an unblemished bull calf, six unblemished male lambs, and one unblemished ram. ⁷ The grain offering will be one ephah for the bull, one ephah for the ram, and a grain offering of his own gift for the lambs, and oil, a hin for each ephah. ⁸ When the prince enters through the inner gate, he will follow the same way to exit. ⁹ When the people of the land come to the presence of the LORD on the appointed festivals, whoever enters through the gate facing north to bow will exit through the gate facing south. Whoever enters through the gate facing south will exit through the gate facing north. No one will return through the gate by which they came, but will go straight out. ¹⁰ The prince will be among them when they enter, and he will go out with them when they leave. ¹¹ On the festivals and appointed times, the grain offering will be one ephah for the bull, one ephah for the ram, and a grain offering of their own gift for the lambs, and oil, a hin for each ephah. ¹² If the prince offers a voluntary burnt offering or a voluntary peace offering to the LORD, the gate facing east will be opened for him. He will offer his burnt offering and his peace offering as he does on the Sabbath, and then go out. After his departure, the gate will be closed. ¹³ A one-year-old unblemished male lamb will be offered as a daily burnt offering to the LORD. It will be offered every morning. ¹⁴ A grain offering will be offered with it every morning, one sixth of an ephah, and oil, one third of a hin to mix with the flour. This will be a perpetual requirement, an offering to the LORD. ¹⁵ You will offer the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil every morning. It will be a perpetual burnt offering. ¹⁶ Thus says the Lord GOD: If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons from his inheritance, it will become the possession of his sons. It will be an inheritance for them. ¹⁷ If the prince gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it will remain with that servant until the year of release, when it will return to the prince. But the inheritance of his sons will remain with them. ¹⁸ The prince must not take from the inheritance of the people to oppress them, taking their land from them. He must give an inheritance to his sons, so that my people will not be scattered, each from his inheritance. ¹⁹ He brought me to the entrance at the side of the gate, to the rooms of the holy place, facing north. There I saw a space set apart in the inner court. ²⁰ He said to me, "This is the place where the priests will cook the guilt offering and the sin offering, so that the grain offering may be prepared there without taking anything out to the outer courtyard, to sanctify the people." ²¹ He led me into the outer courtyard and walked me around the four corners of the courtyard. I saw a small courtyard at each corner of the outer courtyard. ²² Each of these corner courtyards was forty cubits long and thirty cubits wide, with the same measure for all four. ²³ A walkway ran around each of them, and beneath the walkways were cooking areas built in. ²⁴ He said to me, "These are the kitchens where the servants of the house will cook the sacrifices of the people."

Notes
- v. 14: "one sixth of an ephah" — the Hebrew uses שִׁשִּׁית, a fractional term meaning "one sixth."
- v. 15: "perpetual burnt offering" — the Hebrew uses עוֹלַת תָּמִיד, where תָּמִיד means "perpetual" or "continual."
- v. 17: "year of release" — the Hebrew uses שְׁנַת הַדְּרוֹר, a reference to the Year of Jubilee or a general release year.
- v. 18: "to oppress" — the Hebrew uses הֽוֹנֹתָם, which is a rare form. The common form would be הֹנָם. This is noted in the glossary as "to oppress."
- v. 19: "set apart" — the Hebrew is יָֽמָּה, which can mean "set apart" or "prepared," depending on context.
