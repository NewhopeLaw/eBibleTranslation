# Ezekiel 43

¹ He led me to the gate, the gate facing east. ² And behold, the glory of the God of Israel was coming from the east, the sound of it like the sound of many waters, and the earth gleamed from His glory. ³ The vision I saw was like the vision I had seen when I was brought to destroy the city, and the vision was like the vision I had seen on the river Chebar. I fell on my face. ⁴ And the glory of the LORD entered the house by the way of the gate facing east. ⁵ Then the spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner courtyard, and behold, the glory of the LORD filled the house. ⁶ And I heard someone speaking to me from the house, and a man stood beside me. ⁷ He said to me, "Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the place of the soles of My feet, where I will dwell among the children of Israel forever. And Israel will no longer defile My holy name, neither they nor their kings, by their whoredom and by the deaths of their kings in their high places. ⁸ When they placed their doors opposite My doors and their thresholds opposite My thresholds, and the wall between Me and them was one, they defiled My holy name with their abominations that they committed. So I consumed them in My anger. ⁹ Therefore, now remove their whoredom and the deaths of their kings from Me, and I will dwell among them forever. ¹⁰ You, son of man, show the house of Israel the house, and let them take to heart their sins, and measure the plan. ¹¹ And if they take to heart all that they did, observe the form of the house and its arrangement, its exits and entrances, and all its forms, its laws and all its forms, and write it down for them, so that they may keep all its forms and all its laws and do them. ¹² This is the law of the house on the top of the mountain, all its boundaries around it are holy, holy of holies. This is the law of the house. ¹³ These are the measurements of the altar in cubits, a cubit and a handbreadth, a cubit deep and a cubit wide, and its border to the edge of it all around was a handbreadth. This is the height of the altar. ¹⁴ From the bottom of the ground to the lower court, two cubits, and one cubit wide. From the lower court to the upper court, four cubits, and one cubit wide. ¹⁵ The upper platform was four cubits, and from the upper platform and upward the horns were four. ¹⁶ The upper platform was twelve cubits long, twelve cubits wide, square in all four sides. ¹⁷ The court was fourteen cubits long, fourteen cubits wide, square in all four sides, and its border around it was half a cubit, and its wall was one cubit all around, with stairs facing east. ¹⁸ And He said to me, "Son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: These are the laws of the altar in the day of its construction, to offer a burnt offering on it and to pour out blood on it. ¹⁹ And you shall give to the priests, the Levites, who are from the seed of Zadok, who draw near to Me, says the Lord GOD, a young bull for a sin offering. ²⁰ Take some of its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the court, and on the border all around, and purify it and make atonement for it. ²¹ Take the bull of the sin offering and burn it in the appointed place of the house outside the sanctuary. ²² On the second day you shall bring a male goat without blemish for a sin offering, and purify the altar as they purified it with the bull. ²³ After you have finished making atonement, bring a young bull without blemish and a ram without blemish from the flock. ²⁴ And they shall be brought before the LORD, and the priests shall cast salt on them, and they shall offer them as burnt offerings to the LORD. ²⁵ For seven days you shall prepare a male goat for a sin offering each day, a young bull and a ram without blemish from the flock. ²⁶ For seven days they shall make atonement for the altar and purify it, and fill their hands. ²⁷ And when the days are completed, on the eighth day the priests shall offer on the altar your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, and I will accept you, says the Lord GOD."

Notes
- v. 3: "The vision I saw...": The Hebrew uses the term "the vision" (הַמַּרְאֶה) twice, likely referring to prior prophetic experiences. The reference to "the vision I had seen on the river Chebar" alludes to Ezekiel 1.
- v. 22: "a male goat without blemish": The Greek (Septuagint) reads "two male goats," but the Hebrew has a singular form. The singular is followed here.
- v. 24: "cast salt on them": The term "salt" (מֶלַח) here likely refers to the customary ritual use of salt in sacrifices, as found in Levitical law.
