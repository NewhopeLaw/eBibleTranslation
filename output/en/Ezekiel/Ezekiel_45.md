# Ezekiel 45

¹ When you apportion the land as a possession, set aside a tribute to the LORD from the land, a holy portion from the land, five thousand and twenty thousand in length, and ten thousand in width. Holy it will be in all its boundaries, surrounding. ² From this, there will be an offering, five hundred by five hundred, square, surrounding, and fifty cubits as its margin, surrounding. ³ From this measurement, measure out a length of twenty-five thousand and a width of ten thousand, and in it will be the sanctuary, most holy of holies. ⁴ A holy portion from the land will be for the priests who serve in the sanctuary, for those who draw near to serve the LORD. It will be for them a place for houses, set apart for the sanctuary. ⁵ Twenty-five thousand in length and ten thousand in width will be for the Levites who serve the house, as an inheritance for them, twenty cities for dwellings. ⁶ And the portion of the city you will give, five thousand in width and twenty-five thousand in length, just as the tribute of the holy portion is for all the house of Israel. ⁷ And for the prince, from this side and from that side, regarding the tribute of the holy portion and the portion of the city, facing the tribute of the holy portion and facing the portion of the city, from the western side toward the sea and from the eastern side toward the sunrise, and the length of his territory will be one of the portions, from the boundary of the sea to the boundary of the sunrise. ⁸ The land will be his possession in Israel, and the leaders of Israel will no longer oppress my people. The land will be given to the house of Israel according to their tribes. ⁹ Thus says the Lord GOD: Enough for you, leaders of Israel. Cease violence and oppression, do justice and righteousness. Remove the burdens from my people, says the Lord GOD. ¹⁰ Let there be for you just measures, just weights, and just bushels. ¹¹ The bushel and the ephah will be of the same measure, to hold a tenth of a homer. A tenth of the homer will be the standard. ¹² The shekel will be twenty gerahs. Twenty shekels, five and twenty shekels, fifty shekels will be the maneh for you. ¹³ This is the tribute you will set aside: one sixth of an ephah from the grain, and one sixth of an ephah from the barley. ¹⁴ The measure of oil will be one tenth of a bath from the hundred baths, because ten baths make a homer. ¹⁵ One sheep from the flock of the two hundred of Israel will be for a grain offering, for a burnt offering, and for a peace offering, to atone for them, says the Lord GOD. ¹⁶ All the people of the land will give this tribute to the prince in Israel. ¹⁷ And the prince will bear the burnt offerings, the grain offerings, and the libations on the festivals, on the new moons, and on the Sabbaths, on all the appointed feasts of the house of Israel. He will perform the sin offering, the grain offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offering, to atone for the house of Israel. ¹⁸ Thus says the Lord GOD: On the first day of the first month, you will take a bull without blemish and purify the sanctuary. ¹⁹ The priest will take some of the blood of the sin offering and put it on the doorposts of the house, on the four corners of the courtyard, on the altar, and on the doorposts of the gate of the inner court. ²⁰ And so you will do on the first day of the seventh month, for each person, and you will purify the house. ²¹ On the first day of the fourteenth of the month, you will have the Passover, a festival. Unleavened bread will be eaten for seven days. ²² And on that day, the prince will offer for himself and for the house and for all the people of the land a bull as a sin offering. ²³ And for the seven days of the festival, he will offer burnt offerings to the LORD, seven bulls and seven unblemished rams each day for the seven days, and a male goat as a sin offering each day, and a grain offering. ²⁴ And an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram you will prepare, and oil, a hin for the ephah. ²⁵ On the seventh day, the fifteenth of the month, on the festival, he will do likewise for seven days, as for the sin offering, as for the burnt offering, as for the grain offering, and as for the oil.

Notes
- v. 1: "holy it will be in all its boundaries, surrounding" — the Hebrew uses a poetic and spatial construction to emphasize the holiness of the portion.
- v. 19: "the doorposts of the house" — the term "doorposts" (mezuzot) is used here in reference to the corners or sides of the sanctuary.
- v. 20: "for each person" — the Septuagint suggests "for each lot" or "for each tribe," but the Hebrew is more general. The choice of "each person" preserves that generality.
- v. 21: "unleavened bread will be eaten for seven days" — this is a paraphrase of "you will eat unleavened bread for seven days," for clarity and natural English flow.
- v. 25: "as for the sin offering, as for the burnt offering, as for the grain offering, and as for the oil" — the Hebrew uses four parallel clauses, each introduced with "as for," and these are rendered as such for clarity.
