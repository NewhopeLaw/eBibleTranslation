# Ezekiel 39

¹ And you, son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, "Thus says the Lord, the LORD: I am coming toward you, Gog, prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal. ² I will turn you around and lead you, and I will bring you from the remote parts of the north, and I will lead you against the mountains of Israel. ³ I will break your bow from your left hand and your arrows from your right hand; I will cast them down. ⁴ On the mountains of Israel you will fall—you and all your armies and the peoples with you. You will become food for every kind of bird and beast of the field. ⁵ You will fall on the surface of the field, for I have spoken, declares the Lord. ⁶ I will send fire upon Magog and upon those living securely in the islands, and they will know that I am the LORD. ⁷ I will make my holy name known in the midst of my people Israel, and I will no longer let my holy name be profaned. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel. ⁸ "Here it is, and it will come to pass," declares the Lord, the LORD. "This is the day I have spoken about. ⁹ The people of Israel who live in the cities will go out and set fire to weapons, shields, and spears, bows and arrows, slings and stones, and they will burn them for seven years. ¹⁰ They will not take wood from the field or cut down trees from the forests, for they will burn weapons with fire. They will plunder the plunderers and seize the plunderers, declares the Lord. ¹¹ "On that day I will give Gog a place of burial in Israel, in the Valley of the Passersby at the edge of the sea. The passersby will block it in. ¹² They will bury there Gog and all his army, and they will call the place the Valley of the Army of Gog. ¹³ "The people of Israel will bury them to purify the land for seven months. ¹⁴ The people of the land will bury everyone who passes by, the survivors who are on the surface of the land, to purify it after seven months. They will search for those buried. ¹⁵ Whoever goes through the land and finds a human bone will set up a marker beside it until the buryers come and bury it in the Valley of the Army of Gog. ¹⁶ For the name of the city will be 'The Army,' and the land will be purified. ¹⁷ "You, son of man, say to every bird of the sky and to every beast of the field, 'Come, gather and be assembled, and come from every side to my sacrifice, which I offer to you—a great sacrifice on the mountains of Israel. Eat flesh and drink blood. ¹⁸ Eat the flesh of warriors, drink the blood of the chieftains of the earth—rams, young bulls, and fattened goats. ¹⁹ You will eat fat until you are satisfied and drink blood until you are drunk, from my sacrifice that I offer to you. ²⁰ You will be satisfied at my table with horses and riders, with warriors and every soldier," declares the Lord, the LORD. ²¹ "I will display my glory among the nations, and all the nations will see the judgment I have carried out and the hand I have placed on them. ²² The house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God from that day forward. ²³ The nations will know that it was for their sins that the house of Israel was taken into exile, for they rebelled against me and I turned my face away from them and gave them into the hands of their enemies, and they all fell by the sword. ²⁴ I did this to them for their impurities and their transgressions, and I turned my face away from them. ²⁵ Therefore, thus says the Lord, the LORD: I will now restore the captivity of Jacob and show mercy to all the house of Israel, out of devotion to my holy name. ²⁶ They will bear their disgrace and their infamy for their rebellion against me, and I will no longer withhold my face from them when they live securely on their land, with no one to frighten them. ²⁷ When I bring them back from the nations and gather them from the lands of their enemies, I will be sanctified in them, in the sight of many nations. ²⁸ They will know that I am the LORD their God when I lead them out of the nations and bring them back to their land, and I will not leave them again there. ²⁹ I will no longer hide my face from them, for I will have poured out my Spirit on the house of Israel," declares the Lord, the LORD.

Notes
- v. 11: "Valley of the Passersby" is a contextual rendering of גֵּי הָעֹבְרִים (gei ha-oveirim), which literally means "Valley of the Passersby," but is here given a poetic and interpretive translation to fit the narrative.
- v. 17: "my sacrifice" is a contextual rendering of זִבְחִי (zivchi), which can be more literally "my slaughter" but is best understood here as a divine sacrifice or offering.
- v. 18: "rams, young bulls, and fattened goats" are taken from כִּי אֵילִים כָּרִים וְעַתּוּדִים פָּרִים מְרִיאֵי בָשָׁן (ki eilim carim ve'atudim parim meriai bashan), where "rams" and "young bulls" are standard renderings, and "fattened goats" is a contextual interpretation of מְרִיאֵי בָשָׁן (meriai bashan), often associated with the famous fat cattle of Bashan.
- v. 25: "out of devotion to my holy name" is a contextual rendering of וְקִנֵּאתִי לְשֵׁם קָדְשִֽׁי (vekinneti leshem qadhashi), where "kinnat" is rendered as "devotion" to avoid theological connotations of envy or jealousy.
