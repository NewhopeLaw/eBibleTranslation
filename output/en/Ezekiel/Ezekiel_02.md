# Ezekiel 2

¹ He said to me, “Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak to you.” ¹⁰ He spread it before me, and it was written on both sides, and there was writing in it: lamentations, mourning, and woe. ² Then a spirit came into me, as the LORD had spoken, and it raised me and stood me on my feet, and I heard the one speaking to me. ³ He said to me, “Son of man, I am sending you to the children of Israel, to a rebellious nation that has rebelled against me, they and their fathers, right up to this very day. ⁴ They are a people with stubborn faces and hard hearts. I am sending you to them, and you will say to them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’ ⁵ If they listen or if they refuse, it is because they are a house of rebellion, and they will know that a prophet is among them.” ⁶ You, son of man, do not fear them, and do not be afraid of their words, for they are rebels, and they will surround you and attack you. You are like a scorpion among them. Do not be afraid of their words, and do not shrink from their presence, because they are a house of rebellion. ⁷ You will speak my words to them. If they listen or if they refuse, it is because they are rebels. ⁸ You, son of man, listen to what I am speaking to you. Do not be rebellious like the house of rebellion. Open your mouth and eat what I give you. ⁹ I looked, and behold, a hand was stretched out to me, and in it was a scroll of writing.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: “son of man” is a literal rendering of בן־אדם (ben-adam), used as a title for Ezekiel.  
- v. 9–10: The phrase "scroll of writing" is used to convey the nature of the object given to Ezekiel, with the content described as "lamentations, mourning, and woe," following the Hebrew terms.
