# Ezekiel 25

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, turn your face toward the sons of Ammon and prophesy against them. ³ Say to the sons of Ammon, 'Hear the word of the Lord GOD. Thus says the Lord GOD: Because you rejoiced over My sanctuary being defiled, and over the land of Israel being laid waste, and over the house of Judah being driven into exile, ⁴ therefore behold, I am giving you to the sons of the east for a possession, and they will pitch their tents among you. They will set up their dwellings in you, eat your fruit, and drink your milk. ⁵ I will give Rabbah to the nomads of camels, and the sons of Ammon to a resting place for flocks, so that you will know that I am the LORD.' ⁶ For thus says the Lord GOD: Because you clapped your hands and stamped your foot, rejoicing with all your heart over the land of Israel, ⁷ therefore behold, I am stretching out My hand against you and giving you as spoil to the nations. I will cut you off from the peoples and destroy you from the lands. You will be obliterated, and you will know that I am the LORD." ⁸ Thus says the Lord GOD: Because you said, "Ah, Moab is as good as any other nation. It is like the house of Judah," ⁹ therefore behold, I am opening the arm of Moab from the cities, from its border cities, from the chosen land of the house of Beth-jeshimoth, of Baal-meon, and of Kiriateim, ¹⁰ to the sons of the east, the sons of Ammon. I am giving it to them as a possession, so that the name of the sons of Ammon may be forgotten among the nations. ¹¹ And in Moab I will execute judgments, and they will know that I am the LORD. ¹² Thus says the Lord GOD: Because Edom took vengeance for itself and pursued a vengeance against the house of Judah, and has sinned greatly and taken vengeance, ¹³ therefore thus says the Lord GOD: I am stretching out My hand against Edom, cutting off from it man and beast, and making it a desolation. From Teman and Dedan they will fall by the sword. ¹⁴ And I will execute My vengeance on Edom through the hand of My people Israel, and they will deal with Edom according to My anger and My wrath, and they will know My vengeance," says the Lord GOD. ¹⁵ Thus says the Lord GOD: Because the Philistines acted in vengeance and took vengeance with a heart set on destruction, to destroy an eternal enmity, ¹⁶ therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I am stretching out My hand against the Philistines and cutting off Kereth and destroying the remnant of the seacoast. ¹⁷ And I will execute great vengeance upon them, through acts of judgment and in blazing wrath, and they will know that I am the LORD, when I bring My vengeance upon them."

---

Notes  
- v. 3: "My sanctuary being defiled" is an interpretive rendering of "nichal," which literally means "made a desolation" or "laid waste." The context of rejoicing over the defilement of sacred space supports this translation.  
- v. 5: "Nomads of camels" is a contextual rendering of "noveh gamalim," where "noveh" is "nomad" and "gamal" is "camel."  
- v. 14: "My vengeance" is retained rather than "His vengeance," since it is the Lord speaking and thus the pronoun is appropriately owned by the subject.
