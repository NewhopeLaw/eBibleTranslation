# Ezekiel 13

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² Son of man, prophesy to the prophets of Israel, and say to them, “Listen to the word of the LORD.” ³ Thus says the Lord GOD: “Woe to the prophets who follow their own hearts and do not see anything. ⁴ Like foxes in the wastelands are the prophets of Israel. ⁵ They have not climbed the breaches and have not built a wall for the house of Israel to stand in battle on the day of the LORD. ⁶ They have seen visions in vain and spoken lies, saying, ‘This is what the LORD says,’ when the LORD did not send them, and yet they claim to speak on behalf of the LORD. ⁷ Have you not seen false visions? Have you not told lies? ⁸ Therefore, thus says the Lord GOD: “Because your visions are false and your prophecies are lies, I will come against you,” declares the Lord GOD. ⁹ “My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and who give lies as oracles to mislead my people. They will not be part of the assembly of my people, they will not be recorded in the scroll of the house of Israel, and they will not enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.” ¹⁰ “Because they have led my people astray, saying ‘Peace, peace’ when there is no peace, and because they have built a wall and it collapses, ¹¹ say to those who build the wall, ‘It will collapse!’ and a storm will flood it. I will send stones like boulders for its fastenings, and it will fall. A wind will tear it apart. ¹² When the wall falls, will they not say to you, ‘Where is the plaster that you applied?’ ¹³ Therefore, thus says the Lord GOD: “I will tear apart the wind that rages in my anger and a storm that floods in my wrath. I will send boulders in my wrath to destroy it. ¹⁴ I will break down the wall you built, and I will throw it to the ground, and its foundation will be exposed. It will fall, and you will be consumed in the midst of it. Then you will know that I am the LORD. ¹⁵ I will bring my anger to an end on that wall and on those who plaster it, and I will say to you, ‘The wall is gone, and the plasterers are gone.’ ¹⁶ You prophets of Israel, who prophesy falsely about Jerusalem and claim peace for her when there is no peace, declares the Lord GOD.” ¹⁷ And you, son of man, set your face toward the daughters of your people who prophesy from their own hearts, and prophesy against them. ¹⁸ Say to them, “Thus says the Lord GOD: ‘Woe to those who sew head coverings for all the hands and make veils for every head, in order to entrap lives. They entrap lives, and they themselves are entrapped.’” ¹⁹ “They have defiled me among my people by using twisted threads and deceptive loaves, to kill lives that should not die and to keep alive lives that should not live, by speaking lies to my people who are inclined to listen to lies.” ²⁰ Therefore, thus says the Lord GOD: “I will come against the head coverings you make, in which you entrap lives. I will tear them from your arms and send the lives you have entrapped into dispersion. ²¹ I will tear apart your veils, and I will rescue my people from your hands, and they will no longer be in your hands for entrapment. Then you will know that I am the LORD.” ²² “Because you have led the heart of the righteous astray, and I did not lead it, and because you have strengthened the hands of the wicked to keep them from turning from their evil way and living, ²³ therefore, false visions you will no longer see, and deceptive omens you will no longer give. I will rescue my people from your hands, and you will know that I am the LORD.”

---

Notes  
- v. 10: “they have built a wall” — the metaphor refers to false security or false prophecy, not a literal wall.  
- v. 18: “sew head coverings for all the hands” — metaphor for deceptive practices used to manipulate people.  
- v. 20: “head coverings” and “veils” — metaphorical terms for deceptive practices.
