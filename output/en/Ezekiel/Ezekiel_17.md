# Ezekiel 17

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² Son of man, compose a parable and speak a riddle to the house of Israel. ³ Say, "This is what the Lord GOD says: A great eagle with large wings and long feathers, full of eyes, who has dominion, came into Lebanon and took the top branches of the cedar. ⁴ He plucked the tender sprout of its branches and carried it to the land of Canaan, to a city of traders. He planted it there. ⁵ Then he took a seed from the land and gave it to a field, a fertile field by many waters. He placed it there as a planted vine, causing it to sprout. ⁶ It sprouted and became a vine, low and small in height, with its branches turning toward it and its roots beneath it. It became a vine and produced branches and sent out tendrils. ⁷ Then another great eagle with large wings and much prey came. And this vine turned its roots toward him and extended its branches to him for water from his irrigation channels. ⁸ It was planted in a good field, by many waters, to sprout and bear fruit, to be a flourishing vine. ⁹ Therefore, say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Will it thrive? Will not its roots be pulled up and its fruit withered? All its tender sprouts will dry up. It will not require a strong arm or a great crowd to uproot it from its roots. ¹⁰ And indeed, it is planted. Will it thrive? Will not the moment the hot wind touches it, it withers? Its tender sprouts will wither. ¹¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ¹² Say to the house of Meri: Do you not know what these things are? Say: Here, the king of Babylon has come to Jerusalem, and he took its king and its officials and brought them to him in Babylon. ¹³ He took a seed from the royal line and made a covenant with it, binding it with an oath and taking the leaders of the land. ¹⁴ To be a lowly kingdom, not rising up, to keep his covenant and stand by it. ¹⁵ But he rebelled against him, sending messengers to Egypt to give him horses and a large army. Will he succeed? Will the one who does such things and breaks the covenant escape? ¹⁶ I live, says the Lord GOD, if he is not in the place of the king who made him, the one who despised my oath and broke my covenant, in the middle of Babylon, he will die. ¹⁷ And no great force or large crowd will make Pharaoh help him in battle, with chariots and with towers to cut off many lives. ¹⁸ He has despised an oath to break a covenant, and yet he has given his hand. All these things he has done, so he will not escape. ¹⁹ Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: I live, if I do not repay the oath he despised and the covenant he broke, and place it on his head. ²⁰ And I will cast a net over him and capture him in my snare. I will bring him to Babylon and judge him there for the crime he committed against me. ²¹ And all his fugitives will fall by the sword in every branch. The remainder will scatter to every wind, and you will know that I am the LORD. I have spoken. ²² This is what the Lord GOD says: I will take a shoot from the lofty cedar. I will pluck the tender sprout from the top of its branches and plant it myself on a high mountain, a mountain of Israel. ²³ On a high mountain of Israel I will plant it, and it will sprout and bear fruit, becoming a towering cedar. And all birds of every wing will dwell under its shade, under the shadow of its branches. ²⁴ And all the trees of the field will know that I am the LORD. I bring down the high tree, I raise the low tree. I dry up the green tree and make the dry tree flourish. I am the LORD. I have spoken and I will do it.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "binding it with an oath" — the Hebrew uses "בְּאָלָה" (baalah), which can mean "binding with an oath" or "taking a vow." The context suggests a covenant sealed by an oath.
- v. 17: "with chariots and with towers" — the Hebrew "בְּסֹלְלָה וּבִבְנוֹת דָּיֵק" is translated as "with chariots and with towers," referring to military defenses.
- v. 23: "birds of every wing" — the Hebrew "כֹּל צִפּוֹר כָּל־כָּנָף" is rendered to capture the sense of all kinds of birds.
