# Ezekiel 11

¹ A spirit lifted me up and brought me to the gate of the house of the LORD that faces east, the one looking toward the sunrise. And there, at the gate's entrance, were about twenty-five men. I saw among them Jaazaniah son of Azur and Pedaiah son of Benaya, leaders of the people. ² Then the LORD said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise evil and give wicked advice in this city. ³ They say, 'The city is not near—houses are being built. This is the pot, and we are the flesh in it.' ⁴ Therefore prophesy against them. Prophesy, son of man." ⁵ The spirit of the LORD fell on me, and He said to me, "Say, 'This is what the LORD says: You have spoken in this house of Israel, and I have known the thoughts of your spirit. ⁶ You have multiplied the dead in this city, filling its streets with slain. ⁷ Therefore this is what the LORD says: The dead you have slain in it are the flesh, and this city is the pot. I will bring you out from it. ⁸ The sword you fear, I will bring upon you, declares the LORD. ⁹ I will bring you out from the midst of it and give you into the hands of foreigners. I will execute judgments upon you. ¹⁰ You will fall by the sword on the borders of Israel. I will judge you, and you will know that I am the LORD. ¹¹ This city will no longer be your pot, and you will be the flesh in it. I will judge you on the borders of Israel. ¹² You will know that I am the LORD. You have not walked in My statutes, you have not done My judgments, but you have acted according to the customs of the nations around you.'" ¹³ As I was prophesying, Pedaiah son of Benaya died. I fell facedown, cried out in a loud voice, and said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Are You bringing an end to the remainder of Israel?" ¹⁴ Then the word of the LORD came to me: ¹⁵ "Son of man, your brothers and your kin, the men of your redemption, the whole house of Israel, say to those in Jerusalem, 'Far from us is the LORD. The land is given to us as a possession.' ¹⁶ Therefore say, 'This is what the LORD says: I will certainly scatter them among the nations and disperse them among the lands. Yet I will be a holy place for them in the lands where they go. ¹⁷ Therefore say, 'This is what the LORD says: I will gather you from the peoples and bring you together from the lands where you were scattered. I will give you back the land of Israel. ¹⁸ And when they come there, they will remove all its detestable things and all its abominations from it. ¹⁹ I will give them a new heart and put a new spirit within them. I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh, ²⁰ so that they may walk in My statutes and keep My judgments and do them. They will be My people, and I will be their God. ²¹ But as for the heart that goes after their detestable things and abominations, I will give them their own way to the head, declares the LORD.'" ²² The cherubim lifted up their wings, and the wheels were beside them. The glory of the God of Israel was above them. ²³ Then the glory of the LORD rose from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain that is east of the city. ²⁴ A spirit lifted me up and brought me to Chaldea in a vision, in the spirit of God. The vision that I had seen left me. ²⁵ I spoke to the exiles all the words the LORD had shown me.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "This is the pot, and we are the flesh in it" — this metaphor reflects the idea of being cooked within a vessel, implying vulnerability and impending doom.
- v. 12: "You will know that I am the LORD" — repeated for emphasis and as a divine claim to recognition.
- v. 21: "I will give them their own way to the head" — idiomatic rendering for "let them follow their own destructive path."
- v. 25: "The vision that I had seen left me" — clear reference to the vision's end, consistent with the narrative structure.
