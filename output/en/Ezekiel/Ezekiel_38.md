# Ezekiel 38

¹ The word of the LORD came to me, saying: ² "Son of man, set your face toward Gog, the land of Magog, the prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal. Prophesy against him. ³ Say, 'This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I am coming toward you, Gog, the prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal. ⁴ I will turn you around and place breath in your nostrils. I will bring you and your entire army—horses and horsemen, all of you a vast assembly, fully equipped with shields and helmets, brandishing swords—all of you. ⁵ Cush and Put will be with you, all of them armed with shields and helmets. ⁶ Gomer and all her troops, the house of Togarmah from the northernmost parts, and all her allies—many peoples will be with you. ⁷ Prepare yourself and be ready, you and your entire assembled force that has gathered to you. You will serve as a guard for them. ⁸ After many days, in the last years, you will come to the land that has been restored from war, gathered from many peoples, onto the mountains of Israel, which had been a perpetual ruin, but is now inhabited in peace by many peoples. ⁹ You will rise like a storm, coming as a cloud to cover the land. You and all your troops and the many peoples with you will be like that. ¹⁰ "This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: On that day, thoughts will arise in your mind, and you will devise a wicked plan. ¹¹ You will say, 'I will go up to the land of the abandoned, to the people living in peace, all of them residing in a land without walls, bolts, or gates. ¹² I will take spoil and seize plunder. I will put my hand against the ruins that have been restored, and against the people gathered from the nations, who have made possessions and acquired property. I will descend upon them who dwell at the center of the earth.' ¹³ "Sheba and Dedan and the traders of Tarshish, with all their young ones, will ask you, 'Are you coming to take spoil? Are you gathering your company to take silver and gold, to take possessions and acquire property, to take great spoil?' ¹⁴ "Therefore, prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog, 'This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Will you not know it on the day I make the people of Israel live in peace? ¹⁵ You will come from your place, from the remote parts of the north, with many peoples with you—all of you riding horses, a great assembly and a mighty army. ¹⁶ You will come up against my people Israel like a cloud to cover the land, in the last years. I will bring you against my land, so that the nations may know me when I am sanctified in your presence, Gog." ¹⁷ "This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Are you the one I spoke of long ago through my servants, the prophets of Israel, in days gone by, when I declared I would bring you against them in those years?" ¹⁸ "And it will be on the day when Gog comes against the land of Israel, this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: My anger will be stirred, and I will speak in my fury. ¹⁹ In my jealousy, I will speak in the fire of my wrath. It will be a great shaking on the land of Israel on that day. ²⁰ The fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the beasts of the field, and every creeping thing that moves on the ground, and every human who is on the surface of the earth, will quake at my presence. Mountains will be shattered, and cliffs will fall, and every wall will collapse. ²¹ I will call upon every mountain for a sword," this is what the Lord, the LORD, says, "and a sword will fall upon every human, brother against brother. ²² I will judge him with pestilence and blood, with torrents of rain, and with hailstones of fire and sulfur, raining them down upon him, his allies, and the many peoples with him. ²³ I will magnify myself, I will be sanctified, and I will make myself known in the sight of many nations. They will know that I am the LORD."

---

Notes  
- v. 5: "Cush and Put" follow the Hebrew reading; the Septuagint reads differently but the Hebrew is clear.  
- v. 6: "house of Togarmah" reflects the translation of בֵּית תּוֹגַרְמָה, though the exact meaning of תּוֹגַרְמָה is uncertain.  
- v. 12: "center of the earth" translates אַדְמַת עֶרֶץ, a poetic or geographical reference.  
- v. 16: "center of the earth" is retained as a rendering of עַל־טַבּוּר הָאָֽרֶץ, though "navel of the earth" is also possible.  
- v. 17: "prophets of Israel" is a translation of נְבִיאֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, following the context of divine communication.
