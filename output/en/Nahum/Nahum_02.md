# Nahum 2

¹ Look, messengers are coming with news of peace, running across the hills. O Judah, celebrate your festival. O you who trust in me, fulfill your vows. For never again will he march through you. He will bring your land to an end utterly. ² He has gone up, scattering your foes. He has surrounded your face. He watches the road, tightening the waistband, making strength very great. ³ For the Lord has restored the glory of Jacob as the glory of Israel, for though they were scattered, they were driven away, and their fruit was destroyed. ⁴ The shields of its warriors are bloodstained. Its brave men are drenched in red. The chariots rush in the day of their preparation, and the horsemen reel with terror. ⁵ The horsemen in the streets are confused. In the broad avenues they stumble. Their appearance is like torches, like lightning flashes, dashing to and fro. ⁶ Its nobles are remembered, but they falter in their going. In the panic they rush to its walls and prepare their defenses. ⁷ The gates of the rivers are opened, and the palace is broken down. ⁸ The queen is brought out, her head uncovered. Her servants lead her, weeping like doves, mourning in their hearts. ⁹ And Nineveh, like a pool of water, whose waters are flowing — they flee, but there is no one to guard them. ¹⁰ Silver is scattered, gold is taken; there is no end to the plunder. All the treasures of desirable things are gone. ¹¹ Brokenness and trembling, a crash and a shattering of hearts, and a collapse of knees, and pain in every loins. The faces of them all are like a potsherd that is burned. ¹² Where is the den of lions, and the feeding place for leopards? Where has the lion gone, to lie in ambush in the den of leopards, with no one to terrify? ¹³ The lion has devoured the prey of the leopards and strangled for the cubs of the lion. He has filled his dens with prey, and his lairs with what is torn. ¹⁴ Behold, I am coming against you, says the Lord of Hosts. I will burn your chariots in smoke. Your lions will be devoured by the sword. I will remove from the land your prey, and no more will the labor of your hands be heard.

---

Notes
- v. 9: "like a pool of water" is a poetic metaphor for Nineveh's vulnerability, despite its apparent strength.
- v. 12: "den of lions" and "feeding place for leopards" are metaphorical references to the Assyrian empire's predatory nature and its strongholds.
