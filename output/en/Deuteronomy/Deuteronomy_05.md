# Deuteronomy 5

¹ Moses called all Israel and said to them, "Listen, Israel, to the statutes and judgments I am speaking to you today. Learn them and keep them to do them. ² The LORD your God made a covenant with us at Horeb. ³ Not with our ancestors did the LORD make this covenant, but with us, all of us who are here today, living. ⁴ The LORD spoke to you face to face on the mountain out of the fire, the cloud, and the thick darkness, with a great voice. He did not add anything, and He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me. ⁵ I stood between the LORD and you at that time, to declare to you the word of the LORD, for you were afraid of the fire and did not go up the mountain, saying: ⁶ "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery. ⁷ You must not have other gods besides Me. ⁸ You must not make for yourself an idol or any image of what is in the heavens above, or what is in the earth beneath, or what is in the waters under the earth. ⁹ You must not bow to them or serve them, for I am the LORD your God, a jealous God, who brings the guilt of parents upon their children, upon the third and fourth generations of those who hate Me, ¹⁰ but who shows steadfast love to a thousand generations for those who love Me and keep My commandments. ¹¹ You must not take the name of the LORD your God in vain, for the LORD will not hold guiltless anyone who takes His name in vain. ¹² Keep the Sabbath day to sanctify it, as the LORD your God commanded you. ¹³ Work six days and do all your work, ¹⁴ but the seventh day is the Sabbath of the LORD your God. You must not do any work—you, your son, your daughter, your servant, your maidservant, your ox, your donkey, and any beast you have, or the outsider who is among you, so that your servant and your maidservant may rest as you do. ¹⁵ Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and the LORD your God brought you out from there with a strong hand and an outstretched arm. Therefore, the LORD your God commanded you to keep the Sabbath day and to sanctify it. ¹⁶ Honor your father and your mother, as the LORD your God commanded you, so that you may live long and it may go well for you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you. ¹⁷ You must not murder. ¹⁸ You must not commit adultery. ¹⁹ You must not steal. ²⁰ You must not give false testimony against your neighbor. ²¹ You must not covet your neighbor's wife. You must not desire your neighbor's house, or his field, or his servant, or his maidservant, or his ox, or his donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor." ²² These words the LORD spoke to the whole assembly of you on the mountain out of the fire, the cloud, and the thick darkness, with a great voice. He did not add anything. He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me. ²³ When you heard the voice out of the darkness, and the mountain was burning with fire, all the leaders of your tribes and your elders approached me. ²⁴ And you said, "Indeed, the LORD our God has shown His glory, and we have heard His voice from the fire today. Truly, it is possible for a god to speak to a human and the human to live!" ²⁵ "But now, why should we die? This great fire will consume us. If we hear the voice of the LORD our God any longer, we will die. ²⁶ For who is there among all flesh who has heard the voice of a living God speaking out of fire, as we have, and still survived? ²⁷ Approach, and you may hear everything the LORD our God will say to you. Then you may speak to us everything the LORD our God will speak to you, and we will hear and do it." ²⁸ The LORD heard your words when you spoke to me. He said to me, "What they have spoken is good. Let Me speak to you all that I have commanded them to do, and they will obey and do it." ²⁹ "Oh, that they had such a heart within them to fear Me and keep all My commandments always, so that it might go well for them and their children forever!" ³⁰ Go, tell them to return to their tents. ³¹ But you, remain here with me, and I will speak to you all the commandments, statutes, and judgments that you must teach them. They must do them in the land that I am giving to them as an inheritance. ³² You must keep to do everything the LORD your God commands you. Do not turn right or left. ³³ Walk in every way that the LORD your God commands you, so that you may live and it may go well for you, and you may live long in the land that you will inherit."

---

Notes
- v. 10: "a thousand generations" reflects the Hebrew "לַֽאֲלָפִים" (literally "to a thousand") used with "generations" in context.
- v. 22: "He did not add anything" translates "וַֽלֹּא־יָסָף", which reflects the divine speech being complete and not needing elaboration.
- v. 24: "Truly, it is possible for a god to speak to a human..." captures the wonder and affirmation in the Israelites' words about divine communication.
- v. 33: "Walk in every way..." reflects the Hebrew "בְּכָל־הַדֶּרֶךְ" with "walk" as a natural English metaphor for following a path.
