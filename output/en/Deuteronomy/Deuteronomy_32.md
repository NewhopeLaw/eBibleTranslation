# Deuteronomy 32

¹ Give ear, O heavens, and I will speak; and let the earth hear the words of my mouth. ² Let my teaching fall like rain and my words descend like dew, like gentle rain on new grass and showers on a field. ³ For I will call on the name of the LORD; give glory to our God. ⁴ The Rock—his works are perfect, and all his ways are just. A faithful God, without deceit, righteous and upright is he. ⁵ They have acted corruptly toward him, not their children, but a crooked and perverse generation. ⁶ Should you repay the LORD with evil, O foolish and unwise people? Is he not your Father, who bought you, who formed you and made you? ⁷ Remember the days of old, reflect on the generations past. Ask your father and he will tell you, your elders and they will say to you. ⁸ When the Most High apportioned the nations, when he divided humankind, he set the boundaries of the peoples according to the number of the sons of Israel. ⁹ For the LORD’s portion is his people, Jacob his allotted inheritance. ¹⁰ He found him in a desert land, in an empty, howling wasteland. He encircled him, he instructed him, he guarded him as the apple of his eye. ¹¹ Like an eagle that stirs up its nest, that hovers over its young, spreading out its wings, taking them up, bearing them on its back. ¹² The LORD alone guided him, no foreign god was with him. ¹³ He made him ride on the high places of the earth; he fed him the produce of the fields, and from the rock he gave him honey to eat and oil from flinty stone. ¹⁴ Curds of cows and milk of sheep, with fat of lambs and rams, and with the choicest of wheaten flour, and with blood of the grape he drank wine. ¹⁵ But Jeshurun grew fat and kicked; you grew fat, you grew thick, you became a glutton. Then he forsook the God who made him and despised the Rock of his salvation. ¹⁶ They stirred him to jealousy with foreign gods, with abominations they provoked him. ¹⁷ They sacrificed to demons, not to God, to gods they had not known, new gods, lately acquired, which your ancestors did not fear. ¹⁸ You abandoned the Rock who begot you, and you forgot the God who gave you birth. ¹⁹ And the LORD saw and was displeased; he grew angry with his sons and daughters. ²⁰ He said, "I will hide my face from them, I will see what their end will be, for this generation is perverse, children in whom there is no faith." ²¹ They made me jealous with what is not a god, they provoked me with their vanities. And I will make them jealous with a people who are not a people, I will provoke them with a foolish nation. ²² For a fire has flared up in my anger, and it will burn to the depths of Sheol, it will devour the earth and its harvest, and set ablaze the foundations of the mountains. ²³ I will heap misfortunes on them, my arrows will consume them. ²⁴ They will be consumed with hunger, with burning heat, with bitter disease, and the teeth of beasts I will send among them, along with the venom of crawling things in the dust. ²⁵ Outside the sword will strike, and within terror; both the young man and the maiden, the nursing infant and the old man. ²⁶ I said, "I would have wiped them out, I would have made their memory cease among men." ²⁷ But I feared the taunts of the enemy, lest the adversary say, "Our hand is too high, it is not the LORD who did all this." ²⁸ For they are a nation without understanding, there is no insight in them. ²⁹ If only they had been wise, they would have understood this, they would have discerned their future. ³⁰ How could one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock had sold them, and the LORD had given them up? ³¹ For their rock is not like our Rock, nor are their enemies like ours. ³² For their vine comes from the vine of Sodom, from the fields of Gomorrah; their grapes are bitter grapes, their clusters harsh. ³³ Their wine is the poison of serpents, the cruel venom of vipers. ³⁴ Is this not stored up with me, sealed among my treasures? ³⁵ Vengeance is mine, and retribution, for the time when their foot will falter. For their day of doom is near, and their judgment will come swiftly. ³⁶ For the LORD will judge his people and have compassion on his servants when he sees that their power is gone and there is none remaining, bound or free. ³⁷ He will say, "Where are their gods, the ones they trusted in?" ³⁸ "The gods who ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their offerings—let them arise and help you. Let them be your protection." ³⁹ See now that I am he, and there is no god besides me. It is I who kill and give life, I who wound and heal, and no one can deliver from my hand. ⁴⁰ For I raise my hand to heaven and say: "I live forever." ⁴¹ If I sharpen my sword and my hand takes hold of judgment, I will repay my enemies and pay back those who hate me. ⁴² I will make my arrows drunk with blood, my sword will devour flesh—blood of the slain and captives, from the heads of the enemy's leaders." ⁴³ Rejoice, you nations, with his people, for he will avenge the blood of his servants, and repay his enemies and cleanse the land of his people. ⁴⁴ Then Moses came and recited all the words of this song in the hearing of the people, he and Joshua son of Nun. ⁴⁵ When Moses had finished speaking all these words to all Israel, ⁴⁶ he said to them, "Take to heart all these words that I am commanding you today, which you shall teach your children, to observe and do all the words of this Teaching. ⁴⁷ For this is not an empty thing for you, but it is your life; by this Teaching you will lengthen your days in the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess." ⁴⁸ Then the LORD spoke to Moses on that very day, saying, ⁴⁹ "Go up to this mountain of Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, facing Jericho, and view the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the people of Israel for possession. ⁵⁰ You shall die on the mountain to which you are going up, and you shall be gathered to your people, just as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people. ⁵¹ Because you broke faith with me in the midst of the people of Israel at the waters of Meribah Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, and because you did not consecrate me in the midst of the people of Israel. ⁵² You will look toward the land from a distance, but you shall not go into the land I am giving to the people of Israel."

---

Notes
- v. 1: "heavens" and "earth" are personified as witnesses in poetic tradition.
- v. 18: "Rock who begot you" is a metaphor for God as the creator and sustainer.
- v. 34-35: The "stored up" and "sealed" phrases refer to the LORD's foreknowledge of judgment.
- v. 51: "because you broke faith with me" refers to the incident at Meribah Kadesh, where Moses struck the rock instead of speaking to it as God had commanded.
