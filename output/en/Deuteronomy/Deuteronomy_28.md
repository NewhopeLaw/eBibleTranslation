# Deuteronomy 28

¹ If you obey the voice of the LORD your God, to keep and do all his commandments that I command you today, the LORD your God will set you high above all nations on earth. ² All these blessings will come upon you and overtake you, if you obey the voice of the LORD your God. ³ You will be blessed in the city and blessed in the field. ⁴ You will be blessed in the fruit of your womb, in the fruit of your soil, in the fruit of your livestock, in the increase of your oxen and the flocks of your sheep. ⁵ You will be blessed in your grain stores and in your wine vats. ⁶ You will be blessed when you come in and blessed when you go out. ⁷ The LORD will send your enemies who rise against you into disarray before you. One by one they will flee from you, and you will flee from them in seven directions. ⁸ The LORD will send his blessing with you on all your works, in everything you do, in the land the LORD your God gives you. ⁹ The LORD will establish you as a holy people, as he promised to your ancestors, if you obey the voice of the LORD your God and walk in his ways. ¹⁰ All the nations of the world will see that the name of the LORD is called over you, and they will fear you. ¹¹ The LORD will bless you abundantly in the fruit of your womb, in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your soil, on the land the LORD swore to your ancestors to give you. ¹² The LORD will open his good storehouse, the heavens, to give rain to your land in its season and to bless all your work. You will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow. ¹³ You will be the head and not the tail, and you will be above, not beneath, if you obey the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you today, and you keep them. ¹⁴ You must not deviate from any of the commands I give you today, neither to the right nor to the left, to follow other gods and serve them. ¹⁵ But if you do not obey the voice of the LORD your God, to keep and do all his commandments and decrees that I command you today, all these curses will come upon you and overtake you. ¹⁶ You will be cursed in the city, and cursed in the field. ¹⁷ Your grain stores and your wine vats will be cursed. ¹⁸ The fruit of your womb and the fruit of your soil, the increase of your oxen and the flocks of your sheep, will be cursed. ¹⁹ You will be cursed when you come in and cursed when you go out. ²⁰ The LORD will send plague, fever, and inflammation to you in everything you do, until you are destroyed and quickly perished for the evil you have done, because you have forsaken me. ²¹ The LORD will fasten the plague upon you until he consumes you from the land you are entering to possess. ²² The LORD will strike you with confusion, fever, and chills, with burning wind and blight and mildew. You will be pursued until you are destroyed. ²³ The sky above you will be bronze, and the ground beneath you will be iron. ²⁴ The LORD will give rain for your land as dust, and from the sky it will fall on you until it erases you, until you are quickly destroyed. ²⁵ The LORD will deliver you to your enemies. You will go out against them one by one, and you will flee from them in seven directions. You will be a scattering among all the nations of the earth. ²⁶ Your dead will be food for the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth, and no one will scare them away. ²⁷ The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with festering sores, and with scabies, which you will not be able to cure. ²⁸ The LORD will strike you with blindness and confusion of mind. ²⁹ You will grope at noon like the blind in darkness, and you will not succeed in your ways. You will be only oppressed and plundered every day, and no one will help you. ³⁰ You will take a wife, but another man will sleep with her. You will build a house, but you will not live in it. You will plant a vineyard, but you will not drink its wine. ³¹ Your ox will be butchered before your eyes, but you will not eat from it. Your donkey will be stolen from you, and it will not be returned to you. Your sheep will be given to your enemies, and no one will rescue you. ³² Your eyes will look on, full of horror, and you will have no strength to rescue them. ³³ The yield of your land and all your toil will be devoured by a nation you do not know. You will be only oppressed and crushed every day. ³⁴ You will be driven mad by the sights your eyes see. ³⁵ The LORD will strike you with a severe disease in your knees and shins, which you will not be able to cure, from the soles of your feet to the top of your head. ³⁶ The LORD will lead you and your king away to a nation you have never known, neither you nor your ancestors. There you will serve other gods, wood and stone. ³⁷ And you will be an object of wonder and a byword and a proverb among all the nations to which the LORD will lead you. ³⁸ You will sow much seed in the field but reap little, because the locust will devour it. ³⁹ You will plant vines and care for them, but you will not drink their wine, for the worm will consume them. ⁴⁰ Olive trees will be in your land, but you will not anoint yourself with oil, for your olive trees will drop their fruit. ⁴¹ You will have sons and daughters, but they will not remain with you, for they will be taken into captivity. ⁴² All your trees and the produce of your land will be devoured by locusts. ⁴³ The foreigner who is among you will rise above you, but you will fall beneath. ⁴⁴ He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail. ⁴⁵ All these curses will come upon you and pursue you and overtake you until you are destroyed and quickly perished, because you did not obey the voice of the LORD your God to keep his commandments and decrees that he commanded you. ⁴⁶ These things will be a sign and a wonder in you and your descendants forever. ⁴⁷ Because you did not serve the LORD your God with joy and a good heart, because of the abundance of all things. ⁴⁸ You will serve your enemies whom the LORD will send against you in hunger and thirst, in nakedness and lack of everything. He will place an iron yoke on your neck until he destroys you. ⁴⁹ The LORD will bring a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, like an eagle's swoop— a nation whose language you do not understand. ⁵⁰ A fierce and insolent people who do not respect the old or show mercy to the young. ⁵¹ They will consume the fruit of your livestock and the produce of your land, until they leave you no grain, wine, or oil, no increase of your oxen or flocks of your sheep, until they destroy you. ⁵² They will press you in all your gates until they destroy the high and fortified walls on which you relied in all your land. They will press you in every city in all the land the LORD gave you. ⁵³ You will eat the fruit of your womb, the flesh of your sons and daughters, whom the LORD your God gave you, in your distress and anguish that your enemy will inflict on you. ⁵⁴ The gentle among you will be so cruel toward a brother, a wife in his arms, and the remaining children of his who are left, ⁵⁵ that he will give a piece of his children's flesh to eat, because he has nothing left in his distress and anguish that your enemy will inflict on you in every city. ⁵⁶ The delicate and tender woman, who has not set foot on the ground because of her delicacy, will be cruel in her eyes toward her husband in her arms, her son and daughter. ⁵⁷ She will eat the afterbirth that comes from between her thighs and the child she gives birth to, for lack of food in your distress and anguish that your enemy will inflict on you in your cities. ⁵⁸ If you do not obey and keep all the words of this law written in this book, to fear this glorious and awesome name, the LORD your God. ⁵⁹ The LORD will bring extraordinary plagues on you and your descendants, great and persistent plagues and severe diseases. ⁶⁰ He will send back all the pain of Egypt that you feared, and it will stick to you. ⁶¹ Every affliction and every plague not written in this book of the law, the LORD will bring upon you until he destroys you. ⁶² You will be left in small number, where you were as many as the stars of the sky because you did not obey the voice of the LORD your God. ⁶³ Just as the LORD was glad to do good to you and multiply you, so the LORD will be glad to destroy you and cause you to be quickly removed from the land you are entering to possess. ⁶⁴ The LORD will scatter you among all nations, from one end of the earth to the other. There you will serve other gods, wood and stone, which you and your ancestors have never known. ⁶⁵ And in those nations, you will have no rest, no place of rest for your foot. The LORD will give you a restless heart, eyes that fail, and a soul that melts away. ⁶⁶ Your life will hang before your eyes, and you will fear night and day, and you will not trust your life. ⁶⁷ In the morning you will say, "Would that it were evening!" and in the evening you will say, "Would that it were morning!" because of the fear in your heart and the sights your eyes see. ⁶⁸ The LORD will return you to Egypt in ships, along the road I said you would never see again. You will be sold there to your enemies as slaves and maids, and no one will buy you. ⁶⁹ These are the words of the covenant the LORD commanded Moses to make with the people of Israel in the land of Moab, in addition to the covenant he made with them at Horeb.
