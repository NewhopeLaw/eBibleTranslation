# Deuteronomy 19

¹ When the LORD your God cuts down the nations that the LORD your God gives you their land, and you take it and live in their cities and houses, ² you must set apart for yourself three cities in your land that the LORD your God gives you as your inheritance. ³ You will prepare the roads and divide the boundary of your land that the LORD your God will give you, so that it may serve as a place of refuge for anyone who kills someone. ⁴ This is the case of the killer who may flee there and live: someone who strikes a neighbor unintentionally, and who did not hate him from yesterday or the day before. ⁵ Like one who goes into the forest with his neighbor to cut down trees, and his hand slips with the ax while he is cutting the tree, the ax falls from the tree, and it strikes his neighbor, who dies. That person may flee to one of these cities and live. ⁶ So that the avenger of blood will not pursue the killer, because his heart is hot, and he catches up with him, especially if the distance is long, and strikes him down. Yet there is no judgment of death for this one, because he did not hate him from yesterday or the day before. ⁷ Therefore I command you today, saying: Set apart for yourself three cities. ⁸ If the LORD your God expands your boundary, as He swore to your ancestors, and gives you all the land that He promised to give to your ancestors, ⁹ because you keep all these commandments to do them, to love the LORD your God and to walk in His ways always, you may add three more cities to these three, ¹⁰ so that innocent blood is not shed in your land that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance, and there will be no guilt of blood on you. ¹¹ If there is a person who hates his neighbor, and lies in wait for him, and rises up against him and strikes him down, so that he dies, and the killer flees to one of these cities, ¹² the elders of his city must send and take him from there, and deliver him into the hands of the avenger of blood, and he will die. ¹³ Do not show pity toward him. You must remove the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, and it will be good for you. ¹⁴ You must not move the boundary markers set by your ancestors in the inheritance you receive in the land that the LORD your God gives you as your possession. ¹⁵ A single witness must not testify against a person for any offense or crime. On the testimony of two or three witnesses a matter must be established. ¹⁶ If a false witness arises against a person to accuse him, ¹⁷ then the two men who are in dispute must stand before the LORD, before the priests and the judges who will be in those days, ¹⁸ and the judges will investigate thoroughly. If it turns out the witness is a false witness, who has given false testimony against his brother, ¹⁹ you must do to him just as he plotted to do to his brother. You must remove the evil from among you. ²⁰ And the rest will hear and fear, and no longer do such evil in your midst. ²¹ You must not show pity in a case of life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "cuts down the nations" is a rendering of "יַכְרִית" (karat) in the context of divine judgment and conquest.
- v. 4: "from yesterday or the day before" translates "מִתְּמֹל שִׁלְשֹׁם" (mitmol shilshom), a Hebrew phrase meaning "from yesterday and the day before."
- v. 5: "the ax falls from the tree" is an interpretive rendering of "נָשַׁל הַבַּרְזֶל מִן־הָעֵץ" (nashal habarzel min ha'etz), where "nashal" means "fell off" and "barzel" is "iron" or "metal tool."
- v. 14: "move the boundary markers" renders "תַּסִּיג גְּבוּל" (tasig gevul), a reference to altering land boundaries.
- v. 21: "show pity in a case of life" is a clarification of the Hebrew "לֹא תָחוֹס עֵינֶךָ נֶפֶשׁ בְּנֶפֶשׁ" (lo tachos eincha nefesh b'nephesh), which is a formulaic expression for not sparing in the case of capital punishment.
