# Deuteronomy 27

¹ Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, "Keep all the commandment that I command you today. ² When you cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD your God gives you, you must set up large stones and plaster them with lime. ³ You must write on the stones all the words of this teaching, so that when you cross the Jordan, you may enter the land that the LORD your God gives you, a land flowing with milk and honey, as the LORD, the God of your ancestors, promised you. ⁴ When you cross the Jordan, you must set up these stones on Mount Ebal, as I command you today, and you must plaster them with lime. ⁵ You must build an altar to the LORD your God there, an altar of stones, and you must not use iron on the stones. ⁶ You must build the altar with uncut stones, and you must offer on it burnt offerings to the LORD your God. ⁷ You must sacrifice peace offerings there and eat, and you must rejoice before the LORD your God. ⁸ You must write on the stones all the words of this teaching clearly and fully. ⁹ Then Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, "Be still and listen, Israel. Today you have become the people of the LORD your God. ¹⁰ You must listen to the voice of the LORD your God and do all His commandments and judgments that I command you today. ¹¹ Moses commanded the people that same day, saying: ¹² These tribes must stand to bless the people on Mount Gerizim when you cross the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin. ¹³ These tribes must stand to pronounce the curse on Mount Ebal: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali. ¹⁴ The Levites must respond, and say to all the people of Israel in a loud voice: ¹⁵ "Cursed is the person who makes a carved image or a molten thing, an abomination to the LORD, the work of a craftsman's hands, and sets it in secret." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ¹⁶ "Cursed is the person who dishonors their father or their mother." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ¹⁷ "Cursed is the person who removes their neighbor's boundary marker." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ¹⁸ "Cursed is the person who leads the blind off the road." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ¹⁹ "Cursed is the person who twists the judgment of a foreigner, an orphan, or a widow." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ²⁰ "Cursed is the person who lies with their father's wife, for they have uncovered their father's garment." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ²¹ "Cursed is the person who lies with any beast." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ²² "Cursed is the person who lies with their sister, the daughter of their father or the daughter of their mother." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ²³ "Cursed is the person who lies with their daughter-in-law." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ²⁴ "Cursed is the person who strikes down their neighbor in secret." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ²⁵ "Cursed is the person who takes a bribe to kill an innocent life." And all the people must say, "Amen!" ²⁶ "Cursed is the person who does not uphold the words of this teaching to do them." And all the people must say, "Amen!"

Notes
- v. 11: The phrase וַיְצַו מֹשֶׁה אֶת־הָעָם בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא לֵאמֹֽר ("Moses commanded the people that same day, saying") is a continuation of the previous narrative and is included to maintain narrative flow.
- v. 26: The final curse is a general statement about not upholding the teaching, which is rendered as "does not uphold the words of this teaching to do them" to reflect the intent of the original.
