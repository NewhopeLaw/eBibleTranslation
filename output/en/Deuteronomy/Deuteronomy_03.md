# Deuteronomy 3

¹ We turned and went up the road of Bashan, and Og, king of Bashan, came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei. ¹⁰ All the cities in the lowland, all of Gilead, and all of Bashan as far as Salcah and Edrei, the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan. ¹¹ For only Og, the king of Bashan, was left from the remnant of the Rephaim. Look, his bed is an iron bed. It is still in Rabbah of the children of Ammon. It is nine cubits long and four cubits wide, by the measurement of a man. ¹² And this land we took at that time from Ar, which is on the edge of the stream of Arnon, and half the mountain of Gilead, and its cities I gave to the Reubenites and the Gadites. ¹³ The rest of Gilead and all of Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to half the tribe of Manasseh. All the region of Argob, which is all of Bashan, will be called the land of the Rephaim. ¹⁴ Jair son of Manasseh took all the region of Argob, as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and he named them after himself. The region of Bashan is called the towns of Jair to this day. ¹⁵ And to Machir I gave Gilead. ¹⁶ To the Reubenites and the Gadites I gave part of Gilead, as far as the stream of Arnon, and the boundary, and as far as the stream of Jabbok, which is the boundary of the children of Ammon. ¹⁷ The Arabah and the Jordan and the boundary from Kinnereth as far as the Dead Sea, the salt sea, under the slopes of Pisgah toward the east. ¹⁸ I instructed you at that time, saying, “The LORD your God has given you this land to possess. You will go as pioneers ahead of your brothers, the people of Israel, every warrior.” ¹⁹ But your women, your children, and your livestock, I know are a great burden for you. You may remain in the cities I give you. ² The LORD said to me, “Do not fear him, for I have given him and all his people and his land into your hand. You will treat him as you did Sihon, the king of the Amorites who lived in Heshbon.” ²⁰ Until the LORD gives rest to your brothers, just as He has given it to you, and they too will possess the land the LORD your God is giving them beyond the Jordan. Then you will return to your inheritance, which I have given you. ²¹ And I instructed Joshua at that time, saying, “Your eyes have seen everything the LORD your God has done to these two kings. The LORD your God will do the same to all the kingdoms you are crossing into.” ²² Do not fear them, for the LORD your God is the one who fights for you. ²³ I pleaded with the LORD at that time, saying: ²⁴ “Lord, you have begun to show your servant your greatness and your mighty hand. Who is a god in heaven or on earth who can do such works and such mighty acts as yours?” ²⁵ “Let me, I pray, cross over and see the good land beyond the Jordan, this good mountain and the Lebanon.” ²⁶ But the LORD was angry with me on your account and did not listen to me. And the LORD said to me, “Enough for you! Do not speak to Me about this matter any more.” ²⁷ “Go up to the top of the Pisgah and lift your eyes westward, northward, southward, and eastward, and see with your eyes, for you will not cross over this Jordan.” ²⁸ “And command Joshua and encourage him, for he will go before this people, and he will give them the land you will see.” ²⁹ We camped in the valley opposite Beth Peor. ³ The LORD our God gave Og, king of Bashan, and all his people into our hands, and we struck them until no one was left alive, not even a survivor. ⁴ We captured all his cities at that time, not a single city was there that we did not take from them. We took sixty cities, the whole region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan. ⁵ All these cities had high walls, gates, and bars—except for the cities of the Perizzites, which were numerous and greatly fortified. ⁶ We completely destroyed them, just as we had done to Sihon, the king of Heshbon. We destroyed every city, every man, woman, and child. ⁷ And we took all the livestock and the spoil of the cities for ourselves. ⁸ At that time, we took the land from the hands of two kings of the Amorites who lived beyond the Jordan, from the stream of Arnon to Mount Hermon. ⁹ The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir.

---

Notes
- v. 24: “I pray” is added for clarity, as the Hebrew phrase וַאֲתַחַנַּן (va’etchanan) implies a plea.
- v. 27: “top of the Pisgah” is a more natural rendering of רֹאשׁ הַפִּסְגָּה (rosh ha-pisgah) than “the top of Pisgah.”
- v. 29: “the valley opposite Beth Peor” is a natural rendering of בַּגָּיְא מוּל בֵּית פְּעֽוֹר (bagayyeh muwl beit pe'or).
