# Deuteronomy 12

¹ These are the decrees and judgments that you must observe to do in the land that the LORD, the God of your ancestors, gives you to possess, for all the days that you live on the earth. ² Destroy completely every place where the nations you are about to inherit worshiped their gods, on the high mountains and on the hills and under every leafy tree. ³ Break down their altars, smash their pillars, burn their sacred poles with fire, and destroy the images of their gods, erasing their names from that place. ⁴ Do not act this way toward the LORD your God. ⁵ Rather, seek the place that the LORD your God will choose from among all your tribes to place His name there for His dwelling. Go there, ⁶ and there you must bring your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contributions of your hand, and your vows and your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your cattle and your flocks. ⁷ And there you must eat in the presence of the LORD your God and rejoice in everything you achieve, you and your household, which the LORD your God has blessed you with. ⁸ Do not do anything like what we are doing here today, each person doing what seems right in their own eyes. ⁹ For you have not yet reached the place of rest or the inheritance that the LORD your God is giving you. ¹⁰ When you cross the Jordan and settle in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess, and He drives out all your enemies around you, and you dwell safely, ¹¹ the place that the LORD your God chooses to put His name there, you must bring there everything I command you: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contributions of your hand, and every choice offering you vow to the LORD. ¹² And there you must rejoice in the presence of the LORD your God—you, your sons, your daughters, your male and female servants, and the Levite who is in your gates, for he has no portion or inheritance among you. ¹³ Be careful that you do not offer your burnt offerings in any place you see, ¹⁴ but only in the place that the LORD will choose from one of your tribes—there you must offer your burnt offerings and there you must do everything I command you. ¹⁵ But in any of your towns, you may slaughter and eat meat as the LORD your God has blessed you. The unclean and the clean may eat it, like the gazelle and the deer. ¹⁶ Only do not eat the blood; pour it out on the ground like water. ¹⁷ You must not eat your tithes of grain, new wine, and oil, or the firstborn of your cattle and sheep, or any of your vows or freewill offerings or contributions of your hand in your towns. ¹⁸ Rather, you must eat them in the presence of the LORD your God at the place the LORD your God chooses—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, and the Levite who is in your gates. And you must rejoice in the presence of the LORD your God in everything you achieve by your hand. ¹⁹ Be careful that you do not neglect the Levite as long as you live on your land. ²⁰ When the LORD your God enlarges your borders, as He promised you, and you say, "I want to eat meat, because I long to eat meat," you may eat meat in your towns as much as you desire. ²¹ If the place the LORD your God chooses to put His name is too far from you, you may slaughter from your cattle and your flock that the LORD has given you, as I have commanded you, and eat in your towns as much as you desire. ²² Like the gazelle and the deer, you may eat it—both the unclean and the clean may eat it. ²³ Only be sure not to eat the blood, for the blood is the life, and you must not eat the life with the meat. ²⁴ Do not eat the blood; pour it out on the ground like water, ²⁵ so that it may go well for you and for your children after you, when you do what is right in the eyes of the LORD. ²⁶ But your holy things that you have and your vows, you must bring and present at the place the LORD chooses. ²⁷ And you must do your burnt offerings—the meat and the blood—on the altar of the LORD your God, and the blood of your sacrifices must be poured out on the altar of the LORD your God, and you may eat the meat. ²⁸ Keep and listen to all these words that I command you, so that it may go well for you and for your children after you forever, when you do what is good and right in the eyes of the LORD your God. ²⁹ When the LORD your God cuts down the nations you are going to inherit from before you, and you take possession of them and dwell in their land, ³⁰ be careful not to follow them after they are destroyed from before you and not to seek their gods, saying, "How did these nations worship their gods? I will do the same." ³¹ You must not do this to the LORD your God, for they did abominations to their gods, things the LORD hates, even burning their sons and daughters in fire to their gods.

Notes
- v. 1: "for all the days that you live on the earth" — the Hebrew uses "the days that you live on the earth" in a temporal clause, which is rendered in a natural way.
- v. 17: "your tithes of grain, new wine, and oil" — the Hebrew uses "tithes" (ma'aserot) and lists items to clarify what is being referred to.
- v. 25: "when you do what is right in the eyes of the LORD" — the Hebrew uses "the good and the right," which is translated as "what is right" to maintain clarity.
- v. 30: "abominations to their gods" — the Hebrew uses "abominations" (to'evot), which is rendered in context as "abominations" to preserve its strong connotation.
