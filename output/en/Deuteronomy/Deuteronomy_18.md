# Deuteronomy 18

¹ The Levitical priests, the whole tribe of Levi, must have no inheritance or possession among the Israelites. Their inheritance is the offerings of the LORD. They will eat them. ² They must have no inheritance among their brothers, for the LORD is their inheritance, as He has said to them. ³ This is the law of the priests from the people, from those who offer sacrifices—whether ox or sheep. The one offering it must give to the priest the shoulder, the cheeks, and the stomach. ⁴ The first of your grain, your wine, and your oil, and the first of the fleece of your sheep, you must give to him. ⁵ For the LORD has chosen him from among all your tribes to stand and serve in the name of the LORD. He and his sons will serve in the name of the LORD forever. ⁶ When a Levite comes from one of your towns from all Israel, where he resides, and comes in any desire of his heart to the place the LORD chooses, ⁷ he must serve in the name of the LORD his God just as his Levitical brothers who stand there before the LORD. ⁸ They will eat their share, the same as their brothers, from what they receive from their ancestral portions. ⁹ When you come into the land the LORD your God gives you, do not learn to imitate the abhorrent practices of those nations. ¹⁰ No one among you must be found causing their son or daughter to pass through fire, or be a diviner, an augur, an interpreter of omens, or a sorcerer. ¹¹ Nor must they be a charmer, a diviner by reading signs, a medium, or a necromancer. ¹² For the LORD detests all who do these things. Because of these abhorrent practices, the LORD your God will destroy them from before you. ¹³ Be blameless before the LORD your God. ¹⁴ For these nations you are inheriting listened to enchanters and diviners. But you, the LORD your God has not given you this. ¹⁵ A prophet from among you, from your brothers, like me, the LORD your God will raise up. You must listen to him. ¹⁶ Just as you asked the LORD your God at Horeb, on the day of the assembly, saying, "Let us not hear the voice of the LORD our God any longer, nor see this great fire again, or else we will die," ¹⁷ the LORD said to me, "What they have said is good." ¹⁸ I will raise up for them a prophet from among their brothers, like you, and I will put My words in his mouth. He will speak to them everything I command him. ¹⁹ And the man who does not listen to the words that the prophet will speak in My name, I will hold him accountable. ²⁰ But the prophet who presumes to speak a word in My name that I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must be put to death. ²¹ If you say in your heart, "How can we know what word the LORD has not spoken?" ²² When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD, and the word does not come to pass and is not fulfilled, that word is not one that the LORD has spoken. The prophet has spoken it presumptuously. Do not be afraid of him.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "Be blameless" is a translation of "תָּמִים תִּֽהְיֶה" (tāmîm tihyeh), which is often rendered as "perfect" or "complete." The context of avoiding abhorrent practices suggests "blameless" as the best fit.
- v. 22: The phrase "לֹא תָגוּר מִמֶּֽנּוּ" (lo tagur mimenu) is translated as "Do not be afraid of him." The word "tagur" can mean "to be afraid" or "to honor," but the context of rejecting a false prophet supports "afraid."
