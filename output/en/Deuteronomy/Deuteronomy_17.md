# Deuteronomy 17

¹ Do not sacrifice to the LORD your God an ox or a sheep that has any defect or flaw whatever, for it is abhorrent to the LORD your God. ² Suppose you find in any of your towns that the LORD your God gives you a man or woman who has done evil in the sight of the LORD your God by breaking His covenant. ³ He has gone and served other gods, bowing to them and to the sun or to the moon or to any part of the heavenly host that I have not commanded. ⁴ And it is reported to you and you hear it, you must thoroughly investigate the matter. If the report is found to be true and the abhorrent act has indeed been done in Israel, ⁵ you must take the man or woman who did this evil act out of your town to your gate and stone them to death. ⁶ A person must be put to death on the testimony of two or three witnesses; they must not be put to death on the testimony of only one witness. ⁷ The hands of the witnesses must be the first to execute the death, and the hand of the whole people must be the last. Thus you will purge the evil from among you. ⁸ If a case of dispute arises between you—be it over bloodshed, judgment, or injury, and a matter of contention arises in your gates—then you must arise and go to the place the LORD your God chooses. ⁹ You must go to the Levitical priests and to the judge who is in office at that time. You must inquire and they will inform you of the judgment. ¹⁰ You must do according to the word they declare to you from the place the LORD chooses, and you must carefully do all that they instruct you. ¹¹ According to the teaching they instruct you and according to the judgment they declare to you, you must do; you must not deviate from what they tell you, neither to the right nor to the left. ¹² If a man acts defiantly and does not obey the priest who serves there for the LORD your God or the judge, that man must die. You must purge the evil from Israel. ¹³ All the people must hear and fear, and they must no longer act defiantly. ¹⁴ When you come into the land the LORD your God gives you and possess it and live there, and say, "I will set a king over me like all the nations around me," ¹⁵ you must appoint a king over you whom the LORD your God chooses from among your brothers. You must not appoint a foreigner who is not your brother as king over you. ¹⁶ Only he must not multiply horses for himself or turn the people back to Egypt to multiply horses, for the LORD has said to you, "You must not return that way again." ¹⁷ He must not multiply wives for himself, nor must his heart turn away. Nor must he multiply silver and gold for himself in abundance. ¹⁸ When he is seated on his royal throne, he must write for himself a copy of this teaching in a book, from before the Levitical priests. ¹⁹ It must be with him, and he must read in it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear the LORD his God and to observe all the words of this teaching and these statutes to do them. ²⁰ So that his heart may not become proud over his brothers, and so that he may not turn aside from the command to the right or to the left, so that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he and his children in Israel.

Notes
- v. 3: "any part of the heavenly host" is a translation of "כָּל־צְבָא הַשָּׁמַיִם" (the whole host of heaven) to capture the comprehensive nature of the idolatry.
- v. 16: "return that way again" translates "לֹא תֹסִפוּן לָשׁוּב בַּדֶּרֶךְ הַזֶּה עֽוֹד" and reflects the prohibition against Egypt, a symbol of bondage.
- v. 18: "a copy of this teaching" is a translation of "מִשְׁנֵה הַתּוֹרָה" (a copy of the teaching), with "mishneh" rendered as "copy" to avoid implying a secondary or supplementary status.
