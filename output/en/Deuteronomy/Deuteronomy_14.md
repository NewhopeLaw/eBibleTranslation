# Deuteronomy 14

¹ You are children of the LORD your God. Do not cut yourselves or shave the hair on your foreheads for the dead. ² For you are a people holy to the LORD your God, and the LORD your God has chosen you to be His treasured possession out of all the peoples on the face of the earth. ³ You must not eat anything that is abhorrent. ⁴ These are the animals you may eat: cattle, sheep, and goats. ⁵ The gazelle, the springbok, the antelope, the wild goat, the mountain goat, and the ibex. ⁶ Any animal that has a completely split hoof and chews the cud may be eaten. ⁷ But these you must not eat: the camel, the rock badger, and the hare, for they chew the cud but do not have split hooves. They are unclean for you. ⁸ And the pig, because it has a split hoof but does not chew the cud. You must not eat it; its carcass must not be touched. ⁹ These you may eat from the water: anything that has fins and scales may be eaten. ¹⁰ But anything that does not have fins and scales must not be eaten. It is unclean for you. ¹¹ All clean birds you may eat. ¹² But these you must not eat: the eagle, the vulture, and the osprey, ¹³ the kite, the raven, and the like of them, ¹⁴ the ostrich, the gull, and the sea gull, ¹⁵ the heron, the stork, the hoopoe, ¹⁶ the pelican, the griffon vulture, and the like of them, the cormorant and the nightjar, ¹⁷ the goshawk, the raptor, and the like of them, the bat and the owl. ¹⁸ All other winged creatures that creep are unclean for you. You must not eat them. ¹⁹ All other birds that are clean you may eat. ²⁰ You must not eat anything that has died naturally. Give it to the foreigner in your town so they may eat it, or sell it to a foreigner, for you are a people holy to the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk. ²¹ You must tithe all the yield of your sowing that comes from the field year by year. ²² And you must eat in the presence of the LORD your God at the place the LORD your God will choose to establish His name there—your tithes of grain, new wine, and oil, and the firstborn of your herds and flocks—so that you may learn to fear the LORD your God always. ²³ If the distance is too great for you to carry your tithes, because the place the LORD your God will choose is too far away, and because the LORD your God may bless you, ²⁴ then you may convert your tithe into money, take the money in your hand, and go to the place the LORD your God will choose. ²⁵ Then you may spend the money on anything you desire—cattle, sheep, wine, strong drink, or anything your heart desires. You and your household may eat there in the presence of the LORD your God and rejoice. ²⁶ Do not neglect the Levite living among you, for he has no portion or inheritance among you. ²⁷ At the end of every three years, bring out all your tithes in that year and leave them in your towns. ²⁸ The Levite, who has no portion or inheritance among you, and the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow living among you may come and eat and be satisfied, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands you do.
