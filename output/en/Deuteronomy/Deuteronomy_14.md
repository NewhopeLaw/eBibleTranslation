# Deuteronomy 14

¹ You are children of the LORD your God. Do not cut yourselves or shave the hair between your eyes for the dead. ² For you are a holy people to the LORD your God. The LORD your God has chosen you to be His treasured possession out of all the peoples on the face of the earth. ³ You must not eat anything detestable. ⁴ This is the cattle you may eat: oxen, sheep, and goats, ⁵ deer, gazelle, and wild goat, mountain goat and roe deer, and antelope. ⁶ Any animal that splits the hoof and has cloven hooves, and that chews the cud, you may eat. ⁷ But these you may not eat from those that chew the cud and from those that split the hoof and have cloven hooves: the camel, the rock badger, and the hare, for they chew the cud but do not split the hoof. They are unclean for you. ⁸ And the pig, for it splits the hoof and has cloven hooves but does not chew the cud. You must not eat its flesh; you must not touch its carcass. It is unclean for you. ⁹ These you may eat from all that are in the water: anything that has fins and scales you may eat. ¹⁰ But anything that does not have fins and scales you may not eat. It is unclean for you. ¹¹ All clean birds you may eat. ¹² But these you may not eat from them: the eagle, the vulture, and the osprey, ¹³ the kite, the raven, and the like, ¹⁴ the ostrich, the nightjar, and the gull, ¹⁵ the stork, the heron, and the hoopoe, ¹⁶ the pelican, the kite, and the like, the cormorant and the bat, ¹⁷ the owl, the night owl, and the like, the carrion crow and the nighthawk. ¹⁸ All winged creatures that creep are unclean for you. You must not eat them. ¹⁹ All clean birds you may eat. ²⁰ You must not eat anything that has died naturally. Give it to the foreigner who is in your cities, so they may eat it, or sell it to a foreigner. For you are a holy people to the LORD your God. You must not boil a young goat in its mother’s milk. ²¹ You must tithe all the yield of your seed that comes from the field, year by year. ²² You must eat before the LORD your God, in the place that the LORD your God will choose to put His name there, the tithe of your grain, your new wine, and your oil, the firstborn of your cattle and your sheep. In this way you will learn to fear the LORD your God always. ²³ And if the distance is too great for you, and you cannot carry it, because the place where the LORD your God will choose to put His name is too far from you when He has blessed you, ²⁴ you must exchange it for money, take the money in your hand, and go to the place that the LORD your God will choose. ²⁵ You must give the money for whatever your heart desires: for oxen, for sheep, for wine, for strong drink, for whatever your heart asks. You must eat there before the LORD your God and rejoice, you and your household. ²⁶ You must not neglect the Levite who is in your towns, for he has no portion or inheritance among you. ²⁷ At the end of every three years, bring out all the tithe of your produce in that year and leave it in your towns. ²⁸ The Levite, who has no portion or inheritance among you, and the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow who are in your towns, will come and eat and be satisfied, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands that you do.
