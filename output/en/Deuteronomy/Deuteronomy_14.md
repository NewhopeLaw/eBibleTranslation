# Deuteronomy 14

¹ You are the children of the LORD your God. Do not cut yourselves or shave the hair above your foreheads for the dead. ² For you are a people set apart to the LORD your God. You are the one the LORD has chosen to be his treasured possession out of all the peoples on the face of the earth. ³ Do not eat anything unclean. ⁴ These are the animals you may eat: oxen, sheep, and goats. ⁵ Deer, gazelle, and wild goat; mountain goat, roe deer, and chamois. ⁶ Any animal that chews the cud and has a split hoof entirely split in two, you may eat. ⁷ But among those that chew the cud or that have a split hoof, you must not eat these: the camel, the rock badger, and the hare—for they chew the cud, but their hooves are not split. They are unclean to you. ⁸ The pig is also unclean, for though it splits its hoof and does not chew the cud, it is unclean to you. You must not eat their meat or touch their carcasses. ⁹ These you may eat from the water: anything that has fins and scales. ¹⁰ Any creature in the water without fins and scales you must not eat. It is unclean to you. ¹¹ You may eat every clean bird. ¹² But you must not eat these among them: the eagle, the vulture, and the osprey. ¹³ The kite, the raven, and the like. ¹⁴ Any kind of owl. ¹⁵ The hoopoe, the pelican, and the stork. ¹⁶ The cormorant, the stork, and the like. ¹⁷ The hawk and the bat and the like. ¹⁸ The bat, the owl, and the like. ¹⁹ Every crawling bird is unclean to you. You must not eat them. ²⁰ You may eat every clean bird. ²¹ You must not eat anything that dies of itself. Give it to the resident alien in your towns, and let them eat it or sell it to a foreigner. For you are a people set apart to the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk. ²² You must tithe the entire harvest of your sowing, year after year, from the field. ²³ You must eat it before the LORD your God at the place the LORD your God chooses to put his name there: the tithe of your grain, your wine, and your oil, and the firstborn of your cattle and sheep. This you must do so that you may learn to fear the LORD your God always. ²⁴ But if the distance is too great for you and you are unable to carry your tithe because the place where the LORD your God chooses to put his name is too far away, ²⁵ then you may exchange it for money. Take the money in your hand and go to the place the LORD your God chooses. ²⁶ Then you may spend the money on anything your heart desires: cattle, sheep, wine, strong drink, or anything you wish. Eat it there, before the LORD your God, and rejoice, you and your household. ²⁷ Do not neglect the Levite in your towns, for he has no inheritance or possession among you. ²⁸ At the end of every three years, you must bring out the tithe of your produce from that year and leave it in your towns. ²⁹ Then the Levite, for whom you have no inheritance or possession, and the resident alien, the orphan, and the widow who live in your towns, may come and eat and be satisfied. This you must do so that the LORD your God may bless you in every work of your hands that you do.

---

Notes  
- v. 21: "naveilah" is translated as "anything that dies of itself" rather than "carrion," since "naveilah" can apply to both wild and domestic animals.  
- v. 21: The phrase "do not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk" is rendered as a direct command rather than as a metaphor.  
- v. 25: "tzartah ha-kesef" is translated as "exchange it for money" based on the context of exchanging a tithe for currency.
