# Deuteronomy 34

¹ Moses ascended from Arboth Moab to Mount Nebo, to the summit of Pisgah, facing Jericho. The LORD showed him the entire land — from Gilead to Dan, ² all the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah as far as the western sea, ³ the Negev and the valley as far as Jericho, the city of palm trees, to Zoar. ⁴ The LORD said to him, "This is the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'I will give it to your descendants.' I have shown it to your eyes, but you shall not cross over there." ⁵ Moses, the servant of the LORD, died in the land of Moab, by the mouth of the LORD. ⁶ He was buried in the valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth Peor, but no one knows the place of his burial to this day. ⁷ Moses was 120 years old when he died. His eyes were not dim, nor had his vigor decreased. ⁸ The Israelites wept for Moses in Moab for thirty days, and the days of weeping and mourning for Moses ended. ⁹ Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands upon him. The Israelites listened to him and did as the LORD had commanded Moses. ¹⁰ No prophet has since risen in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face, ¹¹ for all the signs and wonders he did in the land of Egypt against Pharaoh, his servants, and all his land, ¹² and for the mighty hand and the great fear he showed before all the people of Israel.

---

Notes
- v. 4: The phrase "לֵאמֹר לְזַרְעֲךָ אֶתְּנֶנָּה" is translated as "I will give it to your descendants" to maintain clarity and consistency with the narrative voice.
- v. 10: "כְּמֹשֶׁה" is rendered "like Moses" to clarify the comparison.
- v. 11: The phrase "לְפַרְעֹה וּלְכָל־עֲבָדָיו וּלְכָל־אַרְצֽוֹ" is translated to reflect the full scope of the subject: Pharaoh, his servants, and all his land.
