# Deuteronomy 16

¹ Keep the month of Abib and observe the Passover to the LORD your God, for in the month of Abib the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt at night. ² You will sacrifice the Passover to the LORD your God, from sheep and cattle, in the place the LORD your God chooses to cause His name to dwell there. ³ You must not eat anything leavened with it. For seven days you must eat unleavened bread with it, the bread of hardship, for you went out from the land of Egypt in haste, so that you might remember the day of your departure from the land of Egypt all the days of your life. ⁴ No leaven must be seen among you in all your territory for seven days, and none of the meat you slaughter at dusk on the first day must remain until morning. ⁵ You must not sacrifice the Passover at any of your gates that the LORD your God gives you. ⁶ Rather, only at the place the LORD your God chooses to cause His name to dwell there must you sacrifice the Passover at dusk, when the sun goes down—at the time you left Egypt. ⁷ You will cook and eat in the place the LORD your God chooses. In the morning you will turn and go to your tents. ⁸ For six days you will eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day you will observe a solemn rest to the LORD your God. You must not do any work on that day. ⁹ You will count seven weeks for yourself, beginning from the time you start to cut the grain. You will begin counting the weeks from the day after the Sabbath. ¹⁰ Then you will observe the Festival of Weeks to the LORD your God, giving as much as your hand is able, according to the blessing the LORD your God gives you. ¹¹ You will rejoice before the LORD your God—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite in your towns, the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow among you—at the place the LORD your God chooses to cause His name to dwell there. ¹² You must remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and you must keep and do these statutes. ¹³ You will observe the Festival of Booths for seven days after you have gathered in from your threshing floor and your winepress. ¹⁴ You will rejoice in your festival—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite, the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow among you. ¹⁵ For seven days you will observe a festival to the LORD your God in the place the LORD chooses, for the LORD your God will bless you in all your produce and in all your labor, and you will be only joyful. ¹⁶ Three times in a year all your males must appear before the LORD your God in the place He chooses—on the Festival of Unleavened Bread, on the Festival of Weeks, and on the Festival of Booths. No one must appear empty-handed before the LORD. ¹⁷ Each one must give according to the ability of his hand, according to the blessing the LORD your God has given you. ¹⁸ You must appoint judges and officials for yourself in all your towns that the LORD your God gives you, according to your tribes, and they must judge the people with righteous judgment. ¹⁹ You must not show favoritism in judgment. You must not recognize faces. You must not take a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and perverts the words of the righteous. ²⁰ Justice, justice you must pursue, so that you may live and take possession of the land that the LORD your God gives you. ²¹ You must not plant any tree as an Asherah beside the altar of the LORD your God that you make for yourself. ²² You must not set up for yourself a standing stone, which the LORD your God hates.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "after you have gathered in from your threshing floor and your winepress" — the Hebrew reads "when you have gathered in from your barn and from your winepress," following the literal imagery of harvest.
