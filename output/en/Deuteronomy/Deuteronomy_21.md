# Deuteronomy 21

¹ If a dead body is found in the land that the LORD your God gives you to possess, lying in the field, and it is unknown who struck him, ² your elders and judges shall go out and measure the distance to the towns surrounding the dead man, ³ and the nearest town to the dead body shall take action. The elders of that town shall select a heifer, a young cow that has not been worked, and has not drawn a yoke, ⁴ and the elders of that town shall bring the heifer down to a valley that is not tilled, nor used for planting. There, in the valley, they shall break the heifer’s neck. ⁵ Then the Levitical priests, for they have been chosen by the LORD your God to serve Him and to bless in the name of the LORD, shall come forward. All disputes and cases shall be decided according to their word. ⁶ All the elders of that nearest town shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley, ⁷ and they shall declare, “Our hands have not shed this blood, and our eyes have not seen it. ⁸ Pardon, O Lord, Your people Israel, whom You have redeemed, and do not allow innocent blood to remain among Your people Israel, so that it may be forgiven them.” ⁹ But you must remove the innocent blood from among you, so that you may do what is right in the eyes of the LORD. ¹⁰ If you go out to war against your enemies and the LORD your God hands them over to you, and you take captives, ¹¹ and you see among the captives a woman of striking beauty, and you desire her, and you take her for yourself as a wife, ¹² then you must bring her into your house. She must剃 her head and trim her nails. ¹³ She must remove the clothes of a captive from herself and remain in your house for a full month. During that time, she must mourn her father and mother. Only after that may you go to her and take her as your wife. ¹⁴ But if you no longer desire her, you must send her away free, and you must not sell her for money, nor must you treat her cruelly, for you have humiliated her. ¹⁵ If a man has two wives, the one loved and the other unloved, and both give him children, and the firstborn son is from the unloved wife, ¹⁶ then, when the time comes to give him his inheritance, the father must not give the firstborn’s double portion to the son of the loved wife over the son of the unloved wife, who is the firstborn. ¹⁷ Rather, the son of the unloved wife, who is the firstborn, must be recognized as such and given a double portion from all that is found in his father’s possession, for he is the beginning of his strength. The right of the firstborn belongs to him. ¹⁸ If a man has a son who is rebellious and defiant, not listening to his father or his mother, and even after discipline still does not obey them, ¹⁹ then his father and mother must seize him and bring him out to the elders of his town and to the gate of his place, ²⁰ and they must say to the elders of the town, “This son of ours is rebellious and defiant; he does not listen to us. He is a glutton and a drunkard.” ²¹ Then all the men of the town must stone him to death. You must remove the evil from among you, and all Israel will hear and fear. ²² If a crime deserves the death penalty and someone is put to death, you must hang their body on a tree. ²³ You must not leave the body hanging on the tree overnight. You must bury it that same day, for a person who is hanged is cursed by God, and you must not let the cursed thing remain overnight, lest it defile the land the LORD your God gives you as your inheritance.

---

Notes
- v. 23: “a person who is hanged is cursed by God” — the Hebrew uses “קִלְלַת אֱלֹהִים” literally “curse of God.” The rendering “cursed by God” reflects the nuance of divine judgment.
