# Deuteronomy 8

¹ Keep all the commandments I am commanding you today, so that you may live, multiply, and enter to possess the land the LORD swore to your ancestors. ² Remember the entire journey that the LORD your God has led you through in the wilderness these forty years, to test you by affliction and to know what was in your heart—whether you would keep His commandments or not. ³ He afflicted you, He let you hunger, and He fed you with manna, which neither you nor your ancestors had known, so that He might teach you that it is not by bread alone that a person lives, but by every word that comes from the mouth of the LORD. ⁴ Your clothing did not wear out from you, and your foot did not blister these forty years. ⁵ Know in your heart that as a father disciplines his son, so the LORD your God disciplines you. ⁶ Keep the commandments of the LORD your God and walk in His ways, and fear Him. ⁷ For the LORD your God is bringing you into a good land, a land of streams of water, of springs and deep waters emerging in valleys and hills. ⁸ A land of wheat and barley, of vines and fig trees and pomegranates, a land of olive oil and honey. ⁹ A land in which you will not lack for food, and in which you will lack for nothing. It is a land whose stones are iron and from whose mountains you can mine copper. ¹⁰ You will eat, be satisfied, and bless the LORD your God for the good land He has given you. ¹¹ Be careful for yourselves, that you do not forget the LORD your God, failing to keep His commandments, His judgments, and His statutes, which I am commanding you today. ¹² When you have eaten and are satisfied, and have built good houses and lived in them, ¹³ and when your cattle and your flocks have multiplied, and your silver and gold have increased, and all that you have has multiplied, ¹⁴ your heart may become proud and you may forget the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery. ¹⁵ Who led you through the great and terrible wilderness, where there were vipers and scorpions and thirst, where you had no water, who brought water for you from the flinty rock, ¹⁶ who fed you with manna in the wilderness, which your ancestors had never known, to afflict you and test you, in order to do good for you in the end. ¹⁷ And you may say in your heart, “My strength and the power of my hand have made me this great wealth.” ¹⁸ But you must remember the LORD your God, for it is He who gives you the strength to make wealth, in order to confirm the covenant He swore to your ancestors, as this day. ¹⁹ And if you forget the LORD your God and go after other gods and serve them and bow down to them, I solemnly declare to you today that you will surely perish. ²⁰ Just as the nations the LORD will destroy before you, so you will perish, if you do not obey the voice of the LORD your God.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "manna" is retained as a proper name for the miraculous food provided in the wilderness.
- v. 5: "disciplines" is used for יְיַסֵּר to reflect the context of divine instruction and correction.
- v. 9: "mine copper" is a direct rendering of the Hebrew for clarity.
- v. 14: "proud" is used for רָם to reflect the heart's attitude of self-reliance.
- v. 18: "confirmed the covenant" is used for לְהָקִים אֶת־בְּרִיתוֹ to reflect the covenantal context.
- v. 20: "perish" is used for תֹאבֵדוּן and תֹאבֵדוּ to reflect the final consequence of disobedience.
