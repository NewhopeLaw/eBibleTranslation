# Deuteronomy 2

¹ We turned and journeyed through the wilderness along the Red Sea route, as the LORD had spoken to me, and we encircled the mountain of Seir for many days. ² And the LORD said to me: ³ "Enough for you to circle this mountain. Turn yourselves northward. ⁴ Command the people, saying: You are passing through the territory of your brothers, the descendants of Esau, who dwell in Seir. They will fear you, so be very careful. ⁵ Do not provoke them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even the step of a foot, for I have given the mountain of Seir as an inheritance to Esau." ⁶ "Buy food from them with silver and eat, and buy water with silver and drink." ⁷ "For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. You have seen how the LORD your God has led you through this great and terrible wilderness for forty years. The LORD your God has been with you; you lacked nothing." ⁸ And we passed by our brothers, the descendants of Esau, who dwelled in Seir, from the Arabah, from Eilat and from Ezion-geber, and we turned and journeyed through the wilderness to the land of Moab. ⁹ And the LORD said to me: "Do not provoke Moab, and do not fight with them, for I will not give you any of their land for an inheritance, for I have given the land of Ar to the descendants of Lot as an inheritance." ¹⁰ "Previously, the Emim lived there, a great, numerous, and tall people like the Anakim. ¹¹ They are considered Rephaim, just as the Anakim. The Moabites call them Emim. ¹² Previously, the Horites lived in Seir, but the descendants of Esau drove them out and destroyed them, taking their place, just as Israel did in the land of their inheritance, which the LORD gave to them." ¹³ "Now rise up and cross the stream of Zered." And we crossed the stream of Zered. ¹⁴ The number of years we traveled from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the stream of Zared was thirty-eight years, until every last soldier from the generation who had gone out had died, as the LORD had sworn to them. ¹⁵ And the hand of the LORD was against them to destroy them from the camp until they were all gone. ¹⁶ And it came to pass when all the soldiers of that generation had died from among the people: ¹⁷ The LORD spoke to me, saying: ¹⁸ "You are to cross today the border of Moab, to Ar. ¹⁹ And you shall approach near to the sons of Ammon. Do not provoke them, and do not fight with them, for I will not give you any of the land of the children of Ammon as an inheritance, for I have given it as an inheritance to the descendants of Lot." ²⁰ "The land of the Rephaim will be considered. Previously, the Rephaim lived there, and the Ammonites call them Zamzumim. ²¹ A great, numerous, and tall people, like the Anakim. The LORD destroyed them before the Ammonites, who took possession and settled in their place, ²² just as He did for the descendants of Esau, who dwell in Seir. He destroyed the Horites before them and they took possession and settled in their place to this day. ²³ And the Avvim, who lived in the villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim who came out from Caphtor, the LORD destroyed them and they took possession and settled in their place. ²⁴ "Now rise up, cross the stream of Arnon. Behold, I have given it into your hand, Sihon the king of Heshbon the Amorite and his land. Begin to conquer, and provoke battle with him." ²⁵ "Today I will begin to put your fear and dread upon the faces of all the peoples under heaven, who will hear your name and tremble and be in anguish before you." ²⁶ And I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon the king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying: ²⁷ "I will pass through your land. I will go only by the road, I will not turn aside to the right or to the left. ²⁸ I will buy food with silver and eat, and I will buy water with silver and drink, only I will pass by on foot— ²⁹ just as the descendants of Esau who dwell in Seir and the Moabites who dwell in Ar did for me, until I pass over the Jordan into the land the LORD our God is giving to us." ³⁰ But Sihon the king of Heshbon would not let us pass through, for the LORD your God hardened his spirit and made his heart stubborn, to deliver him into your hand, as it is today. ³¹ And the LORD said to me: "See, I have begun to set Sihon and his land before you. Begin to take possession of his land." ³² And Sihon came out to meet us, he and all his people, to battle at Jahaz. ³³ And the LORD our God delivered him over to us, and we struck him and his sons and all his people. ³⁴ We captured all his cities at that time, and we destroyed every city, utterly, men, women, and children, not sparing a single survivor. ³⁵ Only the livestock we took for ourselves, and the spoil of the cities we captured. ³⁶ From Ar, which is on the edge of the stream of Arnon, and the city in the valley, and as far as the region of Gilead, not a single city was too strong for us. The LORD our God gave all of it into our hands. ³⁷ Only we did not approach near to the land of the children of Ammon, not even the border of the river Jabbok or the cities in the mountain, as the LORD our God commanded us.

Notes
- v. 10: "Rephaim" is rendered as a proper noun, with context indicating they are a people group.
- v. 11: "Rephaim" appears again, used in a comparative sense to describe the Emim.
- v. 15: "The hand of the LORD was against them to destroy them" reflects the Hebrew "yad YHWH haytah bahem lehumam," where "humam" is often rendered as "to destroy" or "to consume."
- v. 21: "To this day" is a translation of "ad hayom hazeh," reflecting the ongoing presence of the Ammonites in the land.
- v. 23: "Avvim" and "Caphtorim" are translated as proper nouns, with "Caphtor" likely referring to Crete or a similar region.
- v. 25: "Today I will begin to put your fear and dread" is a direct rendering of the Hebrew, emphasizing the imminent impact of Israel's reputation.
