# Deuteronomy 30

¹ When all these blessings and curses I have set before you come upon you, and you take them to heart among all the nations where the LORD your God has scattered you, ² you must return to the LORD your God and listen to his voice, all that I command you today, you and your children, with all your heart and with all your being. ³ And the LORD your God will restore your captivity, have compassion on you, and gather you again from all the peoples among whom the LORD your God has scattered you. ⁴ Even if you are scattered to the ends of the heavens, from there the LORD your God will gather you, and from there he will take you. ⁵ And the LORD your God will bring you into the land that your ancestors possessed and you will inherit it. He will do good to you, and multiply you more than your ancestors. ⁶ And the LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your descendants, to love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your being, that you may live. ⁷ And the LORD your God will remove all these curses from your enemies and from those who hate you, who have persecuted you. ⁸ You will return and listen to the voice of the LORD, and do all his commandments that I command you today. ⁹ And the LORD your God will bless you in every work of your hands, in the fruit of your womb, in the offspring of your livestock, and in the produce of your land, for the LORD your God will take delight in you for good, as he did in your ancestors. ¹⁰ For you are to obey the voice of the LORD your God, to keep his commandments and decrees written in this book of the law, if you return to the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your being. ¹¹ For this commandment that I command you today is not too difficult for you, nor is it distant. ¹² It is not in heaven, saying, Who will go up to heaven for us and take it, and let us hear it and do it? ¹³ Nor is it across the sea, saying, Who will cross to the other side of the sea for us and take it, and let us hear it and do it? ¹⁴ No, the word is very near to you, in your mouth and in your heart, to do it. ¹⁵ See, I have set before you today life and good, death and evil. ¹⁶ I command you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in his ways, to keep his commandments and decrees and judgments, so that you may live and multiply, and that the LORD your God may bless you in the land you are about to enter and possess. ¹⁷ But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are lured away to bow down to other gods and serve them, ¹⁸ I declare to you today that you will surely perish, and you will not live long in the land you cross the Jordan to enter and possess. ¹⁹ I call to heaven and earth as witnesses today that I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the curse. Choose life, that you and your descendants may live, ²⁰ by loving the LORD your God, listening to his voice, and holding fast to him, for he is your life and the length of your days, so that you may dwell in the land the LORD swore to your ancestors, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, to give them.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "to keep his commandments and decrees written in this book of the law" — the phrase "book of the law" is not in the Hebrew but is inferred from context to clarify the reference to the covenantal text.
- v. 19: "I call to heaven and earth as witnesses today" — the Hebrew uses "the heavens and the earth" as a common biblical witness motif.
- v. 20: "the length of your days" — the Hebrew uses "long life," which is rendered more naturally in English as "length of your days."
