# Deuteronomy 13

¹ Keep every command I am commanding you to do, not adding to it or subtracting from it. ² If a prophet or dreamer of dreams arises among you and gives you a sign or wonder, ³ and the sign or wonder comes about as they have spoken, saying, “Let us go after other gods you have not known, and serve them,” ⁴ you must not listen to the words of that prophet or that dreamer. For the LORD your God is testing you to know whether you truly love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your being. ⁵ Follow the LORD your God, revere Him, keep His commandments, obey His voice, serve Him, and hold fast to Him. ⁶ That prophet or dreamer must be put to death, for they have spoken treason against the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery, to turn you from the path the LORD your God commanded you to walk. Then you must purge the evil from among you. ⁷ If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or daughter, or the wife you cherish, or your close friend secretly entices you, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods you have not known—gods of the peoples around you, near or far, from one end of the earth to the other,” ⁸ do not yield to them or listen to them. Do not show pity or spare them or conceal them. ⁹ Instead, report them to your elders and to your judges, and you must execute them first, and then the whole people must join in and put them to death. ¹⁰ Then you must stone them to death, for they have sought to turn you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt from the house of slavery. ¹¹ And all Israel will hear and fear, and never again will they do such an evil thing among you. ¹² If you hear in one of your cities that the LORD your God is giving you to dwell in, saying, ¹³ “Outlaws have gone out from among you and have led the people of the city astray, saying, ‘Let us go and serve other gods you have not known,’” ¹⁴ and you investigate and find that the report is true, that such an abomination has indeed taken place among you, ¹⁵ you must strike down the inhabitants of that city with the sword. Devote it to destruction, and all who are in it and its livestock as well. ¹⁶ Gather all its plunder in the middle of the city square, and burn the city and all its plunder completely as a whole burnt offering to the LORD your God. It must become a perpetual ruin, never to be rebuilt. ¹⁷ Let no part of the devoted things cling to your hand, so that the LORD may turn from His anger and show you mercy, and have compassion on you and multiply you, as He swore to your ancestors. ¹⁸ Because you will have listened to the voice of the LORD your God, keeping all His commandments I command you today to do what is good in the eyes of the LORD your God.
