# Deuteronomy 1

¹ These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan in the wilderness, in the Arabah, near Suph, between Paran and Tophel, and Lavan, and Hazeroth, and Dizahab. ² It was eleven days from Horeb, along the road of Mount Seir, to Kadesh-barnea. ³ And it happened in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first of the month, that Moses spoke to the Israelites, all that the Lord had commanded him for them. ⁴ After he had struck Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og, king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei. ⁵ Beyond the Jordan in the land of Moab, Moses set out to explain this law, saying: ⁶ "The Lord our God spoke to us at Horeb, saying, 'You have had enough living in this mountain.' ⁷ Turn and go up for yourselves, and enter the mountain of the Amorites, and all their neighbors, in the Arabah, in the mountain, and in the lowland, and in the Negev, and by the seashore, the land of the Canaanites and the Lebanon, until the great river, the river Euphrates. ⁸ Behold, I have placed the land before you. Enter and possess the land that the Lord swore to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them and to their descendants after them. ⁹ And I said to you at that time, 'I cannot bear you alone. ¹⁰ The Lord your God has multiplied you, and behold, you are today as the stars of the heavens in number. ¹¹ The Lord, the God of your ancestors, will increase you a thousand times, and He will bless you as He has spoken to you. ¹² How can I bear your burden and your load, and your disputes alone? ¹³ Choose for yourselves men who are wise, understanding, and experienced in your tribes, and I will appoint them as leaders over you. ¹⁴ And you answered me and said, 'What you have spoken is good, to do it.' ¹⁵ And I took the leaders of your tribes, wise and experienced men, and I set them as leaders over you, as commanders of thousands, commanders of hundreds, commanders of fifties, commanders of tens, and officers in your tribes. ¹⁶ And I commanded your judges at that time, saying, 'Hear between your brothers, and judge righteously between a man and his brother, and between a stranger. ¹⁷ You must not show partiality in judgment. The small and the great, you must hear. You must not fear the face of a man, for the judgment is God's. And the matter that is difficult for you, bring it to me, and I will hear it.' ¹⁸ And I commanded you at that time all the things that you would do. ¹⁹ And we set out from Horeb and went through the great and terrible wilderness that you saw, the road of the mountain of the Amorites, as the Lord our God had commanded us, and we came to Kadesh-barnea. ²⁰ And I said, 'You have come to the mountain of the Amorites, which the Lord our God gives to you. ²¹ Behold, the Lord your God has placed the land before you. Go up, take possession, just as the Lord, the God of your ancestors, has spoken to you. Do not fear and do not be afraid.' ²² And all of you came near to me and said, 'Let us send men before us, and they will explore the land for us, and they will report to us the road by which we should go up, and the cities to which we should come.' ²³ And the matter pleased me, and I took from you twelve men, one man for each tribe. ²⁴ And they turned and went up the mountain, and came to the Valley of Eshcol, and they scouted it. ²⁵ And they took from their hand some of the fruit of the land and brought it to us, and they reported to us, saying, 'Good is the land that the Lord our God gives to us.' ²⁶ But you would not go up, and you rebelled against the mouth of the Lord your God. ²⁷ And you grumbled in your tents and said, 'Because the Lord hates us, He has brought us out from the land of Egypt to give us into the hand of the Amorite, to destroy us. ²⁸ Where are we going up? Our brothers have made our hearts faint, saying, A great and lofty people is there, cities great and fortified up to the heavens, and we saw there the descendants of Anak.' ²⁹ And I said to you, 'Do not be terrified and do not be afraid of them. ³⁰ The Lord your God, who goes before you, He will fight for you, just as He did for you in Egypt before your eyes. ³¹ And in the wilderness that you saw, how the Lord your God carried you, as a man carries his son, all the way that you walked, until you came to this place.' ³² And in this matter you did not believe in the Lord your God. ³³ The One going before you in the way to seek you a place to encamp, in fire by night to see the way you should go by, and in a cloud by day. ³⁴ And the Lord heard the sound of your words and was angry, and He swore, saying: ³⁵ 'If any of these men see the good land, which I swore to give to their ancestors. ³⁶ Except Caleb the son of Jephunneh, he will see it, and to him I will give the land that he walked upon, and to his children, for he has followed the Lord.' ³⁷ And against me the Lord was angry because of you, saying, 'Neither will you go in there.' ³⁸ Joshua the son of Nun, who stands before you, he will go in there. Encourage him, for he will give it to Israel. ³⁹ And your little ones, whom you said would be a burden, your children who do not know this day good or bad—they will go in there, and to them I will give it, and they will possess it. ⁴⁰ And you, turn for yourselves and go into the wilderness along the way of the Red Sea.' ⁴¹ And you answered me and said, 'We have sinned against the Lord our God. We will go up and fight, just as all that the Lord our God commanded us.' And you fastened each man his weapons of war, and you were ready to go up to the mountain. ⁴² And the Lord said to me, 'Say to them, You must not go up, and you must not fight, for I am not with you, and you will not be struck down before your enemies.' ⁴³ And I spoke to you, but you did not listen, and you rebelled against the mouth of the Lord, and you turned aside and went up to the mountain. ⁴⁴ And the Amorite, who lived in that mountain, came out against you, and he chased you as bees do, and he struck you from Seir until Hormah. ⁴⁵ And you returned and wept before the Lord, but the Lord did not listen to your voice, nor did He pay attention to you. ⁴⁶ And you stayed in Kadesh many days, as many days as you had stayed there.

Notes
- v. 1: "beyond the Jordan" — The phrase is understood as referring to the eastern side of the Jordan River.
- v. 3: "in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first of the month" — This reflects the Israelite calendar and is translated in line with the Hebrew reckoning.
- v. 37: "Neither will you go in there" — This is a paraphrase of the Hebrew, which uses a form of "you" that is singular but refers to Moses in the context.
- v. 44: "as bees do" — The Hebrew uses a metaphor that is best rendered in natural English.
- v. 45: "the Lord did not listen to your voice" — The Hebrew uses a poetic form of refusal, indicating no response or intervention.
