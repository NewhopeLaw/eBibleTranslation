# Deuteronomy 31

¹ Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel. ² He said to them, "I am a hundred and twenty years old today. I am no longer able to go out and come in. The LORD has said to me, 'You will not cross this Jordan.'" ³ "The LORD your God is the one going ahead of you. He will destroy these nations before you, and you will possess them. Joshua is the one going ahead of you, as the LORD has spoken." ⁴ "The LORD will do to them as He did to Sihon and Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land, which He destroyed." ⁵ "He will give them to you. And you will do to them all the commandments I have commanded you." ⁶ "Be strong and courageous. Do not fear or be dismayed, for the LORD your God is the one walking with you. He will not let you down, and He will not abandon you." ⁷ Moses called Joshua and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be strong and courageous, for you are the one who will bring this people into the land the LORD has sworn to their ancestors to give them. And you will give it to them as their inheritance." ⁸ "The LORD is the one going ahead of you. He will be with you. He will not let you down, and He will not abandon you. Do not fear and do not be dismayed." ⁹ Moses wrote this law and gave it to the Levitical priests who carry the ark of the covenant of the LORD, and to all the elders of Israel. ¹⁰ He instructed them, saying, "At the end of seven years, in the year of remission, at the festival of booths, ¹¹ when all Israel comes to see the face of the LORD your God at the place He chooses, you must read this law before all Israel, in their hearing. ¹² Assemble the people—men, women, and children, and the foreigners in your towns—to hear and to learn, so they may fear the LORD your God, and obey all the words of this law. ¹³ Their children, who do not yet know, will hear and learn to fear the LORD your God, for all the days you live on the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess." ¹⁴ The LORD said to Moses, "Your time to die is near. Call Joshua, and both of you stand at the Tent of Meeting. I will commission him." Moses and Joshua went and stood at the Tent of Meeting. ¹⁵ The LORD appeared in the Tent in a column of cloud. The column of cloud stood at the entrance of the Tent. ¹⁶ The LORD said to Moses, "You are about to lie down with your ancestors. This people will rise and prostitute themselves after foreign gods of the land they are entering, and they will abandon Me and break My covenant with them. ¹⁷ My anger will flare up against them on that day. I will abandon them and hide My face from them, and they will eat bitter things and be afflicted. On that day they will say, 'Is it not because there is no God near me that these bad things have found me?'" ¹⁸ "I will indeed hide My face from them on that day because of all the evil they have done, for they have turned to other gods." ¹⁹ "Now write this song for yourselves and teach it to the people of Israel. Let it be placed in their mouths, so that this song may be a witness to the people of Israel." ²⁰ "For I am bringing them into the good land I swore to their ancestors, a land flowing with milk and honey. They will eat and be satisfied, and they will grow fat and turn to other gods and serve them, despising Me and breaking My covenant." ²¹ "Then many evils and afflictions will come upon them. This song will respond to them as a witness, for it will not be forgotten from the mouths of their descendants, for I know what they are doing today, before I bring them into the good land I swore to them." ²² Moses wrote this song that day and taught it to the people of Israel. ²³ He commissioned Joshua son of Nun, saying, "Be strong and courageous, for you are the one who will bring the people of Israel into the land I have sworn to them, and I will be with you." ²⁴ When Moses had finished writing the words of this law into the book, until they were complete, ²⁵ he instructed the Levitical priests who carry the ark of the covenant of the LORD, saying, ²⁶ "Take this book of law and place it beside the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and it will be there as a witness against you." ²⁷ "For I know your rebellion and the hardness of your neck. Even now, while I am still alive with you, you have been rebellious toward the LORD. How much more will it be after my death!" ²⁸ "Assemble before me all the elders of your tribes and your officials, so I may speak these words in their hearing and charge them by heaven and earth." ²⁹ "For I know that after my death, you will surely corrupt yourselves, turning from the path I have commanded you. And the evils of the last days will come upon you, because you do evil in the sight of the LORD, provoking Him with the works of your hands." ³⁰ Moses spoke these words in the hearing of all the assembly of Israel, until they were complete.

Notes
- v. 16: "abandon Me and break My covenant" – the Hebrew uses "עֲזָבַנִי וְהֵפֵר אֶת־בְּרִיתִי" where "עֲזָבַנִי" is a rare form of "to abandon" and "הֵפֵר" is "break" (from the root "to break" or "to violate"). The translation reflects the forceful tone of the covenant violation.
- v. 21: "respond to them as a witness" – the Hebrew uses "עָנְתָה", a form of "to answer" or "to respond", suggesting a prophetic function of the song as a witness and a response in times of crisis.
- v. 27: "the hardness of your neck" – the Hebrew "עָרְפְּךָ הַקָּשֶׁה" uses "עַרְפָּה" (neck) and "קָשֶׁה" (hardness), a common metaphor for stubbornness or rebellion.
