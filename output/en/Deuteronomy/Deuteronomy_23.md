# Deuteronomy 23

¹ No man may take his father’s wife, nor may he uncover his father’s nakedness. ² No one who is emasculated or has had his male organ cut off may enter the assembly of the LORD. Even in the tenth generation, no one such as that may enter the assembly of the LORD. ³ No Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of the LORD, even in the tenth generation, no one such as that may enter the assembly of the LORD for all time. ⁴ For they did not meet you with food and water on the road as you came out of Egypt, and they hired Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram Naharaim to curse you. ⁵ But the LORD your God would not listen to Balaam, and the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing, for the LORD your God loves you. ⁶ You must not seek their peace or their prosperity for all your days, forever. ⁷ You must not hate an Edomite, for he is your brother. You must not hate an Egyptian, for you were a foreigner in his land. ⁸ The children who are born to them may enter the assembly of the LORD in the third generation. ⁹ When you go out to war against your enemies, you must guard yourself from every evil thing. ¹⁰ If there is among you a man who is not clean from the discharge of the night, he must go outside the camp. He must not enter within the camp. ¹¹ At evening he must wash in water, and at sunrise he may return to the camp. ¹² You must have a place outside the camp, and you must go out there to relieve yourself. ¹³ You must have a tool with you, a spade. When you relieve yourself outside, you must dig with it, and after covering your excrement, you must return it to its place. ¹⁴ For the LORD your God walks in the midst of your camp to rescue you and to hand your enemies over to you. Therefore, your camp must be holy, so that he does not see any indecency among you and turn away from you. ¹⁵ You must not deliver a slave to his master if the slave has fled to you from his master. ¹⁶ He must live among you, in the place he chooses within one of your gates. You must not oppress him. ¹⁷ There must be no prostitute taken from the daughters of Israel, nor must there be a male prostitute taken from the sons of Israel. ¹⁸ You must not bring a fee paid to a prostitute or the wages of a dog into the house of the LORD your God as any offering, for these are an abomination to the LORD your God. ¹⁹ You must not charge interest on a loan to your brother, whether interest on money, interest on food, or interest on any thing that you may lend. ²⁰ You may charge interest to a foreigner, but not to your brother, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands in the land to which you are going to take possession. ²¹ If you make a vow to the LORD your God, do not delay to pay it. For the LORD your God will demand it from you, and you will incur guilt. ²² If you refrain from making a vow, you will not incur guilt. ²³ What escapes from your lips you must be careful to keep, and you must do exactly what you vowed to the LORD your God, a freewill offering that you promised with your mouth. ²⁴ If you go into your neighbor’s vineyard, you may eat grapes to your heart’s content, but you must not put any in your basket. ²⁵ If you go into your neighbor’s standing grain, you may pluck the heads with your hands, but you must not use a sickle on your neighbor’s standing grain.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "emasculated" is a translation of פְּצֽוּעַ־דַּכָּא, a rare and somewhat unclear term, possibly referring to someone who has had a bodily wound or injury.
- v. 18: "fee paid to a prostitute" and "wages of a dog" are literal translations of מְחִיר כֶּלֶב and אֶתְנַן זוֹנָה, which are metaphorically associated with immorality and impurity in the context.
- v. 21: "delay to pay" reflects the nuance of the verb אְחַרְתָּ, which carries the sense of "postpone" or "linger."
- v. 22: "refrain from making a vow" is a translation of תֶּחְדַּל לִנְדֹּר, where the verb תֶּחְדַּל might also carry a nuance of "hesitate" or "abstain."
