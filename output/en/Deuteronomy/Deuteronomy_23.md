# Deuteronomy 23

¹ No man shall take his father’s wife, nor shall he lay bare his father’s nakedness. ² No one who is emasculated or has his male organs cut off may enter the assembly of the LORD. ³ No one who is a product of a union between a man and a female human may enter the assembly of the LORD, not even in the tenth generation. ⁴ No Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of the LORD, nor even in the tenth generation, forever. ⁵ For they did not meet you with food or water on the road when you came out of Egypt, and they hired Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram Naharaim to curse you. ⁶ But the LORD your God was not willing to listen to Balaam. Instead, the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing, for the LORD your God loves you. ⁷ You must not seek their peace or prosperity for all your days, forever. ⁸ You must not despise an Edomite, for he is your brother; you must not despise an Egyptian, for you were a foreigner in their land. ⁹ The children born to them may enter the assembly of the LORD in the third generation. ¹⁰ When you go out to war against your enemies, you must guard yourself from every evil thing. ¹¹ If there is among you a man who is not clean from the emission of the night, he must go outside the camp and must not enter into the camp. ¹² But it must be that at evening he washes in water, and when the sun sets, he may enter into the camp. ¹³ You must prepare a tool for yourself outside the camp. When you go out, you must use it there. ¹⁴ And a staff must be on your belt, and when you sit outside, you must dig with it and cover up your excrement. ¹⁵ For the LORD your God walks in the midst of your camp to rescue you and to give your enemies into your hands, so that your camp must be holy, and the LORD your God must not see anything indecent among you and turn away from you. ¹⁶ You must not hand over to their master a slave who has escaped to you from their master. ¹⁷ They must live among you, in any of your towns where it pleases them to live. You must not oppress them. ¹⁸ There must be no prostitute from the daughters of Israel, and there must be no male prostitute from the sons of Israel. ¹⁹ You must not bring the price of a prostitute or the wages of a dog into the house of the LORD your God as any offering, for both are an abomination to the LORD your God. ²⁰ You must not take interest from your brother—whether in silver, in food, or in any thing that you lend. ²¹ You may take interest from a foreigner, but not from your brother, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all your work on the land that you are going to possess. ²² If you vow a vow to the LORD your God, do not delay to pay it, for the LORD your God will demand it from you and it will be a sin for you. ²³ But if you refrain from making a vow, it will not be a sin for you. ²⁴ What you speak with your mouth you must guard and do as you vowed to the LORD your God, a voluntary offering that you have spoken with your mouth. ²⁵ When you enter your neighbor’s vineyard, you may eat grapes to your heart’s content, but you must not put any in your container. ²⁶ When you enter your neighbor’s standing grain, you may pluck the heads with your hand, but you must not use a sickle on your neighbor’s standing grain.

---

Notes
- v. 1: “Lay bare his father’s nakedness” is a standard idiom for incestuous relations, as in Leviticus 18:8.
- v. 2: The reference to "emasculated" or "cut off" likely refers to eunuchs or those who have had a genital injury or operation, as per Leviticus 21:20.
- v. 3: “Product of a union between a man and a female human” is a paraphrase for "mamzer," a term often used to denote someone born of an incestuous or mixed-union.
- v. 4-6: The reference to Balaam and his attempt to curse Israel is detailed in Numbers 22-24.
- v. 15-17: The treatment of escaped slaves reflects the broader context of Israelite law and compassion toward the vulnerable.
