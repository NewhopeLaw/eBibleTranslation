# Deuteronomy 11

¹ Love the LORD your God, and keep His charge, His statutes, His judgments, and His commandments all the days. ² Know today that it is not your children, who have not known and have not seen the discipline of the LORD your God, His greatness, His strong hand, and His outstretched arm. ³ His signs and His deeds that He did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and to all his land. ⁴ What He did to the army of Egypt, to their horses and chariots, when He caused the waters of the Red Sea to overwhelm them in front of them as they pursued you, and the LORD destroyed them until this day. ⁵ What He did for you in the wilderness until you came to this place. ⁶ What He did to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, the son of Reuben, whose earth opened its mouth and swallowed them, their households, their tents, and every person standing with them in the midst of all Israel. ⁷ For your eyes have seen all the great deeds the LORD did. ⁸ Keep all the commandments that I command you today, so that you may grow strong and go in and take possession of the land you are crossing into to claim it. ⁹ That you may live long in the land that the LORD swore to your ancestors to give to them and their descendants, a land flowing with milk and honey. ¹⁰ For the land you are going to possess is not like the land of Egypt, from which you came, where you would sow your seed and water it with your feet like a garden of vegetables. ¹¹ But the land you are going to cross into to possess is a land of hills and valleys, which drinks water from the rain of the heavens. ¹² A land that the LORD your God looks after always, the eyes of the LORD your God are on it from the beginning of the year to the end of the year. ¹³ And if you will listen and obey all the commandments that I command you today, to love the LORD your God, to serve Him with all your heart and with all your being, ¹⁴ then I will give you the rain for your land in its season, the early and the late rains, and you will gather your grain, your new wine, and your oil. ¹⁵ I will give grass in your fields for your livestock, and you will eat and be satisfied. ¹⁶ Watch yourselves, so that your heart does not become hardened and you turn away and serve other gods and bow to them. ¹⁷ Then the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and He will shut up the heavens so that there will be no rain, and the land will not yield its produce, and you will perish quickly from the good land the LORD gives you. ¹⁸ Place these words of mine in your heart and mind, and bind them as a sign on your hand and let them be a symbol between your eyes. ¹⁹ Teach them to your children, speaking of them when you sit at home, when you walk on the road, when you lie down, and when you rise up. ²⁰ Write them on the doorframes of your houses and on your gates. ²¹ That your days and the days of your children may be long on the land the LORD swore to your ancestors to give them, as long as the days of the heavens over the earth. ²² For if you carefully keep all these commandments that I command you to do, to love the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, and to cling to Him, ²³ the LORD will drive out all these nations before you, and you will inherit great and mighty nations. ²⁴ Every place your foot treads will be yours, from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the great river, the Euphrates, to the western sea. It will be your boundary. ²⁵ No one will stand against you. The fear and dread of you will rest on all the land you step on, as the LORD has spoken to you. ²⁶ See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse. ²⁷ The blessing, if you obey the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you today. ²⁸ The curse, if you do not obey the commandments of the LORD your God and turn away from the path I command you today to go after other gods you do not know. ²⁹ And it will be, when the LORD your God brings you into the land you are going to possess, that you will place the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal. ³⁰ Are they not across the Jordan, beyond the road toward the setting sun, in the land of the Canaanite, who dwells in the lowlands opposite Gilgal, near the terebinth of Moreh? ³¹ For you are about to cross the Jordan to go in and possess the land that the LORD your God gives you, and you will dwell in it. ³² Keep to do all the statutes and judgments that I set before you today.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Love the LORD your God" — "Love" renders אָהַבְתָּ in the imperative, reflecting the active call to love.
- v. 26-28: The blessing and curse structure is rendered straightforwardly, preserving the conditional nature. The Greek reference adds "eulogy" and "curse" but the Hebrew terms are retained in natural English.
- v. 29: "Mount Gerizim" and "Mount Ebal" — these are the most direct renderings of the place names, matching the traditional identification.
- v. 30: "the terebinth of Moreh" — "terebinth" is a standard translation of אַלּוֹנֵי, and "Moreh" is a transliteration of מֹרֶה, a well-known site.
