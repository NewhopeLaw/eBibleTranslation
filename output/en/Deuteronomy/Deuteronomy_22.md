# Deuteronomy 22

¹ If you see your brother’s ox or his sheep wandering off, do not ignore them. Return them to your brother. ² If your brother is not nearby and you do not know who he is, gather them to your house and keep them with you until your brother comes to claim them and you return them to him. ³ Do the same for his donkey, and do the same for any lost thing of your brother’s, whether it is a cloak or anything else that is lost. You must not ignore it. ⁴ If you see your brother’s donkey or his ox fallen on the road, do not ignore them. Help him raise them up. ⁵ A man must not wear a woman’s garment, nor may a woman wear a man’s garment, for such things are detestable to the LORD your God. ⁶ If you come across a bird’s nest along the road, in any tree or on the ground, with young birds or eggs and the mother sitting on the young or on the eggs, do not take the mother along with the young. ⁷ Let the mother go, and take the young for yourself, so that you may prosper and live long. ⁸ When you build a new house, you must construct a railing around your roof, so that you do not bring bloodshed in your house if someone falls from it. ⁹ Do not plant your vineyard with two kinds of seeds, for if you do, the produce of the vineyard and the seed you planted will be defiled. ¹⁰ Do not plow with an ox and a donkey together. ¹¹ Do not wear a mixture of wool and linen together. ¹² Make tassels for yourself on the four corners of your garment with which you cover yourself. ¹³ When a man takes a wife and lives with her, and then comes to hate her, ¹⁴ and he brings up false charges against her and accuses her of being a harlot, saying, “I took this woman and found no evidence of her being a virgin,” ¹⁵ then the father of the young woman and her mother must bring the evidence of her virginity to the elders at the gate of the town. ¹⁶ The father of the young woman will say to the elders, “I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, and he hated her.” ¹⁷ Now he has brought up false charges against her, saying, ‘I did not find the evidence of my son’s bride to be a virgin.’ Here is the evidence of my daughter’s virginity, and let them spread out the garment before the elders of the city. ¹⁸ Then the elders of that town must take the man and discipline him. ¹⁹ They must fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give it to the girl’s father, for he has brought a bad name upon a virgin of Israel. He must not be allowed to dismiss her for the rest of his life. ²⁰ But if the matter is true and the evidence shows that the girl was not a virgin, ²¹ then they must take the girl to the entrance of her father’s house and stone her to death, for she has done a disgraceful thing in Israel by committing fornication in her father’s house. You must remove the evil from among you. ²² If a man is found lying with a married woman, both the man who lay with the woman and the woman must be put to death, so that you may remove the evil from Israel. ²³ If a young woman is a virgin and is betrothed to a man, and another man finds her in the city and lies with her, ²⁴ then you must bring both of them to the gate of that city and stone them to death, the young woman because she did not cry out in the city, and the man because he has humiliated his neighbor’s wife. You must remove the evil from among you. ²⁵ But if the man finds the betrothed young woman in the field, and he seizes her and lies with her, the man alone must be put to death. ²⁶ The young woman has committed no crime that would result in death, for this is like the case of a man who attacks another to kill him. The matter is the same. ²⁷ For the young woman cried out in the field and there was no one to rescue her. ²⁸ If a man finds a virgin who is not betrothed, and he seizes her and lies with her, and is caught, ²⁹ then the man who lay with her must give fifty shekels of silver to the girl’s father, and he must marry her, because he has humiliated her. He must not be allowed to dismiss her for the rest of his life.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "detestable" (toeh) is used here for the prohibition of cross-dressing, consistent with the general glossary. The term is not here rendered as 'abomination' to avoid archaic connotations.
- v. 20: "the evidence shows" refers to the physical evidence of virginity, as described in v. 17. The Hebrew is clear on the requirement for physical proof.
- v. 29: "fifty shekels of silver" is a direct translation of the Hebrew "ḥāmiššîm kāsef", with "shekels" used as a measure of weight and value.
