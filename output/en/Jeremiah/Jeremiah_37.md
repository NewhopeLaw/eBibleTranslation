# Jeremiah 37

¹ Zedekiah son of Josiah became king in place of Coniah son of Jehoiakim, whom King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon had made to rule over the land of Judah. ² But he and his servants and the people of the land did not listen to the words of the LORD, which the prophet Jeremiah had spoken to them. ³ Then King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest to the prophet Jeremiah, saying, "Please pray for us before the LORD your God." ⁴ But Jeremiah came in and went out among the people, and they did not put him in the house of confinement. ⁵ When Pharaoh's army marched out of Egypt, the Chaldeans who were surrounding Jerusalem heard of it, and they withdrew from Jerusalem. ⁶ Then the word of the LORD came to the prophet Jeremiah, saying, ⁷ "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says to the king of Judah who sent you to inquire of me: 'Here is Pharaoh's army that has come out to help you, but it will return to its own land, Egypt. ⁸ The Chaldeans will return and fight against this city, and they will capture it and burn it with fire.' ⁹ This is what the LORD says, 'Do not deceive yourselves by thinking, "The Chaldeans will surely leave us," for they will not leave. ¹⁰ Even if you were to strike down all the Chaldean forces that are fighting you, and some were left behind, each in their place, they will rise up and burn this city with fire.' ¹¹ And it happened that when the Chaldean forces withdrew from Jerusalem because of Pharaoh's army, ¹² Jeremiah left Jerusalem to go to the land of Benjamin to buy food for himself, passing through the middle of the people. ¹³ Now he was at the gate of Benjamin, and there a man named Seraiah, son of Shelemiah, son of Hananiah, seized Jeremiah the prophet, saying, "You are fleeing toward the Chaldeans." ¹⁴ Jeremiah answered, "That is a lie—I am not fleeing toward the Chaldeans." But he did not listen to him, and Seraiah seized Jeremiah and brought him before the officials. ¹⁵ The officials became angry with Jeremiah and struck him, then sent him to the house of Jonathan the scribe, because they had turned it into a place of confinement. ¹⁶ For Jeremiah had come to the house of the cistern and the chamber of the guard, and there he sat many days. ¹⁷ Then King Zedekiah sent for him, brought him into his house secretly, and asked him, "Is there any word from the LORD?" And Jeremiah said, "Yes, but it is in the hands of the king of Babylon." ¹⁸ Jeremiah then said to King Zedekiah, "What wrong have I done to you or to your servants or to this people, that you put me in a prison house?" ¹⁹ "Where are your prophets who have prophesied to you, 'The king of Babylon will not come against you or this land'?" ²⁰ Now then, my lord the king, please let my plea for mercy come before you. Do not send me back to the house of Jonathan the scribe, or I will die there." ²¹ Then the king gave orders, and they placed Jeremiah in the courtyard of the guard, giving him a daily portion of bread from outside the gate until the bread from the city ran out. So Jeremiah remained in the courtyard of the guard.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "Coniah" is the form used in the Hebrew text, reflecting the name given in the context of Jeremiah's prophecy.  
- v. 13: "Seraiah" is the Septuagint reading; the Hebrew text reads "Yiraiyyahu" (Jiraiyyahu), which is translated here as "Jiraiyyahu."  
- v. 18: "What wrong have I done to you..." — the Hebrew uses a rhetorical structure that questions the justice of the prophet's imprisonment.
