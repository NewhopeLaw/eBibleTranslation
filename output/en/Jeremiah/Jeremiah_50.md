# Jeremiah 50

¹ The word that the LORD spoke against Babylon, against the land of the Chaldeans, by the prophet Jeremiah. ² Announce among the nations and proclaim, and hold up a banner. Proclaim, do not hide. Say, Babylon is captured, Bel is put to shame, the god of the Chaldeans is disgraced. Her images are broken, her idols are shattered. ³ For a nation has come up against her from the north. It will make her land desolate, and no one will dwell in it, from man to beast. They will flee and go. ⁴ In those days and in that time, says the LORD, the children of Israel and the children of Judah will go together, walking and weeping, and they will seek the LORD their God. ⁵ They will ask the way to Zion, saying, "Here is our face. Let us go to the LORD and seek His face. Let us go and take refuge in Him." For an eternal covenant will not be forgotten. ⁶ My people have become lost sheep, their shepherds have led them astray, on the mountains they have driven them, from hill to hill they have gone, forgetting their resting place. ⁷ All who find them devour them. Their enemies say, "We will not be guilty for what they did to the LORD." A field of righteousness and the one who gathered their ancestors. ⁸ Flee from the midst of Babylon and from the land of the Chaldeans. Go out, and be like wandering rams before the flock. ⁹ For behold, I am raising up against Babylon a gathering of nations from the land of the north. They will array themselves against her. From there she will be captured, like an arrow from a skillful warrior who does not return empty. ¹⁰ And the Chaldea will become a spoil, all who plunder her will be satisfied, says the LORD. ¹¹ For you have rejoiced and exulted by taking My inheritance. You have grazed like a calf in the meadow, and like a bull you have bellowed. ¹² Your mother has been greatly ashamed. She who gave birth to you has felt dishonor. She is the last of the nations, a waste. ¹³ It will not be inhabited because of the anger of the LORD. And it will become a complete ruin, and every traveler who passes through Babylon will hiss and scoff at all her wounds. ¹⁴ Array yourselves against Babylon all around, all the bowmen. Shoot at her, do not spare your arrows. ¹⁵ And seize her. Her hands have been paralyzed, her strongholds have fallen, and her walls have been torn down. For it is judgment from God. Repay her as she has done to you. ¹⁶ Cut off her seed who holds the sickle in the time of harvest. From the face of the Greek sword, each will return to his people and each to his land. ¹⁷ Israel, the strayed sheep, the lions have driven away. The first devoured him, the king of Assyria, and the last his bones, the king of Babylon. ¹⁸ Therefore thus says the LORD: I will take vengeance on the king of Babylon and on the land of Babylon, as I took vengeance on the king of Assyria. ¹⁹ And I will restore Israel to his pasture, and he will graze in Carmel and in the mountains of Ephraim and in Gilead, and his soul will be satisfied. ²⁰ In those days and in that time, says the LORD, they will seek the iniquity of Israel, and it will not be found, and the sin of Judah, and it will not be discovered, for I will forgive those whom I spare. ²¹ Come up bitterly against her and against those who dwell in her. Execute judgment with the sword and destroy, says the LORD. Do all that I command you. ²² A voice of war and a great breaking in the land of the Chaldeans. ²³ How is the hammer of all the earth broken and shattered? How has Babylon become a ruin among the nations? ²⁴ You will come against her and not know. Like Babylon, you will be captured. You will be found and taken, because you have stood against the LORD. ²⁵ The LORD has opened His treasure and brought out His weapons of wrath, for it is a work for the LORD, the God of the land of the Chaldeans. ²⁶ For her time is at hand. Open her treasures, search her out like a cave and destroy her, let there be no remnant. ²⁷ Bring down all her fruit, and let them go down to slaughter. Woe to them, for their day has come and the time of their judgment. ²⁸ A voice of those fleeing and being saved from the land of Babylon, to announce in Zion the vengeance of the LORD our God. ²⁹ Announce against Babylon with many, all the ones who pull the bow. Array yourselves against her all around. Let there be no one who escapes her. Repay her according to her deeds. Do to her all that she has done, for she has stood against the LORD, the holy God of Israel. ³⁰ Therefore her young men will fall in her streets, and all her warriors will collapse in that day, says the LORD. ³¹ I am coming to you, the wicked one, says the LORD of Hosts. For your day has come and the time of your judgment. ³² And your wickedness will be weakened and fall, and there will be none to raise it up. And I will ignite a fire in her woods, and it will consume all around her. ³³ Thus says the LORD: The oppressed sons of Israel and the sons of Judah, all together. All who have taken them have held them, they refused to release them. ³⁴ The redeemer is strong, the name of the LORD of Hosts. He will judge their dispute, to calm the land and to provoke those who dwell in Babylon. ³⁵ A sword against the Chaldeans and against those who dwell in Babylon and against their rulers and against their wise men. ³⁶ A sword against their warriors, and they will be paralyzed. A sword against their horses and against their chariots. ³⁷ A sword against their warriors and against the mixed multitude within her. They will be like women. A sword against their treasures, and they will be scattered. ³⁸ A sword against their waters, and they will dry up, for it is a land of idols, and in the islands where they boasted. ³⁹ Therefore jackals will dwell in the islands, and daughters of the desert will dwell in her. She will not be inhabited again forever. ⁴⁰ As God overturned Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors, says the LORD, no man will dwell there, and no son of man will live in it. ⁴¹ Look, a people is coming from the north and a great nation, and many kings will rise from the ends of the earth. ⁴² Holding bow and shield. They are cruel and do not have mercy. Their voice will be like the sea, and on horses they will ride, prepared like fire for battle, against you, daughter of Babylon. ⁴³ The king of Babylon heard their voice, and his hands fell from him. Distress has gripped him, like the pain of a woman in labor. ⁴⁴ Look, like a lion he will rise from the pride of the Jordan to the meadow of Arnon. For I will swiftly drive them away from there, and who is the young man to come to him? Who is like me? And who will oppose me? And who is this shepherd who will stand before me? ⁴⁵ Therefore hear the counsel of the LORD that He has devised against Babylon and the thoughts He has formed against the dwellers of the Chaldeans. Will not the young ones of the flock be taken from them? Will not their pasture be stripped from them? ⁴⁶ At the sound of Babylon's capture, the earth will tremble, and her cry will be heard among the nations.

---

Notes
- v. 17: "the lions have driven away" — The Hebrew text uses a metaphor comparing Babylon to lions that have led Israel astray. This is a metaphor for the Assyrian and Babylonian exiles.
- v. 19: "in Carmel and in the mountains of Ephraim and in Gilead" — The LORD promises to restore Israel to these regions, which are symbolic of the land of Israel.
