# Jeremiah 38

¹ Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Johakim son of Shelemiah, and Pashtur son of Malchiah heard the words that Jeremiah was speaking to the entire people, saying: ² "This is what the LORD says: Those who remain in this city will die by the sword, by famine, and by plague. But those who go out to the Chaldeans will live and will be taken as spoils of war and will survive." ³ "This is what the LORD says: This city will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon's army, and he will capture it." ⁴ Then the officials said to the king, "Please, let this man be put to death. He is encouraging the remaining soldiers in this city and the entire people by his words. This man is not seeking the well-being of this people but their harm." ⁵ King Zedekiah responded, "He is in your hands. The king cannot oppose you." ⁶ So they took Jeremiah and cast him into the cistern of Malchiah, the son of the king, which was in the court of the guard. They let him down with ropes into the cistern, where there was no water but only mud, and Jeremiah sank in the mud. ⁷ Now Ebed-Melech, the Cushite, a eunuch in the palace, heard that they had put Jeremiah into the cistern. The king was sitting at the Benjamin Gate. ⁸ Ebed-Melech left the palace and spoke to the king, saying: ⁹ "My lord the king, the men have done all to Jeremiah the prophet—they have cast him into the cistern, and he is dying from starvation, for there is no bread left in the city." ¹⁰ The king gave orders to Ebed-Melech the Cushite, saying, "Take thirty men with you, and bring the prophet Jeremiah up from the cistern before he dies." ¹¹ So Ebed-Melech took the men with him, went into the palace under the storehouse, and took from there old rags and old scraps of cloth, and let them down to Jeremiah in the cistern with ropes. ¹² Then Ebed-Melech said to Jeremiah, "Put the rags and scraps of cloth under your armpits to keep your hands from slipping off the ropes." And Jeremiah did so. ¹³ They pulled him up with the ropes and brought him out of the cistern, and Jeremiah sat in the court of the guard. ¹⁴ Then the king sent for Jeremiah and brought him in to him at the third entrance in the house of the LORD, and the king said to Jeremiah, "I want to ask you a question. Do not hide anything from me." ¹⁵ Jeremiah answered the king, "If I tell you, you will surely kill me. And if I give you advice, you will not listen to me." ¹⁶ The king swore to Jeremiah in secret, saying, "As the LORD lives, who has given us this life, if I kill you or hand you over to these men, may the LORD strike me down!" ¹⁷ Then Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, "This is what the LORD, the God of Hosts, the God of Israel, says to you: If you go out to the officials of the king of Babylon, your life will be spared, and this city will not be burned with fire. You and your household will live." ¹⁸ "But if you do not go out to the officials of the king of Babylon, this city will be given into the hand of the Chaldeans, and they will burn it with fire, and you will not escape from their hands." ¹⁹ The king said to Jeremiah, "I am afraid of the Jews who have already fallen into the Chaldeans, for they might hand me over to them and they will mistreat me." ²⁰ Jeremiah said, "They will not hand you over. Listen to the word of the LORD that I speak to you, and it will be well with you, and your life will be spared." ²¹ "If you refuse to go out, this is the word that the LORD has shown me: ²² All the women who are left in the palace of the king of Judah will go out to the officials of the king of Babylon. And they will say, 'You have deceived us, and your so-called peace allies have set your feet on slippery ground, causing you to turn back.'" ²³ "Your wives and your children will be taken to the Chaldeans, and you will not escape. You will be captured in the hand of the king of Babylon, and this city will be burned with fire." ²⁴ The king said to Jeremiah, "Let no one know about these words, or you will die." ²⁵ "If the officials hear that I have spoken to you, they will come to you and say to you, 'Tell us what you said to the king. Do not hide it from us, and you will not die. What did the king say to you?'" ²⁶ "Then you must say to them, 'I am pleading for mercy before the king, that he might not send me back to Jonathan's house to die there.'" ²⁷ Then all the officials came to Jeremiah and questioned him, and he told them all the words the king had commanded him. They were silent, for the word of the LORD had not been heard. ²⁸ So Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard until Jerusalem was captured, and he was captured on the same day that Jerusalem was captured.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "the cistern of Malchiah" – Malchiah is likely the son of the king, as stated in the text, and the cistern is part of the royal complex.
- v. 22: "peace allies" – the Hebrew "anashim shelomcha" is translated as "your so-called peace allies" to reflect the irony and the implication that the peace seekers are actually deceivers.
- v. 26: "Jonathan's house" – the Septuagint and some later traditions refer to this as the prison of Jonathan, the scribe. The Hebrew "bet Yonatan" is retained as "Jonathan's house" to align with the narrative context.
- v. 27: "the word of the LORD had not been heard" – this reflects the officials’ reaction to the prophecy, indicating disbelief or rejection of the message.
