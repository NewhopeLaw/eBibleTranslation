# Jeremiah 5

¹ Walk through the streets of Jerusalem and see, and learn, and search in its plazas, whether you can find a man, whether there is one who seeks justice, who seeks faithfulness, and I will forgive her. ² "The LORD lives," they say, "so surely they swear falsely." ³ O LORD, your eyes are upon faithfulness. You have struck them, but they did not waver. You have finished them, but they refused to accept discipline. They have hardened their faces as stone, and refused to return. ⁴ And I said, "Surely they are the poor. They have wandered because they have no knowledge of the way of the LORD, of the judgment of their God." ⁵ I will go to the leaders and speak to them, for they know the way of the LORD, the judgment of their God. But they have all broken the yoke, they have all torn the straps. ⁶ Therefore, they will be devoured by a lion from the forest, by a wolf at their settlements, by a leopard lying in wait for their cities. Every man who goes out from among them will be torn, for their sins are multiplied and their plots are many. ⁷ What will I do with you, my children? You have forsaken me and sworn falsely by gods who are not gods. I fed you and you were unfaithful. You lived in houses of harlots. ⁸ Horses are fattened, they are ready for war. Each man runs to his friend's wife and makes a noise. ⁹ For these things I will not remain silent," says the LORD. "And for a nation like this, I will not hold back my wrath. ¹⁰ Go up to their towers and destroy them, but do not finish. Pull down their supports, for they belong to the LORD. ¹¹ For they have been unfaithful to me, says the LORD, the house of Israel and the house of Judah. ¹² They have lied to the LORD and said, "He is not there. He will not do us any evil. Sword and famine will not come upon us." ¹³ The prophets will become like the wind, and the word is not in them. Thus will it be done to them. ¹⁴ Therefore, thus says the LORD, the God of Hosts: Because you have spoken these words, behold, I have given my words to your mouth as fire, and this people as dry wood, and they will be consumed. ¹⁵ Behold, I am bringing a nation from afar, says the LORD, to the house of Israel. It is a nation enduring, a nation ancient, a nation whose language you do not know, and whose words you will not understand. ¹⁶ Their jaws are like a grave, all of them are warriors. ¹⁷ They will eat your harvest and your bread. They will eat your sons and your daughters. They will eat your flocks and your cattle. They will eat your vines and your figs. They will strip your olive trees, and they will destroy your fortified cities in which you trust, with the sword. ¹⁸ And in those days, says the LORD, I will not make you into an end. ¹⁹ And it shall be, when you say, "Why has the LORD our God done all these things to us?" you will say to them, "Because you have forsaken me and served foreign gods in your land, so you will serve strangers in a land not yours." ²⁰ Declare this in the house of Jacob, and let it be heard in Judah, saying: ²¹ Listen, you foolish people, with no heart. Eyes they have, but they do not see. Ears they have, but they do not hear. ²² Will you not fear me? says the LORD. Will you not tremble before me? I who placed the sand as a boundary for the sea, an eternal decree, which cannot be crossed, and the waves churn but cannot cross it. ²³ But this people has a heart that is stubborn and rebellious. They have turned away and gone. ²⁴ And they did not say in their hearts, "Let us fear the LORD our God, who gives the rain in its season, the early and the late rain, who keeps the appointed time of the harvest for us." ²⁵ Your sins have turned these things away, and your iniquities have kept the good from you. ²⁶ For wicked men were found among my people. They set traps to destroy men and seize them. ²⁷ Like a sieve full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit. Therefore, they have grown great and become rich. ²⁸ They made fat their hearts, and they committed evil. They did not judge the orphan, and they did not defend the cause of the widow. ²⁹ For these things I will not remain silent," says the LORD. "And for a nation like this, I will not hold back my wrath. ³⁰ The land has become a desolation and a shock. ³¹ The prophets prophesy falsely, and the priests rule at their hand, and my people love it. What then will you do in the end?

---

Notes
- v. 1: "search in its plazas" – the Hebrew uses *rekhavot* (plazas) for clarity and to distinguish from streets.
- v. 22: "the sand as a boundary for the sea" – the metaphor of the sea being bounded by sand is a poetic way to express divine control, not a scientific claim.
- v. 25: "and your iniquities have kept the good from you" – the Hebrew uses *hittu* (turn) and *manu* (keep back), which are rendered to retain the poetic parallelism.
- v. 29: "I will not remain silent" – the Hebrew uses *lo ephakod* (I will not remain silent), emphasizing God's active response to injustice.
- v. 31: "and the priests rule at their hand" – the Hebrew uses *yirdu al yedeihem* which is best interpreted as "rule at their hand" to avoid implying the priests are complicit by force.
