# Jeremiah 21

¹ The message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur son of Malchiah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest, saying: ² "Please inquire of the LORD for us, for Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon is fighting against us. Perhaps the LORD will do for us all His wonders and bring the attack away from us." ³ Jeremiah said to them, "Tell Zedekiah: ⁴ This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I will turn the weapons of war in your hands, with which you are fighting against the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who are surrounding you outside the wall, into the city itself. ⁵ I will fight against you with a stretched-out hand and with a strong arm, in anger, in fury, and in great wrath. ⁶ I will strike the people living in this city, both man and beast, with a great plague. They will die. ⁷ After that, the LORD says, I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants, and the people remaining in this city from the plague, the sword, and the famine, into the hands of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon and into the hands of their enemies and those who are seeking their lives. They will kill them with the sword. I will not show compassion to them, nor will I be moved to pity or have mercy. ⁸ And to this people you must say: This is what the LORD says: I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death. ⁹ The one who remains in this city will die by the sword, by famine, and by plague. The one who leaves and falls to the Chaldeans who are surrounding you will live, and his life will become prey for them. ¹⁰ For I have set My face against this city for evil and not for good, the LORD says. It will be given into the hands of the king of Babylon and will be burned with fire. ¹¹ To the house of the king of Judah, listen to the word of the LORD: ¹² House of David, this is what the LORD says: Judge with justice in the morning, and deliver the oppressed from the hand of the oppressor, lest My fury go forth like fire and burn, and no one can put it out, because of the evil of your deeds. ¹³ Look, I am coming to you who dwell in the valley of the plain, the city of Sela, the level valley, the LORD says, to those who say, 'Who will attack us? And who will come into our dwellings?' ¹⁴ I will visit you with the fruit of your own deeds, the LORD says. I will set fire to its forest, and it will devour everything around it."

---

Notes
- v. 6: The Hebrew uses "דֶּבֶר" (dēbēr), which typically means "plague" or "pestilence," not necessarily a spiritual or metaphorical death.
- v. 10: "שַׂמְתִּי פָנַי בָּעִיר הַזֹּאת לְרָעָה" is rendered as "I have set My face against this city for evil" rather than "I have turned My face" or "I have determined," to reflect the firmness of the divine decree.
- v. 12: "מִשְׁפָּט" is translated as "justice" to reflect the legal and moral context, in line with the glossary entry for "תּוֹרָה" and similar terms.
