# Jeremiah 8

¹ At that time, says the LORD, they will bring out the bones of the kings of Judah and the bones of its rulers and the bones of the priests and the bones of the prophets and the bones of those who dwell in Jerusalem from their tombs. ² They will lay them out to the sun and to the moon and to all the host of the heavens, the ones they loved and served, the ones they followed and sought, and to whom they bowed — they will not be gathered nor buried, but will become a spectacle on the surface of the earth. ³ For death will be more desirable than life for all the remaining ones of this evil family wherever I drive them, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁴ Say to them, This is what the LORD says: Does the fallen not rise again? Does the one who turns not return again? ⁵ Why has this people of Jerusalem turned back so stubbornly? Why do they persist in their deceit, refusing to return? ⁶ I have listened and heard: It is not so. They do not speak truth — no one is grieved over his wrongdoing, saying, "What have I done?" All of them have turned back in their restlessness, like a horse rushing into battle. ⁷ Even the turtledove in the heavens knows her appointed times, and the swallow and the crane observe their season of coming — but my people do not know the judgment of the LORD. ⁸ How can you say, "We are wise, and the law of the LORD is with us"? In fact, the quill of falsehood has made the scribes a liar. ⁹ The wise have been put to shame, they have been confounded and captured. Indeed, they have despised the word of the LORD, and what wisdom is there in them? ¹⁰ Therefore I will give their women to others, their fields to inheriters — for from the least to the greatest, all of them have committed treachery, from prophet to priest, all of them doing falsehood. ¹¹ They have bound up the wound of the daughter of my people lightly, saying, "Peace, peace," when there is no peace. ¹² They have made a disgrace a disgrace for them, yet they are not ashamed. They do not know how to be put to shame. Therefore they shall fall among the fallen at the time of their punishment. They shall stumble, says the LORD. ¹³ I will gather them, says the LORD — there will be no grapes on the vine, no figs on the fig tree, and the leaf will wither. I will give them to the one who passes by. ¹⁴ Why are we sitting here? Let us gather and go to the fortified cities and hide there, for the LORD our God has abandoned us and has given us bitter waters to drink, for we have sinned against the LORD. ¹⁵ We had hoped for peace, but there was no good; for a time of healing, but behold, terror. ¹⁶ From Dan a sound is heard — the neighing of its horses. From the voice of the galloping of its steeds the whole earth trembles. They come and devour the land and all that fills it, the city and those who dwell in it. ¹⁷ For behold, I will send among you vipers, adders with no charm — and they will bite you, says the LORD. ¹⁸ My heart laments, my soul is in mourning. ¹⁹ Here is the voice of the daughter of my people coming from a distant land: Is there no LORD in Zion? Is there no king among them? Why have they provoked me with their idols and with the vanities of a foreign land? ²⁰ The harvest has passed, the summer is over, and we have not been saved. ²¹ Because of the brokenness of the daughter of my people I have been broken — I am overcome with pain like a woman in childbirth. ²² There is no balm in Gilead, is there no physician there? Why has no healing risen for the daughter of my people? ²³ Would that my head were waters and my eyes a spring of tears, that I might weep day and night for the slain of the daughter of my people!

---

Notes
- v. 23: The line "Would that my head were waters..." is a poetic expression of deep sorrow and lament, rendered in modern English with a literary tone.
