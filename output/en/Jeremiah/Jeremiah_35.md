# Jeremiah 35

¹ The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying: ² Go to the house of the archers and speak to them, and bring them to the house of the LORD, to one of the side chambers, and give them wine to drink. ³ So I took Jaazaniah the son of Jeremiah the son of Cephaiah, and his brothers and all his sons, and the whole house of the archers. ⁴ I brought them into the house of the LORD, to the room of the sons of Hanan the son of Igdaliah, a man of God, who was next to the room of the officials, which was above the room of Maaseiah the son of Salum, who guarded the threshold. ⁵ I set before the sons of the house of the archers full bowls of wine and cups, and I said to them, "Drink wine." ⁶ But they said, "We will not drink wine, for our father Jonadab the son of Rechab commanded us, saying, 'You will not drink wine, you and your sons, forever. ⁷ You will not build a house, you will not plant a vineyard or a field, and you will not have any of these things. You will live in tents all your days, so that you may live many days on the land where you are sojourning.'" ⁸ And we have heard the voice of Jonadab the son of Rechab, our father, in all that he commanded us, not to drink wine, all our days, ourselves, our wives, our sons, and our daughters. ⁹ And not to build houses to dwell in, and no vineyard or field or seed will be ours. ¹⁰ And we have lived in tents, and we have heard and done all that our father Jonadab commanded us. ¹¹ Now, when Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon came up to the land, we said, "Come, let us go to Jerusalem, because of the might of the Chaldeans and because of the might of Aram," and we lived there. ¹² Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying: ¹³ Thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Go and speak to the man of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, "Do you not learn to listen to my words?" says the LORD. ¹⁴ The command of Jonadab the son of Rechab has stood, whom he gave to his sons, not to drink wine, and to this day they have not drunk, because they have obeyed the command of their father. And I have spoken to you early and often, but you have not listened to me. ¹⁵ And I have sent all my servants the prophets to you, early and often, saying, "Turn back, each of you, from your evil way and do what is good, and do not go after other gods to serve them, and dwell in the land that I gave to you and to your fathers." But you have not inclined your ears, and you have not listened to me. ¹⁶ For the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have obeyed the command of their father, but this people has not listened to me. ¹⁷ Therefore thus says the LORD, God of hosts, God of Israel: "I will bring all the evil that I have spoken against them upon Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem. ¹⁸ And to the house of the archers thus says the LORD: "Because you have obeyed the command of Jonadab your father and have kept all his commands and done according to all that he commanded you, ¹⁹ therefore thus says the LORD of hosts, God of Israel: None of the descendants of Jonadab the son of Rechab will be cut off standing before me all the days of the earth."

---

Notes
- v. 1: The name "Jehoiakim" is spelled with the traditional English form based on the Hebrew.
- v. 18–19: The reference to "none of the descendants of Jonadab... being cut off" reflects the Hebrew's metaphor of a perpetual standing before God, not a literal absence from the earth.
