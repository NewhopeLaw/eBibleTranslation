# Jeremiah 48

¹ This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says about Moab: Alas for Nebo, for it has been plundered, for it has been taken. Its strongholds are shattered, its glory put to shame. ² No more praise for Moab in Heshbon — they have plotted evil against it. Come, let us destroy it, let it be wiped out from among nations. Then you too will be struck down, and the sword will pursue you. ³ A cry of lamentation from Horonaim — plunder and great destruction. ⁴ Moab is broken; raise a cry of mourning for her young. ⁵ For the hill of Luhith weeps; they climb weeping. For the descent of Horonaim they hear the sound of lamentation. ⁶ Flee, escape with your lives, and be like a wild donkey in the desert. ⁷ For because you trusted in your own works and treasures, you too will be captured, and your priests and your leaders will go into exile together. ⁸ A plunderer will come to every city, no city will escape, and the valley will be destroyed and the plain will be wiped out, as the LORD has declared. ⁹ Assign a sign to Moab — she will flee and be captured, and her cities will be made desolate, with no one to live in them. ¹⁰ Cursed is the one who does the work of the LORD carelessly, and cursed is the one who keeps the sword from its blood. ¹¹ You, Moab, have been secure since your youth and quiet to old age — you have not moved from vessel to vessel, nor gone into exile. So your food remains with you, and your scent has not changed. ¹² Therefore, days are coming, says the LORD, when I will send to him those who will trample him, and they will trample him and strip his vessels, and cut off his horns. ¹³ Moab will be shamed because of Chemosh, just as the house of Israel was shamed because of Bethel, their trust. ¹⁴ How can you say, "We are mighty, and men of valor for war"? ¹⁵ Moab has been plundered, her cities taken. Her chosen young men have gone down to slaughter, says the King, the LORD of Hosts. ¹⁶ The day of Moab is near to come, and her calamity will come swiftly. ¹⁷ All around her, raise a cry for her, and let all who know her name say: "How the staff of strength has been broken, the rod of pride!" ¹⁸ Come down from glory, sit in dryness, you who dwell in Dibon — for the plunder of Moab has come upon you, and it has destroyed your strongholds. ¹⁹ Stand by the road and look, you who dwell in Ar, and ask the fugitive and the escaped one, and say, "What has happened?" ²⁰ Moab is shamed, for it has been plundered. Wail and cry out, announce it in the Arnon, for Moab has been plundered. ²¹ And judgment comes to the land of Meishor, to Cholon, to Jahtzah, and to Mepa'ath. ²² And to Dibon, to Nebo, to the house of Debathaim. ²³ And to Kiryathaim, to the house of Gamul, to the house of Meon. ²⁴ And to Kerioth, to Bosor, and to all the cities of Moab, far and near. ²⁵ The horn of Moab is cut off, and her arm is broken, says the LORD. ²⁶ Be drunk, Moab, for you have exalted yourself against the LORD. And the strongholds will be struck down, and Moab will become a mockery and a laughingstock. ²⁷ And if it was a laughingstock for you, O Israel, that you found in your plots, then for your part you too will be shaken. ²⁸ They have abandoned their cities and fled into the rocks. The inhabitants of Moab have become like doves nesting in the mouth of a cleft. ²⁹ I have heard the pride of Moab — she is very proud, her pride and her arrogance — her heart is lifted up. ³⁰ I know her deeds, says the LORD — it was not enough for her to do this, no, she did not do it. ³¹ Therefore, for Moab I will wail on every side, and for all Moab I will cry out, and the people of Kir-cheres will lament. ³² I will mourn for you, O vine of Sibmah, with the lament of Jazer. Your branches have passed over the sea, the cities of Jazer have reached your vines, and on your grapes a plunder has fallen. ³³ Joy and gladness are taken from Carmel and from the land of Moab. Wine is taken from the winepresses — I will no longer drink it, says the one who drinks it, no longer drink it. ³⁴ From the cry of Heshbon to Elealeh, to Jahaz they have given their voice — from Zoar to Horonaim, a third-calf wail, for even the waters of Nimerim will become a wasteland. ³⁵ And I will put an end to Moab, says the LORD, to him who ascends to the altar and offers incense to his gods. ³⁶ Therefore, my heart for Moab is like flutes — and my heart for the people of Kir-cheres is like flutes — because of this, they have perished. ³⁷ Every head will be shaved, every beard will be cut, every hand will be wounded, and every waist will be wrapped in sackcloth. ³⁸ On all the rooftops of Moab and in all her streets there will be mourning — for I have shattered Moab, says the LORD, like a vessel that is of no use. ³⁹ How has she been plundered! How has her back turned! Moab is shamed, and she has become a mockery and a byword for all around her. ⁴⁰ For thus says the LORD: Behold, the eagle will swoop down and spread his wings over Moab. ⁴¹ Keriot is captured and the strongholds are seized. On that day the heart of the warriors of Moab will be like a woman in labor. ⁴² And Moab will be wiped out among the nations, because he exalted himself against the LORD. ⁴³ Fear and dread and the pit are upon you, O dweller in Moab, says the LORD. ⁴⁴ The one who flees from the face of the fear will fall into the pit, and the one who escapes from the pit will be caught in the trap — for I will bring them against Moab in the year of their reckoning, says the LORD. ⁴⁵ In the shadow of Heshbon they have stood with strength — for fire has gone out from Heshbon and flame from between Sihon. It has devoured the corner of Moab and the sons of Shaoan. ⁴⁶ Woe to you, Moab! The people of Chemosh are perished — for your sons have been taken into captivity and your daughters into captivity. ⁴⁷ I will restore the captivity of Moab in the latter days, says the LORD. Thus far is the judgment of Moab.

Notes
- v. 10: "carelessly" reflects the nuance of רְמִיָּה (remiyah), often translated as "negligently" or "casually."
- v. 26: "Chemosh" is the national god of Moab. Transliterated as in the Septuagint and standard references. 
- v. 27: "in your plots" refers to the Israelites' attempts to undermine Moab, as in Deuteronomy 2:24-25.
- v. 30: "not enough for her to do this, no, she did not do it" reflects the complex Hebrew structure, which implies that her actions were excessive and unnecessary.
- v. 36: "like flutes" is a poetic way to render the Hebrew חֲלִלִים (chalilim), which can mean "flutes" or "pipes," and is often used metaphorically to describe mourning.
- v. 37: "every hand will be wounded" reflects the Hebrew גְּדֻדֹת (gedudot), often rendered as "cut" or "stripped," but here taken as "wounded" for clarity and poetic tone.
- v. 47: "the latter days" refers to the Hebrew אַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים (acharit hayyamim), a common phrase for the eschatological future.
