# Jeremiah 4

¹ If Israel turns back, says the LORD, let her turn back to me. If she removes her abominations from her mouth, and no longer acts defiantly before me, ² and if she swears by the living LORD with truth, with justice, and with righteousness, then the nations will be blessed by her and will praise her in the LORD. ³ For this is what the LORD says to the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem: Build up your cities, and do not sow among thorns. ⁴ Circumcise yourselves to the LORD, remove the foreskin of your hearts, you men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem, or I will not accept your offerings. For my anger has been kindled, and it burns—it will not be quenched, because of the evil of your deeds. ⁵ Proclaim in Judah and in Jerusalem, and say: Blow the trumpet through the land. Cry aloud, and say: Assemble yourselves, and we will go into the cities of the siege. ⁶ Raise a banner toward Zion, flee, do not stand still, for I am bringing disaster from the north and great devastation. ⁷ A lion has ascended from its den, a destroyer of nations has set out. It has left its place to make your land desolate, your cities will be ruined, and no one will live in them. ⁸ Therefore, gird on sackcloth, mourn and wail, for the Lord's fierce anger has not turned away from us. ⁹ In that day, says the LORD, the heart of the king will be lost, and the heart of the rulers will be broken. The priests will be stunned, and the prophets will be bewildered. ¹⁰ I said, "Ah, Lord, surely You have deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying 'Peace will be yours,' when in fact the sword reaches the heart." ¹¹ In that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem: "A spirit of wandering in the wilderness, a path for the daughter of my people, not to purity, not to holiness." ¹² A spirit of fullness will come to me now, but I will speak judgments against them. ¹³ Look! Like clouds it rises, and like a storm are its chariots, lighter than eagles are its horses. Woe to us, for we are worn out. ¹⁴ Wash your heart from evil, Jerusalem, that you may be saved. How long will your evil thoughts remain in you? ¹⁵ For a voice of one declaring news comes from Dan, and a sound of trouble from the mountain of Ephraim. ¹⁶ Remember the nations, behold, they are coming. Proclaim in Jerusalem: Watchmen are coming from a distant land, and they have given their voices to the cities of Judah. ¹⁷ They have become like guards around her, for you have forsaken me, says the LORD. ¹⁸ These are your ways and your deeds done to you—this is your evil, because it is bitter, because it has touched your heart. ¹⁹ My insides are in anguish, my heart's affections are broken. My soul is in turmoil, my heart is split. I will not be silent, for my soul has heard the sound of a trumpet, the cry of war. ²⁰ One calamity after another is called, because the whole earth has been laid waste suddenly. My tents have been torn down in an instant, my curtains have been stripped away. ²¹ How long will I see a sign? When will I hear the sound of the trumpet? ²² For my people are fools, they do not know me. They are senseless children, they are not wise. They are wise to do evil, but to do good they do not know. ²³ I have looked at the earth, and behold, it is wasted and empty. I have looked at the heavens, and there is no light in them. ²⁴ I have looked at the mountains, and behold, they tremble, and all the hills are thrown into confusion. ²⁵ I have looked, and behold, there is no man, and all the birds of the heavens have fled. ²⁶ I have looked, and behold, Carmel has become a wasteland, and all the towns have been burned to the ground by the face of the LORD, by the heat of his anger. ²⁷ For this is what the LORD says: The whole earth will become a wasteland, and I will not spare it. ²⁸ Therefore, the earth will mourn, and the heavens above will darken, because I have spoken, and I will not relent, I will not turn back from it. ²⁹ At the sound of the horseman and the bowstring, all joy has fled. They have gone into the caves, and hidden in the groves, and climbed onto the rocks. Every city has been abandoned, no man dwells in it. ³⁰ And you, what will you do? You are captured. What will you do if you dress in crimson, if you adorn yourself with gold, if you apply paint to your eyes in vain? Your lovers despise you, they seek your life. ³¹ For I have heard a voice like a woman in labor, the sound of the daughter of Zion gasping, stretching out her hands. Alas, woe is me, for my life is consumed by those who are slain.

---

Notes
- v. 29: "the sound of the horseman and the bowstring" — the Septuagint reads "the sound of the horseman and the bent bow," but the Hebrew clearly uses "romeh ketsef" (bent bowstring) as a poetic phrase, which I have rendered accordingly.
- v. 30: "apply paint to your eyes" — the Hebrew uses "kaper" (paint/stain), which is typically associated with kohl or eye makeup, so "apply paint to your eyes" is an accurate contextual rendering.
- v. 31: "the sound of the daughter of Zion gasping" — "mabkira" is a rare term, often associated with childbirth, and is best rendered as "gasping" or "groaning."
