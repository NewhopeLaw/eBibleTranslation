# Jeremiah 10

¹ Hear the word that the LORD has spoken to you, house of Israel. ² Thus says the LORD: Do not learn the ways of the nations, and do not be afraid of the signs of the heavens, for the nations fear them. ³ The customs of the nations are empty. A tree cut from the forest is it, the work of a craftsman and a sculptor. ⁴ With silver and gold they beautify it, with a chisel and a hammer they strengthen it, yet it remains fixed and will not move. ⁵ They are like a tamarisk bush—motionless, unable to speak. They are carried, but not by themselves. Do not fear them, for they cannot do harm, nor is there good in them. ⁶ There is none like you, O LORD. You are great, and your name is great in power. ⁷ Who would not fear you, King of the nations? For to you belongs all, and among all the wise of the nations and their kings, there is none like you. ⁸ But they will be burned and consumed. Their instruction is empty; they are like wood. ⁹ Silver from Tarshish will be brought, and gold from Uphaz. The work of the craftsman and the hands of the goldsmith will be their clothing. They are adorned with hyacinth and purple, all the work of skillful men. ¹⁰ The LORD is the true God, the living God, the eternal King. At his wrath the earth quakes, and the nations cannot endure his anger. ¹¹ [Aramaic] Say this to them: The gods who did not make the heavens and the earth shall perish from the earth and from under the heavens. ¹² He who made the earth by his strength, who established the world by his wisdom, and by his understanding stretched out the heavens. ¹³ He causes the sound of the rain to fall from the heavens, and he brings clouds from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain and brings forth the wind from his treasuries. ¹⁴ Every man is confounded from knowledge; every sculptor is disgraced by his idol, for his molten image is false and there is no spirit in them. ¹⁵ They are empty, the work of deceptions. In the time of their reckoning they will perish. ¹⁶ Jacob has no such portion. For he who formed all things is their Maker, and Israel is his inheritance. The name of the LORD of Hosts is his. ¹⁷ My tabernacle is gathered from the land of my peace, dwelling in the quiet. ¹⁸ For thus says the LORD: I will surely scatter the inhabitants of this land in this time, to bring about their destruction. ¹⁹ Alas for me on account of my wound, on account of my grievous wound. I said, “Surely this is my sickness, and I will bear it.” ²⁰ My tent is torn down, and all my ropes are broken. My children have gone out from me and are no more. There is no one who spreads my tent again or raises my curtains. ²¹ For the shepherds have gone astray and did not seek the LORD, so they did not understand. All their pastures have been scattered. ²² A sound is heard; behold, it is coming, and a great earthquake from the land of the north, to make the cities of Judah a desolation, a haunt for jackals. ²³ I know, O LORD, that the way of a person is not in himself, nor does any man walking direct his own steps. ²⁴ Discipline me, O LORD, but with justice, not in your anger, lest you make me too small. ²⁵ Pour out your wrath on the nations that do not know you and on the families that do not call on your name, for they have devoured Jacob and consumed him, and have made his pasture a waste.

---

Notes
- v. 11: The Aramaic section is translated directly, preserving its distinctiveness. The phrase "the gods who did not make the heavens and the earth" reflects the contrast between false gods and the true God.
- v. 16: "Jacob" and "Israel" are used interchangeably here for clarity, reflecting their shared identity in the text.
- v. 23: The metaphor of human inability to direct one's steps is rendered in natural English, preserving the original’s theological point.
