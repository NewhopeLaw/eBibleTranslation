# Jeremiah 51

¹ Thus says the LORD: I am raising up against Babylon and the inhabitants of the Chaldeans a destructive wind. ² And I will send foreigners to Babylon and they will invade it and plunder its land. Woe to Babylon, for it is surrounded on every side in the day of its calamity. ³ Let the archer string his bow, and let him set his shield, and spare not its young men and destroy completely all its might. ⁴ And the slain will fall in the land of the Chaldeans and the pierced will be outside its cities. ⁵ For Israel and Judah have not been abandoned by their God, by the LORD of Hosts, for their land is filled with guilt from the holy ones of Israel. ⁶ Flee from the midst of Babylon and save each one his life, and do not be dismayed by its iniquity, for it is the time of the LORD's vengeance, repayment which He will give to it. ⁷ A golden cup is Babylon in the hand of the LORD, making the whole earth drunk. From its wine the nations have drunk, therefore they are unsteady. ⁸ Suddenly Babylon has fallen and been broken. Wail over her, take a balm for her wound, perhaps she may be healed. ⁹ We treated Babylon, and it was not healed. We abandoned it and went each one to his land, for her judgment has come up to the heavens and has been raised to the stars. ¹⁰ The LORD has brought out His judgment. Come and let us announce in Zion the works of the LORD our God. ¹¹ Prepare the arrows, fill the quivers. The LORD has stirred up the spirit of the kings of Media, for His anger is against Babylon to destroy it, for it is the vengeance of the LORD, vengeance for His people. ¹² On the walls of Babylon, raise a signal, set the watch, raise the guard, prepare the weapons, for the LORD has planned and will do to the inhabitants of Babylon what He spoke. ¹³ Dwelling on many waters and with many treasures. Indeed, the end has come to you, it has struck your heart. ¹⁴ For the LORD has sworn by His own arm, "I will fill you with people like grasshoppers, and they will cry out against you who come down." ¹⁵ Who makes the earth by His strength, who prepares the world by His wisdom, who spreads out the heavens by His understanding. ¹⁶ Who gave a voice to the tumult of waters in the heavens, and brought up clouds from the ends of the earth, made lightning for the rain, and brought forth the wind from His treasures. ¹⁷ Every man is senseless from knowledge, every metalsmith is shamed from his image, for his casting is false, and there is no breath in them. ¹⁸ They are emptiness, the works of deceptions; in the time of their visitation they will vanish. ¹⁹ Not like these is the portion of Jacob. He is the one who made all things, and His name is the LORD of Hosts. ²⁰ You are the dissembler of weapons for me, and I will scatter nations with you and destroy kingdoms with you. ²¹ And I will scatter horses and their riders with you, and chariots and their riders with you. ²² And I will scatter men and women with you, and the old and the young with you, and the young man and the virgin with you. ²³ And I will scatter shepherds and their flocks with you, and farmers and their harvests with you, and rulers and your generals with you. ²⁴ And I will repay Babylon and all the inhabitants of the Chaldeans for all their evils which they did against Zion in your sight, says the LORD. ²⁵ Behold, I am coming to you, the mountain that brings ruin, the one who brings ruin to all the earth. And I will stretch out My hand against you and roll you from the rocks, and I will make you like a burning mountain. ²⁶ And they will not take from you a stone for a corner and a stone for a foundation, for you will become a desolation forever, says the LORD. ²⁷ Raise a signal on the earth, sound the trumpet among the nations, consecrate nations against her, announce concerning her, raise kings from Me, and the Ararat, Minni, and Ashchenaz. Set up against her archers, mount against her horses like a swarm of locusts. ²⁸ Consecrate nations against her, the king of the Medes, all her rulers and all her generals, and all the land of his dominion. ²⁹ And the earth will tremble and be shaken, for the plan of the LORD has risen against Babylon to make the land of Babylon a desolation and no one will dwell in it. ³⁰ The warriors of Babylon have ceased to fight. They will sit in the citadel. Their might has been broken, they have become like women. Their tents have been burned, their bars have been broken. ³¹ The pursuer will run to meet the pursuer, and the messenger to meet the messenger, to announce to the king of Babylon that his city has been captured at the edge. ³² And the fords will be taken and the canals will be burned with fire, and the men of war will be terrified. ³³ For thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, the daughter of Babylon is like an orchard, the time has drawn near for her to be cut down. A little more and the time of her harvest will come. ³⁴ I was eaten, I was torn, I was swallowed like a vessel without a bottom. Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon swallowed me, like a leviathan, filled his belly with my delicacies, expelled me. ³⁵ My labors and my sorrows have gone to Babylon, says the one dwelling in Zion. And my blood is on those dwelling in the Chaldeans, says Jerusalem. ³⁶ Therefore thus says the LORD, I will certainly judge your case and avenge your vengeance, and I will dry up her sea and stop up her springs. ³⁷ And Babylon will become a ruin, a dwelling place for jackals, a desolation and no one will dwell in it. ³⁸ They will cry out like jackals, howl like lion cubs. ³⁹ In their heat I will give them drink and I will make them drunk to cause them to sleep a sleep of eternity and not awaken, says the LORD. ⁴⁰ I will bring them down like rams to the slaughter, like lambs with their lambs. ⁴¹ How has Sheshach been captured and taken, the glory of all the earth? How has Babylon become a desolation among the nations? ⁴² The sea has come upon Babylon in the multitude of its waves and it has been covered. ⁴³ Her cities have become a waste, a dry land and a desert. No one will dwell in them and no human will pass through them. ⁴⁴ And I will deal with Bel in Babylon and I will bring out Marduk from his mouth, and the nations will no longer flow to him. The wall of Babylon will fall. ⁴⁵ Go out from within her, My people, and escape, each one his life from the wrath of the LORD. ⁴⁶ And lest your hearts grow faint and you be afraid at the report heard in the land, and in the next year the report will come, and after it, in the next year, the report will come, and iniquity will come into the land, and ruler upon ruler. ⁴⁷ Therefore behold, days are coming and I will visit the idols of Babylon and the whole land of it will be ashamed and all its slain will fall within it. ⁴⁸ And rejoice over Babylon, heaven and earth and all that is in them, for from the north the plunderers will come, says the LORD. ⁴⁹ And Babylon will fall, the slain of Israel will fall in her, and the slain of all the earth will fall in her. ⁵⁰ Fugitives from the sword have gone away. Do not stand, remember from afar the LORD and let Jerusalem rise to your heart. ⁵¹ We are ashamed, for we have heard a disgrace, our faces are covered, because strangers have entered into the holy places of the house of the LORD. ⁵² Therefore behold, days are coming, says the LORD, and I will visit the idols of it, and in the whole land of it, the slain will cry out. ⁵³ For though Babylon should rise as high as the heavens, and though she should fortify her height, plunderers will come to her from Me, says the LORD. ⁵⁴ A voice of a cry in Babylon, and a great crash in the land of the Chaldeans. ⁵⁵ For the LORD is plundering Babylon and has taken away from her a great voice, and their tumult is given like many waters, and He has given her voice to destruction. ⁵⁶ For a calamity has come upon her, the warriors of her have been captured. Her bowstrings have been broken, for the God of repayment will repay them. ⁵⁷ And I will make drunk her rulers and her wise men, her generals and her warriors, and they will sleep an eternal sleep and not awaken, says the King, the LORD of Hosts, His name. ⁵⁸ Thus says the LORD of Hosts: The walls of Babylon have broadened, the foundations will be dug out, and her high gates will be burned with fire, and the peoples will not toil in vain, and the nations will consume in the beginning and be exhausted. ⁵⁹ The word which the LORD commanded Jeremiah the prophet to say to Seraiah the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, when he went with Zedekiah the king of Judah to Babylon in the fourth year of his reign, and Seraiah was the chief of the palace. ⁶⁰ And Jeremiah wrote all the evil that would come upon Babylon into one book, all these words written concerning Babylon. ⁶¹ And Jeremiah said to Seraiah, When you come to Babylon, and see and read all these words, ⁶² and say, "The LORD, you have spoken concerning this place to destroy it so that no one will dwell in it from man to beast, for it will be a desolation forever." ⁶³ And it will be, when you finish reading this book, you shall tie a stone to it and cast it into the midst of the Euphrates. ⁶⁴ And say, "Thus will Babylon sink and not rise again because of the evil I am bringing upon her," and these are the words of Jeremiah.

Notes
- v. 14: "I will fill you with people like grasshoppers" – the Hebrew uses a metaphor that could also be interpreted as "like locusts," but "grasshoppers" is more commonly used in English Bibles for this context.
- v. 34: "I was eaten, I was torn, I was swallowed like a vessel without a bottom" – the metaphor is complex and could be interpreted in multiple ways, but the rendering follows the most natural reading.
- v. 44: "I will deal with Bel in Babylon" – "Bel" refers to the Babylonian god, and "Marduk" is a specific reference to another Babylonian deity; both are translated directly as proper names.
