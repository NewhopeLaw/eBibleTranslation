# Jeremiah 7

¹ The message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying: ² Stand at the gate of the house of the LORD and there speak this word, saying, "Hear the word of the LORD, all of Judah who come into these gates to worship the LORD. ³ Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Improve your ways and your actions, and I will let you dwell in this place. ⁴ Do not trust in deceptive words, saying, 'The temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD these are.' ⁵ If instead you improve your ways and your actions, if you do justice between a person and their neighbor, ⁶ if you do not oppress the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not follow other gods to your own harm, ⁷ then I will let you dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your ancestors forever and always. ⁸ You are trusting in deceptive words, which will not help you. ⁹ Are those who steal and kill and commit adultery and swear falsely, and burn incense to Baal and follow other gods you have not known, ¹⁰ and then come before me in this house, which is called by my name, saying, 'We are safe to do all these abominations,' ¹¹ do you think this house, which is called by my name, has become a den of robbers in your eyes? I have seen it, says the LORD. ¹² So go now to my place in Shiloh, where I first placed my name, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of my people Israel. ¹³ And now, because you have done all these things, says the LORD, I have spoken to you and you have not listened; I have called to you and you have not answered. ¹⁴ I will treat the house that is called by my name, the place where you trust, and the land that I gave to you and to your ancestors, just as I did to Shiloh. ¹⁵ I will cast you away from my presence, just as I cast away all your brothers, the entire seed of Ephraim. ¹⁶ And you, do not pray for this people, do not offer any intercession or prayer for them, and do not come near me on their behalf, for I do not listen to you. ¹⁷ Do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem? ¹⁸ The sons are gathering wood, and the fathers are kindling the fire, and the women are mixing dough to make cakes for the queen of heaven, and pouring out drink offerings to other gods, to provoke me. ¹⁹ Are they provoking me, says the LORD, when they do this? Is it not so that their faces are put to shame? ²⁰ Therefore, this is what the LORD says: My anger and fury have been poured out on this place, on the people, on the animals, on the trees of the field, and on the fruit of the ground, and it will burn and not be quenched. ²¹ Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Your burnt offerings and sacrifices, bring them and eat the meat. ²² For I did not speak to your ancestors, nor did I command them, on the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt, about burnt offerings and sacrifices. ²³ Rather, I commanded them this one thing: 'Hear my voice, and I will be your God, and you will be my people, and you will walk in all the way I command you, so that it may go well for you.' ²⁴ Yet they did not listen, and they did not incline their ears, but walked in the stubbornness of their evil hearts, becoming backward and not forward. ²⁵ From the day their ancestors came out of the land of Egypt until this day, I have sent all my servants the prophets to them, morning and evening. ²⁶ Yet they did not listen to me, and their ears did not incline, and they hardened their necks more than their ancestors. ²⁷ And you will speak all these words to them, but they will not listen to you, and you will call to them, but they will not answer. ²⁸ And you will say to them, 'This is the people that has not listened to the voice of the LORD their God, and has not accepted instruction. The faithfulness has perished from their mouths.' ²⁹ Shave your head and throw it away, and take up a mourning on your lips, for the LORD has rejected and has cast off this generation. ³⁰ For the sons of Judah have done evil in my sight, says the LORD. They have placed their abominations in the house that is called by my name, to defile it. ³¹ And they have built high places of Topheth in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom to burn their sons and daughters in the fire, which I did not command and did not plan in my heart. ³² Therefore, behold, days are coming, says the LORD, when Topheth and the Valley of the Son of Hinnom will no longer be called, but the Valley of Slaughter. And they will bury in Topheth because there will be no room. ³³ And the corpses of this people will become food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth, and there will be no one to scare them away. ³⁴ And I will stop the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of the bridegroom and the sound of the bride in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, for the land will become a ruin.

---

Notes
- v. 21: The reference to "burnt offerings" and "sacrifices" here is in the context of the LORD's rejection of the people's religious rituals due to their disobedience and idolatry. The instruction to "eat the meat" is likely a poetic way of saying their sacrifices are meaningless to the LORD.
- v. 32: The name "Topheth" and its transformation into "Valley of Slaughter" reflect a prophetic renaming, indicating the severity of God's judgment.
- v. 34: The phrase "sound of joy" and "sound of gladness" is rendered as poetic lines, maintaining the parallelism of the Hebrew text.
