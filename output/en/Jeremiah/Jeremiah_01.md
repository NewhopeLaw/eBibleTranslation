# Jeremiah 1

¹ These are the words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, from the priests in Anathoth in the territory of Benjamin. ² The word of the LORD came to him in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign. ³ It was in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the twelfth year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah—until the deportation of Jerusalem in the fifth month. ⁴ Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying: ⁵ "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, and before you were born I consecrated you. I appointed you a prophet to the nations." ⁶ I said, "Ah, Lord GOD, I do not know how to speak, for I am a mere youth." ⁷ But the LORD said to me, "Do not say, 'I am a mere youth,' for you will go to all whom I send you, and you will speak everything I command you. ⁸ Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you," says the LORD. ⁹ Then the LORD reached out and touched my mouth, and said to me, "I have now placed my words in your mouth. ¹⁰ See, today I appoint you over nations and kingdoms to uproot and to break down, to destroy and to overthrow, to build and to plant." ¹¹ Then the word of the LORD came to me again, saying, "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I replied, "I see a branch of a smooth carob tree." ¹² The LORD said to me, "You have seen well, for I am watching to bring my word to pass." ¹³ Then the word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying, "What do you see?" I answered, "I see a seething pot, tilted toward me from the north." ¹⁴ The LORD said to me, "From the north disaster shall break out on all who live in the land. ¹⁵ For now I am summoning all the families of the northern kingdoms," says the LORD, "and they will come and set each one of their thrones at the gates of Jerusalem, at every one of its walls, and at all the cities of Judah. ¹⁶ And I will pronounce my judgment against them for every evil they have done—how they have abandoned me, burned incense to foreign gods, and bowed down to the works of their hands. ¹⁷ You, however, must gird up your loins, stand up, and speak to them everything I command you. Do not be dismayed before them, or I will dismay you in their presence," says the LORD. ¹⁸ For today I have made you like a fortified city, like a bronze pillar, and like an iron wall, to stand against all the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests, and the people of the land. ¹⁹ They will fight against you, but they will not prevail over you, for I am with you to deliver you," says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 5: The phrase "I knew you before I formed you in the womb" uses the active voice to reflect the Hebrew's causal structure, emphasizing God's initiative.
- v. 11: The "branch of a smooth carob tree" is a literal translation of "makel sheked," preserving the imagery while using the English word "smooth" to reflect "sheked."
- v. 13: "Seething pot" is used for "sir nafuch" to reflect the boiling and danger implied by the word "nafuch" (boiling).
- v. 18: "Bronze pillar" and "iron wall" are retained to reflect the Hebrew's metaphor of strength and endurance.
