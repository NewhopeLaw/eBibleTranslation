# Jeremiah 34

¹ The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, as Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and all his army, and all the kings of the lands under his dominion, and all the peoples were fighting against Jerusalem and its cities. ² Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah, and tell him, "Thus says the LORD: I am about to give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire. ³ You will not escape from his hand, for you will be captured and delivered into his hand. Your eyes will see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and your mouth will speak with his mouth, and you will go to Babylon. ⁴ But listen, Zedekiah king of Judah: Thus says the LORD, "You will not die by the sword. ⁵ You will die in peace, and as your ancestors, the kings who ruled before you, were burned, so you will be burned. They will mourn for you, saying, 'Alas, my lord!' For I have spoken this, declares the LORD." ⁶ Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem. ⁷ And the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and all the cities of Judah that remained, Lachish and Azekah, for these were the fortified cities of Judah. ⁸ The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after King Zedekiah made a covenant with all the people who were in Jerusalem to proclaim release. ⁹ To release each man his male servant and each man his female servant, the Hebrew and the Hebrewess, to let them go free, not to enslave them any longer. ¹⁰ All the officials and all the people who had entered into the covenant to release each man his servant and each woman his servant, free, heard and did so. ¹¹ But afterward they turned and brought back the servants and the female servants they had set free, and they forced them again into servitude, becoming their masters and female servants. ¹² Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying: ¹³ "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: I made a covenant with your ancestors when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery, saying, ¹⁴ 'At the end of seven years you must release your brother the Hebrew, who has been sold to you, and who has served you six years; you must let him go free.' But your ancestors did not listen to me or incline their ears. ¹⁵ And you have now turned and done what is right in my eyes, calling for release, each toward his neighbor. You have made a covenant before me in the house where my name is called upon. ¹⁶ Yet you have turned back and profaned my name, each bringing back the servant and the female servant he had set free, to make them servants and female servants again. ¹⁷ Therefore, thus says the LORD: You have not listened to me to call for release, each toward his brother and each toward his neighbor. I will now call for release for you, declares the LORD, to the sword, to the plague, and to the famine. I will give you over to be a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth. ¹⁸ And I will give the men who have broken my covenant, who did not uphold the words of the covenant they made before me, the calf they cut in two, to be passed between the pieces. ¹⁹ The rulers of Judah, the rulers of Jerusalem, the court officials, and the priests, and all the people of the land who passed between the pieces of the calf. ²⁰ I will give them into the hands of their enemies and into the hands of those who seek their lives. Their corpses will become food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth. ²¹ And I will give Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials into the hands of their enemies and into the hands of those who seek their lives, and into the hands of the army of the king of Babylon, who are coming up against you. ²² I will command, declares the LORD, and I will bring them back to this city. They will fight against it, take it, and burn it with fire, and I will make the cities of Judah desolate, without anyone living in them."

---

Notes
- v. 8: "after King Zedekiah made a covenant" – the Hebrew reads "after the covenant was made by the king," but the context clarifies that Zedekiah is the subject.  
- v. 18: "the calf they cut in two" – the Hebrew uses עֵגֶל (egel), which typically refers to a calf, but in the context of a covenant ritual, it refers to the animal used in the ritual of passing between the pieces.  
- v. 22: "I will command" – the Hebrew is נַצָּוֹה (nitsavoh), a form that can mean "I will command" or "I am commanding," depending on the context. The latter is chosen here for consistency with the prophetic tone.
