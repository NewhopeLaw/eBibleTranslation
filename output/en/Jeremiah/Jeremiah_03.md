# Jeremiah 3

¹ "If a man sends away his wife, and she goes from him, and becomes another man's wife, will she return to him again? Will she not be defiled? And you have played the prostitute with many lovers. Yet you return to me," says the LORD. ² Lift up your eyes to the bare hills and see where you did not lie with them. You sat down for them like a desert traveler in the wilderness and defiled the land with your prostitution and your wickedness. ³ You have had many lovers, and yet you did not call them to account. And your face was like the face of a prostitute, and you were put to shame by all your lovers. ⁴ "Did you not call me 'Father, my youth's guide'?" ⁵ "Will you never be faithful? Will you never remain steadfast? You have spoken and done evil, and you have succeeded." ⁶ The LORD said to me in the days of Josiah the king, "Have you seen what Israel the faithless has done, going up on every high mountain and under every green tree and playing the prostitute there?" ⁷ I thought, "After she has done all these things, she will return to me. But she did not return. And she saw her faithless sister Judah." ⁸ I saw that, because of all the adulteries Israel the faithless had committed, I had sent her away and given her a bill of divorce in her hand. But faithless Judah did not fear, and she went and played the prostitute as well. ⁹ And her prostitution made the land desolate, defiling the stone and the tree. ¹⁰ Yet even after all this, faithless Judah did not return to me with all her heart, but only with deceit," says the LORD. ¹¹ The LORD said to me, "Israel has cleared her conscience from the guilt of Judah the faithless." ¹² Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and say, "Return, Israel, faithless ones," says the LORD. "I will not hide my face from you, for I am a faithful God," says the LORD. "I will not be angry with you forever." ¹³ But know your guilt: you have sinned against the LORD your God, scattering your ways to foreigners, beneath every green tree, and you did not listen to my voice," says the LORD. ¹⁴ Return, children who have turned away," says the LORD. "For I will take you one from a city and two from a family, and bring you to Zion." ¹⁵ And I will give you shepherds according to my heart, who will feed you with knowledge and understanding. ¹⁶ And it will be in the days to come," says the LORD, "that when you are multiplied and increased in the land, you will no longer say, 'The ark of the covenant of the LORD.' And it will not come to mind, nor will it be remembered, nor will it be missed, nor will it be made again. ¹⁷ In those days they will call Jerusalem 'The Throne of the LORD,' and all the nations will gather to it. They will no longer go after the evil desires of their hearts. ¹⁸ In those days the house of Judah will go to the house of Israel, and together they will come from the land of the north to the land I gave to your ancestors. ¹⁹ And I said, "How can I place you among the nations, and give you a desirable land, an inheritance of wild beasts and of nations? And I said, 'You shall call me Father, and not turn away from me.'" ²⁰ But as a woman betrays her husband, so has the house of Israel betrayed me," says the LORD. ²¹ A voice was heard from the hills, the weeping and pleading of the children of Israel, because they had sinned in their ways and forgotten their holy God. ²² Return, you who have turned back," says the LORD. "I will heal your wounds. Behold, we are your servants; for you are the LORD our God. ²³ Surely the mountains and the hills were deceived, but through the LORD our God is the salvation of Israel. ²⁴ And the disgrace consumed the labors of our ancestors from our youth, their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters. ²⁵ We have lain down in our disgrace, and our shame has covered us, because we have sinned against our God, we and our ancestors, from our youth until this day, and we have not obeyed the voice of the LORD our God."

---

Notes
- v. 1: "has become like this" – contextually referring to the people's betrayal and infidelity, not a direct reference to a prior verse. 
- v. 7: "faithless sister Judah" – the term "faithless" is used to mirror the term applied to Israel, emphasizing the shared guilt. 
- v. 10: "with all her heart" – the phrase "בְּכָל־לִבָּהּ" is rendered to emphasize wholeheartedness, which Judah failed to achieve. 
- v. 19: "an inheritance of wild beasts and of nations" – the metaphor reflects the desolation and foreign occupation of the land. 
- v. 25: "we have sinned against our God" – the plural form "we" includes both the speaker and ancestors, aligning with the communal guilt expressed in the chapter.
