# Jeremiah 29

¹ These are the words of the letter that Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem to the elders of the exile, to the priests, to the prophets, and to all the people exiled from Jerusalem to Babylon. ² After Jeconiah the king, the queen mother, the eunuchs, the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem, and all the craftsmen and metalworkers had gone out from Jerusalem. ³ In the hands of Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemaryah son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah had sent to Babylon to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, saying, ⁴ Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, to all the exiles I have sent from Jerusalem to Babylon: ⁵ Build houses and live in them; plant gardens and eat their fruit. ⁶ Take wives and have sons and daughters, and give your daughters to men, and take your sons' wives from the women, and multiply there. Do not decrease. ⁷ Seek the peace of the city where I have exiled you, and pray to the LORD for it, for in its peace, there will be peace for you. ⁸ For thus says the LORD: Do not let your prophets among you and your diviners deceive you, and do not listen to the dreams you dream. ⁹ For they prophesy falsely to you in my name; I have not sent them, says the LORD. ¹⁰ For thus says the LORD: When the full time for Babylon comes—seventy years—then I will visit you and fulfill my good word to you, to bring you back to this place. ¹¹ For I know the plans I have for you, says the LORD, plans for peace and not for evil, to give you a future and a hope. ¹² Then you will call to me, and you will go and pray to me, and I will hear you. ¹³ You will seek me and find me, when you search for me with all your heart. ¹⁴ Then I will be found by you, says the LORD, and I will restore your captivity and gather you from all the nations and all the places where I have driven you, says the LORD, and I will return you to the place from which I have exiled you. ¹⁵ Because you said, We have set up prophets for us in Babylon. ¹⁶ Thus says the LORD to the king sitting on David’s throne and to all the people living in this city, your brothers who did not go out with you into exile: ¹⁷ Thus says the LORD of Hosts: I will send upon them the sword, famine, and pestilence, and I will make them like figs that are too hard to eat, which will not rot or be eaten. ¹⁸ And I will pursue them with the sword, with famine, and with pestilence, and I will give them to be a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, a disgrace, a hissing, and a mockery in all the places where I have driven them. ¹⁹ Because they did not listen to my words, says the LORD, which I sent to them through my servants the prophets, who arose early and went late, but you did not listen, says the LORD. ²⁰ You, listen to the word of the LORD, you exiles sent out from Jerusalem to Babylon. ²¹ Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, to Ahab son of Kolaiah and to Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, the prophets who prophesied to you in my name falsely: I will hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will kill them before your eyes. ²² And they will take from them a curse for all the exile of Judah in Babylon, saying, May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon burned in the fire. ²³ Because they committed abominations in Israel and defiled the wives of their neighbors and spoke a word in my name that I did not command them, and I am the one who knows and am a witness, says the LORD. ²⁴ And to Shemaiah the Nehelamite, you will say: ²⁵ Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Because you sent letters in my name to all the people in Jerusalem and to Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest and to all the priests, saying, ²⁶ The LORD has made you a priest in place of Jehoida the priest, to be in charge of the house of the LORD for every mad prophet and every false prophet, and you will put them in the stocks and in the dungeon. ²⁷ Why did you not rebuke Jeremiah the Anathothite, who prophesied to you? ²⁸ Because of this, he sent to us in Babylon, saying, The days are long; build houses and live in them, and plant gardens and eat their fruit. ²⁹ Then Zephaniah the priest read this letter in the ears of Jeremiah the prophet. ³⁰ Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying: ³¹ Send to all the exiles, saying, Thus says the LORD to Shemaiah the Nehelamite, who prophesied to you, but I did not send him, and he made you trust in falsehood. ³² Therefore, thus says the LORD: I will bring judgment on Shemaiah the Nehelamite and on his descendants. No one among them will live to see the good that I will do to my people, says the LORD, for he has spoken falsely about the LORD.

Notes
- v. 25: "Shemaiah the Nehelamite" is a direct translation of שְׁמַעְיָה הַנֶּחֱלָמִי. The term נֶחֱלָמִי is transliterated as "Nehelamite" to reflect its ethnic or geographic derivation.
- v. 27: "the Anathothite" is a direct translation of הָֽעֲנְּתֹתִי, denoting Jeremiah's hometown.
- v. 28: "The days are long" is a translation of אֲרֻכָּה הִיא. This phrase is rendered as a direct quote from the letter sent by Shemaiah, not as a statement from the LORD.
