# Jeremiah 46

¹ This is the message the LORD gave to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations: ² Against Egypt, regarding Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt, who reigned by the Euphrates River in Carchemish, the place Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon defeated in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. ³ Prepare shields and spears and advance to battle. ⁴ Harness the horses and mount the steeds, and take your positions with helmets. Sharpen the swords and don your armor. ⁵ Why have I seen them like frightened horses retreating? Their warriors are struck down, and they have fled without looking back, says the LORD. ⁶ Let the swift not escape and the mighty not flee northward by the Euphrates. They have stumbled and fallen. ⁷ Who is this flowing like a river? Who is this rising like rivers, whose waters churn? ⁸ Egypt is like a river rising, like rivers whose waters churn. It says, "I will rise and cover the land, destroy the city and its inhabitants." ⁹ Mount the horses, prepare the chariots, and let the warriors go out — Cush and Put with shields, and Lud with bowmen. ¹⁰ That day will be the day of the LORD of Hosts — a day of vengeance, to take vengeance on His enemies. The sword will devour and be satisfied, and drink their blood, for it is a sacrifice to the LORD of Hosts in the northern land, by the Euphrates River. ¹¹ Go up, O Gilead, and get a balm for the daughter of Egypt, the beautiful maiden. In vain you have multiplied your remedies; there is no healing for you. ¹² The nations have heard your disgrace, and your cry has filled the land, for warrior against warrior has fallen; together they have fallen. ¹³ The word the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet about Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon coming to strike the land of Egypt. ¹⁴ Announce in Egypt, proclaim in Migdol, proclaim in Noph and in Tahpanhes: Take your stand and prepare, for the sword is coming around you. ¹⁵ Why has your strong one fled? Your mighty one has not remained, for the LORD has driven him away. ¹⁶ The people have stumbled and fallen, each speaking to their neighbor, "Let us rise and return to our people and to the land of our birth, away from the sword of the invader." ¹⁷ Call the name of Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt, Saon, Hesed, Emowed. ¹⁸ I am alive, says the LORD of Hosts, that as the Tabor in the mountains and as the Carmel in the sea, so it will come. ¹⁹ Make yourself a place of exile, O resident daughter of Egypt. For Noph will become desolation, and it will be called "Not inhabited," because no one will live there. ²⁰ Beautiful, finely adorned Egypt — a goring heifer coming from the north has struck her. ²¹ Her hired men are with her, like feeding oxen in her midst. For they have turned and fled together, they did not stand — because the day of their ruin has come, and the time of their reckoning. ²² Her voice is like a serpent slithering, for by sand they are moving, with axes they will come against her. ²³ They will cut down her forest, says the LORD — for it cannot be reckoned, because it is more numerous than locusts, and there is no number to them. ²⁴ The daughter of Egypt has been shamed, given into the hand of a northern people. ²⁵ The LORD of Hosts, God of Israel, says: I will punish Ammon her son, and Pharaoh and Egypt, and their gods, and their kings, and Pharaoh, and those who trust in him. ²⁶ I will give them into the hands of those who seek their lives and into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his servants. Afterwards, they will dwell as in former days, says the LORD. ²⁷ But you, do not fear, My servant Jacob, and do not be dismayed, Israel. For I will rescue you from far away, and your descendants from the land of exile. Jacob will return and find peace, and will lie down in safety, with no one to make him afraid. ²⁸ You, do not fear, My servant Jacob, says the LORD. For I am with you. I will complete all the nations where I have scattered you, but I will not complete your destruction. I will discipline you in justice and will not leave you unpunished.

---

Notes
- v. 17: The names "Saon, Hesed, Emowed" are transliterations of the Hebrew text, which appears to be a play on words or a mocking title for Pharaoh Neco.
