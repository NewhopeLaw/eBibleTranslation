# Jeremiah 49

¹ To the children of Ammon thus says the LORD: Is there no heir for Israel, or no one to inherit? Why then has their king taken Gad, and their people dwell in their cities? ² Therefore, behold, days are coming, says the LORD, and I will hear the sound of war in Rabbah of the children of Ammon, and it will become a ruin and a waste, and its daughters will be burned with fire, and Israel will inherit its inheritance, says the LORD. ³ Wail, Heshbon, for it has been plundered. The daughters of Rabbah have cried out, girded themselves with sacks and mourned, for their king has gone into exile, and their priests and their leaders have gone with him. ⁴ What do you boast of in the valleys, O daughter of the torrent valley, the one who is in turmoil and who trusts in her treasures, saying, "Who will come to me?" ⁵ Behold, I am bringing terror upon you, says the Lord of Hosts, from all around you, and you will be scattered, each to his face, and there will be none to gather the scattered. ⁶ And after that I will restore the captivity of the children of Ammon, says the LORD. ⁷ To Edom thus says the LORD of Hosts: Is there no more wisdom in Teman? Has counsel perished from the wise? Their wisdom has turned to folly. ⁸ Flee, turn, and hide in the desert, you who dwell in Dedan, for I have brought upon Esau the time of reckoning. ⁹ If raiders come to you, they will leave no young ones; if thieves come in the night, they will destroy enough. ¹⁰ For I have uncovered Edom, I have revealed her secrets, and she has no place to hide, no one left to save her, no one to rescue her seed or her brothers. ¹¹ I will leave your orphans and I will care for your widows. ¹² For thus says the LORD: Indeed, as for those who have no judgment, they shall drink the cup, they shall drink; and as for you, the pure, you shall not go unpunished; you must drink. ¹³ For I have sworn by myself, says the LORD, that Bozrah shall be a waste, a reproach, a ruin and a curse, and all her cities shall be ruins forever. ¹⁴ I have heard from the LORD and my heart is stirred among the nations, gather up and come against her, and rise for battle. ¹⁵ For behold, I have made you small among the nations, despised by men. ¹⁶ You were proud in your heart, you were deceitful with your heart, you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, who take hold of the high places of the hill. From there I will bring you down, says the LORD. ¹⁷ And Edom will become a waste, all who pass through it will hiss and scoff at all her wounds. ¹⁸ Like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors, says the LORD, no one will dwell there, and no man will live in it. ¹⁹ Behold, like a lion he will rise from the Jordan's pride to the meadow of Arnon. I have pursued him and made him flee from there, and who is the young man who will come to him? Who is the shepherd who will stand before me? ²⁰ Therefore, hear the counsel of the LORD that he has planned against Edom and the thoughts he has devised against the dwellers of Teman: Shall not the young ones of the flock be taken from them? Shall not their pasture be laid bare? ²¹ The sound of their fall has caused the earth to tremble. The voice of their cry was heard in the Red Sea. ²² Behold, like an eagle he will rise and swoop down, and he will spread his wings over the fortress, and the heart of the warriors of Edom in that day will be like the pain of a woman in labor. ²³ For Damascus is put to shame, for Hamath and Arpad, for they have heard evil news. They are disquieted, they cannot remain silent. ²⁴ Damascus has been broken, she has turned to flee, and distress has gripped her, and anguish like that of a woman in childbirth. ²⁵ How will the city of the glory of Kirjathaim not be forsaken? ²⁶ Therefore her young men will fall in her streets, and all her warriors will be silent in that day, says the LORD of Hosts. ²⁷ And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus and consume the palaces of Ben-hadad. ²⁸ To Kedar and to the kings of Hazor, whom Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon struck, thus says the LORD: Arise, go up to Kedar and destroy the children of the East. ²⁹ Their tents and their flocks they will take, their curtains and all their goods, and their camels they will carry off to themselves, and they will call out against them a cry of alarm on every side. ³⁰ Flee, be greatly removed, and deeply hidden in your seats, you who dwell in Hazor, says the LORD, for counsel has been devised against you by Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and a plan has been formed against you. ³¹ Arise, go up to a people at ease, dwelling in security, says the LORD, who have no doors or bars, who alone dwell. ³² And their camels will be plunder, their many herds a spoil, and I will bring calamity upon them from every direction, from every side of them, says the LORD. ³³ And Hazor will become a den of jackals, a waste forever; no man will dwell there, and no son of man will live in it. ³⁴ This was the word of the LORD to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, saying: ³⁵ Thus says the LORD of Hosts: I will break the bow of Elam, the first of their might. ³⁶ And I will bring upon Elam four winds from the four corners of the heavens, and I will scatter them to all these winds, and there will not be a nation to which they will not come, the fugitives of Elam. ³⁷ And I will set Elam before their enemies, and before those who seek their life, and I will bring upon them evil, the fury of my anger, says the LORD, and I will send the sword after them until I destroy them. ³⁸ And I will set my throne in Elam and will remove from there king and rulers, says the LORD. ³⁹ And it will be in the latter days I will restore the captivity of Elam, says the LORD.

Notes
- v. 4: "daughter of the torrent valley" is a poetic image based on the Hebrew text.
- v. 11: "I will leave your orphans" is a rephrasing to reflect the Hebrew's emphasis on divine care.
- v. 12: The reference to "the cup" reflects a common biblical metaphor for judgment.
- v. 19: "young man" and "shepherd" are interpretive choices based on the context.
- v. 28: "children of the East" is a general translation of the Hebrew term referring to the descendants of Kedem.
- v. 39: "latter days" is a standard translation for the Hebrew phrase indicating a future restoration.
