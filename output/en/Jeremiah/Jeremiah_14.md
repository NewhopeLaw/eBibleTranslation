# Jeremiah 14

¹ This is what the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah concerning the droughts: ² Judah mourns, and its gates are emptied, its people are darkened in the land, and the cry of Jerusalem has risen, ³ and its leaders have sent their younger men for water. They went to the cisterns, but found no water. They returned their vessels empty. ⁴ The work of the land has perished, because there was no rain. The farmers were shamed, and they covered their heads. ⁵ The deer in the field gave birth and abandoned their young because there was no pasture. ⁶ Wild donkeys stood on the heights. They dragged their breath, their eyes failed, because there was no grass. ⁷ If our sins have risen against us, LORD, do it for your name's sake, for our sins are many before you, for we have sinned against you. ⁸ Hope of Israel, LORD, and you who save in times of trouble — why have you become like a foreigner in the land, like a traveler who turns aside to a lodging? ⁹ Why have you become like a man in dreams, or like a man who cannot save? Yet you are among us, LORD, and your name is called upon us. Do not abandon us. ¹⁰ This is what the LORD says to this people: They have loved to move their feet, and they have not been afraid. The God who brought them will not be pleased with them. Now he will remember their iniquity. ¹¹ And the LORD said to me, Do not pray for this people for good. ¹² For if they fast, I will not hear their plea, and if they offer burnt offerings and sacrifices, I will not be pleased with them. For by sword, famine, and death I will finish them. ¹³ And I said, Ah, Lord, behold, the prophets are prophesying to them, saying, You will not see a sword, and there will be no famine in you. I will give you truth and peace in this land and in this place. ¹⁴ And the LORD said to me, The prophets are prophesying lies in my name. I did not send them, and I did not command them, and I did not speak to them. They see visions of lies, and divinations, and the whims of their hearts. They prophesy lies to you. ¹⁵ Therefore this is what the LORD says about the prophets who prophesy in my name, whom I did not send: They say, Sword and famine will not be in this land. In death they will die, and by famine they will be finished. The prophets themselves will be finished. ¹⁶ And the people to whom they prophesy will be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem by the sword and the famine, and there will be no one to bury them — their women, their sons, and their daughters. I will pour out their evil upon them. ¹⁷ And you will say to them this word: Let your eyes flow with weeping day and night, and let them not cease. For the daughter of my people has been crushed by a great break, by a very grievous wound. ¹⁸ If I go out into the field, behold, the wounded by the sword. And if I enter the city, behold, the suffering from famine. For even the prophet and the priest have gone into a land they do not know. ¹⁹ Have you rejected Judah? Has your soul turned away from Zion? Why have you struck us and there is no healer? We wait for peace, but there is no good. For the time of healing, behold, there is only turmoil. ²⁰ We have known, LORD, our guilt, the iniquity of our ancestors, for we have sinned against you. ²¹ Do not despise for the sake of your name. Do not disgrace the throne of your glory. Remember, do not break your covenant with us. ²² Is there any in the idols of the nations who sends rain? Or if the heavens give their abundance, is it not you, LORD our God? And we wait for you, for you have made all these.

---

Notes
- v. 14: The phrase "visions of lies, and divinations, and the whims of their hearts" is a literal rendering of the Hebrew "חֲזוֹן שֶׁקֶר וְקֶסֶם וֶאֱלִיל וְתַרְמִית לִבָּם" to capture the layered nature of the false prophetic activities.
- v. 22: The reference to "idols of the nations" reflects the Hebrew "בְּהַבְלֵי הַגּוֹיִים," which is rendered as "idols" to align with the context of false gods and the contrast with YHWH.
