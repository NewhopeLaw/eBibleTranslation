# Jeremiah 31

¹ In that time, says the LORD, I will be a God to all the families of Israel, and they will be my people. ² Thus says the LORD: I found a quiet place in the wilderness for those who had been destroyed by the sword, saying to Israel, “Go and do not be destroyed.” ³ The LORD appeared to me from afar, and with an eternal love I have loved you, and so I will draw you with compassion. ⁴ For I will rebuild you, and you, the maiden of Israel, will be rebuilt. Then you will again raise your timbrel and go out in the company of those who rejoice. ⁵ For you will again plant vineyards on the hills of Samaria. You will plant them and enjoy their fruit. ⁶ For there will come a day when they will call out from Mount Ephraim, “Let us go up to Zion, to the LORD our God.” ⁷ For thus says the LORD: Rejoice for Jacob, and let Israel be glad, and let them be exultant at the head of the nations. Let them proclaim aloud and say, “The LORD has saved his people, the remnant of Israel.” ⁸ Behold, I am bringing them from the north, and I will gather them from the ends of the earth, with the lame and the pregnant, together in a great company. They will return here. ⁹ They will come in weeping, and with their pleas I will lead them. I will bring them by streams of water along a straight path where they will not stumble. For I will be a father to Israel, and Ephraim is my firstborn. ¹⁰ Listen, people of the nations, and proclaim it to the distant islands: “The one who scattered Israel will gather him, and will shepherd his flock as a shepherd does his flock.” ¹¹ For the LORD has ransomed Jacob, and will redeem him from hands stronger than his. ¹² And they will come and sing joyfully on the heights of Zion, and they will come to the good things of the LORD, to grain, new wine, and oil, to the flocks and herds. Their lives will be like a well-watered garden, and they will no longer grow weary. ¹³ Then the young women will rejoice in the company of young men, and the old men will be joyful. I will turn their mourning into joy, and I will comfort them and make them rejoice from their sorrow. ¹⁴ I will satisfy the soul of the priests with abundance, and my people will be filled with my goodness, says the LORD. ¹⁵ Thus says the LORD: A voice is heard in Ramah, weeping and mourning, Rachel weeping for her children and refusing to be comforted for her children, because they are no more. ¹⁶ Thus says the LORD: Hush your voice from weeping, and your eyes from tears. For there is a reward for your labor, says the LORD, and they will return from the land of their enemies. ¹⁷ There is hope for your latter days, says the LORD, and your children will return to their boundary. ¹⁸ I have heard Ephraim’s murmurings: “You have disciplined me, and I have learned. I am like a calf that has not been trained. Turn me back, and I will return, for you are the LORD my God.” ¹⁹ After my return I was comforted, and after your forgiveness I repented, ashamed on my cheeks because I bore the reproach of my youth. ²⁰ Beloved son to me is Ephraim, a child of favor. For my word against him was harsh, but I will remember him again. Therefore, my heart yearns for him; I will surely have mercy on him, says the LORD. ²¹ Set up for yourself signposts, make yourself markers. Take your heart and set it on the road. Turn back, O maiden Israel, turn back to your cities. ²² How long will you wander, daughter who is humiliated? For the LORD has created a new thing on the earth: a woman will encircle a man. ²³ Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: In the land of Judah and its cities it will again be said, “Blessed be the LORD of the mountain of justice, the holy hill.” ²⁴ And they will dwell in the cities of Judah and in all the land together, like farmers and shepherds moving in flocks. ²⁵ For I have given drink to the weary soul, and have filled every soul that pined away. ²⁶ Therefore I awoke and looked, and my sleep was sweet to me. ²⁷ Behold, days are coming, says the LORD, and I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with people and with animals. ²⁸ And just as I was watchful over them to tear down, to destroy, to ruin, and to cast away, so I will be watchful over them to build and to plant, says the LORD. ²⁹ In those days they will no longer say, “The fathers ate sour grapes, and the teeth of the children are set on edge.” ³⁰ But each will die for his own sin. Whoever eats sour grapes will have his teeth set on edge. ³¹ Behold, days are coming, says the LORD, and I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah. ³² Not like the covenant I made with their ancestors when I took their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt, which they broke, and I was their master, says the LORD. ³³ But this is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel after those days, says the LORD: I will place my teaching within them, and I will write it on their hearts. And I will be their God, and they will be my people. ³⁴ And they will no longer teach each other, each saying, “Know the LORD,” for all of them will know me, from the least of them to the greatest, says the LORD, for I will forgive their iniquity and I will remember their sin no more. ³⁵ Thus says the LORD, who gives the sun for the light of the day, who ordains the moon and stars for the light of the night, who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar— the name of the LORD of Hosts is his. ³⁶ If these laws depart from before me, says the LORD, then the descendants of Israel will cease to be a nation before me forever. ³⁷ Thus says the LORD: If the heavens above can be measured and the foundations of the earth below searched out, then I will cast away all the descendants of Israel for all their deeds, says the LORD. ³⁸ Behold, days are coming, says the LORD, and the city will be rebuilt for the LORD from Migdol Hananel to the corner gate. ³⁹ And a measuring line will again be stretched out beyond it to the hill of Gareb and will encircle it around to the garbage dump. ⁴⁰ And all the valley of the dead bodies and the garbage dump, with all its fields, as far as the brook Kidron, to the corner of the gate of the horses toward the east, will be sacred to the LORD. It will never again be abandoned or destroyed forever.

---

Notes
- v. 22: The metaphor of a woman encircling a man is a reversal of typical gender roles in the ancient Near East, emphasizing the newness of God's covenant and the restoration of Israel.
- v. 33: "My teaching" (תּוֹרָתִי) is translated as "my teaching" rather than "my law" to reflect its use in a context of covenant renewal and internalization.
- v. 37: The rhetorical hyperbole about the heavens and earth is rendered as a conditional statement to match the Hebrew structure.
