# Jeremiah 23

¹ Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the flock of my pasture, says the LORD. ² Therefore this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says against the shepherds who tend my people: You have scattered my flock and driven them away, and you have not watched over them. I will now bring judgment upon you for the evil of your deeds, says the LORD. ³ I will gather the remnant of my people from all the lands where I have driven them, and I will return them to their pasture, and they will multiply and increase. ⁴ I will set up shepherds over them who will tend them, and they will no longer fear, nor will they be terrified, nor will they be missing, says the LORD. ⁵ Days are coming, says the LORD, when I will raise up for David a righteous shoot, and he will reign as king and will act justly and righteously in the land. ⁶ In his days Judah will be saved and Israel will dwell in safety. This is his name by which they will call him: The LORD Our Righteousness. ⁷ Therefore days are coming, says the LORD, when people will no longer say, "As the LORD lives, who brought the sons of Israel up from the land of Egypt," ⁸ but instead, "As the LORD lives, who brought and led the descendants of the house of Israel from the land of the north and from all the lands where I had driven them. They will live again on their land." ⁹ My heart is broken within me, my bones are trembling, I have become like a man drunk, like one overwhelmed by wine, because of the LORD and because of his holy word. ¹⁰ For the earth is full of adulterers; because of this, the land mourns, the pastures in the wilderness have dried up, and their ways have become evil. ¹¹ Even the prophet and the priest have been unfaithful. In my house I have seen their evil, says the LORD. ¹² Therefore their path will be to them like slippery places in the dark. They will be driven away and fall into them. For I will bring disaster upon them in the year of their punishment, says the LORD. ¹³ In the prophets of Samaria I have seen a fraud: they prophesied by Baal and led Israel astray. ¹⁴ And in the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen a shocking thing: they commit adultery and walk in lies, and many have taken bribes to not turn anyone from his evil way. They have all become to me like Sodom and its inhabitants, like Gomorrah. ¹⁵ Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts says about the prophets: I will feed them with wormwood and give them water of gall, for from the prophets of Jerusalem unfaithfulness has spread throughout the land. ¹⁶ Thus says the LORD of Hosts: Do not listen to the words of the prophets. They are deceiving themselves with visions, speaking from their own hearts and not from the mouth of the LORD. ¹⁷ They say to those who reject the word of the LORD, "Peace be with you," and to all who walk in their own desires, to each who walks in the deceit of his heart, they say, "No evil will come to you." ¹⁸ For who has stood in the council of the LORD to see or hear his word? Who has listened and heard? ¹⁹ Behold, a storm from the LORD, wrath is going out, and a great commotion upon the heads of the wicked. ²⁰ The anger of the LORD will not return until he has done and fulfilled the purposes of his heart, in the latter days you will understand it. ²¹ I did not send the prophets, and they ran. I did not speak to them, and they prophesied. ²² If they had stood in my council and listened to my words, they would have turned my people from their evil ways and from their wicked deeds. ²³ Am I a God near, says the LORD, and not a God far off? ²⁴ If someone hides in secret places, I will not see him, says the LORD. Is it not I who fills the heavens and the earth? says the LORD. ²⁵ I have heard what the prophets, who prophesy in my name, are saying falsely: "I have had a vision, I have had a vision." ²⁶ How long will there be in the hearts of the false prophets, those who prophesy from the desires of their hearts? ²⁷ Those who plan to forget my law in their visions, which they each tell to their neighbor, as their ancestors forgot my name for Baal. ²⁸ The prophet who has a vision, let him speak the vision. And the one with my word, let him speak my word truthfully. What is the chaff compared to the wheat? says the LORD. ²⁹ Is not my word like fire, says the LORD, and like a hammer that breaks a rock in pieces? ³⁰ Therefore I am against the prophets, says the LORD, who steal my words from one another. ³¹ I am against the prophets, says the LORD, who take up their tongues and speak a lie, saying, "A vision, a vision." ³² I am against the prophets of false visions, says the LORD, who tell them and lead my people astray with their lies and their delusions, and I did not send them, nor did I command them. They will bring no benefit to this people, says the LORD. ³³ And if this people or a prophet or a priest asks you, "What is the message of the LORD?" you shall say to them, "What message? I will no longer listen to you," says the LORD. ³⁴ The prophet, the priest, and the people who say, "A message of the LORD," I will punish the man and his house, says the LORD. ³⁵ You shall say to one another, "What did the LORD answer? What did the LORD speak?" ³⁶ Do not remember the message of the LORD anymore, for the message will become a man's word. You have turned the words of the living God, the LORD of Hosts, our God. ³⁷ Say this to the prophet, "What did the LORD answer? What did the LORD speak?" ³⁸ And if you say, "A message of the LORD," then this is what the LORD says because you have said this, "A message of the LORD," I will send to you, saying, "Do not say, 'A message of the LORD.'" ³⁹ Therefore I will take you up and cast you away, and I will abandon you and the city I gave to you and to your ancestors from before me. ⁴⁰ I will give you a lasting disgrace and eternal dishonor that will not be forgotten.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "The LORD Our Righteousness" reflects the Hebrew "YHWH Tsidqenu" (יהוה צדיקנו), which is a proper name for the messianic king.
- v. 26-27: The reference to "Baal" and the forgetting of the LORD's name aligns with a common biblical motif of idolatry and covenant unfaithfulness.
- v. 30-32: The term "prophets of false visions" is used to describe those who fabricate messages and mislead the people, which is a consistent interpretation of the Hebrew text.
