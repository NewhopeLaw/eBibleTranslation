# Jeremiah 12

¹ Righteous are you, O LORD, and I will argue with you, but I will speak of judgments against you. Why does the path of the wicked prosper? Why do those who act treacherously thrive? ² You planted them, and they took root, and they bore fruit. You are near in their mouths, but far from their innermost thoughts. ³ And you, O LORD, know me, you have tested my heart. Let me stand before you like a lamb led to slaughter, and let them consecrate me for the day of my killing. ⁴ How long will the land mourn, and all the vegetation of the field wither because of the evil of those who live in it? The beasts and the birds have all vanished, because they said, "The LORD will not see the end of our ways." ⁵ You have run with swift feet, and they have wearied you. How can you prepare for the horses? In a land of peace you have placed your trust. How will you do in the fury of the Jordan? ⁶ For your brothers and the household of your father have betrayed you. Even they have called after you. Do not trust them, for they speak to you with false words. ⁷ I have abandoned my house, I have forsaken my inheritance. I have given my beloved soul into the hand of her enemies. ⁸ My inheritance has become to me like a lion in the forest. She has made a noise over me with her voice. Therefore, I have hated her. ⁹ Is my inheritance to me a cave of robbers, or a cave surrounding her? Go, gather every beast of the field. Let them come and devour her. ¹⁰ Many shepherds have ruined my vineyard. They have defiled my portion. They have given my desirable portion to the desert, to the wasteland. ¹¹ They have set it as a desolation, a destruction. Because of me it has become a desolation. The whole land is destroyed because there is no one who sets his mind upon it. ¹² On every path in the desert they have come as destroyers. For the sword of the LORD devours from one end of the land to the other. There is no peace for all flesh. ¹³ They have sown wheat and reaped thorns. They have labored but brought no profit. They will be put to shame by their own harvest, by the anger of the LORD. ¹⁴ Thus says the LORD concerning all my evil neighbors who touch the inheritance that I have given to my people, Israel: I will uproot them from their land and cast out the people of Judah from among them. ¹⁵ And it will be after I have cast them out that I will return and have mercy on them, and I will bring each to his inheritance and each to his land. ¹⁶ And it will be, if they learn the way of my people, swearing by my name, "As the LORD lives," just as they taught my people to swear by Baal, then they will be built among my people. ¹⁷ But if they do not listen, I will cast out that people, and they will be destroyed, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "Righteous are you, O LORD" is a rhetorical declaration of God's justice that introduces a challenge from the prophet.
- v. 5: "How can you prepare for the horses" is a metaphor for how the prophet can prepare for conflict or war.
- v. 8: "My inheritance has become to me like a lion in the forest" is a metaphor for how Judah has become dangerous and hostile to the prophet.
- v. 15: "I will return and have mercy on them" reflects God's covenantal faithfulness despite judgment.
- v. 16: "Swearing by my name, 'As the LORD lives'" reflects the covenantal practice of swearing oaths in God's name.
