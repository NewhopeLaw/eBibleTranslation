# Jeremiah 19

¹ This is what the LORD says: Go and buy a clay jar from a potter, and take some of the elders of the people and some of the elders of the priests with you. ² Go out to the Valley of Ben Hinnom that is at the entrance of the Tophet gate, and there proclaim the words I tell you. ³ Say, "Hear the word of the LORD, you kings of Judah and the people of Jerusalem and all who enter through these gates. This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am bringing evil upon this place so that whoever hears of it will wince in horror." ⁴ For they have abandoned me and profaned this place, burning incense to other gods they did not know, neither they nor their ancestors, and the kings of Judah have filled this place with the blood of the innocent. ⁵ They have built high places for Baal to burn their children as offerings — things I did not command, did not speak, and did not intend in my heart. ⁶ Therefore, days are coming, says the LORD, when this place will no longer be called the Valley of Ben Hinnom or the Tophet, but the Valley of Slaughter. ⁷ I will destroy the counsel of Judah and Jerusalem in this place. I will cast them down before their enemies, into the hands of those who seek their lives. I will give their dead to the birds of the sky and the beasts of the land. ⁸ I will make this city a wasteland, a place of mockery. Everyone who passes by it will wince and scoff at all its wounds. ⁹ I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and daughters, each person eating the flesh of their neighbor, in the siege and distress that their enemies and those who seek their lives will impose on them. ¹⁰ You shall break the jar in the presence of the men going with you. ¹¹ And you shall say to them, "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: I will shatter this people and this city just as a potter shatters a clay vessel that can no longer be repaired. They will bury in the Tophet, for no place will be left to bury." ¹² I will do this to the place, says the LORD, and to those who live in it, to make this city like the Tophet. ¹³ The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be like the defiled place — unclean because of all the incense they burned on their rooftops for every host of the sky and poured out libations to other gods. ¹⁴ Then Jeremiah came out from the Tophet, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy. He stood in the courtyard of the house of the LORD and said to all the people, ¹⁵ "This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am bringing upon this city and all its cities everything evil that I have spoken against it, because they hardened their necks to not listen to my words."

---

Notes
- v. 10: The word "break" (שָׁבַר) is rendered consistently with the metaphor in v. 11, where the breaking of the jar symbolizes the destruction of Judah and Jerusalem.
- v. 13: The term "host of the sky" (צְבָא הַשָּׁמַיִם) is used to preserve the poetic and religious context without rendering it as "army of heaven" or similar.
- v. 14: The Septuagint adds "He stood in the courtyard of the house of the LORD," which is not in the Hebrew but is plausible. It is included to maintain narrative flow.
- v. 15: "Hardened their necks" (הִקְשׁוּ אֶת־עָרְפָּם) is a common idiom for stubbornness and rebellion.
