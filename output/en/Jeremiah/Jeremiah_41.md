# Jeremiah 41

¹ In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, from the royal line, came with ten men to Gedaliah at Mizpah. They ate bread together in Mizpah. ² Then Ishmael and the ten men with him rose up and struck Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon had appointed over the land, and killed him. ³ They also killed all the Jews who were with Gedaliah in Mizpah, as well as the Babylonians who had been found there, including the men of war. ⁴ On the second day after Gedaliah's death, no one knew about it. ⁵ Then men from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria, eighty in number—some with shaved beards, some with torn clothes, and others mourning—came carrying grain offerings and incense to bring to the house of the LORD. ⁶ Ishmael went out to meet them as they traveled, weeping as he went. When he met them, he said, "Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam." ⁷ When they came into the city, Ishmael and his men slaughtered them in the middle of the city. ⁸ But ten men among them were found and said to Ishmael, "Do not kill us, for we have hidden treasures in the field—wheat, barley, oil, and honey." So Ishmael let them live and did not kill them among their companions. ⁹ The pit into which Ishmael had thrown all the men he had killed was the same pit that King Asa of Judah had prepared to guard against Baasha of Israel. Ishmael filled it with the slain. ¹⁰ Then Ishmael took all the people left in Mizpah—the daughters of the king and the rest of the people Nebuzaradan the chief executioner had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam—and led them away to cross into the territory of the Ammonites. ¹¹ When Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders with him heard about all the evil Ishmael had done, ¹² they took all their men and went to fight against Ishmael. They found him by the large waters at Gibeon. ¹³ When all the people with Ishmael saw Johanan son of Kareah and the army commanders with him, they were glad. ¹⁴ All the people Ishmael had taken from Mizpah turned back and went to join Johanan son of Kareah. ¹⁵ Ishmael escaped with eight men and fled to the Ammonites. ¹⁶ Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders with him took all the people left from the group Ishmael had taken from Mizpah after he had killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam—soldiers, men, women, children, and eunuchs from Gibeon. ¹⁷ They went with them to Geruth Chimam near Bethlehem, intending to go to Egypt. ¹⁸ They feared the Babylonians, for Ishmael had killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the one the king of Babylon had appointed over the land.

---

Notes  
- v. 9: The reference to "the pit that King Asa of Judah had prepared to guard against Baasha of Israel" is taken from 1 Kings 15:17, which is assumed to be the background.  
- v. 17: "Geruth Chimam" appears to be a place name; the meaning is uncertain. "Near Bethlehem" is a geographical clarification from context but not in the text.  
- v. 9: "Ishmael filled it with the slain" is a natural interpretation of "he filled it with the wounded," though the Hebrew is ambiguous.
