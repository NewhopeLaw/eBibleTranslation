# Jeremiah 28

¹ In that year, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, in the fifth month, Hananiah son of Azur the prophet from Gibeon spoke to me in the house of the LORD in the sight of the priests and all the people, saying: ² Thus says the LORD of Hosts, God of Israel: I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon. ³ In just two years I will return to this place all the vessels of the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took from this place and carried to Babylon. ⁴ And I will return Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, and all the exiles of Judah who went to Babylon, says the LORD, for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon. ⁵ Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Hananiah the prophet in the sight of the priests and in the sight of all the people standing in the house of the LORD. ⁶ And Jeremiah said, "Amen! May the LORD do so, may the LORD confirm your words, that you have prophesied to return the vessels of the house of the LORD and all the exiles from Babylon to this place." ⁷ But now listen to this word that I am speaking to you in your ears and in the ears of all the people: ⁸ The prophets who were before me and before you, from ancient times, prophesied to many lands and great kingdoms — in war, in evil, and in speaking. ⁹ The prophet who prophesies peace, when the word of the prophet comes, the prophet will be known — the one whom the LORD has sent in truth. ¹⁰ Then Hananiah the prophet took the yoke from off the neck of Jeremiah the prophet and broke it. ¹¹ And he said in the sight of all the people, "Thus says the LORD: In this way I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the neck of all the nations." And Jeremiah the prophet went on his way. ¹² Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah after Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke from off the neck of Jeremiah the prophet, saying: ¹³ "Go and say to Hananiah, Thus says the LORD: You have broken wooden yokes, but I will make iron yokes in their place. ¹⁴ For thus says the LORD of Hosts, God of Israel: I have placed an iron yoke on the neck of all these nations, to serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and they will serve him. And I have given the beasts of the field to him." ¹⁵ Then Jeremiah the prophet said to Hananiah the prophet, "Listen, Hananiah, the LORD has not sent you, and you have led this people to trust in falsehood. ¹⁶ Therefore thus says the LORD: I will remove you from the face of the earth this year. You will die, because you have spoken falsely to the LORD." ¹⁷ And Hananiah the prophet died the same year in the seventh month.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "took the yoke from off the neck of Jeremiah" — the Hebrew uses the word מוֹטָה (mōṭāh), often translated as "yoke" or "bar," here referring to a wooden bar or yoke placed on the neck.
- v. 13: "You have broken wooden yokes" — מוֹטֹת עֵץ (mōṭōt ʿêṣ) is translated as "wooden yokes" to distinguish from the iron yokes mentioned in v. 14.
- v. 16: "I will remove you from the face of the earth" — the Hebrew uses the phrase מֵעַל פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה (mēʿal pənê hāʾădāmāh), which is a strong phrase indicating removal or death.
