# Jeremiah 32

¹ The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, this was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon. ² At that time the army of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and the prophet Jeremiah was confined in the courtyard of the guard, which was in the palace of the king of Judah. ³ For Zedekiah king of Judah had put him there, saying, "Why are you prophesying like this? You say, 'This is what the LORD says: Here I am handing this city over to the king of Babylon, and it will be captured.'" ⁴ And Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape from the hands of the Chaldeans, but he will certainly be handed over to the king of Babylon. His mouth will speak with the king's mouth, and his eyes will see the king's eyes. ⁵ Then he will be taken to Babylon and there remain until I visit him, says the LORD. And though you fight against the Chaldeans, you will not succeed. ⁶ Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying: ⁷ "Behold, Hanamel the son of your uncle is coming to you. He will say to you, 'Buy for yourself my field that is in Anathoth, for you have the right of redemption to buy it.'" ⁸ So Hanamel the son of my uncle came to me in the courtyard of the guard according to the word of the LORD, and said to me, "Buy for yourself my field that is in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin. You have the right to redeem it and to claim it. You must buy it." And I knew that this word of the LORD was true. ⁹ I bought the field from Hanamel the son of my uncle and weighed out for him the silver seven shekels and ten shekels of silver. ¹⁰ I wrote the deed of purchase and signed it, and I called witnesses and weighed the silver on the scales. ¹¹ I took the deed of purchase, the sealed copy, and the public document. ¹² I gave the deed of purchase to Baruch the son of Neriah the son of Mahseiah in the presence of Hanamel the son of my uncle and in the presence of the witnesses who had signed the deed of purchase, and in the presence of all the Jews sitting in the courtyard of the guard. ¹³ Then I gave instructions to Baruch in their presence, saying: ¹⁴ "This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, Take this deed of purchase, this sealed copy, and this public document, and put them in an earthen jar so that they may last for many days." ¹⁵ For this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Houses, fields, and vineyards will again be bought in this land. ¹⁶ Then I prayed to the LORD after giving the deed of purchase to Baruch the son of Neriah, saying: ¹⁷ "Ah, Lord YHWH! Look, you made the heavens and the earth by your great power and your outstretched arm. Nothing is too difficult for you. ¹⁸ You show steadfast love to a thousand generations and repay the guilt of the fathers to their children in their bosom. You are the great and mighty God, YHWH. ¹⁹ Great in wisdom and skillful in planning, your eyes are open to all the paths of the sons of men, giving each one according to his way and according to the fruit of his deeds. ²⁰ You placed signs and wonders in the land of Egypt until this day, and in Israel and among men. You made a name for yourself as it is today. ²¹ You brought your people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs and wonders, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with great terror. ²² And you gave them this land, which you swore to their fathers to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey. ²³ They came and took possession of it, but they did not listen to your voice. They did not follow your teaching. All that you commanded them they did not do. And you brought all this evil upon them. ²⁴ Look, the siege ramps have come against the city to capture it, and the city has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, by the sword, by famine, and by plague. And what you spoke has happened, and you see it. ²⁵ But you said to me, 'Buy for yourself the field with silver and get witnesses. And indeed, the city is given into the hands of the Chaldeans.'" ²⁶ Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying: ²⁷ "I am the LORD, the God of all flesh. Is anything too difficult for me? ²⁸ Therefore this is what the LORD says: I am about to give this city into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it. ²⁹ The Chaldeans who are fighting against this city will come in, set it on fire, and burn it, along with the houses on whose rooftops they burned incense to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, to provoke me to anger. ³⁰ For the people of Israel and the people of Judah have done nothing but evil in my sight from their youth. The people of Israel have only provoked me by their works," says the LORD. ³¹ "This city has been a provocation to me from the day it was built until this day, so that I must remove it from before me. ³² For all the evil that the people of Israel and the people of Judah have done to provoke me— their kings and their rulers, their priests and their prophets, and the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem— ³³ they have turned their backs to me and not their faces. Though I taught them early and often, they would not listen to take correction. ³⁴ They have placed their abominations in the house that is called by my name, defiling it. ³⁵ They have built high places for Baal in the valley of Ben Hinnom to sacrifice their sons and daughters to Molech, which I did not command and which did not come into my mind to do this abomination, to make Judah sin. ³⁶ But now this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says concerning this city, which you say is given into the hands of the king of Babylon by the sword, by famine, and by plague: ³⁷ I will surely gather them from all the lands where I have driven them in my anger and my wrath and in great rage. I will return them to this place and settle them in safety. ³⁸ And they will be my people, and I will be their God. ³⁹ I will give them a single heart and a new way, to fear me for all the days to their good and to the good of their children after them. ⁴⁰ And I will make an everlasting covenant with them, which I will not turn back from, to do good to them. I will put my fear in their hearts so that they will not turn away from me. ⁴¹ And I will rejoice over them to do good to them, and I will plant them in this land faithfully, with all my heart and with all my soul. ⁴² For this is what the LORD says: Just as I have brought all the great evil upon this people, so I will bring all the good that I speak about upon them. ⁴³ And fields will again be bought in this land, which you say is waste, without people or animals, given into the hands of the Chaldeans. ⁴⁴ Fields will be bought with silver, and written in a book, sealed, and witnessed in the land of Benjamin and around Jerusalem, in the cities of Judah, in the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the lowland, and in the cities of the Negev, for I will restore their captivity," says the LORD.

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "the tenth year of Zedekiah" is translated as a dependent clause for clarity, but could be interpreted as "the tenth year of Zedekiah's reign."
- v. 17: "YHWH" is retained in the prayer to emphasize the divine name.
- v. 36: "This city" refers to Jerusalem, but could also be interpreted as metaphorical for the nation.
- v. 43: "Abandon" and "waste" are used to reflect the Hebrew "shemamah," which conveys desolation.
