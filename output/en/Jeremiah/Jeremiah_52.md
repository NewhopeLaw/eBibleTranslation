# Jeremiah 52

¹ Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah. ² He did evil in the sight of the Lord, just as Jehoiakim had done. ³ For the anger of the Lord had been kindled against Jerusalem and Judah until he cast them out from his presence. Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon. ⁴ Now in the seventh year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon came with all his army against Jerusalem. They encamped around it and built a siege wall against it all around. ⁵ The city was under siege until the twelfth year of King Zedekiah. ⁶ In the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the famine in the city became severe, and there was no food for the people of the land. ⁷ The city was broken into, and all the men of war fled by night through the gate between the two walls, which was near the king's garden, while the Chaldeans were all around the city. They went by the way of the Arabah. ⁸ The army of the Chaldeans pursued the king and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho. His entire army was scattered from him. ⁹ They captured the king and brought him up to the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath. He passed judgment on him. ¹⁰ The king of Babylon slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, and he slaughtered all the officials of Judah at Riblah. ¹¹ He put out Zedekiah's eyes, and the king of Babylon bound him in chains and led him to Babylon. He put him in prison until the day of his death. ¹² In the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month—this was the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon—Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard, who served the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem. ¹³ He burned the house of the Lord, the king's house, and all the houses of Jerusalem. Every great house he burned with fire. ¹⁴ He broke down all the walls of Jerusalem all around, as far as the army of the Chaldeans that was with the captain of the guard. ¹⁵ The captain of the guard carried off the common people who remained in the city, the rest of the fugitives, and those who had defected to the king of Babylon, along with the rest of the people left in the city. ¹⁶ Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard left some of the poor of the land as vinedressers and farmers. ¹⁷ The pillars of bronze that were in the house of the Lord, the stands and the bronze Sea that were in the house of the Lord, the Chaldeans broke and carried all the bronze to Babylon. ¹⁸ They took the pots, the shovels, the meat hooks, the forks, the bowls, and all the bronze vessels used in the service. ¹⁹ They took the basins, the firepans, the bowls, the pots, the lampstands, the altars, and the cups, whether of gold or silver, and carried them all to Babylon. ²⁰ The two pillars, the one Sea, and the twelve bronze bulls under it, which King Solomon had made for the house of the Lord—there was no weight for all the bronze of these vessels. ²¹ Each pillar was eighteen cubits high, and the height of the pillar was twelve cubits, with a circumference of twelve cubits. Its thickness was four fingers, and it was hollow. ²² There was a capital of bronze on top, five cubits high, with a network and pomegranates all around the capital. All of these were of bronze. Likewise, the second pillar had a network and pomegranates. ²³ The pomegranates were ninety-six in number, and all the pomegranates around the network were a hundred in all. ²⁴ The captain of the guard took Seraiah the high priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and the three gatekeepers. ²⁵ From the city he took one eunuch who was over the men of war, and seven men of the king's court who were found in the city, and the scribe, the chief of the army who was responsible for mustering the people of the land, and sixty men of the people from the city. ²⁶ Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard took them and led them to the king of Babylon at Riblah. ²⁷ The king of Babylon slaughtered them at Riblah in the land of Hamath. And Judah was carried away from its land. ²⁸ This was the number of the people Nebuchadrezzar carried away in the seventh year: 3,023 Judahites. ²⁹ In the eighth year of his reign, he took from Jerusalem 832 people. ³⁰ In the twenty-third year of his reign, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard took from Jerusalem 745 people. All the people carried away in his years totaled 4,600. ³¹ In the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiachin king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-fifth day of the month, Evil-merodach king of Babylon, in the year of his reign, released Jehoiachin from prison. ³² He spoke kindly to him and set his seat above the seats of the other kings who were with him in Babylon. ³³ He changed his prison clothes, and he ate at his table regularly all the days of his life. ³⁴ His daily ration was given to him by the king of Babylon, day by day, all the days of his life until the day of his death.

Notes
- v. 1: "Libnah" is a transliteration of the Hebrew, which is retained as per the proper-name table.
- v. 20: "There was no weight for all the bronze of these vessels" reflects the Hebrew phrase "לֹא־הָיָה מִשְׁקָל לִנְחֻשְׁתָּם כָּל־הַכֵּלִים הָאֵֽלֶּה" — literally, "there was no weight to their bronze, all these vessels."
- v. 31: "Evil-merodach" follows the Greek spelling but is consistent with the Hebrew context.
