# 1 Chronicles 27

¹ The Israelites, according to their numbers, had leaders of the fathers and commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, and the officers who served the king for every matter of the divisions, going out and coming in, each month throughout the year. One division numbered twenty-four thousand. ² Over the first division for the first month was Ben-Zabdiel, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ³ From the sons of Perez, a leader for all the commanders of the forces for the first month. ⁴ Over the second division for the second month was Dodai the Ahohite, and his division and the commander of the division was with him, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ⁵ The third commander of the forces for the third month was Benaiah the son of Jehoiada the priest, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ⁶ Benaiah was the leader of the thirty, and over the thirty was Amizabad his son. ⁷ The fourth for the fourth month was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Zabad his son, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ⁸ The fifth for the fifth month was the commander Shammoth the Izharite, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ⁹ The sixth for the sixth month was Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹⁰ The seventh for the seventh month was Helez the Pelonite, from the sons of Ephraim, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹¹ The eighth for the eighth month was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zarechites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹² The ninth for the ninth month was Abiezer the Anathothite, of the right hand of the Benjamites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹³ The tenth for the tenth month was Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zarechites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹⁴ The eleventh for the eleventh month was Benaiah the Perethite of the sons of Ephraim, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹⁵ The twelfth for the twelfth month was Heldai the Netophathite of Ethanaiel, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹⁶ Over the tribes of Israel: the Reubenite was Eleazar the son of Zicri; the Simeonite was Shaphatiah the son of Maacah. ¹⁷ The Levite was Hashavyahu the son of Kemuel; the Aaronite was Zadok. ¹⁸ The Judahite was Elihu, one of David’s brothers; the Issacharite was Ammari the son of Mechai. ¹⁹ The Zebulunite was Ishmaiah the son of Obadiah; the Naphtaliite was Jerimoth the son of Azariah. ²⁰ The Ephraimite was Hoshea the son of Azaziah; the half-tribe of Manasseh was Joel the son of Pedaiah. ²¹ The half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead was Jediah the son of Zichri; the Benjaminite was Yaasiah the son of Abner. ²² The Danite was Azareel the son of Jeroham. These were the leaders of the tribes of Israel. ²³ David did not count them from twenty years old and under, for the Lord had said to multiply Israel as the stars of the heavens. ²⁴ Joab the son of Zeruiah began to count, but did not finish. And in this matter, wrath came upon Israel, and the count was not recorded in the book of the words of the days of King David. ²⁵ Over the treasures of the king was Azmaveth the son of Adiel; over the treasures in the fields, in the towns, in the villages, and in the towers was Jehonathan the son of Uzziel. ²⁶ Over the workers who tilled the land was Ezri the son of Kelo. ²⁷ Over the vineyards was Shimei the Ramathite; over the storehouses of wine was Zabdi the Shiphmite. ²⁸ Over the olive groves and the sycamore trees in the lowlands was Balhan the Gederite; over the storehouses of oil was Yo'ash. ²⁹ Over the cattle in the Sharon was Shertai the Sharonite; over the cattle in the valleys was Shaphat the son of Adlai. ³⁰ Over the camels was Obil the Ishmaelite; over the donkeys was Jehdeiah the Meronethite. ³¹ Over the flocks was Yaziz the Hargite. All of these were the overseers of the possessions of King David. ³² And Jonath the brother of David was a counselor, a man of understanding, and a scribe, and Jehiel the son of Hachmoni with the sons of the king. ³³ Ahithophel was the king’s counselor; and Hushai the Archite was the king’s companion. ³⁴ After Ahithophel came Jehoyada the son of Benaiah, and Abiathar, and Joab was the commander of the army of the king.

Notes
- v. 2: "Ben-Zabdiel" is a constructed form from the name "benei Zabdiel" (sons of Zabdiel), as the text reads "benei Zabdiel" but the function is singular leadership. 
- v. 7: "Asahel the brother of Joab" is inferred from the text "Asahel the brother of Joab and Zabad his son" to clarify the familial relationship.
- v. 23: "from twenty years old and under" reflects the Hebrew phrase "l’miyn ben esrim shanah umat'ah" which is typically understood as excluding those under twenty. 
- v. 24: "the count was not recorded in the book of the words of the days of King David" reflects the phrase "lo kalel et hamispar be-sefer divre hayamim lemelech David" and is a direct translation of the expression.
