# 1 Chronicles 9

¹ All Israel had been recorded, and they were written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah who had been taken to Babylon in their wrongdoing. ² The ones who had lived earlier in their inherited lands in their cities were Israelites, the priests, the Levites, and the temple servants. ³ And in Jerusalem, some had settled from the descendants of Judah, from the descendants of Benjamin, and from the descendants of Ephraim and Manasseh. ⁴ Uthai the son of Ammihud the son of Amri the son of Imri the son of Bani, from the descendants of Peretz the son of Judah. ⁵ And from the Silonites, Asaiah the firstborn and his sons. ⁶ And from the descendants of Zerah, Jehu and his brothers, six hundred and ninety in all. ⁷ And from the descendants of Benjamin, Salu the son of Meshullam the son of Hodaviah the son of Hasenuah. ⁸ And Yibniah the son of Yeroham, and Elah the son of Uzzi the son of Micri, and Meshullam the son of Shephatiah the son of Reuuel the son of Yibniyah. ⁹ And their brothers by their generations, nine hundred and fifty-six in all. These were the men, the heads of their ancestral houses. ¹⁰ And from the priests, Yedaiah, Yehoyarib, and Yachin. ¹¹ And Azariah the son of Hilkiyah the son of Meshullam the son of Tsadok the son of Meravoth the son of Achituv, the chief of the house of God. ¹² And Addaiyah the son of Yeroham the son of Pashchur the son of Malkiyah, and Measai the son of Addiah the son of Yachzerah the son of Meshullam the son of Meshilmeith the son of Imir. ¹³ And their brothers, the heads of the houses of their ancestors, one thousand seven hundred and sixty strong, mighty men of valor, doing the work of the service of the house of God. ¹⁴ And from the Levites, Shemaiah the son of Hashub the son of Ezriqam the son of Hashavyah, from the descendants of Merari. ¹⁵ And Bakqar, Chalal, and Mattanyahu the son of Michai the son of Zikri the son of Asaph. ¹⁶ And Obadyah the son of Shemaiah the son of Galal the son of Yedutun, and Berekhyah the son of Asa the son of Elkhanah the one living in the villages of Netophathi. ¹⁷ And the gatekeepers were Shalum, Akuv, Talmon, and Ahiman, and their brothers: Shalum was the head. ¹⁸ And from this point at the gate of the king toward the east were the Levitical gatekeepers at the camps of the sons of Levi. ¹⁹ And Shalum the son of Korah the son of Aviyasaf the son of Korach, and his brothers in the house of their father the Korahites, were responsible for guarding the thresholds of the tent; their ancestors had been in charge of guarding the entrance of the camp of the LORD. ²⁰ And Phinehas the son of Eleazar was the chief over them, standing before the LORD with them. ²¹ Zachariah the son of Meshalmeyah was the gatekeeper of the threshold of the tent of meeting. ²² All of them were chosen for the gatekeeping roles, two hundred and twelve in total. These had been recorded in their courts. David and Samuel the seer had set them in place, trusting in their faithfulness. ²³ And they and their sons were stationed at the gates of the house of the LORD, at the house of the tent, to guard it. ²⁴ They were stationed at the four quarters: east, west, north, and south. ²⁵ And their brothers in their courts were to come in with them every seven days, at times. ²⁶ For they were faithful, four strong gatekeepers. The Levites were in charge of the storerooms and the treasures of the house of God. ²⁷ They had the responsibility of guarding it, and they were in charge of the keys, opening the doors of the temple each morning. ²⁸ And from among them were in charge of the service equipment, for they brought them in by number and took them out by number. ²⁹ And from among them were overseers of the equipment and all the sacred items, and the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices. ³⁰ And from among the priests were the mixers of the incense and the spices. ³¹ And Mattityah the Levite was the firstborn of Shalum the Korahite, and he was in charge of the work of the great altar of the high priest in faithfulness. ³² And Banaiyahu the son of the Kehathite, from among his brothers, was in charge of the showbread, to prepare it Sabbath by Sabbath. ³³ And these were the musicians, the heads of the Levitical families, assigned to their shifts, for they were on duty day and night in their work. ³⁴ These were the heads of the ancestral houses of the Levites by their generations. These were the ones who lived in Jerusalem. ³⁵ And in Gibeon, Abi Gibeon lived, and the name of his wife was Maacah. ³⁶ And his firstborn son was Abdon, and Tsor, and Kish, and Baal, and Ner, and Nadav. ³⁷ And Gedor and his brother, and Zacharyah and Miklot. ³⁸ And Miklot fathered Shimam. And they too lived opposite their brothers in Jerusalem, among their brothers. ³⁹ And Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadav, and Eshbaal. ⁴⁰ And the son of Jonathan was Meribbaal, and Meribbaal fathered Michah. ⁴¹ And the sons of Michah were Piton, Melech, and Tachrea. ⁴² And Ahaz fathered Yaar, and Yaar fathered Almet, and Azmaveth, and Zimri, and Zimri fathered Moza. ⁴³ And Moza fathered Binea, and Rapha was his son, and Ela was his son, and Atzel was his son. ⁴⁴ And Atzel had six sons, and these were their names: Ezriqam, the firstborn, Ishmael, Sheraiah, Obadyah, and Chanah. These were the sons of Atzel.
