# 1 Chronicles 1

¹ Adam Seth Enosh ² Cainan Mahalalel Jared ³ Enoch Methuselah Lamech ⁴ Noah Shem Ham and Japheth ⁵ The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ⁶ The sons of Gomer were Ashkenaz, Diphath, and Togarmah. ⁷ The sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim. ⁸ The sons of Ham were Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan. ⁹ The sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan. ¹⁰ Cush fathered Nimrod, the one who began to be mighty in the land. ¹¹ Egypt fathered Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphthuhim, ¹² Pathrusim, Casluhim (from whom the Philistines emerged), and Caphtorim. ¹³ Canaan fathered Sidon his firstborn, and Heth. ¹⁴ He also fathered the Jebusites, the Amorites, and the Girgashites. ¹⁵ The Hivites, the Arkites, and the Sinites. ¹⁶ The Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. ¹⁷ The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. Also, Utz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. ¹⁸ Arphaxad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber. ¹⁹ To Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided, and the name of his brother was Joktan. ²⁰ Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah. ²¹ He also fathered Hadarom, Uzal, and Diklah. ²² Ebal, Abimael, and Sheba. ²³ Ofir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan. ²⁴ Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah. ²⁵ Eber, Peleg, Reu. ²⁶ Serug, Nahor, Terah. ²⁷ Abram, who is Abraham. ²⁸ The sons of Abraham were Isaac and Ishmael. ²⁹ These are their descendants: Ishmael's firstborn was Nebaioth, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mishma. ³⁰ Dumah, Masah, Hadrach, Tema. ³¹ Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These were the sons of Ishmael. ³² The sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine, were Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan were Sheba and Dedan. ³³ The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldadah. All these were the sons of Keturah. ³⁴ Abraham fathered Isaac. The sons of Isaac were Esau and Israel. ³⁵ The sons of Esau were Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korach. ³⁶ The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek. ³⁷ The sons of Reuel were Nahath, Zerah, Shamma, and Mizzah. ³⁸ The sons of Seir were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan. ³⁹ The sons of Lotan were Horim and Homan. His sister was Timna. ⁴⁰ The sons of Shobal were Alvan, Manachath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon were Aiah and Anah. ⁴¹ The sons of Anah were Dishon. The sons of Dishon were Hamran, Eshban, Itheran, and Keran. ⁴² The sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Jaakan. The sons of Dishon were Uz and Aran. ⁴³ These were the kings who ruled in the land of Edom before any king ruled the children of Israel: Bela, son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah. ⁴⁴ Bela died, and Jobab, son of Zerah from Bozrah, ruled in his place. ⁴⁵ Jobab died, and Husham, from the land of the Temanites, ruled in his place. ⁴⁶ Husham died, and Hadad, son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the field of Moab, ruled in his place. The name of his city was Avith. ⁴⁷ Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah ruled in his place. ⁴⁸ Samlah died, and Saul from the land of the River ruled in his place. ⁴⁹ Saul died, and Baal-Hanan, son of Achbor, ruled in his place. ⁵⁰ Baal-Hanan died, and Hadad ruled in his place. The name of his city was Paei, and the name of his wife was Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezah. ⁵¹ Hadad died, and these were the chieftains of Edom: Chieftain Teman, Chieftain Alvah, Chieftain Jetheth, ⁵² Chieftain Oholibamah, Chieftain Elah, Chieftain Pinon, ⁵³ Chieftain Kenaz, Chieftain Teman, Chieftain Mibzar, ⁵⁴ Chieftain Magdiel, Chieftain Iram. These were the chieftains of Edom.

Notes  
- v. 34: The Hebrew text lists Israel as the second son of Isaac, aligning with the narrative of Jacob being renamed Israel, though the text here may reflect a later editorial addition.  
- v. 50: "Mehetabel" is preserved in the name table as a proper name, with the phrase "daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezah" preserving the Hebrew genealogical structure.  
- v. 51–54: The listing of chieftains follows the structure of the Hebrew text, which includes multiple chieftainships but does not specify their relationships or reigns.
