# 1 Chronicles 17

¹ Now when David was living in his house, he said to the prophet Nathan, "Look, I am living in a cedar house, but the ark of the covenant of the LORD is under tent cloths." ² Nathan replied to David, "Do whatever is in your heart, for God is with you." ³ But that night the word of the LORD came to Nathan, saying: ⁴ "Go and tell my servant David, 'This is what the LORD says: You will not build me a house to live in. ⁵ For I have not lived in a house since the day I brought Israel up, even until now. I have moved from tent to tent and from booth to booth. ⁶ In all the places I have walked among all Israel, did I ever speak to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd my people, asking, 'Why have you not built me a cedar house?' ⁷ Now then, tell my servant David, 'This is what the LORD of Hosts says: I have taken you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be a leader over my people Israel. ⁸ I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have removed all your enemies from before you. I will make your name great, like the great names on the earth. ⁹ I will provide a place for my people Israel and plant them there, so that they may dwell securely and no longer be disturbed. The wicked will no longer oppress them, as they were in the beginning. ¹⁰ From the time I appointed judges over my people Israel, I have given them rest from all their enemies. And now the LORD declares to you that he will build you a house. ¹¹ When your time is fulfilled and you go to rest with your ancestors, I will raise up your offspring after you, one of your own sons, and I will establish his kingdom. ¹² He will build me a house, and I will establish his throne forever. ¹³ I will be his father, and he will be my son. My steadfast love will not be withheld from him, as it was from those who were before him. ¹⁴ I will fix him in my house and in my kingdom forever, and his throne will be established forever.' ¹⁵ Nathan told David all the words of this vision. ¹⁶ Then the king David went in and sat before the LORD and said, "Who am I, O LORD my God, and what is my family, that you have brought me to this point? ¹⁷ And yet in your sight, O God, this was small—a thing of little account—that you spoke about the house of your servant, and you have looked upon me as one who looks from a distance. ¹⁸ What more can David say to you? You know your servant, O LORD God. ¹⁹ For the sake of your promise and according to your heart, you have done this great thing to reveal your greatness. ²⁰ O LORD, there is no one like you, and there is no other God besides you, according to all we have heard with our ears. ²¹ And who is like your people Israel, a unique nation on the earth, whom God went forth to redeem for himself as a people, to make you a name of great and awesome deeds, to drive out nations before your people whom you redeemed from Egypt? ²² You have given your people Israel to be your people forever, and you, O LORD, have become their God. ²³ Now then, O LORD, let the word you have spoken about your servant and his house be confirmed forever, and do as you promised. ²⁴ May your name be confirmed and magnified forever, and people will say, 'The LORD of Hosts is the God of Israel; he is the God of Israel, and the house of David, your servant, is established before you.' ²⁵ For you, O LORD my God, have given your servant a heart to build a house for you. So your servant has found the courage to pray before you. ²⁶ Now then, O LORD, you are the God, and you have spoken these good things about your servant. ²⁷ Now you have begun to bless the house of your servant, so that it may endure forever before you. For you, O LORD, have blessed, and may you bless it forevermore."

---

Notes
- v. 17: "as one who looks from a distance" — captures the Hebrew metaphor of "as a vision from afar" (ὡς ὅρασις ἀνθρώπου καὶ ὕψωσάς με in LXX), but rendered in natural English.
