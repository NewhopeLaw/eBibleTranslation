# 1 Chronicles 5

¹ The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel—though he was the firstborn, by reason of the defilement of his father's bed, the right of the firstborn passed to the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel, so that he did not receive the firstborn's share. ² For Judah prevailed over his brothers, and the leader emerged from him, though the right of the firstborn belonged to Joseph. ³ The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. ⁴ The sons of Joel were Shema, his son, Gog, his son, Shimei, his son. ⁵ Micah, his son, Reaiah, his son, Baal, his son. ⁶ Beerah, his son, whom Tilgath-pilneser, king of Assyria, carried away. He was the prince of the Reubenites. ⁷ His brothers were recorded by families in their genealogies: the head was Jeiel, and Zechariah. ⁸ Bela, son of Azaz, son of Shema, son of Joel, who lived in Aroer and all along to Nebo and Baal Meon. ⁹ And to the east, he lived as far as the edge of the desert toward the Euphrates River, because their herds were numerous in the land of Gilead. ¹⁰ In the days of Saul they made war with the Hagrites, and they fell by their hand, and they dwelled in their tents throughout all the east of Gilead. ¹¹ The sons of Gad dwelled opposite them in the land of Bashan, as far as Salcah. ¹² Joel was the head, and Shapham the second, and Jaanai and Shaphat in Bashan. ¹³ Their brothers by their families were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jachan, Zakkai, and Eber—seven in all. ¹⁴ These were the sons of Abihail, son of Hur, son of Jaroah, son of Gilad, son of Michael, son of Jeshishai, son of Jaad, son of Buz. ¹⁵ Ahie, son of Abdial, son of Gunni, was the head of the house of their fathers. ¹⁶ And they dwelled in Gilead, in Bashan and in all their villages, and in all the outposts of Sharon, wherever their territory extended. ¹⁷ All of them were recorded in the days of Jotham, king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam, king of Israel. ¹⁸ The sons of Reuben and Gad and half the tribe of Manasseh were warriors, men bearing shields and swords, and skilled with the bow, trained for war: forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty who went out to battle. ¹⁹ And they made war with the Hagrites, with the Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab. ²⁰ They attacked them and struck them, and the Hagrites with all their tents handed themselves over to them, for they cried to God in the battle, and He answered them, because they trusted in Him. ²¹ They captured their goods: five thousand camels, two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys, and a hundred thousand persons. ²² For many slain fell, because the battle was from God. And they dwelled in their place until the exile. ²³ And the sons of half the tribe of Manasseh dwelled in the land from Bashan as far as Baal Hermon, and to Senir and Mount Hermon. And they multiplied greatly. ²⁴ These were the heads of their families: Ephraim, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel—men of strength, men of renown, leaders of the house of their fathers. ²⁵ But they acted wickedly toward the God of their fathers and lusted after the gods of the peoples around them, whom God had destroyed before them. ²⁶ And the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul, king of Assyria, and the spirit of Tilgath-pilneser, king of Assyria, and he carried them away—Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh—to Halah, Habor, the River Gozan, and to the present day. ²⁷ The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. ²⁸ And the sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. ²⁹ And the sons of Amram were Aaron and Moses and Miriam. And the sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. ³⁰ Eleazar fathered Phinehas. Phinehas fathered Abishua. ³¹ Abishua fathered Bukki. Bukki fathered Uzi. ³² Uzi fathered Zerahiah. Zerahiah fathered Meriyahu. ³³ Meriyahu fathered Amariah. Amariah fathered Ahitub. ³⁴ Ahitub fathered Zadok. Zadok fathered Ahimaaz. ³⁵ Ahimaaz fathered Azariah. Azariah fathered Johanan. ³⁶ Johanan fathered Azariah, who was the priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem. ³⁷ Azariah fathered Amariah. Amariah fathered Ahitub. ³⁸ Ahitub fathered Zadok. Zadok fathered Shalum. ³⁹ Shalum fathered Hilkiah. Hilkiah fathered Azariah. ⁴⁰ Azariah fathered Seraiah. Seraiah fathered Jehozadak. ⁴¹ And Jehozadak went into exile when the Lord carried away Judah and Jerusalem before Nebuchadnezzar.

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "by reason of the defilement of his father's bed" reflects the Hebrew text's reference to Reuben's transgression involving his father's concubine (Genesis 35:22).
- v. 10: "They fell by their hand" is a natural rendering of the Hebrew expression for being defeated and captured.
- v. 26: "Go down to Halah, Habor, the River Gozan" follows the Septuagint's reading, though the Hebrew text here is ambiguous. Given the Septuagint's clarity and the historical context, the reference to Halah, Habor, and the River Gozan is adopted.
- v. 36: The phrase "who was the priest in the house that Solomon built" is added to clarify the reference to Azariah, avoiding confusion with other Azariahs.
