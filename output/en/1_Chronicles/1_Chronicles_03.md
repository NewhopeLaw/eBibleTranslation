# 1 Chronicles 3

¹ These were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn, Amnon, the son of Ahinoam the Izzrahite; the second, Daniel, the son of Abigail the Carmelite; ² the third, Absalom, the son of Maacah, daughter of Talmai, king of Geshur; the fourth, Adonijah, the son of Haggith; ³ the fifth, Shephatiah, the son of Abital; the sixth, Ithream, the son of his wife Egla. ⁴ Six children were born to him in Hebron, where he reigned for seven years and six months. In Jerusalem he reigned for thirty-three years. ⁵ The following were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon—four, the sons of Bath-shua, daughter of Ammiel. ⁶ Also born to him were: Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphelet, ⁷ Noga, Neve, and Japhia, ⁸ Elishama, Eliadah, and Eliphelet—nine in total. ⁹ These were all the sons of David, apart from the sons of his concubines. His sister was Tamar. ¹⁰ Solomon’s son was Rehoboam, whose son was Abijah, whose son was Asa, whose son was Jehoshaphat, ¹¹ whose son was Joram, whose son was Uzziah, whose son was Joash, ¹² whose son was Amaziah, whose son was Azariah, whose son was Jotham, ¹³ whose son was Ahaz, whose son was Hezekiah, whose son was Manasseh, ¹⁴ whose son was Amon, whose son was Josiah. ¹⁵ The sons of Josiah were: the firstborn, Johanan; the second, Jehoiakim; the third, Zedekiah; the fourth, Salum. ¹⁶ The sons of Jehoiakim were: Jeconiah, his son; Zedekiah, his son. ¹⁷ The sons of Jeconiah were: Assir, Shalathiel, his son; ¹⁸ Melchiiram, Pedaias, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedayah. ¹⁹ The sons of Shalathiel were: Zerubbabel, Meshullam, Hananiah, and Shimeath, his sister. ²⁰ Hashub, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Yushab hesed—five in total. ²¹ The son of Hananiah was Phalti and Yishaiah. These were the sons of Rehoboam’s descendants: the sons of Arnun, the sons of Obadyah, the sons of Sekhanyahu. ²² The sons of Sekhanyahu were: Shemaiah, and the sons of Shemaiah were: Chatath, Yigal, Bariah, Nearyah, and Shefat—six in total. ²³ The son of Nearyah was Elioenai, Hezekiah, and Ezerikam—three in total. ²⁴ The sons of Elioenai were: Hodavyahu, Eliashiv, Phalyah, Akub, Johanan, Deliyahu, and Anani—seven in total.

Notes
- v. 9: "His sister was Tamar" — the Hebrew uses "tamar" (תָּמָר) as a proper name, which may also be a reference to the tree "tamar" (date palm), but it is treated as a name here.
