# 1 Chronicles 7

¹ The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron, four in all. ² The sons of Tola: Uzi, Rephaiah, Jeriah, Jachmai, and Jibsam, and Shimuel, heads of their ancestral houses. They were mighty warriors from the lineage of Tola, and their total number in the days of David was twenty-two thousand six hundred. ³ The sons of Uzi: Izrahiah, and the sons of Izrahiah: Michael, Obediah, Joel, and Izhiah, five in all, each a leader. ⁴ From them, according to their ancestral houses, there were groups of soldiers for battle: thirty-six thousand, for they had many wives and children. ⁵ Their brothers, from all the clans of Issachar, were also mighty warriors: seventy-seven thousand total, and they were all counted. ⁶ The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Beker, and Jediael, three in all. ⁷ The sons of Bela: Ezbon, Uzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri, five leaders of their ancestral houses, mighty warriors. Their total number was twenty-two thousand thirty-four. ⁸ The sons of Beker: Zemirah, Yo'ash, Eli'ezer, Eliyo'ena, and Amri, Jeremot, Abiya, and Anathoth, and Almoth. These were the sons of Beker. ⁹ According to their generations, they were leaders of their ancestral houses, mighty warriors: twenty-two thousand in total. ¹⁰ The sons of Jediael: Bilhan, and the sons of Bilhan: Yeu'sh, Benjamin, Ehu'd, and Kenanah, Zitan, Tarsish, and Achishar. ¹¹ All these were the sons of Jediael, leaders of their ancestral houses, mighty warriors: seventeen thousand two hundred soldiers for war. ¹² Shufim and Huphim, the sons of Hushim, were another family. ¹³ The sons of Naphtali: Jahzai'el, Gunni, Yitzer, and Shalum, the sons of Bilhah. ¹⁴ The sons of Manasseh: Asri'el, the son of his Aramean concubine, who bore Machir, the father of Gilead. ¹⁵ Machir took a wife for Huphim and for Shufim. His sister's name was Maacah, and the name of his second sister was Tzlophad, who had daughters. ¹⁶ Maacah, the wife of Machir, bore a son and named him Peresh. His brother was named Shera, and their sons were Ulam and Rakem. ¹⁷ The sons of Ulam: Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh. ¹⁸ His sister, the widow, bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah. ¹⁹ The sons of Semida'e: Ahyan, Shekem, Likhi, and Ani'e'm. ²⁰ The sons of Ephraim: Shuthelah, his son Bered, his son Tahath, his son Eleadah, his son Tahath, his son. ²¹ Zabad, his son, Shuthelach, his son, Ezer and Elead, and they were killed by the men of Gath in the land, because they had come down to take their livestock. ²² Ephraim, their father, mourned for many days, and his brothers came to comfort him. ²³ He went to his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to a son. He named him Beri'ah, for sorrow had come upon his house. ²⁴ His daughter Sheerah built Lower Beth Horon, Upper Beth Horon, and Uzzun Sheerah. ²⁵ Re'phah, his son; Reshef, and Telah, his son; Tahan, his son. ²⁶ La'ad, his son; Ami'hud, his son; Eli'shamah, his son. ²⁷ Non, his son; Yehoshu'a, his son. ²⁸ Their territory and their settlements were Bethel and its villages, to the east Na'aran, to the west Gazer and its villages, Shechem and its villages, as far as Ai and its villages. ²⁹ And the boundary of the sons of Manasseh: Beth Shean and its villages, Ta'anach and its villages, Megiddo and its villages, Dor and its villages. In these cities the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel, settled. ³⁰ The sons of Asher: Jimnah, Ish'vah, Ish'vei, Beri'ah, and their sister Serach. ³¹ The sons of Beri'ah: Hever and Malchiel, who was the father of Birzayit. ³² Hever fathered Japhlet, Shomer, and Hotam, and their sister Shua. ³³ The sons of Japhlet: Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were the sons of Japhlet. ³⁴ The sons of Shamer: Ahie, Rahgah, Chubha, and Aram. ³⁵ The son of Helah: his brother Tzophach, Yimna'e, and Shales, and Amal. ³⁶ The sons of Tzophach: Sua, Charnefer, Shual, Beri, and Yimra. ³⁷ Betsar, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Yitran, and Beerah. ³⁸ The sons of Yeter: Yefuneh, Pispa, and Ara. ³⁹ The sons of Ulla: Arach, Hananiah, and Retsyiah. ⁴⁰ All these were the sons of Asher, leaders of their ancestral houses, selected and mighty warriors, leaders of the commanders. Their total number for military service was twenty-six thousand.

Notes
- v. 23: The name "Beri'ah" is derived from the Hebrew root meaning "in evil" or "in sorrow," reflecting the context of the father's grief.
- v. 24: "Sheerah" is a name of a woman mentioned as the builder of Beth Horon, following the pattern in the Hebrew text.
- v. 20–21: The genealogical listing of Ephraim's descendants is complex, with some names repeated and unclear genealogical relationships. The translation follows the most straightforward reading of the Hebrew text.
- v. 30–40: The names and relationships in the genealogies are preserved as given, with careful attention to the Hebrew text's structure.
