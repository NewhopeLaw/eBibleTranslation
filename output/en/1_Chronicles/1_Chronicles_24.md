# 1 Chronicles 24

¹ The sons of Aaron were Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. ² Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and they had no children. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests. ³ David divided them, along with Sadoc from the sons of Eleazar and Ahimelech from the sons of Ithamar, according to their duties and their service, by their ancestral families. ⁴ The sons of Eleazar proved to be more numerous than the sons of Ithamar in the number of leaders of the people. So David divided them further, assigning to the sons of Eleazar thirteen leaders by ancestral family and to the sons of Ithamar eight leaders by ancestral family. ⁵ They cast lots in the presence of each other, for they were the rulers of the holy things and the rulers of God, among the sons of Eleazar and the sons of Ithamar. ⁶ Samuel the son of Nathaniel the scribe, from among the Levites, recorded them in the presence of the king and the officials, along with Sadoc the priest and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the leaders of the ancestral families of the priests and Levites. One ancestral house was assigned to Eleazar, and one to Ithamar. ⁷ The first lot fell to Jehoiarib, the second to Jediah. ⁸ The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim. ⁹ The fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin. ¹⁰ The seventh to Hakoetz, the eighth to Abijah. ¹¹ The ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah. ¹² The eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Yaqim. ¹³ The thirteenth to Chupah, the fourteenth to Yesaveab. ¹⁴ The fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer. ¹⁵ The seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Hapetzetz. ¹⁶ The nineteenth to Petachyah, the twentieth to Jechzekel. ¹⁷ The twenty-first to Yachin, the twenty-second to Gamul. ¹⁸ The twenty-third to Delayahu, the twenty-fourth to Maaziyahu. ¹⁹ These were the divisions of the sons of Aaron, according to the service they carried out in the house of the LORD, as the Lord had directed through Aaron their ancestor. ²⁰ The remaining Levites, from the descendants of Amram, were Shubaal, and from Shubaal, Jehiyadhu. ²¹ From Rechavyahu, the head was Yishiyah. ²² From the Issacharite Shelmoth, came Yachat. ²³ The sons of Yeriyyahu were Amariyyahu the second, Yachaziel the third, and Yeqameam the fourth. ²⁴ From Uziyel came Michah, and from Michah, Shemir. ²⁵ The brother of Michah was Yishiyah, and from Yishiyah came Zachariyahu. ²⁶ The sons of Merari were Mahli and Musi, the sons of Jaaziyahu. ²⁷ The sons of Merari, through Jaaziyahu, were Shoham and Zakar and Eberi. ²⁸ To Mahli was assigned Elazar, who had no children. ²⁹ To Kish, the sons of Kish were Yerachme'el. ³⁰ The sons of Musi were Mahli, Edar, and Yirimoth. These were the sons of the Levites according to their ancestral families. ³¹ And they too cast lots for their assignments, just like their brothers, the sons of Aaron, in the presence of King David, Sadoc, Ahimelech, and the leaders of the ancestral families of the priests and Levites — the heads of the ancestral families for their younger brothers.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Aaron" is from the Hebrew "aharon," a proper name. The Levitical priesthood is established through Aaron's descendants.
- v. 4: "thirteen leaders" and "eight leaders" are based on the context of the Levitical divisions. The Septuagint (Greek) gives different numbers, but the Hebrew text is followed here.
- v. 19: "as the Lord had directed through Aaron" reflects the phrase "כַּאֲשֶׁר צִוָּהוּ יְהוָה" (as the Lord commanded him), referring to Aaron, not a direct command to David.
- v. 28: "Elazar, who had no children" — the Hebrew is clear that Elazar had no sons, though daughters may be implied. No further clarification is provided in the text.
