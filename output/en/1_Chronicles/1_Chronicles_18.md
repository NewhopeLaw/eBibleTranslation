# 1 Chronicles 18

¹ After this, David defeated the Philistines and brought them under subjection. He captured Gath and its towns from the hand of the Philistines. ² He also defeated Moab, and the Moabites became David’s servants, bringing tribute. ³ David also defeated Hadadezer, king of Zobah, who was going to establish his hand over the Euphrates River. ⁴ From him, David captured a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand foot soldiers. David broke all the chariots, and he left only a hundred chariots. ⁵ When Aram of Damascus came to help Hadadezer, king of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men from Aram. ⁶ David placed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became his servants, bringing tribute. The LORD delivered David in all his campaigns. ⁷ David took the gold shields that were on the servants of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem. ⁸ From Tibhath and Cun, the cities of Hadadezer, David took a great amount of bronze, with which Solomon made the bronze sea, the pillars, and the bronze vessels. ⁹ When Toi, king of Hamath, heard that David had struck down the entire army of Hadadezer, king of Zobah, ¹⁰ he sent his son Joram to King David, to ask him for peace and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him. Toi was a warrior like Hadadezer. All the gold and silver vessels were also given. ¹¹ The king David consecrated these to the LORD, along with the silver and gold he had taken from all the nations: from Edom, from Moab, from the children of Ammon, from the Philistines, and from Amalek. ¹² Abishai the son of Zeruiah defeated eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt. ¹³ David placed garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites became David’s servants. The LORD delivered David in all his campaigns. ¹⁴ David reigned over all Israel, and he did justice and righteousness for all his people. ¹⁵ Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was the recorder. ¹⁶ Zadok the son of Ahitub and Abimelech the son of Abiathar were priests, and Seraiah was the scribe. ¹⁷ Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and the sons of David were the chief attendants to the king.

Notes
- v. 10: "Toi" is the common English rendering of תֹּעוּ (Tohu) from the context.
