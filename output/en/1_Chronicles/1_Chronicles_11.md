# 1 Chronicles 11

¹ All Israel gathered to David in Hebron, saying, "We are your bone and your flesh." ² Both yesterday and the day before, when Saul was king, you were the one leading Israel out and bringing it in. And the LORD your God said to you, "You will shepherd my people Israel, and you will be their commander over them." ³ All the elders of Israel came to the king in Hebron, and David made a covenant with them before the LORD. Then they anointed David as king over Israel, in accordance with the word of the LORD spoken through Samuel. ⁴ David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus. The Jebusites, who were the residents of the land, were there. ⁵ The people of Jebus said to David, "You will not come in here." But David captured the citadel of Zion, and it became the city of David. ⁶ David said, "Whoever strikes the Jebusites first will be chief and commander." Joab son of Zeruiah went up first, and he became chief. ⁷ David settled in the citadel. Because of this, it was called the City of David. ⁸ David built the city all around, from the surrounding area to the outer parts. Joab restored the remaining parts of the city. ⁹ David went on, growing stronger, and the LORD of Armies was with him. ¹⁰ These were the leaders of the mighty warriors who stood with David during his kingship, as he led all Israel to be king in accordance with the word of the LORD over Israel. ¹¹ These are the names of David's mighty men. The first among the thirty was Jashobeam the son of Hachmoni. He raised his spear against three hundred men in one battle. ¹² After him was Eleazar the son of Dodai the Ahohite, one of the three mighty men. ¹³ He was with David at Pas Dammim when the Philistines gathered there for battle. The field was filled with barley, and the people had fled from the Philistines. ¹⁴ They stood in the middle of the barley field and rescued it, striking down the Philistines. The LORD gave a great victory. ¹⁵ Three of the thirty went down to the cave of Adullam to David, while the Philistine camp was stationed in the Valley of Rephaim. ¹⁶ At that time David was in the stronghold, and the Philistine garrison was in Bethlehem. ¹⁷ David longed and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem, which is by the gate?" ¹⁸ The three of them broke through the Philistine camp and drew water from the well of Bethlehem, which was by the gate. They took it and brought it to David. But David would not drink it. Instead, he poured it out to the LORD, ¹⁹ saying, "May it not be that I should drink the blood of these men, who risked their lives to bring it? For at the cost of their lives they brought it." And David would not drink it. These were the deeds of the three mighty men. ²⁰ Abishai, Joab's brother, was the leader of the three. He raised his spear against three hundred men in one battle. He was famous among the three. ²¹ From the three, he was greater than the other two, and he became their commander, but he did not reach the level of the three. ²² Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a man of great strength from Kabzeel, did many brave deeds. He struck down two lions of Moab. He went down and struck a lion in the pit in the snow. ²³ He also struck a mighty Egyptian, a man five cubits tall, whose spear was like a weaver's rod. Benaiah went down to him with a staff, took the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear. ²⁴ These were the deeds Benaiah the son of Jehoiada did. He was famous among the three mighty men. ²⁵ He was honored among the thirty, but he did not reach the level of the three. David placed him in charge of his household. ²⁶ The mighty men among the warriors were Asahel the brother of Joab, Elhanan the son of Dodo from Bethlehem, ²⁷ Samuel the Harenian, Heltai the Paltite, ²⁸ Ira the Iktanite, Abiezer the Anethothite, ²⁹ Sibbechai the Hushathite, Ilai the Ahohite, ³⁰ Maharai the Netophathite, Heled the son of Baanah the Netophathite, ³¹ Ittai the son of Ribai from Gibeah of Benjamin, Benaiah the Pireathite, ³² Hurai from the streams of Gaash, Abiel the Arbathite, ³³ Azmaveth the Barhumite, Elihab the Shalbonite, ³⁴ the sons of Hashem the Gizonite, Jonathan the son of Shage the Hararite, ³⁵ Ahiam the son of Sacar the Hararite, Eliphal the son of Ur, ³⁶ Hepar the Merarite, Ahijah the Pelonite, ³⁷ Hezro the Carmelite, Naarai the son of Ezbai, ³⁸ Joel the brother of Nathan, Mibhar the son of Hagar, ³⁹ Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Berothite, who bore the weapons of Joab the son of Zeruiah, ⁴⁰ Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite, ⁴¹ Uriah the Hittite, Zabad the son of Achlai, ⁴² Adina the son of Shiza the Reubenite, the commander over the Reubenites, and thirty under him, ⁴³ Hanan the son of Maacah, and Joash the Minitite, ⁴⁴ Ozem the Ashhoretite, Shama and Jeiel the sons of Hotham the Aroerite, ⁴⁵ Jehdeiah the son of Shimri, and Joha his brother, the Tizite, ⁴⁶ Eliel the Machathite, Jeribai, and Joshaviah the sons of Elneam, and Ithmah the Moabite, ⁴⁷ Eliel, Obadiah, and Zia the Mezobaite.

Notes
- v. 19: "the blood of these men" — the Hebrew uses נֶפֶשׁ ("life") in a metaphorical way to mean "at the cost of their lives."
- v. 22: "man of great strength" — the Hebrew reads אִֽישׁ־חַיִל, which is a standard phrase meaning "man of valor" or "man of might." Rendered here as "man of great strength" to reflect the context of Benaiah's valor.
