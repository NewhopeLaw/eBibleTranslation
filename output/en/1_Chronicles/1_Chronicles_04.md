# 1 Chronicles 4

¹ The sons of Judah were Perez, Hethron, and Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. ² And Reaiah, son of Shobal, fathered Jahath. Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These were the clans of the Zarhites. ³ And these were the sons of Abi-ezram: Ezrah, Ishma, and Yidbath. The name of their sister was Hitzlaphonni. ⁴ And Penuel, father of Gedor. And Ezer, father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, father of the city of Bethlehem. ⁵ And to Ashhur, father of Tekoa, there were two wives: Hela and Naarah. ⁶ And Naarah bore him Ahuzam, Hepher, and Teimani, and the Hashatarite. These were the sons of Naarah. ⁷ And the sons of Hela were Tzereth, Tsorach, and Ethan. ⁸ And Qoitz fathered Anav and the Hazzevah. And the clans of Acharchel, son of Huruym. ⁹ And there was Jaabez, who was honored above his brothers. His mother named him Jaabez, saying, "I bore him in pain." ¹⁰ And Jaabez called upon the God of Israel, saying, "If you will indeed bless me and enlarge my territory, and if your hand will be with me, and if you will keep me from harm, then I will bring to you what you ask." And God granted what he asked. ¹¹ And Kaleb, brother of Shuah, fathered Mehir. He was the father of Esh-tuon. ¹² And Esh-tuon fathered Beth-rappah and Pasach and Tehenah, the father of the city of Naresh. These were the men of Rekah. ¹³ And the sons of Kenaz were Antial and Sarai. And the sons of Antial were Chatath. ¹⁴ And Meonothai fathered Efrah. And Sarai fathered Yoav, the father of the Valley of the Smiths. For they were smiths. ¹⁵ And the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh were Iru, Elah, and Naam. And the sons of Elah were Kenaz. ¹⁶ And the sons of Jalel were Zif and Ziphah, Tiryah, and Asarayel. ¹⁷ And the son of Ezra was Yeter and Mered and Efer and Yalon. And Tahar bore Miryam and Shammay and Ishbach, the father of Estemoh. ¹⁸ And his wife, Hudiya the daughter of Pharaoh, bore Yared, the father of Gedor, and Heber, the father of Socho, and Yekuthiel, the father of Zanoah. And these were the sons of Betiah, the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered took. ¹⁹ And the sons of the wife of Hodiyah, sister of Naham, were Keilah the Garmite and Estemoh the Maacathi. ²⁰ And the sons of Simon were Amnon and Rinah, son of Hanan, Tilun, and the sons of Yishai—Zoheith and the son of Zoheith. ²¹ The sons of Shelah, the son of Judah, were Er, the father of Lecha, and Laadah, the father of Marseh, and the clans of the house of Beth-abud, to the city of Ashbea. ²² And Yokim and the men of Kozebah, Yoash and Saraph, who dwelled in Moab, and the Jebusite. And these things are ancient. ²³ They are the potters and the ones dwelling in Netav and Gedera, with the king in his service, and they dwelled there. ²⁴ The sons of Simon were Nemu'el and Yamin, Yarib, Zerach, and Shaul. ²⁵ Shalum, his son; Mibsam, his son; Mishma, his son; ²⁶ Shime'i, his son. ²⁷ And to Shime'i there were sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers had few sons, and all the families of his clan did not multiply as did the sons of Judah. ²⁸ And they dwelled in Beer-sheba and Moladah and Charash-uaal. ²⁹ And in Belah and in Etzam and in Toldah. ³⁰ And in Betu'el and in Charmah and in Tsiklag. ³¹ And in Bet-marcaboth and in Charar-susim and in Bet-birai and in Shaarayim. These were their cities until the reign of King David. ³² And their villages were Eitam and A'in, Rimmon, and Tochen and Ashan. These were five cities. ³³ And all their villages around these cities, even to Baal, were their settlements, and they held them for themselves. ³⁴ And Moshebav and Yemlech and Yosha, son of Amatsyah. ³⁵ And Yo'el and Yehu, son of Yosivayah, son of Sarai, son of Asiyel. ³⁶ And Eliyonei and Yaakovah, Yishvohayyah, Asaiyah, Adiyel, Yishimiyel, and Banyah. ³⁷ And Zizayah, son of Shifpi, son of Alon, son of Yedaiyahu, son of Shimri, son of Shemaiah. ³⁸ These are the ones who are recorded by name, leaders in their families, and the house of their fathers multiplied greatly. ³⁹ And they went toward the entrance of Geder, even to the east of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks. ⁴⁰ And they found good and abundant pasture, and the land was wide and open before them, peaceful and quiet, because it was from the descendants of Ham living there before them. ⁴¹ And these men, whose names are recorded in the days of Yehizkiyahu the king of Judah, struck down the tents of the people and the Meunim whom they found there, and they destroyed them until this day, and they dwelled in their place, because there was pasture for their flocks. ⁴² And from among them, five hundred men from the sons of Simon went up to the mountain of Seir, with Phaltiyah, Nearyah, Raphiyah, and Uziyel, the sons of Yishai, their leaders. ⁴³ And they struck the remnant of the Plishtim, the descendants of Amalek, and they dwelled there until this day.

Notes
- v. 10: "the God of Israel" is a translation of "Elohim Yisrael" (אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל), rendered as a title rather than a name.
- v. 13: "Antial" is a constructed name based on the Hebrew "עָתְנִיאֵל" (Athni'el), which is rendered here as "Antial" to reflect the root meaning and phonetic flow.
- v. 17: "Ishbach" is a constructed name based on the Hebrew "יִשְׁבָּח" (Yishbach), meaning "he praised," rendered to maintain a clear English connection to its meaning.
- v. 18: "Yared" is a constructed name based on the Hebrew "יָרְדָה" (Yardah), meaning "he went down."
- v. 20: "Tilun" is a constructed name based on the Hebrew "תִּילוֹן" (Tilon), meaning "rope" or "twist."
- v. 22: "Jebusite" is a generic term for the people of Jebus, here used to describe the inhabitants of a place, not a specific ethnic group.
- v. 26: "Shime'i" is a constructed name based on the Hebrew "שִׁמְעִי" (Shim'ey), meaning "he heard."
- v. 34: "Moshebav" is a constructed name based on the Hebrew "מְשׁוֹבָב" (Moshevav), meaning "he was exiled."
