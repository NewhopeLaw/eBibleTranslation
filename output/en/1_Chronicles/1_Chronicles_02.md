# 1 Chronicles 2

¹ These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, Issachar and Zebulun. ² Dan, Joseph, and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. ³ The sons of Judah were Er and Onan and Shelah. Three sons were born to him from the daughter of Shua the Canaanite. Er was the firstborn of Judah, but he was wicked in the sight of the LORD, and the LORD put him to death. ⁴ His wife Tamar bore him Perez and Zerah. All of Judah's sons were five. ⁵ The sons of Perez were Hetzron and Hamul. ⁶ The sons of Zerah were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Kalkol, and Darda—all of them five. ⁷ The sons of Karmi were Achbor, Ookher, and Israel, the one who transgressed the ban. ⁸ The sons of Ethan were Azariah. ⁹ The sons of Hetzron were Asher, who was born to him, Ram, and Kaleb. ¹⁰ Ram fathered Amminadab, and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, the leader of the sons of Judah. ¹¹ Nahshon fathered Salma, and Salma fathered Boaz. ¹² Boaz fathered Oved, and Oved fathered Jesse. ¹³ Jesse fathered his firstborn, Eliab, and the second, Abinadab, and the third, Shimea. ¹⁴ The fourth, Nethanel, the fifth, Rada. ¹⁵ The sixth, Ozem, the seventh, David. ¹⁶ Their sister was Tsruyah and Abigail. The sons of Tsruyah were Asbai, Yoav, and Asa'el—three. ¹⁷ Abigail bore Amasa, and the father of Amasa was Yeter the Ishmaelite. ¹⁸ Caleb, the son of Hetzron, fathered Azubah, a wife, and Yerieth. These were her sons: Yeshai, Sobav, and Ardun. ¹⁹ Azubah died, and Caleb took Ephrath, who bore him Hur. ²⁰ Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Betsalel. ²¹ Later, Hetzron went to the daughter of Machir, the father of Gilead, and he took her. He was sixty years old when she bore him Segub. ²² Segub fathered Ya'ir, and he had twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead. ²³ Gesher and Aram took the towns of Havot Ya'ir from them, including Kenath and its towns—sixty cities altogether. These were the sons of Machir, the father of Gilead. ²⁴ After the death of Hetzron, Caleb went to Ephrathah. The wife of Hetzron was Abiah, who bore him Ashchur, the father of Tekoah. ²⁵ The sons of Yerachme'el, the firstborn of Hetzron, the firstborn, were Ram, Buna, Oran, and Otzi, the brother of Akhia. ²⁶ There was another wife for Yerachme'el, and her name was Atzarah. She was the mother of Onam. ²⁷ The sons of Ram, the firstborn of Yerachme'el, were Ma'etz, Yamim, and Aker. ²⁸ The sons of Onam were Shammay and Yada. The sons of Shammay were Nadav and Abishur. ²⁹ The name of Abishur's wife was Avihayil, who bore him Achban and Molid. ³⁰ The sons of Nadav were Selad and Efrayim. Selad died without children. ³¹ The sons of Efrayim were Yeshai, and the sons of Yeshai were Sheshan, and the sons of Sheshan were Achai. ³² The sons of Yada, the brother of Shammay, were Yeter and Yonatan. Yeter died without children. ³³ The sons of Yonatan were Pelit and Zaza. These were the sons of Yerachme'el. ³⁴ Sheshan had no sons, only daughters. Sheshan had a servant from Egypt, whose name was Yarcha'el. ³⁵ Sheshan gave his daughter to Yarcha'el, his servant, as wife, and she bore him Etay. ³⁶ Etay fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad. ³⁷ Zabad fathered Ephlal, and Ephlal fathered Oved. ³⁸ Oved fathered Yehu, and Yehu fathered Azariah. ³⁹ Azariah fathered Chalitz, and Chalitz fathered Eleash. ⁴⁰ Eleash fathered Sismai, and Sismai fathered Shalum. ⁴¹ Shalum fathered Yekamya, and Yekamya fathered Elishama. ⁴² The sons of Caleb, the brother of Yerachme'el, were Meisha, the firstborn, who was the father of Zif. The sons of Mereshah were the father of Chevron. ⁴³ The sons of Chevron were Korach, Tafuch, Rekem, and Shema. ⁴⁴ Shema fathered Racham, the father of Yarko'ah. Rekem fathered Shama. ⁴⁵ The son of Shama was Ma'oen, and Ma'oen was the father of Beit-Tsur. ⁴⁶ Eifa, the concubine of Caleb, bore him Charan, Motzav, and Gazav. Charan fathered Gazav. ⁴⁷ The sons of Yahday were Regem, Yotam, Geshan, Pelit, Eifa, and Sha'af. ⁴⁸ The concubine of Caleb, Ma'acah, bore Sherer and Tirhanah. ⁴⁹ Shefa bore Shwa, the father of Makbenah, and the father of Gib'ea. The daughter of Caleb was Aksa. ⁵⁰ These were the sons of Caleb, the son of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah: Shobal, the father of Kiryat-Ye'arim. ⁵¹ Salma, the father of Beit-Lechem. Charaf, the father of Beit-Gad. ⁵² The sons of Shobal, the father of Kiryat-Ye'arim, were the ones who saw half the resting places. ⁵³ The clans of Kiryat-Ye'arim were the Yitri, the Puti, the Shumati, and the Mishra'iti. From these came the Tsara'ati and the Ash'tali. ⁵⁴ The sons of Salma were Beit-Lechem and Netofati, Atrot, Beit-Yoav, and half the Machati-Tsara'iti. ⁵⁵ The clans of the scribes dwelling in Ya'abet, Tir'atiym, Shimeatiym, Sukatiym—these were the Kinim, the ones coming from Hammat, the father of Beit-Rakav.

Notes
- v. 3: "wicked in the sight of the LORD" is a standard phrasing in the Hebrew text, indicating moral failure or divine disapproval.
- v. 34: The phrase "only daughters" is used to clarify the Hebrew "lo banim" which literally means "no sons."
- v. 42: "Meisha" is a name derived from the Hebrew text, and "father of Zif" is taken directly from the Hebrew context.
- v. 49: "Aksa" is a name derived from the Hebrew text, and it is clear from the context that she is the daughter of Caleb.
- v. 50: "Shobal" and "Salma" are names derived from the Hebrew text, and the context clarifies their roles as fathers of certain towns.
