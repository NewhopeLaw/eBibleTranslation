# 1 Chronicles 25

¹ David and the commanders of the army assigned duties to the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun—prophets who played lyres, harps, and cymbals. The total number of those assigned was 288, each fulfilling the tasks set before them. ² The sons of Asaph were Zachar, Joseph, and Nethaniah, and Irael. These were the sons of Asaph, appointed by the king. ³ The sons of Jeduthun were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Shimee, and Azarel, Mattithiah—six in number, all sons of Jeduthun, playing lyres and offering praise and thanksgiving to the Lord. ⁴ The sons of Heman were Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shemuel, Jerimoth, Hanan, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, and Romamti-Ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, Mahazioth. ⁵ These were all the sons of Heman, the seer of the king, in the words of God to uplift a horn. The Lord gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters. ⁶ All of them, along with their father, were set apart for song in the house of the Lord, with lyres, harps, and cymbals, and were under the king’s direction, along with Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman. ⁷ Their total number, together with their brothers who were trained in song under the Lord, was 288—every one who could read. ⁸ They cast lots for their shifts, whether the young or the old, the experienced or the student. ⁹ The first lot fell to Asaph, Joseph, and his brothers and sons, twelve in number. ¹⁰ The second to Zachar, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹¹ The third to Iraiah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹² The fourth to Nathan, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹³ The fifth to Bukkiah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹⁴ The sixth to Isharaiel, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹⁵ The seventh to Jeshaiah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹⁶ The eighth to Mattaniah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹⁷ The ninth to Shimei, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹⁸ The tenth to Azarel, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ¹⁹ The eleventh to Hashabiah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²⁰ The twelfth to Shubaal, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²¹ The thirteenth to Mattithiah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²² The fourteenth to Eremoth, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²³ The fifteenth to Hananiah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²⁴ The sixteenth to Josbekashah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²⁵ The seventeenth to Hanani, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²⁶ The eighteenth to Mallothi, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²⁷ The nineteenth to Eliathah, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²⁸ The twentieth to Hothir, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ²⁹ The twenty-first to Giddalti, his sons and brothers, twelve in number. ³⁰ The twenty-second to Mahazioth, his sons
