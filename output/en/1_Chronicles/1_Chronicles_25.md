# 1 Chronicles 25

¹ David and the commanders of the army set apart for service the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, the prophets who played the lyres, the harps, and the cymbals; the total number of those appointed for this service was two hundred and eighty-eight. ² The sons of Asaph: Zachari, Joseph, and Nethaniah, and Asareelah. These were the sons of Asaph under the direction of Asaph, the prophet who was under the king. ³ The sons of Jeduthun: Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, Mattithiah, and Shimei. These were the six sons of Jeduthun, playing the lyre and offering praise and thanksgiving to the LORD. ⁴ The sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shobael, Jerimoth, Hanan, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-Ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth. ⁵ All of these were the sons of Heman, the seer of the king, who spoke the words of God to uplift the horn. God had given Heman fourteen sons and three daughters. ⁶ These, together with their father, played songs in the house of the LORD with cymbals, with harps, and with lyres for the service of the house of God, under the direction of the king, Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman. ⁷ The total number of those trained in the art of singing to the LORD, all who understood the music, was two hundred and eighty-eight. ⁸ They cast lots for the watchmen, assigning equal responsibility to the young and the old, the experienced and the students. ⁹ The first lot fell to Asaph and his sons, Joseph, Notes
- v. 4: "Joshbekashah" is a composite name, likely meaning "Yahweh is the deliverer of the voice." Rendered as is to preserve the name’s structure in line with the supplied glossary for proper names.
- v. 5: "to uplift the horn" reflects the Hebrew metaphor "to raise the horn," meaning to exalt or honor.
- v. 7: "all who understood the music" is a translation of "pās qōnē’ēn," meaning those who had insight or understanding in the art.
