# 1 Chronicles 15

¹ David built houses in the city of David and prepared a place for the ark of God, and he set up a tent for it. ² Then David said, "It is not for us to carry the ark of God, but for the Levites, for they are the ones the LORD has chosen to carry the ark of the LORD and to serve him forever." ³ So David gathered all Israel together to Jerusalem to bring up the ark of the LORD to the place he had prepared for it. ⁴ He gathered together the sons of Aaron and the Levites. ⁵ From the sons of Kehath, Uriel the leader and his brothers, a hundred and twenty. ⁶ From the sons of Merari, Asahiah the leader and his brothers, two hundred and twenty. ⁷ From the sons of Gershom, Joel the leader and his brothers, a hundred and thirty. ⁸ From the sons of Elizaphan, Shemaiah the leader and his brothers, two hundred. ⁹ From the sons of Hebron, Eliel the leader and his brothers, eighty. ¹⁰ From the sons of Uzziel, Aminadab the leader and his brothers, a hundred and twelve. ¹¹ David summoned Zadok and Abiathar the priests and the Levites: Uriel, Asahiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, Aminadab. ¹² He said to them, "You are the leaders of the fathers of the Levites. Consecrate yourselves, you and your brothers, and bring up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel, to the place I have prepared for it." ¹³ For at the outset, the LORD our God did not let you go first, because we did not seek it according to justice. ¹⁴ So the priests and Levites consecrated themselves to bring up the ark of the God of Israel. ¹⁵ The sons of the Levites carried the ark of God, as Moses had commanded, according to the word of the LORD, written on the poles to bear it. ¹⁶ David said to the leaders of the Levites, "Appoint your brothers, the musicians, with instruments of music—harp, lyre, and cymbals—playing to lift their voices in joy." ¹⁷ The Levites set Heman son of Joel and of his brothers Asaph son of Berechiah, and from the sons of Merari, their brothers Ethan son of Kushiyahu. ¹⁸ With them were their brothers, the second musicians: Zechariah, Ozeiah, Shimramoth, Yechiyel, Eliyahu, Eliab, Banya, Measseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Oved-Edom, and Yeeial the gatekeepers. ¹⁹ The musicians were Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, playing cymbals of bronze to make a sound. ²⁰ Zechariah, Ozeiah, Shimramoth, Yechiyel, Unni, Eliyahu, Measseiah, Banya, playing harps on alamoth. ²¹ Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Oved-Edom, Yeeial, Oziah, playing lyres on the sheminith for strength. ²² And Cenaniah, the leader of the Levites, had charge of the music, for he was discerning. ²³ Berechiah and Elkhanah were gatekeepers for the ark. ²⁴ Shavanyahu, Yosafat, Nathan, Amasai, Zakhariah, Banya, Eliézer the priests, blew trumpets before the ark of God. And Oved-Edom and Yehiyel were gatekeepers for the ark. ²⁵ So David, the elders of Israel, and the commanders of thousands, went up to bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the house of Oved-Edom with joy. ²⁶ And when the LORD gave strength to the Levites who were carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams. ²⁷ David was dressed in a fine linen robe, and all the Levites who were carrying the ark of God, and the musicians, and Chenaniah the leader of the music, who was directing the musicians. And on David was a linen ephod. ²⁸ All Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of the LORD with a loud shout, with the sound of the horn, with trumpets, and with cymbals, with harps and lyres. ²⁹ When the ark of the covenant of the LORD came to the city of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out through the window and saw King David leaping and dancing, and she despised him in her heart.

Notes  
- v. 2: The phrase "לָשֵׂאת אֶת־אֲרוֹן הָֽאֱלֹהִים" is rendered as "to carry the ark of God," not as a command form but as a declarative statement.  
- v. 13: The phrase "לְמַ בָּרִאשׁוֹנָה לֹא אַתֶּם פָּרַץ" is ambiguous but is translated based on the context of divine initiative and human failure to seek.  
- v. 27: "עֵפוֹד בָּֽד" is translated as "a linen ephod," following the glossary.  
- v. 29: "מִיכַל בַּת־שָׁאוּל" is translated as "Michal daughter of Saul," using "daughter" as the standard translation for בַּת.
