# 1 Chronicles 29

¹ King David said to the whole assembly, "Solomon my son, the one whom God has chosen, is a young man and tender-hearted, and the work is great, for it is not for a human but for the LORD our God. ² With all my might, I have prepared materials for my God's house: gold for gold, silver for silver, bronze for bronze, iron for iron, wood for wood, stones of sapphire and precious stones, and all kinds of costly stones, and many stones for building. ³ In addition, from my own desire to build a house for my God, I have a special treasure of gold and silver, which I have given to my God's house in addition to all the materials I have prepared for the holy place. ⁴ Three thousand talents of gold from Ophir, and seven thousand talents of refined, tested silver, to overlay the walls of the houses. ⁵ Gold for gold and silver for silver, and all for the work of the hands of the craftsmen. And whoever is willing to add to the work for the LORD today, let their hand be open. ⁶ Then the leaders of the clans, the leaders of the tribes of Israel, the leaders of thousands and hundreds, and the officers of the king's work, ⁷ gave for the service of the house of God: five thousand talents of gold, five thousand shekels, and ten thousand talents of silver, and eight thousand talents of bronze, and a hundred thousand talents of iron. ⁸ And those who had found stones with them gave them to the storehouse of the house of the LORD, by the hand of Jehiell the Gershonite. ⁹ And the people rejoiced over their willingness, for they had given willingly to the LORD with a whole heart, and David the king also rejoiced greatly. ¹⁰ Then David blessed the LORD before the whole assembly and said, "Blessed are you, O LORD, the God of Israel, our Father from everlasting to eternity. ¹¹ Yours, O LORD, is the greatness, the power, the glory, the victory, and the majesty, for all that is in the heavens and on the earth is yours. The kingdom is yours, O LORD, and you are exalted as head over all. ¹² Both the riches and the honor come from you, and you rule over all. In your hand is power and might, and in your hand is to make great and to give strength to all. ¹³ And now, our God, we thank you and praise the name of your glory. ¹⁴ But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be so bold as to offer this freewill gift? For all things come from you, and from your hand we have given you. ¹⁵ For we are strangers before you, and sojourners, like all our ancestors. Our days are like a shadow on the earth, with no hope. ¹⁶ O LORD, God of our fathers, all this abundance that we have prepared to build a house for your name, from your hand it is, and all of it is yours. ¹⁷ And I know, O God, that you test the heart and desire uprightness. I have given all these willingly with a right heart, and now I have seen with joy that your people who are here have given freely to you. ¹⁸ O LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, our ancestors, keep this forever in the thoughts of the hearts of your people, and direct their hearts toward you. ¹⁹ And give to Solomon my son a willing heart to keep your commandments, your testimonies, and your statutes, and to accomplish all, and to build the house for which I have prepared. ²⁰ Then David said to the whole assembly, "Bless the LORD your God." So all the assembly blessed the LORD, the God of their ancestors, and bowed themselves and paid homage to the LORD and to the king. ²¹ And they offered sacrifices to the LORD the same day, a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and an abundance of sacrifices for all Israel. ²² And they ate and drank before the LORD that day with great joy. And they anointed Solomon the son of David as king for a second time, and Zadok as priest. ²³ Then Solomon sat on the throne of the LORD as king in place of David his father, and he succeeded. And all Israel listened to him. ²⁴ And all the leaders and the mighty men, and also all the sons of King David, gave their hands under Solomon the king. ²⁵ And the LORD exalted Solomon in the eyes of all Israel, and gave him a glory of kingship such as had not been on any king before him in Israel. ²⁶ David the son of Jesse reigned over all Israel. ²⁷ The years that he reigned over Israel were forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. ²⁸ He died in a good old age, full of days, wealth, and honor. And Solomon his son reigned in his place. ²⁹ Now the earlier and later deeds of King David are written in the Book of Samuel the seer, and in the Book of Nathan the prophet, and in the Book of Gad the seer, ³⁰ together with all his reign and his might and the events that came upon him, and upon Israel, and upon all the kingdoms of the lands.

Notes
- v. 2: "the work is great" — the Hebrew phrase "מְלָאכָה גְדוֹלָה" is translated to reflect the magnitude of the task ahead, not as a metaphor.
- v. 4: "Ophir" is the traditional rendering of the Hebrew "עֹפִיר" (Ophir).
- v. 14: "strangers" and "sojourners" are used to capture the Hebrew "גֵּרִים" and "תוֹשָׁבִים", reflecting the transient nature of human life.
- v. 15: "with no hope" — the Hebrew "וְאֵין מִקְוֶֽה" is taken as "with no hope" to reflect the impermanence of human life.
- v. 24: "gave their hands under" — the Hebrew "נָתְנוּ יָד תַּחַת" is a standard idiom for pledging allegiance or support.
