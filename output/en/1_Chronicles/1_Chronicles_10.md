# 1 Chronicles 10

¹ The Philistines fought against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before the Philistines, leaving many dead on Mount Gilboa. ² The Philistines pursued Saul and his sons, and they struck down Jonathan, Abinadav, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul. ³ The battle pressed hard upon Saul, and the archers found him wounded among the weapons. He fell and could no longer move. ⁴ Saul said to the man who carried his armor, “Take your sword and stab me with it, for I see that the Philistines are coming to mock me, and I will not live to be insulted.” But the man who carried his armor refused, terrified as he was. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it. ⁵ When the man who carried his armor saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on his sword and died. ⁶ Saul, his three sons, and his entire household died together. ⁷ All the men of Israel who were in the valley saw that Israel had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead. So they abandoned their cities and fled. The Philistines arrived and settled in them. ⁸ The next day the Philistines came to strip the dead and found Saul and his sons lying fallen on Mount Gilboa. ⁹ They stripped him, took his head and his armor, and sent messengers throughout the land of the Philistines to announce the news to their idols and to the people. ¹⁰ They placed his armor in the temple of their gods and fastened his head in the temple of Dagon. ¹¹ All the men of Gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul. ¹² Then all the brave men rose up and took the bodies of Saul and his sons, bringing them to Jabesh. There they buried their bones beneath the tamarisk tree in Jabesh, and they mourned for seven days. ¹³ Saul died because of his sins, the sins he had committed against the LORD, for he did not keep the word of the LORD and had even consulted a medium to seek guidance. ¹⁴ He did not seek the LORD, and so the LORD put him to death and transferred the kingship to David, the son of Jesse.

---

Notes  
- v. 3: "wounded among the weapons" — the Hebrew literally says "found him by the weapons" (בַּקָּשֶׁת), but the context of battle and the following line about falling from the bow indicates he was struck by arrows and lay among fallen weapons.  
- v. 13: "consulted a medium to seek guidance" — the Hebrew uses the phrase "to seek" (לִדְרֽוֹשׁ), which is commonly used for seeking divine guidance. The reference to "a medium" (בָּאוֹב) suggests a necromancer or seer, but the text does not specify how the communication took place. The glossary term “to seek” has been applied.  
- v. 14: "did not seek the LORD" — the Hebrew phrase is וְלֹֽא־דָרַשׁ בַּֽיהוָה, using the same root *drsh* as in v. 13, emphasizing his failure to seek guidance from God.
