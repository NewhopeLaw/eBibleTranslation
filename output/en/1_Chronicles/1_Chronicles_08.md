# 1 Chronicles 8

¹ Benjamin fathered Bela, his firstborn, Ashbel the second, and Aharach the third. ² Noa the fourth and Rapha the fifth. ³ Bela's sons were Addar, Gera, and Abihud. ⁴ Abishua, Naaman, and Ahio. ⁵ Gera, Shupham, and Huram. ⁶ These were the sons of Ehud. These men were the heads of the ancestral houses of the residents of Gibeon, and they carried them away to Manahath. ⁷ Naaman and Ahiah and Gera, he carried them away and fathered Eza and Achijud. ⁸ Shacharaim fathered sons in the land of Moab, after sending them away—Hushim and his wife Baera. ⁹ He fathered from his wife's month, Jobab, Zebai, Mesham, and Malcham. ¹⁰ And Yeuz, Sacai, and Miram. These were his sons, the heads of the ancestral houses. ¹¹ From Hushim he fathered Abitub and Elpaal. ¹² Elpaal's sons were Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and its daughter towns. ¹³ Bereh and Shema. These were the heads of the ancestral houses of the residents of Aijalon, and they drove out the residents of Gath. ¹⁴ His brother was Shashak and Irimoth. ¹⁵ Zabadiah, Adar, and Adar. ¹⁶ Michael, Ishpah, and Joah, sons of Bereiah. ¹⁷ Zabadiah, Meshulam, Hezeki, and Chaber. ¹⁸ Yishmery, Zeliyah, and Jobab, sons of Elpaal. ¹⁹ Yaquim, Zikri, and Zabdi. ²⁰ Eliena, Tiltai, and Eliel. ²¹ Adaiyah, Baraiyah, and Shimrat, sons of Shimei. ²² Yispan, Eber, and Eliel. ²³ Abdon and Zikri and Chanah. ²⁴ Chananyah, Eilam, and Anothiyah. ²⁵ Yipdayah and Penuel, sons of Shashak. ²⁶ Shamsheary, Shacharyah, and Atalyah. ²⁷ Yaareshyah, Eliyah, and Zikri, sons of Yeroham. ²⁸ These were the heads of the ancestral houses for their generations. These were the ones who dwelled in Jerusalem. ²⁹ In Gibeon, Abi Gibeon dwelled, and the name of his wife was Maacah. ³⁰ His firstborn son was Abdon, and Tsur, and Kish, and Baal, and Nadav. ³¹ And Gedor, his brother, and Zachar. ³² Miklot fathered Shima, and they too dwelled opposite their brothers in Jerusalem, with their brothers. ³³ Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadav, and Eshbaal. ³⁴ The son of Jonathan was Meribbaal, and Meribbaal fathered Michah. ³⁵ Michah's sons were Piton, Melech, Tarea, and Aha. ³⁶ Aha fathered Jehoadah, and Jehoadah fathered Almemeth, and Azmaveth, and Zimri, and Zimri fathered Moza. ³⁷ Moza fathered Binea. Rapha was his son. Eleash was his son. Atzel was his son. ³⁸ And Atzel had six sons, and these were their names: Ezriqam, the firstborn, Ishmael, Sheraiah, Obadyah, and Chanah. All these were sons of Atzel. ³⁹ The sons of Eshek his brother were Oulam, the firstborn, Yeush the second, and Elifaleth the third. ⁴⁰ The sons of Oulam were men mighty in valor, archers, and prolific in sons and grandsons—150 in all. These were the sons of Benjamin.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "after sending them away" likely refers to the act of sending them into exile or migration, as Hushim is mentioned as the one who sent them. The meaning of "month" in "from his wife's month" is uncertain but likely refers to a specific time or period in the context of marriage or childbearing.
