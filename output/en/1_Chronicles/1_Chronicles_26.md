# 1 Chronicles 26

¹ For the divisions of the gates, from the Korahites, Meshullam son of Kore, was in charge of the sons of Asaph. ² Meshullam had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jehiel the second, Zebadiah the third, and Yathniel the fourth. ³ Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, and Elioenai the seventh. ⁴ And for Obadiah son of Edom, his sons were Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, and Sacar the fourth, and Nathan the fifth. ⁵ Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, and Peulatiah the eighth, for God blessed him. ⁶ Shemaiah, his son, had sons who were leaders over their father’s household, because they were warriors. ⁷ Shemaiah’s sons: Ethan, Rehphael, Obed, Elzabad, whose brothers were all warriors, Elihu and Semachiah. ⁸ All of them were from the sons of Obadiah son of Edom, and his sons and brothers, all warriors, capable of performing duties. There were sixty-two from Obadiah son of Edom. ⁹ Meshullam had sons and brothers, all warriors, eighteen in number. ¹⁰ Chosah, from the sons of Merari, had sons: Shimeri was the head, for he had no firstborn, and his father made him head. ¹¹ His sons: Hilkiyah the second, Tavtaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth. All the sons and brothers of Chosah were thirteen. ¹² These were divisions of the gates for the leaders of the men, their duties to serve in the house of the Lord, guarding their brothers in their shifts. ¹³ They cast lots, whether young or old, by the house of their ancestors, for each gate and gate. ¹⁴ The lot fell to the east for Shelmiah and Zechariah his son. With wisdom, they cast the lots, and the lot for the north went out. ¹⁵ To Obadiah the south, and to his sons, the house of the gathering. ¹⁶ To Shuppim and Chosah the west, with the gate of Shelketh by the road that goes up, their watch opposite each other's watch. ¹⁷ To the east, six Levites for the day; to the north, four for the day; to the south, four for the day; and to the gathering, two, two. ¹⁸ To the road, to the west, four, and to the road, two. ¹⁹ These were the divisions of the gates for the sons of Korah and the sons of Merari. ²⁰ And the Levites, their brothers, were in charge of the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of the consecrated things. ²¹ The sons of Laadan, the sons of the Gershonites, were the heads of the families for the Gershonites: the Laadani heads were the Gershonites, the Yechieli. ²² The sons of Yechieli: Setham and his brother Joel were in charge of the treasuries of the house of the Lord. ²³ To the Amrammi, the Izthari, the Hebronni, and the Asaeli. ²⁴ And Shubael son of Gershom, son of Moses, was the overseer of the treasuries. ²⁵ His brothers: Eleazar, Rehabyahu his son, Yesheiah his son, Joram his son, and Zikri his son, and Shalometh his son. ²⁶ Shalometh and his brothers were in charge of all the treasuries of the consecrated things that David the king had dedicated, and the heads of the families for the commanders of thousands, hundreds, and the army leaders. ²⁷ Dedicated from the wars and from the plunder to strengthen the house of the Lord. ²⁸ All that Samuel the seer, and Saul son of Kish, and Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah had dedicated, all that was dedicated by the hand of Shalometh and his brothers. ²⁹ For the Izthari, Cananiah and his sons were in charge of the external service for Israel, as officers and judges. ³⁰ For the Hebronni, Hashabiah and his brothers, warriors, one thousand seven hundred in number, were appointed over the census of Israel beyond the Jordan westward, for all the service of the Lord and the service of the king. ³¹ For the Hebronni, Jeriah was the head of the Hebronni, according to their generations and families. In the fortieth year of David’s reign, they were searched for and found among them warriors in Jazer of Gilead. ³² His brothers, warriors, two thousand seven hundred, heads of the families. And David the king appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh, for every matter of God and the king’s word.

Notes
- v. 1: "Meshullam son of Kore" refers to Meshullam as the son of Kore, not a name.
- v. 2-3: The Hebrew lists the sons in order, and the translation follows the numerical sequence.
- v. 10: "Chosah" is the head of the family because he had no firstborn. The translation clarifies the reason.
- v. 20: "their brothers" refers to the Levites, not to the families of the gates.
- v. 27-28: The dedications include those from Samuel, Saul, Abner, and Joab, all of which are accounted by Shalometh and his brothers.
- v. 30-31: The Hebronni were searched for and found among the warriors in Jazer of Gilead during David's fortieth year.
