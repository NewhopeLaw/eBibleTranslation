# 1 Chronicles 22

¹ David said, "This is the house for the LORD, the God, and this is the altar for the burnt offering for Israel." ² David ordered the gathering of all the foreigners in the land of Israel, and he appointed stonecutters to cut hewn stones for building the house of God. ³ He also prepared a large amount of iron for the nails for the doors and the doorposts and for the fittings. He also prepared a vast quantity of bronze, which could not be weighed. ⁴ He prepared a vast amount of cedar wood, so much that the Sidonians and Tyrians brought cedar wood in great abundance to David. ⁵ David said, "Solomon, my son, is still young and tender, and the house to build for the LORD should be for great honor, for a name and for glory throughout all the land. I will prepare materials for him in abundance before my death." ⁶ David called his son Solomon and gave him instructions to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel. ⁷ David said to Solomon, "In my heart I longed to build a house for the name of the LORD, my God." ⁸ But the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "You have shed much blood and fought great wars, so you will not build a house for my name, because you have shed much blood on the earth in my sight." ⁹ "Look, a son will be born to you. He will be a man of rest, and I will give him rest from all his enemies around him. His name will be Solomon, and I will give peace and quiet to Israel in his days." ¹⁰ He will build a house for my name, and he will be my son, and I will be his father. I will establish his throne over Israel forever." ¹¹ Now, my son, may the LORD be with you, and you will succeed and build the house of the LORD, your God, as he has spoken to you." ¹² But may the LORD give you discernment and understanding, and he will command you over Israel, and you will keep the teaching of the LORD, your God." ¹³ "Then you will be successful if you keep to do the statutes and judgments that the LORD has commanded Moses for Israel. Be strong and courageous. Do not fear and do not be dismayed." ¹⁴ "Now, I have prepared in my poverty for the house of the LORD one hundred thousand talents of gold and a million talents of silver, without counting the bronze and iron, because it is in abundance. I have also prepared wood and stone. You may add to them." ¹⁵ "With you, there are many workers—stonecutters, masons, and carpenters, and every skilled craftsman in every kind of work." ¹⁶ "For gold, silver, bronze, and iron, there is no count. Rise and begin, and may the LORD be with you." ¹⁷ David gave orders to all the leaders of Israel to assist Solomon, his son. ¹⁸ "Is not the LORD, your God, with you? He has given rest around you, because he has placed the inhabitants of the land in your hands, and the land has been subdued before the LORD and before his people." ¹⁹ "Now, set your hearts and your souls to seek the LORD, the God of your ancestors, and rise up and build the sanctuary of the LORD, your God, to bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD and the holy vessels of the God into the house being built for the name of the LORD."

---

Notes
- v. 5: "I will prepare materials for him in abundance before my death" — the Hebrew uses "לָרֹב לִפְנֵי מוֹתֽוֹ", with "לָרֹב" ("in abundance") as a prepositional phrase meaning "in preparation for" or "before".
- v. 14: "one hundred thousand talents of gold and a million talents of silver" — the Hebrew uses "מֵֽאָה־אֶלֶף" and "אֶלֶף אֲלָפִים", which are read as 100,000 and 1,000,000 in modern numerals.
- v. 19: "sanctuary" for "מִקְדַּשׁ" — the term is used here in the sense of a dedicated, holy place for the ark.
