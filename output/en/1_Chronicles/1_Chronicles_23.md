# 1 Chronicles 23

¹ David had grown old and was full of days. He appointed Solomon his son to reign over Israel. ² He gathered together all the leaders of Israel, the priests, and the Levites. ³ The Levites were counted from the age of thirty years and upward. Their total number was thirty-eight thousand. ⁴ Of these, twenty-four thousand were assigned to oversee the work of the house of the LORD, and there were six thousand scribes and judges. ⁵ And four thousand were gatekeepers, and four thousand were singers who praised the LORD with the instruments I have made for giving praise. ⁶ David divided the Levites into divisions among the sons of Levi — Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. ⁷ For the Gershonites, Adan and Shimei. ⁸ The sons of Adan were Jehiel, Zetam, and Joel, three in all. ⁹ The sons of Shimei were Sholomith, Haziel, and Haran, three in all. These were the heads of the ancestral families of the Adanites. ¹⁰ The sons of Shimei were Jahath, Zizah, Ieush, and Beriah, four in all. ¹¹ Jahath was the head, and Zizah was second. But Ieush and Beriah did not produce many sons, so they formed a single house of one ancestral family. ¹² The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four in all. ¹³ The sons of Amram were Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to be consecrated, he and his descendants forever, to burn incense before the LORD, to serve, and to bless in His name forever. ¹⁴ And Moses, the man of God, his descendants were named after the tribe of Levi. ¹⁵ The sons of Moses were Gershom and Eliezer. ¹⁶ The sons of Gershom were Shebuel the head. ¹⁷ The sons of Eliezer were Rehabiah the head. Eliezer had no other sons, but the descendants of Rehabiah multiplied greatly. ¹⁸ The sons of Izhar were Shelomith the head. ¹⁹ The sons of Hebron were Jeriah the head, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth. ²⁰ The sons of Uzziel were Micah the head and Ishiah the second. ²¹ The sons of Merari were Mahli and Musi. The sons of Mahli were Eleazar and Kish. ²² And Eleazar died and had no sons, only daughters. His brothers, the sons of Kish, took them as wives. ²³ The sons of Musi were Mahli, Edar, and Jeremoth, three in all. ²⁴ These were the sons of Levi according to their ancestral families, the heads of the ancestral families for their duties, listed by name according to their generations. They were assigned to perform the work of the house of the LORD from the age of twenty years and upward. ²⁵ For David said, The LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to His people and has settled them in Jerusalem forever. ²⁶ And the Levites did not carry the ark or any of its furnishings for the service of it. ²⁷ For in the words of David, these are the numbers of the sons of Levi from the age of twenty years and upward. ²⁸ For they were stationed at the hands of the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of the LORD — in the courtyards and in the rooms, for the purification of all the holy things, and for the work of the service of God. ²⁹ For the bread of the Presence, for the fine flour for the grain offering, for the unleavened cakes, for the baking pans, for the wafers, and for all the measurements and weights. ³⁰ For standing at the morning and the evening to give praise and to bless the LORD, and likewise at the evening. ³¹ And for all the burnt offerings to the LORD on the Sabbaths, on the new moons, and on the appointed feasts, in number as prescribed to them, continually before the LORD. ³² And they kept the guarding of the Tent of Meeting and the guarding of the holy things, and the guarding of the sons of Aaron, their brothers, for the service of the house of the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The construction "David had grown old and was full of days" reflects a common Hebrew idiom for age and experience.
- v. 13: "Aaron was set apart to be consecrated" reflects the Hebrew phrase "va-yivda’el," which means "he was separated" or "set apart."
- v. 24: "From the age of twenty years and upward" is a translation of "mibben esrim shanah wama’alah," which is a common qualifying phrase in ancient military and service records.
- v. 31: "In number as prescribed to them" is a translation of "kemishpat alayhem," which refers to the legal or canonical regulations given to them.
