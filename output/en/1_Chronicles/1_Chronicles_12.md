# 1 Chronicles 12

¹ These were the ones who came to David at Ziklag, still fleeing from Saul the son of Kish. They were among the mighty warriors who helped in battle. ² Skilled with bows, some shooting right and left, with stones and arrows, these were from the brothers of Saul, from Benjamin. ³ Their leader was Ahiezer and Joash, sons of Shema the Gibeatite, and Zeizel and Phellet, sons of Azemeth, and Barzillai and Jehu the Anethothite. ⁴ And Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty leader among the thirty, and over the thirty. ⁵ And Jeremiah and Jachaziel and Johanan and Josabad the Gederathite. ⁶ Elkana and Irimoth and Bealiah and Shemariahu and Shephathya the Charuphite. ⁷ Elqanah and Yishiyahu and Ezra'el and Yoezer and Yashav'eam the Kerachim. ⁸ And Yo'elah and Zevadyah, sons of Yeroham from the land of Gedor. ⁹ And from the Gadites, those who separated to David from the desert, were strong and mighty warriors, men of battle, armed with spear and sword, with the face of a lion and swift as jackals on the mountains. ¹⁰ Azar was the first, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third. ¹¹ Mismamah the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth. ¹² Atai the sixth, Eliel the seventh. ¹³ Yohanan the eighth, Elzabad the ninth. ¹⁴ Yirmiyahu the tenth, Mekbanay the eleventh. ¹⁵ These were from the sons of Gad, leaders of the army, each commanding a hundred, the small and the great commanding a thousand. ¹⁶ These were the ones who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when he was filling all his ranks, and they drove out all the inhabitants from the valleys to the east and to the west. ¹⁷ And they came from the sons of Benjamin and Judah, as far as the camp of David. ¹⁸ David went out to meet them and said to them, "If you have come to me in peace to help me, my heart will be united with you. But if you have come to betray me to my enemies without guilt, may the God of our fathers witness and judge." ¹⁹ And the spirit clothed Amasai, the leader of the thirty, and he said, "You, David, son of Jesse, peace, peace to you, and peace to those who help you, because your God has helped you." David accepted them and set them as leaders of the bands. ²⁰ And from Manasseh, some fell to David when he came with the Philistines against Saul to battle, but they did not help him, for by counsel the Philistine commanders had sent them, saying, "Let their heads fall upon their master, Saul." ²¹ When he went to Ziklag, these fell to him from Manasseh: Ednah and Tovzabad and Rodiel and Meichai and Iosabad and Eliomoth and Semethai, leaders of thousands from Manasseh. ²² And they helped David in the band, for they were all mighty in strength, and they became leaders in the army. ²³ For day after day they came to help David, until it became a great camp like the camp of God. ²⁴ And these were the names of the leaders of the army who came to David at Hebron, to turn the kingdom of Saul to him, according to the word of the LORD. ²⁵ Sons of Judah, bearers of spear and sword, six thousand eight hundred men of battle. ²⁶ From the sons of Simeon, warriors for battle, seven thousand one hundred. ²⁷ From the Levites, four thousand six hundred. ²⁸ And Yoiaad the leader for Aaron, and with him three thousand seven hundred. ²⁹ And Tzadok, a young man, strong in might, and his father's house were leaders, twenty-two. ³⁰ And from the sons of Benjamin, brothers of Saul, three thousand, and many more of them were watching over the house of Saul. ³¹ And from the sons of Ephraim, twenty thousand eight hundred mighty warriors, men of renown from the houses of their ancestors. ³² And from half the tribe of Manasseh, eighteen thousand, who were named to come to make David king. ³³ And from the sons of Issachar, those who had insight into times, who knew what Israel should do, their leaders were two hundred, and all their brothers followed them. ³⁴ From Zebulun, those going out to battle, armed with all weapons of war, fifty thousand, who came to David not hesitating with heart or mind. ³⁵ And from Naphtali, leaders a thousand, and with them in spear and shield thirty-seven thousand. ³⁶ From Dan, warriors for battle, twenty-eight thousand six hundred. ³⁷ From Asher, those going out to battle, forty thousand. ³⁸ And from beyond the Jordan, from Reuben and Gad and half the tribe of Manasseh, all armed for battle, one hundred twenty thousand. ³⁹ All these were warriors, forming an ordered battle line in wholehearted unity, who came to Hebron to make David king over all Israel. And all Israel with one heart was determined to make David king. ⁴⁰ And they remained with David for three days, eating and drinking, for their brothers had prepared food for them. ⁴¹ And the nearby ones, even as far as Issachar and Zebulun and Naphtali, brought food on donkeys, camels, and mules, and on oxen: bread, flour, raisins, and dried figs, wine and oil, and many oxen and sheep, because there was great joy in Israel.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "still fleeing from Saul" reflects the context of ongoing pursuit rather than a single event.
- v. 9: "armed with spear and sword, with the face of a lion and swift as jackals" is a vivid metaphor for their battle prowess.
- v. 19: "the spirit clothed Amasai" is a literal rendering of the Hebrew, emphasizing the divine empowerment.
- v. 20: "by counsel the Philistine commanders had sent them" clarifies that the Philistines intentionally sent them to betray Saul.
- v. 41: "on donkeys, camels, and mules" aligns with the Hebrew terms for these animals, and "dried figs" is used for clarity over "dried grapes" or similar.
