# 1 Chronicles 16

¹ They brought the ark of God and set it in the middle of the tent David had pitched for it. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God. ² When David had finished offering the burnt and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD. ³ He gave to every man and woman in Israel, from each person, a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a cake. ⁴ He stationed Levites before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to serve, to praise, and to thank the LORD, the God of Israel. ⁵ Asaph was the leader, and with him were Zechariah, Yehiel, Shimramoth, Yechiyel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Banya, and Oved-Edom, with lyres, harps, and cymbals, and Asaph made a loud sound with the cymbals. ⁶ Banya and Yehaziel the priests were blowing trumpets before the ark of the covenant of God continually. ⁷ On that day, David appointed Asaph and his brothers at the head of the praise to the LORD continually. ⁸ Give thanks to the LORD; call upon his name; make known among the nations his deeds. ⁹ Sing to him, make music to him; tell of all his wondrous works. ¹⁰ Glory in his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. ¹¹ Seek the LORD and his strength; seek his face continually. ¹² Remember the wondrous things he has done, his miracles and the judgments from his mouth. ¹³ Descendants of Israel, his servants, the children of Jacob, his chosen ones, ¹⁴ you are the LORD our God in all the earth; his judgments are in every place. ¹⁵ Remember his covenant forever, the word he commanded for a thousand generations, ¹⁶ the covenant he made with Abraham, and the oath he swore to Isaac, ¹⁷ and he established it with Jacob for a decree, and with Israel for an eternal covenant, ¹⁸ saying, "To you I give the land of Canaan, the portion of your inheritance, ¹⁹ when you were few in number, few and sojourners in it. ²⁰ And they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people. ²¹ He did not allow any man to oppress them, and he rebuked kings on their behalf. ²² Do not touch my anointed ones, and do not harm my prophets. ²³ Sing to the LORD, all the earth; declare his salvation from day to day. ²⁴ Declare among the nations his glory; among all peoples his wondrous works. ²⁵ For the LORD is great and greatly to be praised; he is feared above all gods. ²⁶ For all the gods of the nations are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. ²⁷ Honor and majesty are before him; strength and beauty in his place. ²⁸ Give to the LORD, you families of nations, give to the LORD glory and strength. ²⁹ Give to the LORD the glory due his name; bring an offering and come before him. Bow down to the LORD in the splendor of holiness. ³⁰ Tremble before him, all the earth; yes, the world is made to stand firm and not be moved. ³¹ Let the heavens rejoice and let the earth be glad, and let the nations say, "The LORD reigns!" ³² Let the sea roar and its fullness; let the field and everything in it exult. ³³ Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before the LORD, for he is coming to judge the earth. ³⁴ Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; for his steadfast love endures forever. ³⁵ And say, "Save us, God of our salvation, and gather us and rescue us from the nations, to give thanks to your holy name, to glory in your praise." ³⁶ Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting, and let all the people say, "Amen!" and praise the LORD. ³⁷ Then David left Asaph and his brothers there continually before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to serve day by day. ³⁸ Oved-Edom and his brothers sixty-eight in number, and Oved-Edom the son of Yedithun and Chosah as gatekeepers. ³⁹ And he left Zadok the priest and his brother-priests before the tent of the LORD at Gibeon on the high place. ⁴⁰ To offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering continually, morning and evening, and for all that is written in the instruction of the LORD that he commanded Israel. ⁴¹ With them were Heman and Yeduthun and the other chosen ones by name to praise the LORD, for his steadfast love endures forever. ⁴² With them were Heman and Yeduthun with trumpets and cymbals to make a sound, and the instruments of the songs of God, and the sons of Yeduthun as gatekeepers. ⁴³ Then all the people went to their homes, and David returned to bless his household.

---

Notes
- v. 35: The phrase "give thanks to your holy name" reflects the context of communal praise and covenantal identity, though the Hebrew could be read with a slight shift in emphasis.
- v. 40: "Instruction of the LORD" refers to the Torah, used here in a legal or ritual context.
- v. 42: "Instruments of the songs of God" reflects the musical worship associated with Levitical service.
