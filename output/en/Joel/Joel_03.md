# Joel 3

¹ After this, I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh. Your sons and daughters will prophesy, your old men will dream dreams, your young men will see visions. ² Even on the slaves and the servants, I will pour out my Spirit in those days. ³ And I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth: blood and fire and pillars of smoke. ⁴ The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood, before the coming of the great and terrible day of the LORD. ⁵ And whoever calls on the name of the LORD will be rescued, for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be survivors, just as the LORD has said, and among the survivors will be those whom the LORD calls.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "rescued" translates מִלָּטֶה (mil'ateh), meaning to escape or be delivered; "survivors" reflects פְּלֵיטָה (p'leitah), which literally means escape or flight, but here is used in a salvific sense.
