# Joel 4

¹ For behold, in those days and at that time I will restore the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem. ² And I will gather all the nations and bring them down into the Valley of Jehoshaphat, and there I will judge them for my people and for my inheritance Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations and divided up my land. ³ And the nations have cast lots for my people, and have given a child for a harlot, and a girl for wine, and they have drunk. ⁴ And what are you to me, O Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you repaying me, and if you are repaying me, quickly I will return your repayment to your head. ⁵ For you have taken my silver and my gold, and you have brought my goodly treasures into your temples. ⁶ And you have sold the sons of Judah and the sons of Jerusalem to the sons of the Greeks, to remove them from their border. ⁷ I will rouse them from the place where you have sold them, and I will return your repayment to your head. ⁸ And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the sons of Judah, and they will sell them to distant captors, for the LORD has spoken. ⁹ Proclaim this among the nations: Consecrate war. Rouse the warriors, let them come up, all the men of war. ¹⁰ Beat your plowshares into swords and your pruning hooks into spears. Let the weak say, "I am strong." ¹¹ Assemble and come, all the nations around, and gather together there. The Lord has brought down his mighty ones. ¹² Let the nations awaken and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I will sit to judge all the nations around. ¹³ Sow for yourselves, for the harvest is ripe. Come, descend, for the granary is full. Pour out the yokes, for their iniquity is great. ¹⁴ Multitudes, multitudes, in the valley of decision. For near is the day of the LORD in the valley of decision. ¹⁵ The sun and the moon are darkened, and the stars withdraw their light. ¹⁶ And the LORD will roar from Zion, and give his voice from Jerusalem, and the heavens and the earth will tremble, and the LORD will be a refuge for his people and a fortress for the sons of Israel. ¹⁷ And you will know that I am the LORD your God, dwelling in Zion, my holy mountain. And Jerusalem will be holy, and strangers will pass through it no more. ¹⁸ And it will be in that day, the mountains will drip sweet wine, and the hills will flow with milk, and all the streams of Judah will flow with water. And a spring will go out from the house of the LORD and water the stream of the Acacias. ¹⁹ Egypt will become a desolation and Edom a desolate wilderness, because of the violence of the sons of Judah, who have shed innocent blood in their land. ²⁰ But Judah will dwell forever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation. ²¹ I will not cleanse the blood of my people that I did not cleanse, and the LORD dwells in Zion.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "In those days and at that time" reflects the Hebrew temporal clause, indicating a prophetic future time.  
- v. 3: "Cast lots for my people" is a reference to the practice of determining fates by lot, as in the Babylonian exile.  
- v. 19: "Violence of the sons of Judah" may refer to Judah's complicity in or responsibility for the suffering of others, though the text is ambiguous.  
- v. 21: The phrase "I will not cleanse the blood of my people that I did not cleanse" is intentionally repetitive and poetic, emphasizing the seriousness of unrepented sin.
