# Revelation 17

¹ One of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came to me and spoke to me, saying, "Come, I will show you the judgment of the great harlot who is seated on many waters, ² with whom the kings of the earth committed fornication, and those who live on the earth became drunk from the wine of her fornication." ³ He carried me away in the Spirit to a desert. And I saw a woman seated on a red beast, full of blasphemous names, having seven heads and ten horns. ⁴ The woman was dressed in purple and red, and adorned with gold, precious stones, and pearls, holding a golden cup in her hand full of abominations and the unclean things of her fornication, ⁵ and on her forehead was written a name as a mystery: Babylon the great, the mother of fornications and the abominations of the earth. ⁶ And I saw the woman drunk from the blood of the saints and from the blood of the witnesses of Jesus. And I marveled greatly at the sight I saw; ⁷ and the angel said to me, "Why are you amazed? I will tell you the mystery of the woman and of the beast who carries her, the one who has the seven heads and the ten horns. ⁸ The beast you saw was, but is not, and is about to rise from the abyss. And it will go into perdition. And those who live on the earth, whose names were not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world, will wonder, seeing the beast who was and is not and will appear. ⁹ Here is the wisdom: the seven heads are seven mountains, on which the woman is seated. And they are seven kings. ¹⁰ Five have fallen, one is, the other has not yet come, and when he comes, he must remain only a little while. ¹¹ And the beast who was and is not. He himself is the eighth, and he is from the seven, and he is going into perdition. ¹² And the ten horns you saw are ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom, but are given authority as kings for an hour with the beast. ¹³ These will have one mind, and they will give their power and authority to the beast. ¹⁴ These will wage war against the Lamb, and the Lamb will overcome them, for he is the Lord of lords and the King of kings, and those who are with him are called and chosen and faithful. ¹⁵ He said to me, "The waters you saw, on which the harlot is seated, are peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues. ¹⁶ And the ten horns you saw and the beast, they will hate the harlot, and they will make her desolate and naked, and they will eat her flesh, and they will burn her with fire; ¹⁷ for God gave them a mind to carry out their purpose, to do one purpose and to give their kingdom to the beast, until the words of God are fulfilled." ¹⁸ And the woman you saw is the great city that has authority over the kings of the earth.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the judgment of the great harlot" — the Greek uses "κρίμα" (krima), which can mean "judgment" or "crime"; "judgment" is chosen here as it aligns with the context of divine retribution.
- v. 5: "Babylon the great" — the name is written as it appears in the text, even though the context may imply a symbolic reference to a real city.
- v. 8: "the beast who was and is not" — the phrase is intentionally enigmatic and preserved in a way that reflects its mystery.
- v. 11: "he himself is the eighth" — the meaning of "eighth" is symbolic and not meant to be taken numerically; it denotes succession and completeness.
- v. 18: "the great city" — the identity of the city is left symbolic here, as the text does not specify it directly.
