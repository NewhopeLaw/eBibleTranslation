# Revelation 12

¹ And a great sign was seen in the sky, a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars, ² and she was pregnant; and she cried out, in pain and labor to give birth. ³ And another sign was seen in the sky, and behold, a great red dragon, with seven heads and ten horns, and on his heads seven crowns, ⁴ and his tail swept a third of the stars of the sky and cast them to the earth. And the dragon stood in front of the woman who was about to give birth, to devour her child when she gave birth. ⁵ And she gave birth to a son, a male, who was destined to rule all nations with a rod of iron. And the child was caught up to God and to his throne. ⁶ And the woman fled into the wilderness, where she had a place prepared by God, so that she could be nourished there for 1,260 days. ⁷ And a war broke out in the sky, Michael and his angels fighting against the dragon. And the dragon fought, and his angels, ⁸ but they did not prevail, and no place was found for them anymore in the sky. ⁹ And the great dragon, the ancient serpent, called the Devil and Satan, the deceiver of the whole world—was thrown down to the earth, and his angels with him were thrown down. ¹⁰ And I heard a loud voice in the sky saying, "Now has come salvation and power and the kingdom of our God and the authority of his Messiah, because the accuser of our brothers, who accused them before our God all day and night, has been thrown down. ¹¹ And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony, and they did not love their lives so much as to shrink from death. ¹² Therefore rejoice, you heavens and those who dwell in them. Woe to the earth and the sea, for the devil has come down to you, having great wrath, knowing that he has little time." ¹³ And when the dragon realized that he had been thrown down to the earth, he began to persecute the woman who had given birth to the male child. ¹⁴ And the woman was given the two wings of the great eagle, so that she could fly into the wilderness to her place, where she was nourished for a time, and times, and half a time, out of the presence of the serpent. ¹⁵ And the serpent cast out of his mouth a river like a stream behind the woman, to make her be swept away by the river. ¹⁶ And the earth helped the woman. And the earth opened her mouth and drank up the river that the dragon had cast out of his mouth; ¹⁷ and the dragon became angry with the woman and went away to make war with the rest of her offspring, those who keep the commandments of God and hold the testimony of Jesus. ¹⁸ And he stood on the seashore.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "no place was found for them anymore in the sky" — the phrase "τόπος εὑρέθη αὐτῶν" is ambiguous as to whether it refers to the angels or the dragon's forces; the context of defeat suggests the latter, hence the reference to the dragon and his angels no longer having a place in heaven.
- v. 15: "to make her be swept away by the river" — the verb "ποταμοφόρητον" is rare and not found elsewhere in Greek literature; it is rendered here as "be swept away by the river" to convey the metaphor of destruction.
- v. 17: "the rest of her offspring" — the phrase "τῶν λοιπῶν τοῦ σπέρματος αὐτῆς" is best read as "the rest of her offspring," referring to the followers of the woman who are not the literal child but the spiritual descendants.
