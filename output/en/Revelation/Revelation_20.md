# Revelation 20

¹ And I saw an angel coming down from heaven, holding the key to the abyss and a great chain in his hand. ² And he seized the dragon, the ancient serpent, who is the Devil and Satan, and bound him for a thousand years, ³ and threw him into the abyss, and shut it and set a seal upon it, so that he might no longer mislead the nations until the thousand years were completed. After that, he must be released for a brief time. ⁴ And I saw thrones, and those seated on them were given judgment, and the souls of those who had been put to death for the testimony of Jesus and for the word of God, and those who had not worshipped the beast or its image and had not received its mark on their forehead or on their hand. And they lived and reigned with Christ for a thousand years. ⁵ The rest of the dead did not live until the thousand years were completed. This is the first resurrection. ⁶ Blessed and holy is the one who has part in the first resurrection. Over these, the second death has no power, but they will be priests of God and of Christ, and they will reign with him for a thousand years. ⁷ And when the thousand years are completed, Satan will be released from his prison, ⁸ and he will go out to deceive the nations at the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them for battle, whose number is like the sand of the sea. ⁹ And they went up over the breadth of the earth, and surrounded the camp of the saints and the beloved city. And fire came down from heaven and consumed them; ¹⁰ and the deceiver who had led them astray was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false prophet were, and they will be tormented day and night forever and ever. ¹¹ And I saw a great white throne, and the one seated on it, from whose face the earth and the heavens fled away, and no place was found for them. ¹² And I saw the dead, the great and the small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the book of life. And the dead were judged according to what was written in the books, according to their deeds. ¹³ And the sea gave up the dead who were in it, and Death and Hades gave up the dead who were in them, and each one was judged according to their deeds. ¹⁴ And Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death, the lake of fire. ¹⁵ And if anyone was not found written in the book of life, he was thrown into the lake of fire.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the abyss" is a standard rendering of *ἄβυσσος* (abussos) in this context.
- v. 4: "the camp of the saints" renders *παρεμβολὴν τῶν ἁγίων*; "camp" captures the military imagery of *παρεμβολή*.
- v. 15: "the book of life" is a fixed term from earlier in Revelation, retained consistently.
