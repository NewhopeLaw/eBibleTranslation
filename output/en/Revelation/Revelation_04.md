# Revelation 4

¹ After these things I saw, and behold, a door opened in the sky, and the voice I had first heard, like a trumpet speaking to me, said, "Come up here, and I will show you what must come to be." After these things ² immediately I was in spirit, and behold, a throne was set in the sky, and seated upon the throne, ³ and the one seated was like the appearance of a lapis lazuli and sardius, and around the throne a rainbow like the appearance of an emerald. ⁴ Around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and seated upon the thrones were twenty-four elders, wrapped in white robes, with golden crowns on their heads. ⁵ And from the throne came forth lightning and voices and thunder, and before the throne seven lamps of fire burning, which are the seven spirits of God, ⁶ and before the throne was as a sea of glass, like crystal. And in the middle of the throne and around the throne were four living creatures full of eyes before and behind; ⁷ and the first living creature was like a lion, and the second living creature was like a bull, and the third living creature had the face like a human, and the fourth living creature was like a flying eagle; ⁸ and the four living creatures, each one having six wings, full of eyes around and within, day and night without rest, saying, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord, the God, the Almighty, who was, and who is, and who is coming." ⁹ And when the living creatures give glory and honor and thanksgiving to the one seated on the throne, the living One for the ages of the ages, ¹⁰ the twenty-four elders will fall before the one seated on the throne, and worship the living One for the ages of the ages, and cast their crowns before the throne, saying, ¹¹ "You are worthy, the Lord and our God, to receive the glory and the honor and the power, because you created all things, and by your will they were and were created."

---

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "After these things" is repeated at the start and end of the verse, likely for rhetorical emphasis. The second "after these things" is included in the translation to reflect the original structure.
- v. 3: The description of the one seated on the throne is symbolic and poetic. "Lapis lazuli and sardius" are used for color and preciousness, not as literal materials.
- v. 8: The repetition of "holy" three times is retained to match the original Greek, which uses the same form for emphasis.
