# Revelation 19

¹ After these things, I heard a great voice of a large crowd in heaven saying, "Hallelujah! Salvation and glory and power belong to our God, ² for his judgments are true and just, because he judged the great prostitute who corrupted the earth with her fornication, and he avenged the blood of his servants on her." ³ And a second time they said, "Hallelujah! For the smoke of her goes up to the ages of the ages." ⁴ And the twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell down and worshiped God who was seated on the throne, saying, "Amen, Hallelujah." ⁵ And a voice came from the throne saying, "Give praise to our God, all of his servants, and all who fear him, the small and the great." ⁶ And I heard a voice like the sound of a large crowd, like the sound of many waters, and like the sound of mighty thunderclaps, saying, "Hallelujah, for the Lord our God has come to reign. ⁷ Let us rejoice and be glad, and give him glory, for the wedding of the Lamb has come, and his bride has made herself ready, ⁸ and she was given to wear a bright and pure robe of fine linen, for the fine linen is the righteous deeds of the saints." ⁹ And he said to me, "Write: 'Blessed are those who have been invited to the wedding feast of the Lamb.' And he said to me, "These are the true words of God." ¹⁰ And I fell down at his feet to worship him. And he said to me, "See that you do not do that! I am a fellow servant with you and with your brothers who have the testimony of Jesus. Worship God, for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." ¹¹ And I saw the heaven opened, and behold, a white horse, and the one seated on it is called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he judges and wages war. ¹² His eyes are a flame of fire, and on his head are many crowns, and on him is written a name known only to himself, ¹³ and he was wrapped in a robe dipped in blood, and his name was called The Word of God. ¹⁴ And the armies in heaven followed him on white horses, clothed in white and pure linen. ¹⁵ And from his mouth comes a sharp sword, so that with it he may strike the nations, and he will shepherd them with an iron rod, and he himself will trample the winepress of the wrath of the God of all power. ¹⁶ And on his robe and on his thigh is written a name: King of kings and Lord of lords. ¹⁷ And I saw an angel standing in the sun, and with a great voice he called out to all the birds flying in midheaven, "Come, gather for the great supper of God, ¹⁸ so that you may eat the flesh of kings and the flesh of generals and the flesh of the strong and the flesh of horses and of those seated on them, and the flesh of all free and all slaves and of the small and the great." ¹⁹ And I saw the beast and the kings of the earth and their armies gathered together to make war against the one seated on the white horse and against his army. ²⁰ And the beast was captured, and with him the false prophet who had done the signs in his presence, by which he had led astray those who had received the mark of the beast and those who had worshiped his image. And the two were thrown alive into the lake of fire that burns with sulfur. ²¹ And the rest were killed by the sword that came from the mouth of the one seated on the white horse, and all the birds were gorged with their flesh.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "The Word of God" is a direct translation of ὁ Λόγος τοῦ Θεοῦ. This phrase is clearly a title and not a reference to the prologue of John.
- v. 17–18: The angel's call to the birds to the "great supper of God" is interpreted as a symbolic vision, not a literal invitation. The reference to eating flesh is understood as metaphorical, representing the judgment and destruction of the wicked.
- v. 20: "Lake of fire that burns with sulfur" is a translation of λίμνη τοῦ πυρὸς τῆς καιομένης ἐν θείῳ, reflecting both the literal and symbolic meanings of this apocalyptic imagery.
