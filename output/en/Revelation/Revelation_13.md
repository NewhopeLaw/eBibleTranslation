# Revelation 13

¹ And I saw a beast rising from the sea, having ten horns and seven heads, with ten crowns on its horns, and on its heads were blasphemous names. ² The beast I saw was like a leopard, with feet like a bear, and a mouth like a lion’s mouth. And the dragon gave to it his power and his throne and great authority. ³ One of its heads appeared as if it had been slain to death, and the wound of death was healed. And the whole earth marveled after the beast, ⁴ and they bowed down to the dragon because he gave to the beast his authority, and they bowed down to the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast, and who is able to make war with it?" ⁵ And it was given a mouth to speak great things and blasphemies, and it was given authority to act for forty-two months. ⁶ And it opened its mouth to blaspheme against God, to blaspheme His name and His dwelling, those who dwell in heaven. ⁷ And it was given authority to make war with the saints and to conquer them, and it was given authority over every tribe and people and language and nation. ⁸ And all who dwell on the earth will worship it, all whose names have not been written in the book of life of the Lamb who was slain from the foundation of the world. ⁹ If anyone has ears, let him hear. ¹⁰ If anyone is taken into captivity, into captivity he will go. If anyone is to be slain with the sword, with the sword he must be slain. This is the endurance and the faith of the saints. ¹¹ And I saw another beast rising from the earth, having two horns like a lamb and speaking like a dragon. ¹² And it exercises all the authority of the first beast in front of it. And it causes the earth and those who live in it to worship the first beast, whose wound of death was healed. ¹³ And it performs great signs, even making fire come down from heaven to the earth in front of people. ¹⁴ And it deceives those who live on the earth by the signs that were given to it to do in front of the beast, saying to those who live on the earth to make an image to the beast who was wounded by the sword and lived. ¹⁵ And it was given to give breath to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast could also speak and do so that whoever does not worship the image of the beast would be killed. ¹⁶ And it causes all, the small and the great, the rich and the poor, the free and the slaves, to be given a mark on their right hand or on their forehead, ¹⁷ so that no one would be able to buy or sell unless they had the mark, the name of the beast or the number of its name. ¹⁸ Here is wisdom. Let the one who has understanding count the number of the beast, for it is the number of a human; and its number is six hundred sixty-six.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "the Lamb who was slain from the foundation of the world" — the phrase "from the foundation of the world" (ἀπὸ καταβολῆς κόσμου) is placed with the Lamb rather than the book of life, reflecting the Greek structure, which implies that the Lamb was slain since the world's foundation in a theological sense.
- v. 15: "give breath to the image" — "πνεῦμα" is rendered as "breath" to maintain consistency with the imagery of life being given to an image, rather than "Spirit" which would be more appropriate elsewhere.
