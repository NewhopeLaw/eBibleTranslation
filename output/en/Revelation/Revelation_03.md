# Revelation 3

¹ Write to the angel of the assembly in Sardis: These things says the one holding the seven spirits of God and the seven stars: I know your works, that you have a name as if you were alive, but you are dead. ² Be alert and strengthen what remains, which is about to die, for I have not found your works completed before my God. ³ Therefore, remember how you received and heard, and hold fast and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief, and you will not know what hour I will come upon you. ⁴ Yet you have a few names in Sardis who have not defiled their garments, and they will walk with me in white, for they are worthy. ⁵ The one who conquers will thus be clothed in garments of white, and I will not erase his name from the book of life, and I will confess his name before my Father and before his angels. ⁶ The one who has ears, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. ⁷ Write to the angel of the assembly in Philadelphia: These things says the holy one, the true one, the one holding the key of David, the one opening and no one will shut, and shutting and no one will open: ⁸ I know your works—behold, I have set before you an open door, which no one can shut—because you have little power, and you have kept my word, and you have not denied my name. ⁹ Behold, I will cause those from the synagogue of Satan, who say they are Jews, but are not, but are liars—behold, I will make them come and bow down before your feet, and they will know that I love you. ¹⁰ Because you have kept my word of endurance, I will keep you from the hour of testing that is coming upon the whole world, to test those who live on the earth. ¹¹ I am coming soon. Hold fast what you have, so that no one may take your crown. ¹² The one who conquers, I will make him a pillar in my temple, and he will never go out again, and I will write on him the name of my God and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem, which is coming down from my God out of heaven, and my new name. ¹³ The one who has ears, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. ¹⁴ Write to the angel of the assembly in Laodicea: These things says the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of God's creation: ¹⁵ I know your works. You are neither cold nor hot. If you were cold, I would chill you, or if you were hot, I would warm you. ¹⁶ So, because you are lukewarm and neither hot nor cold, I will spit you out of my mouth. ¹⁷ Because you say, I am rich, and have grown rich, and have no need, and you do not know that you are wretched, pitiable, poor, blind, and naked, ¹⁸ I advise you to buy from me gold refined in fire so that you may be rich, and white garments so that you may be wrapped and your shame of nakedness may not be exposed, and ointment to anoint your eyes so that you may see. ¹⁹ I reprove and discipline those whom I love. So then, be zealous and repent. ²⁰ Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and dine with him, and he with me. ²¹ The one who conquers I will give to sit with me on my throne, just as I have conquered and sat with my Father on his throne. ²² The one who has ears, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "I know your works, that you have a name as if you were alive, but you are dead" — the Greek uses a metaphor of having a name but lacking life, which is rendered as a contrast between appearance and reality.
- v. 16: "I will spit you out of my mouth" — the metaphor of divine rejection through spitting is used in its full force, as found in the source.
- v. 20: "I stand at the door and knock" — the imagery is retained as found in the Greek, with the verb "κρούω" (krouō) rendered as "knock."
- v. 21: "I will give to sit with me on my throne" — the future passive construction is rendered as a direct promise.
