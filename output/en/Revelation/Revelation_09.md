# Revelation 9

¹ The fifth angel sounded, and I saw a star fall from the sky to the earth, and it was given the key to the pit of the abyss. ² He opened the pit of the abyss, and smoke rose up from the pit like the smoke of a large furnace, and the sun and the air were darkened by the smoke from the pit. ³ And from the smoke came locusts onto the earth, and they were given authority like that which the scorpions of the earth have. ⁴ And it was commanded that they should not harm the grass of the earth, or any green thing, or any tree, except for people who did not have the seal of God on their foreheads. ⁵ And it was given to them not to kill these people, but to torment them for five months, and the torment of them was like the torment of a scorpion when it strikes a person. ⁶ And in those days people will seek death and will not find it; and they will long to die, but death will flee from them. ⁷ The creatures looked like horses prepared for battle, and on their heads were crowns like gold, and their faces were like human faces, ⁸ and they had hair like women's hair, and their teeth were like those of lions, ⁹ and they had breastplates like iron breastplates, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of many chariots of horses rushing into battle; ¹⁰ and they had tails like scorpions, and stingers, and in their tails was the authority to torment people for five months. ¹¹ They had over them a king, the angel of the abyss. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek he is called Apollyon. ¹² The first woe has passed; behold, two more woes are coming after these. ¹³ The sixth angel sounded, and I heard a voice from one of the horns of the golden altar that is before God, ¹⁴ saying to the sixth angel, who had the trumpet: "Loose the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates." ¹⁵ And the four angels were released, who had been prepared for an hour and a day and a month and a year, to kill a third of the people. ¹⁶ The number of the hippopotam-like troops was two hundred million; I heard the number of them. ¹⁷ And thus I saw the horses in the vision, and those sitting on them, having breastplates of fire and hyacinth and brimstone; and the heads of the horses were like the heads of lions, and from the mouths of them came fire and smoke and brimstone. ¹⁸ From these three plagues a third of the people were killed, from the fire and the smoke and the brimstone coming from the mouths of them. ¹⁹ For the power of the horses is in their mouths and in their tails, for their tails are like serpents, having heads, and with them they do wrong. ²⁰ The rest of the people, who had not been killed in these plagues, did not repent of the works of their hands, so as not to worship demons and the golden and silver and bronze and stone and wooden images, which cannot see or hear or walk, ²¹ and they did not repent of their murders, nor of their sorcery, nor of their sexual immorality, nor of their thefts.

---

Notes
- v. 11: The Hebrew name "Abaddon" and the Greek "Apollyon" are both retained in transliteration, as per the glossary.
- v. 16: "Two hundred million" is a direct translation of δισμυριάδες μυριάδων (two myriads of myriads), calculated as 200,000,000.
