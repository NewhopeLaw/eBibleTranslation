# John 4

¹ When Jesus learned that the Pharisees heard that he was making and baptizing more disciples than John— ² though in fact Jesus himself was not baptizing but his disciples— ³ he left Judea and went back to Galilee. ⁴ He had to go through Samaria. ⁵ So he came to a town in Samaria called Sychar, near the plot of land that Jacob gave to his son Joseph. ⁶ Jacob's well was there, and Jesus, tired from his journey, sat down by the well. It was about the sixth hour. ⁷ A Samaritan woman came to draw water. Jesus said to her, "Give me a drink." ⁸ For his disciples had gone into the town to buy food. ⁹ The Samaritan woman replied, "How is it that you, a Jew, ask me, a Samaritan woman, for a drink? For Jews do not share meals or drink with Samaritans." ¹⁰ Jesus answered her, "If you knew the gift of God and who it is that is saying to you, 'Give me a drink,' you would have asked him and he would have given you living water." ¹¹ The woman said to him, "Sir, you have no bucket, and the well is deep. Where then do you get this living water?" ¹² "Are you greater than our father Jacob, who gave us this well and drank from it, as did his sons and his livestock?" ¹³ Jesus answered her, "Everyone who drinks this water will be thirsty again," ¹⁴ "but whoever drinks the water I give them will never be thirsty again. The water I give becomes in them a spring of water welling up to eternal life." ¹⁵ The woman said to him, "Sir, give me this water, so that I won't be thirsty nor have to come here to draw water." ¹⁶ Jesus said to her, "Go, call your husband and then come here." ¹⁷ The woman replied, "I have no husband." Jesus said to her, "You said, 'I have no husband.'" ¹⁸ "Indeed you have had five husbands, and the one you now have is not your husband. You have spoken truly." ¹⁹ The woman said to him, "Sir, I see that you are a prophet." ²⁰ "Our ancestors worshiped on this mountain, but you Jews claim that the place where worship should occur is in Jerusalem." ²¹ Jesus said to her, "Believe me, woman, a time is coming when you will worship the Father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem." ²² "You Samaritans worship what you do not know; we Jews worship what we know, because salvation comes from the Jews." ²³ "But a time is coming, and is now here, when true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth. Indeed, the Father seeks such people to worship him." ²⁴ "God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and in truth." ²⁵ The woman said to him, "I know that the Messiah is coming, the one called the Christ. When he comes, he will tell us everything." ²⁶ Jesus said to her, "I am he, the one speaking to you." ²⁷ Just then his disciples returned. They were surprised he was speaking with a woman, but no one asked, "What are you seeking?" or "Why are you talking with her?" ²⁸ The woman left her water jar and went into the town. She said to the people, ²⁹ "Come, see a man who told me everything I ever did. Could this be the Christ?" ³⁰ They left the town and went to meet him. ³¹ Meanwhile, the disciples were urging him, "Rabbi, eat something." ³² But Jesus said to them, "I have food to eat that you do not know about." ³³ The disciples said to each other, "Has someone brought him something to eat?" ³⁴ Jesus said to them, "My food is to do the will of the one who sent me and to finish his work." ³⁵ "Do you not say, 'There are still four months until harvest'? I tell you, look up and see the fields, for they are already white for harvest." ³⁶ "The one who harvests receives a wage and gathers fruit for eternal life, so that the sower and the reaper can rejoice together." ³⁷ "For this saying is true: One sows, and another harvests." ³⁸ "I sent you to reap what you did not labor for. Others labored, and you have entered into their labor." ³⁹ Many Samaritans from that town believed in him because of the woman's testimony, "He told me everything I ever did." ⁴⁰ So when the Samaritans came to him, they asked him to stay with them, and he stayed there for two days. ⁴¹ And through his words many more believed. ⁴² They said to the woman, "We no longer believe because of you; we have heard for ourselves and know that this is truly the Savior of the world." ⁴³ After the two days he left for Galilee. ⁴⁴ For Jesus himself had testified that a prophet has no honor in his own hometown. ⁴⁵ When he arrived in Galilee, the Galileans welcomed him, having seen all he had done in Jerusalem at the festival. They had also gone to the festival themselves. ⁴⁶ So he went again to Cana in Galilee, where he had turned water into wine. And there was a royal official whose son was ill in Capernaum. ⁴⁷ When the man heard that Jesus had come from Judea to Galilee, he went to him and asked him to come down and heal his son, for the boy was near death. ⁴⁸ Jesus said to him, "Unless you see signs and wonders, you will not believe." ⁴⁹ The official said to him, "Sir, come down before my child dies." ⁵⁰ Jesus said to him, "Go; your son will live." The man believed the word Jesus spoke to him and went on his way. ⁵¹ While he was going down, his servants met him and said, "Your son is alive." ⁵² He asked them what hour the boy had improved. They said to him, "Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him." ⁵³ Then the father realized that this was the very hour Jesus had said to him, "Your son will live." So he and his whole household believed. ⁵⁴ This was the second sign Jesus did after coming from Judea to Galilee.

Notes
- v. 24: "God is spirit" — this is a literal translation of πνεῦμα ὁ θεός. The abstract "spirit" is retained to reflect the divine nature as non-corporeal.
- v. 54: "second sign" — the reference to a "sign" (σημεῖον) is retained in line with the narrative's use of this term for Jesus' miracles.
