# John 13

¹ Before the festival of Passover, Jesus knew that his hour had come to leave this world and go to the Father. Having loved those who were his own in the world to the end, he loved them. ² Now during the meal, the devil had already placed it into Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, to betray him. ³ Jesus, knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands, and that he had come from God and was going to God, ⁴ rose from the meal, took off his outer garment, and wrapped a cloth around himself. ⁵ Then he poured water into a basin and began to wash the disciples' feet and to wipe them with the cloth he had wrapped around himself. ⁶ He came to Simon Peter. Peter said to him, "Lord, are you going to wash my feet?" ⁷ Jesus answered him, "You do not understand what I am doing now, but you will understand afterward." ⁸ Peter said to him, "You will never wash my feet!" Jesus answered him, "Unless I wash you, you have no share with me." ⁹ Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, not only my feet, but also my hands and my head!" ¹⁰ Jesus said to him, "The one who has bathed needs nothing except to have their feet washed, for they are completely clean. And you are clean, though not all of you." ¹¹ For he knew who was going to betray him; therefore he said, "Not all of you are clean." ¹² When he had washed their feet and taken up his garments and returned to his place, he said to them, "Do you know what I have done for you?" ¹³ You call me Teacher and Lord, and you are right, for so I am. ¹⁴ If then I, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also must wash one another's feet. ¹⁵ I have given you an example so that you should do as I have done to you. ¹⁶ Amen, amen, I say to you, no slave is greater than their master, nor is any messenger greater than the one who sent them. ¹⁷ If you know these things, you are blessed if you do them. ¹⁸ I am not speaking about all of you. I know the ones I have chosen, but this is so that the scripture may be fulfilled: The one who eats my bread has lifted up his heel against me. ¹⁹ I tell you this now, before it happens, so that when it happens, you may believe that I am. ²⁰ Amen, amen, I say to you, whoever receives anyone I send receives me, and whoever receives me receives the one who sent me. ²¹ After saying these things, Jesus was troubled in spirit and testified, "Amen, amen, I say to you, one of you will betray me." ²² The disciples looked at one another, uncertain about whom he was speaking. ²³ One of the disciples, the one reclining on Jesus' chest whom Jesus loved, ²⁴ Simon Peter nudged him and asked, "Tell me who he is, about whom he is speaking." ²⁵ Leaning back in that place on Jesus' chest, that one said to him, "Lord, who is it?" ²⁶ Jesus answered, "It is the one to whom I will give the morsel I have dipped." So he dipped the morsel and gave it to Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon. ²⁷ Then, after receiving the morsel, Satan entered into him. Jesus said to him, "Do what you must do quickly." ²⁸ Now none of those reclining with him knew why Jesus said this to him, ²⁹ for some thought that since Judas had the money bag, Jesus was telling him to buy what was needed for the festival, or to give something to the poor. ³⁰ So he took the morsel and went out immediately. And it was night. ³¹ Then Jesus said, "Now the Son of Man is glorified, and God is glorified in him. ³² If God is glorified in him, God will glorify him in himself, and he will glorify him immediately." ³³ Little children, I am with you a little while longer. You will seek me, and just as I said to the Jews, 'Where I am going, you cannot come,' I say to you now. ³⁴ I give you a new command: that you love one another, just as I have loved you, so that you also should love one another. ³⁵ By this all will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another." ³⁶ Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, where are you going?" Jesus answered him, "Where I am going, you cannot follow me now, but you will follow later." ³⁷ Peter said to him, "Lord, why can I not follow you now? I will give my life for you." ³⁸ Jesus answered, "Will you give your life for me? Amen, amen, I tell you, the rooster will not crow until you deny me three times."

---

Notes
- v. 10: "The one who has bathed" is a metaphor for spiritual cleansing, hence "has bathed" rather than "has been baptized."
- v. 18: "The one who eats my bread" is a reference to the shared meal, not to the Eucharist specifically.
- v. 20: "Whoever receives anyone I send" refers to the acceptance of Jesus' messengers, not necessarily to the modern concept of "receiving" believers.
- v. 27: "Satan entered into him" is a direct translation of the Greek, indicating the spiritual moment of Judas' betrayal.
- v. 38: "Amen, amen" is used to emphasize Jesus' solemn declaration, a common Johannine formula.
