# John 7

¹ After this, Jesus walked in Galilee, for he did not want to walk in Judea, because the Jews were seeking to kill him. ² Now the feast of the Jews was near, the Feast of Booths. ³ His brothers said to him, "Leave from here and go to Judea, so that your disciples may also see the works you are doing. ⁴ For no one does anything in secret and seeks to be known publicly. If you are doing these things, reveal yourself to the world." ⁵ For even his brothers did not believe in him. ⁶ Jesus therefore said to them, "My time has not yet come, but yours is always ready. ⁷ The world cannot hate you, but it hates me, because I testify that its works are evil. ⁸ Go up to the feast. I am not going up to this feast, because my time has not yet been fulfilled." ⁹ Having said these things, he stayed in Galilee. ¹⁰ But when his brothers had gone up to the feast, then he also went up, not publicly but in secret. ¹¹ The Jews were therefore seeking him at the feast and saying, "Where is that one?" ¹² And there was much discussion about him among the crowds. Some said, "He is a good man," while others said, "No, but he leads the people astray." ¹³ Yet no one spoke openly about him for fear of the Jews. ¹⁴ Now Jesus taught in the temple as the feast was going on. ¹⁵ The Jews were astonished, saying, "How does this man know the scriptures without having been taught?" ¹⁶ Jesus answered them and said, "My teaching is not my own but is from the one who sent me. ¹⁷ If anyone is willing to do the will of the one who sent me, he will know whether my teaching is from God or if I am speaking from myself. ¹⁸ The one who speaks from himself seeks his own glory; the one who seeks the glory of the one who sent him is true and there is no unrighteousness in him. ¹⁹ Did not Moses give you the law? Yet none of you keeps the law. Why are you trying to kill me?" ²⁰ The crowd answered, "You have a demon. Who is trying to kill you?" ²¹ Jesus answered them and said, "One work I did, and all of you marvel at it. ²² Moses gave you circumcision—not that it is from Moses but from the ancestors—and you circumcise a man on the Sabbath. ²³ If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses may not be broken, are you angry with me for making a whole man well on the Sabbath? ²⁴ Do not judge by appearances, but judge with righteous judgment." ²⁵ Some from Jerusalem were saying, "Is not this the one they are seeking to kill? ²⁶ And look, he is speaking openly, and they say nothing to him. Could the rulers really know that this is the Messiah? ²⁷ But we know where this one is from. When the Messiah comes, no one will know where he is from." ²⁸ Jesus cried out while teaching in the temple, saying, "You know me and also know where I am from. I did not come on my own, but the one who is true sent me, the one you do not know. ²⁹ I know him, because I am from him, and he sent me." ³⁰ They sought to seize him, but no one laid a hand on him, because his hour had not yet come. ³¹ Yet many from the crowd believed in him, and said, "When the Messiah comes, will he do more signs than this man has done?" ³² The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about him, and the chief priests and the Pharisees sent officers to arrest him. ³³ Jesus said, "A little while longer I am with you, and then I go to the one who sent me. ³⁴ You will seek me and will not find me, and where I am, you cannot come." ³⁵ The Jews said to one another, "Where is this man going that we will not find him? Is he going to teach the Gentiles? What is this word he said, 'You will seek me and will not find me, and where I am, you cannot come'?" ³⁶ On the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried out, saying, "If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink. ³⁷ The one who believes in me, just as the scripture said, rivers of living water will flow from his innermost being." ³⁸ He said this about the Spirit, which those who believed in him were to receive, for the Spirit was not yet given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified. ³⁹ Some from the crowd who heard these words said, "This is truly the prophet. ⁴⁰ Others said, "This is the Messiah." But others said, "Does the Messiah not come from Galilee? ⁴¹ Is it not written in the scriptures that the Messiah is to come from the line of David, from the town of Bethlehem where David was?" ⁴² Thus a division arose among the crowd because of him. ⁴³ Some of them wanted to arrest him, but no one laid hands on him. ⁴⁴ The officers went to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, "Why did you not bring him?" ⁴⁵ The officers answered, "No man ever spoke like this man." ⁴⁶ The Pharisees answered them, "Have you also been deceived? ⁴⁷ Has any one of the rulers or the Pharisees believed in him? ⁴⁸ But this crowd, which does not know the law, is cursed." ⁴⁹ Nicodemus, who had come to them before, one of their own, said to them, ⁵⁰ "Does our law judge a man without first hearing him and learning what he is doing?" ⁵¹ They answered and said to him, "Are you also from Galilee? Investigate and see that a prophet does not rise from Galilee." ⁵² Nicodemus said nothing more to them. ⁵³ Then each went to his own home.

---

Notes
- v. 30: "his hour had not yet come" — a reference to the concept of the appointed time for Jesus' crucifixion and resurrection.
- v. 35: "What is this word he said..." — the rhetorical question reflects the confusion and misunderstanding among the Jews.
- v. 38: "the Spirit, which those who believed in him were to receive" — the reference to the Spirit indicates the future outpouring of the Holy Spirit after Jesus' ascension.
