# John 7

¹ After these things, Jesus was walking in Galilee, because he did not want to walk in Judea, for the Jews were seeking to kill him. ² Now the Jewish festival of Booths was near. ³ His brothers therefore said to him, “Go away from here and go into Judea, so that your disciples may also see the works you do. ⁴ For no one does anything in secret and seeks to be known openly. If you do these things, reveal yourself to the world.” ⁵ For even his brothers did not believe in him. ⁶ Jesus therefore said to them, “My time has not yet come, but your time is always ready. ⁷ The world cannot hate you, but it hates me, because I testify about it that its works are evil. ⁸ You go up to the festival. I am not going up to this festival, because my time has not yet been fulfilled.” ⁹ After saying these things, he remained in Galilee. ¹⁰ But when his brothers had gone up to the festival, then he also went up, not openly, but as in secret. ¹¹ The Jews were therefore looking for him at the festival and saying, “Where is that man?” ¹² And there was much murmuring among the crowds about him. Some said, “He is a good man,” but others said, “No, but he is leading the people astray.” ¹³ Yet no one spoke openly about him, for fear of the Jews. ¹⁴ Now at the festival, when already the middle of the feast was at hand, Jesus went up into the temple and was teaching. ¹⁵ The Jews were therefore amazed, saying, “How does this man know the scriptures without having been taught?” ¹⁶ Jesus answered them and said, “My teaching is not my own, but the one who sent me. ¹⁷ If anyone wants to do his will, he will know about the teaching whether it is from God or whether I speak from myself. ¹⁸ The one who speaks from himself seeks his own glory. The one who seeks the glory of the one who sent him is true, and there is no iniquity in him. ¹⁹ Did Moses not give you the law? Yet none of you keep the law. Why are you trying to kill me?” ²⁰ The crowd answered, “You have a demon. Who is seeking to kill you?” ²¹ Jesus answered them and said, “One work I did, and you all marvel. ²² Moses gave you the circumcision—not because it is from Moses, but from the ancestors—and you circumcise a man on the Sabbath. ²³ If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses will not be broken, are you angry with me for making a man entirely well on the Sabbath?” ²⁴ Do not judge according to appearance, but judge with right judgment. ²⁵ Some of the people of Jerusalem were therefore saying, “Is this not the man they are seeking to kill? ²⁶ And look, he speaks openly, and yet nothing is said to him. Perhaps the rulers really know that this is the Messiah.” ²⁷ But we know where he is from. When the Messiah comes, no one knows where he is from.” ²⁸ Then Jesus cried out while teaching in the temple, saying, “You know me and also know where I am from. I did not come on my own, but the one who is true has sent me, whom you do not know. ²⁹ I know him, because I am from him, and he sent me.” ³⁰ Then they tried to seize him, but no one laid a hand on him, because his hour had not yet come. ³¹ Yet many from the crowd believed in him and said, “When the Messiah comes, will he do more signs than this man has done?” ³² The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about him, and the chief priests and Pharisees sent officers to seize him. ³³ Jesus therefore said, “A little while longer I am with you, and then I go to him who sent me. ³⁴ You will seek me, and you will not find me, and where I am, you cannot come.” ³⁵ The Jews said to one another, “Where is this man going that we will not find him? Could it be that he is going to the dispersed among the Greeks and teaching the Greeks?” ³⁶ What is this word he said, ‘You will seek me and will not find me, and where I am, you cannot come’?” ³⁷ On the last and greatest day of the festival, Jesus stood and cried out, saying, “If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink. ³⁸ The one who believes in me, just as the Scripture said, rivers of living water will flow from his innermost being.” ³⁹ Now this he said about the Spirit, whom those who believed in him would receive, for the Spirit had not yet been given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified. ⁴⁰ Those in the crowd who heard these words said, “This is truly the prophet.” ⁴¹ Others said, “This is the Messiah.” Some said, “But surely the Messiah does not come from Galilee?” ⁴² Has not the Scripture said that the Messiah comes from the offspring of David and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?” ⁴³ So there was a division among the crowd because of him. ⁴⁴ Some of them wanted to seize him, but no one laid a hand on him. ⁴⁵ The officers went to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, “Why did you not bring him?” ⁴⁶ The officers answered, “No man has ever spoken like this man.” ⁴⁷ The Pharisees answered them, “Have you also been led astray? ⁴⁸ Has any one of the rulers or the Pharisees believed in him? ⁴⁹ But this crowd, which does not know the law, is under a curse.” ⁵⁰ Nicodemus, the one who had come to him earlier, said to them, being one of them: ⁵¹ “Does our law judge a man without first hearing him and learning what he does?” ⁵² They answered and said to him, “Are you also from Galilee? Investigate and see that a prophet does not arise from Galilee.” ⁵³ So each went to his own house.
