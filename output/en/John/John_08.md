# John 8

¹ Jesus went to the hill called the Olive Grove. ² At dawn he returned to the temple, and a crowd gathered around him. He sat down and began to teach them. ³ The scribes and the Pharisees brought a woman who had been caught in the act of committing adultery. They placed her in the center of the courtyard. ⁴ They said to Jesus, testing him, "Teacher, this woman was caught in the act of adultery. ⁵ According to the law given by Moses, such women must be stoned. So what do you say?" ⁶ They said this to trap him, so they could accuse him. But Jesus bent down and began to write on the ground with his finger, not seeming to respond. ⁷ When they kept asking, he stood up and said, "Let the one among you who is free from sin be the first to throw a stone at her." ⁸ Then he bent down again and continued writing on the ground. ⁹ The people, hearing this, were convicted by their own consciences and left one by one, starting with the oldest. Only Jesus and the woman remained in the center. ¹⁰ Jesus stood up and said to her, "Where are your accusers? Has no one condemned you?" ¹¹ She replied, "No one, Lord." Jesus said, "Neither do I condemn you. Go now, and from this moment on, do not sin." ¹² Jesus spoke to them again, saying, "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life." ¹³ The Pharisees said to him, "You are giving testimony about yourself; your testimony is not true." ¹⁴ Jesus replied, "Even if I give testimony about myself, my testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I am going. You do not know where I come from or where I am going. ¹⁵ You judge by appearances, but I do not judge anyone. ¹⁶ And if I do judge, my judgment is true, because I am not alone in my judgment, but I and the one who sent me are one. ¹⁷ In your law it is written that the testimony of two people is true. ¹⁸ I am the one who gives testimony about myself, and the one who sent me gives testimony about me." ¹⁹ They asked him, "Where is your father?" Jesus replied, "You do not know me or my father. If you knew me, you would know my father as well." ²⁰ Jesus spoke these words while teaching in the treasury chamber of the temple. No one arrested him, because his time had not yet come. ²¹ Jesus said to them again, "I am going away, and you will seek me, but you will die in your sin. You cannot come where I am going." ²² The Jews said, "Is he going to kill himself? He says, 'Where I am going, you cannot come.'" ²³ Jesus answered, "You belong to what is below, I belong to what is above. You belong to this world, I do not. ²⁴ I have already told you that you will die in your sins, for unless you believe that I am, you will die in your sins." ²⁵ They asked him, "Who are you?" Jesus replied, "Have I told you from the beginning what I am?" ²⁶ "I have much to say about you and much to judge. But the one who sent me is true, and I speak what I have heard from him to the world." ²⁷ They did not realize that he was speaking about God the Father. ²⁸ Jesus said, "When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am, and that I do nothing on my own but speak just as the Father taught me. ²⁹ The one who sent me is with me; he has not left me alone, because I always do what pleases him." ³⁰ As he spoke these words, many believed in him. ³¹ Jesus said to the Jews who had believed in him, "If you remain in my word, you are truly my disciples, ³² and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free." ³³ They replied, "We are descendants of Abraham and have never been enslaved to anyone. How can you say, 'You will be free'?" ³⁴ Jesus answered, "Truly, truly, I tell you, everyone who practices sin is a slave to sin. ³⁵ A slave does not remain in a house forever, but the son does. ³⁶ So if the son sets you free, you truly will be free. ³⁷ I know that you are descendants of Abraham, but you are trying to kill me because my word does not find a place in you. ³⁸ I speak what I have seen from the Father, and you do what you heard from your father." ³⁹ They replied, "Our father is Abraham." Jesus said to them, "If you were children of Abraham, you would do the works of Abraham. ⁴⁰ But now you are trying to kill me, a man who has told you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham did not do this. ⁴¹ You are doing the works of your father." They said, "We are not born of fornication; we have one Father, God." ⁴² Jesus said to them, "If God were truly your Father, you would love me, for I came from God and I am here. I did not come on my own, but he sent me. ⁴³ Why do you not understand what I say? Because you cannot hear my word. ⁴⁴ You belong to your father the devil, and you want to do what he desires. That man was a murderer from the beginning and has never stood in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own resources, for he is a liar and the father of lies. ⁴⁵ But I speak the truth, and you do not believe me. ⁴⁶ Which of you can accuse me of sin? If I speak the truth, why do you not believe me? ⁴⁷ The one who is from God hears the words of God. That is why you do not hear them, because you are not from God." ⁴⁸ The Jews replied, "Are we not right in saying that you are a Samaritan and have a demon?" ⁴⁹ Jesus answered, "I do not have a demon, but I honor my Father, and you dishonor me. ⁵⁰ I do not seek glory for myself; there is one who seeks and will judge." ⁵¹ Jesus said, "Truly, truly, I tell you, if anyone keeps my word, he will never see death." ⁵² The Jews said, "Now we know you have a demon. Abraham died, and so did the prophets, yet you say, 'If anyone keeps my word, he will never taste death.' ⁵³ Are you greater than our father Abraham, who died? And the prophets have died. Who do you claim to be?" ⁵⁴ Jesus replied, "If I were to glorify myself, my glory would be nothing. It is my Father who glorifies me, the one you say is your God, ⁵⁵ though you do not know him. I know him, and I keep his word. ⁵⁶ Abraham your father rejoiced that he would see my day, and he saw it and was glad." ⁵⁷ The Jews said, "You are not even fifty years old, and have you seen Abraham?" ⁵⁸ Jesus said, "Truly, truly, I tell you, before Abraham was, I am." ⁵⁹ They picked up stones to throw at him, but Jesus hid and left the temple.

Notes
- v. 25: "Have I told you from the beginning what I am?" — The Greek "πόθεν ἐγώ εἰμι" can be interpreted as "where I come from" or "who I am," depending on context. Here, the meaning is deliberately ambiguous, as in the original.
- v. 58: "Before Abraham was, I am" — The Greek "ἐγὼ εἰμί" is a direct reference to the divine name YHWH in Exodus 3:14, rendered "I am" in English. The shift from "I am" (εἰμί) in the present tense to "I am" as a declaration of being aligns with the theological implications in the original.
- v. 44: "the father of lies" — The phrase "ὁ πατὴρ αὐτοῦ" is used in reference to the devil, a metaphorical interpretation of John's text, not a literal statement.
