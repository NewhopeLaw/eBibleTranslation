# John 18

¹ Having said this, Jesus went out with his disciples across the Kidron stream, where there was a garden, into which he and his disciples entered. ² Judas, the one betraying him, also knew the place, because Jesus often met there with his disciples. ³ So Judas, taking a detachment from the chief priests and the Pharisees, came there with lanterns and torches and weapons. ⁴ Jesus, knowing all that was to happen to him, went out and said to them, "Whom are you seeking?" ⁵ They answered him, "Jesus the Nazarene." He said to them, "I am he." And Judas, the one betraying him, was standing with them. ⁶ When he said to them, "I am he," they stepped backward and fell to the ground. ⁷ He asked them again, "Whom are you seeking?" They said, "Jesus the Nazarene." ⁸ Jesus answered, "I told you that I am. If you are seeking me, let these others go— ⁹ so that the word may be fulfilled which he said, 'Of those you have given me, I have not lost a single one.'" ¹⁰ Simon Peter, having a sword, drew it and struck the high priest's servant, cutting off his right ear. The servant's name was Malchus. ¹¹ Jesus said to Peter, "Put your sword into the sheath. Must I not drink the cup which the Father has given me?" ¹² Then the detachment, the commander, and the Jewish officers arrested Jesus and bound him. ¹³ They brought him first to Annas, for he was the father-in-law of Caiaphas, who was high priest that year. ¹⁴ Now Caiaphas was the one who had advised the Jews that it was expedient for one man to die for the people. ¹⁵ Simon Peter followed Jesus, as did another disciple. This disciple was known to the high priest, and so he entered with Jesus into the courtyard of the high priest, ¹⁶ but Peter stood at the door outside. The other disciple, the one known to the high priest, went out and spoke to the doorkeeper and brought Peter in. ¹⁷ Then the servant girl, the doorkeeper, said to Peter, "Are you also one of this man's disciples?" He said, "I am not." ¹⁸ The servants and the officers had made a charcoal fire, for it was cold, and they were warming themselves. Peter was with them, standing and warming himself. ¹⁹ The high priest then questioned Jesus about his disciples and his teaching. ²⁰ Jesus answered him, "I have spoken openly to the world. I have always taught in the synagogue and in the temple where all the Jews gather, and I have said nothing in secret. ²¹ Why do you question me? Ask those who heard me what I said to them. They know what I said." ²² When Jesus had said this, one of the officers standing nearby struck Jesus and said, "Is this how you answer the high priest?" ²³ Jesus answered him, "If I have spoken evil, bear witness to the evil. But if I have spoken well, why do you strike me?" ²⁴ Then Annas sent him bound to Caiaphas the high priest. ²⁵ Simon Peter was still standing and warming himself. They said to him, "Are you also one of his disciples?" He denied it and said, "I am not." ²⁶ One of the servants of the high priest, a relative of the one whose ear Peter had cut off, said, "Did I not see you in the garden with him?" ²⁷ Peter denied it again, and immediately the rooster crowed. ²⁸ Then they led Jesus from Caiaphas to the praetorium. It was early morning. But they themselves did not enter the praetorium, so that they might not be defiled but could eat the Passover. ²⁹ Pilate then came out to them and asked, "What charge do you bring against this man?" ³⁰ They answered, "If he were not doing evil, we would not have handed him over to you." ³¹ Pilate said to them, "Take him yourselves and judge him according to your law." The Jews said to him, "We are not permitted to put anyone to death." ³² So that the word Jesus had spoken might be fulfilled, indicating the kind of death he was about to die. ³³ Pilate then entered the praetorium again and called Jesus and said to him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" ³⁴ Jesus answered, "Is it of your own accord that you are asking this, or did others tell you about me?" ³⁵ Pilate answered, "Am I a Jew? Your own people and the chief priests have handed you over to me. What have you done?" ³⁶ Jesus answered, "My kingdom is not from this world. If my kingdom were from this world, my servants would have fought to prevent my being handed over to the Jews. But my kingdom is not from here." ³⁷ Pilate said to him, "Then are you a king?" Jesus answered, "You say that I am a king. For this I was born and for this I have come into the world, to bear witness to the truth. Everyone who is from the truth hears my voice." ³⁸ Pilate said to him, "What is truth?" Having said this, he went out again to the Jews and said to them, "I find no guilt in him. ³⁹ It is customary for you to have one person released for you at the Passover. Would you like me to release to you the King of the Jews?" ⁴⁰ They shouted again, saying, "Not this one, but Barabbas!" Now Barabbas was a robber.

---

Notes
- v. 20: "I have said nothing in secret" reflects the Greek "καὶ ἐν κρυπτῷ ἐλάλησα οὐδέν", translated as "I have said nothing in secret" to preserve the contrast with open speech.
- v. 38: Pilate's question "What is truth?" is retained as is, a direct quote of "Τί ἐστιν ἀλήθεια;".
- v. 40: "Barabbas was a robber" is a contextual explanation of "λῃστής" which can mean "bandit" or "robber" in this context.
