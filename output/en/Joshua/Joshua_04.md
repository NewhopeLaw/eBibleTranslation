# Joshua 4

¹ When the entire people had finished crossing the Jordan, the LORD said to Joshua, ² "Take twelve men from the people, one from each tribe, ³ and tell them, 'Take twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, from the place where the priests’ feet stand firm, and carry them over with you and set them up in the camp where you will spend the night.' ⁴ Joshua called twelve men from the Israelites, one from each tribe, ⁵ and told them, "Go ahead of the ark of the LORD your God into the Jordan, and each of you take up a stone on your shoulder, one for each tribe of the Israelites, ⁶ so that this may be a sign among you. When your children ask you in the future, 'What are these stones to you?' ⁷ you will answer them, 'This is how the waters of the Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of the LORD, as the waters of the Red Sea were cut off before us when the LORD our God dried them up before we passed through. These stones will be a reminder for the Israelites forever.'" ⁸ The Israelites did as Joshua had commanded. They took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, as the LORD had told Joshua, and set them up in the place where they would camp that night. ⁹ Joshua also set up twelve stones in the Jordan, at the place where the priests had stood firm while carrying the ark of the covenant. These stones remain there to this day. ¹⁰ The priests who carried the ark remained standing in the middle of the Jordan until Joshua had completed everything the LORD had commanded him to speak to the people. The people hurried and crossed over. ¹¹ When the entire people had finished crossing, the ark of the LORD and the priests went over before the people. ¹² The descendants of Reuben, the descendants of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh crossed over armed, as Moses had commanded them. ¹³ About forty thousand battle-ready troops went ahead of the LORD for war to the plains of Jericho. ¹⁴ That day the LORD exalted Joshua in the sight of all Israel, and they saw him as they had seen Moses all his days. ¹⁵ The LORD said to Joshua, ¹⁶ "Tell the priests who carry the ark of the covenant to come up out of the Jordan." ¹⁷ Joshua told the priests, "Come up out of the Jordan." ¹⁸ When the priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up from the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of their feet dried on the ground, the waters of the Jordan returned to their course and flowed as they had before, on all their banks. ¹⁹ The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month and camped at Gilgal, on the eastern side of Jericho. ²⁰ Joshua set up the twelve stones they had taken from the Jordan at Gilgal. ²¹ He said to the Israelites, "When your children ask you in the future, 'What are these stones to you?' ²² you will answer them, 'Israel crossed this Jordan on dry ground, ²³ because the LORD your God dried up the waters of the Jordan before you, just as He dried up the Red Sea before us, when He led us out of Egypt. ²⁴ This is so all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of the LORD is mighty, and so you may fear the LORD your God always.'"

---

Notes
- v. 13: "about forty thousand battle-ready troops" — the Hebrew text uses the expression "כְּאַרְבָּעִים אֶלֶף חֲלוּצֵי הַצָּבָא" ("like forty thousand battle-ready troops"), which is rendered as a literal approximation.
