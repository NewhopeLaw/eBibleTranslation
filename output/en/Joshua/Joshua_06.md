# Joshua 6

¹ Jericho was tightly shut, with no one going out or coming in. ² Then the LORD said to Joshua, “Look, I have given Jericho, along with its king and its warriors, into your hand. ³ March around the city, all the warriors, once around the city. Do this for six days. ⁴ Have seven priests carry seven ram’s horns in front of the ark. On the seventh day you will go around the city seven times, and the priests will blow the horns. ⁵ When the trumpet blast is heard and you hear the sound of the horn, have all the people shout with a great shout, and the wall of the city will fall down before them, and they will go up into the city, each man straight ahead. ⁶ Joshua summoned the priests and said to them, “Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven horns of the ram’s horn in front of the ark of the LORD.” ⁷ Then he said to the people, “Go around, and march around the city. Let the vanguard go before the ark of the LORD.” ⁸ When Joshua spoke to the people, the seven priests carrying the seven horns of the ram’s horn went before the ark of the LORD, blowing the horns, and the ark of the covenant of the LORD followed them. ⁹ The vanguard went before the priests blowing the horns, and the rear guard followed the ark of the LORD, blowing the horns all the while. ¹⁰ Joshua commanded the people, “Do not shout, do not let your voice be heard, and do not let a word come from your mouth until I tell you to shout, and then you may shout.” ¹¹ The ark of the LORD went around the city once, and the people returned to the camp and camped there. ¹² Joshua got up early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the LORD. ¹³ The seven priests carrying the seven horns of the ram’s horn went before the ark of the LORD, going and blowing the horns, and the vanguard went before them, and the rear guard followed the ark of the LORD, going and blowing the horns all the while. ¹⁴ They marched around the city the second day once, and the people returned to the camp. So they did for six days. ¹⁵ On the seventh day, at dawn, they rose and marched around the city in the same way, seven times around the city. On that day they marched around the city seven times. ¹⁶ On the seventh time, the priests blew the horns, and Joshua said to the people, “Shout, for the LORD has given the city to you! ¹⁷ The city itself and everything in it must be devoted to destruction for the LORD. Only Rahab the prostitute and all who are with her in her house will live, because she hid the messengers we sent. ¹⁸ But you must be careful not to take anything devoted to destruction, lest you destroy the camp of Israel and bring guilt upon it. ¹⁹ All the silver and gold, and every vessel of bronze and iron, must be holy to the LORD. It will go into the treasury of the LORD.” ²⁰ Then the people shouted, and they blew the horns. When the people heard the sound of the horn, they shouted with a great shout, and the wall of the city fell down before them, and the people went up into the city, each man straight ahead, and captured it. ²¹ They devoted the city to destruction, everything in it, from man to woman, from young to old, and from ox to donkey, with the edge of the sword. ²² As for the two young men who had spied out the land, Joshua said to them, “Go into the prostitute’s house, and bring out from there the woman and all who are with her, just as you swore to her.” ²³ So the two young men who had spied out the land went in and brought out Rahab and her father and mother and her brothers and all who were with her, and they placed them outside the camp of Israel. ²⁴ Then they burned the city with fire, everything in it. Only the silver and gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron, they brought into the treasury of the LORD. ²⁵ And Rahab the prostitute and her entire family lived, and they settled among the Israelites to this day, because she hid the messengers Joshua had sent to spy out Jericho. ²⁶ At that time Joshua made a vow, saying, “Cursed be the man before the LORD who rises up to rebuild this city, Jericho: by his firstborn he will lay its foundation, and by his youngest he will set up its gates.” ²⁷ And the LORD was with Joshua, and his name was known in all the land.

---

Notes
- v. 26: The Hebrew is ambiguous on whether the curse is made by Joshua or by the LORD. The translation assumes Joshua made the vow on the LORD’s authority.
