# Joshua 18

¹ The whole assembly of the people of Israel gathered at Shiloh and set up the tent of meeting there. The land had been subdued before them. ² Now the seven tribes of Israel had not yet received their inheritance. ³ Joshua said to the people of Israel, “How long will you delay to go in and take possession of the land that the Lord, the God of your ancestors, has given to you? ⁴ Choose three men for each tribe and I will send them, and they will walk through the land and describe it by its divisions, and they will return to me.” ⁵ “You will divide it into seven portions. Judah will stand on its boundary in the south, and the house of Joseph will stand on its boundary in the north.” ⁶ “Now you yourselves write the land into seven portions and bring it to me here. I will cast lots for you before the Lord our God here.” ⁷ For the Levites have no share among you, for the priesthood of the Lord is their inheritance. And Gad and Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have taken their inheritance beyond the Jordan to the east, as Moses the servant of the Lord gave to them. ⁸ Then the men set out and went, and Joshua commanded the ones going to survey the land, saying, “Go and walk through the land and describe it and return to me. I will cast lots for you here before the Lord in Shiloh.” ⁹ The men went and walked through the land and described it by its cities into seven portions in a book and brought it to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh. ¹⁰ Joshua cast the lots for them in Shiloh before the Lord, and Joshua divided the land to the people of Israel according to their portions. ¹¹ The lot of the tribe of Benjamin fell by families, and the boundary of their lot lay between the people of Judah and the people of Joseph. ¹² Their boundary began at the northern edge from the Jordan, and the boundary went up to the shoulder of Jericho northward, and it went up on the mountain westward, and its exits were to the desert of Beth-aven. ¹³ And from there the boundary turned to Luz, to the shoulder of Luz (which is Bethel), and it descended to Ataroth-addar, which is on the mountain to the south of the valley of Achor. ¹⁴ The boundary turned and encircled to the sea, from the mountain that faces Beth-horon in the south, and its exits were to Kiriath-baal (which is Kiriath-jearim), a city of the people of Judah. This was the boundary at the sea. ¹⁵ The boundary at the south was from the edge of Kiriath-jearim. It went out westward to the spring of Nephtoah. ¹⁶ It descended to the edge of the hill that faces the valley of Ben-hinnom, which is in the valley of Rephaim northward, and it descended into the valley of Hinnom to the shoulder of the Jebusites in the south, and it descended to the spring of En-rogel. ¹⁷ It turned northward to the spring of En-shemesh, and it went out to the villages opposite Maaleh-adummim, and it descended to the stone of the sons of Reuben. ¹⁸ It passed through the shoulder of the Arabah northward and descended into the Arabah. ¹⁹ It passed through the shoulder of Beth-haccerem northward and its exits were to the tongue of the Salt Sea northward to the edge of the Jordan in the south. This is the boundary of the south. ²⁰ The Jordan formed the boundary to the east. This is the inheritance of the people of Benjamin with all its boundaries around it, according to their families. ²¹ The cities of the tribe of Benjamin, according to their families, were: Jericho, Beth-haccerem, and Emek-keziz. ²² And Beth-arabah, and Tzmarayim, and Bethel. ²³ And Avvim, and Parah, and Efrath. ²⁴ And Kephirah-ammonah, and Ofni, and Geba; twelve cities and their villages. ²⁵ Gibeon, and Ramah, and Beeroth. ²⁶ And Mizpeh, and Cephirah, and Moza. ²⁷ And Rakkem, and Yirpeel, and Taralah. ²⁸ And Tzelal-el, and the Jebusite, which is Jerusalem, and Gibeath-kiriath-jearim, four cities and their villages. This is the inheritance of the people of Benjamin according to their families.

Notes
- v. 11: "by families" — the Hebrew text uses "לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָם" (lit. "by their families").
- v. 12–19: The boundary description follows a detailed geographical pattern, which has been rendered to preserve directional and topographical clarity.
- v. 28: "Gibeath-kiriath-jearim" is a compound name, "mountain of Kiriath-jearim" or "hill of Kiriath-jearim."
