# Joshua 21

¹ The heads of the Levite families approached Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun and the heads of the tribal families of the Israelites. ² They spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, "Yahweh has commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to live in and pasturelands for our livestock." ³ The Israelites gave to the Levites from their inheritance, according to the command of Yahweh, these cities and their pasturelands. ⁴ The lot went out to the families of the Kehathites. The sons of Aaron the priest, from among the Levites, received their cities from the tribe of Judah and from the tribe of Simeon and from the tribe of Benjamin by lot, thirteen cities. ⁵ To the remaining families of the Kehathites, from the tribe of Ephraim and from the tribe of Dan and from half the tribe of Manasseh, thirteen cities were given by lot. ⁶ To the families of the Gershonites, from the tribe of Issachar and from the tribe of Asher and from the tribe of Naphtali and from half the tribe of Manasseh in Bashan, thirteen cities were given by lot. ⁷ To the families of the Merarites, from the tribe of Reuben and from the tribe of Gad and from the tribe of Zebulun, twelve cities were given. ⁸ The Israelites gave to the Levites these cities and their pasturelands, as Yahweh had commanded by the hand of Moses by lot. ⁹ From the tribe of the sons of Judah and from the tribe of the sons of Simeon and from the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, they gave these cities, calling them by name. ¹⁰ To the families of the Kehathites, among the Levites, this lot was first, for they were given the first share. ¹¹ They gave to them Kiriath-Arba, the city of the Anakites, which is Hebron in the hill country of Judah, and its pasturelands surrounding it. ¹² And the field of the city and its surrounding villages were given to Caleb the son of Jephunneh in his inheritance. ¹³ To the sons of Aaron the priest they gave the city of refuge for the killer, Hebron, and its pasturelands, and Libnah and its pasturelands. ¹⁴ And Yitr and its pasturelands, and Estemoa and its pasturelands. ¹⁵ And Holon and its pasturelands, and Debir and its pasturelands. ¹⁶ And Ein and its pasturelands, and Yutta and its pasturelands, and Beth-Shemesh and its pasturelands — nine cities from these two tribes. ¹⁷ From the tribe of Benjamin they gave Gibeah and its pasturelands, and Gibeon and its pasturelands. ¹⁸ Anathoth and its pasturelands, and Almon and its pasturelands — four cities. ¹⁹ All the cities of the sons of Aaron the priests were thirteen cities and their pasturelands. ²⁰ To the families of the Kehathites, the Levites who remained from among the Kehathites, their cities by lot were from the tribe of Ephraim. ²¹ They gave to them the city of refuge for the killer, Shechem and its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim, and Gezer and its pasturelands. ²² And Kibzaim and its pasturelands, and Beth-Horon and its pasturelands — four cities. ²³ From the tribe of Dan they gave Eltekeh and its pasturelands, and Gibbethon and its pasturelands. ²⁴ Aijalon and its pasturelands, and Gat-Ramoth and its pasturelands — four cities. ²⁵ From half the tribe of Manasseh they gave Taanach and its pasturelands, and Gat-Ramoth and its pasturelands — two cities. ²⁶ All cities, ten in total with their pasturelands, for the families of the remaining Kehathites. ²⁷ To the families of the Gershonites, among the Levites, from half the tribe of Manasseh, they gave the city of refuge for the killer, Golan in Bashan and its pasturelands, and Be'erosh and its pasturelands — two cities. ²⁸ From the tribe of Issachar they gave Kishion and its pasturelands, and Daberath and its pasturelands. ²⁹ Yarmuth and its pasturelands, and Ein-Ganim and its pasturelands — four cities. ³⁰ From the tribe of Asher they gave Mishal and its pasturelands, and Abdon and its pasturelands. ³¹ Chelkath and its pasturelands, and Rehob and its pasturelands — four cities. ³² From the tribe of Naphtali they gave the city of refuge for the killer, Kedesh in Galilee and its pasturelands, and Hammoth-Dor and its pasturelands, and Kartan and its pasturelands — three cities. ³³ All the cities of the Gershonites, according to their families, were thirteen cities with their pasturelands. ³⁴ To the families of the Levite Merarites who remained, from the tribe of Zebulun, they gave Yakan and its pasturelands, and Kartah and its pasturelands. ³⁵ Dimnah and its pasturelands, and Nahalal and its pasturelands — four cities. ³⁶ From the tribe of Reuben they gave Bezer and its pasturelands, and Jahzah and its pasturelands. ³⁷ Kedemoth and its pasturelands, and Mephaath and its pasturelands — four cities. ³⁸ From the tribe of Gad they gave the city of refuge for the killer, Ramoth in Gilead and its pasturelands, and Mahanaim and its pasturelands. ³⁹ Heshbon and its pasturelands, and Jaazer and its pasturelands — four cities in total. ⁴⁰ All the cities for the families of the Merarites, the Levites who remained, were twelve cities with their pasturelands. ⁴¹ All the cities of the Levites within the inheritance of the sons of Israel were forty-eight cities with their pasturelands. ⁴² These cities, each with their pasturelands surrounding them, were like this for all these cities. ⁴³ And Yahweh gave Israel all the land that He had sworn to give to their fathers, and they took possession of it and dwelled in it. ⁴⁴ And Yahweh kept them safe on all sides, just as He had sworn to their fathers. No one stood against them in all their enemies. He delivered all their enemies into their hands. ⁴⁵ Not one word from all the good words that Yahweh had spoken to the house of Israel failed. All came to pass.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "to Caleb the son of Jephunneh in his inheritance" — the phrase "in his inheritance" is added for clarity.
- v. 33: "all the cities of the Gershonites, according to their families" — the phrase "according to their families" is added to clarify the distribution pattern.
- v. 43: "Yahweh gave Israel all the land" — the phrase "all the land" is rendered as a general reference to the territory promised to Israel.
- v. 45: "Not one word from all the good words" — the phrase is adjusted to reflect the original's emphasis on the fulfillment of Yahweh's promises.
