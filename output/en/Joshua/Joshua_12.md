# Joshua 12

¹ These are the kings of the land whom the people of Israel struck down and took their land across the Jordan eastward, from the sun rising up, from the wadi of Arnon until Mount Hermon, and all the Arabah eastward. ² Sihon the king of the Amorites, who was living in Heshbon, ruling from Armoth, which is on the edge of the wadi of Arnon, and in the middle of the wadi, and half of Gilead, and as far as the wadi of Jabbok, which is the boundary of the people of Ammon. ³ And the Arabah as far as the Sea of Kinnereth eastward, and as far as the Sea of the Arabah, the Sea of Salt eastward, along the road of Beth Jeshimoth, and from the south below the slopes of Ashdod. ⁴ And the boundary of Og the king of Bashan, who was left of the Rephaim, living in Ashtaroth and in Edrei. ⁵ Ruling from Mount Hermon and from Salecah, and all Bashan as far as the boundary of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and half of Gilead, was the boundary of Sihon the king of Heshbon. ⁶ Moses the servant of the LORD and the people of Israel struck them down, and Moses the servant of the LORD gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. ⁷ These are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the people of Israel struck down across the Jordan westward, from Baal Gad in the Valley of Lebanon as far as Mount Halak going up to Seir, and Joshua gave it to the tribes of Israel as an inheritance according to their divisions. ⁸ In the mountains and in the lowlands and in the Arabah and in the stream valleys and in the desert and in the Negev, the Hittite, the Amorite, and the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite. ⁹ The king of Jericho one, the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel, one. ¹⁰ The king of Jerusalem one, the king of Hebron one. ¹¹ The king of Jarmuth one, the king of Lachish one. ¹² The king of Eglon one, the king of Gezer one. ¹³ The king of Debir one, the king of Geder one. ¹⁴ The king of Hormah one, the king of Arad one. ¹⁵ The king of Libnah one, the king of Adullam one. ¹⁶ The king of Makkedah one, the king of Bethel one. ¹⁷ The king of Tappuah one, the king of Hepher one. ¹⁸ The king of Aphek one, the king of Lasharon one. ¹⁹ The king of Madon one, the king of Hazor one. ²⁰ The king of Shimron Meron one, the king of Acharon one. ²¹ The king of Taanach one, the king of Megiddo one. ²² The king of Kedesh one, the king of Jokneam of Carmel one. ²³ The king of Dor on Naphtali one, the king of the nations at Gilgal one. ²⁴ The king of Tirzah one. All these kings were thirty-one in all.

Notes
- v. 8: The phrase "in the stream valleys" renders נְחָלוֹת (nekhilot), which is best understood as "stream valleys" or "riversides" in this context.
- v. 22: The phrase "the king of Jokneam of Carmel" reflects the Hebrew’s geographical specificity (יְקְנֹעַם לַכַּרְמֶל).
- v. 23: "the king of the nations at Gilgal" translates מֶלֶךְ גּוֹיִם לְגִלְגָּל, where "the nations" likely refers to a non-Israelite population in that region.
- v. 24: The count of thirty-one kings is straightforward from the text, but the Greek version (Septuagint) lists a different number (29), which is not followed here.
