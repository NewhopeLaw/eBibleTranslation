# Joshua 14

¹ These are the ones who inherited the land of Canaan that Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun and the heads of the ancestral houses of the Israelites gave to them as an inheritance. ² Their inheritance was assigned by lot, as the LORD had directed through Moses, to the nine tribes and the half-tribe. ³ For Moses had already given an inheritance to the two tribes and the half-tribe across the Jordan, and the Levites had not received an inheritance among them. ⁴ For the tribe of Joseph had two tribes—Manasseh and Ephraim—and the Levites were not given a share in the land, except for cities to live in and their surrounding areas for their livestock. ⁵ As the LORD had commanded Moses, so the Israelites did. They divided the land. ⁶ The people of Judah approached Joshua at Gilgal and said, "Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite says to you: You know the word the LORD spoke to Moses the man of God concerning me and you in Kadesh Barnea. ⁷ I was forty years old when Moses, the servant of the LORD, sent me from Kadesh Barnea to scout the land, and I returned to him with a report just as my heart had determined. ⁸ My fellow scouts, who went up with me, made the people’s hearts melt with fear, but I followed the LORD my God wholeheartedly. ⁹ So Moses swore on that day, saying, 'The land you walked on will surely be yours and your descendants' forever, because you have followed the LORD my God wholeheartedly.'" ¹⁰ Now the LORD has kept me alive, just as he promised, for this is forty-five years since he made that statement to Moses, when Israel wandered in the wilderness. And now I am eighty-five years old today. ¹¹ I am still as strong today as I was when Moses sent me, just as my strength was then, so is my strength now for war, whether to go out or to come in. ¹² Now give me this mountain that the LORD spoke about on that day, for you heard that day that the Anakim were there and the cities were great, fortified. Perhaps the LORD will be with me, and I can drive them out just as the LORD said." ¹³ So Joshua blessed him, and he gave Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite as an inheritance. ¹⁴ Therefore, Hebron has remained in the inheritance of Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he followed the LORD the God of Israel wholeheartedly. ¹⁵ The name of Hebron was formerly Kiriath Arba, the great city of the Anakim. The land had rest from war.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "I am eighty-five years old today" — the Hebrew text is clear. The Septuagint offers a different age (85 vs. 110), but the Hebrew is followed here.
- v. 15: "Kiriath Arba" is an alternative name for Hebron, as attested in ancient sources. "The great city of the Anakim" reflects the local tradition described in the text.
