# Joshua 13

¹ Joshua was old and advanced in years. The LORD said to him, "You are old and advanced in years, and the land still remains, very much, to be taken for its inheritance." ² This is the land still remaining: all the cities of the Philistines and all the Geshurites. ³ From the Siachor that faces Egypt, as far as the boundary of Ekron, it is counted to the Canaanite, five cities of the Philistines: the Azathite, the Ashdodite, the Ashkelonite, the Gittite, the Ekronite, and the Avvim. ⁴ From Teman, all the land of the Canaanite, and Maarat that belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek, to the boundary of the Amorites. ⁵ And the land of the Gileadites and all Lebanon, from the rising of the sun, from Baal Gad beneath Mount Hermon, as far as the entrance of Hamath. ⁶ All the inhabitants of the hill country, from Lebanon as far as Misrephoth Maim, all the Sidonians. I myself will drive them out before the Israelites. Only you must assign it as an inheritance to Israel, as I have commanded you. ⁷ Now you must divide this land into an inheritance for the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh. ⁸ Along with them the Reubenites and the Gadites had already taken their inheritance that Moses gave them beyond the Jordan eastward, just as Moses the servant of the LORD gave it to them. ⁹ From Ar'eer, which is on the edge of the wadi of Arnon, and the city in the middle of the wadi, and all the lowland from Debir to Dibon. ¹⁰ All the cities of Sihon the king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon, as far as the boundary of the sons of Ammon. ¹¹ And the Gilead and the boundary of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, all Mount Hermon and all Bashan as far as Salecah. ¹² All the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei. He was left of the remnant of the Rephaim. Moses struck him down and took possession of it. ¹³ But the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites, and the Geshurite and the Maacathite live among Israel to this day. ¹⁴ Only to the tribe of Levi Moses did not give an inheritance, for the LORD the God of Israel is their inheritance, just as the LORD had spoken to them. ¹⁵ Moses gave the inheritance to the tribe of Reuben, by their families. ¹⁶ Their boundary was from Ar'eer, which is on the edge of the wadi of Arnon, and the city in the middle of the wadi, and all the lowland as far as Medeba. ¹⁷ Heshbon and all its cities in the lowland, Dibon and the high places of Baal, Beth Baal Me'on. ¹⁸ Jathzah and Kedemoth and Mephaath. ¹⁹ Kirjathaim and Sibmah and Zereth Shahar in the valley of the plain. ²⁰ And Beth Peor and Asdoth Pisgah and Beth Heshimoth. ²¹ And all the cities of the lowland and all the kingdom of Sihon the king of the Amorites, whom Moses struck down, along with the leaders of Midian: Evi and Rekem and Tsur and Hur and Reba, the five kings of Sihon, the inhabitants of the land. ²² And Balaam the son of Beor the diviner the Israelites put to death with the sword among those who were slain. ²³ The boundary of the Reubenites was the Jordan, and this was the inheritance of the sons of Reuben by their families, the cities and their enclosures. ²⁴ Moses gave the inheritance to the tribe of Gad, by their families. ²⁵ Their boundary was Jaazer and all the cities of Gilead and half the land of the sons of Ammon as far as Ar'eer, which is opposite Rabba. ²⁶ From Heshbon as far as Ramath Mizpeh and Betonim and Machanaim as far as the boundary of Debir. ²⁷ In the valley, Beth Hamon and Beth Nimrah and Sukkoth and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon the king of Heshbon, the Jordan and its boundary as far as the end of the Sea of Chinnereth beyond the Jordan eastward. ²⁸ This was the inheritance of the sons of Gad by their families, the cities and their enclosures. ²⁹ Moses gave the inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh, and it became the inheritance of the half-tribe of the sons of Manasseh by their families. ³⁰ Their boundary was from Machanaim, all Bashan, all the kingdom of Og the king of Bashan, and all the villages of Jair that are in Bashan, sixty cities. ³¹ And half Gilead and Ashtaroth and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan, to the sons of Machir the son of Manasseh, to the half-sons of Machir, by their families. ³² These are the ones who received an inheritance from Moses in the plains of Moab beyond the Jordan at Jericho eastward. ³³ And to the tribe of Levi Moses did not give an inheritance. The LORD the God of Israel is their inheritance, just as the LORD had spoken to them.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "Avvim" appears in the Hebrew but is often considered uncertain or possibly a variant form. Rendered as is.
- v. 13: "Maacathite" is the standard rendering for Maacath, but the term here is a noun, hence "Maacathite."
- v. 30: "Jair" is represented in the plural form here, referring to the villages of Jair, as commonly understood in this context.
