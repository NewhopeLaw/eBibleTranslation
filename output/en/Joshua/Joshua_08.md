# Joshua 8

¹ The LORD said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid and do not be discouraged. Take all the warriors with you and go up against Ai. See, I have given into your hand the king of Ai and his people, his city, and his land. ² You will do to Ai and its king as you did to Jericho and its king, except that you may keep for yourselves the plunder and the livestock. But set ambushes for the city behind it.” ³ Joshua rose, and all the warriors with him, to go up against Ai. Joshua chose thirty thousand warriors and sent them by night. ⁴ He gave them orders, saying, “See, you are to lie in ambush behind the city. Do not go too far from the city. You are all to be ready. ⁵ And I, along with all the people with me, will approach the city. When they come out to meet us as before, we will flee from them. ⁶ When they come out after us, you will take them from the city, for they will say, ‘They are fleeing from us as before,’ and they will flee from us. ⁷ Then you will rise from the ambush and seize the city, for the LORD your God has given it into your hands. ⁸ After you seize the city, set the city on fire as the LORD has commanded you. See, I have instructed you.” ⁹ Joshua sent them, and they went to the ambush and settled between Bethel and Ai, near the Jordan Valley. Joshua spent the night among the people. ¹⁰ The next morning Joshua rose early and mustered the people. He and the elders of Israel went before the people of Ai. ¹¹ All the warriors with him went up, and when they approached the city, they camped north of Ai, with the Valley between them and Ai. ¹² Joshua took about five thousand men and placed them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, near the Jordan Valley. ¹³ The people positioned the entire camp in front of the city to the north, and their rear in the Jordan Valley. Joshua walked through the valley that night. ¹⁴ When the king of Ai saw this, he hurried and rose early, and the men of the city came out to meet Israel, he and all his people, to fight at the appointed time, but he did not know that there was an ambush for him behind the city. ¹⁵ Joshua and all Israel pretended to retreat before them, and they chased them down the desert. ¹⁶ All the men of Ai cried out and pursued after them. They left the city open and chased after Israel. ¹⁷ No one in Ai and Bethel failed to go after Israel. They abandoned the city open and chased after Israel. ¹⁸ Then the LORD said to Joshua, “Stretch out the spear that is in your hand toward Ai, for I will give it into your hand.” So Joshua stretched out the spear that was in his hand toward the city. ¹⁹ The ambush rose quickly from their place, and they ran when his hand was stretched out. They went into the city, captured it, and hurried and set the city on fire. ²⁰ When the men of Ai turned back and saw it, they saw the smoke from the city rising into the sky, and they had no strength to escape. This way and that way, the people who had fled into the desert turned back against the pursuers. ²¹ Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had captured the city, and that the smoke from the city rose into the sky. They turned back and attacked the men of Ai. ²² These who had come out of the city to meet them were cut off from Israel in the middle. These from one side and those from the other side struck them until none were left, neither survivors nor those who escaped. ²³ They captured the king of Ai alive and brought him to Joshua. ²⁴ When Israel finished killing all the inhabitants of Ai in the fields and in the desert where they had chased them, they turned back to Ai and attacked it with the sword. ²⁵ On that day all the fallen from man to woman were twelve thousand, every man of Ai. ²⁶ Joshua did not withdraw his hand that was stretched out with the spear until he had destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai. ²⁷ Only the livestock and the plunder from the city Israel took for themselves, as the LORD had commanded Joshua. ²⁸ Joshua burned Ai and made it a heap of ruins that remains desolate to this day. ²⁹ He hanged the king of Ai on a tree until evening. And when the sun had set, Joshua gave orders, and they took down his body from the tree, threw it into the entrance of the city gate, and built over it a large pile of stones that remains to this day. ³⁰ Then Joshua built an altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal. ³¹ Just as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded the Israelites, as it is written in the Book of the Law of Moses: an altar of uncut stones, over which no iron tool had been used. They offered burnt offerings to the LORD and made peace offerings. ³² He wrote there on the stones the words of the covenant of Moses, which he had written for the Israelites. ³³ All Israel and their elders, their officers, and their judges stood on either side of the ark, facing the Levitical priests who bore the ark of the covenant of the LORD, half of them facing Mount Gerizim and half facing Mount Ebal, as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded when he blessed the people of Israel at first. ³⁴ After this, Joshua read aloud all the words of the Law, the blessings and the curses, just as written in the Book of the Law. ³⁵ Nothing was left from all that Moses had commanded that Joshua did not read before the whole assembly of Israel, the women, the children, and the foreigners who lived among them.

---

Notes
- v. 18: The phrase "as the LORD had commanded" is added for clarity, though not explicitly in the text, to reference the earlier divine instruction in v. 2.
- v. 30: "on Mount Ebal" is specified based on context, as the altar built by Joshua is traditionally associated with Mount Ebal (cf. Deut 27:4-8).
