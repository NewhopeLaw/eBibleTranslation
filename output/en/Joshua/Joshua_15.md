# Joshua 15

¹ The lot for the tribe of Judah, according to its clans, was assigned to the border of Edom in the wilderness of Zin toward the south, to the farthest end of the Negev. ² Their boundary to the south began at the edge of the Dead Sea, from the tongue of land that turns toward the Negev. ³ It went out to Maaleh Akarabim and passed through Tzin, and went up to Kadesh Barnea, passed through Hazeroth, and went up to Adar and turned toward Karkah. ⁴ It passed through Azmon and went out to the brook of Egypt, with its exits to the west; this was their boundary to the south. ⁵ Their boundary to the east was the Dead Sea up to the end of the Jordan, and their boundary to the north was along the shore of the sea, from the end of the Jordan. ⁶ The boundary went up to Beth Hagganah, passed through the north to Beth Arabah, went up to the stone of Reuben, the son of Benjamin. ⁷ The boundary went up to Debir, from the Valley of Achor, turning north toward Gilgal, which is opposite the ascent of Adummim, which is in the Negev, and passed the boundary toward the waters of En Shemesh and its exits to Ein Rogel. ⁸ The boundary went up to the Valley of the Benjaminites, to the slope of the Jebusites (which is Jerusalem). The boundary went up to the top of the hill that faces the Valley of Hinnom on the west, which is in the far end of the Valley of Rephaim, to the north. ⁹ The boundary turned from the top of the hill to the springs of Nophoth, and went out to the cities of the hill of Ephron (which is Baalah, the city of the Jebusites). ¹⁰ The boundary turned from Baalah to the west, to the hill of Seir, and passed to the slope of the hill of Jearim (which is Kesalon), and went down to Beth Shemesh, and passed through Timnah. ¹¹ The boundary went out to the slope of Ekron to the north, the boundary turned to Sikron, passed through the hill of Baalah, and went out to Yabneel, and its exits were to the west. ¹² The boundary along the great Sea and this boundary were the boundary of the people of Judah all around, according to their clans. ¹³ To Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, a portion was given in the middle of the people of Judah, as the LORD had commanded Joshua, the city of Kiriath Arba, the father of the Anakim, which is Hebron. ¹⁴ And Caleb drove out the three sons of the Anakim: Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, the descendants of the Anakim. ¹⁵ And he went up to the people living in Debir, which was formerly named Kiriath Sefer. ¹⁶ Caleb said, "Whoever conquers Kiriath Sefer and captures it, to him I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife." ¹⁷ And Othniel, the son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, captured it. So Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife. ¹⁸ And when she came, she urged her father to ask for a field. As she dismounted from her donkey, Caleb asked her, "What is it you want?" ¹⁹ She said, "Give me a blessing, for you have given me a land in the Negev. Give me springs of water." So he gave her the upper and lower springs. ²⁰ This is the inheritance of the tribe of the people of Judah, according to their clans. ²¹ Their cities from the border of the people of Judah to the border of Edom in the Negev were Kaptzaiel, Edar, and Yagur. ²² Kinah, Dimonah, and Adadah. ²³ Kedesh, Hatzor, and Itnan. ²⁴ Zif, Telam, and Bealot. ²⁵ Hazor Hadattah and the cities of Hazor. ²⁶ Amam, Shema, and Moladah. ²⁷ Charaz Gadad, Cheshmon, and Beth Palat. ²⁸ Charaz Shual, Beer Sheba, and Biziotyah. ²⁹ Baalah, Eiim, and 'Asem. ³⁰ Eltolad, Kesil, and Charmah. ³¹ Tziklag, Madmanna, and Sansanna. ³² Leboath, Shilhim, Ein, and Rimmon, all cities, twenty-nine with their surrounding villages. ³³ In the Shephelah: Esthaol, Tzarah, and Ashnah. ³⁴ Zanoah, Ein Ganim, Tappuach, and the Einam. ³⁵ Yarmuth, Adullam, Shokah, and Ezek. ³⁶ Shaarayim, Adithaim, and Hagerah, and Gederothaim, cities, fourteen with their surrounding villages. ³⁷ Tzannan, Chadashah, and Migdal Gad. ³⁸ Dilan, Hamatzpah, and Yaqtaiel. ³⁹ Lakish, Batskat, and 'Egilon. ⁴⁰ Cabbon, Lachmas, and Kittlish. ⁴¹ Gederoth Beth Daggon, Na'amah, and Maqedah, cities, sixteen with their surrounding villages. ⁴² Labin, Ethar, and 'Asan. ⁴³ Yiftach, Ashnah, and Neztib. ⁴⁴ Keilah, Ackzib, and Marea, cities, nine with their surrounding villages. ⁴⁵ Ekron and its villages and its surrounding areas. ⁴⁶ From Ekron to the sea, all that is along the border of Ashdod and its surrounding areas. ⁴⁷ Ashdod, its villages and its surrounding areas; Gaza, its villages and its surrounding areas, to the brook of Egypt and the great sea, and the boundary. ⁴⁸ And in the mountain: Shamer, Yatir, and Socho. ⁴⁹ Dan, and Kiriath Sanah, which is Debir. ⁵⁰ Anab, Eshthemoah, and Anim. ⁵¹ Goshen, Holon, and Giloh, cities, eleven with their surrounding villages. ⁵² Arav, Rumah, and Esnan. ⁵³ Yanum, Beth Tappuch, and Aphakah. ⁵⁴ Chumtah, Kiriath Arba (which is Hebron), and Tziyor, cities, nine with their surrounding villages. ⁵⁵ Maon, Carmel, Zif, and Yutta. ⁵⁶ Yizr'el, Yakde'am, and Zanoch. ⁵⁷ Haqin, Gibeah, and Timnah, cities, ten with their surrounding villages. ⁵⁸ Chalchul, Beth Tsur, and Gedor. ⁵⁹ Maarat, Beth Anoth, and Eltekon, cities, six with their surrounding villages. ⁶⁰ Kiriath Baal (which is Kiriath Yearim) and Hara'ba, cities, two with their surrounding villages. ⁶¹ In the wilderness: Beit Ha'aravah, Middin, and Sekakah. ⁶² The Nibshan, and the city of Salt, and 'Ein Gedi, cities, six with their surrounding villages. ⁶³ And the Jebusites, who lived in Jerusalem, the people of Judah could not drive them out. The Jebusites continued to live alongside the people of Judah in Jerusalem to this very day.

---

Notes
- v. 13: "Kiriath Arba" is rendered as "city of Kiriath Arba" to reflect its role as the ancestral name before being identified as Hebron.
- v. 63: The translation preserves the ambiguity in the Hebrew regarding the relationship between the Jebusites and the people of Judah in Jerusalem, using "continued to live alongside" to reflect the lack of complete expulsion.
