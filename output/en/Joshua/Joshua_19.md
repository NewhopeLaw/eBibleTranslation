# Joshua 19

¹ The second lot fell to Simeon, to the tribe of the sons of Simeon by their families, and their inheritance was within the inheritance of the sons of Judah. ² Their inheritance included Beer-sheba, and Sheba, and Moladah. ³ And Harshal, and Balah, and Ezem. ⁴ And Eltolad, and Betul, and Harsamah. ⁵ And Siklag, and Beth-marcaboth, and Hazarsusah. ⁶ And Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen, thirteen cities and their villages. ⁷ Aien, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan, four cities and their villages. ⁸ And all the surrounding villages of these cities, as far as Baalah, Beer-ramath in the Negev. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Simeon by their families. ⁹ The inheritance of the sons of Simeon came from the territory of the sons of Judah, because the portion of the sons of Judah was larger than theirs, and so the sons of Simeon received an inheritance within their territory. ¹⁰ The third lot went out to the tribe of Zebulun by their families, and their boundary extended as far as Sarid. ¹¹ Their boundary went up to the sea, to Maralah, and to Dabbesheth, and it reached the stream that flows opposite Yaqneam. ¹² It turned back from Sarid to the east, toward the rising of the sun, along the boundary of Kishion, Tabor, and it went out to the Daberath, and it went up to Yaphi. ¹³ From there it passed to the east, to Gittah, Hepher, Ittah, Katsin, and it went out to Rimmon, the town of the Neah. ¹⁴ The boundary encircled the area toward the north to Chanaton, and its exits were to the Valley of Iftach-el. ¹⁵ And Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, and Idalah, and Beth-lechem, twelve cities and their villages. ¹⁶ This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun by their families, these cities and their villages. ¹⁷ The fourth lot went out to Issachar, to the tribe of the sons of Issachar by their families. ¹⁸ Their boundary included Izreel, the Kesuloth, and Sunem. ¹⁹ And Hapharaim, and Shiaon, and Anaharath. ²⁰ And Rabboth, and Kishion, and Abeth. ²¹ And Remeth, and Ein-ganim, and Ein-hadah, and Beth-pazzez. ²² And it reached the boundary of Tabor, and Shachazim, and Beth-shemesh, and its exits were to the Jordan. Sixteen cities and their villages. ²³ This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Issachar by their families, these cities and their villages. ²⁴ The fifth lot went out to the tribe of the sons of Asher by their families. ²⁵ Their boundary began at Chelkath, at Halal, and Beten, and Acsaph. ²⁶ And Almamelek, and Ammad, and Mishal, and it reached Carmel and the border of the Sea, and the border of the Sidonians at Lebah. ²⁷ And it turned back to the east, to Beth-dagon, and it joined Zebulun, and it passed by the Valley of Iftach-el to the north, and it went out to Kabul on the left. ²⁸ And Ebron, and Rehob, and Hammon, and Kana, as far as the great city of Sidon. ²⁹ And the boundary turned to Ramah, and to the spring of Mitzpeh-Tsur, and the boundary turned to Chosah, and its exits were to the sea, from the territory of Ezibbah. ³⁰ And Umah, and Aphek, and Rehob, twenty-two cities and their villages. ³¹ This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Asher by their families, these cities and their villages. ³² The sixth lot went out to the tribe of the sons of Naphtali by their families. ³³ Their boundary began at Helef, at Elon-beth-taanach, and Adam-neggeb, and Yabanial, as far as Lakum, and its exits were to the Jordan. ³⁴ And the boundary turned toward the sea, to Aznot-tabor, and from there it went out to Huqoq, and it reached Zebulun from the south, and Asher from the sea, and Judah from the rising of the sun, toward the Jordan. ³⁵ And the fortified cities of the Sidonians: Tzir, and Hammat-rakkath, and Kinnereth. ³⁶ And Adamah, and Harumah, and Chazor. ³⁷ And Kedesh, and Edrei, and Ein-hazor. ³⁸ And Yiron, and Migdal-el, Chorem, and Beth-anath, and Beth-shamesh, nineteen cities and their villages. ³⁹ This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali by their families, these cities and their villages. ⁴⁰ The seventh lot went out to the tribe of the sons of Dan by their families. ⁴¹ Their boundary included Tzorah, and Eshthal, and Ir-shemesh. ⁴² And Shaalabbin, and Ayalon, and Itlah. ⁴³ And Elon, and Timnah, and Ekron. ⁴⁴ And Eltekeh, and Gipton, and Baalath. ⁴⁵ And Jud, and the sons of Berak, and Gat-rimon. ⁴⁶ And the waters of the Jordan and the Rakkon, along with the boundary near Yafpo. ⁴⁷ And the boundary of the sons of Dan went out from them, and the sons of Dan went up and fought with Leshem, and captured it with the sword, and took possession of it. They dwelt in it and renamed Leshem Dan, after the name of their father. ⁴⁸ This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Dan by their families, these cities and their villages. ⁴⁹ They completed dividing the land according to its boundaries, and the Israelites gave an inheritance to Joshua son of Nun within it. ⁵⁰ According to the word of the Lord, they gave him the city he asked of Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim, and he built the city and dwelt in it. ⁵¹ These are the inheritances which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the fathers by their tribes of the people of Israel, divided by lot at Shiloh before the Lord, at the entrance of the tent of meeting. And they finished dividing the land.

Notes
- v. 47: "Leshem" is identified as a city later renamed Dan by the tribe of Dan. The translation assumes this identification is clear from the context without further explanation.
