# Joshua 3

¹ Joshua arose early in the morning, and he and all the Israelites moved out from Shittim and came to the Jordan. They camped there before crossing over. ² Now it happened after three days that the scouts passed through the camp, ³ and they gave orders to the people, saying, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and the Levitical priests carrying it, you must leave your places and follow behind them. ⁴ Yet there must be a distance between you and it—about two thousand cubits. Do not come near it, so that you may learn the path you are to take, for you have not walked this way before yesterday and today." ⁵ Joshua said to the people, "Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you." ⁶ Joshua said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant and go ahead of the people." So the priests took up the ark of the covenant and went ahead of the people. ⁷ The LORD said to Joshua, "Today I begin to exalt you in the sight of all Israel, so that they may know that just as I was with Moses, so I will be with you." ⁸ "Now command the priests who carry the ark of the covenant, saying, 'When you reach the edge of the Jordan, in the Jordan you must stand.'" ⁹ Joshua said to the Israelites, "Come here and listen to the words of the LORD your God. ¹⁰ By this you will know that the living God is among you and will drive out from your presence the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Hivite, the Perizzite, the Girgashite, the Amorite, and the Jebusite. ¹¹ Behold, the ark of the covenant of the LORD of the whole earth is going over before you into the Jordan." ¹² Now take twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one from each tribe, ¹³ so that when the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD of the earth touch the waters of the Jordan, the waters flowing down will be cut off, and the waters coming down will stand still." ¹⁴ When the people moved out from their tents to cross the Jordan, the priests carrying the ark of the covenant went ahead of the people. ¹⁵ Now as those carrying the ark of the covenant approached the Jordan, the priests’ feet dipped into the edge of the water, and the Jordan was overflowing all its banks during the harvest season. ¹⁶ The waters flowing down from above stood still, rising like a wall a long distance away from the city, while the waters going down toward the Arabah Sea, the Salt Sea, were cut off. The people crossed over opposite Jericho. ¹⁷ The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, while all Israel passed through on dry ground until the entire nation had crossed over.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "about two thousand cubits" — the Hebrew is *alpayim ammah*, where "amah" is a cubit. The distance is literal.
- v. 13: "the waters flowing down will be cut off" — the Hebrew uses *nichrotun*, which conveys a dramatic severing of the water flow.
- v. 16: "the waters coming down toward the Arabah Sea" — the "Arabah Sea" is the Dead Sea, rendered here as *yam ha-melah*, "Sea of Salt."
