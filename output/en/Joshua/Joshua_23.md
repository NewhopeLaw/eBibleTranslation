# Joshua 23

¹ And it came to pass after many days, after the LORD had given rest to Israel from all their enemies around them, that Joshua was old in years. ² And Joshua called all Israel, their elders, their leaders, their judges, and their officers, and said to them, "I am now old in years, and I have passed on in days. ³ And you have seen all that the LORD your God has done to all these nations before you. For it is the LORD your God who has fought for you. ⁴ I have set these nations that remain before you as your inheritance, apportioned to your tribes from the Jordan and all the nations I have destroyed, as far as the great sea, the sun's going down. ⁵ And the LORD your God will Himself drive them out before you, and He will dispossess them from before you, and you will inherit their land, just as the LORD your God spoke to you. ⁶ Therefore be very strong to keep and do all that is written in the Book of the Teaching of Moses, not turning aside from it to the right or to the left. ⁷ You must not go in to the nations that remain among you, and you must not mention the names of their gods, and you must not swear by them, and you must not serve them, and you must not bow down to them. ⁸ But you must cling to the LORD your God, just as you have done until this day. ⁹ And the LORD will drive out before you great and strong nations, and no one has stood before you until this day. ¹⁰ One from among you will chase a thousand, for it is the LORD your God who is fighting for you, just as He spoke to you. ¹¹ And be very careful to love the LORD your God. ¹² For if you turn back and join with the nations that remain among you, and you intermarry with them, and they with you, ¹³ you must know that the LORD your God will no longer drive out these nations before you. Rather, they will be a snare and a trap, a scourge and an arrow in your eyes, until you are destroyed from the good land that the LORD your God gave to you. ¹⁴ And I am going to walk the way of all the earth. And you will know with all your heart and with all your soul that not one word of all the good words the LORD your God spoke to you has failed. All have come to you, not one of them has failed. ¹⁵ And just as all the good words the LORD your God spoke to you have come to you, so too will the LORD bring all the evil words upon you, until He has destroyed you from the good land that the LORD your God gave to you. ¹⁶ Because you break the covenant of the LORD your God that He commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them, the anger of the LORD will flare up against you, and you will be swiftly destroyed from the good land that the LORD gave to you.

---

Notes
- v. 14: The phrase "I am going to walk the way of all the earth" reflects the traditional Hebrew idiom meaning "I am about to die."
- v. 15: "All the evil words" refers to the curses or negative promises in the covenant.
