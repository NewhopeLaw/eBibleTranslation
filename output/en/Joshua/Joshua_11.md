# Joshua 11

¹ When Yabin king of Hazor heard about it, he sent to Jobab king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph, ² to the kings of the north, in the mountains and in the Arabah, to the south of Kinnereth and in the lowlands, and to the leaders of Dor in the west, ³ the Canaanite to the east and west, and the Amorite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Jebusite in the mountains and the Hivite under Hermon in the land of Mizpeh. ⁴ They and all their armies with them came out in great numbers like the sand on the seashore—many, many horses and chariots. ⁵ All these kings joined together and came and encamped together at the waters of Merom, to fight with Israel. ⁶ The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not fear them, for tomorrow at this time I will hand all of them over to Israel, their horses you will uproot and their chariots you will burn with fire." ⁷ So Joshua and all the warriors with him went up against them suddenly at the waters of Merom and fell upon them. ⁸ The LORD handed them over to Israel, and they struck them and pursued them as far as Sidon the great and as far as Misrephoth-maim and as far as the valley of Mizpeh to the east, until they had destroyed them, leaving no survivor. ⁹ Joshua did to them as the LORD had said to him: he uprooted their horses and burned their chariots with fire. ¹⁰ At that time Joshua returned and captured Hazor and struck its king with the sword. For Hazor was the head of all these kingdoms. ¹¹ They struck every living soul in it with the sword, devoting it to destruction, leaving no breath in it, and they burned Hazor with fire. ¹² Joshua captured all the cities of these kings and their kings, striking them with the sword and devoting them to destruction, just as the LORD had commanded Moses, the servant of God. ¹³ But the cities that stood on their mounds, Israel did not burn, except Hazor alone, which Joshua burned. ¹⁴ All the spoil of these cities and the livestock they took for themselves, but every person they struck down with the sword until they had destroyed them, leaving no breath in them. ¹⁵ As the LORD had commanded Moses, the servant of God, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did, not omitting any of the things that the LORD had commanded Moses. ¹⁶ Joshua captured all this land—the mountain, the whole Negev, all the land of Goshen, the lowland, the Arabah, and the hill country of Israel and its lowlands, ¹⁷ from the hill that goes up to Seir, as far as Baal Gad in the valley of Lebanon under Mount Hermon, and all their kings he captured and struck them and put them to death. ¹⁸ Joshua waged war against these kings for many days. ¹⁹ No city was left that Israel did not take, except the Hivites who lived in Gibeon. All of them Israel took by war. ²⁰ For it was by the LORD that their hearts were hardened to face Israel in battle, in order to destroy them, without showing mercy, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²¹ At that time Joshua came and cut off the Anakim from the mountain, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah and all the hill country of Israel, with their cities. Joshua devoted them to destruction. ²² No Anakim were left in the land of the people of Israel except in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod. ²³ Joshua took all the land just as the LORD had spoken to Moses, and Joshua gave it as an inheritance to Israel, dividing it among their tribes, and the land had rest from war.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "the head of all these kingdoms" — the term "head" here reflects the meaning of the Hebrew "rosh" which in this context signifies leadership or prominence.
- v. 22: "in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod" — these cities are mentioned as the places where Anakim survivors are said to have remained, though the Hebrew text does not specify whether the Anakim were present there at the time or as a later tradition.
