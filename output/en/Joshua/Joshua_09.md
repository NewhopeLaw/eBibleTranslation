Notes  
- v. 1: "the Hittite, the Amorite..." — The listing of peoples is traditional, with "Hittite" and others representing groups in the region.  
- v. 15: "made peace" — The Hebrew term for peace here (שָׁלַם) is often used for covenant-making.  
- v. 23: "none of you will be a slave in the house of my God" — The Hebrew uses "עֶבֶד" (slave), but the nuance here is about being excluded from temple service.  
- v. 25: "in your hand" — The phrase בְּיָדֶךָ is a common idiom for being under someone's authority or mercy.
