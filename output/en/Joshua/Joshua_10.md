# Joshua 10

¹ When Adoni-Zedek, king of Jerusalem, heard that Joshua had captured Ai and destroyed it, just as he had done to Jericho and its king, and that the residents of Gibeon had submitted to Israel and were among them, ² he was greatly afraid, for he knew that Gibeon was a large city, like one of the royal cities, and that all its men were warriors. ³ He sent to Hoham, king of Hebron, and to Piram, king of Jarmuth, and to Japhi, king of Lachish, and to Debir, king of Eglon, saying, ⁴ "Come up to me, and help me, and let us go out to fight Gibeon, for it has submitted to Joshua and to the people of Israel." ⁵ So the five Amorite kings, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon, along with all their armies, gathered and encamped against Gibeon and fought against it. ⁶ The men of Gibeon sent to Joshua at the camp in Gilgal, saying, "Do not hold back your hand from us. Come up to us quickly and rescue us. Help us, for all the kings of the Amorites who live in the hills have gathered against us." ⁷ Joshua went up from Gilgal, he and all the people of war with him, all the warriors. ⁸ The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not fear them, for I have given them into your hand. Not one of them will stand before you." ⁹ Joshua came upon them suddenly. All that night he went up from Gilgal. ¹⁰ The LORD threw them into confusion before Israel and struck them with a great defeat at Gibeon. He chased them along the road to Beth-horon and struck them down as far as Azekah and to Makkedah. ¹¹ As they fled before Israel, they were going down the slope of Beth-horon, and the LORD rained down large stones from the sky upon them all the way to Azekah. More of them died from the hailstones than the Israelites killed with the sword. ¹² Then Joshua spoke to the LORD on the day the LORD gave the Amorites over to the Israelites. He said in the sight of Israel, "Sun, stand still over Gibeon, and moon, stand still in the Valley of Ayalon." ¹³ And the sun stood still and the moon stayed, until the Israelites had avenged their enemies. This is written in the Book of the Upright. The sun stood still in the middle of the sky and did not go down for a full day. ¹⁴ There was no day like that before or after it, that the LORD heeded the voice of a human being. ¹⁵ Joshua and all Israel with him returned to the camp at Gilgal. ¹⁶ The five kings fled and hid in a cave at Makkedah. ¹⁷ It was reported to Joshua, saying, "The five kings have been found, hiding in the cave at Makkedah." ¹⁸ Joshua said, "Roll large stones to the mouth of the cave, and set men to guard them." ¹⁹ "But you," he said, "do not remain idle. Pursue your enemies and catch up with them. Do not allow them to enter their cities, for the LORD your God has given them into your hands." ²⁰ So it happened that after Joshua and the Israelites had finished defeating them with a great defeat until they were all destroyed, the survivors fled to the fortified cities. ²¹ All the people returned to the camp at Makkedah in peace. Not a single tongue of the Israelites was cut out. ²² Joshua said, "Open the mouth of the cave and bring out these five kings to me from the cave." ²³ They did so and brought out these five kings from the cave—the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon. ²⁴ When they brought out these kings to Joshua, Joshua called all Israel and said to the commanders of the men of war who had gone with him, "Come close and place your feet on the necks of these kings." They came near and placed their feet on their necks. ²⁵ Then Joshua said to them, "Do not fear and do not be dismayed. Be strong and courageous, for this is how the LORD will do to all your enemies whom you fight." ²⁶ Joshua struck them afterward and put them to death, hanging them on five trees. They remained hanging on the trees until evening. ²⁷ When the sun set, Joshua gave orders, and they took them down from the trees, threw them into the cave where they had hidden, and placed large stones at the mouth of the cave, as they are to this very day. ²⁸ On that day Joshua captured Makkedah, and he struck it with the sword, and he destroyed all who breathed in it. He left no survivors. He did to its king as he had done to the king of Jericho. ²⁹ Then Joshua and all Israel with him moved from Makkedah to Lebo-hamath and encamped against Lebo-hamath. ³⁰ The LORD gave it into the hand of Israel. Joshua captured it and its king, and struck it with the sword, and destroyed all who breathed in it. He left no survivors. He did to its king as he had done to the king of Jericho. ³¹ Then Joshua and all Israel with him moved from Lebo-hamath to Lachish and encamped against it and fought against it. ³² The LORD gave Lachish into the hand of Israel. Joshua captured it on the second day and struck it with the sword, and destroyed all who breathed in it, just as he had done to Lebo-hamath. ³³ At that time, Horam king of Gezer came up to help Lachish, and Joshua struck him and his people until he left no survivors. ³⁴ Then Joshua and all Israel with him moved from Lachish to Eglon and encamped against it and fought against it. ³⁵ The LORD gave Eglon into the hand of Israel. Joshua captured it on that day and struck it with the sword, and destroyed all who breathed in it, just as he had done to Lachish. ³⁶ Then Joshua and all Israel with him moved from Eglon to Hebron and encamped against it. ³⁷ He fought against it and struck it with the sword, and destroyed all who breathed in it, including its king, as he had done to Eglon. ³⁸ Joshua returned and all Israel with him went to Debir and encamped against it. ³⁹ He captured it and its king and its cities, and struck them with the sword. He destroyed all who breathed in it, leaving no survivors. He did to Debir and its king just as he had done to Hebron and its king. ⁴⁰ Joshua struck all the land, the hill country, the Negev, the lowlands, the slopes, and all their kings. He left no survivors and destroyed all who breathed, as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded. ⁴¹ Joshua struck them from Kadesh-barnea to Gaza and all the land of Goshen, as far as Gibeon. ⁴² Joshua captured all these kings and their land with one strike, for the LORD, the God of Israel, fought for Israel. ⁴³ Joshua and all Israel with him returned to the camp at Gilgal.

Notes
- v. 11: "hailstones" — the Hebrew term בָּרָד (barad) can refer to both hail and stones in poetic or figurative language.
- v. 12-13: The miraculous stopping of the sun and moon is treated as a historical event in the text, consistent with the narrative context. The phrase "the Book of the Upright" is a direct translation of the Hebrew "Book of the Upright."
- v. 26-27: The placement of the kings' bodies in the cave and the stones over the entrance is clearly described in the text, with no ambiguity.
- v. 28-42: The pattern of conquest and destruction is repeated across these verses, each city and king being dealt with in the same way. No choice was required in translating these repetitive structures.
