# Joshua 2

¹ Joshua son of Nun sent two men as spies from Shittim, saying, "Go, look at the land and at Jericho." They went and entered the house of a prostitute named Rahab, where they stayed. ² A report came to the king of Jericho: "Look, some men from the people of Israel came here tonight to spy out the land." ³ The king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who came to you, who entered your house, for they came to spy out the entire land." ⁴ The woman took the two men and hid them, then said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from." ⁵ The gate was about to be closed in the dark, and the men had already gone out. I did not know where they had gone. Run quickly after them, perhaps you will catch up with them." ⁶ She led them up onto the roof and hid them among the stalks of flax she had laid there on the roof. ⁷ The men ran after them along the road toward the Jordan, toward the crossings. The gate was shut behind them just after the pursuers had gone out. ⁸ They had not yet lain down, and she went up to them on the roof. ⁹ She said to the men, "I know that the Lord has given you the land, and that fear of you has fallen upon us. Everyone who dwells in the land is crushed before you. ¹⁰ We have heard how the Lord dried up the sea for you when you left Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you destroyed." ¹¹ We heard it, and our hearts melted. No spirit remains in any of us because of you, for the Lord your God is God in heaven above and on earth below. ¹² Now, please swear to me by the Lord that you will show me kindness, and that you will also show kindness to my family. Give me a sign of truth." ¹³ Save my father and mother, my brothers and sisters, and everyone of my family. Rescue our lives from death." ¹⁴ The men said to her, "Our lives are in your hands. If you tell anyone about us, when the Lord gives us the land, we will show you kindness and truth." ¹⁵ She let them down with a rope through the window, for her house was on the city wall, and she lived in the wall. ¹⁶ She said to them, "Go into the hills, so that the pursuers will not find you. Stay there for three days until the pursuers return, and then you may go on your way." ¹⁷ The men said to her, "We are free of your oath, the one you made us swear." ¹⁸ "Look," they said, "we will tie this crimson thread in the window through which we went down. You must gather your father and your mother, your brothers and your family, and bring them all to your house. ¹⁹ Anyone who goes out of the door of your house toward the outside will have his blood on his head, and we will be innocent. But anyone who is with you in the house, their blood will be on our heads if there is any hand in it." ²⁰ And if you tell anyone about us, we will be free of the oath you made us swear." ²¹ She said, "So it shall be," and sent them away. They went and came to the hills, where they stayed for three days until the pursuers returned and searched the entire road, but they did not find them. ²² Then the two men returned from the hills and crossed over, and came to Joshua son of Nun, and told him everything that had happened to them. ²³ Then they said to Joshua, "The Lord has given us the whole land, and indeed, all who dwell in it are crushed before us."

²⁴ ---  
Notes  
- v. 8: "had not yet lain down" — the Hebrew is ambiguous whether the men were not yet asleep or not yet lying down at all. The latter is more literal, but the former is more natural in context.  
- v. 12: "show me kindness" — the Hebrew is *chasad*, translated as "kindness" to avoid theological connotations of "mercy" or "lovingkindness" where not intended.  
- v. 14: "Our lives are in your hands" — reflects the Hebrew idiom for placing one’s fate in someone else’s control.
