# Joshua 24

¹ Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, called together their elders, their leaders, and their judges, and had them stand before God. ² And Joshua said to all the people, "This is what the LORD God of Israel says: Your ancestors lived beyond the river, from Terah, the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor, and they served other gods. ³ And I took your father Abraham from beyond the river, and I led him through all the land of Canaan, and I multiplied his offspring and gave him Isaac. ⁴ And I gave Isaac Jacob and Esau, and I gave Esau the hill country of Seir to possess, while Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt. ⁵ And I sent Moses and Aaron, and I struck Egypt as I did within it, and afterward I brought you out. ⁶ And I brought your ancestors out of Egypt, and you came into the sea, and Egypt pursued your ancestors with chariots and with horses to the Red Sea. ⁷ And you cried out to the LORD, and He placed darkness between you and the Egyptians, and He brought the sea over them, and it covered them. You saw with your own eyes what I did to Egypt, and you dwelled in the wilderness for many days. ⁸ And I brought you into the land of the Amorites who lived beyond the Jordan, and they fought against you, and I gave them into your hand, and you took possession of their land and I destroyed them before you. ⁹ And Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, rose up to fight against Israel, and he sent and called Balaam the son of Beor to curse you. ¹⁰ But I did not listen to Balaam, and he blessed you again and again, and I rescued you from his hand. ¹¹ And you crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho, and the men of Jericho, the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Girgashite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite fought against you, and I gave them into your hand. ¹² And I sent a hornet ahead of you, and it drove them out from before you, two kings of the Amorites—not with your sword, nor with your bow. ¹³ And I gave you land that you did not tread upon, and cities that you did not build, and you dwell in them, vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant, yet you eat from them. ¹⁴ Now, fear the LORD and serve Him with wholeness and with truth, and remove the gods that your ancestors served beyond the river and in Egypt, and serve the LORD. ¹⁵ But if it is evil in your eyes to serve the LORD, choose today whom you will serve, whether the gods your ancestors served beyond the river or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you now dwell. As for me and my household, we will serve the LORD." ¹⁶ And the people answered and said, "Far be it from us to abandon the LORD to serve other gods. ¹⁷ For the LORD our God is the one who brought us and our ancestors up from the land of Egypt, the house of slavery, who performed these great signs before our eyes and preserved us on all the road we traveled and among all the peoples we passed through. ¹⁸ And the LORD drove out all the peoples and the Amorite who dwelled in the land before us. We too will serve the LORD, for He is our God." ¹⁹ And Joshua said to the people, "You cannot serve the LORD, for He is a holy God, a jealous God who will not bear your sins and your iniquities. ²⁰ If you abandon the LORD and serve foreign gods, He will turn and do evil to you and consume you after He has done good to you." ²¹ And the people said to Joshua, "No, but we will serve the LORD." ²² And Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen for yourselves the LORD to serve Him." And they said, "We are witnesses." ²³ And now, remove the foreign gods that are among you, and turn your hearts to the LORD, the God of Israel." ²⁴ And the people said to Joshua, "We will serve the LORD our God, and we will obey His voice." ²⁵ And Joshua made a covenant with the people that day, and he set for them a law and a judgment in Shechem. ²⁶ And Joshua wrote these words in the book of the Teaching of God, and he took a large stone and set it there under the terebinth that was near the sanctuary of the LORD. ²⁷ And Joshua said to the people, "This stone will be a witness to us, for it has heard all the words the LORD spoke to us. And it will be a witness among you, lest you deny your God." ²⁸ And Joshua sent the people away, each to their inheritance. ²⁹ And it came to pass after these things that Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died at the age of 110 years. ³⁰ And they buried him in his inheritance in Timnath-serah, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, to the north of Mount Gaash. ³¹ And Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who lived long after Joshua and who knew all the works the LORD had done for Israel. ³² And the bones of Joseph, which the children of Israel had brought up from Egypt, were buried in Shechem, in the part of the field that Jacob bought from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for a hundred pieces of silver. And it became an inheritance for the descendants of Joseph. ³³ And Eleazar son of Aaron died, and they buried him in the hill country of Pinehas his son, which had been given to him in the hill country of Ephraim.

Notes
- v. 20: "kavod" in the Greek version is rendered as "covenant," but the Hebrew text does not include this word. The meaning is inferred from the context.
- v. 33: The Hebrew text ends at v. 33, but the Septuagint includes additional content. The translation adheres strictly to the Hebrew text.
