# Joshua 22

¹ Joshua then called to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. ² He said to them, "You have kept all that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, and you have listened to all I have commanded you. ³ You have not abandoned your brothers for many days until now, and you have kept the charge of the commandment of the LORD your God. ⁴ And now the LORD your God has given your brothers rest, as He promised them. And now turn and go to your tents in the land that Moses the servant of the LORD gave you beyond the Jordan. ⁵ But be very careful to do the command and the teaching that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you—to love the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commandments, to hold fast to Him, and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your being." ⁶ Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents. ⁷ To the half-tribe of Manasseh, Moses had given the region of Bashan, and Joshua gave the other half to them with their brothers beyond the Jordan to the west. And when Joshua sent them away to their tents, he blessed them. ⁸ He said to them, "Return to your tents with many goods, with great herds and much silver and gold, and with much bronze and iron, and with many garments. Divide the spoil of your enemies with your brothers." ⁹ Then the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh departed from the midst of the sons of Israel at Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, to go to the land of Gilead, to the land of their inheritance, which they had received by the command of the LORD through Moses. ¹⁰ They came to the region of the Jordan in the land of Canaan, and the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built there a large altar as a visible sign. ¹¹ The sons of Israel heard it said, "The sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built an altar at the border of the land of Canaan, in the region of the Jordan, on the side of the sons of Israel." ¹² When the sons of Israel heard this, they gathered the whole assembly of the sons of Israel at Shiloh to go up against them in battle. ¹³ The sons of Israel sent to the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead, Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, ¹⁴ and ten leaders with him, one leader from each ancestral house of the tribes of Israel, each the head of a thousand in Israel. ¹⁵ They went to the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead, and they spoke to them, saying, ¹⁶ "This is what the whole congregation of the LORD says: What crime is this that you have committed against the God of Israel by turning back from the LORD today to build for yourselves an altar, to rebel against the LORD today?" ¹⁷ Is it too little for us that we have not cleansed ourselves from the sin of Peor to this very day, and a plague has struck the congregation of the LORD? ¹⁸ And now you are turning back from the LORD today, and tomorrow His anger will flare up against the whole congregation of Israel. ¹⁹ But if your land of inheritance is unclean, cross over to the land of the inheritance of the LORD, where the tabernacle of the LORD dwells, and take possession among us. And do not rebel against the LORD, and do not rebel against us by building for yourselves an altar apart from the altar of the LORD our God." ²⁰ "Was it not enough for Achan the son of Zerah that he committed a crime by taking from the devoted things, so that anger fell upon the whole congregation of Israel? And he alone bore his guilt." ²¹ Then the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the leaders of the thousands of Israel. ²² "O God, God, the LORD is God, the LORD is God; He knows, and Israel will know. If we have committed a crime or transgression against the LORD, do not let Him spare us today, ²³ and if we have built this altar to turn back from following the LORD or to offer on it burnt offerings and grain offerings or to make peace offerings, let the LORD Himself require it. ²⁴ But we have done it out of concern, fearing that tomorrow your children might say to our children, 'What have you to do with the LORD, the God of Israel? ²⁵ For the LORD has set a boundary between us and you, the Jordan. You have no share in the LORD. And your children will make our children cease from fearing the LORD.' ²⁶ So we said, 'Let us build this altar, not for burnt offerings or for sacrifices, ²⁷ but as a witness between us and you, and between our generations to come, for our service to the LORD in our burnt offerings, in our sacrifices, and in our peace offerings. So that your children will not say to our children tomorrow, "You have no share in the LORD." ²⁸ And if tomorrow they say to us or to our generations, 'What is this altar you have built?' we will say, 'See the replica of the altar of the LORD, which our fathers built, not for burnt offerings or for sacrifices, but as a witness between us and you, and between our generations to come.' ²⁹ Far be it from us to rebel against the LORD or to turn back from following the LORD today by building an altar for burnt offerings, for grain offerings, or for sacrifices apart from the altar of the LORD our God that stands before His tabernacle." ³⁰ When Phinehas the priest and the leaders of the congregation and the chiefs of the thousands of Israel who were with him heard the words that the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the sons of Manasseh spoke, it was good in their sight. ³¹ Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest said to the sons of Reuben and to the sons of Gad and to the sons of Manasseh, "Today we know that the LORD is among us, that you have not committed this crime against the LORD. You have rescued the sons of Israel from the hand of the LORD." ³² Then Phineas the son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned from the sons of Reuben and from the sons of Gad to the land of Canaan to the sons of Israel, and they gave their report. ³³ The matter was good in the sight of the sons of Israel. And the sons of Israel blessed God. And they did not say to them to go up in battle to destroy the land where the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad were living. ³⁴ The sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad called the altar "A Witness Between Us That the LORD Is God."

Notes
- v. 10: "a large altar as a visible sign" — the Hebrew phrase "מִזְבֵּחַ עַל־הַיַּרְדֵּן מִזְבֵּחַ גָּדוֹל לְמַרְאֶֽה" is rendered as "a large altar as a visible sign" to capture the meaning of the altar being built "for sight" or "as a sign."
- v. 34: "A Witness Between Us That the LORD Is God" — the name of the altar is derived from the Hebrew phrase "עֵד הוּא בֵּינֹתֵינוּ כִּי יְהוָה הָאֱלֹהִֽים" which means "It is a witness between us that the LORD is God."
