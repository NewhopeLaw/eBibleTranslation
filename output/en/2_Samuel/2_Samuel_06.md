# 2 Samuel 6

¹ David gathered more young men in Israel, thirty thousand in all. ² He set out from Hebron with all the people who were with him, to bring up the ark of God from there, which is called by the name of the LORD of Armies, who sits enthroned on the cherubim. ³ They placed the ark of God on a new cart and brought it up from the house of Abinadab, which was in Gibeah. Uzza and his brother Ahio were guiding the new cart. ⁴ They brought it up from the house of Abinadab at Gibeah, and Ahio was walking ahead of the ark. ⁵ David and all the house of Israel were dancing before the LORD with all the terebinth trees, with harps, lyres, tambourines, flutes, and cymbals. ⁶ When they came to the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzza reached out his hand to the ark of God and took hold of it, for the oxen had nearly stumbled. ⁷ The LORD was angry at Uzza and struck him down there for his irreverence, and he died there beside the ark of God. ⁸ David was angry because the LORD had broken out against Uzza, and he named the place Perez-Uzza to this day. ⁹ David was afraid of the LORD that day and said, "How can the ark of the LORD come to me?" ¹⁰ He did not want to bring the ark of the LORD into the city of David, so he diverted it to the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite. ¹¹ The ark of the LORD remained in the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite for three months, and the LORD blessed Obed-Edom and his entire household. ¹² It was reported to King David, "The LORD has blessed the house of Obed-Edom and all that he has, because of the ark of God." So David went down and brought up the ark of God from the house of Obed-Edom to the city of David with joy. ¹³ Whenever the bearers of the ark of the LORD took six steps, David sacrificed a bull and a fattened animal. ¹⁴ David was dancing before the LORD with all his might, wearing only a linen ephod. ¹⁵ David and all the house of Israel were bringing up the ark of the LORD with shouting and with the sound of the horn. ¹⁶ When the ark of the LORD came into the city of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out the window, saw King David leaping and dancing before the LORD, and despised him in her heart. ¹⁷ They brought the ark of the LORD and placed it in its place within the tent David had pitched for it. David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD. ¹⁸ When David had finished offering the burnt and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of Armies. ¹⁹ He distributed to every man and woman in all the great multitude of Israel, from each man to each woman, a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a cake of raisins. Then all the people went to their homes. ²⁰ David returned to bless his household. Michal daughter of Saul came out to meet David and said, "How the king of Israel has honored himself today, exposing himself like one of the common servants!" ²¹ David said to Michal, "Before the LORD, who chose me above your father and all his house to appoint me ruler over the people of the LORD, over Israel, I will dance and make merry before the LORD. ²² I will be even more undignified than this, and I will be low in my own eyes, and among the female servants you spoke of, I will be honored." ²³ Michal daughter of Saul had no children until the day of her death.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "young men" (בָּחוּר) refers to warriors or men of valor, not just youths.
- v. 3: "new cart" (עֲגָלָה חֲדָשָׁה) is the same cart used in v. 4; the repetition is preserved for clarity.
- v. 5: "terebinth trees" (עֲצֵי בְרוֹשִׁים) is a poetic term for trees used in processions or as part of the festive setting.
- v. 10: "city of David" refers to Jerusalem.
- v. 14: "linen ephod" (עֵפוֹד בָּֽד) reflects the simple garment worn for religious dance, not a priestly vestment.
- v. 16: "looked out the window" (נִשְׁקְפָה בְּעַד הַחַלּוֹן) uses the poetic form to describe Michal's observation.
- v. 21: "I will dance and make merry" (שִׂחֲקִים) is rendered more naturally as "make merry" than "dance" alone.
- v. 22: "undignified" (נְקַלֹּתִי) is a rare word, meaning "to be exposed" or "to be shamed."
