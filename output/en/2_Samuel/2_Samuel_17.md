# 2 Samuel 17

¹ Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Let me choose twelve thousand men, and I will rise up and chase after David tonight. ² I will come upon him when he is weary and his hands are slack, and I will strike terror into him, so that all the people with him will flee, and I will strike the king alone. ³ I will return all the people to you, just as a bride returns to her husband. Indeed, you seek only one man's life, and the whole people will be at peace." ⁴ The plan seemed good in the eyes of Absalom and in the eyes of all the elders of Israel. ⁵ Absalom said, "Call also Hushai the Arkite, and let us hear what he says as well." ⁶ Hushai came to Absalom, and Absalom said to him, "This is what Ahithophel has advised. Shall we do according to his advice? Or shall you advise something else?" ⁷ Hushai said to Absalom, "This advice that Ahithophel has given this time is not good. ⁸ You know your father and his men — they are very strong and stubborn of heart, like a wild lioness in the field or like a charging bull. Your father is a man of war and will not stay with the people. ⁹ Now he is surely hiding in one of the ravines or in one of the places. If you attack suddenly, the first to hear it will say, 'A plague has befallen the people who follow Absalom.' ¹⁰ He is also a man of valor, whose heart is like that of a lion. He will not be captured, because all Israel knows that your father is strong and his men are mighty warriors. ¹¹ I advise that you gather all Israel for you, from Dan to Beersheba, as the sand on the seashore in number, and you yourself will lead them ahead. ¹² We will come upon him in one of the places where he is found, and we will fall upon him like the morning dew falls on the ground — not a man or his men will be left, not one. ¹³ If he should gather himself in a city, let all Israel carry ropes to that city, and we will drag him out until we reach the stream, so that not even a single stone there will be left." ¹⁴ Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "The advice of Hushai the Arkite is better than the advice of Ahithophel. For the Lord has commanded to thwart the good advice of Ahithophel, so that the Lord might bring disaster to Absalom." ¹⁵ Hushai said to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, "This and that Ahithophel advised Absalom and the elders of Israel, and this and that I advised. ¹⁶ Now send quickly and inform David, saying, 'Do not spend the night in the desert encampments, and indeed, cross over quickly, lest the king and all the people with him be swallowed up.' ¹⁷ Jonathan and Ahima'az were standing at En-rogel, and the young woman went and informed them. They themselves would go and inform King David, but they could not be seen going into the city. ¹⁸ A boy saw them and told Absalom, and so the two of them hurried and went to a man's house in Bahurim. He had a well in his courtyard, and they went down there. ¹⁹ The woman took the cover and spread it over the face of the well and placed a covering on top of it, so that the matter was not known." ²⁰ Absalom's servants came to the woman at the house and said, "Where are Ahima'az and Jonathan?" The woman told them, "They crossed over the stream. They searched and did not find them, and they returned to Jerusalem." ²¹ After they had gone away, they climbed out of the well and went and told King David. They said to David, "Rise up and cross the river quickly, for Ahithophel has advised such against you." ²² David rose up and all the people with him, and they crossed the Jordan until the morning light, and not a single man was left behind who did not cross the Jordan. ²³ Ahithophel saw that his advice had not been followed, so he saddled his donkey, rose up, and went to his house, to his city. He gave orders to his household, and then hanged himself and died. He was buried in the tomb of his father. ²⁴ David came to Mahanaim, and Absalom crossed the Jordan — he and all the people of Israel with him. ²⁵ Absalom set Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man, and his name was Jether, the Israelite, who had gone in to Abigail the daughter of Nahash, sister of Zeruiah, mother of Joab. ²⁶ All Israel and Absalom encamped in the land of Gilead. ²⁷ When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi the son of Nahash from Rabbah of the children of Ammon, Machir the son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim, ²⁸ brought beds, basins of iron, bowls, fine flour, and barley, meal and wheat, oil and wine, oxen and milk-fed sheep, to feed David and the people with him, for they said, "The people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the desert." ²⁹ They brought these to David and to the people with him to eat, for the people were saying, "We are hungry, exhausted, and thirsty in the desert."

---

Notes
- v. 10: "the heart is like that of a lion" — a metaphor for courage and strength.
- v. 17: "En-rogel" is a place name; "En" means "spring" and "rogel" means "messenger" or "watchtower."
- v. 25: "Amasa... Jether" — Amasa is the name of the man; Jether is the name of his father. The Hebrew clarifies the relationship.
