# 2 Samuel 23

¹ These are the last words of David, the declaration of David, the son of Jesse, and the declaration of the man who was raised up as anointed of the God of Jacob, and the songs of the pleasantness of Israel. ² The spirit of the LORD spoke through me, and his word was on my tongue. ³ The God of Israel spoke to me, the Rock of Israel said, "The one who rules in righteousness, the one who rules in the fear of God." ⁴ He will be like the morning light, like the sun rising without shadow, like the moon in its fullness after rain, like the fresh growth from the earth. ⁵ Indeed, not so is my house with God, for he made with me an everlasting covenant, arranged in all things, and guarded, for all my salvation and all my desire will not sprout. ⁶ But the descendants of Eli will be like a thorn with their points directed upward, for they will not be taken by hand. ⁷ And any man who touches them will be pierced with iron and wood, and they will be burned with fire in the desert. ⁸ These are the names of the mighty men of David, who sat at the table with him, the third in rank, Husham, the head of the three, he was Adino the Eznite, who killed eight hundred men in one assault. ⁹ And after him was Eleazar, the son of his uncle, the son of his brother, among the three mighty men with David, when he provoked the Philistines in their gathering for battle, and the men of Israel rose up. ¹⁰ He rose up and struck the Philistines until his hand grew weary and clung to the sword, and the LORD brought about a great salvation that day, and the people followed him only to take plunder. ¹¹ And after him was Shammah, the son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines had gathered at Lehi, and there was a plot of the field full of lentils, and the people fled from the face of the Philistines. ¹² He stood in the middle of the plot and rescued it and struck the Philistines, and the LORD brought about a great salvation. ¹³ Three of the thirty, leaders, went down to the time of harvest to David at the cave of Adullam, while the Philistine garrison was at the valley of Rephaim. ¹⁴ And David was at the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was at that time in Bethlehem. ¹⁵ David longed and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem that is at the gate?" ¹⁶ And the three mighty men broke through the camp of the Philistines and drew water from the well of Bethlehem that is at the gate and took it and brought it to David, and David did not want to drink it, but poured it out to the LORD. ¹⁷ And he said, "Far be it from me, O LORD, that I should do this! Should I drink the blood of the men who went at the risk of their lives? I will not drink it." These three did. ¹⁸ And Abishai the brother of Joab, the son of Zeruiah, was the head of the three, and he raised his spear against three hundred men and took them, and his name was among the three. ¹⁹ Of the three, he was the most honored, and he became their commander, but he did not come to the three. ²⁰ And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a man of great might from Kabzeel, he struck down two sons of Ariel of Moab, and he went down and struck a lion in the middle of a pit in the snow. ²¹ And he struck down an Egyptian, a man of great stature, and in the Egyptian's hand was a spear like a rod of wood. And he went down to him with the staff and took the spear from the hand of the Egyptian, and killed him with his own spear. ²² These things did Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and his name was among the three mighty men. ²³ He was the most honored of the three, and he did not come to the three, and David placed him among his attendants. ²⁴ And Asahel the brother of Joab, he was among the thirty. Eleazar the son of Dodo, the brother of his uncle, from Bethlehem, ²⁵ Shammoth the Harodite, Elika the Harodite, ²⁶ Helez the Paltite, Irad the son of Ikkesh the Tekeite, ²⁷ Abiezer the Anethothite, Mebunnai the Hushathite, ²⁸ Zalmon the Achoreite, Mahari the Netophathite, ²⁹ Heleb the son of Baanah the Netophathite, Ittai the son of Ribai from the cities of the sons of Benjamin, ³⁰ Benaiah of Pirathon, Hiddai from the streams of Gai, ³¹ Abi-albon the Arabite, Azmaveth the Barhumite, ³² Eliab the Shalbonite, the sons of Jashen, Jonathan, ³³ Shammah the Hararite, Ahiam the son of Sharar the Ararite, ³⁴ Eliphelet the son of Ahasbai the Maachathite, Eliam the son of Ahithophel the Gilonite, ³⁵ Hittai the Carmelite, Paarai the Arbite, ³⁶ Igal the son of Nathan from Zobah, Bani the Gadite, ³⁷ Zelek the Ammonite, Nahari the Beerotite, the bearer of the equipment of Joab the son of Zeruiah, ³⁸ Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite, ³⁹ Uriah the Hittite— thirty-seven in all.

Notes
- v. 6: "the descendants of Eli" is a possible interpretation of the Hebrew, though the name "Eli" is not otherwise mentioned in the context. The text may reference a group or lineage rather than an individual.
- v. 18: "he did not come to the three" may refer to Abishai not being counted among the three original mighty men, but being honored among them.
- v. 20-23: The reference to Benaiah as being "among the three mighty men" but not counted among them may reflect a ranking or distinction in status.
