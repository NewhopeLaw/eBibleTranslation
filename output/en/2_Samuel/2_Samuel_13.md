# 2 Samuel 13

¹ After this, Absalom, the son of David, had a sister named Tamar, and Amnon, the son of David, fell in love with her. She was very beautiful, and Amnon was consumed with passion for his sister Tamar, who was a virgin. ² Amnon was so troubled that he fell ill, because Tamar was too beautiful for him to endure. He was deeply distressed because he could not have her. ³ Amnon had a friend named Jonadab, the son of Shimeah, David’s brother. Jonadab was a very wise man. ⁴ He asked him, “Why are you so quiet, son of the king, all day long? Won’t you tell me anything?” Amnon answered Jonadab, “I love Tamar, my brother Absalom’s sister.” ⁵ Jonadab said to him, “Lie down on your bed and pretend to be sick. When the king comes to see you, ask him to send for Tamar, your sister, so she may prepare some food and bring it to you. Let her cook it in your sight, and eat from her hand.” ⁶ So Amnon lay down and pretended to be sick. The king came to see him, and Amnon said to the king, “Please, let Tamar, my sister, come and prepare some food for me. Let her cook it in my sight and feed it to me, so I may eat from her hand.” ⁷ David sent word to Tamar at home, saying, “Go to your brother Amnon’s house. Prepare food for him.” ⁸ So Tamar went to her brother Amnon’s house, where he was lying down. She took the dough, kneaded it, made the cakes in his sight, and baked the cakes. ⁹ She took the mixing bowl and poured out the bread in front of him. But Amnon refused to eat, and he said, “Get all the men out of my presence.” So they all left him. ¹⁰ Amnon said to Tamar, “Bring the food you prepared into the inner chamber, so I can eat from your hand.” She took the cakes she had made and brought them to her brother Amnon in the inner chamber. ¹¹ When she came to him to feed him, he grabbed her and said to her, “Lie with me, my sister.” ¹² Tamar said to him, “No, my brother, don’t do this evil to me. Such a thing is not done in Israel. Don’t do this disgraceful thing.” ¹³ “Where can I go to remove my shame?” she asked. “You will be one of the fools in Israel. Now, say something to the king, for he will not refuse me.” ¹⁴ But Amnon would not listen to her. He was stronger than her and violated her, lying with her. ¹⁵ Amnon hated her greatly. In fact, the hatred he felt for her was far greater than the love he had felt. He told her, “Get up, go away!” ¹⁶ Tamar said to him, “No, the greatest wrong you have done to me is now to send me away. But he refused to listen to her. ¹⁷ Then he called his personal servant and said, “Send her out from me and shut the door after her.” ¹⁸ She was wearing a long-sleeved tunic, for the daughters of the king, being virgins, wore such garments. His servant sent her out, and he locked the door after her. ¹⁹ Tamar put ashes on her head and tore the long-sleeved tunic she was wearing. She put her hands on her head and went out, weeping as she went. ²⁰ She said to her brother Absalom, “Didn’t my brother Amnon lie with me? Now, my brother, please be quiet. He is your brother. Don’t set your heart against this matter.” But Tamar lived in her brother Absalom’s house in mourning. ²¹ King David heard all these things and was very angry. ²² But Absalom did not speak to Amnon, either good or bad, from that day on. Absalom hated Amnon because he had violated his sister Tamar. ²³ After two years, when shearing time came, Absalom invited all the king’s sons to a feast in Baal Hazor, which is in the territory of Ephraim. ²⁴ Absalom went to the king and said, “Your servant has a feast. Please, let the king and his servants go with me.” ²⁵ The king said to Absalom, “No, my son, don’t go all of us together. I would not want to be a burden to you.” But Absalom pressed him, and the king refused to go. He blessed him. ²⁶ Absalom said, “If you will not go, then at least let my brother Amnon go with us.” The king asked, “Why should he go with you?” ²⁷ Absalom pressed him again, and the king sent Amnon and all his other sons along with him. ²⁸ Absalom commanded his servants, “Watch carefully. When Amnon’s heart is cheered by the wine, and I say to you, ‘Strike Amnon and kill him,’ then act. Don’t be afraid, for I have given you this command. Be strong and show yourselves as men of valor.” ²⁹ The servants of Absalom did to Amnon as Absalom had commanded. Then all the king’s sons got up, mounted their mules, and fled. ³⁰ While they were still on the road, the report reached David, saying, “Absalom has killed all the king’s sons. Not one of them was left.” ³¹ The king stood up, tore his clothes, and lay on the ground. All his servants who were with him also tore their clothes. ³² Jonadab, the son of Shimeah, David’s brother, answered and said, “My lord, the king, don’t suppose that all the young men, the king’s sons, are dead. Only Amnon has died. This was planned by Absalom from the day he violated his sister Tamar. ³³ Now, my lord the king, don’t let it weigh on your heart that all your sons are dead. Only Amnon has died.” ³⁴ Absalom escaped, and the watchman raised his eyes and saw that a large crowd was coming along the road from the side of the hill. He went and told the king, “I see some men coming along the road from the side of the hill. It seems to me that they are the king’s sons.” ³⁵ Jonadad said to the king, “They are the king’s sons. Just as your servant said, so it has happened.” ³⁶ As he was still speaking, the king’s sons arrived. They raised their voices and wept, and the king and all his servants wept with great weeping. ³⁷ Absalom fled and went to Talmai, the son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. David mourned for his son all the days. ³⁸ Absalom fled and went to Geshur. He remained there for three years. ³⁹ David did not send to bring him back, because he was still grieving over Amnon, because he had died.

Notes
- v. 15: "He hated her greatly" — the Hebrew says "he hated her a great hatred," which is a rare phrase used to emphasize the intensity of Amnon's hatred.
