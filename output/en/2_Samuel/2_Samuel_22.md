# 2 Samuel 22

¹ David spoke these words of the song to the Lord on the day the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. ² He said: “The Lord is my rock and my fortress, my deliverer. ³ My God is my strong refuge, in whom I trust. He is my shield, my horn of salvation, my stronghold, my refuge, my savior. He rescues me from violence. ⁴ I will praise the Lord, and I will be saved from my enemies. ⁵ For I was surrounded by those who crushed me to death, by streams of lawlessness overwhelming me. ⁶ The cords of Sheol wrapped around me, the snares of death overwhelmed me. ⁷ In my distress I called to the Lord, I cried to my God. From his palace he heard my voice, and my cry reached his ears. ⁸ Then the earth trembled and the heavens shuddered, the foundations of the heavens trembled, because the Lord was angry. ⁹ Smoke rose in his anger, fire from his mouth devoured charcoals, it burned from him. ¹⁰ He bent the heavens and came down, and a cloud beneath his feet. ¹¹ He rode on a cherub and flew, appearing on the wings of the wind. ¹² He wrapped himself in darkness as a canopy, deep clouds as his tent, thick clouds from the sky. ¹³ From his brightness, flashes of light burned coals of fire. ¹⁴ The Lord thundered from heaven and the Most High gave forth his voice. ¹⁵ He sent out arrows and scattered them, lightning and made them tremble. ¹⁶ The channels of the sea were seen, the foundations of the world were uncovered, at the Lord’s outburst, at the breath of his anger. ¹⁷ He sent from on high and lifted me, he drew me out of many waters. ¹⁸ He rescued me from my mighty enemy, from those who hated me, for they were stronger than I. ¹⁹ He brought me out before the day of my distress, and the Lord became my support. ²⁰ He led me into a wide place, and rescued me, for he had pleasure in me. ²¹ The Lord repaid me according to my righteousness, according to the purity of my hands he returned to me. ²² For I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not rebelled against my God. ²³ For all his judgments were before me, and his statutes I did not turn aside from. ²⁴ I have remained blameless before him and kept myself from my sin. ²⁵ And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness, according to the purity of my hands in his sight. ²⁶ With the righteous, he shows righteousness; with the blameless, he makes them blameless. ²⁷ With the pure, he makes them pure; with the crooked, he makes them crooked. ²⁸ He rescues the poor from the arrogant, he brings the eyes down from the lofty. ²⁹ For you are my lamp, O Lord, and the Lord will light my darkness. ³⁰ For with you I run against a band, with my God I leap over a wall. ³¹ The God whose way is perfect, whose word is refined, a shield to all who take refuge in him. ³² Who is like God, our rock, the Lord who is the fortress of our lives? ³³ The God who is my strength, who makes my path blameless. ³⁴ He sets my feet like those of a deer, and he steadies me on the heights. ³⁵ He teaches my hands for battle, and he makes a bronze sword rest on my arms. ³⁶ You have given me the shield of your salvation, and your answers have multiplied me. ³⁷ You have expanded my steps beneath me, and my feet have not slipped. ³⁸ I will pursue my enemies and destroy them. I will not turn back until they are finished. ³⁹ I will crush them and they will not rise. They will fall beneath my feet. ⁴⁰ You have strengthened me with might for battle, you will bend those who rise against me beneath me. ⁴¹ You gave my enemies their backs, those who hated me, and I destroyed them. ⁴² They cried out, but there was no savior. To the Lord they cried, but he did not answer them. ⁴³ I will treat them like dust of the earth, like clay of the streets I will crush them. ⁴⁴ You will deliver me from the disputes of the people, you will protect me as the leader of nations. A people I did not know will serve me. ⁴⁵ Foreigners will be deceived by me. They will listen with their ears to what I say. ⁴⁶ Foreigners will be dismayed and come out from their strongholds. ⁴⁷ The Lord lives, and blessed be my rock, and exalted be my God, the savior of my life. ⁴⁸ The God who gives me vengeance and humbles peoples beneath me. ⁴⁹ And he will bring me out from my enemies, and he will lift me up from those who rise against me. From the wicked man he will rescue me. ⁵⁰ Therefore I will praise you, O Lord, among the nations, and to your name I will sing. ⁵¹ Great are the deliverances of his king, and the one who does steadfast love to his anointed, to David and to his descendants forever.

Notes
- v. 21: "the purity of my hands" — the Hebrew uses "bor yadi" which can be read as "the purity of my hands" or "the strength of my hands." The context of righteousness and divine reward supports the former.
- v. 49: "from the wicked man" — the Hebrew text uses "ish chamasi," which can mean "man of violence" or "wicked man." Both are acceptable, but "wicked man" fits the poetic parallelism better.
