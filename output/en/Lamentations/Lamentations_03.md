# Lamentations 3

¹ I am the man who has seen poverty, struck by the rod of His anger. ² He has led me and carried me into darkness, and there is no light. ³ Yet He has turned His hand against me all day long. ⁴ He has worn out my flesh and my skin; He has broken my bones. ⁵ He has built a wall against me and surrounded my head with a thornbush. ⁶ He has seated me in darkness like those long dead. ⁷ He has built a wall behind me; I cannot escape. He has burdened me with a chain. ⁸ Even if I cry out and beg, He has sealed my prayer. ⁹ He has built my paths with a wall, and He has made my ways crooked. ¹⁰ He is a lion hunting me, a leopard lurking in secret. ¹¹ He has led me in a roundabout way and has thrown me into desolation. ¹² He has bent His bow and set me as a target for an arrow. ¹³ He has driven His arrows into my kidneys, the arrows of His quiver. ¹⁴ I have become a laughingstock to all my people; their song through the day. ¹⁵ He has filled me with bitterness, made me drunk on gall. ¹⁶ He has ground my teeth with gravel, made me swallow ashes. ¹⁷ My soul has abandoned peace; I have forgotten what is good. ¹⁸ I said, “My strength has perished, and my hope from the LORD.” ¹⁹ Remember my affliction and my bitterness, the wormwood and the pain. ²⁰ Remember them, O LORD, and do not let my soul forget. ²¹ This I will recall to my heart, therefore I will hope. ²² The steadfast love of the LORD is not exhausted, His mercy is not spent. ²³ They are renewed each morning; great is Your faithfulness. ²⁴ The LORD is my portion, said my soul, therefore I will hope in Him. ²⁵ The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul that seeks Him. ²⁶ It is good to wait in silence for the salvation of the LORD. ²⁷ It is good for a man to bear the yoke in his youth. ²⁸ Let him sit alone in silence, if the burden is upon him. ²⁹ Let him put his face into the dust—perhaps there is hope. ³⁰ Let him give his cheek to the one who strikes him, be filled with insults. ³¹ For the LORD will not reject forever. ³² But with compassion He will have mercy, because of His great steadfast love. ³³ For He does not take pleasure in the death of the righteous, nor does He afflict the children of men. ³⁴ To crush all prisoners underfoot, every captive of the earth. ³⁵ To pervert the judgment of a man, to set it before the face of the Most High. ³⁶ To frustrate a man in his cause, the LORD does not see. ³⁷ Who has spoken and it came to be? The LORD has not commanded it. ³⁸ From the mouth of the Most High, neither evil nor good goes forth. ³⁹ Why should a living man complain, a man about his sins? ⁴⁰ Let us examine our ways, let us search and return to the LORD. ⁴¹ Let our hearts lift up their hands to the God in heaven. ⁴² We have sinned, we have been rebellious, and You have not forgiven. ⁴³ You have covered with anger and pursued us, You have killed and not shown mercy. ⁴⁴ You have covered with a cloud, so that our prayer cannot pass through. ⁴⁵ You have made us a disgrace and a mockery among the nations. ⁴⁶ All our enemies have opened their mouths against us. ⁴⁷ Fear and dread have come to us, the sword and the burning. ⁴⁸ From the lofty places I will pour down my tears, over the destruction of my daughter, the daughter of my people. ⁴⁹ My eyes have become dim, they will not be still until the LORD looks from heaven. ⁵⁰ My eyes are on me from all the daughters of my city. ⁵¹ My eyes are weary, my soul is worn out, by the hand of all the daughters of my city. ⁵² They caught me like a bird, my enemies for no reason. ⁵³ They cut off my life in the pit, laid a stone over me. ⁵⁴ Water poured over my head, I said, “I am cut off.” ⁵⁵ I called on Your name, O LORD, from the depths of the pit. ⁵⁶ My voice You heard; do not hide Your ear from my plea, from my cry for help. ⁵⁷ You came near on the day I called on You, You said, “Do not fear.” ⁵⁸ You have multiplied the disputes of my soul, You have redeemed my life. ⁵⁹ You have seen, O LORD, my wrongs; judge my cause. ⁶⁰ You have seen all their vengeance, all their schemes against me. ⁶¹ You have heard their taunts, O LORD, all their schemes against me. ⁶² The lips of those who rise against me, their thoughts against me all day. ⁶³ Their sitting and their standing, look—watch their eyes. ⁶⁴ Return to them, O LORD, their deeds, what their hands have done. ⁶⁵ Give them a heart of stone, Your wrath against them. ⁶⁶ Pursue them in anger and destroy them from beneath the heavens, O LORD.

Notes  
- v. 10: "He is a lion hunting me, a leopard in secret" — the Hebrew uses metaphor to describe divine judgment.  
- v. 22-23: "The steadfast love of the LORD is not exhausted, His mercy is not spent. They are renewed each morning" — the Hebrew uses poetic parallelism, which is preserved in the translation.  
- v. 53: "They cut off my life in the pit" — "cut off" is a poetic metaphor for being killed or destroyed.  
- v. 54: "Water poured over my head, I said, 'I am cut off'" — "cut off" is a metaphor for being destroyed or abandoned.  
- v. 64-66: The final verses are a plea for divine justice. The translation preserves the imperative tone and poetic form.
