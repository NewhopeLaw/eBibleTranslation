# Lamentations 4

¹ How has the gold grown dim, the fine silver tarnished! The sacred stones have been scattered at the head of all the streets. ² The sons of Zion, who were precious, who were adorned with gold, how they are counted as clay vessels, the work of a potter’s hands! ³ Even jackals have stripped their breast, they have suckled their cubs on the daughter of my people, like doves in the wilderness. ⁴ The tongue of the nursing child has clung to the roof of its mouth with thirst. The young infants have asked for bread; the baker has no more to give. ⁵ Those who once feasted on delicacies have perished in the streets; those who were nurtured in luxury have ended up in ash heaps. ⁶ The iniquity of the daughter of my people has grown greater than the sin of Sodom, which was overthrown in a moment, and no hand was laid upon it. ⁷ Their Nazirites were whiter than snow; their skin was fairer than milk; their bodies were redder than rubies, their appearance like sapphire. ⁸ Their countenance has turned black, no one is recognized in the streets; their skin clings to their bones, it has become as dry as wood. ⁹ The victims of the sword were better than those who died of hunger—those who perished with their flesh pierced by the field’s thorns. ¹⁰ The hands of compassionate women have boiled their children, they have become food for them in the ruin of the daughter of my people. ¹¹ The LORD has spent His wrath, He has poured out His fierce anger; He has kindled a fire in Jerusalem, and it has devoured the foundations of her palaces. ¹² The kings of the earth did not believe, all who dwell on the earth, that an adversary and an enemy would enter the gates of Jerusalem. ¹³ From the sins of her prophets, the iniquities of her priests, who have shed the blood of the righteous in the midst of her. ¹⁴ They wandered in blindness in the streets, stained with blood, and no one could touch them. They searched for their fine garments, but found none. ¹⁵ “Depart, unclean,” they cried to them. “Depart, depart, do not touch us!” For they have been scattered and driven away. They said among the nations, “They shall no longer dwell with us.” ¹⁶ The face of the LORD has turned against them; He will no longer look upon them. The face of the priests He did not take into account, and the elders He did not spare. ¹⁷ Still we wait in vain for our help, in vain we look for relief. We have waited for a nation that does not save. ¹⁸ We have been overtaken in our steps from the kingdom, our paths have been cut off in the streets. Our end has come near; our days are fulfilled, for our end has arrived. ¹⁹ Our pursuers were lighter than eagles in the sky. They waited for us on the mountains, they lay in ambush for us in the wilderness. ²⁰ Our breath, the anointed of the LORD, was taken in their traps, as we said, “In His shadow we shall live among the nations.” ²¹ Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom, you who dwell in the land of Uz. Even you too shall be given the cup; you shall drink and stagger. ²² The punishment for your iniquity, daughter of Zion, has come to an end. She will no longer be exiled. The punishment for your iniquity, daughter of Edom, has been revealed, the sins of you have been exposed.

---

Notes  
- v. 7: "redder than rubies" is a contextual rendering of "admonu" and "sapphire" for "gizrotam" — literal alternatives would be "cut like rubies" or "like sapphire their cutting," but the poetic comparison is retained.  
- v. 21: "daughter of Edom" and "land of Uz" are contextual identifications of the poetic imagery, not explicit in the Hebrew but implied by the reference to Edom.  
- v. 21: The Septuagint adds "the cup of the LORD" as a direct reference to judgment, which is reflected here.
