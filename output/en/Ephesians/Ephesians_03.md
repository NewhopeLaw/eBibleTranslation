# Ephesians 3

¹ For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus, am serving you Gentiles— ² if indeed you have heard of the administration of God’s grace that was given to me for you, ³ for it was revealed to me in a vision the mystery, as I briefly wrote before, ⁴ which you can discern as you read and understand my insight into the mystery of Christ, ⁵ a mystery not made known to previous generations of human beings, but now revealed to God’s holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit— ⁶ that the Gentiles are fellow heirs, and fellow members, and fellow participants of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel, ⁷ of which I became a servant according to the gift of God’s grace given to me by the power of His energy— ⁸ to me, the least of all God’s holy ones, this grace was given—to proclaim to the Gentiles the insearchable riches of Christ, ⁹ and to make known to all what is the administration of the mystery that has been hidden for ages in God, who created all things, ¹⁰ so that now the manifold wisdom of God might be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms through the church, ¹¹ according to His eternal purpose, which He set forth in Christ Jesus our Lord, ¹² in whom we have boldness and access with confidence through faith in Him. ¹³ Therefore I urge you not to be discouraged by my sufferings for you, which are your glory. ¹⁴ For this reason I kneel before the Father, ¹⁵ from whom every fatherly name in heaven and on earth is named, ¹⁶ that He may grant you, according to the riches of His glory, to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in the inner man, ¹⁷ that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, rooted and grounded in love, ¹⁸ so that you may be able to comprehend with all the holy ones what is the breadth and length and height and depth, ¹⁹ and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God. ²⁰ Now to Him who is able to do exceeding abundantly beyond all that we ask or think, according to the power that works in us, ²¹ to Him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations forever and ever. Amen.

---

Notes
- v. 9: "φωτίσαι πάντας τίς ἡ οἰκονομία" is best rendered as "to make known to all what is the administration"—a complex construction where "πάντας" is dative, meaning "to all."
- v. 18: "καταλαβέσθαι σὺν πᾶσιν τοῖς ἁγίοις τί τὸ πλάτος καὶ μῆκος καὶ ὕψος καὶ βάθος" is a poetic, metaphorical structure. The phrase is best translated as "to comprehend... what is the breadth and length and height and depth," without adding interpretive adjectives like "infinite" or "unfathomable."
