# Ephesians 4

¹ So then, I, Paul, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to live in a way worthy of your calling. ² With all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love, ³ striving to preserve the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace, ⁴ one body and one Spirit, just as you were called in one hope of your calling, ⁵ one Lord, one faith, one baptism, ⁶ one God and Father of all, who is above all and through all and in all. ⁷ But to each one of us grace was given according to the measure of the gift of Christ. ⁸ Therefore it says, "When He ascended on high, He led captivity captive, He gave gifts to men." ⁹ Now what does "He ascended" mean except that He had also descended into the lower parts of the earth? ¹⁰ The one who descended is the same who ascended far above all the heavens, in order to fill all things. ¹¹ And He gave some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, some as shepherds and teachers, ¹² for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the building up of the body of Christ, ¹³ until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature person, to the measure of the fullness of Christ, ¹⁴ so that we may no longer be children, tossed about and carried around by every wind of teaching, by the cunning of people in their deceitful schemes, ¹⁵ but speaking the truth in love, we ourselves will grow up in Him, who is the head, Christ, ¹⁶ from whom the whole body, fitted and held together by every joint with which it is equipped, when each part is working properly, causes the growth of the body for the building up of itself in love. ¹⁷ Therefore I say this and testify in the Lord: you must no longer walk as the Gentiles walk, in the futility of their minds, ¹⁸ darkened in understanding, alienated from the life of God, because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardness of their hearts, ¹⁹ having given themselves over to sensuality, indulging in every kind of impurity with greed. ²⁰ But you did not learn that way about Christ, ²¹ assuming that you heard about Him and were taught in Him, just as truth is in Jesus, ²² to put off your former way of life, the old self that is being corrupted according to deceitful desires, ²³ to be renewed in the spirit of your minds, ²⁴ and to put on the new self, created after God in true righteousness and holiness. ²⁵ Therefore, putting away falsehood, speak truthfully with your neighbor, for we are members of one another. ²⁶ Be angry but do not sin. Do not let the sun go down on your anger, ²⁷ nor give place to the devil. ²⁸ Let the thief no longer steal, but rather let him labor, doing honest work with his own hands, so that he may have something to give to anyone in need. ²⁹ Let no corrupting talk come from your mouth, but only what is good for building up, as fits the occasion, that it may give grace to those who hear. ³⁰ And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, with whom you were sealed for the day of redemption. ³¹ Let all bitterness, wrath, anger, clamor, and slander be put away from you, along with all malice. ³² Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ also forgave you.

---

Notes
- v. 9: "the lower parts of the earth" is a reference to Christ's descent, possibly to Sheol or Hades, interpreted here as a metaphorical or spiritual descent rather than a literal geographic one.
- v. 14: "every wind of teaching" reflects the metaphor of being blown by the teachings of deceptive people, not literal winds.
- v. 16: "every joint" is a metaphor for the interdependent roles within the body of Christ, not actual physical joints.
