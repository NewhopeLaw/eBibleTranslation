# Ephesians 5

¹ So then, be imitators of God, as beloved children, ² and walk in love, just as Christ also loved us and gave himself up for us, a sacrifice and offering to God as a pleasing aroma. ³ But all kinds of immorality and uncleanness or greed must not even be named among you, for they are fitting for holy ones, ⁴ nor any indecency or foolish speech or crude humor, which are not fitting, but rather thankfulness. ⁵ For you must know this, understanding that every immoral person or unclean one or avaricious one, who is an idolater, does not inherit the kingdom of Christ and God. ⁶ Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the children of disobedience. ⁷ Therefore do not become partakers with them; ⁸ for you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light, ⁹ for the fruit of the light is in every kind of goodness and righteousness and truth, ¹⁰ discerning what is pleasing to the Lord; ¹¹ and do not take part in the fruitless works of darkness, but rather even expose them, ¹² for what is done in secret among them is shameful to speak of; ¹³ but when everything is exposed by the light, it becomes visible, ¹⁴ for everything that becomes visible is light. Therefore it is said, "Wake up, you who are sleeping, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you." ¹⁵ So be careful how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, ¹⁶ redeeming the time, for the days are evil. ¹⁷ Therefore do not be foolish, but understand what is the will of the Lord, ¹⁸ and do not get drunk on wine, which leads to debauchery, but be filled with the Spirit, ¹⁹ speaking to yourselves with psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making music in your hearts to the Lord, ²⁰ always giving thanks for everything in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God the Father, ²¹ submitting to one another in the fear of Christ. ²² Wives to your own husbands, as to the Lord, ²³ for the husband is the head of the wife, just as Christ is the head of the church, himself the savior of the body. ²⁴ But as the church is subject to Christ, so also the wives to their husbands in everything. ²⁵ Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave himself up for her, ²⁶ to sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of water with the word, ²⁷ to present her to himself glorious, the church, having no spot or wrinkle or anything like that, but that she may be holy and blameless. ²⁸ So husbands must also love their own wives as their own bodies. The one who loves his wife loves himself, ²⁹ for no one has ever hated his own body, but he nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ also does the church, ³⁰ for we are members of his body. ³¹ For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and will cling to his wife, and the two will become one flesh. ³² This is a great mystery, but I speak in reference to Christ and the church. ³³ Nevertheless, each of you, as individuals, must love your own wife as yourself, and the wife must fear her husband.

---

Notes
- v. 21: "submit to one another in the fear of Christ" — the Greek uses "φόβῳ" (fear), which can mean reverence or awe rather than fear in the sense of terror.
- v. 28: "the one who loves his wife loves himself" — the Greek uses a reflexive pronoun ("ἑαυτόν"), which is translated as "himself" to avoid confusion with the object of love.
