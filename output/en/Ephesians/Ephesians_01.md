# Ephesians 1

¹ Paul, an apostle of the Messiah Jesus by the will of God, to the holy ones who are in Ephesus and to the faithful in the Messiah Jesus— ² grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ³ Blessed is the God and Father of our Lord Jesus the Messiah, who has blessed us in every spiritual blessing in the heavens in Christ, ⁴ just as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, to be holy and blameless before him in love, ⁵ predestining us for adoption as sons through Jesus the Messiah, according to the good pleasure of his will, ⁶ to the praise of the glory of his grace, with which he has graced us in the Beloved, ⁷ in whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace ⁸ which he has lavished on us in all wisdom and understanding, ⁹ having made known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he purposed in himself, ¹⁰ to bring about the administration of the fullness of the times, to unite all things in the Messiah, things in the heavens and things on the earth, in him, ¹¹ in whom also we were made heirs, having been predestined according to the purpose of the one who works all things according to the counsel of his will, ¹² to the end that we may be for the praise of his glory, we who had already hoped in the Messiah. ¹³ In whom you also, having heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, and having believed, were sealed with the Spirit of the promise, the holy one, ¹⁴ who is the down payment of our inheritance, for the redemption of the possession, to the praise of his glory. ¹⁵ For this reason, I also, having heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints, ¹⁶ do not cease to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers, ¹⁷ so that the God of our Lord Jesus the Messiah, the Father of glory, may grant you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him, ¹⁸ having enlightened the eyes of your heart, so that you may know what is the hope of his calling, what is the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints, ¹⁹ and what is the surpassing greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his might ²⁰ which he worked in the Messiah, raising him from the dead and seating him at his right hand in the heavens, ²¹ far above every ruler and authority and power and dominion and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come; ²² and all things he placed under his feet, and gave him to be head over all things to the church, ²³ which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills all things in all.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: The phrase "τοῖς ἁγίοις τοῖς οὖσιν [ἐν Ἐφέσῳ]" is translated as a single clause to reflect the original's structure and intent.  
- v. 10: "οἰκονομίαν τοῦ πληρώματος τῶν καιρῶν" is rendered as "administration of the fullness of the times" to capture the theological nuance of "oikonomia."  
- v. 21: "παντὸς ὀνόματος ὀνομαζομένου" is translated as "every name that is named" to reflect the participial construction and its emphasis on inclusion.
