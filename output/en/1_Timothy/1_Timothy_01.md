# 1 Timothy 1

¹ Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and Christ Jesus, our hope, ² to Timothy, my true child in faith: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord. ³ As I urged you when I was going to Macedonia, remain in Ephesus so that you may command some not to teach false doctrine, ⁴ nor to pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which give rise to mere speculation rather than the stewardship of God, which is by faith. ⁵ But the goal of the command is love, from a pure heart and a good conscience and sincere faith. ⁶ By these some have strayed and gone into vain talk, ⁷ wanting to be law-teachers, yet not understanding what they say or about what they are persuaded. ⁸ We know that the law is good if anyone uses it lawfully, ⁹ knowing this, that the law is not laid down for the righteous, but for lawbreakers and rebels, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for those who strike their fathers and mothers, for murderers, ¹⁰ for the sexually immoral, for men who practice homosexuality, for enslavers, for liars, for perjurers, and for whatever else is contrary to the sound doctrine, ¹¹ according to the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, which was entrusted to me. ¹² I thank him who has given me strength, Christ Jesus our Lord, because he judged me faithful, appointing me to his service, ¹³ though I was formerly a blasphemer, a persecutor, and a violent aggressor. But I was shown mercy because I acted in ignorance in unbelief, ¹⁴ and the grace of our Lord overflowed for me with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. ¹⁵ The saying is trustworthy and deserving of full acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am the foremost. ¹⁶ But I was shown mercy for this reason, so that in me, as the first, Christ Jesus might display his perfect patience as an example to those who were to believe in him for eternal life. ¹⁷ To the King of the ages, immortal, invisible, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen. ¹⁸ This charge I entrust to you, child Timothy, according to the prophecies previously spoken about you, that by them you may wage the good warfare, ¹⁹ holding on to faith and a good conscience, which some have rejected and shipwrecked their own faith; ²⁰ among whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan that they may learn not to blaspheme.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Christ Jesus" is used to maintain a clear distinction between Jesus and Christ as titles, following the Greek's emphasis on the Messiahship.
- v. 7: "law-teachers" is a rendering of νομοδιδάσκαλοι that avoids archaic phrasing.
- v. 8: "uses it lawfully" reflects the meaning of νομίμως rather than a direct translation of the Greek.
- v. 10: "men who practice homosexuality" reflects the meaning of ἀρσενοκοίταις, which is debated but generally understood to refer to specific sexual practices.
- v. 20: "handed over to Satan" reflects the common understanding of παρέδωκα τῷ Σατανᾷ as a metaphor for spiritual discipline or exile.
