# 1 Timothy 3

¹ The saying is trustworthy: If anyone aspires to be an overseer, it means they desire a noble task. ² An overseer must be above reproach, married to one wife, temperate, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, and a teacher, ³ not given to drinking, not violent, but gentle, not quarrelsome, and not a lover of money. ⁴ They must manage their own household well, keeping their children under control with all dignity, ⁵ for if someone does not know how to manage their own household, how will they care for God's assembly? ⁶ They must not be a recent convert, or they might fall into condemnation and become pawns of the devil. ⁷ Moreover, they must have a good reputation among outsiders, so they do not fall into disgrace or become a trap for the devil. ⁸ In the same way, deacons must be dignified, not given to slander, not addicted to wine, and not greedy for money, ⁹ keeping the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience. ¹⁰ These people must also be tested first, and then serve if they prove themselves beyond reproach. ¹¹ In the same way, the women must be dignified, not slanderers, temperate, and faithful in everything. ¹² Deacons must be married to only one wife and must manage their children and their own households well. ¹³ For those who serve well gain a good standing for themselves and great boldness in the faith that is in Christ Jesus. ¹⁴ I am writing these things to you so that, if I delay coming, you will know how to conduct yourself in God's house, ¹⁵ which is the assembly of the living God, the pillar and foundation of the truth. ¹⁶ And this is acknowledged to be a great mystery of godliness: He who appeared in flesh, was vindicated in spirit, was seen by angels, was proclaimed among the nations, was believed in the world, and was taken up in glory.

---

Notes
- v. 16: The final verse is a poetic summary, often interpreted as a Christological hymn. The structure "He who appeared in flesh..." is rendered in a flowing English prose that respects the original's rhythm while maintaining clarity.
