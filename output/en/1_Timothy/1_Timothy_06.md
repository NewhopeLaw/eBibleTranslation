# 1 Timothy 6

¹ Those who are under a yoke as slaves must consider their own masters to be worthy of all honor, so that the name of God and our teaching might not be blasphemed. ² But those who have believing masters must not be disrespectful, since they are brothers. Rather, they must serve all the more because they are believers and beloved who share in the benefits of good works. Teach and urge these things. ³ If anyone teaches a different doctrine and does not agree with the healthy words of our Lord Jesus Christ and the teaching that is in accordance with piety, ⁴ they are puffed up with conceit, knowing nothing, and are sick with endless speculations and arguments from which arise envy, quarrels, blasphemies, and evil suspicions, ⁵ constant disruptions from people who are corrupted in mind and deprived of the truth, who consider piety to be a means of profit. ⁶ But true devotion with contentment is a great gain; ⁷ for we brought nothing into the world, and we can’t take anything out of it. ⁸ But if we have food and clothing, with these we will be content. ⁹ Those who desire to be rich fall into temptation and a snare, into many foolish and harmful desires, which plunge people into destruction and ruin. ¹⁰ For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. Some have strayed from the faith because of it and pierced themselves with many pains. ¹¹ But you, O man of God, flee these things. Pursue righteousness, devotion, faith, love, endurance, and gentleness. ¹² Fight the good fight of the faith; take hold of the eternal life to which you were called and to which you made the good confession in the presence of many witnesses. ¹³ I charge you before God, the one who gives life to all things, and before Christ Jesus, who gave the good confession before Pontius Pilate, ¹⁴ to keep the commandment without spot or blemish until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ, ¹⁵ which at the proper time the blessed and only Sovereign, the King of kings and Lord of lords, will reveal, ¹⁶ the only one who has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light, whom no one has seen or can see. To him be glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen. ¹⁷ Command those who are rich in this present age not to be arrogant, nor to place their hope in the uncertainty of wealth, but in God, who richly provides us everything to enjoy, ¹⁸ to do good, to be rich in good works, to be generous and willing to share, ¹⁹ laying up for themselves a good foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of the life that is truly life. ²⁰ O Timothy, guard the deposit entrusted to you, avoiding the profane and empty chatter, and the contradictions of what is falsely called knowledge, ²¹ which some have professed and thereby gone astray from the faith. Grace be with you all.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "those who are under a yoke as slaves" follows the meaning of "ὑπὸ ζυγὸν δοῦλοι" literally and contextually.
- v. 3: "healthy words" renders "ὑγιαίνουσι λόγοις" as a translation of "sound teachings."
- v. 15: "the blessed and only Sovereign" renders "ὁ μακάριος καὶ μόνος δυνάστης," aligning with the elevated title for God in this context.
- v. 16: "To him be glory and dominion forever and ever" is a traditional rendering of the final benediction, following the structure of similar doxologies.
