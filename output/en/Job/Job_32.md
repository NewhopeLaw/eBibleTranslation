# Job 32

¹ The three men ceased to answer Job, for they saw that he was righteous in his own eyes. ² Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, from the family of Ram, became greatly angry with Job. His anger burned against Job because Job justified himself before God. ³ His anger burned also against the three friends, because they had failed to find an answer, and had instead declared Job guilty. ⁴ Elihu waited to speak to Job, for they were older than him in years. ⁵ Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouths of the three men, and his anger burned. ⁶ Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I am young in years, but you are the aged. Therefore I held my peace, and feared to declare my knowledge to you. ⁷ I thought, Days will speak, and many years will make known wisdom. ⁸ But in truth, it is the spirit in a person, and the breath of the Almighty who gives understanding. ⁹ The many are not wise, and the aged do not understand justice. ¹⁰ Therefore I say, Listen to me, and I will declare my knowledge to you as well. ¹¹ I have listened to your words, and have given ear to your reasonings, until you have made your sayings plain. ¹² But I was considering, and behold, there was no one to answer Job, none among you to confute his words. ¹³ Lest you say, We have found wisdom, and God will not give an answer, though it is a human being. ¹⁴ For no arguments were arranged for me, and in your words I did not answer him. ¹⁵ I held my peace, for they did not answer. I waited, but they spoke no more. ¹⁶ And I waited, for they did not speak. They stood in silence, and answered no more. ¹⁷ Therefore I will answer, I too. I too will declare my knowledge. ¹⁸ For my words are ready within me, my heart is overfull as if it were wine, restrained no longer. ¹⁹ My heart is like new wineskins ready to burst. ²⁰ I will speak, and find relief for myself. I will open my mouth and answer. ²¹ I will not show partiality to any man, nor will I flatter any person. ²² For I do not know how to flatter, and my Maker will not lift me up.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "the family of Ram" — the Hebrew phrase מִמִּשְׁפַּחַת רָם is translated as "from the family of Ram" to preserve the familial connection.
- v. 21: "my Maker will not lift me up" — the Hebrew literally reads "my Maker will not give me weight." The phrase כִּמְעַט יִשָּׂאֵנִי עֹשֵׂנִי is metaphorical, suggesting that if I were to flatter, my Maker would not grant me significance.
