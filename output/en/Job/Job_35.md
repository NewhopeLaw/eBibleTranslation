# Job 35

¹ Elihu answered and said: ² Have you considered this as justice, saying, "My righteousness is before God"? ³ For if you say, "What will He do for you? What will He take from your sin?" ⁴ I will answer you with words, and your companions with you. ⁵ Look to the heavens and see; and behold, the clouds are higher than you. ⁶ If you have sinned, what will you do to Him? Or if your sins multiply, what will you do to Him? ⁷ If you are righteous, what will you give to Him? Or who will receive a gift from your hand? ⁸ To a man like yourself, your sin is reckoned; and to a human, your righteousness. ⁹ From the many who are oppressed, they cry out; they raise their voices from numerous hands. ¹⁰ Yet no one says, "Where is the God who made me, who gives songs in the night? ¹¹ Who has set me apart from the beasts of the earth and from the winged creatures of the sky?" ¹² There they cry out, and He does not answer, because of the arrogance of the wicked. ¹³ Indeed, He does not listen to emptiness; and the Almighty does not look upon it. ¹⁴ Though you say, "He will not judge according to your case, nor will He bring judgment before Him." ¹⁵ And now, because He does not yet notice His anger, and does not know a great misstep. ¹⁶ Job speaks emptily, his mouth is filled with ignorance, and his words are heavy with thoughtlessness.

---

Notes
- v. 10: The phrase "gives songs in the night" reflects the poetic imagery of divine provision and comfort, consistent with the Hebrew. 
- v. 13: The translation of "שַׁוְּא" as "emptiness" and "שַׁדַּי לֹא יְשׁוּרֶֽנָּה" as "the Almighty does not look upon it" maintains the contrast between divine indifference and human expectation.
