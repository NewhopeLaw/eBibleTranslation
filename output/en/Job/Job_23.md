# Job 23

¹ Job answered and said: ² Today, too, my complaint is bitter, the hand of my complaint is heavy on my affliction. ³ Oh that I knew where I might find Him, that I might come even to His dwelling place! ⁴ I would set my case before Him and fill my mouth with arguments. ⁵ I would learn what He would answer me, and understand what He would say to me. ⁶ Would a mighty one contend with me? No, only He would set it upon me. ⁷ There is the just cause to be with Him, and I will escape forever from my judge. ⁸ I went ahead, but He was not there; I looked behind, but I could not find Him. ⁹ On His left He made me, but I did not grasp it; He wrapped His right hand, and I did not see. ¹⁰ For He knows the way of mine, He has tested me as silver. ¹¹ My foot has held fast to His path; His way I have kept and did not lag. ¹² I did not turn away from His commands, but I kept His words within me. ¹³ And He alone, who can oppose Him? What He desires, He does. ¹⁴ For He fulfills His decree with many who are with Him. ¹⁵ Therefore, before Him I am in awe, I consider and tremble because of Him. ¹⁶ The God who has made my heart soft, and the Almighty has stirred me. ¹⁷ For I was not concealed from darkness, and from my face He covered the gloom.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "I did not turn away from His commands" — "commands" renders מִצְוָה, which can also be "instruction" or "commandment."
- v. 13: "He alone, who can oppose Him?" — The Hebrew implies a rhetorical challenge to the idea of resisting God's will.
- v. 17: "I was not concealed from darkness" — The Hebrew is metaphorical, indicating that Job feels hidden or obscured by the divine.
