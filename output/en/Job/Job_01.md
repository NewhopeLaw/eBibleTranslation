# Job 1

¹ There was a man in the land of Uz named Job. That man was whole, upright, and reverent of God, and he turned away from evil. ² Seven sons and three daughters were born to him. ³ His herds were seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred she-asses. He had a great number of servants and was greater in wealth than all the men of the east. ⁴ His sons used to go and hold feasts in their turn, each at home, and they would summon their three sisters to come and eat and drink with them. ⁵ When the days of the feast had passed, Job would send and purify them, rising early in the morning and offering a burnt offering for each of them, for Job said, Perhaps my children have sinned in their hearts and cursed God. In this way Job did every day. ⁶ One day the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and the adversary was among them. ⁷ The LORD said to the adversary, From where have you come? The adversary replied to the LORD, I have walked about on the earth and walked to and fro in it. ⁸ The LORD said to the adversary, Have you considered my servant Job, for there is no one like him on the earth, a whole and upright man who fears God and turns away from evil? ⁹ The adversary replied to the LORD, Does Job fear God for no reason? ¹⁰ Have you not made a fence around him, his house, and all that he has? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his herds have spread throughout the land. ¹¹ But now, stretch out your hand and strike all that he has. If he curses you to your face, then you will know. ¹² The LORD said to the adversary, Behold, all that he has is in your power, but do not lay a hand on the man himself. Then the adversary went out from before the LORD. ¹³ One day Job's sons and daughters were eating and drinking in the house of his eldest son. ¹⁴ A messenger came to Job and said, The oxen were plowing and the donkeys grazing alongside them. ¹⁵ Then the Sabeans attacked and took them, and they struck the young men with the sword. I alone escaped to tell you. ¹⁶ While he was still speaking, another messenger came and said, Fire from God fell from the sky and burned the sheep and the young men, and consumed them. I alone escaped to tell you. ¹⁷ While he was still speaking, yet another messenger came and said, The Chaldeans formed three divisions, and they attacked the camels and took them, and they struck the young men with the sword. I alone escaped to tell you. ¹⁸ While he was still speaking, yet another messenger came and said, Job's sons and daughters were eating and drinking in the house of his eldest son. ¹⁹ A great wind came from the desert and struck the four corners of the house, so that the house fell on the young people, and they died. I alone escaped to tell you. ²⁰ Job then rose, tore his robe, and shaved his head. He fell to the ground and worshiped. ²¹ He said, Naked I came from my mother's womb, and naked I will return there. The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away. May the name of the LORD be blessed. ²² In all this Job did not sin or accuse God of wrongdoing.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "peripherēs" (περιεφραξας) in the Septuagint suggests a "fence," which is adopted here to reflect the Hebrew "sacath" (סָכַת) meaning "surrounded."
- v. 15: The Septuagint clarifies "Sabeans" as the attackers, which is consistent with the Hebrew text's "Sabeans" (שְׁבָא).
- v. 17: The Septuagint's "Chaldeans" (ἱ παῖδες) is used to reflect the Hebrew "Casdim" (כַּשְׂדִּים), a term for a group of people.
