# Job 30

¹ Now the young mock me, and I, who used to revere their fathers, now they treat me as a stranger, like a foreign shepherd. ² What good is the strength of their hands to me? Their whole livelihood has withered away. ³ They are hungry and thirsty, withered like desert wastelands. They are driven from one place to another, groaning under a burden they cannot carry. ⁴ They gather salt from thorny plants, and their food comes from the roots of ragged plants. ⁵ Driven from their homes, they live in caves and in holes in the ground like thieves. ⁶ They dwell among streams, in the clefts of rocks, and in the ruins of the earth. ⁷ They live among shrubs, they lie beneath the bushes in the wasteland. ⁸ They are fools and the nameless, and their memory is blotted out from the land. ⁹ But now I am their music, and I am a proverb to them. ¹⁰ They despise me and keep their distance from me; they do not refrain from spitting in my face. ¹¹ For he opened his bow and shot arrows at me, and he loosed the bridle from my face. ¹² Their right hand pierced me, they spread out their feet to trip me, and they laid their paths for my destruction. ¹³ They plundered my paths, they stripped me of my cloak. ¹⁴ They poured out arrows like a wide stream; under the threat of destruction, they hurled me down. ¹⁵ My prosperity turned back on me like a storm, my hope was swept away like the wind, and my salvation passed like a cloud. ¹⁶ Now my soul is poured out over me, and days of suffering seize me. ¹⁷ At night my bones are shattered, and my sinews do not rest. ¹⁸ By great strength, they stripped me of my clothing, as a tunic they took from me and clothed me in ashes. ¹⁹ I was cast down to the dust and ashes. ²⁰ I cried out to you, but you did not answer; I stood and you paid no attention to me. ²¹ You turned into my enemy, with a strong hand you drove me away. ²² You lifted me up in the wind, you whirled me about, and cast me into ruin. ²³ For I know that death will bring me back, and the tent of every living being is the grave. ²⁴ Yet if I had any power to help myself, or if I had sought aid from another, I would have found it. ²⁵ But I did weep for the suffering of the poor, I groaned when I saw the afflicted man. ²⁶ For I expected good things, but evil came. I waited for light, but came darkness. ²⁷ My bowels are boiled without rest; days of suffering overtook me. ²⁸ I walked about in agony, without comfort; I stood in the assembly and cried out. ²⁹ I was like a brother to jackals, like a companion to ostriches. ³⁰ My skin turned black on me, and my bones burned from the heat. ³¹ My lyre became a dirge for me, and my harp a voice of weeping.

Notes
- v. 8: "nameless" is a rendering of בְּלִי־שֵׁם, which literally means "without name," used here to describe the despised and forgotten.
- v. 9: "music" is a rendering of קִתָֽרָה, referring to a musical instrument, used metaphorically here to describe how Job is now a source of mockery for the young.
- v. 18: "clothed me in ashes" reflects the Hebrew metaphor of being stripped and then clothed in shame and ruin, with ashes symbolizing mourning and humiliation.
