# Job 25

¹ Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said: ² Dominion and terror are with Him, making peace in His heights. ³ How many are His troops? And who has not risen to shine in His presence? ⁴ How then can a mortal be just before God? How can one born of a woman be clean? ⁵ Even the moon is not pure in His sight, nor do the stars remain clean in His eyes. ⁶ How much less a corrupt mortal, a son of man who is but a worm.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "Dominion and terror are with Him" reflects "meshel vafachad imo," where "meshel" is rendered as "dominion" and "fachad" as "terror."
- v. 3: "How many are His troops?" translates "hayesh mispar legadav," where "mispar" is "number" and "legadav" refers to "His troops."
- v. 4: "How then can a mortal be just before God?" translates "umah yitzdaq enosh im-el," where "enosh" is "mortal" and "im-el" is "before God."
- v. 5: "Even the moon is not pure in His sight" reflects a poetic interpretation of "hayna' el yerah" where "yerah" could be "moon," and "lo ya'ihil" is "does not shine."
- v. 6: "How much less a corrupt mortal" translates "af ki enosh rimah," where "rimah" is "corrupt" or "wicked."
