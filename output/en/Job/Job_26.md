# Job 26

¹ Then Job answered and said: ² What have you helped one with no strength? What have you strengthened a hand that has no power? ³ What have you advised one with no wisdom? What have you revealed to one who has no insight? ⁴ To whom have you spoken words? And whose breath has come from you? ⁵ The dead in the deep tremble, along with those in its dwellings. ⁶ The realm of the dead is bare before him, and there is no covering for destruction. ⁷ He stretches the north over emptiness, and hangs the earth on nothing. ⁸ He binds up the waters in his clouds, and the cloud is not torn open beneath them. ⁹ He holds back the face of his throne, and hurls his cloud before it. ¹⁰ He marks a boundary over the surface of the waters, even to the limit of light with darkness. ¹¹ Pillars of the heavens tremble, and are shaken by his rebuke. ¹² By his power he quieted the sea, and by his understanding he struck down Rahab. ¹³ By his breath the skies are made pure; he pierced the fleeing serpent with his hand. ¹⁴ These are but the edges of his ways, and how faint is the whisper of his word! Who can grasp his mighty deeds?

---

Notes
- v. 12: "Rahab" is a poetic name for a mythic sea monster; retained as "Rahab" to reflect the Hebrew term (רָהַב).  
- v. 13: "fleeing serpent" (חָנַשׁ) is a poetic image for a hostile force, possibly linked to primordial chaos; "fleeing serpent" reflects the Hebrew verb.
