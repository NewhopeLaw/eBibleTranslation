# Job 24

¹ Why are times not kept by the Almighty, and why do those who know Him not see His days? ² Boundaries are transgressed by the wicked; they seize flocks and drive them off. ³ They lead away the donkey of orphans, they take the ox of widows as pledge. ⁴ They push the poor from the road together; the poor of the land are hidden. ⁵ Look, they are like wild donkeys in the desert, they rush out to find prey, they go forth to look for food in the waste. ⁶ They cut down in the field at night, and the vineyard of the wicked they strip bare. ⁷ They lie in the cold without a covering, and the sackcloth is their covering. ⁸ Drenched from the snow of the mountains, and without shelter, they huddle beneath the rock. ⁹ They seize the orphan's inheritance and oppress the poor. ¹⁰ They lie about without clothing, and the hungry carry the sheaf. ¹¹ They dwell among the cliffs, they dig in the rocks and thirst. ¹² From the city of the dead they are snatched, and the soul of the slain cries out, yet God does not give His judgment. ¹³ These are the ones who have rebelled against light, they do not know His ways, nor do they walk in His paths. ¹⁴ The murderer rises at dawn, the poor and the needy are slain, and at night he is as a thief. ¹⁵ And the eye of the adulterer watches, saying, "The eye will not see me," and he sets a veil over his face. ¹⁶ He digs in darkness, they seal themselves in the day, they do not know light. ¹⁷ For to them the morning is like the shadow of death, for they know not how to shun the shadow of death. ¹⁸ He is lighter than the surface of the water; their portion is cursed on the earth, they do not turn from the ways of the vineyards. ¹⁹ Their roots are scorched, their branches are dried— because they stole the cradle of orphans. ²⁰ Let his mother forget him, let his sweetness be taken away. Let them not remember him again, and let him be broken like a wicked tree. ²¹ One who tends a barren woman will not bear fruit, and a widow will not be made to flourish. ²² And he who strengthens the weak by his might will rise and not trust in the living. ²³ He gives him confidence and he leans— but his eyes are on his ways. ²⁴ They rise a little, and they are not there; they are brought low like all, they are scattered like the heads of ears. ²⁵ And if not so, who is there to disprove me, to set my words as nothing?

---

Notes
- v. 17: "the shadow of death" is a consistent rendering of the Hebrew "צלמות" in context.
- v. 18: "lighter than the surface of the water" reflects the Hebrew metaphor, not literal weight.
- v. 24: "the heads of ears" (heads of grain) is a poetic image, rendered clearly in English.
