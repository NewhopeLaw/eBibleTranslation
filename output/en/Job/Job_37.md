# Job 37

¹ Yet for this my heart quakes and departs from its place. ² Listen, listen to the roar of His voice, for from His mouth it comes forth. ³ He spreads it out beneath all the heavens, and His light upon the wings of the earth. ⁴ Behind it He shouts with a voice, He thunders in the voice of His glory, and He does not spare them, for His voice is heard. ⁵ He thunders, the mighty in His voice, doing wonders, great works which we do not know. ⁶ For to the snow He says, “Fall on the earth,” and to the rain, and to the rain of His might. ⁷ In the hand of every man He seals, that all the sons of men may know His work. ⁸ And the wild beasts come under the covert, and they rest in their dens. ⁹ From the chamber comes a storm, and from the cold of the north, cold. ¹⁰ By the breath of God He gives frost; the waters are frozen at His word. ¹¹ Also, He appoints a cloud for the rain, scatters His light through the cloud. ¹² And He turns the clouds in circles, His paths are in their wheels, doing all He has commanded them upon the face of the earth’s wheel. ¹³ Whether for a rod, or for the land, or for lovingkindness, He will find it. ¹⁴ Attend to this, Job. Stand and consider the wonders of God. ¹⁵ Do you know when God sets them in place, and makes His light appear from the cloud? ¹⁶ Do you know how He spreads out the layers of the cloud, the wondrous works of the Perfect One? ¹⁷ Who among you makes your garment warm, and the quiet land still from the south? ¹⁸ He fastens the vault with you, strong as a molten mirror. ¹⁹ What can we say to Him? We cannot form words in the face of His darkness. ²⁰ Will it be told to Him that I speak? If a man speaks, will it be consumed? ²¹ And now, they have not seen bright light, it is hidden in the clouds, and the wind passes and purges them. ²² From the north, golden radiance comes — on God, a terrible splendor. ²³ The Almighty — no one can find Him, great in might and in justice, and abundant in righteousness — He does not oppress. ²⁴ Therefore, let men fear Him; none of the wise of heart sees Him.

Notes
- v. 12: "His paths are in their wheels" — the Hebrew text uses poetic imagery of cyclical movement, which is captured here to reflect the divine orchestration of the clouds.
