# Job 28

¹ Indeed, there is a source for silver and a place where gold is refined, ² iron is taken from the dust and copper from stones is mined. ³ He sets limits for darkness and all the ends; he searches out the stone of darkness and shadow, ⁴ breaking a channel through the dirt, from the home of the forgotten, from beneath the footstep, it is abandoned and far from human reach. ⁵ From the earth comes the bread, and beneath it is turned as if it were fire. ⁶ A place of sapphire—its stones are for him, and dust of gold is his. ⁷ A path no bird has known, no falcon’s eye has seen. ⁸ Neither have the sons of chaos marked it, nor has the lion passed over it. ⁹ He stretched out his hand to the leviathan, overturned the root of the mountains, ¹⁰ split the rivers of the deep, and his eye saw every precious thing. ¹¹ He uncovered the secrets of rivers, and from the deep he brought forth light. ¹² But where can wisdom be found? and where is the place of understanding? ¹³ No one knows its price, nor is it found among the living. ¹⁴ The deep says, "It is not in me," and the sea says, "It is not with me." ¹⁵ No lock can secure it beneath, and no weight can measure its worth. ¹⁶ It cannot be bought with gold of Ophir, with onyx, or sapphire. ¹⁷ Neither can gold or glass match it, nor its value be compared to the finest vessels. ¹⁸ It cannot be matched to topaz or to the most precious gems, no measure of wisdom can be found in them. ¹⁹ It is not matched by the pith of Cush, no pure gold can buy it. ²⁰ And where can wisdom come from? and where is the place of understanding? ²¹ It is hidden from the eyes of every living being and concealed from the birds of the sky. ²² Perdition and death have heard of it, they have heard its name. ²³ God alone knows its way, and he alone understands its place. ²⁴ For he looks to the ends of the earth and sees all beneath the heavens. ²⁵ He assigns a weight to the wind and measures out the waters. ²⁶ When he set a law for the rain and a path for the thunder’s voice, ²⁷ then he saw wisdom and declared it, prepared it and examined it. ²⁸ And he said to the human: "To fear the Lord is wisdom, and to turn from evil is understanding."

---

Notes
- v. 28: The phrase "וַיֹּאמֶר לָֽאָדָם הֵן יִרְאַת אֲדֹנָי הִיא חָכְמָה וְסוּר מֵרָע בִּינָֽה" is translated as a direct quote from God to humanity, which is the most straightforward reading. The terms "fear the Lord" and "turn from evil" are rendered as "fear the Lord is wisdom" and "turn from evil is understanding" to reflect the declarative tone.
