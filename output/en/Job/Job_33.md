# Job 33

¹ Job, listen now to me. Pay attention to all my words. ² Behold, I have opened my mouth; my tongue has spoken in earnest. ³ My heart is upright in my words, and my lips speak with clear understanding. ⁴ The Spirit of God made me, and the breath of the Almighty gave me life. ⁵ If you are able, answer me, face to face, stand before me. ⁶ I am like you, created from clay; from the same earth, we were formed. ⁷ My intention is not to intimidate you, and my hand does not weigh heavily on you. ⁸ I heard you speak in my ears, and your voice reached my ears. ⁹ You say, "I am clean, without sin; I am blameless, and no wrong has come from me." ¹⁰ But he has found a charge against you, and he counts you as an enemy. ¹¹ He has placed your foot in a trap, and he guards all your paths. ¹² How then do you say, "I am in the right," when God is greater than any human being? ¹³ Why do you argue with him, when all his words do not answer you? ¹⁴ For God speaks in one way, and in two ways he does not turn back. ¹⁵ In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls on people in their sleep, ¹⁶ then he reveals their ear and seals their instruction. ¹⁷ To turn a person from their action and to conceal pride from a man. ¹⁸ To keep a soul from the pit and to preserve life from perishing in the sword. ¹⁹ And to teach a person through pain on their bed, and many of their bones will ache. ²⁰ Their appetite will reject food, and their soul will long for what it desires. ²¹ Their flesh will decay from sight, and their bones, which were unseen, will appear. ²² Their soul draws near to the pit, and their life to the realm of the dead. ²³ If there is a mediator, even one of a thousand, to speak on a man’s behalf, ²⁴ then he will be gracious to him and say, "Deliver him from going down to the pit, I have found a ransom." ²⁵ His flesh will be renewed like a youth, and he will return to the days of his youth. ²⁶ He will pray to God, and it will be accepted by him. He will see God's face in gladness, and he will restore to a person his righteousness. ²⁷ A person will declare to others, "I have sinned and perverted what was right, and it was not fair to me." ²⁸ He will redeem his soul from descending into the pit, and his life will see the light. ²⁹ All these things God does with a person—twice or three times. ³⁰ To bring back a soul from the pit, so that it may see the light of life. ³¹ Job, listen to me. Be silent, and I will speak. ³² If there are words, answer me. Speak, for I long to justify you. ³³ If not, you listen to me. Be silent, and I will teach you wisdom.

---

Notes
- v. 14: The Hebrew "בִּשְׁתַּיִם לֹא יְשׁוּרֶֽנָּה" is interpreted as "in two ways he does not turn back," reflecting the idea of divine intransigence in speech.
- v. 23: The phrase "מַלְאָךְ מֵלִיץ" is translated as "mediator" to capture the concept of an intercessor. The Septuagint has "angelos thanatēforos," but the Hebrew supports a more general sense.
- v. 30: The phrase "לֵאוֹר בְּאוֹר הַֽחַיִּים" is translated as "so that it may see the light of life," preserving the poetic emphasis on restoration.
