# Job 31

¹ I made a covenant with my eyes not to look upon a maiden with desire. ² What portion is God’s from above, and what inheritance does the Almighty give from on high? ³ Is it not ruin for the wicked, and ruin for those who do evil? ⁴ Has he not seen my ways, and does he not count all my steps? ⁵ If I have walked in falsehood, or my foot has rushed into deceit, ⁶ let God weigh me with the scales of righteousness, and let him know the integrity of my ways. ⁷ If my step has turned aside from the path, or my heart has followed my eyes, or my hands have been defiled with guilt, ⁸ then let me sow and another eat, and let my offspring be uprooted. ⁹ If my heart has been enticed toward a woman, and I have lain in wait at my neighbor’s doorway, ¹⁰ then let my wife serve another, and let others trample her. ¹¹ For that is a great sin, an act of violence against the husband. ¹² For it is a fire that devours to destruction, and it would root out all my harvest. ¹³ If I have despised the right of my servant or my maid in their dispute with me, ¹⁴ what then would I do when God rises up, when he calls to account, and what answer would I give him? ¹⁵ Did not the one who made me in the womb also form them? Did not the same hand shape us all in the womb? ¹⁶ If I have witheld what the poor desired, or caused the eyes of the widow to fail, ¹⁷ if I have eaten my bread alone, and the orphan did not eat from it, ¹⁸ for from my youth they were raised as my children; I brought them up from my mother’s womb. ¹⁹ If I have seen anyone perishing for lack of clothing, and the poor without a covering, ²⁰ if my loins did not bless them, if the fleece of my sheep did not warm them, ²¹ if I have raised my hand against the orphan, knowing that I have help at the gate, ²² then let my shoulder fall from the socket, and my arm be broken at the elbow. ²³ For fear seized me, and I did not dare to approach him. ²⁴ If I have placed gold as my confidence and called it my trust, ²⁵ if I have rejoiced in the abundance of my wealth, when my hand found much fortune, ²⁶ if I have gazed at the sun when it shone, or the moon moving in splendor, ²⁷ and my heart has been secretly enticed, and my hand has kissed my mouth in secret, ²⁸ then this is also my great iniquity, because I have lied to the Most High. ²⁹ If I have taken joy in the death of my enemy, or rejoiced when calamity struck him, ³⁰ let me not allow my mouth to curse him, nor let my lips speak ill of him. ³¹ If those who mourn with me have not said, “Where is he who would give us his flesh to eat? May he not be deprived!” ³² if a foreigner has not found lodging in my home, or the traveler has not opened my gate, ³³ if I have covered my sins as a man does, and hidden my guilt in my heart, ³⁴ because I feared a great multitude, and the families have taunted me, yet I did not let my mouth go out past the threshold, ³⁵ who will give me an answer so that I may contend with the Almighty? Let me write my case and confront it with him. ³⁶ If I have put my sin on my shoulder, and placed it as a crown for myself, ³⁷ I would have told the number of my steps, and like a ruler I would have led them. ³⁸ If my land has cried out against me, and its furrows weep together, ³⁹ if I have eaten its strength without money, or taken the life of its master, ⁴⁰ then let thorns come out with the wheat, and let briers grow with the barley. These are the words of Job.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "the Almighty" is a translation of *shadai*, a divine title often associated with YHWH but used independently here.
- v. 28: "the Most High" is a rendering of the phrase *mima'alah*, which aligns with the divine title in the context.
- v. 34: "families have taunted me" reflects a possible interpretation of the Hebrew phrase, which is otherwise obscure.
- v. 35: "confront it with him" clarifies the meaning of the Hebrew phrase, which refers to writing down a case for legal confrontation.
- v. 40: "these are the words of Job" is a concluding editorial note in the original text, likely pointing to the end of a section.
