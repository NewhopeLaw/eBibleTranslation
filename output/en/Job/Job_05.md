# Job 5

¹ Call out now, is there anyone who answers you? To which of the holy ones will you turn? ² For a fool is struck down by his anger, and a man of understanding is killed by his jealousy. ³ I have seen a fool planting roots, but suddenly I plucked up his nourishment. ⁴ His children are driven from safety, and they are crushed in the gate with no one to rescue them. ⁵ What he has gathered, the hungry will eat, and he will take it to the dry ground, and the thirsty will exhaust his strength. ⁶ For evil does not come from the dust, and toil does not spring from the soil. ⁷ For man is born to toil, and the sons of wrath are lifted up like sparks. ⁸ But I would seek the God who made me and would place my plea before the Almighty. ⁹ The one who does great things beyond understanding, wonders without number, ¹⁰ who pours rain on the earth and sends water on the wilderness, ¹¹ who brings low the lofty and lifts up the lowly, who rescues the oppressed, ¹² who breaks the plans of the cunning, so that their hands do no accomplish what is right, ¹³ who captures the wise in their craftiness, and the counsel of the shrewd is quickly undone, ¹⁴ who meets the daylight with darkness, and the noonday feels as though it were night. ¹⁵ Who rescues from the sword's mouth and from the hand of the strong, the weak, ¹⁶ so that the poor have hope, and the mouth of the wicked is shut. ¹⁷ Behold, happy is the man whom God corrects, and do not despise the discipline of the Almighty. ¹⁸ For He will strike, yet He will heal; He will wound, yet His hands will restore. ¹⁹ He will rescue you from six troubles, and in the seventh, evil will not touch you. ²⁰ In famine, He will deliver you from death, and in war, from the hand of the sword. ²¹ From the lash of the tongue He will hide you, and you will not fear when evil comes. ²² You will laugh at the outlaw and the lawless, and from the wild beasts of the land you will not fear. ²³ For with the stones of the field is your covenant, and the wild beasts of the field will make peace with you. ²⁴ You will know that peace dwells in your tent, and your dwelling place will not be disturbed. ²⁵ You will know that your descendants are numerous, like the grass of the earth. ²⁶ You will come in with a full harvest, like grain brought in at the proper time, like a sheaf of gleaning brought in at its season. ²⁷ Look, we have examined these things; this is the truth we have heard. You, then, know it for yourself.

---

Notes
- v. 20: "From the lash of the tongue He will hide you" — the Hebrew "מִשֹּׁט לָשׁוֹן" is rendered as "from the lash of the tongue," reflecting the metaphor of a whip-like tongue.
- v. 23: "with the stones of the field is your covenant" — the Hebrew "כִּי עִם־אַבְנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה בְרִיתֶךָ" is translated to reflect the idea of a covenant secured with the very foundation of the land.
