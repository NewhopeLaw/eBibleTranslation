# Job 21

¹ Then Job responded and said: ² Listen, listen to my words, and let this be your comfort. ³ Lift me up, and I will speak, and then you may mock. ⁴ Am I speaking to a man, or what reason is there that my spirit should not be stirred? ⁵ Turn to me, and listen, and place your hand over your mouth. ⁶ For if I remember, I am terrified, and my flesh trembles with panic. ⁷ Why do the wicked live? Why do they grow strong in power? ⁸ Their offspring are established before them, and their descendants are in their sight. ⁹ Their homes are peaceful, free from fear, and no rod of God is over them. ¹⁰ Their ox does not labor in vain, their donkey does not miscarry. ¹¹ Their children are like grazing sheep, and their children dance. ¹² They lift up the shoulder and the lyre, and rejoice in the sound of the harp. ¹³ They spend their days in good, and in a moment they are taken to Sheol. ¹⁴ They say to God, "Turn away from us, we do not wish to know your ways." ¹⁵ What is El, that we should serve him? What is the benefit of Shaddai, that we should seek him? ¹⁶ For their good is not in their hand, and the counsel of the wicked is far from me. ¹⁷ How many are the lamps of the wicked that go out, and their wealth comes upon them in anger, a heap of sorrows! ¹⁸ They are like chaff before the wind, and like dust swept away by the storm. ¹⁹ God lays up their iniquity for their sons, and they repay it to them, and they know it. ²⁰ Their eyes see their ruin, and by the wrath of Shaddai they drink. ²¹ For what is his desire in his house after him? The number of his days is cut short. ²² Is God teaching knowledge? Is He the one who judges from above? ²³ One dies in fullness of strength, whole and in peace. ²⁴ His body is full of fat, and the marrow of his bones is sweet. ²⁵ And another dies with a bitter soul, and has not tasted good. ²⁶ Both of them lie together on the dust, and decay covers them. ²⁷ I know your thoughts, and the plots you have formed against me. ²⁸ For you say, "Where is the house of the noble? Where are the tents of the wicked?" ²⁹ Have you not asked those who pass by the road, and their signs you do not recognize? ³⁰ For in the day of calamity their wickedness is hidden, in the day of their transgression it is carried away. ³¹ Who will declare his way to his face? And who will repay him what he has done? ³² Yet he is brought to the grave, and watches over the mound. ³³ They sweeten for him the stones of the stream, and behind him every man walks, and in front of him there is no number. ³⁴ How can you comfort me with emptiness? And your answers leave no effect.

---

Notes
- v. 21: "the number of his days is cut short" — the Hebrew is ambiguous between "the number of his days is cut short" and "the number of his new moons is cut short," but the context of life and death points to the former.
- v. 22: "Is God teaching knowledge?" — the Hebrew could also be rendered "Is it not God who teaches knowledge?" but the literal translation preserves the rhetorical question style.
