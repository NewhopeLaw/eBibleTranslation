# Job 12

¹ Then Job answered and said: ² Surely you are the people, and with you wisdom will perish. ³ But I also have a heart, like any of you, and I was not born to be lesser than you. And who is there like you? ⁴ You who mock your friend, who laugh at God—will the righteous be mocked and remain whole? ⁵ Surely the lamp is for the wicked, and the fire is for those who cause trouble. ⁶ The tents of robbers are left to desolation; and those who provoke God are crushed in their confidence, for God has placed them in his hand. ⁷ But ask the beasts, and they will teach you; the birds of the sky will tell you. ⁸ Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you; the fish of the sea will declare it to you. ⁹ Who among all these does not know that the hand of the LORD has done this? ¹⁰ In whose hand is the life of every living thing, and the breath of every human being. ¹¹ Does not the ear test the words, and the mouth taste its food? ¹² Wisdom belongs to the aged, and understanding to those who live long. ¹³ With him is wisdom and power, to him belong insight and understanding. ¹⁴ He breaks down and builds up no one; he closes a man’s way and does not open it. ¹⁵ He withholds the waters and they dry up; he releases them and they flood the land. ¹⁶ With him is might and insight; to him belong the erring and the wise. ¹⁷ He makes counselors wander, and judges are turned into fools. ¹⁸ He removes the belt from kings, and binds their waist with a rope. ¹⁹ He leads priests away as captives, and the strong are shattered. ²⁰ He takes away speech from the faithful and takes away understanding from the elders. ²¹ He pours contempt on nobles and makes the mighty powerless. ²² He reveals secrets from darkness and brings out the shadow of death into the light. ²³ He exalts nations, and then lays them waste; he crushes nations and brings them to rest. ²⁴ He turns the hearts of leaders of the earth, and makes them wander in a way they do not know. ²⁵ They grope in darkness with no light and stumble like a drunkard.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "crushed in their confidence" reflects the Hebrew context of "battlements" and "those who provoke God," suggesting a loss of assurance rather than literal destruction.
- v. 23: "brings them to rest" translates the ambiguous Hebrew term יַנְחֵם, which may mean to comfort or to rest, chosen here to parallel the meaning of destruction in the first half.
- v. 24: "makes them wander in a way they do not know" captures the Hebrew לֹא־דָֽרֶךְ, which is a rare phrase meaning "not the way" or "unknown way."
