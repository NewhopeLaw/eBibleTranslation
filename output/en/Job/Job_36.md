# Job 36

¹ Elihu continued and said: ² Let me speak a little longer, and I will show you, for still there are words left in me. ³ I will take knowledge from afar, and righteousness from my works I will declare. ⁴ For my words are not false; they are not unjust in their understanding. ⁵ Know that God does not abandon the innocent; the mighty is strong in heart. ⁶ He does not give life to the wicked, and the judgment of the poor He gives. ⁷ He does not turn His eyes from the righteous, and with kings He places them on the throne and exalts them forever. ⁸ But the bound in fetters are caught in the cords of hardship. ⁹ And He reveals their deeds and their sins, when they become too great. ¹⁰ And He uncovers their ears to discipline, and He says, "Let them return from their wrongdoing." ¹¹ If they listen and serve, they will complete their days in goodness, and their years in pleasantness. ¹² But if they do not listen, in His sending they will pass away and perish without knowledge. ¹³ And the rebellious of heart set their anger, they do not cry out, because He has bound them. ¹⁴ So the soul of the young perishes, and their life among the holy ones. ¹⁵ He rescues the poor in his poverty, and He uncovers the ears of the oppressed. ¹⁶ And He turned you away from the mouth of your enemy — a wide abyss beneath you, and your table descended full of ashes. ¹⁷ You are filled with the judgment of the wicked, judgment and justice will uphold you. ¹⁸ For wrath, lest He entice you with doubt, and great atonement, let it not turn you aside. ¹⁹ Will your ransom not be of use to you in distress, nor all the strong in power? ²⁰ Do not long for the night to rise, for peoples to come against you. ²¹ Guard yourself, do not turn to wrongdoing, for you have chosen this instead of hardship. ²² Know that the mighty sets Himself apart in His strength — who is like Him, the Teacher? ²³ Who has marked His ways for Him, and who has said, "You did wrong"? ²⁴ Remember that His works are great, which men have transgressed. ²⁵ Every person has seen this; a mortal gazes from afar. ²⁶ Know that the mighty cannot be known — His years are uncounted, and no one comprehends. ²⁷ For He draws off the drops of water, they flow as rain to His clouds. ²⁸ From which the streams descend, they surge upon the face of mankind. ²⁹ Even though He spreads out the clouds, He covers the beams of His dwelling. ³⁰ Behold, He spreads light over Himself, and He covers the roots of the sea. ³¹ For through these He judges peoples, He gives food to the one who hears. ³² With His hands He covers the light, and He commands it over the cloud. ³³ He declares it to His friend — the Shepherd, even over what is rising.

Notes
- v. 13: "the rebellious of heart" — the Hebrew term חַנְפֵי־לֵב is rendered as "rebellious of heart," capturing the sense of a hardened, defiant spirit.
- v. 29: "the beams of His dwelling" — the reference to the "beams" (תְּשֻׁאוֹת) is poetic, likely referring to the architectural structure of God's heavenly dwelling, here rendered as "beams."
- v. 33: "the Shepherd" — the Greek reference to "the Shepherd" is not present in the Hebrew, which uses רֵעוֹ ("friend" or "fellow"). The Hebrew is more neutral, though poetic.
