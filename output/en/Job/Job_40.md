# Job 40

¹ And the LORD answered Job out of the storm, saying: ² "Will the one who disputes with the Almighty teach him? Let God answer him, if he will. ³ Then Job answered the LORD and said: ⁴ "Behold, I am of little account; what shall I answer you? I lay my hand over my mouth. ⁵ Once I have spoken, and I will not answer; twice, and I will say no more. ⁶ Then the LORD answered Job out of the storm, saying: ⁷ "Now gird yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall make me know. ⁸ "Will you disannul my judgment? Will you condemn me, that you may be justified? ⁹ "Do you have an arm like God? Or a voice like him thundering? ¹⁰ "Now gird yourself with glory and majesty, clothe yourself with splendor and beauty. ¹¹ "Pour out the rage of your anger, and look on every proud one, and humble him; ¹² "Look on every proud one, bring him low, and subdue the wicked where they stand. ¹³ "Hide them all in the dust together, their faces with the dust of the pit. ¹⁴ "And I will myself commend you, for you will save me with your right hand. ¹⁵ "Behold now, the beasts I have made with you; they eat grass like the ox. ¹⁶ "Behold now, their strength is in their loins, and their might is in the muscles of their belly. ¹⁷ "Their tail sways like a cedar, the sinews of their thighs are interwoven. ¹⁸ "Their bones are rods of bronze, their limbs like bars of iron. ¹⁹ "He is the first of the works of God; let the one who made him draw near to him with his sword. ²⁰ "For the mountains bring him their produce, and all the beasts of the field play there. ²¹ "He lies beneath the lotus trees, in the covert of the reeds and marsh. ²² "The shade of the lotus trees covers him, and the willows of the stream surround him. ²³ "When the river overflows, it does not alarm him; he trusts, though the Jordan rushes to his mouth. ²⁴ "In his eyes he takes them in, arrows pierce his eye. ²⁵ "He draws the leviathan with a hook, and with a rope he binds the tongue of the serpent. ²⁶ "Will you put a crest in his nose, and pierce his jaw with a hook? ²⁷ "Will he make many pleas to you? Will he speak to you gentle words? ²⁸ "Will he make a covenant with you? Will he take him as a servant forever? ²⁹ "Will you play with him as with a bird, or bind him for your handmaidens? ³⁰ "Will companions trade him for pieces, will they divide him among the merchants? ³¹ "Will you fill his hide with scales, and his head with the shadow of the sea? ³² "Put your hand on him; remember the battle—do not repeat it anymore."

---

Notes
- v. 19: "He is the first of the works of God" — the Hebrew uses מִשְׁנֶה (mishneh), which may also be rendered "offspring" or "duplicate," but "first" is chosen here for clarity and the context of divine creation.
