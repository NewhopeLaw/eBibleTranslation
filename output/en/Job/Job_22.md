# Job 22

¹ Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said: ² Should a wise man not speak? Should someone who has knowledge not respond? ³ Does the Lord take pleasure in the just? Or if you are upright, does He accept your ways? ⁴ Is it before you that He argues, that He comes to you in judgment? ⁵ Is not your iniquity great, your sins without number? ⁶ For you have taken pledges from your brothers in vain, and stripped the naked of their garments. ⁷ You gave no water to the weary to drink, and you withheld bread from the hungry. ⁸ You caused a man to take possession of land, and he who had no strength you made to dwell in it. ⁹ You sent widows away empty-handed, and the strength of orphans you broke. ¹⁰ Therefore snares surround you, and sudden terror makes you panic. ¹¹ Or darkness, which you cannot see, and a flood of waters covers you. ¹² Does not God dwell in the heavens? Does He see the height of the stars, which He has exalted? ¹³ And you say, "What does God know? He judges through the thick darkness." ¹⁴ His clouds are a covering for Him, and He does not see; His circle in the heavens He walks. ¹⁵ Does He keep the ancient path, which the wicked have walked? ¹⁶ Those whose foundation was laid but not in time, whose foundations were washed away by a river. ¹⁷ They said to God, "Depart from us. What can the Almighty do to us?" ¹⁸ And He filled their houses with good things, and the counsel of the wicked is far from me. ¹⁹ The righteous will see and rejoice, and the innocent will mock them. ²⁰ But if they are not destroyed, then fire will consume them. ²¹ Return now, and you will be at peace with Him. Then your hope will come out in good. ²² Take instruction from His mouth, and lay up His words in your heart. ²³ If you return to the Almighty, He will restore you, and remove iniquity far from your tent. ²⁴ Then you will set your treasure on the dust, and your gold in the rock of a stream. ²⁵ And the Almighty will be in your distress, and silver will be refined for you. ²⁶ Then you will delight in the Almighty, and lift your face to God. ²⁷ You will make a request to Him, and He will hear you. Your vows you will pay. ²⁸ You will decide on a matter, and it will be set before you. On your paths light will shine. ²⁹ For when you fall, you will say, "Exaltation!" and the eyes that are bowed will be rescued. ³⁰ He will deliver the innocent one, and you will be rescued by clean hands.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "the height of the stars" is a reference to their exalted position, not their physical height.
- v. 16: "whose foundation was laid but not in time" reflects the Hebrew text's metaphor for unstable or doomed foundations.
- v. 24: "gold in the rock of a stream" is a poetic metaphor for hidden or tested treasure.
- v. 25: "silver will be refined for you" uses "refined" to capture the metaphor of purification through trial.
