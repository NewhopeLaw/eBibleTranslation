# Leviticus 20

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Tell the Israelites: Any Israelite man or any foreigner who dwells among Israel who gives any of their offspring to Molech must be put to death. The people of the land must stone them with stones. ³ I will set my face against that man, and I will cut him off from his people, because he has given one of his offspring to Molech, to profane my sanctuary and to desecrate my holy name. ⁴ If the people of the land turn a blind eye to that man when he gives one of his offspring to Molech, and do not put him to death, ⁵ I will set my face against that man and against his household, and I will cut him off and all the ones who follow him in idolatry after Molech, from among their people. ⁶ Any person who turns to omens or to diviners, to follow after them in idolatry, ⁷ I will set my face against that person and will cut him off from his people. ⁸ You must consecrate yourselves and be holy, for I am the LORD your God. ⁹ You must keep my statutes and do them. I am the LORD who consecrates you. ¹⁰ Any man who curses his father or his mother must be put to death. He has cursed his father or mother; his blood will be on him. ¹¹ Any man who lies with the wife of his father has uncovered his father’s nakedness. Both of them must be put to death. Their blood will be on them. ¹² Any man who lies with his daughter-in-law must be put to death. Both of them have acted disgracefully; their blood will be on them. ¹³ Any man who lies with a man as one lies with a woman commits an abomination. Both of them must be put to death. Their blood will be on them. ¹⁴ Any man who takes a woman and her mother is an abomination. You must burn him and them with fire. There must not be an abomination in your midst. ¹⁵ Any man who lies with an animal must be put to death, and the animal must be killed. ¹⁶ Any woman who approaches any animal to lie with it, you must kill her and the animal. Their blood will be on them. ¹⁷ Any man who takes his sister, whether his father’s daughter or his mother’s daughter, and sees her nakedness, and she sees his nakedness, it is a disgrace. They will be cut off in the sight of their people. He has uncovered the nakedness of his sister; they will bear their guilt. ¹⁸ Any man who lies with a woman who is his relative, and uncovers the source of her life, and she uncovers the source of his life, both of them must be cut off from among their people. ¹⁹ The nakedness of your aunt by marriage or by your father must not be uncovered, for they have uncovered their kinsman’s life. They will bear their guilt. ²⁰ Any man who lies with his sister-in-law has uncovered his kinsman’s nakedness. They must both die childless. ²¹ Any man who takes his brother’s wife is an unclean thing. He has uncovered his brother’s nakedness. They must both die childless. ²² You must keep all my statutes and all my laws, and do them, so that the land to which I am bringing you to dwell in will not vomit you out. ²³ You must not walk in the customs of the nations I am driving out before you, for all these things they did, and I abhorred them. ²⁴ I have said to you, “You will inherit their land and I will give it to you as a possession, a land flowing with milk and honey.” I am the LORD your God, who has set you apart from the peoples. ²⁵ You must distinguish between the clean and unclean animals, between the clean and unclean birds. You must not abhor your own lives by eating any of the creatures on the earth that I have set apart for you as unclean. ²⁶ You must be holy to me, for I am holy. I have set you apart from the peoples to be mine. ²⁷ Any man or woman who is a diviner or an augur must be put to death. The people must stone them with stones. Their blood will be on them.

---

Notes
- v. 17: "nakedness" is a technical term in Levitical law referring to incestuous or forbidden relationships.
- v. 18: "source of her life" and "source of his life" refer to reproductive organs in a euphemistic and culturally specific way.
- v. 19: "your aunt by marriage" is a translation of "dodah," which can mean "kinswoman" or "sister-in-law" depending on context.
- v. 20: "sister-in-law" is a translation of "dodah" in this context.
- v. 21: "brother’s wife" is a literal translation, though in some contexts it may refer more broadly to a close relative.
