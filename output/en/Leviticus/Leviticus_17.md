# Leviticus 17

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the people of Israel and tell them this command that the LORD has given: ³ Any man among the people of Israel who slaughters a bull, a sheep, or a goat in the camp, or who slaughters it outside the camp, ⁴ and does not bring it to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting to present it to the LORD at the Tent of Meeting, the blood of that man will be considered his own. He has shed blood and will be cut off from his people. ⁵ This will ensure that the people of Israel bring their sacrifices, which they have been slaughtering in the fields, to the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting to the priest. They will slaughter them there as sacrifices of well-being to the LORD. ⁶ The priest will pour out the blood at the base of the altar of the LORD, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and will burn the fat as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ⁷ They must no longer offer their sacrifices to the goat idols after which they have turned to follow. This shall be an eternal statute for them, throughout their generations. ⁸ You must tell them this: Any man among the people of Israel or among the foreigners living among you who offers an offering or sacrifice, ⁹ and does not bring it to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting to present it to the LORD, that man will be cut off from his people. ¹⁰ Any man among the people of Israel or among the foreigners living among you who eats any blood—I will set my face against that person who has eaten the blood and will cut them off from their people. ¹¹ For the life of flesh is in the blood, and I have given it to you on the altar to make atonement for your lives, because the blood is the means of atonement for life. ¹² Therefore, I have told the people of Israel that no one among you may eat blood, and the foreigner living among you may not eat blood either. ¹³ Any man among the people of Israel or among the foreigners living among you who hunts a wild animal or bird that may be eaten must pour out the blood and cover it with earth. ¹⁴ For the life of every living thing is its blood. I have told the people of Israel that you must not eat blood, because the life of every living thing is its blood. Anyone who eats it must be cut off. ¹⁵ Any person who eats what was torn by an animal, whether a resident Israelite or a foreigner, must wash their clothes and bathe in water. They will be unclean until evening but will be clean after that. ¹⁶ If they do not wash their clothes or bathe their body in water, they will bear the guilt of their sin.

---

Notes
- v. 14: "the life of every living thing is its blood" — the Hebrew uses "כָּל־בָּשָׂר" (every flesh), which is rendered as "every living thing" to preserve the universal scope.
- v. 16: "bear the guilt of their sin" — the Hebrew "נָשָׂא עֲוֺנֽוֹ" is translated idiomatically to reflect the consequence of not following the purification requirement.
