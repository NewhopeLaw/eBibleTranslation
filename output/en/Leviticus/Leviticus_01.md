# Leviticus 1

¹ The LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting, saying: ² Speak to the people of Israel and say to them: When any of you brings a gift near to the LORD, from the domestic animals, from the cattle and from the flock, you shall bring your gift near. ³ If the gift is a burnt offering from the cattle, a male without defect you shall bring it to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and he shall offer it of his own free will before the LORD. ⁴ He shall lay his hand upon the head of the burnt offering, and it shall be accepted for him, to make atonement for him. ⁵ Then he shall slaughter the bull before the LORD, and the priests, the sons of Aaron, shall present the blood and sprinkle the blood around the altar at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ⁶ The one offering the burnt offering shall flay it and cut it up into its parts. ⁷ The priests, the sons of Aaron, shall place fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire. ⁸ The priests, the sons of Aaron, shall arrange the parts, the head, and the fat upon the wood that is on the fire on the altar. ⁹ The entrails and the legs he shall wash with water, and the priest shall burn all of it on the altar as a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹⁰ If the gift is from the flock, from the male goats or from the rams, as a burnt offering, a male without defect he shall bring it. ¹¹ He shall slaughter it on the side of the altar to the north, before the LORD. The priests, the sons of Aaron, shall sprinkle the blood around the altar. ¹² He shall cut it up into its parts, and the priest shall arrange the head and the fat upon the wood that is on the fire on the altar. ¹³ The entrails and the legs he shall wash with water, and the priest shall offer all of it and burn it on the altar as a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹⁴ If the gift is a burnt offering from the birds, from the turtledoves or from the pigeons, he shall bring it near. ¹⁵ The priest shall bring it to the altar and pluck its head, and the priest shall burn it on the altar, and the blood shall drip down on the side of the altar. ¹⁶ He shall remove its crop with its feathers and cast it beside the altar to the east, to the place of the ashes. ¹⁷ He shall tear it open at its wings, but not divide it, and the priest shall burn it on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire. It is a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 14: "the turtledoves or from the pigeons" — the Hebrew terms are תֹּרִים (torim, turtledoves) and בְּנֵי הַיּוֹנָה (benei hayyona, young doves/pigeons). Both are understood as acceptable birds for sacrifice, and the plural form suggests they are categories rather than specific counts.
