# Leviticus 9

¹ On the eighth day, Moses summoned Aaron, his sons, and the elders of Israel. ² He said to Aaron, "Take for yourself a bull calf for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering, both unblemished, and present them before the LORD." ³ And to the elders of Israel, he said, "Take a male goat for a sin offering, and a bull and a ram, both unblemished, for a burnt offering. ⁴ Also, take a bull and a ram for a peace offering, and bring them to the Tent of Meeting, along with a grain offering mixed with oil; for today the LORD will appear to you." ⁵ The people did as Moses commanded and brought all the items to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. All the congregation stood before the LORD. ⁶ Moses said, "This is the thing that the LORD has commanded. Do it, and the glory of the LORD will appear to you." ⁷ Moses said to Aaron, "Come near to the altar and offer your sin offering and your burnt offering, making atonement for yourself and for the people. Then offer the offering of the people and make atonement for them, as the LORD has commanded." ⁸ Aaron came near to the altar and slaughtered the bull calf, his sin offering. ⁹ His sons brought the blood to him, and he dipped his finger in the blood and put it on the horns of the altar. Then he poured the blood at the base of the altar. ¹⁰ He took the fat and the kidneys and the lobe of the liver from the bull, as the LORD had commanded Moses, and burned them on the altar. ¹¹ He burned the meat and the hide with fire outside the camp. ¹² Then Aaron offered the burnt offering. His sons brought the blood to him, and he sprinkled it around the altar. ¹³ He brought the burnt offering to him, cut it into pieces, and offered the head and the pieces on the altar. ¹⁴ He washed the inner parts and the legs with water and burned them on the altar, a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to the LORD. ¹⁵ He brought the offering of the people and took the male goat, the sin offering for the people, and slaughtered it, offering it as he had done with the first. ¹⁶ He offered the burnt offering and performed it according to the regulation. ¹⁷ He brought the grain offering and filled his hands with it, offering it on the altar in addition to the morning burnt offering. ¹⁸ Aaron slaughtered the bull and the ram, the peace offering for the people. His sons brought the blood to him, and he sprinkled it around the altar. ¹⁹ He took the fat from the bull and the ram—the loins, the fat covering the entrails, the two kidneys and their fat, and the lobe of the liver— ²⁰ and placed them on the breast and burned the fat on the altar. ²¹ He lifted the breast and the right thigh as a wave offering before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²² Aaron raised his hands toward the people and blessed them. Then he stepped down from offering the sin offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offering. ²³ Moses and Aaron entered the Tent of Meeting, and when they came out, they blessed the people. The glory of the LORD appeared to all the people. ²⁴ Fire came out from before the LORD and consumed the burnt offering and the fat. The people saw it and rejoiced, falling on their faces in awe.

---

Notes
- v. 21: "the breast and the right thigh" — the text refers to these as the portions to be waved, but the translation assumes the standard sacrificial terminology.
- v. 24: "Fire came out from before the LORD" — the Hebrew uses a construction suggesting a divine manifestation, not a literal fire source.
