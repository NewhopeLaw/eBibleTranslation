# Leviticus 21

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Tell the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them: No one among you shall become ritually unclean in the midst of your people, ³ except for the closest relatives: your father, your mother, your son, your daughter, and your brother. ⁴ And for your sister, the unmarried one who is near to you, who has not married another, you may become unclean. ⁵ You must not make a bald spot on your head for the dead or shave the corners of your beard or cut any gashes on your body. ⁶ They must be holy to their God, and they must not profane the name of their God, because they offer the bread of their God, the food of their God; they must be holy. ⁷ They must not take a prostitute or a woman who has been defiled, and they must not take a woman who has been divorced from her husband, for they are holy to their God. ⁸ I will consecrate them, for they are the ones who offer the bread of their God. They must be holy, for I am the LORD who consecrates you. ⁹ The daughter of a priest, if she becomes a prostitute and dishonors her father, shall be burned with fire. ¹⁰ The high priest, the one who is set apart above his brothers, who has had the anointing oil poured on his head and has had his hands filled when he put on the garments—his head must not be shaven and his garments must not be torn. ¹¹ He must not enter any place where a body is, and he must not become unclean for his father or his mother. ¹² He must not leave the sanctuary, and he must not profane the sanctuary of his God, for the oil of the anointing of his God is on him. I am the LORD. ¹³ He must take a woman in her virginity. ¹⁴ He must not take a widow, a divorced woman, or a woman who is a prostitute; he must take only a virgin from his own people as his wife. ¹⁵ He must not defile his offspring among his people. I am the LORD who consecrates him. ¹⁶ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁷ Speak to Aaron and say: Any man from the descendants of your lineage, throughout their generations, who has a defect must not come near to offer the bread of his God. ¹⁸ For any man who has a defect must not come near. No one who is blind, or who has a broken limb, or who is lame, ¹⁹ or who has a broken hand or a broken foot, ²⁰ or who is hunched, or who is short, or who has a defect in the eye, or who has a boil, leprosy, or a continual discharge, ²¹ any man from the descendants of Aaron the priest who has a defect must not come near to offer the offerings to the LORD. He must not come near to offer the bread of his God, because he has a defect. ²² He may eat the sacred portions from the holy things and from the offerings. ²³ But he must not enter the veil or come near the altar, because he has a defect, and he must not profane my sanctuary, for I am the LORD who consecrates them. ²⁴ Moses spoke to Aaron and his sons and to all the people of Israel.

---

Notes
- v. 10: The phrase "who has had the anointing oil poured on his head and has had his hands filled when he put on the garments" reflects the ritual anointing and consecration of the high priest (Exodus 29).
- v. 21: The term "has a defect" (מוּם) is a standard term in Levitical law for disqualifying priests or animals for sacrificial offerings.
- v. 23: "The veil" refers to the inner veil of the sanctuary, separating the holy of holies from the holy place, which only the high priest could enter (Leviticus 16).
