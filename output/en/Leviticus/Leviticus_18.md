# Leviticus 18

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Speak to the Israelites and tell them, I am the LORD your God. ³ Do not follow the customs of the land of Egypt where you lived, and do not follow the customs of the land of Canaan where I am bringing you. Do not follow their practices. ⁴ Obey my laws and keep my statutes by living by them. I am the LORD your God. ⁵ Keep my statutes and laws, and live by them. The human who keeps them will live. I am the LORD. ⁶ No one shall come near any flesh of their own to uncover nakedness. I am the LORD. ⁷ Do not uncover the nakedness of your father or the nakedness of your mother. Your mother is you, and you must not uncover her nakedness. ⁸ Do not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife. It is your father's nakedness. ⁹ Do not uncover the nakedness of your sister, whether born to your father or mother, whether born at home or outside. Do not uncover the nakedness of your sister. ¹⁰ Do not uncover the nakedness of your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter. Their nakedness is your own. ¹¹ Do not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law, who is your son's wife. Do not uncover her nakedness, for she is your daughter-in-law. ¹² Do not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister. She is your father's relative. ¹³ Do not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister. She is your mother's relative. ¹⁴ Do not uncover the nakedness of your uncle, nor come near your aunt's wife. She is your relative. ¹⁵ Do not uncover the nakedness of your wife's daughter. She is your daughter-in-law. Do not uncover her nakedness, for she is your daughter-in-law. ¹⁶ Do not uncover the nakedness of your sister, your brother's wife. It is your brother's nakedness. ¹⁷ Do not take a woman and her daughter together, nor take her granddaughter or her daughter's daughter to uncover their nakedness. They are relatives, and it is an abomination. ¹⁸ Do not take a woman to marry her sister, to provoke jealousy by uncovering her nakedness while she is still alive. ¹⁹ Do not come near a woman during the time of her menstrual impurity to uncover her nakedness. ²⁰ Do not lie with your neighbor's wife and defile her. ²¹ Do not offer any of your offspring to Molech, nor defile the name of your God. I am the LORD. ²² Do not lie with a man as one lies with a woman. It is an abomination. ²³ Do not lie with any animal to defile yourself with it, nor have a woman stand before an animal to mate with it. It is detestable. ²⁴ Do not defile yourselves in any of these things, for in these the nations I am driving out before you have defiled themselves. ²⁵ The land became defiled, and I punished it for their guilt, and the land vomited out its inhabitants. ²⁶ Keep my statutes and laws. Do not engage in any of these detestable things, neither the native nor the foreigner living among you. ²⁷ For all these things were done by the people of the land before you, and the land became defiled. ²⁸ Do not let the land vomit you out for defiling it, as it vomited out the people who were before you. ²⁹ For anyone who does any of these detestable things, the lives of those who do them will be cut off from their people. ³⁰ Keep my commandments and do not engage in any of the detestable customs that people did before you, so that you do not defile yourselves by them. I am the LORD your God.

---

Notes
- v. 17: "they are relatives" — the Hebrew uses "sharer" (שְׁאֵר), which is a rare term for "relative" or "kinsman," used here to emphasize the familial connection.
- v. 21: "Molech" — the Hebrew is "molech" (מֹלֶךְ), retained as a proper noun. Some manuscripts and translations use "Baal" or other deities, but the Masoretic text clearly says "Molech."
- v. 22: "an abomination" — the Hebrew is "to'evah" (תּוֹעֵבָה), consistently translated as "abomination" in this chapter.
- v. 23: "detestable" — the Hebrew is "tevel" (תֶּבֶל), translated here as "detestable" to align with the context of moral repugnance.
