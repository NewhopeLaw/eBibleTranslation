# Leviticus 2

¹ When a person brings a gift near to the LORD, it shall be fine flour. They shall pour oil over it and place incense on it; it is a gift. ² They shall bring it to the sons of Aaron the priests, and the priest shall take a handful from the flour and oil with all the incense. The priest shall burn it as a memorial on the altar, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ³ The remainder of the gift belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is most holy from the offerings of the LORD. ⁴ If the gift is baked in an oven, it shall be unleavened cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, or unleavened wafers anointed with oil. ⁵ If the gift is from a griddle, it shall be unleavened fine flour mixed with oil. ⁶ If it is broken into pieces, oil shall be poured over it. It is a gift. ⁷ If the gift is from a pan, it shall be made with oil. ⁸ You shall bring the gift that is made from these to the LORD. The priest shall present it to the altar. ⁹ The priest shall take a portion from the gift as a memorial, and burn it on the altar as a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹⁰ The remainder of the gift belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is most holy from the fire offerings of the LORD. ¹¹ Any gift you bring to the LORD must not be leavened. You must not present any leaven or honey as a fire offering to the LORD. ¹² You must bring a firstfruit offering to the LORD, but it must not be burned on the altar as a pleasing aroma. ¹³ Every gift of yours must be salted. You must not fail to bring the salt of the covenant of your God with your gift. On every offering you bring, salt must be presented. ¹⁴ If you bring a firstfruit offering to the LORD, you must bring new grain roasted in fire. You must bring the offering of your firstfruits as a grain offering. ¹⁵ You shall pour oil over it and place incense on it. It is a gift. ¹⁶ The priest shall burn the memorial portion from the grain, from the oil, and from all the incense. It is a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "firstfruit offering" (רֵאשִׁית) refers to the initial portion dedicated to the LORD, not a specific type of offering.
- v. 14: "new grain roasted in fire" (אָבִיב קָלוּי בָּאֵשׁ) is a reference to freshly harvested grain prepared by roasting, not leavened bread.
