# Leviticus 10

¹ The sons of Aaron, Nadav and Avihu, each took their censers, placed fire in them, and added incense, and brought before the LORD a fire not commanded. ² Fire came out from before the LORD and consumed them, and they died before the LORD. ³ Moses said to Aaron, "This is what the LORD said: 'By those who come near to me I will be sanctified, and before all the people I will be glorified.'" And Aaron was silent. ⁴ Moses called to Mishael and Eltzaphan, the sons of Aaron's uncle Uzi'el, and said to them, "Come near, carry your brothers out from the entrance of the sanctuary to the camp." ⁵ They came near and carried them in their tunics out to the camp, as Moses had said. ⁶ Moses said to Aaron and to Eleazar and Itamar, his sons who remained, "Do not let your hair fall loose, and do not tear your garments, or you will die, and the whole congregation will be angry. But your brothers, the whole house of Israel, shall mourn the fire that the LORD has burned." ⁷ Do not go out from the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, or you will die, for the oil of anointing is on you." And they did as Moses said. ⁸ The LORD spoke to Aaron, saying, ⁹ "You and your sons must not drink wine or strong drink when you come into the Tent of Meeting, or when you approach the altar, or you will die. It is a perpetual statute throughout your generations. ¹⁰ To distinguish between the sacred and the common, and between the unclean and the clean. ¹¹ And to teach the children of Israel all the statutes that the LORD has spoken to them by the hand of Moses." ¹² Moses spoke to Aaron and to Eleazar and Itamar, his sons who remained, "Take the offering that remains from the fire of the LORD and eat it in the holy place, for it is most holy. ¹³ You must eat it in a sacred place, for it is your due and your sons' due from the fire offerings of the LORD, for so I have been commanded." ¹⁴ "The breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution you must eat in a clean place, you and your sons and daughters with you, for it is your due and your sons' due from the peace offerings of the children of Israel. ¹⁵ The thigh of the contribution and the breast of the wave offering must be brought along with the fat of the offerings, to be presented as a wave offering before the LORD. It will be yours and your sons' and daughters' with you, a perpetual statute, as the LORD has commanded." ¹⁶ Moses searched for the goat, the sin offering, and found it had already been burned. He was angry with Eleazar and Itamar, the sons of Aaron who remained, and said, ¹⁷ "Why did you not eat the sin offering in the sacred place, since it is most holy, and it was given to you to bear the guilt of the congregation, to make atonement for them before the LORD?" ¹⁸ "See, the blood was not brought into the inner sanctuary. You might have eaten it in the sacred place, as I commanded." ¹⁹ Aaron said to Moses, "Today my sons brought their sin offerings and their burnt offerings before the LORD, and they brought these things to me. Should I eat the sin offering today, and it please in the sight of the LORD?" ²⁰ Moses heard, and it pleased him.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "a fire not commanded" translates *eish zarah* literally, which is typically rendered as "strange fire" in English Bibles. The term here carries the sense of unauthorized or unordained fire.
- v. 6: "the whole house of Israel" reflects the Hebrew *kol beit Yisrael*, which refers to the entire nation.
- v. 19: The construction in Hebrew is elliptical; Aaron is not explicitly stating that he "should eat the sin offering today" but is expressing concern about whether it is acceptable to do so under the circumstances.
