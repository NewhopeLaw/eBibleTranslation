# Leviticus 6

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Command Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the teaching of the burnt offering. The burnt offering shall be consumed on the altar through its burning, all night until the morning, and the fire on the altar shall remain burning there. ³ The priest shall wear a linen tunic and linen trousers next to his body, and shall gather up the ashes that the fire consumed of the burnt offering from the altar and place them beside the altar. ⁴ Then he shall remove his garments and put on other garments, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place. ⁵ The fire on the altar shall remain burning there, not being extinguished. The priest shall burn wood on it each morning, and arrange the burnt offering on it, and offer the fat of the peace offerings on it. ⁶ A perpetual fire shall remain burning on the altar. It must not be extinguished. ⁷ This is the teaching of the grain offering: The sons of Aaron shall bring it before the LORD to the front of the altar. ⁸ They shall lift up from it a handful of the flour of the grain offering and some of its oil with all the incense that is on the grain offering, and the priest shall burn it as a pleasing aroma on the altar, a memorial of it to the LORD. ⁹ The remainder of it shall be eaten by Aaron and his sons. It shall be eaten as unleavened bread in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting. ¹⁰ It must not be baked with leaven. I have given their share of it from the food offerings of the LORD. It is most holy, like the sin offering and like the guilt offering. ¹¹ Every male among the sons of Aaron shall eat it, a perpetual statute in your generations from the food offerings of the LORD. Whoever touches them shall be consecrated. ¹² The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹³ This is the offering that Aaron and his sons shall present to the LORD on the day when he is anointed. A tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening. ¹⁴ It shall be made on a pan with oil, and it shall be baked in clusters. Bring it as a grain offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹⁵ The priest who is anointed in his place, from his sons, shall make this offering. It is a perpetual statute. It must all be burned. ¹⁶ Every grain offering of the priest must be entirely burned. It must not be eaten. ¹⁷ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁸ Speak to Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the teaching of the sin offering. It shall be slaughtered in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, before the LORD. It is most holy. ¹⁹ The priest who offers it shall eat it in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting. ²⁰ Whoever touches the flesh of it shall be consecrated. And if any of its blood is splashed on a garment, whoever it splashes on shall wash the garment in a holy place. ²¹ Any vessel of earthenware in which it is boiled shall be broken. If it is boiled in a bronze vessel, it shall be rinsed and washed in water. ²² Every male among the priests shall eat it. It is most holy. ²³ Any remains of the sin offering that is brought into the tent of meeting for atonement in the holy place must not be eaten. It shall be burned with fire.

---

Notes  
- v. 2: "consumed on the altar through its burning" — literal rendering of the Hebrew, which emphasizes the uninterrupted nature of the fire and the offering's complete combustion.  
- v. 10: "I have given their share of it from the food offerings of the LORD" — reflects the Hebrew context that the priests’ portion is not their own but a sacred allotment from a holy offering.  
- v. 23: "must not be eaten" — the Hebrew uses a form of "to eat" in the negative, which is best rendered as a prohibition.
