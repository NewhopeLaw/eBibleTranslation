# Leviticus 4

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Speak to the people of Israel, saying: If a person sins unintentionally among all the commandments of the LORD that must not be done, and does any one of them, ³ if the anointed priest sins and causes the people to be guilty, he shall bring to the LORD, for his sin, a bull without blemish from the herd as a sin offering. ⁴ He shall bring the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before the LORD, and lay his hand on the bull’s head and slaughter it before the LORD. ⁵ The anointed priest shall take some of the bull’s blood into the Tent of Meeting. ⁶ The priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it before the LORD, seven times, against the front of the veil of the sanctuary. ⁷ The priest shall place some of the blood on the horns of the altar of the incense before the LORD in the Tent of Meeting, and pour out all the bull’s blood at the base of the altar of burnt offerings at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ⁸ Then he shall remove all the fat of the bull sin offering: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat on the entrails, ⁹ the two kidneys and the fat on them, the fat on the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which is attached to the kidneys. ¹⁰ As the fat of the bull of the peace offering is removed, so shall the priest burn it on the altar of the burnt offering. ¹¹ The bull’s hide and all its flesh, from its head to its legs, its entrails and its hind parts, ¹² shall be taken outside the camp to a clean place, to the ground where ashes are poured out, and it shall be burned on wood in the fire, at the place where ashes are poured out. It shall be burned. ¹³ If the entire assembly of Israel sins unintentionally and a matter is hidden from the eyes of the congregation, and they do any one of the commandments of the LORD that must not be done and become guilty, ¹⁴ when their sin becomes known, the assembly shall bring a bull without blemish as a sin offering and present it before the Tent of Meeting. ¹⁵ The elders of the congregation shall lay their hands on the head of the bull before the LORD, and it shall be slaughtered before the LORD. ¹⁶ The anointed priest shall take some of the bull’s blood into the Tent of Meeting. ¹⁷ He shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it before the LORD, seven times, against the front of the veil. ¹⁸ He shall place some of the blood on the horns of the altar that is before the LORD in the Tent of Meeting, and pour out all the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offerings at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ¹⁹ He shall remove all the fat of the bull and burn it on the altar. ²⁰ He shall do to the bull what he did to the first bull; the priest shall make atonement for them, and it shall be forgiven them. ²¹ He shall take the bull outside the camp and burn it as the first bull was burned. It is a sin offering for the assembly. ²² If a ruler sins and does any one of the commandments of the LORD his God, which must not be done, unintentionally and becomes guilty, ²³ when his sin becomes known to him, he shall bring his offering: a male goat without blemish. ²⁴ He shall lay his hand on the head of the goat and slaughter it in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered before the LORD. It is a sin offering. ²⁵ The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings and pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offerings. ²⁶ He shall remove all the fat of the goat as the fat of the peace offering is removed and the priest shall burn it on the altar, as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. The priest shall make atonement for him, and it shall be forgiven him. ²⁷ If a person among the common people sins unintentionally, doing any one of the commandments of the LORD that must not be done and becomes guilty, ²⁸ when his sin becomes known to him, he shall bring his offering: a female goat without blemish. ²⁹ He shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it in the place of the burnt offering. ³⁰ The priest shall take some of the blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings, and pour out all the blood at the base of the altar. ³¹ He shall remove all the fat of the goat as the fat of the peace offering is removed and the priest shall burn it on the altar, as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. The priest shall make atonement for him, and it shall be forgiven him. ³² If the offering he brings is a sheep, he shall bring a female goat without blemish. ³³ He shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered. ³⁴ The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings, and pour out all the blood at the base of the altar. ³⁵ He shall remove all the fat of the goat as the fat of the sheep of the peace offering is removed, and the priest shall burn it on the altar, on the fire offerings to the LORD. The priest shall make atonement for him regarding his sin, and it shall be forgiven him.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the LORD" – יהוה (YHWH) is translated as "the LORD" consistently.
- v. 3: "the anointed priest" – The Hebrew term כֹּהֵן הַמָּשִׁיחַ is translated as "the anointed priest" to reflect the role of the high priest.
- v. 6: "seven times" – שֶׁבַע פְּעָמִים is translated as "seven times" to preserve the symbolic number.
- v. 19: "as the fat of the bull of the peace offering is removed" – The instruction to remove the fat follows the same procedure as for the peace offering.
- v. 22: "a ruler" – The Hebrew term נָשִׂיא is translated as "a ruler" to reflect a position of leadership.
- v. 35: "on the fire offerings to the LORD" – The phrase "עַל אִשֵּׁי יְהוָה" is translated to reflect the connection of the offering to the fire offerings made to the LORD.
