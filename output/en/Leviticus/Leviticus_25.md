# Leviticus 25

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying: ² Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them: When you come into the land that I give to you, the land must rest for the LORD. ³ For six years you may sow your field and for six years you may prune your vineyard and gather its fruit. ⁴ In the seventh year, there must be a sabbath of complete rest for the land, a sabbath to the LORD. You must not sow your field or prune your vineyard. ⁵ You must not reap the growth of what springs up of itself in your field, nor must you harvest the grapes of your unpruned vineyard. The land must have a year of complete rest. ⁶ The sabbath of the land will be food for you, for you, your servant, your maid, your hired worker, and the foreigner who resides among you. ⁷ For your livestock and the wild animals in your land, all the produce will be for food. ⁸ You must count for yourself seven sabbaths of years, seven years seven times, and you will have seven sabbaths of years, totaling forty-nine years. ⁹ You must sound the horn of a loud blast in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month. On the Day of Atonement, sound the horn in all your land. ¹⁰ You must consecrate the fiftieth year, and you must proclaim liberty throughout the land for all who live in it. It will be a year of jubilee for you. Each person must return to their inheritance, and each person must return to their family. ¹¹ This year will be a year of liberty. The fiftieth year will be a sabbath to you. You must not sow, and you must not reap the growth of what springs up, nor harvest the grapes of your vineyard. ¹² For it is a year of liberty, a sacred year for you. You may eat from the fields what they produce. ¹³ In this year of the jubilee, each person must return to their inheritance. ¹⁴ When you sell to your neighbor or buy from the hand of your neighbor, you must not deceive one another. ¹⁵ According to the number of years after the jubilee, you may buy from your neighbor, and according to the number of the harvest, you must sell to them. ¹⁶ The price must be higher when the number of years is greater, and the price must be lower when the number of years is fewer, because you are buying according to the number of the harvest. ¹⁷ You must not deceive one another, and you must fear your God, for I am the LORD your God. ¹⁸ You must do my statutes and keep my judgments and perform them, and you will dwell securely in the land. ¹⁹ And the land will yield its fruit, and you will eat to your satisfaction and dwell securely in it. ²⁰ And if you say, "What will we eat in the seventh year, if we do not sow and do not gather in our harvest?" ²¹ I will send my blessing to you in the sixth year, and it will produce fruit for three years. ²² And you will sow in the eighth year, and you will eat from the old produce until the ninth year, until the new harvest arrives. You will eat the old until the new comes. ²³ The land must not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is mine. You are but strangers and sojourners before me. ²⁴ And in all the land of your possession, you must give redemption for the land. ²⁵ If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his inheritance, then his nearest relative must come and redeem what his brother sold. ²⁶ If a man has no one to redeem it, and his hand is able to find enough to redeem it, ²⁷ he must calculate the years of the sale and return the excess to the man who sold it to him, and the person must return to his inheritance. ²⁸ If his hand is not able to find enough to return it, then what he sold must remain in the hand of the buyer until the year of the jubilee, and in the jubilee it will go out, and the person must return to his inheritance. ²⁹ If a man sells a house in a walled city, its redemption is valid until the end of a full year. The redemption period will last one full year. ³⁰ If it is not redeemed before the year is complete, the house in the walled city will become the permanent possession of the buyer, and it will not go out in the jubilee. ³¹ But houses in unwalled villages will be considered as fields. Their redemption will be valid, and they will go out in the jubilee. ³² And the cities of the Levites, the houses of their cities, must remain as perpetual redemption to the Levites. ³³ If a Levite redeems it, then what was sold in the city of his inheritance will go out in the jubilee, because the houses of the cities of the Levites are their inheritance among the children of Israel. ³⁴ And the fields of their suburbs must not be sold, for the field is their eternal possession. ³⁵ If your brother becomes poor and his hand falters with you, you must support him like a stranger or a sojourner, and he must live with you. ³⁶ Do not take interest from him or multiply profits, and you must fear your God. Let your brother live with you. ³⁷ Do not lend him your money at interest, and do not exact more from him than his food. ³⁸ I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan, to be your God. ³⁹ If your brother becomes poor with you and sells himself to you, he must not serve you as a slave. ⁴⁰ He must be with you as a hired worker or a sojourner, until the year of the jubilee. ⁴¹ Then he and his children must go out with him, and he must return to his family and to his inheritance. ⁴² For they are my servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. They must not be sold as slaves. ⁴³ Do not rule over them with harshness. You must fear your God. ⁴⁴ And your male and female servants may be taken from the nations around you. From them you may buy male and female servants. ⁴⁵ Also from the children of the sojourners who are with you, from them you may buy, and from their families who are with you in your land. They must become your possession. ⁴⁶ You must pass them on to your children after you, to be an inheritance for you forever. You may rule over them, but your brother, the children of Israel, you must not rule over one another with harshness. ⁴⁷ If a foreigner or sojourner with you becomes rich, and your brother who is with you sells himself to the foreigner or to the sojourner among you, or to a member of the family of a sojourner, ⁴⁸ after he is sold, a redemption must be made for him. One of his relatives must redeem him. ⁴⁹ A brother, or his uncle, or a cousin from his family must redeem him, or if his hand is able, he must redeem himself. ⁵⁰ He must calculate with the one who bought him, from the year of the sale until the year of the jubilee, and the price of his redemption must be like the wages of a hired worker for the years that remain. ⁵¹ If there are more years, he must return the redemption price from the silver of his purchase accordingly. ⁵² But if there are fewer years remaining until the year of the jubilee, he must calculate accordingly for the years, and return the redemption price as a hired worker. ⁵³ He must serve year by year with you, and you must not rule over him with harshness in your sight. ⁵⁴ But if he is not redeemed in these ways, he and his children must go out in the year of the jubilee. ⁵⁵ For the children of Israel are my servants; they are my servants whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.

Notes
- v. 24: "give redemption for the land" – the term used here is גְּאֻלָּה, which is consistently translated as "redemption" in the context of land or people, following the glossary.
