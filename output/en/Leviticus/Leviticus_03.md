# Leviticus 3

¹ If the offering is a fellowship sacrifice, whether from the cattle, the person must present a male or female without blemish before the LORD. ² The person shall lay their hand on the head of their offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. The sons of Aaron the priests shall sprinkle the blood on the altar all around. ³ From the fellowship sacrifice, the priest shall present to the LORD the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails. ⁴ The priest shall also remove the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, the fat that is on the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which is attached to the kidneys. ⁵ The sons of Aaron shall place these on the altar as part of the burnt offering, on top of the wood that is on the fire. It is a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ⁶ If the offering is from the flock, a fellowship sacrifice to the LORD, the person must present a male or female without blemish. ⁷ If the offering is a ram, it shall be presented before the LORD. ⁸ The person shall lay their hand on the head of their offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. The sons of Aaron shall sprinkle the blood on the altar all around. ⁹ The priest shall present to the LORD part of the fellowship sacrifice—the fat covering the entrails and all the fat on the entrails, ¹⁰ the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, the fat that is on the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which is attached to the kidneys. ¹¹ The priest shall burn this on the altar as a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹² If the offering is a female goat, it shall be presented before the LORD. ¹³ The person shall lay their hand on the head of the offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. The sons of Aaron shall sprinkle the blood on the altar all around. ¹⁴ The priest shall present to the LORD part of the offering—the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails, ¹⁵ the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, the fat that is on the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which is attached to the kidneys. ¹⁶ The priest shall burn this on the altar as a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. Every fat offering belongs to the LORD. ¹⁷ This is an eternal statute for your generations, wherever you dwell: no fat or blood shall you eat.

---

Notes
- v. 17: The rendering of "no fat or blood shall you eat" follows the Hebrew לֹא תֹאכֵֽלוּ ("you shall not eat") as a prohibition, not a conditional or negative imperative. The phrase כָּל־חֵלֶב וְכָל־דָּם ("all fat and all blood") is treated as an absolute command.
