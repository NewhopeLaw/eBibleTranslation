# Leviticus 22

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Speak to Aaron and his sons, and tell them to guard the sacred portions of the children of Israel, and they must not profane the name of my holiness, which they set apart for me. I am the LORD. ³ Say to them throughout your generations: Any man from your lineage who comes near the sacred things that the children of Israel set apart for the LORD, and who is unclean, shall be cut off from my presence. I am the LORD. ⁴ Any man from the descendants of Aaron who is a leper or a man with an issue of semen must not eat of the sacred things until he is cleansed. Anyone who touches any unclean person, or a man from whom the seed of life has flowed, ⁵ or any man who touches a creeping thing that defiles him, or a person who defiles him in any of his uncleanness, ⁶ the one who touches these things shall be unclean until evening, and must not eat of the sacred things unless he washes his body in water. ⁷ When the sun sets, he is cleansed, and then he may eat of the sacred things, for it is his food. ⁸ A carcass or a torn animal must not be eaten, for it would defile you. I am the LORD. ⁹ You must guard my charge, and you must not bear guilt and die for it, for if you profane it, I am the LORD who consecrates them. ¹⁰ Any outsider must not eat of the sacred things. A hired worker or a resident alien must not eat of the sacred things. ¹¹ But if a priest buys a person with his money, he may eat of it, and the members of his household may eat of his bread. ¹² A daughter of a priest, when she marries a foreigner, must not eat of the sacred offerings, for she has become a devoted portion. ¹³ A daughter of a priest, when she becomes a widow or divorced, and has no children, must return to her father’s house like in her youth, and eat of her father’s bread. Any outsider must not eat of it. ¹⁴ A man who eats of the sacred things by mistake must repay to the priest the fifth part in addition to the sacred portion, and give the sacred thing to the priest. ¹⁵ They must not profane the sacred things of the children of Israel, which they have brought up to the LORD. ¹⁶ And they shall bear guilt for the sin of desecration when they eat their sacred things, for I am the LORD who consecrates them. ¹⁷ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁸ Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the people of Israel, and say to them: Any man from the house of Israel or from the foreigners living among them in Israel who brings an offering as a vow or as a freewill gift to the LORD for a burnt offering, ¹⁹ must bring a male without blemish from the cattle, the sheep, or the goats. It must be acceptable to you. ²⁰ Any animal with a defect must not be brought near, for it will not be acceptable to you. ²¹ If a man brings a peace offering to the LORD as a vow or as a freewill offering, from the cattle or from the flock, it must be without blemish. There must be no defect in it. ²² A blind, maimed, or crippled animal, or one with a broken limb or scab, or a boil, must not be brought near to the LORD. You must not offer them as a burnt offering to the LORD. ²³ A bull or a sheep that is clipped or has a cut ear may be given as a freewill offering, but it must not be accepted as a vow offering. ²⁴ A crushed, a maimed, a torn, or a cut animal must not be brought near to the LORD. You must not do these things in your land. ²⁵ From the hand of a foreigner you must not offer the bread of your God from any of these, for they are marred, flawed. They will not be accepted for you. ²⁶ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²⁷ When a bull or a sheep or a goat is born, it must remain with its mother for seven days. From the eighth day and onward, it must be accepted as an offering to the LORD. ²⁸ You must not slaughter a bull and its calf on the same day. ²⁹ If you offer a thanksgiving offering to the LORD, you must offer it in a way that is acceptable to you. ³⁰ You must eat it on the same day; you must not leave any of it until morning. I am the LORD. ³¹ You must keep my commandments and do them. I am the LORD. ³² You must not profane my name, and I will be sanctified among the children of Israel. I am the LORD who consecrates you. ³³ I am the one who brought you out from the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "man with an issue of semen" is a literal rendering of זָב, which refers to a man experiencing seminal emissions, which rendered him ritually unclean.
- v. 13: "has no children" — the Hebrew text says "no seed for her," a more neutral phrase, but the context implies childlessness.
- v. 23: "clipped or has a cut ear" — שָׂרוּעַ is "clipped" (as in cropped ears), and קָלוּט is "cut ear" (likely referring to a missing ear, perhaps from injury or branding).
- v. 25: "crushed, a maimed, a torn, or a cut animal" — מוּעַךְ, כָּתוּת, נָתוּק, כָּרוּת are all terms for physical blemishes or defects in sacrificial animals.
- v. 27: "it must remain with its mother for seven days" — the Hebrew uses שִׁבְעַת יָמִים תַּחַת אִמּוֹ, which can be interpreted as "under the mother" or "with the mother," referring to nursing or close proximity.
