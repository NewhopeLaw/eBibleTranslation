# Leviticus 8

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Take Aaron and his sons with you, along with the garments, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread. ³ And you shall gather the whole assembly at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ⁴ Moses did as the LORD had commanded him, and the assembly gathered at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ⁵ Moses said to the assembly, "This is the matter that the LORD has commanded to be done." ⁶ Moses brought Aaron and his sons near, and he washed them with water. ⁷ He put on Aaron the tunic and fastened it with the sash. He clothed him with the robe and placed the ephod over him, fastening it with the skillfully woven band of the ephod, and he invested him with it. ⁸ He placed the breastpiece on him and put the Urim and the Thummim in the breastpiece. ⁹ He set the turban on Aaron's head and fastened on the turban, in front of his face, the golden plate—the holy diadem—as the LORD had commanded Moses. ¹⁰ Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the Tent and everything in it, consecrating them. ¹¹ He poured some of it on the altar seven times, anointing the altar and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand, to consecrate them. ¹² Then he poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him to consecrate him. ¹³ Moses brought Aaron's sons near and clothed them with tunics, fastened them with sashes, and placed the caps on their heads, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ¹⁴ Then he presented the bull for the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bull for the sin offering. ¹⁵ Moses slaughtered it and took its blood, and he put it on the horns of the altar all around with his finger, and he sprinkled the altar with the blood, and he poured the blood at the base of the altar, to consecrate it and to make atonement for it. ¹⁶ Moses took all the fat that was on the entrails and the lobe of the liver and both kidneys and their fat, and he burned them on the altar. ¹⁷ He burned the bull, its hide, its flesh, and its dung with fire outside the camp, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ¹⁸ Then Moses brought the ram for the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram. ¹⁹ Moses slaughtered it and poured out its blood on the altar all around. ²⁰ Then he cut the ram into pieces and Moses burned the head, the pieces, and the fat. ²¹ He washed the inner parts and the legs with water, and Moses burned the whole ram on the altar for a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ²² Moses brought the second ram, the ram for the ordination, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram. ²³ Moses slaughtered it and took some of its blood and put it on Aaron's right earlobe, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. ²⁴ Moses brought Aaron's sons near and put some of the blood on the right earlobe of each of them, on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot, and he poured the blood around the altar. ²⁵ He took the fat and the right thigh, the fat that was on the entrails, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys and their fat, and the right foreleg. ²⁶ From the basket of the unleavened bread that was before the LORD, he took one unleavened cake, one cake of bread with oil, and one wafer. He placed them on the fat and the right thigh. ²⁷ He placed all of these on the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved them as a wave offering before the LORD. ²⁸ Moses took them from their hands and burned them on the altar on top of the burnt offering of ordination, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to the LORD. ²⁹ Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before the LORD. It became Moses' portion, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³⁰ Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar and sprinkled it on Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments. He thus consecrated Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments. ³¹ Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting and eat it there, along with the bread from the basket of ordination, as I have been commanded to say, Aaron and his sons shall eat it." ³² Whatever remains of the meat and the bread you shall burn with fire. ³³ You must not go out from the entrance of the Tent of Meeting for seven days until the day of your ordination is completed. For seven days you will complete your time of ordination. ³⁴ As the LORD has done on this day, he has commanded to do in order to make atonement for you. ³⁵ You must remain at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting day and night for seven days and keep the charge of the LORD, so that you do not die, for so I have been commanded." ³⁶ Aaron and his sons did all the things that the LORD had commanded Moses to do.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "consecrated them" — the verb קִדְּשָׁם could also be interpreted as "sanctified them," but "consecrated" aligns with the context of ritual preparation.
- v. 31: "eat it there" — the phrase פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד is rendered as "at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting" to emphasize the sacred location.
- v. 35: "the charge of the LORD" — מִשְׁמֶרֶת יְהוָה is a common term for guarding or keeping a divine charge, often translated as "charge" or "commandment."
