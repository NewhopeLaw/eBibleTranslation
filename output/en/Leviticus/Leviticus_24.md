# Leviticus 24

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Command the children of Israel to bring to you pure oil pressed from olives, for lighting a lamp continually. ³ Outside the veil of the Testimony in the Tent of Meeting, Aaron must set it up. He must light it from evening until morning before the LORD continually. It must be a perpetual statute for your generations. ⁴ On the pure lampstand, he must light the lamps before the LORD continually. ⁵ You must take fine flour and bake twelve loaves, each one two-tenths of an ephah. ⁶ You must arrange them in two rows, six in each row, on the pure table before the LORD. ⁷ You must place on each row frankincense, pure and without salt, and it must be a memorial offering, a food offering to the LORD. ⁸ On the Sabbath, on the Sabbath, he must set them before the LORD continually, from the children of Israel. It must be a covenant of perpetual renewal. ⁹ It will belong to Aaron and his sons, and they must eat it in a holy place, for it is most holy to them from the offerings to the LORD. It is a perpetual statute. ¹⁰ A son of an Israelite woman and an Egyptian man was born among the children of Israel. The son of the Israelite woman and the Israelite man quarreled in the camp. ¹¹ The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name and cursed. They brought him to Moses, and the name of his mother was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan. ¹² They put him in custody until they could determine what the LORD would command. ¹³ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁴ Bring the one who cursed outside the camp. All who heard must lay their hands on his head, and the whole assembly must stone him. ¹⁵ You must tell the children of Israel: Any person who curses their God must bear their sin. ¹⁶ Anyone who blasphemes the Name of the LORD must be put to death. The whole assembly must stone them, whether they are a foreigner or a native Israelite. Whoever blasphemes the Name must die. ¹⁷ Any person who strikes a life must die. ¹⁸ One who strikes an animal must make restitution: life for life. ¹⁹ If a person harms their neighbor, they must be treated according to the injury they caused. ²⁰ Wound for wound, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. As one causes harm to a person, so it must be done to them. ²¹ One who strikes an animal must make restitution, and one who strikes a person must die. ²² One judgment must be given for you, whether to the foreigner or the native, for I am the LORD your God. ²³ Moses spoke to the children of Israel, and they brought the one who cursed outside the camp and stoned him. The children of Israel did as the LORD had commanded Moses.

---

Notes
- v. 5: "fine flour" refers to sifted or refined flour, as typically used in offerings.
- v. 8: "covenant of perpetual renewal" reflects the recurring nature of the offering on the Sabbath.
- v. 10: The mixed parentage of the blasphemer is described as "a son of an Israelite woman and an Egyptian man" to clarify his identity.
- v. 16: "blasphemes the Name" refers to the Name of the LORD (YHWH).
- v. 22: "one judgment" translates the Hebrew "מִשְׁפַּט אֶחָד" — one law or one standard of judgment.
