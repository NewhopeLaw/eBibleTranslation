# Leviticus 5

¹ If a person sins and hears the sound of an oath, whether as a witness or having seen or known, and does not inform and becomes guilty, ² or if a person touches any unclean thing, whether a carcass of an unclean animal or of an unclean beast or of an unclean creeping thing, and it remains hidden from him, making him unclean and he becomes guilty, ³ or if someone touches uncleanness from a human being, from any uncleanness by which he becomes unclean, and it remains hidden from him, but he later learns and becomes guilty, ⁴ or if a person makes a vow with his lips, to do evil or good, for any oath a person makes, and it remains hidden from him, but he later learns and becomes guilty for one of these, ⁵ then when he becomes guilty for one of these, he shall confess the sin he has committed ⁶ and bring his guilt offering to the LORD for his sin: a female from the flock, a yearling lamb or a female goat for a sin offering, and the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin, and it shall be forgiven him. ⁷ But if his hand does not reach the price of a lamb, he shall bring for his sin offering two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the LORD: one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering. ⁸ He shall bring them to the priest, and the priest shall offer the sin offering first, and the priest shall break the head of the bird at the base of its neck, not tearing it open. ⁹ Then the priest shall sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar, and the rest of the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar; it is a sin offering. ¹⁰ The second bird he shall offer as a burnt offering, as the law prescribes, and the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin, and it shall be forgiven him. ¹¹ If his hand does not reach the price of two turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he shall bring his offering for the sin he has committed: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a sin offering. He must not put oil on it or incense on it, for it is a sin offering. ¹² He shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take from it a handful of fine flour as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar, a pleasing aroma to the LORD; it is a sin offering. ¹³ The priest shall make atonement for him for his sin, one of these, and it shall be forgiven him. The remainder shall belong to the priest, like the grain offering. ¹⁴ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁵ When a person sins unintentionally in a matter of the holy things of the LORD, and becomes guilty, he shall bring to the LORD for his guilt a male lamb without blemish from the flock, according to the value of the silver shekel, according to the sacred shekel, as a guilt offering. ¹⁶ He shall make restitution for the holy thing he has sinned against, and add a fifth of its value to it, and give it to the priest, and the priest shall make atonement for him with the male lamb of the guilt offering, and it shall be forgiven him. ¹⁷ If a person sins and does any one of the commandments of the LORD that must not be done, and does not know it, and becomes guilty, ¹⁸ he shall bring a male lamb without blemish from the flock, according to the value, as a guilt offering to the priest. The priest shall make atonement for him for his ignorance, for he did not know, and it shall be forgiven him. ¹⁹ It is a guilt offering, a guilt offering to the LORD. ²⁰ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²¹ If a person sins and acts deceitfully by the LORD, and deceives a neighbor in a deposit or in a pledge or in robbery or in wronging a neighbor, ²² or if he finds lost property and deceives in it, and swears falsely about any of all the things a person does to sin in this way, ²³ then when he sins and becomes guilty, he shall return the stolen thing he stole, or the wrong he wronged, or the deposit given to him, or the lost thing he found, ²⁴ or anything about which he swore falsely, and he shall make restitution for it, and add a fifth to its value, and give it to the one who is due it, on the day of his guilt offering. ²⁵ He shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD: a male lamb without blemish from the flock, according to the value, as a guilt offering to the priest. ²⁶ The priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD, and it shall be forgiven him for any one of all the things he did to commit guilt in them.

---

Notes
- v. 13: The phrase "like the grain offering" is used to clarify the priest's share, not the offering's nature.
- v. 15: "according to the value of the silver shekel" translates the specific measure of the sacred shekel for restitution.
- v. 19: The repetition is preserved to emphasize the gravity of the guilt offering.
