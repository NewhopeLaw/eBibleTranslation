# Leviticus 11

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying, ² "Speak to the children of Israel, saying, These are the creatures among the land animals that you may eat. ³ Any animal that has a split hoof and that chews the cud, among the land animals, you may eat. ⁴ But these you may not eat: the camel, for it chews the cud but does not have a split hoof—it is unclean for you. ⁵ And the rock badger, for it chews the cud but does not split its hoof—it is unclean for you. ⁶ And the hare, for it chews the cud but does not split its hoof—it is unclean for you. ⁷ And the pig, for it has a split hoof and splits it completely, but it does not chew the cud—it is unclean for you. ⁸ You must not eat their flesh, and you must not touch their carcasses—they are unclean for you. ⁹ These you may eat from all that are in the water: anything that has fins and scales, in the seas and in the streams, you may eat. ¹⁰ And anything that does not have fins or scales in the seas or in the streams, from all the swarming things of the water and from every living creature that is in the water, is detestable to you. ¹¹ These will be detestable to you. Their flesh you must not eat, and their carcasses you must detest. ¹² Anything that does not have fins or scales in the water is detestable to you. ¹³ And these you must detest from the birds, and they must not be eaten, for they are detestable: the raven, the griffon vulture, and the sea gull, ¹⁴ and the kite, the osprey, and the like of these, ¹⁵ and the heron, the hoopoe, and the pelican, and the like of these, ¹⁶ and the falcon, and the like of these, ¹⁷ and the owl, the nightjar, and the gull, ¹⁸ and the pelican, the stork, and the heron, ¹⁹ and the hoopoe, the bat, and the kite, and the like of these, and the owl and the nightjar. ²⁰ And every creeping thing among the birds that goes on four legs is detestable to you. ²¹ But these you may eat from the creeping things of the birds that walk on four legs—those that have jointed legs above their feet, with which they leap along the earth. ²² These you may eat from among them: the locust, the grasshopper, the cricket, and the like of these. ²³ Any creeping thing among the birds that has four legs is detestable to you. ²⁴ And these will make you unclean: anyone who touches a carcass of them will be unclean until evening. ²⁵ And whoever carries a carcass of them must wash their clothes and be unclean until evening. ²⁶ All land animals that split the hoof but do not split it completely, and that do not chew the cud, are unclean for you. Anyone who touches their carcasses will be unclean. ²⁷ And all creatures that walk on their hands among the land animals that walk on four legs are unclean for you. Anyone who touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening. ²⁸ And whoever carries their carcasses must wash their clothes and be unclean until evening. These are unclean for you. ²⁹ And these are unclean for you from the creeping things that crawl on the earth: the weasel, the mouse, and the lizard, ³⁰ the gecko, the monitor lizard, the skink, the chameleon, and the lizard. ³¹ These are unclean for you from all the creeping things that crawl on the earth. Anyone who touches them, after they die, will be unclean until evening. ³² And anything upon which any of them fall, when they die, will be unclean. From any wooden vessel, or cloth, or leather, or sack, anything that is made to carry them must be washed in water and be unclean until evening, and then it will be clean. ³³ And any clay vessel into which any of these fall, whatever is in it will be unclean, and the vessel itself must be broken. ³⁴ Any food that is eaten, if water has come upon it, will be unclean. And any drink that is drunk, from any vessel, will be unclean. ³⁵ And anything upon which any of their carcasses fall will be unclean: the oven and the cooking pots must be broken. These are unclean and unclean for you. ³⁶ But a well or a cistern or a reservoir of water will remain clean. Anyone who touches their carcasses will be unclean. ³⁷ If any of their carcasses fall on any sown seed that has been planted, it will remain clean. ³⁸ But if water is poured on the seed and any of their carcasses fall on it, it will be unclean for you. ³⁹ If any of the animals you have for eating die, anyone who touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening. ⁴⁰ And whoever eats from their carcasses must wash their clothes and be unclean until evening. And whoever carries their carcasses must wash their clothes and be unclean until evening. ⁴¹ And every creeping thing that crawls on the earth is detestable to you. It must not be eaten. ⁴² All that walks on its belly, and all that walks on four legs, and all that has many feet among the creeping things that crawl on the earth, you must not eat, for they are detestable. ⁴³ Do not detest your souls in any of the creeping things that crawl on the ground, and do not become unclean by them, and you must not be defiled by them. ⁴⁴ For I am the LORD your God. You must consecrate yourselves and be holy, for I am holy. You must not defile yourselves in any of the creeping things that move about on the earth. ⁴⁵ For I am the LORD who brought you up from the land of Egypt to be your God. You must be holy, for I am holy. ⁴⁶ This is the law regarding the land animals, the birds, and every living creature that moves in the water, and every creature that crawls on the earth. ⁴⁷ To distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between the creature that may be eaten and the creature that must not be eaten.

Notes
- v. 13: "the sea gull" — the Hebrew term here is ambiguous, but "sea gull" is a plausible rendering for the context.
