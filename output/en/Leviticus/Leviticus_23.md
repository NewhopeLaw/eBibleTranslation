# Leviticus 23

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: These are the appointed feasts of the LORD which you must call holy convocations. These are my feasts: ³ Six days work may be done, but on the seventh day, the Sabbath of rest, a holy convocation shall be held. You must not do any work; it is the Sabbath of the LORD your God in all your dwellings. ⁴ These are the feasts of the LORD, the holy convocations which you must call in their appointed times. ⁵ On the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, at twilight, the Passover is held to the LORD. ⁶ And on the fifteenth day of this month, the Feast of Unleavened Bread is held to the LORD. Seven days you must eat unleavened bread. ⁷ On the first day there must be a holy convocation for you; you must not do any work of service. ⁸ And you must offer a burnt offering to the LORD for seven days. On the seventh day, there must be a holy convocation for you; you must not do any work of service. ⁹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁰ Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land which I give you, and you reap its harvest, you must bring a sheaf of the firstfruits of your harvest to the priest. ¹¹ And the priest must wave the sheaf before the LORD on your behalf, on the day after the Sabbath. ¹² And you must offer on the day you wave the sheaf a male lamb, a year old, without blemish, as a burnt offering to the LORD. ¹³ Its offering must be two tenths of fine flour, mixed with oil; it is an offering by fire to the LORD, a pleasing aroma; and you must pour out its drink offering, a quarter of a hin. ¹⁴ You must not eat leavened bread, nor dried cakes, nor any green ears until the very same day that you have brought the offering of your God. It is a perpetual statute for your generations in all your dwellings. ¹⁵ You must count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day that you brought the sheaf of the wave offering; seven full weeks must be counted. ¹⁶ Until the day after the seventh Sabbath, you must count fifty days, and you must present a new offering to the LORD. ¹⁷ You must bring from your dwellings two loaves of bread, made of two tenths of fine flour. They must be leavened and baked, as firstfruits to the LORD. ¹⁸ And you must present with the loaves seven male lambs, a year old, without blemish, and one bull from the herd, and two rams. They must be a burnt offering to the LORD, and their grain offerings and their drink offerings must be a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹⁹ And you must present one male goat for a sin offering, and two male lambs, a year old, for a peace offering. ²⁰ And the priest must wave them with the loaves of the firstfruits as a wave offering before the LORD, with the two lambs; they must be holy to the LORD for the priest. ²¹ And you must proclaim on that very day that it is a holy convocation for you; you must not do any work of service. It is a perpetual statute for your generations in all your dwellings. ²² And when you reap the harvest of your land, you must not reap the corners of your field, nor must you gather the gleanings of your harvest. You must leave them for the poor and the foreigner. I am the LORD your God. ²³ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²⁴ Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: On the seventh month, on the first day of the month, there must be a day of rest, a memorial with the blowing of trumpets, a holy convocation for you. ²⁵ You must not do any work of service. You must offer a burnt offering to the LORD. ²⁶ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²⁷ Now on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement. It must be a holy convocation for you; you must humble your souls and offer a burnt offering to the LORD. ²⁸ You must not do any work on this very day, for it is a day of atonement, to make atonement for you before the LORD your God. ²⁹ For any soul that does not humble itself on this very day, that soul must be cut off from its people. ³⁰ And any soul that does any work on this very day, that soul must be destroyed from among its people. ³¹ You must do no work. It is a perpetual statute for your generations in all your dwellings. ³² It is a Sabbath of rest for you, and you must humble your souls. From the ninth day of the month at evening, until the following evening, you must keep your Sabbath. ³³ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ³⁴ Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Booths for seven days to the LORD. ³⁵ On the first day there must be a holy convocation; you must not do any work of service. ³⁶ For seven days you must offer burnt offerings to the LORD. On the eighth day, there must be a holy convocation for you; you must offer burnt offerings to the LORD. It is a closing convocation; you must not do any work of service. ³⁷ These are the feasts of the LORD which you must call holy convocations, to present burnt offerings, grain offerings, and sacrifices by their appointed times. ³⁸ In addition to the Sabbaths of the LORD, in addition to your gifts, in addition to all your vows, and in addition to all your freewill offerings which you give to the LORD. ³⁹ Now on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the produce of the land, you must keep the feast of the LORD for seven days. On the first day there must be a rest, and on the eighth day, a rest. ⁴⁰ And you must take for yourselves on the first day, the fruit of a beautiful tree, branches of palm trees, boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook. And you must rejoice before the LORD your God for seven days. ⁴¹ You must keep this feast as a feast to the LORD for seven days in the year, a perpetual statute for your generations in the seventh month. ⁴² You must dwell in booths for seven days. Every native Israelite must dwell in booths. ⁴³ That your generations may know that I made the children of Israel dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God. ⁴⁴ And Moses declared the feasts of the LORD to the children of Israel.

---

Notes
- v. 14: "leavened bread, nor dried cakes, nor any green ears" — the Hebrew uses distinct terms for these foods, emphasizing their different statuses and uses.
- v. 15: "seven full weeks" — the count is from the day after the Sabbath to the day after the seventh Sabbath, totaling fifty days (the Omer count).
- v. 43: "I made the children of Israel dwell in booths" — reference to the Exodus, where the Israelites lived in temporary shelters during their journey.
