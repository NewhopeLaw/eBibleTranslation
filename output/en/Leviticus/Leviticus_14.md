# Leviticus 14

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² This is the law of the leprous person on the day of their cleansing. When they come to the priest, ³ the priest will go out to the outside of the camp and examine the person. If the priest sees that the leprous infection has healed from the person, ⁴ the priest will command the one who is cleansed to bring two live birds, clean and unblemished, along with cedar wood, a crimson thread, and hyssop. ⁵ The priest will command that one of the birds be slaughtered in a clay vessel over living water. ⁶ Then the priest will take the live bird, the cedar wood, the crimson thread, and the hyssop, and dip them along with the live bird into the blood of the bird that was slaughtered, mixed with the living water. ⁷ The priest will sprinkle this mixture on the one who is cleansed seven times and declare them clean. Then the priest will release the live bird into the open field. ⁸ The cleansed person will wash their clothes, shave off all their hair, and bathe in water. Afterward, they may return to the camp and live outside their tent for seven days. ⁹ On the seventh day, they will shave off all the hair of their head, beard, eyebrows, and any remaining hair. They will wash their clothes and bathe in water, and then they will be clean. ¹⁰ On the eighth day, they will bring two unblemished male lambs, one unblemished ewe lamb a year old, and three tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, and one log of oil. ¹¹ The priest who is cleansing will stand the cleansed person and these animals before the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ¹² The priest will take the one male lamb and offer it as a guilt offering, along with the log of oil. He will wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. ¹³ The priest will slaughter the lamb in the same place where the sin offering is slaughtered and where the burnt offering is offered, in the Holy Place, for the guilt offering is treated like the sin offering. It is most holy and belongs to the priest. ¹⁴ The priest will take some of the blood of the guilt offering and put it on the right ear lobe of the cleansed person, on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot. ¹⁵ The priest will take the oil in his left hand. ¹⁶ The priest will dip his right finger in the oil in his left hand and sprinkle it before the LORD seven times. ¹⁷ The remaining oil in the priest’s left hand will be placed on the right ear lobe of the cleansed person, on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot, on top of the blood of the guilt offering. ¹⁸ The remaining oil that was on the priest’s hand will be poured on the head of the cleansed person. The priest will then make atonement before the LORD for them. ¹⁹ The priest will offer the sin offering and make atonement for the cleansed person from their uncleanness. Then the priest will slaughter the burnt offering. ²⁰ The priest will offer the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. The priest will make atonement for them and they will be clean. ²¹ If the person is poor and unable to afford these things, they will bring one unblemished male lamb as a guilt offering, one tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and one log of oil. ²² They will also bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons, which they are able to afford. One will be a sin offering and the other a burnt offering. ²³ On the eighth day, they will bring these to the priest at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before the LORD. ²⁴ The priest will take the lamb of the guilt offering and the log of oil and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. ²⁵ The priest will slaughter the lamb of the guilt offering and take some of its blood, placing it on the right ear lobe of the cleansed person, on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot. ²⁶ The priest will take the oil and pour it into his left hand. ²⁷ He will dip his right finger in the oil in his left hand and sprinkle it before the LORD seven times. ²⁸ The priest will place some of the oil from his hand on the right ear lobe of the cleansed person, on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot, on top of the blood of the guilt offering. ²⁹ The remaining oil from the priest’s hand will be poured on the head of the cleansed person. The priest will then make atonement before the LORD for them. ³⁰ The priest will do with one of the turtledoves or young pigeons, whatever the person is able to afford, ³¹ making one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering, along with the grain offering. The priest will make atonement for the cleansed person before the LORD. ³² This is the law for the one who has a leprous infection and cannot afford the required offerings for their cleansing. ³³ The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying: ³⁴ When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving you as your possession, I will put a leprous infection in the houses of the land of your possession. ³⁵ When the owner of the house notices the infection, they will inform the priest, saying, "I have seen something like a leprous infection in my house." ³⁶ The priest will command that the house be emptied before he enters to examine it, so that nothing in the house becomes unclean. After that, the priest will enter the house to examine it. ³⁷ If the priest sees the infection on the walls of the house—pits, greenish or reddish patches, and their appearance is lower than the wall surface— ³⁸ the priest will go out to the entrance of the house and isolate the house for seven days. ³⁹ On the seventh day, the priest will return and examine the house. If the infection has spread on the walls of the house, ⁴⁰ the priest will command that the stones where the infection is found be removed and cast outside the city to an unclean place. ⁴¹ The interior of the house will be scraped out, and the dust removed and thrown outside the city to an unclean place. ⁴² Other stones will be brought and placed in place of the removed stones. Other dust will be taken and used to plaster the house. ⁴³ If the infection returns and spreads in the house after the stones are removed, after the house is scraped, and after it is replastered, ⁴⁴ the priest will enter the house and see that the infection has spread. It is a spreading leprous infection in the house. The house is unclean. ⁴⁵ The house will be torn down, including its stones, wood, and all its dust, and thrown outside the city to an unclean place. ⁴⁶ Anyone who enters the house during the time it is isolated will be unclean until evening. ⁴⁷ The person sleeping in the house will wash their clothes and will be unclean until evening. The one eating in the house will wash their clothes and will be unclean until evening. ⁴⁸ If the priest enters and sees that the infection has not spread in the house after it was replastered, the priest will declare the house clean, for the infection has healed. ⁴⁹ The priest will take two live birds, cedar wood, a crimson thread, and hyssop. ⁵⁰ The priest will slaughter one of the birds in a clay vessel over living water. ⁵¹ He will take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the crimson thread, and the live bird, and dip them into the blood of the bird that was slaughtered, along with the living water. He will sprinkle this mixture on the house seven times. ⁵² The priest will purify the house with the blood of the bird, the living water, the live bird, the cedar wood, the hyssop, and the crimson thread. ⁵³ The priest will release the live bird into the open field outside the city. He will make atonement for the house, and it will be clean. ⁵⁴ This is the law for every leprous infection and for the scab. ⁵⁵ And for the infection of garments and houses. ⁵⁶ And for the boil and the rash and the white spot. ⁵⁷ For instruction on the day of uncleanness and the day of cleansing. This is the law of the leprous infection.

Notes
- v. 22: "two turtledoves or two young pigeons" — The Hebrew uses תּוֹרִים and בְּנֵי יוֹנָה, which refer to common birds used in sacrifices.
- v. 43: "spreading leprous infection" — The translation reflects the context that the infection is recurring and spreading despite prior cleansing procedures.
- v. 56: "boil and the rash and the white spot" — These terms reflect the various forms of skin conditions outlined in the law.
