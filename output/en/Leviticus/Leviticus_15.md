# Leviticus 15

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying: ² Speak to the people of Israel and say to them: Any man among you who has a discharge from his body is unclean. ³ This is the law of his uncleanness: When the discharge flows from him, any part of his body that is covered by the discharge becomes unclean. This is his uncleanness. ⁴ Every bed on which the man with the discharge lies becomes unclean, and any object on which he sits becomes unclean. ⁵ Whoever touches the bed must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ⁶ Whoever sits on the object on which the man with the discharge has sat must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ⁷ Whoever touches the skin of the one with the discharge must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ⁸ If someone who is clean touches the one with the discharge, they must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ⁹ Any riding animal on which the man with the discharge rides becomes unclean until evening. ¹⁰ Whoever touches anything under the one with the discharge remains unclean until evening. Whoever carries those things must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ¹¹ Whoever touches anything that the one with the discharge has touched, without washing their hands, must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ¹² Any clay vessel that the one with the discharge touches must be broken, and any wooden vessel must be rinsed in water. ¹³ When the one with the discharge is cleansed, they must count seven days for their purification. On the seventh day, they must wash their clothes and bathe their body in fresh water, and they will be clean. ¹⁴ On the eighth day, they must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and give them to the priest. ¹⁵ The priest will make one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering. The priest will make atonement for the one with the discharge before the LORD, and they will be clean. ¹⁶ When a man has an ejaculation of semen, he must bathe his whole body in water and remain unclean until evening. ¹⁷ Any clothing or leather on which there is an ejaculation must be rinsed in water and remain unclean until evening. ¹⁸ When a man lies with a woman and there is an ejaculation, both must bathe in water and remain unclean until evening. ¹⁹ When a woman has a menstrual flow, her body is unclean. For seven days, she is in her period of impurity. Whoever touches her becomes unclean until evening. ²⁰ Any bed on which she lies becomes unclean, and any object on which she sits becomes unclean. ²¹ Whoever touches her bed must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ²² Whoever touches any object on which she has sat must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ²³ If the bed or the object on which she sat becomes unclean, whoever touches it remains unclean until evening. ²⁴ If a man lies with her during her impurity and the impurity is transferred to him, he becomes unclean for seven days, and any bed on which he lies becomes unclean. ²⁵ If a woman has a flow of blood beyond the time of her regular period, or if she has a flow during her regular period, all the days of the flow count as her days of impurity. ²⁶ Any bed on which she lies for all the days of her flow is unclean, and any object on which she sits is unclean, just as during her regular period. ²⁷ Whoever touches these things becomes unclean, must wash their clothes, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. ²⁸ When a woman is cleansed from her flow, she must count seven days for her purification and then be clean. ²⁹ On the eighth day, she must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting. ³⁰ The priest will make one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering, and will make atonement for her before the LORD from the uncleanness of her flow. ³¹ You must keep the people of Israel separate from their uncleanness, so that they do not die by their uncleanness while defiling my dwelling among them. ³² This is the law of the man with a discharge and the one who has an ejaculation of semen, who becomes unclean through it. ³³ This is also the law of the woman who is in her period of impurity, the man with a discharge, the male and female, and the man who lies with someone who is unclean.

---

Notes  
- v. 1: "the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron" — standard formulaic construction.  
- v. 4–12: The repeated structure of uncleanness and cleansing is preserved as in the source.  
- v. 16–18: The ejaculation of semen is treated as a source of uncleanness, similar to the discharge.  
- v. 19–27: The laws for women during their period of impurity are presented in a parallel structure to the laws for men.  
- v. 31: "You must keep the people of Israel separate from their uncleanness" — reflects the priestly concern for holiness and separation.
