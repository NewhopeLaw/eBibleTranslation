# 2 Chronicles 8

¹ After twenty years that Solomon had built the house of the LORD and his own house, ² the cities that Huram had given to Solomon, Solomon built them and settled Israelites there. ³ Solomon went to Hamath Zobah and fortified it. ⁴ He built Tadmor in the desert and all the storage cities he built in Hamath. ⁵ He built Beth-horon the Upper and Beth-horon the Lower, fortified cities, with walls, gates, and bars. ⁶ He built Baalath, and all the storage cities that belonged to Solomon, and the cities for his chariots and for his horsemen, and all the cities that Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and in all the land under his rule. ⁷ All the people left from the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not Israelites. ⁸ From their descendants, who had remained in the land and had not been completely destroyed by the Israelites, Solomon brought them as forced labor, and they are still there to this day. ⁹ But from the people of Israel, Solomon did not make any of them slaves for his work, because they were men of war, commanders, and leaders of his chariots and his horsemen. ¹⁰ These were the commanders of the men of war under King Solomon—five hundred and two hundred overseers of the people. ¹¹ Solomon brought Pharaoh’s daughter up from the city of David to the house he had built for her, for he said, “Let no queen live in the city of David, the king of Israel, because the place where the ark of the LORD has come is holy.” ¹² Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar he had built before the inner sanctuary. ¹³ He observed the regulations for daily offerings as required by Moses, for the sabbaths, new moons, and the set feasts—three times a year: on the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Booths. ¹⁴ He arranged the divisions of the priests according to the rulings of David their father, and the Levites according to their duties to serve and to sing in the presence of the priests, each according to their assigned days, and the gatekeepers according to their divisions, as David, the man of God, had commanded. ¹⁵ Solomon did not deviate from any of the commands he gave the priests and Levites in any matter or in the treasuries. ¹⁶ All the work of Solomon was completed from the day he began to the day it was finished. The house of the LORD was made whole. ¹⁷ Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and Eloth, on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom. ¹⁸ And Huram sent ships by the hands of his servants, men who knew the sea, who came with Solomon’s servants to Ophir. From there they brought four hundred and fifty talents of gold, and they delivered it to King Solomon.

---

Notes
- v. 11: The phrase “the place where the ark of the LORD has come is holy” reflects the context that the ark had been brought to the city of David (Jerusalem), now considered sacred because of its connection to the ark.
- v. 18: "Four hundred and fifty talents of gold" is a direct translation of the Hebrew measures given; no conversion or contextual adjustment is made.
