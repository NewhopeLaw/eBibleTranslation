# 2 Chronicles 20

¹ After this, the sons of Moab and the sons of Ammon, and with them some from the Meunites, came to Jehoshaphat for war. ² They came and told Jehoshaphat, "A great army has come to you from beyond the sea, from Syria, and behold, they are at Hazazon-tamar (that is, Engedi)." ³ Jehoshaphat was afraid, and he turned his face to seek the LORD and called for a fast throughout all Judah. ⁴ The people of Judah gathered to seek the LORD, and from all the cities of Judah they came to seek the LORD. ⁵ Jehoshaphat stood before the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem in the house of the LORD at the new courtyard. ⁶ He said, "O LORD, God of our ancestors, are you not the God in heaven above, and are you not the one who rules over all the kingdoms of the nations, and in whose hand is power and might, so that no one can stand against you? ⁷ Are you not our God, who drove out the inhabitants of this land before our ancestors, and gave it to the offspring of Abraham your beloved forever? ⁸ They lived in it and built for you a sanctuary in your name, saying: ⁹ 'If evil comes upon us—sword, judgment, or famine, or plague—we will stand before this house and before you, for your name is on this house. And from our distress we will cry out to you, and you will hear and save us.' ¹⁰ Now behold, the sons of Ammon and Moab and Mount Seir, to whom you did not give Israel to pass through, when they came out of the land of Egypt, for they turned aside from them and did not destroy them. ¹¹ Now behold, they are coming against us to drive us out from your inheritance that you have given us. ¹² O LORD, our God, will you not judge them? For we have no strength against this great army coming against us, and we do not know what to do. But our eyes are on you." ¹³ All Judah stood before the LORD, their children, their wives, and their households. ¹⁴ Then Jahaziel son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah the Levite, one of the sons of Asaph, had the spirit of the LORD upon him in the midst of the assembly. ¹⁵ He said, "Listen, all Judah, and you inhabitants of Jerusalem, and you, King Jehoshaphat: thus says the LORD to you, 'You need not fear or be dismayed by this great army, for the war is not yours but the LORD's. ¹⁶ Tomorrow you shall go down against them, for they are coming up by the ascent of Ziz, and you will find them at the end of the valley by the wilderness of Jeruel. ¹⁷ You need not fight this battle. Be strong, stand firm, and see the salvation of the LORD with Judah and Jerusalem. Do not fear or be dismayed. Tomorrow go out against them, and the LORD will be with you.'" ¹⁸ Then Jehoshaphat bowed with his face to the ground, and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell before the LORD to worship the LORD. ¹⁹ The Levites, from the sons of Kohath and from the sons of the Korahites, gave a great voice to praise the LORD, the God of Israel. ²⁰ At dawn they rose early and went out to the wilderness of Tekoa. And when they went out, Jehoshaphat stood and said, "Hear me, Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem. Put your trust in the LORD your God, and you will be established. Put your trust in his prophets, and you will succeed." ²¹ Then he consulted with the people and set up singers before the LORD and those who praised with cymbals, harps, and lyres. They went out in front of the vanguard, singing, "Give thanks to the LORD, for his steadfast love endures forever." ²² As they began to sing and praise, the LORD caused an ambush against the sons of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir, who had come against Judah, and they were struck down. ²³ The sons of Ammon and Moab rose against the inhabitants of Mount Seir to destroy and annihilate them. But when they finished destroying the inhabitants of Seir, they turned on one another, each attacking his neighbor, until they were all destroyed. ²⁴ Judah went up to the watchtower in the wilderness and looked toward the army. Behold, they were fallen, lying on the ground, with no one escaping. ²⁵ Then Jehoshaphat and his people went to plunder their spoils, and they found in them a great amount of goods, wealth, and precious items. They took so much plunder that they were unable to carry it, and they spent three days gathering the spoils, for there was so much. ²⁶ On the fourth day, they gathered at the Valley of Blessing, for there they blessed the LORD. That is why the name of the place is called the Valley of Blessing to this day. ²⁷ All the men of Judah and Jerusalem returned to Jerusalem, with Jehoshaphat at their head, returning to Jerusalem in joy, for the LORD had made their hearts rejoice over their enemies. ²⁸ They entered Jerusalem with lyres, harps, and trumpets into the house of the LORD. ²⁹ The fear of the LORD came upon all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the LORD had fought against the enemies of Israel. ³⁰ So the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was quiet, and his God gave him rest on every side. ³¹ Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah thirty-five years in total. He reigned in Jerusalem twenty-five years. His mother's name was Azubah daughter of Shilhi. ³² He walked in the ways of his father Asa and did not turn aside from them, doing what is right in the sight of the LORD. ³³ Yet the high places were not removed, and the people had not yet directed their hearts to the LORD, the God of their ancestors. ³⁴ The rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, the first and the last, are written in the book of Jehu the son of Hanani, which was brought into the book of the kings of Israel. ³⁵ After this, Jehoshaphat king of Judah made an alliance with Ahaziah king of Israel, who did evil. ³⁶ He joined with him to build ships to go to Tarshish, and they built ships at Ezion-geber. ³⁷ Then the prophet Eliezer son of Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, "Because you have made an alliance with Ahaziah, the LORD has shattered your work. The ships were broken, and they did not go to Tarshish."

Notes
- v. 35: "Ahaziah" is the Greek form; the Hebrew is "Achaziah" (עֲכַזְיָהוּ).
- v. 36: "Ezion-geber" is a transliteration of עֵזוֹן גָּבֶר.
- v. 37: "Eliezer son of Dodavahu of Mareshah" is a transliteration of the Hebrew names: אֱלִיעֶזֶר בֶּן דֹּדָוָהוּ מִמָּרֵשָׁה.
