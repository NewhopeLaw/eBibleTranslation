# 2 Chronicles 1

¹ Solomon son of David was strengthened over his kingship, and the LORD his God was with him, and he was raised to a high place. ² Solomon spoke to all Israel— to the commanders of thousands and hundreds, to the judges, and to all the leaders of the people of Israel, the heads of the clans— ³ and Solomon and all the assembly went up to the high place in Gibeon, for the tent of meeting of God was there, which Moses the servant of the LORD had made in the wilderness. ⁴ But the ark of God had been brought up by David from Kiriath Jearim, and David had prepared a tent for it in Jerusalem. ⁵ And the bronze altar that Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur had made was there before the tent of the LORD, and Solomon and the assembly searched for it. ⁶ Solomon went up there to the bronze altar before the LORD in the tent of meeting, and he offered upon it a thousand burnt offerings. ⁷ That night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, Ask what I should give you. ⁸ Solomon said to God, You have shown great kindness to David my father, and you have made me king in his place. ⁹ Now, LORD God, confirm your promise to David my father, for you have made me ruler over a great people, as numerous as the dust of the earth. ¹⁰ Now give me wisdom and discernment, that I may go out before this people and may govern them, for who can judge this great people of yours? ¹¹ God said to Solomon, Because this was in your heart, and you did not ask for wealth, possessions, or the life of your enemies, nor did you ask for many days, but you asked for wisdom and discernment to govern my people over whom I have made you ruler— ¹² wisdom and discernment I give you, and wealth and possessions and honor such as no king before you or after you will have. ¹³ Solomon went to the high place in Gibeon that is in Jerusalem, before the tent of meeting, and he reigned over Israel. ¹⁴ Solomon gathered chariots and horses, and he had a thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horses, and he stationed them in the cities for chariots and with the king in Jerusalem. ¹⁵ The king set silver and gold in Jerusalem as if they were stones, and he set the cedars as if they were sycamores in the lowlands in great abundance. ¹⁶ The procurement of horses for Solomon was from Egypt, and the merchants of the king bought them. ¹⁷ They went out and brought the chariots from Egypt for six hundred silver shekels each, and the horses for a hundred and fifty each; and thus all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram bought them. ¹⁸ Solomon said, I will build a house for the name of the LORD and a house for my kingship.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "was strengthened over his kingship" reflects the nuance of וַיִּתְחַזֵּק as "strengthened himself over," indicating an assertion of authority.
- v. 13: "the high place in Gibeon that is in Jerusalem" is a literal rendering of the Hebrew, which may refer to a location or a textual anomaly. The Septuagint reads differently, but the Hebrew is followed here.
- v. 17: "a hundred and fifty each" is a literal translation of "and fifty and a hundred," which appears to be a reversed word order in the Hebrew.
