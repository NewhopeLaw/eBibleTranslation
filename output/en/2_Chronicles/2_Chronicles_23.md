# 2 Chronicles 23

¹ In the seventh year, Jehoida strengthened himself and took the commanders of hundreds, Azariah son of Jehoram and Ishmael son of Jehohanan and Azariah son of Obed and Masaias son of Adaias and Elisaphat son of Zicri, with him, in covenant. ² They went throughout Judah, and gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah and the heads of the fathers of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem. ³ The whole assembly made a covenant with the priest at the house of the Lord with the king, and said to them, "Behold, the son of the king will reign, as the Lord has spoken concerning the sons of David." ⁴ "This is what you shall do: a third of you, on the sabbath, shall be with the priests and the Levites at the gates. ⁵ A third shall be in the king's house, and a third at the gate of the foundation. And all the people shall be in the courtyards of the house of the Lord. ⁶ "Let not anyone enter the house of the Lord, except the priests and the Levitical attendants, for they alone are holy. Let all the people keep the charge of the Lord." ⁷ "Let the Levites surround the king on every side, each with his weapon in hand. And whoever comes into the house shall be put to death. Let them be with the king when he comes and when he goes out." ⁸ The Levites and all Judah did according to all that the priest Jehoida had commanded. Each one took his men, from the beginning of the sabbath to the going out of the sabbath, for Jehoida had not released the divisions. ⁹ The priest Jehoida gave to the commanders of hundreds the spears and the shields and the weapons that had belonged to King David, which were in the house of the Lord. ¹⁰ He set all the people, each one with his weapon in hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, to the altar and to the house, around the king, in a circle. ¹¹ They brought out the son of the king and placed on him the crown and the signet, and they anointed him. Jehoida and his sons declared, "Long live the king!" ¹² When Atalyah heard the sound of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people at the house of the Lord. ¹³ She saw, and behold, the king was standing by the pillar at the entrance, and the commanders and the trumpets were with the king, and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets, and the singers were singing with musical instruments and praising. Atalyah tore her robes and cried out, "Treason! Treason!" ¹⁴ The priest Jehoida sent the commanders of hundreds and the captains of the army, and said to them, "Take her out of the temple, and whoever follows her, let him be put to death by the sword. For the priest said, 'Let her not be put to death in the house of the Lord.'" ¹⁵ So they laid hands on her, and she went to the entrance of the gate of the horses of the king's house, and they put her to death there. ¹⁶ Jehoida made a covenant between himself and the people and the king to be a people to the Lord. ¹⁷ All the people of the land went to the house of Baal, and they tore it down, and its altars and its images they broke. They put to death Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars. ¹⁸ Jehoida the priest gave the duties of the house of the Lord into the hands of the priests and Levites, whom David had apportioned over the house of the Lord, to offer burnt offerings to the Lord, as it is written in the law of Moses, with joy and with singing, by the hand of David. ¹⁹ He set the gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the Lord, so that no one unclean in any matter might enter. ²⁰ He took the commanders of hundreds, the nobles, and the rulers of the people, and all the people of the land, and brought down the king from the house of the Lord, and they went through the upper gate into the king's house, and they seated the king on the throne of the kingdom. ²¹ All the people of the land and the city rejoiced, and the city was quiet. They put Atalyah to death with the sword.

Notes
- v. 13: "Treason! Treason!" is a translation of the Hebrew קֶשֶׁר קָֽשֶׁר, literally "seize, seize." This rendering reflects the Septuagint's "Epitithemenoi epitithesthe," which is a cry of alarm or call to action.
- v. 14: The Hebrew uses the phrase "the priest said" to clarify that the restriction on killing Atalyah in the temple was given by Jehoida. This is made explicit in the English translation.
