# 2 Chronicles 5

¹ When all the work that Solomon had done for the house of the LORD was completed, Solomon brought the holy things of David his father and the silver and gold, and all the vessels, and placed them in the storehouses of the house of God. ² Solomon then summoned the elders of Israel and all the heads of the tribes, the leaders of the fathers of the children of Israel, to Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the city of David, that is, Zion. ³ All the men of Israel assembled with the king during the feast, which was the seventh month. ⁴ And all the elders of Israel came. And the Levites bore the ark. ⁵ They brought up the ark and the tent of meeting and all the holy vessels that were in the tent, and the Levitical priests bore them up. ⁶ King Solomon and all the assembly of Israel who had gathered with him, stood before the ark, offering sheep and oxen in such great numbers that they could not be counted or numbered for their abundance. ⁷ The priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the holy of holies, beneath the wings of the cherubim. ⁸ The cherubim spread out their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered the ark and its poles from above. ⁹ The poles extended, and their heads were visible from the holy of holies toward the inner sanctuary, but they could not be seen from outside. They remain there to this day. ¹⁰ Nothing was in the ark except the two tablets that Moses placed in Horeb, the ones the LORD made with the children of Israel when they came out from Egypt. ¹¹ When the priests came out from the holy place, for all the priests present had been sanctified, there was no order of service by divisions. ¹² The Levitical singers, all the sons of Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their brothers and their relatives, clothed in fine linen, with cymbals, lyres, and harps, stood east of the altar, and with them were one hundred and twenty priests sounding the trumpets. ¹³ The priests and the singers made one voice to give a unified sound to praise and thank the LORD, lifting a great voice with trumpets, cymbals, and musical instruments, declaring, “Praise the LORD, for He is good! His steadfast love endures forever!” And the house was filled with the cloud, the glory of the LORD. ¹⁴ The priests could not stand to serve because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD had filled the house of God.

Notes
- v. 10: "Horeb" is used as a generic location reference for the mountain where Moses received the tablets, rather than "Sinai," which is the more common name. This follows the source text's reference to "Horeb" (חֹרֵב).
