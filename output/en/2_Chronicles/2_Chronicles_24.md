# 2 Chronicles 24

¹ When Yoash was seven years old, he began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Tzivyah from Beer Sheva. ² Yoash did what was right in the eyes of the Lord all the days of Yehoyada the priest. ³ Yehoyada took for him two wives, and they bore him sons and daughters. ⁴ Later, there came to Yoash's heart the desire to restore the house of the Lord. ⁵ He gathered the priests and Levites and said to them, "Go out into the cities of Judah and collect from all Israel silver to strengthen the house of your God, year after year. And you must act quickly in the matter, but the Levites were slow to act." ⁶ The king summoned Yehoyada the leader and said to him, "Why have you not made the Levites bring the contribution from Judah and Jerusalem, the contribution of Moses the servant of the Lord, and the congregation of Israel, to the tent of meeting?" ⁷ For Athalyah, the lawless one, and her sons had broken into the house of God and had turned the holy things of the house of the Lord into Baal. ⁸ The king said, "Let a chest be made and placed at the gate of the house of the Lord outside." ⁹ They proclaimed in Judah and Jerusalem to bring to the Lord the contribution of Moses the servant of the God of Israel in the wilderness. ¹⁰ All the leaders and the people rejoiced and brought and cast into the chest until it was full. ¹¹ When the chest was being brought to the officials of the king at the hands of the Levites, and they saw that the silver was abundant, the king's scribe and the chief priest's officer came and emptied the chest and returned it to its place. They did this day by day, and they gathered much silver. ¹² The king and Yehoyada gave it to those doing the work of the service of the house of the Lord. They hired stonecutters and carpenters to restore the house of the Lord and also hired metalworkers in iron and copper to strengthen the house of the Lord. ¹³ The workers did the work, and the length of the work in their hands was great. They raised up the house of the Lord on its foundation and made it strong. ¹⁴ When they finished, they brought what was left of the silver to the king and Yehoyada, and they made vessels for the house of the Lord—vessels for service, burnt offerings, and bowls of gold and silver. They offered continual burnt offerings in the house of the Lord during the days of Yehoyada. ¹⁵ Yehoyada grew old and fulfilled his days and died at the age of a hundred and thirty when he died. ¹⁶ They buried him in the city of David with the kings, for he had done good in Israel and with God and his house. ¹⁷ After the death of Yehoyada, the leaders of Judah came and paid homage to the king. Then the king listened to them. ¹⁸ They abandoned the house of the Lord, the God of their ancestors, and served the Asherim and the idols. Thus, the anger of the Lord burned against Judah and Jerusalem because of this sin. ¹⁹ He sent prophets to them to turn them back to the Lord, but they testified to them and they did not listen. ²⁰ The spirit of God clothed Zechariah son of Yehoyada the priest, and he stood above the people and said to them, "Thus says the God, 'Why are you transgressing the commandments of the Lord? And you will not prosper, because you have abandoned the Lord, and He will abandon you.'" ²¹ They fastened him and stoned him at the command of the king in the courtyard of the house of the Lord. ²² Yet Yoash the king did not remember the kindness that Yehoyada his father had shown him, and he killed his son. And when he died, he said, "Let the Lord see and avenge!" ²³ It came to pass in the time of the year that the army of Aram came upon him and entered Judah and Jerusalem, and they destroyed all the leaders of the people from among the people, and they sent all their spoil to the king of Damascus. ²⁴ For in their distress, the army of Aram came, and the Lord gave them into their hands, very much, because they had abandoned the Lord, the God of their ancestors, and Yoash had made judgments. ²⁵ When they withdrew from him because he had abandoned them in great abundance, his servants fastened him with the blood of the sons of Yehoyada the priest, and they killed him on his bed and he died. They buried him in the city of David, but they did not bury him in the tombs of the kings. ²⁶ Those who fastened him were Zabad son of Shim'at the Ammonite and Yehozabad son of Shim'rit the Moabite. ²⁷ And his sons, Yir'eh the burden-bearer and Yisov the temple builder, are written in the records of the Book of Kings. And Amatsiyahu his son reigned in his place.

---

Notes
- v. 20: "The spirit of God clothed" — likely a metaphor for divine inspiration, rather than literal clothing.
- v. 22: "Yet Yoash the king did not remember the kindness..." — "did not remember" here is used metaphorically to indicate a failure to reciprocate or honor the kindness shown.
- v. 24: "Because they had abandoned the Lord... and Yoash had made judgments" — the connection between the abandonment of God and Yoash's judgments is interpretive but clear from context.
