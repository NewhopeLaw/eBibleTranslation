# 2 Chronicles 17

¹ Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place. Jehoshaphat strengthened himself over Israel. ² He stationed military forces in all the fortified cities of Judah and set up garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured. ³ The LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the ways of David his father at the beginning and did not seek the Baals. ⁴ For he sought the God of his father and followed His commandments, not walking in the ways of Israel. ⁵ The LORD established the kingdom in his hand, and all Judah gave tribute to Jehoshaphat. He had great riches and honor in abundance. ⁶ His heart was lifted up in the ways of the LORD. Moreover, he removed the high places and the Asherah poles from Judah. ⁷ In the third year of his reign, he sent his officials Benjamin, and Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Michaiah, ⁸ with them the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jonathan, and Adonijah, and Tobijah, Levites, with them were also the priests Eliashib and Jehoram. ⁹ They went throughout Judah and with them the book of the Teaching of the LORD. They traveled to every city in Judah and taught the people. ¹⁰ And the fear of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands surrounding Judah, so that they did not make war against Jehoshaphat. ¹¹ The Philistines brought Jehoshaphat tribute and silver in abundance, and the Arabians brought him flocks of seven thousand rams and seven thousand ramskins. ¹² Jehoshaphat went on and grew stronger, until he reached great heights. He built fortified cities in Judah and walled cities. ¹³ He had many works in the land of Judah and mighty warriors in Jerusalem. ¹⁴ This was the muster of them by their ancestral houses: in Judah, the commanders of thousands. Adnah was the commander, with him were three hundred thousand mighty warriors. ¹⁵ And with him was Jehonathan, the commander, with him were two hundred and eighty thousand. ¹⁶ And with him was Amasai son of Zicri, who gave himself willingly to the LORD, with him were two hundred thousand mighty warriors. ¹⁷ And from Benjamin, the mighty warrior Eliada, with him were two hundred thousand who handled bow and shield. ¹⁸ And with him was Jehozabad, with him were one hundred and eighty thousand expert warriors. ¹⁹ These were the men serving the king, in addition to those the king had stationed in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.

Notes
- v. 1: "strengthened himself over Israel" — the Hebrew "וַיִּתְחַזֵּק עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵֽל" is rendered as a metaphor for gaining influence or superiority.
- v. 7: Names of officials differ slightly between the Masoretic text and other witnesses, but the Hebrew here is clear.
- v. 11: "ramskins" — the Hebrew "תְּיָשִׁים" is best rendered as ramskins, a type of leather used in ancient times.
- v. 14–19: The numerical lists are retained as in the source text. The numbers are likely symbolic or approximate.
