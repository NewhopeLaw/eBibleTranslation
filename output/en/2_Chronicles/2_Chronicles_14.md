# 2 Chronicles 14

¹ Asa did what was good and upright in the eyes of the LORD his God. ² He removed the foreign altars and the high places, and he broke down the pillars and cut down the Asherim. ³ He told Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their ancestors, and to keep the Teaching and the commandments. ⁴ He removed the high places and the altars of incense from all the cities of Judah, and the kingdom had peace before him. ⁵ He built fortified cities in Judah, for the land was at rest, and there was no war in those years, because the LORD had given him rest. ⁶ He said to Judah, "Let us build these cities, and let us make walls and towers, gates and bars. While the land is still before us, since we have sought the LORD our God, we have sought him, and he has given us rest on every side. So they built and succeeded." ⁷ Asa had an army of thirty thousand men bearing shields and spears from Judah, and two hundred thousand men of Benjamin bearing shields and wielding bows. All these were mighty warriors. ⁸ Then Zerah the Cushite came out against them with an army of a million men and three hundred chariots, and he came to Mareshah. ⁹ Asa went out to meet him, and they arranged battle in the Valley of Zephathah at Mareshah. ¹⁰ Asa cried out to the LORD his God and said, "O LORD, there is none like you to help between many and those without strength. O LORD, God of our ancestors, help us, for you are our refuge, and in your name we have come against this mighty horde. O LORD, our God, do not let a man prevail against you." ¹¹ The LORD struck the Cushites before Asa and Judah, and the Cushites fled. ¹² Asa and his people pursued them to Gerar, and so many of the Cushites fell that they had no strength to recover. For they were broken before the LORD and before his forces. They carried off a very great spoil. ¹³ They struck all the surrounding villages of Gerar, for the fear of the LORD had fallen upon them, and they stripped all the cities, for they took great spoil from them. ¹⁴ They also struck the enclosures of livestock and captured many flocks, camels and donkeys, and they returned to Jerusalem.

---

Notes
- v. 6: The line "while the land is still before us" reflects the Hebrew "כִּי־הָיָה פַֽחַד־יְהוָה עֲלֵיהֶם" in context, which here refers to the land being available for conquest or settlement.
