# 2 Chronicles 28

¹ Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD, as David his father had done. ² He walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, and even made molten images for the Baals. ³ He burned sacrifices in the Valley of Ben Hinnom and burned his children in fire, following the abominations of the nations whom the LORD had driven out before the children of Israel. ⁴ He offered sacrifices and burned incense on the high places, on the hills, and under every green tree. ⁵ Then the LORD his God delivered him into the hand of the king of Aram, and they struck him and took from him a great many captives, and brought them to Damascus. He was also delivered into the hand of the king of Israel, who struck him with a great blow. ⁶ Pekah the son of Remaliah struck down in Judah one hundred and twenty thousand in a single day—all men of valor—because they had abandoned the LORD God of their ancestors. ⁷ Zechariah the mighty of Ephraim struck down Maaseiah the king’s son and Azrikam the ruler of the house, and Elkanah the second to the king. ⁸ The children of Israel carried off two hundred thousand women, boys, and girls from their brothers, and also took a great amount of plunder from them, and brought the plunder to Samaria. ⁹ There was a prophet of the LORD there, whose name was Oded. He went out to meet the army that came to Samaria and said to them, "Behold, because the anger of the LORD God of your ancestors is on Judah, He has given them into your hands, and you have killed them in rage so great that it has reached the heavens. ¹⁰ And now, you sons of Judah and Jerusalem say, 'We will carry off as slaves and concubines those from our brothers.' Do you not know that you yourselves are guilty before the LORD your God? ¹¹ Now then, listen to me and return the captives you have taken from your brothers, for the anger of the LORD is upon you." ¹² Then some men from the leaders of the children of Ephraim—Azariah the son of Jehohanan, Berechiah the son of Meshillemoth, Jechizkiah the son of Shaul, and Amasa the son of Hadlai—went out to meet the returning army. ¹³ They said to them, "Do not bring the captives here, for you are saying to us, 'We will add to our sins and to our guilt, for our guilt is already great and the anger of the LORD is on Israel.'" ¹⁴ Then the returning soldiers abandoned the captives and the plunder before the leaders and all the congregation. ¹⁵ Then the men who had been set apart by name took up the captives. They clothed all who were naked from the plunder, gave them clothes, and put shoes on them, gave them food to eat and drink, and covered them, and led them with donkeys to every weak person. They brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their brothers, and they returned to Samaria. ¹⁶ At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria to help him. ¹⁷ Moreover, the Edomites came and struck Judah and took captives. ¹⁸ The Philistines also spread out into the lowlands and the Negev of Judah and captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Socoh and its villages, Timnah and its villages, and Gimzo and its villages, and they lived there. ¹⁹ For the LORD had brought Judah low because of Ahaz, the king of Israel, because he had abandoned the LORD God of his ancestors. ²⁰ Then Tilgath-pilneser the king of Assyria came against him, and he pressed hard on him, but did not strengthen him. ²¹ For Ahaz had taken the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king's house and the treasures of the leaders and gave them to the king of Assyria, but it did not bring him help. ²² At the time of his distress, he added to his guilt before the LORD, who is the king, Ahaz. ²³ He burned sacrifices to the gods of Damascus who had struck him, and said, "For the gods of the kings of Aram help them, so I will burn sacrifices to them and they will help me." But they became a snare to him and to all Israel. ²⁴ Ahaz gathered together the furnishings of the house of God and cut up the furnishings of the house of God, and closed the doors of the house of the LORD, and made for himself altars in every corner of Jerusalem. ²⁵ In every city and city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, and so he provoked the anger of the LORD God of his ancestors. ²⁶ Now the rest of his acts and all his ways, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. ²⁷ Then Ahaz lay down with his ancestors and was buried in the city of David, because they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel. And his son Hezekiah reigned in his place.

Notes
- v. 1: "sixteen years" — the Hebrew text says "six and ten" (שֵׁשׁ־עֶשְׂרֵה), which means sixteen.
- v. 10: "you yourselves are guilty" — the Hebrew uses a metaphor meaning "you are the ones who are the evidence" or "you are the ones who bear the guilt."
- v. 11: "return the captives" — the term שִׁבְיָה is used for captives taken in war.
- v. 13: "you are saying to us" — the plural form indicates a group addressing the army.
- v. 21: "but it did not bring him help" — the Hebrew uses the phrase "but not for help to him."
- v. 23: "gods of Damascus" — the Hebrew refers to the gods of Aram, using Damascus as a synecdoche for the region.
