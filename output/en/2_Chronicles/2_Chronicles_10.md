# 2 Chronicles 10

¹ Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all Israel had come to make him king. ² When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard this, while he was still in Egypt (he had fled from Solomon the king and had been living in Egypt), Jeroboam returned from Egypt. ³ They sent and called him, and Jeroboam and all Israel came and spoke to Rehoboam, saying: ⁴ "Your father made our burden heavy, and now lighten the hard labor and the heavy yoke your father placed on us, and we will serve you." ⁵ Rehoboam said to them, "Come back to me in three days, and I will give you an answer." Then the people went away. ⁶ The king consulted with the elders who had stood before Solomon his father while he was still alive, asking, "What advice would you give me to answer this people?" ⁷ They said to him, "If you are kind to this people and please them, and speak good words to them, they will be your servants all their days." ⁸ But the king ignored the advice of the elders and consulted with the young men who had grown up with him and stood before him. ⁹ He said to them, "What advice would you give me to answer this people who have spoken to me, saying, 'Lighten the yoke your father placed on us'?" ¹⁰ The young men who had grown up with him said to him, "Say to the people, 'My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist. ¹¹ My father placed a heavy yoke on you, and I will add to it; my father disciplined you with whips, and I will discipline you with scorpions.'" ¹² So Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam on the third day, as the king had told them, saying, "Come back to me on the third day." ¹³ The king answered them harshly, and Rehoboam ignored the advice of the elders. ¹⁴ Instead, he spoke to them according to the advice of the young men, saying, "My father made your yoke heavy, and I will add to it; my father disciplined you with whips, and I will discipline you with scorpions." ¹⁵ The king did not listen to the people, for this had come about as a judgment from God to fulfill the word the Lord had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat. ¹⁶ All Israel, when the king refused to listen to them, responded to the king, saying, "What share do we have in David? What inheritance do we have in the son of Jesse? Go to your tents, Israel! Look to your house, David!" And all Israel went to their tents. ¹⁷ But the Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah continued to reign under Rehoboam. ¹⁸ King Rehoboam sent Adoram, who was in charge of the corvée, and the Israelites stoned him to death. King Rehoboam hurried to get into his chariot to flee to Jerusalem. ¹⁹ So Israel has rebelled against the house of David to this day.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist" — a metaphor for Rehoboam’s arrogance; the Hebrew is rendered literally as per the source.
- v. 15: "this had come about as a judgment from God" — the Hebrew implies that the people's rejection of Rehoboam was part of a divine judgment, as foretold to Jeroboam.
- v. 16: "Go to your tents, Israel!" — the Hebrew uses the phrase "to your tents" (לְאֹהָלֶיךָ) to convey the idea of returning to one’s own place or homeland.
