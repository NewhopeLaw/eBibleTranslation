# 2 Chronicles 29

¹ Hezekiah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Abijah, the daughter of Zechariah. ² He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as David his father had done. ³ In the first year of his reign, in the first month, he opened the doors of the house of the LORD and restored them. ⁴ He gathered the priests and the Levites and assembled them in the east courtyard. ⁵ He said to the Levites, "Listen to me, Levites! Now purify yourselves, and purify the house of the LORD, the God of your ancestors, and remove the uncleanness from the holy place. ⁶ For our ancestors have done evil in the eyes of the LORD our God. They have abandoned Him and turned their faces away from the dwelling place of the LORD and given Him the back. ⁷ They shut the doors of the inner sanctuary, they extinguished the lamps, and they did not burn incense or offer burnt offerings in the holy place to the God of Israel. ⁸ So the anger of the LORD fell on Judah and Jerusalem, and He gave them to be mocked, to be a byword, and to be cursed, as you can see with your own eyes. ⁹ Indeed, our ancestors have fallen by the sword, and our sons, daughters, and wives have been taken into captivity because of this. ¹⁰ Now I have a strong desire in my heart to make a covenant with the LORD, the God of Israel, so that His anger may turn away from us. ¹¹ My children, do not be indifferent, for He has chosen you to stand before Him, to serve Him and to be His ministers, offering incense." ¹² So the Levites: Mattathiah the son of Amasai, and Joel the son of Azariah from the descendants of Kohath; and from the descendants of Merari: Kish the son of Abdi, and Azariah the son of Jehallel; and from the descendants of Gershon: Jaaziah the son of Zimri, and Eden the son of Jaaziah; ¹³ and from the descendants of Elizaphan: Shemiramoth and Jeiel; and from the descendants of Asaph: Zechariah and Mattaniah; ¹⁴ and from the descendants of Heman: Jehiah and Shimei; and from the descendants of Jeduthun: Shemaiah and Uzziel. ¹⁵ They gathered their brothers, sanctified themselves, and went in according to the command of the king by the word of the LORD to purify the house of the LORD. ¹⁶ The priests went into the inner part of the house of the LORD to purify it. They removed all the uncleanness they found in the temple of the LORD and brought it out to the court of the house of the LORD, where the Levites took it outside to the Kidron Valley. ¹⁷ They began purification on the first day of the first month, and on the eighth day of the month they entered the inner sanctuary of the LORD to purify the house of the LORD for eight days, completing the work on the sixteenth day of the first month. ¹⁸ They went in to King Hezekiah and said, "We have purified the entire house of the LORD, the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels, the table of the bread of the Presence and all its vessels. ¹⁹ We have restored all the vessels that King Ahaz had removed in his reign when he did evil. We have cleansed them and placed them before the altar of the LORD." ²⁰ Then Hezekiah rose early and gathered the leaders of the city and went up to the house of the LORD. ²¹ They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven male lambs, and seven male goats for a sin offering, for the kingdom, for the sanctuary, and for Judah. He said to the sons of Aaron the priests to offer them on the altar of the LORD. ²² The priests slaughtered the bulls, and the priests received the blood and poured it out at the base of the altar. The rams were slaughtered, and their blood was poured out at the base of the altar. The male lambs were slaughtered, and their blood was poured out at the base of the altar. ²³ They brought the male goats for the sin offering before the king and the assembly, and the people laid their hands on them. ²⁴ The priests slaughtered them, and the priests sprinkled their blood on the altar to make atonement for all Israel, for the king had said, "The burnt offering and the sin offering are for all Israel." ²⁵ He placed the Levites in the house of the LORD with cymbals, with stringed instruments, and with harps, according to the command of David, and of Gad the seer, and of Nathan the prophet, for the command of the LORD was by the hand of His prophets. ²⁶ The Levites stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets. ²⁷ Hezekiah commanded to raise the burnt offering on the altar, and when the burnt offering began, the song of the LORD began, accompanied by the trumpets and by the instruments of King David of Israel. ²⁸ The entire assembly worshiped, the singers sang, and the trumpeters played, until the burnt offering was completed. ²⁹ When the burnt offering had been completed, the king and all who were present bowed and worshiped. ³⁰ Hezekiah and the leaders told the Levites to praise the LORD with the words of David and of Asaph the seer. They praised the LORD with joy, and they bowed and worshiped. ³¹ Hezekiah said in reply, "Now you have filled your hands for the LORD. Bring near and bring offerings and thanksgiving to the house of the LORD." The assembly brought offerings and thanksgiving, and all who were of generous heart brought burnt offerings. ³² The number of the burnt offerings brought by the assembly was seventy bulls, a hundred rams, and two hundred male lambs—all of these were burnt offerings to the LORD. ³³ The consecrated animals were six hundred bulls and three thousand sheep. ³⁴ But the priests were few and could not skin all the burnt offerings quickly enough. The Levites helped them until the work was completed and until the priests were cleansed, for the Levites had been eager to assist the priests. ³⁵ The burnt offering was abundant, including the fat of the peace offerings and the drink offerings for the burnt offerings. The service of the house of the LORD was completed. ³⁶ Hezekiah and all the people were glad about the work the LORD had prepared for the people, because it had happened suddenly.

Notes
- v. 1: "Abijah" is a transliteration of the Hebrew name אֲבִיָּה.
- v. 19: The phrase "had removed in his reign when he did evil" is a contextual interpretation of the Hebrew, reflecting Ahaz's actions under divine disfavor.
- v. 21: The phrase "sin offering, for the kingdom, for the sanctuary, and for Judah" is a rendering of the Hebrew, emphasizing the scope of atonement.
- v. 24: "The burnt offering and the sin offering are for all Israel" is a direct translation of the king's statement, highlighting the collective nature of the sacrifices.
- v. 30: "Praise the LORD with the words of David and of Asaph the seer" is a contextual rendering to reflect the Levitical role in worship.
