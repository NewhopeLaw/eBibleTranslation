# 2 Chronicles 15

¹ Azariah son of Oded had the spirit of God upon him. ² He went out before Asa and said to him, "Listen, Asa and all Judah and Benjamin: The LORD is with you when you are with him, and if you seek him, he will be found by you. But if you abandon him, he will abandon you." ³ For many days Israel has had no true God, no priest who teaches, and no teaching. ⁴ But if they return to the LORD, the God of Israel, and seek him, they will find him. ⁵ At that time there is no peace for the one going in or the one coming out, for great commotions are upon all the inhabitants of the lands. ⁶ Nation will war against nation, and city against city, because the gods have stirred up trouble in every distress. ⁷ But you—be strong and do not let your hands fall, for there is a reward for your work." ⁸ When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of the prophet Oded, he grew strong and expelled the abominations from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities that he had taken in the hill country of Ephraim, and he restored the altar of the LORD that was before the porch of the LORD. ⁹ He gathered all Judah and Benjamin and the foreigners who lived with them from Ephraim and Manasseh and Simeon, for many from Israel had been drawn to him when they saw that the LORD, his God, was with him. ¹⁰ They assembled in Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of Asa’s reign. ¹¹ On that day they offered to the LORD from the plunder bulls seven hundred and rams seven thousand. ¹² They entered into a covenant to seek the LORD, the God of their ancestors, with all their heart and with all their soul. ¹³ Anyone who did not seek the LORD, the God of Israel, was to be put to death, from the least to the greatest, whether man or woman. ¹⁴ They swore a great oath to the LORD, with loud voices and with trumpets, shofars, and cymbals. ¹⁵ All Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they had sworn with all their heart and with all their will, and they had sought him, and he had been found by them. The LORD gave them rest on every side. ¹⁶ Also, Maacah the mother of King Asa removed her from the position of queen mother, because she had made an Asherah pole for herself. Asa cut down the Asherah pole, and he burned it in the Wadi Kidron. ¹⁷ Yet the high places were not removed from Israel. However, Asa’s heart was at peace throughout his days. ¹⁸ He brought the sacred vessels of his father David and his own sacred things, silver and gold and utensils, to the house of God. ¹⁹ There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa’s reign.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "the prophecy of the prophet Oded" — the Hebrew reads "the prophecy of Oded the prophet," but "Oded the prophet" is a title, not a name. I have rendered it as "the prophecy of the prophet Oded" to preserve the structure.
- v. 16: "the position of queen mother" — the Hebrew uses "g’birah," a term typically rendered as "queen mother," which is a conventional title.
- v. 17: "Asa’s heart was at peace" — the Hebrew phrase "leb Asa hayah shalem" is idiomatic and conveys a state of contentment or inner peace.
