# 2 Chronicles 31

¹ After all this, all the Israelites who were found in the cities of Judah tore down the pillars, cut down the sacred trees, and broke down the high places and the altars from all Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh, as far as the Negev. All the Israelites returned to their inheritance and their cities. ² Hezekiah stationed the divisions of the priests and the Levites in their divisions, each according to their duties, for the priests and Levites for burnt offerings and for peace offerings, to serve and to give thanks and to praise at the gates of the encampments of the LORD. ³ He provided from his own possessions the contribution for burnt offerings—the morning and evening burnt offerings, the burnt offerings for the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the appointed feasts, as written in the law of the LORD. ⁴ He instructed the people living in Jerusalem to give the portion for the priests and the Levites, so that they might be strong in the service of the LORD. ⁵ As soon as the word went out, the people of Israel brought in abundance the firstfruits of grain, wine, and oil, and honey, and all the produce of the field. They brought in the tithes in great measure. ⁶ The people of Israel and Judah who lived in the cities of Judah also brought in tithes of cattle and sheep, and tithes of the holy things consecrated to the LORD their God. They made great heaps of offerings. ⁷ In the third month they began to lay up the offerings in heaps, and in the seventh month they completed them. ⁸ When Hezekiah and the leaders came and saw the heaps, they blessed the LORD and His people Israel. ⁹ Hezekiah questioned the priests and the Levites about the heaps. ¹⁰ Then Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, said to him, "Since the offering began to be brought to the house of the LORD, we have eaten and are satisfied, and there is plenty left. For the LORD has blessed His people, and there is much left over." ¹¹ Hezekiah ordered them to prepare storehouses in the house of the LORD, and they did so. ¹² They brought in the tithes and the firstfruits and the holy things in faithfulness, and over them was appointed Conaniah the Levite and Shimei his brother as overseers. ¹³ Jedediah, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachereth, Mahath, and Benaiah were appointed as officials under the hands of Conaniah and Shimei his brother, by command of Hezekiah the king and Azariah the ruler of the house of God. ¹⁴ Kore the son of Imnah the Levite, who was in charge at the east gate for the freewill offerings of God, gave out the portions to the people. ¹⁵ Under his hand were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah—Levites in the cities of the priests—in faithfulness, distributing to their brothers by divisions, the great and the small. ¹⁶ In addition, they were responsible for counting all the males from the age of three years and upward, everyone who came into the house of the LORD, day by day, according to their service in their divisions. ¹⁷ The Levites were responsible for counting the priests by their families and the Levites from the age of twenty years and upward, in their duties in their divisions. ¹⁸ They also were responsible for counting every child, along with their wives, sons, and daughters, for the whole assembly, for they were consecrated in faithfulness. ¹⁹ For the sons of Aaron the priests who lived in their cities in every town and city had men named by name to give portions to every male among the priests and to each one counted among the Levites. ²⁰ Hezekiah did this in all Judah, and did what was good, upright, and true before the LORD his God. ²¹ And in every undertaking Hezekiah began in the service of the house of God and in the law and the commandments, he sought his God with all his heart, and he did it and succeeded.

Notes
- v. 14: "freewill offerings of God" — the term used here for "freewill offerings" (נְדָבֹת) is a standard term in Hebrew for voluntary gifts, often given to the temple.
- v. 19: "sons of Aaron the priests" — the phrase is a standard designation for the priestly lineage.
- v. 20: "did what was good, upright, and true" — the Hebrew uses three adjectives (טּוֹב, יָשָׁר, אֱמֶת) to emphasize moral and spiritual integrity, which are rendered here to reflect their distinct shades of meaning.
- v. 21: "with all his heart" — the Hebrew phrase לְכָל־לְבָבוֹ is a common expression for wholehearted devotion.
