# 2 Chronicles 25

¹ Amatsiyahu was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Yeho'adah from Jerusalem. ² He did what was right in the eyes of the Lord, though not with a whole heart. ³ When the kingdom was firmly established in his hand, he executed the servants who had struck down his father the king. ⁴ He did not kill their children, in accordance with what is written in the Book of Moses, as the Lord had commanded: "Parents must not be put to death for their children, and children must not be put to death for their parents. A person must be put to death only for their own sin." ⁵ Amatsiyahu gathered Judah and organized them by ancestral houses, appointing commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds for all Judah and Benjamin. He registered them from twenty years old and above, and he found three hundred thousand capable young men fit for battle, each holding a sword and shield. ⁶ From Israel he hired a hundred thousand brave warriors for a hundred talents of silver. ⁷ A man of God came to him and said, "King, do not let the army of Israel go with you, for the Lord is not with Israel, not with all the descendants of Ephraim." ⁸ If you insist on going, do so, but the Lord will not give you victory over your enemies. For God is capable of helping and of casting down." ⁹ Amatsiyahu said to the man of God, "But what shall I do with the hundred talents of silver I have already paid to the Israelite army?" And the man of God answered, "The Lord has more than enough to give you in addition to this." ¹⁰ Amatsiyahu dismissed the Israelite army who had come to him, and they were very angry at Judah. They returned to their homes in sullen rage. ¹¹ Amatsiyahu strengthened himself and led his people, and they went to the Valley of Salt, where he defeated the people of Seir, killing ten thousand of them. ¹² The people of Judah captured ten thousand of the slain and brought them to the top of the cliff, and they threw them down from the cliff. All of them were dashed to pieces. ¹³ The men in the army that Amatsiyahu had sent away, who had not gone with him to battle, raided the cities of Judah, from Samaria to Beth Horon, and they struck down three thousand people, and they carried off great spoil. ¹⁴ After Amatsiyahu returned from defeating the Edomites, he brought the gods of the people of Seir, and set them up for himself as gods, and before them he bowed and to them he burned incense. ¹⁵ The anger of the Lord burned against Amatsiyahu, and He sent prophets to him, who said, "Why have you sought the gods of a people who could not deliver their own people from your hand?" ¹⁶ While the prophet was speaking to him, he said to him, "Are you the king's adviser? Keep quiet, or you will be punished." So the prophet stopped, and said, "I know that the Lord has decided to destroy you because of this, for you have not listened to His advice." ¹⁷ Amatsyahu king of Judah made plans and sent messengers to Yoash son of Yeho'achaz, son of Yehu, king of Israel, saying, "Let us meet face to face." ¹⁸ Yoash king of Israel sent messengers to Amatsiyahu king of Judah, saying, "A thorn in Lebanon sent a message to a cedar in Lebanon, saying, 'Give me your daughter as a wife.' Then a wild beast of the forest came from Lebanon and trampled the thorn." ¹⁹ You say, 'I have struck down Edom,' and your heart is lifted up. Now remain in your own house, why do you provoke trouble and fall you and Judah with you?" ²⁰ But Amatsiyahu did not listen, for this happened by the will of God, to hand him over to his enemies, because he had sought the gods of Edom. ²¹ Then Yoash king of Israel ascended and they met face to face, he and Amatsiyahu king of Judah, in Beth Shemesh of Judah. ²² And Judah was defeated in the sight of Israel, and they fled each to their tents. ²³ Yoash captured Amatsiyahu king of Judah, the son of Yoash, the son of Yeho'achaz, in Beth Shemesh, and brought him to Jerusalem. And he tore down the wall of Jerusalem from the gate of Ephraim to the corner gate, four hundred cubits. ²⁴ All the silver and gold and all the vessels found in the temple of the Lord, with the goods of the god of Edom, and the treasures of the king's palace, and the sons of the mixed marriages, he brought back to Samaria. ²⁵ Amatsiyahu son of Yoash king of Judah reigned fifteen years after the death of Yoash son of Yeho'achaz king of Israel. ²⁶ The rest of the acts of Amatsiyahu, the first and the last, are written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. ²⁷ From the time Amatsiyahu turned away from following the Lord, the men of Jerusalem laid a trap for him and he fled to Lachish. They sent after him to Lachish, and he was killed there. ²⁸ They carried him on horses and buried him with his ancestors in the city of David.

Notes
- v. 10: "in sullen rage" (chari'ot aph) reflects the emotional intensity of the Israelite army's anger, rather than a literal translation that might suggest a different nuance.
