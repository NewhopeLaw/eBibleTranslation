# 2 Chronicles 19

¹ Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned to his house in Jerusalem in peace. ² Jehu son of Hanani the prophet went out to meet him and said to King Jehoshaphat, "Should you help the wicked and love those who despise the LORD? Therefore, the LORD's anger is against you." ³ But good things were found with you, for you have removed the Asherim from the land and have set your heart to seek the LORD. ⁴ Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem and went out among the people from Beer-sheba to the hill country of Ephraim and brought them back to the LORD, the God of their ancestors. ⁵ He appointed judges in the land in all the fortified cities of Judah, a judge in every city. ⁶ He said to the judges, "See what you do, for you are judging not for a person, but for the LORD, and with you is the word of judgment. ⁷ Now let the fear of the LORD be upon you; be careful and act, for there is no injustice or showing of favor or taking a bribe with the LORD our God. ⁸ Moreover, in Jerusalem Jehoshaphat appointed some of the Levites and priests and the leaders of Israel for the judgment of the LORD and disputes, and they returned to Jerusalem. ⁹ He instructed them, saying, "This is what you must do in the fear of the LORD, in faithfulness, and with a whole heart: ¹⁰ Every dispute that comes to you from your brothers living in their cities—between blood and blood, between commandment and command, between statutes and judgments—you must warn them and not let them be guilty before the LORD. Otherwise, wrath will come upon you and your brothers. So you must act and not be guilty. ¹¹ See, Amariah the chief priest is over you for all the word of the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael the ruler of the house of Judah for all the word of the king. And scribes and Levites are before you. Be strong and act, and may the LORD be with the good."

---

Notes  
- v. 10: The phrase "between blood and blood" is a rare and difficult expression. It likely refers to disputes involving life or death matters, or conflicts between blood relatives.  
- v. 11: "Amariah the chief priest" and "Zebadiah son of Ishmael" are proper names, as per the name table.  
- v. 11: "Scribes" and "Levites" are translated as per context and common usage in such judicial roles.
