# 2 Chronicles 6

¹ Then Solomon said, "O LORD, you have said that you would dwell in thick clouds. ² I have built for you a house of habitation, a permanent place for you to dwell. ³ The king turned his face and blessed the entire assembly of Israel, and the entire assembly of Israel stood. ⁴ He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who has spoken with his mouth to my father David and fulfilled with his hand what he promised, saying: ⁵ 'Since the day I brought my people out of the land of Egypt, I have not chosen a city from all the tribes of Israel to build a house for my name to be there, nor have I chosen a man to be ruler over my people Israel. ⁶ I have chosen Jerusalem for my name to be there, and I have chosen David to be over my people Israel. ⁷ It was in the heart of my father David to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. ⁸ And the LORD said to my father David, 'Because you had it in your heart to build a house for my name, you have done well, for you had it in your heart. ⁹ Yet you will not be the one to build the house, for your son who will be born from your loins will build the house for my name. ¹⁰ And the LORD has fulfilled his word, which he spoke, and I have risen up in the place of my father David and have sat on the throne of Israel as the LORD promised, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. ¹¹ And there I have placed the ark in which is the covenant the LORD made with the children of Israel. ¹² Then he stood before the altar of the LORD in the presence of the entire assembly of Israel and spread out his hands. ¹³ For Solomon had made a bronze platform and placed it in the middle of the court, five cubits in length and five cubits in width and three cubits in height, and he stood on it and knelt before the entire assembly of Israel and spread out his hands toward heaven. ¹⁴ He said, "O LORD, God of Israel, there is no god like you in heaven or on earth, who keeps covenant and steadfast love with your servants who walk before you with all their heart. ¹⁵ You have kept for your servant David, my father, what you promised him, and you have spoken with your mouth, and with your hand you have fulfilled it as this day. ¹⁶ And now, O LORD, God of Israel, keep for your servant David, my father, what you promised him, saying, 'You shall not lack a man before me sitting on the throne of Israel, unless your children keep their way to walk in my law as you walked before me. ¹⁷ And now, O LORD, God of Israel, let your word be confirmed, which you promised to your servant David. ¹⁸ For indeed, can God really dwell with humanity on earth? Even the heavens and the heaven of heavens cannot contain you, so how much less this house that I have built? ¹⁹ And you will look upon the prayer of your servant and his supplication, O LORD, my God, to listen to the plea and the prayer your servant is making before you this day. ²⁰ Let your eyes be open toward this house day and night, toward the place you said you would put your name there, to listen to the prayer your servant is making toward this place. ²¹ And you will hear the plea of your servant and of your people Israel whenever they pray toward this place. And you will hear from your place in heaven and forgive. ²² If a man sins against another and is made to swear an oath to him, and he comes and swears before your altar in this house, ²³ then you will hear from heaven and do, and judge your servants, to repay the wicked with his way on his head and to give the righteous his due according to his righteousness. ²⁴ And if your people Israel are struck down before an enemy because they have sinned against you, and they return and confess your name and pray and seek your favor in this house, ²⁵ then you will hear from heaven and forgive the sins of your people Israel, and you will bring them back to the land you gave them and their ancestors. ²⁶ If the sky is closed and there is no rain because they have sinned against you, and they pray toward this place and confess your name, and they return from their sins because you have humbled them, ²⁷ then you will hear from heaven and forgive the sins of your servants and your people Israel, and you will show them the good way in which they should walk, and you will send rain on your land, which you gave to your people as their inheritance. ²⁸ If there is a famine in the land, a plague, blight and mildew, locusts and grasshoppers, or if their enemy attacks them in the gates of their cities, any disease or any illness, ²⁹ and any prayer or any supplication that arises from a man or from all your people Israel, each one knowing his affliction and his pain, and he spreads out his hands toward this house, ³⁰ then you will hear from heaven, your settled place, and you will heal and give to each man according to his ways, as you know his heart, for you alone know the heart of all the children of humanity. ³¹ So that they may fear all your ways, all the days that they live on the face of the land which you gave to our ancestors. ³² And also for the foreigner who is not of your people Israel, but who comes from a distant land because of your great name, and your strong hand and your outstretched arm, and they come and pray toward this house, ³³ then you will hear from heaven, your settled place, and you will do all that the foreigner calls to you, so that all the peoples of the earth may know your name and fear you as your people Israel do, and know that your name is called upon this house which I have built. ³⁴ When your people go out to war against their enemies on the road you send them, and they pray to you along the way of this city you have chosen and the house you have built for your name, ³⁵ then you will hear from heaven their prayer and their supplication, and you will do justice for them. ³⁶ For they will sin against you, for there is no man who does not sin, and you will strike them and give them into the hands of their enemies, and they will be taken captive to a distant or near land. ³⁷ And they will return their hearts in the land where they were taken, and they will return and pray to you in their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted wickedly. ³⁸ And they will return to you with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity where they were taken, and they will pray toward the land you gave to their ancestors and the city you chose and the house you built for your name. ³⁹ And you will hear from heaven, your settled place, their prayer and their supplication, and you will do judgment and show mercy to your people who have sinned against you. ⁴⁰ Now, O my God, let your eyes be open and let your ears be attentive to the prayer of this place. ⁴¹ And now, arise, O LORD, God, to your resting place. You and the ark of your might. O LORD, God, let your priests be clothed with salvation, and let your faithful ones rejoice in good. ⁴² O LORD, God, do not turn your face away. Remember the steadfast love of David, your servant.

Notes
- v. 14: "shomar" is translated as "keeps" to reflect covenant faithfulness.
- v. 18: "ki ha'umnam yeshesh bes et-ha-adam al-ha-aret" is rendered to reflect the rhetorical question about divine presence in a physical house.
- v. 30: "ki ata yadat et levav benei ha-adam" is translated to emphasize God's unique knowledge of human hearts.
- v. 33: "ve'ata ashamta es kol amei ha-aret et shemcha" is rendered to reflect the purpose of making God's name known to all nations.
- v. 42: "zachor et chesed David avdecha" is translated to emphasize the remembrance of David's steadfast love.
