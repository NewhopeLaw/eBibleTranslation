# 2 Chronicles 4

¹ Solomon made a bronze altar twenty cubits in length and twenty cubits in width, ten cubits in height. ² He made a large bronze basin, ten cubits from rim to rim, round all around, five cubits in height, and a thirty-cubit line encircling it. ³ He made ornamental oxen beneath it, encircling the basin all around; ten cubits in circumference, two rows of cast oxen. ⁴ The oxen stood on twelve oxen, three facing north, three facing west, three facing south, and three facing east. The basin rested above them, the backs of the oxen turned inward. ⁵ The basin’s thickness was a handbreadth; its rim was shaped like a cup, with lily-like buds adorning it. It could hold three thousand baths. ⁶ He made ten basins and placed five on the right and five on the left, for washing the parts of the burnt offerings; the priests washed in the basin. ⁷ He made ten golden lampstands, according to their pattern, and placed five on the right and five on the left in the temple. ⁸ He made ten golden tables and placed five on the right and five on the left in the temple. He also made one hundred golden bowls. ⁹ He made the courtyard for the priests and the large courtyard, with doors to the courtyard, and its doorposts were overlaid with bronze. ¹⁰ He placed the basin in the right-hand corner of the building, facing east, opposite the south side. ¹¹ Huram also made the basins, the snuffers, and the tongs for the altar, and he completed all the work he did for King Solomon in the house of the LORD. ¹² He made two pillars, with two capitals on top of each, and two networks to cover the two capitals of the pillars. ¹³ He made four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, two rows of pomegranates for each network, to cover the two capitals of the pillars. ¹⁴ He made the bases and the basins on the bases. ¹⁵ He made one basin and twelve oxen beneath it. ¹⁶ Huram also made the basins, the snuffers, the tongs, the meat hooks, and all their accessories, from bronze, for King Solomon in the house of the LORD. ¹⁷ The king cast them in the Jordan Valley, in the clay ground between Sukkoth and Zeredah. ¹⁸ Solomon made all these vessels in abundance, for the weight of the bronze was not weighed out. ¹⁹ Solomon made all the vessels in the house of the LORD, the golden altar, the tables, and the bread of the Presence on them. ²⁰ He made the lampstands and their lamps for lighting them, as prescribed, in front of the inner sanctuary—solid gold. ²¹ The buds, the lamps, and the snuffers were all of gold, as were the bowls and the tongs and the ladles. ²² The doors of the house, the inner doors to the Most Holy Place, and the doors of the temple were all of gold.

Notes
- v. 5: "three thousand baths" — the Hebrew is שְׁלֹשֶׁת אֲלָפִים יָכִֽיל, where יָכִֽיל is "bath" (a liquid measure). The Septuagint adds "and it was completed," but the Hebrew text ends the verse with the capacity alone.
