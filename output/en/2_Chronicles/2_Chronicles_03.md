# 2 Chronicles 3

¹ Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, which appeared to David his father, where David had prepared in the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite. ² He began to build in the second month, on the second day, in the fourth year of his reign. ³ Now these were the measurements Solomon set for building the house of God: the length in the first measure was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits. ⁴ The porch that projected from the length of the house was twenty cubits in width, and the height was one hundred twenty cubits. He covered it inside with pure gold. ⁵ The great house he overlaid with cedarwood and covered it with good gold, and he carved on it gourds and chains. ⁶ He adorned the house with costly stones for beauty, and the gold was Ophir gold. ⁷ He overlaid the house, the beams, its walls, and its doors, with gold, and carved cherubim on the walls. ⁸ He made the Holy of Holies, its length matching the width of the house, twenty cubits, and its width twenty cubits. He covered it with pure gold, six hundred talents worth of cherubim. ⁹ The weight of the hooks for the chains was fifty shekels of gold. He overlaid the capitals with gold. ¹⁰ In the Holy of Holies he made two cherubim of carved work and covered them with gold. ¹¹ The wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits long. The one wing of each was five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing five cubits, touching the wing of the other cherub. ¹² The other wing of each cherub was five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing five cubits, joined to the wing of the other cherub. ¹³ The wings of these cherubim stretched out twenty cubits, and they stood on their feet, their faces toward the house. ¹⁴ He made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and he wove cherubim into it. ¹⁵ He made two pillars before the house, thirty-five cubits in height, and the capitals on their tops were five cubits. ¹⁶ He made chains in the porch and placed them on the tops of the pillars, and he made a hundred pomegranates and placed them on the chains. ¹⁷ He set the pillars before the temple, one on the right and one on the left. He named the one on the right Jachin and the one on the left Boaz.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Mount Moriah" is a rendering of הַמּוֹרִיָּה, a place name. Some traditions equate this with the site of the Temple.
- v. 5: "gourds and chains" refers to תִּמֹרִים וְשַׁרְשְׁרֽוֹת, terms for ornamental motifs often used in temple descriptions.
- v. 6: "Ophir gold" is a standard rendering of זְהַב פַּרְוָֽיִם (Parvaim), a source of gold known from ancient trade.
- v. 17: The names "Jachin" and "Boaz" are traditional renderings of יָכִין and בֹּֽעַז, though their meanings are not fully known.
