# 2 Chronicles 2

¹ Solomon mustered seventy thousand men as carriers and eighty thousand as stonecutters in the mountains, and over them were three thousand six hundred commanders. ² Solomon sent to Hiram king of Tyre, saying, As you did with David my father and sent him cedar trees to build for himself a house in which to dwell— ³ behold, I am building a house for the name of the LORD my God, to consecrate it for him, to burn incense of sweet spices before him, and to place a continual offering, and to offer burnt offerings in the morning and in the evening, on the Sabbaths and on the new moons and on the appointed feasts of the LORD our God forever, for the people of Israel. ⁴ The house that I am building is great, for great is our God above all gods. ⁵ Who is able to build a house for him? For the heavens and the heavens of the heavens cannot contain him, and who am I that I should build a house for him, except to burn incense before him? ⁶ So now send me a man who is wise, who can work in gold and silver and bronze and iron and purple and crimson and blue, and who knows how to make engravings, together with your wise men who are with me in Judah and Jerusalem, whom David my father has appointed. ⁷ Also send me cedar trees, cypress trees, and pines from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut trees in Lebanon, and indeed, my servants are with your servants. ⁸ Send me trees in abundance, for the house that I am building is great and wondrous. ⁹ And behold, to the workers who are felling the trees, I have given grain for their food, twenty thousand cors of wheat, twenty thousand cors of barley, twenty thousand baths of wine, and twenty thousand baths of oil. ¹⁰ And Hiram king of Tyre answered in writing, saying, Because the LORD loves his people, he has made you their king. ¹¹ And Hiram said, Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who made the heavens and the earth, who gave to David the king a wise son, one who understands knowledge and insight, who will build a house for the LORD and a house for his kingdom. ¹² And now I will send a wise man, one who understands insight, to Hiram my father. ¹³ His mother is from the daughters of Dan, and his father is a Tyrian, one who knows how to work in gold and silver and bronze and iron, in stones and wood, in purple, in blue, and in fine linen, and to make engravings and to devise every device that may be given to him, along with your wise men and the wise men of your master David, my father. ¹⁴ And now the grain, the barley, the oil, and the wine that my lord has spoken about, let them be given to your servants. ¹⁵ And we ourselves will cut trees from Lebanon according to all your needs, and we will bring them to you on rafts to the sea of Joppa, and you will bring them to Jerusalem. ¹⁶ Solomon counted all the foreign residents in the land of Israel, after the count that David his father had made, and they were found to be one hundred fifty thousand and three thousand six hundred. ¹⁷ And he made seventy thousand of them carriers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountains, and three thousand six hundred commanders to supervise the people in their labor.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "Because the LORD loves his people" — the Hebrew uses "because the LORD your God loves his people," but the context implies Hiram is speaking, so "your" is omitted for clarity.
- v. 13: "a Tyrian" — the Hebrew uses "son of a Tyrian," rendered here as "a Tyrian" for natural English.
