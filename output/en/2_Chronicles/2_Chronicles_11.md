# 2 Chronicles 11

¹ Rehoboam went to Jerusalem and assembled the people of Judah and Benjamin—180,000 warriors—to fight against Israel in order to reclaim the kingdom for Rehoboam. ² The word of the LORD came to Shemaiah, a man of God, saying: ³ "Speak to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, saying: ⁴ 'This is what the LORD says: Do not go up and do not fight against your brothers. Return to your homes, for this matter has come from me.' So they listened to the word of the LORD and returned from going to Rehoboam." ⁵ Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and built fortified cities in Judah. ⁶ He built Beth-lehem, Etam, and Tekoa, ⁷ and Beth-zur, Socoh, and Adullam, ⁸ and Gath, and Marisa, and Ziph, ⁹ and Adoraim, and Lakish, and Azekah, ¹⁰ and Zeror, and Aijalon, and Hebron—these were in Judah and Benjamin, fortified cities. ¹¹ He strengthened the fortifications and placed commanders in them, with supplies of food, oil, and wine. ¹² In every city and in every town he placed shields and spears, and he greatly strengthened them. So Judah and Benjamin were his. ¹³ The Levites and the priests who were in all Israel gathered to him from every border. ¹⁴ For the Levites had abandoned their inheritance and land and had gone to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had driven them away from serving the LORD. ¹⁵ He had set up priests for the high places, the idols, and the calves he had made. ¹⁶ Then those from every tribe of Israel who had set their hearts to seek the LORD, the God of Israel, came to Jerusalem to offer sacrifices to the LORD, the God of their ancestors. ¹⁷ So the kingdom of Judah was strengthened, and Rehoboam son of Solomon was made strong for three years, for he walked in the ways of David and Solomon for three years. ¹⁸ Rehoboam took a wife, Mahalath, daughter of Jerimoth son of David. Her mother was Baiah, daughter of Eliab from Gilead. ¹⁹ She gave birth to him three sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham. ²⁰ Then he took Maacah, daughter of Absalom, and she bore him Abijah, and Attai, and Zechariah, and Shelomith. ²¹ Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and concubines, for he had eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters. ²² He set Abijah, the son of Maacah, as head over his brothers, for he was to be king. ²³ Rehoboam built and expanded more than all his sons in all the territories of Judah and Benjamin and in all the fortified cities. He gave them abundant provisions and took in many wives.

Notes
- v. 14: "had driven them away from serving the LORD" — the Hebrew uses the verb "to cast out," which may imply both physical and spiritual exclusion. The context of Levitical service is clear.
- v. 21: "had eighteen wives and sixty concubines" — the Hebrew reads "eighteen wives and concubines," but the context and parallel in v. 22 suggest a total of 18 wives and 60 concubines. The translation follows this interpretation.
