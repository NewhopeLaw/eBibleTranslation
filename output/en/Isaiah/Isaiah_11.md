# Isaiah 11

¹ A shoot will come from the stump of Jesse, and a branch from his roots will bear fruit. ² The LORD's Spirit will rest on him— the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, the Spirit of counsel and might, the Spirit of knowledge and reverence for the LORD. ³ He will judge the poor with fairness, and he will decide cases with justice for the oppressed. With his eyes he will not look favorably, and with his ears he will not listen to deceit. ⁴ He will judge the poor rightly and give a fair decision for the lowly of the earth. With the rod of his mouth he will strike the earth, and with the breath of his lips he will put the wicked to death. ⁵ Righteousness will be the belt of his waist, and faithfulness the sash of his feet. ⁶ The wolf will live with the lamb, and the leopard lie down with the young goat. The calf and the young lion will graze together, and a little child will lead them. ⁷ The cow and the bear will feed together, their young will lie down together. The lion, like the ox, will eat hay. ⁸ The nursing child will play over the hole of an adder, and the weaned child will put his hand on the nest of a serpent. ⁹ They will do no harm or destroy anywhere on my holy mountain, for the earth will be filled with knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea. ¹⁰ On that day the root of Jesse, who stands as a signal for the peoples, will be the one to whom the nations will look. His resting place will be honor. ¹¹ On that day the LORD will extend his hand again to claim the remnant of his people from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros, from Cush, from Elam, from Shinar, from Hamath, and from the coastlands of the sea. ¹² He will raise a banner for the nations and gather the exiles of Israel and bring back the scattered of Judah from the four corners of the earth. ¹³ The jealousy of Ephraim will be gone, and the enemies of Judah will be cut off. Ephraim will not envy Judah, and Judah will not trouble Ephraim. ¹⁴ The Philistines will be driven out together to the west. Together they will plunder the people of the east, Edom and Moab, and the Ammonites will obey them. ¹⁵ The LORD will dry up the Sea of Egypt and lift his hand over the Euphrates with a driving wind. He will strike it with seven streams, so that it can be crossed in sandals. ¹⁶ A highway will be for the remnant of his people who are left from Assyria, just as it was for Israel when they came up from the land of Egypt.

---

Notes
- v. 14: "the Philistines will be driven out together to the west" – the Hebrew uses "עָפוּ בְכָתֵף" which literally means "fled with the wing," but the context suggests a forced migration or scattering.
