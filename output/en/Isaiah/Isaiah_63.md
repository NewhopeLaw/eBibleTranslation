# Isaiah 63

¹ Who is this who comes from Edom, with dyed garments from Bozrah? This one who is radiant in his attire, marching in great strength. I speak with abundant righteousness to save. ² Why is your clothing red, and your garments like one who treads in a winepress? ³ I have walked alone, with no man among me, and I have trampled them in my anger, and I have crushed them in my fury, and their blood is on my garments, and I have stained every one of my clothes. ⁴ For a day of vengeance is in my heart, and the year of my redemption has come. ⁵ I looked, but there was no one to help, and I listened, but there was no one to support. My own arm brought me salvation, and my fury itself propped me up. ⁶ I have trampled the peoples in my anger, and I have crushed them in my fury, and I have poured out their blood on the earth. ⁷ I will remember the steadfast love of the LORD, and declare His praises, according to all that the LORD has lavished on us. Great is His goodness to the house of Israel, because He is merciful and His steadfast love is abundant. ⁸ And He said, “Surely they are My people, children who will not deal falsely.” And He became their savior. ⁹ In all their affliction He was afflicted, and the angel of His presence saved them. In His love and in His mercy He redeemed them, and He carried them and bore them all their days. ¹⁰ But they rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit, so He turned against them as an enemy; He fought against them. ¹¹ Then He remembered the days of old, Moses His servant. Where is the One who brought up His people through the sea, with His shepherds among His flock? Where is the One who placed His Holy Spirit in their midst? ¹² Who guided Moses with His right hand, the arm of His glory, who split the waters before them to make for Himself an eternal name? ¹³ Who led them through the depths like a horse through the wilderness, and they stumbled not? ¹⁴ Like cattle descending into the valley, the wind of the LORD sent them forth. Thus You guided Your people, to make for Yourself a name of glory. ¹⁵ Look from the heavens and see from Your holy dwelling place. Where is Your zeal and Your might? Your mercies and Your compassion, which restrained Yourself toward us? ¹⁶ For You are our Father, though Abraham does not know us and Israel does not recognize us. You, O LORD, are our Father. Our Redeemer from of old, and Your name is forever. ¹⁷ Why have You led us away from Your ways, O LORD? Why have You hardened our hearts so we do not fear You? Return for the sake of Your servants, for the sake of the tribes of Your inheritance, ¹⁸ that Your people, the holy ones, might inherit Your sorrow, and Your enemies might trample Your sanctuary. ¹⁹ We have always been ruled by no one, for You have not governed us, and Your name was not called upon us. If You had torn the heavens, we would have descended, and the mountains would have flowed before You.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "I speak with abundant righteousness to save" reflects the meaning of "medaber bitzedaqah rav lehoshe'ach" as a statement of purpose.
- v. 3: "I have trampled them... and I have crushed them" renders "parah darachtiti" and "eremsem" in context as actions of divine judgment.
- v. 19: "We have always been ruled by no one" is a contextual rendering of "hayinu me'olam lo mashaltah b'ham" to reflect the people's sense of abandonment.
