# Isaiah 54

¹ Rejoice, barren one who has not borne children, burst into song and cry aloud, you who have not labored in childbirth, for many are the children of the desolate more than those of the wife. The LORD has spoken. ² Expand the space of your tent and the curtains of your dwellings. Do not hold back; lengthen your ropes and drive in your stakes firmly. ³ For your borders will stretch to the right and to the left, and your children will inherit nations, and you will settle cities that were once desolate. ⁴ Do not fear, for you will not be put to shame; do not be dismayed, for you will not be disgraced. You will forget the shame of your youth, and the reproach of your widowhood you will no longer remember. ⁵ For your Maker is your Husband, the LORD of Hosts is his name, and your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, is God over all the earth. ⁶ For like a woman abandoned and grieving in spirit, you were called by the LORD, and like a wife of youth who is despised, your God has said. ⁷ For a brief moment I have forsaken you, but with great compassion I will gather you. ⁸ In a moment of wrath I turned my face away from you, but with eternal kindness I will have mercy on you, says your Redeemer, the LORD. ⁹ For this is like the waters of Noah to me, when I swore that never again would the waters of Noah flood the earth. So I have sworn that never again will I be angry with you, nor rebuke you. ¹⁰ For though the mountains may shift and the hills be displaced, my steadfast love will not shift from you, nor will my covenant of peace be displaced, says the one who has compassion on you, the LORD. ¹¹ You who were afflicted and not consoled, behold, I am laying your foundations with sapphires and setting your walls with turquoise stones. ¹² And I will make your gates into glowing jewels and your gates into precious stones, and all your boundaries into choice stones. ¹³ And all your children will be taught by the LORD, and great peace will be among your children. ¹⁴ In righteousness you will be established, far from oppression and violence. You will not fear, nor will terror come near you. ¹⁵ Behold, strangers will dwell with you, and those who were once exiles will settle with you. And whoever attacks you will fall to the ground. ¹⁶ Behold, I have created the blacksmith who fans the coals and produces a weapon for his task. But I myself have created the destroyer to devastate. ¹⁷ Every weapon formed against you will not succeed, and every tongue that rises against you in judgment will be condemned. This is the inheritance of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is from me, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 12: "glowing jewels" and "precious stones" are interpretive translations of the poetic descriptions of beauty and value.
- v. 16: The contrast between the blacksmith and the destroyer reflects a deliberate rhetorical structure in the original text.
