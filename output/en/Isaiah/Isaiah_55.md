# Isaiah 55

¹ O you who are thirsty, come to the waters, and you who have no silver, come, buy and eat! Come, buy wine and milk without money and without price. ² Why should you spend silver on bread and your wages on what does not satisfy? Listen, eat what is good, and let your soul delight in rich food. ³ Incline your ear and come to me. Listen, and your soul will live. I will make with you an everlasting covenant, the steadfast love of David, faithful and sure. ⁴ I have given him as a witness to the peoples, a commander and teacher for the nations. ⁵ A nation you do not know will call to you, and a people who do not know you will run to you, for the sake of the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, because he has honored you. ⁶ Seek the LORD while he may be found, call upon him while he is near. ⁷ Let the wicked abandon his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts. Let him return to the LORD, and he will have mercy on him, and to our God, for he is generous to forgive. ⁸ For my thoughts are not your thoughts, and your ways are not my ways, says the LORD. ⁹ For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways and my thoughts than your thoughts. ¹⁰ For just as rain and snow come down from the heavens and do not return there until they have watered the earth, making it sprout and flourish, giving seed to the sower and bread to the eater, ¹¹ so will my word be, which goes forth from my mouth. It will not return to me empty, but will accomplish what I desire and succeed in the purpose for which I sent it. ¹² For you will go out in joy and be led in peace. The mountains and the hills will break forth into singing before you, and all the trees of the field will clap their hands. ¹³ Instead of the thorn shall come up the cypress; instead of the brier shall come up the myrtle. And the LORD will make this a sign and a witness for all time, never to be cut off.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "the steadfast love of David" is a poetic rendering of חַסְדֵי דָוִד, which refers to the covenantal faithfulness associated with David.
- v. 9: "my thoughts are not your thoughts" and "your ways are not my ways" echoes the Hebrew parallelism and the contrast between divine and human understanding.
- v. 11: "my word" refers to God's spoken word or message, which is personified as having action and purpose.
