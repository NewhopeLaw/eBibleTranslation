# Isaiah 61

¹ The spirit of the Lord is upon me, because the Lord has anointed me to bring good news to the humble. He has sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim release for captives and sight for the blind. ² To proclaim the year of the Lord’s favor and the day of our God’s vengeance, to comfort all who mourn. ³ To give to the mourners of Zion a crown instead of ashes, oil of gladness instead of mourning, and a mantle of praise instead of a spirit of fainting. And they will be called oaks of righteousness, a planting of the Lord for his glory. ⁴ They will rebuild the ancient ruins, raise up the desolate places from before, and restore the ruined cities, the places long forsaken. ⁵ And strangers will graze your flocks, and the sons of foreigners will be your plowmen and vine-dressers. ⁶ But you, you will be called the priests of the Lord, and it will be said of you, “Ministers of our God.” You will eat the wealth of the nations and glory in their splendor. ⁷ Instead of your shame, which you endured, and the reproach with which you were reproached, you will receive a double portion, and in their land you will inherit a double. Joy eternal will be theirs. ⁸ For I am the Lord who loves justice, who hates robbery in the offering. I will establish their work in truth, and I will make an everlasting covenant with them. ⁹ And their descendants will be known among the nations, and their offspring in the midst of the peoples. Everyone who sees them will recognize them, for they are a blessed seed of the Lord. ¹⁰ I will greatly rejoice in the Lord, my soul will exult in my God. For he has clothed me with garments of salvation, and wrapped me in a robe of righteousness, as a bridegroom decks himself with a crown, and as a bride adorns herself with her jewels. ¹¹ For as the earth produces its growth, and as a garden causes what is sown in it to spring up, so the Lord God will cause righteousness and praise to spring up before all the nations.

Notes
- v. 1: "The spirit of the Lord is upon me" — the Hebrew uses ר֖וּחַ אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, "the spirit of the Lord YHWH," which is rendered in full.
- v. 2: "the day of our God’s vengeance" — the Hebrew לֵאלֹהֵינוּ לְנַחֵם כָּל־אֲבֵלִֽים is a complex construction; the rendering aims to preserve the parallelism and the meaning of divine action in comfort and justice.
- v. 6: "Ministers of our God" — the Greek version uses "leitourgoi theou" ("ministers of God"), which is used here to mirror the Hebrew’s emphasis on service.
- v. 10: "as a bridegroom decks himself with a crown" — the Hebrew uses כֶּחָתָן יְכַהֵן פְּאֵר, with פְּאֵר as a "crown" or "ornament," and the metaphor is rendered accordingly.
