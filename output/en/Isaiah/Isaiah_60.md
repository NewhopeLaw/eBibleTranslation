# Isaiah 60

¹ Arise, shine, for your light has come, and the glory of the LORD has risen upon you. ² For behold, darkness will cover the earth, and thick darkness will be over the peoples, but the LORD will rise upon you, and his glory will be seen upon you. ³ And nations will come to your light, and kings to your brightness. ⁴ Raise your eyes all around, and look: they have all gathered and come to you. Your sons will come from afar, and your daughters will be carried on the arms of their bearers. ⁵ Then you will see and be radiant, and your heart will fear and be enlarged, for the abundance of the sea will be turned to you, and the wealth of the nations will come to you. ⁶ Caravans of camels will cover you, camels of Midian and Dedan, all of them from Sheba, bringing gold, incense, and proclaiming the praise of the LORD. ⁷ All the flocks of Kedar will be gathered to you, the rams of Nebaioth will serve you, and they will ascend as acceptable offerings on my altar, and the house of my glory will be made glorious. ⁸ Who are these, like clouds flying, like doves to their windows? ⁹ For the islands will wait for me, and the ships of Tarshish will come first, to bring your sons from afar, their silver and gold with them, for the name of the LORD your God, and for the Holy One of Israel, because you are honored. ¹⁰ Foreigners will build your walls, and their kings will serve you, for in my wrath I struck you, and in my favor I have shown you mercy. ¹¹ Your gates will be open continually, day and night they will not be shut, to bring to you the wealth of the nations and their kings being led. ¹² For the nation and the kingdom that did not serve you will perish; and the peoples will be completely consumed. ¹³ The glory of Lebanon will come to you, cypress, fir, and cedar together, to beautify the place of my sanctuary, and the place of my feet I will honor. ¹⁴ And the sons of your oppressors will come to you, bowing themselves down at the soles of your feet, and all who despised you will kneel before you, and they will call you the City of the LORD, the Zion of the Holy One of Israel. ¹⁵ Because you were forsaken and hated, with no one passing through, I will make you a glory forever, a joy from generation to generation. ¹⁶ You will nurse the milk of nations, and the wealth of kings you will drink, and you will know that I, the LORD, am your savior, the strong redeemer of Jacob. ¹⁷ Instead of bronze I will bring gold, and instead of iron I will bring silver; instead of wood, bronze, and instead of stones, iron. And I will make your officials peace and your judges righteousness. ¹⁸ Violence will no longer be heard in your land, nor plunder nor destruction within your borders. Your walls will be called salvation, and your gates, praise. ¹⁹ You will have no more sun for light by day, nor will the brightness of the moon shine on you; but the LORD will be your eternal light, and your God will be your glory. ²⁰ Your sun will not set, nor your moon wane; for the LORD will be your eternal light, and the days of your mourning will be ended. ²¹ Your people, all of them, will be righteous; they will possess the land forever, the sprout of my planting, the work of my hands for glory. ²² The least will become a thousand, and the small one a strong nation. I, the LORD, will hasten it in its time.

---

Notes
- v. 7: "acceptable offerings" reflects the context of sacrificial worship and divine acceptance.
- v. 13: "cypress, fir, and cedar" is a literal translation of the Hebrew terms for the types of trees, used to convey the richness and beauty of the materials.
- v. 17: "your officials peace" is a direct translation, with "peace" serving as a noun representing the role or quality of leadership.
- v. 22: "I, the LORD, will hasten it in its time" is a straightforward rendering of the Hebrew, with "in its time" referring to the appointed time for fulfillment.
