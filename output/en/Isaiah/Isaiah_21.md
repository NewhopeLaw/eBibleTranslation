# Isaiah 21

¹ A burden concerning the desert of the sea. It comes from the south, through the desert, from a land of terror. ² A grim vision was told to me: the betrayer betrays, the plunderer plunders. Elam and Media, my oppressors, have all fallen silent. ³ Therefore, my loins are filled with pain, as if with the pangs of a woman in childbirth. I am distressed from hearing, terrified from seeing. ⁴ My heart is disoriented, my spirit overwhelmed. I am filled with dread at what has befallen me. ⁵ Set the table, pour the wine. Let the officials rise and drink, take up the shield. ⁶ For the Lord said to me, "Go, set a watchman, who will report what he sees." ⁷ He saw riders, a pair of horsemen, a rider on a donkey, a rider on a camel, and listened to a great, attentive silence. ⁸ Then he called out to the lion: "Lord, I stand constantly by day, and I remain on watch through every night." ⁹ Then he said, "Here comes a rider, a pair of horsemen!" And he answered, "Babylon has fallen! All her carved images have been shattered to the ground." ¹⁰ My soul is shattered, and the son of my sorrow has spoken what I heard from the Lord of Hosts, the God of Israel. I have made it known to you. ¹¹ A burden from Dumah came to me, a watchman from Seir: "What of the night? What of the night?" ¹² The watchman replied, "Morning is coming, and also night. If you wait for it, wait, for it will surely return." ¹³ A burden in the desert, in the desert of Arabia. The caravans of Dedan are lodged in the roads. ¹⁴ Bring water to the thirsty; meet the wanderers of the land of Tema with bread. ¹⁵ Because of the destructions they have fled, because of the sword abandoned, and because of the bow driven back, and because of the weight of war. ¹⁶ For the Lord said to me, "In one year it will be as the wages of a hired laborer, and the glory of all Kedar will come to an end. ¹⁷ The remainder of the number of bowmen, the warriors of the sons of Kedar, will be few, for the Lord, the God of Israel, has spoken."

---

Notes
- v. 10: "my soul is shattered" reflects the Hebrew נֶפֶלֶתִי (medfashet), which is a rare term, often taken as "my soul is broken" or "my soul is shattered."
- v. 16: "as the wages of a hired laborer" reflects the Hebrew כִּשְׁנֵי שָׂכִיר (kisheini sakir), which is a rare phrase. The meaning is that the glory will fade quickly, like the wages of a temporary worker who does not remain long.
