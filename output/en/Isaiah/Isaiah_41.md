# Isaiah 41

¹ Be silent before me, islands, and you peoples, let your might be exchanged. Come forth, and they will speak together for judgment, let them draw near and be brought to trial. ² Who has stirred up from the east righteousness? He who calls it to his foot, and gives it dominion before the nations, and kings will surrender. He gives their swords as dust, their bows as stubble scattered. ³ He will pursue them, he will pass through in peace, his way will not come into being. ⁴ Who has done this, and made it? He who called the generations from the beginning. I am the LORD, the first, and with the last I am — I am the same. ⁵ The islands saw and were afraid, the ends of the earth trembled, they approached and were brought to trial. ⁶ One man will help his neighbor, and each will say to his brother, "Take heart." ⁷ The smith strengthens the founder, and the striker beats the hammer. One will say to the anvil, "Good is the fit," and he will fasten it with nails, so it will not waver. ⁸ But you, Israel, my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, seed of Abraham, the one I love. ⁹ Whom I have taken from the ends of the earth, and called from the farthest corners. I said to you, "You are my servant; I have chosen you, and I will not abandon you." ¹⁰ Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God. I who uphold you, I who help you, I who strengthen you by my righteous right hand. ¹¹ See, those who oppose you will be put to shame; all who rise against you will be as nothing, and those who war against you will be destroyed. ¹² You will seek them, but they will not be found. The men who conspired against you will vanish, and those who fought against you will be as nothing. ¹³ For I, the LORD your God, hold your right hand. I say to you, "Do not fear, I will help you." ¹⁴ You little worm Jacob, you worm Israel — do not fear. I will help you, says the LORD, your redeemer, the Holy One of Israel. ¹⁵ I will make you a new threshing sledge, a sharp threshing sledge with double edges. You will thresh the mountains, and beat them into dust, and the hills you will make like chaff. ¹⁶ You will winnow them, and the wind will carry them away, and the storm will scatter them. But you will rejoice in the LORD, and in the Holy One of Israel you will be glad. ¹⁷ The poor and the needy seek water, but there is none, their tongues are parched with thirst. I, the LORD, will answer them. I, the God of Israel, will not abandon them. ¹⁸ I will open rivers on the mountains and springs in the mid-valleys. I will turn the desert into pools of water, and the dry land into fountains. ¹⁹ I will plant cedars in the desert, acacia and myrtle, and oil trees in the wilderness. I will set the plain to flourish with boxwood and the rock to sprout together. ²⁰ That they may see and recognize, and consider and understand together, that the hand of the LORD has done this, and the Holy One of Israel has created it. ²¹ Bring forth your case, says the LORD. Let the adversaries of Jacob present their arguments, says the King of Israel. ²² Let them come forth and declare to us what will be, or what was — declare it, and let us consider, and know the end. Or let the coming things be made known to us. ²³ Declare to us the things to come, and we will know that you are gods. Do good or do evil, and let us be astonished and see together. ²⁴ See, you are nothing, your works are a waste — you are but wind and emptiness. ²⁵ I have stirred up from the north one who awakens, from the rising sun he who calls by my name. He will come to destroy rulers like clay, and like a potter treading the mire. ²⁶ Who announced from the beginning and from the past, and we can know? Who declared it beforehand, and we can say, "Yes, it is true"? There is none to declare, there is none to hear your words. ²⁷ The first to Zion I will send, and I will give a messenger to Jerusalem. ²⁸ I will look, but there is no one, I will ask, but there is no one to answer. I will ask them, but they will not reply. ²⁹ See, all of them are vanity, their works are nothing — their images are wind and emptiness.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "islands" is a poetic or symbolic reference to distant peoples or nations, not literal islands.
- v. 2: "from the east" echoes the motif of divine action originating from the east, a common metaphor in Hebrew cosmology.
- v. 4: "I am the LORD, the first, and with the last I am — I am the same" reflects the divine eternity and constancy.
- v. 14: "worm Jacob" is a figurative expression emphasizing Israel's weakness and insignificance.
- v. 25: "one who awakens" is likely a metaphor for a powerful invading force or divine agent.
- v. 26: "there is none to declare, there is none to hear your words" underscores the futility of human prophecy compared to the LORD's unique revelation.
