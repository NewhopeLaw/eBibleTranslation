# Isaiah 2

¹ The word that Isaiah son of Amoz saw regarding Judah and Jerusalem. ² In the last days, the mountain of the LORD will be established as the highest of the mountains, and it will rise above the hills. To it, all the nations will stream. ³ Many peoples will go and say, "Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob, so that he may teach us his ways, and we may walk in his paths." For from Zion the law will go out, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem. ⁴ He will judge between the nations, and will arbitrate for many peoples. They will beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will not lift sword against nation, and they will no longer learn war. ⁵ O house of Jacob, come, let us walk in the light of the LORD. ⁶ For you have abandoned your people, the house of Jacob, because they are full from the east, and they are like Canaanite idolaters, like the children of foreigners. ⁷ The land is filled with silver and gold, and there is no end to its treasures. The land is filled with horses, and there is no end to its chariots. ⁸ The land is filled with idols, the work of human hands. To what they made with their hands they bow down, to the work of their fingers. ⁹ The person is humbled, and the man is brought low, and you will not look upon them. ¹⁰ Enter the rock, and hide in the dust, from the presence of the fear of the LORD and from the glory of his majesty, when he rises to shatter the earth. ¹¹ For the eyes of the high ones are humbled, and the lofty ones are brought low, and the LORD alone will be exalted in that day. ¹² For a day of the LORD of Hosts will come upon every proud one and every lofty one, upon every high and exalted one, and they will be brought low. ¹³ Upon every cedar in Lebanon, the lofty and exalted, and upon every cypress in Bashan, ¹⁴ upon every high mountain, and upon every lofty hill, ¹⁵ upon every high tower, and upon every fortified wall, ¹⁶ upon every ship of Tarshish, and upon every stately vessel. ¹⁷ The lofty one of the human will be brought low, and the exalted ones will be humbled, and the LORD alone will be exalted in that day. ¹⁸ And the idols, all of them, will vanish. ¹⁹ They will go into the caves of the rock and into the crevices of the dust, from the presence of the fear of the LORD and from the glory of his majesty, when he rises to shatter the earth. ²⁰ In that day, the person will cast away his silver idols and his golden idols, which they made to bow to, to the fruitless and the shadowless. ²¹ To go into the crannies of the solid rock and into the fissures of the rocks, from the presence of the fear of the LORD and from the glory of his majesty, when he rises to shatter the earth. ²² Cease, you people, from the human, in whom is the breath of life, for what is he?

---

Notes  
- v. 1: The phrase "the word that Isaiah saw" (הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר חָזָה יְשַֽׁעְיָהוּ) is rendered as "The word that Isaiah son of Amoz saw" to reflect the prophetic vision.  
- v. 2: "In the last days" (בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים) is a future eschatological reference, not a chronological one.  
- v. 4: "Canaanite idolaters" (כַּפְּלִשְׁתִּים) is a reference to Philistines, but the context here is metaphorical, referring to idolatrous practices.  
- v. 13: "Cedars in Lebanon" (אַרְזֵי הַלְּבָנוֹן) and "cypresses in Bashan" (אַלּוֹנֵי הַבָּשָֽׁן) are symbolic of human pride and grandeur.  
- v. 22: "Cease, you people, from the human" (חִדְלוּ לָכֶם מִן־הָאָדָם) is a warning not to place trust in human strength or wisdom.
