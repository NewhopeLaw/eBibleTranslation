# Isaiah 24

¹ Behold, the LORD is ruining the earth, and He will waste it and reveal its face and scatter its inhabitants. ² It will be as the people are to the priest, as the servant to his master, as the maid to her mistress, as the buyer to the seller, as the lender to the borrower, as the one who lends to the one who owes him. ³ The earth will be ruined and plundered, for the mouth of the LORD has spoken these things. ⁴ The earth mourns and perishes, and the inhabited world is in anguish, and the lofty people of the earth. ⁵ The earth has transgressed because of its inhabitants, for they have broken the law, changed the statutes, and violated the eternal covenant. ⁶ Therefore, a curse devours the earth, and its inhabitants are guilty. Therefore, the people living on the earth are diminished, and only a few people remain. ⁷ Wine mourns, the vine weeps, all who were joyful in spirit are in sorrow. ⁸ The joy of the tambourine has ceased, the sound of revelry has ceased, the joy of the lyre has ceased. ⁹ In song they will no longer drink wine; the cup will be bitter to those who drink. ¹⁰ The city of ruin is broken, every house is closed, no one enters. ¹¹ They cry out over the wine in the streets, all joy is gone; the joy of the earth is stripped away. ¹² The city is left in ruins, and the gate is struck down. ¹³ For this will be within the earth among the peoples, like the gleaning of an olive tree, like the gleanings if the harvest is over. ¹⁴ They will raise their voice, they will sing with glory. They will shout from the sea. ¹⁵ Therefore, in the islands of the sea, the glory of the LORD will be honored, the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. ¹⁶ We have heard the songs from the ends of the earth. A witness to the righteous. I said, “Oh, how deep, how deep! Woe to the betrayers, they have betrayed, and the betrayer has been betrayed.” ¹⁷ Fear and dread and anguish are upon you, O inhabitant of the earth. ¹⁸ And it will be that the one fleeing from the sound of fear will fall into the trap, and the one who escapes from the trap will be caught in the snare, for from on high traps have been opened, and the foundations of the earth have trembled. ¹⁹ The land is shaken, the earth is in turmoil, the earth is tossed about. ²⁰ The earth reels like a drunkard and sways like a hut, and the guilt of it has grown heavy upon it, and it will fall and not rise again. ²¹ And in that day the LORD will punish the host in the heavens above, and the kings of the earth on the earth below. ²² And they will be gathered together, taken in captivity, locked in a pit, and shut up in a prison, and after many days they will be visited. ²³ And the moon will be abashed, and the sun will be put to shame, for the LORD of Hosts reigns on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and before the elders He will be honored.

---

Notes
- v. 16: “Oh, how deep, how deep!” is a poetic rendering of the Hebrew phrase meaning something like “how secret, how secret!” or “how mysterious, how mysterious!” depending on the interpretation of the root. The phrase is used to express deep emotion or shock at betrayal.
- v. 23: The Septuagint and some other traditions render this verse differently regarding the sun and moon, but the Hebrew text is clear and does not suggest a literal covering of the sun and moon.
