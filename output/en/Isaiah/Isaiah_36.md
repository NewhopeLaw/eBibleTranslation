# Isaiah 36

¹ In the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah's reign, Sennacherib king of Assyria marched against all the fortified cities of Judah and captured them. ² The king of Assyria sent Rabshakeh, his commander, with a large force from Lachish to Jerusalem, where he stood at the upper pool of the conduit in the field of the fuller. ³ Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph the recorder went out to meet him. ⁴ Rabshakeh said to them, "Tell Hezekiah, 'This is what the great king, king of Assyria, says: On what are you relying, Hezekiah, that you are defying me? ⁵ Do you think words and strategy can save you? If you trust in the Lord, who is he to deliver you from my hand? Now you are defying me.' ⁶ "You are relying on that broken reed, Egypt. If anyone leans on it, it will pierce their hand. So is Pharaoh, king of Egypt, for all who rely on him." ⁷ "And if you say to me, 'We trust in the Lord our God,' is it not he whom Hezekiah has removed from before you, saying, 'Worship only at this altar in Jerusalem'? ⁸ "Now, come to my master the king of Assyria and I will give you two thousand horses, if you are able to provide riders for them. ⁹ "How then can you resist even one of my master's lowliest officials? You are relying on Egypt for chariots and horsemen. ¹⁰ "Moreover, have I come up to destroy this land without the Lord? The Lord himself said to me, 'Go up to this land and destroy it.'" ¹¹ Eliakim, Shebna, and Joah said to Rabshakeh, "Please speak to us in Aramaic, for we understand it. Do not speak to us in Hebrew in the hearing of the people on the wall." ¹² But Rabshakeh said, "Is it for your master and for you that my master sent me to speak these words? Is it not for the people on the wall, to eat their own dung and drink their own urine with you?" ¹³ So Rabshakeh stood and called out in a loud voice in Hebrew, "Listen to the words of the great king, king of Assyria: ¹⁴ 'This is what the king says: Do not let Hezekiah deceive you. He cannot deliver you from my hand. ¹⁵ 'And do not let Hezekiah persuade you to trust in the Lord by saying, "The Lord will surely deliver us and this city will not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria." ¹⁶ 'Do not listen to Hezekiah. This is what the king of Assyria says: Make peace with me and come out to me. Each of you may eat from his own vineyard and his own fig tree, and drink water from his own cistern, ¹⁷ until I come and take you to a land like your own, a land of grain and wine, a land of bread and vineyards. ¹⁸ 'Do not let Hezekiah deceive you by saying, "The Lord will deliver us." Have the gods of the nations delivered anyone from my hand? ¹⁹ 'Where is the god of Hamath or the god of Arpad? Where is the god of Sepharvaim, or Hittite Samaria? Did any of them save their land from my hand? ²⁰ 'Which of all the gods of these nations has been able to rescue his people from my hand? So how can the Lord deliver Jerusalem from my hand?' ²¹ They remained silent and did not reply a word, for the king had commanded, "Do not answer him." ²² Then Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the scribe, and Joah the recorder went to Hezekiah, tearing their clothes, and told him the words of Rabshakeh.

Notes
- v. 8: "two thousand horses" — The Hebrew specifies "two thousand horses," but the intended meaning is likely symbolic or exaggerated. The translation follows the literal text.
