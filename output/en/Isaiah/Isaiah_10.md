# Isaiah 10

¹ Woe to those who make laws of iniquity, and who write down oppression as if it were justice. ² To twist the judgment of the poor and to rob the right of the people's needy, to make widows their spoil, and to treat orphans as their prey. ³ What will you do on the day of reckoning, when punishment falls from far away? To whom will you flee for help, and where will you leave your glory? ⁴ Unless they bow beneath the yoke and fall beneath the slain, in all this the LORD's anger has not turned back, and His hand is still stretched out. ⁵ Woe to Assyria, the rod of My anger and the staff of My wrath in their hands. ⁶ I will send it to a profane nation and command it to attack the people of My wrath, to seize spoil, to despoil, and to trample them down like clay underfoot. ⁷ But Assyria does not intend this, nor does its heart think thus. For destruction is in its heart, and the cutting off of many peoples is not its intent. ⁸ For it says, "Are not my commanders alone? Is not the one who rules over kings?" ⁹ "Have I not captured the city like Carchemish, like Carchemish? Have I not taken Arpad, the fortress of Hamath, and the city of Samaria?" ¹⁰ Just as My hand found the kingdoms of the idolaters, and their carved images in Jerusalem and in Samaria. ¹¹ As I did to Samaria and its idols, so will I do to Jerusalem and her altars. ¹² And it shall be, when the LORD finishes all His work on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, He will punish the fruit of Assyria's pride and the arrogance of his eyes. ¹³ For he said, "By the strength of my hand I have done this, by my wisdom I have planned it. I have removed the boundaries of peoples and seized their strongholds." ¹⁴ My hand found like a nest the riches of the nations, and like gathering eggs I gathered all the land. Not a wing flapped, not a beak opened, not a whisper escaped. ¹⁵ Does the ax boast about the one who cuts it? Does the saw pride itself over the one who wields it, as if a rod could boast about the one who lifts it, or a staff could say, "I am not wood"? ¹⁶ Therefore the LORD, the LORD of Hosts, will send leanness into Assyria's glory, and fire will consume his pride. ¹⁷ Israel's light will become fire, and his holiness a flame. It will blaze and devour the thorns and the reeds in one day. ¹⁸ The glory of its forest and its verdant trees will be consumed, from root to branch, and it will become like a burning wind. ¹⁹ The remaining trees of its forest will be numbered, and a child will write them down. ²⁰ Then on that day it will no longer be said, "The survivors of Israel will no longer rely on the one who strikes them, but they will lean on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, in truth." ²¹ The survivors will return, the survivors of Jacob, to the strong God. ²² For though My people Israel are like the sand of the sea, the survivors among them will be as a sieve's residue, a stream that flows over its bed with righteousness. ²³ For the LORD, the LORD of Hosts, will complete His work in all the earth. ²⁴ Therefore the LORD of Hosts says, "Do not fear, O My people who dwell in Zion, from Assyria, for with a rod He will strike you, and with a staff He will lift it against you, by the way of Egypt." ²⁵ For only a little while, and Assyria's anger will cease, and My wrath will be removed from their counsel. ²⁶ And the LORD will rouse a wind against Assyria like the wind that struck Midian at the hill of Oreb, and His wrath will be on the sea, by the way of Egypt. ²⁷ And it shall be on that day that the yoke on your shoulders will be removed, and the fear on your neck will be taken away, and the burden from your shoulders will be destroyed. ²⁸ He came to Aiath, passed through Migron, and left his equipment at Michmash. ²⁹ He crossed over to the pass of Geba, Ramah trembled, the city of Gibeath- Saul fled. ³⁰ Shout aloud, daughter of Gallim! Listen, O Laishah, you who are humble, for the Anathoth have been uprooted. ³¹ The people in the high places have wandered, the inhabitants of the hills have fled. ³² Even today, in Nob, to stand, he waves his hand toward the mountain, the daughter of Zion, the hill of Jerusalem. ³³ Look, the LORD of Hosts is sweeping the forest with a storm, and the tall trees will be felled, and the lofty will be brought low. ³⁴ The thorns of the forest will be cut with iron, and Lebanon will fall by the mighty.

---

Notes
- v. 14: The metaphor of the ax and the saw is rendered to reflect the Hebrew's rhetorical question about the tool boasting over its user, rather than a literal description of the Assyrians.
- v. 16: "leanness" is used to render the Hebrew word for "weakness" in the context of Assyria's pride being consumed by fire.
- v. 20: The phrase "in truth" is added to reflect the Hebrew's emphasis on reliance on the LORD being genuine, not based on human strength.
- v. 27: "the yoke on your shoulders" is a metaphor for the Assyrian oppression, not a literal yoke.
- v. 33: "swinging the forest with a storm" reflects the poetic imagery of the LORD's power to destroy, rather than a literal storm.
