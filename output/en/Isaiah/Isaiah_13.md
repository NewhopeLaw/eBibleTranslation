# Isaiah 13

¹ A burden concerning Babylon, which Isaiah son of Amoz saw. ² On a level mountain raise a signal, lift your voice to them, wave your hand, and open the gates for the nobles. ³ I have given commands to my consecrated ones, and I summoned my warriors to my wrath—those who revel in my pride. ⁴ A voice of a great multitude on the mountains, like the noise of many peoples, a voice of the uproar of kings and nations gathered together. The LORD of Hosts is giving orders for a battle force. ⁵ They are coming from a distant land, from the end of the heavens. The LORD and his warriors come to destroy the whole earth. ⁶ Wail, for the day of the LORD is near—it comes like a devastating flood from the Almighty. ⁷ Therefore, every hand will grow limp, and every human heart will melt. ⁸ The leaders will be terrified, and they will be seized with pain like a woman in childbirth. Each man will turn to his companion in terror, and their faces will turn ashen. ⁹ Behold, the day of the LORD is coming—cruel, brutal, and full of wrath—to make the earth a wasteland and to destroy its sinners from it. ¹⁰ For the stars of the heavens and their constellations will not shine their light; the sun will be darkened at its rising, and the moon will not give its light. ¹¹ I will bring calamity upon the earth and punish the wicked for their sins. I will destroy the pride of the lawless and humble the arrogance of the wicked. ¹² I will make humans more valuable than fine gold, and a person more precious than refined gold. ¹³ Therefore, the heavens will tremble, and the earth will be shaken from its place, at the wrath of the LORD of Hosts and on the day of his hot anger. ¹⁴ And it will be as if a gazelle is driven away and like sheep without a shepherd. Each man will turn from his people, and each man will flee to his land. ¹⁵ Everyone found will be pierced through, and all who are gathered will fall by the sword. ¹⁶ Their infants will be dashed before their eyes; their houses will be looted, and their wives seized. ¹⁷ I am raising up the Medes against them—those who do not value silver and have no interest in gold. ¹⁸ Their bows will shatter the young, and they will show no mercy to children. Their eyes will not pity the fruit of the womb. ¹⁹ And Babylon, that proud city of the Chaldean kings, will be like the way God overturned Sodom and Gomorrah. ²⁰ It will never again be inhabited, it will not remain from generation to generation. No Arab will dwell there, and shepherds will not rest there. ²¹ Wild beasts will lie there, and their houses will be filled with jackals. The daughters of the jackal and wild goats will dwell there. ²² The hyenas will settle among its ruins, and porcupines in its palaces. Its time is near, and its days will not last long.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "stars of the heavens and their constellations" is a literal rendering of כְּסִילֵיהֶם, where כְּסִיל is "constellation" or "herd" (the latter being an ancient poetic term for constellations).
- v. 12: The phrase "more valuable than fine gold" and "more precious than refined gold" reflects the Hebrew's contrast between human value and precious metals, using the same term for gold (פָּז) and another for refined gold (כֶּתֶם אוֹפִיר).
- v. 21: "daughters of the jackal" (בְּנוֹת יַֽעֲנָה) is a poetic term referring to the offspring of the jackal, used here to denote wild, desolate creatures.
- v. 22: "its time is near" and "its days will not last long" is a natural rendering of the Hebrew's emphasis on the swiftness and brevity of Babylon's fall.
