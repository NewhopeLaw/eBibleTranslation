# Isaiah 48

¹ Listen to this, house of Jacob, you called by the name Israel, and from Judah, those who have come forth, you who swear by the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, remembering not in truth nor in righteousness. ² For from the holy city you are called, and you lean on the God of Israel. The LORD of Hosts is his name. ³ I have declared the former things from long ago, and they came out from my mouth. I did them suddenly, and they arrived. ⁴ I know that you are stubborn, and your neck is an iron sinew, and your forehead is bronze. ⁵ I declared these things to you from long ago, before they came upon you, so that you would not say, "I made them," or "I commanded them." ⁶ You have heard, yet you did not know. I have made new things for you that you have not known, things that you have not heard of before. ⁷ Now they are created, not from long ago. You have not heard of them before. Do not say, "I already knew them." ⁸ You did not hear, you did not know. From the beginning your ears were not opened. For I knew that you would betray, and a lawbreaker has been called to you from the womb. ⁹ For the sake of my name I show my anger, and my glory I will bring upon you, so that I will not cut you off. ¹⁰ Look, I have refined you, not with silver, I have chosen you from the furnace of need. ¹¹ For my sake, for my sake I do this. How else can my name be profaned, and my glory given to another? ¹² Listen to me, Jacob, and Israel, whom I call. I am the first, and I am the last. ¹³ My hand founded the earth, and my right hand stretched out the heavens. I call to them, and they stand together. ¹⁴ Gather all of you and listen. Who among them has declared these? The one I love will accomplish my will in Babylon, and my offspring among the Chaldeans. ¹⁵ I myself have spoken, I myself have called him. I brought him, and I made his way prosperous. ¹⁶ Come near to me, listen to this. I did not speak in secret from the beginning, nor did I speak from the hidden. When it was there, I was there, and now the LORD God has sent me and his Spirit. ¹⁷ Thus says the LORD, your redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: I am your God, teaching you what is beneficial, guiding you in the way you should walk. ¹⁸ If only you had listened to my commandments, your peace would have been like a river, and your righteousness like the waves of the sea. ¹⁹ Then your descendants would have been like the sand, and the offspring of your womb like its dust. Their name would not be cut off, nor be destroyed before me. ²⁰ Go out from Babylon, flee from the Chaldeans. Proclaim with a cry of joy, declare this, proclaim it until the ends of the earth. Say, "The LORD has redeemed his servant Jacob." ²¹ They did not thirst in the desert, for he led them water from the rock. He split the rock and the waters poured out. ²² There is no peace, says the LORD, for the wicked.

---

Notes
- v. 1: The phrase "called by the name Israel" reflects the Hebrew's use of "nikra'im b'shem Yisrael," where "nikra'im" is plural for "called."
- v. 5: "I made them" and "I commanded them" refer to idolatry. The original Hebrew alludes to idol worship, but the translation avoids explicit mention of idols to preserve the ambiguity.
- v. 9: "For the sake of my name" reflects the Hebrew "lemashtay shemi," where "lemashtay" means "for the sake of."
- v. 11: "For my sake" is a translation of "lemashtay," again indicating purpose.
- v. 17: "Teaching you what is beneficial" reflects the context of instruction and guidance, rather than a strict "law."
- v. 22: The Hebrew uses "resha'im" ("wicked"), but the translation preserves the force of the original without assuming theological definitions.
