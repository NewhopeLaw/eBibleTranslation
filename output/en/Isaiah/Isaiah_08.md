# Isaiah 8

¹ The LORD said to me, "Take a large scroll and write on it with a man's stylus: 'A swift plunder, a quick spoil.'" ² And I will appoint for myself trustworthy witnesses: Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah. ³ I went to the prophetess, and she became pregnant and gave birth to a son. The LORD said to me, "Call his name Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz, ⁴ for before the boy knows how to call 'Father' or 'Mother,' he will carry off the wealth of Damascus and the plunder of Samaria before the king of Assyria." ⁵ The LORD added to speak to me again, saying: ⁶ "Because this people has rejected the gentle flow of the Shiloah, and rejoiced in Rezin and the son of Remaliah, ⁷ therefore behold, the Lord is raising up against them the waters of the mighty and numerous river—the king of Assyria and all his glory. He will rise over all their channels and go over all their embankments. ⁸ He will sweep over Judah, will flood and pass through, will reach the neck, and his wings will spread over the full breadth of your land, O my people." ⁹ "O peoples, be stunned and shudder, and listen, all distant lands. Gird yourselves and shudder, gird yourselves and shudder. ¹⁰ Devise a plan and it will be shattered; speak a word and it will not stand, for the Lord is with us." ¹¹ For thus said the LORD to me: "With a strong hand I will chastise this people, going in the way of this people, saying: ¹² Do not say that all the words of this people are bound, and do not fear its dread. Do not be terrified. ¹³ Sanctify the LORD of Hosts, and Him you shall fear, and Him you shall dread. ¹⁴ He will become a sanctuary and a stone of stumbling, a rock of offense to both houses of Israel, a snare and a trap to the inhabitants of Jerusalem. ¹⁵ And many will stumble over it, fall and break, be pierced and captured." ¹⁶ "Seal up the testimony, shut it in the law among my disciples." ¹⁷ "And I will wait for the one who hides His face from the house of Jacob, and I will hope in Him." ¹⁸ "Behold, I and the children whom the LORD has given me are signs and wonders in Israel from the LORD of Hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion." ¹⁹ "And if they say to you, 'Seek the mediums and the diviners who chirp and mutter,' should a people seek their God? Should they inquire of the living for the dead?" ²⁰ "To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, it will be as if there were no morning." ²¹ "And one shall pass through her in distress and hunger. And it will be, if he is hungry, he will rage and curse his king and his god and look upward. ²² "And to the earth he will look and see darkness and gloom, a cloud and blackness, a cloud spreading over, and a cloud of darkness." ²³ "For the land that was once not desolate will be troubled at first, the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, and at last the Way of the Sea beyond the Jordan, the land of the Gentiles."

---

Notes
- v. 14: "A sanctuary and a stone of stumbling, a rock of offense" — the Hebrew terms are metaphorical, and the translation follows the idiomatic meaning, not a direct literal rendering.  
- v. 15: "Will stumble over it, fall and break, be pierced and captured" — the Hebrew uses a sequence of verbs to emphasize the consequences of rejecting the divine sign.  
- v. 16: "Seal up the testimony, shut it in the law among my disciples" — the translation reflects the poetic structure and the symbolic act of preservation.
