# Isaiah 62

¹ For the sake of Zion I will not be silent, and for the sake of Jerusalem I will not rest, until her righteousness shines like dawn and her salvation blazes like a torch. ² Nations will see your righteousness, and all kings your glory. A new name will be given to you, which the mouth of the Lord will establish. ³ You will be a crown of beauty in the hand of the Lord, and a royal diadem in the hand of your God. ⁴ No longer will you be called Forsaken, and your land will no longer be called Desolate. You will be called My Delight, and your land Inhabited, for the Lord delights in you, and your land will be settled. ⁵ Just as a young man marries a bride, so your children will marry you. Just as a bridegroom rejoices over his bride, so your God will rejoice over you. ⁶ On the walls of Jerusalem I have stationed watchmen all day and all night, who will never be silent. Those who declare the name of the Lord, let them not be silent. ⁷ Do not let them be silent until justice is established, until Jerusalem is praised throughout the land. ⁸ The Lord has sworn by His right hand and by the strength of His arm: I will no longer give your grain to be food for your enemies, nor will the children of foreigners drink the wine you have labored to produce. ⁹ But those who gather it will eat, and they will praise the Lord. Those who gather it will drink in My holy courts. ¹⁰ Go through, go through the gates. Prepare the road for the people. Clear it, clear it—make the path straight. Remove the stones from the road; raise a banner to the nations. ¹¹ Look, the Lord has proclaimed to the ends of the earth: Say to the daughter of Zion, Look, your salvation comes, His reward is with Him, and His work is before Him. ¹² And they will call them the Holy People, the Redeemed of the Lord. To you it will be said, Sought-Out City, not Abandoned.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "justice shines like dawn" and "salvation blazes like a torch" reflect poetic imagery, with "justice" and "salvation" as personified virtues.
- v. 4: "Forsaken" and "Desolate" are interpretive renderings of עֲזוּבָה and שְׁמָמָה, which are often linked to the exile context.
- v. 12: "Holy People, the Redeemed of the Lord" reflects the use of קֹדֶשׁ and גְּאוּלֵי. The final title for Zion is rendered as "Sought-Out City, not Abandoned," based on דְּרוּשָׁה and לֹא נֶעֱזָֽבָה.
