# Isaiah 65

¹ I was found by those who did not ask, discovered by those who did not seek me. I said, "Here I am, here I am," to a people who did not call my name. ² I stretched out my hands all day to a people who rebelled, walking in a crooked path, following their own corrupt thoughts. ³ A people who provoked me continually, sacrificing in the gardens and offering incense on the bricks to things that are not gods. ⁴ Those who dwell in tombs and caves, sleeping in the night, eating pig flesh and drinking broth from polluted vessels; their utensils are all defiled. ⁵ Those who say, "Stay away from us! Don't come near, for we are too pure for you." But these are the ones who have become smoke in my nostrils, a fire that burns constantly. ⁶ For behold, it is written before me; I will not remain silent, but I will repay their actions into their own laps. ⁷ Their sins and the sins of their ancestors, says the LORD, which they burned on the mountains and offered on the hills, have brought shame upon me. I will measure out their punishment in full upon their laps. ⁸ This is what the LORD says: Just as the grape is found in the winepress and someone says, "Don't crush it, for there is a blessing in it," so I will do for the sake of my servants, to ensure not all is destroyed. ⁹ I will bring forth descendants from Jacob and inheritors from Judah. They will possess the holy mountain, and my chosen ones and servants will dwell there. ¹⁰ The Sharon will become a grazing ground for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a resting place for herds, for my people who seek me. ¹¹ But you who abandon the LORD, who forget my holy mountain, who set up a table for Gad and fill a cup for Meni, ¹² I will number you for the sword, and all of you will fall to the slaughter. You will be shattered, because I called and you did not answer; I spoke and you did not listen, but you chose to do evil in my sight and to pursue what I did not desire. ¹³ Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: My servants will eat, but you will be hungry. My servants will drink, but you will be thirsty. My servants will rejoice, but you will be ashamed. ¹⁴ My servants will sing for joy from a glad heart, but you will cry out in pain from a broken spirit, and you will wail from a crushed soul. ¹⁵ You will be left as a curse for my chosen ones, and the LORD will destroy you, while for my servants I will call forth a new name. ¹⁶ The one who is blessed in the land will be blessed by the God of truth, and the one who takes an oath in the land will swear by the God of truth, because the former troubles will be forgotten and hidden from my sight. ¹⁷ For behold, I am creating new heavens and a new earth. The former things will not be remembered, nor will they come to mind. ¹⁸ Instead, joy and gladness will be found there. For I am creating Jerusalem as a source of joy and my people as a cause of rejoicing. ¹⁹ I will rejoice over Jerusalem and be glad over my people. There will be no more heard in it the sound of weeping or the voice of crying. ²⁰ No one there will die young, and no old man will not complete his days. For the young man will die at a hundred, and the sinner who is a hundred years old will be cursed. ²¹ They will build houses and live in them; they will plant vineyards and eat their fruit. ²² They will not build and another live in it; they will not plant and another eat it. For the days of my people will be like the days of a tree, and the work of their hands will endure for my chosen. ²³ They will not labor in vain, nor will they give birth to children for disaster, for they are the blessed seed of the LORD, and their descendants will be with them. ²⁴ Before they call, I will answer; while they are still speaking, I will hear. ²⁵ The wolf and the lamb will graze together, the leopard and the young goat will rest together, the calf and the young lion will lie down together, and the dust will be the food of the serpent. None will harm or destroy on all my holy mountain, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 11: "Meni" is a proper name, likely referring to a deity. It is retained as is, since the context is religious and it functions as a name.
- v. 15: "Meni" is repeated in v. 11, so it is consistent with the glossary to keep it as is.
- v. 16: "God of truth" reflects the context of "elohim emet," where "emeth" is rendered as "truth" rather than "faithfulness," given the emphasis on trustworthiness in the context.
- v. 25: "Leopard" is used for "tal" (תל), which is commonly identified as a leopard in biblical contexts.
