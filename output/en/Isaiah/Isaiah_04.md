# Isaiah 4

¹ On that day, seven women will grasp a single man, saying, "We will eat our bread and wear our garments, as long as your name is called over us. Please, take away our disgrace." ² On that day, the shoot of the LORD will sprout for beauty and for glory, and the fruit of the earth will be for splendor and for adornment, for the survivors of Israel. ³ The survivors who remain in Zion and those left in Jerusalem will be declared holy; they will all be called those who are written for life in Jerusalem. ⁴ For the LORD will wash away the filth of the sons and the daughters of Zion and will purge the blood from the midst of her with a spirit of judgment and a spirit of fire. ⁵ And the LORD will create over all the places of Mount Zion and over its place of assembly a canopy of clouds by day and a smoke and a glowing fire by night, for over all its glory it will be a covering. ⁶ And it will be a booth for the shadow of the day from the heat, and for a refuge and a place of concealment from the wind and the rain.

---

Notes
- v. 2: "the shoot of the LORD" reflects the Hebrew "ṣemāḵ YHWH" (צֶמַח יְהוָה), where "ṣemāḵ" (צֶמַח) is a shoot or sprout. The Septuagint reads "ὁ θεὸς ἐν βουλῇ" (God in counsel), but the Hebrew is clear and does not support that translation.
