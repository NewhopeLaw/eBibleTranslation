# Isaiah 34

¹ Draw near, nations, to listen; and peoples, give ear. Let the earth and all it contains hear; the world and all its people. ² For the anger of the LORD is against all the nations, and his wrath against all their host. He has doomed them, giving them up to slaughter. ³ Their slain will be flung about, their corpses will rise in the stench, and the mountains will be gored with their blood. ⁴ All the host of the heavens will vanish, the sky itself will roll up like a scroll, and every host of it will fall like leaves from the vine, like falling leaves from a fig tree. ⁵ For my sword has been drunk in the heavens. Behold, it will descend upon Edom and upon the people of destruction for judgment. ⁶ The sword of the LORD is filled with blood, it is drenched with fat, with the blood of rams and lambs, with the fat of kids and rams. For it is a sacrifice to the LORD in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in the land of Edom. ⁷ And the strong ones will fall with them, the bulls and the rams. The land will be drunk with their blood, and their fat will drench it. ⁸ For it is the day of the LORD's vengeance, a year of recompense for the cause of Zion. ⁹ And her streams will become pitch, her soil into sulfur, and her land into burning pitch. ¹⁰ Night and day, it will not be quenched. Its smoke will rise forever, from generation to generation it will be desolate, for an eternal destruction, forever. ¹¹ It will be inhabited by jackals and hyenas, and the howler and the raven will dwell there. A line of wildness will be stretched over it, and its stones will be heaps of ruin. ¹² Her rulers will be none, for all her kings and governors will be no more. ¹³ Thorny bushes will spring up in her cities, and thistles in her strongholds. It will become a haunt for jackals, a pasture for ostriches. ¹⁴ The wild beasts will meet the jackals, and the satyr will cry to his neighbor. Only there will the howler find rest and the satyr a place to lie. ¹⁵ There the viper will coil and bear young, and the raven will brood there. Only there will the jackals gather and the she-doves find rest. ¹⁶ Seek the book of the LORD, and read— not one of them is left. The she-doves have not been counted, for the mouth of the LORD has commanded, and his spirit has gathered them. ¹⁷ And he has cast their lot for them, and his hand has divided them by a line. They will possess it forever, from generation to generation they will dwell there.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "fall like leaves from the vine, like falling leaves from a fig tree" — the Hebrew uses vivid natural imagery to convey total destruction; "falling leaves" is a natural English rendering of the metaphor.
- v. 10: "for an eternal destruction, forever" — the Hebrew uses "נְצָחִים" and "לְנֵצַח" which both mean "forever" or "eternally," hence the doubling in the translation.
- v. 15: "she-doves" — the Hebrew term "אִשָּׁה רְעוּתָהּ" is translated here as "she-doves," reflecting a poetic and contextually fitting rendering.
