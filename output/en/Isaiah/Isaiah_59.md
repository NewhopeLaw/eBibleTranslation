# Isaiah 59

¹ Is it that the hand of the LORD is too short to save, or that his ear is too dull to hear? ² But your sins separate you from your God, and your iniquities have hidden his face from you, so that he does not hear. ³ For your hands are stained with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken lies, and your tongue has uttered injustice. ⁴ No one calls out in righteousness, and no judgment is done with integrity. They trust in falsehood and speak emptily, conceiving trouble and giving birth to iniquity. ⁵ They hatch the eggs of vipers and weave a spider’s web. Whoever eats their eggs will die, and what is crushed will burst out into a snake. ⁶ Their webs will not serve as clothing, and their works will not cover those who wear them. For their works are works of iniquity, and their hands do violent deeds. ⁷ Their feet rush to do evil, and they hasten to shed innocent blood. Their thoughts are thoughts of iniquity, devising oppression and destruction in their paths. ⁸ They do not know the way of peace, and there is no justice in their roads. Their paths are crooked; whoever walks in them does not know peace. ⁹ Therefore, justice has been removed from us, and righteousness does not reach us. We wait for light, but it is darkness. We long for dawn, but we walk in the night. ¹⁰ We grope along the wall like the blind, feeling our way like those without eyes. We stumble at midday as though it were twilight, like those on the verge of death. ¹¹ We are all like a wild beast, like a bird without a nest, waiting for justice, but there is none, for salvation is far from us. ¹² For our offenses are many before you, and our sins have stood against us. For our offenses are with us, and we know our iniquities. ¹³ We have rebelled and denied the LORD, and we have turned our backs on our God. We have spoken falsely and acted perversely, devising evil from our hearts. ¹⁴ Justice has been turned back, and righteousness stands far away. For truth has stumbled in the street, and justice cannot enter. ¹⁵ Truth has vanished, and integrity is found nowhere. The LORD has looked and was displeased, for there is no justice. ¹⁶ He saw that there was no man, and he was astonished that no one intervened. Then his own arm brought salvation, and his own righteousness upheld him. ¹⁷ He clothed himself with righteousness as a coat of mail, and a helmet of salvation was on his head. He put on garments of vengeance and wrapped himself in a cloak of zeal. ¹⁸ According to their deeds, so will he repay them, wrath for their enemies, retribution for those who hate him, he will repay them. ¹⁹ From the west, the name of the LORD will be feared, and from the east, his glory. For the coming of the fierce wrath of the LORD will be like a stream, and the breath of the LORD will rush with fury. ²⁰ A redeemer will come to Zion, and to those in Jacob who turn from transgression, says the LORD. ²¹ And this is my covenant with them, says the LORD: my Spirit upon you, and my words in your mouth. They will not depart from your mouth, nor from the mouth of your children, nor from the mouth of your children’s children, says the LORD, forever and ever.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "Is it that the hand of the LORD is too short to save..." — the Hebrew uses a rhetorical structure denying a false assumption, hence the translation "Is it that..." rather than "The hand of the LORD is not too short..."
- v. 21: "my Spirit upon you, and my words in your mouth" — the Hebrew uses the second person singular, but the context implies a covenant for the people (collective "you"). The translation treats it as such.
