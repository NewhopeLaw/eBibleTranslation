# Isaiah 49

¹ Listen to me, islands, and pay attention, distant nations. From the womb, the LORD called me by name. From the birth-pangs of my mother, my name was known to him. ² He made my mouth like a sharp sword, and under the shadow of his hand he hid me. He made me a polished arrow, and in his quiver he concealed me. ³ And he said to me, “You are my servant, Israel, in whom I will be glorified.” ⁴ But I said, “I have labored in vain, I have spent my strength for nothing. My judgment is with the LORD, and my work with my God.” ⁵ And now says the LORD, who formed me from the womb to be his servant, to bring Jacob back to him, and that Israel might be gathered to him, “I am honored in the eyes of the LORD, and my God is my strength.” ⁶ And he said, “It is too small a thing that you should be my servant to raise up the tribes of Jacob and to restore the survivors of Israel. I will also make you a light to the nations, that my salvation may reach to the end of the earth.” ⁷ Thus says the LORD, the redeemer of Israel, the Holy One of Israel: “To the despised one, to the one abhorred by the nations, to the servant of rulers—kings will see and stand, rulers will bow, because of the LORD, who is faithful, the Holy One of Israel, who has chosen you.” ⁸ Thus says the LORD: “In the time of favor I have answered you, in the day of salvation I have helped you. I have formed you, and I have given you as a covenant to the people, to establish the land, to restore its desolate places. ⁹ To say to those in chains, ‘Come out,’ to those in darkness, ‘Be revealed.’ They shall feed along all roads, their pasture shall be on all high places. ¹⁰ They shall not hunger or thirst, nor shall the scorching wind or the sun strike them. For he who shows mercy will guide them, and by fountains of water he will lead them. ¹¹ And I will make all the mountains a road, and all the valleys a highway. ¹² Behold, these are coming from afar, and these from the north and from the sea, and these from the land of Sinim. ¹³ Rejoice, heavens, and be glad, earth; break forth in singing, you mountains. For the LORD has comforted his people and shown mercy to his afflicted ones. ¹⁴ But Zion said, “The LORD has forsaken me, and my God has forgotten me.” ¹⁵ Can a mother forget the child at her breast, or be without compassion for the son of her womb? Even if these should forget, I will not forget you. ¹⁶ See, I have inscribed you on my palms; your walls are ever before me. ¹⁷ Your children will come quickly from your destroyers, and those who laid waste to you will depart from you. ¹⁸ Lift up your eyes all around and see: they all gather to you. The living One says, “I am the LORD, and none will be absent. All of them will come to you as a garment, and you will be adorned like a bride. ¹⁹ For your ruins and your desolation and the land that was laid waste—now they will be filled with inhabitants, and those who devoured you will be far from you. ²⁰ You will say in your heart, “Who bore these for me, when I was barren and a widow? And who raised these? I was left alone, yet these are where?” ²¹ And you will say in your heart, “Who gave birth to these for me? I was a widow and barren, yet who raised these? I was left alone, and these—where are they?” ²² Thus says the Lord GOD: “I will raise my hand to the nations and lift up my banner to the peoples. And they will bring your sons in their arms and your daughters on their shoulders. ²³ And kings will be your foster fathers, and their queens your nursing mothers. They will bow down to you with their faces to the earth, and lick the dust of your feet. And you will know that I am the LORD, and those who wait for me will not be put to shame.” ²⁴ Can the prey be taken from the mighty, or the captive rescued from the tyrant? ²⁵ Thus says the LORD: “Yes, even the captive of the mighty will be taken, and the prey of the tyrant will be rescued. I will contend with those who contend with you, and I will save your children. ²⁶ And I will feed your oppressors with their own flesh, and they will drink their own blood like new wine. And all flesh will know that I am the LORD your savior, the strong redeemer of Jacob.”

---

Notes
- v. 1: "islands" and "distant nations" reflect the address to the wider world, as in the original Hebrew.
- v. 12: "land of Sinim" is a rare term, likely referring to an eastern or distant land, possibly a translation of the Hebrew for "Sinai" or a variant of "Sena," but in context it means a far-off land.
- v. 17: "The living One says" is an interpretive rendering of "חַי־אָנִי," where "I am the living One," emphasizing divine eternity and presence.
- v. 25: "tyrant" is used for עָרִיץ, which carries the sense of a violent, lawless person, not just a ruler.
