# Isaiah 19

¹ Woe to Egypt! For the LORD rides on a swift cloud and is coming to Egypt. The gods of Egypt will tremble before him, and the heart of Egypt will grow faint within it. ² I will incite Egypt against itself, and people will fight brother against brother and neighbor against neighbor, city against city and kingdom against kingdom. ³ The spirit of Egypt will grow faint within it, and I will consume its counsel. And they will seek the gods, the idols, and the household gods, and the sorcerers. ⁴ I will hand Egypt over to the power of harsh rulers, and a stern king will rule over them, says the Lord of Hosts. ⁵ The waters will be cut off from the sea, the river will dry up and become barren. ⁶ The canals will run dry, the banks of the river will be destroyed, all the reservoirs of water will dry up, and papyrus and reeds will wither. ⁷ The lush plants along the river, and all the seed sown by the river will dry up, scattered and gone. ⁸ The fishermen will lament, as will all who cast nets in the river, those who spread nets and those who fish on the surface of the waters. ⁹ The weavers of linen and the spinners of flax will be put to shame. ¹⁰ All who work in the loom will be crushed, and all who make beer will mourn, their spirits broken. ¹¹ The leaders of Zoan are fools, the wise counselors of Pharaoh have given bad advice. How can you say, “I am the son of wise men, the son of ancient kings”? ¹² Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you, and let them know what the LORD of Hosts has planned for Egypt. ¹³ The leaders of Zoan have been thrown into confusion; the leaders of Memphis have been carried away. And Egypt will be led astray by every group. ¹⁴ For the LORD has clouded Egypt’s judgment, and Egypt has been led astray in all its works, like a drunkard who staggers in his vomit. ¹⁵ Then Egypt will have no success, neither beginning nor end, neither head nor tail. ¹⁶ On that day the Egyptians will be like women, trembling and terrified by the flapping of the hand of the LORD of Hosts, which he raises against them. ¹⁷ And the land of Judah will be a terror to Egypt, and everyone who mentions it will be afraid, because of the plan the LORD of Hosts has made for them. ¹⁸ On that day five cities in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear to the LORD of Hosts. One of them will be called the City of Destruction. ¹⁹ On that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the land of Egypt and a monument at its border to the LORD. ²⁰ It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt, for when they cry out to the LORD because of their oppressors, he will send a savior and a redeemer to rescue them. ²¹ The LORD will be known to Egypt, and the Egyptians will know the LORD on that day. They will offer sacrifices and make vows to the LORD and fulfill them. ²² The LORD will strike Egypt with a plague, and then heal it. He will turn to them and heal them. ²³ On that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria, and the Assyrians will come into Egypt, and the Egyptians will go into Assyria. The Egyptians will serve the Assyrians. ²⁴ On that day Israel will be the third partner with Egypt and Assyria, blessed among the nations. ²⁵ The LORD of Hosts says, "Blessed is my people, Egypt, and my work, Assyria, and my inheritance, Israel."

---

Notes
- v. 11: "I am the son of wise men, the son of ancient kings" – the Hebrew is somewhat ambiguous, but the meaning is conveyed as a rhetorical question challenging the wisdom of Egypt’s leaders.
- v. 18: "the City of Destruction" – the Hebrew is "the city of the cistern" (עיר החרס), which may be a play on words or a reference to a symbolic name. "City of Destruction" is a plausible interpretation, though "City of the Cistern" is also valid.
