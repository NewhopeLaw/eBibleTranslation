# Isaiah 45

¹ This is what the LORD says to his anointed, to Cyrus, whom I have strengthened with my right hand, to subdue nations before him and to undo the sashes of kings, to open doors before him so that gates will not be shut. ² I will go before you and make the rough places level. I will break down bronze doors and shatter iron bars. ³ I will give you treasures hidden in the dark and secrets buried in secret places, so that you will know that I am the LORD, the one who calls you by name, the God of Israel. ⁴ For the sake of my servant Jacob, and Israel, my chosen, I have called you by name and given you a place of honor, though you did not know me. ⁵ I am the LORD, and there is no other. I will strengthen you, and you did not know me. ⁶ So that those from the east of the sun and from the west may know that there is none besides me, I am the LORD, and there is no other. ⁷ I create light and make darkness. I bring peace and create calamity; I, the LORD, do all these things. ⁸ Let the heavens pour down from above, and let the clouds rain out justice. Let the earth open and let salvation and righteousness spring up together. I, the LORD, have created these. ⁹ Alas, the potter, who made the clay, and the plowman, who tills the soil—would the clay say to the potter, "What are you doing?" or the work have no hands? ¹⁰ Would the created thing say to the creator, "What are you going to give birth to?" or the mother, "What will you bring forth?" ¹¹ This is what the LORD, the holy one of Israel, the maker of the coming things, says: "Ask me about my sons, or give orders about the works of my hands." ¹² I made the earth and the man upon it. I stretched out my hands to the heavens and gave orders to every host of them. ¹³ I will raise him up with righteousness, and all his ways will be straight. He will build my city and send out my captives without price or ransom, says the LORD of Hosts. ¹⁴ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: "Egypt has labored in vain, and the commerce of Cush is empty, and the men of Sheba, who are so tall, will come to you. They will be your servants and follow you, bound in chains. They will come to you and bow down to you. They will pray to you, for in you is the God, and there is no other besides you." ¹⁵ Indeed, you are God, though we did not know it. The God of Israel, the Savior. ¹⁶ They will all be put to shame and confounded together. The makers of idols will be confounded, those who take up a wooden idol. ¹⁷ Israel is saved by the LORD. Eternal salvation will never be put to shame, nor will it be confounded, for all generations evermore. ¹⁸ For this is what the LORD says, the one who made the heavens: "I am the God who laid the foundation of the earth, who did not make it in vain, but made it to be inhabited. I am the LORD, and there is none else." ¹⁹ I did not speak in secret, nor in a land of darkness. I did not say to the descendants of Jacob, 'Seek in vain.' I am the one who speaks righteousness and declares truth. ²⁰ Gather together and come close, all you who are fleeing from the nations. Those who carry their wooden idols and pray to gods who cannot save. ²¹ Declare and bring forward, let them advise together. Who has ever heard of this before? Who has announced it from ancient times? I am the LORD, and there is no other. I am the righteous God and the Savior, and there is none besides me. ²² Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth. For I am God, and there is none else. ²³ By myself I have sworn; justice will come from my mouth, and it will not return. Every knee will bow to me, and every tongue will swear by me. ²⁴ Only in the LORD will this be said: "Righteousness and strength, let him come to me, and let all who oppose him be put to shame." ²⁵ In the LORD the descendants of Israel will be justified and will glory.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "his anointed" refers to Cyrus, not a messianic figure, as the context indicates a political leader.
- v. 14: "the men of Sheba" refers to people from the region of Sheba, likely present-day Yemen.
- v. 24: "Only in the LORD will this be said" reflects the Hebrew construction, where the statement is attributed to the LORD's declaration about himself.
