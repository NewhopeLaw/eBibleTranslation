# Isaiah 9

¹ The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light. Those who dwelt in the land of death's shadow, light has shone upon them. ² You have multiplied the nation, you have increased its joy. They rejoice before you as with the joy of harvest, as with the gladness when dividing spoils. ³ For as in the day of Midian, the yoke that weighed them down and the rod that struck their necks, you have shattered it. ⁴ For every boot that stomps and every robe stained with blood will be fuel for the fire, consumed by flame. ⁵ For a child is born to us, a son is given to us, and the government rests on his shoulder. His name is called Wonderful Counselor, God the Mighty, Father of Eternity, Prince of Peace. ⁶ Of the increase of his government and of peace, there will be no end. Upon the throne of David and over his kingdom, to establish it and to uphold it with justice and righteousness, from now and forever. The zeal of the LORD of Hosts will accomplish this. ⁷ A message has gone forth from the LORD to Jacob, and it has fallen upon Israel. ⁸ And all the people—Ephraim and the one dwelling in Samaria—will know in pride and in a heart grown arrogant: ⁹ "The bricks have fallen, let us build with stones; the fig trees are cut down, let us replace them with cedars." ¹⁰ And the LORD will uproot the enemies of Rezin from over him and will cover his adversaries. ¹¹ Aram from the east and Philistia from the west will devour Israel with every mouth. Yet for all this, His anger has not turned back, and His hand is still stretched out. ¹² And the people did not return until struck, and they did not seek the LORD of Hosts. ¹³ And the LORD will cut off from Israel the head and the tail, the palm and the thornbush, in one day. ¹⁴ The elder and the honorable is the head, and the prophet who teaches falsehood is the tail. ¹⁵ And the ones who bless this people will be deceivers, and those they trust will be swallowed. ¹⁶ Therefore, the LORD will not rejoice in their young, and He will not have compassion on their orphans, for they are all wicked and evil, and every mouth speaks folly. For all this, His anger has not turned back, and His hand is still stretched out. ¹⁷ For iniquity has burned like fire, a flame and a thornbush will devour, and it will blaze among the branches of the forest, and the pride of thick smoke will darken. ¹⁸ Through the burning of the LORD of Hosts the land will be scorched, and the people will be as fuel for fire. One man will not spare his brother. ¹⁹ And he will seize from the right and be hungry, and eat from the left, yet not be satisfied. A man will eat the flesh of his own arm. ²⁰ Manasseh will attack Ephraim, and Ephraim will attack Manasseh, and together they will be against Judah. Yet for all this, His anger has not turned back, and His hand is still stretched out.

Notes
- v. 1: "land of death's shadow" renders בְּאֶרֶץ צַלְמָוֶת (tzalmavet), a rare term often interpreted as "land of the shadow of death."
- v. 5: "Father of Eternity" captures אֲבִי עַד (Avi Ad), where "Ad" is a poetic form of "everlasting" or "eternal."
- v. 6: "the zeal of the LORD of Hosts" reflects the phrase קִנְאַת יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת (kinat YHWH tseva'ot), where "zeal" is a standard rendering for kinah in this context.
- v. 19: "eat the flesh of his own arm" is a literal but vivid rendering of יֹאכֵֽלוּ בְּשַׂר־זְרֹעוֹ (yokhelu basar zerav), emphasizing self-destruction.
