# Isaiah 12

¹ On that day you will say, “I will praise you, O LORD, for you were angry with me, you turned your anger away, and you comforted me.” ² “Behold, my God is my salvation—I will trust and not fear, for the glory of the LORD is my strength. He has become my salvation.” ³ With joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation. ⁴ On that day you will say, “Give thanks to the LORD, proclaim his name, make known among the nations his deeds, declare that his name is exalted.” ⁵ Sing to the LORD, for he has done great things; let this be proclaimed throughout the whole earth. ⁶ Rejoice and be glad, O inhabitants of Zion, for great is the Holy One of Israel in your midst.

---

Notes
- v. 2: “He has become my salvation” reflects the switch from second-person address in the first part of the verse to a third-person summary of God’s action in the second. The Hebrew uses a narrative-style shift common in prophetic poetry.
