# Isaiah 30

¹ Woe to rebellious children, says the LORD, who make plans without me and forge decrees without my spirit, adding sin upon sin! ² Those who go down to Egypt, not asking me for help, trusting in the shadow of Pharaoh. ³ But Pharaoh will be a covering for you, a disgrace for those who trust in the shadow of Egypt. ⁴ For its officials will be in Zoan, its messengers will perish — they have fled, and they will be brought to an end. ⁵ All who hire help from a people who will not help them, who will not be a help but a disgrace and a reproach. ⁶ The burden of beasts from the south, from a land of distress and anguish — leopards and lions, lionesses and their cubs — they will carry their wealth on donkeys, on camels, and it will not help them. ⁷ Egypt is worthless and empty. Its help is vain. Therefore I call her "Rahab," for they have not acted wisely. ⁸ Now go and write it down for them, set it in writing as a lasting testimony until the end of time. ⁹ For this is a people of rebellion, deceitful children, who do not want to listen to the teaching of the LORD. ¹⁰ They say to the prophets, "Do not see for us," to the seers, "Do not foresee for us. Speak to us a pleasant word, prophesy a vision, show us a sign of peace." ¹¹ Turn away from the road, remove the path, take away from us the Holy One of Israel. ¹² Therefore this is what the Holy One of Israel says: Because you have despised this word and trusted in oppression and deceit, and leaned on it, ¹³ this iniquity will fall upon you like a breaking wall, suddenly collapsing, like a city that is struck down — suddenly the fall will come. ¹⁴ It will be crushed like the shattering of a potter’s jar, shattered to the point of no repair; they will not find a shard in it to make a fire to kindle, or to take water from a cistern. ¹⁵ For this is what the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, says: In returning and resting you will be saved, in quietness and trust will be your strength — but you would not. ¹⁶ You said, "No, for on horses we will flee. Therefore we will flee," and "On a swift rider we will ride. Therefore they will be swift to pursue you." ¹⁷ A thousand will flee at the roar of one, and at the cry of five you will all flee — until you are left like a terebinth on the mountain and like a banner on the hill. ¹⁸ Therefore the Lord will wait to be gracious to you, and in mercy He will rise to show you compassion — because the Lord is a God of justice. Happy are those who trust in Him. ¹⁹ For the people who dwell in Zion, in Jerusalem, will no longer weep. When He shows compassion, He will hear you, when you cry out — He will answer you. ²⁰ And the Lord will give you bitter bread and water of bitterness to drink, and your eyes will see your teachers no more. ²¹ And your ears will hear a word behind you, saying, "This is the way, walk in it," if you will believe, or if you will stubbornly refuse. ²² Then you will defile the gilding of your idols, the gold of your masks — you will strip them away like a garment, and say to them, "Out, vanish!" ²³ Then He will give you rain for your seed, and the harvest of the earth will be food and oil in abundance. Your flocks will graze in broad pastures in that day. ²⁴ The thousand oxen and the ten-thousand of the herd that work the land will eat crushed grain in the manger, in the threshing floor. ²⁵ On every high mountain and every lofty hill there will be flowing water in that day — the day when the slain fall and the towers fall. ²⁶ The light of the moon will be like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun will be seven times brighter, like the light of seven days — in the day when the Lord binds the wound of His people and heals the blow of His people. ²⁷ Behold, the name of the Lord comes from afar, burning with anger and full of fury — the weight of His words is heavy with wrath, and His tongue is a consuming fire. ²⁸ His breath is like a stream surging to the throat, dividing nations with a reed of falsehood, and the wind of His breath will strike the face of the peoples. ²⁹ You will have music like a holy feast, joy of heart like one walking with a lyre, coming to the mountain of the Lord, to the Rock of Israel. ³⁰ And the Lord will make the sound of His glory heard, and He will show the arm of His strength in wrath, with fire consuming, with thunder and storm, with hailstones. ³¹ For at the sound of the Lord Assyria will fall, struck down by the rod. ³² And all who carry a rod, set in place, on which the Lord will lay His hand — with drums and with harps, with battles they will be struck down. ³³ For it is prepared from of old, from ancient days. It was set in place — a deep pit, a heap of firewood, a flame of burning sulfur roiling in it, by the breath of the Lord.
