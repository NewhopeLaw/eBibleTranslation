# Isaiah 26

¹ On that day a song will be sung in the land of Judah: A strong city is our salvation, and walls and ramparts have been set up. ² Open the gates, and the righteous people who keep faith will enter. ³ He who guards in truth will be steady, he who keeps peace will rest secure, for in him we trust. ⁴ Trust in the LORD forever, for the LORD is an eternal rock. ⁵ He has brought down the lofty dwellers; the strong city he will bring low, bringing it down to the dust, until it reaches the ground. ⁶ The feet of the poor will tread it, the steps of the needy will press it. ⁷ The way of the righteous is level, the paths of the upright are straight. ⁸ Yes, the way of the LORD is justice. For his name we have longed, for his remembrance our soul has yearned. ⁹ My soul has longed for you in the night, my spirit has sought you at dawn — for the judgment you have given is on the earth, and the teaching of justice is learned by those who dwell in the world. ¹⁰ The wicked have been judged, but they have not learned righteousness. On earth, they persist in wrongdoing; they do not see the glory of the LORD. ¹¹ O LORD, raise your hand, let them not see it. Let them see and be put to shame. Let the jealous rage of your people consume them like fire. ¹² O LORD our God, you will grant us peace, for you have done all our works. ¹³ O LORD our God, when rulers above us were not you, we remembered your name alone. ¹⁴ The dead do not live, the departed do not rise. You have visited them and destroyed them, and all their memory has perished. ¹⁵ You have increased the nation, O LORD, you have increased the nation, and you have brought glory to the ends of the earth. ¹⁶ O LORD, in distress we have called to you, in distress we have learned your discipline. ¹⁷ Like a woman in labor who draws near to birth, groaning in her pangs, so have we been before you, O LORD. ¹⁸ We have conceived in terror, we have given birth in pain, for your breath of salvation we have labored — but the earth has not brought forth, and the inhabitants of the world have not fallen. ¹⁹ Your dead will live, their corpses will rise. Wake, and sing for joy, you who dwell in the dust, for your dew, the dew of light, is the dew of the LORD, and the earth will give birth to the dead. ²⁰ Go into your chambers, my people, and shut your doors behind you; hide for a little while, until the anger passes. ²¹ For behold, the LORD is coming from his place to punish the sinners who dwell on the earth. The earth will reveal its blood, and no longer conceal its slain.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "an eternal rock" — the Hebrew term "צֹוֹר עוֹלָמִים" is interpreted as "eternal rock" rather than a name like "everlasting fortress," preserving the metaphor.
- v. 13: "when rulers above us were not you" — the Hebrew "בְּעָלוּנוּ אֲדֹנִים זֽוּלָתֶךָ" is interpreted as a poetic reference to human rulers rather than divine ones.
- v. 18: "your breath of salvation" — the Hebrew "רוּחַ יְשׁוּעֹת" is taken as a singular "breath of salvation" rather than plural "spirits of salvation."
- v. 19: "the dew of light" — the Hebrew "טַל אוֹרֹת" is taken as "dew of light" to maintain the poetic imagery of divine renewal.
