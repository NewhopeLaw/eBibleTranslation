# Isaiah 43

¹ Now this is what the LORD says, the God who formed you, Jacob, and the one who shaped you, Israel: "Do not be afraid, for I have redeemed you. I have called you by name. You are mine." ² When you pass through waters, I am with you. Rivers will not overwhelm you. When you walk through fire, you will not be burned. The flame will not scorch you. ³ For I am the LORD your God, the holy one of Israel, your savior. I have given Egypt and Cush as your ransom, and the Sabeans in exchange for you. ⁴ You are precious in my sight, honored, and I love you. I will give people in your place, and nations for your life. ⁵ Do not be afraid, for I am with you. From the east I will bring your offspring, and from the west I will gather you. ⁶ I will say to the north, "Give them up," and to the south, "Do not withhold." Bring my sons from distant lands, and my daughters from the ends of the earth. ⁷ Everyone who is called by my name I have created for my glory. I formed them, and I made them. ⁸ I will send a people who are blind, and they will have eyes. I will send those who are deaf, and they will have ears. ⁹ All the nations have gathered together, and rulers from among them will say, "Who has declared these things? Who has told us of the past? Let their witnesses come forward and justify themselves. Let them listen and say, 'It is true.'" ¹⁰ You are my witnesses, says the LORD, and my servant whom I have chosen, so that you may know and believe me, and understand that I am the one. Before me, no god was formed, and after me, none will be. ¹¹ I am the LORD, and there is no savior besides me. ¹² I have declared and saved, and I have proclaimed, and there was no stranger among you. You are my witnesses, says the LORD, and I am God. ¹³ From the beginning, I am the one, and no one can snatch from my hand. I act, and who can reverse it? ¹⁴ This is what the LORD, the redeemer of Israel, the holy one of Israel, says: "For your sake, I will send Babylon away, and I will bring down all who flee. The Chaldeans will be captured in their ships." ¹⁵ I am the LORD your God, the holy one of Israel, the one who has appointed Israel as your king. ¹⁶ This is what the LORD says, the one who makes a way through the sea and a path through the mighty waters. ¹⁷ The one who brings out chariots and horses, and an army together, will lie down. They will not rise again. Their might will be put out like a wick. ¹⁸ Do not remember the former things, nor consider the past. ¹⁹ Look, I am doing a new thing, now it is sprouting. Do you not perceive it? I will make a road in the desert and rivers in the wasteland. ²⁰ The creatures of the field will honor me, ostriches and daughters of the desert, because I have placed water in the desert and rivers in the wasteland to water my chosen people. ²¹ This people I formed for myself will recount my praise. ²² You did not call me, Jacob. You did not weary yourself, Israel. ²³ You did not bring me a sacrifice of rams. You did not honor me with your offerings. I did not weary you with grain offerings or burden you with frankincense. ²⁴ You did not buy me a gift of silver. You did not delight me with the fat of your offerings. Instead, you have burdened me with your sins and grieved me with your iniquities. ²⁵ I am I, the one who wipes out your transgressions for my own sake, and I will not remember your sins. ²⁶ Recall, and let us argue it out. Declare your justification, that you may be vindicated. ²⁷ Your ancestors sinned first, and your rulers rebelled against me. ²⁸ I will defile the leaders of your holy places, and I will give Jacob to destruction, and Israel to disgrace.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "Cush" and "Sabeans" refer to ancient regions or peoples. The Hebrew terms כּוּשׁ (Cush) and סְבָא (Sabeans) are rendered based on the glossary and standard biblical usage.
- v. 14: "Chaldeans" is a standard rendering of the Hebrew כַּשְׂדִּים (Casdim).
- v. 26: The imperative "Declare your justification" reflects the context of a summons to stand in judgment, not a request for a general recount.
