# 2 Thessalonians 2

¹ We ask you, brothers, about the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to him, ² so that you may not be disturbed quickly from your mind nor be shaken, whether by a spirit nor by a word nor by an epistle as from us, as if the day of the Lord has already arrived. ³ No one deceive you in any way. For unless the apostasy comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction, ⁴ the opponent and one exalted over every so-called god or object of worship, so that he places himself in the temple of God, showing himself that he is a god. ⁵ Don't you remember that when I was still with you, I told you these things? ⁶ And now you know what is holding him back, so that he is revealed in his own time. ⁷ For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only the one who is holding back now will remain until he is removed. ⁸ Then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of his mouth and destroy with the revelation of his coming. ⁹ The coming of this one is in the power of Satan, in every power and signs and false wonders, ¹⁰ and in every deception of wickedness for those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth in order to be saved. ¹¹ And for this reason, God sends them a power of deception, to believe in falsehood, ¹² so that all who do not believe the truth but delight in wickedness may be judged. ¹³ But we must always give thanks to God for you, beloved brothers, because God chose you as a firstfruits for salvation through sanctification of the Spirit and belief in the truth, ¹⁴ in order that you were called by our gospel, to obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ. ¹⁵ Therefore, brothers, stand firm and hold to the traditions you were taught, whether by word or by our epistle. ¹⁶ But the Lord Jesus Christ himself and our God the Father, who loved us and gave us eternal comfort and good hope through grace, ¹⁷ may encourage your hearts and establish you in every good work and word.

---

Notes
- v. 4: "the temple of God" — the Greek uses *naos*, which may refer to a specific inner sanctum or to the whole temple complex; the context suggests the former.
- v. 9: "the coming of this one is in the power of Satan" — the Greek uses *parousia*, which usually means "coming" or "arrival," but here is used of the deceptive influence of the lawless one.
- v. 11: "a power of deception" — the Greek *dynamis planēs* is a noun phrase, not a verb; the translation captures this as a divine "power of deception."
