# Matthew 13

¹ On that day, Jesus came out from the house and sat by the sea. ² Large crowds gathered to him, so much so that he got into a boat and sat down, while the whole crowd stood on the shore. ³ He spoke to them in parables, saying: "Listen! A sower went out to sow. ⁴ As he sowed, some seeds fell beside the path, and the birds came and ate them. ⁵ Others fell on rocky ground where the soil was shallow. Immediately they sprang up, because the soil was not deep, ⁶ but when the sun rose, they were scorched. Because they had no root, they withered. ⁷ Others fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked them. ⁸ Others fell on good soil, and they bore fruit, some a hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty." ⁹ Let the one who has ears listen. ¹⁰ The disciples came to him and asked, "Why do you speak to them in parables?" ¹¹ He answered them, "Because it has been given to you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but it has not been given to them. ¹² For whoever has, more will be given to them, and they will have an abundance. But whoever does not have, even what they have will be taken from them. ¹³ Therefore, I speak to them in parables, because they see but do not see, and they hear but do not hear or understand. ¹⁴ And this prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled in them: 'You will hear and not understand, and you will see and not perceive. ¹⁵ For the heart of this people has grown dull, and their ears are slow to hear, and their eyes they have closed, so that they may not see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart and turn to me, and I will heal them.' ¹⁶ But blessed are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear. ¹⁷ Truly I tell you, many prophets and righteous people longed to see what you see, but did not see it, and to hear what you hear, but did not hear it. ¹⁸ So pay attention to the parable of the sower. ¹⁹ Everyone who hears the word of the kingdom but does not understand it—this is the one who receives it along the path, and the evil one comes and takes away what was sown in their heart. This is the one who is sown along the path. ²⁰ The one sown on rocky ground is the one who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy, ²¹ yet has no root in themselves, but is temporary. When hardship or persecution comes because of the word, immediately they stumble. ²² The one sown among thorns is the one who hears the word, but the cares of the world and the deceitfulness of wealth choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful. ²³ The one sown on good soil is the one who hears the word and understands it. This one bears fruit, some a hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty." ²⁴ He told them another parable: "The kingdom of heaven is like a man who sowed good seed in his field. ²⁵ But while people were sleeping, his enemy came and sowed weeds among the wheat and went away. ²⁶ When the plants sprouted and bore grain, the weeds appeared also. ²⁷ The servants of the master came and said to him, 'Sir, didn't you sow good seed in your field? Where then did the weeds come from?' ²⁸ He said to them, 'An enemy did this.' The servants said to him, 'Then do you want us to go and gather them up?' ²⁹ But he said, 'No, for in gathering up the weeds you might uproot the wheat along with them. ³⁰ Let both grow together until the harvest, and at harvest time I will tell the reapers: Gather the weeds first and tie them in bundles to be burned, but gather the wheat into my barn.'" ³¹ He told them another parable: "The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed, which a person took and sowed in their field. ³² Though it is the smallest of all seeds, when it grows, it becomes the largest of the plants and becomes a tree, so that birds of the sky come and nest in its branches." ³³ He told them another parable: "The kingdom of heaven is like yeast, which a woman took and mixed into three measures of flour until all was leavened." ³⁴ He told all these things to the crowds in parables, and he did not speak to them without parables, ³⁵ so that what was said through the prophet might be fulfilled: 'I will open my mouth in parables, I will announce what has been hidden from the foundation of the world.' ³⁶ Then he left the crowds and went into the house. His disciples came to him, saying, "Explain to us the parable of the weeds in the field." ³⁷ He answered, "The one who sowed the good seed is the Son of Man. ³⁸ The field is the world. The good seed is the sons of the kingdom. The weeds are the sons of the evil one, ³⁹ and the enemy who sowed them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age, and the reapers are angels. ⁴⁰ Just as the weeds are gathered and burned with fire, so will it be at the end of the age. ⁴¹ The Son of Man will send his angels, and they will gather out of his kingdom all causes of sin and all lawbreakers, ⁴² and they will throw them into the fiery furnace, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth. ⁴³ Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father. Let the one who has ears listen. ⁴⁴ The kingdom of heaven is like a treasure hidden in a field, which a person found and hid. In his joy he goes and sells everything he has and buys that field. ⁴⁵ Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant seeking fine pearls. ⁴⁶ When he found a pearl of great value, he went and sold everything he had and bought it. ⁴⁷ Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a net that was cast into the sea and gathered every kind of fish. ⁴⁸ When it was full, they dragged it up onto the shore. They sat down and collected the good into baskets, but threw away the bad. ⁴⁹ So it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come out and separate the wicked from among the righteous, ⁵⁰ and they will throw them into the fiery furnace, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth. ⁵¹ Have you understood all these things?" They said to him, "Yes." ⁵² He said to them, "Therefore every scribe who has been trained for the kingdom of heaven is like a householder who brings out from their treasury new and old things." ⁵³ When Jesus finished these parables, he left that place. ⁵⁴ Coming into his hometown, he taught in their synagogue so that they were astonished and said, "Where did this man get such wisdom and mighty deeds? ⁵⁵ Isn't this the carpenter's son? Is not his mother called Mary? And aren't his brothers James and Joseph and Simon and Judas? ⁵⁶ And aren't all his sisters with us? Where then did this man get all these things?" ⁵⁷ They were scandalized by him. But Jesus said to them, "A prophet is not without honor except in their own town and in their own home." ⁵⁸ He did not do many mighty deeds there because of their unbelief.

Notes
- v. 5: "shallow" (ὀλίγην) is a direct translation of the Greek, meaning lacking in depth.
- v. 14: "fulfilled in them" (ἀναπληροῦται αὐτοῖς) uses "fulfilled" to capture the sense of completion or realization.
- v. 29: "Let both grow together" retains the imperative force of the Greek "ἄφετε συναυξάνεσθαι" in a natural English idiom.
- v. 35: "hidden from the foundation of the world" (ἀπὸ καταβολῆς) is a direct rendering.
- v. 41: "Son of Man" is used as a title for Jesus in line with the context and the use in the preceding verses.
- v. 55: "carpenter's son" translates τοῦ τέκτονος υἱός literally but accurately, avoiding the assumption of a specific title or honorific context.
