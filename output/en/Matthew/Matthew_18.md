# Matthew 18

¹ At that time the disciples approached Jesus and asked, "Who is the greatest in the kingdom of the heavens?" ² He called a child to himself, placed the child in the middle of them, ³ and said, "Amen, I say to you, unless you turn and become like these children, you will not enter the kingdom of the heavens. ⁴ Whoever humbles himself like this child is the greatest in the kingdom of the heavens. ⁵ And whoever receives one such child in my name receives me." ⁶ "Whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in me to stumble, it would be better for him if a millstone were placed around his neck and he were thrown into the sea. ⁷ Woe to the world because of what causes people to stumble. For it must come, but woe to the person through whom it comes. ⁸ If your hand or your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away. It is better for you to enter life maimed or crippled than to have two hands or two feet and be thrown into eternal fire. ⁹ And if your eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out and throw it away. It is better for you to enter the kingdom with one eye than to have two eyes and be thrown into the hell of fire. ¹⁰ See that you do not despise one of these little ones. I tell you that their angels in the heavens always see the face of my Father who is in the heavens." ¹¹ "What do you think? If a man has a hundred sheep and one of them goes astray, will he not leave the ninety-nine on the mountains and go in search of the one that is lost? ¹² And if he finds it, amen, I say to you, he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine that did not go astray. ¹³ So it is not the will of your Father in the heavens that even one of these little ones should be lost." ¹⁴ "If your brother sins against you, go and point out his fault just between the two of you. If he listens to you, you have gained your brother. ¹⁵ But if he does not listen, take one or two others along with you, so that every matter may be established by the testimony of two or three witnesses. ¹⁶ If he still refuses to listen, tell it to the assembly; and if he refuses to listen even to the assembly, let him be to you like a pagan and a tax collector." ¹⁷ "Amen I say to you, whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven. ¹⁸ Again, amen I say to you, if two of you on earth agree about anything you ask for, it will be done for you by my Father in heaven. ¹⁹ For where two or three are gathered in my name, I am there among them." ²⁰ Then Peter approached him and asked, "Lord, how many times should I forgive my brother when he sins against me? Up to seven times?" ²¹ Jesus said to him, "Not up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven. ²² For this reason the kingdom of the heavens is like a king who decided to settle accounts with his servants. ²³ When he began to settle, a man who owed him ten thousand talents was brought to him. ²⁴ Since he could not pay, the lord ordered him to be sold, along with his wife, children, and all his possessions, to repay the debt. ²⁵ Falling down before him, the servant begged, 'Have patience with me, and I will repay you everything.' ²⁶ Moved with compassion, the lord released him and forgave him the debt. ²⁷ But when that servant went out, he found one of his fellow servants who owed him a hundred silver coins. He seized him and began to choke him, saying, 'Pay me back what you owe me!' ²⁸ Falling to his knees, his fellow servant begged him, 'Have patience with me, and I will repay you.' ²⁹ But he refused. Instead, he went out and threw him into prison until he paid back the debt. ³⁰ When the other servants saw what had happened, they were deeply troubled and went to tell their lord all that had taken place. ³¹ Then his lord summoned him and said, 'You wicked servant! I canceled all that debt for you because you pleaded with me. ³² Should you not also have had mercy on your fellow servant, just as I had mercy on you?' ³³ And in anger the lord handed him over to the jailers until he should pay back in full what was owed. ³⁴ So also my heavenly Father will do to you if you do not forgive each other from your hearts." ³⁵ "Therefore, the kingdom of the heavens is like a king who decided to settle accounts with his servants."

Notes
- v. 35: The final verse in the chapter is numbered 35, but the narrative continues with a new parable introduced in v. 21. The structure here is a continuation of the previous theme (forgiveness) and introduces a new parable. The verse is included in full based on the source.
