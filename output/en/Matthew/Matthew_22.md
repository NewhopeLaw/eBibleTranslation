# Matthew 22

¹ Jesus answered again with a parable, saying to them: ² "The kingdom of heaven is like a king who made a wedding for his son, ³ and he sent his servants to call those who had been invited to the wedding, but they would not come. ⁴ So he sent again other servants, saying, 'Tell those who have been invited, My feast is ready, my oxen and fatted cattle have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding.' ⁵ But some ignored it, going off to their farm, another to his business, ⁶ and the rest seized the king's servants, mistreated them, and killed them. ⁷ The king was angry and sent his army, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city. ⁸ Then he said to his servants, 'The wedding is ready, but those who were invited were not worthy. ⁹ Go therefore to the crossroads of the roads, and invite everyone you find to the wedding.' ¹⁰ So those servants went out into the roads and gathered all they found, both bad and good, and the wedding was filled with people reclining. ¹¹ Then the king came in to look at the guests reclining and saw there a man who was not wearing a wedding garment. ¹² He said to him, 'Friend, how did you come in here without a wedding garment?' The man was speechless. ¹³ Then the king said to the servants, 'Tie his hands and feet and cast him into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.' ¹⁴ For many are called, but few are chosen." ¹⁵ Then the Pharisees went away and plotted to trap him in a word. ¹⁶ They sent their disciples to him with the Herodians, saying, "Teacher, we know you are truthful and teach God's way in truth, and you care for no one's face, for you do not look to people's appearances. ¹⁷ So tell us, then, what do you think? Is it lawful to give a poll tax to Caesar or not?" ¹⁸ But Jesus, understanding their evil, said, "Why do you test me, you hypocrites? ¹⁹ Show me the coin of the poll tax." Then they brought him a denarius. ²⁰ And he said to them, "Whose image and inscription is this?" ²¹ They said to him, "Caesar's." Then he said to them, "Therefore give to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's." ²² When they heard this, they marveled, and letting him go, they left. ²³ On that day the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to him and questioned him, ²⁴ saying, "Teacher, Moses said, 'If a man dies without children, his brother must marry his wife and raise up offspring for his brother.' ²⁵ We had seven brothers, and the first married, died, leaving no children, and gave the wife to his brother, ²⁶ similarly the second and the third, up to the seventh. ²⁷ After all of them, the woman died. ²⁸ In the resurrection, therefore, whose wife will she be? For all seven had her." ²⁹ Jesus answered them, "You are mistaken, not knowing the scriptures nor the power of God. ³⁰ For in the resurrection, people neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven. ³¹ But regarding the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken to you by God, saying, ³² 'I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'? He is not the God of the dead, but of the living." ³³ When the crowds heard this, they were astonished at his teaching. ³⁴ The Pharisees, hearing that he had silenced the Sadducees, gathered together. ³⁵ And one of them, a lawyer, questioned him, testing him, ³⁶ "Teacher, which is the great commandment in the law?" ³⁷ He said to him, "You must love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind. ³⁸ This is the great and first commandment. ³⁹ A second is like it: You must love your neighbor as yourself. ⁴⁰ On these two commandments depend the whole law and the prophets." ⁴¹ When the Pharisees had gathered together, Jesus asked them, ⁴² saying, "What do you think of the Messiah? Whose son is he?" They said to him, "David's." ⁴³ He said to them, "Then how does David in spirit call him Lord, saying, ⁴⁴ 'The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies under your feet'? ⁴⁵ If then David calls him Lord, how is he David's son?" ⁴⁶ And no one was able to answer him a word, nor did anyone from that day dare to question him again.

Notes
- v. 28: "For all seven had her" — the Greek uses "πάντες γὰρ ἔσχον αὐτήν," which is rendered as "For all seven had her" to reflect the possessive sense in the resurrection question.
- v. 38: "the whole law and the prophets" — the phrase "ὅλος ὁ νόμος κρέμαται καὶ οἱ προφῆται" is translated to reflect the idea that all the law and the prophets hang on these two commandments.
- v. 43: "David in spirit" — the phrase "περὶ τοῦ πνεύματος" is rendered as "in spirit" to clarify that Jesus is referring to the inspired words of David in the psalm.
- v. 44: "The Lord said to my Lord" — the Greek "Εἶπεν κύριος τῷ κυρίῳ μου" is translated directly to preserve the poetic and theological nuance of the psalmic quote.
