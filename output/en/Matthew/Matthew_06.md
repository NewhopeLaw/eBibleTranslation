# Matthew 6

¹ But be careful that your righteousness is not done before the people to be seen by them, for if so, you have no reward from your Father who is in the heavens. ² When therefore you do acts of mercy, do not sound a trumpet before you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the marketplaces, to be praised by the people. Truly I say to you, they have already received their reward. ³ But when you do an act of mercy, let your left hand not know what your right hand is doing, ⁴ so that your mercy may be in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will repay you. ⁵ And when you pray, do not be like the hypocrites. They love to stand in the synagogues and the corners of the marketplaces to pray, to be seen by the people. Truly I say to you, they have already received their reward. ⁶ But when you pray, go into your room, and closing your door, pray to your Father in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will repay you. ⁷ When you pray, do not heap up empty phrases as the Gentiles do, for they think that because of their many words they will be heard. ⁸ Therefore do not be like them, for your Father knows what you need before you ask him. ⁹ This then is how you are to pray: Our Father in the heavens, may your name be kept holy, ¹⁰ may your kingdom come, may your will be done, as in heaven so on earth. ¹¹ Give us this day our daily bread, ¹² and forgive us our debts, as we have also forgiven our debtors; ¹³ and do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one. ¹⁴ For if you forgive others their trespasses, your Father in the heavens will also forgive you. ¹⁵ But if you do not forgive others, neither will your Father in the heavens forgive your trespasses. ¹⁶ When you fast, do not look gloomy as the hypocrites do, for they disfigure their faces so that their fasting may be seen by the people. Truly I say to you, they have already received their reward. ¹⁷ But when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face, ¹⁸ so that your fasting may not be seen by the people, but by your Father in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will repay you. ¹⁹ Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where rust and moth destroy, and where thieves dig through and steal. ²⁰ But store up treasures for yourselves in heaven, where neither rust nor moth destroy, and where thieves do not dig through nor steal. ²¹ For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also. ²² The eye is the lamp of the body. So if your eye is good, your whole body will be full of light. ²³ But if your eye is evil, your whole body will be full of darkness. And if the light in you is darkness, how great is that darkness! ²⁴ No one can serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other, or be loyal to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and money. ²⁵ Therefore I say to you, do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, nor about your body, what you will wear. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing? ²⁶ Look at the birds of the air: they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not more valuable than they? ²⁷ And which of you, being anxious, can add a single hour to his lifespan? ²⁸ And about clothing, why are you anxious? Consider how the lilies of the field grow. They neither toil nor spin, ²⁹ yet I say to you that even Solomon in all his glory was not dressed like one of these. ³⁰ Now if God so clothes the grass of the field, which is alive today and thrown into the oven tomorrow, will he not much more clothe you, you of little faith? ³¹ Therefore do not be anxious, saying, What shall we eat? Or, What shall we drink? Or, What shall we wear? ³² For the Gentiles seek all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them all. ³³ But seek first the kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you. ³⁴ Therefore do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble.

---

Notes
- v. 22: "The eye is the lamp of the body" — a metaphor comparing the eye to a lamp, with the lamp representing light and vision.
- v. 24: "money" for μαμωνᾷ — refers to wealth or material possessions, often used in a metaphorical sense for idolatry of riches.
- v. 33: "seek first the kingdom and his righteousness" — the phrase is a call to prioritize spiritual values over material concerns.
