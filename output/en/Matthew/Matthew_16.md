# Matthew 16

¹ The Pharisees and Sadducees came to Jesus to test him, asking him to show them a sign from heaven. ² He replied to them, "In the evening you say, 'It will be fine,' for the sky is red; ³ and in the morning you say, 'Today it will be stormy,' for the sky is red and threatening. You can tell what the sky looks like, but you cannot discern the signs of the times. ⁴ A wicked and adulterous generation seeks a sign, but no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah." And leaving them, he departed. ⁵ When the disciples arrived at the other side, they had forgotten to bring bread. ⁶ Jesus said to them, "Watch carefully and beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees." ⁷ They were discussing among themselves, saying, "We didn't bring any bread." ⁸ Jesus, understanding their thoughts, said, "Why are you thinking among yourselves, you of little faith, that you didn't bring any bread? ⁹ Still do you not understand, and do you not remember the five loaves for the five thousand and how many baskets you collected? ¹⁰ Or the seven loaves for the four thousand and how many small baskets you collected? ¹¹ How is it that you still do not understand that I was not speaking to you about bread? Beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees." ¹² Then they realized that he was not warning them about the yeast in the bread, but about the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees. ¹³ Now Jesus came into the region of Caesarea Philippi and asked his disciples, "Who do people say the Son of Man is?" ¹⁴ They said, "Some say John the Baptist, others say Elijah, and others Jeremiah or one of the prophets." ¹⁵ He said to them, "And you — who do you say I am?" ¹⁶ Simon Peter answered, "You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God." ¹⁷ Jesus replied to him, "Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jonah, because flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father in heaven. ¹⁸ And I also tell you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my assembly, and the gates of Hades will not overpower it. ¹⁹ I will give you the keys to the kingdom of heaven, and whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven." ²⁰ Then he strictly ordered the disciples not to tell anyone that he was the Messiah. ²¹ From then on, Jesus began to show his disciples that he must go to Jerusalem and suffer many things from the elders, chief priests, and scribes, and be killed, and on the third day be raised. ²² Then Peter took him aside and began to rebuke him, saying, "God forbid, Lord! This must not happen to you!" ²³ But he turned and said to Peter, "Get behind me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to me, for you are not thinking the things of God, but the things of humans." ²⁴ Then Jesus said to his disciples, "If anyone wants to come after me, let them deny themselves, take up their cross, and follow me. ²⁵ For whoever wants to save their life will lose it, but whoever loses their life for my sake will find it. ²⁶ What good will it be for someone to gain the whole world, yet lose their life? Or what can someone give in exchange for their life? ²⁷ For the Son of Man will come in the glory of his Father with his angels, and then he will repay each person according to their deeds. ²⁸ Truly I tell you, there are some standing here who will not taste death until they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom."

---

Notes
- v. 10: "small baskets" (σπυρίδας) — the term is used only here and in the next verse, referring to small containers for leftovers.
- v. 18: "Peter" / "rock" — the name Πέτρος and the noun πέτρα are both from the root "stone"; the translation distinguishes the person from the metaphor.
- v. 28: "coming in his kingdom" — the phrase echoes the earlier "Son of Man" motif, likely pointing to a future revelation or eschatological event.
