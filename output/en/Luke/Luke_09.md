# Luke 9

¹ He called together the twelve and gave them authority and power over every demon and to heal diseases, ² and he sent them out to proclaim the kingdom of God and to heal the sick, ³ and he said to them, "Take nothing for the journey, neither staff nor traveler's bag nor bread nor silver, nor even two tunics. ⁴ "And wherever you enter a house, stay there and from there depart. ⁵ "And whoever does not receive you, as you leave the town, shake the dust off your feet for a witness against them." ⁶ They went out and passed through the villages, proclaiming the good news and healing everywhere. ⁷ Herod the tetrarch heard about all these things and was greatly perplexed, because some were saying, "John has been raised from the dead," ⁸ some, "Elijah has appeared," and others, "A prophet from ancient times has risen." ⁹ Herod said, "John I beheaded—so who is this about whom I hear such things?" And he was trying to see him. ¹⁰ The apostles returned and reported to him everything they had done. And he took them aside and went to a town called Bethsaida alone. ¹¹ But when the crowds learned of it, they followed him. And he welcomed them and spoke to them about the kingdom of God and healed those who needed healing. ¹² The day began to decline. The twelve came to him and said, "Send the crowd away so they can go into the surrounding villages and countryside and find lodging and food, because we are in a remote place here." ¹³ But he said to them, "You give them something to eat." They said, "We have no more than five loaves and two fish, unless we go and buy food for all these people." ¹⁴ For about five thousand men were there. He said to his disciples, "Have them sit down in groups of about fifty." ¹⁵ They did so and seated the whole crowd. ¹⁶ He took the five loaves and the two fish, looked up to heaven, blessed them, and broke them, and gave the food to the disciples to set before the crowd. ¹⁷ All ate and were satisfied. And what was left over was collected in twelve baskets. ¹⁸ Now it happened when he was praying alone that the disciples came near and asked him, "Who do the crowds say that I am?" ¹⁹ They answered, "John the Baptist, others Elijah, and others that a prophet from ancient times has risen." ²⁰ He said to them, "But who do you say that I am?" Peter answered, "The Messiah of God." ²¹ But he rebuked them and commanded them not to tell anyone, ²² saying, "The Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and scribes, and be killed, and on the third day be raised." ²³ He said to all, "If anyone wants to come after me, let them deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me. ²⁴ "For whoever wants to save their life will lose it, but whoever loses their life for my sake will save it. ²⁵ "What good is it for someone to gain the whole world, yet lose themselves or be destroyed?" ²⁶ For whoever is ashamed of me and my words, the Son of Man will be ashamed of them when he comes in his glory and in his Father's and the holy angels' glory. ²⁷ I tell you the truth, there are some standing here who will not taste death until they see the kingdom of God." ²⁸ After these words it happened about eight days later that he took Peter, John, and James and went up to the mountain to pray. ²⁹ Now while he was praying, his appearance changed, and his clothing became dazzling white. ³⁰ Then behold, two men were talking with him, who were Moses and Elijah, ³¹ who appeared in glory and spoke about his departure that he was about to fulfill in Jerusalem. ³² Peter and those with him were weighed down by sleep. But waking up, they saw his glory and the two men who stood with him. ³³ And it happened as they were parting from him, Peter said to Jesus, "Master, it is good for us to be here. Let us make three tents, one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah," not knowing what he was saying. ³⁴ While he was speaking, a cloud came and overshadowed them. They were afraid when they entered into the cloud. ³⁵ A voice came out of the cloud, saying, "This is my Son, the beloved. Listen to him." ³⁶ When the voice had stopped, Jesus was found alone. And they kept silent and told no one in those days what they had seen. ³⁷ The next day, descending from the mountain, a large crowd met him. ³⁸ Now behold, a man from the crowd cried out, saying, "Teacher, I implore you to look on my son, for he is my only child. ³⁹ "And behold, a spirit seizes him, and suddenly he cries out, and convulses him with foam. Hardly does it leave him, but it crushes him. ⁴⁰ "And I begged your disciples to cast it out, but they could not." ⁴¹ Jesus, responding, said, "O faithless and perverse generation, how long will I be with you and bear with you? Bring your son here." ⁴² But while the man was still coming, the demon threw the boy to the ground and convulsed him. Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit and healed the boy and gave him back to his father. ⁴³ And all were astonished at the greatness of God. But while all were marveling at everything he did, he said to his disciples, ⁴⁴ "Let these words sink into your ears, for the Son of Man is about to be handed over into human hands." ⁴⁵ But they did not understand this statement, and its meaning was hidden from them, so that they did not perceive it, and they were afraid to ask him about this statement. ⁴⁶ A dispute arose among them, which of them would be the greatest. ⁴⁷ Jesus, perceiving their thoughts, took a child and placed it by his side, ⁴⁸ and said to them, "Whoever receives this child in my name receives me, and whoever receives me receives the one who sent me. For whoever is least among you all is the greatest." ⁴⁹ John answered, "Teacher, we saw someone casting out demons in your name and we tried to stop him, because he does not follow with us." ⁵⁰ Jesus said to him, "Do not stop him, for whoever is not against you is for you." ⁵¹ Now it happened when the days for his being taken up were fulfilled, he set his face to go to Jerusalem, ⁵² and he sent messengers ahead of him. They went and entered a Samaritan village to prepare for him, ⁵³ but they did not receive him because his face was set toward Jerusalem. ⁵⁴ Seeing this, James and John said, "Lord, do you want us to command fire to come down from heaven and consume them?" ⁵⁵ But he turned and rebuked them. ⁵⁶ And they went to another village. ⁵⁷ Now while they were going along the road, someone said to him, "I will follow you wherever you go." ⁵⁸ Jesus said to him, "Foxes have dens and birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay his head." ⁵⁹ He said to another, "Follow me." But the man said, "Lord, let me go first and bury my father." ⁶⁰ Jesus said to him, "Leave the dead to bury their own dead. But you, go and proclaim the kingdom of God." ⁶¹ Another said, "I will follow you, Lord, but first let me say farewell to those at my house." ⁶² Jesus said to him, "No one who puts their hand on the plow and looks back is fit for the kingdom of God."

Notes
- v. 15: "Have them sit down in groups of about fifty" — the Greek word "κλισίας" (klishias) is translated as "groups" to avoid implying literal tents or shelters.
- v. 28: "about eight days later" — the Greek phrase "ὡσεὶ ἡμέραι ὀκτὼ" is translated idiomatically to reflect the approximate time.
- v. 52: "to prepare for him" — the Greek "ἑτοιμάσαι αὐτῷ" is rendered with "to prepare for him" to reflect the Samaritan villagers' intended hospitality.
- v. 62: "puts their hand on the plow" — the metaphor is retained in English as a familiar idiom.
