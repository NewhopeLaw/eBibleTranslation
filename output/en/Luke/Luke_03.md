# Luke 3

¹ In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governing Judea, and Herod was governing Galilee, and his brother Philip was governing Iturea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias was governing Abilene, ² during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John, the son of Zacharias, in the wilderness. ³ He went into all the region around the Jordan, proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins, ⁴ just as it is written in the scroll of the prophet Isaiah: A voice of one calling in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord, make his paths straight. ⁵ Every valley shall be filled in, and every mountain and hill made low; and the crooked shall become straight, and the rough places smooth; ⁶ and all flesh shall see the salvation of God. ⁷ So he began to say to the crowds who were going out to be baptized by him, You brood of vipers, who warned you to flee from the wrath to come? ⁸ Therefore, produce fruit in keeping with repentance. And do not begin to say among yourselves, We have Abraham as our father, for I tell you, God is able to raise up children for Abraham from these stones. ⁹ Even now the axe is laid to the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that does not produce good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire. ¹⁰ The crowds asked him, What then must we do? ¹¹ He answered them, Whoever has two tunics should give to the one who has none, and whoever has food should do the same. ¹² Tax collectors also came to be baptized and asked him, Teacher, what must we do? ¹³ He said to them, Do not collect more than what is prescribed to you. ¹⁴ Soldiers also asked him, And what must we do? And he said to them, Do not intimidate anyone or accuse falsely, and be satisfied with your wages. ¹⁵ As the people were waiting and all minds were speculating in their hearts whether John might be the Christ, ¹⁶ John answered them all, saying, I indeed baptize you with water, but the one who is stronger than me is coming, whose sandals I am not worthy to untie. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire. ¹⁷ His winnowing fork is in his hand to clear his threshing floor and to gather the wheat into his granary, but the chaff he will burn up with unquenchable fire. ¹⁸ So with many other exhortations he proclaimed good news to the people. ¹⁹ But Herod the tetrarch, being reproved by him concerning Herodias, the wife of his brother, and all the evils Herod had done, ²⁰ added to all this by imprisoning John in prison. ²¹ Now it happened that, when all the people had been baptized and Jesus himself had been baptized and was praying, the heavens opened. ²² And the Holy Spirit descended upon him in bodily form like a dove, and a voice came from heaven, You are my beloved Son; in you I am well pleased. ²³ Now Jesus himself was about thirty years old. He was thought to be the son of Joseph, ²⁴ the son of Eli, ²⁵ the son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Janna, ²⁶ the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, the son of Naggai, ²⁷ the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semai, the son of Joseph, the son of Judah, ²⁸ the son of Joanna, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubbabel, the son of Salathiel, the son of Neri, ²⁹ the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmadam, the son of Er, ³⁰ the son of Jesus, the son of Eliézer, the son of Jorim, the son of Mattath, ³¹ the son of Semei, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonam, the son of Eliakim, ³² the son of Melea, the son of Menna, the son of Mattath, the son of Nathan, the son of David, ³³ the son of Jesse, the son of Jobab, the son of Boaz, the son of Salmon, the son of Nahshon, ³⁴ the son of Amminadab, the son of Admin, the son of Arni, the son of Eshrom, the son of Pharez, the son of Judah, ³⁵ the son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Terah, the son of Nahor, ³⁶ the son of Serug, the son of Reu, the son of Peleg, the son of Eber, the son of Salah, ³⁷ the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech, ³⁸ the son of Methuselah, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Mahalalel, the son of Cainan,

Notes
- v. 35: The Greek text includes "Θάρα" (Thara) and "Ναχὼρ" (Nahor) in the genealogy. The rendering follows the standard English translation of these names.
- v. 37: "Καϊνὰμ" is rendered as "Cainan," consistent with the Septuagint and some early Christian traditions.
- v. 38: The genealogy concludes with "Σὴθ" (Seth) and "Ἀδὰμ" (Adam), with "θεοῦ" (God) in apposition to Adam, indicating theological interpretation.
