# Luke 3

¹ In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governing Judea, and Herod was governing Galilee, and his brother Philip was governing Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias was governing Abilene, ² while Annas and Caiaphas were high priests, a message from God came upon John, the son of Zechariah, in the wilderness. ³ He went into all the region around the Jordan, proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins, ⁴ as it is written in the book of the words of the prophet Isaiah: A voice of one crying in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord, make his paths straight. ⁵ Every valley shall be filled, and every mountain and hill shall be brought low, and the crooked shall become straight, and the rough places smooth; ⁶ and all flesh shall see the salvation of God. ⁷ So he was saying to the crowds going out to be baptized by him: You brood of vipers, who warned you to flee from the coming wrath? ⁸ Therefore bear fruits worthy of repentance; and do not begin to say among yourselves, We have Abraham as our father, for I tell you that God is able to raise up children of Abraham from these stones. ⁹ Even now the ax is laid to the root of the trees; every tree therefore that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire. ¹⁰ And the crowds asked him, What then shall we do? ¹¹ He answered them, He who has two coats must share with him who has none; and he who has food must do the same. ¹² Tax collectors also came to be baptized, and they said to him, Teacher, what shall we do? ¹³ He said to them, Collect no more than what is required of you. ¹⁴ And soldiers asked him, What about us? What shall we do? And he said to them, Do not intimidate anyone or accuse falsely, and be content with your wages. ¹⁵ Now while the people were waiting, and all were questioning in their hearts about John, whether perhaps he might be the Christ, ¹⁶ John answered by saying to all of them, I indeed baptize you with water, but he who is mightier than I is coming, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to loosen; he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire. ¹⁷ His winnowing fan is in his hand, to clean his threshing floor and to gather the wheat into his granary, but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire. ¹⁸ So with many other exhortations he proclaimed the good news to the people. ¹⁹ But Herod the tetrarch, being reproved by him for Herodias, the wife of his brother, and for all the evil things Herod had done, ²⁰ added to them all this, and put John in prison. ²¹ Now it happened when all the people were baptized, and Jesus also had been baptized and was praying, the heavens opened, ²² and the Holy Spirit descended on him in bodily form like a dove, and a voice came from heaven, You are my beloved Son; with you I am well pleased. ²³ Jesus himself was about thirty years old when he began his ministry, being the son, as people thought, of Joseph, ²⁴ the son of Heli, ²⁵ the son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Janna, ²⁶ the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, the son of Naggai, ²⁷ the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semai, the son of Joseph, the son of Judah, ²⁸ the son of Joanna, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubabel, the son of Salathiel, the son of Neri, ²⁹ the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmadam, the son of Er, ³⁰ the son of Jesus, the son of Eleazar, the son of Jorim, the son of Mattath, the son of Levi, ³¹ the son of Simeon, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonam, the son of Eliakim, ³² the son of Melea, the son of Menna, the son of Mattath, the son of Nathan, the son of David, ³³ the son of Jesse, the son of Job, the son of Boaz, the son of Sala, the son of Nahshon, ³⁴ the son of Amminadab, the son of Admin, the son of Arni, the son of Eshrom, the son of Perez, the son of Judah, ³⁵ the son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Thara, the son of Nahor, ³⁶ the son of Serug, the son of Ragau, the son of Phalec, the son of Eber, the son of Sala, ³⁷ the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech, ³⁸ the son of Methuselah, the son of Enosh, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God.

Notes
- v. 1: The phrasing "in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar" is a dependent clause reading; the Greek structure is similar to a temporal clause, and English is best rendered clearly as a dependent clause.
- v. 23: "As people thought" reflects the Greek construction, where Joseph is not necessarily the biological father, but is commonly perceived to be so.
- v. 24–38: The genealogy is translated as a continuous list of paternal relationships, with "the son of" repeated for clarity and consistency with the original structure.
