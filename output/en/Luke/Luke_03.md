# Luke 3

¹ In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governing Judea, and Herod was tetrarch of Galilee, and his brother Philip tetrarch of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias tetrarch of Abilene, ² during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John the son of Zechariah in the wilderness. ³ He went into all the area around the Jordan, preaching a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins, ⁴ just as it is written in the scroll of the words of Isaiah the prophet: A voice of one calling in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord, make his paths straight. ⁵ Every valley will be filled and every mountain and hill made low, and the crooked will become straight and the rough places smooth, ⁶ and all flesh will see the salvation of God. ⁷ So John said to the crowds going out to be baptized by him: You offspring of vipers, who warned you to flee from the coming wrath? ⁸ Therefore produce fruit in keeping with repentance. Do not begin to say among yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father,' for I tell you that God is able to raise up children for Abraham from these stones. ⁹ The ax is already at the root of the trees, so every tree that does not bear good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire. ¹⁰ The crowds asked him, 'Then what should we do?' ¹¹ John answered, 'Whoever has two tunics should give to the one who has none, and whoever has food do the same.' ¹² Tax collectors also came to be baptized and asked him, 'Teacher, what should we do?' ¹³ He told them, 'Do not collect any more than you are ordered.' ¹⁴ Soldiers also asked him, 'And what should we do?' He said to them, 'Do not intimidate anyone or accuse falsely, and be content with your wages.' ¹⁵ Now the people were waiting and all were questioning in their hearts about John, whether he might be the Christ. ¹⁶ John answered all of them, 'I baptize you with water, but one who is stronger than I is coming, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to untie. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire. ¹⁷ His winnowing fork is in his hand to clear his threshing floor and to gather the wheat into his barn, but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire.' ¹⁸ So John, with many other exhortations, proclaimed good news to the people. ¹⁹ But Herod the tetrarch, who was being rebuked by him about Herodias, his brother's wife, and about all the evil things Herod had done, ²⁰ added to all this, and locked John up in prison. ²¹ Now when all the people were baptized and Jesus had also been baptized and was praying, the heavens were opened, ²² and the Holy Spirit descended on him in a bodily form like a dove, and a voice came from heaven: You are my beloved Son; with you I am well pleased. ²³ Now Jesus, who was the beginning of his ministry being about thirty years old, being, as was supposed, the son of Joseph, ²⁴ the son of Eli, ²⁵ the son of Mattathias, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, ²⁶ the son of Jonathon, the son of Joseph, ²⁷ the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, ²⁸ the son of Naggai, the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semein, ²⁹ the son of Joseph, the son of Judah, the son of Matthias, ³⁰ the son of Simon, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonam, ³¹ the son of Eliakim, the son of Melea, the son of Menna, the son of Mattatha, ³² the son of Nathan, the son of David, ³³ the son of Jesse, the son of Jobab, the son of Boaz, the son of Sala, ³⁴ the son of Nahshon, the son of Amminadab, the son of Admin, the son of Arni, ³⁵ the son of Esrom, the son of Pharez, the son of Judah, the son of Jacob, ³⁶ the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Terah, the son of Nahor, ³⁷ the son of Serug, the son of Reu, the son of Peleg, the son of Eber, the son of Shelah, ³⁸ the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech, ³⁹ the son of Methuselah, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Mahalalel, the son of Cainan, ⁴⁰ the son of Enos, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God.
