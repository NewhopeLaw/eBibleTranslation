# Luke 8

¹ And it happened in the following days that he traveled from town to village, preaching and proclaiming the kingdom of God, the twelve with him, ² and certain women who had been healed from evil spirits and ailments: Mary called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out, ³ and Joanna, the wife of Chuza, Herod's steward, and Susanna, and others many, who provided for them out of what they had. ⁴ Now when a great crowd was gathering and people were coming to him from the surrounding cities, he spoke in a parable: ⁵ "A sower went out to sow his seed. And as he sowed, some fell along the path, and it was trampled underfoot, and the birds of the sky devoured it. ⁶ And other seed fell on the rock, and sprang up, but withered away because it had no root. ⁷ And other seed fell among the thorns, and the thorns, growing up with it, suffocated it. ⁸ And other seed fell on the good soil, and sprang up, and bore fruit a hundredfold." As he said these things, he called out, "Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear." ⁹ The disciples asked him what this parable might mean. ¹⁰ And he said, "To you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God, but to the rest it is given in parables, so that seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand." ¹¹ "This is the meaning of the parable: the seed is the word of God. ¹² Those along the path are those who hear, and then the devil comes and takes away the word from their hearts, so that they may not believe and be saved. ¹³ Those on the rock are those who, when they hear, receive the word with joy, but these have no root, and in a time of testing they fall away. ¹⁴ That which fell among the thorns is those who hear, but as they go their way, they are choked by cares, riches, and pleasures of life, and their fruit does not mature. ¹⁵ But that which fell on the good soil, these are those with a good and noble heart, who hear the word, hold it fast, and bear fruit with endurance." ¹⁶ No one, having lit a lamp, covers it with a vessel or puts it under a bed, but puts it on a lampstand, so that those entering may see the light. ¹⁷ For there is nothing hidden that will not be revealed, and nothing secret that will not come to light. ¹⁸ Therefore watch how you listen; for whoever has, to him more will be given, and whoever does not have, even what he seems to have will be taken from him." ¹⁹ His mother and his brothers came to him, but they could not approach him because of the crowd. ²⁰ And it was told him, "Your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wanting to see you." ²¹ But he answered them, saying, "My mother and my brothers are these who hear the word of God and do it." ²² Now it happened on one of the days that he got into a boat with his disciples, and he said to them, "Let us cross to the other side of the lake," and they set out. ²³ While sailing, they fell asleep. But a storm of wind came upon the lake, and they were filling up and in danger. ²⁴ Coming to them, they woke him, saying, "Master, master, we are perishing!" And he awoke and rebuked the wind and the waves of the water, and they ceased, and there was calm. ²⁵ He said to them, "Where is your faith?" And they were afraid and marveled, saying to one another, "Who is this, that even the winds and the water obey him?" ²⁶ They arrived at the region of the Gerasenes, which is opposite Galilee. ²⁷ And when he stepped on the land, a man from the city, who had demons, met him. For a long time he had not worn clothing, and he did not live in a house, but in the tombs. ²⁸ Seeing Jesus, he cried out and fell before him, saying with a loud voice, "What do you have to do with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I implore you, do not torment me; ²⁹ for you have commanded the unclean spirit to go out from this man. For many years he has been bound by chains and in fetters, kept under guard, and whenever he broke free, the demon would drag him into the deserts." ³⁰ Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" And he said, "Legion," because many demons had gone into him. ³¹ And they begged him not to command them to go into the abyss. ³² Now there was a large herd of pigs feeding on the hillside. And the demons begged him to let them go into these. And he gave them permission. ³³ When the demons went out from the man, they entered the pigs, and the herd rushed down the cliff into the lake and drowned. ³⁴ The shepherds saw what had happened and fled, and told it in the city and in the countryside. ³⁵ And they went out to see what had happened and found the man from whom the demons had gone out, clothed and in his right mind, sitting at the feet of Jesus, and they were afraid. ³⁶ Those who saw it told them how the man who had been demon-possessed had been saved. ³⁷ All the people of the surrounding area of Gerasenes requested that he leave them, for they were greatly frightened. And he went back into the boat and returned. ³⁸ But the man from whom the demons had gone out begged to go with him. And Jesus sent him away, saying, ³⁹ "Return to your house and tell how much God has done for you." And he went through the whole city, proclaiming how much Jesus had done for him. ⁴⁰ Now as Jesus was returning, the crowds met him, for all were expecting him. ⁴¹ And behold, a man named Jairus, a ruler of the synagogue, came and fell at the feet of Jesus, pleading with him to go into his house, ⁴² for he had a daughter, his only child, about twelve years old, and she was dying. But as he went, the crowd pressed in on him. ⁴³ Now a woman who had suffered from a discharge of blood for twelve years, who had spent all her living on physicians and could not be healed by any, ⁴⁴ came from behind and touched the fringe of his cloak. And immediately her flow of blood stopped. ⁴⁵ And Jesus said, "Who is the one who touched me?" But when all denied it, Peter said, "Master, the crowds are crowding and pressing against you." ⁴⁶ But Jesus said, "Someone touched me, for I know that power has gone out from me." ⁴⁷ When the woman saw that she had not been hidden, she trembled and came and fell at his feet, telling him in the presence of all the people why she had touched him and how she had been healed immediately. ⁴⁸ And he said to her, "Daughter, your faith has saved you. Go in peace." ⁴⁹ While he was still speaking, someone came from the ruler of the synagogue, saying, "Your daughter has died; do not bother the teacher anymore." ⁵⁰ But Jesus, hearing this, answered him, "Do not fear, only believe, and she will be saved." ⁵¹ When he arrived at the house, he did not allow anyone to enter with him except Peter and John and James, and the child's father and mother. ⁵² They were all weeping and mourning for her. But he said, "Do not weep, for she is not dead but sleeping." ⁵³ They laughed at him, knowing she was dead. ⁵⁴ But taking her hand, he called out, saying, "Child, get up." ⁵⁵ And her spirit returned, and she stood up immediately. And he ordered that something be given to her to eat. ⁵⁶ Her parents were amazed, but he commanded them not to tell anyone what had happened.

Notes
- v. 1: "the twelve with him" — the Twelve refers to the apostles, but is not named explicitly here.
- v. 10: "mysteries of the kingdom of God" — the term "mystery" in this context denotes divine truths revealed only to the faithful.
- v. 38: "the man from whom the demons had gone out" — the reference is to the healed man in v. 27.
- v. 45: "power has gone out from me" — the use of "power" (dynamis) is intentional to emphasize the miraculous nature of the healing.
- v. 54: "Child, get up" — "Child" (pais) is used to address the girl tenderly, showing Jesus' compassion.
- v. 55: "her spirit returned" — the Greek uses "pneuma," which can mean "spirit," "wind," or "life," depending on context. Here, it refers to her returning to life.
