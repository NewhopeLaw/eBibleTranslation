# Luke 22

¹ The Passover festival, called the Feast of Unleavened Bread, was approaching. ² The chief priests and the scribes were looking for a way to arrest him, because they feared the people. ³ Then Satan entered Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, ⁴ and he went and conferred with the chief priests and the officers about how he might hand him over to them. ⁵ They were pleased and agreed to give him money. ⁶ So he consented and was looking for an opportunity to hand him over without the crowd being present. ⁷ Now the day of the festival had arrived, when it was necessary to sacrifice the Passover. ⁸ He sent Peter and John, saying, “Go and prepare the Passover meal for us so that we may eat it.” ⁹ They asked him, “Where would you have us prepare it?” ¹⁰ He said, “When you have entered the city, a man carrying a water jar will meet you. Follow him into the house into which he enters. ¹¹ Then you will say to the master of the house, ‘The Teacher asks you, Where is the guest room where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?’ ¹² And he will show you a large room already furnished. There, prepare the meal.” ¹³ So they went and found everything as he had told them, and they prepared the Passover. ¹⁴ And when the hour came, he reclined at table with the apostles. ¹⁵ He said to them, “I have longed greatly to eat this Passover with you before I suffer, ¹⁶ for I tell you, I will not eat it again until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God.” ¹⁷ Then he took a cup, gave thanks, and said, “Take this and divide it among yourselves; ¹⁸ for I tell you, I will not drink of the fruit of the vine again until the kingdom of God comes.” ¹⁹ Then he took the bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, “This is my body, given for you. Do this in remembrance of me.” ²⁰ And the cup after supper, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you.” ²¹ But look, the hand of the one betraying me is with me at the table. ²² For the Son of Man indeed goes as it has been determined, but woe to the man through whom he is betrayed.” ²³ And they began to argue among themselves, asking which of them it could be who would do this. ²⁴ A dispute also arose among them, about who among them was the greatest. ²⁵ He said to them, “The kings of the nations rule over them, and those in power are called benefactors. ²⁶ Not so with you. Instead, let the one who is greater among you become like the younger, and the one who leads like the one who serves, ²⁷ for which is greater, the one reclining at the table or the one serving? Is it not the one reclining? Yet I am among you as the one who serves. ²⁸ You are the ones who have remained with me in my trials. ²⁹ And I assign to you, just as my Father assigned to me a kingdom, ³⁰ so that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and you will sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel. ³¹ “Simon, Simon, look, Satan has asked to sift you all like wheat, ³² but I have prayed for you, that your faith may not fail. And you, when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.” ³³ He said to him, “Lord, I am ready to go with you both to prison and to death.” ³⁴ He said, “I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today before you deny me three times.” ³⁵ Then he said to them, “When I sent you out without money bag, or knapsack, or sandals, did you lack anything?” They said, “Nothing.” ³⁶ He said to them, “But now, whoever has a money bag, let him carry it, and in the same way a knapsack. And whoever does not have one, let him sell his cloak and buy a sword. ³⁷ For I tell you, this Scripture must be fulfilled in me, ‘And with the wicked he was numbered,’ and indeed, what relates to me is at hand.” ³⁸ They said, “Lord, look, here are two swords.” He said to them, “It is enough.” ³⁹ He went out and went, as was his custom, to the Mount of Olives. The disciples followed him. ⁴⁰ When he arrived at the place, he said to them, “Pray that you do not enter into temptation.” ⁴¹ And he withdrew from them about a stone’s throw, and knelt down and prayed, ⁴² saying, “Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me. Yet not my will, but yours be done.” ⁴³ An angel from heaven appeared to him to strengthen him. ⁴⁴ And in his agony he prayed more earnestly. And his sweat became like great drops of blood falling to the ground. ⁴⁵ Then he rose from prayer and came to the disciples and found them sleeping from sorrow, ⁴⁶ and he said to them, “Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray, so that you may not enter into temptation.” ⁴⁷ While he was still speaking, there came a crowd, and Judas, one of the twelve, led them, and he approached Jesus to greet him with a kiss. ⁴⁸ Jesus said to him, “Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?” ⁴⁹ When those who were around Jesus saw what was about to happen, they said, “Lord, shall we strike with the sword?” ⁵⁰ And one of them struck the high priest’s slave and cut off his right ear. ⁵¹ Then Jesus answered, “Let these things be.” And touching his ear, he healed him. ⁵² Then Jesus said to the chief priests and the officers of the temple and the elders who had come out against him with swords and clubs, ⁵³ “Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs? I was daily with you in the temple, and you did not lay hands on me. But this is your hour, and the power of darkness.” ⁵⁴ They seized him and led him away and brought him into the house of the high priest. Peter followed at a distance. ⁵⁵ Now a fire had been kindled in the middle of the courtyard, and the disciples were sitting around it. Peter sat among them. ⁵⁶ When a certain servant girl saw him sitting in the light, she looked at him and said, “This man was with him.” ⁵⁷ He denied it, saying, “Woman, I do not know him.” ⁵⁸ A little later, someone else saw him and said, “You are one of them.” But Peter said, “Man, I am not.” ⁵⁹ And after about an hour, another, with greater confidence, said, “Certainly, this man was with him, for he is a Galilean.” ⁶⁰ Peter said, “Man, I do not know what you are saying.” And immediately, while he was still speaking, the rooster crowed. ⁶¹ The Lord turned and looked at Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said to him, “Before the rooster crows today, you will deny me three times.” ⁶² And he went out and wept bitterly. ⁶³ The men who were holding Jesus mocked and beat him, ⁶⁴ and covering him, they asked him, “Prophesy! Who is the one who struck you?” ⁶⁵ And they said many other things against him, blaspheming. ⁶⁶ When day came, the elders of the people, the chief priests and the scribes, assembled, and they led him to their council, ⁶⁷ saying, “If you are the Christ, tell us.” He said to them, “If I tell you, you will not believe, ⁶⁸ and if you ask me, you will not answer.” ⁶⁹ “But from now on, the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God.” ⁷⁰ They all said, “Then are you the Son of God?” He said to them, “You say that I am.” ⁷¹ They said, “What need do we have for further testimony? For we have heard it from his own mouth.”

Notes  
- v. 19: The phrase "given for you" is omitted in the shorter text tradition, but the fuller text is included here as per the majority manuscript evidence.  
- v. 20: The longer ending of the cup, "the new covenant in my blood," is included based on the majority manuscript tradition.  
- v. 37: "And with the wicked he was numbered" is a reference to Isaiah 53:12. The phrase "what relates to me is at hand" is a paraphrase of the Greek text for clarity.  
- v. 46: "Sleeping from sorrow" reflects the Greek phrase "from grief" (ἀπὸ λύπης), indicating emotional weariness.
