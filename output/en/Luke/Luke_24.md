# Luke 24

¹ On the first day of the week, at dawn, they went to the tomb, bringing the spices they had prepared. ² They found the stone rolled away from the tomb. ³ Entering, they did not find the body. ⁴ Just as they were wondering about this, two men in shining garments appeared beside them. ⁵ In great fear, they bowed their faces to the ground. The men said to them, "Why are you looking for the living among the dead? ⁶ He is not here, but he has risen. Remember how he told you while still in Galilee, ⁷ saying, 'The Son of Man must be handed over to the hands of sinners and be crucified, and on the third day rise again.'" ⁸ Then they remembered his words. ⁹ Returning from the tomb, they told all these things to the eleven apostles and the rest. ¹⁰ It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the others with them who told the apostles. ¹¹ But their words seemed like nonsense to the apostles, and they did not believe them. ¹² But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. He stooped to look in and saw only the linen cloths. Then he went home, amazed at what had happened. ¹³ Now behold, two of them were going to a village called Emmaus, about seventy stades from Jerusalem, ¹⁴ and they were talking with one another about all these events. ¹⁵ And as they talked and debated, Jesus himself came up and walked with them, ¹⁶ but their eyes were kept from recognizing him. ¹⁷ He said to them, "What are these words you are exchanging with each other as you walk?" They stood still, looking sad. ¹⁸ One of them, named Cleopas, answered, "Are you the only stranger in Jerusalem and have you not known the things that have happened there in these days?" ¹⁹ He said to them, "What things?" They replied, "The things concerning Jesus the Nazarene, who was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people, ²⁰ how the chief priests and our rulers handed him over to be condemned to death, and they crucified him. ²¹ We had hoped that he was the one who would redeem Israel. But in addition to all this, it is now the third day since these things happened. ²² Also, certain women from our group have astonished us. They went to the tomb at dawn, ²³ and did not find his body. Instead, they came back saying they had seen a vision from angels who said he is alive. ²⁴ Some of our companions went to the tomb and found it just as the women had said, but they did not see him." ²⁵ Then he said to them, "O foolish ones, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken! ²⁶ Was it not necessary for the Christ to suffer these things and to enter into his glory?" ²⁷ Then beginning with Moses and all the prophets, he explained to them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself. ²⁸ When they came near the village to which they were going, he made as if to go on further. ²⁹ But they urged him, saying, "Stay with us, for it is toward evening, and the day is already leaning." And he went in to stay with them. ³⁰ Now when he was at the table with them, he took the bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them. ³¹ Then their eyes were opened, and they recognized him. And he vanished from their sight. ³² They said to each other, "Was not our heart burning within us while he was talking with us on the road and explaining the scriptures to us?" ³³ Then they got up at that hour and returned to Jerusalem. There they found the eleven gathered together with those with them, ³⁴ saying, "Indeed, the Lord has risen and appeared to Simon." ³⁵ And the two were telling what had happened on the road and how he had been recognized by them in the breaking of the bread. ³⁶ While they were still speaking, he himself stood among them. ³⁷ They were startled and terrified, thinking they were seeing a ghost. ³⁸ He said to them, "Why are you troubled, and why do doubts arise in your hearts? ³⁹ Look at my hands and my feet. It is I myself. Touch me and see. A ghost does not have flesh and bones, as you see I have." ⁴⁰ And saying this, he showed them his hands and his feet. ⁴¹ But while they were still disbelieving for joy and marveling, he said to them, "Do you have anything here to eat?" ⁴² They gave him a piece of broiled fish, ⁴³ and he took it and ate in front of them. ⁴⁴ Then he said to them, "These are my words, which I spoke to you while still with you, that everything written about me in the Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms must be fulfilled." ⁴⁵ Then he opened their minds to understand the scriptures, ⁴⁶ and he said to them, "Thus it is written that the Christ must suffer and rise from the dead on the third day, ⁴⁷ and that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his name to all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem. ⁴⁸ You are witnesses of these things. ⁴⁹ And behold, I send upon you what my Father promised. But stay in the city until you are clothed from above with power." ⁵⁰ Then he led them out as far as Bethany, and lifting up his hands, he blessed them. ⁵¹ And while he was blessing them, he was parted from them and taken up into heaven. ⁵² They worshiped him and returned to Jerusalem with great joy, ⁵³ and they were continually in the temple praising God.
