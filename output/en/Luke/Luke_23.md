# Luke 23

¹ When the whole crowd rose, they led him to Pilate. ² They began to accuse him, saying, "We found this man stirring up our people and forbidding to give taxes to Caesar and saying that he is the Messiah, a king." ³ Pilate asked him, saying, "Are you the king of the Jews?" He answered him, "You say so." ⁴ Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, "I find no cause for this man." ⁵ But they pressed on, saying, "He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, from Galilee to this place." ⁶ When Pilate heard that he was from Galilee, he asked whether the man was a Galilean. ⁷ Having learned that he was under Herod's jurisdiction, he sent him to Herod, who was also in Jerusalem at that time. ⁸ Herod was very pleased to see Jesus, for he had long wanted to see him because he had heard about him, and he had hoped to see some sign done by him. ⁹ He questioned him with many words, but Jesus gave him no answer. ¹⁰ The chief priests and the scribes stood nearby, accusing him fiercely. ¹¹ Herod, together with his soldiers, mocked him and, having clothed him in a brilliant robe, sent him back to Pilate. ¹² Herod and Pilate became friends that same day, for before they had been at enmity with one another. ¹³ Pilate, having summoned the chief priests, the rulers, and the people, ¹⁴ said to them, "You brought this man before me as one turning the people away. And behold, I have examined him before you and found in this man no cause for the accusations you make against him. ¹⁵ Nor did Herod, for I sent him back to you. And behold, nothing worthy of death has been done by him. ¹⁶ I will therefore discipline him and release him." ¹⁸ But the crowd, crying out together, said, "Away with this man! Release to us Barabbas!" ¹⁹ He was a man who had been thrown into prison for a certain revolt that had happened in the city and for murder. ²⁰ Pilate, wishing to release Jesus again, addressed the crowd. ²¹ But they kept crying out, saying, "Crucify him! Crucify him!" ²² The third time he said to them, "What evil has this man done? I have found in him no cause for death. I will therefore discipline him and release him." ²³ But they pressed on with loud voices, demanding that he be crucified, and their voices prevailed. ²⁴ Pilate then decided to grant their request. ²⁵ He released the man who had been thrown into prison for rebellion and murder, the one they asked for, and handed Jesus over to their will. ²⁶ As they led him away, they seized a certain Simon of Cyrene, who was coming from the countryside, and laid the cross on him to carry behind Jesus. ²⁷ A large crowd followed him, as well as women who were mourning and lamenting for him. ²⁸ Turning to them, Jesus said, "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for me, but for yourselves and for your children. ²⁹ For behold, days are coming when they will say, 'Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that did not bear, and the breasts that did not nurse.' ³⁰ Then they will begin to say to the mountains, 'Fall on us!' and to the hills, 'Cover us!' ³¹ For if they have done this when the wood is green, what will happen when it is dry?" ³² Two other criminals were led away with him to be put to death. ³³ When they arrived at the place called The Skull, there they crucified him and the criminals, one on his right and one on his left. ³⁴ Jesus said, "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing." And they cast lots for his clothes. ³⁵ The crowd stood watching. The rulers also mocked him, saying, "He saved others, let him save himself if he is the Messiah of God, the Chosen One." ³⁶ The soldiers also mocked him, approaching and offering him sour wine, ³⁷ and saying, "If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself." ³⁸ There was also an inscription over him: "This is the King of the Jews." ³⁹ One of the criminals who were crucified with him blasphemed him, saying, "Are you not the Messiah? Save yourself and us." ⁴⁰ But the other rebuked him, saying, "Do you not fear God, since you are under the same sentence? ⁴¹ We indeed receive what we deserve for our deeds, but this man has done nothing wrong." ⁴² And he said, "Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom." ⁴³ He said to him, "Truly I tell you, today you will be with me in paradise." ⁴⁴ It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness fell over the whole land until the ninth hour, ⁴⁵ as the sun was obscured. And the curtain of the sanctuary was torn in two. ⁴⁶ Jesus, crying out with a loud voice, said, "Father, into your hands I commit my spirit." Having said this, he breathed his last. ⁴⁷ The centurion, seeing what had taken place, praised God and said, "Certainly this man was righteous." ⁴⁸ All the crowds who had gathered for this spectacle, when they saw what had happened, beat their breasts and returned. ⁴⁹ All those who were acquainted with him, standing at a distance, saw these things, and the women who had followed him from Galilee. ⁵⁰ Now a man named Joseph, who was a council member, a good and righteous man— ⁵¹ this man had not consented to their decision and action— from the city of Arimathea, a town of the Jews, who was expecting the kingdom of God, ⁵² came to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus, ⁵³ and taking it down, wrapped it in a linen cloth, and placed it in a tomb cut into the rock, where no one had yet been laid. ⁵⁴ It was the day of Preparation, and the Sabbath was approaching. ⁵⁵ The women who had come with him from Galilee followed and saw the tomb and how his body was laid, ⁵⁶ and returning, they prepared spices and ointments. And the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.

Notes
- v. 18: The name "Barabbas" is a transliteration as per the Greek form and common English rendering.
- v. 34: "Forgive them" is the active voice used in the Greek imperative, hence the translation as a command.
- v. 43: "Paradise" is used for "παράδεισος," which in this context is best understood as a reference to the blissful presence of God.
- v. 50: The word "council member" is used for "βουλευτής," reflecting the role rather than a direct translation.
- v. 51: "Consented to their decision and action" reflects the compound verb "συγκατατεθειμένος" as an active agreement to the actions of the council.
