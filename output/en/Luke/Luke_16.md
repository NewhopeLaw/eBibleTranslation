# Luke 16

¹ He said to his disciples, "A certain rich man had a steward, and this steward was accused to him of wasting his possessions. ² So he called him in and said to him, 'What is this I hear about you? Give an account of your stewardship, for you can no longer serve as steward.' ³ The steward said to himself, 'What will I do, since my master is taking away the stewardship from me? I am not strong enough to dig, and I am ashamed to beg. ⁴ I have decided what to do, so that when I am removed from the stewardship, they will receive me into their homes.' ⁵ So he called each of the debtors of his master and said to the first one, 'How much do you owe my master?' ⁶ He answered, 'A hundred jars of olive oil.' He said to him, 'Take your bill and write quickly fifty.' ⁷ Then he said to another, 'How much do you owe?' He replied, 'A hundred sacks of wheat.' He said to him, 'Take your bill and write eighty.' ⁸ The master praised the dishonest steward for his shrewdness, for the children of this age are more shrewd in dealing with their own generation than the children of light. ⁹ And I say to you, make friends for yourselves with the money of unrighteousness, so that when it fails, you may be received into the eternal dwellings. ¹⁰ Whoever is faithful in a little is also faithful in much, and whoever is dishonest in a little is also dishonest in much. ¹¹ If then you have not been faithful in the unrighteous wealth, who will entrust to you the true riches? ¹² And if you have not been faithful with what is not yours, who will give you what is your own? ¹³ No servant can serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and money. ¹⁴ Now the Pharisees, who were lovers of money, heard all these things and scoffed at him. ¹⁵ He said to them, 'You are the ones who justify yourselves before men, but God knows your hearts. For what is highly prized among men is an abomination in the sight of God. ¹⁶ The law and the prophets were in effect until John; since then the kingdom of God is proclaimed, and everyone is forcing their way into it. ¹⁷ It is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for one stroke of a letter in the law to be dropped out. ¹⁸ Everyone who divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery, and whoever marries a woman who has been divorced from her husband commits adultery. ¹⁹ Now a certain rich man was clothed in purple and fine linen and feasted sumptuously every day. ²⁰ A poor man named Lazarus was laid at his gate, full of sores, ²¹ and longing to be fed with what fell from the rich man's table. Even the dogs came and licked his sores. ²² It happened that the poor man died and was carried away by the angels to Abraham's bosom. The rich man also died and was buried. ²³ And in Hades, lifting up his eyes, he was in torment, and he saw Abraham far off and Lazarus in his bosom. ²⁴ He called out and said, 'Father Abraham, have mercy on me and send Lazarus to dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue, for I am in anguish in this flame.' ²⁵ Abraham said, 'Child, remember that you received your good things in your lifetime, and Lazarus in like manner bad things; but now he is comforted here, and you are in anguish. ²⁶ And besides all this, between us and you a great chasm has been established, in order that those who would cross from here to you may not be able, nor to cross from there to us.' ²⁷ He said, 'Then I beg you, father, that you would send him to my father's house, ²⁸ for I have five brothers, so that he may warn them, so that they may not also come into this place of torment.' ²⁹ Abraham said, 'They have Moses and the prophets; let them hear them.' ³⁰ He said, 'No, father Abraham, but if someone goes to them from the dead, they will repent.' ³¹ Abraham said to him, 'If they do not listen to Moses and the prophets, neither will they be convinced even if someone rises from the dead.'

---

Notes
- v. 13: "money" is a rendering of μαμωνᾷ, a term often used metaphorically for wealth or material possessions.
- v. 26: "a great chasm" translates the Greek χάσμα μέγα, which literally means "a great ditch" or "gap."
- v. 30: "from the dead" translates ἐκ νεκρῶν, literally "from the dead ones."
- v. 31: "Moses and the prophets" refers to the Hebrew Bible as understood by Jesus and his contemporaries.
