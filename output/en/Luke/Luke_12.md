# Luke 12

¹ When the crowds were gathering in such great numbers that they were trampling one another, he turned to his disciples and said, "Watch yourselves from the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy. ² Nothing is hidden that will not be revealed, and nothing secret that will not be known. ³ Therefore, whatever you have said in the dark will be heard in the light, and what you have whispered in the inner rooms will be proclaimed from the rooftops. ⁴ I tell you, my friends, do not fear those who kill the body and after that can do nothing more. ⁵ I will show you whom to fear: Fear the one who, after having killed, has the power to cast into hell. Yes, I tell you, fear him. ⁶ Are not five sparrows sold for two assarions? Not one of them is forgotten before God. ⁷ Even the hair of your head is all counted. Do not fear; you are of more value than many sparrows. ⁸ I tell you, whoever acknowledges me before people, the Son of Man will acknowledge before the angels of God. ⁹ But whoever denies me before people will be denied before the angels of God. ¹⁰ And whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven, but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven. ¹¹ When you are brought before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, do not worry about how or what you are to answer, or what you are to say. ¹² For the Holy Spirit will teach you in that hour what you should say." ¹³ Someone from the crowd said to him, "Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me." ¹⁴ But he said to him, "Man, who made me a judge or an arbiter over you?" ¹⁵ Then he said to them, "Beware and be on guard against all greed, for one's life does not consist in the abundance of what one possesses." ¹⁶ He told them a parable, saying, "There was a rich man whose land produced a bountiful crop. ¹⁷ He was reasoning to himself, saying, 'What shall I do, since I have no room to store my crops?' ¹⁸ And he said, 'I will do this: I will tear down my barns and build larger ones, and there I will store all my grain and my goods, ¹⁹ and I will say to my soul, Soul, you have many good things laid up for many years; rest, eat, drink, be merry.'" ²⁰ But God said to him, "Fool, this night your soul is required of you, and the things you have prepared, whose will they be?" ²¹ So is the one who stores up treasure for himself and is not rich toward God. ²² He said to his disciples, "For this reason I tell you, do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat, nor about your body, what you will wear. ²³ For life is more than food, and the body more than clothing. ²⁴ Consider the ravens: they neither sow nor reap, they have no storeroom or granary, and yet God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than the birds! ²⁵ And who among you by being anxious can add a cubit to his height? ²⁶ If you cannot do even the least thing, why do you worry about the rest? ²⁷ Consider the lilies, how they grow: they do not toil nor do they spin, yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not clothed like one of these. ²⁸ If then God so clothes the grass in the field, which is alive today and thrown into the oven tomorrow, how much more will he clothe you, you of little faith! ²⁹ And do not seek what you will eat and what you will drink, nor be worried. ³⁰ For all the nations of the world seek these things, but your Father knows that you need them. ³¹ Rather, seek the kingdom of God, and these things will be given to you as well. ³² Do not be afraid, little flock, for your Father has been pleased to give you the kingdom. ³³ Sell your possessions and give alms; provide yourselves with moneybags that do not grow old, a treasure in the heavens that does not fade away, where no thief comes near and no moth destroys. ³⁴ For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also. ³⁵ Let your belts be girded and your lamps burning, ³⁶ and you yourselves like men waiting for their master, who may come at the third watch of the night, so that when he comes and knocks they may open to him at once. ³⁷ Happy are those servants whom the master finds awake when he comes. Truly I tell you, he will dress himself to serve, come up and have them recline at the table, and will come and wait on them. ³⁸ If he comes in the second watch or the third and finds them so, blessed are those servants. ³⁹ But know this: if the householder had known at what hour the thief was coming, he would not have let his house be broken into. ⁴⁰ You too must be ready, for the Son of Man is coming at an hour you do not expect. ⁴¹ Peter said, "Lord, is this parable addressed to us or to everyone?" ⁴² And the Lord said, "Who then is the faithful and prudent manager, whom the master will set over his household to give them their portion of food at the right time? ⁴³ Happy is the servant whom the master finds doing so when he comes. ⁴⁴ Truly I tell you, he will put him in charge of all his possessions. ⁴⁵ But if that servant says in his heart, 'My master is delayed in coming,' and begins to beat the male and female servants, to eat and drink and get drunk, ⁴⁶ the master of that servant will come on a day when he does not expect it and at an hour he does not know, and will cut him in two and assign his portion to the unfaithful. ⁴⁷ And that servant who knows the master's will and does not prepare or act accordingly will receive many blows. ⁴⁸ But the one who does not know and does things worthy of punishment will receive few blows. From everyone to whom much is given, much will be required, and from the one to whom much has been entrusted, more will be demanded. ⁴⁹ I have come to cast fire upon the earth, and how I wish it were already kindled! ⁵⁰ I have a baptism to be baptized with, and how great is my distress until it is accomplished! ⁵¹ Do you think that I have come to give peace on earth? No, I tell you, but rather division. ⁵² For from now on there will be five in one house divided, three against two and two against three. ⁵³ They will be divided, father against son and son against father, mother against daughter and daughter against mother, mother-in-law against daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law." ⁵⁴ He also said to the crowds, "When you see a cloud rising in the west, you immediately say, 'A rainstorm is coming,' and so it does. ⁵⁵ And when the south wind is blowing, you say, 'There will be a heatwave,' and it happens. ⁵⁶ Hypocrites! You know how to interpret the appearance of the earth and the sky, but how is it that you do not know how to interpret this time? ⁵⁷ Why even from yourselves do you not judge what is right? ⁵⁸ For when you go with your accuser before the ruler, on the way, make peace with him, so that he may not drag you before the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison. ⁵⁹ I tell you, you will not get out of there until you have paid the last penny."

---

Notes
- v. 10: The phrase "will not be forgiven" refers to the specific context of blasphemy against the Holy Spirit, a unique and weighty statement.
- v. 18: "Tear down my barns and build larger ones" reflects a natural Greek construction, not a literal instruction to destroy and rebuild.
- v. 49: "I have come to cast fire upon the earth" is symbolic of Jesus' mission to bring conflict and transformation.
- v. 59: "Last penny" is a translation of "last lepton," a small coin, used here to emphasize complete settlement.
