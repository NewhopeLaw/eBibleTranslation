# Luke 17

¹ He said to his disciples, "It is impossible for offenses not to come, but woe to the one through whom they come. ² It would be better for him if a millstone were placed around his neck and he were thrown into the sea than to cause one of these little ones to stumble. ³ Be on guard for yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him. ⁴ If he sins against you seven times in a day and seven times turns back to you saying, 'I repent,' you must forgive him. ⁵ The apostles said to the Lord, "Increase our faith." ⁶ The Lord said, "If you had faith the size of a mustard seed, you could say to this sycamore, 'Be uprooted and planted in the sea,' and it would obey you. ⁷ Which of you, having a servant plowing or tending sheep, would say to him when he comes in from the field, 'Come at once and sit down to eat'? ⁸ Would he not rather say to him, 'Prepare something to eat for me, and gird yourself and serve me until I have eaten and drunk, and afterward you will eat and drink'? ⁹ Does the servant have gratitude because he did what was commanded? ¹⁰ So you also, when you have done everything you were commanded, say, 'We are unworthy servants; we have done what we were supposed to do.' ¹¹ It happened that as he was going toward Jerusalem, he was passing through between Samaria and Galilee. ¹² And when he entered a certain village, ten lepers met him, who stood at a distance. ¹³ They raised a cry saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us." ¹⁴ When he saw them, he said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests." And it happened that as they went away, they were cleansed. ¹⁵ One of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back with a loud voice praising God, ¹⁶ and fell face down at his feet, thanking him. And he was a Samaritan. ¹⁷ Jesus responded and said, "Were not ten cleansed? Where are the other nine? ¹⁸ Was none of them found to return and give glory to God except this outsider?" ¹⁹ And he said to him, "Rise and go. Your faith has saved you." ²⁰ When asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God would come, he answered them and said, "The kingdom of God does not come with observation, ²¹ nor will they say, 'Here it is,' or 'There it is,' for behold, the kingdom of God is among you." ²² He said to the disciples, "Days are coming when you will long for one of the days of the Son of Man to see it, and you will not see it. ²³ And they will say to you, 'Here it is,' and 'There it is.' Do not go, nor follow them. ²⁴ For just as lightning that flashes from under one heaven to the other shines, so will the Son of Man be in his day. ²⁵ But first, he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation. ²⁶ Just as it was in the days of Noah, so will it be in the days of the Son of Man. ²⁷ They were eating, drinking, marrying, being given in marriage, until the day Noah entered the ark, and the flood came and destroyed them all. ²⁸ Likewise, it was in the days of Lot: they were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, building. ²⁹ But on the day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained from heaven and destroyed them all. ³⁰ So it will be on the day the Son of Man is revealed. ³¹ In that day, whoever is on the housetop and their belongings are in the house must not go down to get them, and whoever is in the field must not turn back. ³² Remember the wife of Lot. ³³ Whoever seeks to preserve their life will lose it, but whoever loses their life will preserve it. ³⁴ I tell you, in this night there will be two on one bed. One will be taken and the other left. ³⁵ There will be two grinding at one mill. One will be taken, and the other left." ³⁶ They replied to him, "Where, Lord?" He said to them, "Where the body is, there the vultures will gather." ³⁷ And they replied to him, "Where, Lord?" He said to them, "Where the body is, there the vultures will gather."
