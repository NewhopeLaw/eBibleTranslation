# 1 John 3

¹ See what love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God—and that is what we are. Because of this, the world does not know us, because it did not know him. ² Beloved, we are now children of God, and it has not yet been made manifest what we will be. We do know this: when he is revealed, we will be like him, because we will see him just as he is. ³ And everyone who has this hope in him purifies themselves, just as he is pure. ⁴ Everyone who commits sin also commits lawlessness, and sin is lawlessness. ⁵ And you know that he was revealed in order to take away sins, and in him there is no sin. ⁶ Everyone who remains in him does not sin. Everyone who does sin has not seen him or known him. ⁷ Children, let no one deceive you. The one who does righteousness is righteous, just as he is righteous. ⁸ The one who does sin is from the devil, because the devil has sinned from the beginning. For this reason the Son of God was revealed, in order to destroy the works of the devil. ⁹ Everyone born of God does not commit sin, because God’s seed remains in them, and they cannot sin, because they are born of God. ¹⁰ In this the children of God and the children of the devil are clearly revealed: whoever does not do righteousness is not from God, nor is the one who does not love their brother. ¹¹ For this is the message you have heard from the beginning, that we should love one another. ¹² Do not be like Cain, who was from the evil one and murdered his brother. Why did he murder him? Because his works were evil, and his brother’s were righteous. ¹³ Do not be surprised, brothers, if the world hates you. ¹⁴ We know that we have passed from death into life, because we love the brothers. The one who does not love remains in death. ¹⁵ Everyone who hates their brother is a murderer, and you know that no one who has the eternal life remains in them. ¹⁶ In this we have come to know love: that he laid down his life for us, and so we also ought to lay down our lives for the brothers. ¹⁷ Whoever has the means of this world and sees their brother in need, yet withholds compassion from them, how can the love of God remain in them? ¹⁸ Children, let us not love in word or with the tongue, but in action and in truth. ¹⁹ In this we will know we are from the truth, and we will be at peace before him. ²⁰ For if our hearts accuse us, God is greater than our hearts and knows all things. ²¹ Beloved, if our hearts do not accuse us, we have boldness before God, ²² and whatever we ask we receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do what pleases him. ²³ And this is his commandment: that we believe in the name of his Son Jesus the Messiah and love one another, just as he has commanded us. ²⁴ And the one who keeps his commandments remains in him, and he in them. And in this we know that he remains in us, by the Spirit he has given to us.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "ἁγνίζει ἑαυτὸν" is translated as "purifies themselves" rather than "cleanses themselves" to preserve the active voice and the sense of personal discipline.
- v. 17: "τὸν βίον τοῦ κόσμου" is translated as "the means of this world" to reflect the material blessings or possessions in the present life.
- v. 20: "καταγινώσκῃ" is translated as "accuse us" to reflect its use in the context of inner conviction or self-reproach.
