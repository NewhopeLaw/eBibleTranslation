# Mark 16

¹ When the Sabbath was over, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices so they could go and anoint him. ² Very early, at the first day of the week, they came to the tomb at sunrise. ³ They said to each other, "Who will roll the stone away from the entrance of the tomb?" ⁴ Looking up, they saw that the stone had already been rolled back—it was very large. ⁵ Entering the tomb, they saw a young man dressed in a white robe sitting on the right. They were startled. ⁶ He said to them, "Do not be startled. You are looking for Jesus the Nazorean, the one who was crucified. He has risen—he is not here. Look, this is the place where they laid him. ⁷ But go and tell his disciples and Peter that he is going ahead of you to Galilee. You will see him there, just as he told you." ⁸ They left the tomb and ran away, trembling and amazed. They said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid. ⁹ After rising, Jesus appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom he had driven out seven demons. ¹⁰ She went and told those who had been with him and were mourning and weeping. ¹¹ But when they heard that he was alive and had been seen by her, they did not believe. ¹² Later, he appeared in a different form to two of them walking in the country. ¹³ These went and told the others, but they did not believe them either. ¹⁴ Finally, while the eleven were sitting together, he appeared to them and rebuked their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they did not believe those who had seen him after he had risen. ¹⁵ He said to them, "Go into the world and proclaim the good news to the whole creation. ¹⁶ The one who believes and is baptized will be saved, but the one who disbelieves will be condemned. ¹⁷ These signs will accompany those who believe: in my name they will cast out demons, they will speak in new tongues, ¹⁸ they will pick up serpents, and if they drink something deadly, it will not harm them. They will lay hands on the sick, and they will recover." ¹⁹ So the Lord Jesus, after speaking to them, was taken up into heaven and sat at the right hand of God. ²⁰ They went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the accompanying signs.

Notes
- v. 8: The phrase "καὶ οὐδενὶ οὐδὲν εἶπαν" (and to no one did they say anything) is rendered clearly, preserving the emphasis on their fear and silence.
- v. 9–20: These verses are enclosed in square brackets in many critical editions of Mark, indicating they are not found in the earliest and most reliable manuscripts. However, as instructed, all source text is translated.
