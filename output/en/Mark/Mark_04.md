# Mark 4

¹ Again, Jesus began teaching by the sea. A great crowd gathered around him, so much so that he got into a boat and sat in the sea, while the whole crowd stood on the shore. ² He taught them in many parables, and in his teaching he said to them: ³ "Listen. A sower went out to sow. ⁴ And as he sowed, some seed fell beside the path, and the birds came and ate it. ⁵ Some seed fell on rocky ground where the soil was shallow, and immediately it sprang up because the soil wasn't deep. ⁶ And when the sun rose, it was scorched, and because it had no root, it withered. ⁷ Some seed fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked it, and it gave no fruit. ⁸ Some seed fell on good soil, and it produced grain, growing up and increasing, and it gave thirty, sixty, and a hundredfold." ⁹ And he said, "Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear." ¹⁰ When it was evening, those around him, including the twelve, asked him about the parables. ¹¹ He said to them, "To you is given the secret of the kingdom of God, but to those outside everything is in parables, ¹² so that seeing they may see and not perceive, and hearing they may hear and not understand, lest they turn back and be forgiven." ¹³ And he said to them, "Do you not understand this parable? How will you understand all the parables? ¹⁴ The sower sows the word. ¹⁵ These are the ones along the path where the word is sown. When they hear, immediately Satan comes and takes away the word that was sown in them. ¹⁶ And these are the ones sown on rocky ground. When they hear the word, they receive it immediately with joy, ¹⁷ but they have no root in themselves and are temporary. And when tribulation or persecution comes because of the word, immediately they stumble. ¹⁸ And others are those sown among the thorns. These are the ones who hear the word, ¹⁹ but the cares of the world and the deceitfulness of wealth and the desires for other things come in and choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful. ²⁰ And these are the ones sown on good soil, who hear the word and accept it and produce grain, thirty, sixty, and a hundredfold." ²¹ And he said to them, "Is a lamp brought to be put under a basket or under a bed, but rather on a lampstand? ²² For nothing is hidden except to be revealed, and nothing is secret except to come to light. ²³ Let whoever has ears to hear, hear." ²⁴ And he said to them, "Be careful what you hear. With the measure you use, it will be measured to you, and more will be added to you. ²⁵ For whoever has, to him more will be given, and from whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away." ²⁶ And he said, "This is how the kingdom of God is, as a man who sows seed on the ground. ²⁷ He sleeps and gets up by night and day, and the seed sprouts and grows, though he knows not how. ²⁸ The earth produces by itself, first the blade, then the head, then the full grain in the head. ²⁹ And when the grain is ripe, he immediately sends the sickle, because the harvest has come." ³⁰ And he said, "How can we compare the kingdom of God, or in what parable shall we set it? ³¹ It is like a mustard seed, which, when sown on the ground, is the smallest of all the seeds on the earth. ³² And when it is sown, it grows up and becomes greater than all the garden vegetables, and makes branches so large that the birds of the sky can nest under its shade." ³³ With parables like these he spoke to them as much as they could hear. ³⁴ He did not speak to them in parables, but he explained everything to his own disciples privately. ³⁵ And on that day, when evening had come, he said to them, "Let us go across to the other side." ³⁶ Leaving the crowd behind, they took him in the boat, and other boats were with him. ³⁷ And a great windstorm arose, and the waves were beating into the boat, so much so that the boat was already filling up. ³⁸ He was in the stern, asleep on the cushion. And they woke him up and said to him, "Teacher, do you not care that we are perishing?" ³⁹ And he awoke, and rebuked the wind and said to the sea, "Be silent, be still." And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm. ⁴⁰ And he said to them, "Why are you afraid? Do you still have no faith?" ⁴¹ And they were greatly afraid, and they said to one another, "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"

Notes
- v. 12: The phrase "lest they turn back and be forgiven" reflects the original's "ἵνα... μήποτε ἐπιστρέψωσιν καὶ ἀφεθῇ αὐτοῖς."
- v. 25: The rendering "even what he has will be taken away" reflects the original's "καὶ ὃ ἔχει ἀρθήσεται ἀπʼ αὐτοῦ."
