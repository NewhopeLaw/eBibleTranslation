# Mark 15

¹ Early in the morning, the chief priests held a council with the elders and scribes and the whole Sanhedrin. They bound Jesus, led him away, and handed him over to Pilate. ² Pilate asked him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" Jesus answered him, "You say so." ³ The chief priests brought many accusations against him. ⁴ Pilate asked him again, "Do you give no answer? See how many charges they bring against you." ⁵ But Jesus gave no further answer, so that Pilate was amazed. ⁶ Now at the festival, Pilate used to release a prisoner for them, the one they requested. ⁷ A man named Barabbas was in prison with the rebels, who had committed murder in the uprising. ⁸ The crowd began to demand what they usually did. ⁹ Pilate answered them, "Do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?" ¹⁰ For he knew that the chief priests had handed him over out of envy. ¹¹ But the chief priests incited the crowd to ask for Barabbas to be released to them instead. ¹² Pilate asked them again, "Then what would you like me to do with the one you call the King of the Jews?" ¹³ They cried out again, "Crucify him!" ¹⁴ Pilate said to them, "What evil has he done?" But they shouted all the more, "Crucify him!" ¹⁵ Wanting to satisfy the crowd, Pilate released Barabbas to them, and after flogging Jesus, he handed him over to be crucified. ¹⁶ The soldiers led him into the courtyard of the palace, which was called the Praetorium, and they called the whole cohort together. ¹⁷ They clothed him in a purple robe, and after weaving a crown of thorns, they placed it on his head. ¹⁸ They began to greet him, "Hail, King of the Jews!" ¹⁹ And they struck his head with a reed, spat on him, and knelt in mock worship. ²⁰ When they had finished mocking him, they removed the purple robe and put his own clothes back on him. Then they led him out to crucify him. ²¹ They forced a certain Simon of Cyrene, who was coming in from the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to carry his cross. ²² They brought him to Golgotha, which is translated as the Place of the Skull. ²³ They offered him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not take it. ²⁴ Then they crucified him and divided his clothes by casting lots for them. ²⁵ It was the third hour when they crucified him. ²⁶ And there was a written sign above him, which read: "The King of the Jews." ²⁷ They crucified two rebels with him, one on his right and one on his left. ²⁸ Those who passed by insulted him, shaking their heads and saying, "So you are the one who destroys the temple and rebuilds it in three days? ²⁹ Save yourself now, come down from the cross!" ³⁰ Likewise, the chief priests mocked each other along with the scribes, saying, "He saved others, but he cannot save himself. ³¹ Let this Messiah, the King of Israel, come down from the cross now, so that we may see and believe." And those crucified with him also insulted him. ³² It was the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour. ³³ At the ninth hour, Jesus cried out with a loud voice, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" which means, "My God, my God, why have you abandoned me?" ³⁴ Some of the bystanders who heard this said, "Look, he is calling for Elijah!" ³⁵ Someone ran, soaked a sponge with sour wine, put it on a reed, and gave it to him to drink, saying, "Let's see if Elijah comes to take him down." ³⁶ Jesus uttered a loud cry and breathed his last. ³⁷ And the curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom. ³⁸ The centurion who stood facing Jesus saw how he died and said, "Truly, this man was the Son of God." ³⁹ There were also women watching from a distance, among them Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome. ⁴⁰ These women had followed him when he was in Galilee and had cared for him, and there were many other women who had come up with him to Jerusalem. ⁴¹ When evening had come, and it was the day of Preparation, which is the day before the Sabbath, ⁴² Joseph of Arimathea, a respected member of the council and himself a man who was expecting the kingdom of God, came forward. He boldly went in to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. ⁴³ Pilate was surprised that Jesus was already dead. He called the centurion and asked him if Jesus had already died. ⁴⁴ And having learned from the centurion, Pilate gave the body to Joseph. ⁴⁵ Joseph bought a linen shroud, took Jesus down, wrapped him in the shroud, and placed him in a tomb that had been cut out of the rock. He rolled a stone against the entrance of the tomb. ⁴⁶ Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where he was laid.

Notes
- v. 28: "Those who passed by insulted him..." — the original reads "καὶ οἱ παραπορευόμενοι ἐβλασφήμουν αὐτόν..." which is an accusative clause, here rendered as a narrative continuation for clarity.
