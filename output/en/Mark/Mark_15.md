# Mark 15

¹ Early in the morning the chief priests held a council with the elders and scribes and the whole Sanhedrin. They bound Jesus, led him away, and handed him over to Pilate. ² Pilate asked him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" Jesus answered him, "You say it." ³ The chief priests accused him of many things. ⁴ Pilate asked him again, "Do you give no answer? See how many charges they bring against you!" ⁵ But Jesus gave no further answer, so that Pilate was amazed. ⁶ It was customary for the governor to release one prisoner to the people at the festival. They often asked for the one they wanted. ⁷ A man named Barabbas was in prison with the rebels who had committed murder in a riot. ⁸ The crowd went up and began to ask for a favor, as was their custom. ⁹ Pilate asked them, "Would you like me to release to you the King of the Jews?" ¹⁰ He knew this was from envy, for the chief priests had handed Jesus over to him. ¹¹ The chief priests, however, incited the crowd to request the release of Barabbas instead. ¹² Pilate asked them again, "Then what should I do with the one you call the King of the Jews?" ¹³ The crowd shouted again, "Crucify him!" ¹⁴ Pilate said to them, "What evil has he done?" But they shouted all the louder, "Crucify him!" ¹⁵ Wanting to satisfy the crowd, Pilate released Barabbas to them and handed Jesus over after flogging him to be crucified. ¹⁶ The soldiers led Jesus into the courtyard of the palace, which is called the Praetorium, and they called together the whole cohort. ¹⁷ They clothed him in a purple robe and placed a crown of thorns on his head. ¹⁸ They began to salute him, "Hail, King of the Jews!" ¹⁹ They struck his head with a reed and spat at him. Then they knelt down in mock worship. ²⁰ After they had mocked him, they stripped off the purple robe and put his own clothes back on him. Then they led him out to crucify him. ²¹ They compelled a passerby, Simon of Cyrene, who was coming from the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to carry Jesus' cross. ²² They brought him to Golgotha, which means the Place of the Skull. ²³ They gave him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not drink it. ²⁴ They crucified him and divided his clothes, casting lots to see who would get what. ²⁵ It was the third hour when they crucified him. ²⁶ The inscription of the charge against him read, "The King of the Jews." ²⁷ They crucified two rebels with him, one on his right and one on his left. ²⁹ Those passing by mocked him, shaking their heads and saying, "Aha! The one who was destroying the temple and rebuilding it in three days—" ³⁰ "Save yourself by coming down from the cross." ³¹ Likewise, the chief priests mocked one another, saying, "He saved others, but he cannot save himself." ³² "Let the Messiah, the King of Israel, come down from the cross now, so that we may see and believe." And those who were crucified with him also taunted him. ³³ Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour. ³⁴ At the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" which means, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" ³⁵ Some of those standing nearby heard this and said, "Look, he is calling for Elijah!" ³⁶ One ran, took a sponge, filled it with sour wine, and held it to his mouth with a reed, saying, "Let us see if Elijah will come to take him down." ³⁷ Jesus gave a loud cry and breathed his last. ³⁸ The curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom. ³⁹ The centurion who stood opposite Jesus and saw how he breathed his last said, "Truly this man was the Son of God." ⁴⁰ There were also women looking on from a distance, among them Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome. ⁴¹ These women had followed Jesus from Galilee and had cared for him. Many other women who had come up with him to Jerusalem were also there. ⁴² When evening had come, since it was the day of Preparation, that is, the day before the Sabbath, ⁴³ Joseph of Arimathea, a prominent council member who was himself waiting for the kingdom of God, came boldly to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. ⁴⁴ Pilate was surprised that Jesus had already died, so he called the centurion and asked him if Jesus had already died. ⁴⁵ When he learned from the centurion, Pilate gave the body to Joseph. ⁴⁶ Joseph bought a linen cloth, took Jesus down, wrapped him in the linen, and placed him in a tomb that had been cut out of the rock. Then he rolled a stone against the door of the tomb. ⁴⁷ Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses watched where he was laid.

Notes
- v. 28: The verse "And those who passed by reviled him..." is missing in the source text, but it is present in some manuscripts and appears in many versions. It is omitted here per the provided source.
- v. 27: "from the right hand" is translated as "on his right" to match natural English phrasing.
- v. 34: The Aramaic phrase is translated as "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" to reflect the meaning rather than a direct transliteration.
- v. 44: "Pilate was surprised" reflects the Greek "ἐθαύμασεν" as a natural interpretation of Pilate's reaction.
- v. 46: "rolled a stone against the door" is a direct translation of the Greek for the action described.
