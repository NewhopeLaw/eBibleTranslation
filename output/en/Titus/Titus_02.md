# Titus 2

¹ But you must speak what is suitable for sound teaching. ² Elders must be temperate, dignified, self-controlled, sound in faith, love, and endurance. ³ Likewise, older women must be reverent in conduct, not slanderers, not excessive in wine, and good teachers, ⁴ so that they may encourage the younger women to love their husbands, to love their children, ⁵ to be self-controlled, chaste, housekeepers, good, obedient to their own husbands, so that the word of God may not be spoken against. ⁶ Likewise, encourage the younger men to be self-disciplined, ⁷ showing yourself in all respects a model of good works, in your teaching with integrity and dignity, ⁸ with a sound and unassailable message, so that one who opposes will be ashamed and have nothing evil to say about us. ⁹ Slaves must be subject to their own masters in all things, being well-pleasing, not argumentative, ¹⁰ not pilfering, but showing complete faithfulness in every good thing, so that they may adorn the teaching of our Savior God in all respects. ¹¹ For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation for all people, ¹² teaching us to renounce ungodliness and worldly desires and to live self-controlled, upright, and godly lives in the present age, ¹³ as we look for the blessed hope and the appearing of the glory of the great God and our Savior Jesus Christ, ¹⁴ who gave himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and to purify for himself a people who are his own, eager to do good works. ¹⁵ These things speak, exhort, and rebuke with all authority. Let no one look down on you.

---

Notes
- v. 11: “For the grace of God has appeared” — The use of *ἐπεφάνη* (appeared) with reference to grace is interpretively nuanced, but the context suggests a reference to the incarnation. The rendering reflects the theological weight of the moment.
