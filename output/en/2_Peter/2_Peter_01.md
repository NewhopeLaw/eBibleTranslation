# 2 Peter 1

¹ Simon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have received a share equal to ours in the faith through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ: ² May grace and peace be multiplied to you in the knowledge of God and of our Lord Jesus. ³ For everything that is given to us for life and godliness has been given through the knowledge of him who called us with his own glory and excellence, ⁴ through which have been given to us the great and precious promises, so that through them you may become partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through desire. ⁵ And indeed, with every effort, you must add to your faith virtue, and to virtue knowledge, ⁶ to knowledge self-control, and to self-control endurance, and to endurance godliness, ⁷ to godliness brotherly affection, and to brotherly affection love. ⁸ For these things being present and abounding make you neither idle nor unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ. ⁹ For whoever lacks these things is blind, having lost sight, forgotten the cleansing of his past sins. ¹⁰ Therefore, brothers, be all the more eager to confirm your call and election, because doing these things you will never fall. ¹¹ For in this way a rich entrance will be granted to you into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. ¹² Therefore, I will always remind you of these things, even though you know them and are established in the present truth. ¹³ It is fitting for me, as long as I am in this tent, to stir you up through reminders, ¹⁴ knowing that the removal of this tent is soon, just as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me. ¹⁵ I will therefore always be eager to have you remember these things after my departure. ¹⁶ For we did not follow cleverly devised myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we became eyewitnesses of his majesty. ¹⁷ For he received from God the Father honor and glory, a voice being borne to him of this sort from the glorious majesty: "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased"— ¹⁸ and this voice we ourselves heard being borne from heaven when we were with him on the holy mountain. ¹⁹ And we have a more reliable prophetic word, to which you do well to pay attention as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts. ²⁰ First of all, you must know this: no prophecy of Scripture is a matter of one's own interpretation, ²¹ for no prophecy was ever carried along by the will of man, but men carried along by the Holy Spirit spoke from God.

---

Notes
- v. 11: "rich entrance" translates "πλουσίως ἐπιχορηγηθήσεται ὑμῖν ἡ εἴσοδος" as a metaphor for divine blessing and access, not literal wealth.
- v. 20: "no prophecy of Scripture is a matter of one's own interpretation" translates "πᾶσα προφητεία γραφῆς ἰδίας ἐπιλύσεως οὐ γίνεται" with an interpretive rendering to clarify the prohibition against private interpretation of prophetic texts.
- v. 21: "carried along by the Holy Spirit" reflects the metaphor of divine inspiration in "ὑπὸ πνεύματος ἁγίου φερόμενοι."
