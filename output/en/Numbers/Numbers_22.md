# Numbers 22

Notes
- v. 15: "greater and more honored than these" — the Hebrew uses a comparative form, indicating that the new messengers are superior to the previous ones.
- v. 28: "opened the mouth of the donkey" — the Hebrew uses a common idiom for enabling someone to speak, not necessarily indicating literal speech.
- v. 30: "Have I not been your donkey" — the Hebrew uses a rhetorical question to emphasize the donkey's long-standing relationship with Balaam.
