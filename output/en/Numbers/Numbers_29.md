# Numbers 29

¹ On the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you must hold a sacred assembly. You must not do any laborious work. It shall be a day of blowing trumpets for you. ² You must bring to the LORD a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma: one unblemished bull from the herd, one unblemished ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs. ³ Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the ram. ⁴ One tenth for each lamb, for the seven lambs. ⁵ And one male goat for a sin offering, to atone for you. ⁶ In addition to the monthly burnt offering and its grain offering, and the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings, as their appointed portions, a pleasing aroma, a burnt offering to the LORD. ⁷ On the tenth day of this seventh month you must hold a sacred assembly. You must afflict your souls. You must not do any work. ⁸ You must bring to the LORD a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma: one unblemished bull from the herd, one unblemished ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs. ⁹ Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the ram. ¹⁰ One tenth for each lamb, for the seven lambs. ¹¹ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the sin offering of atonement, and the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings, as their appointed portions, a pleasing aroma, a burnt offering to the LORD. ¹² And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month you must hold a sacred assembly. You must not do any laborious work. You must celebrate a festival to the LORD for seven days. ¹³ You must bring to the LORD a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma: thirteen unblemished bulls from the herd, two unblemished rams, and fourteen unblemished year-old male lambs. ¹⁴ Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for each bull, for the thirteen bulls, and two tenths for each ram, for the two rams. ¹⁵ One tenth for each lamb, for the fourteen lambs. ¹⁶ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ¹⁷ On the second day, twelve unblemished bulls from the herd, two unblemished rams, and fourteen unblemished year-old male lambs. ¹⁸ Their grain offering and their drink offerings shall be for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, as their appointed portions. ¹⁹ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ²⁰ On the third day, eleven unblemished bulls from the herd, two unblemished rams, and fourteen unblemished year-old male lambs. ²¹ Their grain offering and their drink offerings shall be for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, as their appointed portions. ²² And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ²³ On the fourth day, ten unblemished bulls from the herd, two unblemished rams, and fourteen unblemished year-old male lambs. ²⁴ Their grain offering and their drink offerings shall be for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, as their appointed portions. ²⁵ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ²⁶ On the fifth day, nine unblemished bulls from the herd, two unblemished rams, and fourteen unblemished year-old male lambs. ²⁷ Their grain offering and their drink offerings shall be for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, as their appointed portions. ²⁸ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ²⁹ On the sixth day, eight unblemished bulls from the herd, two unblemished rams, and fourteen unblemished year-old male lambs. ³⁰ Their grain offering and their drink offerings shall be for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, as their appointed portions. ³¹ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ³² On the seventh day, seven unblemished bulls from the herd, two unblemished rams, and fourteen unblemished year-old male lambs. ³³ Their grain offering and their drink offerings shall be for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, as their appointed portions. ³⁴ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ³⁵ On the eighth day, a solemn assembly must be held for you. You must not do any laborious work. ³⁶ You must bring to the LORD a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma: one unblemished bull from the herd, one unblemished ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs. ³⁷ Their grain offering and their drink offerings shall be for the bull, for the ram, and for the lambs, in their number, as their appointed portions. ³⁸ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offerings. ³⁹ These you must do to the LORD at your appointed feasts, in addition to your vows and freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, your grain offerings, your drink offerings, and your peace offerings.

Notes
- v. 38: "sin offering" is used for שְׂעִיר חַטָּאת. The rendering is consistent with previous instances in the chapter.
