# Numbers 32

¹ The Reubenites and the Gadites had a large number of livestock, and they saw the land of Jazer and the land of Gilead, and they noticed that it was a land suitable for pasture. ² Then the Gadites and the Reubenites came to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the leaders of the community, and they said, ³ "Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Chesbon, Elealeh, Shbam, Nebo, and Bven — ⁴ this is the land that the LORD has given before the community of Israel, a land for pasture, and it is pasture for your servants." ⁵ They continued, "If we have found favor in your eyes, please grant this land to your servants as an inheritance. Do not cross the Jordan." ⁶ Moses said to the Gadites and the Reubenites, "Are your brothers going to war, and you will remain here? ⁷ Why would you discourage the hearts of the people of Israel from crossing over to the land that the LORD has given them? ⁸ This is what your ancestors did when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to see the land. ⁹ They went up to the Valley of Eshcol, they saw the land, and they discouraged the hearts of the people of Israel from entering the land that the LORD had given them. ¹⁰ The anger of the LORD burned on that day, and he swore, saying, ¹¹ 'If those men who went up from Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, who see good and evil, see the land that I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob — yet they do not follow after me, ¹² except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, and Joshua son of Nun, for they did follow after the LORD — ¹³ the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and he led them astray in the desert for forty years, until all the generation that had done evil in the sight of the LORD was consumed. ¹⁴ And now you — you are rising as a rebellious generation of sinners to add still more to the anger of the LORD against Israel. ¹⁵ If you turn back after him, he will still add to letting you remain in the desert, and you will ruin this whole people.' ¹⁶ They came near to him and said, "Let us build for our livestock enclosures here, and cities for our children." ¹⁷ "We ourselves will go armed before the people of Israel, until we have brought them to their place. And our children will dwell in fortified cities because of the inhabitants of the land." ¹⁸ "We will not return to our houses until the people of Israel have each received their inheritance." ¹⁹ "We will not inherit with them beyond the Jordan or beyond, for our inheritance has already come to us beyond the Jordan toward the east." ²⁰ Moses said to them, "If you do this thing, if you go armed before the LORD into battle, ²¹ then every warrior will cross the Jordan before the LORD until he has driven out his enemies from before him. ²² The land will be subdued before the LORD, and then you will return, and you will be clean before the LORD and before Israel, and this land will be yours as an inheritance before the LORD. ²³ But if you do not do this, you will have sinned against the LORD, and you will know your sin when it overtakes you." ²⁴ "Build for yourselves cities for your children and enclosures for your livestock, and do as you have said from your mouths." ²⁵ The Gadites and the Reubenites said to Moses, "Your servants will do as my lord commands." ²⁶ "Our children, our women, and all our livestock will be there in the cities of Gilead." ²⁷ "But your servants will cross, every warrior armed, before the LORD into battle, as my lord says." ²⁸ Moses gave orders to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the leaders of the fathers' houses of the people of Israel. ²⁹ Moses said to them, "If the Gadites and the Reubenites cross with you, every warrior armed into battle before the LORD, and the land is subdued before you, then you will give them the land of Gilead as an inheritance." ³⁰ "But if they do not cross armed with you into battle, then they will inherit with you in the land of Canaan." ³¹ The Gadites and the Reubenites answered, "What the LORD has spoken to your servants, we will do." ³² "We will cross armed before the LORD into the land of Canaan, and our inheritance will be given to us beyond the Jordan." ³³ Moses gave to the Gadites, to the Reubenites, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land with its cities, including all its boundaries, the cities of the land around them. ³⁴ The Gadites built Dibon, Ataroth, and Arroer. ³⁵ And Atroth-Shophan, and Jazer, and they raised them up. ³⁶ And Beth-Nimrah and Beth-Haran, fortified cities and sheep enclosures. ³⁷ The Reubenites built Heshbon and Elealeh and Kiryataim, ³⁸ and Nebo and Baal-Meon, which were named, and Sibmah. And they named the cities they built after their names. ³⁹ The descendants of Machir son of Manasseh, Gilead, took it and subdued the Amorite who dwelled there. ⁴⁰ Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he dwelled there. ⁴¹ Jair son of Manasseh went and captured their villages and named them after himself, Havvoth-Jair. ⁴² Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages and named them after himself, Nobah.

Notes
- v. 9: "the people of Israel" — the Hebrew uses the plural form of "people," referring to the Israelites in general.
- v. 13: "led them astray in the desert" — the Hebrew uses the word "ne'eman" which can mean "to lead astray" or "to wander."
- v. 20: "armed before the LORD" — the Hebrew uses "chalutz" which means "warrior" or "one who goes out to war."
- v. 34-38: The names of the cities are transliterated from the Hebrew. Some are not fully clear in the original text but are rendered as best as possible based on standard transliteration.
