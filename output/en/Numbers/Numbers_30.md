# Numbers 30

¹ Moses spoke to the Israelites all that the LORD had commanded him. ² Moses spoke to the leaders of the tribes of Israel, saying, "This is the instruction that the LORD has given: ³ When a person makes a vow to the LORD, or swears an oath to bind themselves, they must not break their word. Whatever comes from their mouth they must carry out. ⁴ If a woman makes a vow to the LORD, or binds herself by an oath, while she is in her father's house in her youth, ⁵ and her father hears of her vow or the oath she has bound herself with, and remains silent about it, then all her vows and all her oaths that she bound upon herself will stand. ⁶ But if her father objects to her on the day he hears of it, none of her vows or her oaths that she bound upon herself will stand. The LORD will forgive her because her father objected to her. ⁷ If she becomes a wife, and her vows or the oaths she bound upon herself are in force, ⁸ and her husband hears of them on the day he hears of them and remains silent, then all her vows and the oaths she bound upon herself will stand. ⁹ But if her husband objects to her on the day he hears of them, he will void her vow and the oaths she bound upon herself, and the LORD will forgive her. ¹⁰ A widow or a divorced woman, any vow she makes or any oath she binds upon herself, will stand. ¹¹ If a woman is under the authority of her husband and makes a vow or binds herself by an oath, ¹² and her husband hears of it and remains silent, not objecting to her, then all her vows and the oaths she bound upon herself will stand. ¹³ But if her husband voids them on the day he hears of them, whatever comes from her lips—her vows and her oaths that she bound upon herself—will not stand. Her husband has voided them, and the LORD will forgive her. ¹⁴ Every vow and every oath to bind a soul, her husband will uphold, and her husband will void. ¹⁵ If her husband remains silent from day to day, he upholds all her vows or all her oaths that are upon her. He upholds them because he remained silent when he heard of them. ¹⁶ But if he voids them after he hears of them, he will bear the guilt. ¹⁷ These are the decrees that the LORD commanded Moses between a man and his wife, between a father and his daughter while she is in her father's house.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "A widow or a divorced woman" — the Hebrew uses the terms "χήρας" (χήρα) and "ἐκβεβλημένης" (ἐκβάλλω), which are translated here as "widow" and "divorced woman."
- v. 16: "he will bear the guilt" — the Hebrew uses the word "נָשָׂא אֶת־עֲוֺנָֽהּ", which implies taking responsibility for the sin or wrongdoing.
