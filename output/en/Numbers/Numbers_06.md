# Numbers 6

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Speak to the Israelites and tell them: if a man or a woman makes a vow to be a Nazirite, to dedicate themselves to the LORD, ³ they must abstain from wine and strong drink, from any fermented wine or vinegar of wine, and from any fermented vinegar of strong drink. They must not drink any kind of grape juice, nor eat fresh or dried grapes. ⁴ For the entire duration of their vow, they must not consume anything made from the fruit of the vine—from the cluster to the husk. ⁵ For the entire time of their vow, a razor must not touch their head. Until the days of their vow to the LORD are fulfilled, they must remain holy, growing their hair freely as a sign of separation. ⁶ For the entire time of their vow to the LORD, they must not defile themselves for anyone who is dead. ⁷ They must not go near their father, mother, brother, or sister if any of them die, for their Nazirite vow is on their head. ⁸ For the entire time of their vow, they remain holy to the LORD. ⁹ If someone who is under a Nazirite vow dies suddenly and unexpectedly, defiling their head, they must shave their head the day they become clean. On the seventh day they must shave their head. ¹⁰ On the eighth day they must bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ¹¹ The priest is to offer one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering, and make atonement for them for the defilement they have incurred, so that they may be cleansed on that day. ¹² They must then present a ram as a guilt offering, for the days of their vow were invalidated because their head became unclean. ¹³ This is the law of the Nazirite: when the days of their vow are fulfilled, they must bring themselves to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ¹⁴ They must present a sacrifice to the LORD: one unblemished year-old ram as a burnt offering, one unblemished year-old ewe as a sin offering, and one unblemished ram as a peace offering. ¹⁵ They must also bring a basket of unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, along with their grain offering and libations. ¹⁶ The priest must present these before the LORD and offer the sin offering and the burnt offering. ¹⁷ The priest must also prepare the ram as a peace offering to the LORD, along with the basket of unleavened bread, and offer the priest’s grain offering and libation. ¹⁸ The Nazirite must then shave their head at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and the priest must take the hair from the Nazirite’s head and place it on the fire burning under the peace offering. ¹⁹ The priest must take one of the breast portions of the ram, one unleavened cake from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and place them in the Nazirite’s hands after they have shaved their head. ²⁰ The priest must then wave these before the LORD as a wave offering. They are holy to the priest, for the breast and the thigh belong to the priest. After this, the Nazirite may drink wine. ²¹ This is the law for the Nazirite who makes a vow to the LORD. In addition to the vow they have made, they must carry out these rituals according to the law of their Nazirite vow. ²² The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²³ Speak to Aaron and his sons, telling them: this is how you must bless the Israelites. Say to them: ²⁴ The LORD bless you and keep you. ²⁵ The LORD make His face shine upon you and be gracious to you. ²⁶ The LORD lift His face toward you and grant you peace. ²⁷ Thus they will place My name on the Israelites, and I will bless them.

---

Notes  
- v. 4: The phrase "from the cluster to the husk" is a poetic way of saying "from the whole grapevine to its last parts," emphasizing total avoidance of grape products.  
- v. 21: "Carry out these rituals according to the law of their Nazirite vow" reflects the Hebrew "כְּפִי נִדְרוֹ כֵּן יַעֲשֶׂה עַל תּוֹרַת נִזְרֽוֹ" — literally, "according to the strength of their vow, so they must do according to the law of their Nazirite vow."
