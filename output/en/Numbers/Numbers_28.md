# Numbers 28

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ² "Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'These are my offerings, the bread of my altar, the pleasing aroma I require of you. You must bring them to me at their appointed times. ³ Say to them, This is the offering you must bring to the LORD: two unblemished year-old male lambs each day as a continual burnt offering. ⁴ Present one lamb in the morning and the other lamb at twilight. ⁵ And a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, a quarter of a hin of oil. ⁶ This burnt offering, made at Mount Sinai, is a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ⁷ And its drink offering shall be a quarter of a hin for each lamb, poured out as a drink offering, a libation of joy to the LORD. ⁸ And the second lamb you shall present at twilight, as for the morning offering and its drink offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ⁹ On the day of the Sabbath, you must bring two unblemished year-old male lambs, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and their drink offering. ¹⁰ A Sabbath offering on the Sabbath, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering. ¹¹ And on the first days of your months, you must bring a burnt offering to the LORD: two unblemished bulls from the herd, one unblemished ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs. ¹² Their grain offering shall be three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each bull, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for the ram. ¹³ One tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for each lamb—a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹⁴ Their drink offerings shall be half a hin for each bull, a third of a hin for the ram, and a quarter of a hin for each lamb. This is the monthly burnt offering, every month throughout the year. ¹⁵ And one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering. ¹⁶ And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, you must observe the Passover to the LORD. ¹⁷ And on the fifteenth day of this month, you must hold a festival. For seven days you must eat unleavened bread. ¹⁸ On the first day there shall be a sacred assembly. You must not do any laborious work. ¹⁹ And you must bring a burnt offering to the LORD: two unblemished bulls from the herd, one unblemished ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs. ²⁰ And their grain offering shall be three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each bull, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for the ram. ²¹ One tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for each lamb, for the seven lambs. ²² And one male goat for a sin offering, to atone for you. ²³ In addition to the morning burnt offering, which is the continual burnt offering and its drink offering. ²⁴ You must do this for seven days, each day bringing a grain offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering. ²⁵ On the seventh day, there shall be a sacred assembly. You must not do any laborious work. ²⁶ And on the day of the first fruits, when you present a new grain offering to the LORD after your weeks are completed, there shall be a sacred assembly. You must not do any laborious work. ²⁷ And you must bring a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma: two unblemished bulls from the herd, one unblemished ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs. ²⁸ Their grain offering shall be three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each bull, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for the ram. ²⁹ One tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for each lamb, for the seven lambs. ³⁰ And one male goat for a sin offering, to atone for you. ³¹ In addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, they must be without blemish and their drink offerings must be poured out.

Notes
- v. 2: "pleasing aroma" is the standard translation of רֵיחַ נִיחֹחִי (re'ach niyho'khi), referring to a sacrifice accepted by God.
- v. 7: "libation of joy" is a contextual rendering of נֶסֶךְ שֵׁכָר (nesekh shekar), which literally means "wine libation."
- v. 14: "half a hin" is the standard rendering of חֲצִי הַהִין (khatzi ha-hin); the measure "hin" was a liquid unit in ancient Israel.
- v. 16-17: "Passover" and "festival" refer to the events described in Exodus 12 and Leviticus 23, respectively.
- v. 26: "day of the first fruits" refers to the Festival of Weeks (Shavuot), described in Leviticus 23:15-22.
