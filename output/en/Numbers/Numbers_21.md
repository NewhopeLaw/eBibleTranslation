# Numbers 21

¹ The Canaanite king of Arad, who lived in the Negev, heard that Israel was coming by the King's Highway, and he fought against Israel, capturing some of them as prisoners. ² Israel made a vow to the LORD, saying, "If you deliver this people into my hand, I will devote their cities to destruction." ³ The LORD heard the voice of Israel and delivered the Canaanites into their hands, and Israel devoted them and their cities to destruction. They named the place "Charmah." ⁴ They set out from Mount Hor by the route of the Red Sea to bypass the land of Edom. The people's spirits became faint on the journey. ⁵ The people spoke against God and against Moses: "Why have you brought us up from Egypt to die in the wilderness? There is no bread and no water, and our souls are disgusted by this light bread." ⁶ The LORD sent venomous serpents among the people, and they bit the people, so many Israelites died. ⁷ The people came to Moses and said, "We have sinned by speaking against the LORD and against you. Pray to the LORD, and let him remove the serpents from us." Moses prayed on behalf of the people. ⁸ The LORD said to Moses, "Make a serpent and put it on a pole, and everyone who is bitten and looks at it will live." ⁹ Moses made a bronze serpent and put it on a pole. And if anyone was bitten by a serpent and looked at the bronze serpent, they lived. ¹⁰ The Israelites set out and encamped at Oboth. ¹¹ They set out from Oboth and encamped at the Valley of Eglah, in the desert facing Moab toward the east. ¹² From there they set out and encamped at the Valley of Zered. ¹³ From there they set out and encamped beyond the Wadi of Arnon, which is in the desert extending from the boundary of the Amorites, for the Wadi of Arnon is the boundary of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites. ¹⁴ Therefore, it is said in the Book of the Wars of the LORD: "Waheb in Suphah, and the Wadi of Arnon. ¹⁵ And the Wadi of the River that flows toward the settlement of Ar, and leans against the boundary of Moab." ¹⁶ From there is the well, that well about which the LORD said to Moses: "Gather the people together, and I will give them water." ¹⁷ Then Israel sang this song: "Spring up, O well! Let us sing to it. ¹⁸ The well that the chieftains dug, the nobles of the people, with the scepter and with their staffs, in the desert, in the wastelands." ¹⁹ From Mattanah to Nachal-Ohalab, from Nachal-Ohalab to Bnot. ²⁰ From Bnot to the Valley of the Valley of the Dead, which is in the field of Moab, facing the top of Pisgah and looking toward the wilderness. ²¹ Moses sent messengers to Sihon, king of the Amorites, saying, ²² "I will cross through your land. I will not turn aside into fields or vineyards. I will not drink water from your wells. I will go along the king's highway until I have crossed your boundary." ²³ But Sihon did not permit Israel to cross through his territory. Sihon gathered all his people and went out to meet Israel in the desert at Jahaz, and he came to meet Israel. ²⁴ Israel struck him with the sword and took possession of his land from Arnon to Jabbok, as far as the people of Ammon. For the boundary of the people of Ammon was strong. ²⁵ Israel took all these cities and Israel settled in every city of the Amorites, in Heshbon and in all its daughter-cities. ²⁶ For Heshbon was the city of Sihon, king of the Amorites. He had fought against the king of Moab before and had taken all his land from his hand as far as Arnon. ²⁷ Therefore, the rulers say, "Come to Heshbon, let it be built. Let the city of Sihon be established. ²⁸ For fire has gone out from Heshbon, a flame from the city of Sihon. It has devoured the city of Moab and the people of the high places of Arnon." ²⁹ Woe to you, Moab! You are destroyed, O people of Kemosh! Your sons were taken captive, and your daughters into captivity, by the king of the Amorites, Sihon. ³⁰ And Naharaim was destroyed, Heshbon to Dibon, and Nophah to Medeba. ³¹ Israel settled in the land of the Amorites. ³² Moses sent to scout the region of Jazer and captured its daughter-cities. Israel dispossessed the Amorite who was there. ³³ They turned and went up by the road of Bashan. Og, king of Bashan, came out to meet them, along with all his people, for battle at Edrei. ³⁴ The LORD said to Moses, "Do not fear him, for I have given him into your hand, along with all his people and all his land. Do to him as you did to Sihon, the king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon." ³⁵ So they struck him and his sons and all his people until they left no survivors. Israel took possession of his land.

Notes
- v. 1: "Canaanite king of Arad" — "Canaanite" is added as a clarifying descriptor based on the context, not present in the Hebrew but implied.
- v. 16: "the well" — the Hebrew בְּאֵרָה is a poetic or poetic-sounding form of בְּאֵר ("well"), used here to evoke a liturgical or poetic tone.
- v. 18: "the scepter" — the Hebrew מְחֹקֵק may be a poetic or corrupted form of מַכְּטֶּפ ("scepter"), though some scholars read it as "driller." The poetic tone and context suggest "scepter."
- v. 20: "the Valley of the Valley of the Dead" — this is a literal translation of בָּמוֹת הַגַּיְא. The double "valley" comes from the Hebrew preposition בְּ ("in") plus גַּיְא ("valley").
- v. 29: "people of Kemosh" — Kemosh is the national god of Moab; the name is transliterated as in the Septuagint and other sources.
- v. 30: "Naharaim" — a place name, likely a poetic or archaic form; the Hebrew נַחֲרִיאֵל is read as נַחֲרַיִם, "two rivers," as in other versions.
