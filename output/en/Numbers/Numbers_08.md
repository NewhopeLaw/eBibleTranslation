# Numbers 8

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Speak to Aaron and say to him: When you set the lamps, the seven lamps shall shine toward the front of the lampstand. ³ Aaron did so: he set the lamps toward the front of the lampstand, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ⁴ This is the work of the lampstand: hammered solid gold, from its base to its bloom. It was a single piece, made according to the vision the LORD had shown Moses. That is how he made the lampstand. ⁵ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ⁶ Take the Levites from among the Israelites and purify them. ⁷ This is how you must purify them: you shall pour water of atonement over them, and they shall each shave all the hair from their bodies. They shall wash their clothes and be clean. ⁸ They must take a bull calf and its grain offering—fine flour mixed with oil—and a second bull calf for a sin offering. ⁹ You shall bring the Levites before the Tent of Meeting and assemble the whole community of Israel. ¹⁰ You shall bring the Levites before the LORD. The Israelites shall lay their hands on the Levites. ¹¹ Then Aaron shall present the Levites as a wave offering before the LORD, from the Israelites. And the Levites shall serve the service of the LORD. ¹² The Levites shall lay their hands on the heads of the bulls. One you shall offer as a sin offering, and one as a burnt offering to the LORD, to atone for the Levites. ¹³ You shall set the Levites before Aaron and his sons, and you shall present them as a wave offering to the LORD. ¹⁴ You shall separate the Levites from among the Israelites, and they shall be mine. ¹⁵ Afterward, the Levites shall come to serve the Tent of Meeting. You shall purify them and present them as a wave offering. ¹⁶ For they are given to me from among the Israelites in place of every firstborn. All the firstborn among the Israelites I have taken for myself. ¹⁷ Every firstborn in Israel, of man and beast, is mine. On the day I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set them apart for myself. ¹⁸ I took the Levites in place of every firstborn among the Israelites. ¹⁹ I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from among the Israelites, to serve the service of the Israelites in the Tent of Meeting, to atone for the Israelites, so that no plague may come among the Israelites when they come near the holy place. ²⁰ So Moses and Aaron and the whole community of Israel did to the Levites all that the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites. The Israelites did it to them as he had directed. ²¹ The Levites were purified, and they washed their clothes. Then Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD, and Aaron offered atonement for them to purify them. ²² Afterward, the Levites came to perform their service at the Tent of Meeting, before Aaron and his sons, as the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites. So Israel did to them. ²³ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²⁴ This is the duty of the Levites: from the age of twenty-five and upward, they shall enter to serve the service of the Tent of Meeting. ²⁵ But at fifty years old, they must retire from the service and no longer work. ²⁶ They may serve their brothers in the Tent of Meeting, guarding their duties, but they will not perform the work themselves. This you must do for the Levites in their guarding duties.

---

Notes  
- v. 12: "One you shall offer as a sin offering, and one as a burnt offering to the LORD"—the Levites themselves are the subject, so the Levites are to offer the bulls, but the Levites are not yet priests. The context implies that Aaron or the priests perform the offerings on their behalf.  
- v. 16: "in place of every firstborn"—the Levites are taken in substitution for the firstborn of Israel, as per the command in Exodus 13:1–2.  
- v. 17: "On the day I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt"—this refers to the tenth plague. The Levites are thus set apart in place of the firstborn who were spared.  
- v. 26: "They may serve their brothers in the Tent of Meeting, guarding their duties"—the Levites past fifty are not to perform the active service but to assist in oversight and maintenance.
