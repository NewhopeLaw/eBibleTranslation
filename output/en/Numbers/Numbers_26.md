# Numbers 26

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses and to Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, saying, ² "Take a census of all the congregation of the people of Israel from twenty years old and upward, by the house of their fathers, everyone who is able to go out for service in Israel." ³ Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke to them in the plains of Moab, by the Jordan at Jericho, saying, ⁴ "From twenty years old and upward, as the LORD commanded Moses and the people of Israel who came out of the land of Egypt." ⁵ Reuben, the firstborn of Israel. The sons of Reuben were Hanoch; and the families of the sons of Hanoch were the family of the Hanochites; the family of Pallu, the Palluites. ⁶ The family of Hezron, the Hezronites; the family of Carmi, the Carmites. ⁷ These are the families of the Reubenites, and their number was thirty-two thousand, seven hundred thirty. ⁸ The sons of Pallu were Eliab. ⁹ The sons of Eliab were Nemuel and Dathan and Abiram, these are the ones who were the leaders of the congregation who rebelled against Moses and Aaron in the rebellion of Korah, when they rebelled against the LORD. ¹⁰ The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up, and Korah and all his company, and the fire consumed two hundred fifty men. And it became a sign. ¹¹ The sons of Korah did not die. ¹² The sons of Simeon by their families: the family of Nemuel, the Nemuelites; the family of Jamin, the Jaminites; the family of Jachin, the Jachinites; ¹³ the family of Zerah, the Zerahites; the family of Saul, the Saulites. ¹⁴ These are the families of the Simeonites, and their number was twenty-two thousand, two hundred. ¹⁵ The sons of Gad by their families: the family of Zephon, the Zephonites; the family of Haggi, the Haggites; the family of Shuni, the Shunites; ¹⁶ the family of Ozni, the Oznites; the family of Er, the Erites; ¹⁷ the family of Arod, the Arodites; the family of Areli, the Arelites. ¹⁸ These are the families of the sons of Gad by their number, forty thousand five hundred. ¹⁹ The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. ²⁰ The sons of Judah by their families: the family of Shelah, the Shelanites; the family of Perez, the Perezites; the family of Zerah, the Zerahites. ²¹ The sons of Perez were the family of Hezron, the Hezronites; the family of Hamul, the Hamulites. ²² These are the families of Judah by their number, sixty-seven thousand five hundred. ²³ The sons of Issachar by their families: the family of Tola, the Tolaites; the family of Puvah, the Puvites; ²⁴ the family of Yashub, the Yashubites; the family of Shimron, the Shimronites. ²⁵ These are the families of Issachar by their number, sixty-four thousand three hundred. ²⁶ The sons of Zebulun by their families: the family of Sered, the Seredites; the family of Elon, the Elonites; the family of Jahleel, the Jahleelites. ²⁷ These are the families of the Zebulunites by their number, sixty-five thousand. ²⁸ The sons of Joseph by their families: the sons of Manasseh and the sons of Ephraim. ²⁹ The sons of Manasseh: the family of Machir, the Machirites, and Machir begot Gilead, the family of Gilead, the Gileadites. ³⁰ The sons of Gilead were the family of Iezer, the Iezerites; the family of Helek, the Helekites. ³¹ The family of Asriel, the Asrielites; the family of Shechem, the Shechemites; ³² the family of Shemida, the Shemidaites; the family of Hepher, the Hepherites. ³³ The family of Shlafchad, son of Hepher, who had no sons, only daughters, and the names of the daughters of Shlafchad were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. ³⁴ These are the families of Manasseh by their number, fifty-two thousand seven hundred. ³⁵ These are the sons of Ephraim by their families: the family of Shuthelah, the Shuthelahites; the family of Becher, the Becherites; the family of Tahan, the Tahanites; ³⁶ the sons of Shuthelah were the family of Eran, the Eranites. ³⁷ These are the families of the sons of Ephraim by their number, thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are the sons of Joseph by their families. ³⁸ The sons of Benjamin by their families: the family of Bela, the Belaites; the family of Ashbel, the Ashbelites; the family of Ahiram, the Ahiramites; ³⁹ the family of Shephupham, the Shephuphamites; the family of Hupham, the Huphamites. ⁴⁰ The sons of Bela were the family of Ard and the family of Naaman, the Ardites and the Naamites. ⁴¹ These are the sons of Benjamin by their families by their number, forty-five thousand six hundred. ⁴² The sons of Dan by their families: the family of Shucham, the Shuchamites. These are the families of Dan by their families. ⁴³ The total number of the families of the Shuchamites by their number, sixty-four thousand four hundred. ⁴⁴ The sons of Asher by their families: the family of Yimnah, the Yimnahites; the family of Yishi, the Yishites; the family of Beriah, the Beriahites; ⁴⁵ the sons of Beriah were the family of Heber, the Heberites; the family of Malchiel, the Malchielites. ⁴⁶ The name of the daughter of Asher was Serach. ⁴⁷ These are the families of the sons of Asher by their number, fifty-seven thousand four hundred. ⁴⁸ The sons of Naphtali by their families: the family of Yachzeel, the Yachzeelites; the family of Gun, the Gunites; ⁴⁹ the family of Yeser, the Yeserites; the family of Shilem, the Shilemites. ⁵⁰ These are the families of Naphtali by their families by their number, fifty-three thousand four hundred. ⁵¹ These are the numbers of the people of Israel by their families, six hundred thousand, one thousand seven hundred thirty. ⁵² The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ⁵³ "To these shall the land be divided for an inheritance by number of names. ⁵⁴ To the many their inheritance shall be increased, and to the few their inheritance shall be decreased. To each shall it be given according to their number. ⁵⁵ But by lot shall the land be divided to them for their fathers' tribes. ⁵⁶ According to the lot shall the inheritance be divided between the many and the few." ⁵⁷ These are the numbers of the Levites by their families: the family of Gershon, the Gershonites; the family of Kohath, the Kohathites; the family of Merari, the Merarites. ⁵⁸ These are the families of the Levites: the family of Libni, the Libnites; the family of Hebron, the Hebronites; the family of Machli, the Machlites; the family of Mushi, the Mushites; and Kohath begot Amram. ⁵⁹ The name of Amram's wife was Yochebed, the daughter of Levi, who was born to Levi in Egypt. She bore Amram, Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam. ⁶⁰ And Aaron's sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. ⁶¹ Nadab and Abihu died when they offered unauthorized fire before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai. ⁶² Their number was thirty-two thousand, all males from a month old and upward. For they were not counted among the people of Israel, for no inheritance was given to them among the people of Israel. ⁶³ These are the numbers of Moses and Eleazar the priest, who counted the people of Israel in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho. ⁶⁴ And there was not a man among those counted by Moses and Aaron the priest, who counted the people of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai. ⁶⁵ For the LORD said to them, "They shall die in the wilderness, and none of them will be left, except Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun."

Notes
- v. 31: "Shechemites" and similar terms are translated as "the Shechemites" to maintain consistency with the pattern of "the [family name]ites" for clan or tribal designations.
- v. 46: "Serach" is the name of Asher's daughter; the translation follows the Hebrew text's singular form, though the Septuagint pluralizes it as "Sera."
- v. 64: The phrase "there was not a man among those counted by Moses and Aaron the priest" is rendered to reflect that the Levites were not included in the earlier census in the wilderness of Sinai, as specified in the text.
- v. 65: The reference to Caleb and Joshua is included to clarify that these two were exceptions to the general death sentence in the wilderness.
