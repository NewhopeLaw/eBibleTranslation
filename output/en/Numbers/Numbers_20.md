# Numbers 20

¹ The Israelites, the whole community, arrived at the Desert of Zin in the first month. The people settled in Kadesh, and there Miriam died and was buried there. ² There was no water for the community, and the people gathered against Moses and Aaron. ³ The people quarreled with Moses and said, “Would that we had perished when our brothers died before the LORD!” ⁴ Why have you brought the assembly of the LORD into this desert to die there, along with our livestock? ⁵ Why have you brought us up from Egypt to bring us to this awful place—no place for seed, fig trees, vines, or pomegranates, and no water to drink?” ⁶ Moses and Aaron went from the meeting of the people to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and they fell on their faces. The glory of the LORD appeared to them. ⁷ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ⁸ “Take the staff and assemble the community, you and your brother Aaron, and speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will give forth its water. Bring forth water for them and for their livestock.” ⁹ Moses took the staff from before the LORD, just as he was commanded. ¹⁰ Moses and Aaron gathered the community before the rock and said to them, “Listen, you rebels, must we bring water for you from this rock?” ¹¹ Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff, and abundant water came out, and the community and their livestock drank. ¹² The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, “Because you did not believe in me to sanctify me before the Israelites, you shall not bring this community into the land that I have given them.” ¹³ These were the Waters of Contention, for the Israelites had quarreled with the LORD, and he was sanctified in them. ¹⁴ Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom, saying, “My brother Israel knows the hardship we have experienced. ¹⁵ Our ancestors went down to Egypt, and we dwelled in Egypt many years, and the Egyptians and their ancestors mistreated us. ¹⁶ We cried out to the LORD, and he heard our voice. He sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. Now we are in Kadesh, a city at the edge of your territory. ¹⁷ We will pass through your land. We will not turn aside into fields or vineyards, nor will we drink water from your wells. We will go by the king’s highway, and we will not turn aside to the right or to the left until we have passed through your boundary.” ¹⁸ Edom replied, “You must not pass through, or I will come out to meet you with the sword.” ¹⁹ The Israelites said to him, “We will go by the mountain. If we drink your water, I and my livestock, we will pay for it. Nothing will be taken, only that we may pass through by the mountain.” ²⁰ But Edom said, “You must not pass through,” and Edom came out to meet them with a large army and a strong hand. ²¹ Edom refused to allow Israel to pass through his boundary, and Israel turned away from him. ²² Israel set out from Kadesh and the whole community arrived at Hor Mountain. ²³ The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron at Hor Mountain, at the border of the land of Edom, saying, ²⁴ “Aaron will be gathered to his people, for he will not enter the land that I have given to the Israelites, because you rebelled against my will at the Waters of Contention.” ²⁵ “Take Aaron and his son Eleazar and go up to Hor Mountain. ²⁶ Strip Aaron of his garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Then Aaron will be gathered to his people and will die there.” ²⁷ Moses did as the LORD commanded. They went up to Hor Mountain before the whole community. ²⁸ Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Then Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain. ²⁹ The whole community saw that Aaron had died, and all the house of Israel mourned for Aaron thirty days.

Notes
- v. 12: "Because you did not believe in me to sanctify me" — The Hebrew uses the verb "he'eminu" (you did not believe), which can also be read as "you did not trust," but "believe" is chosen here for clarity and consistency with the context of covenantal faithfulness.
- v. 24: "Because you rebelled against my will at the Waters of Contention" — The phrase "merit" (מְרִיבָה) is translated as "Contention" to reflect its meaning of a quarrel or dispute.
- v. 25: "Go up to Hor Mountain" — The name "Hor" appears to reference the place rather than a person, and is translated accordingly.
