# Numbers 16

¹ Korah, son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On, son of Pelet, all from the tribe of Reuben— ² they rose up before Moses, along with 250 Israelite men, leaders of the assembly, members of the council, and men of renown. ³ They gathered against Moses and Aaron and said to them, “You have gone too far! For the whole community is holy, and the Lord is among them. Why then do you raise yourselves above the assembly of the Lord?” ⁴ Moses heard this and fell facedown. ⁵ He said to Korah and to his whole assembly, “By morning, the Lord will show who belongs to him, who is the holy one, and will bring him near. The one whom he chooses, he will bring near to himself.” ⁶ “Do this: Take censers, Korah and all your assembly. ⁷ Place fire in them and put incense on them tomorrow before the Lord. And the man whom the Lord chooses will be the one who is holy. You Levites, have you gone too far!” ⁸ Moses said to Korah, “Listen, you Levites. ⁹ Is it too small a thing for you that the God of Israel has set you apart from the assembly of Israel, to bring you near to him to do the service of the tabernacle of the Lord, and to stand before the assembly to serve them? ¹⁰ He brought you and all your brothers, the sons of Levi, with you. Yet you seek the priesthood as well? ¹¹ So you and all your assembly have conspired against the Lord. And what is Aaron that you quarrel with him?” ¹² Moses sent to call Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab. They said, “We will not come up. ¹³ Is it too small a thing for you that you brought us out from a land flowing with milk and honey to kill us in the wilderness? And now you would reign over us as well?” ¹⁴ “Even if you brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey, and gave us an inheritance of fields and vineyards, would you blind these men? We will not come up.” ¹⁵ Moses became very angry and said to the Lord, “Do not accept their offering. I have not taken a donkey from any of them, nor have I wronged any of them.” ¹⁶ Moses said to Korah, “You and your whole assembly come before the Lord tomorrow, you and Aaron. ¹⁷ Each of you take your censer and put incense on them tomorrow before the Lord. Each of the 250 men, and you and Aaron, each of you take your censer.” ¹⁸ They took their censers and put fire in them and added incense. Then they stood at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, with Moses and Aaron. ¹⁹ Korah gathered all the assembly at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. The glory of the Lord appeared to the entire assembly. ²⁰ The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying: ²¹ “Separate yourselves from among this assembly, and I will consume them in an instant.” ²² They fell on their faces and said, “O God, who breathes into all flesh, will one man sin and You be angry with the whole assembly?” ²³ The Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ²⁴ “Speak to the assembly, saying, ‘Move away from around the tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.’” ²⁵ Moses got up and went to Dathan and Abiram. The elders of Israel went with him. ²⁶ He spoke to the assembly, saying, “Move away from the tents of these wicked men. Do not touch anything that belongs to them, lest you share in all their sins.” ²⁷ They moved away from around the tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram came out and stood at the entrance of their own tents, with their wives and children and little ones. ²⁸ Moses said, “By this you will know that the Lord has sent me to do all these works, and that I have not spoken from my own heart. ²⁹ If these men die as all other men die, or if they suffer the fate of all mankind, then the Lord has not sent me. ³⁰ But if the Lord brings about something new, and the earth opens its mouth and swallows them up with all their possessions and they descend alive into Sheol, then you will know that these men have insulted the Lord.” ³¹ As he finished speaking all these words, the ground under them split open, ³² and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and their households and all the men who were with Korah and all their possessions. ³³ They, with all that belonged to them, went down alive into Sheol. The earth closed over them, and they perished from the assembly. ³⁴ All Israel around them fled at their outcry, for they said, “Lest the earth swallow us up.” ³⁵ And fire came from the Lord and consumed the 250 men offering the incense.

Notes
- v. 12: "We will not come up" — the Hebrew text does not specify whether they refuse to come up to the Tent of Meeting or to Israel's leadership in general. The context suggests the former.
- v. 30: "descend alive into Sheol" — the Hebrew uses שְׁאֹלָה, which is commonly associated with Sheol, the abode of the dead. The term "alive" is used to contrast with the expected fate of the dead, emphasizing the miraculous nature of the swallowing.
- v. 35: "fire came from the Lord and consumed..." — the reference to fire is distinct from the earth swallowing Korah's group, indicating two separate judgments.
