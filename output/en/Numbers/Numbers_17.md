# Numbers 17

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² “Say to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, take the censers from the burning and scatter the fire from them, for they have become holy. ³ The censers of these sinners, you shall hammer into sheets and use them to overlay the altar, for they brought their incense before the LORD and they became holy, and they will be a sign for the people of Israel.” ⁴ Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers used by those who had burned incense and hammered them into sheets to overlay the altar, ⁵ to serve as a reminder to the people of Israel, so that no one from among the Israelites who is not from the line of Aaron might ever again bring incense before the LORD and not suffer the fate of Korah and his assembly, as the LORD had spoken through Moses. ⁶ All the assembly of the people of Israel rebelled against Moses and Aaron the next day, saying, “You have killed the people of the LORD!” ⁷ When the assembly gathered against Moses and Aaron, they turned toward the Tent of Meeting, and behold, the cloud had covered it and the glory of the LORD appeared. ⁸ Moses and Aaron went to the front of the Tent of Meeting. ⁹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁰ “Separate yourself from among this assembly, and I will consume them in an instant.” They fell facedown. ¹¹ Moses said to Aaron, “Take your censer and put fire from the altar in it, and add incense, and go quickly to the assembly and make atonement for them, for the anger has already begun to burn before the LORD, and the plague has already begun.” ¹² Aaron did as Moses had said and ran into the center of the assembly, and behold, the plague had already begun among the people. He placed the incense and made atonement for the people. ¹³ He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was restrained. ¹⁴ The dead from the plague totaled fourteen thousand seven hundred, in addition to the people who had died because of Korah. ¹⁵ Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and the plague was restrained. ¹⁶ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁷ “Speak to the people of Israel, and take from them a rod, a rod per house of their fathers, from each of their leaders, one rod per house of their fathers, twelve rods in total. Write each man’s name on his rod. ¹⁸ Also write the name of Aaron on the rod for the house of Levi, for there will be one rod per head of each house of their fathers. ¹⁹ Place them in the Tent of Meeting before the testimony, where I have appointed you to be. ²⁰ The man whose rod I choose shall sprout, and I will silence the complaints of the people of Israel, which they raise against you.” ²¹ Moses spoke to the people of Israel, and they gave him twelve rods, one rod per leader, from each house of their fathers, and the rod of Aaron was among the rods of the others. ²² Moses placed the rods before the LORD in the Tent of Meeting. ²³ The next day, Moses went into the Tent of Meeting, and behold, the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi had sprouted, producing buds and blossoms and ripe almonds. ²⁴ Moses took all the rods out before the people of Israel. They saw, and each man took his rod. ²⁵ The LORD said to Moses, “Return the rod of Aaron to its place before the testimony, to remain there as a sign for the people of Merari, so that their complaints might be silenced from me, and they will not die.” ²⁶ Moses did as the LORD had commanded him. ²⁷ The people of Israel said to Moses, “Look, we have perished! We are lost! We are all lost!” ²⁸ Whoever comes near the Tent of the LORD will die. Are we going to perish completely?”

---

Notes  
- v. 5: The phrase "not suffer the fate of Korah" is a literary reference to the recent event in chapter 16, which is summarized here as a divine warning.  
- v. 10: "Separate yourself from among this assembly" — the plural "you" is implied in the Hebrew but not directly stated.  
- v. 27-28: The people's fear here echoes the panic at the end of chapter 16 after the earth swallowed Korah's assembly. The Hebrew "גָּוַעְנוּ" and "אָבַדְנוּ" are intentionally repeated for rhetorical effect.
