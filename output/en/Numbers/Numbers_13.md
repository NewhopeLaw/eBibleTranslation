# Numbers 13

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ² "Send men and they shall scout the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the people of Israel. Send one man from each tribe of their ancestral families, all their leaders." ³ Moses sent them from the Desert of Paran, as the LORD had commanded, all the men who were heads of the people of Israel. ⁴ These were their names: from the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur. ⁵ From the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori. ⁶ From the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh. ⁷ From the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph. ⁸ From the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun. ⁹ From the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu. ¹⁰ From the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi. ¹¹ From the tribe of Joseph, from the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi son of Susi. ¹² From the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli. ¹³ From the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael. ¹⁴ From the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi. ¹⁵ From the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi. ¹⁶ These were the names of the men Moses sent to scout the land. Moses also named Hoshea son of Nun Joshua. ¹⁷ Moses sent them to scout the land of Canaan and said to them, "Go up through the Negev and climb the mountain." ¹⁸ "See the land, what it is like, and the people who live there. Are they strong or weak? Are they few or many? ¹⁹ What is the land they live in? Is it good or bad? What are the cities in which they live? Are they in open camps or in fortified cities? ²⁰ Is the land fertile or barren? Are there trees in it or not? Be strong, and take some fruit from the land. It is the season of the first fruits of grapes." ²¹ They went up and scouted the land from the Desert of Sinai to Rehob, as far as Hamath. ²² They climbed into the Negev and came to Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the Anakites, lived. Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt. ²³ They came to the Valley of Eshcol and cut down a branch with a single cluster of grapes, which they carried on a pole between two men. They also took some pomegranates and figs. ²⁴ The place was named the Valley of Eshcol because the Israelites cut down the cluster of grapes there. ²⁵ They returned from scouting the land after forty days. ²⁶ They went and came to Moses, and to Aaron and all the congregation of the people of Israel at Kadesh in the Desert of Paran. They brought back word to the whole assembly and showed them the fruit of the land. ²⁷ They told him and said, "We went into the land you sent us to, and indeed, it is flowing with milk and honey, and this is its fruit. ²⁸ However, the people living in the land are strong, and the cities are very well fortified. We also saw the descendants of Anak there." ²⁹ The Amalekites live in the Negev, and the Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites in the hill country. The Canaanites live along the coast and beside the Jordan. ³⁰ Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, "We can go up and take the land, for we are certainly able to do it." ³¹ But the men who had gone up with him said, "We cannot go up against the people, for they are stronger than we are." ³² They spread a bad report about the land they had scouted to the people of Israel, saying, "The land we passed through to scout is a land that devours its inhabitants. All the people we saw there were giants." ³³ There we saw the Nephilim, the descendants of Anak. And we seemed to ourselves like grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight."

Notes
- v. 33: "Nephilim" is the standard rendering of נְפִילִים, though it's a rare and debated term. It is retained here as a known cultural reference point.
