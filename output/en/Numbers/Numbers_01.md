# Numbers 1

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, on the first day of the second month in the second year after the Israelites left the land of Egypt, saying: ² Take a census of the entire assembly of the Israelites, by families, by the house of their ancestors, listing by name each male according to their groups. ³ From every man who is twenty years old and upward, all who are able to go out to war in Israel, you and Aaron shall number them by their divisions. ⁴ And with you shall be a man from each tribe, each the head of the house of his ancestral family. ⁵ And these are the names of the men who will stand with you: for the tribe of Reuben, Elisur son of Shedeur; ⁶ for the tribe of Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai; ⁷ for the tribe of Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab; ⁸ for the tribe of Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar; ⁹ for the tribe of Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon; ¹⁰ for the sons of Joseph, for the tribe of Ephraim, Elisama son of Ammihud; for the tribe of Manasseh, Gamliel son of Pedahzur; ¹¹ for the tribe of Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni; ¹² for the tribe of Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai; ¹³ for the tribe of Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran; ¹⁴ for the tribe of Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel; ¹⁵ for the tribe of Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan. ¹⁶ These are the men who were called by the community, the leaders of the tribes, the heads of the thousands of Israel. ¹⁷ Moses and Aaron took these men whose names were listed, ¹⁸ and they gathered the entire community together on the first day of the second month, and they were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, from twenty years old and upward, every one able to go out to war. ¹⁹ As the LORD had instructed Moses, they numbered them in the wilderness of Sinai. ²⁰ The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ²¹ The number of the tribe of Reuben was 46,500. ²² The sons of Simeon were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ²³ The number of the tribe of Simeon was 59,300. ²⁴ The sons of Gad were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ²⁵ The number of the tribe of Gad was 45,650. ²⁶ The sons of Judah were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ²⁷ The number of the tribe of Judah was 74,600. ²⁸ The sons of Issachar were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ²⁹ The number of the tribe of Issachar was 54,400. ³⁰ The sons of Zebulun were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ³¹ The number of the tribe of Zebulun was 57,400. ³² The sons of Joseph, the sons of Ephraim, were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ³³ The number of the tribe of Ephraim was 40,500. ³⁴ The sons of Manasseh were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ³⁵ The number of the tribe of Manasseh was 32,200. ³⁶ The sons of Benjamin were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ³⁷ The number of the tribe of Benjamin was 35,400. ³⁸ The sons of Dan were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ³⁹ The number of the tribe of Dan was 62,700. ⁴⁰ The sons of Asher were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ⁴¹ The number of the tribe of Asher was 41,500. ⁴² The sons of Naphtali were numbered by their families, by the house of their ancestors, by the listing of their names, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war: ⁴³ The number of the tribe of Naphtali was 53,400. ⁴⁴ These were the numbers Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel, twelve men, one from each tribe, numbered. ⁴⁵ All the numbers of the Israelites, by the house of their ancestors, every male twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go out to war in Israel: ⁴⁶ The total number was 603,550. ⁴⁷ The Levites, by their ancestral tribe, were not numbered among them. ⁴⁸ The LORD spoke to Moses, ⁴⁹ saying: Do not number the tribe of Levi, and do not take their count among the Israelites. ⁵⁰ Instead, you shall assign the Levites over the Tent of the Testimony, over all its furnishings, and over everything that belongs to it. They will carry the Tent and all its furnishings, and they will serve it, and they will encamp around the Tent. ⁵¹ When the Tent is to be moved, the Levites will take it down, and when the Tent is to be set up, the Levites will set it up. And whoever comes near without permission shall be put to death. ⁵² The Israelites shall camp each with their own group, each with their own banner, for their divisions. ⁵³ The Levites shall camp around the Tent of the Testimony, and there shall be no plague among the Israelite community, and the Levites will guard the charge of the Tent of the Testimony. ⁵⁴ The Israelites did everything the LORD had commanded Moses. They did just as he had instructed.

Notes
- v. 1: “the wilderness of Sinai” — the location is specified as Sinai, not the more general “wilderness.”
- v. 10: “for the sons of Joseph, the sons of Ephraim” — the Hebrew structure clearly separates Manasseh and Ephraim as distinct tribes under Joseph.
- v. 46: “603,550” — the total is calculated as per the listed numbers, with a clear sum.
- v. 49: “Do not number the tribe of Levi” — the Hebrew uses the imperative form, not a suggestion.
- v. 51: “whoever comes near without permission shall be put to death” — the Hebrew uses the verb *yumat* (he will die), but the context implies a command for execution.
