# Numbers 9

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai in the second year after they left the land of Egypt, in the first month, saying: ² Let the Israelites observe the Passover at its appointed time. ³ On the fourteenth day of the first month, at twilight, they must observe it according to its fixed time. According to the regulations and statutes of the Passover, they must observe it. ⁴ Moses spoke to the Israelites to observe the Passover. ⁵ The Israelites observed the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month, at twilight, in the wilderness of Sinai, as the LORD had commanded Moses. So the Israelites observed the Passover. ⁶ There were men who had become ritually unclean from a human death and could not observe the Passover on that day. They came before Moses and Aaron on that day. ⁷ These men said to them, "We are unclean because of a human death. Why should we be excluded from presenting the offering of the LORD among the Israelites at its appointed time?" ⁸ Moses said to them, "Stand here, and I will hear what the LORD commands concerning you." ⁹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁰ Say to the Israelites, "Any man who is unclean because of a human death or is far away and cannot observe the Passover at its appointed time, let him observe the Passover for the LORD in the second month. ¹¹ On the fourteenth day of the second month, at twilight, they must observe it, eating it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. ¹² They must not leave any of it until morning, and they must not break any of its bones. They must observe it according to the regulations of the Passover. ¹³ But if a man is clean and not on a journey, yet he neglects to observe the Passover, that person shall be cut off from their people. For the offering of the LORD was not presented at its appointed time. That person shall bear their guilt." ¹⁴ And if a foreigner resides among you and wants to observe the Passover for the LORD, let them observe it according to the regulations and statutes of the Passover. One law shall apply to you and to the foreigner and to the native of the land. ¹⁵ On the day the tabernacle was raised, the cloud covered the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting. At dusk, it appeared over the tabernacle like a sight of fire until morning. ¹⁶ So it was always the case: the cloud covered it by day, and the sight of fire by night. ¹⁷ When the cloud lifted from the Tent of Meeting, then afterward the Israelites journeyed. Wherever the cloud settled, there the Israelites encamped. ¹⁸ At the command of the LORD, the Israelites journeyed. At the command of the LORD, they encamped. As long as the cloud remained over the tabernacle, the Israelites remained encamped. ¹⁹ When the cloud lingered over the tabernacle for many days, the Israelites kept the LORD’s charge and did not journey. ²⁰ And if the cloud remained a certain number of days over the tabernacle, at the command of the LORD they encamped, and at the command of the LORD they journeyed. ²¹ Sometimes the cloud would remain from evening until morning, and when it lifted in the morning, they journeyed. Sometimes it would remain during the day and the night, and when it lifted, they journeyed. ²² Whether for two days, one month, or a number of days, whenever the cloud remained over the tabernacle to stay, the Israelites remained encamped and did not journey. When it lifted, they journeyed. ²³ At the command of the LORD they encamped, and at the command of the LORD they journeyed. The Israelites kept the charge of the LORD at the command of the LORD through Moses.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "the wilderness of Sinai" – the Hebrew specifies "Sinai" as the location, not "Sinai wilderness" as in some translations.
- v. 10-13: The conditional structure and the consequences for neglecting the Passover are rendered in a natural but faithful way, preserving the severity of the divine command.
- v. 15: "the Tent of Meeting" is used for אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד, which is the fixed term for the sacred space where God meets with Moses.
- v. 15-22: The cloud and fire imagery is presented as symbolic guidance, with the Israelites following the cloud's movements literally and figuratively as God's will.
- v. 23: The final summary reiterates the Israelites' obedience to God's direct instructions, using "kept the charge" for מִשְׁמֶרֶת ("charge" or "guard").
