# Numbers 5

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Command the Israelites to send out from the camp everyone who has a skin disease, and everyone who is discharging, and every person who is unclean. ³ Send out both men and women, and send them outside the camp so that they do not defile their camp, which I dwell in among them. ⁴ The Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they sent them outside the camp just as the LORD had said to Moses. ⁵ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ⁶ Speak to the Israelites: if a man or a woman commits any of the sins of humanity and breaks faith, the person becomes guilty. ⁷ The guilty person must confess the sin they have committed and return what they have wrongfully taken, adding a fifth of its value to it, and give it to the one they wronged. ⁸ If the person has no one to take the restitution, the restitution goes to the LORD for the priest, apart from the guilt offering that atones for the person. ⁹ All the tithes of all the holy things of the Israelites that are brought to the priest will belong to the priest. ¹⁰ Anything set apart as holy by an individual will belong to the individual who set it apart. Whatever a person gives to the priest will belong to the priest. ¹¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹² Speak to the Israelites and tell them: if a man suspects his wife and she has been unfaithful to him, ¹³ and someone has had sexual relations with her secretly, so that it is hidden from her husband and she has been defiled, and there is no witness against her, and she has not been caught in the act, ¹⁴ if the man is seized by a spirit of jealousy and suspects his wife, whether she is defiled or not, ¹⁵ the man must bring his wife to the priest and bring an offering for her—tenths of an ephah of barley flour. He must not pour oil on it or place frankincense on it, for it is a memorial offering, a reminder of sin. ¹⁶ The priest must bring her before the LORD. ¹⁷ The priest must take holy water in a clay bowl and take some dust from the floor of the Tent of Meeting, and the priest must put the dust into the water. ¹⁸ The priest must stand the woman before the LORD and uncover her head, and place the memorial offering—the offering of jealousy—on her hands. The priest must also hold the water of the curse in his hand. ¹⁹ The priest must then swear the woman and say to her: if you have not had sexual relations with anyone other than your husband, and you have not defiled yourself, then you will be clean from the water of this curse. ²⁰ But if you have been unfaithful to your husband and have defiled yourself, and someone else has had sexual relations with you apart from your husband, ²¹ the priest must swear the woman with the oath of divine judgment and say to her: may the LORD cause you to be cursed among your people, by making your thigh fall and your belly swell. ²² And this water of the curse will enter your body to cause your belly to swell and your thigh to fall. The woman must then say, "Amen, Amen." ²³ The priest must write the curses on a scroll and then blot them out in the water of the curse. ²⁴ The priest must make the woman drink the water of the curse, and the water will enter her to cause judgment. ²⁵ The priest must take the offering of jealousy from the woman’s hand, wave it before the LORD, and bring it to the altar. ²⁶ The priest must take a portion of the offering and burn it on the altar, and then give the woman the water to drink. ²⁷ If the woman is defiled and has broken faith with her husband, then the water of the curse will enter her to cause her belly to swell and her thigh to fall, and she will be a curse among her people. ²⁸ But if the woman is not defiled and is clean, then she will be free and able to bear children. ²⁹ This is the law of jealousy for when a woman is suspected by her husband and has been defiled. ³⁰ Or when a man is seized by a spirit of jealousy and suspects his wife, and he brings the woman before the LORD. The priest must carry out all of this law for her. ³¹ The man will be acquitted of wrongdoing, and the woman will bear the consequences of her sin.

---

Notes
- v. 15: "tenths of an ephah of barley flour" — the Hebrew specifies a tenth of an ephah of barley meal, and the flour is not to be anointed with oil or frankincense, which is consistent with it being a memorial offering rather than a regular grain offering.
- v. 21: "make your thigh fall and your belly swell" — this reflects the physical curses described in the text, with the water of the curse causing visible signs of punishment on the defiled woman.
