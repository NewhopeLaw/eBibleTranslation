# Numbers 18

¹ The LORD spoke to Aaron, saying, “You and your sons and your father’s house with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary, you and your sons with you shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood. ² And also your brothers, the tribe of Levi, the house of your father, bring near to you, and they shall attach themselves to you and serve you, you and your sons with you, in front of the Tent of Meeting. ³ They shall keep your charge and the charge of the whole Tent, but they shall not come near the sacred objects or the altar, and they will not die — you and they. ⁴ They shall be attached to you and keep the charge of the Tent of Meeting for all its service, and a stranger shall not come near you. ⁵ You shall keep the charge of the sacred things and the charge of the altar, so that there will no longer be wrath against the children of Israel. ⁶ I have indeed taken your brothers the Levites from among the children of Israel as a gift to you, given to the LORD, to serve the service of the Tent of Meeting. ⁷ You and your sons with you shall keep the charge of your priesthood for all matters of the altar and for the inner part behind the curtain, and you shall serve the service of the gift I have given you. The stranger who comes near shall die. ⁸ The LORD spoke to Aaron, “Behold, I have given you the charge of my gifts, all the sacred things of the children of Israel, to you I have given them as a gift and to your sons forever. ⁹ This shall be for you from the most holy things, from all their offerings, from all their gifts, from all their peace offerings and from every guilt offering they bring to me, from all the most holy things that are for you and your sons. ¹⁰ In the most holy things you shall eat them. Every male may eat them — you and your sons. They are holy to you. ¹¹ This shall also be for you, the gifts of their gifts, from all their wave offerings of the children of Israel. To you I have given them and to your sons and daughters with you, forever, as a perpetual statute. Every clean person in your house may eat them. ¹² All the firstfruits of oil and all the firstfruits of wine and grain, the firstfruits they give to the LORD — to you I have given them. ¹³ The firstfruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to the LORD — to you it shall be. Every clean person in your house may eat them. ¹⁴ Every devoted thing in Israel shall be for you. ¹⁵ Every firstborn of flesh, whether from man or beast, which they bring to the LORD — it shall be for you. But the firstborn of man you must redeem, and the firstborn of unclean animals you must redeem. ¹⁶ The redemption of a firstborn male shall be from the age of one month, and you must redeem them with five shekels of silver according to the sanctuary weight — twenty gerahs to the shekel. ¹⁷ But the firstborn of oxen, sheep, or goats you must not redeem; they are holy. Their blood you shall pour out on the altar, and their fat you shall burn as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. ¹⁸ Their flesh shall be yours, like the breast of the wave offering and like the right thigh — it shall be yours. ¹⁹ All the gifts of the sacred things that the children of Israel lift up to the LORD I have given to you and to your sons and daughters with you, forever — a covenant of salt, an eternal covenant before the LORD for you and for your descendants with you. ²⁰ The LORD said to Aaron, “In their land you shall not inherit, and no portion shall be yours among them. I am your portion and your inheritance among the children of Israel. ²¹ To the sons of Levi I have given all the tithe in Israel as an inheritance, in place of their service, which they perform in the service of the Tent of Meeting. ²² And the children of Israel shall no longer come near the Tent of Meeting to carry a sin that leads to death. ²³ The Levite himself shall perform the service of the Tent of Meeting, and they shall bear their iniquity — a statute forever throughout their generations, and among the children of Israel they shall not inherit a portion. ²⁴ For I have given the tithe of the children of Israel, which they lift up to the LORD as a gift, to the Levites as an inheritance. Therefore I said to them, They shall not inherit a portion among the children of Israel. ²⁵ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²⁶ “You shall speak to the Levites and say to them, When you take the tithe from the children of Israel, which I have given to you from them as an inheritance, you shall lift up a gift to the LORD from the tithe, a tenth of the tithe. ²⁷ And your gift shall be reckoned to you as the grain from the threshing floor and as the fullness from the winepress. ²⁸ So you too must lift up a gift to the LORD from all your tithes — from all the tithes you take from the children of Israel — and you must give from them a gift to Aaron the priest. ²⁹ From all your gifts you must lift up a gift to the LORD, even the holiness of all of them. ³⁰ And you shall say to them, When you lift up the holiness from them, it shall be reckoned to the Levites as the increase from the threshing floor and as the increase from the winepress. ³¹ And you shall eat it in every place — you and your household — for it is your wages, a reward for the service you perform in the Tent of Meeting. ³² And you shall not bear guilt in lifting up the holiness from them, and you shall not profane the sacred things of the children of Israel, so that you may not die.”

---

Notes
- v. 10: "In the most holy things you shall eat them" — the Hebrew uses "kedosh ha-kodashim" (most holy things), which is a fixed phrase for objects of highest sanctity, here applied to the food portions.
- v. 15: "the firstborn of unclean animals you must redeem" — the Levitical law distinguishes between clean and unclean animals; the Levite must redeem the unclean ones, but not the clean.
- v. 19: "a covenant of salt" — salt symbolized an enduring, binding agreement in ancient Near Eastern culture, hence "covenant of salt" as an idiom for a perpetual covenant.
- v. 20: "I am your portion and your inheritance" — the Hebrew uses "achalti" for "portion" and "nachlati" for "inheritance," both typically used in land contexts, but here applied metaphorically to God.
- v. 26: "a tenth of the tithe" — the Levites were to give a tenth of the tithe they received to the priests; this is a nested system of tithing.
- v. 31: "your wages, a reward for the service you perform" — the Levites were not given land but received food and offerings as their compensation for serving in the sanctuary.
