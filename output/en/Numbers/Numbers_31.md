# Numbers 31

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ² "Take vengeance for the Israelites against the Midianites. After you have been gathered to your people, ³ Moses spoke to the people, saying, "Conscript men from among you for battle, and they shall take their stand against the LORD against Midian to exact vengeance from Midian. ⁴ A thousand from each tribe, a thousand from each tribe, from all the tribes of Israel, you shall send for battle. ⁵ And twelve thousand men were conscripted from the thousands of Israel, as men for battle. ⁶ Moses sent them — a thousand from each tribe, for battle, and Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest with them, along with the holy things and the trumpets of alarm in his hand. ⁷ And they took their stand against Midian, as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they killed every male. ⁸ And they killed the kings of Midian on their slain, even Evi, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian, and also Balaam the son of Beor they killed with the sword. ⁹ And the Israelites captured the women of Midian and their children and their cattle and their livestock and all their goods, and they took everything as spoil. ¹⁰ And they burned every city of theirs where they dwelled, and every encampment with fire. ¹¹ And they took all the spoil and all the plunder from man and beast. ¹² And they brought it to Moses and to Eleazar the priest and to the assembly of the Israelites, the captives and the plunder and the spoil, to the camp, to the plains of Moab beyond the Jordan at Jericho. ¹³ And Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the leaders of the community went out to meet them outside the camp. ¹⁴ And Moses was angry with the officers of the army, the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds who had returned from the battle. ¹⁵ And he said to them, "Have you spared every female? ¹⁶ Behold, these were the ones who led the Israelites astray in the matter of Balaam, to bring guilt upon the LORD in the matter of Peor, and the plague came upon the community of the LORD. ¹⁷ Now therefore, kill every male among the children, and every woman who has known a man by lying with him. ¹⁸ But as for all the girls who have not known a man by lying with him, keep them alive for yourselves. ¹⁹ And you, remain outside the camp for seven days. Everyone who has killed a person and everyone who has touched a corpse must purify themselves on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives. ²⁰ And every garment, and every leather object, and every work of hair, and every wooden object, you shall purify. ²¹ And Eleazar the priest said to the men of war who had come from the battle, "This is the statute of the law that the LORD commanded Moses: ²² Only the gold and silver, the copper and iron, the tin and lead, ²³ whatever can be made to pass through the fire you shall pass through the fire, and it shall be clean. But all that cannot pass through the fire you shall pass through water. ²⁴ And you shall wash your garments on the seventh day, and you shall be clean. And then you may enter the camp." ²⁵ And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ²⁶ "Take the head count of the spoil captured, from man to beast, you and Eleazar the priest and the heads of the fathers' houses of the community. ²⁷ And you shall divide the spoil between those who went out to the battle and those who remained in the camp. ²⁸ And you shall take a tax for the LORD from the men who went out to the battle, one soul from every five hundred, from the people and from the cattle and from the donkeys and from the sheep. ²⁹ From their half you shall take, and you shall give to Eleazar the priest as a contribution to the LORD. ³⁰ And from the half of the Israelites you shall take one from every fifty, from the people and from the cattle and from the donkeys and from all the beasts, and you shall give them to the Levites who keep the charge of the tent of meeting. ³¹ And Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³² And the plunder that was left over, the extra that the men of war had taken — sheep, six hundred thousand and seventy thousand and five thousand; ³³ and cattle, seventy-two thousand; ³⁴ and donkeys, sixty-one thousand; ³⁵ and human life, of the women who had not known a man by lying with him, all souls, thirty-two thousand. ³⁶ And the half was the portion of those who had gone out to war, the number of the sheep, three hundred thirty thousand and seven thousand five hundred; ³⁷ and the tax for the LORD from the sheep was six hundred seventy-five; ³⁸ and the cattle, thirty-six thousand; and the tax for the LORD was seventy-two; ³⁹ and the donkeys, thirty thousand and five hundred; and the tax for the LORD was sixty-one; ⁴⁰ and human lives, sixteen thousand; and the tax for the LORD was thirty-two souls. ⁴¹ And Moses gave the tax, the contribution to the LORD, to Eleazar the priest, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ⁴² And from the half of the Israelites, which Moses had divided from the men of war; ⁴³ and the half from the community, from the sheep, three hundred thirty thousand and thirty thousand and five hundred; ⁴⁴ and cattle, thirty-six thousand; ⁴⁵ and donkeys, thirty thousand and five hundred; ⁴⁶ and human lives, sixteen thousand. ⁴⁷ And Moses took from the half of the Israelites the one from every fifty, from the people and from the beasts, and gave them to the Levites who keep the charge of the tent of meeting, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ⁴⁸ And the commanders who were in charge of the thousands of the army, the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, came near to Moses. ⁴⁹ And they said to Moses, "Your servants have taken the head count of the men of war in our charge, and not one of them is missing. ⁵⁰ And we brought a contribution for the LORD, each man who found rings of gold, earrings, and pendants, and rings and nose rings, to atone for our lives before the LORD." ⁵¹ And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, every wrought vessel. ⁵² And all the gold of the offering that they had brought to the LORD was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels, from the commanders of the thousands and commanders of the hundreds. ⁵³ The men of war had taken their own spoil. ⁵⁴ And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the commanders of the thousands and hundreds and brought it into the tent of meeting as a memorial for the Israelites before the LORD."

Notes
- v. 16: "brought guilt upon" — the Hebrew uses "למסור-מעל", which is a difficult phrase; "bring guilt upon" is chosen to reflect the idea of leading into sin.
- v. 41: "tax" — the Hebrew uses "מֶכֶס", which can be translated as "contribution", "tax", or "tribute", depending on context.
- v. 52: "shekels" — the Hebrew uses "שְׁקֵל", which is a unit of weight; "shekel" is retained as the standard rendering.
