# Numbers 33

¹ These are the stages of the Israelites that they traveled from the land of Egypt with their armies under the hand of Moses and Aaron. ² Moses wrote down their departures and stages according to the word of the LORD. These are their stages of travel. ³ They set out from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month. The following day after the Passover, the Israelites left Egypt with a high hand before all the Egyptians. ⁴ The Egyptians were burying those whom the LORD had struck, every firstborn. The LORD had judged their gods. ⁵ The Israelites set out from Rameses and camped at Succoth. ⁶ They set out from Succoth and camped at Etham, which is on the edge of the wilderness. ⁷ They set out from Etham and turned back toward the Red Sea, facing Pi-Hahiroth, which is before Baal-Zephon, and camped before Migdol. ⁸ They set out from before Pi-Hahiroth and crossed through the middle of the sea into the wilderness. They traveled a three-day journey through the wilderness and camped at Marah. ⁹ They set out from Marah and came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees. They camped there. ¹⁰ They set out from Elim and camped by the Red Sea. ¹¹ They set out from the Red Sea and camped in the wilderness of Sin. ¹² They set out from the wilderness of Sin and camped at Dophkah. ¹³ They set out from Dophkah and camped at Alush. ¹⁴ They set out from Alush and camped at Rephidim, where there was no water for the people to drink. ¹⁵ They set out from Rephidim and camped in the wilderness of Sinai. ¹⁶ They set out from the wilderness of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-Hattaavah. ¹⁷ They set out from Kibroth-Hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth. ¹⁸ They set out from Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah. ¹⁹ They set out from Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez. ²⁰ They set out from Rimmon-perez and camped at Lebo-hamath. ²¹ They set out from Lebo-hamath and camped at Reseph. ²² They set out from Reseph and camped at Keheloth. ²³ They set out from Keheloth and camped at Mount Shapher. ²⁴ They set out from Mount Shapher and camped at Haradah. ²⁵ They set out from Haradah and camped at Makheloth. ²⁶ They set out from Makheloth and camped at Tahath. ²⁷ They set out from Tahath and camped at Tarah. ²⁸ They set out from Tarah and camped at Mitzpeh. ²⁹ They set out from Mitzpeh and camped at Cheshbon-rah. ³⁰ They set out from Cheshbon-rah and camped at Moseroth. ³¹ They set out from Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan. ³² They set out from Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-hagidgad. ³³ They set out from Hor-hagidgad and camped at Yatbathah. ³⁴ They set out from Yatbathah and camped at Avraham. ³⁵ They set out from Avraham and camped at Ezion-geber. ³⁶ They set out from Ezion-geber and camped in the wilderness of Zin, which is Kadesh. ³⁷ They set out from Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor at the edge of the land of Edom. ³⁸ Aaron the priest went up to Mount Hor at the word of the LORD and died there in the fortieth year after the Israelites left the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month. ³⁹ Aaron was 123 years old when he died on Mount Hor. ⁴⁰ The Canaanite king of Arad, who lived in the Negev of the land of Canaan, heard about the Israelites coming. ⁴¹ They set out from Mount Hor and camped at Tzalmonah. ⁴² They set out from Tzalmonah and camped at Punon. ⁴³ They set out from Punon and camped at Oboth. ⁴⁴ They set out from Oboth and camped in the wadis of the land of Moab. ⁴⁵ They set out from the wadis and camped at Dibon-gad. ⁴⁶ They set out from Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim. ⁴⁷ They set out from Almon-diblathaim and camped at the hills of the land of Moab facing Nebo. ⁴⁸ They set out from the hills of the land of Moab and camped at the plains of Moab across the Jordan near Jericho. ⁴⁹ They camped along the Jordan from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim in the plains of Moab. ⁵⁰ The LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan near Jericho, saying: ⁵¹ Speak to the Israelites and tell them, "When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan, ⁵² you must dispossess all the people living in the land before you. You must destroy all their images and all their molten images. You must tear down all their altars. ⁵³ You will dispossess the land and live in it, for I have given you the land to possess it. ⁵⁴ You will divide the land as an inheritance among your clans by lot. To the many, you will give a greater portion, and to the few, a smaller portion. Wherever the lot falls for a family, that will be their inheritance. You will inherit the land by the clans of your ancestors. ⁵⁵ But if you fail to dispossess the people living in the land before you, the ones you leave behind will become thorns in your sides and arrows in your flanks. They will trouble you in the land where you live. ⁵⁶ And just as I intended to do to them, I will do to you."

---

Notes
- v. 37-38: The Hebrew text names Mount Hor as the location of Aaron's death. The Septuagint adds "Mount Hor" as "Hor-hagidgad" in v. 32, but the Hebrew here reads "Hor-hagidgad" as the previous camp, and "Mount Hor" as the location of Aaron's death, which is consistent with the Masoretic text. No departure from the Hebrew is needed.
