# Numbers 10

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Make for yourself two silver trumpets; you shall make them hollow and they shall serve you for calling the assembly, and for setting the camps in motion. ³ When you sound them, all the assembly shall gather at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ⁴ And if you sound only one, the leaders, the heads of the thousands of Israel, shall gather to you. ⁵ When you sound a long blast, the camps that are stationed to the east shall set out. ⁶ When you sound another long blast, the camps that are stationed to the west shall set out. A long blast shall be sounded for their departure. ⁷ But when you gather the assembly, you shall sound, but you shall not sound a long blast. ⁸ The sons of Aaron the priests shall sound with the trumpets; this shall be a statute for you forever throughout your generations. ⁹ When you go to war in your land against an enemy who oppresses you, you shall sound the trumpets and be remembered before the LORD your God, and you shall be rescued from your enemies. ¹⁰ On the day of your joy and on your appointed festivals and on the beginning of your months, you shall sound the trumpets over your burnt offerings and over your peace offerings; they shall be a reminder before your God. I am the LORD your God. ¹¹ It happened in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month, that the cloud lifted from the Tent of Meeting. ¹² The Israelites set out from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran. ¹³ The Israelites set out for the first time at the command of the LORD by the hand of Moses. ¹⁴ The standard of the camp of the people of Judah set out first, with Nahshon son of Amminadab as its leader. ¹⁵ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Issachar was Nathananel son of Zuar. ¹⁶ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Zebulun was Eliab son of Helon. ¹⁷ Then the Tent was taken down, and the people of Gershon and the people of Merari, who carried the Tent, set out. ¹⁸ The standard of the camp of the people of Reuben set out next, with Elizur son of Shedeur as its leader. ¹⁹ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Simeon was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai. ²⁰ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Gad was Eliasaph son of Deuel. ²¹ The people of Kehath, who carried the holy objects, set out, and they erected the Tent until they arrived. ²² The standard of the camp of the people of Ephraim set out, with Elisama son of Ammihud as its leader. ²³ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Manasseh was Gamaliel son of Pedahzur. ²⁴ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Benjamin was Abidan son of Gideoni. ²⁵ The standard of the camp of the people of Dan set out last, to gather up all the camps, with Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai as its leader. ²⁶ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Asher was Pagiel son of Ocran. ²⁷ And over the division of the tribe of the people of Naphtali was Ahira son of Enan. ²⁸ These were the journeys of the people of Israel, when they set out. ²⁹ Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses’ brother-in-law: "We are journeying to the place the LORD has spoken of, saying, 'I will give it to you.' Come with us, and we will do good for you, for the LORD has spoken well concerning Israel." ³⁰ He said to him, "I will not go, but I will return to my land and to my kindred." ³¹ Moses said, "Do not abandon us, for you know that this is the place where we have stayed in the wilderness, and you will be our eyes." ³² And if you go with us, then the good that the LORD will do for us, we will do for you." ³³ The Israelites set out from the mountain of the LORD by a three-day journey, and the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD went ahead of them by a three-day journey to scout out a resting place for them. ³⁴ The cloud of the LORD was over them by day when they set out from the camp. ³⁵ It happened when the Ark set out, Moses said: "Rise up, O LORD, and scatter Your enemies; let those who hate You flee before You." ³⁶ And when it rested, he said: "Return, O LORD, to the ten thousands of Israel."

---

Notes
- v. 5: "long blast" is the translation for תְּרוּעָה (teru'ah), a specific type of trumpet blast used in religious and military contexts.
- v. 6: The second תְּרוּעָה (teru'ah) is for the west, and a third תְּרוּעָה is implied for the north in the Septuagint, but the Hebrew text only mentions the west explicitly.
- v. 35-36: These verses are traditionally understood as poetic acclamations by Moses as the Ark moved and rested. The exact wording and structure are preserved as in the source text.
