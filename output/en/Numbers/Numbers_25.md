# Numbers 25

¹ Israel settled in Shittim, and the people began to prostitute themselves with the daughters of Moab. ² They invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to their gods. ³ Israel became attached to Baal of Peor, and the anger of the LORD burned against Israel. ⁴ The LORD said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and execute them before the LORD at the entrance of the sun, so that the wrath of the LORD may turn away from Israel." ⁵ Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Put to death every man among you who has become attached to Baal of Peor." ⁶ And behold, a man from the people of Israel came in and brought his Midianite relative to his fellow Israelites in the sight of Moses, and in the sight of the whole assembly of the people of Israel, while they were weeping at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ⁷ Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it and rose from the middle of the assembly, taking a spear in his hand. ⁸ He went after the man of Israel into the tent and pierced both the man of Israel and the woman through the belly. The plague was halted from the people of Israel. ⁹ And those who died in the plague numbered twenty-four thousand. ¹⁰ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ¹¹ "Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, has turned my anger away from the people of Israel, because he was zealous on my behalf among them, and I did not consume the people of Israel in my jealousy." ¹² Therefore say, 'I am giving him my covenant of peace.' ¹³ And it shall be for him and his descendants after him a covenant of priesthood for all time, because he was zealous for his God and atoned for the people of Israel." ¹⁴ The name of the Israelite man who was struck down, who was struck with the Midianite woman, was Zimri, the son of Salu, a leader of the house of Simeon. ¹⁵ The name of the Midianite woman who was struck down was Cozbi, the daughter of Zur, a leader of the house of the clan of Midian. ¹⁶ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ¹⁷ "Take vengeance on the Midianites and strike them, ¹⁸ for they were adversaries to you by their deceit; they have deceived you through Peor, and through Cozbi, the daughter of a leader of Midian, her sister who was struck down on the day of the plague through Peor." ¹⁹ And after the plague this took place.

---

Notes  
- v. 3: "Baal of Peor" reflects the Hebrew "Baal Peor" (בעל פְּעוֹר), where the name is treated as a proper noun.  
- v. 17-18: The reference to Midianites as "adversaries by their deceit" is based on the Hebrew "צֹרְרִים" (adversaries) and "בְּנִכְלֵיהֶם" (in their deceits), which is rendered as "by their deceit."  
- v. 18: "her sister who was struck down" refers to Cozbi, identified as "אֲחֹתָם" (their sister), though she is not explicitly a blood sister to the Midianites. The phrase is retained as a poetic or metaphorical expression.
