# Numbers 34

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ² Command the Israelites and tell them, "When you enter the land of Canaan, this will be the land that you will inherit as your possession, the land of Canaan with its boundaries. ³ And your southern boundary will be from the wilderness of Sin as far as the territory of Edom, and your southern boundary will be from the edge of the Dead Sea eastward. ⁴ And the boundary will encircle you from the south toward the ascent of Akarabim, and it will pass through Zin, and its outlet will be from the south to Kadesh Barnea, and it will go out to the valley of Arad, and it will pass through Asmon. ⁵ And the boundary will encircle from Asmon the stream of Egypt, and its outlet will be the sea. ⁶ And the western boundary will be for you the Great Sea. This will be for you the western boundary. ⁷ And this will be your northern boundary: from the Great Sea you will measure for yourselves the Mount Hor; ⁸ and from Mount Hor you will measure for yourselves, going toward Hamath, and the outlet of the boundary will be at Zedad; ⁹ and the boundary will go out to Ziphron, and its outlet will be at the valley of Enan. This will be for you the northern boundary. ¹⁰ And you will measure for yourselves the eastern boundary from the valley of Enan to Shepham; ¹¹ and the boundary will descend from Shepham to Riblah, from the east toward the springs, and it will descend toward the hillside of the Sea of Chinnereth eastward. ¹² And the boundary will descend to the Jordan, and its outlet will be the Dead Sea. This will be the land for you with all its boundaries around. ¹³ And Moses commanded the Israelites, saying, "This is the land that you will inherit by lot, as the LORD has commanded to give to the nine tribes and the half-tribe. ¹⁴ For the tribe of the sons of Reuben has taken its inheritance by the house of their fathers, and the tribe of the sons of Gad has taken its inheritance by the house of their fathers, and the half-tribe of Manasseh has taken its inheritance. ¹⁵ Two tribes and the half-tribe have taken their inheritance beyond the Jordan, near Jericho eastward, in the land of the Canaanites. ¹⁶ And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁷ These are the names of the men who will inherit the land for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun. ¹⁸ And one leader from each tribe you will take to inherit the land for you. ¹⁹ And these are the names of the men: for the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh; ²⁰ and for the tribe of the sons of Simeon, Shmuel son of Ammihud; ²¹ for the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Kislom; ²² and for the tribe of the sons of Dan, Bukki son of Yagil; ²³ for the sons of Joseph, the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, Hananel son of Eped; ²⁴ and for the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, Kemuel son of Shiphtan; ²⁵ and for the tribe of the sons of Zebulun, Elisaphan son of Parneach; ²⁶ and for the tribe of the sons of Issachar, Paltiel son of Ozan; ²⁷ and for the tribe of the sons of Asher, Ahijud son of Shelomi; ²⁸ and for the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, Phadei'el son of Ammihud; ²⁹ these are the ones whom the LORD commanded to inherit for the Israelites in the land of Canaan.

Notes
- v. 4: The Hebrew term "חֲצַר־אַדָּר" is translated as "the valley of Arad" based on the context and common interpretation.
- v. 12: "יַרְדֵּנָה" is rendered as "the Jordan" based on context.
