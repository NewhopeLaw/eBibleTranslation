# Numbers 35

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab, by the Jordan at Jericho, saying: ² Command the Israelites and they shall give to the Levites, from their inheritance, cities to dwell in, and surrounding suburbs they shall give to the Levites. ³ And these cities will be for them to dwell in, and their suburbs will be for their livestock and for all their animals. ⁴ And the suburbs of the cities that you give to the Levites will extend from the wall of the city outward a thousand cubits all around. ⁵ And you shall measure outside the city the east side two thousand cubits and the south side two thousand cubits and the west side two thousand cubits and the north side two thousand cubits. And the city will be in the middle of this. These will be the suburbs of the cities for you. ⁶ And the cities that you give to the Levites will include the six cities of refuge, which you will give as places of escape for the one who kills a person. And you will give forty-two cities in addition to these. ⁷ All the cities that you give to the Levites will be forty-eight cities, these and their suburbs. ⁸ And the cities that you give from the inheritance of the sons of Israel, from the more you will add, and from the less you will deduct. Each tribe will give cities to the Levites according to the inheritance that they have received. ⁹ And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹⁰ Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan, ¹¹ you will set aside for yourselves cities that will be cities of refuge, and the one who kills a person accidentally will flee there. ¹² And these cities will be for you as places of refuge from the avenger of blood, and the one who kills will not die until he stands before the assembly for judgment. ¹³ And the cities that you give will be six cities of refuge for you. ¹⁴ Three cities you will give beyond the Jordan, and three cities you will give in the land of Canaan. These will be cities of refuge. ¹⁵ For the Israelites and for the foreigner and the resident among them, these six cities will be for refuge, for any person who kills another accidentally to flee there. ¹⁶ But if someone strikes another with an iron weapon and he dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must die. ¹⁷ Or if someone strikes another with a stone from his hand with which he dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must die. ¹⁸ Or if someone strikes another with a wooden object from his hand with which he dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must die. ¹⁹ The avenger of blood shall kill the murderer. If he encounters him, he shall kill him. ²⁰ If someone pushes another with hatred, or hurls an object at him from an ambush and he dies, ²¹ or strikes him with his hand in anger and he dies, the one who struck him must die. He is a murderer. The avenger of blood shall kill the murderer when he encounters him. ²² But if someone strikes another suddenly, without enmity, or hurls an object at him without ambush, ²³ or with a stone with which he dies, without knowing it, and it falls on him and he dies, and he was not his enemy and did not seek to harm him, ²⁴ then the assembly will judge between the one who struck and the avenger of blood according to these laws. ²⁵ And the assembly will rescue the one who killed from the hand of the avenger of blood and will return him to the city of refuge where he fled, and he will dwell there until the death of the high priest who was anointed with holy oil. ²⁶ If the one who killed goes out beyond the boundary of the city of refuge where he fled, ²⁷ and the avenger of blood finds him outside the boundary of the city of refuge, the avenger of blood may kill the one who killed, and he will not be guilty. ²⁸ For in the city of refuge he must dwell until the death of the high priest. But after the death of the high priest, the one who killed will return to the land of his inheritance. ²⁹ These will be for you as a statute of judgment for your generations in all your dwellings. ³⁰ Any person who kills another must be put to death by the testimony of witnesses. A single witness shall not be sufficient to put a person to death. ³¹ And you must not accept a ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death, for he must die. ³² And you must not accept a ransom for the one who fled to a city of refuge to return to live in the land until the death of the priest. ³³ And you must not defile the land in which you live, for the blood will defile the land, and the land will not be atoned for the blood that is shed in it, except by the blood of the one who shed it. ³⁴ And you must not pollute the land where you dwell, in which I dwell among you, for I am the LORD who dwells among the Israelites.

---

Notes
- v. 20: "ambush" (ἐνέδρου) reflects the context of premeditated violence, not literal hunting traps.
- v. 23: "without knowing it" reflects the nuance of not having intent or awareness when the object is hurled.
- v. 25: "high priest who was anointed with holy oil" clarifies the specific priest in context, not just "high priest."
- v. 33: "pollute" is used for "תַּחֲנִיפוּ" to avoid the archaic "defile" while preserving the sense of moral corruption.
