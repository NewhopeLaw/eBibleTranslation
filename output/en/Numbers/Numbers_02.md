# Numbers 2

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying: ² Each person shall camp with their banner, by the family of their ancestors, facing the Tent of the Testimony, around it the Israelites shall camp. ³ The ones camping east, toward the sunrise, were the banner of the camp of Judah, with its divisions. The leader of the Israelites, the sons of Judah, was Nahshon son of Amminadab. ⁴ His number, the people under his command, was seventy-four thousand six hundred. ⁵ The ones camping with him were the tribe of Issachar, and the leader of the sons of Issachar was Nethanel son of Zuar. ⁶ His number, the people under his command, was fifty-four thousand four hundred. ⁷ The tribe of Zebulun, and the leader of the sons of Zebulun was Eliab son of Helon. ⁸ His number, the people under his command, was fifty-seven thousand four hundred. ⁹ All the people counted in the camp of Judah totaled 186,400, including all its divisions. They were the first to set out. ¹⁰ The banner of the camp of Reuben, to the south, with its divisions. The leader of the sons of Reuben was Elizur son of Shedeur. ¹¹ His number, the people under his command, was sixty-four thousand five hundred. ¹² The ones camping with him were the tribe of Simeon, and the leader of the sons of Simeon was Shlumiel son of Zurishaddai. ¹³ His number, the people under his command, was fifty-nine thousand three hundred. ¹⁴ The tribe of Gad, and the leader of the sons of Gad was Eliasaph son of Reuel. ¹⁵ His number, the people under his command, was forty-five thousand six hundred fifty. ¹⁶ All the people counted in the camp of Reuben totaled 151,450, including all its divisions. They were the second to set out. ¹⁷ The Tent of the Testimony was to move within the midst of the camps. Just as they encamped, so they would journey, each person by their command, according to their banners. ¹⁸ The banner of the camp of Ephraim, to the west, with its divisions. The leader of the sons of Ephraim was Elishama son of Ammihud. ¹⁹ His number, the people under his command, was forty thousand five hundred. ²⁰ The ones camping with him were the tribe of Manasseh, and the leader of the sons of Manasseh was Gamliel son of Pedahzur. ²¹ His number, the people under his command, was thirty-two thousand two hundred. ²² The tribe of Benjamin, and the leader of the sons of Benjamin was Abidan son of Gideoni. ²³ His number, the people under his command, was thirty-five thousand four hundred. ²⁴ All the people counted in the camp of Ephraim totaled 107,100, including all its divisions. They were the third to set out. ²⁵ The banner of the camp of Dan, to the north, with its divisions. The leader of the sons of Dan was Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai. ²⁶ His number, the people under his command, was sixty-two thousand seven hundred. ²⁷ The ones camping with him were the tribe of Asher, and the leader of the sons of Asher was Pagiel son of Ocran. ²⁸ His number, the people under his command, was forty-one thousand five hundred. ²⁹ The tribe of Naphtali, and the leader of the sons of Naphtali was Ahira son of Enan. ³⁰ His number, the people under his command, was fifty-three thousand four hundred. ³¹ All the people counted in the camp of Dan totaled 160,600, including all its divisions. They were the last to set out, by their banners. ³² These were the counts of the Israelites, by the family of their ancestors, all the people counted in the camps, including their divisions: 603,550 in all. ³³ The Levites were not counted among them, as the LORD had commanded Moses. ³⁴ The Israelites did everything the LORD had commanded Moses. Just as he had instructed, so they encamped, each person by their clan, by the family of their ancestors.

Notes
- v. 28: "mī-‘eḥad" in the count of Asher is read as meaning "one and forty" (41,500), following the standard count structure.
