# Numbers 27

¹ The daughters of Shlafchad, son of Hepher, son of Gilad, son of Machir, son of Menashe, of the families of Joseph, came forward. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. ² They stood before Moses, before Eleazar the priest, and before the chieftains and the whole assembly at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and they said, ³ "Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not among the assembly that gathered before the LORD at the assembly of Korah, dying through his own sin. He had no sons. ⁴ Why should the name of our father be removed from his family? Give us an inheritance among our father’s brothers." ⁵ Moses brought their case before the LORD. ⁶ The LORD said to Moses, ⁷ "The daughters of Shlafchad are right in what they are saying. You must give them an inheritance among their father’s brothers. Pass the inheritance of their father on to them. ⁸ Tell the people of Israel, 'If a man dies and leaves no son, you must pass his inheritance to his daughter. ⁹ If he has no daughter, you must give his inheritance to his brothers. ¹⁰ If he has no brothers, you must give his inheritance to his father’s brothers. ¹¹ If his father has no brothers, you must give his inheritance to the nearest relative from his clan, and he shall inherit it.' This will be a statute for the people of Israel, as the LORD commanded Moses." ¹² The LORD said to Moses, "Go up to the Mount of Abarim and view the land I have given to the people of Israel. ¹³ You will see it, but you will not enter it. You will be gathered to your people, just as your brother Aaron was gathered to his people in the mountain of Or. ¹⁴ For you rebelled against my word in the wilderness of Sin, in the assembly, in the matter of the waters at Meribah of Kadesh in the wilderness of Sin. You failed to sanctify me before them. Those were the waters of Meribah in the wilderness of Sin." ¹⁵ Moses spoke to the LORD, ¹⁶ "O Lord, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the assembly, ¹⁷ someone who will go out before them and come before them, who will lead them out and bring them in, so that the assembly of the LORD may not be like sheep without a shepherd." ¹⁸ The LORD said to Moses, "Take Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom the spirit is, and lay your hand upon him. ¹⁹ Stand him before Eleazar the priest and before the whole assembly, and commission him in their sight. ²⁰ Bestow some of your glory on him, so that the whole people of Israel will obey him. ²¹ Before Eleazar the priest he shall stand and inquire for the people before the LORD by the judgment of the Urim. At his word they shall go out and at his word they shall come in, he and the whole people of Israel and the whole assembly." ²² Moses did as the LORD commanded him. He took Joshua and stood him before Eleazar the priest and before the whole assembly. ²³ Moses placed his hands on him and commissioned him, just as the LORD had directed through Moses.

---

Notes  
- v. 12: "Mount of Abarim" is a literal translation of "Har Abarim."  
- v. 13: "in the mountain of Or" is a direct translation of "ba-har ha-Or." Some interpretations identify this with Mount Hor.  
- v. 14: "the waters of Meribah" is repeated to clarify the reference to the same event mentioned in Numbers 20.  
- v. 18-23: The commissioning of Joshua is structured to reflect the transfer of leadership from Moses to Joshua, following the pattern of priestly investiture.
