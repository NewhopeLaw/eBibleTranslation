# Numbers 7

¹ When Moses had completed setting up the Tent, he anointed it and consecrated it, along with all its furnishings, the altar and its furnishings, anointing and consecrating them. ² The chieftains of Israel, the heads of their ancestral houses—these were the chieftains of the tribes, the ones standing over the census— ³ brought their offerings before the LORD: six oxen carts and twelve oxen, one cart per pair of chieftains and one ox per chieftain, bringing them before the Tent. ⁴ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ⁵ Take from them and let them serve the work of the Tent of Meeting; you shall give them to the Levites, according to their service. ⁶ Moses took the carts and the oxen and gave them to the Levites. ⁷ He gave two carts and four oxen to the sons of Gershon, according to their service. ⁸ And he gave four carts and eight oxen to the sons of Merari, according to their service, under the supervision of Itamar son of Aaron the priest. ⁹ But to the sons of Kehat he did not give any, since the sacred service was carried on their shoulders. ¹⁰ The chieftains brought the dedication offerings of the altar on the day it was anointed. The chieftains brought their offerings before the altar. ¹¹ The LORD said to Moses: One chieftain per day, one chieftain per day, shall bring their offerings for the dedication of the altar. ¹² On the first day, the one who brought his offering was Nahshon son of Amminadab, chieftain of the tribe of Judah. ¹³ His offering was a basket of silver weighing three hundred shekels, a silver bowl of seventy shekels by the sanctuary shekel, both full of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering. ¹⁴ One golden spoon filled with incense: ¹⁵ One young bull, one ram, one year-old male lamb for a burnt offering. ¹⁶ One male goat for a sin offering. ¹⁷ And for the peace offering: two oxen, five rams, five male goats, five year-old male lambs. This was the offering of Nahshon son of Amminadab. ¹⁸ On the second day, the one who brought his offering was Nathaniel son of Zuar, chieftain of the tribe of Issachar. ¹⁹ His offering was a basket of silver weighing three hundred shekels, a silver bowl of seventy shekels by the sanctuary shekel, both full of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering. ²⁰ One golden spoon filled with incense: ²¹ One young bull, one ram, one year-old male lamb for a burnt offering. ²² One male goat for a sin offering. ²³ And for the peace offering: two oxen, five rams, five male goats, five year-old male lambs. This was the dedication of the altar after it was anointed. ⁸⁹ When Moses entered the Tent of Meeting to speak with him, he heard the voice speaking to him from above the mercy seat, which was over the Ark of the Covenant, between the two cherubim. And the LORD spoke to him.
