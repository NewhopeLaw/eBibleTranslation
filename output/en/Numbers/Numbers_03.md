# Numbers 3

¹ These are the descendants of Aaron and Moses on the day the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. ² These are the names of Aaron’s sons: Nadav, the firstborn, and Abihu, Elazar, and Itamar. ³ These are the names of Aaron’s sons, the priests anointed to serve, whose hands had been filled to perform the priestly duties. ⁴ Nadav and Abihu died before the LORD by offering unauthorized fire before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai; they had no children, and it was Elazar and Itamar who served before Aaron their father. ⁵ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ⁶ Bring the tribe of Levi near and set them before Aaron the priest to serve him. ⁷ They are to guard his responsibilities and the responsibilities of the whole assembly before the Tent of Meeting, to perform the duties of the Tabernacle. ⁸ They are to guard all the furnishings of the Tent of Meeting and the responsibilities of the Israelites in performing the duties of the Tabernacle. ⁹ You are to assign the Levites to Aaron and his sons; they are to be given to them from the Israelites. ¹⁰ You are to assign Aaron and his sons to guard the Tent of Meeting; they must guard their priestly responsibilities and whatever belongs to the altar and the inner sanctuary. Any outsider who approaches must be put to death. ¹¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ¹² I have taken the Levites from among the Israelites in place of every firstborn that opens the womb among the Israelites. The Levites will belong to me. ¹³ For every firstborn is mine; on the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated to myself every firstborn in Israel, from human to beast. They belong to me. I am the LORD. ¹⁴ The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying: ¹⁵ Register the Levites by their ancestral families, by their clans. Count every male from a month old and up. ¹⁶ Moses and Aaron registered the Levites as the LORD commanded. ¹⁷ The Levites’ families by name were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. ¹⁸ These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei. ¹⁹ These are the names of the sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. ²⁰ These are the names of the sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites by their ancestral houses. ²¹ For Gershon, the families of Libni and Shimei. These are the families of the Gershonites. ²² Their count, by number of every male from a month old and up, totaled 7,500. ²³ The Gershonite families encamped behind the Tent, westward. ²⁴ The leader of the ancestral house of the Gershonites was Eliasaf son of Lael. ²⁵ Their responsibility was the Tent of Meeting, the tent itself, its covering, and the curtain that closed the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ²⁶ They were also in charge of the curtains of the courtyard, the curtain at the entrance of the courtyard that was over the Tabernacle and the altar, and all the cords of their service. ²⁷ For Kohath, the families of the Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites. These are the families of the Kohathites. ²⁸ Their count, by number of every male from a month old and up, totaled 8,600, those who guarded the holy things. ²⁹ The Kohathite families encamped on the southern side of the Tabernacle. ³⁰ The leader of the ancestral houses of the Kohathites was Elizaphan son of Uzziel. ³¹ Their responsibility was the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, and all the vessels of the sanctuary that they used, as well as the curtain and all their service. ³² The overall leader of the Levitical leaders was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, who was in charge of the guarding of the holy things. ³³ For Merari, the families of Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of Merari. ³⁴ Their count, by number of every male from a month old and up, totaled 6,200. ³⁵ The leader of the ancestral house of the Merari families was Zuriel son of Abihail. The Merari families encamped on the northern side. ³⁶ Their responsibility was the bases of the Tabernacle, its bars, its posts, and its sockets, and all its equipment, as well as all its service. ³⁷ The posts of the courtyard around it, their bases, their pegs, and their cords. ³⁸ Those who camped before the Tabernacle toward the east, before the Tent of Meeting, were Moses and Aaron and his sons, guarding the sanctuary for the Israelites. Any outsider who approached must be put to death. ³⁹ The total count of the Levites, as Moses and Aaron registered them at the LORD’s command by their families, of every male from a month old and up, was 22,000. ⁴⁰ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: Register every firstborn male among the Israelites from a month old and up, and take a count of their names. ⁴¹ You are to take the Levites for me. I am the LORD. They will be in place of every firstborn among the Israelites, and the livestock of the Levites will be in place of every firstborn among the livestock of the Israelites. ⁴² Moses registered the Israelites as the LORD had commanded him. ⁴³ The total count of the firstborn males among the Israelites, by name from a month old and up, was 22,273. ⁴⁴ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ⁴⁵ Take the Levites in place of every firstborn among the Israelites, and take their livestock in place of their livestock. The Levites will belong to me. I am the LORD. ⁴⁶ The excess of those who were redeemed, the 273 more than the Levites among the firstborn of the Israelites, ⁴⁷ must be redeemed with five shekels per head, according to the sanctuary shekel, which is twenty gerahs to the shekel. ⁴⁸ You are to give the silver to Aaron and his sons as a redemption offering for the excess. ⁴⁹ Moses took the silver of the redemption from the Israelites who were more than the Levites. ⁵⁰ From the firstborn of the Israelites, he collected 1365 silver shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel. ⁵¹ Moses gave the silver of the redemption to Aaron and his sons, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

---

Notes
- v. 49: "Moses took the silver of the redemption from the Israelites who were more than the Levites" – the Hebrew is clear that the redemption was for the excess Israelite firstborn not accounted for by the Levites.
- v. 50: "1365 silver shekels" – the number is derived from the count in v. 43 (22,273 firstborn) minus the Levites (22,000), multiplied by the redemption rate of 5 shekels per excess.
