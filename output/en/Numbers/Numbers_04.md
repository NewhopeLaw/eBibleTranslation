# Numbers 4

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying: ² Register the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi by their clans, by their ancestral houses, ³ from the age of thirty years and upward, to fifty years old, everyone who comes to serve, to do the work in the Tent of Meeting. ⁴ This is the work of the sons of Kohath in the Tent of Meeting—holy of holies. ⁵ When the camp is to move, Aaron and his sons will come and remove the curtain of the veil, and cover it with it, to cover the ark of the testimony. ⁶ They will place on it a covering of tachash leather and spread over it a cloth of solid blue above it, and fasten its clasps. ⁷ And over the table of the presence they will spread a cloth of solid blue and place on it the dishes, the bowls, and the spoons, and the jars for the libations, and the continual bread will remain on it. ⁸ And they will spread over it a cloth of crimson and cover it with a covering of tachash leather, and fasten its clasps. ⁹ They will take a cloth of blue and cover the lampstand of the light, its lamps, its wicks, its snuffers, and all the vessels of the oil used in it. ¹⁰ And they will place it and all its vessels into a covering of tachash leather, and set it on the carrying poles. ¹¹ And over the golden altar they will spread a cloth of blue and cover it with a covering of tachash leather, and fasten its clasps. ¹² And all the vessels of service used in the holy things, they will place into a cloth of blue and cover them with a covering of tachash leather, and set them on the carrying poles. ¹³ They will take down the altar and spread over it a cloth of deep red. ¹⁴ And they will place on it all the vessels used in it, the censers, the shovels, the basins, the ladles, and all the vessels of the altar, and spread over it a covering of tachash leather, and fasten its clasps. ¹⁵ When Aaron and his sons finish covering the holy things and all the vessels of the holy things when the camp is to move, afterward the sons of Kohath will come to carry, and they must not touch the holy things or they will die. These are the things the sons of Kohath carry in the Tent of Meeting. ¹⁶ The charge of Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is the anointing oil, the incense of the mixture, the continual offering, and the anointing oil, the charge of the entire Tent and everything in it in the holy place, and all its work. ¹⁷ The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying: ¹⁸ Do not destroy the tribe of the sons of Kohath from among the Levites. ¹⁹ Do this for them, that they may live and not die when they approach the holy of holies: Aaron and his sons will come and set them each in their carrying task. ²⁰ And they must not look upon the holy things suddenly and die. ²¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ²² Register the sons of Gershon also, by their ancestral houses, by their clans, ²³ from the age of thirty years and upward to fifty years old, you will register them, everyone who comes to serve, to do the work in the Tent of Meeting. ²⁴ This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites, to serve and to carry: ²⁵ They will carry the curtains of the Tent and the Tent of Meeting, its covering, and the covering of tachash leather over it, and the curtain of the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ²⁶ And the curtains of the courtyard, and the curtain of the entrance of the gate of the courtyard that is over the Tent and over the altar around it, and their cords, and all the vessels of their service, and everything they are to do, they will serve. ²⁷ By the mouth of Aaron and his sons will be all the work of the sons of Gershon for all their carrying and all their service. You will register their load under their watch. ²⁸ This is the work of the clans of the sons of Gershon in the Tent of Meeting, and their watch is in the hand of Itamar son of Aaron the priest. ²⁹ The sons of Merari by their clans, by their ancestral houses, you will register them. ³⁰ From the age of thirty years and upward to fifty years old, you will register them, everyone who comes to serve, to do the work in the Tent of Meeting. ³¹ And these are the things they are to watch over for all their carrying in the Tent of Meeting: the bases of the Tent, its bars, its posts, and its sockets; ³² the posts of the courtyard around it, their bases, their pegs, and their cords for all their vessels and for all their service. You will register them by name, and all the vessels of their watch. ³³ This is the work of the clans of the sons of Merari for all their work in the Tent of Meeting, in the hand of Itamar son of Aaron the priest. ³⁴ Moses and Aaron and the leaders of the assembly registered the sons of Kohath by their clans, by their ancestral houses, ³⁵ from the age of thirty years and upward to fifty years old, everyone who came to serve and to do work in the Tent of Meeting. ³⁶ Their registration by their clans was 2,750. ³⁷ This is the registration of the clan of Kohath, everyone who served in the Tent of Meeting, as Moses and Aaron registered them by the word of the LORD in Moses' hand. ³⁸ The sons of Gershon were registered by their clans, by their ancestral houses, ³⁹ from the age of thirty years and upward to fifty years old, everyone who came to serve and to do the work in the Tent of Meeting. ⁴⁰ Their registration by their clans, by their ancestral houses, was 2,630. ⁴¹ This is the registration of the sons of Gershon, everyone who served in the Tent of Meeting, as Moses and Aaron registered them by the word of the LORD in Moses' hand. ⁴² The sons of Merari were also registered by their clans, by their ancestral houses, ⁴³ from the age of thirty years and upward to fifty years old, everyone who came to serve in the work of the Tent of Meeting. ⁴⁴ Their registration by their clans, by their ancestral houses, was 3,200. ⁴⁵ This is the registration of the clan of the sons of Merari, as Moses and Aaron registered them by the word of the LORD in Moses' hand. ⁴⁶ All those registered, whom Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel registered among the Levites, by their clans and by their ancestral houses, ⁴⁷ from the age of thirty years and upward to fifty years old, everyone who came to serve the service and the carrying in the Tent of Meeting. ⁴⁸ Their registration totaled 8,580. ⁴⁹ By the word of the LORD they were registered in Moses' hand, man by man for his service and for his carrying. And they were registered as the LORD had directed Moses.

---

Notes
- v. 3: "from the age of thirty years and upward" — the Hebrew term is literally "from thirty years and up" but the context implies a range to fifty years.
- v. 16: "the continual offering" — refers to the continual burnt offering, as contextually implied.
- v. 37, 41, 45: "as Moses and Aaron registered them" — the original uses a form of the verb "to count" (פָּקַד) which also means "to appoint" or "to entrust," indicating careful attention to their duties.
- v. 48: "8,580" — the sum of the Levitical registrations in this chapter (2,750 + 2,630 + 3,200) equals 8,580, which is consistent with the total given here.
