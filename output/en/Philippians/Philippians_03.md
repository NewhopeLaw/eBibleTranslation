# Philippians 3

¹ The rest, brothers and sisters, rejoice in the Lord. To write these things to you is not burdensome to me, but it is safe for you. ² Watch out for the dogs, watch out for the evil workers, watch out for the circumcision. ³ For we are the circumcision, who worship God in the Spirit and glory in Christ Jesus and put no confidence in the flesh— ⁴ even though I myself have confidence also in the flesh. If anyone else has a basis for confidence in the flesh, I have even more: ⁵ circumcised on the eighth day, of the people of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews, in regard to the law a Pharisee, ⁶ in zeal a persecutor of the church, in righteousness under the law blameless. ⁷ But whatever gains I had, I have counted as loss because of Christ. ⁸ Moreover, I count everything as loss for the surpassing value of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord, for whose sake I have suffered the loss of all things and count them as dung, so that I may gain Christ ⁹ and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own based on the law, but one that is through faith in Christ—the righteousness that comes from God on the basis of faith, ¹⁰ so that I may know him and the power of his resurrection and may share in his sufferings, becoming like him in his death, ¹¹ in order if possible to attain the resurrection from the dead. ¹² Not that I have already obtained this or have already been made perfect, but I press on to take hold of that for which I was also taken hold of by Christ Jesus. ¹³ Brothers and sisters, I do not consider myself to have taken hold of it yet. One thing I do: forgetting what lies behind and reaching forward to what lies ahead, ¹⁴ I press toward the goal for the prize of the heavenly calling of God in Christ Jesus. ¹⁵ Therefore, as many as are mature, let this mind be in you. And if you think otherwise, this too God will reveal to you. ¹⁶ But as for what we have already attained, let us walk by that same standard. ¹⁷ Join with me in following my example, brothers and sisters, and observe those who live according to the model you have in us, ¹⁸ for many live as enemies of the cross of Christ, of whom I have told you often and now say with weeping, ¹⁹ whose end is destruction, whose god is their stomach, and whose glory is their shame, who set their minds on earthly things. ²⁰ For our citizenship is in heaven, from which we also eagerly await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, ²¹ who will transform our lowly body to be like his glorious body, according to the working of the power that enables him to subject all things to himself.

---

Notes
- v. 9: "σκύβαλα" is translated as "dung" to reflect the strong dismissive tone without archaizing the language.
- v. 19: "ὧν τὸ τέλος ἀπώλεια" is translated as "whose end is destruction" to reflect the final fate of those described.
- v. 19: "ὧν ὁ θεὸς ἡ κοιλία" is translated as "whose god is their stomach" to capture the metaphor of misplaced devotion.
- v. 21: "μετασχηματίσει" is rendered as "transform" to emphasize the radical change implied.
