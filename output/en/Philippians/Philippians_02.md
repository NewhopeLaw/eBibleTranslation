# Philippians 2

¹ If then there is any encouragement in Christ, any comfort from love, any fellowship in the Spirit, any compassion and mercy, ² complete my joy by being of one mind, having the same love, being united in spirit and of one mind, ³ not doing anything from rivalry or conceit, but in humility consider each other better than yourselves, ⁴ each of you not looking to his own interests, but also to the interests of others. ⁵ Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus, ⁶ who, existing in the form of God, did not consider equality with God something to be grasped, ⁷ but emptied himself, taking the form of a slave, being made in the likeness of men. ⁸ And being found in appearance as a man, he humbled himself, becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross. ⁹ Therefore, God has also highly exalted him, and given him the name which is above every name, ¹⁰ so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of those in heaven, on earth, and under the earth, ¹¹ and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father. ¹² So then, beloved, just as you have always obeyed, not only in my presence, but much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, ¹³ for it is God who is at work in you both to will and to do for his good pleasure. ¹⁴ Do all things without grumbling and without disputing, ¹⁵ so that you may become blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish in a crooked and twisted generation, in which you appear as lights in the world, ¹⁶ holding the word of life, so that I may not have run in vain, nor have labored in vain. ¹⁷ But even if I am being poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and rejoice with all of you. ¹⁸ Likewise, you also be glad and rejoice with me. ¹⁹ But I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, so that I also may be cheered by news of you. ²⁰ For I have no one like-minded who will genuinely care for your affairs, ²¹ for all look out for their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ. ²² But you know his proven character, that as a son with a father he has served with me in the gospel. ²³ Therefore, I hope to send him as soon as I see how things go with me, ²⁴ but I trust in the Lord that I myself also will come soon. ²⁵ I have judged it necessary to send Epaphroditus to you, my brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, and your envoy and minister to my needs, ²⁶ because he has been longing for all of you and was distressed because you heard he was sick. ²⁷ For indeed he was sick, nearly to death, but God had mercy on him, not only on him but also on me, so that I would not have sorrow upon sorrow. ²⁸ Therefore, I have sent him more urgently, so that when you see him again, you may be glad and I may be less sorrowful. ²⁹ Receive him therefore in the Lord with all joy, and honor such people, ³⁰ because he nearly died for the work of Christ, risking his life to complete what was lacking in your service to me.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "equality with God" reflects the context of the hymn and the concept of Christ's preexistence, not a literal "grasping" for equality.
- v. 8: "death on a cross" preserves the specificity of the crucifixion, which is a key part of the humiliation.
- v. 11: "Jesus Christ is Lord" reflects the Greek "Ἰησοῦς Χριστὸς κύριος," where "Lord" is a title for YHWH in the OT, here applied to Jesus.
