# Philippians 2

¹ So if there is any comfort in Christ, any affection of love, any fellowship of the Spirit, any tenderness and compassion, ² complete my joy by thinking the same way, having the same love, being united in spirit, and thinking one thing, ³ not acting in rivalry or conceit, but humbling yourselves, each regarding the other as better than yourselves, ⁴ not looking to your own interests, but also to the interests of others. ⁵ Let this mind be in you that was also in Christ Jesus, ⁶ who, although he existed in the form of God, did not consider equality with God something to be seized, ⁷ but emptied himself, taking the form of a servant, becoming like human beings; and being found in appearance as a human, ⁸ he humbled himself, becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross. ⁹ Therefore God also highly exalted him and gave him the name above every name, ¹⁰ so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, ¹¹ and every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father. ¹² So then, beloved, just as you have always obeyed, not only when I was present, but now even more when I am absent, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, ¹³ for it is God who is at work in you both to will and to do for his good pleasure. ¹⁴ Do all things without grumbling or disputing, ¹⁵ so that you may be blameless and pure, children of God without blemish in a crooked and perverse generation, in which you appear as lights in the world, ¹⁶ holding fast the word of life, so that in the day of Christ I may have reason to glory, because I did not run or toil in vain. ¹⁷ But even if I am being poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and share my joy with all of you. ¹⁸ And you too should rejoice and share my joy. ¹⁹ I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, so that I too may be encouraged by news of you. ²⁰ For I have no one else of kindred spirit who will genuinely care for your welfare, ²¹ for all look out for their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ. ²² But you know his proven worth, for he has served as a son with me in the gospel. ²³ So I hope to send him as soon as I see how things go with me. ²⁴ But I have confidence in the Lord that I myself also will come soon. ²⁵ I considered it necessary to send Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, and your apostle and minister in my service, ²⁶ for he longed for all of you and was distressed because you heard he was ill. ²⁷ For indeed he was ill, nearly to death. But God had mercy on him, not only on him but also on me, so that I would not have sorrow upon sorrow. ²⁸ Therefore I sent him all the more eagerly, so that when you see him again you may rejoice, and I may be less sorrowful. ²⁹ So welcome him in the Lord with all joy, and honor such people, ³⁰ for he was close to death because of the work of Christ, risking his life to supply what was lacking in your service to me.

---

Notes
- v. 6: "equality with God" reflects the translation of "ἴσα θεῷ" as a comparative construction rather than a literal "being equal to God."
- v. 11: "Lord" for "κύριος" reflects its usage as a title for Jesus, consistent with the gospel tradition.
