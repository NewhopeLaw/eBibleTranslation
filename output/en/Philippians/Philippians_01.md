# Philippians 1

¹ Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus, to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are in Philippi, with the overseers and deacons— ² grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ³ I thank my God whenever I remember you, ⁴ always in every prayer of mine for all of you, making my prayer with joy, ⁵ because of your partnership in the gospel from the first day until now, ⁶ being fully convinced that the one who began a good work in you will complete it until the day of Jesus Christ, ⁷ which is only right for me to think this way about all of you, since you are in my heart, both in my imprisonment and in my defense and confirmation of the gospel, for all of you are partners in the grace with me. ⁸ For God is my witness how I long for all of you in the love of Christ Jesus. ⁹ And this I pray, that your love may abound more and more in knowledge and every kind of discernment, ¹⁰ so that you may approve what is excellent, and so be pure and blameless for the day of Christ, ¹¹ filled with the fruit of righteousness that comes through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God. ¹² I want you to know, brothers, that what has happened to me has resulted in progress of the gospel, ¹³ so that my imprisonment in Christ has become known in the whole imperial guard and to all the rest, ¹⁴ and most of the brothers in the Lord, being confident in the Lord because of my imprisonment, are speaking the word more boldly without fear. ¹⁵ Some indeed preach Christ out of rivalry and strife, some in good faith. ¹⁶ The former do so out of love, knowing that I am here for the defense of the gospel. ¹⁷ The latter proclaim Christ out of rivalry, not sincerely, thinking to raise trouble for me in my imprisonment. ¹⁸ What then? Only this, that in every way, whether in pretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed, and in that I rejoice. Yes, and I will continue to rejoice, ¹⁹ for I know that this will turn out for my salvation through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, ²⁰ according to my expectation and hope that in nothing I will be ashamed, but with all boldness, just as always, so now also Christ will be magnified in my body, whether by life or by death. ²¹ For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain. ²² If to live in the flesh means fruit from work, I do not know which I prefer; ²³ I am caught between the two, having a desire to depart and be with Christ, which is by far the better thing, ²⁴ yet to remain in the flesh is more necessary for your sake. ²⁵ And in this confidence I know that I will remain and continue with all of you for your progress and for the joy of your faith, ²⁶ so that your boasting in Christ Jesus may abound in me through my presence among you again. ²⁷ Only conduct yourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or am absent and hear about you, I know that you stand firm in one spirit, contending as one in the faith of the gospel, ²⁸ without being frightened in anything by your opponents— which for them is a sign of destruction, but for you of salvation, and this from God. ²⁹ For it has been granted to you not only to believe in Christ but also to suffer for him, ³⁰ having the same struggle that you have seen in me and now hear about in me.

Notes
- v. 1: "doulos" is translated as "servants" to reflect the literal meaning without adopting the term "bondservants" or "slaves" which may carry unintended connotations in English.
- v. 7: "συγκοινωνούς μου τῆς χάριτος" is translated as "partners in the grace" to reflect the shared spiritual bond.
- v. 18: "προφάσει" and "ἀληθείᾳ" are rendered "in pretense" and "in truth" to capture the contrast in motives among those who proclaim Christ.
- v. 21: The phrase "ἐμοὶ γὰρ τὸ ζῆν Χριστὸς καὶ τὸ ἀποθανεῖν κέρδος" is translated as "To me to live is Christ, and to die is gain," maintaining the poetic structure and the personal nature of Paul's declaration.
- v. 29: "ὑπὲρ Χριστοῦ" is translated as "for Christ" to emphasize the active participation in his cause, rather than a passive "on behalf of."
