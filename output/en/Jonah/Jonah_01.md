# Jonah 1

¹ The word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying: ² "Go at once to the great city of Nineveh and proclaim there against it, for their wickedness has come up to me." ³ But Jonah got up to flee to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD. He went down to Joppa, found a ship going to Tarshish, paid the fare, and went aboard to sail with them to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD. ⁴ But the LORD hurled a great wind upon the sea, and there was a mighty storm on the sea so violent the ship threatened to break apart. ⁵ The sailors were terrified and each cried out to his god. They threw the cargo in the ship into the sea to lighten the ship. Jonah, however, had gone down to the innermost part of the ship, lain down, and fallen into a deep sleep. ⁶ The ship's master came to him and said, "What are you doing sleeping? Get up, call out to your god! Perhaps the god will spare us and we won't perish." ⁷ Then they said to one another, "Let us cast lots to discover whose fault this disaster is." They cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah. ⁸ They said to him, "Tell us, please, whose fault is this calamity that has befallen us? What is your occupation, where are you from, what is your homeland, and to what people do you belong?" ⁹ He replied to them, "I am a Hebrew, and I fear the LORD, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land." ¹⁰ The men were terrified and said to him, "What have you done?" For they knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the LORD, for he had told them. ¹¹ "What should we do to you," they asked, "so that the sea may quiet down for us? For the sea is growing increasingly violent." ¹² Jonah said to them, "Pick me up and throw me into the sea, and the sea will quiet down for you. For I know it is because of me that this great storm is upon you." ¹³ The men tried to steer the ship back toward land, but they could not, for the sea kept growing more violent against them. ¹⁴ Then they cried out to the LORD and said, "Please, O LORD, do not let us perish for the life of this man, and do not charge us with innocent blood, for you, LORD, have done as you pleased." ¹⁵ So they took Jonah and threw him into the sea. The sea ceased its raging. ¹⁶ The men were awestruck by a great fear of the LORD and offered sacrifices to the LORD and made vows.

---

Notes
- v. 9: "I am a Hebrew" — the Hebrew term "Ivri" is used in its ethnic/cultural sense, not "Hebrew" as a language.
- v. 14: "innocent blood" — the Hebrew uses "dam nakiv," which can be read as "innocent blood" or "blood of an innocent person," depending on whether the adjective modifies "blood" or the implied "man." The translation follows the former.
