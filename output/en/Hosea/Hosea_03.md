# Hosea 3

¹ The LORD said to me, "Go again, love a woman who is deeply in love with her lovers and an adulteress, in the same way that the LORD loves the children of Israel, who turn to other gods and love the clusters of raisins." ² I acquired her for myself for fifteen pieces of silver, and a homer of barley and a measure of wine. ³ I said to her, "You will live with me for many days, not as a prostitute, not belonging to any man, and I will be to you as a husband." ⁴ For many days the children of Israel will have no king, no ruler, no sacrifice, no sacred pillar, no ephod, and no teraphim. ⁵ Later the children of Israel will return and seek the LORD their God, and David their king, and they will fear the LORD and his goodness in the latter days."

---

Notes
- v. 1: "a woman who is deeply in love with her lovers and an adulteress" translates "אֲהֻבַת רֵעַ וּמְנָאָפֶת" with "loved by her lovers" as a stylistic choice to reflect the Hebrew's layered meaning of being beloved and yet unfaithful.
- v. 4: "ephod" and "teraphim" are retained as technical terms for religious artifacts.
