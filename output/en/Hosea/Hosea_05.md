# Hosea 5

¹ Hear this, priests, and listen, house of Israel, and let the house of the king pay attention, for judgment is upon you. For you have become a trap to those on the watch, a net stretched out on the high places. ² The hunters have snared their prey. I, however, will be your discipline for all of you. ³ I have known Ephraim, and Israel cannot be separated from me. Yet now Ephraim has committed adultery, Israel has become unclean. ⁴ They will not give up their schemes to return to their God, because a spirit of prostitution is in them, and they do not know the LORD. ⁵ And the pride of Israel will be humiliated before its face, and Israel and Ephraim will stumble in their guilt, and Judah will stumble with them. ⁶ With sheep and with oxen they will go to seek the LORD, but they will not find him, for he has turned away from them. ⁷ For they have been unfaithful to the LORD, for they have borne alien children. Now the new moon will devour their portion. ⁸ Blow the horn on the hill, sound the alarm in the high places, cry out in the house of Aven. Benjamin has gone astray. ⁹ Ephraim will be destroyed in the day of reckoning. Among the tribes of Israel I have declared my faithfulness. ¹⁰ The rulers of Judah have become like boundary markers moved. On them I will pour out my wrath like water. ¹¹ Ephraim has been overcome by his adversary, he has trampled justice, because he has chosen to follow the worthless. ¹² And I have become like a moth to Ephraim, and like a thorn to the house of Judah. ¹³ Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound; and Ephraim went to Assyria, and sent envoys to the king of Jareb. But he cannot heal you, and there will be no remedy taken from you. ¹⁴ For I will be like a jackal to Ephraim, and like a lion to the house of Judah. I myself will tear and go, I will carry and there will be no one to rescue. ¹⁵ I will go and return to my place, until they are put to shame and seek my face in their distress.

---

Notes
- v. 1: "watch" refers to "mizpah" (מִצְפָּה), a term meaning "watchtower" or "place of watching," rendered naturally in English.
- v. 1: "high places" refers to "tabor" (תָּבֽוֹר), a term for elevated or fortified sites.
- v. 4: "spirit of prostitution" is a direct translation of "רוּחַ זְנוּנִים" (ruach zanunim), emphasizing moral corruption.
- v. 8: "house of Aven" refers to "בֵּית אָוֶן" (beit avin), likely a reference to Beth-aven, a name for Bethel, which the prophet treats as a place of falsehood.
- v. 13: "king of Jareb" is a translation of "מֶלֶךְ יָרֵב" (melekh yareb), where "Jareb" is a name or epithet of uncertain meaning, possibly a reference to Assyria or a symbolic designation.
