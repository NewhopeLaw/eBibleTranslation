# Hosea 4

¹ Hear the word of the LORD, O people of Israel, for the LORD brings a lawsuit against the inhabitants of the land, because there is no truth, no steadfast love, and no knowledge of God in the land. ² Swearing, lying, murder, stealing, and adultery have spread, and bloodshed follows bloodshed. ³ Therefore the land will mourn, and all its inhabitants will wither, along with the beasts of the field and the birds of the sky, and even the fish of the sea will disappear. ⁴ But let no one contend, and let no one rebuke, for your people are like those who argue with a priest. ⁵ You will stumble by day, and the prophet with you will stumble by night, and I will destroy your mother. ⁶ My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I will reject you from being my priest. You have forgotten the teaching of your God, so I will forget your children. ⁷ The more they increase, the more they have sinned against me. I will bring dishonor to their glory. ⁸ They devour the sin of my people and take their guilt into their hearts. ⁹ Therefore, as the people are, so will the priest be; I will bring their ways upon them and their schemes back to them. ¹⁰ They eat and are not satisfied, they fornicate and are not fruitful, because they have abandoned the LORD to keep to prostitution. ¹¹ Whoredom and wine and new wine take away the heart. ¹² My people consult their wooden idols, and their staffs speak to them, for a spirit of prostitution has led them astray, and they have fornicated away from their God. ¹³ They offer sacrifices on the tops of the mountains, they burn incense on the hills, under the terebinth, the oak, and the ash tree—because its shade is pleasant. Therefore your daughters will fornicate, and your brides will commit adultery. ¹⁴ I will not punish your daughters when they fornicate, nor your brides when they commit adultery, for they have gone with the harlots and sacrificed with the temple prostitutes, and their people do not understand, they will be entangled. ¹⁵ If you commit adultery, O Israel, let not Judah be found guilty, and let them not go to Gilgal, nor enter Beth-aven, nor swear, "As the LORD lives." ¹⁶ For like a stubborn heifer, Israel has turned away. Now the LORD will pasture him like a lamb in a broad place. ¹⁷ Ephraim has become a companion to idols; let him remain under his guilt. ¹⁸ His strength has been used up, he has fornicated. He has loved disgrace, and his disgrace is in his shield. ¹⁹ A wind has turned his mind back, and they will be put to shame because of their sacrifices.

---

Notes  
- v. 13: "ash tree" — Hebrew "ela" (אלָה), traditionally "ash," though some suggest "cypress" or "evergreen." Chosen "ash" as most stable identification.  
- v. 15: "Beth-aven" — literal translation of Beth-aven (בֵּית עָוֶן), meaning "house of iniquity," rather than the traditional "Bethel" (house of God), which is a theological reinterpretation.  
- v. 17: "companion to idols" — Hebrew "ḥaber eidlîm" (חָבֵר עֲצַבִּים), translated literally as "companion to idols." Some versions use "alliance with idols."  
- v. 18: "his disgrace is in his shield" — "magen" (מָגִנֶּה) is "shield," but here it may refer metaphorically to a visible emblem or symbol of disgrace.  
- v. 19: "wind has turned his mind back" — "tzarar ruah" (צָרַר רוּחַ) is a poetic construction; "wind" (ruah) as spirit or breath, here metaphorically as the divine breath or influence.
