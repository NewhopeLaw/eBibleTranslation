# Hosea 2

¹ The number of the people of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or numbered. In the place where it is said to them, "You are not my people," it will be said to them, "Children of the living God." ² The people of Judah and the people of Israel will be gathered together and will be given a single leader. They will rise from the land, for the day of Jezreel will be great. ³ Say to your brothers, "You are my people," and to your sisters, "You are shown mercy." ⁴ Quarrel with your mother, quarrel, for she is not my wife and I am not her husband. I will remove her prostitution from her face and her adultery from between her breasts. ⁵ Lest I strip her bare and expose her as on the day she was born, and make her like a wasteland and parch the land like a dry region, and kill her through thirst. ⁶ I will not show mercy to her children, for they are children of prostitution. ⁷ For her mother has committed prostitution, her mother who bore her has shamed herself, saying, "I will go after my lovers who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink." ⁸ Therefore, behold, I will block her path with thorns and wall up her enclosure so that her paths cannot be found. ⁹ She will pursue her lovers but will not overtake them, and she will seek them but will not find them. Then she will say, "I will go back to my first husband, for it was better with me then than now." ¹⁰ Yet she does not know that I gave her the grain and the wine and the oil, and I multiplied silver for her. Yet she used the silver and the gold to make a Baal. ¹¹ Therefore, I will return and take my grain at its proper time and my wine at its season, and I will remove her wool and her flax, which cover her nakedness. ¹² Now I will expose her indecency in the sight of her lovers, and no one will rescue her from my hand. ¹³ And I will end all her joy, her festivals, her new moons, and her Sabbaths, and all her appointed times. ¹⁴ And I will destroy her vineyard and her fig trees, which she said, "These are my wages, which my lovers gave me." I will make them a forest, and wild animals will eat them. ¹⁵ I will call to mind the days of her lovers, the time when she used to dress for them and adorned herself with her nose rings and her ornaments, and walk after her lovers, forgetting me, declares the LORD. ¹⁶ Therefore, behold, I will lead her into the desert and speak to her heart. ¹⁷ I will give her her vineyards from there, and the valley of Achor as a gate of hope. There she will respond as in the days of her youth, as in the time when she came up from the land of Egypt. ¹⁸ It will be in that day, declares the LORD, that you will call me "My husband," and you will not call me "My Baal." ¹⁹ I will remove the names of the lovers from her mouth, and they will be remembered no more by name. ²⁰ I will make a covenant with them in that day with the beasts of the field and the birds of the sky and the creeping things of the earth. I will break the bow and the sword and war from the land and will let them lie in safety. ²¹ I will betroth you to me forever; I will betroth you to me in righteousness and in justice, in steadfast love and in mercy. ²² I will betroth you to me in faithfulness, and you will know the LORD. ²³ It will be in that day, declares the LORD, that I will respond to the heavens, and they will respond to the earth. ²⁴ And the earth will respond to the grain, the wine, and the oil, and they will respond to Jezreel. ²⁵ I will sow her for myself in the land, and I will show mercy to the one who was not shown mercy, and I will say to the one who was not my people, "You are my people," and they will say, "You are my God."

---

Notes
- v. 12: "indecency" reflects the root meaning of "nivlat" (נַבְלָתָהּ), which can also imply moral corruption or disgrace.
- v. 13: "new moons" and "Sabbaths" are retained as fixed terms for religious observances, though the Hebrew could imply more general joyous times.
- v. 19: "Baal" pluralized as "Baalim" is rendered as "my Baal" to retain the singular form in the context of personal betrayal.
- v. 25: "Not My People" (Lo 'Ami) is not translated directly but reflected in the meaning through the phrase "one who was not my people."
- v. 21: "betroth" is used to mirror the metaphor of marriage in the covenant, avoiding the more modern-sounding "marry" which might dilute the symbolic weight.
