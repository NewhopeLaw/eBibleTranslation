# Hosea 9

¹ Do not rejoice, O Israel, like the nations, for you have committed infidelity, having abandoned your God. You cherished gifts over every grain of wheat. ² Neither grain nor wine will nourish them, for the wine has deceived them. ³ They will not dwell in the land of the LORD. Ephraim will return to Egypt, and in Assyria they will eat unclean food. ⁴ They will not offer wine to the LORD, nor will they make pleasing sacrifices to him. Their sacrifices are like bread of mourning. All who eat them will be defiled, for their bread is for their own lives, and it will not enter the house of the LORD. ⁵ What will you do on the day of the festival, on the day of the LORD’s feast? ⁶ For behold, they will go from bondage; Egypt will receive them, and Memphis will bury them. Their treasures will be taken by plunderers, and thorns will rise in their tents. ⁷ The days of reckoning have come, the days of repayment. Israel will be afflicted like a foolish prophet, a man of spirit, because of the abundance of your sins and the multitude of your deceit. ⁸ Ephraim watches for his gods; a prophet has set a trap for all his ways, with madness in the house of his god. ⁹ They have deepened their ruin, like the days of Gibeah. He will remember their iniquity and punish their sins. ¹⁰ I found Israel as a cluster of grapes in the wilderness, like the first fig on a fig tree. I saw your ancestors as they came to Baal-peor, and they defiled themselves by joining the prostitute and became detestable as those they loved. ¹¹ Ephraim is like a bird that flaps its wings—glory from birth, from the womb, and from the time of their first cries. ¹² If they rear their children, I will bereave them. Woe to them! I am the flesh of their flesh. ¹³ Ephraim, as I saw the women of Tzor planting a vineyard, so I will bring destruction upon Ephraim, and I will send out their children to slaughter. ¹⁴ Give them, O LORD, what you choose to give—give them a barren womb and dry breasts. ¹⁵ All their evil is in Gilgal, for there I began to hate them because of the wickedness of their deeds. From my house I will drive them; I will no longer love them. All their rulers are rebels. ¹⁶ Ephraim is struck at the root—its branches have dried up. It will bear no fruit; even if it bears, I will kill the desires of its womb. ¹⁷ My God rejects them, for they did not listen to him. They will wander among the nations.

---

Notes
- v. 11: "from the time of their first cries" translates "מֵהֵרָיֽוֹן" as a poetic reference to infancy and early cries.
- v. 13: "the women of Tzor planting a vineyard" is a metaphor for Ephraim's own destructive choices. The reference to Tzor (Sor) is a poetic allusion, not a direct historical reference.
- v. 15: "the wickedness of their deeds" translates "עַל רֹעַ מַעַלְלֵיהֶם," where "מַעַלְלֵיהֶם" is a rare term meaning "deeds" or "actions."
- v. 16: "the desires of its womb" translates "מַחֲמַדֵּי בִטְנָֽם," where "מַחֲמַדֵּי" means "desires" or "longings."
