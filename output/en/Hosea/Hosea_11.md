# Hosea 11

¹ Israel was a child, and I loved him. From Egypt I called him, My son. ² Just as I called them, so they turned away from My presence. They sacrificed to the Baals and offered incense to idols. ³ Yet I trained Ephraim, carried him on My arms. They did not know that I was healing them. ⁴ I drew them with human ties, with cords of love. I became for them like one who lifts a yoke to their cheeks, and I would feed them. ⁵ They will not return to the land of Egypt, for Assyria is their king, because they refused to return. ⁶ A sword will consume in their cities, and devour in their strongholds. It will eat from their own counsel. ⁷ My people are bound for exile, and their glory will be brought low, never again to be lifted. ⁸ How can I give you, O Ephraim? How can I guard you, O Israel? How can I give you, like Adamah or like Sepharim? My heart has turned from them, and my compassion has been taken away. ⁹ I will not execute my fierce anger; I will not again destroy Ephraim, for I am God and not a man, holy in your midst, and I will not enter a city. ¹⁰ They will follow after the LORD, like a lion roaring, for He Himself will roar. And the children will tremble from the sea. ¹¹ They will tremble like a bird from Egypt, like a dove from the land of Assyria, and I will settle them in their homes, says the LORD.

---

Notes
- v. 8: "Adamah" and "Sepharim" are transliterated from Hebrew names (עַדְמָה and צְבַיִם) but are not otherwise glossed in the glossary. These are left in transliteration as they are proper names referring to locations or symbolic places.
