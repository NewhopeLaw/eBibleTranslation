# Zechariah 3

¹ The Lord showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord, and the adversary standing at his right hand to oppose him. ² The Lord said to the adversary, "May the Lord rebuke you, adversary! Yes, the Lord who has chosen Jerusalem — is this one not a burning coal snatched from the fire?" ³ Now Joshua was dressed in soiled garments and was standing before the angel. ⁴ The angel replied and said to those standing before him, "Remove the soiled garments from him." Then he said to Joshua, "I have taken your iniquity away from you, and I am clothing you with festive garments." ⁵ Then they said, "Place a clean turban on his head," and they placed the clean turban on his head and clothed him. The angel of the Lord was standing there. ⁶ The angel of the Lord then addressed Joshua, saying: ⁷ "This is what the Lord of Hosts says: If you will walk in my ways and keep my commands, then you will judge my house and guard my courts. I will give you free movement among these who are standing here." ⁸ "Now listen, Joshua the high priest. You and the men sitting before you — you are men who are watched by the Lord, for I am bringing my servant the Sprout." ⁹ "For behold, the stone that I have placed before Joshua — on a single stone are seven eyes. I myself will dig a trench, says the Lord of Hosts, and I will root out all the guilt of that land in a single day." ¹⁰ "On that day, says the Lord of Hosts, each of you will call your neighbor under the vine and under the fig tree."

---

Notes
- v. 9: "I myself will dig a trench" — the verb "pithach" is used metaphorically for opening or exposing, here in a context of judgment. The rendering follows the sense of revealing or rooting out sin.
- v. 8: "the Sprout" — the Hebrew "tsemach" is a rare term, often translated as "Branch" or "Sprout" in other versions. It is used here to refer to a messianic figure.
