# Zechariah 8

¹ The word of the LORD of Hosts came, saying: ² This is what the LORD says: I have a great zeal for Zion, a great jealousy for her. ³ This is what the LORD says: I will return to Zion and dwell in the midst of Jerusalem. And Jerusalem will be called the city of truth, and the mountain of the LORD of Hosts will be the holy mountain. ⁴ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Elders and elder women will sit again in the streets of Jerusalem, each holding his staff in his hand because of many days. ⁵ And the streets of the city will be filled with children and infants playing in its streets. ⁶ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: In the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, things will seem strange. And in my eyes, they will also seem strange, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁷ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: I will rescue my people from the land of the east and from the land of the rising sun. ⁸ And I will bring them, and they will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem. And they will be to me a people, and I will be to them a God in truth and in righteousness. ⁹ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Let the hands of those who hear these words in these days from the mouths of the prophets be strong — the ones who were present when the foundation of the house of the LORD of Hosts was laid, when the temple was begun to be built. ¹⁰ For before those days, the wages of the human will not be given, and the wages of the livestock will not be found. And for the one going out and the one coming in, there will be no peace from the enemy. And I will send every human into his neighbor. ¹¹ But now, I will not do to the remnant of this people as in the former days, says the LORD of Hosts. ¹² For I will give peace. The vine will give its fruit, and the earth will give its produce, and the heavens will give their dew. And I will give the remnant of this people all these things. ¹³ And it will be, as you were a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Do not be afraid. Be strong in your hands. ¹⁴ For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: Just as I resolved to do evil to you when I was provoked by your fathers, says the LORD of Hosts, and I did not relent. ¹⁵ So now I have resolved and planned in these days to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah. Do not be afraid. ¹⁶ These are the things you are to do: speak truth to one another, judgment and peace you will judge in your gates. ¹⁷ And do not let each one plan evil in his heart against his neighbor. And do not love a false oath, for all these things I have hated, says the LORD. ¹⁸ And the word of the LORD of Hosts came to me, saying: ¹⁹ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: The fourth fast, the fifth fast, the seventh fast, and the tenth fast will become for the house of Judah a time of rejoicing and a time of joy and good festivals. And you must love truth and peace. ²⁰ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Again nations will come, and people from many cities. ²¹ And inhabitants of one city will go to another, saying, “Let us go to seek the face of the LORD and to seek the LORD of Hosts. I too will go.” ²² And many peoples and mighty nations will come to seek the face of the LORD of Hosts in Jerusalem and to seek the face of the LORD. ²³ This is what the LORD of Hosts says: In those days, ten men from every language of the nations will take hold of the corner of a Judean man, saying, “Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.”

---

Notes
- v. 14: "I will not relent" — the Hebrew uses "lo nichamti" (לֹא נִחַמְתִּי), a clear reference to not showing compassion or regret, consistent with the idea of not relenting in judgment.
- v. 23: "the corner of a Judean man" — the Hebrew uses "kenaf ish yehudi" (כְּנַף אִישׁ יְהוּדִי), which literally refers to the corner of a man's garment, a common metaphor for seeking protection or guidance.
