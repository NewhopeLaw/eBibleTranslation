# Zechariah 4

¹ The angel who was speaking with me stirred me awake, like a man roused from his sleep. ² He asked me, “What do you see?” I replied, “I see a golden lampstand entirely of gold, with a reservoir on its top, and seven lamps on it — seven and seven oil pipes for the lamps on the top.” ³ There were also two olive trees, one on the right side of the reservoir and one on its left. ⁴ I asked the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?” ⁵ The angel who was speaking with me answered me, “Do you not know what these are?” I said, “No, my lord.” ⁶ He replied, “This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: 'It is not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit,' says the LORD of Hosts. ⁷ Who are you, O great mountain before Zerubbabel? Before long you will be leveled. Then bring the stone to the builder, and it will be laid with a shout of ‘Grace, grace!’ for it.” ⁸ Then the word of the LORD came to me: ⁹ “The hands of Zerubbabel have founded this house, and his hands will complete it. Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent me to you. ¹⁰ For who dares despise the days of small things? They will rejoice and see the stone in Zerubbabel’s hand — these seven are the eyes of the LORD, which roam through the whole earth.” ¹¹ I asked him again, “What are these two olive trees on the right and left of the lampstand?” ¹² I asked a second time, “What are the two oil branches flowing from the two golden spouts that pour out the oil?” ¹³ He said to me, “Do you not know what these are?” I replied, “No, my lord.” ¹⁴ He said to me, “These are the two sons of Oil who stand before the Lord of the whole earth.”

---

Notes
- v. 7: “Grace, grace for it” reflects the Hebrew שִׁבְעוֹת חֵן חֵן לָהּ, where the doubling of חֵן (grace) is preserved in a natural English expression.
- v. 14: “the two sons of Oil” renders “בני־הַיִּצְהָר” — the name יִצְהָר (Yitzer) is traditionally linked to the Hebrew for “oil,” but the exact meaning is uncertain. “Oil” is taken as a metaphor for the olive trees and their nourishing function.
