# Zechariah 2

¹ I raised my eyes and saw — behold, four horns. ² I asked the angel speaking to me, "What are these?" He said to me, "These are the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem." ³ Then the Lord showed me four craftsmen. ⁴ I asked, "What are these coming to do?" He said, "These are the craftsmen who have counteracted the horns that scattered Judah. Each of them struck down a horn of the nations that had raised their horns against the land of Judah to scatter it." ⁵ I raised my eyes again and saw — behold, a man holding a measuring line. ⁶ I asked, "Where are you going?" He said to me, "To measure Jerusalem to see how wide and how long it is." ⁷ Then the angel speaking to me was going out, and another angel was coming out to meet him. ⁸ He said to him, "Run and tell that young man, 'Jerusalem will be inhabited in fullness, with many people and cattle in its midst.'" ⁹ I myself will be its wall of fire all around, and I will be its glory within," says the Lord. ¹⁰ "Ho! Ho! Flee from the land of the north," says the Lord. "For I am scattering you in all the four winds of heaven," says the Lord. ¹¹ "Ho, Zion! Escape, you who dwell in the daughter of Babylon." ¹² For thus says the Lord of Hosts: "After glory I have sent me to the nations that have plundered you, for whoever touches you touches the apple of my eye." ¹³ "For behold, I will lift my hand against them, and they will become plunder for their servants. And you will know that the Lord of Hosts has sent me to you." ¹⁴ "Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Zion, for behold, I am coming and I will dwell in your midst," says the Lord. ¹⁵ "Many nations will come to the Lord on that day, and they will become my people. I will dwell among you, and you will know that the Lord of Hosts has sent me to you." ¹⁶ "The Lord will claim Judah as his inheritance; it is his portion on the holy land. And he will yet choose Jerusalem again." ¹⁷ "Let all flesh tremble before the Lord, for he has stirred himself from his holy dwelling."

---

Notes
- v. 4: The Hebrew uses "לִקְרָאתֽוֹ" for "to meet him," which can mean "to face him" or "to confront him." The context of divine judgment suggests "to confront him" is more appropriate.
- v. 17: "מִמְּעוֹן קָדְשֽׁוֹ" is translated as "his holy dwelling" to reflect the source text's reference to the Lord's sacred place.
