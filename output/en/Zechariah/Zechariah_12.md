# Zechariah 12

¹ A burden of the word of the LORD concerning Israel. Thus says the LORD, the one who spreads out the heavens and establishes the earth and forms the breath of the human in his inward parts. ² I will make Jerusalem a trembling threshold for all the peoples around, and a siege shall be set upon Judah for Jerusalem. ³ And it will come to pass in that day that I will make Jerusalem a stone of stumbling for all the peoples. Every one who lifts it will be wounded, and all the nations of the earth will gather together against it. ⁴ In that day, says the LORD, I will strike every horse in confusion and its rider in madness, and I will open my eyes upon the house of Judah, and all the horses of the peoples I will strike with blindness. ⁵ And the commanders of Judah will say in their hearts, “Let us seek shelter with the inhabitants of Jerusalem in the LORD of Hosts, the God of them.” ⁶ In that day I will make the commanders of Judah like a fire in the woods and like a torch in the stack of grain, and they will devour on the right and on the left all the peoples around, and Jerusalem will again dwell securely in Jerusalem. ⁷ And the LORD will save the tents of Judah first, that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem may not be exalted over Judah. ⁸ In that day the LORD will defend the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and the one who stumbles among them in that day will be like David, and the house of David will be like God, like an angel of the LORD before them. ⁹ And it will come to pass in that day that I will seek to destroy every nation that comes upon Jerusalem. ¹⁰ And I will pour out on the house of David and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and of supplication, and they will look to me, the one they have pierced, and they will mourn for him as one mourns for an only son, and they will grieve for him as one grieves for a firstborn. ¹¹ In that day the mourning in Jerusalem will be great as the mourning of Hadad-rimmon in the valley of Megiddon. ¹² The land will mourn, family by family. The family of the house of David alone, and their wives alone, the family of the house of Nathan alone, and their wives alone, ¹³ the family of the house of Levi alone, and their wives alone, the family of the house of Shimei alone, and their wives alone, ¹⁴ all the families that remain — family by family alone, and their wives alone.

---

Notes
- v. 11: "Hadad-rimmon" is a known ancient mourning event, but its specific context is uncertain; the name is preserved as in the Hebrew.
