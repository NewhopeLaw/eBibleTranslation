# Zechariah 9

¹ A burden of the word of the LORD in the land of Sedir and Damascus — its rest is near, for the LORD looks upon humanity and all the tribes of Israel. ² And Hamath — her boundary will be cut off — as well as Tyre and Sidon, because they are exceedingly wise. ³ Tyre built herself a fortress and stored silver like dust and gold like clay of the roads. ⁴ Therefore the LORD will claim her as his inheritance and strike the strength of her power at the sea — and she will be consumed in fire. ⁵ Ashkelon will see and fear, and Gaza will feel great pain, and Ekron — because her gaze has grown haughty — and her king will perish. Ashkelon will not remain. ⁶ And a foreigner will dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines. ⁷ I will remove the blood from their mouth and their abominations from between their teeth. Even they will be left for our God, and they will become like a commander in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusite. ⁸ And I will set a safeguard for my house, so that the one going out and the one returning will not pass through again. No more will an aggressor come upon them, for now I have seen it with my own eyes. ⁹ Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion! Shout, daughter of Jerusalem! Behold, your King is coming to you — righteous and a savior, humble and riding on a donkey, and on a colt, the foal of a donkey. ¹⁰ And I will remove chariots from Ephraim and horses from Jerusalem. The bow of war will be broken, and I will speak peace to the nations. He will rule from sea to sea and from the river to the ends of the earth. ¹¹ Also you — through the blood of your covenant I have released your prisoners from the pit, which had no water in it. ¹² Return, prisoners of hope. Today I will restore to you double. ¹³ For I have bent for myself a bow for Judah, I have filled Ephraim, and I have stirred your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and I have made you like a warrior’s sword. ¹⁴ And the LORD will look upon them and send forth his arrow like lightning. And the Lord of Hosts will blow the trumpet and go forth in the storms of his might. ¹⁵ The LORD of Hosts will protect them. They will eat and devour flintstones and drink wine like it is water, and they will be filled as with a ladle from the sacrifices. ¹⁶ And the LORD their God will save them on that day as a flock of his people, for holy stones will roll on his land. ¹⁷ For what is good and what is beautiful — grain for the young men, and wine for the virgins, fragrant and flowing in abundance.

---

Notes
- v. 10: "from sea to sea and from the river to the ends of the earth" — refers to the geographical expanse of the promised dominion, interpreted as the breadth of the ancient Near East.
- v. 13: "your sons, Greece" — a reference to a potential future conflict between Judah and the Greek world, possibly influenced by Hellenistic context.
- v. 15: "they will eat and devour flintstones" — metaphorical language for the people being empowered and strengthened by divine blessing.
