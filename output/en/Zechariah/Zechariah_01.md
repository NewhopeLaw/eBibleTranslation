# Zechariah 1

¹ In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo the prophet, saying: ² The LORD was greatly angry with your ancestors. ³ Say to them: This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Return to me, says the LORD of Hosts, and I will return to you, says the LORD of Hosts. ⁴ Do not be like your ancestors, to whom the earlier prophets called out, saying, "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Turn back now from your evil ways and your evil deeds." But they did not listen, nor did they pay attention to me, says the LORD. ⁵ Where are your ancestors now? And will the prophets live forever? ⁶ Yet the words of the LORD and his decrees, which I commanded my servants the prophets, did they not reach your ancestors? They turned and said, "Just as the LORD of Hosts had planned to do to us, so it has happened to us." ⁷ On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo the prophet, saying: ⁸ In the night I saw a man riding a red horse, standing among the myrtle trees in the valley. Behind him were red, sorrel, and white horses. ⁹ I asked, "What are these, my lord?" The angel who spoke to me said, "I will show you what these are." ¹⁰ The man standing among the myrtle trees answered, "These are the ones the LORD has sent to patrol the earth." ¹¹ They answered the angel of the LORD, who stood among the myrtle trees, saying, "We have walked through the land, and behold, the whole land is at rest and quiet." ¹² The angel of the LORD replied, "Lord of Hosts, how long will you not have compassion on Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, which you have been angry with for seventy years?" ¹³ The LORD answered the angel who was speaking to me with kind and comforting words. ¹⁴ Then the angel who was speaking to me said, "Declare this: This is what the LORD of Hosts says: I have a great jealousy for Jerusalem and a great anger toward the nations that have done evil, even though I was only slightly angry with them." ¹⁵ "A great anger I am angry with the nations that have provoked me, for they have only stirred up more evil." ¹⁶ "Therefore, this is what the LORD says: I will look on Jerusalem with mercy. My house will be rebuilt there, says the LORD of Hosts, and a measuring line will be stretched over Jerusalem." ¹⁷ "Moreover, declare this: This is what the LORD of Hosts says: My cities will yet spread with good things, and the LORD will yet have compassion on Zion and will choose Jerusalem again."

---

Notes
- v. 3: The phrase "the LORD of Hosts" is used consistently for "יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת".
- v. 12: "seventy years" represents a symbolic period of divine judgment, not necessarily a literal count.
- v. 13: The word "kind and comforting" is used for the Hebrew "דְּבָרִים טוֹבִים דְּבָרִים נִחֻמִֽים", which captures both the positive and consoling nature of the message.
