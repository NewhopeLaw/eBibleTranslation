# Zechariah 6

¹ I turned and raised my eyes and saw, and behold, four chariots were coming out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of bronze. ² In the first chariot were red horses, and in the second chariot were black horses. ³ In the third chariot were white horses, and in the fourth chariot were dappled horses, like speckled ones. ⁴ I replied and said to the angel speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?” ⁵ The angel answered and said to me, “These are the four winds of the heavens, coming out to stand before the Lord of the whole earth.” ⁶ “In which the black horses go out toward the land of the north, and the white ones follow behind them, and the dappled ones go out toward the land of the south, ⁷ and the speckled ones go out, seeking to patrol the earth. And they said, ‘Go, patrol the earth,’ and they patrolled the earth.” ⁸ Then he cried out to me and said, “See the ones going out toward the land of the north. They have laid to rest my spirit in the land of the north.” ⁹ Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying, ¹⁰ “Take from the captives, from Heldai, from Tobijah, and from Jedaiah. You will go in that day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah who has come from Babylon. ¹¹ You will take silver and gold, and you will make crowns, and you will set them on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest. ¹² And you will say to him, ‘Thus says the Lord of Hosts: Here is a man, a shoot, is his name, and from beneath him a shoot will grow, and he will build the house of the Lord. ¹³ And he will build the house of the Lord, and he will bear glory, and he will sit and rule on his throne. And a priest will be at his right hand, and a counsel of peace will be between the two of them.’” ¹⁴ The crowns will be a sign for Heldai, for Tobijah, and for Jedaiah, and for the son of Zephaniah as a reminder in the house of the Lord. ¹⁵ And those who are far off will come and build in the house of the Lord, and you will know that the Lord of Hosts has sent me to you. And it will be, if you listen to the voice of the Lord your God, it will be well with you.

---

Notes
- v. 12: “a shoot, is his name” reflects the Hebrew “tzemach” (צֶמַח) as both noun and name, meaning “shoot” or “branch.”
- v. 13: The phrase “a counsel of peace will be between the two of them” reflects the Hebrew “va’etsat shalom tihyeh bein shneyhem,” which combines the idea of a shared plan and harmony.
- v. 14: “a sign for Heldai, for Tobijah, and for Jedaiah” translates the Hebrew “l’chelum u’l’toviyah u’l’yeida,” which uses poetic parallelism and metaphor typical of prophetic language.
