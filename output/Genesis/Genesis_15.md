# Genesis 15

¹ After these events, the word of the LORD came to Abram in a vision, saying, "Do not fear, Abram. I am your shield, and your reward will be exceedingly great." ² Abram said, "O Lord GOD, what will you give me, since I continue childless, and the heir of my household is Eliezer of Damascus?" ³ Abram added, "You have given me no offspring, and so a servant born in my house will inherit me." ⁴ But the word of the LORD came to him, "This one will not inherit you. No, the one who will come from your own body will inherit you." ⁵ He took him outside and said, "Look now toward the heavens and count the stars, if you are able to count them." He said to him, "So shall your offspring be." ⁶ Abram believed the LORD, and he counted it to him as righteousness. ⁷ He said to him, "I am the LORD who brought you from Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to possess it." ⁸ Abram asked, "O Lord GOD, by what will I know that I will possess it?" ⁹ He said to him, "Take for me a heifer three years old, a female goat three years old, a ram three years old, a turtledove, and a young pigeon." ¹⁰ Abram took all these, cut them in two, and placed each half opposite the other. But he did not cut the birds. ¹¹ Birds of prey descended on the carcasses, and Abram drove them away. ¹² The sun was about to set, and a deep sleep fell upon Abram. A dark and terrifying shadow passed over him. ¹³ He said to Abram, "Know this for certain: your descendants will be strangers in a land not theirs. They will serve and be oppressed there for four hundred years. ¹⁴ But I will judge the nation they serve, and afterward they will go out with great possessions. ¹⁵ As for you, you will go to your fathers in peace. You will be buried at a good old age." ¹⁶ But in the fourth generation they will return here, for the sin of the Amorites is not yet complete." ¹⁷ The sun had set, and it was dark. A smoking furnace and a flaming torch passed between the pieces. ¹⁸ On that day the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, "To your offspring I give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the Euphrates: ¹⁹ the Kenite, the Kenezzite, and the Kadmonite, ²⁰ the Hittite, the Perizzite, and the Rephaim, ²¹ the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Girgashite, and the Jebusite."

---

Notes
- v. 8: "By what will I know" — the use of "בַּמָּה" is idiomatic, often rendered as "by what" or "in what way."
- v. 16: "the sin of the Amorites is not yet complete" — the Hebrew uses "עֲוֺן" (iniquity/sin) and "שָׁלֵם" (complete/satisfied), indicating a moral or judicial delay.
- v. 17: "a smoking furnace and a flaming torch passed between the pieces" — the imagery is poetic and symbolic, representing the seal of the covenant.
