# Genesis 14

¹ In the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Kedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of the Goiim, ² they made war against Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, and Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, that is, Zoar. ³ All of these joined together in the Valley of Siddim, which is the Salt Sea. ⁴ They had served Kedorlaomer for twelve years, but in the thirteenth year they rebelled. ⁵ And in the fourteenth year, Kedorlaomer came with the kings who were with him, and they defeated the Rephaim in Ashteroth-Karnaim, the Zuzim in Ham, the Emim in the Valley of Kiriathaim, ⁶ the Horites in Mount Hermon, Seir, as far as the region of the Arabah, which is in the wilderness. ⁷ They returned and came to Eyn Mishpat, which is Kadesh, and they defeated all the land of the Amalekites, and also the Amorites who lived in Hazazon Tamar. ⁸ Then the king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, that is, Zoar, went out and made war in the Valley of Siddim, ⁹ against Kedorlaomer king of Elam, Tidal king of the Goiim, Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar—four kings against five. ¹⁰ The Valley of Siddim had pools of bitumen. The kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled and fell there, while the survivors escaped to the hills. ¹¹ They took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and their food and went away. ¹² They also took Lot, the nephew of Abram, along with his possessions, for he was living in Sodom. ¹³ A fugitive came and reported to Abram the Hebrew, who was living among the terebinth trees of Mamre the Amorite, a brother of Eshkol and of Aner, both of whom were allied to Abram. ¹⁴ When Abram heard that his brother had been captured, he led out his trained men, born in his household, 318 in all, and pursued them to Dan. ¹⁵ During the night, he and his men divided among them and attacked, routing them and chasing them as far as Hobah, which is to the left of Damascus. ¹⁶ He recovered all the goods and also rescued his brother Lot and his possessions, along with the women and the people. ¹⁷ The king of Sodom went out to meet him after his return from defeating Kedorlaomer and the kings who were with him, at the Valley of the King, which is the Valley of Shaveh. ¹⁸ Melchizedek, king of Salem, brought out bread and wine. He was a priest of God Most High. ¹⁹ He blessed him and said, "Blessed be Abram by God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth. ²⁰ And blessed be God Most High, who has delivered your enemies into your hand." Abram gave him a tenth of everything. ²¹ The king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the people, and take the goods for yourself." ²² Abram replied to the king of Sodom, "I have lifted my hand to the LORD, God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth, ²³ that I will not take so much as a thread or a sandal strap from what is yours, lest you say, ‘I have made Abram rich.’" ²⁴ "Except for what the young men have eaten, and the share of the men who went with me—Anar, Eshkol, and Mamre. Let them take their share."

Notes
- v. 23: "So much as a thread or a sandal strap" renders מִחוּט וְעַד שְׂרֽוֹךְ־נַעַל literally and idiomatically, capturing the idea of taking even the smallest item.
- v. 23: "I have made Abram rich" is a paraphrase of the implied meaning of the Hebrew, not a direct translation of any specific phrase.
