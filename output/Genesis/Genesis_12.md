# Genesis 12

¹ The LORD said to Abram, "Go forth from your land, from your birthplace, and from your father's house to the land that I will show you. ² I will make you into a great nation, and I will bless you, and I will make your name great. You will be a source of blessing. ³ I will bless those who bless you and curse those who curse you, and through you, all the families of the earth will be blessed." ⁴ Abram went as the LORD had spoken to him, and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran. ⁵ Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his nephew, along with all their possessions and all the people they had acquired in Haran. They journeyed to the land of Canaan and arrived there. ⁶ Abram passed through the land as far as the site of Shechem, to the terebinth of Moreh. At that time, the Canaanites were already in the land. ⁷ The LORD appeared to Abram and said, "I will give this land to your descendants." There Abram built an altar to the LORD who had appeared to him. ⁸ Then he moved from there into the hill country east of Bethel and pitched his tent—Bethel to the west and Ai to the east. There he built an altar to the LORD and called upon the name of the LORD. ⁹ Abram continued to travel, journeying southward. ¹⁰ A famine struck the land, and Abram went down to Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine was severe in the land. ¹¹ When he was about to enter Egypt, he said to his wife Sarai, "I know that you are a very beautiful woman. ¹² When the Egyptians see you, they will say, 'This is his wife,' and they may kill me, but they will let you live. ¹³ Please say you are my sister, so that it may go well with me because of you, and so that I may live because of you." ¹⁴ When Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful. ¹⁵ Pharaoh's officials saw her and praised her highly to Pharaoh, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's house. ¹⁶ Because of her, Pharaoh treated Abram well, giving him flocks and herds, male and female donkeys, male and female slaves, and camels. ¹⁷ But the LORD sent a great plague upon Pharaoh and his house because of Sarai, Abram's wife. ¹⁸ Pharaoh called Abram and said, "What have you done to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife? ¹⁹ Why did you say, 'She is my sister,' so that I took her as my wife? Now then, take your wife and go." ²⁰ Pharaoh gave orders to his men, and they sent Abram away—with his wife and all that he had.

Notes
- v. 1: "Go forth from your land" — "לֶךְ־לְךָ" is a rare and emphatic expression, often translated as "Go for yourself" or "Go out to yourself," but here rendered as "Go forth" to preserve the intensity and direction of the divine command.
- v. 3: "curse those who curse you" — the Hebrew uses "אָאֹר" from "עָרַר," meaning to curse or speak against; the translation mirrors the parallel structure of the blessing.
- v. 13: "Please say you are my sister" — the Hebrew "אֲחֹתִי" is "my sister"; Abram asks Sarai to lie about their relationship.
- v. 17: "a great plague" — the Hebrew "נְגָעִים גְּדֹלִים" is literally "great plagues" or "serious afflictions," translated as "a great plague" to maintain clarity and conciseness.
