# Genesis 10

¹ These are the descendants of the sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. They each had sons after the flood. ² The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ³ The sons of Gomer were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. ⁴ The sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim. ⁵ From these the island-dwelling nations separated, each to their own lands, by their language, in their families, by their nations. ⁶ The sons of Ham were Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan. ⁷ The sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan. ⁸ Cush fathered Nimrod, who became a mighty man on the earth. ⁹ He was a mighty hunter before the LORD. That is why it is said, “Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before the LORD.” ¹⁰ The beginning of his kingdom was Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar. ¹¹ From that land, Assyria went out and built Nineveh, Rehoboth-Ir, and Calah. ¹² And Resen, between Nineveh and Calah—that is the great city. ¹³ The sons of Egypt were Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphthuhim, ¹⁴ Pathrusim, Casluhim (from whom the Philistines came), and Caphtorim. ¹⁵ Canaan fathered Sidon his firstborn, and Heth, ¹⁶ and the Jebusites, the Amorites, and the Girgashites, ¹⁷ the Hivites, the Arkites, and the Sinites, ¹⁸ the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. Later, the clans of the Canaanites dispersed. ¹⁹ The boundary of the Canaanites extended from Sidon as far as Gerar, to Gaza, as far as Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, as far as Lasha. ²⁰ These are the sons of Ham, by their families, by their languages, in their lands, by their nations. ²¹ Shem, too, had sons born to him, and he became the father of all the sons of Eber, the older brother of Japheth. ²² The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram. ²³ The sons of Aram were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash. ²⁴ Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber. ²⁵ To Eber were born two sons. The name of one was Peleg, for in his time the earth was divided. His brother’s name was Joktan. ²⁶ Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, ²⁷ Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, ²⁸ Obal, Abimael, and Sheba, ²⁹ Ofir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan. ³⁰ Their settlements extended from Mesha as far as Sephar, the mountain east of the desert. ³¹ These are the sons of Shem, by their families, by their languages, in their lands, by their nations. ³² These are the families of the sons of Noah, by their generations, by their nations. From these the nations dispersed on the earth after the flood.

Notes
- v. 5: "island-dwelling nations" reflects "אִיֵּי הַגּוֹיִם" (islands of the nations), referring to seafaring or coastal peoples.
- v. 9: "before the LORD" is likely metaphorical, referring to being recognized or feared by people rather than a divine favor.
- v. 15: "Canaan fathered Sidon his firstborn" — "firstborn" is added for clarity, though Hebrew does not use the word for "firstborn" (בְּכֹר) here; it is implied.
- v. 25: "Peleg" is interpreted as meaning "division," and the phrase "in his time the earth was divided" reflects the naming rationale in context.
- v. 30: "mountain east of the desert" is a contextual rendering of "הַקֶּֽדֶם" (the East), referring to the general region east of the Arabian desert.
