STYLE (ئۇيغۇرچە)
- Write Uyghur in the Uyghur Arabic script (UEY) only: never Latin or Cyrillic Uyghur, and never Turkish, Uzbek, Kazakh or Chinese words where Uyghur has its own. The text runs right to left; numbers and punctuation follow Uyghur usage (، ؛ ؟).
- Natural, clear modern Uyghur with a light literary tone; not the heavy Arabic and Persian loans of older religious prose where a plain Uyghur word says the same.
- Prose in flowing paragraphs; poetry line by line (one clause or couplet to a line).
- Faithful to the meaning; do not paraphrase freely, but do not copy Hebrew or Greek word order where it breaks Uyghur, which puts the verb last.
- Speech in « » quotation marks. Proper names as spelled in the name table.
- God addressing people uses سەن or سىلەر as the context asks; people addressing God are reverent but plain (سەن, as in prayer).

TERMINOLOGY (fixed renderings, consistent across the whole Bible)
- The name YHWH (יהוה) -> «پەرۋەردىگار». Elohim as the title of God -> «خۇدا». YHWH Elohim together -> «پەرۋەردىگار خۇدا». Adonai YHWH -> «رەب پەرۋەردىگار».
- ha-adam (הָאָדָם) -> «ئىنسان»; it does not become the name ئادەم until the narrative plainly uses it as a name.
- Genesis 1:1 follows the clause reading: «خۇدا ئاسمان بىلەن زېمىننى يارىتىشقا باشلىغاندا…»
- Kyrios (κύριος) -> «رەب» («پەرۋەردىگار» only where it plainly quotes an Old Testament YHWH text).
- Christos (χριστός) -> «مەسىھ» (as title and as name). Jesus -> «ئەيسا».
